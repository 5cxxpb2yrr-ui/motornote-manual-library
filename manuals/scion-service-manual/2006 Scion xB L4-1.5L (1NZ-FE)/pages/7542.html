DTC B1156/15 Front Airbag Sensor RH Malfunction
DTC B1157/15 Front Airbag Sensor RH Malfunction

DESCRIPTION

DTC Detection Condition:




The front airbag sensor RH circuit consists of the center airbag sensor assembly and the front airbag sensor RH. DTC B1156/15 or B1157/15 is recorded when a malfunction is detected in the front airbag sensor RH circuit.
