Initial Inspection

COMPRESSOR AND PULLEY

INSPECTION




1. INSPECT COMPRESSOR WITH PULLEY ASSEMBLY
a. Measure the resistance between terminals 1 and 2.

Standard resistance: 160 to 320 at 20 degrees C (68 degrees F)

If the value is not as specified, replace the compressor assembly.
