Service Category

Section

Applicability

Introduction

The purpose of this TSB is to provide precautions to follow when installing an automatic transmission/transaxle assembly to avoid MIL "ON;" shifting issues, and/or transmission damage. Use the information in this bulletin when installing an automatic transmission/transaxle assembly.
