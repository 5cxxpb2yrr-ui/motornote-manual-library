Engine - Engine Compartment Squealing Noise

ENGINE
EG031-07

Title:
ACCESSORY DRIVE BELT NOISE

Models:
'04 - '06 Scion xA & xB

June 20, 2007

Introduction
Some 2004 - 2006 model year Scion XA and xB vehicles may exhibit a squealing noise coming from the engine compartment during vehicle operation. The generator drive belt tension specification has been updated to correct this condition. Please use the following repair procedure to address customer concerns.

Applicable Vehicles

^ 2004 - 2006 model year Scion XA and xB vehicles.

Repair Procedure

1. Verify that the noise is coming from the generator V-belt.

NOTE:
Replace the generator V-belt if there is any sign of fraying, scoring, or glazing on the belt.
