DTC 62

DTC B0133/62 Short to B+ in Front Pretensioner Squib RH Circuit

DESCRIPTION

DTC Detection Condition:




The front pretensioner squib RH circuit consists of the center airbag sensor assembly and the front seat outer belt assembly RH (seat belt pretensioner RH).

This circuit instructs the SRS to deploy when deployment conditions are met.

DTC B0133/62 is recorded when a short to B+ is detected in the front pretensioner squib RH circuit.
