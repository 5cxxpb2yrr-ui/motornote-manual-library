Recall 07V048000: Cruise Control Module

MAKE/MODELS: MODEL/BUILD YEARS:

Toyota/Scion XA 2006-2007

Toyota/Scion XB 2006-2007

MANUFACTURER: Southeast Toyota Distributors

NHTSA CAMPAIGN ID NUMBER: 07V048000 MFG'S REPORT DATE: January 16, 2007

COMPONENT: Vehicle Speed Control

POTENTIAL NUMBER OF UNITS AFFECTED: 716

SUMMARY:
Certain Rostra Precision Controls speed controls may have a faulty module. These controls were installed by Southeast Toyota dealers or at their vehicle processing center on certain passenger vehicles. When the speed control "ON/OFF" switch is pressed once to turn the unit off, the throttle does not return to idle. When the "ON/OFF" switch is pressed a second time, the throttle will return to idle.

CONSEQUENCE:
A crash could be caused in the event that the cruise control does not allow the engine to return to idle on the first depression of the "ON/OFF" switch.

REMEDY:
Dealers will inspect and/or replace the speed control module. The manufacturer has not yet provided an owner notification schedule for this campaign. Owners may contact Southeast Toyota at 1-800-301-6859.

NOTES:
Customers may contact The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration's Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY: 1-800-424-9153); or go to http://www.safercar.gov.
