Transmission Position Sensor/Switch: Adjustments

PARK/NEUTRAL POSITION SWITCH

ADJUSTMENT





1. ADJUST PARK/NEUTRAL POSITION SWITCH ASSEMBLY
a) Loosen the 2 bolts of the park/neutral position switch and set the shift lever to the N position.
b) Align the groove and neutral basic line.
c) Hold the switch in this position and tighten the 2 bolts.
Torque: 5.4 Nm (55 kgf-cm, 48 inch lbs.)
d) After adjustment, perform the inspection.
