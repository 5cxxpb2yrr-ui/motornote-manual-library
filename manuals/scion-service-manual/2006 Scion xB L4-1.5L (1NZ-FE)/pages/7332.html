B1807



SUPPLEMENTAL RESTRAINT SYSTEM: AIRBAG SYSTEM: B1805/52-B1808/52: Short in Front Passenger Side Squib Circuit

B1805/52 Short in Front Passenger Side Squib Circuit

B1806/52 Open in Front Passenger Side Squib Circuit

B1807/52 Short to GND in Front Passenger Side Squib Circuit

B1808/52 Short to B+ in Front Passenger Side Squib Circuit

SYSTEM DESCRIPTION
The front passenger side squib circuit consists of the center airbag sensor and front passenger airbag.
The circuit instructs the SRS to deploy when deployment conditions are met. These DTCs are recorded when a malfunction is detected in the front passenger side squib circuit.
