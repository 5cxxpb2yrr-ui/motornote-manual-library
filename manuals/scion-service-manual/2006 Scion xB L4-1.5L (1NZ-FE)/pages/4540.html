Disassembly and Reassembly

LICENSE PLATE LIGHT ASSEMBLY

DISASSEMBLY




1. REMOVE LICENSE PLATE LIGHT BULB
a. Remove the license plate light socket as shown in the illustration.
b. Remove the light bulb from the socket.

REASSEMBLY

1. INSTALL LICENSE PLATE LIGHT BULB
a. Install the light bulb to the light socket.
b. Install the light socket to the rear light.
