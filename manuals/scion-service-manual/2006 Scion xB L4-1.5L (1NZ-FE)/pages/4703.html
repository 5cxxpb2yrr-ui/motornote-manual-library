Seat Belt Reminder Buzzer: Testing and Inspection

CHECK SEAT BELT WARNING REMINDER BUZZER FOR DRIVER SIDE




a. Disconnect the B7 switch connector.
b. Connect terminals 1 and 3 of the wire harness side connector.
c. Turn the ignition switch ON, and check that the warning buzzer sounds.

OK:
Warning buzzer sounds.
