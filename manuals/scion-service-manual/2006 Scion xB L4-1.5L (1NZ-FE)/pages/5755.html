BRAKE BOOSTER

REMOVAL
1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

CAUTION: Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to prevent airbag and seat belt pretensioner activation.

2. DRAIN BRAKE FLUID

NOTICE: Wash off brake fluid immediately if it comes in contact with any painted surface.

3. REMOVE AIR CLEANER CAP SUB-ASSEMBLY
4. DISCONNECT CLUTCH RESERVOIR TUBE (for Manual Transaxle)
a) Slide the clip and disconnect the clutch reservoir tube.
5. REMOVE BRAKE MASTER CYLINDER SUBASSEMBLY
a) Remove the brake master cylinder.
6. REMOVE BATTERY
7. REMOVE BRAKE ACTUATOR ASSEMBLY WITH BRACKET
a) Remove the brake actuator with bracket.





8. REMOVE BRAKE BOOSTER ASSEMBLY
a) Slide the clip and disconnect the vacuum hose from the brake booster.





b) Remove the brake pedal return spring, clip and clevis pin.
c) Loosen the push rod clevis lock nut.





d) Remove the 4 nuts and push rod clevis.
e) Pull out the brake booster and gasket.
