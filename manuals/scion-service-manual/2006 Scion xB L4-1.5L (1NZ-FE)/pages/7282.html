B0135

DTC B0135/73 Short in Front Pretensioner Squib LH Circuit

DESCRIPTION

DTC Detection Condition:




The front pretensioner squib circuit consists of the center airbag sensor assembly and the front seat outer belt assembly LH (seat belt pretensioner LH).

This circuit instructs the SRS to deploy when deployment conditions are met.

DTC 0135/73 is recorded when a short circuit is detected in the front pretensioner squib LH circuit.
