DTC 13

DTC C0273/13 Open in ABS Motor Relay Circuit

DESCRIPTION

DTC Detection Condition:




The ABS MTR relay supplies power to the ABS pump motor. While the ABS is activated, the ECU turns the ABS MTR relay ON and operates the ABS pump motor.
