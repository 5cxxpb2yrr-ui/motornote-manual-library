Idle Speed: Testing and Inspection

INSPECT ENGINE IDLE SPEED
a) Warm up the engine.
b) When using the intelligent tester or OBD II scan tool:
1) Connect the intelligent tester or OBD II scan tool to the DLC3.
2) Enter DATA LIST MODE on the intelligent tester or OBD II scan tool.

Standard idle speed

Standard idle speed:





NOTICE:
^ When checking the idle speed, the transmission is in the park or neutral position.
^ Check the idle speed with the cooling fan OFF.
^ Switch off all accessories and air conditioning before connecting the intelligent tester or OBD II scan tool.

HINT: Refer to the intelligent tester or OBD II scan tool operator's manual if you need help to select the DATA LIST.





c) When not using the intelligent tester or OBD II scan tool:
1) Using SST, connect the tachometer test lead to terminal 9 (TAC) of the DLC3.
SST 09843-18040
2) Check the idle speed.
