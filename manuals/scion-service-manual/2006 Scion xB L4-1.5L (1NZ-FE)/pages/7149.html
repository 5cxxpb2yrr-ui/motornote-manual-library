OUTER MIRROR SWITCH

INSPECTION

1. INSPECT OUTER MIRROR SWITCH ASSEMBLY







a. Select L on the left/right adjustment switch: Measure the resistance of the switch.
If the result is not as specified, replace the switch assembly.




b. Select R on the left/right adjustment switch: Measure the resistance of the switch.
If the result is not as specified, replace the switch assembly.
