Without Scan Tool

CHECK DTC (When not Using Intelligent Tester)




a. Using SST, connect terminals 13 (TC) and 4 (CG) of the DLC 3.

SST 09843-18040

b. Turn the ignition switch ON.
c. Read the DTC from the ABS warning light and VSC warning light on the combination meter.






HINT:
- If no code appears, check the TC and CG circuit or ABS warning light and VSC warning light circuits.

Trouble Area
ABS warning light circuit
VSC warning light circuit
TC and CG terminal circuit

- If more than 1 DTC is detected at the same time, the DTCs will be displayed in numerical order.
- As an example, the blinking patterns for a normal system code and DTCs 11 and 21 are shown in the illustration.
- DTCs are explained in the Diagnostic Trouble Code Chart.

d. After completing the check, disconnect terminals 13 (TC) and 4 (CG) of the DLC 3. If 2 or more DTCs are detected at the same time, the DTCs will be displayed in numerical order.
