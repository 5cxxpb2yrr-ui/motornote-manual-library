B1615



SUPPLEMENTAL RESTRAINT SYSTEM: AIRBAG SYSTEM: B1615/14: Front Airbag Sensor LH Circuit Malfunction

B1615/14 Front Airbag Sensor LH Circuit Malfunction

SYSTEM DESCRIPTION
The front airbag sensor LH consists of the diagnostic circuit and front deceleration sensor. The center airbag sensor receives signals from the front deceleration sensor, and determines whether or not the SRS should be activated.

DTC B1615/14 is recorded when a malfunction is detected in the front airbag sensor LH circuit.
