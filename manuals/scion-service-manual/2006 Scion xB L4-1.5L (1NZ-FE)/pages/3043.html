Front Door Lock

INSPECTION

1. INSPECT FRONT DOOR LOCK ACTUATOR ASSEMBLY LH







a. Apply battery voltage to the actuator terminals and check operation of the door lock motor.
If the result is not as specified, replace the actuator.







b. Measure the resistance of the door lock and unlock switch.
If the result is not as specified, replace the door lock assembly.




c. Measure the resistance of the position switch.
If the result is not as specified, replace the actuator.

2. INSPECT FRONT DOOR LOCK ACTUATOR ASSEMBLY RH







a. Apply battery voltage to the actuator terminals and check operation of the door lock motor.
If the result is not as specified, replace the actuator.







b. Measure the resistance of the door lock and unlock switch.
If the result is not as specified, replace the door lock assembly.







3. INSPECT POWER WINDOW REGULATOR MASTER SWITCH ASSEMBLY (DOOR CONTROL SWITCH)
a. Measure the resistance of the door control switch.
If the result is not as specified, replace the switch assembly.







4. INSPECT DOOR CONTROL SWITCH ASSEMBLY
a. Measure the resistance of the door control switch.
If the result is not as specified, replace the switch assembly.
