Heating and Air Conditioning: Service and Repair

AIR CONDITIONING AMPLIFIER

REMOVAL

1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

CAUTION: Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to prevent airbag and seat belt pretensioner activation.

2. REMOVE HEATER CONTROL KNOB
3. REMOVE INSTRUMENT CLUSTER FINISH PANEL CENTER
4. REMOVE RADIO RECEIVER ASSEMBLY




5. REMOVE AIR CONDITIONING AMPLIFIER ASSEMBLY
a. Disconnect the connector.
b. Detach the claw and remove the A/C amplifier.

INSTALLATION




1. INSTALL AIR CONDITIONING AMPLIFIER ASSEMBLY
a. Attach the claw to install the A/C amplifier.
b. Connect the connector.

2. INSTALL RADIO RECEIVER ASSEMBLY
3. INSTALL INSTRUMENT CLUSTER FINISH PANEL CENTER
4. INSTALL HEATER CONTROL KNOB
5. CONNECT CABLE TO NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL
