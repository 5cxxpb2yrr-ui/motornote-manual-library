B0100

DTC B0100/13 Short in Driver Side Squib Circuit

DESCRIPTION

DTC Detection Condition:




The driver side squib circuit consists of the center airbag sensor assembly, the spiral cable sub-assembly and the steering path.

This circuit actuates the SRS to deploy when deployment conditions are met.

DTC B0100/13 is recorded when a short circuit is detected in the driver side squib circuit.
