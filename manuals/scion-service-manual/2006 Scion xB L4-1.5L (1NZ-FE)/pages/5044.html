Front Seat Outer Belt Assembly

INSPECTION




1. INSPECT FRONT SEAT OUTER BELT ASSEMBLY

NOTE: Do not disassemble the retractor.

a. When the inclination of the retractor is 15 degrees or less, check that the belt can be pulled from the retractor. When the inclination of the retractor is over 45 degrees , check that the belt locks.
If the operation is not as specified, replace the belt.
