A/C - Passenger Front Floor Wet

HEATING & AIR CONDITIONING
AC005-05

Title:
FRONT PASSENGER FOOT AREA WET

Models:
'03 - '05 ECHO & '04 - '06 Scion xA & xB

September 13, 2005

Introduction

On some ECHO, Scion xA, and Scion xB vehicles, a condition may exist that allows the evaporator drain hose to become detached, causing the passenger foot well to become wet. This bulletin provides a procedure to correct this condition.

Applicable Vehicles
^ 2003 - 2005 model year ECHO vehicles produced BEFORE the Production Change Effective VIN shown below.

^ 2004 - 2006 model year Scion XA and xB vehicles produced BEFORE the Production Change Effective VINs shown below.

Repair Procedure





1. Inspect the evaporator drain hose to ensure that it is the cause of the water leak. If the evaporator hose is out of position, continue with this procedure.





2. Install the retaining clamp on the drain hose.





3. Push the drain hose up onto the evaporator drain tube. Ensure that the drain hose is up far enough for the retaining clamp to fully engage the tube.





4. Release the holding clip.
