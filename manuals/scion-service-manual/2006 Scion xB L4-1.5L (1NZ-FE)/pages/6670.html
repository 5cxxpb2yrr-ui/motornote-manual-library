Engine Oil Pressure: Specifications

Standard Oil Pressure
At idle 29 kPa (0.3 kgf/cm2, 4.2 psi) or more
At 3,000 rpm 150 to 550 kPa (1.5 to 5.6 kgf/cm2, 22 to 80 psi) or more
