Aerodynamics





To improve aerodynamic performance, the following measures have been taken.

^ The front edge of the hood and the area around the headlights offers a smooth shape with minimal level differences for smooth airflow characteristics.

^ The under shape of the front bumper collects the flow of air under the floor.

^ The bodyline from the roof to the trunk rid allows the air to flow smoothly the top of the roof to the rear.

Bumper

1. Front Bumper and Rear Bumper





^ A large front lower bumper, rear under spoiler, and side mud guards have been provided as standard equipment, and the bottom edge of the front bumper has been lowered to improve aerodynamics.
