Capacity Specifications




OIL CAPACITY

With filter change 3.9 qt. (3.7 L, 3.3 Imp. qt.)
Without filter change 3.6 qt. (3.4 L, 3.0 Imp. qt.)
Dry Fill 4.3 qt. (4.1 L, 3.6 Imp. qt.)


Approximate quantity to fill engine oil from "Low" to "Full" on dipstick is 1.6 qt. (1.5 L, 1.3 Imp. qt.)
