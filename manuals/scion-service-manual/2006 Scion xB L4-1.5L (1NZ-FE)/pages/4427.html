Power Window Master Switch

POWER WINDOW MASTER SWITCH

INSPECTION

1. INSPECT POWER WINDOW REGULATOR MASTER SWITCH ASSEMBLY
















a. Measure the resistance between each terminal of the connector when the switch is operated.
If the result is not as specified, replace the master switch assembly.







b. Apply battery voltage to the master switch terminals and check the AUTO light.
If the result is not as specified, replace the master switch assembly.
