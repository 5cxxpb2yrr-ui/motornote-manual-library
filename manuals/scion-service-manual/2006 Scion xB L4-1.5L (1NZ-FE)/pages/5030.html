Center Airbag Sensor

REMOVAL

CAUTION:Be sure to read the precautionary notices concerning the SRS airbag system before servicing it.

1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

CAUTION: Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to prevent airbag and seat belt pretensioner activation.

2. REMOVE INSTRUMENT PANEL FINISH PANEL LOWER CENTER




3. REMOVE CENTER AIRBAG SENSOR ASSEMBLY
a. Disconnect the airbag sensor connectors.
b. Using a T40 "torx" socket, remove the 4 screws and airbag sensor.

INSTALLATION

CAUTION: Be sure to read the precautionary notices concerning the SRS airbag system before servicing it.




1. INSTALL CENTER AIRBAG SENSOR ASSEMBLY
a. Using a T40 "torx" socket, install the airbag sensor with the 4 screws.

Torque: 20 N.m (200 kgf.cm, 14 ft.lbf)

CAUTION:
- If the airbag sensor has been dropped, or there are any cracks, dents or other defects in the case, bracket or connector, replace it with a new one.
- When installing the airbag sensor, be careful that the SRS wiring does not interfere with other parts and that it is not pinched between other parts.

b. Connect the airbag sensor connectors.

2. INSTALL INSTRUMENT PANEL FINISH PANEL LOWER CENTER
3. CONNECT CABLE TO NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL
4. CHECK SRS WARNING LIGHT
a. Check the SRS warning light.
