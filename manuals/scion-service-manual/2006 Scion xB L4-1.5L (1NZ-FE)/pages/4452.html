Removal and Installation

REAR COMBINATION LIGHT ASSEMBLY

REMOVAL

HINT:
- Use the same procedures for the RH side and LH side.
- The procedures listed below are for the LH side.

1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

CAUTION: Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to prevent airbag and seat belt pretensioner activation.

2. REMOVE REAR COMBINATION LIGHT SERVICE COVER LH




3. REMOVE REAR COMBINATION LIGHT ASSEMBLY LH
a. Disconnect the connector.
b. Remove the 2 nuts and rear combination light.

INSTALLATION

HINT:
- Use the same procedures for the RH side and LH side.
- The procedures listed below are for the LH side.




1. INSTALL REAR COMBINATION LIGHT ASSEMBLY LH
a. Install the rear combination light with the 2 nuts.
b. Connect the connector.

2. INSTALL REAR COMBINATION LIGHT SERVICE COVER LH
3. CONNECT CABLE TO NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL
