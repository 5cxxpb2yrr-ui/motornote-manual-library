B1610/13 Front Airbag Sensor RH Circuit Malfunction

SYSTEM DESCRIPTION
The front airbag sensor RH consists of the diagnostic circuit and front deceleration sensor. The center airbag sensor receives signals from the front deceleration sensor and determines whether or not the SRS should be activated.

DTC B1610/13 is recorded when a malfunction is detected in the front airbag sensor RH circuit.
