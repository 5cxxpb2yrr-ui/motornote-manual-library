Front # 2

REMOVAL

HINT:
- Use the same procedures for the RH side and LH side.
- The procedures listed below are for the LH side.

1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

CAUTION: Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to prevent airbag and seat belt pretensioner activation.

2. REMOVE NO. 2 INSTRUMENT PANEL SPEAKER PANEL SUB-ASSEMBLY




3. REMOVE NO. 2 FRONT SPEAKER ASSEMBLY LH
a. Remove the 2 bolts and speaker.
b. Disconnect the connector.

INSTALLATION

HINT:
- Use the same procedures for the RH side and LH side.
- The procedures listed below are for the LH side.




1. INSTALL NO. 2 FRONT SPEAKER ASSEMBLY LH
a. Connect the connector.
b. Install the speaker with the 2 bolts.

2. INSTALL NO. 2 INSTRUMENT PANEL SPEAKER PANEL SUB-ASSEMBLY
3. CONNECT CABLE TO NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL
