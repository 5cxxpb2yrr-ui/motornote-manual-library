Outer Rear View Mirror

INSPECTION







1. INSPECT OUTER REAR VIEW MIRROR ASSEMBLY LH
a. Apply battery voltage to the mirror and check the operation of the mirror.
If the result is not as specified, replace the mirror assembly.







2. INSPECT OUTER REAR VIEW MIRROR ASSEMBLY RH
a. Apply battery voltage to the mirror and check the operation of the mirror.
If the result is not as specified, replace the mirror assembly.
