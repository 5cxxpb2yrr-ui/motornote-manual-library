Diagnosis System

DIAGNOSIS SYSTEM

CHECK INDICATOR




a. Check the warning lights.
1. Release the parking brake lever.
2. When the ignition switch ON, check that the ABS warning light, VSC warning light, TRAC OFF indicator light, brake warning light and SLIP indicator light illuminate for approximately 3 seconds.

HINT:
- When the parking brake is applied or brake fluid level is low, the brake warning light illuminates.
- If the indicator check result is not normal, proceed to troubleshooting for the ABS warning light, VSC warning light, TRAC OFF indicator light, brake warning light and SLIP indicator light circuit.
If the indicator remains on, proceed to troubleshooting for the light circuit given.

Trouble Area
ABS warning light circuit
VSC warning light circuit
Brake warning light circuit
TRAC OFF warning light circuit
SLIP indicator light circuit
