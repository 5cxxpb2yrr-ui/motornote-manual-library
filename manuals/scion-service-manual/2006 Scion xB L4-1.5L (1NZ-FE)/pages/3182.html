Rear Door Lock

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

INSPECTION







1. INSPECT REAR DOOR LOCK ACTUATOR ASSEMBLY LH
a. Apply battery voltage to the actuator terminals and check operation of the door lock motor.
If the result is not as specified, replace the actuator.







2. INSPECT REAR DOOR LOCK ACTUATOR ASSEMBLY RH
a. Apply battery voltage to the actuator terminals and check operation of the door lock motor.
If the result is not as specified, replace the actuator.
