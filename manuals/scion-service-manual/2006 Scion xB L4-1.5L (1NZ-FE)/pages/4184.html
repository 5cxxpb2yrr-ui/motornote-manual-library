Introduction

This service bulletin provides information on the proper identification of the 5 solenoids used in the U340E and U431E transmissions.

Applicable Vehicles
^ 2000 - 2005 model year Celica vehicles equipped with 1ZZ-FE engine.
^ 2000 - 2005 model year ECHO vehicles.
^ 2004 - 2006 model year Scion XA and Scion xB vehicles.
