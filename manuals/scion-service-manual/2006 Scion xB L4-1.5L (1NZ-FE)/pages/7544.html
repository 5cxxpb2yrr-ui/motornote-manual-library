DTC 17

DTC B1180/17 Short in Driver Side Squib 2nd Step Circuit

DESCRIPTION

DTC Detection Condition:




The driver side squib (2nd step) circuit consists of the center airbag assembly, the spiral cable sub-assembly and the steering pad.

This circuit instructs the SRS to deploy when deployment conditions are met.

DTC B1180/17 is recorded when a short circuit is detected in the driver side squib (2nd step) circuit.
