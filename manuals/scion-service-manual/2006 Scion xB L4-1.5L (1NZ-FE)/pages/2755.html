Check Steering Wheel Freeplay

ON-VEHICLE INSPECTION





1. CHECK STEERING WHEEL FREE PLAY
a) Stop the vehicle with the tires pointing straight ahead. Position the steering wheel in the center position.
b) Turn the steering wheel gently right and left, and check the steering wheel free play.
Maximum free play: 30 mm (1.18 inch)
