DTC B1153/25 Seat Position Airbag Sensor Circuit Malfunction

DESCRIPTION

DTC Detection Condition:




The seat position sensor circuit consists of the center airbag sensor assembly and the seat position sensor.

DTC B1153/25 is recorded when a malfunction is detected in the seat position sensor circuit.
