Engine Oil



New standard for engine oil, GF-4. See TSB EG018-06 032906 for additional information.

Engine Oil Type

Grade API SL Energy Conserving or ILSAC multigrade
Viscosity 5W-30

Note: If 5W-30 is not available, 10W-30 may be used. However, it should be replaced with 5W-30 at the next oil change.
