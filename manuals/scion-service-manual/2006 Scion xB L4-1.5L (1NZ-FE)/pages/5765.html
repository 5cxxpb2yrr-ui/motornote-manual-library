Removal

BRAKE MASTER CYLINDER

COMPONENTS:





REMOVAL
1. DRAIN BRAKE FLUID

NOTICE: Wash off brake fluid immediately if it comes in contact with any painted surface.

2. REMOVE AIR CLEANER CAP SUB-ASSEMBLY
3. DISCONNECT CLUTCH RESERVOIR TUBE (for Manual Transaxle)
a) Slide the clip and disconnect the clutch reservoir tube.





4. REMOVE BRAKE MASTER CYLINDER SUBASSEMBLY
a) Disconnect the level warning light switch connector.
b) Using SST, disconnect the 2 brake lines from the brake master cylinder.
SST 09023-00101





c) Remove the 2 nuts, and pull out the brake master cylinder.





5. REMOVE BRAKE MASTER CYLINDER RESERVOIR SUB-ASSEMBLY
a) Pull out the brake master cylinder reservoir stopper.





b) Remove the brake master cylinder reservoir and 2 grommets from the brake master cylinder.
c) Remove the cap and strainer from the brake master cylinder reservoir.
