Removal and Installation

SIDE TURN SIGNAL LIGHT ASSEMBLY

REMOVAL

HINT:
- Use the same procedures for the RH side and LH side.
- The procedures listed below are for the LH side.

1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

CAUTION: Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to prevent airbag and seat belt pretensioner activation.




2. REMOVE SIDE TURN SIGNAL LIGHT ASSEMBLY LH
a. Pull the side turn signal light LH as shown in the illustration and detach the 2 claws.
b. Disconnect the connector and remove the light.

INSTALLATION

HINT:
- Use the same procedures for the RH side and LH side.
- The procedures listed below are for the LH side.

1. INSTALL SIDE TURN SIGNAL LIGHT ASSEMBLY LH
a. Connect the connector and install the side turn signal light.
b. Attach the side turn signal light with the 2 claws and install the light.

2. CONNECT CABLE TO NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL
