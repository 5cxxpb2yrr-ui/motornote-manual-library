Disassembly and Reassembly

SIDE TURN SIGNAL LIGHT ASSEMBLY

DISASSEMBLY

HINT:
- Use the same procedures for the RH side and LH side.
- The procedures listed below are for the LH side.




1. REMOVE SIDE TURN SIGNAL LIGHT BULB
a. Remove the light socket as shown in the illustration.
b. Remove the light bulb from the light socket.

REASSEMBLY

HINT:
- Use the same procedures for the RH side and LH side.
- The procedures listed below are for the LH side.

1. INSTALL SIDE TURN SIGNAL LIGHT BULB
a. Install the light bulb to the light socket.
b. Install the light socket.
