Torque Specification





Clutch cover bolts 19 Nm (195 kgf-cm, 14 ft. lbs.)

Align the matchmark on the cover with that on the flywheel.

Tighten the 6 bolts in order, and start with the bolt located near the knock pin on the top.

Clutch Disc





Minimum rivet depth 0.3 mm (0.012 inch)





Maximum disc runout 0.8 mm (0.031 inch)
