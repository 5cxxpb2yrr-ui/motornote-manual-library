Rear Seat Assembly: Removal

  1. REMOVE REAR SEAT CUSHION WITH COVER PAD SUB-ASSEMBLY 
    1. Detach the seat cushion's 2 front hooks from the vehicle body.
      NOTE: Follow the instructions below carefully as the cushion frame deforms easily.
      1. Choose a hook to detach first. Place your hands near the hook as shown in the illustration. Then lift the seat cushion to detach the hook.
      2. Repeat for the other hook.
    2. Detach the seat cushion's rear hook.
    3. Remove the seat cushion.
      Fig 1: Detaching Seat Cushion Front Hooks From Vehicle Body
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  2. REMOVE REAR SEATBACK ASSEMBLY LH 
    1. Tilt the seatback down toward the front of the vehicle.
    2. Turn up the seatback cover as shown in the illustration.
    3. Remove the 2 bolts and seatback.
      Fig 2: Turning Up Seatback Cover
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  3. REMOVE REAR SEATBACK ASSEMBLY RH 

    HINT:

    Use the same procedures described for the LH side.

  4. REMOVE REAR SEATBACK LOCK STRIKER SUBASSEMBLY LH 
    1. Remove the 2 bolts and seatback lock striker.
      Fig 3: Removing Bolts
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  5. REMOVE REAR SEATBACK LOCK STRIKER SUBASSEMBLY RH 
    1. Remove the 2 bolts and seatback lock striker.
  6. REMOVE CHILD RESTRAINT SEAT ANCHOR BRACKET SUB-ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the 4 bolts and anchor bracket.
