Inspection Procedure

  1. CHECK PASSENGER AIRBAG ON/OFF INDICATOR OPERATION 
    1. Turn the ignition switch ON.
    2. Check the passenger airbag ON / OFF indicator operation.

      HINT:

      Refer to the normal condition of the passenger airbag ON / OFF indicator (see PROBLEM SYMPTOMS TABLE  ).

      Fig 1: Passenger Airbag ON / OFF Indicator Blinking Pattern
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

      Result: 

      RESULT

      ON / OFF Indicator Illumination Proceed to
      Always ON A
      OFF B

    B : Go to step   5 

    A : Go to next step 

  2. CHECK CONNECTION OF CONNECTOR 
    1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
    2. Disconnect the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal, and wait for at least 90 seconds.
    3. Check that the connectors are properly connected to the center airbag sensor and the front passenger seat belt warning light.

      OK: 

      The connectors are properly connected. 

    NG : CONNECT CONNECTOR 

    OK : Go to Next Step 

  3. CHECK FRONT PASSENGER SEAT BELT WARNING LIGHT 
    1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
    2. Disconnect the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal, and wait for at least 90 seconds.
    3. Disconnect the connector from the center airbag sensor.
    4. Connect the cable to the negative (-) battery terminal, and wait for at least 2 seconds.
    5. Turn the ignition switch ON.
    6. Check the passenger airbag ON / OFF indicator operation.

      OK: 

      Neither ON nor OFF passenger airbag ON/OFF indicator comes on. 

    OK : Go to step   8 

    Fig 2: Identifying Front Passenger Seat Belt Warning Light Display
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    NG : Go to Next Step 

  4. CHECK INSTRUMENT PANEL WIRE 
    1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
    2. Disconnect the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal, and wait for at least 90 seconds.
    3. Disconnect the connector from the front passenger seat belt warning light.
    4. Using a service wire, connect terminals P15-3 (PAON) and P15-6 (P-AB) of connector "C".
      NOTE: Do not forcibly insert the service wire into the terminals of the connector when connecting them.
    5. Measure the resistance of the wire harness side connector.

      Standard resistance 

      TESTER CONNECTION - SPECIFIED CONDITION (RESISTANCE)

      Terminal Connection Specified Condition
      A20-23 (PAON) - A20-17 (P-AB) Below 1 Ω
      Fig 3: Connecting Terminals P15-3 (PAON) And P15-6 (P-Ab) Of Connector "C"
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    6. Disconnect the service wire from connector "C".
    7. Measure the resistance of the wire harness side connector.

      Standard resistance 

      TESTER CONNECTION - SPECIFIED CONDITION (RESISTANCE)

      Terminal Connection Specified Condition
      A20-17 (P-AB) - Body ground 1 MΩ or higher
      A20-23 (PAON) - Body ground
      A20-17 (P-AB) - A20-23 (PAON)
    8. Connect the negative (-) terminal cable to the battery, and wait for at least 2 seconds.
    9. Turn the ignition switch ON.
    10. Measure the voltage of the wire harness side connector.

      Standard voltage 

      TESTER CONNECTION - SPECIFIED CONDITION (VOLTAGE)

      Terminal Connection Specified Condition
      A20-23 (PAON) - Body ground Below 1 V
      A20-17 (P-AB) - Body ground

    NG : REPAIR OR REPLACE INSTRUMENT PANEL WIRE 

    Fig 4: Checking Instrument Panel Wire
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    OK : REPLACE FRONT PASSENGER SEAT BELT WARNING LIGHT

    1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
    2. Disconnect the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal, and wait for at least 90 seconds.
    3. Check that the connectors are properly connected to the center airbag sensor and the hazard warning signal switch.

      OK:

  7. CHECK WIRE HARNESS (POWER SOURCE) 
    1. Connect the cable to the negative (-) battery terminal, and wait for at least 2 seconds.
    2. Turn the ignition switch ON.
    3. Measure the voltage of the wire harness side connector.

      Standard voltage

      Terminal Connection Specified Condition
      P15-2 (B) - Body ground 10 to 14 V

    NG : REPAIR OR REPLACE POWER SOURCE CIRCUIT

    OK : Go to Next Step 

  8. CHECK FRONT PASSENGER SEAT BELT WARNING LIGHT 
    1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
    2. Disconnect the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal, and wait for at least 90 seconds.
    3. Remove the connector from the front passenger seat belt warning light.
    4. Connect the cable to the negative (-) battery terminal, and wait for at least 2 seconds.
    5. Using a service wire, connect terminals P15-3 (PAON) and P15-4 (ILL-) of the front passenger seat belt warning light connector.
    6. Using a service wire, connect terminals P15-6 (P-AB) and P15-4 (ILL-) of the front passenger seat belt warning light connector.
    7. Turn the ignition switch ON.
    8. Check the passenger airbag ON / OFF indicator operation.

      OK: 

      Passenger airbag ON / OFF indicator comes on 

    NG : REPLACE FRONT PASSENGER SEAT BELT WARNING LIGHT 

    Fig 8: Connecting Terminals P15-6 (P-Ab) And P15-4 ILL Of Front Passenger Seat Belt Warning Light Connector
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    OK : Go to Next Step 

  9. CHECK CENTER AIRBAG SENSOR ASSEMBLY 
    1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
    2. Disconnect the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal, and wait for at least 90 seconds.
    3. Connect the connector to the center airbag sensor.
    4. Connect the connector to the front passenger seat belt warning light.
    5. Connect the cable to the negative (-) battery terminal, and wait for at least 2 seconds.
    6. Turn the ignition switch ON, and wait for at least 60 seconds.
    7. Clear the DTCs stored in the memory (see DTC CHECK / CLEAR  ).
    8. Turn the ignition switch to the LOCK position.
    9. Turn the ignition switch ON, and wait for at least 60 seconds.
    10. Check for DTCs (see DTC CHECK / CLEAR  ).

      OK: 

      DTC B1660/43 is not output. 

      HINT:

      DTCs other than B1660/43 may be output at this time, but they are not related to this check.

    NG : REPLACE CENTER AIRBAG SENSOR ASSEMBLY
