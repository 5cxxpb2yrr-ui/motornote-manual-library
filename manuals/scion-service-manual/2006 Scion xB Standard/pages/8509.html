Inspection Procedure

  1. CHECK CAN BUS LINE (MAIN BUS LINE FOR DISCONNECTION, BUS LINES FOR SHORT CIRCUIT) 
    1. Measure the resistance of the DLC3.

      Standard resistance 

      TESTER CONNECTION SPECIFIED CONDITION

      Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition Proceed to
      D1-6 (CANH) - D1-14 (CANL) Ignition switch OFF 54 to 69 Ω OK
      D1-6 (CANH) - D1-14 (CANL) Ignition switch OFF 69 Ω or higher NG-A
      D1-6 (CANH) - D1-14 (CANL) Ignition switch OFF Below 54 Ω NG-B

    NG-A : CHECK CAN MAIN BUS LINE (FOR DISCONNECTION) 

    NG-B : CHECK CAN BUS LINE (FOR SHORT CIRCUIT) 

    Fig 1: Identifying DL3C Connector Terminals
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    OK : Go to Next Step 

  2. CHECK CAN BUS LINE (FOR SHORT TO B+) 
    1. Measure the resistance of the DLC3.

      Standard resistance 

      TESTER CONNECTION SPECIFIED CONDITION

      Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
      D1-6 (CANH) - D1-16 (BAT) Ignition switch OFF 1 MΩ or higher
      D1-14 (CANL) - D1-16 (BAT) Ignition switch OFF 1 MΩ or higher

    NG : CHECK CAN BUS LINE (FOR SHORT TO B+) 

    Fig 2: Identifying DL3C Connector Terminals
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    OK : Go to Next Step 

  3. CHECK CAN BUS LINE (FOR SHORT TO GND) 
    1. Measure the resistance of the DLC3.

      Standard resistance 

      TESTER CONNECTION SPECIFIED CONDITION

      Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
      D1-4 (CG) - D1-6 (CANH) Ignition switch OFF 3 kΩ or higher
      D1-4 (CG) - D1-14 (CANL) Ignition switch OFF 3 kΩ or higher

    NG : CHECK CAN BUS LINE (FOR SHORT TO GND)
