On-Vehicle Inspection

  1. REMOVE PTC HEATER RELAY (Marking: HTR SUB1) 
    1. Remove the heater relay from the engine room relay block.
  2. INSPECT PTC HEATER RELAY 
    1. Measure the resistance of the relay.

      Standard resistance 

      TESTER CONNECTION AND SPECIFIED CONDITION CHART

      Tester Connection Specified Condition
      3 - 5 10 kΩ or higher
      3 - 5 Below 1 Ω
      (when battery voltage is applied to terminals 1 and 2)

      If the value is not as specified, replace the PTC heater relay.

  3. INSTALL PTC HEATER RELAY
