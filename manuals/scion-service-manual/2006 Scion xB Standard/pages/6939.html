Intermediate Shaft: Inspection

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. INSPECT REVERSE CLUTCH DISC 
    1. Visually check if the sliding surface of the disc, plate and flange are worn or burnt. Replace as necessary.

      HINT:

      • If the lining of the disc peeling off or discolored, or even if a part of the printed mark is defaced, replace all discs.
      • Before assembling new discs, soak them in ATF for at least 15 minutes.

  2. INSPECT DIRECT CLUTCH DISK 
    1. Visually check if the sliding surface of the disc, plate and flange are worn or burnt. Replace as necessary.

      HINT:

      • If the lining of the disc peeling off or discolored, or even if a part of the printed mark is defaced, replace all discs.
      • Before assembling new discs, soak them in ATF for at least 15 minutes.
        Fig 2: Inspecting Direct Clutch Disk
        Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  3. INSPECT DIRECT CLUTCH RETURN SPRING SUBASSEMBLY 
    1. Using a vernier caliper, measure the free length of the spring together with the spring seat.

      Standard free length: 32.9 mm (1.2953 in.) 

      Fig 3: Measuring Free Length Of Spring Using Vernier Caliper
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  4. INSPECT DIRECT CLUTCH PISTON SUB-ASSEMBLY 
    1. Shake the direct clutch pin and check that a check ball is not stuck.
    2. Apply low pressure air to the check ball with compressed air and check that no air leak is identified.
      Fig 4: Applying Compressed Air To Check Ball
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  5. INSPECT INTERMEDIATE SHAFT SUB-ASSEMBLY 
    1. Shake the direct clutch pin and check that a check ball is not stuck.
    2. Apply low pressure compressed air to the check ball and check that no air leak is identified.
