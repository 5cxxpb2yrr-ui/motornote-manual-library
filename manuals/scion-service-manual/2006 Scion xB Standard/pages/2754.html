Vacuum Switching Valve: Removal

  1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL 
    CAUTION: Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to prevent airbag and seat belt pretensioner activation.
  2. REMOVE AIR CLEANER CAP 
    1. Disconnect the MAF meter connector.
    2. Disconnect the purge VSV connector.
    3. Disconnect the 2 wire harness clamps from the air cleaner cap.
    4. Disconnect the 2 purge line hoses.
    5. Disconnect the purge line hose from the hose clamp.
    6. Unlock the 2 clamps holding the air cleaner cap to the air cleaner case.
    7. Loosen the hose clamp and remove the air cleaner cap.
