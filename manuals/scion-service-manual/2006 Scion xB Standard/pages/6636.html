Front Shock Absorber With Coil Spring: Disposal

  1. DISPOSE OF FRONT SHOCK ABSORBER ASSEMBLY LH 
    1. Fully extend the shock absorber rod.
    2. Using a drill, make a hole in the cylinder somewhere between A and B as shown in the illustration to discharge the gas inside.
      CAUTION: Be careful when drilling as shreds of metal may fly out, and always use the proper safety equipment.
      NOTE: The gas is colorless, odorless and harmless.
