Engine Oil Replacement Reminder Reset - Procedure 05

To reset MAINT reminder indicator after engine oil change:

  1. Switch the display to the odometer.
  2. Turn ignition switch to OFF (LOCK).
  3. While pressing the MODE button, turn ignition switch to ON (DO NOT start engine).
  4. Hold MODE button down for at least 5 seconds. Display should indicate "000000".
