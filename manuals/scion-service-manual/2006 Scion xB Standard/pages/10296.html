Vane Pump: Removal

  1. REMOVE FRONT WIPER ARM AND BLADE ASSEMBLY RH 
  2. REMOVE FRONT WIPER ARM AND BLADE ASSEMBLY LH 
  3. REMOVE HOOD TO COWL TOP SEAL (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  4. REMOVE COWL TOP VENTILATOR LOUVER RH (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  5. REMOVE COWL TOP VENTILATOR LOUVER LH (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  6. REMOVE WINDSHIELD WIPER LINK ASSEMBLY (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  7. REMOVE COWL PANEL SUB-ASSEMBLY 
  8. REMOVE VANE PUMP OIL RESERVOIR COVER 
  9. DRAIN POWER STEERING FLUID 
  10. REMOVE FRONT WHEEL RH 
  11. REMOVE ENGINE UNDER COVER RH 
  12. DISCONNECT PRESSURE FEED TUBE ASSEMBLY 
    1. Using SST, disconnect the pressure feed tube from the vane pump.

      SST 09023-12701 

    2. Slide the clip and disconnect the return hose.
      NOTE: Take care not to spill fluid on the V belt.
    3. Disconnect the oil pressure sensor connector.
      Fig 1: Disconnecting Pressure Feed Tube From Vane Pump
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  13. REMOVE REAR VANE PUMP STAY 
    1. Remove the bolt, stay and heat insulator.
  14. REMOVE VANE PUMP ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove bolts B and C, and the adjusting strut.
    2. Loosen bolt A sufficiently so that the vane pump can be removed.

      HINT:

      Bolt A cannot be removed.
