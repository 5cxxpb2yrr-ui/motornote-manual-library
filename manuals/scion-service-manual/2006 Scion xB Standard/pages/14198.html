SUBJECT: CUSTOMER SUPPORT PROGRAM PROPOSED CLASS ACTION SETTLEMENT - POSSIBLE EXTENSION OF WARRANTY ON WINDSHIELD FOR MODEL YEAR 2006 SCION xB VEHICLES

On August 24, 2010, Scion is launching a Customer Support Program (CSP) as part of the settlement of a class action lawsuit. This notice is to inform you of the settlement and CSP to assure you and your staff are able to answer inquiries from customers and dealership associates, as well as to advise you of necessary warranty operation codes, etc. (This information is an update to the communication you received in late April 2010.) 

BACKGROUND

In August of 2007, a class action was filed alleging that certain "Scion xBs are equipped with defective windshields that can crack with little or no impact." Although Scion stands by its product, in an effort to promote customer satisfaction and to resolve this litigation, Scion is offering this CSP as part of the settlement, which was approved by the court in July 2010. The settlement and CSP are effective on August 24, 2010.

The following vital information is provided to inform you and your staff of the program notification schedule and your degree of involvement.

1. OWNER NOTIFICATION - COMPLETED

Owners received notification in the April - May, 2010 timeframe, and a copy of that notice is included for your reference. Additional information is also available on a dedicated website, www.xBWindshieldSettlement.com.  No additional mailings will be sent.

2. EXTENDED WARRANTY PERIOD

The CSP, which is effective as of August 24, 2010, extends the limited warranty applicable to the windshields from 3 years/36,000 miles to 6 years/60,000 miles, and specify that cracks in Genuine Toyota windshields equipped in MY 2006 Scion vehicles will be covered by that warranty unless it is obvious that the crack was caused by something that would normally cause a windshield to crack (e.g.  , a collision with another vehicle or stationary object, a natural disaster, vandalism, a baseball, or the like).

3. NUMBER OF VEHICLES INVOLVED

If the settlement is approved, approximately 90,000 model year 2006 Scion xB vehicles will be eligible for this Customer Support Program.

4. DETAILS of CSP

The CSP, which is effective as of August 24, 2010, extends the limited warranty applicable to the windshields, from 3 years/36,000 miles to 6 years/60,000 miles, and specify that cracks in Genuine Toyota windshields in MY 2006 Scion xB vehicles will be covered by that warranty unless it is obvious that the crack was caused by something that would normally cause a windshield to crack (e.g.  , a collision with another vehicle or stationary object, a natural disaster, vandalism, a baseball, or the like). Customers whose vehicles experience a covered crack in the windshield are entitled to receive one  of the following:

  1. Reimbursement for the cost of a previously replaced cracked windshield, if the replacement was done before the settlement becomes final;

    or 

  2. Replacement of a cracked windshield at their Scion dealer.

For covered windshield replacements occurring before the August 24, 2010, customers should submit a claim form. The claim form is on the reverse side of the notice that was mailed to current and prior owners; it is also available at www.xBWindshieldSettlement.com.  Claim forms must be postmarked by September 24, 2010; late forms will not be considered.

For covered windshield replacements occurring on or after August 24, 2010, customers should bring their vehicle to an authorized Toyota dealer.

5. REPLACEMENT OF WINDSHIELDS UNDER THE CSP

As noted above, the CSP extends the limited warranty applicable to the windshields, from 3 years/36,000 miles to 6 years/60,000 miles, and specify that cracks in Genuine Toyota windshields in MY 2006 Scion xB vehicles will be covered by that warranty unless it is obvious that the crack was caused by something that would normally cause a windshield to crack (e.g.  , a collision with another vehicle or stationary object, a natural disaster, vandalism, a baseball, or the like).

If a customer requests replacement of their vehicle's windshield under the CSP, the dealer should determine eligibility under the CSP, that is,

  1. Confirm that the vehicle is within 6 years/60,000 miles of the DOFU; and
  2. Visually inspect the vehicle to confirm that it is not obvious that the crack was caused by something that would normally cause a windshield to crack (e.g.  , a collision with another vehicle or stationary object, a natural disaster, vandalism, a baseball, or the like).
    1. "pen test" (or a similar test) may not be used to ascertain eligibility under the CSP.

If a customer requests replacement of their vehicle's windshield under the CSP but the dealer believes that it is obvious the crack was caused by something that would normally cause a windshield to crack, the dealer should document in detail  the bases for its conclusion on the RO. The dealer should also advise the customer of their right to appeal that determination and, if requestyed, provide the customer with a copy of the Appeal Form (attached). Because the customer does have a right to appeal, it is critical that the RO explicitly set forth the bases for the dealer's denial of the replacement.

If the replacement is covered by the CSP, please refer to the repair manual for removal and installation instructions. 

6. REIMBURSEMENT CLAIM PROCESSING INSTRUCTIONS

Please refer to the Warranty Policy Bulletin (Bulletin No. POL10-03) for reimbursement claim processing instructions.

Please review this entire CSP package with the appropriate associates so that they may provide the necessary support to your dealers and maximize our combined customer satisfaction efforts.

SCION XB WINDSHIELD CLASS ACTION SETTLEMENT Q&A

Background 

In August of 2007, a class action was filed alleging that certain "Scion xBs are equipped with defective windshields that can crack with little or no impact." Although Scion stands by its product, in an effort to promote customer satisfaction and to resolve this litigation, Scion is launching a Customer Support Program (CSP) as part of the settlement of this matter. The CSP, which is effective August 24, 2010, extends the limited warranty applicable to the windshields from 3 years/36,000 miles to 6 years/60,000 miles, and specify that cracks in Genuine Toyota windshields equipped in MY 2006 Scion vehicles will be covered by that warranty unless it is obvious that the crack was caused by something that would normally cause a windshield to crack (e.g., a collision with another vehicle or stationary object, a natural disaster, vandalism, a baseball, or the like).

The proposed settlement was approved by the Court in July 2010. Affected customers were notified of the proposed settlement in April-May 2010. Additional information is also available on a dedicated website, www.xBWindshieldSettlement.com.  No additional mailings will be sent

Q1: Which vehicles are eligible for the CSP? 

A1: All 2006 Model Year Scion xB vehicles.

Q2: How many vehicles were manufactured during this production range? 

A2: There are approximately 90,000 MY 2006 Scion xB vehicles.

Q3: WHAT ARE THE REASONS FOR LAUNCHING THIS CSP? 

A3: In August of 2007, a class action was filed alleging that certain "Scion xBs are equipped with defective windshields that can crack with little or no impact." Although Scion stands by its product, in an effort to promote customer satisfaction and to resolve this litigation, Scion is offering this CSP as part of the settlement.

Q4: Why are the eligible vehicles limited to this range? 

A4: The windshield complies with Toyota's rigorous durability and quality standards as well as satisfies all regulatory requirements. However, during our investigation we discovered that, Toyota has received reports of more windshields cracks of MY 2006 Scion xB occurred, in comparison with the windshields of MY 04-05 model Year Scion xB.

Q5: What is the cause of the condition? 

A5: Scion investigated these reports, but found no clear technical factor or product defect which has caused this condition. However, there was a change in the production process of the windshield glass in September of 2005 which may have caused slight variations in the flexibility of the glass, within the specification range, on some windshields.

Q6: What will Toyota/Scion do? 

A6: Scion is planning to launch a Customer Support Program (CSP) that will be effective on August 24, 2010. Owners have already been notified. The CSP will extend the limited warranty applicable to the windshields, from 3 years/36,000 miles to 6 years/60,000 miles, and specify that cracks in Genuine Toyota windshields in MY 2006 Scion vehicles will be covered by that warranty unless it is obvious that the crack was caused by something that would normally cause a windshield to crack (e.g.  , a collision with another vehicle or stationary object, a natural disaster, vandalism, a baseball, or the like). Customers whose vehicles experience a covered crack in the windshield are entitled to receive one  of the following:

  1. Reimbursement for the cost of a previously replaced cracked windshield, if the replacement was done before the settlement becomes final;

    or 

  2. Replacement of a cracked windshield at their Scion dealer.

Q7: Is this a recall? 

A7: No. This is a Customer Support Program (warranty extension) and there are no mechanical defects with the vehicle.

Q8: Are there any other models involved in this CSP? 

A8: No. This CSP is limited to 2006 model year Scion xB vehicles.

Q9: How does a customer receive benefits? 

A9: Customers should refer to the previously mailed notice, visit www.xBWindshieldSettlement.com  or call 1-866-985-0134 for information about how to request reimbursement for a previously replaced windshield.

Q9a: How do I obtain reimbursement for a past windshield replacement? 

A9a: If your MY 2006 Scion xB's windshield cracked within the extended warranty and it is not obvious that the crack was caused by something that would normally cause a windshield to crack (e.g.  , a collision with another vehicle or stationary object, a natural disaster, vandalism, a baseball, or the like), you may be eligible for reimbursement. You must complete and return a claim form to apply for benefits; the form must be postmarked by September 23, 2010. The claim form was on the reverse side of the notice sent in the mail. You may also obtain one by visiting www. xBWindshieldSettlement.com  or by calling 1-866-985-0134.

If you previously submitted a reimbursement request, please be advised that processing of the requests will not begin until August 24, and will take up to six to eight weeks.

Q9b: How do I obtain replacement of a cracked windshield once the CSP is launched? 

A9b: If your MY 2006 Scion xB's windshield cracked within the extended warranty and it is not obvious that the crack was caused by something that would normally cause a windshield to crack (e.g.  , a collision with another vehicle or stationary object, a natural disaster, vandalism, a baseball, or the like), you may be eligible for replacement of the windshield if the Court approves the settlement. To have the windshield replaced on or after August 24, 2010, please visit your authorized Scion dealership. Please note that you are not eligible for replacement of a windshield if you have requested/received reimbursement for a prior windshield replacement. Also, if you disagree with your Scion dealer's determination of coverage under the extended warranty, please visit www.xBWindshieldSettlement.com  or call 1-866-985-0134 for further information.

Q10: What should a customer do if they currently have a cracked windshield? 

A10: If your windshield is cracked now, you may have your vehicle repaired and retain and submit receipts for future reimbursement consideration through the CSP. However, if the windshield is replaced on or after August 24, 2010, the replacement must be completed at an authorized Scion dealership to be eligible for coverage under this CSP.

Q11: What if customers have questions regarding their vehicle unrelated to the CSP? 

A11: Any Scion dealer will be more than happy to answer any questions.  If the customer requires further assistance, they may contact the Scion Customer Experience Center at 1-866-548-1851 Monday through Friday, 5:00 am to 6:00 pm, or Saturday 7:00 am to 4:00 pm Pacific Standard Time.
