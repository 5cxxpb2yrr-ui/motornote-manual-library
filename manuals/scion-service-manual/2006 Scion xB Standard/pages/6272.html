DTC B0121/26 Seat Belt Buckle Switch RH Circuit Malfunction: Description

The seat belt buckle switch RH circuit consists of the center airbag sensor assembly and the front seat inner belt assembly RH (seat belt buckle switch RH).

DTC B0121/26 is recorded when a malfunction is detected in the seat belt buckle switch RH circuit.

DTC DETECTION CONDITION AND TROUBLE AREA

DTC No. DTC Detection Condition Trouble Area
B0121/26
  • Short circuit in seat belt buckle switch RH wire harness (to ground)
  • Short circuit in seat belt buckle switch RH wire harness (to B+)
  • Open circuit in RBE+ wire harness or RBE- wire harness of front seat inner belt RH
  • Seat belt buckle switch RH malfunction
  • Center airbag sensor assembly
  • Front seat inner belt assembly RH (seat belt buckle switch RH)
  • Center airbag sensor assembly
  • Floor wire
