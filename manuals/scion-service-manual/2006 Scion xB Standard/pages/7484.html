Removal (2005/12- )

CAUTION: Be sure to read the precautionary notices concerning the SRS airbag system before servicing it (see PRECAUTION  ).
  1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL 
    CAUTION: Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to prevent airbag and seat belt pretensioner activation.
  2. REMOVE STEERING PAD ASSEMBLY 
    1. Straighten the front wheels.
    2. Using a T30 "Torx" socket, loosen the 2 screws until the groove along each screw circumference catches on the screw case.
      Fig 1: Removing Steering Pad Assembly
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    3. Pull out the steering pad from the steering wheel and support the steering pad with one hand as shown in the illustration.
      NOTE: When removing the steering pad, do not pull the airbag wire harness.
    4. Using a screwdriver, disconnect the 2 airbag connectors.
      NOTE: When handling the airbag connectors, do not damage the airbag wire harness.
    5. Disconnect the horn connector and remove the steering pad.
