TYPICAL MALFUNCTION THRESHOLDS - P0115

ECT sensor voltage Less than 0.14 V, or more than 4.91 V

TYPICAL MALFUNCTION THRESHOLDS - P0117

TYPICAL MALFUNCTION THRESHOLDS - P0118
