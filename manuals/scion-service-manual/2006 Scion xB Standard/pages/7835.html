Thermistor Assembly: Removal

  1. REMOVE HEATER RADIATOR ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the heater radiator (see REMOVAL  ).
  2. REMOVE THERMISTOR ASSEMBLY 
    1. Detach the claw and remove the clamp.
    2. Remove the thermistor.
