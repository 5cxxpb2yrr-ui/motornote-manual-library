Floor Shift Assembly: Disassembly

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. REMOVE TRANSMISSION CONTROL SWITCH 
    1. Disconnect the connector from the shift lever plate.
      Fig 1: Disconnecting Connector From Shift Lever Plate
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. Remove the transmission control switch connector from the shift lever plate.
    3. Disengage the secondary locking device.
    4. Release the locking lug of terminals 2 and 4, and then pull out the terminals from the rear.
      Fig 2: Removing Locking Lug Of Terminals
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  2. REMOVE SHIFT LEVER KNOB SUB-ASSEMBLY 
    1. Separate the wire harness of the transmission control switch from the shift lever plate.
    2. Remove the 2 screws and shift lever knob.
      Fig 3: Separating Wire Harness
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
      NOTE: Be careful not to apply excessive force to the transmission control switch wire harness.
    3. Remove the shift lever knob cover.
    4. Remove the shift lever knob button and spring.
    5. Detach the 2 claws and remove the transmission control switch.
      Fig 4: Removing Shift Lever Knob Button And Spring
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  3. REMOVE POSITION INDICATOR UPPER HOUSING 
    1. Remove the shift lock release button cover from the position indicator upper housing.
    2. Remove the position indicator upper housing from the shift lever.
    3. Remove the position indicator slide cover from the shift lever plate.
    4. Remove the position indicator plate from the position indicator upper housing.
      Fig 5: Removing Position Indicator Upper Housing From Shift Lever
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  4. REMOVE SHIFT LOCK CONTROL SWITCH 
    1. Remove the screw and shift lock control switch.
      Fig 6: Removing Screw And Shift Lock Control Switch
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  5. REMOVE SHIFT LOCK SOLENOID 
    1. Remove the shift lock solenoid from the floor shift.
      Fig 7: Removing Shift Lock Solenoid From Floor Shift
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  6. REMOVE INDICATOR LIGHT WIRE SUB-ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the indicator light wire from the floor shift.
      Fig 8: Removing Indicator Light Wire From Floor Shift
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  7. REMOVE SHIFT LOCK RELEASE BUTTON 
    1. Remove the shift lock release button and spring from the floor shift.
      Fig 9: Removing Shift Lock Release Button And Spring From Floor Shift
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
