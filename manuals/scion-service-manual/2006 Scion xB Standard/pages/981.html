Wiper Switch: Inspection

  1. INSPECT WINDSHIELD WIPER SWITCH ASSEMBLY 
    1. Measure the resistance of the switch.

      Standard resistance: 

      Front wiper switch 

      TERMINALS RESISTANCE SPECIFICATION (FRONT WIPER SWITCH)

      Tester Connection Switch Condition Specified Condition
      7 (+1) - 8 (+B) MIST Below 1 Ω
      6 (+S) - 7 (+1) OFF Below 1 Ω
      6 (+S) - 7 (+1) INT Below 1 Ω
      7 (+1) - 8 (+B) LO Below 1 Ω
      8 (+B) - 9 (+2) HI Below 1 Ω
      Fig 1: Identifying Windshield Wiper Switch Connectors
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

      Front washer switch 

      TERMINALS RESISTANCE SPECIFICATION (FRONT WASHER SWITCH)

      Tester Connection Switch Condition Specified Condition
      4 (W) - 5 (EW) OFF 10 kΩ or higher
      4 (W) - 5 (EW) ON Below 1 Ω

      Rear wiper switch 

      TERMINALS RESISTANCE SPECIFICATION (REAR WIPER SWITCH)

      Tester Connection Switch Condition Specified Condition
      3 (WR) - 5 (EW) WASH 1 Below 1 Ω
      2 (+1R) - 3 (WR) - 5 (EW) OFF 10 kΩ or higher
      2 (+1R) - 5 (EW) ON Below 1 Ω
      3 (WR) - 2 (+1R) - 5 (EW) WASH 2 Below 1 Ω

      If the result is not as specified, replace the switch assembly.

    2. Check the intermittent operation
      1. Connect the voltmeter's positive (+) lead to terminal 7 (+1) and the voltmeter's negative (-) lead to terminal to terminal 5 (EW).
      2. Connect the battery's positive (+) lead to terminal 8 (+B) and the battery's negative (-) lead to terminals 5 (EW) and 6 (+S).
      3. Turn the wiper switch to the INT position.
      4. Connect the battery's positive (+) lead to terminal 6 (+S) for 5 seconds.
      5. Connect the battery's negative (-) lead to terminal 6 (+S). Operate the intermittent wiper relay and check the voltage between terminals 7 (+1) and 5 (EW).

        OK:

        If the result is not as specified, replace the switch assembly.

    3. Check the front washer operation.
      1. Turn the wiper switch off.
      2. Connect the battery's positive (+) lead to terminal 8 (+B) and the battery's negative (-) lead to terminal 6 (+S) and 5 (EW).
      3. Connect the voltmeter's positive (+) lead to terminal 7 (+1) and the voltmeter's negative (-) lead to terminal 5 (EW).
    4. Turn the washer switch on and off, and check voltage between terminal 7 (+1) and 5 (EW).

      If the result is not as specified, replace the switch assembly.
