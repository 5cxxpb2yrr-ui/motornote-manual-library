Engine Oil Replacement Reminder Reset - Procedure 02

To reset MAINT reminder indicator after engine oil change:

  1. Turn ignition switch to OFF with ODOMETER display showing.
  2. While pressing the trip meter RESET button, turn ignition switch to ON.
  3. Hold trip meter RESET button down for at least 5 seconds. Odometer should indicate "000000", and MAINT reminder light goes out.
  4. If system fails to reset, reminder light will remain flashing.
