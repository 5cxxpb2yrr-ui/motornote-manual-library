Typical Malfunction Thresholds

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

P0787: Range check (Low resistance) 

TYPICAL MALFUNCTION THRESHOLDS

Intelligent power MOS diagnosis fail signals detected while the solenoid is operated Fail at solenoid resistance: 8 Ω or less

P0788: Range check (High resistance) 

TYPICAL MALFUNCTION THRESHOLDS

Intelligent power MOS diagnosis fail signals detected while the solenoid is not operated Fail at solenoid resistance: 100 kΩ or more
