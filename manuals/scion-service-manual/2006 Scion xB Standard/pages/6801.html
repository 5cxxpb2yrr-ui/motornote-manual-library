DTC P0711 Transmission Fluid Temperature Sensor "A" Performance: Description

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Refer to DTC P0710  (see ).

DTC DETECTION CONDITION

DTC No. DTC Detection Condition Trouble Area
P0711 Both (a) and (b) are detected (2 trip detection logic):
  1. 12 sec. after engine start, temperature of atmosphere and that of engine coolant temperature are more than -10°C (14°F)
  2. After normal driving for over 17 min. and 9 km (5.6 miles), ATF temperature is less than 10°C (50°F)
  • Transaxle fluid level
  • ATF temperature sensor
  • ECM
