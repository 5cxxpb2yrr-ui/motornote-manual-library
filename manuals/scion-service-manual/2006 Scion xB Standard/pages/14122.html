Introduction

To minimize battery discharge during transportation and storage, the DOME fuse has been removed at the assembly plant.

Applicable Vehicles

  • 2005 - 2006  model year Scion xB  vehicles.

Installation Procedure

  1. The removed DOME fuse (15A) is stored in the blank space of the Relay Block in the Engine Compartment.
    Fig 1: Locating DOME Fuse
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  2. During Pre-Delivery Service (PDS), install the DOME fuse (15A) in the Relay Block as shown.
    NOTE: If the vehicle is stored at the dealership for a long period of time after PDS, disconnect the negative (-) battery terminal to prevent battery discharge. Refer to TSB PG017-02, "Battery Maintenance for In-Stock Vehicles & Pre-Delivery," for battery maintenance information.
