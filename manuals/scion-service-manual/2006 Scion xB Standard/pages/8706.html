Rear Axle Beam: Removal

  1. REMOVE REAR WHEEL 
  2. DISCONNECT SKID CONTROL SENSOR WIRE 
  3. DISCONNECT REAR NO. 4 BRAKE TUBE (See  REMOVAL   ) 
  4. DISCONNECT REAR NO. 3 BRAKE TUBE (See  REMOVAL   ) 
  5. DISCONNECT NO. 3 PARKING BRAKE CABLE ASSEMBLY (See  REMOVAL   ) 
  6. DISCONNECT NO. 2 PARKING BRAKE CABLE ASSEMBLY (See  REMOVAL   ) 
  7. REMOVE REAR BRAKE DRUM SUB-ASSEMBLY (See  DISASSEMBLY  ) 
  8. REMOVE REAR AXLE HUB AND BEARING ASSEMBLY LH 
    1. Remove the nut and disconnect the No. 3 parking brake cable from the axle beam.
      Fig 1: Removing No. 3 Parking Brake Cable Nut
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. Remove the 4 bolts and axle hub and bearing.
    3. Disconnect the brake from the axle beam.
  9. REMOVE REAR AXLE HUB AND BEARING ASSEMBLY RH 

    HINT:

    Use the same procedures described for the LH side.

  10. LOOSEN REAR AXLE BEAM ASSEMBLY 
  11. DISCONNECT REAR SHOCK ABSORBER ASSEMBLY LH (See  REAR SHOCK ABSORBER   ) 
  12. DISCONNECT REAR SHOCK ABSORBER ASSEMBLY RH (See  REAR SHOCK ABSORBER   ) 
  13. REMOVE REAR COIL SPRING LH (See  REAR SHOCK ABSORBER   ) 
  14. REMOVE REAR COIL SPRING RH 

    HINT:

    Use the same procedures described for the LH side.

    Fig 2: Removing Axle Hub Bolts
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  15. REMOVE REAR AXLE BEAM ASSEMBLY 
    1. Support the axle beam with a jack.
    2. Remove the 2 bolts and the axle beam.
