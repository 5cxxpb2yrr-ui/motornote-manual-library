Heater Radiator Assembly: Removal

  1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL 
    CAUTION: Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to prevent airbag and seat belt pretensioner activation.
  2. DRAIN ENGINE COOLANT 
  3. DISCHARGE REFRIGERANT FROM REFRIGERATION SYSTEM (See  REPLACEMENT   ) 
  4. DISCONNECT SUCTION HOSE SUB-ASSEMBLY (See  REMOVAL   ) 
  5. DISCONNECT LIQUID TUBE SUB-ASSEMBLY (See  REMOVAL   ) 
  6. DISCONNECT HEATER WATER INLET HOSE 
    1. Using pliers, grip the claw of the clip and slide the clip to disconnect the heater water hose.
      Fig 1: Disconnecting Heater Water Inlet Hose
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  7. DISCONNECT HEATER WATER OUTLET HOSE 

    HINT:

    Use the same procedures described for the inlet hose.

  8. REMOVE INSTRUMENT PANEL SUB-ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the instrument panel (see REMOVAL ).
  9. REMOVE LOWER INSTRUMENT PANEL ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the instrument panel (see REMOVAL ).
  10. REMOVE ECM (See  REMOVAL   ) 
  11. REMOVE AIR CONDITIONING BLOWER ASSEMBLY (See  REMOVAL   ) 
  12. REMOVE AIR CONDITIONING AMPLIFIER ASSEMBLY 
  13. REMOVE DEFROSTER NOZZLE ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the clip.
    2. Detach the 2 claws and remove the defroster nozzle.
      Fig 2: Removing Defroster Nozzle Assembly
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  14. REMOVE DEFROSTER DAMPER CONTROL CABLE SUB-ASSEMBLY 
    1. Disconnect the outer cable.
    2. Disconnect the inner cable and remove the defroster damper control cable.
      Fig 3: Disconnecting Inner Cable And Removing Defroster Damper Control Cable
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  15. REMOVE AIR MIX DAMPER CONTROL CABLE SUBASSEMBLY 
    1. Disconnect the air outer cable.
    2. Disconnect the inner cable and remove the air mix damper control cable.
      Fig 4: Disconnecting Inner Cable And Removing Air Mix Damper Control Cable
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  16. DISCONNECT NO. 2 AIR DUCT REAR 
  17. DISCONNECT NO. 1 AIR DUCT REAR 
    Fig 5: Disconnecting No. 1 Air Duct Rear
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  18. REMOVE HEATER RADIATOR ASSEMBLY 
    1. Disconnect the 3 connectors.
    2. Remove the bolt and disconnect the ground wire from the PTC heater.
      Fig 6: Removing Bolt And Disconnecting Ground Wire From PTC Heater
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    3. Remove the 2 nuts and heater radiator.
      Fig 7: Removing 2 Nuts And Heater Radiator
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
