Parking Brake Cable: Removal

HINT:

  • Use the same procedures for the No. 2 parking brake cable and No. 3 parking brake cable.
  • The procedures listed below are for the No. 3 parking brake cable.
  1. REMOVE SHIFT LEVER KNOB SUB-ASSEMBLY (for Manual Transaxle) 
  2. REMOVE REAR CONSOLE BOX COVER 
  3. REMOVE CONSOLE BOX CARPET 
  4. REMOVE REAR CONSOLE BOX ASSEMBLY (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  5. REMOVE NO. 1 WIRE ADJUSTING NUT 
    1. Remove the No. 1 wire adjusting nut.
  6. REMOVE FRONT FLOOR PANEL BRACE 
    1. Remove the 2 bolts and floor panel brace.
  7. REMOVE FRONT EXHAUST PIPE ASSEMBLY (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  8. REMOVE FRONT NO. 2 FLOOR HEAT INSULATOR 
    1. Remove the 3 bolts and heat insulator.
  9. DISCONNECT NO. 3 PARKING BRAKE CABLE ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the nut and 3 bolts, and then disconnect the parking brake cable from the body.
    2. Disconnect the parking brake cable from the parking brake equalizer.
  10. REMOVE REAR WHEEL 
  11. REMOVE REAR BRAKE DRUM SUB-ASSEMBLY (See  DISASSEMBLY  ) 
  12. DISCONNECT PARKING BRAKE SHOE STRUT LH (See  DISASSEMBLY  ) 
  13. REMOVE FRONT BRAKE SHOE (See  DISASSEMBLY  ) 
  14. REMOVE PARKING BRAKE SHOE STRUT LH (See  DISASSEMBLY  ) 
  15. REMOVE REAR BRAKE AUTOMATIC ADJUST LEVER LH (See  DISASSEMBLY  ) 
  16. REMOVE REAR BRAKE SHOE (See  DISASSEMBLY  ) 
  17. REMOVE NO. 3 PARKING BRAKE CABLE ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the bolt and then disconnect the parking brake cable from the backing plate.
