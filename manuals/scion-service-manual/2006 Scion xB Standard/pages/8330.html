Combination Meter: Disassembly

  1. REMOVE COMBINATION METER GLASS 
    1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 3 claws and remove the combination meter glass.

      HINT:

      Tape the screwdriver tip before use.

  2. REMOVE NO. 1 COMBINATION METER COVER 
    1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 6 claws and remove the combination meter cover.

      HINT:

      Tape the screwdriver tip before use.
