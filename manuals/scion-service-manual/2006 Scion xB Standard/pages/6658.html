Rear Axle Beam: Disassembly

  1. REMOVE REAR AXLE CARRIER BUSH LH 
    1. Using a chisel and hammer, bend the 2 portions of the bush's rib.

      HINT:

      Bend the bush's rib until the claw of SST can be hung as shown in the illustration.

    2. Using SST, remove the bush from the axle beam.

      SST 09309-37010, 09950-40011 (09951-04010, 09952-04010, 09953-04010, 09954-04020, 09955-04051, 09957-04010, 09958-04011) 

      NOTE: If the axle beam has been scratched, apply paint.
  2. REMOVE REAR AXLE CARRIER BUSH RH 

    HINT:

    Use the same procedures described for the LH side.
