Inspection Procedure

  1. CHECK INSTRUMENT PANEL WIRE (FRONT PASSENGER SIDE SQUIB CIRCUIT (2nd STEP)) 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to the LOCK position.
    2. Disconnect the negative (-) terminal cable from the battery, and wait for at least 90 seconds.
    3. Disconnect the connectors from the center airbag sensor assembly and the front passenger airbag assembly.
    4. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

      Standard resistance 

      TESTER CONNECTION - SPECIFIED CONDITION (RESISTANCE)

      Tester Connection (Connector "C") Specified Condition
      A12-3 (P2+) - Body ground 1 MΩ or higher
      A12-4 (P2-) - Body ground

    NG : REPAIR OR REPLACE INSTRUMENT PANEL WIRE 

    Fig 1: Checking Instrument Panel Wire (Front Passenger Side Squib Circuit (2nd Step))
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    OK : Go to Next Step 

  2. CHECK CENTER AIRBAG SENSOR ASSEMBLY 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to the LOCK position.
    2. Using SST, connect terminals P2+ and P2- of connector "C".

      SST 09843-18040 

      NOTE: Do not forcibly insert SST into the terminals of the connector when connecting.
    3. Connect the negative (-) terminal cable to the battery, and wait for at least 2 seconds.
    4. Turn the ignition switch to the ON position, and wait for at least 60 seconds.
    5. Clear the stored DTCs in the memory (see DTC CHECK / CLEAR  ).
    6. Turn the ignition switch to the LOCK position.
    7. Turn the ignition switch to the ON position, and wait for at least 60 seconds.
    8. Check for the DTCs (see DTC CHECK / CLEAR  ).

      OK: 

      DTC B1187/55 is not output. 

      HINT:

      Codes other than code B1187/55 may be output at this time, but they are not related to this check.

    NG : REPLACE CENTER AIRBAG SENSOR ASSEMBLY 

    Fig 2: Connecting Terminals P2+ And P2- Of Connector "C"
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    OK : Go to Next Step 

  3. CHECK FRONT PASSENGER AIRBAG ASSEMBLY (FRONT PASSENGER SIDE SQUIB (2nd STEP)) 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to the LOCK position.
    2. Disconnect the negative (-) terminal cable from the battery, and wait for at least 90 seconds.
    3. Disconnect the SST from the connector "C".
    4. Connect the connector to the front passenger airbag assembly.
    5. Connect the negative (-) terminal cable to the battery, and wait for at least 2 seconds.
    6. Turn the ignition switch to the ON position, and wait for at least 60 seconds.
    7. Clear the stored DTCs in the memory (see DTC CHECK / CLEAR  ).
    8. Turn the ignition switch to the LOCK position.
    9. Turn the ignition switch to the ON position, and wait for at least 60 seconds.
    10. Check for the DTCs (see DTC CHECK / CLEAR  ).

      OK: 

      DTC B1187/55 is not output. 

      HINT:

      Codes other than code B1187/55 may be output at this time, but they are not related to this check.

    NG : REPLACE FRONT PASSENGER AIRBAG ASSEMBLY
