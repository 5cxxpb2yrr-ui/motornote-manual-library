Typical Malfunction Thresholds

Heated oxygen sensor voltage (P0130): 

TYPICAL MALFUNCTION THRESHOLDS - HEATED OXYGEN SENSOR VOLTAGE (P0130)

Occasion either of following conditions is met More than 2 times
Minimum HO2 sensor voltage 0.4 V or more for 25 seconds or more
Maximum HO2 sensor voltage 0.55 V or less for 25 seconds or more

Heated oxygen sensor voltage stuck Lean (P2195): 

TYPICAL MALFUNCTION THRESHOLDS - HEATED OXYGEN SENSOR VOLTAGE STUCK LEAN (P2195)

Occasions following condition is met 2 times
Maximum heated oxygen sensor voltage 0.55 V or less for 25 seconds or more

Heated oxygen sensor voltage stuck Rich (P2196): 

TYPICAL MALFUNCTION THRESHOLDS - HEATED OXYGEN SENSOR VOLTAGE STUCK RICH (P2196)

Occasions following condition is met 2 times
Minimum heated oxygen sensor voltage 0.4 V or more for 25 seconds or more
