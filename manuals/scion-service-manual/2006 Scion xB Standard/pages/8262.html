Engine Coolant Temperature Sensor: Inspection

  1. INSPECT ENGINE COOLANT TEMPERATURE SENSOR 
    1. Measure the resistance of the sensor.

      Standard resistance 

      TESTER CONNECTION - SPECIFIED CONDITIONS

      Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
      1 - 2 20°C (68°F) 2.29 to 2.60 kΩ
      1 - 2 80°C (176°F) 0.301 to 0.327 kΩ
      NOTE: If checking the engine coolant temperature sensor in water, be careful not to allow water to intrude the terminals. After checking, dry the sensor.

      If the result is not as specified, replace the engine coolant temperature sensor.
