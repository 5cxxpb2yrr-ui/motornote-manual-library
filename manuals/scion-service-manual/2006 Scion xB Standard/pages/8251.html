Camshaft Timing Oil Control Valve Assembly: Removal

  1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL 
    CAUTION: Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to prevent airbag and seat belt pretensioner activation.
  2. REMOVE FAN BELT ADJUSTING BAR 
    1. Remove the bolt, nut and adjusting bar.
      Fig 1: Removing Fan Belt Adjusting Bar
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  3. REMOVE CAMSHAFT TIMING OIL CONTROL VALVE ASSEMBLY 
    1. Disconnect the oil control valve connector.
    2. Remove the bolt and oil control valve.
    3. Remove the O-ring from the oil control valve.
