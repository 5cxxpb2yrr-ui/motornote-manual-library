DTC Check / Clear (2005/12- )

  1. CHECK DTC (USING SST CHECK WIRE) 
    1. Check for the DTCs (present trouble code).
      1. Turn the ignition switch ON, and wait for approximately 60 seconds.
      2. Using SST, connect terminals 4 (CG) and 13 (TC) of the DLC3.

        SST 09843-18040 

        NOTE: Connect the terminals to the correct positions to avoid a malfunction.
    2. Check for the DTCs (past trouble code).
      1. Using SST, connect terminals 4 (CG) and 13 (TC) of the DLC3.

        SST 09843-18040 

        NOTE: Connect the terminals to the correct positions to avoid a malfunction.
      2. Turn the ignition switch ON, and wait for approximately 60 seconds.
        Fig 1: Connecting Terminals TC And CG Of DLC3
        Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    3. Read the DTCs.
      1. Read the blinking patterns of the DTCs.

        Examples of the blinking patterns for the normal system code and DTCs 11 and 31 are shown in the illustration.

        • Normal system code indication (without past trouble code).

          The light blinks twice per second.

        • Normal system code indication (with past trouble code).

          When the past trouble code is stored in the center airbag sensor, the light blinks once per second.

        • DTC indication

          The first blinking pattern indicates the first DTC. If there is more than one DTC, a 2.5 second pause occurs between each blinking pattern. After all blinking patterns are shown, a 4.0 second pause occurs. Then all blinking patterns are repeated.

        HINT:

        • If 2 or more malfunctions are found, the indication begins with the smaller numbered code.
        • If DTCs are indicated without connecting the terminals, check the TC AND CG TERMINAL CIRCUIT  .
          Fig 2: Identifying DTC Blinking Patterns
          Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  2. CLEAR DTC (USING SST CHECK WIRE) 
    1. When the ignition switch is turned OFF, the DTCs are erased.

      HINT:

      Depending on the DTC, the code may not be cleared by turning the ignition switch OFF. In this case, proceed to the next procedure.

    2. Using SST, connect terminals TC and CG of the DLC3, and then turn the ignition switch ON.

      SST 09843-18040 

    3. After DTCs are output, disconnect terminal 13 (TC) of the DLC3 within 3 to 10 seconds. Check that the SRS warning light illuminates 3 seconds later.
    4. After the SRS warning light illuminates, connect terminal 13 (TC) of the DLC3 within 2 to 4 seconds. Check that the SRS warning light turns off within 2 to 4 seconds.
    5. After the SRS warning light turns off, disconnect terminal 13 (TC) of the DLC3 within 2 to 4 seconds. Check that the SRS warning light illuminates within 2 to 4 seconds.
    6. After the SRS warning light illuminates, connect terminal 13 (TC) of the DLC3 within 2 to 4 seconds. Check that the SRS warning light turns off within 2 to 4 seconds. Also check that the normal system code is output within 1 second.

      If DTCs are not erased, repeat this procedure until the codes are erased.

      Fig 3: Connecting Terminal 13 (TC) Of DLC3 Within 2 To 4 Seconds
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  3. CHECK FOR DTC (USING INTELLIGENT TESTER) 
    1. Connect the intelligent tester (with CAN VIM) to the DLC3.
    2. Turn the ignition switch ON.
    3. Check for DTCs by following the prompts on the tester screen.

      HINT:

      Refer to the intelligent tester operator's manual for further details.

  4. CLEAR DTC (USING INTELLIGENT TESTER) 
    1. Connect the intelligent tester (with CAN VIM) to the DLC3.
    2. Turn the ignition switch ON.
    3. Clear the DTCs by following the prompts on the tester screen.

      HINT:

      Refer to the intelligent tester operator's manual for further details.
