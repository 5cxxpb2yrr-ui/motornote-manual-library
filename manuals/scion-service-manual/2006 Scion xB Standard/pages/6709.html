Clutch Master Cylinder: Removal

  1. REMOVE BRAKE MASTER CYLINDER SUBASSEMBLY 
  2. REMOVE BRAKE ACTUATOR ASSEMBLY 
  3. REMOVE BRAKE BOOSTER ASSEMBLY 
  4. REMOVE CLUTCH PEDAL SPRING 
  5. DISCONNECT CLUTCH MASTER CYLINDER PUSH ROD CLEVIS 
    1. Remove the clip and pin and disconnect the push rod clevis from the clutch pedal.
  6. DRAIN BRAKE FLUID 
    NOTE: Wash brake fluid off immediately if it adheres to any painted surface.
    Fig 1: Removing Clip And Pin
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  7. DISCONNECT CLUTCH RESERVOIR TUBE 
    1. Loosen the clip and disconnect the tube from the clutch master cylinder.

      HINT:

      Use a container to catch the fluid.

      Fig 2: Disconnecting Clutch Reservoir Tube
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  8. DISCONNECT CLUTCH MASTER CYLINDER TO FLEXIBLE HOSE TUBE 
    1. Using SST, disconnect the flexible hose tube.

      SST 09023-00101 

      HINT:

      Use a container to catch the fluid.

  9. REMOVE CLUTCH MASTER CYLINDER ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the 2 nuts and clutch master cylinder.
