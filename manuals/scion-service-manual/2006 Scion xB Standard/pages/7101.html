Floor Shift Assembly: Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. REMOVE CONSOLE BOX REAR COVER 
  2. REMOVE CONSOLE BOX CARPET 
  3. REMOVE REAR CONSOLE BOX ASSEMBLY 
    1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 8 claws and remove the rear console box cover.
      Fig 1: Removing Console Box Cover
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
      NOTE: Tape the screwdriver tip before use.
    2. Remove the console box carpet.
    3. Remove the screw.
    4. Disconnect the connector.
    5. Using a screwdriver, detach the 2 clips and remove the console box.
      Fig 2: Removing Clips And Console Box
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  4. DISCONNECT FLOOR SHIFT PARKING LOCK CABLE ASSEMBLY 
    1. Rotate the adjust lock cap clockwise 90° so that it faces the front of the vehicle, and release it.
    2. Push the claw and disconnect the floor shift parking lock cable from the shift lock release link.
      Fig 3: Pushing Claw And Disconnecting Floor Shift Parking Lock Cable From Shift Lock Release Link
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    3. Using a screwdriver, disconnect the floor shift parking lock cable from the shift lever plate.
      Fig 4: Disconnecting Floor Shift Parking Lock Cable From Shift Lever Plate
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  5. DISCONNECT TRANSMISSION CONTROL CABLE ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the nut, and disconnect the transmission control cable from the floor shift.
    2. Using a screwdriver, disconnect the transmission control cable from the shift lever plate.
      Fig 5: Removing Nut, And Disconnecting Transmission Control Cable From Floor Shift
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  6. REMOVE FLOOR SHIFT ASSEMBLY 
    1. Disconnect the 2 connectors.
    2. Remove the 2 wire harness clamps from the floor shift.
    3. Remove the 4 bolts and floor shift.
      Fig 6: Removing Bolts And Floor Shift
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
