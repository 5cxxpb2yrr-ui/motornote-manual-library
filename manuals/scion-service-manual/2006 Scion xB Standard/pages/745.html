Side Turn Signal Light Assembly: Installation

HINT:

  • Use the same procedures for the RH side and LH side.
  • The procedures listed below are for the LH side.
  1. INSTALL SIDE TURN SIGNAL LIGHT ASSEMBLY LH 
    1. Connect the connector and install the side turn signal light.
    2. Attach the side turn signal light with the 2 claws and install the light.
  2. CONNECT CABLE TO NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL
