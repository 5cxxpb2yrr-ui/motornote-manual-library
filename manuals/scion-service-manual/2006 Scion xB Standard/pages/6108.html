Removal & Installation (2004-2006)

  1. The air conditioning filters are behind the glove box. See Fig 1.
    Fig 1: Locating A/C Filter No. 1 & No. 2
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  2. Open glove compartment door.
  3. Push in sides of the upper part of the glove compartment door to release the stoppers. See Fig 2. Pull glove compartment door rearward to remove it.
    Fig 2: Removing Glove Compartment Door
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  4. Remove filter case by disconnecting claws on each side of case cover. See Fig 3.
    Fig 3: Removing Filter Case
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  5. Remove air filter from the case. See Fig 4.
    Fig 4: Removing Filter From Case
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  6. Blow the dust of the filter No. 1 with compressed air from the backside of the filter No. 1. See Fig 5. If it is dirty or torn, it should be replaced.
    Fig 5: Cleaning Filter No. 1
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  7. Clean the surface of the filter No. 2 using a moist cloth. See . If it is dirty or torn, it should be replaced.
    Fig 6: Cleaning Surface Of Filter No. 2
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  8. To install filter, reverse removal procedure.
NOTE: The air filters should be installed properly in position. The use of air conditioning with the air filters removed may cause deteriorated dustproof performance and than affect air conditioning performance.
