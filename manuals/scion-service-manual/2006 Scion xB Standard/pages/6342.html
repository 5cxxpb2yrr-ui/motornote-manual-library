System Description

The front airbag sensor LH consists of the diagnostic circuit and front deceleration sensor. The center airbag sensor receives signals from the front deceleration sensor, and determines whether or not the SRS should be activated. DTC B1615/14 is recorded when a malfunction is detected in the front airbag sensor LH circuit.

DTC DETECTION CONDITION AND TROUBLE AREA

DTC No. DTC Detection Condition Trouble Area
B1615/14 When one of following conditions is met:
  • Center airbag sensor receives line short signal, open signal, short to ground signal or B+ short signal in front airbag sensor LH circuit for 2 seconds
  • Front airbag sensor LH malfunction
  • Center airbag sensor malfunction
  • Front airbag sensor LH
  • Center airbag sensor assembly
  • Instrument panel wire
  • Engine room main wire
