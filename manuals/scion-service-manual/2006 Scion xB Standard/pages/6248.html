DTC B0100/13 Short in Driver Side Squib Circuit: Description

The driver side squib circuit consists of the center airbag sensor assembly, the spiral cable sub-assembly and the steering pad.

This circuit actuates the SRS to deploy when deployment conditions are met.

DTC B0100/13 is recorded when a short circuit is detected in the driver side squib circuit.

DTC DETECTION CONDITION AND TROUBLE AREA

DTC No. DTC Detection Condition Trouble Area
B1000/13
  • Short circuit between D+ wire harness and D- wire harness of driver side squib
  • Driver side squib malfunction
  • Spiral cable sub-assembly malfunction
  • Center airbag sensor assembly malfunction
  • Steering pad (driver side squib)
  • Spiral cable sub-assembly
  • Center airbag sensor assembly
  • Instrument panel wire
