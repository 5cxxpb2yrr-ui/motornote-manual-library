Service Data

1NZ-FE LUBRICATION SERVICE DATA

Oil pressure: at idle 29 kPa (0.3 kgf/cm2 , 4.2 psi) or more
Oil pressure: at 3, 000 rpm 150 to 550 kPa (1.5 to 5.6 kgf/cm2 , 22 to 80 psi) or more
Oil pump: Tip clearance Standard: 0.060 to 0.180 mm (0.0024 to 0.0071 in.)
Maximum: 0.28 mm (0.0110 in.)
Oil pump: Body clearance Standard: 0.250 to 0.325 mm (0.0098 to 0.0128 in.)
Maximum: 0.425 mm (0.0167 in.)
Oil pump: Side clearance Standard: 0.03 to 0.09 mm (0.0012 to 0.0035 in.)
Maximum: 0.15 mm (0.0059 in.)
