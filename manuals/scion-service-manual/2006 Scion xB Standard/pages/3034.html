Typical Malfunction Thresholds

Mass Air Flow Meter Rationality (High Voltage): 

TYPICAL MALFUNCTION THRESHOLDS - MASS AIR FLOW METER RATIONALITY (HIGH VOLTAGE)

Mass air flow meter voltage More than 2.2 V (varies with throttle position sensor voltage)

Mass Air Flow Meter Rationality (Low Voltage): 

TYPICAL MALFUNCTION THRESHOLDS - MASS AIR FLOW METER RATIONALITY (LOW VOLTAGE)

Mass air flow meter voltage Less than 0.65 V (varies with throttle position sensor voltage)
