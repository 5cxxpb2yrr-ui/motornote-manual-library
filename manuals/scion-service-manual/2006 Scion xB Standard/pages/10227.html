FRONT SUSPENSION LOWER NO. 1 ARM (for Automatic Transaxle): Installation

  1. TEMPORARILY INSTALL FRONT SUSPENSION NO. 1 LOWER ARM SUB-ASSEMBLY LH 
    1. Temporarily install the lower arm with the 2 bolts and nut.
      Fig 1: Installing Lower Arm With Bolts And Nut
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  2. CONNECT FRONT SUSPENSION CROSSMEMBER SUB-ASSEMBLY 
    1. Using SST, align the holes of the suspension crossmember and body.

      SST 09670-00010 

    2. Perform the procedure above on the other side.
      Fig 2: Aligning Holes Of Suspension Crossmember And Body
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    3. Install the suspension crossmember with the 4 bolts.

      Torque: 70 N*m (715 kgf*cm, 52 ft.*lbf) for bolt A 

      116 N*m (,180 kgf*cm, 86 ft.*lbf) for bolt B 

      Fig 3: Installing Suspension Crossmember With Bolts
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    4. Install the bolt and 2 nuts.

      Torque: 80 N*m (816 kgf*cm, 59 ft.*lbf) 

  3. INSTALL FRONT SUSPENSION MEMBER REINFORCEMENT LH 
    1. Install the reinforcement with the 2 bolts.

      Torque: 47 N*m (480 kgf*cm, 35 ft.*lbf) 

  4. INSTALL FRONT SUSPENSION MEMBER REINFORCEMENT RH 
    1. Install the reinforcement with the 2 bolts.

      Torque: 47 N*m (480 kgf*cm, 35 ft.*lbf) 

      Fig 4: Installing Suspension Crossmember With Nuts
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  5. CONNECT PRESSURE FEED TUBE ASSEMBLY 
    1. Connect the pressure feed tube with the bolt.

      Torque: 7.8 N*m (80 kgf*cm, 69 in.*lbf) 

      Fig 5: Connecting Pressure Feed Tube With Bolt
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  6. CONNECT POWER STEERING LINK ASSEMBLY 
    1. Install the steering link with the 4 bolts and 4 nuts.

      Torque: 74 N*m (755 kgf*cm, 55 ft.*lbf) 

      NOTE: Do not turn the nut.
      Fig 6: Installing Steering Link With Bolts And Nuts
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  7. CONNECT FRONT STABILIZER BAR 
    Fig 7: Installing Stabilizer Bolt
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    1. Install the 2 retainers and cushion.
    2. While holding the stabilizer bolt with a wrench, install the nut.

      Torque: 18 N*m (180 kgf*cm, 13 ft.*lbf) 

      HINT:

      Support the lower arm with a jack.

    3. Perform the procedure above on the other side.
  8. CONNECT FRONT SUSPENSION NO. 1 LOWER ARM SUB-ASSEMBLY LH 
    1. Connect the lower arm to the steering knuckle with the nut.

      Torque: 98 N*m (1,000 kgf*cm, 72 ft.*lbf) 

    2. Install a new clip.
      NOTE: If the holes for the clip are not aligned, tighten the nut further up to 60°.
  9. CONNECT FRONT SUSPENSION NO. 1 LOWER ARM SUB-ASSEMBLY RH 

    HINT:

    Use the same procedures described for the LH side.

  10. INSTALL FRONT WHEEL 

    Torque: 103 N*m (1,050 kgf*cm, 76 ft.*lbf) 

  11. FULLY TIGHTEN FRONT SUSPENSION NO. 1 LOWER ARM SUB-ASSEMBLY LH 
    1. Fully tighten the lower arm with the 2 bolts.

      Torque: 88 N*m (900 kgf*cm, 65 ft.*lbf) for bolt A 

      132 N*m (1,350 kgf*cm, 97 ft.*lbf) for bolt B 

      NOTE: Do not turn the nut.
  12. INSTALL HOOD SUB-ASSEMBLY 
  13. INSPECT AND ADJUST HOOD SUB-ASSEMBLY 
  14. INSPECT AND ADJUST FRONT WHEEL ALIGNMENT 
    1. Inspect and adjust the front wheel alignment (see FRONT WHEEL ALIGNMENT  ).
