Evaporator Temperature Sensor: Inspection

  1. INSPECT COOLER THERMISTOR 
    1. Measure the resistance of the thermistor.

      Standard resistance 

      TESTER CONNECTION AND SPECIFIED CONDITION CHART

      Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
      1 - 2 -10°C (14°F) 7.40 to 9.20 kΩ
      1 - 2 -5°C (23°F) 5.65 to 7.00 kΩ
      1 - 2 0°C (32°F) 4.35 to 5.40 kΩ
      1 - 2 5°C (41°F) 3.40 to 4.20 kΩ
      1 - 2 10°C (50°F) 2.68 to 3.30 kΩ
      1 - 2 15°C (59°F) 2.10 to 2.60 kΩ
      1 - 2 20°C (68°F) 1.66 to 2.10 kΩ
      1 - 2 25°C (77°F) 1.32 to 1.66 kΩ
      1 - 2 30°C (86°F) 1.05 to 1.35 kΩ
      NOTE:
      • Even slightly touching the sensor may change the resistance value. Be sure to hold the connector of the sensor.
      • When measuring, the sensor temperature must be almost the same as the ambient temperature.

      HINT:

      As the temperature increases, the resistance decreases (see the graph). If the resistance value is not as specified, replace the sensor.
