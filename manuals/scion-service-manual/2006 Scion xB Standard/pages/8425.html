Clutch Unit: Inspection

  1. INSPECT CLUTCH DISC ASSEMBLY 
    1. Using a vernier caliper, measure the rivet head depth.

      Minimum rivet depth: 0.3 mm (0.012 in.) 

      If the depth is less than the minimum, replace the clutch disc assembly.

    2. Install the disc on the transaxle.
      NOTE: Take care not to insert the clutch disc assembly in the wrong direction.
      Fig 1: Measuring Rivet Head Depth
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    3. Using a dial gauge, measure the disc runout.

      Maximum runout: 0.8 mm (0.031 in.) 

      If the runout is greater than the maximum, replace the clutch disc assembly.

      Fig 2: Measuring Disc Runout
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  2. INSPECT CLUTCH COVER ASSEMBLY 
    1. Using a vernier caliper, measure the diaphragm spring for depth and width of wear.

      Maximum wear 

      MEASUREMENT REFERENCE

      Measurement Maximum Wear
      A (Depth) 0.5 mm (0.020 in.)
      B (Width) 6.0 mm (0.236 in.)

      If the wear is greater than the maximum, replace the clutch cover assembly.

      Fig 3: Measuring Diaphragm Spring For Depth And Width Of Wear
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  3. INSPECT FLYWHEEL SUB-ASSEMBLY 
    1. Using a dial gauge, measure the flywheel runout.

      Maximum runout: 0.1 mm (0.004 in.) 

      If the runout is greater than the maximum, replace the flywheel sub-assembly.

      Fig 4: Measuring Flywheel Runout
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  4. INSPECT CLUTCH RELEASE BEARING ASSEMBLY 
    1. Check that the bearing moves smoothly without abnormal resistance by turning the sliding parts of the bearing (contact surfaces with the clutch cover) while applying force in the axial direction.
    2. Inspect the bearing for damage or wear. If necessary, replace the release bearing assembly.
