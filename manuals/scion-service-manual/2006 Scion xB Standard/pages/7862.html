On-Vehicle Inspection

  1. INSPECT FUSE (HORN) 
    1. Measure the resistance of the fuse.

      Standard resistance: 

      Below 1 Ω 

      If the result is not as specified, replace the fuse.

      Fig 1: Measuring Resistance Of Fuse
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  2. REMOVE HORN RELAY (Marking: HORN) 
    1. Remove the engine room relay block cover.
    2. Remove the horn relay from the engine room relay block.
  3. INSPECT HORN RELAY 
    1. Measure the resistance of the horn relay.

      Standard resistance 

      TESTER CONNECTION AND SPECIFIED CONDITION CHART

      Tester Connection Specified Condition
      3 - 5 10 kΩ or higher
      3 - 5 Below 1 Ω (when battery voltage is applied to terminals 1 and 2)

      If the result is not as specified, replace the relay.

  4. INSTALL HORN RELAY
