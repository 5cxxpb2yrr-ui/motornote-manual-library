Typical Enabling Conditions

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
TYPICAL ENABLING CONDITIONS

The monitor will run whenever the following DTCs are not present. P0500: VSS
P0748: Shift solenoid SL1
P0778: Shift solenoid SL2
P0982, P0983: Shift solenoid S4
Shift change Shift change is completed before starting next shift change operation
ECM selected gear 3rd or 4th
Output shaft rpm 1,000 rpm or more
NSW switch OFF
R switch OFF
L switch OFF
Engine Running
Transmission range switch Not detected
