Disassembly (2005/12- )

HINT:

  • Use the same procedures for the RH side and LH side.
  • The procedures listed below are for the LH side.
CAUTION: Wear protective gloves. Sharp surfaces on the seatback spring and seat adjuster may injure your hands.
  1. REMOVE FRONT SEAT HEADREST ASSEMBLY 
  2. REMOVE RECLINING ADJUSTER RELEASE HANDLE LH 
    1. Raise the reclining adjuster release handle to reveal the claw.
    2. Using a screwdriver, detach the claw and remove the release handle.

      HINT:

      Tape the screwdriver tip before use.

      Fig 1: Identifying Release Handle Removal Procedure Using Taped Screwdriver
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  3. REMOVE FRONT SEAT CUSHION SHIELD LH 
    1. Remove the screw.
    2. Using a screwdriver, detach the 2 claws and clip, and remove the seat cushion shield.

      HINT:

      Tape the screwdriver tip before use.

  4. REMOVE FRONT SEAT INNER BELT ASSEMBLY LH (See  REMOVAL  ) 
    Fig 2: Detaching Claws And Clip
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  5. REMOVE FRONT SEAT CUSHION SHIELD INNER LH 
    1. Remove the screw.
    2. Using a screwdriver, detach the claw and clip, and remove the cushion shield.

      HINT:

      Tape the screwdriver tip before use.

      Fig 3: Removing Screw
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  6. REMOVE FRONT SEATBACK COVER 
    1. Remove the 3 hog rings.
      Fig 4: Removing Hog Rings
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. Fold back the seatback cover and remove the 3 hog rings.
      Fig 5: Identifying Hog Rings Removal Procedure From Seatback Cover
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    3. w/Seat hook: Remove the screw and seatback hook.
      Fig 6: Removing Screw And Seatback Hook
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    4. Remove the 2 hog rings.
      Fig 7: Locating Back Side Hog Rings
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    5. Remove the 2 hog rings.
      Fig 8: Locating Front Side Hog Rings
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    6. Remove the 4 hog rings.
      Fig 9: Removing Front Side Hog Rings
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    7. Remove the 2 hog rings.
      Fig 10: Identifying Front Side Hog Ring Location
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    8. Fold back the seatback cover to the top of the seat, and then detach the 4 claws and remove the 2 headrest supports.
      Fig 11: Removing Headrest Supports
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    9. Remove the 2 hog rings and the seatback cover from the seatback frame.
    10. Remove the hog ring and remove the seatback cover.
    11. Remove the seatback pad.
  7. REMOVE FRONT SEAT CUSHION COVER 
    1. Remove the hog rings.
      Fig 12: Removing Front Side Hog Ring And Seatback Cover
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. Detach the claw of the front part of the seat adjuster.
    3. Detach the hook and remove the seat cushion cover (with pad) from the seat adjuster.
    4. Remove the hog rings and seat cushion cover.
      Fig 13: Detaching Hook And Claw
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  8. REMOVE RECLINING ADJUSTER INSIDE COVER LH 
    1. Remove the screw and inside cover.
      Fig 14: Removing Inside Cover Screw
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  9. REMOVE RECLINING ADJUSTER INSIDE COVER RH 
    1. Remove the screw and inside cover.
      Fig 15: Removing Screw And Inside Cover
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
