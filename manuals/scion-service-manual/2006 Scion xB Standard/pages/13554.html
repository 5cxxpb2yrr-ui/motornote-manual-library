DTC C1241/41 Low Battery Positive Voltage or Abnormally High Battery Positive Voltage: Description

DETECTION CONDITION CHART

DTC No. DTC Detection Condition Trouble Area
C1241/41 When either of following is detected:
  1. Both conditions continue for at least 10 seconds
    • Vehicle speed is more than 3 km/h (2 mph)
    • IG1 terminal voltage is less than 9.5 V
  2. All conditions continue for at least 0.2 seconds
    • Solenoid relay remains ON
    • Relay contact is open
    • IG1 terminal voltage is less than 9.5 V
  • Battery
  • Charging system
  • Power source circuit
