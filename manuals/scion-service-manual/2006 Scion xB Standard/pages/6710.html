Clutch Master Cylinder: Disassembly

  1. REMOVE CLUTCH MASTER CYLINDER KIT 
    1. Using a pin punch and hammer, tap out the slotted spring pin.
    2. Remove the inlet union and grommet.
    3. Loosen the lock nut, and remove the push rod clevis.
    4. Remove the lock nut from the push rod.
    5. Remove the boot from the cylinder body.
      Fig 1: Tapping Slotted Spring Pin
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    6. While pushing the push rod, remove the snap ring with snap ring pliers.
    7. Remove the push rod from the cylinder body.
      NOTE: The piston may pop up out of the cylinder body. Therefore, slowly remove the push rod from the cylinder body.
    8. Remove the stop plate from the push rod.
    9. Remove the piston with spring from the cylinder body.
      NOTE: Be careful not to damage the inside of the cylinder body.
