TYPICAL MALFUNCTION THRESHOLDS

Park/Neutral position switch ON
R switch ON

L switch ON
B. All of the following conditions are met: -
Park/Neutral position switch OFF
R switch OFF
D switch OFF
2 switch OFF
L switch OFF
