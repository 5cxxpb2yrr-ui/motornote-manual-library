Steering Column Assembly: Disassembly

  1. REMOVE STEERING COLUMN UPPER WITH SWITCH BRACKET ASSEMBLY 
    1. Using a centering punch, mark the center of the 2 tapered-head bolts.
    2. Using a 3 to 4 mm (0.12 to 0.16 in.) drill, drill a hole in the 2 bolt heads.
    3. Using a screw extractor, remove the 2 bolts, steering column clamp and steering column upper with switch bracket.
      Fig 1: Removing Steering Column Clamp And Steering Column Upper Bolts
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  2. REMOVE STEERING MAIN SHAFT BUSH 

    Using a screwdriver and hammer, tap out the steering main shaft bush.

    Fig 2: Tapping Out Steering Main Shaft Bush
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  3. REMOVE STEERING MAIN SHAFT ASSEMBLY 
    1. Using a snap ring expander, remove the snap ring on the upper side.
      Fig 3: Removing Snap Ring On Upper Side
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. Using an extension bar and hammer, tap out the steering main shaft.
      Fig 4: Tapping Out Steering Main Shaft
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    3. Using a snap ring expander, remove the snap ring on the lower side.
      Fig 5: Removing Snap Ring On Lower Side
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  4. REMOVE IGNITION SWITCH LOCK CYLINDER ASSEMBLY 
    1. Place the ignition switch lock cylinder in the ACC position.
    2. Push down the stop pin with a screwdriver, and pull out the cylinder.
      Fig 6: Pushing Down Stop Pin With Screwdriver, And Pull Out Cylinder
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  5. REMOVE UNLOCK WARNING SWITCH ASSEMBLY 
    1. Disconnect the unlock warning switch connector from the ignition switch.
    2. Remove the unlock warning switch.
  6. REMOVE IGNITION SWITCH ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the 2 screws and ignition or starter switch from the steering column bracket.
      Fig 7: Removing Ignition Or Starter Switch Screws
