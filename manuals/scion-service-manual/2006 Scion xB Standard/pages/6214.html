Engine Oil Replacement Reminder Reset - Procedure 04

To reset MAINT reminder indicator after engine oil change:

  1. Using the MODE button, switch the odometer display to TRIP A while the engine is running.
  2. Turn ignition switch to OFF with odometer showing TRIP A.
  3. While pressing the MODE button, turn ignition switch to ON (DO NOT start engine).
  4. Hold MODE button down for at least 5 seconds. TRIP A display should indicate "000000".
