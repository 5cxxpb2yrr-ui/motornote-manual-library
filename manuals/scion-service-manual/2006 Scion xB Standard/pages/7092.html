Valve Body Assembly: Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. REMOVE ENGINE UNDER COVER LH 
  2. DRAIN AUTOMATIC TRANSAXLE FLUID 
    1. Remove the drain plug and gasket, and drain ATF.
    2. Install a new gasket and the drain plug.

      Torque: 49 N*m (500 kgf*cm, 36 ft.*lbf) 

  3. REMOVE AUTOMATIC TRANSAXLE OIL PAN SUBASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the 19 bolts, oil pan and gasket.
      NOTE: Some fluid will remain in the oil pan. Remove all the oil pan bolts, and carefully remove the oil pan sub-assembly.
      Fig 1: Removing Bolts, Oil Pan And Gasket
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. Remove the 2 magnets from the oil pan.
      Fig 2: Removing Magnets From Oil Pan
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    3. Examine particles in the pan.
      1. Use the removed magnets to collect any steel chips. Look carefully at the chips and particles in the pan and on the magnet to anticipate what type of wear you will find in the transaxle. Steel (magnetic): bearing, gear and plate wear Brass (non-magnetic): bushing wear
        Fig 3: Examine Particles In Pan
        Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  4. REMOVE VALVE BODY OIL STRAINER ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the 3 bolts and oil strainer.
      NOTE: Be careful as some fluid will come out with the oil strainer.
      Fig 4: Removing Bolts And Oil Strainer
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. Remove the O-ring from the oil strainer.
      Fig 5: Removing O-Ring From Oil Strainer
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  5. REMOVE TRANSMISSION VALVE BODY ASSEMBLY 
    1. Disconnect the 5 connectors.
    2. Remove the bolt, lock plate and ATF temperature sensor.
      Fig 6: Removing ATF Temperature Sensor
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    3. Remove the 2 bolts, cover and detent spring.
      Fig 7: Removing Bolts, Cover And Detent Spring
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    4. Remove the 13 bolts and valve body.
      NOTE: Be careful not to drop the check valve body, spring and accumulator piston.
      Fig 8: Removing Bolts And Valve Body
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    5. Remove the check ball body and spring.
      Fig 9: Removing Check Ball Body And Spring
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    6. Remove the 2 apply gaskets.
      Fig 10: Applying Gaskets
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    7. Remove the 5 bolts and 5 shift solenoid valves.
      Fig 11: Removing Bolts And Shift Solenoid Valves
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
