Floor Shift Assembly: Installation

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. INSTALL FLOOR SHIFT ASSEMBLY 
    1. Install the floor shift with the 4 bolts.
      Fig 1: Installing Floor Shift With Bolts
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

      Torque: 12 N*m (122 kgf*cm, 9 ft.*lbf) 

      HINT:

      Tighten them in the order A, B, C and D.

    2. Install the 2 wire harness clamps on the shift leverplate.
  2. CONNECT TRANSMISSION CONTROL CABLE ASSEMBLY 
    1. Adjust the shift lever to the N position, push the lever to the R position side and hold it in place.
    2. While pulling the cable, connect the transmission control cable with the nut.

      Torque: 12 N*m (122 kgf*cm, 9 ft.*lbf) 

    3. Connect the 2 connectors.
      Fig 2: Connecting Transmission Control Cable With Nut
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  3. CONNECT FLOOR SHIFT PARKING LOCK CABLE ASSEMBLY 
    1. Insert the floor shift parking lock cable end rod in the slit of the shift lever plate.
    2. Insert the casing cap of the cable into the shift lever plate through-hole.

      HINT:

      Check that it is inserted securely.

      Fig 3: Inserting Casing Cap Of Cable Into Shift Lever Plate Through-Hole
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    3. Insert the lever pin into the hole in the floor shift parking lock cable end.

      HINT:

      • At this time, ensure that the shift lever is in the N position.
      • After installation, make sure that the lever pin is pulled by the floor shift parking lock cable and is stopped with the stopper.
        Fig 4: Inserting Lever Pin Into Hole In Floor Shift Parking Lock Cable End
        Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    4. Hold the slide cap and move it back and forth. Check that the spring for automatic adjustment works properly.
      Fig 5: Holding Slide Cap And Move Back And Forth
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    5. Rotate the adjust lock cap counterclockwise by 90°so that it faces the front of the vehicle, and attach it.
      Fig 6: Adjusting Lock Cap
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

      HINT:

      • Install the floor shift parking lock cable on the shift lever side with the ignition switch in the ACC position.
      • Make sure the slide amount of the spring for automatic adjustment is 8 mm (0.31 in.).
  4. INSPECT AND ADJUST SHIFT LEVER POSITION 
    1. Check that the starter operates when the shift lever is in the P or N position and does not operate in other position.
    2. Operate the shift lever and check that the reading on the indicator matches the actual position.
    3. Check that the ignition key can be pulled out with the shift lever in the P position.
    4. Check that the ignition switch cannot be turned to the LOCK position with the shift lever in any position other than P.
    5. Check that the shift lever can be shifted from the P position to other positions when the shift lock release button is pressed.
  5. INSPECT SHIFT LEVER POSITION 
    1. Inspect the shift lever position (see ADJUSTMENT  ).
  6. ADJUST SHIFT LEVER POSITION 
    1. Adjust the shift lever position (see ADJUSTMENT  ).
  7. INSTALL REAR CONSOLE BOX ASSEMBLY 
    1. Attach the 2 clips to install the console box.
    2. Connect the connector.
    3. Install the screw.
    4. Install the console box carpet.
      Fig 7: Installing Console Box And Screw
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    5. Attach the 8 claws to install the rear console box cover.
      Fig 8: Installing Rear Console Box Cover
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
