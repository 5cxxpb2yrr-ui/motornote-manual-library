Roof Drip Side Finish Moulding: Removal

HINT:

  • Use the same procedures for the RH side and LH side.
  • The procedures listed below are for the LH side.
  1. REMOVE ROOF DRIP SIDE FINISH MOULDING LH 
    1. Apply protective tape around the edges of the moulding.
    2. Using a moulding remover, detach the clips both in the front and rear ends of the moulding, and then remove the moulding.
      NOTE:
      • Do not remove the clips.
      • If the clips are damaged or removed accidentally, replace them.
