Front Stabilizer Bar: Installation

  1. INSTALL FRONT STABILIZER BAR 
    1. Install the stabilizer bar, 2 bushes and 2 brackets with the 4 bolts.

      Torque: 37 N*m (377 kgf*cm, 27 ft.*lbf) 

      NOTE: Install the bushes with the slit facing the rear of the vehicle.

      HINT:

      Install the bushes on the outside of each paint line.

  2. CONNECT FRONT SUSPENSION CROSSMEMBER SUB-ASSEMBLY (See  FRONT SUSPENSION LOWER NO. 1 ARM (FOR MANUAL TRANSAXLE)   ) 
  3. CONNECT PRESSURE FEED TUBE ASSEMBLY (See  FRONT SUSPENSION LOWER NO. 1 ARM (FOR MANUAL TRANSAXLE)   ) 
  4. CONNECT POWER STEERING LINK ASSEMBLY (See  FRONT SUSPENSION LOWER NO. 1 ARM (FOR MANUAL TRANSAXLE)   ) 
  5. CONNECT FRONT STABILIZER BAR (See  FRONT SUSPENSION LOWER NO. 1 ARM (FOR MANUAL TRANSAXLE)   ) 
  6. CONNECT FRONT SUSPENSION NO. 1 LOWER ARM SUB-ASSEMBLY LH (See  FRONT SUSPENSION LOWER NO. 1 ARM (FOR MANUAL TRANSAXLE)   )

  7. CONNECT FRONT SUSPENSION NO. 1 LOWER ARM SUB-ASSEMBLY RH (See  FRONT SUSPENSION LOWER NO. 1 ARM (FOR MANUAL TRANSAXLE)   ) 
  8. INSTALL FRONT WHEEL 

    Torque: 103 N*m (1,050 kgf*cm, 76 ft.*lbf) 

  9. INSTALL HOOD SUB-ASSEMBLY 
  10. INSPECT HOOD SUB-ASSEMBLY 
    1. Inspect the hood (see ON-VEHICLE INSPECTION ).
  11. ADJUST HOOD SUB-ASSEMBLY 
    1. Adjust the hood (see ON-VEHICLE INSPECTION ).
  12. INSPECT AND ADJUST FRONT WHEEL ALIGNMENT 
    1. Inspect and adjust the front wheel alignment (see FRONT WHEEL ALIGNMENT  ).
