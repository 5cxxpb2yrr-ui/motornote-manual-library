Differential Case: Inspection

  1. INSPECT FRONT DIFFERENTIAL PINION THRUST WASHER 
    1. Using a micrometer, measure the thickness.
      Fig 1: Measuring Thickness
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

      Minimum thickness: 0.94 mm (0.0370 in.) 

      If the thickness is less than the minimum, replace the front differential pinion thrust washer.

  2. REMOVE FRONT NO. 1 DIFFERENTIAL PINION SHAFT 
    1. Using a micrometer, measure the diameter of the front No. 1 differential pinion shaft.
      Fig 2: Measuring Diameter Of Front Differential Pinion Shaft
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

      Minimum diameter: 16.982 mm (0.6685 in.) 

      If the diameter is less than the minimum, replace the front No. 1 differential pinion shaft.
