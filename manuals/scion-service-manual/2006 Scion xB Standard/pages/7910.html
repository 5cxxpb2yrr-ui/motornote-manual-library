Charging (1NZ-FE): Generator: Removal

  1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL 
    CAUTION: Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to prevent airbag and seat belt pretensioner activation.
  2. REMOVE GENERATOR ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the terminal cap.
    2. Remove the nut and generator wire.
    3. Disconnect the generator connector.
    4. Remove the wire harness clamp.
      Fig 1: Removing Nut And Terminal Cap

    5. Loosen bolts A and B.
    6. Release the generator V-belt tension, and remove the generator V-belt.
      Fig 2: Locating Bolts A And B
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    7. Remove bolt A, the nut and the adjusting bar.
      Fig 3: Removing Bolt A And Nut
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    8. Remove bolt B and generator.
