Rear Seat Assembly: Disassembly

CAUTION: Wear protective gloves. Sharp areas on the seatback frame may injure your hands.
  1. REMOVE REAR SEAT HEADREST ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the 3 headrests.
  2. REMOVE REAR SEATBACK HINGE SUB-ASSEMBLY LH 
    1. Remove the bolt and seatback hinge.
  3. REMOVE REAR SEATBACK HINGE SUB-ASSEMBLY RH 
    1. Remove the bolt and seatback hinge.
  4. REMOVE REAR SEATBACK LOCK RELEASE BUTTON 
    1. Remove the 2 buttons.
  5. REMOVE REAR SEAT SHOULDER BELT COVER (for RH Side) 
    1. Using a screwdriver, pry out the shoulder belt cover.

      HINT:

      Tape the screwdriver tip before use.

      Fig 1: Locating Shoulder Belt Cover
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  6. REMOVE TETHER ANCHOR COVER 
    1. Using a screwdriver, pry out the 3 tether anchor covers.

      HINT:

      Tape the screwdriver tip before use.

      Fig 2: Prying Out Tether Anchor Covers Using Screwdriver
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  7. REMOVE REAR SEAT HEADREST SUPPORT 
    1. Using a clip remover, remove the 17 clips.
      NOTE: If clips are damaged during removal, replace them.
    2. Remove the hog rings.
      Fig 3: Locating Clips And Hog Rings
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    3. Detach the claws and remove the 6 headrest supports.

      HINT:

      In order to detach the claw of the headrest support, insert your hand between the seatback frame and the seatback cover (with pad).

      Fig 4: Detaching Claws And Removing Headrest Supports
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  8. REMOVE REAR SEATBACK STOP BUTTON GROMMET 
    1. Detach the claws and remove the 2 grommets.

      HINT:

      In order to detach the claw of the headrest support, insert your hand between the seatback frame and the seatback cover (with pad).

      Fig 5: Removing Grommets
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  9. REMOVE REAR SEATBACK COVER WITH PAD LH 
    1. Remove the seatback cover (with pad) from the seatback frame.
  10. REMOVE REAR SEATBACK COVER WITH PAD RH 
    1. Remove the seatback cover (with pad) from the seatback frame.
  11. REMOVE REAR SEATBACK LOCK ASSEMBLY LH 
    1. Remove the 2 bolts and lock.
  12. REMOVE REAR SEATBACK LOCK ASSEMBLY RH 
    1. Remove the 2 bolts and lock.
  13. REMOVE REAR SEAT BELT ASSEMBLY OUTER CENTER 
    1. Remove the nut and belt.
      Fig 6: Removing Nuts
