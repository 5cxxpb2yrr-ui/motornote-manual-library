Front Airbag Sensor: Removal

CAUTION: Be sure to read the precautionary notices concerning the SRS airbag system before servicing it (see AIRBAG SYSTEM  ).
  1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL 
    CAUTION: Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to prevent airbag and seat belt pretensioner activation.
  2. REMOVE NO. 1 AIR CLEANER INLET 
    1. Remove the bolt and air cleaner inlet.
      Fig 1: Removing Bolt And Air Cleaner Inlet
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  3. REMOVE FRONT AIRBAG SENSOR LH 
    1. Disconnect the sensor connector.
    2. Remove the 2 bolts and sensor.
      Fig 2: Removing Bolts And Sensor
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  4. REMOVE FRONT AIRBAG SENSOR RH 
    1. Disconnect the sensor connector.
    2. Remove the 2 bolts and sensor.
