Engine Oil Replacement Reminder Reset - Procedure 01

To reset MAINT reminder indicator after engine oil change:

  1. Turn ignition switch to OFF with the odometer display showing.
  2. While pressing the trip meter RESET button, turn ignition switch to ON.
  3. Hold trip meter RESET button down for at least 5 seconds. The MAINT reminder light goes off, and the odometer display shows zero for 1 second and then changes back to the odometer reading.
  4. If system fails to reset, reminder light will remain on keep flashing.
