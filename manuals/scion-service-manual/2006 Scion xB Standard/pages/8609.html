Cylinder Head, Intake Manifold, Exhaust Manifold: Installation

  1. INSTALL CYLINDER HEAD GASKET 
    1. Place a new cylinder head gasket on the cylinder block with the Lot No. stamp facing upward.
      Fig 1: Placing New Cylinder Head Gasket On Cylinder Block With Lot No Stamp Facing Upward
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
      NOTE:
      • Remove any oil from the contact surface.
      • Be careful of the mounting orientation.
      • Place the cylinder head on the gasket gently so as not to damage the gasket at the bottom part of the head.
  2. INSTALL CYLINDER HEAD 

    HINT:

    The cylinder head bolts are tightened in 2 successive steps.

    1. Apply seal packing as shown in the illustration.

      Seal packing: Part No. 08826-00100 

      Standard seal diameter: 4.5 to 5.5 mm (0.177 to 0.217 in.) 

      NOTE:
      • Remove any oil from the contact surface.
      • Install the cylinder head within 3 minutes of applying seal packing.
    2. Apply a light coat of engine oil to the threads of the cylinder head bolts.
      Fig 2: Identifying Seal Packing Application Area
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    3. Using several steps, install and tighten the 10 cylinder head bolts and plate washers uniformly with an 8 mm bi-hexagon wrench in the sequence shown in the illustration.
      Fig 3: Identifying Cylinder Head Bolts Tightening Sequence
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

      Torque: 29 N*m (300 kgf*cm, 22 ft.*lbf) 

    4. Mark the front of the cylinder head bolt with paint.
    5. Retighten the cylinder head bolts by an additional 90° and then another additional 90°, as shown in the illustration.
      Fig 4: Retightening Cylinder Head Bolts
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    6. Check that the paint mark is now 180° opposite to the front.
  3. INSTALL CAMSHAFT TIMING GEAR 
    1. Put the camshaft timing gear assembly and the camshaft together with the straight pin off the groove, as shown in the illustration.
    2. Turn the camshaft timing gear assembly clockwise while pushing it lightly towards the camshaft. Push further at the position where the pin fits into the groove.
      Fig 5: Installing Camshaft Timing Gear
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
      NOTE: Be careful not to turn the camshaft timing gear to the retard angle (to the right).
    3. Check that there is no clearance between the gear fringe and camshaft.
    4. Tighten the flange bolt with the camshaft timing gear fixed.

      Torque: 64 N*m (653 kgf*cm, 47 ft.*lbf) 

    5. Check that the camshaft timing gear assembly can move to the retard angle (to the right) and is locked in the most retarded position.
  4. INSTALL CAMSHAFT TIMING SPROCKET 
    1. Clamp the camshaft in a vise.
    2. Align the knock pin hole of the camshaft timing sprocket with the knock pin of the camshaft, and install the camshaft timing sprocket.
      Fig 6: Installing Camshaft Timing Sprocket
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

      Torque: 64 N*m (653 kgf*cm, 47 ft.*lbf) 

      NOTE: Be careful not to damage the camshaft.
  5. INSTALL CAMSHAFT 
    1. Apply a light coat of engine oil to the camshaft journals.
    2. Place the camshaft on the cylinder head with the timing mark on the camshaft timing gear facing upward.
    3. Examine the front marks and numbers, and tighten the bolts in the sequence shown in the illustration.
      Fig 7: Identifying Bolts Tightening Sequence
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

      Torque: 13 N*m (129 kgf*cm, 9 ft.*lbf) 

      NOTE: Tighten each bolt uniformly, keeping the camshaft level.
    4. Install the No. 1 bearing cap.

      Torque: 23 N*m (235 kgf*cm, 17 ft.*lbf)

  32. INSTALL INTAKE MANIFOLD 
    1. Install a new gasket onto the intake manifold.
    2. Install the intake manifold with the 3 bolts and 2 nuts. Using several steps, uniformly tighten the bolts and nuts in the sequence shown in the illustration.
      Fig 8: Identifying Intake Manifold Bolts Tightening Sequence

  36. INSTALL FRONT EXHAUST PIPE

      Torque: 7.5 N*m (76 kgf*cm, 66 in.*lbf) 

    2. Connect the radiator reservoir tank hose.
    3. Connect the No. 3 radiator hose.
    4. Connect the radiator inlet hose.
