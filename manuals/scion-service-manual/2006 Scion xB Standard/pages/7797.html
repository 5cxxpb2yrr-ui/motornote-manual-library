Air Conditioning And Blower Unit: Disassembly

  1. REMOVE AIR FILTER CASE 
    1. Detach the 2 claws and remove the air filter case.
      Fig 1: Detaching 2 Claws And Removing Air Filter Case
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  2. REMOVE DAMPER SERVO SUB-ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the 3 screws and damper servo.
      Fig 2: Removing 3 Screws And Damper Servo
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  3. REMOVE BLOWER RESISTOR 
    1. Disconnect the connector.
    2. Remove the 2 screws and blower resistor.
      Fig 3: Removing 2 Screws And Blower Resistor
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  4. REMOVE BLOWER MOTOR COVER 
    1. Disconnect the rod in the order as shown in the illustration.
      Fig 4: Disconnecting Rod In Order As Shown
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. Remove the 4 screws and blower motor cover.
      Fig 5: Removing 4 Screws And Blower Motor Cover
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  5. REMOVE BLOWER WITH FAN MOTOR SUBASSEMBLY 
    1. Disconnect the connector.
    2. Remove the 3 screws and blower motor.
      Fig 6: Removing 3 Screws And Blower Motor
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  6. REMOVE COOLER THERMISTOR 
    1. Detach the clamp and remove the cooler thermistor.
      NOTE: Do not damage the packing when removing the thermistor.
      Fig 7: Detaching Clamp And Removing Cooler Thermistor
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  7. REMOVE AIR CONDITIONING TUBE ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the packing.
      Fig 8: Removing Packing
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. Using a 5 mm hexagon wrench, remove the bolt and A/C tube.
    3. Remove the 2 O-rings from the A/C tube.
      Fig 9: Removing 2 O-Rings From A/C Tube
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  8. REMOVE COOLER EXPANSION VALVE 
    1. Using a 4 mm hexagon wrench, remove the 2 bolts and cooler expansion valve.
      Fig 10: Removing 2 Bolts And Cooler Expansion Valve
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  9. REMOVE COOLER EVAPORATOR SUB-ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the screw.
    2. Detach the 2 claws and remove the air duct.
      Fig 11: Detaching 2 Claws And Removing Air Duct
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    3. Remove the 3 screws and evaporator cover.
    4. Remove the cooler evaporator.
      Fig 12: Removing Cooler Evaporator
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    5. Remove the 2 O-rings from the cooler evaporator.
      Fig 13: Removing 2 O-Rings From Cooler Evaporator
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
