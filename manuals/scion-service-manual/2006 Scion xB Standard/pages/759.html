Room Light Assembly: Inspection

  1. INSPECT NO. 1 ROOM LIGHT ASSEMBLY 
    1. Check that the light operates properly.

      OK 

      TESTER CONNECTION AND SPECIFIED CONDITION

      Tester Connection Switch Condition Specified Condition
      Battery positive (+) --> Terminal 2
      Battery negative (-) --> Terminal 1      		 ON Light comes on
      Battery positive (+) --> Terminal 2
      Battery negative (-) --> Terminal 1      		 DOOR Light comes on
      Battery positive (+) --. Terminal 2
      Battery negative (-) --> Terminal 1      		 OFF Light does not come on
      Fig 1: Identifying Room Light Assembly Connectors

      If the result is not as specified, replace the light assembly.
