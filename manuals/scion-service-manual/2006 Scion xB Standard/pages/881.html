Rear Bumper: Disassembly

  1. REMOVE REAR BUMPER PROTECTOR LH 
    1. Using a clip remover, remove the 3 clips and bumper protector.
  2. REMOVE REAR BUMPER PROTECTOR RH 
    1. Using a clip remover, remove the 3 clips and bumper protector.
  3. REMOVE REAR BUMPER PIECE LH 
    1. Using a clip remover, remove the 5 clips.
    2. Using a screwdriver, detach the 8 claws and remove the bumper piece LH (with bumper piece RH).

      HINT:

      Tape the screwdriver tip before use.

  4. REMOVE REAR BUMPER PIECE RH 
    1. Using a clip remover, remove the clip.
    2. Using a screwdriver, detach the claws and remove the bumper piece LH from the bumper piece RH.

      HINT:

      Tape the screwdriver tip before use.

  5. REMOVE REAR BUMPER EXTENSION RH 
    1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 2 claws and remove the extension.

      HINT:

      Tape the screwdriver tip before use.

  6. REMOVE REAR SIDE MARKER LIGHT ASSEMBLY LH 
    1. Remove the nut and marker light.
  7. REMOVE REAR SIDE MARKER LIGHT ASSEMBLY RH 
    1. Remove the nut and marker light.
