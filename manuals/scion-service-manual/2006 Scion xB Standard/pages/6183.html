On-Vehicle Inspection

  1. REMOVE HEATER BLOWER MOTOR RELAY (Marking: HEATER) 
    1. Remove the relay from the instrument panel junction block.
  2. INSPECT HEATER BLOWER MOTOR RELAY 
    1. Measure the resistance of the relay.

      Standard resistance 

      TESTER CONNECTION AND SPECIFIED CONDITION CHART

      Tester Connection Specified Condition
      3 - 4 Below 1 Ω
      3 - 5 10 kΩ or higher
      3 - 5 Below 1 Ω
      (when battery voltage is applied to terminals 1 and 2)

      If the result is not as specified, replace the relay.

  3. INSTALL HEATER BLOWER MOTOR RELAY
