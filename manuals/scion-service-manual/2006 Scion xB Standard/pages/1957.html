Rear Console Box: Removal

  1. REMOVE SHIFT LEVER KNOB SUB-ASSEMBLY (for Manual Transaxle) 
  2. REMOVE REAR CONSOLE BOX ASSEMBLY 
    1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 8 claws and remove the rear console box cover.

      HINT:

      Tape the screwdriver tip before use.

      Fig 1: Detaching Claws
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. Remove the console box carpet.
    3. Remove the screw.
    4. Disconnect the connector.
    5. Using a screwdriver, detach the 2 clips and remove the console box.

      HINT:

      Tape the screwdriver tip before use.
