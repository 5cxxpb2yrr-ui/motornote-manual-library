Brake Master Cylinder: Installation

  1. INSTALL BRAKE MASTER CYLINDER RESERVOIR SUB-ASSEMBLY 
    1. Apply lithium soap base glycol grease to 2 new grommets and install them to the brake master cylinder.
    2. Install the cap and strainer to the brake master cylinder reservoir.
      Fig 1: Applying Lithium Soap Base Glycol Grease To Grommets
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    3. Install the brake master cylinder reservoir with the reservoir stopper.
      NOTE: Install the stopper securely to the groove of the reservoir and master cylinder.
      Fig 2: Installing Brake Master Cylinder Reservoir With Reservoir Stopper
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  2. INSTALL BRAKE MASTER CYLINDER SUBASSEMBLY 
    1. Install the brake master cylinder to the booster with the 2 nuts.

      Torque: 13 N*m (127 kgf*cm, 9 ft.*lbf) 

      Fig 3: Installing Brake Master Cylinder To Booster With Nuts
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. Using SST, connect the 2 brake lines to the brake master cylinder.

      SST 09023-00101 

      Torque: 15 N*m (155 kgf*cm, 11 ft.*lbf) without SST 

      14 N*m (143 kgf*cm, 10 ft.*lbf) with SST 

      HINT:

      Use a torque wrench with a fulcrum length of 30 cm (11.81 in.).

    3. Connect the level warning light switch connector.
      Fig 4: Connecting Brake Lines To Brake Master Cylinder
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  3. CONNECT CLUTCH RESERVOIR TUBE (for Manual Transaxle) 
    1. Slide the clip and connect the clutch reservoir tube.
  4. INSTALL AIR CLEANER CAP SUB-ASSEMBLY 
  5. FILL RESERVOIR WITH BRAKE FLUID (See  BRAKE FLUID   ) 
  6. BLEED AIR FROM BRAKE MASTER CYLINDER (See  BRAKE FLUID   ) 
  7. BLEED AIR FROM BRAKE LINE (See  BLEEDING   ) 
  8. BLEED AIR FROM CLUTCH LINE (for Manual Transaxle) (See  INSTALLATION  ) 
  9. CHECK BRAKE FLUID LEVEL IN RESERVOIR (See  BLEEDING   ) 
  10. CHECK FOR BRAKE FLUID LEAKAGE
