DTC B0136/74 Open in Front Pretensioner Squib LH Circuit: Description

The front pretensioner squib LH circuit consists of the center airbag sensor assembly and the front seat outer belt assembly LH (seat belt pretensioner LH).

This circuit instructs the SRS to deploy when deployment conditions are met.

DTC B0136/74 is recorded when an open circuit is detected in the front pretensioner squib LH circuit.
