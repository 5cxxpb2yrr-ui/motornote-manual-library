Clutch Master Cylinder: Installation

  1. INSTALL CLUTCH MASTER CYLINDER ASSEMBLY 
    1. Install the clutch master cylinder with the 2 nuts.

      Torque: 9.0 N*m (80 kgf*cm, 81 in.*lbf) 

  2. CONNECT CLUTCH MASTER CYLINDER TO FLEXIBLE HOSE TUBE 
    1. Using SST, connect the flexible hose tube.

      SST 09023-00101 

      Torque: 15 N*m (155 kgf*cm, 11 ft.*lbf) 

      Fig 1: Installing Clutch Master Cylinder With Nuts
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  3. CONNECT CLUTCH RESERVOIR TUBE 
    1. Connect the clutch reservoir tube to the clutch master cylinder with the clip.
      NOTE:
      • Connect the clutch reservoir tube so that it will not be twisted.
      • Face the white mark upward.
        Fig 2: Connecting Clutch Reservoir Tube To Clutch Master Cylinder With Clip
        Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  4. CONNECT CLUTCH MASTER CYLINDER PUSH ROD CLEVIS 
    1. Apply MP grease to the contact surface of the pin and clevis bush.
    2. Connect the clevis to the clutch pedal with the pin and clip.

      HINT:

      Install the pin from the left side of the vehicle.

    3. Install the clip to the pin.
      Fig 3: Applying MP Grease To Contact Surface Of Pin And Clevis Bush
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  5. INSTALL CLUTCH PEDAL SPRING 
  6. INSTALL BRAKE BOOSTER ASSEMBLY 
  7. INSTALL BRAKE ACTUATOR ASSEMBLY 
  8. INSTALL BRAKE MASTER CYLINDER SUBASSEMBLY 
  9. BLEED CLUTCH PIPE LINE 
    1. Fill the brake reservoir with brake fluid and bleed the clutch system.

      Torque: 8.4 N*m (86 kgf*cm, 74 in.*lbf) 

  10. CHECK BRAKE FLUID 

    HINT:

    • Check for leakage in the brake system.
    • Check for leakage in the clutch system.
  11. CHECK FLUID LEVEL IN RESERVOIR 
  12. INSPECT AND ADJUST CLUTCH PEDAL SUBASSEMBLY
