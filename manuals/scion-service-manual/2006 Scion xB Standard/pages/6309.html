DTC B1156/15 Front Airbag Sensor RH Malfunction; DTC B1157/15 Front Airbag Sensor RH Malfunction: Description

The front airbag sensor RH circuit consists of the center airbag sensor assembly and the front airbag sensor RH. DTC B1156/15 or B1157/15 is recorded when a malfunction is detected in the front airbag sensor RH circuit.

DTC DETECTION CONDITION AND TROUBLE AREA

DTC No. DTC Detection Condition Trouble Area
B1156/15
B1157/15
  • Short circuit in front airbag sensor RH wire harness (to B+)
  • Short circuit in front airbag sensor RH wire harness (to ground)
  • Short circuit between +SR wire harness and -SR wire harness of front airbag sensor RH
  • Open circuit in +SR wire harness or -SR wire harness of front airbag sensor RH
  • Front airbag sensor RH malfunction
  • Center airbag sensor assembly malfunction
  • Front airbag sensor assembly
  • Center airbag sensor assembly
  • Instrument panel wire
  • Engine room main wire
