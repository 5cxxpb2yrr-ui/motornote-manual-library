Inspection Procedure

  1. CHECK FOR DTC 
    1. Turn the ignition switch ON.
    2. Check that the ABS warning light illuminates.
    3. Check for DTCs (see DTC CHECK / CLEAR  ).

      Result 

      RESULT REFERENCE

      Result Proceed to
      DTC is not output (when using intelligent tester) A
      DTC is not output (when not using intelligent tester) B
      DTC is output C

    B: Go to step   7 

    C: REPAIR CIRCUIT INDICATED BY OUTPUT DTC 

    A: Go to next step 

  2. INSPECT SKID CONTROL ECU CONNECTOR 
    1. Check that each ECU connector is properly installed.

      OK: Each ECU connector is properly installed. 

    NG: CONNECT CONNECTOR TO ECU CORRECTLY 

    OK: Go to Next Step 

  3. READ VALUE OF INTELLIGENT TESTER (IG1 VOLTAGE) 
    1. Check the DATA LIST for proper functioning of the IG voltage.

      Skid control ECU with actuator 

      MEASUREMENT ITEM DIAGNOSTICS CHART

      Item Measurement Item / Range (Display) Normal Condition Diagnostic Note
      IG VOLTAGE ECU power supply voltage / NORMAL / TOO LOW NORMAL: 9.5 to 14 V
      TOO LOW: Below 9.5 V      		 -

      Result 

      RESULT REFERENCE

      Result Proceed to
      Display is NORMAL A
      Display is not NORMAL B

    B: Go to step   11 

    A: Go to next step 

  4. CHECK WIRE HARNESS (SKID CONTROL ECU - BODY GROUND) 
    1. Disconnect the S3 ECU connector.
    2. Measure the resistance of the wire harness side connector.

      Standard resistance 

      TESTER CONNECTION SPECIFIED CONDITION

      Tester Connection Specified Condition
      S3-32 (GND1) - Body ground Below 1 Ω
      S3-1 (GND2) - Body ground
      Fig 1: Measuring Resistance Of Wire Harness Side Connector
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    NG: REPAIR OR REPLACE HARNESS AND CONNECTOR 

    OK: Go to Next Step 

  5. CHECK ABS WARNING LIGHT 
    1. Disconnect the S3 ECU connector.
    2. Turn the ignition switch ON.
    3. Using a service wire, connect terminal S3-29 (WA) to S3-32 (GND1) or S3-1 (GND2) of the connector. Check the ABS warning light.

      OK: ABS warning light turns off. 

    4. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
    5. Remove the service wire.
    6. Turn the ignition switch ON.
      Fig 2: Checking ABS Warning Light
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    7. Check the ABS warning light.

      OK: ABS warning light illuminates. 

      HINT:

      When replacing the skid control ECU with actuator, perform the zero point calibration (see CALIBRATION  ).

    OK: REPLACE BRAKE ACTUATOR ASSEMBLY 

    NG: Go to Next Step 

  6. CHECK WIRE HARNESS (COMBINATION METER - SKID CONTROL ECU AND BODY GROUND) 
    1. Disconnect the S3 ECU connector.
    2. Disconnect the C6 meter connector.
    3. Measure the resistance of the wire harness side connectors.

      Standard resistance 

      TESTER CONNECTION SPECIFIED CONDITION

      Tester Connection Specified Condition
      S3-29 (WA) - C6-11 Below 1 Ω
      S3-29 (WA) - Body ground 10 kΩ or higher

    NG: REPAIR OR REPLACE HARNESS AND CONNECTOR 

    Fig 3: Measuring Resistance Of Wire Harness Side Connectors
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    OK: REPLACE COMBINATION METER ASSEMBLY 

  7. INSPECT SKID CONTROL ECU CONNECTOR 
    1. Check that each ECU connector is properly installed.

      OK: Each ECU connector is properly installed. 

    NG: CONNECT CONNECTOR TO ECU CORRECTLY 

    OK: Go to Next Step 

  8. CHECK WIRE HARNESS (SKID CONTROL ECU - BATTERY AND BODY GROUND) 
    1. Disconnect the S3 ECU connector.
    2. Measure the voltage of the wire harness side connector.

      Standard voltage 

      TESTER CONNECTION SPECIFIED CONDITION

      Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
      S3-46 (IG1) - Body ground Ignition switch ON 10 to 14 V
    3. Measure the resistance of the wire harness side connector.

      Standard resistance 

      TESTER CONNECTION SPECIFIED CONDITION

      Tester Connection Specified Condition
      S3-32 (GND1) - Body ground Below 1 Ω
      S3-1 (GND2) - Body ground
      Fig 4: Measuring Voltage Of Wire Harness Side Connector
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    NG: REPAIR OR REPLACE HARNESS AND CONNECTOR 

    OK: Go to Next Step 

  9. CHECK ABS WARNING LIGHT 
    1. Disconnect the S3 ECU connector.
    2. Turn the ignition switch ON.
    3. Using a service wire, connect terminal S3-29 (WA) to S3-32 (GND1) or S3-1 (GND2) of the connector. Check the ABS warning light.

      OK: ABS warning light turns off. 

    4. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
    5. Remove the service wire.
    6. Turn the ignition switch ON.
    7. Check the ABS warning light.
      Fig 5: Checking ABS Warning Light
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

      OK: ABS warning light illuminates. 

      HINT:

      When replacing the skid control ECU with actuator, perform the zero point calibration (see CALIBRATION  ).

    OK: REPLACE BRAKE ACTUATOR ASSEMBLY 

    NG: Go to Next Step 

  10. CHECK WIRE HARNESS (COMBINATION METER - SKID CONTROL ECU AND BODY GROUND) 
    1. Disconnect the S3 ECU connector.
    2. Disconnect the C6 meter connector.
    3. Measure the resistance of the wire harness side connectors.

      Standard resistance 

      TESTER CONNECTION SPECIFIED CONDITION

      Tester Connection Specified Condition

      S3-29 (WA) - Body ground 10 kΩ or higher

    NG: REPAIR OR REPLACE HARNESS AND CONNECTOR

    OK: REPLACE COMBINATION METER ASSEMBLY 

  11. CHECK WIRE HARNESS (SKID CONTROL ECU - BATTERY) 
    1. Disconnect the S3 ECU connector.
    2. Measure the voltage of the wire harness side connector.

      Standard voltage 

      TESTER CONNECTION SPECIFIED CONDITION

      Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
      S3-46 (IG1) - Body ground Ignition switch ON 10 to 14 V

      HINT:

      When replacing the skid control ECU with actuator, perform the zero point calibration (see CALIBRATION  ).

    NG: REPAIR OR REPLACE HARNESS AND CONNECTOR 

    OK: REPLACE BRAKE ACTUATOR ASSEMBLY
