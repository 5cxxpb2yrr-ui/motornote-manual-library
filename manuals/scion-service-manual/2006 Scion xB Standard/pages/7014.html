Inspection Procedure

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. INSPECT STOP LIGHT SWITCH 
    1. Remove the S8 stop light switch.
    2. Measure the resistance of the switch.

      Standard resistance 

      TESTER CONNECTION SPECIFIED CONDITION

      Tester Connection Switch Condition Specified Condition
      1 - 2 Pin pushed (pedal released) 10 kΩ or higher
      1 - 2 Pin not pushed (pedal depressed) Below 1 Ω
      3 - 4 Pin pushed (pedal released) 10 kΩ or higher
      3 - 4 Pin not pushed (pedal depressed) Below 1 Ω

    NG: REPLACE STOP LIGHT SWITCH 

    Fig 1: Inspecting Stop Light Switch
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    OK: Go to Next Step 

  2. CHECK WIRE HARNESS (STOP LIGHT SWITCH - ECM) 
    1. Measure the voltage of the wire harness side connector.

      Standard voltage 

      TESTER CONNECTION SPECIFIED CONDITION

      Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
      E4-4 (STP) - Body ground Brake pedal is depressed 7.5 to 14 V
      E4-4 (STP) - Body ground Brake pedal is released Below 1.5 V
      Fig 2: Checking Wire Harness (Stop Light Switch - ECM)
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    NG: REPAIR OR REPLACE HARNESS AND CONNECTOR 

    OK: REPLACE ECM
