Vane Pump: Disassembly

  1. FIX VANE PUMP ASSEMBLY 
    1. Using SST, secure the vane pump in a vise.

      SST 09630-00014 (09631-00132) 

  2. REMOVE VANE PUMP OIL RESERVOIR CAP SUBASSEMBLY 
  3. REMOVE VANE PUMP OIL RESERVOIR ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the 3 bolts and oil reservoir.
    2. Remove the O-ring from the oil reservoir.
      Fig 1: Securing Vane Pump In Vise
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  4. REMOVE FRONT PUMP BRACKET 
    1. Remove the 2 bolts, front bracket and rear bracket.
  5. REMOVE FLOW CONTROL VALVE 
    1. Remove the pressure port union.
    2. Remove the O-ring from the pressure port union.
    3. Remove the flow control valve and compression spring.
  6. REMOVE POWER STEERING OIL PRESSURE SENSOR 
    1. Remove the oil pressure sensor from the front housing.
      Fig 2: Removing Flow Control Valve And Compression Spring
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  7. REMOVE REAR VANE PUMP HOUSING 
    1. Remove the 4 bolts and rear housing from the front housing.
    2. Remove the O-ring from the rear housing.
      Fig 3: Removing Rear Vane Pump Housing Bolts
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  8. REMOVE SHAFT SUB-ASSEMBLY WITH PULLEY 
    1. Using 2 screwdrivers, remove the snap ring from the shaft.
    2. Remove the shaft.
  9. REMOVE VANE PUMP ROTOR 
    1. Remove the 10 vane pump plates.
    2. Remove the vane pump rotor.
  10. REMOVE VANE PUMP CAM RING 
    Fig 4: Removing Snap Ring From Shaft
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  11. REMOVE FRONT VANE PUMP SIDE PLATE 
    1. Remove the side plate from the front housing.
    2. Remove the O-ring from the side plate.
      Fig 5: Removing O-Ring From Side Plate
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    3. Remove the O-ring from the front housing.
      Fig 6: Removing O-Ring From Front Housing
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  12. REMOVE VANE PUMP HOUSING OIL SEAL 
    1. Using a screwdriver and cloth, remove the housing oil seal.
      NOTE: Be careful not to damage the housing front.
      Fig 7: Removing Housing Oil Seal
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
