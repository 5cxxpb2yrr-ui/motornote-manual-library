Introduction

The purpose of this TSB is to provide updated throttle body part number information for certain vehicles equipped with the 2AZ-FE engine. The drain pipe has been eliminated on throttle body service parts. Please use the information below when replacing the throttle body assembly.

Repair Procedure

When replacing the throttle body assembly, remove and discard the throttle body hose that no longer has a port on the throttle body assembly. The port connected to the other end of the throttle body hose can be left open to the atmosphere - it does NOT need to be sealed.
