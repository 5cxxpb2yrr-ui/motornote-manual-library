Differential Case: Disassembly

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. REMOVE FRONT DIFFERENTIAL CASE FRONT TAPERED ROLLER BEARING 
    1. Using SST, remove the front differential case front tapered roller bearing from the differential case.

      SST 09950-40011 (09951-04010, 09952-04010, 09953-04030, 09954-04010, 09955-04061, 09957-04010, 09958-04011), 09950-60010 (09951-00390) 

      Fig 1: Removing Differential Case Front Tapered Roller Bearing From Differential Case
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. Using SST, remove the front differential case front tapered roller bearing outer race from the transaxle housing.

      SST 09387-00041 (09387-02010, 09387-02020) 

      Fig 2: Removing Front Differential Case Front Tapered Roller Bearing Outer Race From Transaxle Housing
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  2. REMOVE FRONT DIFFERENTIAL CASE REAR TAPERED ROLLER BEARING 
    1. Using SST, remove the front differential case rear tapered roller bearing from the differential case.

      SST 09950-40011 (09951-04010, 09952-04010, 09953-04020, 09954-04010, 09955-04061, 09957-04010, 09958-04011), 09950-60010 (09951-00430) 

      Fig 3: Removing Front Differential Case Rear Tapered Roller Bearing From Differential Case
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. Using SST, remove the outer race and shim.

      SST 09612-65014 (09612-01010) 

      Fig 4: Removing Outer Race And Shim
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  3. REMOVE SPEEDOMETER DRIVE GEAR 
    1. Remove the speedometer drive gear from the differential case.
      Fig 5: Removing Speedometer Drive Gear From Differential Case
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  4. REMOVE FRONT DIFFERENTIAL RING GEAR 
    1. Place matchmarks on the front differential ring gear and differential case.
      Fig 6: Identifying Matchmarks On Front Differential Ring Gear And Differential Case
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. Using SST and a hammer, unstake the lock plate.

      SST 09930-00010 

      Fig 7: Removing Lock Plate
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    3. Remove the 8 bolts and lock plate.
      Fig 8: Removing Bolts
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    4. Using a plastic-faced hammer, tap out the front differential ring gear from the differential case.
      Fig 9: Taping Out Front Differential Ring Gear From Differential Case
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  5. REMOVE FRONT DIFFERENTIAL PINION SHAFT STRAIGHT PIN 
    1. Using a pin punch and hammer, unstake the differential case.
      Fig 10: Removing Front Differential Pinion Shaft Straight Pin
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. Using a pin punch and hammer, tap out the straight pin.
      Fig 11: Taping Out Straight Pin
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  6. REMOVE FRONT DIFFERENTIAL NO. 1 PINION SHAFT 
    1. Remove the pinion shaft from the front differential case.
      Fig 12: Removing Pinion Shaft From Front Differential Case
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  7. REMOVE FRONT DIFFERENTIAL SIDE GEAR 
    1. Remove the 2 pinions, 2 thrust washers, 2 side gears and 2 thrust washers from the front differential case.
      Fig 13: Removing Front Differential Side Gear
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  8. REMOVE DIFFERENTIAL DRIVE PINION PLUG 
    1. Using a brass bar and plastic-faced hammer, tap out the differential drive pinion plug.
      Fig 14: Taping Out Differential Drive Pinion Plug
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  9. REMOVE COUNTER DRIVEN GEAR 
    1. Using SST and a press, press out the counter driven gear from the differential drive pinion.

      SST 09950-60010 (09951-00350), 09950-70010 (09951-07150) 

      Fig 15: Pressing Out Counter Driven Gear From Differential Drive Pinion
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
