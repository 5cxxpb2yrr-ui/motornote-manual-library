DTC B0135/73 Short in Front Pretensioner Squib LH Circuit: Description

The front pretensioner squib circuit consists of the center airbag sensor assembly and the front seat outer belt assembly LH (seat belt pretensioner LH).

This circuit instructs the SRS to deploy when deployment conditions are met.

DTC 0135/73 is recorded when a short circuit is detected in the front pretensioner squib LH circuit.

DTC DETECTION CONDITION AND TROUBLE AREA

DTC No. DTC Detection Condition Trouble Area
B0135/73
  • Short circuit between PL+ wire harness and PL- wire harness of front pretensioner squib LH
  • Front pretensioner squib LH malfunction
  • Center airbag sensor assembly malfunction
  • Front seat outer belt assembly LH (front pretensioner squib LH)
  • Center airbag sensor assembly
  • Floor wire
