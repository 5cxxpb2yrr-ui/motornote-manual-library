Short in CAN Bus Lines: Description

The CAN bus lines are considered to be shorted when the resistance between terminals 6 (CANH) and 14 (CANL) of the DLC3 is below 54 Ω.

SYMPTOM DESCRIPTION

Symptom Trouble Area
Resistance between terminals 6 (CANH) and 14 (CANL) of DLC3 is below 54 Ω
  • Short in CAN bus lines
  • Skid control ECU with actuator
  • Steering sensor
  • Yaw rate sensor
  • ECM
  • A/C amplifier
  • Junction connector
