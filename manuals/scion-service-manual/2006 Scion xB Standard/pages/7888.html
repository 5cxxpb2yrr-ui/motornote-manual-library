On-Vehicle Cleaning

  1. CHECK FINS FOR BLOCKAGE 
    1. Check that the radiator and condenser are not blocked with leaves, dirt, or insects. Clean the hose connections.
      Fig 1: Identifying Fins
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

      If the fins are blocked, wash them with water or a steam cleaner.

      NOTE:
      • If the distance between the steam cleaner and core is too close, the fins may be damaged.
      • Keep the following injection distance.

        Standard injection distance

        INJECTION PRESSURE SPECIFICATION

        Injection Pressure Specified Condition
        2,942 to 4,903 kPa (30 to 50 kgf/cm2 , 427 to 711 psi) 300 mm (11.81 in.)
        4,903 to 7,845 kPa (50 to 80 kgf/cm2 , 711 to 1,138 psi) 500 mm (19.69 in.)
      • If the fins are bent, straighten them with a screwdriver or pliers.
      • Never apply water directly onto the electronic components.
    2. Dry the fins with compressed air.
