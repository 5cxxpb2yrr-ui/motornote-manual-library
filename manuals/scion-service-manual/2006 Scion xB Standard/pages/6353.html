Inspection Procedure

  1. CHECK FOR DTC 
    1. Turn the ignition switch ON, and wait for at least 60 seconds.
    2. Clear the DTCs stored in the memory (see DTC CHECK / CLEAR  ).
    3. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
    4. Turn the ignition switch ON, and wait for at least 60 seconds.
    5. Check the DTCs (see DTC CHECK / CLEAR  ).

      OK: 

      DTC B1655/37 is not output. 

      HINT:

      Codes other than DTC B1655/37 may be output at this time, but they are not related to this check.

    OK : USE SIMULATION METHOD TO CHECK 

    Fig 1: Checking DTC
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    NG : Go to Next Step 

  2. CHECK CONNECTION OF CONNECTOR 
    1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
    2. Disconnect the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal, and wait for at least 90 seconds.
    3. Check that the connectors are properly connected to the center airbag sensor and the front seat inner belt RH.

      OK: 

      The connectors are properly connected. 

    NG : CONNECT CONNECTOR 

    OK : Go to Next Step 

  3. CHECK NO. 2 FLOOR WIRE (OPEN) 
    1. Disconnect the connectors from the center airbag sensor and the front seat inner belt LH.
    2. Using a service wire, connect terminals A19-11 (DBE+) and A19-12 (DBE-) of connector "B".
      NOTE: Do not forcibly insert a service wire into the terminals of the connector when connecting them.
    3. Measure the resistance of the wire harness side connector.

      Standard resistance 

      TESTER CONNECTION - SPECIFIED CONDITION (RESISTANCE)

      Tester Connection Specified Condition
      B9-3 - B9-2 Below 1 Ω

    NG : REPAIR OR REPLACE NO. 2 FLOOR WIRE 

    Fig 2: Checking No. 2 Floor Wire (Open)
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    OK : Go to Next Step 

  4. CHECK NO. 2 FLOOR WIRE (SHORT) 
    1. Disconnect the service wire from connector "B".
    2. Measure the resistance of the wire harness side connector.

      Standard resistance 

      TESTER CONNECTION - SPECIFIED CONDITION (RESISTANCE)

      Tester Connection Specified Condition
      B9-3 - B9-2 1 MΩ or higher

    NG : REPAIR OR REPLACE NO. 2 FLOOR WIRE 

    Fig 3: Checking No. 2 Floor Wire (Short)
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    OK : Go to Next Step 

  5. CHECK NO. 2 FLOOR WIRE (SHORT TO B+) 
    1. Connect the cable to the negative (-) battery terminal, and wait for at least 2 seconds.
    2. Turn the ignition switch ON.
    3. Measure the voltage of the wire harness side connector.

      Standard voltage 

      TESTER CONNECTION - SPECIFIED CONDITION (VOLTAGE)

      Tester Connection Specified Condition
      B9-3 - Body ground Below 1 V
      B9-2 - Body ground

    NG : REPAIR OR REPLACE NO. 2 FLOOR WIRE 

    Fig 4: Checking No. 2 Floor Wire (Short To B+)
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    OK : Go to Next Step 

  6. CHECK NO. 2 FLOOR WIRE (SHORT TO GROUND) 
    1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
    2. Disconnect the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal, and wait for at least 90 seconds.
    3. Measure the resistance of the wire harness side connector.

      Standard resistance 

      TESTER CONNECTION - SPECIFIED CONDITION (RESISTANCE)

      Tester Connection Specified Condition
      B9-3 - Body ground 1 MΩ or higher
      B9-2 - Body ground

    NG : REPAIR OR REPLACE NO. 2 FLOOR WIRE 

    Fig 5: Checking No. 2 Floor Wire (Short To Ground)
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    OK : Go to Next Step 

  7. CHECK FRONT SEAT INNER BELT ASSEMBLY LH 
    1. Connect the connectors to the center airbag sensor and the front seat inner belt LH.
    2. Connect the cable to the negative (-) battery terminal, and wait for at least 2 seconds.
    3. Turn the ignition switch ON, and wait for at least 60 seconds.
    4. Clear the DTCs stored in the memory (see DTC CHECK / CLEAR  ).
    5. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
    6. Turn the ignition switch ON, and wait for at least 60 seconds.
    7. Check the DTCs (see DTC CHECK / CLEAR  ).

      OK: 

      DTC B1655/37 is not output. 

      HINT:

      Codes other than DTC B1655/37 may be output at this time, but they are not related to this check.

    OK : USE SIMULATION METHOD TO CHECK 

    Fig 6: Checking Front Seat Inner Belt Assembly LH
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    NG : Go to Next Step 

  8. REPLACE FRONT SEAT INNER BELT ASSEMBLY LH 
    1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
    2. Disconnect the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal, and wait for at least 90 seconds.
    3. Replace the front seat inner belt LH (see REMOVAL ).

      HINT:

      Perform inspection using parts from a normal vehicle if possible.

    NEXT 

  9. CHECK CENTER AIRBAG SENSOR ASSEMBLY 
    1. Connect the cable to the negative (-) battery terminal, and wait for at least 2 seconds.
    2. Turn the ignition switch ON, and wait for at least 60 seconds.
    3. Clear the DTCs stored in the memory (see DTC CHECK / CLEAR  ).
    4. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
    5. Turn the ignition switch ON, and wait for at least 60 seconds.
    6. Check the DTCs (see DTC CHECK / CLEAR  ).

      OK: 

      DTC B1655/37 is not output. 

      HINT:

      Codes other than DTC B1655/37 may be output at this time, but they are not related to this check.

      Fig 7: Checking Center Airbag Sensor Assembly
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    NG : REPLACE CENTER AIRBAG SENSOR ASSEMBLY 

    OK : END
