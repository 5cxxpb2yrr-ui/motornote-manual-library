Fuel Injector: Installation

  1. INSTALL FUEL INJECTOR ASSEMBLY 
    1. Install new insulators onto each fuel injector.
    2. Apply a light coat of grease or gasoline to new O-rings and install them onto each fuel injector.
    3. Apply a light coat of grease or gasoline to the place where each fuel delivery pipe comes into contact with each O-ring.
      Fig 1: Installing O-Ring And Insulator
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    4. While turning each fuel injector clockwise and counterclockwise, push it into each fuel delivery pipe.
      Fig 2: Installing Fuel Injector
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
      NOTE:
      • Be careful not to twist the O-ring.
      • After installing the fuel injectors, check that they turn smoothly. If they do not, replace the O-ring of the improperly installed fuel injector with a new one, and install the fuel injector again.
  2. INSTALL FUEL DELIVERY PIPE SUB-ASSEMBLY 
    1. Install the 2 spacers onto the cylinder head.
    2. Install the fuel delivery pipe with the 4 fuel injectors.
      Fig 3: Installing Fuel Delivery Pipe
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

      Torque: 19 N*m (194 kgf*cm, 14 ft.*lbf) for bolt A 

      9.0 N*m (92 kgf*cm, 80 in.*lbf) for bolt B 

      NOTE:
      • Be careful not to drop the fuel injectors when installing the fuel delivery pipe.
      • Check that the fuel injectors rotate smoothly after installing the fuel delivery pipe.
    3. Connect the 4 fuel injector connectors.
  3. CONNECT FUEL TUBE SUB-ASSEMBLY 
    1. Connect the fuel tube.
      1. Check the connected part of the pipe for damage or foreign objects.
        Fig 4: Pushing Pipe Into Connector
        Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
      2. Align the axis of the connector with the axis of the pipe, and push the pipe into the connector until the connector makes a "click" sound. If the connection is tight, apply a small amount of fresh engine oil to the tip of the pipe.
      3. After finishing the connection, check that the pipe and connector are securely connected by pulling them.
        Fig 5: Checking Proper Connection Of Pipe And Connector
        Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. Install the fuel pipe clamp.
  4. INSTALL CYLINDER HEAD COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY (See  ADJUSTMENT  ) 
  5. INSTALL IGNITION COIL (See  INSTALLATION  ) 
  6. CONNECT CABLE TO NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL 
  7. CHECK FOR FUEL LEAKS (See  FUEL INJECTOR   ) 
  8. INSTALL NO. 2 CYLINDER HEAD COVER 
    1. Install the cylinder head cover.
    2. First tighten the nuts labeled A, and then tighten the nuts labeled B.
      Fig 6: Installing Cylinder Head Cover
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

      Torque: 7.0 N*m (71 kgf*cm, 62 in.*lbf)
