Oxygen Sensor/Air Fuel Ratio Sensor Drive Cycle 1

Fig 1: Drive Cycle Pattern 1

  1. Preconditions  The monitor will not run unless:
    • MIL is OFF
  2. Drive Pattern 
    1. Connect the hand-held tester or OBD II scan tool to DLC3 to check monitor status and preconditions (refer to step "A").
    2. Start the engine and allow it to idle for 2 minutes or more.
    3. Drive the vehicle at 25mph (40km/h) or more at least for 50 seconds.
    4. Stop the vehicle and allow the engine to idle for 40 seconds or more.
    5. Perform steps (3) and (4) ten times.
    6. If readiness status does not switch to complete, ensure the preconditions are met, turn the ignition switch to OFF and then repeat steps (1) through (5).
