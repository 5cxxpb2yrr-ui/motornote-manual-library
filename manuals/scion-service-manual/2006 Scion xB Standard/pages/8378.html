Emission Control: Canister: Removal

  1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL 
    CAUTION: Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to prevent airbag and seat belt pretensioner activation.
  2. REMOVE CANISTER 
    1. Disconnect the connector and detach the 2 clamps.
      Fig 1: Disconnecting Connector
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. Disconnect the 2 tubes and hose.
      1. Using a screwdriver, pry up the retainer.

        HINT:

        Do not remove the retainer.

        Fig 2: Prying Up Retainer
        Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
      2. Disconnect the purge line hose.
      3. Disconnect the air inlet line tube from the canister.
      4. Disconnect the vent line tube from the canister (leak detection pump).
        Fig 3: Disconnecting Air Inlet Line Tube From Canister
        Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    3. Remove the 3 bolts and canister.
      Fig 4: Removing Canister
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
