DTC B1900/73 Short in Driver Side Front Pretensioner Squib Circuit; DTC B1901/73 Open in Driver Side Front Pretensioner Squib Circuit; DTC B1902/73 Short to GND in Driver Side Front Pretensioner Squib Circuit; DTC B1903/73 Short to B+ in Driver Side Front Pretensioner Squib Circuit: Description

The driver side front pretensioner squib circuit consists of the center airbag sensor and the front seat outer belt LH. The circuit instructs the SRS to deploy when deployment conditions are met. These DTCs are recorded when a malfunction is detected in the front pretensioner squib LH circuit.

DTC DETECTION CONDITION AND TROUBLE AREA

DTC No. DTC Detection Condition Trouble Area
B1900/73 When one of following conditions is met:
  • Center airbag sensor receives line short circuit signal 5 times in driver side front pretensioner squib circuit during primary check
  • Front pretensioner squib driver side malfunction
  • Center airbag sensor malfunction
  • Front seat outer belt LH assembly (Driver side front pretensioner squib)
  • Center airbag sensor assembly
  • Floor wire
B1901/73 When one of following conditions is met:
  • Center airbag sensor receives open circuit signal in driver side front pretensioner squib circuit for 2 seconds
  • Front pretensioner squib driver side malfunction
  • Center airbag sensor malfunction
  • Front seat outer belt LH assembly (Driver side front pretensioner squib)
  • Center airbag sensor assembly
  • Floor wire
B1902/73 When one of following conditions is met:
  • Center airbag sensor receives short circuit to ground signal in driver side front pretensioner squib circuit for 0.5 seconds
  • Front pretensioner squib driver side malfunction
  • Center airbag sensor malfunction
  • Front seat outer belt LH assembly (Driver side front pretensioner squib)
  • Center airbag sensor assembly
  • Floor wire
B1903/73 When one of following conditions is met:
  • Center airbag sensor receives short circuit to B+ signal in driver side front pretensioner squib circuit for 0.5 seconds
  • Front pretensioner squib driver side malfunction
  • Center airbag sensor malfunction
  • Front seat outer belt LH assembly (Driver side front pretensioner squib)
  • Center airbag sensor assembly
  • Floor wire
