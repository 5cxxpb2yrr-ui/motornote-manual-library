Automatic Transaxle Fluid: Adjustment

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. CHECK TRANSAXLE FLUID LEVEL 

    HINT:

    Drive the vehicle so that the engine and transaxle are at normal operating temperature.

    Fluid temperature: 70 to 80°C (158 to 176°F) 

    1. Park the vehicle on a level surface and set the parking brake.
    2. With the engine idling and the brake pedal depressed, adjust the shift lever to each position from P to L position, and return it to the P position.
    3. Take out the ATF dipstick and wipe it clean.
    4. Fully insert it back into the pipe.
    5. Take it out and check that the fluid level is at the HOT position.
