High Mounted Stop Light Assembly: Removal

  1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL 
    CAUTION: Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to prevent airbag and seat belt pretensioner activation.
  2. REMOVE HIGH MOUNTED STOP LIGHT COVER 
    1. Detach the 4 claws and remove the stop light cover.
      Fig 1: Removing High Mounted Stop Light Cover
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  3. REMOVE HIGH MOUNTED STOP LIGHT ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the 2 screws.
    2. Disconnect the connector and remove the stop light.
      Fig 2: Removing Stop Light Screws
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    3. Remove the stop light bulb as shown in the illustration.
      Fig 3: Removing Stop Light Bulb
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
