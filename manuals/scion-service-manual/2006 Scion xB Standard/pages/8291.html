Starting (1NZ-FE): Starter: Removal

  1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL 
    CAUTION: Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to prevent airbag and seat belt pretensioner activation.
  2. REMOVE ENGINE UNDER COVER LH 
    1. Remove the 4 bolts, nut and cover.
  3. REMOVE ENGINE UNDER COVER RH 
    1. Remove the 4 bolts and cover.
  4. REMOVE FLYWHEEL HOUSING SIDE COVER 
    1. Remove the flywheel housing side cover by pulling it out as shown in the illustration.
      Fig 1: Removing Flywheel Housing Side Cover By Pulling It Out
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  5. REMOVE STARTER ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the nut and starter wire.
    2. Disconnect the starter connector.
    3. Remove the 2 bolts and starter.
