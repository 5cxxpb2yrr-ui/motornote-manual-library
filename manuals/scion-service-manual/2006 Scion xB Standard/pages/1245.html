STEERING ANGLE SENSOR (w/VSC): Removal

  1. PRECAUTION 
    1. Follow the PRECAUTION (see PRECAUTION ).
  2. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL 
    CAUTION: Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to prevent airbag and seat belt pretensioner activation.
  3. PLACE FRONT WHEELS FACING STRAIGHT AHEAD 
  4. REMOVE STEERING PAD ASSEMBLY 
  5. REMOVE STEERING WHEEL ASSEMBLY 
  6. REMOVE STEERING COLUMN COVER 
  7. REMOVE SPIRAL CABLE SUB-ASSEMBLY 
  8. REMOVE STEERING SENSOR 
    1. Release the 4 claw fittings and remove the steering sensor from the spiral cable.
