Park / Neutral Position Switch: Adjustment

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. ADJUST PARK/NEUTRAL POSITION SWITCH ASSEMBLY 
    1. Loosen the 2 bolts of the park/neutral position switch and set the shift lever to the N position.
    2. Align the groove and neutral basic line.
    3. Hold the switch in this position and tighten the 2 bolts.

      Torque: 5.4 N*m (55 kgf*cm, 48 in.*lbf)
