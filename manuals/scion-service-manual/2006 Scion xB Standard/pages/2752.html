Emission Control: Canister: Installation

  1. INSTALL CANISTER 
    1. Install the canister with the 3 bolts.

      Torque: 19 N*m (194 kgf*cm, 14 ft.*lbf) 

      Fig 1: Installing Canister
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. Connect the 2 tubes and hose.
      Fig 2: Connecting Tubes And Hose
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
      1. Connect the air inlet line tube to the canister (leak detection pump).
      2. Connect the vent line tube to the canister.
      3. Connect the purge line hose.

        HINT:

        Install the hose to the canister, and then attach the retainer.

        Fig 3: Connecting Air Inlet Line Tube To Canister (Leak Detection Pump)
        Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    3. Connect the connector and attach the 2 clamps.
      Fig 4: Connecting Connector
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  2. CONNECT CABLE TO NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL
