Inspection Procedure

HINT:

When U0121/94, U0123/62, U0124/95 or U0126/63 is output along with C1210/36 or C1336/39, inspect and repair the trouble areas indicated by U0123/62, U0124/95 or U0126/63 first.

  1. PERFORM ZERO POINT CALIBRATION OF YAW RATE SENSOR (DECELERATION SENSOR) 
    1. Perform the zero point calibration of the yaw rate sensor (see CALIBRATION  ).

    NEXT 

  2. CHECK YAW RATE SENSOR (INSTALLATION) 
    1. Check that the yaw rate sensor has been installed properly.

    NG: INSTALL YAW RATE SENSOR CORRECTLY 

    OK: Go to Next Step 

  3. RECONFIRM DTC 
    1. Clear the DTCs (see DTC CHECK / CLEAR  ).
    2. Turn the ignition switch ON.
    3. Check for DTCs (see DTC CHECK / CLEAR  ).

      OK: Same DTCs are output. 

      HINT:

      When replacing the skid control ECU with actuator, perform the zero point calibration (see CALIBRATION  ).

    NG: END 

    OK: REPLACE YAW RATE SENSOR
