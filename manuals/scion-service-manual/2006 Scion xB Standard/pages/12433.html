Timing Chain: Removal

  1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL 
    CAUTION: Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to prevent airbag and seat belt pretensioner activation.
  2. REMOVE FRONT WHEEL RH 
  3. REMOVE NO. 2 CYLINDER HEAD COVER 
    1. Remove the 4 nuts and No. 2 cylinder head cover.
      Fig 1: Removing No 2 Cylinder Head Cover
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  4. REMOVE IGNITION COIL 
    1. Remove the 4 bolts and pull out the 4 ignition coils.
      Fig 2: Removing Ignition Coil
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  5. REMOVE VENTILATION HOSE 
    1. Disconnect the ventilation hose from the cylinder head cover.
      Fig 3: Removing Ventilation Hose
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  6. REMOVE NO. 2 VENTILATION HOSE 
    1. Disconnect the No. 2 ventilation hose from the cylinder head cover.
      Fig 4: Removing No 2 Ventilation Hose
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  7. REMOVE CYLINDER HEAD COVER 
    1. Remove the 9 bolts, 2 nuts and cylinder head cover.
      Fig 5: Removing Cylinder Head Cover
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  8. REMOVE GENERATOR V BELT (See  REMOVAL   ) 
  9. REMOVE GENERATOR (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  10. REMOVE ENGINE UNDER COVER RH 
  11. DRAIN ENGINE OIL (See  REPLACEMENT  ) 
  12. DRAIN ENGINE COOLANT (See  ON-VEHICLE INSPECTION  ) 
  13. REMOVE VANE PUMP V BELT (See  REMOVAL   ) 
  14. REMOVE WATER PUMP PULLEY (See  DISASSEMBLY   ) 
  15. REMOVE WATER PUMP (See  DISASSEMBLY   ) 
  16. REMOVE CRANKSHAFT PULLEY 
    1. Set the No. 1 cylinder to the TDC/compression.
      1. Turn the crankshaft pulley until its timing notch and the timing mark 0 of the chain cover are aligned.
      2. Check that both timing marks on the camshaft timing sprocket and the camshaft timing gear are facing upward as shown in the illustration.

        If not, turn the crankshaft 1 complete revolution (360°) and align the marks as above.

        Fig 6: Aligning Crankshaft Pulley Timing Notch And Timing Mark 0 Of Chain Cover
        Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. Using SST, remove the pulley bolt.
      Fig 7: Removing Pulley Bolt
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

      SST 09213-58012 (91111-50845), 09330-00021 

      If necessary, remove the pulley with SST.

      SST 09950-50013 (09951-05010, 09952-05010, 09953-05020, 09954-05021) 

  17. REMOVE CAMSHAFT TIMING OIL CONTROL VALVE ASSEMBLY 
    1. Disconnect the oil control valve connector.
    2. Remove the bolt, oil control valve and O-ring.
      Fig 8: Removing Camshaft Timing Oil Control Valve Assembly
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  18. REMOVE CRANKSHAFT POSITION SENSOR (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  19. REMOVE ENGINE MOUNTING BRACKET INSULATOR RH 
    1. Place a wooden block on a jack underneath the engine.
      Fig 9: Placing Wooden Block On A Jack Underneath Engine
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. Remove the bolt which is used to fix the liquid tube to the mounting insulator RH.
    3. Remove the 5 bolts and nut, and then remove the mounting insulator RH.
      Fig 10: Removing Mounting Insulator RH
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  20. REMOVE ENGINE MOUNTING BRACKET RH 
    1. Remove the 4 bolts and mounting bracket RH.
      Fig 11: Removing Engine Mounting Bracket RH
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  21. REMOVE TIMING CHAIN COVER (See  DISASSEMBLY   ) 
  22. REMOVE OIL PUMP SEAL (See  REPLACEMENT   ) 
  23. REMOVE NO. 1 CHAIN TENSIONER 
    1. Remove the 2 bolts and chain tensioner.
      NOTE:
      • Do not rotate the crankshaft with the chain tensioner removed.
      • When rotating the camshaft with the timing chain removed, rotate the crankshaft counterclockwise 40° from the TDC and align the oil jet hole with the paint mark to prevent the pistons from coming into contact with the valves.
        Fig 12: Aligning Oil Jet Hole With Paint Mark
        Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  24. REMOVE CHAIN TENSIONER SLIPPER 
    1. Remove the bolt and tensioner slipper.
  25. REMOVE NO. 1 CHAIN VIBRATION DAMPER 
    1. Remove the 2 bolts and chain vibration damper.
  26. REMOVE CHAIN
