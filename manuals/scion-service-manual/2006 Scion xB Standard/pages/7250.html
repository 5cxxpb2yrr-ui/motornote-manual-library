Problem Symptoms Table (2005/12- )

HINT:

  • Use the table below to help determine the cause of the problem symptom. The potential causes of the symptoms are listed in order of probability in the "Suspected area" column of the table. Check each symptom by checking the suspected areas in the order they are listed. Replace parts as necessary.
  • Proceed to the troubleshooting procedures for each circuit in the table below.

Airbag system: 

AIRBAG SYSTEM PROBLEM SYMPTOM (2005/12- )

Symptom Suspected area
SRS warning light remains on (When ignition switch is ON, SRS warning light sometimes turns on after approximately 6 seconds) SRS warning light circuit
SRS warning light remains on (SRS warning light remains illuminated even when DTC is not output) SRS warning light circuit
SRS warning light does not come on (When ignition switch is ON, SRS warning light does not illuminate) SRS warning light circuit
TC and CG terminal circuit (SRS warning light is blinking but pattern is not DTC or normal system code) TC terminal circuit
TC and CG terminal circuit (Terminals TC and CG are connected but blinking pattern is not DTC or normal system code) TC terminal circuit
