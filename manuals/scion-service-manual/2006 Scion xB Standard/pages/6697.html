Diagnosis For Vehicle Pull And Tire Vibration

  1. CHECK THAT WHEEL NUTS ARE FULLY TIGHTENED 

    NG: REPAIR 

    OK: Go to Next Step 

  2. CHECK TIRE PRESSURE 

    NG: Go to step   5 

    OK: Go to Next Step 

  3. CHECK WHEEL BALANCE 

    NG: Go to step   7 

    OK: Go to Next Step 

  4. INSPECT BEARING LOOSENESS AND AXLE HUB RUNOUT 

    NG: REPAIR 

    OK: REPLACE TIRE(S) 

  5. ADJUST TIRE PRESSURE 

    Go to Next Step 

  6. CHECK WHEEL BALANCE 

    OK: Go to step   8 

    NG: Go to Next Step 

  7. BALLANCE WHEELS 

    Go to Next Step 

  8. INSPECT BEARING LOOSENESS AND AXLE HUB RUNOUT 

    NG: REPAIR 

    OK: RETURN VEHICLE TO CUSTOMER
