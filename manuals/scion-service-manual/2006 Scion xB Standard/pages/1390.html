Front Drive Shaft Assembly: Installation

HINT:

  • Use the same procedures for the RH side and LH side.
  • The procedures listed below are for the LH side.
  1. INSTALL FRONT DRIVE SHAFT ASSEMBLY LH 
    1. Manual transaxle:

      Coat the spline of the inboard joint shaft with gear oil.

    2. Automatic transaxle:

      Coat the spline of the inboard joint shaft with ATF.

    3. Align the shaft splines and tap in the drive shaft with a brass bar and hammer.

      HINT:

      Whether the inboard joint shaft is in contact with the pinion shaft or not can be known from the sound or feeling when driving it in.

      NOTE:
      • Set the hole snap ring with the opening side facing downward.
      • Be careful not to damage the boot and oil seal.
        Fig 1: Installing Front Drive Shaft Assembly LH
        Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  2. INSTALL FRONT DRIVE SHAFT ASSEMBLY RH 

    HINT:

    Use the same procedure for the LH side.

  3. CONNECT FRONT DRIVE SHAFT ASSEMBLY 
    1. Connect the drive shaft to the front axle.
      NOTE:
      • Be careful not to damage the outboard joint boot.
      • Be careful not to damage the speed sensor rotor.
      • Do not excessively push the front axle assembly.
  4. CONNECT TIE ROD END SUB-ASSEMBLY 
    1. Connect the tie rod end to the steering knuckle with the nut.

      Torque: 49 N*m (500 kgf*cm, 36 ft.*lbf) 

    2. Install a new cotter pin.
      NOTE: If the holes for the cotter pin are not aligned, tighten the nut an additional 60°.
  5. CONNECT FRONT NO. 1 SUSPENSION LOWER ARM SUB-ASSEMBLY 
    1. Connect the front suspension lower arm to the steering knuckle with the nut.

      Torque: 98 N*m (999 kgf*cm, 72 ft.*lbf) 

    2. Install a new clip.
      NOTE: If the holes for the clip are not aligned, tighten the nut an additional 60°.
  6. CONNECT FRONT STABILIZER BAR 
    1. Connect the front stabilizer bar with the 2 cushion retainers, 2 cushions and nut, as shown in the illustration.
      NOTE: Be sure to install the cushion and retainer in the correct direction.
    2. Hold the bolt with a wrench and install the nut.

      Torque: 18 N*m (184 kgf*cm, 13 ft.*lbf) 

      Fig 2: Installing Front Stabilizer Bar
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  7. INSTALL FRONT SPEED SENSOR 
    1. Install the speed sensor and flexible hose on the shock absorber with the bolt and clip.
      Fig 3: Installing Bolt And Clip
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

      Torque: 29 N*m (300 kgf*cm, 22 ft.*lbf) 

      HINT:

      The positions of the bolt and clip on the right side differ from those of the left side.

    2. Install the speed sensor on the steering knuckle with the bolt.
      Fig 4: Installing Front Speed Sensor
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

      Torque: 8.0 N*m (82 kgf*cm, 71 in.*lbf) 

      NOTE:
      • Be careful not to damage the speed sensor.
      • Keep the speed sensor clean.
      • Do not twist the sensor wire when installing the speed sensor.
  8. INSTALL FRONT AXLE HUB NUT 
    1. Install a new hub nut.

      Torque: 216 N*m (2,200 kgf*cm, 159 ft.*lbf) 

    2. Using a chisel and hammer, stake the hub nut.
