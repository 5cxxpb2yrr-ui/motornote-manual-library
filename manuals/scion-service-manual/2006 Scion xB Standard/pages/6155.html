Air Inlet Control Servo Motor: Inspection

  1. INSPECT DAMPER SERVO SUB-ASSEMBLY 
    1. Check that the damper servo moves as follows.

      OK : Go to Next Step 

      TESTER CONNECTION AND SPECIFIED CONDITION CHART

      Measuring Condition Specified Condition
      Battery positive (+) --> Terminal 7
      Battery negative (-) --> Terminal 1, 8      		 RECIRC
      Battery positive (+) --> Terminal 7
      Battery negative (-) --> Terminal 2, 8      		 RECIRC/FRESH
      Battery positive (+) --> Terminal 7
      Battery negative (-) --> Terminal 3, 8      		 FRESH
