Inspection Procedure

CAUTION: Be sure to perform the following procedures before troubleshooting to avoid unexpected airbag deployment.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  2. Disconnect the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal, and wait for at least 90 seconds.
  3. Disconnect the connector from the center airbag sensor.
  4. Disconnect the connector from the steering pad.
  1. CHECK CONNECTION OF CONNECTOR 
    1. Disconnect the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal, and wait for at least 90 seconds.
    2. Check that the connectors are properly connected to the center airbag sensor and combination meter.

      OK: 

      Connectors are connected. 

    NG : CONNECT CONNECTOR 

    OK : Go to Next Step 

  2. CHECK COMBINATION METER ASSEMBLY 
    1. Disconnect the C6 meter connector.
    2. Turn the ignition switch ON.
    3. Measure the voltage of the wire harness side connector.

      Standard voltage 

      TESTER CONNECTION - SPECIFIED CONDITION (VOLTAGE)

      Tester Connection Specified Condition
      C6-23 - Body ground 8 to 14 V

    NG : REPAIR OR REPLACE HARNESS AND CONNECTOR

    OK : Go to Next Step 

  3. CHECK SRS WARNING LIGHT OPERATION 
    1. Replace the center airbag sensor.
    2. Check that the SRS warning light illuminates normally.

      OK: 

      SRS warning light illuminates normally. 

    NG : REPLACE COMBINATION METER ASSEMBLY 

    OK : Go to Next Step 

  4. REPLACE CENTER AIRBAG SENSOR ASSEMBLY 
    1. Replace the center airbag sensor.
    2. Check that the SRS warning light illuminates normally.

      OK: 

      SRS warning light illuminates normally. 

    NG : REPLACE COMBINATION METER ASSEMBLY 

    OK : END
