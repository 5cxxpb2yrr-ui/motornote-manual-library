Automatic Transaxle Unit: Inspection

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. INSPECT INPUT SHAFT END PLAY 
    1. Using a dial indicator, measure the end play in the axial direction.

      Standard end play: 0.374 to 1.292 mm (0.0147 to 0.0509 in.) 

      If the end play is not as specified, select and replace the thrust needle roller bearing.

      Fig 1: Measuring Input Shaft End Play
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  2. INSPECT INTERMEDIATE SHAFT ASSEMBLY 
    1. Using a dial indicator, measure the end play of the intermediate shaft.

      Standard end play: 0.204 to 0.966 mm (0.008 to 0.038 in.) 

      Fig 2: Measuring End Play Of Intermediate Shaft
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  3. INSPECT 2ND COAST AND OVERDRIVE BRAKE DISC 
    1. Visually check if the sliding surface of the disc, plate and flange are worn or burnt. Replace as necessary.

      HINT:

      • If the lining of the disc is peeling off or discolored, or even if a part of the printed number is defaced, replace all discs.
      • Before assembling new discs, soak them in ATF for at least 15 minutes.
        Fig 3: Identifying Brake Disc
        Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  4. INSPECT 1-WAY CLUTCH ASSEMBLY 
    1. Hold the rear planetary sun gear and turn the 1-way clutch. The 1-way clutch should turn freely clockwise and should lock counterclockwise.
      Fig 4: Inspecting 1-Way Clutch Assembly
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  5. INSPECT 2ND BRAKE BRAKE DISC 
    1. Visually check if the sliding surface of the disc, plate and flange are worn or burnt. Replace as necessary.

      HINT:

      • If the lining of the disc is peeling off or discolored, or even if a part of the printed mark is defaced, replace all discs.
      • Before assembling new discs, soak them in ATF for at least 15 minutes.
        Fig 5: Identifying Brake Disc
        Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  6. INSPECT NO. 2 1-WAY CLUTCH 
    1. Install the 1-way clutch and thrust washer to the rear planetary sun gear.
    2. Hold the rear planetary sun gear and turn the 1-way clutch. The 1-way clutch should turn freely counterclockwise and should lock clockwise.
      Fig 6: Inspecting No. 2 1-Way Clutch
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  7. INSPECT 1ST AND REVERSE BRAKE DISC 
    1. Visually check if the sliding surface of the disc, plate and flange are worn or burnt. Replace as necessary.

      HINT:

      • If the lining of the disc is peeling off or discolored, or even if a part of the printed mark is defaced, replace all discs.
      • Before assembling new discs, soak them in ATF for at least 15 minutes.
        Fig 7: Identifying Reverse Brake Disc
        Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  8. INSPECT 1ST AND REVERSE BRAKE RETURN SPRING SUB-ASSEMBLY 
    1. Using a vernier caliper, measure the free length of the spring together with the spring seat.

      Standard free length: 13.96 mm (0.5496 in.) 

      Fig 8: Measuring Free Length Of Spring
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  9. INSPECT INPUT SHAFT ASSEMBLY 
    1. Make sure that the input shaft turns smoothly.
