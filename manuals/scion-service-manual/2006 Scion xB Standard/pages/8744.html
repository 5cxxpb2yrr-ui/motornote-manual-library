Engine Oil Replacement Reminder Reset - Procedure 07

To reset MAINT reminder indicator after engine oil change:

  1. While the engine is running, switch the multi-information display to the SETTINGS DISPLAY, using the TRIP button on the right side of the steeing wheel..
  2. Select "Scheduled Maintenance" on the settings display.
  3. Select YES on the "Scheduled Maintenance" screen.
  4. "The Data Has Been Reset" will be displayed on the multi-information display when the reset procedure has been completed.
