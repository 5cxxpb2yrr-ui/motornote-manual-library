Park / Neutral Position Switch: Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. REMOVE BATTERY 
  2. DISCONNECT TRANSMISSION CONTROL CABLE ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the nut and disconnect the control cable from the control shaft lever.
    2. Remove the clip and disconnect the control cable from the control cable bracket.
      Fig 1: Removing Nut And Disconnecting Control Cable From Control Shaft Lever
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  3. REMOVE PARK/NEUTRAL POSITION SWITCH ASSEMBLY 
    1. Disconnect the connector from the park/neutral position switch.
    2. Remove the nut, washer and control shaft lever.
    3. Pry out the lock washer and remove the manual valve shaft nut.
    4. Remove the 2 bolts and pull out the park/neutral position switch.
