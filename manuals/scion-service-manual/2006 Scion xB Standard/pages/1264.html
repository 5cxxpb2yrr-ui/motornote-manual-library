Front Brake Pad, Brake Caliper & Brake Rotor: Removal

HINT:

  • Use the same procedures for the LH side and RH side.
  • The procedures listed below are for the LH side.
  1. REMOVE FRONT WHEEL 
  2. DRAIN BRAKE FLUID 
    NOTE: Wash off brake fluid immediately if it comes in contact with any painted surface.
  3. REMOVE FRONT DISC BRAKE CYLINDER SUBASSEMBLY LH 
    1. Remove the union bolt and gasket from the front disc brake cylinder, and then disconnect the flexible hose.
      Fig 1: Removing Union Bolt From Front Disc Brake Cylinder
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. Hold the brake cylinder slide pin, and remove the 2 bolts and brake cylinder.
  4. REMOVE FRONT DISC BRAKE PAD 
    1. Remove the 2 pads with anti squeal shims.
  5. REMOVE FRONT ANTI SQUEAL SHIM 
    1. Remove the No. 1 and No. 2 shims from each pad.
  6. REMOVE FRONT DISC BRAKE PAD SUPPORT PLATE 
    1. Remove the 2 support plates from the cylinder mounting.
      Fig 2: Removing Brake Cylinder Bolts
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  7. REMOVE FRONT DISC BRAKE CYLINDER SLIDE PIN 
    1. Remove the 2 slide pins from the cylinder mounting.
      Fig 3: Removing Slide Pins From Cylinder Mounting
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  8. REMOVE FRONT DISC BRAKE DUST BOOT 
    1. Remove the 2 bush dust boots from the cylinder mounting.
      Fig 4: Removing Bush Dust Boots From Cylinder Mounting
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  9. REMOVE FRONT DISC BRAKE CYLINDER MOUNTING LH 
    1. Remove the 2 bolts and cylinder mounting.
      Fig 5: Removing Cylinder Mounting Bolts
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  10. REMOVE FRONT DISC 
    1. Place matchmarks on the disc and axle hub.
    2. Remove the disc.
      Fig 6: Placing Matchmarks On Disc And Axle Hub
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
