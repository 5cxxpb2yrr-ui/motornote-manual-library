Voltage Check

  1. Establish conditions in which voltage is present at the check point.

    Example:

    1. Ignition SW on
    2. Ignition SW and SW 1 on
    3. Ignition SW, SW 1 and Relay on (SW 2 off)
  2. Using a voltmeter, connect the negative lead to a good ground point or negative battery terminal, and the positive lead to the connector or component terminal.

This check can be done with a test light instead of a voltmeter.
