Heater Control Assembly: Removal

  1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL 
    CAUTION: Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to prevent airbag and seat belt pretensioner activation.
  2. REMOVE HEATER CONTROL KNOB 
    1. Remove the 3 heater control knobs.
      Fig 1: Removing 3 Heater Control Knobs
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  3. REMOVE INSTRUMENT CLUSTER FINISH PANEL CENTER 
    1. Remove the 2 screws.
    2. Detach the 6 clips and 4 claws, and then remove the instrument cluster finish panel.
    3. Disconnect the connectors.
      Fig 2: Removing Instrument Cluster Finish Panel
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  4. REMOVE HEATER CONTROL AND ACCESSORY ASSEMBLY 
    1. Detach the 3 screws and disconnect the heater control and accessory assembly.
    2. Disconnect the connector.
      Fig 3: Detaching 3 Screws And Disconnecting Heater Control And Accessory Assembly
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    3. Using a screwdriver, detach the claw of the cable clamp and disconnect the defroster damper control cable.
      NOTE:
      • Be careful not to bend the cable wire.
      • If the cable wire bends, the defroster damper control cable operation may be affected.

      HINT:

      Tape the screwdriver tip before use.

      Fig 4: Detaching Claw Of Cable Clamp And Disconnecting Defroster Damper Control Cable
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    4. Using a screwdriver, detach the claw of the cable clamp and disconnect the air mix damper control cable.
      NOTE:
      • Be careful not to bend the cable wire.
      • If the cable wire bends, the air mix damper control cable operation may be affected.

      HINT:

      Tape the screwdriver tip before use.

    5. Remove the heater control and accessory assembly.
      Fig 5: Detaching Claw Of Cable Clamp And Disconnecting Air Mix Damper Control Cable
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  5. REMOVE AIR INLET DAMPER CONTROL LEVER 
    1. Detach the claw, and remove the air inlet damper control lever.
      Fig 6: Removing Air Inlet Damper Control Lever
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  6. REMOVE HEATER SWITCH ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the 2 screws and heater switch.
      Fig 7: Removing 2 Screws And Heater Switch
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
