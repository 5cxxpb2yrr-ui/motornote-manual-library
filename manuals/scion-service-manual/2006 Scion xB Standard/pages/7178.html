Inspection Procedure

  1. INSPECT FUSE (ALT, ABS NO. 1) 
    1. Remove the ABS NO. 1 H-fuse from the engine room junction block.
    2. Remove the ALT H-fuse from the fusible link.
    3. Measure the resistance of the H-fuses.

      Standard resistance: Below 1 Ω 

    NG: REPLACE FUSE 

    OK: Go to Next Step 

  2. CHECK WIRE HARNESS (SKID CONTROL ECU - BATTERY AND BODY GROUND) 
    1. Disconnect the S3 ECU connector.
    2. Measure the voltage of the wire harness side connector.

      Standard voltage 

      TESTER CONNECTION SPECIFIED CONDITION

      Tester Connection Specified Condition
      S3-31 (+BS) - Body ground 10 to 14 V
    3. Measure the resistance of the wire harness side connector.

      Standard resistance 

      TESTER CONNECTION SPECIFIED CONDITION

      Tester Connection Specified Condition
      S3-32 (GND1) - Body ground Below 1 Ω
      S3-1 (GND2) - Body ground
      Fig 1: Checking Wire Harness

    NG: REPAIR OR REPLACE HARNESS AND CONNECTOR 

    OK: Go to Next Step 

  3. RECONFIRM DTC 

    HINT:

    These codes are output when a problem is detected in the brake actuator assembly.

    As the ABS motor relay is built into the brake actuator assembly, ABS motor relay and solenoid unit inspection cannot be performed.

    Be sure to check if the DTC is output before replacing the brake actuator assembly.

    1. Clear the DTCs (see DTC CHECK / CLEAR  ).
    2. Drive the vehicle at 6 km/h (4 mph) or more.
    3. Check for DTCs (see DTC CHECK / CLEAR  ).

      Result 

      RESULT REFERENCE

      Result Proceed to
      DTC is output A
      DTC is not output B

      HINT:

      When replacing the skid control ECU with actuator, perform the zero point calibration (see CALIBRATION  ).

    B: END 

    A: REPLACE BRAKE ACTUATOR ASSEMBLY
