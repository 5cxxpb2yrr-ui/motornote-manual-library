Thermistor Assembly: Inspection

  1. INSPECT THERMISTOR ASSEMBLY 
    1. Measure the resistance between terminals 1 and 2 of thermistor.

      Standard resistance 

      CONNECTION AND SPECIFIED CONDITION CHART

      Condition Specified Condition
      Approx. -30°C (-86°F) 80 to 100 kΩ
      Approx. 25°C (77°F) 4.8 to 5.2 kΩ
      Approx. 40°C (104°F) 2.507 to 2.807 kΩ
      Approx. 100°C (212°F) 0.3 to 0.37 kΩ

      If the result is not as specified, replace the thermistor.
