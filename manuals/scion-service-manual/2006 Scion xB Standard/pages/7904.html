Oil Pump: Disassembly

  1. REMOVE OIL PUMP SEAL 
    1. Using a screwdriver, remove the oil seal.
      Fig 1: Removing Oil Pump Seal
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

      HINT:

      Tape the screwdriver tip before use.

  2. REMOVE OIL PUMP RELIEF VALVE 
    1. Remove the plug, spring and relief valve.
  3. REMOVE OIL PUMP 
    1. Remove the 2 bolts, 3 screws and oil pump cover.
      Fig 2: Removing Oil Pump
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. Remove the oil pump rotor set.
      NOTE: Do not change the arrangement or turn over the 2 removed rotors.
