Introduction

Use the following procedure to perform initialization of the radio during Pre-Delivery Service (PDS).

NOTE: Any time the battery terminal has been disconnected, initialize the radio after reconnecting the battery terminal.

Applicable Vehicles

  • 2005 - 2006  model year Scion xB  vehicles.

2005 MY Scion xB Initialization Procedure

  1. With the radio ON, press and hold the SSP button until you hear a beep, and the current Equalizer setting is displayed.
  2. Press the "+" or "-" VOL button to change the display to read "xB."
  3. Press the SSP button to complete initialization.

2006 MY Scion xB Initialization Procedure

  1. With the radio ON, press and hold the SSP button until you hear a beep, and the current Equalizer setting is displayed.
  2. Turn the VOL knob to change the display to read "Car type xB."
  3. Press the SSP button to complete initialization.
