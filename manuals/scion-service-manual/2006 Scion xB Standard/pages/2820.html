Oil Pump: Removal

  1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL 
    CAUTION: Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to prevent airbag and seat belt pretensioner activation.
  2. REMOVE FRONT WHEEL RH 
  3. REMOVE NO. 2 CYLINDER HEAD COVER (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  4. REMOVE IGNITION COIL (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  5. DISCONNECT VENTILATION HOSE (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  6. DISCONNECT NO. 2 VENTILATION HOSE (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  7. REMOVE CYLINDER HEAD COVER (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  8. REMOVE GENERATOR V BELT (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  9. REMOVE GENERATOR (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  10. REMOVE ENGINE UNDER COVER RH 
  11. DRAIN ENGINE OIL (See  REPLACEMENT   ) 
  12. DRAIN ENGINE COOLANT (See  ON-VEHICLE INSPECTION  ) 
  13. REMOVE VANE PUMP V BELT (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  14. REMOVE WATER PUMP PULLEY (See  DISASSEMBLY  ) 
  15. REMOVE WATER PUMP (See  DISASSEMBLY  ) 
  16. REMOVE CRANKSHAFT PULLEY (See  DISASSEMBLY  ) 
  17. REMOVE CAMSHAFT TIMING OIL CONTROL VALVE ASSEMBLY (See  DISASSEMBLY  ) 
  18. REMOVE CRANKSHAFT POSITION SENSOR (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  19. REMOVE ENGINE MOUNTING BRACKET INSULATOR RH (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  20. REMOVE ENGINE MOUNTING BRACKET RH (See  DISASSEMBLY  ) 
  21. REMOVE TIMING CHAIN COVER (OIL PUMP) 
    1. Remove the 15 bolts and nut.
      Fig 1: Removing Bolts And Nut
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. Using a screwdriver with its tip wrapped in tape, remove the oil pump by prying between the cylinder head and cylinder block.
      Fig 2: Removing Oil Pump
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
      NOTE: Be careful not to damage the contact surface of the oil pump, cylinder head and cylinder block.
    3. Remove the 2 O-rings from the cylinder block and No. 1 oil pan.
