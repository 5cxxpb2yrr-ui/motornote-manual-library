Center Cluster Module Switch: Removal

  1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL 
    CAUTION: Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to prevent airbag and seat belt pretensioner activation.
  2. REMOVE HEATER CONTROL KNOB 
    1. Remove the 3 heater control knobs.
      Fig 1: Removing 3 Heater Control Knobs
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  3. REMOVE INSTRUMENT CLUSTER FINISH PANEL CENTER 
    1. Remove the 2 screws.
    2. Detach the 6 clips and 4 claws, and then remove the instrument cluster finish panel.
    3. Disconnect the connectors.
      Fig 2: Removing Instrument Cluster Finish Panel
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  4. REMOVE CENTER CLUSTER MODULE SWITCH 
    1. Remove the 2 screws.
    2. Detach the 3 claws and remove the center cluster module switch.
      Fig 3: Removing Center Cluster Module Switch
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
