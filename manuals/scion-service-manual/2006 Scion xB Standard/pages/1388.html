Front Drive Shaft Assembly: Disassembly

HINT:

  • Use the same procedures for the RH side and LH side.
  • The procedures listed below are for the LH side.
  1. REMOVE FRONT NO. 2 AXLE INBOARD JOINT BOOT CLAMP 
    1. One touch clamp type:

      Using a screwdriver, remove the inboard joint boot clamp as shown in the illustration.

    2. Claw engagement type:

      Using needle nose pliers, remove the inboard joint boot clamp as shown in the illustration.

      Fig 1: Removing Front No. 2 Axle Inboard Joint Boot Clamp
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  2. REMOVE FRONT AXLE INBOARD JOINT BOOT CLAMP 
    1. One touch clamp type:

      Using a screwdriver, remove the inboard joint boot clamp as shown in the illustration.

    2. Claw engagement type:

      Using needle nose pliers, remove the inboard joint boot clamp as shown in the illustration.

      Fig 2: Removing Front Axle Inboard Joint Boot Clamp
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  3. REMOVE FRONT DRIVE SHAFT INBOARD JOINT BOOT 
    1. Disconnect the inboard joint boot from the inboard joint.
  4. REMOVE FRONT DRIVE INBOARD JOINT ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the old grease from the inboard joint.
    2. Put matchmarks on the inboard joint and outboard joint shaft.
      NOTE: Do not use a punch for marking.
    3. Remove the inboard joint from the outboard joint shaft.
      Fig 3: Identifying Matching Marks On Inboard Joint And Outboard Joint Shaft
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    4. Using a snap ring expander, remove the inner shaft snap ring.
      Fig 4: Removing Inner Shaft Snap Ring
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    5. Put matchmarks on the outboard joint shaft and tripod joint.
      NOTE: Do not use a punch for marking.
    6. Using a brass bar and hammer, tap out the tripod joint from the outboard joint shaft.
      NOTE: Do not tap the roller.
    7. Remove the No. 2 inboard joint boot clamp, inboard joint boot and inboard joint boot clamp.
      Fig 5: Putting Matchmarks On Outboard Joint Shaft And Tripod Joint
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  5. REMOVE DRIVE SHAFT DAMPER SETTING CLAMP (RH DRIVE SHAFT) 
    1. One touch clamp type:

      Using a screwdriver, remove the damper setting clamp as shown in the illustration.

    2. Claw engagement type:

      Using needle nose pliers, remove the damper setting clamp as shown in the illustration.

  6. REMOVE FRONT DRIVE SHAFT DAMPER RH (RH DRIVE SHAFT) 
    1. Remove the drive shaft damper RH.
      Fig 6: Removing Damper Setting Clamp
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  7. REMOVE FRONT NO. 2 AXLE OUTBOARD JOINT BOOT CLAMP 
    1. Using a screwdriver, remove the outboard joint boot clamp.
      Fig 7: Removing Front No. 2 Axle Outboard Joint Boot Clamp
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  8. REMOVE FRONT AXLE OUTBOARD JOINT BOOT CLAMP 
    1. Using a screwdriver, remove the outboard joint boot clamp.
      Fig 8: Removing Front Axle Outboard Joint Boot Clamp
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  9. REMOVE FRONT AXLE OUTBOARD JOINT BOOT 
    1. Remove the outboard joint boot from the outboard joint shaft.
    2. Remove the old grease from the outboard joint.
  10. REMOVE FRONT DRIVE INBOARD JOINT HOLE SNAP RING 
    1. Using a screwdriver, pry off the hole snap ring.
      Fig 9: Removing Front Drive Inboard Joint Hole Snap Ring
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  11. REMOVE FRONT DRIVE SHAFT DUST COVER 
    1. Using SST and a press, press out the dust cover.

      SST 09950-00020 

      NOTE: Be careful not to drop the inboard joint assembly.
      Fig 10: Removing Front Drive Shaft Dust Cover
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
