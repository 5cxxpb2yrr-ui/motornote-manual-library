FRONT SUSPENSION LOWER NO. 1 ARM (for Manual Transaxle): Removal

HINT:

Remove the suspension lower arm RH (Manual Transaxle) following the same procedures as the suspension lower arm LH (Automatic Transaxle).

  1. REMOVE FRONT WHEEL 
  2. DISCONNECT FRONT SUSPENSION NO. 1 LOWER ARM SUB-ASSEMBLY LH (See  FRONT SUSPENSION LOWER NO. 1 ARM (FOR AUTOMATIC TRANSAXLE)   ) 
  3. DISCONNECT FRONT STABILIZER BAR (See  FRONT SUSPENSION LOWER NO. 1 ARM (FOR AUTOMATIC TRANSAXLE)   ) 
  4. REMOVE FRONT SUSPENSION NO. 1 LOWER ARM SUB-ASSEMBLY LH 
    1. Remove the 2 bolts, nut and lower arm.
      NOTE: Do not turn the nut.
