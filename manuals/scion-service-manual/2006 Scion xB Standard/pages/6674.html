FRONT SUSPENSION LOWER NO. 1 ARM (for Automatic Transaxle): Removal

HINT:

The removal procedures on the front suspension No. 1 lower arm sub-assembly can be performed without removing the power steering link and front suspension crossmember.

  1. REMOVE FRONT WHEEL 
  2. REMOVE HOOD SUB-ASSEMBLY 
  3. SUSPEND ENGINE ASSEMBLY 
  4. DISCONNECT FRONT SUSPENSION NO. 1 LOWER ARM SUB-ASSEMBLY LH 
    1. Remove the clip and nut.
    2. Using SST, disconnect the lower arm from the steering knuckle.

      SST 09628-00011 

      Fig 1: Disconnecting Lower Arm From Steering Knuckle
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  5. DISCONNECT FRONT SUSPENSION NO. 1 LOWER ARM SUB-ASSEMBLY RH 

    HINT:

    Use the same procedures described for the LH side.

  6. DISCONNECT FRONT STABILIZER BAR 
    1. While holding the front stabilizer bolt with the spanner, remove the nut.
    2. Remove the 2 retainers and cushion, and then remove the front stabilizer bolt.
    3. Perform the procedure above on the other side.
      Fig 2: Removing Front Stabilizer Bolt
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  7. DISCONNECT POWER STEERING LINK ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the 4 bolts and 4 nuts, and disconnect the steering link.

      HINT:

      Perform this procedure with the steering link suspended.

      Fig 3: Disconnecting Power Steering Link Assembly
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  8. DISCONNECT PRESSURE FEED TUBE ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the bolt and disconnect the pressure feed tube.
  9. REMOVE FRONT SUSPENSION MEMBER REINFORCEMENT LH 
    1. Remove the 2 bolts and reinforcement.
  10. REMOVE FRONT SUSPENSION MEMBER REINFORCEMENT RH 
    1. Remove the 2 bolts and reinforcement.
      Fig 4: Removing Pressure Feed Tube Bolt
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  11. DISCONNECT FRONT SUSPENSION CROSSMEMBER SUB-ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the bolt and 2 nuts.
      Fig 5: Removing Front Suspension Crossmember Sub-Assembly Nuts
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. Support the crossmember with a jack.
    3. Remove the 4 bolts and disconnect the crossmember.
      Fig 6: Removing Crossmember Bolts
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  12. REMOVE FRONT SUSPENSION NO. 1 LOWER ARM SUB-ASSEMBLY LH 
    1. Remove the 2 bolts, nut and lower arm.
      NOTE: Do not turn the nut.
      Fig 7: Removing Bolts, Nut And Lower Arm
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
