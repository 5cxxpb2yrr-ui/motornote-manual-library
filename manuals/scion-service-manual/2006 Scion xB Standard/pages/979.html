Rear Wiper Rubber: Installation

  1. INSTALL REAR WIPER RUBBER 
    1. Install the 2 wiper rubber backing plates to the wiper rubber.
      NOTE: Be sure to install the wiper rubber backing plates in the right direction.
      Fig 1: Identifying Rear Wiper Rubber
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. Insert the wiper rubber from the claw position of the middle of the wiper blade into the end position.
      Fig 2: Installing Rear Wiper Rubber
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    3. After passing the wiper rubber through the end side claw, allow it to stick out from the rear end stopper and slide it through the front tip side claw.
  2. INSTALL REAR WIPER BLADE 
    1. Attach the rear wiper blade to the wiper.

      HINT:

      Install the wiper blade so that a click sound is heard.

      Fig 3: Installing Rear Wiper Rubber
