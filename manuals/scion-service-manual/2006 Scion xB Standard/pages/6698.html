Diagnosis For Irregular Tire Wear

  1. CHECK TIRE PRESSURE 

    NG: Go to step   3 

    OK: Go to Next Step 

  2. CHECK WHEEL ALIGNMENT 

    NG: Go to step   5 

    OK: ROTATE TIRES OR REPLACE TIRE(S) 

  3. ADJUST TIRE PRESSURE 

    Go to Next Step 

  4. CHECK WHEEL ALIGNMENT 

    NG: Go to step 5 

    OK: ROTATE TIRES OR REPLACE TIRE(S) 

  5. PERFORM ALIGNMENT PROCEDURE 

    NEXT: ROTATE TIRES OR REPLACE TIRE(S)
