Heater Radiator Assembly: Inspection

  1. INSPECT NO. 2 COOLER WIRING 
    1. Measure the resistance of the switch.

      Standard resistance 

      MAX HOT switch 

      TESTER CONNECTION AND SPECIFIED CONDITION CHART

      Tester Connection Switch Condition Specified Condition
      2 - 3 ON Below 1 Ω
      2 - 3 OFF 10 kΩ or higher

      Foot mode switch 

      TESTER CONNECTION AND SPECIFIED CONDITION CHART

      Tester Connection Switch Condition Specified Condition
      Front Defroster Switch 1 - 3 ON Below 1 Ω
      1 - 3 OFF 10 kΩ or higher
      Foot Mode Switch 1 - 3 ON Below 1 Ω
      1 - 3 OFF 10 kΩ or higher

      If the result is not as specified, replace the No. 2 cooler wiring.
