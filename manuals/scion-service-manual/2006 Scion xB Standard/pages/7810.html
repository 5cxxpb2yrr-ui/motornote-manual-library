Air Inlet Control Servo Motor: Removal

  1. REMOVE INSTRUMENT PANEL SUB-ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the instrument panel (see REMOVAL ).
  2. REMOVE LOWER INSTRUMENT PANEL ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the instrument panel (see REMOVAL ).
  3. REMOVE DAMPER SERVO SUB-ASSEMBLY 
    1. Disconnect the connector.
    2. Remove the 3 screws and damper servo.
