Compressor And Pulley: Removal

  1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL 
    CAUTION: Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to prevent airbag and seat belt pretensioner activation.
  2. DISCHARGE REFRIGERANT FROM REFRIGERATION SYSTEM (See  REPLACEMENT   ) 
  3. REMOVE FAN AND GENERATOR V BELT (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  4. DISCONNECT NO. 1 COOLER REFRIGERANT DISCHARGE HOSE 
    1. Remove the bolt and disconnect the cooler refrigerant discharge hose from the compressor.
    2. Remove the O-ring from the cooler refrigerant discharge hose.
      NOTE: Seal the openings of the disconnected parts using vinyl tape to prevent moisture and foreign matter from entering.
      Fig 1: Removing O-Ring From Cooler Refrigerant Discharge Hose

  5. DISCONNECT SUCTION HOSE SUB-ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the bolt and disconnect the suction hose from the compressor.
    2. Remove the O-ring from the suction hose.
      NOTE: Seal the openings of the disconnected parts using vinyl tape to prevent moisture and foreign matter from entering.
  6. REMOVE ENGINE UNDER COVER RH

  7. REMOVE COMPRESSOR WITH PULLEY ASSEMBLY 
    1. Disconnect the connector.
    2. Remove the 4 bolts and compressor.
