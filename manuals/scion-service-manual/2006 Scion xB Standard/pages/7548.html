Rear Seat Inner Belt Assembly: Removal

  1. REMOVE REAR SEAT CUSHION WITH COVER PAD SUB-ASSEMBLY (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  2. REMOVE REAR SEAT INNER BELT ASSEMBLY LH 
    1. Remove the bolt and inner belt.
  3. REMOVE REAR SEAT INNER BELT ASSEMBLY RH 
    1. Remove the bolt and inner belt.
  4. REMOVE REAR SEATBACK ASSEMBLY LH (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  5. REMOVE REAR SEATBACK ASSEMBLY RH (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  6. REMOVE CHILD RESTRAINT SEAT TETHER ANCHOR BRACKET SUB-ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the 4 bolts and anchor bracket.
