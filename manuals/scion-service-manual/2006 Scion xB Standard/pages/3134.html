Typical Malfunction Thresholds

All: 

TYPICAL MALFUNCTION THRESHOLDS - ALL

EVAP purge-cut Executing

Fuel trim Lean: 

TYPICAL MALFUNCTION THRESHOLDS - FUEL TRIM LEAN

Average fuel tleftearning value 35% or more (varies with engine coolant temperature [ECT])

Fuel trim Rich: 

TYPICAL MALFUNCTION THRESHOLDS - FUEL TRIM RICH

Average fuel trim learning value -35% or less (varies with ECT)
