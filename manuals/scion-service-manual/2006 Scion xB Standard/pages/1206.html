Inspection Procedure

  1. RECONFIRM DTC 
    1. Clear the DTC (see DTC CHECK / CLEAR  ).
    2. Turn the ignition switch ON.
    3. Drive the vehicle at a speed of 15 km/h (9 mph) and turn the steering wheel to the right and left.
    4. Check for DTC (see DTC CHECK / CLEAR  ). If a DTC of the ABS and VSC is output, make a note of them.
