Front Brake Pad, Brake Caliper & Brake Rotor: Disassembly

HINT:

  • Use the same procedures for the LH side and RH side.
  • The procedures listed below are for the LH side.
  1. REMOVE CYLINDER BOOT 
    1. Using a screwdriver, remove the set ring and cylinder boot.
      NOTE: Be careful not to damage the brake piston and cylinder.
      Fig 1: Removing Set Ring And Cylinder Boot
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  2. REMOVE FRONT DISC BRAKE PISTON 
    1. Place a cloth between the brake piston and brake cylinder.
    2. Use compressed air to remove the piston from the cylinder.
      CAUTION: Do not place your fingers in front of the piston when using compressed air.
      NOTE: Be careful not to spatter the brake fluid.
      Fig 2: Removing Piston From Cylinder
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  3. REMOVE PISTON SEAL 
    1. Using a screwdriver, pry out the piston seal from the brake cylinder.
      NOTE: Be careful not to damage the inner cylinder and cylinder groove.
  4. REMOVE FRONT DISC BRAKE BLEEDER PLUG 
    1. Remove the bleeder plug from the brake cylinder.
