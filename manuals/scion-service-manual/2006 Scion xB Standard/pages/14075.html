Introduction

Some 2004 - 2006 model year Scion xA and xB vehicles may exhibit asquealing noise coming from the engine compartment during vehicle operation. The generator drive belt tension specification has been updated to correct this condition. Please use the following repair procedure to address customer concerns.

Applicable Vehicles

  • 2004 - 2006  model year Scion xA  and xB  vehicles.

Repair Procedure

  1. Verify that the noise is coming from the generator V-belt.
    NOTE: Replace the generator V-belt if there is any sign of fraying, scoring, or glazing on the belt.
  2. Remove and replace the generator V-belt.

    Updated generator V-belt specification:

    V-BELT SPECIFICATION

    METHOD SPECIFICATION CONDITION
    Belt Tension Gauge 455 - 545 N (102-123 lbf) Belt tension should be adjusted after the engine is at operating temperature with no less than 20 minutes of run time on the NEW belt
    Belt Deflection (w/o gauge) 10.5 - 11.5 mm (0.41 - 0.45 in.) Using a Pressing Force of 98 N (22 lbf)
