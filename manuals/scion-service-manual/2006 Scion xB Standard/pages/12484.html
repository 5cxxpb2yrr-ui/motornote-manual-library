Drive Belt: Installation

  1. INSTALL VANE PUMP V BELT 
    1. Temporarily install the belt on each pulley.
  2. INSTALL GENERATOR V BELT 
    1. Temporarily install the belt on each pulley.
  3. ADJUST GENERATOR V BELT 
    1. Insert the adjusting bar between the engine RH mount bracket and generator. Push the bar towards the vehicle front to adjust the belt tension.
      Fig 1: Adjusting Generator V Belt
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
      NOTE: Do not insert the bar between the oil control valve and generator. It could damage the oil control valve.
    2. First tighten bolt A, and then tighten bolt B.
      Fig 2: Tightening Bolts
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

      Torque: 18.5 N*m (189 kgf*cm, 14 ft.*lbf) for bolt A 

      54 N*m (550 kgf*cm, 40 ft.*lbf) for bolt B 

  4. ADJUST VANE PUMP V BELT 
    1. Using the bar, adjust the belt tension.
    2. First tighten bolt B, and then tighten bolt A.
      Fig 3: Tightening Bolts
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

      Torque: 44 N*m (449 kgf*cm, 33 ft.*lbf) 

  5. CHECK DRIVE BELT TENSION (See  ON-VEHICLE INSPECTION   ) 
  6. INSTALL ENGINE UNDER COVER RH
