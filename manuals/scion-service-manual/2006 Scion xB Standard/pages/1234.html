Brake Actuator: Installation

  1. INSTALL BRAKE ACTUATOR ASSEMBLY 
    1. Install the brake actuator to the bracket with the 2 nuts.

      Torque: 5.4 N*m (55 kgf*cm, 48 in.*lbf) 

  2. INSTALL BRAKE ACTUATOR ASSEMBLY WITH BRACKET 
    1. Install the brake actuator with bracket with the 3 bolts.

      Torque: 19 N*m (194 kgf*cm, 14 ft.*lbf) 

      Fig 1: Installing Brake Actuator With Bracket With Bolts
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. Using SST, connect the 6 brake lines to the correct positions of the brake actuator, as shown in the illustration.

      SST 09023-00101 

      Torque: 15 N*m (155 kgf*cm, 11 ft.*lbf) without SST 

      14 N*m (143 kgf*cm, 10 ft.*lbf) with SST 

      HINT:

      Use a torque wrench with a fulcrum length of 30 cm (11.81 in.).

    3. Connect the brake actuator connector.
  3. FILL RESERVOIR WITH BRAKE FLUID (See  BLEEDING  ) 
  4. BLEED AIR FROM BRAKE MASTER CYLINDER (See  BLEEDING  ) 
  5. BLEED AIR FROM BRAKE LINE (See  BLEEDING  ) 
  6. BLEED AIR FROM BRAKE ACTUATOR (w/VSC) (See  BLEEDING  ) 
  7. CHECK BRAKE FLUID LEVEL IN RESERVOIR (See  BLEEDING  ) 
  8. CHECK FOR BRAKE FLUID LEAKAGE 
  9. CONNECT CABLE TO NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL 
    Fig 2: Connecting Brake Lines To Correct Positions Of Brake Actuator

    1. Check the brake actuator with the intelligent tester (see BRAKE ACTUATOR  ).
