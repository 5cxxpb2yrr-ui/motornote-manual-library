Rear Seat Outer Belt Assembly: Removal

HINT:

  • Use the same procedures for the RH side and LH side.
  • The procedures listed below are for the RH side.
  1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL 
    CAUTION: Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to prevent airbag and seat belt pretensioner activation.
  2. REMOVE REAR DOOR SCUFF PLATE RH (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  3. REMOVE REAR DOOR OPENING TRIM WEATHERSTRIP RH 
  4. REMOVE NO. 1 DECK BOARD 
  5. REMOVE NO. 2 DECK BOARD 
  6. REMOVE DECK FLOOR BOX RH 
  7. REMOVE DECK FLOOR BOX LH 
  8. REMOVE SPARE WHEEL COVER ASSEMBLY 
  9. REMOVE REAR SEAT CUSHION WITH COVER PAD SUB-ASSEMBLY (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  10. REMOVE REAR SEATBACK ASSEMBLY LH (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  11. REMOVE REAR SEATBACK ASSEMBLY RH (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  12. REMOVE BACK DOOR WEATHERSTRIP 
  13. REMOVE REAR DECK TRIM COVER (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  14. REMOVE DECK TRIM SIDE PANEL ASSEMBLY RH (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  15. REMOVE ROOF SIDE GARNISH INNER RH (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  16. REMOVE REAR SEAT 3 POINT TYPE BELT ASSEMBLY RH 
    1. Remove the 2 bolts and outer belt.
  17. REMOVE REAR SEAT BELT ASSEMBLY OUTER CENTER 

    HINT:

    RH side only.

    1. Remove the rear seat headrest assembly (see REMOVAL ).
    2. Remove the rear seatback lock release button (see DISASSEMBLY ).
    3. Remove the 2 tether anchor covers (see DISASSEMBLY ).
    4. Remove the 4 rear seat headrest supports (see DISASSEMBLY ).
    5. Remove the rear seatback stop button grommet (see DISASSEMBLY ).
    6. Remove the rear seatback cover (with pad) RH (see DISASSEMBLY ).
    7. Remove the nut on the retractor side and completely remove the belt.
