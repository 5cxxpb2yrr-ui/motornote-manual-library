Front Wiper Rubber: Removal

HINT:

  • Use the same procedures for the RH side and LH side.
  • The procedures listed below are for the LH side.
  1. REMOVE FRONT WIPER BLADE LH 
    1. Remove the wiper blade from the wiper arm.
      NOTE: Do not fold down the front wiper arm with the front wiper blade being removed from it.
  2. REMOVE WIPER RUBBER LH 
    1. Remove the wiper rubber from the wiper blade.
    2. Remove the 2 wiper rubber backing plates from the wiper rubber.
