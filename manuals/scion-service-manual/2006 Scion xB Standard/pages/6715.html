Clutch Release Cylinder: Disassembly

  1. REMOVE CLUTCH RELEASE UNION 
    1. Remove the union bolt, union and 2 gaskets.
  2. REMOVE CLUTCH RELEASE CYLINDER KIT 
    1. Remove the boot from the cylinder body.
    2. Remove the push rod from the cylinder body.
    3. Remove the piston from the cylinder body.
      NOTE: Be careful not to damage the inside of the cylinder body.
    4. Remove the spring from the cylinder body.
    5. Remove the bleeder plug cap from the bleeder plug.
  3. REMOVE RELEASE CYLINDER BLEEDER PLUG
