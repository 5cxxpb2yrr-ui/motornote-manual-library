Inspection Procedure

  1. CHECK CAN BUS LINE FOR SHORT (DLC3 SUB BUS LINE) 
    1. Disconnect the J5 junction connector.
    2. Measure the resistance of the DLC3.

      Standard resistance 

      TESTER CONNECTION SPECIFIED CONDITION

      Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
      D1-6 (CANH) - D1-14 (CANL) Ignition switch OFF 1 MΩ or higher

    NG : REPAIR OR REPLACE DLC3 SUB BUS LINE AND CONNECTOR (CANH, CANL) 

    Fig 1: Identifying DL3C Connector Terminals
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    OK : Go to Next Step 

  2. CHECK CAN BUS LINE FOR SHORT (ECM) 
    1. Reconnect the J5 junction connector.
    2. Disconnect the E4 ECM connector.
    3. Measure the resistance of the DLC3.

      Standard resistance 

      TESTER CONNECTION SPECIFIED CONDITION

      Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
      D1-6 (CANH) - D1-14 (CANL) Ignition switch OFF 1 MΩ or higher

    OK : REPLACE ECM 

    Fig 2: Identifying ECM Connector Terminals
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    NG : Go to Next Step 

  3. CHECK CAN BUS LINE FOR SHORT (A/C AMPLIFIER) 
    1. Reconnect the E4 ECM connector.
    2. Disconnect the A17 amplifier connector.
    3. Measure the resistance of the DLC3.

      Standard resistance 

      TESTER CONNECTION SPECIFIED CONDITION

      Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
      D1-6 (CANH) - D1-14 (CANL) Ignition switch OFF 1 MΩ or higher

    OK : REPLACE A/C AMPLIFIER 

    Fig 3: Identifying E4 ECM A/C Amplifier Connector
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    NG : Go to Next Step 

  4. CHECK CAN BUS LINE FOR SHORT (SKID CONTROL ECU)

      Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition

    OK : REPLACE BRAKE ACTUATOR ASSEMBLY

  7. CHECK CAN BUS LINE FOR SHORT (ECM - JUNCTION CONNECTOR) 
    1. Reconnect the Y1 sensor connector.
    2. Disconnect the J5 connector from the junction block.
    3. Disconnect the E4 ECM connector.
    4. Measure the resistance of the wire harness side connector.

      Standard resistance 

      TESTER CONNECTION SPECIFIED CONDITION

      Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
      E4-33 (CANH) - E4-34 (CANL) Ignition switch OFF 1 MΩ or higher

    NG : REPAIR OR REPLACE CAN MAIN BUS LINE AND CONNECTOR (ECM - JUNCTION CONNECTOR) 

    Fig 7: Identifying Y1 ECM Sensor Connector Terminals
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
