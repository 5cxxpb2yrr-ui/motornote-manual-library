Traction Control Disable

WARNING: Placing a non-compatible vehicle on a single-axle dynamometer could result in a safety hazard to technicians and damage to vehicle. Vehicles which use All-Wheel Drive (AWD) or traction control may not be clearly marked. Use common sense and take all necessary precautions when placing any vehicle on the dynamometer. Determine between full-time 4WD and All-Wheel Drive (AWD) for testing purposes. The following tables include information related to full-time 4WD and AWD vehicles, and how to disable the traction control system (if disengageable).
WARNING: DO NOT operate vehicle on a 2-wheel dynamometer if the DO NOT TEST column is marked.

(1) Not equipped with Traction Control system.

(3) To disable system, push TCS switch, located on lower dash, right of steering wheel. The TRAC-OFF indicator light will illuminate when system is disabled.
(4) To disable system, push TCS or VSC switch, located on instrument panel to the left of steering wheel. The TRAC-OFF indicator light will illuminate when system is disabled.
(5) To turn-off both TRAC and VSC systems, press and HOLD button for more than 3 seconds (while vehicle is stopped). A message will be shown on the multi-information display and VSC off indicator light will come on. Pushing the button briefly turns off ONLY the TRAC system. When only the TRAC system is turned off, the TRAC system will turn back on when vehicle speed increases. However, when both TRAC and VSC systems are turned off, the systems will not turn on even when vehicle speed increases.
