Cooling (1NZ-FE): Radiator: Removal

  1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL 
    CAUTION: Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to prevent airbag and seat belt pretensioner activation.
  2. DRAIN ENGINE  COOLANT  
  3. DISCONNECT RADIATOR HOSE INLET 
  4. DISCONNECT RADIATOR HOSE OUTLET 
  5. DISCONNECT OIL COOLER INLET HOSE (for Automatic Transaxle) 
  6. DISCONNECT OIL COOLER OUTLET HOSE (for Automatic Transaxle) 
  7. REMOVE RADIATOR GRILLE (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  8. REMOVE FRONT FENDER LINER LH (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  9. REMOVE FRONT FENDER LINER RH (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  10. REMOVE FRONT BUMPER COVER (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  11. REMOVE HOOD LOCK ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the 3 bolts, clip and hood lock.
      Fig 1: Removing Hood Lock
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  12. REMOVE HOOD LOCK SUPPORT BRACE 
    1. Remove the 5 bolts and support brace.
      Fig 2: Removing Hood Lock Support Brace
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  13. REMOVE RADIATOR SUPPORT OPENING COVER 
    1. Remove the 2 clips and support opening cover.
      Fig 3: Removing Radiator Support Opening Cover
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  14. REMOVE RADIATOR SUPPORT SUB-ASSEMBLY UPPER 
    1. Disconnect the connector from the horn.
    2. Remove the 2 bolts and disconnect the condenser from the radiator support upper.
    3. Remove the 4 bolts and radiator support upper.
      Fig 4: Removing Radiator Support Sub-Assembly Upper
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  15. REMOVE RADIATOR ASSEMBLY 
    1. Disconnect the connector from the cooling fan motor and remove the radiator from the engine compartment.
    2. Remove the 3 bolts and detach the fan shroud with fan.
      Fig 5: Removing Radiator Assembly
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    3. Remove the 2 bolts and water filler.
    4. Remove the 2 radiator support cushions and radiator support lower.
      Fig 6: Removing Water Filler
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
