Radar Sensor Check Procedure

  1. ARM the system. 

    Press the LOCK button on the remote control or lock the doors with the key.

    (Remember, the system will not ARM if any door is open.)

    Fig 1: Identifying LOCK Button
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  2. Wait until the STATUS Monitor's LED starts flashing. 

    The LED will flash 30 seconds after the last door or hatch is closed.

    Fig 2: Identifying STATUS Monitor's LED
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  3. Move inside the vehicle. 

    When movement occurs inside the vehicle, an alarm will be triggered immediately. If sensor detects 2nd movement within 60 seconds after the 1st warn away alarm trigger, then a regular ALARM activates for 60 seconds.

    NOTE: If you find that the system does not perform as expected, contact your Toyota dealer for more information.
    Fig 3: Moving Inside Vehicle
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  4. DISARM the system to stop the ALARM. 

    Press the UNLOCK button on the remote control or use the key to DISARM the system.

    Fig 4: Identifying UNLOCK Button
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
