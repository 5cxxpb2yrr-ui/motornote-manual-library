Rear Wiper Rubber: Removal

  1. REMOVE REAR WIPER BLADE 
    1. Raise the wiper blade to the position as where the claw detach with a click sound, as shown in the illustration.
      NOTE: Be careful not to break the claw.
    2. Pull the rear wiper blade straight from the wiper arm toward the left side of the vehicle.
      NOTE: Do not fold the rear wiper arm with the rear wiper blade having been removed from it.
      Fig 1: Removing Rear Wiper Blade
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  2. REMOVE REAR WIPER RUBBER 
    1. Pull on the end of the wiper rubber protruding from the blade stopper as shown in the illustration.
      NOTE: Do not forcibly pull out the wiper rubber as, the backing plates will be deformed or the blade claw will be damaged.

      HINT:

      Pushing the rubber from the ends towards the middle will allow it to be removed more easily.
