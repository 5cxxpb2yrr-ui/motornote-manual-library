Inspection Procedure

  1. CHOOSE DIAGNOSIS METHOD 
    1. Choose the diagnosis method.

      Method 

      METHOD REFERENCE

      Method Proceed to
      Using intelligent tester A
      Not using intelligent tester B

    B: Go to step   4 

    A: Go to next step 

  2. READ VALUE OF INTELLIGENT TESTER (REAR SPEED SENSOR) 
    1. Check the DATA LIST for proper functioning of the rear speed sensor.

      Skid control ECU with actuator 

      MEASUREMENT ITEM DIAGNOSTIC CHART

      Item Measurement Item / Range (Display) Normal Condition Diagnostic Note
      WHEEL SPD RR Wheel speed sensor (RR) reading / min.: 0 km/h (0 mph), max.: 326 km/h (202 mph) Actual wheel speed Speed similar to that of speedometer
      WHEEL SPD RL Wheel speed sensor (RL) reading / min.: 0 km/h (0 mph), max.: 326 km/h (202 mph) Actual wheel speed Speed similar to that of speedometer

      OK: There is almost no difference between actual wheel speed and displayed speed value. 

    NG: Go to step   4 

    OK: Go to Next Step 

  3. CHECK IF DTC OUTPUT RECURS 
    1. Clear the DTCs (see DTC CHECK / CLEAR  ).
    2. Drive the vehicle at approximately 32 km/h (20 mph) or more for 60 seconds or more.
    3. Check for DTCs (see DTC CHECK / CLEAR  ).

      Result 

      RESULT REFERENCE

      Result Proceed to
      DTC is output A
      DTC is not output B

      HINT:

      When replacing the skid control ECU with actuator, perform the zero point calibration (see CALIBRATION  ).

    B: END 

    A: REPLACE BRAKE ACTUATOR ASSEMBLY 

  4. INSPECT REAR SPEED SENSOR 
    1. Disconnect the A15 and A16 sensor connectors.
    2. Measure the resistance of the speed sensors.

      Standard resistance: 

      LH 

      TESTER CONNECTION SPECIFIED CONDITION

      Tester Connection Specified Condition
      1 (RL-) - 2 (FL+) 1.0 to 1.4 kΩ at 20°C
      1 (RL-) - Body ground 10 kΩ or higher
      2 (RL+) - Body ground

      RH 

      TESTER CONNECTION SPECIFIED CONDITION

      Tester Connection Specified Condition
      1 (RR-) - 2 (RR+) 1.0 to 1.4 kΩ at 20°C
      1 (RR-) - Body ground 10 kΩ or higher
      2 (RR+) - Body ground

      HINT:

      Check the speed sensor signal after the speed sensor replacement (see HOW TO PROCEED WITH TROUBLESHOOTING  ).

    NG: REPLACE REAR SPEED SENSOR 

    OK: Go to Next Step 

  5. CHECK WIRE HARNESS (SKID CONTROL ECU - REAR SPEED SENSOR) 
    1. Disconnect the S3 ECU connector.
    2. Disconnect the A15 and A16 speed sensor connectors.
    3. Measure the resistance of the wire harness side connectors.

      Standard resistance: 

      LH 

      TESTER CONNECTION SPECIFIED CONDITION

      Tester Connection Specified Condition
      S3-20 (RL+) - A15-2 Below 1 Ω
      S3-6 (RL-) - A15-1
      A15-2 - Body ground 10 kΩ or higher
      A15-1 - Body ground

      RH 

      TESTER CONNECTION SPECIFIED CONDITION

      Tester Connection Specified Condition
      S3-5 (RR+) - A16-2 Below 1 Ω
      S3-19 (RR-) - A16-1
      A16-2 - Body ground 10 kΩ or higher
      A16-1 - Body ground

    NG: REPAIR OR REPLACE HARNESS AND CONNECTOR 

    Fig 2: Checking Wire Harness
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    OK: Go to Next Step 

  6. CHECK REAR SPEED SENSOR (INSTALLATION) 
    1. Check the sensor installation.

      OK: There is no clearance between the sensor and rear axle carrier. 

      HINT:

      Check the speed sensor signal after replacement (see HOW TO PROCEED WITH TROUBLESHOOTING  )

    NG: REPLACE REAR SPEED SENSOR 

    Fig 3: Checking Rear Speed Sensor
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    OK: Go to Next Step 

  7. INSPECT SPEED SENSOR (TIP) 
    1. Remove the rear speed sensor.
    2. Check the sensor tip.

      OK: No scratches or foreign objects on the sensor tip. 

      HINT:

      Check the speed sensor signal after replacement (see HOW TO PROCEED WITH TROUBLESHOOTING  )

    NG: CLEAR OR REPAIR SPEED SENSOR 

    OK: Go to Next Step 

  8. INSPECT REAR AXLE HUB AND BEARING ASSEMBLY (SENSOR ROTOR) 
    1. Remove the axle hub and disconnect the rear speed sensor.

      OK: No scratches, missing teeth or foreign objects. 

      HINT:

      Check the speed sensor signal after replacement (see HOW TO PROCEED WITH TROUBLESHOOTING  )

    NG: REPLACE REAR AXLE HUB AND BEARING ASSEMBLY 

    Fig 4: Inspecting Rear Axle Hub And Bearing Assembly
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    OK: Go to Next Step 

  9. CHECK IF DTC OUTPUT RECURS 
    1. Clear the DTCs (see DTC CHECK / CLEAR  ).
    2. Drive the vehicle at approximately 30 km/h (19 mph) or more for 60 seconds or more.
    3. Check for DTCs (see DTC CHECK / CLEAR  ).

      Result 

      RESULT REFERENCE

      Result Proceed to
      DTC is output A
      DTC is not output B

      HINT:

      When replacing the skid control ECU with actuator, perform the zero point calibration (see CALIBRATION  ).

    B: END 

    A: REPLACE BRAKE ACTUATOR ASSEMBLY
