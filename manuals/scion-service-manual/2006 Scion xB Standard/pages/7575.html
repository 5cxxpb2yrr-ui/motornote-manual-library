Front Bumper: Removal

  1. REMOVE RADIATOR GRILLE 
    1. Using a clip remover, remove the 3 clips and radiator grille.
  2. REMOVE FRONT FENDER LINER LH 
    1. Remove the 2 screws.
    2. Partially remove the fender liner.

      HINT:

      It is not necessary to fully remove the fender liner. Partially remove it so that the bumper cover can be removed in a later step.

      Fig 1: Removing Radiator Grille

  3. REMOVE FRONT FENDER LINER RH 

    HINT:

    Use the same procedures for the RH side and LH side.

  4. REMOVE FRONT BUMPER COVER 
    1. Put protective tape under the front fender.
    2. Remove the clip and 2 bolts from the upper side of the bumper cover.
    3. Using a screwdriver, detach the 2 claws and remove the 2 bolts, 6 screws and bumper cover.

      HINT:

      Tape the screwdriver tip before use.

  5. REMOVE FRONT BUMPER REINFORCEMENT 
    1. Remove the 4 nuts and reinforcement.
