Rear Coil Spring: Removal

  1. REMOVE REAR WHEEL 
  2. DISCONNECT SKID CONTROL SENSOR WIRE 
    1. Disconnect the sensor connector.
    2. Remove the bolt and disconnect the sensor wire from the axle beam.

      HINT:

      Perform the procedures above for the other side.

      Fig 1: Removing Sensor Wire Bolt
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  3. DISCONNECT REAR NO. 4 BRAKE TUBE 
    1. Using SST, disconnect the rear brake tube.

      SST 09023-00101 

    2. Remove the clip.
      NOTE: The clip should not be reused.
  4. DISCONNECT REAR NO. 3 BRAKE TUBE 

    HINT:

    Use the same procedures described for the No. 4 tube.

    Fig 2: Disconnecting Rear Brake Tube
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  5. DISCONNECT NO. 3 PARKING BRAKE CABLE ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the nut and disconnect the parking brake cable from the axle beam.
  6. DISCONNECT NO. 2 PARKING BRAKE CABLE ASSEMBLY 

    HINT:

    Use the same procedures described for the No. 3 cable.

    Fig 3: Removing Parking Brake Cable Nut
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  7. LOOSEN REAR AXLE BEAM ASSEMBLY 
    1. Loosen the 2 bolts of the rear axle beam.
      Fig 4: Removing Rear Axle Beam Bolts
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  8. DISCONNECT REAR SHOCK ABSORBER ASSEMBLY LH 
    1. Support the right and left sides of the rear axle beam with jacks.
    2. Remove the nut and washer, and disconnect the shock absorber.
  9. DISCONNECT REAR SHOCK ABSORBER ASSEMBLY RH 

    HINT:

    Use the same procedures described for the LH side.

  10. REMOVE REAR COIL SPRING LH 
    1. Remove the coil spring, upper insulator and lower insulator by slowly lowering the jack.
      Fig 5: Removing Shock Absorber Nut
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
