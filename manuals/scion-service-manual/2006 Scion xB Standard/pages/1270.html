Rear Brake Shoe, Wheel Cylinder & Brake Drum: Disassembly

HINT:

  • Use the same procedures for the LH side and RH side.
  • The procedures listed below are for the LH side.
  1. CHECK BRAKE SHOE LINING THICKNESS 
    1. Remove the hole plug, and check the shoe lining thickness through the hole.

      Minimum thickness: 1.0 mm (0.039 in.) 

      If the lining thickness is less than the minimum, replace the brake shoe.

  2. REMOVE REAR WHEEL 
  3. DRAIN BRAKE FLUID 
    NOTE: Wash off brake fluid immediately if it comes in contact with any painted surface.
    Fig 1: Checking Shoe Lining Thickness Through Hole
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  4. REMOVE REAR BRAKE DRUM SUB-ASSEMBLY 
    1. Release the parking brake lever, and remove the brake drum.

      HINT:

      If the brake drum cannot be removed easily, perform the following procedures.

    2. Remove the pin plug, insert a screwdriver into the hole on the backing plate, and hold the automatic adjust lever away from the adjuster.
    3. Using another screwdriver, compress the brake shoe adjuster by turning the adjusting wheel.
      Fig 2: Compressing Brake Shoe Adjuster By Turning Adjusting Wheel
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  5. DISCONNECT PARKING BRAKE SHOE STRUT LH 
    1. Using SST, disconnect the shoe return spring from the front brake shoe and the rear brake shoe.

      SST 09703-30011 

      NOTE: Be careful not to damage the wheel cylinder boot.
      Fig 3: Disconnecting Shoe Return Spring From Front Brake Shoe And Rear Brake Shoe
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  6. REMOVE FRONT BRAKE SHOE 
    1. Using SST, remove the shoe hold down spring cup, shoe hold down spring and pin.

      SST 09718-00010 

    2. Remove the front brake shoe and tension spring.
  7. REMOVE PARKING BRAKE SHOE STRUT LH 
    1. Remove the parking brake shoe strut from the rear brake shoe.
      Fig 4: Removing Shoe Hold Down Spring Cup, Shoe Hold Down Spring And Pin
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  8. REMOVE REAR BRAKE AUTOMATIC ADJUST LEVER LH 
    1. Remove the adjust lever tension spring and automatic adjust lever.
      Fig 5: Identifying Adjust Lever Tension Spring And Automatic Adjust Lever
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  9. REMOVE REAR BRAKE SHOE 
    1. Using SST, remove the shoe hold down spring cup, shoe hold down spring and pin.

      SST 09718-00010 

      Fig 6: Removing Shoe Hold Down Spring Cup, Shoe Hold Down Spring And Pin
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. Using needle-nose pliers, disconnect the No. 3 parking brake cable from the parking brake shoe lever.
      Fig 7: Disconnecting No. 3 Parking Brake Cable From Parking Brake Shoe Lever
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  10. REMOVE REAR BRAKE PARKING BRAKE SHOE LEVER SUB-ASSEMBLY 
    1. Using a screwdriver, remove the C-washer and parking brake shoe lever.
      Fig 8: Removing C-Washer And Parking Brake Shoe Lever
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  11. REMOVE REAR WHEEL BRAKE CYLINDER ASSEMBLY LH 
    1. Using SST, disconnect the brake line.

      SST 09023-00101 

    2. Remove the bolt and wheel brake cylinder.
  12. REMOVE REAR WHEEL CYLINDER CUP KIT 
    1. Remove the 2 cylinder dust boots from the wheel brake cylinder.
    2. Remove the 2 pistons and compression spring.
    3. Remove the 2 wheel cylinder cups from each piston.
      Fig 9: Removing Wheel Brake Cylinder Bolt
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  13. REMOVE REAR BRAKE DRUM BLEEDER PLUG 
    1. Remove the bleeder plug from the wheel brake cylinder.
