Rear Shock Absorber: Inspection

  1. INSPECT REAR SHOCK ABSORBER ASSEMBLY LH 
    1. Compress and extend the shock absorber rod, and check that there is no abnormal resistance or unusual sound.

      If there is any abnormality, replace the shock absorber with a new one.

      NOTE: When disposing of the shock absorber, see DISPOSAL (see DISPOSAL  ).
