Oxygen Sensor/Air Fuel Ratio Sensor Heater Drive Cycle 4

Fig 1: Drive Cycle Pattern 4
Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  1. 1.Connect the Techstream to the DLC3.
  2. 2.Turn the ignition switch to ON and turn the Techstream ON.
  3. 3.Clear DTCs (even if no DTCs are stored, perform the clear DTC operation).
  4. 4.Turn the ignition switch off and wait for at least 30 seconds.
  5. 5.Turn the ignition switch to ON and turn the Techstream on [A].
  6. 6.Start the engine and idle it for 5 minutes or more [B].
  7. 7.With the vehicle stationary, depress the accelerator pedal and maintain an engine speed of 3000 rpm for 1 minute [C].
  8. 8.Idle the engine for 5 minutes or more [D].
  9. 9.Enter the following menus: Powertrain / Engine / Trouble Codes [E].
  10. 10.Read the DTCs.
    NOTE:
    • If a DTC is output, the system is malfunctioning.
    • If a DTC is not output, perform the following procedure.
  11. 11.Enter the following menus: Powertrain / Engine / Utility / All Readiness.
  12. 12.Input the DTC: P0031, P0032, P0037, P0038, P0051, P0052, P0057 or P0058.
  13. 13.Check the DTC judgment result.
    JUDGMENT RESULT

    TECHSTREAM DISPLY DESCRIPTION
    NORMAL
    • DTC judgment completed
    • System normal
    ABNORMAL
    • DTC judgment completed
    • System abnormal
    INCOMPLETE
    • DTC judgment not completed
    • Perform driving pattern after confirming DTC enabling conditions
    UNKNOWN
    • Unable to perform DTC judgment
    • Number of DTCs which do not fulfill DTC preconditions has reached ECU's memory limit
  14. 14.If the judgment result shows INCOMPLETE or UNKNOWN, perform steps [B] through [E] again.
  15. 15.If no DTC is output, perform a universal trip and check for permanent DTCs.
    NOTE:
    • If a permanent DTC is output, the system is malfunctioning.
    • If no permanent DTC is output, the system is normal.
