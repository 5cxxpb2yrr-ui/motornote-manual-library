Inspection Procedure

  1. CHECK AIR CONDITIONING AMPLIFIER ASSEMBLY (SOL+ VOLTAGE) 
    1. Remove the A/C amplifier but do not disconnect the connectors.
    2. Start the engine.
    3. Turn the A/C switch ON and blower switch ON.
    4. Using an oscilloscope, check the waveform of the amplifier.

  2. INSPECT COMPRESSOR ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the compressor.
    2. Measure the resistance of the compressor.

  3. CHECK WIRE HARNESS (COMPRESSOR - AIR CONDITIONING AMPLIFIER) 
    1. Disconnect the A2 compressor connector.
    2. Disconnect the A17 amplifier connector.
    3. Measure the resistance of the wire harness side connectors.
