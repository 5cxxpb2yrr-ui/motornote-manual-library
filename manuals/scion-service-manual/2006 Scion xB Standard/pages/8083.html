Typical Enabling Conditions

TYPICAL ENABLING CONDITIONS

Monitor runs whenever following DTCs not present P0100 - P0103 (MAF meter)
P0110 - P0113 (IAT sensor)
P0115 - P0118 (ECT sensor)
P0120 - P0123 (TP sensor)
P0125 (insufficient ECT for closed loop)
P0327 - P0328 (knock sensor)
P0335 (crankshaft position sensor)
P0340 (camshaft position sensor)
P0500 (VSS)
Battery voltage 8 V or more
Engine RPM 450 rpm to 6,600 rpm
Both of following 1 and 2 met -
1. Engine coolant temperature -10°C (14°F) or more
2. Either of following conditions (a) or (b) met -
(a) Engine coolant temperature at engine start More than -7°C (19°F)
(b) Engine coolant temperature More than 20°C (68°F)
TYPICAL ENABLING CONDITIONS

Throttle position learning Completed
VVT system Not operated by scan tool
Fuel cut OFF

Monitor period of emission-related misfire: 

TYPICAL ENABLING CONDITIONS - MONITOR PERIOD OF EMISSION-RELATED MISFIRE

First 1,000 revolutions after engine start, or Check Mode Crankshaft 1,000 revolutions
Except above Crankshaft 1,000 revolutions x 4

Monitor period of emission-related misfire: 

TYPICAL ENABLING CONDITIONS - MONITOR PERIOD OF EMISSION-RELATED MISFIRE

Except above Crankshaft 200 revolutions x 3
All of following conditions 1, 2 and 3 are met Crankshaft 200 revolutions
1. Driving cycles 1st
2. Check Mode OFF
3. RPM Less that 3,800 revolutions
