Inspection Procedure

  1. CHOOSE DIAGNOSIS METHOD 
    1. Choose the diagnosis method.

      Method 

      METHOD REFERENCE

      Method Proceed to
      Using intelligent tester A
      Not using intelligent tester B

    B: Go to step   4 

    A: Go to next step 

  2. READ VALUE OF INTELLIGENT TESTER (FRONT SPEED SENSOR) 
    1. Check the DATA LIST for proper functioning of the front speed sensor.

      Skid control ECU with actuator 

      MEASUREMENT ITEM DIAGNOSTIC CHART

      Item Measurement Item / Range (Display) Normal Condition Diagnostic Note
      WHEEL SPD FR Wheel speed sensor (FR) reading / min.: 0 km/h (0 mph), max.: 326 km/h (202 mph) Actual wheel speed Speed similar to that of speedometer
      WHEEL SPD FL Wheel speed sensor (FL) reading / min.: 0 km/h (0 mph), max.: 326 km/h (202 mph) Actual wheel speed Speed similar to that of speedometer

      OK: There is almost no difference between actual wheel speed and displayed speed value. 

    NG: Go to step   4 

    OK: Go to Next Step 

  3. CHECK IF DTC OUTPUT RECURS 
    1. Clear the DTCs (see DTC CHECK / CLEAR  ).
    2. Drive the vehicle at approximately 32 km/h (20 mph) or more for 60 seconds or more.
    3. Check for DTCs (see DTC CHECK / CLEAR  ).

      Result 

      RESULT REFERENCE

      Result Proceed to
      DTC is output A
      DTC is not output B

      HINT:

      When replacing the skid control ECU with actuator, perform the zero point calibration (see CALIBRATION  ).

    B: CHECK FOR INTERMITTENT PROBLEMS 

    A: REPLACE BRAKE ACTUATOR ASSEMBLY 

  4. INSPECT FRONT SPEED SENSOR 
    1. Disconnect the A4 and A5 sensor connectors.
    2. Measure the resistance of the speed sensors.

      Standard resistance: 

      LH 

      TESTER CONNECTION SPECIFIED CONDITION

      Tester Connection Specified Condition
      1 (FL+) - 2 (FL-) 0.6 to 2.5 kΩ at 40 to 120°C
      1 (FL+) - Body ground 10 kΩ or higher
      2 (FL-) - Body ground

      RH 

      TESTER CONNECTION SPECIFIED CONDITION

      Tester Connection Specified Condition
      1 (FR+) - 2 (FR-) 0.6 to 2.5 kΩ at 40 to 120°C
      1 (FR+) - Body ground 10 kΩ or higher
      2 (FR-) - Body ground
      Fig 1: Inspecting Front Speed Sensor
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

      HINT:

      • Check the speed sensor signal after the speed sensor replacement (see HOW TO PROCEED WITH TROUBLESHOOTING  ).
      • When replacing the speed sensor, clean the installation hole of the sensor.

    NG: REPLACE FRONT SPEED SENSOR 

    OK: Go to Next Step 

  5. CHECK WIRE HARNESS (SKID CONTROL ECU - FRONT SPEED SENSOR) 
    1. Disconnect the S3 ECU connector.
    2. Disconnect the A4 and A5 speed sensor connectors.
    3. Measure the resistance of the wire harness side connectors.

      Standard resistance: 

      LH 

      TESTER CONNECTION SPECIFIED CONDITION

      Tester Connection Specified Condition
      S3-18 (FL+) - A4-1 (FL+) Below 1 Ω
      S3-4 (FL-) - A4-2 (FL-)
      A4-1 (FL+) - Body ground 10 kΩ or higher
      A4-2 (FL-) - Body ground

      RH 

      TESTER CONNECTION SPECIFIED CONDITION

      Tester Connection Specified Condition
      S3-3 (FR+) - A5-1 (FR+) Below 1 Ω
      S3-17 (FR-) - A5-2 (FR-)
      A5-1 (FR+) - Body ground 10 kΩ or higher
      A5-2 (FR-) - Body ground

    NG: REPAIR OR REPLACE HARNESS AND CONNECTOR 

    Fig 2: Checking Wire Harness
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    OK: Go to Next Step 

  6. CHECK FRONT SPEED SENSOR (INSTALLATION) 
    1. Check the speed sensor installation.

      OK: Installation bolt is tightened properly. There is no clearance between sensor and front steering knuckle. 

      Torque: 8.0 N*m (82 kgf*cm, 71 in.*lbf) 

      HINT:

      • Check the speed sensor signal after the speed sensor replacement (see HOW TO PROCEED WITH TROUBLESHOOTING  ).
      • When replacing the speed sensor, clean the installation hole of the sensor.
        Fig 3: Checking Front Speed Sensor
        Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    NG: SECURELY REINSTALL FRONT SPEED SENSOR 

    OK: Go to Next Step 

  7. INSPECT SPEED SENSOR (TIP) 
    1. Remove the front speed sensor.
    2. Check the sensor tip.

      OK: No scratches or foreign matter on sensor tip. 

      HINT:

      Check the speed sensor signal after cleaning or replacing the speed sensor (see HOW TO PROCEED WITH TROUBLESHOOTING  ).

    NG: CLEAN OR REPLACE SPEED SENSOR 

    OK: Go to Next Step 

  8. INSPECT SPEED SENSOR ROTOR 
    1. Remove the front axle hub.
    2. Check the sensor rotor serrations.

      OK: No scratches, missing teeth or foreign matter on sensor rotor. 

      HINT:

      • If foreign matter is attached, remove it and check the output waveform after reassembly.
      • Check the speed sensor signal after cleaning or replacing the speed sensor rotor (see HOW TO PROCEED WITH TROUBLESHOOTING  ).
        Fig 4: Inspecting Speed Sensor Rotor
        Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    NG: CLEAN OR REPLACE SPEED SENSOR ROTOR 

    OK: Go to Next Step 

  9. CHECK IF DTC OUTPUT RECURS 
    1. Clear the DTCs (see DTC CHECK / CLEAR  ).
    2. Drive the vehicle at approximately 30 km/h (19 mph) or more for 60 seconds or more.
    3. Check for DTCs (see DTC CHECK / CLEAR  ).

      Result 

      RESULT REFERENCE

      Result Proceed to
      DTC is output A
      DTC is not output B

      HINT:

      When replacing the skid control ECU with actuator, perform the zero point calibration (see CALIBRATION  ).

    NG: END 

    A: REPLACE BRAKE ACTUATOR ASSEMBLY
