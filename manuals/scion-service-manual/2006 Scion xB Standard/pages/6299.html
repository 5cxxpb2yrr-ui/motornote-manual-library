DTC B0138/72 Short to B+ in Front Pretensioner Squib LH Circuit: Description

The front pretensioner LH circuit consists of the center airbag sensor assembly and the front seat outer belt assembly (seat belt pretensioner LH).

This circuit instructs the SRS to deploy when deployment conditions are met.

DTC B0138/72 is recorded when a short to B+ is detected in the front pretensioner squib LH circuit.

DTC DETECTION CONDITION AND TROUBLE AREA

DTC No. DTC Detection Condition Trouble Area
B0138/72
  • Short circuit in front pretensioner squib LH wire harness (to B+)
  • Front pretensioner LH malfunction
  • Center airbag sensor assembly
  • Front seat outer belt assembly LH (front pretensioner squib LH)
  • Center airbag sensor assembly
  • Floor wire
