Water Pump: Removal

  1. DRAIN ENGINE  COOLANT  
  2. REMOVE ENGINE UNDER COVER RH 
  3. REMOVE GENERATOR V BELT (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  4. REMOVE ENGINE MOUNTING INSULATOR SUBASSEMBLY RH (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  5. REMOVE WATER PUMP PULLEY 
    1. Using SST, hold the pump pulley.
      Fig 1: Removing Water Pump Pulley
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

      SST 09960-10010 (09962-01000, 09963-00600) 

    2. Remove the 3 bolts and pump pulley.
  6. REMOVE WATER PUMP ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the 3 bolts and 2 nuts.
    2. Remove the water pump and gasket.
