Second Brake Piston: Inspection

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. INSPECT 2ND BRAKE PISTON RETURN SPRING SUB-ASSEMBLY 
    1. Using a vernier caliper, measure the free length of the spring together with the spring seat.

      Standard free length: 14.65 mm (0.5768 in.)
