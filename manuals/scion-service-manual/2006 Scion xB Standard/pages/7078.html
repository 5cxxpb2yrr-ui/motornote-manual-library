Transmission Revolution Sensor: Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. REMOVE AIR CLEANER ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the air cleaner (see REMOVAL ).
  2. REMOVE AIR CLEANER BRACKET 
    1. Disconnect the wire harness clamp.
    2. Remove the 2 bolts and air cleaner bracket.
      Fig 1: Removing Bolts And Air Cleaner Bracket
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  3. REMOVE TRANSMISSION REVOLUTION SENSOR 
    1. Disconnect the connector.
    2. Remove the bolt and transmission revolution sensor.
    3. Remove the O-ring from the sensor.
