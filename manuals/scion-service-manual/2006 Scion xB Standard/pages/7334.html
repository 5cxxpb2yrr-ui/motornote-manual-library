Inspection Procedure

  1. CHECK SEAT POSITION SENSOR 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to the LOCK position.
    2. Disconnect the negative (-) terminal cable from the battery, and wait for at least 90 seconds.
    3. Connect the negative (-) terminal cable to the battery, and wait for at least 2 seconds.
    4. Turn the ignition switch to the ON position, and wait for at least 60 seconds.
    5. Clear the stored DTCs in the memory (see DTC CHECK / CLEAR  ).
    6. Turn the ignition switch to the LOCK position.
    7. Turn the ignition switch to the ON position, and wait for at least 60 seconds.
    8. Check for the DTCs (see DTC CHECK / CLEAR  ).

      OK: 

      DTC B1153/25 is not output. 

      HINT:

      Codes other than code B1153 may be output at this time, but they are not related to this check.

    OK : PROBLEM SYMPTOMS SIMULATION 

    Fig 1: Checking Seat Position Sensor
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    NG : Go to Next Step 

  2. CHECK CENTER AIRBAG SENSOR ASSEMBLY 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to the LOCK position.
    2. Disconnect the negative (-) terminal cable from the battery, and wait for at least 90 seconds.
    3. Check that the connector is properly connected to the center airbag sensor assembly.

      OK: 

      Connector is connected. 

    NG : CONNECT CONNECTORS 

    OK : Go to Next Step 

  3. CHECK SEAT POSITION SENSOR 
    1. Check that the connector is properly connected to the seat position sensor.

      OK: 

      Connector is connected. 

    NG : CONNECT CONNECTORS 

    OK : Go to Next Step 

  4. CHECK SEAT POSITION SENSOR CIRCUIT (OPEN) (CENTER AIRBAG SENSOR ASSEMBLY - SEAT POSITION SENSOR) 
    1. Disconnect the connectors from the center airbag sensor assembly and the seat position sensor.
    2. Using SST, connect one terminal to the other of connector "E".

      SST 09843-18040 

      NOTE: Do not forcibly insert SST into the terminals of the connector when connecting.
    3. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

      Standard resistance 

      TESTER CONNECTION - SPECIFIED CONDITION (RESISTANCE)

      Tester Connector (Connector "B") Specified Condition
      A9-3 (LSP+) - A9-8 (LSP-) Below 1 Ω

    NG : Go to step   11 

    Fig 2: Disconnecting Connectors From Center Airbag Sensor Assembly And Seat Position Sensor
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    OK : Go to Next Step 

  5. CHECK SEAT POSITION SENSOR CIRCUIT (TO GROUND) (CENTER AIRBAG SENSOR ASSEMBLY - SEAT POSITION SENSOR) 
    1. Disconnect the SST from the connector "E".
    2. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

      Standard resistance 

      TESTER CONNECTION - SPECIFIED CONDITION (RESISTANCE)

      Tester Connection (Connector "B") Specified Condition
      A9-3 (LSP+) - Body ground 1 MΩ or higher
      A9-8 (LSP-) - Body ground

    NG : Go to step   12 

    Fig 3: Checking Seat Position Sensor Circuit (Center Airbag Sensor Assembly - Seat Position Sensor)

    OK : Go to Next Step 

  6. CHECK SEAT POSITION SENSOR CIRCUIT (CENTER AIRBAG SENSOR ASSEMBLY - SEAT POSITION SENSOR) 
    1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

      Standard resistance 

      TESTER CONNECTION - SPECIFIED CONDITION (RESISTANCE)

      Tester Connection (Connector "B") Specified Condition
      A9-3 (LSP+) - A9-8 (LSP-) 1 MΩ or higher

    NG : Go to step   13 

    Fig 4: Checking Seat Position Sensor Circuit (Center Airbag Sensor Assembly - Seat Position Sensor)
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    OK : Go to Next Step 

  7. CHECK SEAT POSITION SENSOR CIRCUIT (TO +B) (CENTER AIRBAG SENSOR ASSEMBLY - SEAT POSITION SENSOR) 
    1. Connect the negative (-) terminal cable to the battery, and wait for at least 2 seconds.
    2. Measure the voltage according to the value(s) in the table below.

      Standard voltage 

      TESTER CONNECTION - SPECIFIED CONDITION (VOLTAGE)

      Tester Connection (Connector "B") Condition Specified Condition
      A9-3 (LSP+) - Body ground Ignition switch ON Below 1 V
      A9-8 (LSP-) - Body ground

    NG : Go to step   14 

    Fig 5: Checking Seat Position Sensor Circuit (Center Airbag Sensor Assembly - Seat Position Sensor)
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    OK : Go to Next Step 

  8. CHECK SEAT POSITION SENSOR 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to the LOCK position.
    2. Disconnect the negative (-) terminal cable from the battery, and wait for at least 90 seconds.
    3. Connect the connectors to the seat position sensor and the center airbag sensor assembly.
    4. Connect the negative (-) terminal cable to the battery, and wait for at lest 2 seconds.
    5. Turn the ignition switch to the ON position, and wait for at least 60 seconds.
    6. Clear the stored DTCs in the memory (see DTC CHECK / CLEAR  ).
    7. Turn the ignition switch to the LOCK position.
    8. Turn the ignition switch to the ON position, and wait for at least 60 seconds.
    9. Check for the DTCs (see DTC CHECK / CLEAR  ).

      OK: 

      DTC B1153/25 is not output. 

      HINT:

      Codes other than code B1153/23 may be output at this time, but they are not related to this check.

    NG : Go to step 9 

    Fig 6: Checking Seat Position Sensor
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    OK : PROBLEM SYMPTOMS SIMULATION 

  9. REPLACE SEAT POSITION SENSOR 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to the LOCK position.
    2. Disconnect the negative (-) terminal cable from the battery, and wait for at least 90 seconds.
    3. Replace the seat position sensor (see ON-VEHICLE INSPECTION  ).

    NEXT 

  10. CHECK CENTER AIRBAG SENSOR ASSEMBLY 
    1. Connect the negative (-) terminal cable to the battery, and wait for at least 2 seconds.
    2. Turn the ignition switch to the ON position, and wait for at least 60 seconds.
    3. Clear the stored DTCs in the memory (see DTC CHECK / CLEAR  ).
    4. Turn the ignition switch to the LOCK position.
    5. Turn the ignition switch to the ON position, and wait for at least 60 seconds.
    6. Check for the DTCs (see DTC CHECK / CLEAR  ).

      OK: 

      DTC B1153/25 is not output. 

      HINT:

      Codes other than code B1153/25 may be output at this time, but they are not related to this check.

    NG : REPLACE CENTER AIRBAG SENSOR ASSEMBLY

      SST 09843-18040

      Standard resistance 

      TESTER CONNECTION - SPECIFIED CONDITION (RESISTANCE)

      Tester Connection (Connector "D") Specified Condition
      LSP+ - LSP- Below 1 Ω
