Power Steering Link: Disassembly

  1. FIX POWER STEERING LINK ASSEMBLY 
    1. Using SST, secure the steering link.

      SST 09612-00012 

      HINT:

      Wrap the SST with vinyl tape before use.

      Fig 1: Securing Steering Link
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  2. REMOVE TIE ROD END SUB-ASSEMBLY LH 
    1. Put matchmarks on the tie rod end LH and rack end.
    2. Loosen the lock nut and remove the tie rod end and lock nut.
  3. REMOVE TIE ROD END SUB-ASSEMBLY RH 

    HINT:

    Use the same procedures described for the LH side.

  4. REMOVE STEERING RACK BOOT CLIP 
    1. Remove the 2 boot clips.
      Fig 2: Putting Matchmarks On Tie Rod End LH And Rack End
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  5. REMOVE NO. 2 STEERING RACK BOOT CLAMP 
    1. Using a screwdriver, remove the rack boot clamp.
      NOTE: Be careful not to damage the boot.
  6. REMOVE NO. 1 STEERING RACK BOOT CLAMP 

    HINT:

    Use the same procedures described for the No. 2 side.

  7. REMOVE NO. 2 STEERING RACK BOOT 
    Fig 3: Removing Rack Boot Clamp
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  8. REMOVE NO. 1 STEERING RACK BOOT 
  9. REMOVE STEERING RACK END SUB-ASSEMBLY 
    1. Using SST, remove the 2 rack ends.

      SST 09922-10010 

      NOTE: Use SST 09922-10010 in the direction shown in the illustration.
      Fig 4: Removing Rack Ends
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  10. REMOVE RACK GUIDE 
    1. Using SST, remove the spring cap lock nut.

      SST 09922-10010 

      NOTE: Use SST 09922-10010 in the direction shown in the illustration.
      Fig 5: Removing Spring Cap Lock Nut
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. Using a 19 mm hexagon wrench, remove the rack guide spring cap.
    3. Remove the rack guide spring, rack guide spring spacer and rack guide.
      Fig 6: Removing Rack Guide Spring Cap
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  11. REMOVE POWER STEERING CONTROL VALVE 
    1. Put matchmarks on the control valve and rack housing.
    2. Remove the 2 bolts and control valve.
    3. Remove the gasket.
  12. REMOVE CYLINDER END STOPPER 
    1. Using needle nose pliers, remove the hole snap ring.
    2. Pull out the cylinder end stopper.
      Fig 7: Removing Control Valve Bolts
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  13. REMOVE POWER STEERING RACK 
    1. Using SST and a press, press out the steering rack with rack bush.

      SST 09950-70010 (09951-07200) 

      Fig 8: Pressing Out Steering Rack With Rack Bush
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  14. REMOVE POWER STEERING RACK BUSH 
    1. Remove the rack bush from the steering rack.
    2. Using SST, remove the rack bush oil seal.

      SST 09612-24014 (09613-22011) 

    3. Using a screwdriver, remove the O-ring from the rack bush.
      Fig 9: Removing Rack Bush Oil Seal
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  15. REMOVE RACK STEERING PISTON RING 
    1. Using a screwdriver, remove the piston ring and O-Ring.
      NOTE: Be careful not to damage the grooves for the piston ring and O-ring.
      Fig 10: Removing Piston Ring And O-Ring
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  16. REMOVE POWER STEERING RACK HOUSING OIL SEAL 
    1. Using SST and a press, press out the housing oil seal.

      SST 09950-60010 (09951-00200, 09951-00240, 

      09952-06010), 09950-70010 (09951-07360) 

      NOTE: Be careful not to damage the rack housing.
      Fig 11: Pressing Out Housing Oil Seal
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
