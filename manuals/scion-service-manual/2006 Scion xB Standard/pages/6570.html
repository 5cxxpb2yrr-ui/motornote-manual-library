Power Steering Link: Removal

  1. REMOVE FRONT WIPER ARM AND BLADE ASSEMBLY RH 
  2. REMOVE FRONT WIPER ARM AND BLADE ASSEMBLY LH 
  3. REMOVE HOOD TO COWL TOP SEAL (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  4. REMOVE COWL TOP VENTILATOR LOUVER RH (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  5. REMOVE COWL TOP VENTILATOR LOUVER LH (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  6. REMOVE WINDSHIELD WIPER MOTOR AND LINK ASSEMBLY (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  7. REMOVE COWL PANEL SUB-ASSEMBLY 
  8. INSPECT FRONT WHEELS FACING STRAIGHT AHEAD 
  9. REMOVE STEERING COLUMN HOLE COVER PLATE (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  10. REMOVE STEERING SLIDING YOKE SUBASSEMBLY 
    1. Fix the steering wheel with the seat belt in order to prevent rotation.

      HINT:

      This operation is also useful to prevent damaging the spiral cable.

      Fig 1: Fixing Steering Wheel With Seat Belt In Order To Prevent Rotation
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. Put matchmarks on the sliding yoke and intermediate shaft.
    3. Loosen the bolt, and then disconnect the sliding yoke from the intermediate shaft.
      Fig 2: Putting Matchmarks On Sliding Yoke And Intermediate Shaft
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  11. DISCONNECT NO. 1 STEERING COLUMN HOLE COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY 
    1. Detach clip A and disconnect the hole cover.
      NOTE: Be careful not to damage clip B.
      Fig 3: Disconnecting No. 1 Steering Column Hole Cover Sub-Assembly
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  12. REMOVE HOOD SUB-ASSEMBLY 
  13. DRAIN POWER STEERING FLUID 
  14. REMOVE FRONT WHEELS 
  15. DISCONNECT TIE ROD END SUB-ASSEMBLY LH (See  DISASSEMBLY   ) 
  16. DISCONNECT TIE ROD END SUB-ASSEMBLY RH 

    HINT:

    Use the same procedures described for the LH side.

  17. REMOVE ENGINE UNDER COVER LH 
  18. REMOVE ENGINE UNDER COVER RH 
  19. DISCONNECT PRESSURE FEED TUBE ASSEMBLY 
    1. Slide the clip and disconnect the return hose.
    2. Using SST, disconnect the pressure feed tube.

      SST 09023-12701 

    3. Using SST, remove the return tube.

      SST 09023-12701 

      Fig 4: Disconnecting Pressure Feed Tube Assembly
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    4. Remove the bolt and disconnect the tube clamp.
  20. DISCONNECT NO. 1 FRONT SUSPENSION ARM SUB-ASSEMBLY LOWER LH (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  21. DISCONNECT NO. 1 FRONT SUSPENSION ARM SUB-ASSEMBLY LOWER RH 

    HINT:

    Use the same procedures described for the LH side.

    Fig 5: Removing Tube Clamp Bolt
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  22. SUSPEND ENGINE ASSEMBLY 
    1. Install the 2 No. 1 engine hangers with the bolts in the correct direction.

      Parts No.: 

      No. 1 engine hanger: 12281-21010 

      Bolt: 91642-81025 

      Torque: 40 N *  m (408 kgf *  cm, 30 ft. *  lbf) 

    2. Hold the engine with the sliding device and chain hoist.
      NOTE: Do not hang the engine by hooking the chain to any other parts.
      Fig 6: Installing No. 1 Engine Hangers With Bolts In Correct Direction
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  23. REMOVE FRONT SUSPENSION CROSSMEMBER SUB-ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the 4 bolts and 2 reinforcements.
      Fig 7: Removing Reinforcements Bolts
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. Remove the bolt and 2 nuts, and disconnect the engine mounting insulator rear.
    3. Using a transmission jack, support the crossmember.
      Fig 8: Removing Engine Mounting Insulator Rear Bolt And Nuts
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    4. Remove the 4 bolts and crossmember.
  24. REMOVE POWER STEERING RACK HOUSING HEAT INSULATOR 
    1. Remove the 2 bolts and heat insulator.
  25. REMOVE NO. 1 STEERING COLUMN HOLE COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY 
    Fig 9: Removing Crossmember Bolts
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  26. REMOVE STEERING INTERMEDIATE SHAFT 
    1. Put matchmarks on the steering link and intermediate shaft.
    2. Remove the bolt and intermediate shaft.
      Fig 10: Putting Matchmarks On Steering Link And Intermediate Shaft
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  27. REMOVE POWER STEERING LINK ASSEMBLY 
    1. Put matchmarks on the steering link and No. 2 bracket.
      Fig 11: Putting Matchmarks On Steering Link And No. 2 Bracket
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. Remove the 4 bolts, nuts, No. 2 bracket, No. 2 grommet and steering link.
      NOTE:
      • Torque the bolt with the nut fixed as the nut has its own stopper.
      • Do not turn the nut.
      Fig 12: Removing Bolts, Nuts, No. 2 Bracket, No. 2 Grommet And Steering Link
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  28. REMOVE STEERING TURN PRESSURE TUBE RH 
    1. Using SST, remove the turn pressure tube.

      SST 09023-38201 

    2. Remove the 2 O-rings from the right turn pressure tube.
  29. REMOVE STEERING TURN PRESSURE TUBE LH 

    HINT:

    Use the same procedures described for the RH side.

    Fig 13: Removing O-Rings From Right Turn Pressure Tube
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
