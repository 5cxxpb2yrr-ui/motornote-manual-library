INTRODUCTION

Some 2004 - 2006 model year xB vehicles may exhibit a condition where the back door garnish becomes loose, and in some cases continued usage of the loose garnish may result in breakage. Please follow the procedure contained in this bulletin, which supplements the Repair Manual, to replace the back door garnish if it is loose or broken.

REPAIR PROCEDURE

NOTE: Do NOT use power tools for this repair procedure.
  1. Disconnect cable from the negative (-) battery terminal.
    CAUTION: Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to prevent airbag and seat belt pretensioner activation.
  2. Remove the back door trim board.

    Using a plastic pry tool, remove the 11 clips and trim board.

  3. Remove the back door outside garnish.

    Remove the 3 nuts and detach the clip, then remove the garnish.

    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  4. Remove the rear lamp set, switch, and mounting stud.
    1. Remove the 2 lamp screws (silver) and the 2 switch screws (black).
    2. Remove the mounting stud.
    3. Set ALL removed parts aside for installation into the NEW garnish later in this repair.
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  5. Assemble the NEW back door garnish.
    1. Lay the NEW back door garnish (P/N 76811-52122-##) down on a clean protected work surface to prevent scratching of the painted surface.
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. Remove the protective backing and install the foam strip (P/N 76821-52030, Back Door Garnish Protector) around the outer portion of the garnish as shown.
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    3. Transfer the mounting stud that was removed in step B to the NEW garnish.
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    4. Install the new mounting clip (P/N 90467-10077, Clip), as shown in Figure 6.
    5. Install the 3 foam washers (P/N 90562-04007, Ring) onto each of the 3 mounting studs.
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    6. Once the foam washers have been installed, a strip of foam must be cut and placed on the NEW garnish to isolate the rear lamp wire harness.

      Cut a foam strip from the Wind Noise Kit (P/N 08231-00813).

      Foam Strip Dimensions: 95 mm x 7 mm (3.74 in. x 0.28 in.) 

      HINT 

      Cut foam from sheet as shown to get the most strips from the sheet. 

      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    7. Place foam strip between the dotted alignment marks on the NEW garnish.
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    8. Transfer back door opener switch assembly over to the NEW garnish.
      1. Install switch, and fasten with the 2 black screws previously removed from the OLD garnish in step A.
      2. Be sure the black wire harness protector is positioned as shown to protect the wires.
        Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    9. Transfer the rear lamp set to the NEW garnish. Use the silver screws previously removed from the OLD garnish in step A.
      1. Route wire harness as shown, and make sure the wire harness is secured behind the previously installed foam strip.
        Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
      2. Secure the switch wire harness under the rear lamp set wire harness, and route both wire harnesses under the light bulb housing as circled below.
        Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  6. Insert the wire harness grommet into the rear hatch door and ensure it is seated properly to prevent leaks. Install the NEW back door garnish on the rear hatch door using the 3 previously removed nuts.

    Torque: 3 N*m (30.6 kgf*cm, 26.6 in*lbf) 

    NOTE: Do NOT use power tools during installation.
  7. Reassemble vehicle in reverse order of disassembly steps 1-3.
  8. Confirm proper operation of the rear lamp set and back door opener switch.
