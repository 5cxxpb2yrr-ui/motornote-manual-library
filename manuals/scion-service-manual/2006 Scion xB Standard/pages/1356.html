Inspection Procedure

  1. CHOOSE DIAGNOSIS METHOD 
    1. Choose the diagnosis method.

      Method 

      METHOD REFERENCE

      Method Proceed to
      Using intelligent tester A
      Not using intelligent tester B

    B: Go to step   3 

    A: Go to next step 

  2. PERFORM ACTIVE TEST BY INTELLIGENT TESTER (TRAC OFF INDICATOR LIGHT) 
    1. Select the ACTIVE TEST, generate a control command, and then check that the TRAC OFF indicator light illuminates.

      Skid control ECU with actuator 

      MEASUREMENT ITEM DIAGNOSTIC CHART

      Item Measurement Item / Range (Display) Diagnostic Note
      VSC / TRC OFF IND Turns VSC / TRAC OFF indicator light ON / OFF Observe combination meter

      OK: VSC / TRAC OFF indicator light illuminates. 

    NG: Go to step   5 

    OK: Go to Next Step 

  3. INSPECT TRACTION CONTROL SWITCH 
    1. Remove the switch.
    2. Measure the resistance of the switch.

      Standard resistance 

      TESTER CONNECTION SPECIFIED CONDITION

      Tester Connection Switch Condition Specified Condition
      2 - 5 Pushed (ON) Below 1 Ω
      Not pushed (OFF) 10 kΩ or higher

    NG: REPLACE TRACTION CONTROL SWITCH 

    Fig 1: Inspecting Traction Control Switch
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    OK: Go to Next Step 

  4. CHECK WIRE HARNESS (TRACTION CONTROL SWITCH - SKID CONTROL ECU AND BODY GROUND) 
    1. Disconnect the S3 ECU connector.
    2. Disconnect the T3 switch connector.
    3. Measure the resistance of the wire harness side connectors.

      Standard resistance 

      TESTER CONNECTION SPECIFIED CONDITION

      Tester Connection Specified Condition
      T3-5 - S3-43 (CSW) Below 1 Ω
      T3-2 - Body ground

      HINT:

      When replacing the skid control ECU with actuator, perform the zero point calibration (see CALIBRATION  ).

    NG: REPAIR OR REPLACE HARNESS AND CONNECTOR 

    Fig 2: Measuring Resistance Of Wire Harness Side Connectors
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    OK: REPLACE BRAKE ACTUATOR ASSEMBLY 

  5. CHECK WIRE HARNESS (COMBINATION METER - SKID CONTROL ECU) 
    1. Disconnect the C6 meter connector.
    2. Disconnect the S3 ECU connector.
    3. Measure the resistance of the wire harness side connectors.

      Standard resistance 

      TESTER CONNECTION SPECIFIED CONDITION

      Tester Connection Specified Condition
      C6-19 - S3-35 (WT) Below 1 Ω

    NG: REPAIR OR REPLACE HARNESS AND CONNECTOR 

    Fig 3: Measuring Resistance Of Wire Harness Side Connectors
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    OK: REPLACE COMBINATION METER ASSEMBLY
