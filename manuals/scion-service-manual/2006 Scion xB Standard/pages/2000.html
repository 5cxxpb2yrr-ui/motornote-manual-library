Low Pitched Horn: Installation

  1. INSTALL LOW PITCHED HORN 
    1. Attach the horn stay to the body panel and then install the horn with the bolt.

      Torque: 19.5 N*m (199 kgf*cm, 14 ft.*lbf) 

    2. Connect the horn connector.
