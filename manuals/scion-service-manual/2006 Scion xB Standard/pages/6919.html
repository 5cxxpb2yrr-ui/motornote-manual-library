Automatic Transaxle Assembly: Installation

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. INSPECT TORQUE CONVERTER CLUTCH ASSEMBLY 
    1. Inspect the torque converter clutch (see TORQUE CONVERTER CLUTCH AND DRIVE PLATE  ).
  2. INSTALL TORQUE CONVERTER CLUTCH ASSEMBLY 
    1. Using a vernier caliper, measure the dimension "A" between the transaxle fitting part of the engine and the converter clutch fitting part of the drive plate.
      Fig 1: Measuring Dimension "A" Between Transaxle Fitting Part Of Engine And Converter Clutch
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. Set the key at the top of the front oil pump drive gear and put a mark on the housing.
      Fig 2: Aligning Key Top Of Front Oil Pump Drive Gear And Put Mark On Housing
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    3. Put a mark on the torque converter clutch so that its groove is clearly indicated.
      Fig 3: Aligning Mark On Torque Converter Clutch
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    4. Align the marks on the case and converter clutch, and fit the splined part of the input shaft to the spline part of the turbine runner.
      Fig 4: Aligning Marks On Case And Converter Clutch
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    5. Rotating the converter clutch, fit the spline part of the stator shaft with that of the stator.

      HINT:

      Rotate it approximately 180°.

      Fig 5: Rotating Converter Clutch
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    6. Rotating the converter clutch, align the marks on the case and converter clutch again, and fit the key of the oil pump drive gear into the keyway of the converter.
      NOTE: When rotating the converter, do not push it with excessive force.
      Fig 6: Aligning Marks On Case And Converter Clutch
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    7. Using a vernier caliper and straightedge, measure the dimension "B" shown in the illustration and check that "B" is greater than "A".

      HINT:

      Dimension "A" is the measurement between the transaxle fitting part of the engine and the converter clutch fitting part of the drive plate.

      Standard dimension: A + 1 mm (0.04 in.) or more 

      NOTE: Remember to subtract the thickness of the straightedge.
      Fig 7: Measuring Between Transaxle Fitting Part Of Engine And Converter Clutch
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  3. INSTALL TRANSMISSION OIL FILLER TUBE SUBASSEMBLY 
    1. Temporarily install the oil cooler outlet tube.
    2. Temporarily install the oil cooler inlet tube.
    3. Coat a new O-ring with ATF, and install it on the oil filler tube.
    4. Install the oil filler tube on the automatic transaxle.
    5. Install the breather hose on the oil filler tube.
    6. Install the oil cooler tube clamp with the 2 bolts.

      Torque: 5.5 N*m (56 kgf*cm, 49 in.*lbf) 

    7. Install the ATF level gauge.
      Fig 8: Installing Oil Cooler Tube Clamp With Bolts
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  4. INSTALL NO. 1 OIL COOLER OUTLET TUBE 
    1. Using SST, tighten the oil cooler outlet tube.

      SST 09023-12701 

      Torque: 34 N*m (347 kgf*cm, 25 ft.*lbf) 

  5. INSTALL NO. 1 OIL COOLER INLET TUBE 
    1. Using SST, tighten the oil cooler inlet tube.

      SST 09023-12701 

      Torque: 34 N*m (347 kgf*cm, 25 ft.*lbf) 

      Fig 9: Tightening Oil Cooler Inlet Tube And Outlet Tube
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  6. INSTALL ENGINE MOUNTING BRACKET LH 
    1. Install the engine mounting bracket LH on the automatic transaxle with the 3 bolts.

      Torque: 64 N*m (653 kgf*cm, 47 ft.*lbf) 

      Fig 10: Installing Engine Mounting Bracket LH On Automatic Transaxle With Bolts
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  7. INSTALL AUTOMATIC TRANSAXLE ASSEMBLY 
    1. Install the automatic transaxle on the engine with the 7 bolts.

      Torque: 30 N*m (301 kgf*cm, 22 ft.*lbf) 

      Fig 11: Installing Automatic Transaxle On Engine With Bolts
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. Install the 6 torque converter mounting bolts.

      Torque: 27 N*m (280 kgf*cm, 20 ft.*lbf) 

      HINT:

      First install the white colored bolt, and then the remaining 5 bolts.

      Fig 12: Installing Torque Converter Mounting Bolts
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  8. INSTALL FLYWHEEL HOUSING UNDER COVER 
    1. Install the flywheel housing under cover.
      Fig 13: Installing Flywheel Housing Under Cover
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  9. INSTALL ENGINE MOUNTING BRACKET REAR 
    1. Install the engine mounting bracket on the automatic transaxle with the 3 bolts.

      Torque: 49 N*m (500 kgf*cm, 36 ft.*lbf) 

  10. INSTALL FRONT SUSPENSION CROSSMEMBER SUB-ASSEMBLY 
    1. Install the front suspension crossmember (see INSTALLATION ).
      Fig 14: Installing Engine Mounting Bracket On Automatic Transaxle With Bolts
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  11. INSTALL ENGINE MOUNTING INSULATOR REAR 
    1. Install the engine mounting insulator rear on the engine mounting bracket with the bolt and nut.

      Torque: 64 N*m (653 kgf*cm, 47 ft.*lbf) 

      Fig 15: Installing Engine Mounting Insulator Rear On Engine Mounting Bracket With Bolt And Nut
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  12. CONNECT ENGINE MOUNTING BRACKET LH 
    1. Connect the engine mounting bracket to the engine mounting insulator with the 2 bolts.

      Torque: 49 N*m (500 kgf*cm, 36 ft.*lbf) 

      Fig 16: Connecting Engine Mounting Bracket LH
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. Connect the ground cable to the engine mounting bracket with the bolt.

      Torque: 26 N*m (260 kgf*cm, 19 ft.*lbf) 

      Fig 17: Connecting Ground Cable To Engine Mounting Bracket With Bolt
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  13. INSTALL STARTER ASSEMBLY 
    1. Install the starter with the 2 bolts.

      Torque: 37 N*m (380 kgf*cm, 27 ft.*lbf) 

    2. Connect the connecter.
    3. Install the starter wire with the nut.

      Torque: 9.8 N*m (100 kgf*cm, 87 in.*lbf) 

      Fig 18: Installing Starter With Bolts
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    4. Install the flywheel housing side cover.
      Fig 19: Installing Flywheel Housing Side Cover
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  14. INSTALL DRIVE SHAFT HEAT INSULATOR SUBASSEMBLY 
    1. Temporarily tighten the bolt labeled A.
    2. Fully tighten the 2 bolts labeled B.

      Torque: 18 N*m (180 kgf*cm, 13 ft.*lbf) 

    3. Fully tighten the bolt labeled A.

      Torque: 18 N*m (180 kgf*cm, 13 ft.*lbf) 

      Fig 20: Installing Drive Shaft Heat Insulator Subassembly
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  15. INSTALL FRONT DRIVE SHAFT ASSEMBLY LH 
    1. Install the front drive shaft (see REASSEMBLY ).
  16. INSTALL FRONT DRIVE SHAFT ASSEMBLY RH
