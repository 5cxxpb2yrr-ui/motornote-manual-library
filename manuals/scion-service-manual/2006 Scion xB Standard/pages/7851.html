Heater Switch: Inspection

  1. INSPECT HEATER SWITCH ASSEMBLY 
    1. Measure the resistance of the switch.

      Standard resistance 

      TESTER CONNECTION AND SPECIFIED CONDITION CHART

      Tester Connection Switch Condition Specified Condition
      Inlet Air Position Detection Switch 1 - 2 OFF 10 kΩ or higher
      1 - 2 ON Below 1 Ω
      Defroster Mode Detection Switch 3 - 4 OFF 10 kΩ or higher
      3 - 4 ON Below 1 Ω

      If the result is not as specified, replace the heater switch.
