Removal (2005/12- )

  1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL 
    CAUTION: Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to prevent airbag and seat belt pretensioner activation.
  2. DISCHARGE REFRIGERANT FROM REFRIGERATION SYSTEM 
  3. DISCONNECT SUCTION HOSE SUB-ASSEMBLY 
    1. Install SST to the piping clamp.

      SST 09870-00015 

      NOTE: Be careful not to deform the hose when pushing down on SST.

      HINT:

      Confirm the direction of the piping clamp claw and SST by referring to the illustration shown on the caution label.

      Fig 1: Installing SST To Piping Clamp
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. Push down on SST and detach the clamp lock.
      NOTE: Be careful not to deform the hose when pushing down on SST.
    3. Pull SST slightly, push the release lever, and then remove the piping clamp with SST.
      Fig 2: Removing Piping Clamp With SST While Pushing Release Lever
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    4. Remove the bolt and disconnect the bracket.
      Fig 3: Removing Bolt And Disconnecting Bracket
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    5. Disconnect the suction hose.
      NOTE:
      • Do not use tools, such as a screwdriver, to remove the tube.
      • Cap the open fittings immediately to keep moisture or dirt out of the system.
        Fig 4: Disconnecting Suction Hose
        Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    6. Remove the 2 O-rings from the suction hose.
      NOTE: Seal the openings of the disconnected parts using vinyl tape to prevent moisture and foreign matter from entering.
  4. DISCONNECT LIQUID TUBE SUB-ASSEMBLY 

    HINT:

    Use the same procedures described for the suction hose.

  5. REMOVE INSTRUMENT PANEL SUB-ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the instrument panel (see REMOVAL ).
  6. REMOVE LOWER INSTRUMENT PANEL ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the instrument panel (see REMOVAL ).
  7. REMOVE AIR CONDITIONING AMPLIFIER ASSEMBLY 
  8. REMOVE ECM COVER 
    1. Detach the 2 claws and remove the cover.
      Fig 5: Detaching 2 Claws And Removing Cover
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  9. REMOVE ECM 
    1. Disconnect the connectors.
    2. Remove the 2 screws.
    3. Detach the 2 claws and remove the ECM.
      Fig 6: Removing ECM
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  10. REMOVE AIR CONDITIONING BLOWER ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the clamp.
    2. Disconnect the connectors.
      Fig 7: Removing Air Conditioning Blower Assembly
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    3. Detach the 2 clamps and disconnect the wire harness.
      Fig 8: Detaching 2 Clamps And Disconnecting Wire Harness
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    4. Remove the 4 bolts and 3 nuts. Then remove the A/C blower.
      Fig 9: Removing 4 Bolts And 3 Nuts And A/C Blower
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    5. Disconnect the 2 clamps and remove the wire harness.
      Fig 10: Disconnecting 2 Clamps And Removing Wire Harness
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
