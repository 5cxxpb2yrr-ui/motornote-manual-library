Inspection Procedure

  1. CHOOSE DIAGNOSIS METHOD 
    1. Choose the diagnosis MTRhod.

      MTRhod 

      MTRHOD REFERENCE

      MTRhod Proceed to
      Using intelligent tester A
      Not using intelligent tester B

    B: Go to step   3 

    A: Go to next step 

  2. PERFORM ACTIVE TEST BY INTELLIGENT TESTER (ABS MOTOR RELAY) 
    1. Select the ACTIVE TEST, generate a control command, and then check that the ABS motor relay operates.

      Skid control ECU with actuator 

      MEASUREMENT ITEM DIAGNOSTIC CHART

      Item Measurement Item / Range (Display) Diagnostic Note
      ABS MOT RELAY Turns ABS motor relay ON / OFF Operation sound of motor can be heard

      OK: Operation sound of motor can be heard. 

      HINT:

      When replacing the skid control ECU with actuator, perform the zero point calibration (see CALIBRATION  ).

    OK: REPLACE BRAKE ACTUATOR ASSEMBLY 

    NG: Go to Next Step 

  3. INSPECT FUSE (ABS, ALT) 
    1. Remove the ABS and ALT H-fuses from the fusible link block.
    2. Measure the resistance of the H-fuses.

      Standard resistance: Below 1 Ω 

    NG: REPLACE FUSE 

    OK: Go to Next Step 

  4. INSPECT RELAY (Marking: ABS MTR) 
    1. Remove the ABS MTR relay from the ABS relay block.
    2. Measure the resistance of the relay.

      Standard resistance 

      TESTER CONNECTION SPECIFIED CONDITION

      Tester Connection Specified Condition
      3 - 5 10 kΩ or higher
      Below 1 Ω (when battery voltage is applied to terminals 1 and 2)

    NG: REPLACE RELAY 

    Fig 1: Inspecting Relay
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    OK: Go to Next Step 

  5. INSPECT RELAY (Marking: ABS CUT) 
    1. Remove the ABS CUT relay from the ABS relay block.
    2. Measure the resistance of the relay.

      Standard resistance 

      TESTER CONNECTION SPECIFIED CONDITION

      Tester Connection Specified Condition
      3 - 5 10 kΩ or higher
      Below 1 Ω (when battery voltage is applied to terminals 1 and 2)

    NG: REPLACE RELAY 

    Fig 2: Inspecting Relay
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    OK: Go to Next Step 

  6. CHECK WIRE HARNESS (ABS CUT RELAY - ABS MTR RELAY AND BATTERY) 
    1. Remove the ABS CUT relay and ABS MTR relay from the ABS relay block.
    2. Measure the voltage of the wire harness side connector.

      Standard voltage 

      TESTER CONNECTION SPECIFIED CONDITION

      Tester Connection Specified Condition
      ABS CUT relay terminal 5 - Body ground 10 to 14 V
    3. Measure the resistance of the wire harness side connector.

      Standard resistance 

      TESTER CONNECTION SPECIFIED CONDITION

      Tester Connection Specified Condition
      ABS CUT relay terminal 3 - ABS MTR relay terminal 5 Below 1 Ω
      ABS CUT relay terminal 1 - ABS MTR relay terminal 1
      Fig 3: Checking Wire Harness
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    NG: REPAIR OR REPLACE HARNESS AND CONNECTOR 

    OK: Go to Next Step 

  7. CHECK WIRE HARNESS (SKID CONTROL ECU - BODY GROUND) 
    1. Disconnect the S3 ECU connector.
    2. Measure the resistance of the wire harness side connector.

      Standard resistance 

      TESTER CONNECTION SPECIFIED CONDITION

      Tester Connection Specified Condition
      S3-32 (GND1) - Body ground Below 1 Ω
      S3-1 (GND2) - Body ground

    NG: REPAIR OR REPLACE HARNESS AND CONNECTOR 

    Fig 4: Checking Wire Harness
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    OK: Go to Next Step 

  8. CHECK WIRE HARNESS (SKID CONTROL ECU - ABS CUT RELAY AND ABS MTR RELAY) 
    1. Disconnect the S3 ECU connector.
    2. Remove the ABS CUT relay and ABS MTR relay from the ABS relay block.
    3. Measure the resistance of the wire harness side connectors.

      Standard resistance 

      TESTER CONNECTION SPECIFIED CONDITION

      Tester Connection Specified Condition
      S3-14 (MRF) - ABS CUT relay terminal 2 Below 1 Ω
      S3-2 (BM) - ABS MTR relay terminal 3
      S3-15 (MR) - ABS MTR relay terminal 2
      S3-45 (R+) - ABS MTR relay terminal 3

      HINT:

      When replacing the skid control ECU with actuator, perform the zero point calibration (see CALIBRATION  ).

    NG: REPAIR OR REPLACE HARNESS AND CONNECTOR 

    Fig 5: Checking Wire Harness
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    OK: REPLACE BRAKE ACTUATOR ASSEMBLY
