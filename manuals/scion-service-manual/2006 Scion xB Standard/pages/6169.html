On-Vehicle Inspection

  1. INSPECT CONDENSER WITH RECEIVER ASSEMBLY 
    1. If the fins of the condenser are dirty, clean them with water. Dry the fins with compressed air.
      NOTE: Be careful not to damage the condenser.
    2. If a fin of the condenser is bent, use a screwdriver or pliers to straighten it.
  2. CHECK CONDENSER FOR LEAKAGE OF REFRIGERANT 
    1. Using a gas leak detector, check the pipe joints for gas leakage.

      If gas leakage is detected in a joint, check the torque of the joint.
