On-Vehicle Inspection

  1. CHECK CENTER AIRBAG SENSOR ASSEMBLY (VEHICLE NOT INVOLVED IN COLLISION AND AIRBAG NOT DEPLOYED) 
    1. Perform a diagnostic system check (see DTC CHECK / CLEAR  ).
  2. CHECK CENTER AIRBAG SENSOR ASSEMBLY (VEHICLE INVOLVED IN COLLISION AND AIRBAG NOT DEPLOYED) 
    1. Perform a diagnostic system check (see DTC CHECK / CLEAR  ).
  3. CHECK CENTER AIRBAG SENSOR ASSEMBLY (VEHICLE INVOLVED IN COLLISION AND AIRBAG DEPLOYED) 
    1. Replace the center airbag sensor.
      CAUTION: For removal and installation of the center airbag sensor assembly, be sure to follow the correct procedures.
