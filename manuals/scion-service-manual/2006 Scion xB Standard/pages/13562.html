DTC C1251/51 Open in Pump Motor Circuit: Description

The ABS pump MTR is located inside the brake actuator.

DETECTION CONDITION CHART

DTC No. DTC Detection Condition Trouble Area
C1251/51 When either condition below is met:
  • Actuator pump MTR does not operate properly
  • Actuator pump MTR circuit remains open for at least 2 seconds
  • Skid control ECU with actuator
  • Skid control ECU with actuator circuit
