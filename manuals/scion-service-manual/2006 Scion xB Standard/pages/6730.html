On-Vehicle Inspection

  1. INSPECT TRANSAXLE OIL 
    1. Stop the vehicle on a level surface.
    2. Remove the filler plug and gasket.
    3. Check that the oil level is between 0 to 5 mm (0 to 0.20 in.) from the bottom lip of the filler plug opening.
      Fig 1: Checking Oil Level
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

      If the result is not as specified, add transaxle oil.

      Oil grade: API GL-4 or GL-5 

      Viscosity: SAE 75W- 90 

      Standard capacity: 1.9 liters (2.0 US qts, 1.7 Imp. qts) 

      NOTE:
      • Too much or too little oil will lead to transaxle problems.
      • After adjusting the oil level, drive the vehicle and check the oil level again.
    4. Check for oil leakage when the oil level is low.

      If leakage is found, repair the area necessary to stop the leak. Replace damaged parts as necessary.

    5. Install a new gasket and the filler plug.

      Torque: 39 N*m (400 kgf*cm, 29 ft.*lbf)
