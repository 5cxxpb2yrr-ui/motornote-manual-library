Clutch Unit: Removal

  1. REMOVE MANUAL TRANSAXLE ASSEMBLY 
  2. REMOVE CLUTCH RELEASE FORK SUB-ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the fork together with the clutch release bearing from the transaxle.
  3. REMOVE CLUTCH RELEASE FORK BOOT 
  4. REMOVE CLUTCH RELEASE BEARING ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the bearing from the clutch release fork.
      Fig 1: Removing Bearing From Clutch Release Fork
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  5. REMOVE RELEASE BEARING HUB CLIP 
  6. REMOVE RELEASE FORK SUPPORT 
    1. Remove the support and release fork from the transaxle.
  7. REMOVE CLUTCH COVER ASSEMBLY 
    1. Place matchmarks on the cover and flywheel.
    2. Loosen each set bolt one turn at a time until spring tension is released.
    3. Remove the 6 set bolts and pull off the cover.
      NOTE: Do not drop the clutch disc assembly.
  8. REMOVE CLUTCH DISC ASSEMBLY
