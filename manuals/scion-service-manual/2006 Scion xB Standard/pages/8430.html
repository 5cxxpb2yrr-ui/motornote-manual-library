Clutch Start Switch: Inspection

  1. INSPECT CLUTCH START SWITCH ASSEMBLY 
    1. Measure the resistance between the terminals when the switch is ON and OFF.

      Standard resistance 

      RESISTANCE SPECIFICATION

      Switch Position Specified Condition
      ON (pushed) Below 1 Ω
      OFF (free) 10 kΩ or higher
