Oil Pump: Disassembly

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. REMOVE FRONT OIL PUMP BODY O-RING 
    1. Remove the O-ring from the oil pump.
  2. INSPECT STATOR SHAFT ASSEMBLY (See  INSPECTION   ) 
    Fig 1: Removing O-Ring From Oil Pump
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  3. REMOVE STATOR SHAFT ASSEMBLY 
    1. Using a T30 "torx" socket wrench, remove the 10 screws.
  4. INSPECT CLEARANCE OF OIL PUMP ASSEMBLY (See  INSPECTION   ) 
    Fig 2: Removing Stator Screws
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  5. REMOVE FRONT OIL PUMP DRIVE GEAR 
    1. Remove the front oil pump drive gear from the oil pump body.
      Fig 3: Removing Front Oil Pump Drive Gear From Oil Pump Body
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  6. REMOVE FRONT OIL PUMP DRIVEN GEAR 
    1. Remove the front oil pump driven gear from the oil pump body.
      Fig 4: Removing Front Oil Pump Driven Gear From Oil Pump Body
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  7. REMOVE FRONT OIL PUMP OIL SEAL 
    1. Using SST, tap out the oil seal from the oil pump body.

      SST 09308-00010 

  8. INSPECT FRONT OIL PUMP BODY SUB-ASSEMBLY (See  REASSEMBLY   ) 
    Fig 5: Removing Front Oil Pump Oil Seal Using SST
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  9. REMOVE CLUTCH DRUM OIL SEAL RING 
    1. Using a screwdriver, remove the 2 oil seals from the stator shaft.
      Fig 6: Removing Oil Seals From Stator Shaft
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
