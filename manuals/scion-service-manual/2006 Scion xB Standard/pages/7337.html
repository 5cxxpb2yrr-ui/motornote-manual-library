Inspection Procedure

  1. CHECK FRONT AIRBAG SENSOR RH CIRCUIT (TO B+) (CENTER AIRBAG SENSOR ASSEMBLY - FRONT AIRBAG SENSOR RH) 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to the LOCK position.
    2. Disconnect the negative (-) terminal cable from the battery, and wait for at least 90 seconds.
    3. Disconnect the connectors between the center airbag sensor assembly and the front airbag sensor RH.
    4. Connect the negative (-) terminal cable to the battery, and wait for at least 2 seconds.
    5. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

      Standard voltage 

      TESTER CONNECTION - SPECIFIED CONDITION (VOLTAGE)

      Tester Connection (Connector "B") Condition Specified Condition
      A10-9 (+SR) - Body ground Ignition switch ON Below 1 V
      A10-20 (-SR) - Body ground

    NG : Go to step   7 

    Fig 1: Checking Front Airbag Sensor RH Circuit (To B+) (Center Airbag Sensor Assembly - Front Airbag Sensor RH)
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    OK : Go to Next Step 

  2. CHECK FRONT AIRBAG SENSOR RH CIRCUIT (TO GROUND) (FRONT AIRBAG SENSOR RH - CENTER AIRBAG SENSOR ASSEMBLY) 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to the LOCK position.
    2. Disconnect the negative (-) terminal cable from the battery, and wait for at least 90 seconds.
    3. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

      Standard resistance 

      TESTER CONNECTION - SPECIFIED CONDITION (RESISTANCE)

      Tester Connection (Connector "B") Specified Condition
      A10-9 (+SR) - Body ground 1 MΩ or higher
      A10-20 (-SR) - Body ground

    NG : Go to step   8 

    Fig 2: Checking Front Airbag Sensor RH Circuit (To Ground) (Front Airbag Sensor RH - Center Airbag Sensor Assembly)
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    OK : Go to Next Step 

  3. CHECK FRONT AIRBAG SENSOR RH CIRCUIT (SHORT) (FRONT AIRBAG SENSOR RH - CENTER AIRBAG SENSOR ASSEMBLY) 
    1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

      Standard resistance 

      TESTER CONNECTION - SPECIFIED CONDITION (RESISTANCE)

      Tester Connection (Connector "B") Specified Condition
      A10-9 (+SR) - A10-20 (-SR) 1 MΩ or higher

    NG : Go to step   9 

    Fig 3: Checking Front Airbag Sensor RH Circuit (Short) (Front Airbag Sensor RH - Center Airbag Sensor Assembly)
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    OK : Go to Next Step 

  4. CHECK FRONT AIRBAG SENSOR RH CIRCUIT (OPEN) (FRONT AIRBAG SENSOR RH - CENTER AIRBAG SENSOR ASSEMBLY) 
    1. Using a service wire, connect terminals +SR and -SR of connector "E".
      NOTE: Do not forcibly insert a service wire into the terminals of the connector when connecting.
    2. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

      Standard resistance 

      TESTER CONNECTION - SPECIFIED CONDITION (RESISTANCE)

      Tester Connection (Connector "B") Specified Condition
      A10-9 (+SR) - A10-20 (-SR) Below 1 Ω

    NG : Go to step   10 

    Fig 4: Connecting Terminals +SR And -SR Of Connector "E"
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    OK : Go to Next Step 

  5. INSPECT FRONT AIRBAG SENSOR RH 
    1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

      Standard resistance 

      TESTER CONNECTION - SPECIFIED CONDITION (RESISTANCE)

      Tester Connection (Connector "B") Specified Condition
      +SR - -SR 738 to 902 Ω

    NG : REPLACE FRONT AIRBAG SENSOR RH 

    Fig 5: Inspecting Front Airbag Sensor RH
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    OK : Go to Next Step 

  6. CHECK CENTER AIRBAG SENSOR ASSEMBLY 
    1. Connect the connectors to the front airbag sensor RH and the center airbag sensor assembly.
    2. Connect the negative (-) terminal cable to the battery, and wait for at least 2 seconds.
    3. Turn the ignition switch to the ON position, and wait for at least 60 seconds.
    4. Clear the stored DTCs in the memory (see DTC CHECK / CLEAR  ).
    5. Turn the ignition switch to the LOCK position.
    6. Turn the ignition switch to the ON position, and wait for at least 60 seconds.
    7. Check for the DTCs (see DTC CHECK / CLEAR  ).

      OK: 

      DTC B1156/15 or B1157/15 is not output. 

      HINT:

      Codes other than code B1156/15 or B1157/15 may be output at this time, but they are not related to this check.

    NG : REPLACE CENTER AIRBAG SENSOR ASSEMBLY 

    Fig 6: Checking Center Airbag Sensor Assembly
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    OK : PROBLEM SYMPTOMS SIMULATION 

  7. CHECK ENGINE ROOM MAIN WIRE HARNESS (TO B+) (CONNECTOR - FRONT AIRBAG SENSOR RH) 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to the LOCK position.
    2. Disconnect the negative (-) terminal cable from the battery, and wait for at least 90 seconds.
    3. Disconnect the engine room main wire connector from the instrument panel wire.
    4. Connect the negative (-) terminal cable to the battery, and wait for at lest 2 seconds.
    5. Measure the voltage according to the value(s) in the table below.

      Standard voltage 

      TESTER CONNECTION - SPECIFIED CONDITION (VOLTAGE)

      Tester Connection (Connector "D") Condition Specified Condition
      IA3-2 (+SR) - Body ground Ignition switch ON Below 1 V
      IA3-1 (-SR) - Body ground

    NG : REPAIR OR REPLACE ENGINE ROOM MAIN WIRE 

    Fig 7: Checking Engine Room Main Wire Harness (To B+) (Connector - Front Airbag Sensor RH)
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    OK : REPAIR OR REPLACE INSTRUMENT PANEL WIRE 

  8. CHECK ENGINE ROOM MAIN WIRE HARNESS (TO GROUND) (CONNECTOR - FRONT AIRBAG SENSOR RH) 
    1. Disconnect the engine room main wire connector from the instrument panel wire.
    2. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

      Standard resistance 

      TESTER CONNECTION - SPECIFIED CONDITION (RESISTANCE)

      Tester Connection (Connector "D") Specified Condition
      IA3-2 (+SR) - Body ground 1 MΩ or higher
      IA3-1 (-SR) - Body ground
