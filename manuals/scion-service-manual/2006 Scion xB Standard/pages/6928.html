Oil Pump: Reassembly

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. INSTALL CLUTCH DRUM OIL SEAL RING 
    1. Install the 2 seal rings to the stator shaft assembly.
      Fig 1: Installing Seal Rings To Stator Shaft Assembly
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  2. INSTALL FRONT OIL PUMP OIL SEAL 
    1. Using SST and a hammer, tap in the oil seal to the oil pump body.

      SST 09950-60010 (09951-00550), 09950-70010 (09951-07100) 

      Standard oil seal depth: 0 +-0.5 mm (0 +-0.020 in.) 

      Fig 2: Taping In Oil Seal To Oil Pump Body
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  3. INSTALL FRONT OIL PUMP DRIVEN GEAR 
    1. Apply ATF to the front oil pump driven gear and install it to the oil pump body with the marked side facing upward.
      Fig 3: Installing To Oil Pump Body With Marked Side Facing Upward (1 Of 2)
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  4. INSTALL FRONT OIL PUMP DRIVE GEAR 
    1. Apply ATF to the front oil pump drive gear and install it to the oil pump body with the marked side facing upward.
      Fig 4: Installing To Oil Pump Body With Marked Side Facing Upward (2 Of 2)
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  5. INSTALL STATOR SHAFT ASSEMBLY 
    1. Using a T30 "torx" socket wrench, install the 10 screws.
      Fig 5: Identifying Stator Shaft Assembly
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  6. INSPECT OIL PUMP ASSEMBLY 
    1. Turn the drive gear with 2 screwdrivers and make sure it rotates smoothly.
      NOTE: Be careful not to damage the lip of the oil seal.
      Fig 6: Turning Drive Gear With Screwdrivers
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  7. INSTALL FRONT OIL PUMP BODY O-RING 
    1. Apply ATF to the O-ring and install it to the oil pump.
      Fig 7: Applying ATF To O-Ring To Oil Pump
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
