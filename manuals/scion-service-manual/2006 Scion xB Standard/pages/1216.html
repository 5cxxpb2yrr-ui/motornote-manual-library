TRAC OFF Indicator Light, TRAC OFF Switch Circuit: Description

This is the TRAC control switch. When the TRAC control switch is pushed, TRAC control turns off and the TRAC OFF indicator light turns on. When the ignition switch is turned OFF and then ON, the TRAC control automatically turns on again.
