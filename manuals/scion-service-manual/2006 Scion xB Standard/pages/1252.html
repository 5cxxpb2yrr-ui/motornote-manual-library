Brake Pedal: Removal

  1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL 
    CAUTION: Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to prevent airbag and seat belt pretensioner activation.
  2. REMOVE LOWER INSTRUMENT PANEL ASSEMBLY (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  3. DISCONNECT MASTER CYLINDER PUSH ROD CLEVIS 
    1. Disconnect the stop light switch connector.
    2. Remove the brake pedal return spring.
    3. Remove the clip and clevis pin, and disconnect the master cylinder push rod clevis from the brake pedal.
      Fig 1: Removing Master Cylinder Push Rod Clevis Clip And Clevis Pin
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  4. REMOVE BRAKE PEDAL SUPPORT SUB-ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the bolt from the brake pedal support.
      Fig 2: Removing Bolt From Brake Pedal Support
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. Remove the 4 nuts and brake pedal support.
  5. REMOVE BRAKE PEDAL SUB-ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the brake pedal shaft and nut from the brake pedal support.
    2. Remove the brake pedal and 2 bushes.
    3. Remove the stop lamp switch cushion.
  6. REMOVE STOP LIGHT SWITCH ASSEMBLY 
    1. Loosen the stop light switch lock nut.
    2. Remove the stop light switch from the brake pedal support.
      Fig 3: Removing Brake Pedal Support Nuts
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  7. REMOVE BRAKE PEDAL PAD 
    1. Remove the brake pedal pad from the brake pedal.
