Clutch Pedal: Installation

  1. INSTALL CLUTCH PEDAL SUPPORT SUBASSEMBLY 
    1. Install the support on the vehicle with the 2 nuts and bolt.

      Torque: 12 N*m (120 kgf*cm, 9 ft.*lbf) 

      Fig 1: Installing Support On Vehicle With Nuts And Bolt
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  2. CONNECT CLUTCH MASTER CYLINDER PUSH ROD CLEVIS 
    1. Apply MP grease to the contact surface of the pin and clevis bush.
    2. Connect the clevis to the clutch pedal with the pin and clip.

      HINT:

      Install the pin from the left side of the vehicle.

      Fig 2: Applying MP Grease To Contact Surface Of Pin And Clevis Bush
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

  4. INSPECT AND ADJUST CLUTCH PEDAL SUBASSEMBLY
