Back Door: Disassembly

  1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL 
    CAUTION: Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to prevent airbag and seat belt pretensioner activation.
  2. REMOVE BACK DOOR TRIM BOARD 
    1. Using a clip remover, remove the 11 clips and trim board.
  3. REMOVE BACK DOOR GARNISH OUTSIDE (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  4. REMOVE CENTER STOP LIGHT ASSEMBLY (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  5. REMOVE REAR WIPER MOTOR ASSEMBLY (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  6. REMOVE BACK DOOR LOCK ASSEMBLY 
    1. Disconnect the door lock connector.
    2. Remove the 3 bolts and door lock.

      HINT:

      Remove the door lock through the service hole.

  7. REMOVE BACK WINDOW GLASS CHANNEL LH 
    1. Using a clip remover, remove the 2 clips.
    2. Using a heat light, heat the channel.

      Standard heating temperature: 

      20 to 30°C (68 to 86°F) 

      NOTE: Do not heat the body and channel excessively.
    3. Using a screwdriver, remove the channel.

      HINT:

      Tape the screwdriver tip before use.

  8. REMOVE BACK WINDOW GLASS CHANNEL RH 
    1. Use the same procedures described for the LH side.
  9. REMOVE DOOR PULL HANDLE 
    1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 4 claws and remove the pull handle.

      HINT:

      Tape the screwdriver tip before use.

  10. REMOVE BACK DOOR STAY SUB-ASSEMBLY LH (See  BACK DOOR SUPPORT   ) 
  11. REMOVE BACK DOOR STAY SUB-ASSEMBLY RH 
    1. Use the same procedures described for the LH side.
  12. REMOVE BACK DOOR STOPPER LOWER 
    1. Remove the bolt and stopper LH and RH.
