Inspection Procedure

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. INSPECT TRANSMISSION WIRE (SHIFT SOLENOID VALVE SLT) 
    1. Disconnect the E1 wire connector.
    2. Measure the resistance of the transmission wire.

      Standard resistance 

      TESTER CONNECTION SPECIFIED CONDITION

      Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
      3 (SLT+) - 8 (SLT-) 20°C (68°F) 5.0 to 5.6 Ω
      3 (SLT+) - Body ground 20°C (68°F) 10 kΩ or higher
      8 (SLT-) - Body ground 20°C (68°F) 10 kΩ or higher

    NG: Go to step   3 

    Fig 1: Inspecting Transmission Wire (Shift Solenoid Valve Slt)
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    OK: Go to Next Step 

  2. CHECK WIRE HARNESS (TRANSMISSION WIRE - ECM) 
    1. Disconnect the E6 ECM connector.
    2. Measure the resistance of the wire harness side connector.

      Standard resistance 

      TESTER CONNECTION SPECIFIED CONDITION

      Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
      E6-12 (SLT+) - E6-13 (SLT-) 20°C (68°F) 5.0 to 5.6 Ω
      E6-12 (SLT+) - Body ground 20°C (68°F) 10 kΩ or higher
      E6-13 (SLT-) - Body ground 20°C (68°F) 10 kΩ or higher
      Fig 2: Disconnecting E6 ECM Connector
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    NG: REPAIR OR REPLACE HARNESS AND CONNECTOR 

    OK: REPLACE ECM 

  3. INSPECT SHIFT SOLENOID VALVE SLT 
    1. Remove the shift solenoid valve SLT.
    2. Measure the resistance of the solenoid valve.

      Standard resistance: 5.0 to 5.6 Ωat 20°C (68°F) 

    3. Connect the positive (+) lead with a 21 W bulb to terminal 2 and the negative (-) lead to terminal 1 of the solenoid valve connector, then check the movement of the valve.
    4. Check the operating noise of terminal 1 of the solenoid valve.

      OK: Solenoid makes operating noise. 

    NG: REPLACE SHIFT SOLENOID VALVE SLT 

    Fig 3: Measuring Resistance Of Solenoid Valve
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    OK: REPAIR OR REPLACE TRANSMISSION WIRE
