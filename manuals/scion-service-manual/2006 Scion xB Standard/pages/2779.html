Water Pump: Inspection

  1. INSPECT WATER PUMP ASSEMBLY 
    1. Visually check the water hole and air hole for coolant leakage.
      Fig 1: Inspecting Water Pump Assembly
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

      If leakage is found, replace the water pump assembly.

    2. Turn the pulley, and check that the pump bearing moves smoothly and quietly.

      If it moves roughly or noisily, replace the water pump assembly.
