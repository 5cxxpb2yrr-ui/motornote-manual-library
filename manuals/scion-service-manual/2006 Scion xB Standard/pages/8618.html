Rear Door: Disassembly

HINT:

  • Use the same procedure for the RH side and LH side.
  • The procedures listed below are for the LH side.
  1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL 
    CAUTION: Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to prevent airbag and seat belt pretensioner activation.
  2. REMOVE DOOR PULL HANDLE 
    1. Remove the screw, and then remove the handle upward.
      Fig 1: Removing Door Pull Handle
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  3. REMOVE REAR POWER WINDOW REGULATOR MOTOR ASSEMBLY LH 
    1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 2 claws labeled A in the illustration. Remove the armrest base panel upper together with the switch.

      HINT:

      Tape the screwdriver tip before use.

    2. Disconnect the connector.
    3. Using a screwdriver, detach the 2 claws labeled B in the illustration. Remove the switch.

      HINT:

      Tape the screwdriver tip before use.

      Fig 2: Removing Armrest Base Panel Upper With Switch
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  4. REMOVE REAR DOOR TRIM BOARD SUBASSEMBLY LH 
    1. Remove the screw.
    2. Using a clip remover, detach the 7 clips.
    3. Remove the trim board upward.

      HINT:

      In order to prevent the door panel from being damaged, use protective tape to cover the areas indicated by the arrow marks in the illustration.

    4. Using a screwdriver, detach the claws, and remove the weatherstrip inner.

      HINT:

      Tape the screwdriver tip before use.

      Fig 3: Removing Rear Door Trim Board Subassembly LH
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  5. REMOVE REAR DOOR INSIDE HANDLE SUBASSEMBLY LH 
    1. Using a screwdriver, detach claw A as shown in the illustration.

      HINT:

      Tape the screwdriver tip before use.

    2. Move the inside handle as indicated by the arrow mark in the illustration. This will release the claw and allow removal of the inside handle.
    3. Disconnect the rod from the inside handle.
      Fig 4: Removing Rear Door Inside Handle Subassembly LH
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  6. REMOVE REAR DOOR SERVICE HOLE COVER LH 
    1. Remove the 2 screws and bracket.
      Fig 5: Removing Screws And Bracket
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. Remove the service hole cover.
      NOTE: Remove the remaining tape on the door.
  7. REMOVE REAR DOOR GLASS WEATHERSTRIP ASSEMBLY OUTER LH 
    Fig 6: Removing Tape On Door
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  8. REMOVE REAR DOOR WINDOW DIVISION BAR SUB-ASSEMBLY LH 

    HINT:

    Insert a cushion inside the door panel to prevent the glass from being scratched.

    NOTE: Be careful not to damage the glass.
    1. Temporarily install the power window regulator switch.
    2. Lower the glass as much as possible.
    3. Remove the glass run.
    4. Remove the 2 bolts and screw.
      Fig 7: Inserting Cushion Inside Door Panel
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    5. Rotate the division bar and remove it, as indicated by the arrow marks in the illustration.
      Fig 8: Removing Division Bar
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  9. REMOVE QUARTER WINDOW GLASS LH 
    1. Remove the window glass as indicated by the arrow mark in the illustration.
      NOTE: Be careful not to damage the glass.
    2. Remove the quarter window glass weatherstrip.
  10. REMOVE REAR DOOR GLASS SUB-ASSEMBLY LH 
    1. Carefully pull the door glass upward and remove it from the door panel.

      HINT:

      Insert a cushion inside the door panel to prevent the door glass from being scratched.

      Fig 9: Removing Quarter Window Glass LH
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  11. REMOVE REAR DOOR WINDOW REGULATOR SUBASSEMBLY LH 
    1. Disconnect the window regulator connector.
    2. Remove the 4 bolts and window regulator.

      HINT:

      Remove the window regulator through the service hole.

      NOTE: Be careful when removing the bolts as the window regulator may fall and become damaged.
      Fig 10: Removing Rear Door Window Regulator Subassembly LH
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  12. REMOVE REAR POWER WINDOW REGULATOR MOTOR ASSEMBLY LH 
    1. Using a T25 "Torx" driver, remove the 3 screws and motor.
      Fig 11: Removing Rear Power Window Regulator Motor Assembly LH
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  13. REMOVE REAR DOOR LOCK ASSEMBLY LH 
    1. Disconnect the snap and No. 2 remote control rod as indicated by the arrow marks.
      Fig 12: Disconnecting Snap And No 2 Remote Control Rod
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. Twist the knob and remove it, as shown in the illustration.
    3. Using a screwdriver, detach the 2 claws and remove the guide.

      HINT:

      Tape the screwdriver tip before use.

      Fig 13: Twisting Knob
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    4. Using a screwdriver, detach the 4 claws and remove the bellcrank with the remote control rod and No. 2 remote control rod.

      HINT:

      Tape the screwdriver tip before use.

    5. Remove the remote control rod and remote control rod No. 2.
      Fig 14: Removing Bellcrank With Remote Control Rod And No 2 Remote Control Rod
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    6. Using a T30 "Torx" socket, remove the 3 screws and door lock.

      HINT:

      Remove the door lock through the service hole.

      NOTE: Be careful when removing the bolts as the door lock may fall and become damaged.
      Fig 15: Removing Screws And Door Lock
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    7. Remove the 2 screws and actuator.
      Fig 16: Removing Screws And Actuator
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  14. REMOVE REAR DOOR OUTSIDE HANDLE ASSEMBLY LH 
    1. Remove the bolt, nut and, outside panel hole seal.

      HINT:

      Remove the door lock through the service hole.

      NOTE: Be careful when removing the bolt and nut as the seal may fall and become damaged.
      Fig 17: Removing Bolt, Nut And Outside Panel Hole Seal
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. Remove the bolt and handle.
      NOTE: Be careful when removing the bolt as the outside handle may fall and become damaged.
      Fig 18: Removing Bolt And Handle
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  15. REMOVE REAR DOOR CHECK ASSEMBLY LH 
    1. Remove the 3 bolts and check.

      HINT:

      Remove the check through the service hole.

  16. REMOVE REAR DOOR WEATHERSTRIP LH 
    1. Using a clip remover, detach the clip and remove the weatherstrip.

      HINT:

      If clips are damaged during removal, replace them.

      Fig 19: Removing Bolts And Check
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
