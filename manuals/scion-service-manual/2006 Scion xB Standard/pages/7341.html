DTC B1180/17 Short in Driver Side Squib 2nd Step Circuit: Description

The driver side squib (2nd step) circuit consists of the center airbag assembly, the spiral cable subassembly and the steering pad.

This circuit instructs the SRS to deploy when deployment conditions are met.

DTC B1180/17 is recorded when a short circuit is detected in the driver side squib (2nd step) circuit.

DTC DETECTION CONDITION AND TROUBLE AREA

DTC No. DTC Detection Condition Trouble Area
B1180/17

  • Driver side squib (2nd step) malfunction
