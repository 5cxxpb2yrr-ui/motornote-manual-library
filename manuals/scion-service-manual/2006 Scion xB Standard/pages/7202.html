Inspection Procedure

  1. CHOOSE DIAGNOSIS METHOD 
    1. Choose the diagnosis method.

      Method 

      METHOD REFERENCE

      Method Proceed to
      Using intelligent tester A
      Not using intelligent tester B

    B: Go to step   3 

    A: Go to next step 

  2. READ VALUE OF INTELLIGENT TESTER (MASTER CYLINDER PRESSURE SENSOR) 
    1. Check the DATA LIST for proper functioning of the master cylinder pressure sensor.

      Skid control ECU with actuator 

      MEASUREMENT ITEM DIAGNOSTIC CHART

      Item Measurement Item / Range (Display) Normal Condition Diagnostic Note
      MAS CYL PRESS 1 Master cylinder pressure sensor 1 reading / Min.: 0 V, Max.: 5 V When brake pedal is released: 0.3 to 0.9 V Reading increases when brake pedal is depressed

      OK: Brake fluid pressure value (voltage) changes from 0.3 to 0.9 V. 

      HINT:

      When replacing the skid control ECU with actuator, perform the zero point calibration (see CALIBRATION  ).

    OK: REPLACE BRAKE ACTUATOR ASSEMBLY 

    NG: Go to Next Step 

  3. CHECK WIRE HARNESS (SKID CONTROL ECU - BATTERY) 
    1. Disconnect the S3 ECU connector.
    2. Measure the voltage of the wire harness side connector.

      Standard voltage 

      TESTER CONNECTION SPECIFIED CONDITION

      Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
      S3-27 (STP) - Body ground Brake pedal is depressed 8 to 14 V
      Brake pedal is released Below 1 V

      HINT:

      When replacing the skid control ECU with actuator, perform the zero point calibration (see CALIBRATION  ).

    OK: REPLACE BRAKE ACTUATOR ASSEMBLY
