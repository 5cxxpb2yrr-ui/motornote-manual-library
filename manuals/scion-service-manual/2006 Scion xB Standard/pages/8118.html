Inspection Procedure

HINT:

Read freeze frame data using the intelligent tester. Freeze frame data records the engine condition when malfunctions are detected. When troubleshooting, freeze frame data can help determine if the vehicle was moving or stationary, if the engine was warmed up or not, if the air-fuel ratio was lean or rich, and other data from the time the malfunction occurred.

  1. CHECK WHETHER DTC OUTPUT RECURS (DTC P0351, P0352, P0353 OR P0354) 
    1. Connect the intelligent tester to the DLC3.
    2. Turn the ignition switch ON and turn the tester ON.
    3. Clear DTCs (see DTC CHECK / CLEAR  ).
    4. Shuffle arrangement of the ignition coils (among No. 1 to No. 4 cylinders).
      NOTE: Do not shuffle the connectors.
    5. Perform a simulation test.
    6. Check DTCs displayed on the tester.

      Result 

      RESULT

      Display (DTC Output) Proceed to
      P0351, P0352, P0353 or P0354 A
      Other ignition coil DTC output B

    B: REPLACE IGNITION COIL 

    A: Go to next step 

  2. INSPECT ECM (IGT1, IGT2, IGT3, IGT4 AND IGF SIGNAL) 
    1. Inspect the ECM using an oscilloscope.
      Fig 1: Inspecting ECM Using An Oscilloscope
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. While cranking or idling, check the waveform between the terminals of the E5 ECM connectors.

    OK: Go to Next Step 

  4. INSPECT IGNITION COIL (POWER SOURCE) 
    1. Disconnect the I2, I3, I4 or I5 ignition coil connector.
    2. Measure the resistance of the ignition coil connectors.

    3. Turn the ignition switch ON.
    4. Measure the voltage between the terminals of the wire harness side connectors.

    OK: REPLACE IGNITION COIL
