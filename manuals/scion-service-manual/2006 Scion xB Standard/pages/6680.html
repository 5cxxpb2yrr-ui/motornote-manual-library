FRONT SUSPENSION LOWER NO. 1 ARM (for Manual Transaxle): Installation

  1. TEMPORARILY INSTALL FRONT SUSPENSION NO. 1 LOWER ARM SUB-ASSEMBLY LH 
    1. Temporarily install the lower arm with the 2 bolts and nut.
  2. CONNECT FRONT SUSPENSION NO. 1 LOWER ARM SUB-ASSEMBLY LH (See  FRONT SUSPENSION LOWER NO. 1 ARM (FOR MANUAL TRANSAXLE)   ) 
  3. CONNECT FRONT STABILIZER BAR (See  FRONT SUSPENSION LOWER NO. 1 ARM (FOR MANUAL TRANSAXLE)   ) 
  4. INSTALL FRONT WHEEL 

    Torque: 103 N*m (1,050 kgf*cm, 76 ft.*lbf) 

    Fig 1: Installing Lower Arm With Bolts And Nut
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  5. FULLY TIGHTEN FRONT SUSPENSION NO. 1 LOWER ARM SUB-ASSEMBLY LH 
    1. Install the lower suspension arm with the 2 bolts.

      Torque: 88 N*m (900 kgf*cm, 65 ft.*lbf) for bolt A 

      132 N*m (1,350 kgf*cm, 97 ft.*lbf) for bolt B 

      NOTE: Do not turn the nut.
  6. INSPECT AND ADJUST FRONT WHEEL ALIGNMENT 
    1. Inspect and adjust the front wheel alignment (see FRONT WHEEL ALIGNMENT  ).
