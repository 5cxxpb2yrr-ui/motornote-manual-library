Monitor Description

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

The ECM commands gear shifts by turning the shift solenoid valves ON/OFF. When there is an open or short circuit in any shift solenoid valve circuit, the ECM detects the problem, illuminates the MIL and stores the DTC. Also, the ECM performs the fail-safe function and turns the other normal shift solenoid valves ON/OFF. In case of a short circuit, the ECM stop sending the current to the short circuited solenoid.
