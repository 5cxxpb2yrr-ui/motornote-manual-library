Typical Enabling Conditions

The stop light switch remains on while GO and STOP is repeated 5 times.

GO and STOP are defined as follows:

TYPICAL ENABLING CONDITIONS

The monitor will run whenever the following DTCs are not present None
GO: Vehicle speed 30 km/h (18.65 mph) or more
STOP: Vehicle speed Less than 3 km/h (1.86 mph)
