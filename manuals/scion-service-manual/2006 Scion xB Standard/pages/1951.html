    Fig 1: Bolt, Screw And Nut Table
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    HINT:

    All bolts, screws and nuts relevant to installing and removing the lower instrument panel are shown along with their alphabet code in the table above.

  2. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL 
    CAUTION: Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to prevent airbag and seat belt pretensioner activation.
  3. REMOVE INSTRUMENT PANEL SUB-ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the instrument panel (see REMOVAL  ).

  8. REMOVE WINDSHIELD WIPER SWITCH ASSEMBLY (See  REMOVAL  )

  10. REMOVE FRONT DOOR SCUFF PLATE RH (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  11. REMOVE COWL SIDE TRIM BOARD LH (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  12. REMOVE COWL SIDE TRIM BOARD RH (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  13. REMOVE HEATER CONTROL AND ACCESSORY ASSEMBLY (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  14. REMOVE INSTRUMENT CLUSTER FINISH PANEL SUB-ASSEMBLY LOWER CENTER 
    1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 2 claws and 2 clips.

      HINT:

      Tape the screwdriver tip before use.

    2. Disconnect the connectors, and remove the instrument cluster finish panel.
      Fig 2: Detaching Claws And Clips
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  15. REMOVE INSTRUMENT PANEL FINISH PANEL LOWER CENTER 
    1. Using a clip remover, remove the clip.
    2. Using a screwdriver, detach the 3 claws, and remove the instrument panel finish panel.

      HINT:

      Tape the screwdriver tip before use.

      Fig 3: Removing Clip Using Clip Remover
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  16. REMOVE GLOVE COMPARTMENT DOOR ASSEMBLY 
    1. Push the right side wall, and then push the left wall to release the stoppers.
      Fig 4: Pushing Right And Left Side Wall To Release Stoppers
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. Pull the glove compartment door rearward to remove it.

  23. REMOVE LOWER INSTRUMENT PANEL ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the 3 bolts.
      Fig 6: Removing Bolts
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. Remove the 3 bolts.
      Fig 7: Removing Bolts
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    3. Using a clip remover, remove the clip.
      Fig 8: Removing Clip Using Clip Remover
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    4. Remove the 3 bolts.
      Fig 9: Removing Bolts
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    5. Remove the bolt and No. 1 instrument panel mounting bracket.
      Fig 10: Removing Bolt And No. 1 Instrument Panel Mounting Bracket
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    6. Remove the screw.
    7. Disconnect the connector, and remove the door control receiver.
      Fig 11: Removing Screw
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    8. Remove the bolt.
    9. Disconnect the hood lock control cable.
    10. Remove the 2 bolts, 2 nuts and 2 screws.
    11. Remove the 2 screws and 3 nuts, and disconnect the instrument panel junction block.
    12. Disconnect the connectors, and then remove the instrument panel.
