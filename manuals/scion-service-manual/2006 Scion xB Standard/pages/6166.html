Compressor And Pulley: Inspection

  1. INSPECT COMPRESSOR WITH PULLEY ASSEMBLY 
    1. Measure the resistance between terminals 1 and 2.

      Standard resistance: 

      10.1 to 11.1 Ω 

      If the value is not as specified, replace the compressor assembly.
