Ignition Switch: Removal

  1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL 
    CAUTION: Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to prevent airbag and seat belt pretensioner activation.
  2. REMOVE STEERING COLUMN COVER 
    1. Remove the 3 screws, cover lower and upper.

      HINT:

      Turn the steering wheel to the right and left as necessary to remove the 3 screws and cover lower.

      Fig 1: Aligning Steering And Removing Screws
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  3. DISCONNECT CONNECTOR 
    1. Disconnect the 2 connectors and wire harness clamps from the steering column.
      Fig 2: Disconnecting Connectors And Wire Harness Clamps From Steering Column
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  4. REMOVE STEERING COLUMN UPPER WITH SWITCH BRACKET ASSEMBLY 
    1. Using a centering punch, mark the center of the 2 tapered-head bolts.
      Fig 3: Identifying Tapped Head Bolt Location
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. Using a 3 to 4 mm (0.12 to 0.16 in.) drill, drill a hole in the 2 bolt heads.
    3. Using a screw extractor, remove the 2 bolts, clamp and steering column upper with switch bracket.
  5. REMOVE IGNITION SWITCH ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the 2 screws and switch from the steering column bracket.
      Fig 4: Removing Screws From Steering Column Bracket
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
