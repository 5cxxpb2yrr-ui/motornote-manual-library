Front Shock Absorber With Coil Spring: Disassembly

  1. FIX FRONT SHOCK ABSORBER WITH COIL SPRING 
    1. Install the 2 nuts and bolt on the bracket at the lower side of the shock absorber and secure it in a vise.
    2. Using SST, compress the coil spring.

      SST 09727-30021 (09727-00010, 09727-00021, 09727-00031) 

      NOTE: Do not use an impact wrench. It will damage the SST.
      Fig 1: Compressing Coil Spring

  2. REMOVE FRONT SUSPENSION SUPPORT DUST COVER LH 
    1. Remove the dust cover from the suspension support.
  3. REMOVE FRONT SUPPORT TO FRONT SHOCK ABSORBER LH NUT 
    1. Using the 2 nuts and a screwdriver or an equivalent to secure the front shock absorber, remove the center nut.
      NOTE: Be careful not to damage the suspension support stud bolts.
