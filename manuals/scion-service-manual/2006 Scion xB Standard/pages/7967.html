Monitor Strategy

MONITOR STRATEGY

Related DTCs P0100: Mass air flow meter range check (Fluctuating)
P0102: Mass air flow meter range check (Low voltage)
P0103: Mass air flow meter range check (High voltage)
Required Sensors/Components (Main) MAF meter
Required Sensors/Components (Related) Crankshaft position sensor
Frequency of Operation Continuous
Duration 3 seconds
MIL Operation Immediate: Engine RPM less than 4,000 rpm 2 driving cycles: Engine RPM 4,000 rpm or more
Sequence of Operation None
