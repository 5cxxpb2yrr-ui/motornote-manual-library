Typical Enabling Conditions

TYPICAL ENABLING CONDITIONS

Monitor runs whenever following DTCs not present P0100 - P0103 (MAF sensor)
P0115 - P0118 (ECT sensor)
P0125 (insufficient ECT for closed loop)
P0335 (crankshaft position sensor)
P0340 (camshaft position sensor)
P0351 - P0354 (igniter)

P0011 and P0012 (Advance/retard malfunction): 

TYPICAL ENABLING CONDITIONS - P0011 AND P0012 (ADVANCE/RETARD MALFUNCTION)

Battery voltage 11 V or more
Engine RPM 450 to 4,000 rpm
Engine coolant temperature 75 to 100°C (167 and 212°F)
