Disassembly (2005/07-2005/12)

HINT:

  • Use the same procedures for the RH side and LH side.
  • The procedures listed below are for the LH side.
CAUTION: Wear protective gloves. Sharp surfaces on the seatback spring and seat adjuster may injure your hands.
  1. REMOVE FRONT SEAT HEADREST ASSEMBLY 
  2. REMOVE FRONT SEAT INNER BELT ASSEMBLY LH 
    1. Remove the bolt and inner belt.
  3. REMOVE RECLINING ADJUSTER RELEASE HANDLE LH 
    1. Raise the reclining adjuster release handle to reveal the claw.
    2. Using a screwdriver, detach the claw and remove the release handle.

      HINT:

      Tape the screwdriver tip before use.

      Fig 1: Detaching Claw And Removing Release Handle Using Screwdriver
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  4. REMOVE FRONT SEAT CUSHION SHIELD LH 
    1. Remove the 2 screws.
    2. Using a screwdriver, detach the 2 claws and remove the seat cushion shield.

      HINT:

      Tape the screwdriver tip before use.

      Fig 2: Removing Screws
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  5. REMOVE FRONT SEAT CUSHION SHIELD INNER LH 
    1. Remove the clip and screw.
    2. Using a screwdriver, detach the claw and remove the cushion shield.

      HINT:

      Tape the screwdriver tip before use.

      Fig 3: Detaching Claw And Removing Cushion Shield
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  6. REMOVE FRONT SEATBACK ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the hog rings.
      Fig 4: Identifying Hog Rings Location
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. Fold back the seatback cover and remove the hog rings.
    3. Disconnect the clamp.
      Fig 5: Removing Hog Rings
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    4. Remove the 4 bolts and seatback.
      Fig 6: Removing Bolts And Seatback
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  7. REMOVE FRONT SEATBACK COVER 
    1. w/Seat hook: Remove the screw and seatback hook.
      Fig 7: Removing Screw And Seatback Hook
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. Fold back the seatback cover to the top of the seat, and then detach the 4 claws and remove the 2 headrest supports.
    3. Remove the hog rings and the seatback cover (with pad) from the seatback spring.
    4. Remove the hog ring and remove the seatback cover.
      Fig 8: Detaching Claws And Removing Headrest Supports
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  8. REMOVE FRONT SEAT CUSHION COVER 
    1. Remove the hog rings.
    2. Detach the claw of the front part of the seat adjuster.
    3. Detach the hook and remove the seat cushion cover (with pad) from the seat adjuster.
    4. Remove the hog rings and seat cushion cover.
      Fig 9: Detaching Front Part Seat Adjuster Claws
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  9. REMOVE RECLINING ADJUSTER INSIDE COVER LH 
    1. Remove the screw and inside cover.
  10. REMOVE RECLINING ADJUSTER INSIDE COVER RH 
    1. Remove the screw and inside cover.
  11. REMOVE SEAT POSITION AIRBAG SENSOR (See  REMOVAL  )
