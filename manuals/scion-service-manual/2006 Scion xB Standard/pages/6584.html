Removal (2005/12- )

  1. PRECAUTION 
    1. (see PRECAUTION ).
  2. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL 
    CAUTION: Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to prevent airbag and seat belt pretensioner activation.
  3. PLACE FRONT WHEELS FACING STRAIGHT AHEAD 
  4. REMOVE STEERING PAD ASSEMBLY (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  5. REMOVE STEERING WHEEL ASSEMBLY 
    1. Disconnect the connector.
    2. Remove the steering wheel set nut.
    3. Put matchmarks on the steering wheel and main shaft.
    4. Using SST, remove the steering wheel.

      SST 09950-50013 (09951-05010, 09952-05010, 09953-05020, 09954-05021) 

      Fig 1: Putting Matchmarks On Steering Wheel And Main Shaft
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  6. REMOVE STEERING COLUMN COVER 
    1. Remove the 3 screws and column cover lower.
      Fig 2: Removing Column Cover Lower Screws
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. Remove the 2 bolts and column cover upper.
      Fig 3: Removing Column Cover Upper Bolts
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  7. DISCONNECT FLOOR SHIFT PARKING LOCK CABLE ASSEMBLY (for Automatic Transaxle) 
    1. With the key in the ACC position, detach the claw and pull out the floor shift parking lock cable.
  8. REMOVE SPIRAL CABLE SUB-ASSEMBLY (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  9. REMOVE HEADLIGHT DIMMER SWITCH ASSEMBLY (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  10. REMOVE WINDSHIELD WIPER SWITCH ASSEMBLY (See  REMOVAL  ) 
    Fig 4: Disconnecting Floor Shift Parking Lock Cable Assembly (For Automatic Transaxle)
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  11. REMOVE STEERING COLUMN HOLE COVER PLATE 
    1. Remove the clip and column hole cover plate.
  12. DISCONNECT STEERING INTERMEDIATE SHAFT ASSEMBLY 
    1. Put matchmarks on the sliding yoke and intermediate shaft.
    2. Remove the bolt and intermediate shaft.
      Fig 5: Putting Matchmarks On Sliding Yoke And Intermediate Shaft
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  13. REMOVE STEERING INTERMEDIATE SHAFT ASSEMBLY 
    1. Put matchmarks on the main shaft and intermediate shaft.
    2. Remove the bolt and intermediate shaft.
      Fig 6: Putting Matchmarks On Main Shaft And Intermediate Shaft
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  14. REMOVE STEERING COLUMN ASSEMBLY 
    1. Disconnect the connectors and wire harness clamps from the steering column.
    2. Remove the bolt and steering column.
      Fig 7: Removing Steering Column Bolt
