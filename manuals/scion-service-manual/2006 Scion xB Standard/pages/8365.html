Fuel Tank: Removal

  1. DISCHARGE FUEL SYSTEM PRESSURE (See  REMOVAL   ) 
  2. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL 
    CAUTION: Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to prevent airbag and seat belt pretensioner activation.
  3. REMOVE REAR SEAT CUSHION ASSEMBLY (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  4. REMOVE REAR SEAT CUSHION SUPPORT BRACKET (See  REMOVAL   ) 
  5. REMOVE REAR FLOOR SERVICE HOLE COVER (See  REMOVAL   ) 
  6. REMOVE FUEL PUMP TUBE SUB-ASSEMBLY (See  REMOVAL   ) 
  7. REMOVE NO. 2 FUEL EVAPORATION TUBE SUB-ASSEMBLY (See  REMOVAL   ) 
  8. REMOVE EXHAUST PIPE ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the exhaust pipe assembly (see REMOVAL ).
  9. DRAIN FUEL 
  10. DISCONNECT NO. 2 PARKING BRAKE CABLE ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the 2 bolts and disconnect the cable clamp as shown in illustration A below.
  11. DISCONNECT NO. 3 PARKING BRAKE CABLE ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the 2 bolts and disconnect the cable clamp as shown in illustration B below.
  12. REMOVE NO. 1 FUEL TANK PROTECTOR 
    1. Remove the 2 clips, fuel tank protector bracket and protector as shown in illustration G below.
  13. REMOVE FUEL TANK ASSEMBLY 
    1. Disconnect the fuel tank to filler pipe hose from the fuel tank as shown in illustration C below.
    2. Disconnect the fuel vapor hose from the fuel tank as shown in illustration C below.
    3. Disconnect the fuel tank vent hose from the canister as shown in illustration D below.

      HINT:

      Pull up the retainer and pull out the vent hose.

    4. Disconnect the fuel pump tube as shown in illustration E below.
      1. Disengage the lock claw by lifting up the checker, as shown in the illustration.
        Fig 1: Disengaging Lock Claw By Lifting Up Checker
        Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
      2. Check for dirt or mud on the pipe and around the connector before disconnection. Clean as necessary.
        Fig 2: Disconnecting Connector And Pipe
        Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
      3. Disconnect the connector and pipe by hand.
      4. If the connector and the pipe are stuck, pinch the connector, and push and pull the pipe to disconnect it.
        NOTE: Do not use any tools.
      5. Check for dirt or mud on the seal surface of the disconnected pipe. Clean as necessary.
      6. To protect the disconnected pipe and connector from damage and contamination, cover it with a plastic bag.
        Fig 3: Covering Disconnected Pipe And Connector With Plastic Bag
        Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    5. Remove the 4 bolts and fuel tank as shown in illustration A, B, C and F below.
    Fig 4: Removing Fuel Tank
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  14. REMOVE FUEL PUMP TUBE SUB-ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the tube joint clip, and then pull out the pump tube (see REMOVAL  ).
      Fig 5: Removing Fuel Pump Tube
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  15. REMOVE NO. 2 FUEL EVAPORATION TUBE SUB-ASSEMBLY 
    1. Pinch the No. 2 fuel evaporation tube clip, and then pull out the No. 2 fuel evaporation tube (see REMOVAL  ).
  16. REMOVE NO.3 FUEL TUBE CLAMP 
    1. Detach the 2 claws and remove the clamp.
      Fig 6: Removing Fuel Tube Clamp
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  17. REMOVE CHECK VALVE PROTECTOR 
    1. Remove the 3 bolts and protector.
      Fig 7: Removing Check Valve Protector
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  18. REMOVE NO. 2 FUEL EVAPORATION TUBE SUB-ASSEMBLY 
    1. Pinch the tube connector clip, and then pull out the evaporation tube.
      Fig 8: Removing Fuel Evaporation Tube
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  19. REMOVE FUEL TANK VENT HOSE 
    1. Pull up the retainer and pull out the vent hose.
  20. REMOVE CHECK VALVE SUPPORT 
    1. Remove the 3 bolts and valve support.
      Fig 9: Removing Check Valve Support
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  21. REMOVE FUEL TANK INLET VALVE ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the inlet valve from the fuel tank.
  22. REMOVE NO. 1 FUEL TUBE CLAMP 
    1. Detach the 2 claws and remove the clamp.
      Fig 10: Removing Fuel Tube Clamp
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  23. REMOVE FUEL TANK SIDE PLATE 
    1. Remove the side 4 plates from the fuel tank.
      Fig 11: Removing Fuel Tank Side Plate
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  24. REMOVE FUEL TANK TO FILLER PIPE HOSE 
    1. Loosen the clamp.
    2. Remove the hose from the fuel tank inlet pipe.
