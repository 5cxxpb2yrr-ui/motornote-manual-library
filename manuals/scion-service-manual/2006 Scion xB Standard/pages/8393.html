Ventilation Valve: Installation

  1. INSTALL VENTILATION VALVE 
    1. Apply adhesive to 2 or 3 threads of the valve.
      Fig 1: Applying Adhesive To Threads Of Ventilation Valve
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

      Adhesive: Part No. 08833-00070, THREE BOND 1324 or equivalent. 

    2. Install the ventilation valve.

      Torque: 27 N*m (275 kgf*cm, 20 ft.*lbf) 

  2. CONNECT VENTILATION HOSE 
    1. Connect the ventilation hose to the ventilation valve.
      Fig 2: Connecting Ventilation Hose To Ventilation Valve
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  3. INSTALL NO. 2 CYLINDER HEAD COVER (See  INSTALLATION  )
