Rear Combination Light Assembly: Disassembly

HINT:

  • Use the same procedures for the RH side and LH side.
  • The procedures listed below are for the LH side.
  1. REMOVE REAR COMBINATION LIGHT BODY GASKET LH 
  2. REMOVE REAR COMBINATION LIGHT BULB 
    1. Remove the 3 light sockets as shown in the illustration.
    2. Remove the 3 light bulbs from the light sockets.
      Fig 1: Removing Rear Combination Light Bulb
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  3. REMOVE REAR COMBINATION LIGHT LENS LH 
    1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 11 claws and remove the rear combination light lens.
  4. REMOVE REAR COMBINATION LIGHT LENS GASKET LH
