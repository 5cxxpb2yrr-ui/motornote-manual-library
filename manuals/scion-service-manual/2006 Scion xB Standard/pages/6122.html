  2. INSPECT COOLER THERMISTOR 
    1. Remove the cooler thermistor (evaporator temperature sensor).
    2. Measure the resistance of the thermistor.
      Fig 1: Measuring Resistance Of Thermistor
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

      Standard resistance 

      TESTER CONNECTION AND SPECIFIED CONDITION CHART

      Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
      1 - 2 -10°C (14°F) 7.30 to 9.10 kΩ
      1 - 2 -5°C (23°F) 5.65 to 6.95 kΩ
      1 - 2 0°C (32°F) 4.40 to 5.35 kΩ
      1 - 2 5°C (41°F) 3.40 to 4.15 kΩ
      1 - 2 10°C (50°F) 2.70 to 3.25 kΩ
      1 - 2 15°C (59°F) 2.14 to 2.58 kΩ
      1 - 2 20°C (68°F) 1.71 to 2.05 kΩ
      1 - 2 25°C (77°F) 1.38 to 1.64 kΩ
      1 - 2 30°C (86°F) 1.11 to 1.32 kΩ
      NOTE:
      • Even slightly touching the thermistor may change the resistance value. Be sure to hold the connector of the sensor.
      • When measuring the resistance, the thermistor temperature must be the same as the ambient temperature.

      HINT:

      As the temperature increases, the resistance decreases (see graph).

    NG: REPLACE COOLER THERMISTOR 

    OK : Go to Next Step 

  3. CHECK WIRE HARNESS (COOLER THERMISTOR - AIR CONDITIONING AMPLIFIER) 
    1. Disconnect the A6 thermistor connector.
    2. Disconnect the A17 amplifier connector.
    3. Measure the resistance of the wire harness side connectors.
