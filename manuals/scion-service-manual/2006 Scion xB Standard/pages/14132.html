Certification Label Criteria

A replacement certification label may be requested if:

  • The vehicle is in an accident and the label is damaged or is attached to a part that will be replaced during the repair.
  • The label is stolen.
NOTE:
  • Processing of a replacement label will be delayed if the original (or copy/picture) certification label does NOT accompany the request. 
  • A replacement label MAY NOT be available if the vehicle is more than 5 years old.

Procurement Procedure

To request a replacement label, complete a copy of the form at the end of this bulletin. Your dealer parts account will be billed $40.00  for each replacement of a damaged or stolen label.

NOTE: All replacement labels for damaged and/or stolen vehicles are subject to approval by the Technical Compliance Department. If you have any specific questions, contact (310) 468-3390.
