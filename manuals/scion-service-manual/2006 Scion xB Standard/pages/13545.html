DTC C1224/44 NE Signal Circuit: Description

The skid control ECU receives engine revolution speed signals (NE signals) from the ECM.

TROUBLE AREA INDEX REFERENCE

DTC No. DTC Detection Condition Trouble Area
C1224/44 Detection of either condition 1. or 2.:
  1. When vehicle speed is 30 km/h (19 mph) or more and data reception from ECM is in normal condition, open or short circuit of engine revolution signal continues for 10 seconds or more
  2. When TRAC is in operation, open or short circuit of engine revolution signal continues for 0.24 seconds or more
