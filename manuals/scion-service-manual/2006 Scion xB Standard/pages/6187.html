Heater Control Assembly: Installation

  1. INSTALL HEATER SWITCH ASSEMBLY 
    1. Install the heater switch with the 2 screws.
      Fig 1: Installing Heater Switch With 2 Screws
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  2. INSTALL AIR INLET DAMPER CONTROL LEVER 
    1. Attach the claw to install the air inlet damper control lever.
  3. INSTALL HEATER CONTROL AND ACCESSORY ASSEMBLY 
    1. Connect the inner cable end of the air mix damper control cable on the heater control and accessory assembly.
      Fig 2: Connecting Inner Cable End Of Air Mix Damper Control Cable
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. Connect the outer cable of the air mix damper control cable on the cable clamp.
      NOTE:
      • Be careful not to bend the cable wire.
      • If the cable wire bends, the air mix damper control cable operation may be affected.

      HINT:

      • Operating the heater control knob, check that it properly stops at both ends of MAX. COOL and MAX. HOT, and no recoil is identified.
      • Check that the outer cable is not detached (moved) from the heater control and accessory assembly when the cable is pulled.
        Fig 3: Connecting Outer Cable Of Air Mix Damper Control Cable On Cable Clamp
        Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    3. Connect the inner cable end of the defroster damper control cable on the heater control and accessory assembly.
    4. Connect the outer cable of the defroster damper control cable on the cable clamp.
      NOTE:
      • Be careful not to bend the cable wire.
      • If the cable wire bends, the heater control and accessory assembly operation may be affected.
        Fig 4: Connecting Outer Cable Of Defroster Damper Control Cable On Cable Clamp
        Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

      HINT:

      • Operating the heater control knob, check that it properly stops at both ends of FACE and DEF, and no recoil is identified.
      • Check that the outer cable is not detached (moved) from the heater control and accessory assembly when the cable is pulled.
    5. Connect the connector and install the heater control and accessory assembly.
    6. Connect the heater control and accessory assembly, and attach the 3 screws.
  4. INSTALL INSTRUMENT CLUSTER FINISH PANEL CENTER 
    1. Connect the connectors.
      Fig 5: Installing Instrument Cluster Finish Panel Center
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. Attach the 6 clips and 4 claws to install the instrument cluster finish panel.
    3. Install the 2 screws.
      Fig 6: Installing Instrument Cluster Finish Panel
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  5. INSTALL HEATER CONTROL KNOB 
    1. Install the 3 heater control knobs.
