Inspection Procedure

HINT:

  • If troubleshooting (wire harness inspection) is difficult to perform, remove the front passenger seat installation bolts to see the undersurface of the seat cushion.
  • In the above case, hold the seat so that it does not fall down. Holding the seat for a long period of time may cause a problem, such as seat rail deformation. Hold the seat only as necessary.
  1. CHECK FOR DTC 
    1. Turn the ignition switch ON.
    2. Clear the DTCs stored in the memory (see DTC CHECK / CLEAR  ).

      HINT:

      First clear DTCs stored in the occupant classification ECU and then in the center airbag sensor.

    3. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
    4. Turn the ignition switch ON.
    5. Check the DTCs (see DTC CHECK / CLEAR  ).

      OK: 

      DTC B1793 is not output. 

      HINT:

      Codes other than DTC B1793 may be output at this time, but they are not related to this check.

    OK : USE SIMULATION METHOD TO CHECK 

    NG : Go to Next Step 

  2. CHECK CONNECTION OF CONNECTOR 
    1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
    2. Disconnect the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal, and wait for at least 90 seconds.
    3. Check that the connectors are properly connected to the occupant classification ECU and the occupant classification sensors.

      OK: 

      The connectors are properly connected. 

    NG : CONNECT CONNECTOR 

    OK : Go to Next Step 

  3. CHECK FRONT SEAT WIRE RH (SHORT TO B+) 
    1. Disconnect the connectors from the occupant classification ECU and the 4 occupant classification sensors.
    2. Connect the cable to the negative (-) battery terminal.
    3. Turn the ignition switch ON.
    4. Measure the voltage of the wire harness side connector.

      Standard voltage 

      TESTER CONNECTION - SPECIFIED CONDITION (VOLTAGE)

      Tester Connection Specified Condition
      O6-5 (SVC3) - Body ground Below 1 V
      O6-6 (SVC4) - Body ground
      O6-11 (SVC1) - Body ground
      O6-12 (SVC2) - Body ground

    NG : REPAIR OR REPLACE FRONT SEAT WIRE RH 

    Fig 1: Checking Front Seat Wire RH (Short To B+)
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    OK : Go to Next Step 

  4. CHECK FRONT SEAT WIRE RH (SHORT TO GROUND) 
    1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
    2. Disconnect the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal.
    3. Measure the resistance of the wire harness side connector.

      Standard resistance 

      TESTER CONNECTION - SPECIFIED CONDITION (RESISTANCE)

      Tester Connection Specified Condition
      O6-5 (SVC3) - Body ground 1 MΩ or higher
      O6-6 (SVC4) - Body ground
      O6-11 (SVC1) - Body ground
      O6-12 (SVC2) - Body ground

    NG : REPAIR OR REPLACE FRONT SEAT WIRE RH 

    Fig 2: Identifying Occupant Classification ECU Terminals
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    OK : Go to Next Step 

  5. CHECK FRONT SEAT WIRE RH (OPEN) 
    1. Measure the resistance of the wire harness side connectors.
      Fig 3: Checking Front Seat Wire RH (Open)
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

      Standard resistance 

      TESTER CONNECTION - SPECIFIED CONDITION (RESISTANCE)

      Tester Connection Specified Condition
      O6-5 (SVC3) - O8-1 (SVC3) Below 1 Ω
      O6-6 (SVC4) - O10- 1 (SVC4)
      O6-11 (SVC1) - O7-1 (SVC1)
      O6-12 (SVC2) - O9-1 (SVC2)

    NG : REPAIR OR REPLACE FRONT SEAT WIRE RH 

    OK : Go to Next Step 

  6. CHECK FRONT SEAT WIRE RH (SHORT) 
    1. Measure the resistance of the wire harness side connector.

      Standard resistance 

      TESTER CONNECTION - SPECIFIED CONDITION (RESISTANCE)

      Tester Connection Specified Condition
      O6-5 (SVC3) - O6-6 (SVC4) 1 MΩ or higher
      O6-6 (SVC4) - O6-11 (SVC1)
      O6-11 (SVC1) - O6-12 (SVC2)
      O6-12 (SVC2) - O6-5 (SVC3)
      O6-12 (SVC2) - O6-6 (SVC4)
      O6-11 (SVC1) - O6-5 (SVC3)

    NG : REPAIR OR REPLACE FRONT SEAT WIRE RH 

    Fig 4: Checking Front Seat Wire RH (Short)
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    OK : Go to Next Step 

  7. CHECK FOR DTC 
    1. Connect the connectors to the occupant classification ECU and the 4 occupant classification sensors.
    2. Connect the cable to the negative (-) battery terminal.
    3. Turn the ignition switch ON.
    4. Clear the DTCs stored in the memory (see DTC CHECK / CLEAR  ).

      HINT:

      First clear DTCs stored in the occupant classification ECU and then in the center airbag sensor.

    5. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
    6. Turn the ignition switch ON.
    7. Check the DTCs (see DTC CHECK / CLEAR  ).

      OK: 

      DTC B1793 is not output. 

      HINT:

      Codes other than DTC B1793 may be output at this time, but they are not related to this check.

    OK : USE SIMULATION METHOD TO CHECK 

    NG : Go to Next Step 

  8. REPLACE OCCUPANT CLASSIFICATION ECU 
    1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
    2. Disconnect the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal.
    3. Replace the occupant classification ECU (see REMOVAL ).

      HINT:

      Perform the inspection using parts from a normal vehicle if possible.

    NEXT 

  9. PERFORM ZERO POINT CALIBRATION 
    1. Connect the cable to the negative (-) battery terminal.
    2. Connect the intelligent tester to the DLC3.
    3. Turn the ignition switch ON.
    4. Using the intelligent tester, perform "Zero point calibration" (see INITIALIZATION  ).

      OK: 

      "COMPLETED" is displayed. 

    NG : Go to step   12 

    OK : Go to Next Step 

  10. PERFORM SENSITIVITY CHECK 
    1. Using the intelligent tester, perform the "Sensitivity check" (see INITIALIZATION  ).

      Standard value: 

      27 to 33 kg (59.52 to 72.75 lb) 

    NG : Go to step   12 

    OK : Go to Next Step 

  11. CHECK FOR DTC 
    1. Connect the cable to the negative (-) battery terminal.
    2. Turn the ignition switch ON.
    3. Clear the DTCs stored in the memory (see DTC CHECK / CLEAR  ).

      HINT:

      First clear DTCs stored in the occupant classification ECU and then in the center airbag sensor.

    4. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
    5. Turn the ignition switch ON.
    6. Check the DTCs (see DTC CHECK / CLEAR  ).

      OK: 

      DTC B1793 is not output. 

      HINT:

      Codes other than DTC B1793 may be output at this time, but they are not related to this check.

    OK : END 

    NG : Go to Next Step 

  12. REPLACE FRONT SEAT ASSEMBLY RH 
    1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
    2. Disconnect the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal.
    3. Replace the front seat RH (see REMOVAL ).

    NEXT 

  13. PERFORM ZERO POINT CALIBRATION 
    1. Connect the cable to the negative (-) battery terminal.
    2. Connect the intelligent tester to the DLC3.
    3. Turn the ignition switch ON.
    4. Using the intelligent tester, perform "Zero point calibration" (see INITIALIZATION  ).

      HINT:

      "COMPLETED" is displayed on the tester when the calibration is completed.

    NEXT 

  14. PERFORM SENSITIVITY CHECK 
    1. Using the intelligent tester, perform the "Sensitivity check" (see INITIALIZATION  ).

      Standard value: 

      27 to 33 kg (59.52 to 72.75 lb) 

    NEXT : END
