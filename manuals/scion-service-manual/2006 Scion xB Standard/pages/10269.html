Front Stabilizer Bar: Removal

  1. REMOVE FRONT WHEEL 
  2. REMOVE HOOD SUB-ASSEMBLY 
  3. SUSPEND ENGINE ASSEMBLY 
  4. DISCONNECT FRONT SUSPENSION NO. 1 LOWER ARM SUB-ASSEMBLY LH (See  FRONT SUSPENSION LOWER NO. 1 ARM (FOR AUTOMATIC TRANSAXLE)   ) 
  5. DISCONNECT FRONT SUSPENSION NO. 1 LOWER ARM SUB-ASSEMBLY RH (See  FRONT SUSPENSION LOWER NO. 1 ARM (FOR AUTOMATIC TRANSAXLE)   ) 
  6. DISCONNECT FRONT STABILIZER BAR (See  FRONT SUSPENSION LOWER NO. 1 ARM (FOR AUTOMATIC TRANSAXLE)   ) 
  7. DISCONNECT POWER STEERING LINK ASSEMBLY (See  FRONT SUSPENSION LOWER NO. 1 ARM (FOR AUTOMATIC TRANSAXLE)   ) 
  8. DISCONNECT PRESSURE FEED TUBE ASSEMBLY (See  FRONT SUSPENSION LOWER NO. 1 ARM (FOR AUTOMATIC TRANSAXLE)   ) 
  9. DISCONNECT FRONT SUSPENSION CROSSMEMBER SUB-ASSEMBLY (See  INSPECTION   ) 
  10. REMOVE FRONT STABILIZER BAR 
    1. Remove the 4 bolts, 2 bushes and No. 1 stabilizer bracket LH and RH.
    2. Remove the stabilizer bar.
