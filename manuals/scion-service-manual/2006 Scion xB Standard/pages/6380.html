Inspection Procedure

  1. PREPARE FOR INSPECTION 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to the LOCK position.
    2. Disconnect the negative (-) terminal cable from the battery, and wait for at least 90 seconds.
    3. Disconnect the connectors from the center airbag sensor assembly (see ON-VEHICLE INSPECTION  ).

    NEXT 

  2. CHECK WIRE HARNESS (CENTER AIRBAG SENSOR ASSEMBLY - BATTERY) 
    1. Connect the negative (-) terminal cable to the battery, and wait for at least 2 seconds.
    2. Measure the voltage according to the value(s) in the table below.

      Standard voltage 

      TESTER CONNECTION - SPECIFIED CONDITION (VOLTAGE)

      Tester Connection Condition Specified Condition
      A10-5 (IG2) - Body ground Ignition switch ON 11 to 14 V

    NG : REPAIR OR REPLACE HARNESS AND CONNECTOR, CHARGING SYSTEM AND BATTERY 

    Fig 1: Checking Wire Harness (Center Airbag Sensor Assembly - Battery)
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    OK : Go to Next Step 

  3. CHECK WIRE HARNESS (CENTER AIRBAG SENSOR ASSEMBLY - BODY GROUND) 
    1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

      Standard resistance 

      TESTER CONNECTION - SPECIFIED CONDITION (RESISTANCE)

      Tester Connection Specified Condition
      A10-27 (E1) - Body ground Below 1 Ω
      A10-28 (E2) - Body ground

    NG : REPAIR OR REPLACE HARNESS AND CONNECTOR 

    Fig 2: Checking Wire Harness (Center Airbag Sensor Assembly - Body Ground)
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    OK : Go to Next Step 

  4. CHECK SRS WARNING LIGHT 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to the LOCK position.
    2. Disconnect the negative (-) terminal cable from the battery, and wait for at least 90 seconds.
    3. Connect the center airbag sensor assembly connector.
    4. Connect the negative (-) terminal cable to the battery, and wait for at least 2 seconds.
    5. Turn the ignition switch to the ON position, and wait for at least 6 seconds.
    6. Operate all components of the electrical system (defogger, wiper, headlight, heater, blower, etc.) and check that SRS warning light does not come on.

      OK: 

      SRS warning light does not come on. 

    NG : REPLACE CENTER AIRBAG SENSOR ASSEMBLY 

    OK : END
