Open in CAN Main Bus Line: Description

The CAN main bus line and DLC3 sub bus line may be disconnected when the resistance between terminals 6 (CANH) and 14 (CANL) of the DLC3 is 69 Ωor higher.

SYMPTOM DESCRIPTION

Symptom Trouble Area
Resistance between terminals 6 (CANH) and 14 (CANL) of DLC3 is more than 69 Ω
  • CAN main bus line or connector
  • ECM
  • A/C amplifier
  • DLC3 sub bus line and connector
  • Junction connector
