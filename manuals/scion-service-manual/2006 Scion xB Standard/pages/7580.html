Rear Bumper: Removal

  1. REMOVE REAR BUMPER SIDE SEAL LH 
    1. Using a clip remover, remove the 2 clips and screw.
    2. Using a screwdriver, detach the claw and remove the side seal.

      HINT:

      Tape the screwdriver tip before use.

  2. REMOVE REAR BUMPER SIDE SEAL RH 

    HINT:

    Use the same procedures described for the LH side.

    Fig 1: Removing Clips And Screw
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  3. REMOVE REAR BUMPER COVER 
    1. Put protective tape under the quarter panel.
    2. Using a screwdriver, detach the 2 claws and remove the 6 bolts and bumper cover.

      HINT:

      Tape the screwdriver tip before use.

    3. Disconnect the rear side marker light wire harness's connector from the vehicle body.
    4. Disconnect the wire harness's connectors and detach the clamps. Then, remove the wire harness.
  4. REMOVE REAR BUMPER SHIELD BRACKET CENTER 
    1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 8 claws and remove the 3 shield brackets.

      HINT:

      Tape the screwdriver tip before use.

  5. REMOVE REAR BUMPER REINFORCEMENT SUBASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the 6 nuts and reinforcement.
  6. REMOVE REAR BUMPER SIDE RETAINER LH 
    1. Remove the 2 screws and 2 side retainers.
  7. REMOVE REAR BUMPER SIDE RETAINER RH 
    1. Remove the 2 screws and 2 side retainers.
