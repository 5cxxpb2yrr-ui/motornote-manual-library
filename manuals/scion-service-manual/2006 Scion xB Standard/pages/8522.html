Open in One Side of CAN Sub Bus Line: Description

If 2 or more ECUs and/or sensors do not appear on the intelligent tester's "BUS CHECK" screen via the CAN VIM, one side of the CAN sub bus line may be open (One side of the CANH [sub bus line] /CANL [sub bus line] of the ECU and/or sensor is open).
