Front Shock Absorber With Coil Spring: Inspection

  1. INSPECT FRONT SHOCK ABSORBER ASSEMBLY LH 
    1. Compress and extend the shock absorber rod and check that there is no abnormal resistance or unusual sound during operation. If there is any abnormality, replace the shock absorber with a new one.
