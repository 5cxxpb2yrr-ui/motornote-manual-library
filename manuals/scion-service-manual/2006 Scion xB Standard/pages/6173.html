Air Conditioning: Condenser: Installation

  1. INSTALL CONDENSER WITH RECEIVER ASSEMBLY 
    1. Install the condenser w/receiver.
      Fig 1: Installing Condenser W/Receiver
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  2. CONNECT LIQUID TUBE SUB-ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the vinyl tape attached to the pipe and condenser.
    2. Sufficiently apply compressor oil to a new O-ring and hose joint.

      Compressor oil: 

      ND-OIL 8 or equivalent 

    3. Install the O-ring to the liquid tube.
    4. Install the liquid tube sub-assembly to the condenser with the bolt.

      Torque: 5.4 N*m (55 kgf*cm, 48 in.*lbf) 

      Fig 2: Installing Liquid Tube Sub-Assembly To Condenser With Bolt
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  3. CONNECT NO. 1 COOLER REFRIGERANT DISCHARGE HOSE 
    1. Remove the vinyl tape attached to the hose and condenser.
    2. Sufficiently apply compressor oil to a new O-ring and hose joint.

      Compressor oil: 

      ND-OIL 8 or equivalent 

    3. Install the O-ring to the cooler refrigerant discharge hose.
    4. Connect the No. 1 cooler refrigerant discharge hose to the condenser with the bolt.

      Torque: 5.4 N*m (55 kgf*cm, 48 in.*lbf) 

      Fig 3: Connecting No. 1 Cooler Refrigerant Discharge Hose To Condenser With Bolt
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
