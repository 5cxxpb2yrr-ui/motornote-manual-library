Tire & Wheel: Troubleshooting: Inspection

  1. INSPECT TIRE 
    1. Check the tires for wear and proper inflation pressure.

      Standard cold tire inflation pressure 

      STANDARD COLD TIRE INFLATION PRESSURE

      Tire Size Front Pressure Rear Pressure
      185/60R15 84T 200 kPa (2.0 kgf/cm2 , 29 psi) 200 kPa (2.0 kgf/cm2 , 29 psi)
    2. Using a dial indicator, check the tire runout.

      Standard tire runout: 1.4 mm (0.055 in.) or less

  3. INSPECT WHEEL BALANCE 
    1. Check and adjust the off-the-car balance.
    2. If necessary, check and adjust the on-the-car balance.

      Standard imbalance after adjustment: 8.0 g (0.018 lb) or less 

  4. INSPECT BEARING LOOSENESS 
    1. Inspect front bearing looseness. See ON-VEHICLE INSPECTION .
    2. Inspect rear bearing looseness. See ON-VEHICLE INSPECTION .
  5. INSPECT AXLE HUB RUNOUT 
    1. Inspect front axle hub runout. See ON-VEHICLE INSPECTION .
    2. Inspect rear axle hub runout. See ON-VEHICLE INSPECTION .
