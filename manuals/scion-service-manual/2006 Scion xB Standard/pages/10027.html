Introduction

2006 model year Scion xA and xB vehicles are equipped with anti-rust covers to protect the disc brake rotors from weather elements and initial rust before the vehicle is delivered to the customer. The anti-rust covers should remain on the wheels as long as possible before delivery. This bulletin provides the removal procedure.

NOTE:
  • Do NOT remove the anti-rust covers during Pre-Delivery Service (PDS) if the vehicle will be stored after PDS.
  • Remove the anti-rust covers just prior to delivery to customers.

Applicable Vehicles

  • 2006  model year Scion xA  and xB  vehicles.

Removal Procedure

  1. Refer to Fig 1 for the outside disc brake anti-rust cover attachment.
    Fig 1: Removing Outside Disc Brake Anti-Rust Cover
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  2. Move the 2 clips in the direction of the arrows to release the lock.
    Fig 2: Moving Clips
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  3. Push in the 2 clips to unhook the hooks from the wheel holes and pull the cover to remove it.
    Fig 3: Pulling Cover
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    NOTE:
    • If the clip falls off the cover, be sure to recover the clip.
    • Avoid scratching the wheel when removing the cover.

    DISPOSAL PROCEDURE: 

    When disposing of the cover, obey local sanitation rules. 

    Materials: 

    • Cover: Fiber molded (paper) 
    • Clip: Polypropeleyne
