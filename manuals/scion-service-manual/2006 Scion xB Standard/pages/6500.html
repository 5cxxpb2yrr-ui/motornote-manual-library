Front Seat Outer Belt Assembly With PRETENSIONER: Removal

HINT:

  • Use the same procedures for the RH side and LH side.
  • The procedures listed below are for the RH side.
  1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL 
    NOTE: Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to prevent airbag and seat belt pretensioner activation.
  2. REMOVE FRONT DOOR SCUFF PLATE RH (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  3. REMOVE COWL SIDE TRIM BOARD RH (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  4. REMOVE FRONT DOOR OPENING TRIM WEATHERSTRIP RH 
  5. REMOVE REAR DOOR SCUFF PLATE RH (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  6. REMOVE REAR DOOR OPENING TRIM WEATHERSTRIP RH 
  7. REMOVE CENTER PILLAR GARNISH LOWER RH (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  8. REMOVE FRONT SEAT OUTER BELT ASSEMBLY RH 
    CAUTION: Carefully read the notices for the pretensioner in the SRS airbag system section and the front seat outer belt section (see AIRBAG SYSTEM ).
    1. Remove the floor anchor cover.
    2. Remove the bolt and the outer belt on the floor anchor side.
    3. Remove the shoulder anchor cover.
    4. Remove the bolt and outer belt on the shoulder anchor side.
    5. Disconnect the pretensioner connector as shown in the illustration.
    6. Remove the 2 bolts on the retractor side and remove the outer belt completely.
  9. REMOVE CENTER PILLAR GARNISH UPPER RH (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  10. REMOVE FRONT SHOULDER BELT ANCHOR ADJUSTER ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the 2 bolts and anchor adjuster.
