Front Door Lock: Inspection

  1. INSPECT FRONT DOOR LOCK ACTUATOR ASSEMBLY LH 
    1. Apply battery voltage to the actuator terminals and check operation of the door lock motor.

      OK : Go to Next Step 

      Fig 1: Inspecting Front Door Lock Actuator Assembly LH
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
      TESTER CONNECTION SPECIFIED CONDITION

      Measurement Condition Specified Condition
      Battery positive (+) --> Terminal 2
      Battery negative (-) --> Terminal 1      		 Lock
      Battery positive (+) --> Terminal 1
      Battery negative (-) --> Terminal 2      		 Unlock

      If the result is not as specified, replace the actuator.

    2. Measure the resistance of the door lock and unlock switch.

      Standard resistance 

      Fig 2: Measuring Resistance Of Door Lock And Unlock Switch
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
      TESTER CONNECTION SPECIFIED CONDITION

      Tester Connection Switch Condition Specified Condition
      3 - 5 Lock Below 1 Ω
      3 - 5, 3 - 6 OFF 10 kΩ or higher
      3 - 6 Unlock Below 1 Ω

      If the result is not as specified, replace the door lock assembly.

    3. Measure the resistance of the position switch.

      Standard resistance 

      MEASUREMENT SPECIFIED CONDITION

      Tester Connection Switch Condition Specified Condition
      3 - 4 Lock 10 kΩ or higher
      3 - 4 Unlock Below 1 Ω

      If the result is not as specified, replace the actuator.
