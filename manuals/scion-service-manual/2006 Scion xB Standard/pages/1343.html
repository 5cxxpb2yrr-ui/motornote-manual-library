Inspection Procedure

  1. CHOOSE DIAGNOSIS METHOD 
    1. Choose the diagnosis method.

      Method 

      METHOD REFERENCE

      Method Proceed to
      Using intelligent tester A
      Not using intelligent tester B

    B: Go to step   3 

    A: Go to next step 

  2. PERFORM ACTIVE TEST BY INTELLIGENT TESTER (ABS MTR RELAY) 
    1. Select the ACTIVE TEST, generate a control command, and then check that the ABS MTR relay operates.

      Skid control ECU with actuator 

      ITEM VEHICLE CONDITION DIAGNOSTICS CHART

      Item Vehicle Condition / Test Details Diagnostics Note
      ABS MOT RELAY Turn ABS MTR relay ON / OFF Operation sound of MTR can be heard

      OK: Operation sound of ABS MTR is heard. 

      HINT:

      When replacing the skid control ECU with actuator, perform the zero point calibration (see CALIBRATION  ).

    OK: REPLACE BRAKE ACTUATOR ASSEMBLY 

    NG: Go to Next Step 

  3. CHECK WIRE HARNESS (SKID CONTROL ECU - BATTERY AND BODY GROUND) 
    1. Disconnect the S3 ECU connector.
    2. Measure the voltage of the wire harness side connector.

      Standard voltage 

      TESTER CONNECTION SPECIFIED CONDITION

      Tester Connection Specified Condition
      S3-31 (+BS) - Body ground 10 to 14 V
    3. Measure the resistance of the wire harness side connector.

      Standard resistance 

      TESTER CONNECTION SPECIFIED CONDITION

      Tester Connection Specified Condition
      S3-32 (GND1) - Body ground Below 1 Ω
      S3-1 (GND2) - Body ground

      HINT:

      When replacing the skid control ECU with actuator, perform the zero point calibration (see CALIBRATION  ).

    NG: REPAIR OR REPLACE HARNESS AND CONNECTOR 

    OK: REPLACE BRAKE ACTUATOR ASSEMBLY
