Front Door: Disassembly

HINT:

  • Use the procedures listed below for the RH side and LH side.
  • The procedures listed below are for the LH side.
  1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL 
    CAUTION: Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to prevent airbag and seat belt pretensioner activation.
  2. REMOVE FRONT DOOR LOWER FRAME BRACKET GARNISH LH 
    1. Using a screwdriver, detach the clip and claw, and remove the garnish.

      HINT:

      Tape the screwdriver tip before use.

      Fig 1: Detaching Clip And Claw
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  3. REMOVE DOOR PULL HANDLE 
    1. Remove the screw, and then remove the handle upward.
      Fig 2: Removing Screw
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  4. REMOVE POWER WINDOW REGULATOR MASTER SWITCH ASSEMBLY 
    1. Using a screwdriver, detach the clip and claw, and remove the armrest base panel together with the switch.

      HINT:

      Tape the screwdriver tip before use.

    2. Disconnect the switch connector.
      Fig 3: Disconnecting Switch Connector
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    3. Remove the 3 screws and switch from the armrest base panel.
      NOTE: Be careful when removing the screw as the master switch may fall and become damaged.
    4. Passenger's side:

      Using a screwdriver, detach the 2 claws and remove the power window regulator switch.

      HINT:

      Tape the screwdriver tip before use.

      NOTE: Be careful when disengaging the claws as the switch may fall and become damaged.
      Fig 4: Removing Screws And Switch From Armrest Base Panel
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  5. REMOVE FRONT DOOR TRIM BOARD SUBASSEMBLY LH 
    1. Remove the screw and clip A as shown in the illustration.
    2. Using a screwdriver, detach the 7 clips.

      HINT:

      Tape the screwdriver tip before use.

    3. Remove the trim board upward.

      HINT:

      In order to prevent the door panel from being damaged, use protective tape to cover the areas indicated by the arrow marks in the illustration.

    4. Using a screwdriver, detach the claws and remove the weatherstrip inner.

      HINT:

      Tape the screwdriver tip before use.

      Fig 5: Removing Front Door Trim Board Subassembly LH
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  6. REMOVE FRONT DOOR INSIDE HANDLE SUBASSEMBLY LH 
    1. Using a screwdriver, detach the claw A as shown in the illustration.

      HINT:

      Tape the screwdriver tip before use.

    2. Move the inside handle in the direction indicated by the arrow mark in the illustration. This will release the claw and allow removal of the inside handle.
    3. Disconnect the cable from the inside handle.
      Fig 6: Removing Front Door Inside Handle Subassembly LH
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  7. REMOVE FRONT DOOR SERVICE HOLE COVER LH 
    1. Remove the 2 screws and bracket.
      Fig 7: Removing Front Door Service Hole Cover LH
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. Remove the service hole cover.
      NOTE: Remove the remaining tape on the door.
  8. REMOVE OUTER REAR VIEW MIRROR ASSEMBLY LH (See  REMOVAL  ) 
  9. REMOVE FRONT DOOR GLASS WEATHERSTRIP ASSEMBLY OUTER LH 
  10. REMOVE FRONT NO. 1 SPEAKER ASSEMBLY (See  REMOVAL  ) 
    Fig 8: Removing Tape On Door
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  11. REMOVE FRONT DOOR GLASS SUB-ASSEMBLY LH 

    HINT:

    Insert a cushion inside the door panel to prevent the glass from being scratched.

    1. Driver's side:

      Temporarily install the window regulator master switch.

    2. Passenger's side:

      Temporarily install the power window regulator switch.

      Fig 9: Removing Bolts Holding Door Glass To Window Regulator
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    3. Move the door glass until the bolts appear in the service holes.
    4. Remove the 2 bolts holding the door glass to the window regulator.
      NOTE: Be careful when removing the bolts as the glass may fall and become damaged.
    5. Remove the door glass in the direction indicated by the arrow marks in the illustration.
      NOTE: Be careful not to damage the glass.
    6. Remove the glass run.
    7. Driver's side:

      Remove the window regulator master switch.

    8. Passenger's side:

      Remove the power window regulator switch.

      Fig 10: Removing Glass
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    9. Using a screwdriver, detach the claw and remove the door window glass clip as indicated by the arrow mark in the illustration.

      HINT:

      Tape the screwdriver tip before use.

      Fig 11: Detaching Claw
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  12. REMOVE FRONT DOOR WINDOW REGULATOR SUB-ASSEMBLY LH 
    1. Disconnect the window regulator connector.
    2. Remove the 6 bolts and window regulator.

      HINT:

      Remove the window regulator through the service hole.

      NOTE: Be careful when removing the bolts as the window regulator may fall and become damaged.
      Fig 12: Removing Bolts And Window Regulator
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  13. REMOVE FRONT POWER WINDOW REGULATOR MOTOR ASSEMBLY LH 
    1. Using a T25 "Torx" driver, remove the 3 screws and motor.
      Fig 13: Removing Screws And Motor
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  14. REMOVE FRONT DOOR FRAME SUB-ASSEMBLY REAR LOWER LH 
    1. Remove the bolt and frame.

      HINT:

      Remove the frame through the service hole.

      NOTE: Be careful when removing the bolt as the frame may fall and become damaged.
      Fig 14: Removing Bolt And Frame
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  15. REMOVE FRONT DOOR LOCK ASSEMBLY LH 
    1. Disconnect the snap and outside locking link from the outside handle as indicated by the black arrow marks in the illustration.
    2. Disconnect the snap and lock open rod from the outside handle as indicated by the white arrow marks in the illustration.
      Fig 15: Disconnecting Snap And Outside Locking Link From Outside Handle
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    3. Twist the knob and remove it, as shown in the illustration.
    4. Using a screwdriver, detach the 2 claws and remove the guide.

      HINT:

      Tape the screwdriver tip before use.

      Fig 16: Twisting Knob
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    5. Remove the bolt.
    6. Using a T30 "Torx" socket, remove the 3 screws and door lock.

      HINT:

      Remove the door lock through the service hole.

      NOTE: Be careful not to drop the window regulator as it may become damaged.
    7. Remove the cable from the lock.
      Fig 17: Removing Screws And Door Lock
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    8. Remove the 2 screws and actuator.
      Fig 18: Removing Screws And Actuator
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  16. REMOVE FRONT DOOR OUTSIDE HANDLE ASSEMBLY LH 
    1. Remove the 2 bolts and handle together with the cylinder.
      NOTE: Be careful when removing the bolts as the outside handle may fall and become damaged.
      Fig 19: Removing Bolts And Handle With Cylinder
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. Unlock the cylinder by turning it as indicated by the arrow mark in the illustration.
    3. Remove the cylinder.
      Fig 20: Unlocking Cylinder
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  17. REMOVE FRONT DOOR CHECK ASSEMBLY LH 
    1. Remove the 3 bolts and door check.

      HINT:

      Remove the check through the service hole.

  18. REMOVE FRONT DOOR WEATHERSTRIP LH 
    1. Using a clip remover, detach the clips and remove the weatherstrip.

      HINT:

      If clips are damaged during removal, replace them.

      Fig 21: Removing Front Door Weatherstrip LH
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
