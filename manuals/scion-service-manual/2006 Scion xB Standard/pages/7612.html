Headlight Assembly: Disassembly

HINT:

  • Use the same procedures for the RH side and LH side.
  • The procedures listed below are for the LH side.
  1. REMOVE HEADLIGHT SOCKET COVER 
    1. Remove the headlight socket cover.
      Fig 1: Removing Headlight Socket Cover

  2. REMOVE NO. 1 HEADLIGHT BULB 
    1. Remove the No. 1 headlight bulb as shown in the illustration.
      Fig 2: Removing Headlight Bulb
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  3. REMOVE CLEARANCE LIGHT BULB 
    1. Remove the clearance light bulb, clearance light socket as shown in the illustration.
    2. Remove the clearance light bulb from the clearance light socket.
