Inspection Procedure

  1. INSPECT BATTERY 
    1. Measure the voltage of the battery.

      Standard voltage: 

      11 to 14 V 

    NG : RECHARGE OR REPLACE BATTERY 

    OK : Go to Next Step 

  2. CHECK CONNECTION OF CONNECTOR 
    1. Disconnect the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal, and wait for at least 90 seconds.
    2. Check that the connector is properly connected to the center airbag sensor.

      OK: 

      Connector is connected. 

    NG : CONNECT CONNECTOR 

    OK : Go to Next Step 

  3. PREPARE FOR INSPECTION 
    CAUTION: Be sure to perform the following procedures before troubleshooting to avoid unexpected airbag deployment.
    1. Disconnect the connector from the center airbag sensor.
    2. Disconnect the connector from the steering pad connector.

    NEXT 

  4. CHECK CENTER AIRBAG SENSOR ASSEMBLY 
    1. Connect the cable to the negative (-) battery terminal, and wait for at least 2 seconds.
    2. Turn the ignition switch ON.
    3. Measure the voltage and resistance of the wire harness side connector.

      Standard voltage 

      TESTER CONNECTION - SPECIFIED CONDITION (VOLTAGE)

      Tester Connection Specified Condition
      A20-5 (IG2) - Body ground 8 to 14 V

      Standard resistance 

      TESTER CONNECTION - SPECIFIED CONDITION (RESISTANCE)

      Tester Connection Specified Condition
      A20-25 (E1) - Body ground Below 1 Ω
      Fig 1: Checking Center Airbag Sensor Assembly
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    NG : REPAIR OR REPLACE HARNESS AND CONNECTOR 

    OK : Go to Next Step 

  5. CHECK COMBINATION METER ASSEMBLY (POWER SOURCE) 
    1. Disconnect the C6 meter connector.
    2. Turn the ignition switch ON.
    3. Measure the voltage of the wire harness side connector.

      Standard voltage 

      TESTER CONNECTION - SPECIFIED CONDITION (VOLTAGE)

      Tester Connection Specified Condition
      C6-23 - Body ground 8 to 14 V

    NG : REPAIR OR REPLACE HARNESS AND CONNECTOR 

    Fig 2: Disconnecting C6 Meter Connector
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    OK : Go to Next Step 

  6. CHECK WIRE HARNESS (CENTER AIRBAG SENSOR - COMBINATION METER AND BODY GROUND) 
    1. Disconnect the A20 ECU connector.
    2. Disconnect the C6 meter connector.
    3. Measure the resistance of the wire harness side connectors.

      Standard resistance 

      TESTER CONNECTION - SPECIFIED CONDITION (RESISTANCE)

      Tester Connection Specified Condition
      A20-14 (LA) - C6-34 Below 1 Ω

    NG : REPAIR OR REPLACE HARNESS AND CONNECTOR 

    Fig 3: Disconnecting A20 ECU Connector
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    OK : Go to Next Step 

  7. CHECK SRS WARNING LIGHT OPERATION 
    1. Disconnect the A20 ECU connector with the C6 meter connector connected.
    2. Turn the ignition switch ON.
    3. Check that the warning light illuminates 10 seconds after turning the ignition switch ON.

      OK: 

      Warning light illuminates 10 seconds after turning ignition switch ON. 

    NG : REPLACE COMBINATION METER ASSEMBLY 

    OK : Go to Next Step 

  8. CHECK CENTER AIRBAG SENSOR ASSEMBLY 
    1. Disconnect the A20 ECU connector.
    2. Turn the ignition switch ON.
    3. Measure the voltage of the wire harness side connector.

      Standard voltage 

      TESTER CONNECTION - SPECIFIED CONDITION (VOLTAGE)

      Tester Connection Specified Condition
      A20-14 (LA) - Body ground 8 to 14 V

    NG : REPAIR OR REPLACE HARNESS AND CONNECTOR 

    Fig 4: Disconnecting A20 ECU Connector
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

    OK : Go to Next Step 

  9. REPLACE CENTER AIRBAG SENSOR ASSEMBLY 
    1. Replace the center airbag sensor.
    2. Check that the SRS warning light illuminates normally.

      OK: 

      SRS warning light illuminates normally. 

    NG : REPLACE COMBINATION METER ASSEMBLY 

    OK : END
