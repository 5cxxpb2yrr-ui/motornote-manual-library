DTC Check / Clear

  1. CHECK DTC 

    HINT:

    When DTC B1150/23 is detected as a result of troubleshooting for the airbag system, perform troubleshooting for the occupant classification system.

    1. Connect the intelligent tester to the DLC3.
    2. Turn the ignition switch ON.
    3. Read the DTC by following the prompts on the tester screen.

      HINT:

      Refer to the intelligent tester operator's manual for further details.

  2. CLEAR DTC 
    1. Connect the intelligent tester to the DLC3.
    2. Turn the ignition switch ON.
    3. Erase the DTC by following the directions on the tester screen.

      HINT:

      Refer to the intelligent tester operator's manual for further details.
