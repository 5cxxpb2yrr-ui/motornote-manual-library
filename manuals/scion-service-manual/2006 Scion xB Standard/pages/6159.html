Heater Radiator Assembly: Disassembly

  1. REMOVE AIR DUCT ASSEMBLY 
    1. Detach the 4 claws and remove the air duct.
      Fig 1: Detaching 4 Claws And Removing Air Duct
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  2. REMOVE HEATER COVER 
    1. Remove the screw.
    2. Detach the 2 claws and remove the heater cover.
      Fig 2: Detaching 2 Claws And Removing Heater Cover
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  3. REMOVE THERMISTOR ASSEMBLY 
    1. Detach the claw and remove the clamp.
    2. Remove the thermistor.
      Fig 3: Removing Thermistor
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  4. REMOVE HEATER RADIATOR UNIT SUB-ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the 4 screws and No. 2 cooler wiring.
      Fig 4: Removing 4 Screws And No. 2 Cooler Wiring
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. Remove the screw and bracket.
    3. Detach the clamp and disconnect the connector.
    4. Remove the heater radiator unit.
      Fig 5: Removing Heater Radiator Unit
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
