System Description

The front airbag sensor RH consists of the diagnostic circuit and front deceleration sensor. The center airbag sensor receives signals from the front deceleration sensor and determines whether or not the SRS should be activated.

DTC B1610/13 is recorded when a malfunction is detected in the front airbag sensor RH circuit.

DTC DETECTION CONDITION AND TROUBLE AREA

DTC No. DTC Detection Condition Trouble Area
B1610/13 When one of following conditions is met:
  • Center airbag sensor receives line short signal, open signal, short to ground signal or B+ short signal in front airbag sensor RH circuit for 2 seconds
  • Front airbag sensor RH malfunction
  • Center airbag sensor malfunction
  • Front airbag sensor RH
  • Center airbag sensor assembly
  • Instrument panel wire
  • Engine room main wire
