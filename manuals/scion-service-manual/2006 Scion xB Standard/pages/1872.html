Charging System: Precaution

  1. Check that the battery cables are connected to the correct terminals. 
  2. Disconnect the battery cables when the battery is quickly charged. 
  3. Do not perform tests with a high voltage insulation resistance tester. 
  4. Never disconnect the battery cable while the engine is running. 
  5. Check that the charging cable nut is tightened on terminal B of the generator and the fuse box. 
  6. If the generator is installed to the vehicle, do not perform checks on terminal F.
