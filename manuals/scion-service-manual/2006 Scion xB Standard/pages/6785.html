DTC P0705 Transmission Range Sensor Circuit Malfunction (PRNDL Input): Description

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

The park/neutral position switch detects the shift lever position and sends signals to the ECM.

DTC DETECTION CONDITION

DTC No. DTC Detection Condition Trouble Area
P0705 2 or more switches are ON simultaneously for P (NSW), R, N (NSW), D, 2 and L positions (2 trip detection logic)
  • Short in park/neutral position switch circuit
  • Park/Neutral position switch
  • ECM
