FRONT SUSPENSION LOWER NO. 1 ARM (for Automatic Transaxle): Inspection

  1. INSPECT FRONT SUSPENSION NO. 1 LOWER ARM SUB-ASSEMBLY LH 
    1. As shown in the illustration, flip the ball joint stud back and forth 5 times before installing the nut.
    2. Using a torque wrench, turn the nut continuously at a rate of 2 to 4 seconds per turn and take the torque reading on the fifth turn.

      HINT:

      A bolt without a torque specification is shown in the standard bolt chart (see TORQUE SPECIFICATIONS ).
