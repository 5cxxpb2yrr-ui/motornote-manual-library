Charging (1NZ-FE): Generator: Disassembly

  1. REMOVE GENERATOR PULLEY 
    1. Hold SST (A) with a torque wrench, and tighten SST (B) clockwise to the specified torque.
      Fig 1: Securing SST To Rotor Shaft
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

      SST 09820-63010 (09820-06010, 09820-06020) 

      Torque: 39 N*m (400 kgf*cm, 29 ft.*lbf) 

      NOTE: Check that SST is secured to the rotor shaft.
    2. Mount SST (C) in a vise.
    3. Insert SST (B) into SST (C), and attach the pulley nut to SST (C).
      Fig 2: Identifying SST Installation Position
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    4. To loosen the pulley nut, turn SST (A) in the direction shown in the illustration.
      Fig 3: Identifying Pull Nut Loosening Direction
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
      NOTE: To prevent damage to the rotor shaft, do not loosen the pulley nut more than one-half of a turn.
    5. Remove the generator from SST (C).
    6. Turn SST (B), and remove SST (A and B).
    7. Remove the pulley nut and pulley.
      Fig 4: Removing SST A And B
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  2. REMOVE REAR END COVER 
    1. Remove the nut and terminal insulator.
    2. Remove the bolt, 3 nuts, plate terminal and end cover.
      Fig 5: Removing Nuts And Terminal Insulator
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  3. REMOVE GENERATOR BRUSH HOLDER ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the brush holder cover from the brush holder.
    2. Remove the 2 screws and brush holder.
  4. REMOVE GENERATOR REGULATOR ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the 3 screws and generator regulator.
      Fig 6: Removing Brush Holder Cover Nuts
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  5. REMOVE GENERATOR HOLDER WITH RECTIFIER 
    1. Remove the 4 screws and holder with rectifier.
      Fig 7: Removing Screws And Holders
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  6. REMOVE GENERATOR RECTIFIER END FRAME 
    1. Remove the 4 rubber insulators.
    2. Remove the seal plate.
    3. Remove the 4 nuts and cord clip.
      Fig 8: Removing Nuts And Cord Clips
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    4. Using SST, remove the rectifier end frame.
      Fig 9: Removing Rectifier End Frame Using SST
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

      SST 09286-46011 

  7. REMOVE GENERATOR ROTOR ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the generator washer from the rotor.
    2. Remove the rotor from the drive end frame.
      NOTE: Do not drop the rotor.
