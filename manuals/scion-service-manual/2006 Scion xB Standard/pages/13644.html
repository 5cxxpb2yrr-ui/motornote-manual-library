Removal (2005/07-2005/12)

HINT:

  • Use the same procedures for the RH side and LH side.
  • The procedures listed below are for the LH side.
  1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL 
    CAUTION: Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to prevent airbag and seat belt pretensioner activation.
  2. REMOVE SEAT TRACK OUTER COVER LH 
    1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 4 claws and remove the seat track outer cover.

      HINT:

      Tape the screwdriver tip before use.

      Fig 1: Locating Claws
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  3. REMOVE SEAT TRACK BRACKET COVER INNER LH 
    1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 2 claws and remove the seat track bracket cover.
      Fig 2: Locating Seat Track Bracket Cover
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002

      HINT:

      Tape the screwdriver tip before use.

  4. REMOVE FRONT SEAT ASSEMBLY 
    1. Remove the 4 bolts.
    2. Disconnect the connectors.
    3. Remove the seat.
      NOTE: Be careful not to damage the vehicle body.
      Fig 3: Removing Bolts
