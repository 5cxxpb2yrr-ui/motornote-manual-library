Engine Hood/Door: Hood: Adjustment

HINT:

  • Centering bolts are used to mount the hood hinge and hood lock. The hood and hood lock cannot be adjusted with the centering bolts on. Substitute the centering bolts with standard bolts (w/washers) when making adjustments.
  • A bolt without a torque specification is shown in the standard bolt chart (SS-2).
    Fig 1: Identifying Centering Bolt And Standard Bolt
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  1. ADJUST HOOD SUB-ASSEMBLY 
    1. Adjust the hood's position.
      1. Loosen the 4 hinge bolts on the hood side.
        Fig 2: Loosening Hinge Bolts On Hood Side
        Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
      2. Move the hood and adjust the clearance between the hood and front fender. Make sure that the clearance is within the standard range.

        Standard clearance (A): 

        4.2 +-1.5 mm (0.165 +-0.059 in.) 

      3. Tighten the hood side hinge bolts after the adjustment.

        Torque: 11.5 N*m (117 kgf*cm, 8.5 ft.*lbf) 

        Fig 3: Adjusting Clearance Between Hood And Front Fender
        Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. Adjust the cushion rubber so that the height of the hood and the fender are aligned.
      1. Raise or lower the hood's front end by turning the cushion rubber. Make sure that the clearance is within the standard range.

        Standard clearance: 

        0.0 +-1.5 mm (0.000 +-0.059 in.) 

        Fig 4: Adjusting Cushion Rubber
        Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    3. Adjust the hood lock.
      1. Remove the 3 clips and grille (See REMOVAL ).
      2. Loosen the 3 hood lock bolts.
        Fig 5: Loosening Hood Lock Bolts
        Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
      3. Adjust the hood lock position so that the striker can enter it smoothly.
      4. Tighten the hood lock bolts after the adjustment.

        Torque: 7.0 N*m (71 kgf*cm, 62 in.*lbf) 

        Fig 6: Tightening Hood Lock Bolts
        Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
