Inspection Procedure

HINT:

Read freeze frame data using the intelligent tester. Freeze frame data records the engine condition when malfunctions are detected. When troubleshooting, freeze frame data can help determine if the vehicle was moving or stationary, if the engine was warmed up or not, if the air-fuel ratio was lean or rich, and other data from the time the malfunction occurred.

  1. CHECK VALVE TIMING (CHECK TIMING CHAIN FOR LOOSENESS AND JUMPED TOOTH) 
    1. Remove the cylinder head cover (see REMOVAL ).
    2. Turn the crankshaft pulley, then align its groove with the timing mark "0" on the timing chain cover.
    3. Check that both timing marks on the camshaft timing sprocket and camshaft timing gear are facing upward as shown in the illustration.

      If not, turn the crankshaft 1 revolution (360°), then align the marks as above.

    NG: ADJUST VALVE TIMING 

    OK: Go to Next Step 

  2. REPLACE ECM 

    After replacing the ECM or adjusting intake valve timing, confirm that the DTC output does not recur.

    1. Connect the intelligent tester to the DLC3.
    2. Turn the ignition switch ON and turn the tester ON.
    3. Clear DTCs (see DTC CHECK / CLEAR  ).
    4. Switch the ECM from normal mode to check mode (see CHECK MODE PROCEDURE  ).
    5. Start the engine and warm it up.
    6. Allow the engine to idle for 1 minute or more, and then drive the vehicle for 1 minute or more.
    7. Confirm that no DTC is set using the tester.
