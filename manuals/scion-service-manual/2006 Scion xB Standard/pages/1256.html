Brake Master Cylinder: Removal

  1. DRAIN BRAKE FLUID 
    NOTE: Wash off brake fluid immediately if it comes in contact with any painted surface.
  2. REMOVE AIR CLEANER CAP SUB-ASSEMBLY 
  3. DISCONNECT CLUTCH RESERVOIR TUBE (for Manual Transaxle) 
    1. Slide the clip and disconnect the clutch reservoir tube.
  4. REMOVE BRAKE MASTER CYLINDER SUBASSEMBLY 
    1. Disconnect the level warning light switch connector.
    2. Using SST, disconnect the 2 brake lines from the brake master cylinder.

      SST 09023-00101 

      Fig 1: Disconnecting Brake Lines From Brake Master Cylinder
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    3. Remove the 2 nuts, and pull out the brake master cylinder.
      Fig 2: Removing Brake Master Cylinder Nuts
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
  5. REMOVE BRAKE MASTER CYLINDER RESERVOIR SUB-ASSEMBLY 
    1. Pull out the brake master cylinder reservoir stopper.
      Fig 3: Pulling Out Brake Master Cylinder Reservoir Stopper
      Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    2. Remove the brake master cylinder reservoir and 2 grommets from the brake master cylinder.
    3. Remove the cap and strainer from the brake master cylinder reservoir.
