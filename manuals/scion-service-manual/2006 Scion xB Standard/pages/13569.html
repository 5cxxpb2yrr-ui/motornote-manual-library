VSC Warning Light Remains ON: Description

If the ECU stores a DTC, the VSC warning light illuminates.

DETECTION CONDITION CHART

DTC No. DTC Detection Condition Trouble Area
Always ON Malfunction in skid control ECU with actuator
  • VSC warning light circuit
  • Power source circuit
  • Skid control ECU with actuator
