DTC C1249/49 Open in Stop Light Switch Circuit: Description

This circuit recognizes brake operation by sending a stop light signal to the skid control ECU.

DETECTION CONDITION CHART

DTC No.1 DTC Detection Condition Trouble Area
C1249/49 Both conditions continue for at least 0.3 seconds:
  • IG1 terminal voltage is between 9.5 and 17.2 V
  • Open in stop light switch circuit
  • Stop light switch assembly
  • Stop light switch circuit
