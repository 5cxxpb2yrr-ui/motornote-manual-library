Rocker Panel Moulding: Removal

HINT:

  • Use the same procedures for the RH side and LH side.
  • The procedures listed below are for the LH side.
  1. REMOVE BODY ROCKER PANEL MOULDING LH 
    1. Using a clip remover, remove the 8 retainers.
    2. Remove the 2 moulding end covers and 2 bolts.
    3. Detach the 6 clips and remove the moulding.
