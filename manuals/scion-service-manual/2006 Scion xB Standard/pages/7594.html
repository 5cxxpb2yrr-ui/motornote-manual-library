Roof Drip Side Finish Moulding: Installation

HINT:

  • Use the same procedures for the RH side and LH side.
  • The procedures listed below are for the LH side.
  • When installing the clip, heat the vehicle body and clip using a heat light.

    Heating temperature 

    HEATING TEMPERATURE

    Item Temperature
    Vehicle body 40 to 60°C (104 to 140°F)
    Clip 20 to 30°C (68 to 86°F)
    NOTE: Do not heat the vehicle body and clip excessively.
  1. REPLACE ROOF DRIP SIDE FINISH MOULDING CLIP 

    HINT:

    Use the procedures below to replace damaged or accidentally removed clips. If this step is not necessary, go to the next step.

    NOTE: After press-fitting the clips, wait 30 minutes or more before installing the moulding.

    HINT:

    • Minimum hardening time: 30 minutes
    • Time needed for complete hardening: 24 hours
    1. Remove the double-sided tape that remains on the moulding installation area of the vehicle body, and then clean the surface with white gasoline.
    2. Heat the clip installation surfaces of the vehicle body and the installation part of the clips.
    3. Install new clips in the positions shown in the illustration. Press-fit the clips by hand.
      Fig 1: Identifying Clip Installation Position

  2. INSTALL ROOF DRIP SIDE FINISH MOULDING LH 
    1. Apply protective tape around the edges of the moulding.
    2. Attach the clips both in the front and rear ends of the moulding, and then install the moulding.
    3. Remove the protective tape from the edges of the moulding.
