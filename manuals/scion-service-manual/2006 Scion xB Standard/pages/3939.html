Oxygen Sensor/Air Fuel Ratio Sensor Drive Cycle 5

  1. Preconditions  The monitor will not run unless:
    • 2 minutes or more have elapsed since the engine was started.
    • The Engine Coolant Temperature (ECT) is 75°C (167°F) or higher.
    • Cumulative driving time at a vehicle speed of 48 km/h (30 mph) or more exceeds 6 minutes.
    • Air-fuel ratio feedback control is performed.
    • Fuel-cut control is performed for 8 seconds or more (for the Rear HO2 Sensor Monitor)
  2. Drive Pattern for Front and Rear HO2 Sensors  
    1. Connect an intelligent tester or OBD II scan tool to the DLC3 (Procedure "A").
    2. Turn the ignition switch to ON (Procedure "B").
    3. Turn the tester or scan tool ON (Procedure "C").
    4. Clear DTCs (Procedure "D").
    5. Start the engine, and warm it up until the ECT reaches 75°C (167°F) or higher (Procedure "E").
    6. Drive the vehicle at 30 mph (48 km/h) or more for at least 40 seconds (Procedure "F").
    7. Allow the engine to idle for 20 seconds or more (Procedure "G").
    8. Repeat procedure "F" and "G" at least 8 times in one driving cycle (Procedure "H").
    9. Change the transmission to 2nd gear (Procedure "I").
    10. Accelerate the vehicle to 30 mph (48 km/h) or more by depressing the accelerator pedal for at least 10 seconds (Procedure "J").
    11. Soon after performing procedure "J" above, release the accelerator pedal for at least 10 seconds without depressing the brake pedal, in order to execute fuel-cut control (Procedure "K").
    12. Allow the vehicle to decelerate until the vehicle speed declines to less than 6 mph (10 km/h) (Procedure "L").
    13. Repeat procedures from "J" through "L" above at least twice in one driving cycle (Procedure "M").
  3. Monitor Status 
    1. Check the Readiness Monitor status displayed on the tester.
    2. If the status does not switch to COMPL (complete), make sure that the preconditions have been met, and then perform procedure from "E" through "M" in Drive Pattern above.
