Terminals Of Ecu

  1. CHECK COMBINATION METER ASSEMBLY 
    Fig 1: Identifying Combination Meter Assembly Terminals
    Courtesy of © TOYOTA, LICENSE AGREEMENT TMS1002
    1. Disconnect the C6 meter connector.
    2. Measure the resistance and voltage of the wire harness side connector.
      TERMINALS DESCRIPTION

      Terminal No. Wiring Color Terminal Description Condition Specified Condition
      C6-1 - C6-21 L-Y - BR Battery (+B circuit) Always 10 to 14 V
      C6-21 - Body ground BR - Body ground Body ground Below 1 Ω
      C6-23 - C6-21 R-B - BR IG1 signal Ignition switch ON 10 to 14 V
      Ignition switch OFF Below 1 V
      C6-40 - C6-21 B-R - BR IG2 signal Ignition switch ON 10 to 14 V
      Ignition switch OFF Below 1 V

      If the result is not as specified, there may be a malfunction on the wire harness side.

    3. Reconnect the C6 meter connector.
    4. Measure the voltage of the connector.
