Outline Of Diagnosis

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

Detect the malfunction of the intake air temperature sensor output properties.

After the engine starts after the specified period of soaking time has elapsed, diagnose by correlation between intake air temperature sensor value, engine coolant temperature sensor value and ambient temperature sensor value. Judge as NG when the differences are both above the specified value by comparing between intake air temperature and engine coolant temperature, intake air temperature and ambient air temperature.
