Clutch Switch

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Install the clutch switch.
  2. Move the clevis pin of push rod to left and right, retain it at the position where it moves smoothly, and measure the clutch pedal full stroke A.

    Specification: 

    130 - 135 mm (5.12 - 5.31 in)

    Tightening torque: 

    T: 8 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.9 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. If the clutch pedal full stroke is out of the standard value, adjust it. Refer to CLUTCH SYSTEM>CLUTCH PEDAL>ADJUSTMENT .
  4. Connect the connector to the clutch switch.
  5. Install the knee airbag module. Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>KNEE AIRBAG MODULE>INSTALLATION .
  6. Install the instrument panel lower cover. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>INSTRUMENT PANEL LOWER COVER>INSTALLATION .
  7. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
