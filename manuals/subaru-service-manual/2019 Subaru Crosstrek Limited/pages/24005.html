PCV Connector: Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery.

    Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .

  2. Drain engine coolant (engine side),

    Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>ENGINE COOLANT>REPLACEMENT>DRAINING OF ENGINE COOLANT>ENGINE SIDE .

  3. Remove the electric compressor assembly,

    Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>COMPRESSOR>REMOVAL .

  4. Disconnect the water hose (A) and PCV hose No. 2 (B), and remove the PCV connector (C).
