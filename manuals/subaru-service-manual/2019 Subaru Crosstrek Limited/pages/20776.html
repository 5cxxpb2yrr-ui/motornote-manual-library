Blind Spot Detection / Rear Cross Traffic Alert System: General Description: Caution

  • Before disconnecting connectors of sensors or units, be sure to disconnect the ground terminal from battery. When replacing the electrical parts provided with memory functions that store contents specified by a customer, record the memory contents before disconnecting the battery ground terminal.
  • For precautions for rear vehicle detection function, refer to "CAUTION" in "Blind Spot Detection/Rear Cross Traffic Alert (DIAGNOSTICS)" information. Ref. to BLIND SPOT DETECTION/REAR CROSS TRAFFIC ALERT (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
