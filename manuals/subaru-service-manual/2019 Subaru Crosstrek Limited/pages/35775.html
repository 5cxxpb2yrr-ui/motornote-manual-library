Outline Of Diagnosis

When the vehicle is being driven, if the voltage at the main power supply, IG1 is a specific value or more due to a malfunction in the auxiliary battery and the electric brake booster control module judges that there is an increase in the power supply voltage of the main power supply, IG1 and illuminates the MIL and stores a DTC.
