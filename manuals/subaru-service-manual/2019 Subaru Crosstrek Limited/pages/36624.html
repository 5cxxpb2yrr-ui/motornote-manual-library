DTC U0100: Lost Communication With ECM/PCM "A"

DTC detecting condition: 

CAN data from ECM is abnormal.

Trouble symptom: 

  • Energy flow status is not displayed correctly in the energy flow screen. (Hybrid model)
  • Favorite screen Accelerator opening angle does not change. (Except for hybrid model)
  • Favorite screen Oil temperature does not change.
  • Favorite screen Water temperature does not change.
NOTE: For the diagnostic procedure, refer to DTC U0074. Refer to MULTI-FUNCTION DISPLAY (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE WITH DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>DTC U0074 CONTROL MODULE COMMUNICATION BUS "BODY-CAN" OFF .
