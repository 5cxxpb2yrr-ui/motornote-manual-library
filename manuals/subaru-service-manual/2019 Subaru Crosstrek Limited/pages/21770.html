Monitor Description

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

If the difference between the requested generator torque and the actual generator torque exceeds a predetermined value, the Motor generator control ECU determines that there is a malfunction in the execution or monitoring of the generator torque. Then, the Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly will illuminate the MIL and store a DTC.
