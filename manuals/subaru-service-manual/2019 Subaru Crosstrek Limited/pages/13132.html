Bushing Rear - Front Arm

  1. Put an alignment mark on the front arm assembly based on the center of recess portion of the bushing rear - front arm.
  2. Using the ST and a press, remove the bushing rear - front arm.

    Preparation tool: 

    ST1: REMOVER (20299AG000)

    ST2: BASE (20299AG010)
