DTC P3000-16: Hybrid/EV Battery Control System Circuit Voltage Below Threshold: Notes

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

1. - 

Outline of diagnosis: 

  • Receive error signal from Battery ECU assembly
  • When any cell voltage widely decreased

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

Hybrid system stopped

CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
NOTE:
  • Because DTC P300016 may be output due to decreased SOC caused by long-term leave in N range or lack of fuel, perform complete interview.
  • Inability of engine start may disable to charge HV battery and cause DTC P300016 output.
  • In the case of diagnosis of the HV battery in which the remaining capacity of each battery cell varies, the Hybrid Fail Lamp may come on informing of the diagnosis result. In this case, check the DTC of hybrid system. If DTC P300016 is output, wait for 10 minutes or more in READY ON state until recovery of voltage and then clear the DTC.
  1. CHECK DTC. 

    Read the following DTCs using Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) . Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    • P-HEV High Voltage Battery
    • P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control

    When "DTC P300000 or P300016 is displayed", go to  A.

    When DTCs related to [P-HEV High Voltage Battery] other than P300000 or P300016 is simultaneously displayed, go to  B.

    When DTCs related to [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control] other than P300000 or P300016 is simultaneously displayed, go to  C.

    Yes: 

    1. Go to  2.
    2. Perform the diagnosis for the simultaneously displayed DTCs.
    3. Perform the diagnosis for the simultaneously displayed DTCs.

    No:  -

  2. CHECK DTC. 

    Read the DTC of [Engine Control System] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to ENGINE (DIAGNOSTICS) (H4DO (HEV))>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Are any DTCs displayed?

    Yes:  Perform the diagnosis for the displayed DTCs. Refer to ENGINE (DIAGNOSTICS) (H4DO (HEV))>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    No:  Go to  3.

  3. CHECK CONNECTOR CONNECTION CONDITION (Battery ECU assembly). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Check that the Service plug is disconnected. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .
    2. Check the connecting condition of connectors (HV37), (HV38), (HV41), (HV40) and (HV39) in Battery ECU assembly and check the relevant terminals for contact pressure.

    Is there looseness at the connector connection, or any poor contact, deformation or intrusion of water or foreign matters at the relevant terminals?

    Yes:  Connect the connector securely.

    Moreover, if the terminal is damaged, replace the HV battery. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY .

    No:  Go to  4.

  4. CHECK ENGINE START. 

    Check if the engine starts.

    CAUTION: Never repeat READY ON operations after clearing memory of DTC after reproduction of DTC P300016 because a malfunction, otherwise, may occur.
    NOTE: Set the shift lever to "P" and depress the accelerator pedal and the engine starts.

    Does the engine start?

    Yes:  Leave the shift lever placed in P and perform charging in the idling state until Auto Start Stop occurs.

    No:  Go to  5.

  5. CHECK CRANK PULLEY ROTATION (P POSITION). 
    1. Check that the shift is placed in P position.
    2. Lift up the vehicle at all wheels.
    3. Rotate the crank pulley clockwise by hand and check if the crank shaft rotates.
      CAUTION:
      • Turn to READY ON during inspection. Be sure to check for IG OFF before inspection to prevent the engine starting.
      • It is occasionally difficult to rotate the crank pulley due to resistance with compression in the engine. Therefore check whether or not the rotation can be done (locked/unlocked) by applying appropriate torque by hand (a torque that can rotate the crank pulley in normal engine condition of the same type).

    Does the crank pulley rotate?

    Yes:  Charge HV battery.

    NOTE: This is not a malfunction of HV battery. Therefore perform recharging instead of replacement to restore the HV battery.

    No:  Go to  6.

  6. CHECK CRANK PULLEY ROTATION (N POSITION). 
    1. Depress the brake pedal and shift the select lever to N position.
    2. Rotate the crank pulley clockwise by hand and check if the crank shaft rotates.
      CAUTION: Turn to READY ON during inspection. Be sure to check for IG OFF before inspection to prevent the engine starting.
      It is occasionally difficult to rotate the crank pulley due to resistance with compression in the engine. Therefore check whether or not the rotation can be done (locked/unlocked) by applying appropriate torque by hand (a torque that can rotate the crank pulley in normal engine condition of the same type).

    Does the crank pulley rotate?

    Yes:  Go to  8.

    No:  Go to  7.

  7. CHECK ENGINE START. 
    1. Repair or replace the engine.
    2. Check if the engine starts.
      CAUTION: Never repeat READY ON operations after clearing memory of DTC after reproduction of DTC P300016 because a malfunction, otherwise, may occur.
      NOTE: Set the shift lever to "P" and depress the accelerator pedal and the engine starts.

    Does the engine start?

    Yes:  Leave the shift lever placed in P and perform charging in the idling state until Auto Start Stop occurs.

    No:  Charge HV battery.

    NOTE: This is not a malfunction of HV battery. Therefore perform recharging instead of replacement to restore the HV battery.
  8. CHECK ENGINE START. 
    1. Replace Transmission assembly. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TH2A)>TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY .
    2. Check if the engine starts.
      CAUTION: Never repeat READY ON operations after clearing memory of DTC after reproduction of DTC P300016 because a malfunction, otherwise, may occur.
      NOTE: Set the shift lever to "P" and depress the accelerator pedal and the engine starts.

    Does the engine start?

    Yes:  Leave the shift lever placed in P and perform charging in the idling state until Auto Start Stop occurs.

    No:  Charge HV battery.

    NOTE: This is not a malfunction of HV battery. Therefore perform recharging instead of replacement to restore the HV battery.
