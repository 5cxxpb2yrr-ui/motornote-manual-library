Brake Vacuum Hose & Pipe

  1. Remove the air intake boot. Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>AIR INTAKE BOOT>REMOVAL .
    NOTE: With the PCV hose connected, remove the air intake boot from the air cleaner case (rear) and throttle body.
  2. Remove the vacuum hose & pipe.
    NOTE: Perform marking of the vacuum hose on the vacuum pump side as shown in the figure below.
