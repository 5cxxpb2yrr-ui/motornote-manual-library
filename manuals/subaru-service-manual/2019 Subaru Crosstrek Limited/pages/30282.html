High Pressure Solenoid Valve

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Using the refrigerant recovery system, discharge refrigerant. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>REFRIGERANT RECOVERY PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .
  2. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  3. Remove expansion valve - cooler assembly. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>EXPANSION VALVE>REMOVAL>COOLER VALVE .
  4. Remove the HI solenoid valve.
    1. Remove the harness clips, connector clips, and bolts, and remove the HI solenoid valve.
    CAUTION: Seal the engaging parts of the disconnected pipe and expansion valve - cooler assembly with plugs or vinyl tape to prevent foreign matter from entering.
