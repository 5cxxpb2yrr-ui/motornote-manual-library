Pressure Sensor: Installation

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
CAUTION: Apply compressor oil to O-rings of the sensor and install it completely.
  1. Install the new pressure sensor.

    Tightening torque: 

    10.8 N.m (1.1 kgf-m, 8.0 ft-lb)

  2. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  3. Charge refrigerant. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>REFRIGERANT CHARGING PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .
