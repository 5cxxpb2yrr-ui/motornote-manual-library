Torque Converter Assembly: Removal

  1. Remove the transmission assembly from the vehicle. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL .
  2. Pull out the torque converter assembly horizontally.
    CAUTION: Do not scratch the inside of engaging parts.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the O-ring from the input shaft.
