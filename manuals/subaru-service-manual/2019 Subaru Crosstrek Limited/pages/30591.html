Differential Case Assembly

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Loosen the twelve bolts and remove hypoid driven gear.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Drive out the straight pin from differential assembly toward hypoid driven gear side.

    Special Tool: 

    ST: 899904100 STRAIGHT PIN REMOVER

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Pull out the pinion shaft, and remove the differential bevel pinion, differential bevel gear and adjusting washer.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Using the ST, remove the taper roller bearing.

    Special Tool: 

    ST: 899524100 PULLER SET

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
