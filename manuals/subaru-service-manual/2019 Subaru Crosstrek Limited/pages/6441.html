Rear Drive Shaft: Removal

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Ref. to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Lift up the vehicle, and then remove the rear wheels.
  3. Remove the axle nut.
    CAUTION: Do not loosen the axle nut while the rear axle is loaded. Doing so may damage the hub unit bearing.
    1. Lift the crimped section of axle nut.
    2. Remove the axle nut using a socket wrench while depressing the brake pedal.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the sensor assembly - headlight beam leveler. (Model with auto headlight beam leveler) Ref. to LIGHTING SYSTEM>REAR HEIGHT SENSOR>REMOVAL .
  5. Drain differential gear oil. Ref. to DIFFERENTIALS>DIFFERENTIAL GEAR OIL>REPLACEMENT .
  6. Remove the rear exhaust pipe. Ref. to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>REAR EXHAUST PIPE>REMOVAL .
  7. Remove the center exhaust cover, and remove the propeller shaft. Ref. to DRIVE SHAFT SYSTEM>PROPELLER SHAFT>REMOVAL .
  8. Loosen the joint of rear differential and rear drive shaft assembly.
    • T type: Pull out the rear drive shaft assembly by fitting the ST to the bolt (a) as shown in the figure.
    CAUTION: Fit the ST to the bolts as shown in the figure to prevent damage of the side bearing retainer.

    Preparation tool: 

    ST: DRIVE SHAFT REMOVER (28099 Pa100)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • VA1 type: Pull out the rear drive shaft assembly by placing the tire lever (b) to the bolt (a) as shown in the figure.
    CAUTION: To prevent damage to the side bearing retainer, use the tire lever while pushing it against the bolt as shown in the figure.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Remove the rear differential.
    1. Support the rear differential with the transmission jack.
    2. Remove the bolts which hold the rear differential front member from the rear sub frame.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Remove the self-locking nuts which hold the rear differential to the rear sub frame, and remove the mass damper.
    • Except for Crosstrek hybrid model
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • Crosstrek hybrid model
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  10. Completely pull out the rear drive shaft assembly while lowering the rear differential.
    CAUTION: Be careful not to damage the drive shaft boot.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  11. Remove the rear drive shaft assembly from the rear axle housing.
NOTE: If it is hard to remove, use the ST.

Preparation tool: 

ST1: AXLE SHAFT PULLER (926470000)

ST2: AXLE SHAFT PULLER PLATE (28099 Pa110)

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
