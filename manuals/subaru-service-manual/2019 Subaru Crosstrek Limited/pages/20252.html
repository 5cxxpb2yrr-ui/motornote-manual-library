DTC P0A96-96: Hybrid/EV Battery Cooling Fan 2 Component Internal Failure: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Outline of diagnosis: 

When the actual speed of Battery cooling blower assembly LH is out of specification with respect to the target speed of Battery ECU assembly

(At malfunction of battery cooling fan frequency Lo (or Hi), this DTC is not detected)

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

Normal driving

NOTE: For the diagnostic procedure, refer to DTC P0A8196. Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE WITH DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>DTC P0A8196 HYBRID/EV BATTERY COOLING FAN 1 COMPONENT INTERNAL FAILURE .
