Engine Lubrication System Trouble in General: Inspection

Before performing diagnostics, make sure that the engine oil level is correct and no oil leakage exists.

Trouble Possible cause Corrective action
The oil pressure warning light illuminates. a. Oil pressure switch failure Replace the oil pressure switch.
b. Low oil pressure
  • Clogging of oil filter
 Replace the oil filter.
  • Clogged or broken oil strainer
 Clean or replace the oil strainer.
  • Clogged oil passage
 Clean or replace faulty parts.
  • Defective oil pump
 Replace the chain cover.
  • Insufficient engine oil (degradation, etc.)
 Replace engine oil.
c. Short circuit of the harness Repair the harness.
The oil pressure warning light does not illuminate. a. Malfunction of combination meter Replace the combination meter.
b. Oil pressure switch failure Replace the oil pressure switch.
c. Open circuit of harness Repair the harness.
