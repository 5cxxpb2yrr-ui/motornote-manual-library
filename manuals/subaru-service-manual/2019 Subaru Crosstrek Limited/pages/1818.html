Diagnostic Procedure With Cancel Code: 14

Detected when the select lever is shifted to the neutral position, or when an error related to the neutral position switch occurs.

Trouble symptom: 

Cruise control cannot be set.

Wiring diagram: 

Cruise control system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (IMPREZA MODEL)>CRUISE CONTROL SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>CRUISE CONTROL SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to CRUISE CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK TRANSMISSION TYPE. 

    Is the transmission type CVT?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Go to  5. (MT model)

  2. CHECK INHIBITOR SWITCH CIRCUIT. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the inhibitor switch connector.
    3. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    4. Measure the voltage between inhibitor switch connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (T7) No. 6 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    Is the voltage 10 V or more?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Check for open or short circuit in the harness between inhibitor switch connector and ECM.

  3. CHECK INHIBITOR SWITCH CIRCUIT. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the ECM connector.
    3. Measure the resistance between inhibitor switch connector and ECM.

      Connector & terminal 

      (T7) No. 9 - (B134) No. 50:

    Is the resistance less than 10 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Repair the harness.

  4. PERFORM UNIT INSPECTION OF INHIBITOR SWITCH. 

    Remove and check the inhibitor switch. Refer to CRUISE CONTROL SYSTEM>INHIBITOR SWITCH .

    Is the inhibitor switch OK?

    Yes:  Replace the ECM.

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE CONTROL MODULE (ECM) .

    No:  Replace the inhibitor switch.

  5. CHECK NEUTRAL POSITION SWITCH CIRCUIT. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the neutral position switch connector.
    3. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    4. Measure the voltage between neutral position switch connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (T12) No. 1 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    Is the voltage 10 V or more?

    Yes:  Go to  6.

    No:  Check for open or short in the harness between neutral position switch and ECM.

  6. CHECK NEUTRAL POSITION SWITCH CIRCUIT. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the ECM connector.
    3. Measure the resistance between neutral position switch connector and ECM.

      Connector & terminal 

      (T12) No. 2 - (B134) No. 50:

    Is the resistance less than 10 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  7.

    No:  Repair the harness.

  7. PERFORM UNIT INSPECTION OF NEUTRAL POSITION SWITCH. 

    Remove and check the neutral position switch. Refer to CRUISE CONTROL SYSTEM>NEUTRAL POSITION SWITCH .

    Is the neutral position switch OK?

    Yes:  Replace the ECM.

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE CONTROL MODULE (ECM) .

    No:  Replace the neutral position switch.
