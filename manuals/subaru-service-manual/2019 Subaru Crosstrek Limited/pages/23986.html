EGR Cooler: Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
CAUTION: If the engine coolant is split over the engine or the exhaust pipe, completely wipe it off to avoid emission of smoke or causing a fire.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery,

    Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .

  2. When the EGR temperature sensor is not removed from the EGR cooler
    NOTE: When the EGR temperature sensor is not removed from the EGR cooler, also perform the following steps.
    1. Disconnect the EGR temperature sensor connector.
  3. When the EGR temperature sensor is removed from the EGR cooler
    NOTE: When the EGR temperature sensor is removed from the EGR cooler, also perform the following steps.
    1. Remove the EGR temperature sensor.

      Refer to EMISSION CONTROL (AUX. EMISSION CONTROL DEVICES) (H4DO (HEV))>EGR TEMPERATURE SENSOR>REMOVAL .

  4. Remove the EGR control valve.

    Refer to EMISSION CONTROL (AUX. EMISSION CONTROL DEVICES) (H4DO (HEV))>EGR CONTROL VALVE>REMOVAL .

  5. Remove the clip.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Disconnect the water hose (A) and the water hose (B).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Remove the EGR cooler.
    CAUTION: When removing the EGR cooler, plug the water hoses to avoid emission of smoke or fire due to engine coolant flowing into the front exhaust pipes. If the engine coolant enter into the front exhaust pipes, completely remove and clean it.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Remove the water hose.
    NOTE: Remove if necessary.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
