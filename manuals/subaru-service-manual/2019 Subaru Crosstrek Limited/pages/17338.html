Cvt Model (VA1 Type)

CAUTION: Be sure to wrap shaft splines with vinyl tape to protect the boot from scratches.
NOTE: Use specified grease.

Grease: Ref. to RECOMMENDED MATERIALS>GREASE .

  1. Install the inner race to the shaft.
    1. Install the boot (EBJ or BJ) to the specified position, and fill it with 50 - 60 g (1.76 - 2.12 oz) of specified grease.
    2. Place the boot (DOJ) at the center of shaft.
    3. Insert the cage onto the shaft.
      NOTE: Insert the cage with the cutout portion facing the shaft end, since the cage has an orientation.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    4. Install the inner race on shaft and fix the snap ring in place with pliers.
    NOTE: Confirm that the snap ring is completely fitted in the shaft groove.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Install the cage to the inner race.
    1. Install the cage (B) with the protruding section aligned with the track on the inner race (A), and turn by a half pitch.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Fill 80 - 90 g (2.82 - 3.17 oz) of specified grease into the interior of the outer race (DOJ).
    3. Apply a thin coat of specified grease to the cage pocket and ball.
    4. Insert the balls into the cage pocket.
  3. Connect the shaft to the outer race (DOJ).
    1. Align the outer race (DOJ) track and ball positions, and place the shaft, inner race, cage and balls in the original positions, and then fix outer race (DOJ) in place.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Install the snap ring in the groove of the outer race (DOJ).
      CAUTION: Be careful of the following items during installation:
      • Make sure that the balls, cage and inner race are completely fitted in the outer race (DOJ).
      • Use care not to place the matched position of snap ring in the ball groove of outer race (DOJ).
      • Pull the shaft lightly and assure that the snap ring is completely fitted in the groove.
    3. Apply 20 - 30 g (0.71 - 1.06 oz) of specified grease evenly to the entire inner surface of boot (DOJ). Also apply grease to the shaft.
    4. Install the boot (DOJ) taking care not to twist it.
      NOTE:
      • The inside of the large end of boot (DOJ) and the boot groove shall be cleaned so as to be free from grease and other substances.
      • When installing the boot (DOJ), position the outer race (DOJ) at center of the stroke.
    5. Prepare a new boot band, pinch (a) and (e) of the boot band with needle nose pliers, and align it with the groove of the boot (DOJ).
      CAUTION:
      • Be careful not to damage the boot.
      • When tightening boot, use care so that the air within the boot is appropriate.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    6. Pinch (a) and (e) of the boot band with needle nose pliers, and align the concave claw (b) with the convex claw (c) for temporarily tightening position.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    7. Check the positions of the boot (DOJ) and boot band, align the concave claw (b) with the convex claw (d), and tighten the boot band.
  4. Install the boot band to the boot (EBJ or BJ) using the same procedures as for the boot (DOJ).
  5. Extend and retract the boot (DOJ) repeatedly to provide an equal coating of grease.
