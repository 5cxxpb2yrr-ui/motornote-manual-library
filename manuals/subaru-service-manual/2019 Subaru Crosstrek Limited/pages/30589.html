Front Differential Assembly: Removal

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Remove the manual transmission assembly from the vehicle. refer to MANUAL TRANSMISSION AND DIFFERENTIAL (6MT)>MANUAL TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL .
  2. Remove the transfer case together with the extension case assembly. refer to MANUAL TRANSMISSION AND DIFFERENTIAL (6MT)>TRANSFER CASE AND EXTENSION CASE ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the transmission case, refer to MANUAL TRANSMISSION AND DIFFERENTIAL (6MT)>TRANSMISSION CASE>REMOVAL .
  4. Remove the drive pinion shaft assembly. refer to MANUAL TRANSMISSION AND DIFFERENTIAL (6MT)>DRIVE PINION SHAFT ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL 
  5. Remove the main shaft assembly. refer to MANUAL TRANSMISSION AND DIFFERENTIAL (6MT)>MAIN SHAFT ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL 
  6. Remove the front differential assembly.
    CAUTION:
    • Do not confuse the right and left taper roller bearing outer races.
    • Be careful not to damage the oil seal of differential side retainer.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Remove the differential side retainers using the ST.

    Special Tool: 

    ST: 18630AA010 WRENCH COMPL RETAINER

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Remove the taper roller bearing outer race from the transmission case using the PULLER ASSY (398527700).
