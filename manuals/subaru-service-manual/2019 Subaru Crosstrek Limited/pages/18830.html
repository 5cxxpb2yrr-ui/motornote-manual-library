Reverse Idler Gear: Adjustment

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Select the appropriate reverse idler gear bushing from the table below, and adjust until the clearance (a) between the reverse idler gear and reverse idler gear washer is within the standard value.

    Specification: 

    0.1 - 0.3 mm (0.004 - 0.012 in)
