How To Reset The Full Close Stop Position

When the full close stop position becomes misaligned, it can be reset by performing the following procedure.

  1. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
  2. Operate and hold the switch - power window in "AUTO UP" direction (fully pull up the switch) for three seconds or more.
    NOTE: For the driver's side window, this can be done with the switch power window main, and for the passenger's side window the switch - power window main or the passenger's side switch - power window can be used.
  3. Check that the glass assembly - front door starts rolling down and fully closes.
  4. Operate the switch - power window in "AUTO DOWN" direction to check whether the window fully opens properly.
