Boot - Steering Gearbox

Inspect both sides of the boot - steering gearbox as follows, and correct the defects if necessary.

  1. The (A) and (B) positions of the boot - steering gearbox are fitted in (A) and (C) grooves of the gearbox and the rod.
  2. Clips are fitted onto the boot grooves to the positions (A) and (B) of the boot.
  3. Check that there is no cracks or holes in the boot.
NOTE: Rotate position (B) of the boot - steering gearbox against the torsion produced by the adjustment of toe-in etc. Apply grease to the groove (C).
