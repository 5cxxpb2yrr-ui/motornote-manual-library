Air Mix Door Actuator: Removal

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
CAUTION: If reusing the motor - actuator mix, perform the following procedures to prevent incorrect installation.
  1. While the part is installed to the vehicle, turn the temperature control dial to the MAX COOL position.
  2. Wait for 30 seconds in the condition 1, and remove the motor - actuator mix.
  1. Disconnect the battery ground terminal and wait for at least 60 seconds before starting work. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the pocket assembly and the back panel - pocket. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>GLOVE BOX>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the heat pump control module. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>CONTROL UNIT>REMOVAL .
  4. Remove the motor - actuator mix.
    1. Disconnect the connector.
    2. Remove the screws and detach the motor - actuator mix.
