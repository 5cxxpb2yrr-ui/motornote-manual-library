Audio / Video System: Audio: Removal

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the grille assembly - CTR ventilation. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>AIR VENT GRILLE>REMOVAL>CENTER GRILLE ASSEMBLY .
  3. Remove the panel - center UPR.
    1. Release the clips and claws, then detach the panel - center UPR.
  4. Remove the audio assembly.
    1. Remove the screws.
    2. Release the clips and disconnect the connector, and then remove the audio assembly.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the audio bracket.
    1. Remove the screws, and then remove the audio bracket.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    NOTE:
    • Remove the screws on RH side in the same procedure as on the LH side.
    • For models with telematics, the data communication module is attached on the upper side of the audio assembly.
