Except For Hybrid Model

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery.

    Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY>GROUND TERMINAL REMOVAL OR INSTALLATION .

  2. Remove the trim panel - trunk side (4 door model).

    Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>TRUNK ROOM TRIM>REMOVAL .

  3. Remove the mat - rear floor, the spacer - rear floor side and the cap - rear suspension (5 door model).
    1. Remove the mat - rear floor and the spacer - rear floor side.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Remove the cap - rear suspension (5 door model).
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Lift up the vehicle, and then remove the rear wheels.
  5. Remove the sensor assembly - headlight beam leveler. (Model with auto headlight beam leveler)

    Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>REAR HEIGHT SENSOR>REMOVAL .

  6. Remove the bolts and nuts, and lower the rear lateral link (rear).
    1. Remove the bolts from the lower side of rear shock absorber assembly.
    2. Disconnect the rear axle housing from the rear lateral link (rear).
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Remove the nuts on the upper side of rear shock absorber assembly.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Lower the rear lateral link (rear), and remove the rear shock absorber assembly.
