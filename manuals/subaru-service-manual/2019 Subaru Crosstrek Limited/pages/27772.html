DTC P3124-1F: Lost Communication Between Drive Motor "A" And HV ECU Circuit Intermittent: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. - 

    Detecting condition: 

    When DTC P0C7917, P0D3319, P1C5D19 or P1C5F19 error judgment occurs and lost communication between Motor generator control ECU and Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly are detected (1 trip detection logic)

    Trouble symptom: 

    -

    Wiring diagram: 

    Hybrid system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to P-HEV DRIVE MOTOR (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK DTC. 
    1. Using the Subaru Select Monitor. Perform the clear memory of [P-HEV Drive Motor]. Refer to COMMON (DIAG)>CLEAR MEMORY .
    2. Using the Subaru Select Monitor. Read [Diagnosis Related Information] of [P-HEV Drive Motor].

      Refer to P-HEV DRIVE MOTOR (DIAGNOSTICS)>WORK SUPPORT .

    3. Read the DTC of [P-HEV Drive Motor] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to P-HEV DRIVE MOTOR (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

      Is diagnostic related information, DTC P31241F or P312487 displayed?

      Yes:  Go to  2.

      No:  Even if DTC is displayed, the circuit has returned to a normal condition at this time.

      Reproduce the failure, and then perform the diagnosis again.

      NOTE: In this case, temporary poor contact of connector, temporary open or short circuit of Wire harness may be the cause.
  2. CHECK Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly (WAVEFORM CHECK). 

    Connect an oscilloscope to Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly terminal and measure the waveform.

    Measured terminal 

    CH2: (B606) No. 20 (HMCL) ←→ (i369) No. 6 (E1)

    CH1: (B606) No. 28 (HMCH) ←→ (i369) No. 6 (E1)

    Gauge set: 1 V/DIV, 200 us./DIV

    Condition: IG ON

    NOTE:
    • Check with the connector connected.
    • Waveform is normal if a pulse generates.
    • Waveform pulse changes depending on communication conditions.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.

    Is the waveform shown in the figure measured at IG ON?

    Yes:  Replace Inverter with converter assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>INVERTER AND CONVERTER ASSEMBLY .

    No:  Go to  3.

  3. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly - Inverter with converter assembly) (SHORT TO POWER SUPPLY). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Check that the Service plug is disconnected. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .
    2. Disconnect Inverter with converter assembly connector (HV23).
    3. Disconnect Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector (B606).
    4. Connect the ground terminal to Battery (12V). Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    5. Turn to IG ON.
    6. Using a tester, measure the voltage between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (B606) No. 28 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

      (B606) No. 20 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

      Is the voltage less than 1 V?

      Yes:  Go to  4.

      No:  Repair or replace the Wire harness or the connector.

  4. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly - Inverter with converter assembly) (OPEN). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Turn the ignition to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the ground terminal from Battery (12V). Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    3. Using a tester, measure the resistance between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector and Inverter with converter assembly connector.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV23) No. 20 - (B606) No. 28:

      (HV23) No. 24 - (B606) No. 20:

      Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

      Yes:  Go to  5.

      No:  Repair or replace the Wire harness or the connector.

  5. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly - Inverter with converter assembly) (SHORT TO GROUND OR LINE-TO-LINE SHORT). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between terminals in Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector, and between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (B606) No. 28 - (B606) No. 1 - 35:

    (B606) No. 28 - Chassis ground:

    (B606) No. 20 - (B606) No. 1 - 35:

    (B606) No. 20 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance 10 kΩ or more?

    Yes:  Go to  6.

    No:  Repair or replace the Wire harness or the connector.

  6. CHECK Inverter with converter assembly. 
    1. Connect the connector (HV23) of Inverter with converter assembly.
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector terminals.

      Connector & terminal 

      (B606) No. 28 - (B606) No. 20:

      Is the resistance 80 - 170 Ω?

      Yes:  Replace Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL MODULE .

      No:  Replace Inverter with converter assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>INVERTER AND CONVERTER ASSEMBLY .
