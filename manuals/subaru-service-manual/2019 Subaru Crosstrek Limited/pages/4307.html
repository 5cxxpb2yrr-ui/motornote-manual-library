Bumper Beam

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the bumper face - front.
  3. Remove the energy absorber.
  4. Remove the bolts and disconnect the connector, and remove the active grille shutter. (Model with active grille shutter)
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the bolts and disconnect the connector, and remove the frame assembly - active grille shutter. (Model without active grille shutter)
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Release the clips and claws, then remove the air guide LWR. (Hybrid model)
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Remove the bolts, and remove the bumper beam COMPL - front.
Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
