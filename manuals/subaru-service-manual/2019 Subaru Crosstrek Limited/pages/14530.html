Location in system diagram Contents of inspection Reason
*a Check the connector connection and continuity between Battery ECU assembly - HV battery junction block assembly. Check for poor connector engagement, and open circuit in the CHRB and CHRG excitation lines.
*b Check the HV battery junction block assembly connector connection. Check for poor contact of the HV battery junction block assembly connector.
*c Check the high voltage cable connector connection and continuity between Electric vehicle charger assembly - HV battery junction block assembly. Check for poor connector engagement and open circuit.
*d Check Electric vehicle charger assembly internal circuits. Check for open circuit in Electric vehicle charger assembly internal circuits.
*e Check HV battery junction block assembly internal circuits. Check for open circuit in HV battery junction block assembly internal circuits.
