Transfer Driven Gear: Inspection

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Taper roller bearing

    Replace the bearings in the following cases.

    • In case of broken or rusty bearings
    • In case of worn or damaged bearings
    • When the bearings fail to turn smoothly or emit noise in rotation after transmission gear oil has been applied.
  2. Transfer driven gear

    If the tooth face of driven gear and the shaft are excessively broken or damaged, replace the driven gear.
