Models With EyeSight

CAUTION:
  • When standing up the arm assembly - windshield wiper, perform the driver's side first in order not to interfere the arms with each other.
  • When standing up the arm assembly - windshield wiper, make sure to hold the arm portion. Holding the blade portion may cause the blade deformation.
Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  1. Remove the cover - windshield wiper arm.
  2. Remove the arm assembly - windshield wiper.
    1. Remove the nuts, and remove the arm assembly - windshield wiper.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Put alignment marks (a) at two places and disconnect the hose from the nozzle.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Pull out the hose and remove the arm assembly - windshield wiper assistor.
  3. When removing the hose from the joint - washer hose, refer to the removal of "Nozzle - wiper arm washer & hose". Refer to WIPER AND WASHER SYSTEMS>FRONT WASHER NOZZLE AND HOSE>REMOVAL>NOZZLE - WIPER ARM WASHER & HOSE (MODEL WITH EYESIGHT) .
    CAUTION: Do not remove the nozzle - wiper arm washer from the arm assembly - windshield wiper assistor.
    If nozzle replacement is required, replace the arm assembly - windshield wiper assistor as a unit.
