Diagnostic Method

If the duration of time while the following conditions are met is longer than the time indicated, judge as NG.

JUDGMENT VALUE SPECIFICATION

Malfunction Criteria Threshold Value
Diagnosis 1 
CAN data from EBB control module		 Stuck
Diagnosis 2 
SUM value of CAN data from EBB control module		 Malfunction

Time needed for diagnosis: 

  • Diagnosis 1:  2.5 s
  • Diagnosis 2:  3 times
