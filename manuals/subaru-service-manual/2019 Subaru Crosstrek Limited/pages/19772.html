DTC P0AD9-15: Hybrid/EV Battery Positive Contractor Circuit Short To Auxiliary Battery Or Open: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

1. - 

Outline of diagnosis: 

Open circuit or +B power supply sneak path of system main relay SMRB system

Defective circuit on primary side of SMR (+)

(2 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

Hybrid system stopped

Wiring diagram: 

Hybrid system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK DATA MONITOR (SMRB CONDITION)  .
    1. Turn to IG ON.
    2. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, display the following items in the [Data monitor] of [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control], and record them. Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DATA MONITOR .
    • SMRB Status

    Is the data [OFF]?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Go to  6.

  2. CHECK CONNECTION CONDITION OF CONNECTOR (Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly CONNECTOR)  .

    Check the connecting condition of connector (i369) in Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly and check the relevant terminals for contact pressure, deformation and intrusion of water or foreign matter. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>INVERTER AND CONVERTER ASSEMBLY .

    Is there looseness at the connector mating, or any poor contact, deformation or intrusion of water or foreign matter at the relevant terminals?

    Yes:  Connect the connector securely. Or repair the poor contact of connector.

    No:  Go to  3.

  3. CHECK CONNECTION CONDITION OF CONNECTOR (Rear harness LH CONNECTOR)  .

    Check the connecting condition of connector (R467) in Rear harness LH and check the relevant terminals for contact pressure, deformation and intrusion of water or foreign matter.

    Is there looseness at the connector mating, or any poor contact, deformation or intrusion of water or foreign matter at the relevant terminals?

    Yes:  Connect the connector securely. Or repair the poor contact of connector.

    No:  Go to  4.

  4. CHECK CONNECTION CONDITION OF CONNECTOR ({Rear harness LH} CONNECTOR)  .

    Check the connecting condition of connector (R534) in Rear harness LH and check the relevant terminals for contact pressure, deformation and intrusion of water or foreign matter.

    Is there looseness at the connector mating, or any poor contact, deformation or intrusion of water or foreign matter at the relevant terminals?

    Yes:  Connect the connector securely. Or repair the poor contact of connector.

    No:  Go to  5.

  5. CHECK CONNECTION CONDITION OF CONNECTOR (HV battery junction block CONNECTOR)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Check that the Service plug is disconnected. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .
    2. Check the connecting condition of connector (HV49) in HV battery junction block and check the relevant terminals for contact pressure, deformation and intrusion of water or foreign matter.

    Is there looseness at the connector mating, or any poor contact, deformation or intrusion of water or foreign matter at the relevant terminals?

    Yes:  Connect the connector securely. Or repair the poor contact of connector.

    No:  Even if DTC is displayed, the circuit has returned to a normal condition at this time. Reproduce the failure, and then perform the diagnosis again.

    NOTE: In this case, temporary poor contact of connector, temporary open or short circuit of Wire harness may be the cause.
  6. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (SMRB VOLTAGE)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Connect the ground terminal to Battery (12V). Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    2. Turn to IG ON.
    3. Using a tester, measure the voltage between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector and chassis ground. (With the connector connected)

    Connector & terminal 

    (i369) No. 5 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    Is the voltage less than 1 V?

    Yes:  Go to  7.

    No:  Go to  8.

  7. CHECK CONNECTION CONDITION OF CONNECTOR (Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly CONNECTOR)  .
    1. Turn the ignition to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the ground terminal from Battery (12V). Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    3. Check the connecting condition of connector (i369) in Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly and check the relevant terminals for contact pressure, deformation and intrusion of water or foreign matter. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL MODULE .

    Is there looseness at the connector mating, or any poor contact, deformation or intrusion of water or foreign matter at the relevant terminals?

    Yes:  Connect the connector securely. Or repair the poor contact of connector.

    No:  Replace Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL MODULE .

  8. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly - CHASSIS GROUND)  .
    1. Turn the ignition to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the ground terminal from Battery (12V). Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    3. Disconnect Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector (i369).
    4. Using a tester, measure the resistance between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (i369) No. 5 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance 20.6 - 40.8 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  9.

    No:  Go to  10.

  9. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (SHORT TO POWER SUPPLY LINE)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Disconnect HV battery junction block connector (HV49).
    2. Connect the ground terminal to Battery (12V). Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    3. Turn to IG ON.
    4. Using a tester, measure the voltage between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (i369) No. 5 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    Is the voltage less than 1 V?

    Yes:  Replace Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL MODULE .

    No:  Repair or replace the Wire harness between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector and HV battery junction block connector.

  10. CHECK CONNECTION CONDITION OF CONNECTOR (Rear harness LH CONNECTOR)  .

    Check the connecting condition of connector (R467) in Rear harness LH and check the relevant terminals for contact pressure, deformation and intrusion of water or foreign matter.

    Is there looseness at the connector mating, or any poor contact, deformation or intrusion of water or foreign matter at the relevant terminals?

    Yes:  Connect the connector securely. Or repair the poor contact of connector.

    No:  Go to  11.

  11. CHECK CONNECTION CONDITION OF CONNECTOR (Rear harness LH CONNECTOR)  .

    Check the connecting condition of connector (R534) in Rear harness LH and check the relevant terminals for contact pressure, deformation and intrusion of water or foreign matter.

    Is there looseness at the connector mating, or any poor contact, deformation or intrusion of water or foreign matter at the relevant terminals?

    Yes:  Connect the connector securely. Or repair the poor contact of connector.

    No:  Go to  12.

  12. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly - HV battery junction block) (OPEN)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Disconnect HV battery junction block connector (HV49).
    2. Disconnect Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector (i369).
    3. Using a tester, measure the resistance between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector and HV battery junction block connector.

    Connector & terminal 

    (HV49) No. 1 - (i369) No. 5:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  13.

    No:  Repair or replace the Wire harness between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector and HV battery junction block connector.

  13. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly - HV battery junction block) (SHORT TO GROUND)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector terminals.

    Connector & terminal 

    (i369) No. 5 - (i369) No. 1 - 35:

    Is the resistance 10 kΩ or more?

    Yes:  Go to  14.

    No:  Repair or replace the Wire harness between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector and HV battery junction block connector.

  14. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (HV battery junction block - CHASSIS GROUND) (OPEN)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between HV battery junction block connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (HV49) No. 2 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  15.

    No:  Repair or replace Wire harness between HV battery junction block connector and chassis ground.

  15. CHECK HV battery junction block UNIT (SMRB)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.

    Using the tester, measure the resistance between HV battery junction block terminals.

    Connector & terminal 

    (HV49) No. 1 - (HV49) No. 2:

    Is the resistance 20.6 - 40.8 Ω? (When -40 - 80°C (-40 - 176°F))

    Yes:  Even if DTC is displayed, the circuit has returned to a normal condition at this time. Reproduce the failure, and then perform the diagnosis again.

    NOTE: In this case, temporary poor contact of connector, temporary open or short circuit of Wire harness may be the cause.

    No:  Replace HV battery junction block. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>RELAY AND FUSE .
