Ball Bearing (Front Cover Side)

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

Check the ball bearing (front cover side). Check the following items and replace the ball bearing if necessary.

  • Check that there is no deformation, rust or other damages.
  • Check that there is no resistance or looseness when rotating the ball bearings by hand.
  • Check for grease leakage.
