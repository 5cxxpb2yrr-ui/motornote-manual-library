DTC P0A9B-1C: Hybrid/EV Battery Temperature Sensor "A" Voltage Out Of Range: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

1. - 

Outline of diagnosis: 

When characteristic of HV battery temperature sensor 0 is faulty

Deviation with any other HV battery temperature sensor output is large

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

Normal driving

Wiring diagram: 

Hybrid system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK DTC. 

    Read the following DTCs using Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) . Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    • P-HEV High Voltage Battery
    • P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control
    NOTE:
    System Related DTC
    P-HEV High Voltage Battery P060A47 HYBRID/EV BATTERY ENERGY CONTROL MODULE MONITORING PROCESSOR WATCHDOG/SAFETY MCU FAILURE
    P060B49 HYBRID/EV BATTERY ENERGY CONTROL MODULE A/D PROCESSING INTERNAL ELECTRONIC FAILURE
    P060687 HYBRID/EV BATTERY ENERGY CONTROL MODULE PROCESSOR TO MONITORING PROCESSOR MISSING MESSAGE
    P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control P0A1F94 HYBRID/EV BATTERY ENERGY CONTROL MODULE UNEXPECTED OPERATION

    When "Any of DTC P0A9B1C, P0AC51C, P0ACA1C, P0AE81C, P0BC21C, P0C331C, P0C7C1C, P0C811C, P0C881C, P0C8D1C, P0C921C, P0C971C, P0CA81C, P0CAD1C or P0CB21C is displayed" or "DTC other than above is displayed", go to  A.

    When DTCs related to [P-HEV High Voltage Battery], mentioned above, are simultaneously displayed, go to  B.

    When DTCs related to [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control], mentioned above, are simultaneously displayed, go to  C.

    Yes: 

    1. Go to  2.
    2. Perform the diagnosis for the simultaneously displayed DTCs.
      NOTE: If more than one DTC is simultaneously displayed, perform the diagnosis in the order described in the above.
    3. Perform the diagnosis for the simultaneously displayed DTCs.
    NOTE: If more than one DTC is simultaneously displayed, perform the diagnosis in the order described in the above.

    No:  -

  2. CHECK DTC. 

    Check DTC when the vehicle is brought in.

    When "DTC P0A9B1C, P0AC51C or P0ACA1C" is displayed, go to  A.

    When "DTC P0AE81C, P0BC21C or P0C331C" is displayed, go to  B.

    When "DTC P0C7C1C, P0C811C or P0C881C" is displayed, go to  C.

    When "DTC P0C8D1C, P0C921C or P0C971C" is displayed, go to  D.

    When "DTC P0CA81C, P0CAD1C or P0CB21C" is displayed, go to  E.

    Yes: 

    1. Go to  3.
    2. Go to  6.
    3. Go to  9.
    4. Go to  12.
    5. Go to  15.

    No:  -

  3. CHECK HV battery (HV battery temperature sensor 0 - 2). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Check that the Service plug is disconnected. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .
    2. Disconnect Battery ECU assembly connector (HV42).
    3. Using a tester, measure the resistance between Battery ECU assembly connector terminals.

      Connector & terminal 

      HV battery temperature sensor 0 

      (HV42) No. 17 - (HV42) No. 39:

      HV battery temperature sensor 1 

      (HV42) No. 40 - (HV42) No. 39:

      HV battery temperature sensor 2 

      (HV42) No. 16 - (HV42) No. 39:

    NOTE:
    Thermistor temperature Inspection conditions Standard
    20 - 30°C (68 - 86°F) IG OFF 8.22 - 12.2 kΩ
    30 - 40°C (86 - 104°F) IG OFF 5.74 - 8.41 kΩ
    40 - 50°C (104 - 122°F) IG OFF 4.09 - 5.91 kΩ

    Is the resistance as shown above?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Replace HV battery. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY .
