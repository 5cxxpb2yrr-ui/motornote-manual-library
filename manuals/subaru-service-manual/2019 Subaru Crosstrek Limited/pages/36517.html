Front Fog Light Assembly: Removal

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Lift up the vehicle.
  3. Remove the under cover - front. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>FRONT UNDER COVER>REMOVAL .
  4. Turn over the mud guard - front.
    1. Remove the clip.
    2. Remove the screws, and turn over the mud guard - front.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the fog light assembly - front.
    1. Disconnect the connector.
    2. Remove the bolt and clip.
    3. Remove the guides, then remove the fog light assembly - front.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    NOTE: Perform the same procedures for LED models and models with SRF.
