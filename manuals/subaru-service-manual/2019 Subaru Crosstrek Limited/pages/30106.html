Clutch Pedal: Adjustment

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. If the full stroke A of the clutch pedal is not within the specified value, loosen the lock nuts of the clutch switch and adjust the full stroke A of the clutch pedal with the clutch switch.
    CAUTION: When adjusting the full stroke A of the clutch pedal, do not turn the clutch switch.

    Specification: 

    130 - 135 mm (5.12 - 5.31 in)

    Tightening torque: 

    T: 8 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.9 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Depress and release the clutch pedal two or three times to ensure that the clutch pedal and release lever operate smoothly.

    If the clutch pedal and release lever do not operate smoothly, bleed air from the clutch hydraulic system. Refer to CLUTCH SYSTEM>CLUTCH FLUID AIR BLEEDING .

  3. Measure the clutch pedal full stroke length again to ensure that it is within the standard value. If it is not within the standard value, repeat adjustment procedures from the beginning.

    Specification: 

    130 - 135 mm (5.12 - 5.31 in)

  4. Push the release lever until the operating cylinder push rod retracts. Check that the brake fluid level in the reservoir tank increases.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. If the brake fluid level increases, hydraulic clutch play is correct.
  6. If the brake fluid level does not increase or push rod does not retract, readjust the clutch pedal.
  7. Check the brake fluid level using the scale on the outside of reservoir tank. Refer to CLUTCH SYSTEM>CLUTCH FLUID>INSPECTION .
