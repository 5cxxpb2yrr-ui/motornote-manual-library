External Connection Terminal: Removal

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the cover assembly - front and the console box assembly. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>CONSOLE BOX>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the unit assembly - AUX.
    1. While pressing the claw, press it in from the rear to remove the unit assembly - AUX.
