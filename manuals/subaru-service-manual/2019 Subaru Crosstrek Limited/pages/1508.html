Acceleration Or Deceleration Does Not Occur According To The Preceding Vehicle. The Preceding Vehicle Is Difficult To Be Detected. Braking Force Is Weaker Than Usual. Alarm For Requiring More Brake Pedal Force Does Not Sound

  1. CHECK USING CHECK LIST. 

    Using CHECK LIST (ADAPTIVE CRUISE CONTROL), check the condition and respond to it. Check list (adaptive cruise control) Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>CHECK LIST (ADAPTIVE CRUISE CONTROL) .

    • Check that the current status applies to the condition that the use of adaptive cruise control is not desired.
    • Check that the current status applies to the condition or the object that is hard to be recognized by the stereo camera.
    • Check that the current status applies to the driving condition that the preceding vehicle cannot be recognized or the vehicle on the next lane or the object on the roadside may be recognized by mistake.
    • Check that the current status applies to the condition that brake force may become poor.
    • Check that the current status applies to the condition that the alarm for requiring more brake pedal force does not sound even in a short vehicle distance.

    Is there a relevant item in the check list?

    Yes:  When there is a relevant item, it can be the cause of malfunction. Eliminate the cause, and check that the malfunction has been improved.

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. INSPECTION OF WINDSHIELD GLASS AND DASHBOARD. 

    Check the windshield glass and the dashboard. Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>INSPECTION>WINDSHIELD GLASS AND DASHBOARD .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Repair or replace the faulty portion according to the inspection result.

  3. INSPECTION OF FRONT WIPER. 

    Check the front wiper. Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>INSPECTION>FRONT WIPER .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Repair or replace the faulty portion according to the inspection result.

  4. CHECK STEREO CAMERA. 

    Check the stereo camera. Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>INSPECTION>STEREO CAMERA .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  5.

    No:  Repair or replace the faulty portion according to the inspection result.

  5. CHECK BRAKE. 

    Bleed air from brake system. Refer to BRAKE>AIR BLEEDING>PROCEDURE>BRAKE SYSTEM (HYBRID MODEL) .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  6.

    No:  Repair or replace the faulty portion according to the inspection result.

  6. ADJUST OPTICAL AXIS OF THE STEREO CAMERA. 

    Perform optical axis adjustment of the stereo camera. Refer to EYESIGHT>CAMERA ADJUSTMENT, INSPECTION>PROCEDURE .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Replace the stereo camera. Refer to EYESIGHT>STEREO CAMERA>REMOVAL .

    No:  Repair or replace the faulty portion according to the inspection result.
