Inspection Mode W

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
DTC Item Condition Number of driving cycles on the detecting condition
P1492 COIL 1 EGR "A" CONTROL CIRCUIT LOW - 1
P1493 COIL 1 EGR "A" CONTROL CIRCUIT HIGH - 1
P1494 COIL 2 EGR "A" CONTROL CIRCUIT LOW - 1
P1495 COIL 2 EGR "A" CONTROL CIRCUIT HIGH - 1
P1496 COIL 3 EGR "A" CONTROL CIRCUIT LOW - 1
P1497 COIL 3 EGR "A" CONTROL CIRCUIT HIGH - 1
P1498 COIL 4 EGR "A" CONTROL CIRCUIT LOW - 1
P1499 COIL 4 EGR "A" CONTROL CIRCUIT HIGH - 1
WARNING: When performing inspection mode W on a public road, pay sufficient attention to the traffic condition and give the highest priority to safe driving.
NOTE: Perform the inspection mode after warming up the engine except that the engine coolant temperature at engine start is specified.
  1. Check that the battery voltage is 12 V or more and fuel remains approx. half [20 - 40 L (5.3 - 10.6 US gal, 4.4 - 8.8 Imp gal)].
  2. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, clear the memory of [Engine Control System]. Refer to COMMON (DIAG)>CLEAR MEMORY .
  3. Drive according to the drive pattern described below.
