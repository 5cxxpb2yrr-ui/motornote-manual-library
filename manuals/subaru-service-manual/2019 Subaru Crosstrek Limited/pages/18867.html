Clutch Pipe And Hose: Inspection

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Check the clutch pipe, tank hose and clutch hose for breaks and damage. Check the joint for brake fluid leakage.

If crack, breakage or damage is found, repair or replace the faulty clutch pipe, tank hose or clutch hose.
