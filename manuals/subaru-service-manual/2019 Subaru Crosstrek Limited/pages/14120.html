DTC P31B3-00: Hybrid/EV Battery Voltage High: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

1. - 

Outline of diagnosis: 

When the maximum cell voltage of HV battery is more than the standard with charging in progress

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

  • Reduced output power
  • Engine not stopped

Wiring diagram: 

Hybrid system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK DTC. 

    Read the following DTCs using Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) . Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    • P-HEV High Voltage Battery
    • P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control

    When "Only DTC P31B300 is displayed", go to  A.

    When DTCs related to [P-HEV High Voltage Battery] other than P31B300 are simultaneously displayed, go to  B.

    When DTCs related to [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control] other than P31B300 are simultaneously displayed, go to  C.

    Yes: 

    1. Go to  2.
    2. Perform the diagnosis for the simultaneously displayed DTCs.
    3. Perform the diagnosis for the simultaneously displayed DTCs.

    No:  -

  2. CHECK FREEZE FRAME DATA. 

    Using the Subaru Select Monitor, read the value in [Freeze frame data] for [P-HEV High Voltage Battery]. Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>FREEZE FRAME DATA .

    NOTE: DTC may be detected in continuous IG ON state. Therefore check the freeze frame data to make judgment.

    Are all of the following conditions established?

    • [Ready Signal] OFF
    • [SMRP Control Status], [SMRB Control Status], [SMRG Control Status] OFF
    • [Hybrid Battery Current] is -0.5 A or less

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Replace Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL MODULE .

  3. CHECK HV battery junction block (SMRB). 
    1. Check that the Service plug is disconnected. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .
    2. Disconnect the connectors (HV44) and (HV43) from HV battery junction block.
      CAUTION: Be sure to insulate the disconnected high voltage connector by wrapping it with insulating tape. To do this, wrap the insulating tape from Wire harness along to the connector.
    3. Using a tester, measure the resistance between HV battery junction block connector terminals.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV44) No. 1 - (HV43) No. 1:

    Is the resistance 10 kΩ or more?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Replace HV battery junction block. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>RELAY AND FUSE .

  4. CHECK HV battery junction block (SMRG, SMRP). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Disconnect the connectors (HV46) and (HV47) from HV battery junction block.
      CAUTION: Be sure to insulate the disconnected high voltage connector by wrapping it with insulating tape. To do this, wrap the insulating tape from Wire harness along to the connector.
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between HV battery junction block connector terminals.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV47) No. 1 - (HV46) No. 1:

    Is the resistance 10 kΩ or more?

    Yes:  Go to  5.

    No:  Replace HV battery junction block. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>RELAY AND FUSE .

  5. CURRENT SENSOR OFFSET LEARNING. 
    1. Restore the vehicle to its original state.
    2. Turn to READY ON.
    3. Perform driving test.
      CAUTION: Perform driving with slow acceleration/deceleration. Quick acceleration/deceleration must be avoided.
    4. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, display the following items in the [Data monitor] of [P-HEV High Voltage Battery]. Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>DATA MONITOR .
      • Hybrid Battery Current
      NOTE:
      • Drive the vehicle within the range of -50 - 50 A on [Hybrid Battery Current for Hybrid Battery Control] value of [Data monitor] item.
      • There is no instruction about driving distance and driving time.
    5. Turn to IG OFF and wait for 30 seconds or more.
    6. Turn to IG ON.
    7. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, display the following items in the [Data monitor] of [P-HEV High Voltage Battery]. Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>DATA MONITOR .
      • Hybrid Battery Current
    8. Check that the value of [Hybrid Battery Current] is in a range of -0.5 - 0.5 A in IG ON state.

    At IG ON, is the value of [Hybrid Battery Current for Hybrid Battery Control] in the range of -0.5 - 0.5 A?

    Yes:  Finish the diagnosis.

    NOTE:
    • If the current sensor offset learning is not completed, this DTC may be displayed temporarily.
    • When the value of [Hybrid Battery Current Sensor Voltage for Hybrid Battery Control] is in a range of -0.5 - 0.5 A in IG ON state, current sensor offset learning is completed.

    No:  Perform driving test again.

    NOTE: Even if the current sensor offset learning is not completed, current sensor value will be compensated by repeating the driving test 7 times at maximum.
