MT Gear Select Cable: Inspection

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Check that exposed length (a) of MT gear select cable is within specification.

    Exposed length (a) of MT gear select cable:

    Specification: 11 mm (0.4 in)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. If it is not within the specification, perform the adjustment. Refer to CONTROL SYSTEMS>MT GEAR SELECT CABLE>ADJUSTMENT .
