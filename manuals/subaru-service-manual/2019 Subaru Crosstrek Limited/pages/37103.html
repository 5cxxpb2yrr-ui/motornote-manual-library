Relay and Fuse: Location

Main fuse box (M/B) Fuse 10 A (data communication module(1)) (A)
Fuse 30 A (audio) (B)
Fuse 20 A (power amplifier) (C)
Relay & fuse box (F/B) Fuse 20 A (front accessory power supply socket) (D)
Fuse 15 A (rear accessory power supply socket) (E)
Fuse 15 A (audio, external connection terminal, data communication module(1)) (F)
(1) Model with telematics
NOTE: For other related fuses, refer to the wiring diagram.
  • Impreza model: Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (IMPREZA MODEL)>POWER SUPPLY CIRCUIT>WIRING DIAGRAM .
  • Crosstrek model (except for hybrid): Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>POWER SUPPLY CIRCUIT>WIRING DIAGRAM .
  • Crosstrek hybrid model: Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>POWER SUPPLY CIRCUIT>WIRING DIAGRAM .
