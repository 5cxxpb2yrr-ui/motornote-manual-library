Hydraulic Unit

Check the hydraulic unit.

  • When using the brake tester Refer to VEHICLE DYNAMICS CONTROL (VDC)>VDC CONTROL MODULE AND HYDRAULIC CONTROL UNIT (VDCCM & H/U)>INSPECTION>CHECKING THE HYDRAULIC UNIT VDC OPERATION USING A PRESSURE GAUGE .
  • When not using the brake tester Refer to VEHICLE DYNAMICS CONTROL (VDC)>VDC CONTROL MODULE AND HYDRAULIC CONTROL UNIT (VDCCM & H/U)>INSPECTION>CHECKING THE HYDRAULIC UNIT ABS OPERATION BY PRESSURE GAUGE .
