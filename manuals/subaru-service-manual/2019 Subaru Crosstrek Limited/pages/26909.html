Monitor Description

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

The Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly monitors the +B power source voltage and detects momentary interruptions.

If there is a momentary interruption in the +B power source voltage, the Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly will illuminate the MIL and store a DTC.
