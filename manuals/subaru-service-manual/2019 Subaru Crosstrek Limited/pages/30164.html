Hose And Pipe: Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
CAUTION: Airbag system satellite safing sensor is located under the front center of the rear seat cushion. Be careful not to apply strong impact to the sensor when working with the rear seat cushion removed.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery and wait for at least 60 seconds before starting work. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the mat - rear floor.
    1. Release the clip and hook, and then remove the mat - rear floor.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the rear seat cushion assembly. Refer to SEATS>REAR SEAT>REMOVAL .
  4. Remove the trim panel - rear apron LH. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>REAR QUARTER TRIM>REMOVAL .
  5. Remove the duct assembly - gas out.
    1. Release the clip and claws, and remove the duct assembly - gas out.
