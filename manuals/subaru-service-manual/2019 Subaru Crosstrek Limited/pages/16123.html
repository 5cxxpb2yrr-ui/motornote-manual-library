Inverter Front Water Pipe

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Install the bracket and install the clamp (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    7.5 N.m (0.8 gf-m, 5.5 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Connect the inverter water pump outlet hose to the quick connector.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. When the bracket was replaced
    1. Install the bracket and install the clip (A).

      Tightening torque: 

      7.5 N.m (0.8 gf-m, 5.5 ft-lb)

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Install the air cleaner case (front) and the air cleaner element.

      Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>AIR CLEANER CASE>INSTALLATION .

  4. Connect the inverter radiator inlet hose (A), inverter water pump outlet hose (B), inverter center water pipe outlet hose (C) and inverter center water pipe inlet hose (D) to the inverter front water pipe.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Set the inverter front water pipe together with the inverter radiator inlet hose, inverter water pump outlet hose, inverter center water pipe inlet hose and inverter center water pipe outlet hose.
  6. Install the bolt securing the inverter front water pipe.

    Tightening torque: 

    7.5 N.m (0.8 gf-m, 5.5 ft-lb)

  7. Install the clip (C), and connect the connector (A) and connector (B).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Install the nut to secure the inverter front water pipe to the bracket.

    Tightening torque: 

    7.5 N.m (0.8 gf-m, 5.5 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Install the air intake case (rear).

    Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>AIR CLEANER CASE>INSTALLATION .

  10. Install the air intake boot.

    Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>Air Intake Boot>INSTALLATION .

  11. Connect the inverter center water pipe outlet hose (B) and inverter center water pipe inlet hose (C) to the inverter center water pipe.
  12. Install the inverter center water pipe outlet hose (B) and inverter center water pipe inlet hose (C) to the clamp (A).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  13. Install the crossmember support - rear RH.

    Refer to FRONT SUSPENSION>CROSSMEMBER SUPPORT>INSTALLATION .

  14. Connect the inverter radiator inlet hose to the radiator.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  15. Connect the battery ground terminal.

    Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .

  16. Fill engine coolant. (Inverter assembly side)

    Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>ENGINE COOLANT>REPLACEMENT>FILLING OF ENGINE COOLANT>INVERTER ASSEMBLY SIDE .
