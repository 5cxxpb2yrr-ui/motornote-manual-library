Pre-Delivery Inspection (PDI) Procedure: Lighting

  1. Check the headlight operations. When pulling out the key, check if the headlight illuminates by turning the headlight switch from OFF to ON.
  2. Check the stop light operation.
  3. Check other lights for normal operations.
