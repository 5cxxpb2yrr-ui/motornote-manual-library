Front Fog Light Assembly: Assembly

CAUTION: Use a dry clean cloth so that no grease or water adheres to the glass portion of the bulb.

Install the front fog light bulb.

NOTE: Since the parts for LED model is a non-disassembled part, it cannot be removed. Replace the fog light assembly - front if faulty.
