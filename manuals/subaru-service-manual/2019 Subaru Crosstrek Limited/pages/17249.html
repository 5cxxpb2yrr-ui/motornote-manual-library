Fuel Delivery And Evaporation Lines: Installation: Notes

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Install the fuel sub delivery tube. (Crosstrek model)
    1. Set the fuel sub delivery tube to the fuel tank.
      NOTE:
      • Route the fuel sub delivery tube in front of the stand-off as shown in the figure.
      • Check the fuel sub delivery tube to make sure it's not twisted.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Install the fuel sub level sensor. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FUEL SUB LEVEL SENSOR>INSTALLATION .
    3. Install the fuel pump. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FUEL PUMP>INSTALLATION .
  2. Install the fuel pipe assembly to the pipe clamp.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Install the pipe clamp to the fuel pipe assembly, and then install the fuel pipe bracket B and fuel pipe bracket C.

    Tightening torque: 

    18 N.m (1.8 kgf-m, 13.3 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Connect the brake pipes. Refer to BRAKE>BRAKE HOSE>INSTALLATION>REAR BRAKE HOSE . Refer to BRAKE>BRAKE PIPE>INSTALLATION>CENTER BRAKE PIPE .
  5. Install the floor under protector RH. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>FLOOR UNDER PROTECTOR>INSTALLATION .
  6. Connect the purge tube to the fuel pipe assembly. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>INSTALLATION>QUICK CONNECTOR CONNECTION (TYPE G) .
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Connect individual tubes and hoses to the fuel tank. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>COMPONENT>FUEL TANK .
    • For information about connecting fuel delivery tube, refer to "Fuel Delivery and Evaporation Lines". Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>INSTALLATION>QUICK CONNECTOR CONNECTION (TYPE D) .
    • For information about connecting the circulate tube, refer to "Fuel Delivery and Evaporation Lines". Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>INSTALLATION>QUICK CONNECTOR CONNECTION (TYPE G) .
    • For information about connecting the vent tube, refer to "Fuel Delivery and Evaporation Lines". Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>INSTALLATION>QUICK CONNECTOR CONNECTION (TYPE F) . Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>INSTALLATION>QUICK CONNECTOR CONNECTION (TYPE H) .
    • For information about connecting fuel filler hose, refer to "Fuel Delivery and Evaporation Lines". Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>INSTALLATION>FUEL FILLER HOSE CONNECTION .
  8. Install the fuel tank. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FUEL TANK>INSTALLATION .
  9. Install the pipe clamp to the fuel pipe assembly, and then install the fuel pipe bracket A.

    Tightening torque: 

    18 N.m (1.8 kgf-m, 13.3 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  10. Lower the vehicle.
  11. Install the connector to the fuel pipe bracket A.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  12. Connect the evaporation hose to fuel pipe assembly.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  13. Connect the quick connector on the fuel delivery tube to the fuel pipe assembly. Refer to FUEL INJECTION FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>INSTALLATION>QUICK CONNECTOR CONNECTION (TYPE A) .
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  14. Install the fuel delivery tube clamp to the fuel delivery tube.
Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
