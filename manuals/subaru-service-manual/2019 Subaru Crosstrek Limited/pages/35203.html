Rear Hub Unit Bearing: Assembly

  1. Install the rear hub unit bearing to the ST securely.

    Preparation tool: 

    ST: HUB STAND (927080000)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Using a press, press new hub bolts (b) until their seating surfaces contact the rear hub unit bearing (a).
NOTE: Use the 12 mm (0.47 in) dia. holes in the HUB STAND to prevent bolts from tilting.
