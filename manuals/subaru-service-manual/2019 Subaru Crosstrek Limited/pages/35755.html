Outline Of Diagnosis

If the learning value of the pressure-increasing solenoid valve or the pressure-reducing solenoid valve is not stored and the vehicle is being driven at a certain speed or more, the electric brake booster control module judges that the offset learning of the linear solenoid valve is not performed and illuminates the MIL and stores this DTC.
