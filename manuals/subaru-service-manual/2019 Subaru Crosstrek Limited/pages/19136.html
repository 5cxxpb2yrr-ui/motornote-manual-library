Low-Pressure Hose

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Using the refrigerant recovery system, discharge refrigerant. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>REFRIGERANT RECOVERY PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .
  2. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  3. Remove the collector cover. Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>COLLECTOR COVER>REMOVAL .
  4. Disconnect the hose - pressure discharge.
    1. Remove the bolts and remove the hose - pressure discharge.
    CAUTION: Use a plug or vinyl tape to seal the engaging parts of the disconnected hose and electric compressor assembly to prevent foreign matter from entering.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the hose - pressure suction.
    1. Remove the bolts and remove the hose - pressure suction.
    CAUTION:
    • Do not apply excessive force to the hose.
    • Use a plug or vinyl tape to seal the engaging parts of the disconnected hose and electric compressor assembly to prevent foreign matter from entering.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
