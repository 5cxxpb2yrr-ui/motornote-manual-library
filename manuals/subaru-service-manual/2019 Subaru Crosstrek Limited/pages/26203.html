Monitor Description

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

The Battery ECU assembly monitors the Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly via CAN communication. If the Battery ECU assembly detects a malfunction in the hybrid vehicle control module, it will illuminate the MIL and store a DTC.
