Diagnostic Method

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

If the duration of time while the following conditions are met is longer than the time indicated, judge as NG.

JUDGMENT VALUE SPECIFICATION

Malfunction Criteria Threshold Value
| Intake air temperature 4 sec. after hybrid system start - Ambient temperature 4 sec. after hybrid system start| More than value of Map1
| Intake air temperature 4 sec. after hybrid system start - EGR gas temperature 4 sec. after hybrid system start| More than value of Map2
