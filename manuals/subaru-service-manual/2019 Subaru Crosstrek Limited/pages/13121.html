IMPREZA Model

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Remove the strut mount protection cap.
  2. Loosen the nut for a strut mount CTR by 2 turns.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Lift up the vehicle, and then remove the front wheels.
  4. Remove the under cover - front.

    Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>FRONT UNDER COVER>REMOVAL .

  5. Remove the crossmember support - rear.

    Refer to FRONT SUSPENSION>CROSSMEMBER SUPPORT>REMOVAL .

  6. Disconnect the tie-rod end.
    NOTE: Securely hook the tie-rod ball joint puller (e) to the collar (f).
    1. Pull out the cotter pin (a).
    2. Remove the castle nut (b) and washer (c).
    3. Using a tie-rod ball joint puller (e), detach the tie-rod end (d).

      Preparation tool: 

      Tie-rod ball joint puller

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Remove the left and right stabilizer links (bottom part).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Remove the flange nut on the bottom of the ball joint.
    NOTE:
    • To prevent damage of the front arm ball joint boot, place a wooden block, etc. between front arm and ball joint.
    • Mark the engaging position of the ball joint and front arm assembly before loosening the flange nuts. Doing so will facilitate aligning the position when assembling and lessen adjusting allowance for wheel alignment.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Remove the drive shaft.

    Refer to DRIVE SHAFT SYSTEM>FRONT DRIVE SHAFT>REMOVAL .

  10. Remove the ball joint.
    NOTE: Securely hook the tie-rod ball joint puller (e) to the collar (f).
    1. Pull out the snap pin (a), and remove the flange nut (b) and washer (c).
    2. Use a tie-rod ball joint puller (e) to detach the ball joint (d).

      Preparation tool: 

      Tie-rod ball joint puller

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Pull out the ball joint.
