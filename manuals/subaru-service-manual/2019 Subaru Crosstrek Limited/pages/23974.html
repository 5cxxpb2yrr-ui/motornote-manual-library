Section 21 (Emission Control Systems (Aux. Emission Control Devices) (HEV)): Canister: Removal

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery.

    Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .

  2. Remove the rear wheel LH.

    Refer to WHEEL AND TIRE SYSTEM>TIRE AND WHEEL>REMOVAL .

  3. Lift up the vehicle.
  4. Remove the mud guard - rear LH.

    Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>MUD GUARD>REMOVAL .

  5. Disconnect the vent tube No. 1 (A) and remove from the tube clamp,

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>REMOVAL>QUICK CONNECTOR DISCONNECTION (TYPE H) .

  6. Disconnect the purge tube No. 2 (B) and remove from the tube clamp.

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>REMOVAL>QUICK CONNECTOR DISCONNECTION (TYPE E) .

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Disconnect the connector (A), and remove the canister and drain filter as an assembly.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Disconnect the connector (A), and remove the harness from the canister protector.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Remove the nut (A) securing the drain filter.
  10. Remove the drain filter.

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>REMOVAL>QUICK CONNECTOR DISCONNECTION (TYPE G) .

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  11. Remove the canister protector (A).
  12. Disconnect the vent tube No. 1 (B).

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>REMOVAL>QUICK CONNECTOR DISCONNECTION (TYPE H) .

  13. Disconnect the purge tube No. 2 (C).

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>REMOVAL>QUICK CONNECTOR DISCONNECTION (TYPE D) .

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  14. Disconnect the vent tube No. 2 (A) from the electric vent solenoid valve assembly, and remove from the tube clamp.

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>REMOVAL>QUICK CONNECTOR DISCONNECTION (TYPE H) .

  15. Disconnect the purge tube No. 1 (B) from the fuel pipe assembly, and remove from the tube clamp.

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>REMOVAL>QUICK CONNECTOR DISCONNECTION (TYPE E) .

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
