DTC P04ED: EVAP System Large Leak Detected - Fresh Air Side: Notes

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

1. - 

DTC detecting condition: 

Detected when two consecutive driving cycles with fault occur.

Trouble symptom: 

Gasoline odor

NOTE: After the faulty parts are repaired or replaced, perform the final check in Basic Diagnostic Procedure. Refer to ENGINE (DIAGNOSTICS) (H4DO (HEV))>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .
  1. CHECK PURGE CONTROL SOLENOID VALVE  .

    Check the purge control solenoid valve. Refer to EMISSION CONTROL (AUX. EMISSION CONTROL DEVICES) (H4DO (HEV))>PURGE CONTROL SOLENOID VALVE>INSPECTION .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Replace the purge control solenoid valve. Refer to EMISSION CONTROL (AUX. EMISSION CONTROL DEVICES) (H4DO (HEV))>PURGE CONTROL SOLENOID VALVE .

  2. CHECK MECHANICAL FAULTS  .

    Check the evaporative emission control system.

    NOTE: Check the following items.
    • Evaporation line damage (crack, hole etc.)
    • Clogged evaporation line
    • Disconnection of evaporation line
    • Misconnection of evaporation line Refer to EMISSION CONTROL (AUX. EMISSION CONTROL DEVICES) (H4DO (HEV))>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>COMPONENT .
    • Mechanical Relief Valve stuck Refer to EMISSION CONTROL (AUX. EMISSION CONTROL DEVICES) (H4DO (HEV))>ELECTRIC VENT SOLENOID VALVE ASSEMBLY>INSPECTION>CHECK MECHANICAL RELIEF VALVE .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Repair the poor contact of ECM connector.

    No:  Repair or replace faulty parts.
