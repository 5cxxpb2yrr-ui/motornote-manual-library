Basic Inspection

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Before performing diagnosis, check the following items which might affect the A/C problems.

  1. Check the battery. Refer to STARTING/CHARGING SYSTEMS (H4DO (HEV))>BATTERY .
  2. Check the main and other fuses, harnesses and connectors. Also check grounding.
  3. Check the refrigerant pressure. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>REFRIGERANT PRESSURE WITH MANIFOLD GAUGE SET .
