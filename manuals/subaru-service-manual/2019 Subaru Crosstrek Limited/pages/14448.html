DTC P1C00-9E: Voltage Sensor After Boosting By PFC Boosting Circuit Overvoltage Detection Circuit Stuck On: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Outline of diagnosis: 

Charger post-boost overvoltage detection circuit (PFC boost circuit) became stuck in ON state.

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

Plug-in charging suspended

NOTE: This DTC is output when the Electric vehicle charger assembly detects an error in the DCDC converter overvoltage circuit.
Therefore if this DTC was detected, replace the Electric vehicle charger assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>ELECTRIC VEHICLE CHARGER .
