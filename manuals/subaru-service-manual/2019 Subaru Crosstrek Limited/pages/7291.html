Hybrid Model

• V-BELT 

  1. When working on the vehicle
    NOTE: When working on the vehicle, perform the following steps also.
    1. Remove the front under cover. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>FRONT UNDER COVER>REMOVAL .
  2. Cut off the V-belt with a nipper, etc. to remove.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.

• IDLER PULLEY 

  1. Remove the V-belts. Refer to MECHANICAL (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>V-BELT>REMOVAL>HYBRID MODEL>V-BELT .
  2. Remove the idler pulley.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
