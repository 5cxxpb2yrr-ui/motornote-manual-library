Outline Of Diagnosis

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Detect malfunctions of the thermostat function.

Judge as NG when any one of the following conditions is established.

  • When the actual engine coolant temperature does not reach the maximum temperature for executing other OBDII diagnosis and (Estimated engine coolant temperature - Actual engine coolant temperature) exceeds the predetermined value (judgment 1)
  • When the actual engine coolant temperature does not reach the range within -11°C (-19.8°F) from the regulated temperature and (Estimated engine coolant temperature - actual engine coolant temperature) exceeded the predetermined value. (Judgment 2)
