DTC U0422: Invalid Data Received From Body Control Module

DTC detecting condition: 

CAN data from body integrated unit is abnormal.

Trouble symptom: 

  • After the "Welcome" is displayed, even if the ignition is turned to ACC ON, the screen remains in black and the system does not startup.
  • When the ignition is turned to ACC ON while the "Welcome" screen is being displayed, the screen remains the same.
  • When the driver's door is opened while the "Good-bye" screen is being displayed, the screen remains the same.
  • Frequency of advice to take a rest increases.
  • Brightness of multi-function display does not change.
  • Brake light status is not displayed correctly in the electrical parts operation screen.
  • Headlight status is not displayed correctly in the electrical parts operation screen.
  • Front fog light status is not displayed correctly in the electrical parts operation screen.
  • Rear fog light status is not displayed correctly in the electrical parts operation screen.
  • Headlight AUTO switch status is not displayed correctly in the electrical parts operation screen.
NOTE: For the diagnostic procedure, refer to DTC U0074. Refer to MULTI-FUNCTION DISPLAY (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE WITH DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>DTC U0074 CONTROL MODULE COMMUNICATION BUS "BODY-CAN" OFF .
