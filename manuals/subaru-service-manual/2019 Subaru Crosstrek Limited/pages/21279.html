Diagnostic Procedure For Subaru Select Monitor Communication: Inspection

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Trouble symptom: 

Cannot communicate with [P-HEV Drive Motor].

Detecting condition: 

  • Defective Wire harness connector
  • Power supply circuit malfunction
  • Inverter with converter assembly malfunctioning
  • Defective CAN communication circuit
  • Defective Subaru Select Monitor

Wiring diagram: 

Hybrid system. Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to P-HEV DRIVE MOTOR (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK FUSE. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Check the fuse. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>RELAY AND FUSE . Is there any fault?

      Replace the fuse. If the replaced fuse blows out easily, repair the short circuit to ground in the Wire harness between the fuse and the - Inverter with converter assembly.

      Go to  2.

  2. CHECK POWER SUPPLY CIRCUIT (OPEN). 
    1. Disconnect the Inverter with converter assembly connector.
    2. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    3. Using a tester, measure the voltage between Inverter with converter assembly connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (HV23) No. 1 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    (HV23) No. 5 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    Is the voltage 10 V or more?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Repair the open circuit in Wire harness between fuse -Inverter with converter assembly.

  3. CHECK GROUND CIRCUIT (OPEN). 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between Inverter with converter assembly connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (HV23) No. 4 - Chassis ground:

    (HV23) No. 8 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    Yes:  Repair the open circuit in Wire harness between Inverter with converter assembly - chassis ground.

  4. CHECK LAN SYSTEM. 
    1. Connect the Inverter with converter assembly connector.
    2. Inspect LAN system. Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .

    Is there any fault?

    Yes:  Perform the inspection according to the diagnosis for LAN system.

    Yes:  Go to  5.

  5. CHECK Inverter with converter assembly. 

    Communicate with [P-HEV Drive Motor].

    Is communication possible?

    Yes:  The circuit has returned to a normal condition at this time. Reproduce the failure, and then perform the diagnosis again.

    NOTE: In this case, temporary poor contact of connector, temporary open or short circuit of Wire harness may be the cause.

    No:  Replace Inverter with converter assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>INVERTER AND CONVERTER ASSEMBLY .
