Reverse Brake Assembly: Inspection

  • Inspect the drive plate facing for wear and damage.
  • Check the driven plate for discoloration (burnt color).
  • Check for worn snap ring, fatigue or damaged return spring or deformed spring retainer.
  • Make sure the clearance between retaining plate and snap ring of reverse brake is within the limit. If it exceeds the limit, replace with a new drive plate and select and adjust the retaining plate within the initial standard value.
