Learning Control: General Description

  • Follow The Messages Displayed On The Subaru Select Monitor When Working.
  • When The Following Work Is Performed, Perform Learning Work For The Transmission. Replacement Of Tcm/Replacement Or Disassembly Of Transmission Assembly/Replacement Of Control Valve Body/Clearing At Leaning Value Is Executed.
