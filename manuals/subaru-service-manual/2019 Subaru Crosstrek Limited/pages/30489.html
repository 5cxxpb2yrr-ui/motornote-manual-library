Fuel Level Sensor: Inspection

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Check that the fuel level sensor has no damage.
  2. Measure the fuel level sensor float position.
    NOTE: When inspecting the fuel level sensor, perform the work with the sensor installed to the fuel pump.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Float position Standard
    FULL-Fuel tank seating surface H1 205.9 ± 5.0 mm (8.106 ± 0.197 in)
    EMPTY-Fuel tank seating surface H2 20.9 ± 5.0 mm (0.823 ± 0.197 in)
  3. Check the resistance between fuel level sensor terminals by the connector on top of the fuel pump.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Float position Terminal No. Standard
    FULL (A) 1 and 4 17.4 ± 2.0 Ω
    EMPTY (B) 410.0 ± 4.5 Ω
