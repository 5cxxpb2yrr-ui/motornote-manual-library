Oil Seal: Replacement

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery, refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Lift up the vehicle.
  3. Clean the transmission exterior.
  4. Using the TORX® bit T70, remove the drain plug, and drain the transmission gear oil completely.
    CAUTION:
    • Immediately after the vehicle has been running or after idling for a long time, the gear oil will be hot. Be careful not to receive burns.
    • Be careful not to spill the gear oil on the exhaust pipe, to prevent emission of smoke or causing a fire. If gear oil is spilled, wipe it off completely.
  5. Replace the gasket with a new part and install the drain plug using the TORX® bit T70.

    Tightening torque: 

    44 N.m (4.5 kgf-m, 32.5 ft-lb) (aluminum gasket, silver)

    70 N.m (7.1 kgf-m, 51.6 ft-lb) (copper gasket, brown)

    70 N.m (7.1 kgf-m, 51.6 ft-lb) (metal gasket, black)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Remove the rear exhaust pipe. refer to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>REAR EXHAUST PIPE>REMOVAL .
  7. Remove the center exhaust cover.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Remove the propeller shaft. refer to DRIVE SHAFT SYSTEM>PROPELLER SHAFT>REMOVAL .
  9. Using the ST, remove the oil seal.

    Special Tool: 

    ST: 398527700 PULLER ASSY

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  10. Using the ST, install a new oil seal.
    NOTE: When grease is not applied to the oil seal lips, apply the transmission gear oil to the oil seal lips.

    Special Tool: 

    ST: 498057300 INSTALLER

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  11. Install the propeller shaft. refer to DRIVE SHAFT SYSTEM>PROPELLER SHAFT>INSTALLATION .
  12. Install the center exhaust cover.

    Tightening torque: 

    18 N.m (1.8 kgf-m, 13.3 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  13. Install the rear exhaust pipe, refer to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>REAR EXHAUST PIPE>INSTALLATION .
  14. Lower the vehicle.
  15. Pour in transmission gear oil and check the oil level, refer to MANUAL TRANSMISSION AND DIFFERENTIAL (6MT)>TRANSMISSION GEAR OIL .
  16. Connect the battery ground terminal. refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
