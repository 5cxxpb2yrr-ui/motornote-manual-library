EV Charger Power Supply System

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .

Wiring diagram: 

Charging system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>CHARGING SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  1. CHECK DTC. 

    Using the Subaru Select Monitor, read all DTCs. Refer to COMMON (diag)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Are any DTCs displayed?

    Yes:  Perform the diagnosis for the displayed DTCs.

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK Electric vehicle charger assembly (GROUND). 

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between the Electric vehicle charger assembly case and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    Electric vehicle charger assembly case - Chassis ground:

    NOTE: Because the Electric vehicle charger assembly uses a case ground structure, if the check results are outside the standard range, check the tightening conditions of the ground bolt.

    Is the resistance less than 1 kΩ?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Tighten the Electric vehicle charger assembly connecting bolts.

  3. CHECK THE Electric vehicle charger assembly (AMD2 VOLTAGE). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Check that the Service plug is disconnected. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .
    2. Connect the negative terminal of the Battery (12V). Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    3. Using a tester, measure the voltage between Electric vehicle charger assembly connector and chassis ground. (With the connector connected)

      Connector & terminal 

      (R516) No. 1 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    Is the voltage 11 - 14 V?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Go to  5.

  4. CHECK THE Electric vehicle charger assembly (IGCT VOLTAGE). 
    1. Turn to IG ON.
    2. Using a tester, measure the voltage between Electric vehicle charger assembly connector and chassis ground. (With the connector connected)

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV31) No. 6 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    Is the voltage 11 - 14 V?

    Yes:  The power supply circuit is normal.

    No:  Go to  6.

  5. CHECK THE FUSE (SBF-9 30A FUSE). 
    1. Turn the ignition to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the negative terminal of the Battery (12V). Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    3. Remove the SBF-9 30A FUSE from the Relay & fuse box (F/B). Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>RELAY AND FUSE .
    4. Check SBF-9 30A FUSE.

    Is the SBF-9 30A FUSE OK?

    Yes:  Repair or replace the Wire harness between Relay & fuse box (F/B) and Electric vehicle charger assembly connector.

    No:  Go to  9.

  6. CHECK THE FUSE (F/B NO. 11 10A FUSE). 
    1. Turn the ignition to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the negative terminal of the Battery (12V). Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    3. Remove the F/B NO. 11 10A FUSE from the Relay & fuse box (F/B). Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>RELAY AND FUSE .
    4. Check F/B NO.ll 10A FUSE.

    Is the IGCT SCENE FUSE OK?

    Yes:  Go to  7.

    No:  Go to  10.

  7. CHECK THE Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Relay & fuse box (F/B) - Electric vehicle charger assembly). 
    1. Disconnect Electric vehicle charger assembly connector (HV31).
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between Relay & fuse box (F/B) and Electric vehicle charger assembly connector.

      Connector & terminal 

      F/B NO.ll 10A FUSE (unit side) - (HV31) No. 6:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  8.

    No:  Repair or replace the Wire harness between Relay & fuse box (F/B) and Electric vehicle charger assembly connector.

  8. CHECK RELAY UNIT (IGCT scene relay). 
    1. Remove the IGCT scene relay from the Main fuse box (M/B).
    2. Check the IGCT scene relay unit. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>RELAY AND FUSE .

    Is the IGCT scene relay OK?

    Yes:  Repair or replace the harness between IGCT scene relay holder and F/B NO. 11 10A FUSE holder.

    No:  Replace IGCT scene relay. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>RELAY AND FUSE .

  9. CHECK THE Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Electric vehicle charger assembly - Main fuse box (M/B)). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Disconnect Electric vehicle charger assembly connector (HV31).
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between terminals in Electric vehicle charger assembly connector, and between Electric vehicle charger assembly connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (R516) No. 1 - Chassis ground:

      (R516) No. 1 - (R516) No. 2:

    Is the resistance 10 kΩ or more?

    Yes:  Replace SBF-9 30A FUSE. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>RELAY AND FUSE .

    No:  Repair or replace the Wire harness between Electric vehicle charger assembly and SBF-9 30A FUSE. Replace SBF-9 30A FUSE. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>RELAY AND FUSE .

  10. CHECK THE Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Electric vehicle charger assembly - Main fuse box (M/B)). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Disconnect Electric vehicle charger assembly connector (HV31).
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between terminals in Electric vehicle charger assembly connector, and between Electric vehicle charger assembly connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV31) No. 6 - Chassis ground:

      (HV31) No. 6 - (HV31) No. 1 - 40:

    Is the resistance 10 kΩ or more?

    Yes:  Replace F/B NO.ll 10A FUSE. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>RELAY AND FUSE .

    No:  Repair or replace the Wire harness between Electric vehicle charger assembly and F/B NO. 11 10A FUSE. Replace F/B NO.ll 10A FUSE.

GENERAL DESCRIPTION 

Circuit explanation 

The Electric vehicle charger assembly operates using the continuous power supply from the AMD2 terminal. When IG is ON and during charging, voltage is applied to the IGCT terminal from the IGCT scene relay that is started by the Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly. The Electric vehicle charger assembly is also connected to ground via the case.
