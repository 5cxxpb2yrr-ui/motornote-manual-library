Transfer Drive Gear: Inspection

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Ball bearing

    Replace the bearings in the following cases.

    • In case of broken or rusty bearings
    • In case of worn or damaged bearings
    • When the bearings fail to turn smoothly or emit noise in rotation after transmission gear oil has been applied.
  2. Transfer drive gear

    If the drive gear tooth surface and shaft are excessively broken or damaged, replace the drive gear.

  3. Measure the clearance between snap ring and inner race of ball bearing with a thickness gauge.

    Specification: 

    0.01 - 0.15 mm (0.0004 - 0.0059 in)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.

    If the measurement is not within specification, select a suitable snap ring and replace it.
