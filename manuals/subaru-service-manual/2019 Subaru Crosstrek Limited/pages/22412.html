Outline Of Diagnosis

After replacing the electric brake booster control module or the brake accumulator assembly, if air bleeding is not performed and the vehicle is driven at a certain speed or more, the electric brake booster control module judges that air bleeding has not been completed and illuminates the MIL and stores this DTC.
