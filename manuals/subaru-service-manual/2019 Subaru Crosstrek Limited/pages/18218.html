Front Door Panel

CAUTION: When the panel assembly - front door or the door hinge is replaced with a new part, apply sealant to the door hinge contacting surface.
  1. Put a wooden block and the like on a jack and place the panel assembly - front door on it.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Apply grease to the sliding area of door hinges, and install to vehicle.
  3. Adjust the height by the jack, and temporarily install the panel assembly - front door to the upper hinge - front door and the lower hinge - front door.
    NOTE:
    • When installing the panel assembly - front door, make sure that a uniform clearance is created around the panel.
    • Perform installation while paying attention to the assembly direction of the grommet.
  4. Adjust the clearance around the panel assembly - front door. Refer to EXTERIOR BODY PANELS>FRONT DOOR>ADJUSTMENT .
  5. Tighten the mounting bolts of the panel assembly - front door to the specified torque.

    Tightening torque: 

    25 N.m (2.5 kgf-m, 18.4 ft-lb)

  6. Tighten the bolts and nuts of the checker assembly - front door.

    Tightening torque: 

    Refer to "COMPONENT" of "General Description". Refer to EXTERIOR BODY PANELS>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>COMPONENT>FRONT DOOR .

  7. Attach the door sash tape. Refer to EXTERIOR BODY PANELS>DOOR SASH TAPE>INSTALLATION .
  8. Install the cover B pillar - front.
  9. Before installing each part, check the following items.
    • Rod of the latch and actuator assembly - front is free from deformation.
    • Grease is applied sufficiently to the rod joints of the latch and actuator assembly - front.
    • Cable of the latch and actuator assembly - front is free from deformation such as fray.
    • Grease is applied sufficiently to the cable joints of the latch and actuator assembly - front.
  10. Install door internal parts in the reverse order of removal.
    • Weather strip - front door
    • Handle - door outer

      Tightening torque: 

      7.5 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.5 ft-lb)

    • Latch & actuator ASSY - front
      CAUTION:
      • Do not re-use the screws. Always replace with a new part.
      • Insert the rod of the latch & actuator assembly - front into the frame assembly - front door outer, and connect using the rod clamp.

      Preparation tool: 

      TORX® T30

      Tightening torque: 

      6.5 N.m (0.7 kgf-m, 4.8 ft-lb)

    • Regulator & motor assembly - front for the front door

      Tightening torque: 

      7.5 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.5 ft-lb)

    • Sash assembly - partition

      Tightening torque: 

      7.5 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.5 ft-lb)

    • Install the cover B pillar - front door INN.
      CAUTION:
      • Always replace with a new part, because engagement of the claw weakens.
      • Do not damage the sash or sash tape INN while installing.
    • Running channel - front door
      CAUTION: Check that the running channel - front door is securely fixed to the panel assembly front door and to the sash assembly partition.
    • Glass assembly - front door, and glass - front door partition

      Tightening torque: 

      7.5 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.5 ft-lb)

  11. Connect door harness connector.
  12. Install the speaker assembly - front.
  13. Install the outer mirror assembly.

    Tightening torque: 

    7.5 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.5 ft-lb)

  14. Install the front sealing cover.
  15. Install the trim panel - front door. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>DOOR TRIM>INSTALLATION .
  16. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY>GROUND TERMINAL REMOVAL OR INSTALLATION .
