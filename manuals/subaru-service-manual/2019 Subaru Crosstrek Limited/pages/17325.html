Rear Hub Unit Bearing: Installation

  1. Install the rear brake back plate and rear hub unit bearing to the rear axle housing.
    CAUTION:
    • Be careful not to damage the magnetic encoder.
    • Do not get closer the tool which charged magnetism to magnetic encoder.
    • Use a new bolt.

    Tightening torque: 

    Impreza model: 65 N.m (6.6 kgf-m, 47.9 ft-lb)

    Crosstrek model: 85 N.m (8.7 kgf-m, 62.7 ft-lb)

  2. Install the rear drive shaft assembly.
    CAUTION:
    • Do not hammer the drive shaft when installing it.
    • Be sure to use a new axle nut.
    • Be careful not to damage the spline of the drive shaft.
    • Be careful not to damage the magnetic encoder.
    1. Insert the rear drive shaft assembly into the hub spline, and pull it into the specified position.
    2. Tighten the axle nut temporarily.
  3. Install the rear disc rotor and rear disc brake.
    CAUTION: Use a new bolt.

    Tightening torque: 

    For tightening torque, refer to "BRAKE" instructions. Ref. to BRAKE>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>COMPONENT>REAR DISC BRAKE .

  4. Install the brake hose bracket and rear ABS wheel speed sensor.

    Tightening torque: 

    Brake hose bracket: 33 N.m (3.4 kgf-m, 24.3 ft-lb)

    Rear ABS wheel speed sensor: 7.5 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.5 ft-lb)

  5. While pressing the brake pedal, tighten the new axle nuts to the specified torque.
    CAUTION: Do not load the rear axle before tightening the axle nut. Doing so may damage the hub unit bearing.

    Tightening torque: 

    Impreza model: 240 N.m (24.5 kgf-m, 177 ft-lb)

    Crosstrek model: 190 N.m (19.4 kgf-m, 140.1 ft-lb)

  6. After tightening the axle nut, lock it securely.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Connect the battery ground terminal. Ref. to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  8. Install the rear wheels, and perform the following inspections.

Tightening torque: 

120 N.m (12.2 kgf-m, 88.5 ft-lb)

  1. Check the wheels for smooth rotation.
  2. Check that there is no looseness by moving the upper and lower portions of rear tire in an axial direction with the brake pedal released.
  • Looseness exists  → Check the rear hub unit bearing. Ref. to DRIVE SHAFT SYSTEM>REAR HUB UNIT BEARING>INSPECTION .
