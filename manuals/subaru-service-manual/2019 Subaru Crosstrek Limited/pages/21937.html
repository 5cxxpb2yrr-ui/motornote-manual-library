Communication For Initializing Impossible

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

Detecting condition: 

Defective harness connector

Trouble symptom: 

Communication is impossible between EEB Control Module and Subaru Select Monitor.

Wiring diagram: 

Electronic controlled brake system Ref. to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>ELECTRIC CONTROL BRAKE SYSTEM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Ref. to BRAKE CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK IGNITION SWITCH. 

    Is the ignition switch ON?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Turn the ignition switch to ON, and select Brake Control using the Subaru Select Monitor.

  2. CHECK BATTERY. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Measure the battery voltage.

    Is the voltage 11 V or more?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Charge or replace the battery.

  3. CHECK BATTERY TERMINAL. 

    Is there poor contact at battery terminal?

    Yes:  Replace or tighten the battery terminal.

    No:  Go to  4.

  4. CHECK INSTALLATION OF EEB Control Module CONNECTOR. 

    Turn the ignition switch to OFF.

    Is the EEB Control Module connector inserted into the EEB Control Module until the clamp locks?

    Yes:  Go to  5.

    No:  Insert the EEB Control Module connector into EEB Control Module.

  5. CHECK LAN SYSTEM. 

    Check the LAN system. Ref. to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  6.

    No:  Perform the diagnosis for LAN system.

  6. CHECK SUBARU SELECT MONITOR COMMUNICATION. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    2. Check whether communication to the electronic controlled brake system can be executed normally.

    Is the [Select Function] screen displayed?

    Yes:  Check DTC of the electronic controlled brake system. Ref. to ELECTRIC BRAKE BOOSTER (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    No:  Go to  7.

  7. CHECK SUBARU SELECT MONITOR COMMUNICATION. 

    Connect the Subaru Select Monitor to another vehicle to check whether the communication for initializing can be executed normally.

    Does the Subaru Select Monitor communicate normally?

    Yes:  Go to  8.

    No:  Repair or replace the Subaru Select Monitor.

  8. CHECK POWER SUPPLY CIRCUIT. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to ON. (Engine OFF)
    2. Disconnect the coupling connector.
    3. Measure the ignition power supply voltage between the intermediate connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (B652) No. 11 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    (B652) No. 23 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    (B652) No. 27 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    (B652) No. 28 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    Is the voltage 10 - 16 V?

    Yes:  Go to  9.

    No:  Repair the open circuit of harness between EEB Control Module and the battery.

  9. CHECK HARNESS CONNECTOR BETWEEN INTERMEDIATE CONNECTOR AND CHASSIS GROUND. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Measure the resistance of harness between the intermediate connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (B652) No. 26 - Chassis ground:

    (B652) No. 29 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance less than 10 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  10.

    No:  Repair the open circuit of the EEB Control Module ground circuit and poor contact of the connector.

  10. CHECK POOR CONTACT OF CONNECTOR. 

    Is there poor contact of control module power supply, ground circuit and data link connector?

    Yes:  Go to  11.

    No:  Repair the connector.

  11. POOR CONTACT IN INTERMEDIATE HARNESS. 
    1. Disconnect the EEB Control Module connector.
    2. Check continuity of harness between the EEB Control Module connector and the intermediate connector.

    Connector & terminal 

    (AD82) No. 8 - (AD81) No. 26:

    (AD82) No. 9 - (AD81) No. 29:

    (AD82) No. 13 - (AD81) No. 27:

    (AD82) No. 17 - (AD81) No. 11:

    (AD82) No. 22 - (AD81) No. 23:

    (AD82) No. 30 - (AD81) No. 28:

    Is there continuity?

    Yes:  Replace the brake booster assembly (with the master cylinder). Ref. to BRAKE>BRAKE BOOSTER .

    No:  Replace the intermediate harness.
