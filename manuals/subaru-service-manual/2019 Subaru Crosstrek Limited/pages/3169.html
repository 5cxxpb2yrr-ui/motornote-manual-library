Navigation System

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

Symptoms Reference
Power will not turn ON. Refer to INFOTAINMENT (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>POWER WILL NOT TURN ON .
The screen freezes and does not accept any operation entry. Refer to INFOTAINMENT (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>THE SCREEN FREEZES AND DOES NOT ACCEPT ANY OPERATION ENTRY .
The screen is blacked out, or the screen is not displayed. Refer to INFOTAINMENT (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>THE SCREEN IS BLACKED OUT, OR THE SCREEN IS NOT DISPLAYED .
System is reset. Refer to INFOTAINMENT (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>SYSTEM IS RESET .
CDs cannot be played back. Refer to INFOTAINMENT (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>CDS CANNOT BE PLAYED BACK .
Sound does not come out from the speaker. Refer to INFOTAINMENT (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>SOUND DOES NOT COME OUT FROM THE SPEAKER .
Bluetooth connection cannot be established. Refer to INFOTAINMENT (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>BLUETOOTH CONNECTION CANNOT BE ESTABLISHED .
Bluetooth does not operate. Refer to INFOTAINMENT (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>BLUETOOTH DOES NOT OPERATE .
Radio volume is low or interference noise occurs. Refer to INFOTAINMENT (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>RADIO VOLUME IS LOW OR INTERFERENCE NOISE OCCURS .
Wrong location of vehicle, or no GPS reception. Refer to INFOTAINMENT (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>WRONG LOCATION OF VEHICLE, NO GPS RECEPTION .
Error message is displayed. Refer to INFOTAINMENT (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>LIST>ERROR MESSAGE IS DISPLAYED .
SUBARU STARLINK is faulty. Refer to INFOTAINMENT (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>LIST>CHECK ABNORMALITY OF SUBARU STARLINK .
