Passenger's Airbag Module: Removal

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

CAUTION: Before handling the airbag system components, refer to "CAUTION" of "General Description" in "AIRBAG SYSTEM". Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
  2. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery and wait for at least 60 seconds before starting work. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    NOTE:
    • For CVT models, release the shift lock and shift the select lever to the "N" position. Refer to CONTROL SYSTEMS>SELECT LEVER>REMOVAL .
    • For power seat models, remove the console box and move the front seat backward before removing the battery ground terminal.
  3. Remove the boot - hand brake, the cover assembly - front and the console box assembly. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>CONSOLE BOX>REMOVAL .
  4. Remove the center grille assembly. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>AIR VENT GRILLE>REMOVAL>CENTER GRILLE ASSEMBLY .
  5. Remove the panel - center UPR, and remove the audio assembly or navigation assembly. Refer to ENTERTAINMENT & MONITORING>AUDIO>REMOVAL .
  6. Remove the heater control assembly. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>CONTROL PANEL>REMOVAL .
  7. Remove the panel center LWR, and remove the tray - center UPR. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>CENTER CONSOLE>REMOVAL .
  8. Remove the cover assembly - instrument panel LWR driver. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>INSTRUMENT PANEL LOWER COVER>REMOVAL .
  9. Remove the knee airbag module. Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>KNEE AIRBAG MODULE>REMOVAL .
  10. Remove the driver's airbag module. Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>DRIVER'S AIRBAG MODULE>REMOVAL .
  11. Remove the steering wheel. Refer to POWER ASSISTED SYSTEM (POWER STEERING)>STEERING WHEEL>REMOVAL .
  12. Remove the visor assembly - combination meter, the cover assembly - visor LWR, and the combination meter assembly. Refer to INSTRUMENTATION/DRIVER INFO>COMBINATION METER>REMOVAL .
  13. Release the claws and clips, and remove the cover - starter SW. (Model without keyless access with push button start)
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  14. Remove the panel - starter SW and the push button ignition switch. (Model with keyless access with push button start) Refer to SECURITY AND LOCKS>PUSH BUTTON IGNITION SWITCH>REMOVAL .
  15. Remove the trim panel - front pillar UPR on the left and right sides. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>UPPER INNER TRIM>REMOVAL .
  16. Remove the grille speaker side and the speaker assembly - tweeter on the left and right sides. Refer to ENTERTAINMENT & MONITORING>FRONT SPEAKER>REMOVAL>TWEETER .
  17. Remove the glove box assembly. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>GLOVE BOX>REMOVAL .
  18. Remove the multi-function display assembly. Refer to INSTRUMENTATION/DRIVER INFO>MULTI-FUNCTION DISPLAY (MFD)>REMOVAL .
  19. Remove the grille - front defroster.
    1. Release the clips, and then raise the grille - front defroster.
    2. Disconnect the sunload sensor connector, and remove the grille - front defroster.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  20. Remove the bolts of the passenger's airbag module, and disconnect the connector. Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>AIRBAG CONNECTOR>PROCEDURE>PASSENGER'S AIRBAG MODULE AND SEAT BELT PRETENSIONER .
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  21. Remove the screws and release the claws, then detach the cover assembly - column.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  22. Remove the switch assembly - combination.
    1. Disconnect each connector, and loosen the clamp to release the claws.
    2. Pull out the switch assembly - combination from the column assembly - steering.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  23. Remove the panel COMPL - instrument.
    1. Remove the harness clamp of the speaker assembly - tweeter.
    2. Release the clamps of the sunload sensor and multi-function display harnesses, and pull them out toward yourself.
      NOTE: Confirm the harness routing of the sunload sensor and multi-function display harnesses.
    3. Remove the screws, and remove the panel COMPL - instrument.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  24. Remove the tapping screws and release the claws, and remove the passenger's airbag module.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  25. For handling of the removed airbag module, refer to "CAUTION". Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
