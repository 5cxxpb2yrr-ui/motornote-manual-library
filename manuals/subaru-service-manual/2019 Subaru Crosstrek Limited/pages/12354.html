Heater Duct: Removal: Foot

LH SIDE 

CAUTION: Before handling the airbag system components, refer to "CAUTION" of "General Description" in "AIRBAG SYSTEM". Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery and wait for at least 60 seconds before starting work. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the cover assembly - instrument panel LWR driver. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>INSTRUMENT PANEL LOWER COVER>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the knee airbag module. Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>KNEE AIRBAG MODULE>REMOVAL .
  4. Remove the duct - foot driver.
    1. Remove the screws securing the spacer - foot and release the claws, then detach the duct - foot driver together with the spacer - foot.
      CAUTION: If the duct - foot is detached from the spacer - foot, replace the duct - foot with a new part. If the duct - foot is reused, the holding force of the claws and clips is reduced and the part may come off.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.

RH SIDE 

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the pocket assembly and the back panel - pocket. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>GLOVE BOX>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the duct - foot passenger.
    1. Remove the screws and remove the duct - foot passenger.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
