Rear Oil Seal

  1. Check that there is no oil leaking from rear oil seal. If there is any oil leak, replace the rear oil seal with a new part according to the following procedures described below.
    1. When working on the vehicle
      NOTE: When working on the vehicle, perform the following steps also.
      1. Remove the transmission main body from the vehicle.

        Except for hybrid model (CVT model): 

        Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL .

        5MT model: 

        Refer to MANUAL TRANSMISSION AND DIFFERENTIAL (5MT)>MANUAL TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL .

        6MT model: 

        Refer to MANUAL TRANSMISSION AND DIFFERENTIAL (6MT)>MANUAL TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL .

        Hybrid model: 

        Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TH2A)>TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL .

    2. Remove the crankshaft position sensor plate with drive plate. (Except for hybrid model (CVT model)) Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>DRIVE PLATE>REMOVAL .
    3. Remove the crankshaft position sensor plate with flywheel. (MT model and hybrid model)

      MT model: 

      Refer to CLUTCH SYSTEM>FLYWHEEL>REMOVAL .

      Hybrid model: 

      Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TH2A)>FLYWHEEL>REMOVAL .

    4. Remove the rear oil seal from cylinder block using the oil seal puller, etc.
      CAUTION: Be careful not to damage the crankshaft during work.
    5. Apply a coat of engine oil to the oil seal inner periphery and outer periphery, and install new rear oil seal using ST1 and ST2.

      Preparation tool: 

      ST1: OIL SEAL GUIDE (18671AA020)

      ST2: OIL SEAL INSTALLER (18657AA030)

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    6. Install in the reverse order of removal after replacement.
