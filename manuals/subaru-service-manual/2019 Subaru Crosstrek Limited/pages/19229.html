DTC B1476: A/C Inverter Low Voltage

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

DTC detecting condition: 

When the low voltage power supply system of A/C inverter is faulty.

Trouble symptom: 

Compressor does not operate.

NOTE: When this DTC is output, replace Electric compressor ASSEMBLY. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>COMPRESSOR .
