DTC P1C69-1F: Generator Phase U-V-W Current Sensor Circuit Intermittent: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Detecting condition: 

When DTC P0C7917, P0D3319, P1C5D19 or P1C5F19 is detected as abnormal, and the total value of UVW-phase sensor output deviates from the proper value and detected as abnormal.

(1 trip detection logic)

-
