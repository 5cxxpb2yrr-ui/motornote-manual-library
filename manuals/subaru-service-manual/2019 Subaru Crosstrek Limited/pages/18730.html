MT Gear Shift Cable: Removal

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Lift up the vehicle.
  3. Remove the front exhaust pipe. Refer to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FRONT EXHAUST PIPE>REMOVAL .
  4. Remove the center exhaust cover.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the cable clamp.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Remove the dust cover.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Remove the snap pin and washer.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Remove the bolts holding the shift bracket.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Remove the shift bracket together with the MT gear shift cable.
    CAUTION: Do not apply excessive load while holding the part (a).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  10. Remove the cable cover plate.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  11. Disconnect the MT gear shift cable.
    CAUTION:
    • Be careful not to deform the cable inner.
    • Do not apply excessive load while holding the part (a).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    NOTE: Pull out down the MT gear shift cable to spread the spring.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  12. Remove the clamp.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  13. Pull out the MT gear shift cable.
CAUTION:
  • Do not bend MT gear shift cable at a sharp angle.
  • Do not twist the cable inner excessively.
Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
