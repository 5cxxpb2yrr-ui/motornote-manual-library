Low Pressure Solenoid Valve

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Using the refrigerant recovery system, discharge refrigerant. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>REFRIGERANT RECOVERY PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .
  2. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  3. Remove the bumper face - front. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>FRONT BUMPER>REMOVAL .
  4. Disconnect the pipe - evaporator cooling and hose - pressure suction valve.
    1. Remove the bolt and disconnect the pipe - evaporator cooling and hose - pressure suction valve.
    CAUTION: Seal the engaging parts of the disconnected pipe, hose and LOW solenoid valve with plugs or vinyl tape to prevent foreign matter from entering.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the LOW solenoid valve.
    1. Disconnect the connector.
    2. Remove the bolts and remove the LOW solenoid valve.
    CAUTION: Seal the engaging parts of the disconnected condenser assembly and LOW solenoid valve with plugs or vinyl tape to prevent foreign matter from entering.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
