Transfer Clutch: Removal

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Remove the transmission assembly from the vehicle. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL .
  2. Remove the extension case. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>EXTENSION CASE>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the transfer clutch assembly.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the thrust bearing.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the seal ring from transfer clutch assembly.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Remove the seal ring from the transfer driven gear assembly.
