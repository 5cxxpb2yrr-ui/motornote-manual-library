Ambient Sensor: Inspection

  1. Visually check the ambient sensor for dirt or damage, and clean or replace as necessary.
  2. Measure the resistance between connector terminals.
    CAUTION: During inspection, be careful not to touch the sensor end in order to avoid misjudgment due to body temperature.

    Preparation tool: 

    Circuit tester

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.

  3. Replace the ambient sensor if the inspection result is not within the standard value.
