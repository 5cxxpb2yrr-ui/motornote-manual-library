Reduction Driven Gear: Disassembly

  1. Flatten the tab of the lock nut.
  2. Using the ST1, ST2 and ST3, counter the rotation of the reduction driven gear assembly, and remove the lock nut.

    Preparation tool: 

    ST1: PULLEY WRENCH (18355AA000)

    ST2: PULLEY WRENCH PIN SET (18334AA000)

    ST3: SOCKET WRENCH (35) (499987003)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the ball bearing using the ST1 and ST2.

    Preparation tool: 

    ST1: INSTALLER (499757002)

    ST2: REMOVER (498077300)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Using the ST, remove the parking gear and transfer drive gear.

    Preparation tool: 

    ST: REMOVER (899864100)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
