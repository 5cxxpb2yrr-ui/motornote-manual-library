Engine Control Module (ECM): Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Before starting work, refer to "WARNING (HYBRID SYSTEM)" in "General Description". Refer to PRECAUTION/WARNING>WARNING (HYBRID SYSTEM) .
  2. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  3. Disconnect the connector in numerical order as shown in the figure.
    CAUTION: Make sure that the lock is released before moving the lever in order to avoid damaging the connector.
    NOTE: To disconnect the connector, press the portion (a) shown in the figure to release the lock and move the lever in the direction of arrow.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the air conditioner wire and power cable assembly from the ECM bracket.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the ECM from vehicle.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Remove the ECM bracket.
