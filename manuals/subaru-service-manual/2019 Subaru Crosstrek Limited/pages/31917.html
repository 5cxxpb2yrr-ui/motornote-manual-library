MT Gear Select Cable: Removal

CAUTION: Always use new MT gear select cable if the cable is removed from selector lever COMPL of transmission side.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the clip from the air intake boot, and loosen the clamp.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the air intake boot, and place it aside so that it does not interfere with the work.
    CAUTION: Be careful not to pull out the PCV hose.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the coupling harness.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the clips and remove the air breather hose.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Disconnect the transmission radio ground cord terminal.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Remove the pitching stopper. Refer to MANUAL TRANSMISSION AND DIFFERENTIAL (6MT)>TRANSMISSION MOUNTING SYSTEM>REMOVAL>PITCHING STOPPER .
  8. Lift up the vehicle.
  9. Remove the front exhaust pipe. Refer to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FRONT EXHAUST PIPE>REMOVAL .
  10. Remove the center exhaust cover.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  11. Remove the propeller shaft. Refer to DRIVE SHAFT SYSTEM>PROPELLER SHAFT>REMOVAL .
  12. Remove the cable clamp.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  13. Place the transmission jack under the transmission.
  14. Remove the mounting bolt of front crossmember and rear crossmember, and lower the transmission approx. 100 mm (4 in).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  15. Insert a spanner between the selector lever COMPL and the MT gear select cable, and remove the MT gear select cable by raising the handle.
    CAUTION: Do not apply excessive load while holding the part (a).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    NOTE:
    • Use a spanner with 11 to 13 in size.
    • The thickness of the spanner must not exceed 6 mm (0.2 in).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  16. Remove the MT gear select cable and select bracket as a single unit.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  17. Remove the cable cover plate.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  18. Disconnect the MT gear select cable.
    CAUTION:
    • Be careful not to deform the cable inner.
    • Do not apply excessive load while holding the part (a).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    NOTE: Push the MT gear select cable in the direction of arrow, and remove it.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  19. Remove the clamp.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  20. Pull out the MT gear select cable.
CAUTION: Do not bend the MT gear select cable sharply.
Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
