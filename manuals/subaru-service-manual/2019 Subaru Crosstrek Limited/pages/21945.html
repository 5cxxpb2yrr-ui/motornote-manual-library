Preparation For Memory Clear

  • If the procedures of following Steps 1 - 8 are completed within the specified time (about 10 seconds), it becomes possible to perform memory clear of the target DTC.
  • If the procedures of Steps 1 - 8 are recognized by EBB Control Module and memory clear for the target DTCs becomes possible, a buzzer will sound.
  1. Turn the ignition switch to ON. (P range)
  2. Depress the brake pedal and operate the shift lever. (N range → P range)
  3. Release the brake pedal.
  4. "Depress → Release" the brake pedal three times.
  5. Depress the brake pedal and shift the select lever to N range.
  6. Release the brake pedal and perform the Step  4 procedure again. (N range)
  7. Shift the shift lever to P range.
  8. Perform Step  4 procedure again. (P range)
