Rear Combination Light Assembly: Inspection

  1. Apply battery voltage between the connector terminals to check the lighting condition of the light.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • 4 door model
      Terminal No. Inspection conditions Specification
      3 (+) - 2 (-) Apply battery voltage. Stop light on
      1 (+) - 2 (-) Tail/side marker light on
      4 (+) - 2 (-) Turn signal light on
    • 5 door model
    Terminal No. Inspection conditions Specification
    2 (+) - 4 (-) Apply battery voltage. Stop light on
    1 (+) - 4 (-) Tail/side marker light on
    3 (+) - 4 (-) Turn signal light on
  2. Replace the harness and bulb if it is found defective.
    NOTE: Since LED (Light Emitting Diode) is used for the stop light, replace the light assembly - rear combination if the light does not illuminate.
