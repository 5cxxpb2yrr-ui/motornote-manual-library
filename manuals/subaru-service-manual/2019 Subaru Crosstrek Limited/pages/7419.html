Drain Filter: Removal

  1. Remove the rear wheel RH. Refer to WHEEL AND TIRE SYSTEM>TIRE AND WHEEL>REMOVAL .
  2. Lift up the vehicle.
  3. Remove the mud guard - rear RH. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>MUD GUARD>REMOVAL .
  4. Disconnect the drain tube (A). Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>Fuel Delivery and Evaporation Lines>REMOVAL>QUICK CONNECTOR DISCONNECTION (TYPE E) .
  5. Remove the bolts which hold the drain filter bracket, and remove the drain filter.
    NOTE: Using a marker pen, make alignment marks (a) on the fuel filler pipe and the drain filter bracket.
