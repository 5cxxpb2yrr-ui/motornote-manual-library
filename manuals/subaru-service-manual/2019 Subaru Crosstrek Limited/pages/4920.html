Diagnostic Method

The pump motor is operating continuously for 178 seconds or more. (When the relay is malfunctioning for 98 seconds or more.)

JUDGMENT VALUE SPECIFICATION

Malfunction Criteria Threshold Value
Case 1 
Brake Booster Motor Voltage		 More than 6 V
Case 2 
Brake Booster Motor Voltage		 More than 6 V

Time needed for diagnosis: 

  • Case 1:  178 s
  • Case 2:  98 s

Malfunction indicator light illumination:  Illuminates as soon as a malfunction occurs.
