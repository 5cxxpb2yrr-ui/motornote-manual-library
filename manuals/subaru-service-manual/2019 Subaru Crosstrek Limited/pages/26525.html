Explanation For Circuits

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly receives or sends the signal from ECM, yaw rate sensor, steering sensor, VDC control module and hydraulic control unit (VDCCM & H/U), Body Integrated unit, AIRBAG CONTROL MODULE, A/C control module and Keyless access CM via CAN communication.
