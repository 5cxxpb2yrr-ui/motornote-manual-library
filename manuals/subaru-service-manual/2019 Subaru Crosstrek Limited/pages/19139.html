Return Pipe

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Using the refrigerant recovery system, discharge refrigerant. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>REFRIGERANT RECOVERY PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .
  2. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  3. Remove the collector cover. Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>COLLECTOR COVER>REMOVAL .
  4. Move the air intake boot. Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>AIR INTAKE BOOT>REMOVAL .
    NOTE: With the PCV hose connected, remove the air intake boot from the air cleaner case (rear) and throttle body.
  5. Remove the hose - pressure discharge. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>HOSE AND PIPE>REMOVAL>HIGH-PRESSURE HOSE .
  6. Remove the pipe - return assembly.
    1. Disconnect the connector and connector clip.
    2. Remove the nut and bolt, and then disconnect the pipe - return assembly.
      CAUTION:
      • Do not apply excessive force to the pipe.
      • Seal the engaging parts of the disconnected hose and air conditioning unit with plugs or vinyl tape to prevent foreign matter from entering.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Remove the clamp - pipe and bolt, and then remove the pipe - return assembly.
    CAUTION:
    • Do not apply excessive force to the pipe.
    • Seal the engaging parts of the disconnected pipe and the hose with plugs or vinyl tape to prevent foreign matter from entering.
