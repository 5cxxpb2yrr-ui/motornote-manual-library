DTC P1C82-49: High Voltage Power Resource Circuit Over Loading: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Outline of diagnosis: 

Malfunction of high voltage circuit between battery and inverter

Load short circuit (small) occurred during precharging (between SMRP ON to SMRG ON)

Battery current large during precharging (Current flew even after precharging)

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

Hybrid system stopped

NOTE: For the diagnostic procedure, refer to DTC P1C8149. Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE WITH DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>DTC P1C8149 HIGH VOLTAGE POWER RESOURCE CIRCUIT CONSUMPTION CIRCUIT SHORT .
