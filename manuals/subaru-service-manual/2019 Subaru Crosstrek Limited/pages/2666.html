Vanity Mirror Light: Removal

NOTE:
  • The vanity mirror light is integrated into the sun visor assembly. Replace the sun visor assembly if faulty. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>SUN VISOR>REMOVAL .
  • Do not remove the sun visor assembly if removing the bulb only.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the sun visor assembly. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>SUN VISOR>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the vanity mirror light bulb.
    CAUTION:
    • Use a dry clean cloth so that no grease or water adheres to the glass portion of the bulb.
    • When using a flat tip screwdriver, use an appropriate sized tool for the insertion opening, apply protective tape or cloth around the tool, and be careful not to cause damage.
    1. Release the claws and remove the lens.
    2. Remove the vanity mirror light bulb.
