Outline Of Diagnosis

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Detect the malfunction of the engine coolant temperature sensor characteristics. After the engine starts after the specified period of soaking time has elapsed, diagnose by correlation between engine coolant temperature sensor value, engine oil temperature sensor value and ambient temperature sensor value. Judge as NG when the differences are both above the specified value by comparing between engine coolant temperature and ambient temperature, engine coolant temperature and engine oil temperature.
