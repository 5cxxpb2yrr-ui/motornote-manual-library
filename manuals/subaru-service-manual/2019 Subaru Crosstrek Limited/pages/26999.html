This indicates that Inside of inverter temperature became abnormal.

The following cases are assumed to be malfunction causes:

  • Malfunction of Inside of inverter
    • Inverter inner circuit malfunction
    • Malfunction of unit that controls inverter
    • Malfunction of inverter control sensor (coolant temperature sensor)
  • Hybrid cooling system failure
    • Leakage, insufficiency or freeze of coolant. Clogging in coolant passage.
    • Grille clogging
