Air Conditioner Does Not Stop Even When The Off Switch Is Pressed

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Trouble symptom: 

Even when the OFF switch is pressed, the blower fan does not turn off, the inlet opening remains in FRESH mode, and the compressor does not turn off.

Trouble causes: 

  • CAN communication failure
  • A/C control panel failure
  • Blower motor failure
  • Intake door actuator failure
  • Compressor failure
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK A/C CONTROL PANEL. 
    1. Turn to READY ON.
    2. Press the OFF switch of the A/C control panel.
    3. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, check the following items in the [Data monitor] of [Air Conditioner]. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DATA MONITOR .
      • Blower Fan Level
      • Fresh/Recirculate Air Door Actuator Position Target

    Does [Blower Fan Level] indicate 0, and [Fresh/Recirculate Air Door Actuator Position Target] indicate 100%?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Replace the A/C control panel. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>CONTROL PANEL .

  2. CHECK BLOWER MOTOR. 

    With the OFF switch pressed, check the blower motor operation.

    Is the blower motor stopped?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>AIR GOES OUT OF CONTROL .

  3. CHECK INTAKE DOOR ACTUATOR. 

    With the OFF switch pressed, check the FRESH/RECIRC door operation.

    Does the FRESH/RECIRC door set to FRESH?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>UNABLE TO SWITCH SUCTION VENTS .

  4. CHECK COMPRESSOR. 

    With the OFF switch pressed, check the compressor operation.

    Is the compressor stopped?

    Yes:  The circuit has returned to a normal condition at this time. Reproduce the failure, and then perform the diagnosis again.

    NOTE: In this case, temporary poor contact of connector, temporary open or short circuit of harness may be the cause.

    No:  Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>COLD AIR DOES NOT COME OUT EVEN WHEN THE A/C SWITCH IS PRESSED. THE GLASS CANNOT BE DEFOGGED. (COMPRESSOR DOES NOT OPERATE (ELECTRIC)) .
