Washer Tank

CAUTION: Before installing the bumper face, match the claws on the bracket - front bumper with the engaging position of flange section on the bumper face side. If the engaging position is not correct, the flange section may be broken or the clearance between fender panel and bumper face may not be uniform.
  1. Install the tank - washer assembly.

    Tightening torque: 

    6 N.m (0.6 kgf-m, 4.4 ft-lb)

  2. Connect the hose - washer.
  3. Connect each connector.
  4. Add new washer fluid.
  5. Install the bumper face - front. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>FRONT BUMPER>INSTALLATION .
  6. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  7. Adjust the fog light beam. (Model with fog light) Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>FRONT FOG LIGHT ASSEMBLY>ADJUSTMENT>FOG LIGHT AIMING .
