Diagnostic Method

Judge as NG when the following conditions are established.

JUDGMENT VALUE SPECIFICATION

Malfunction Criteria Threshold Value
Data received from control modules connected to LIN No change
or
Checksum error
or
Internal malfunction flag of generator (communication failure warning)		 ON
