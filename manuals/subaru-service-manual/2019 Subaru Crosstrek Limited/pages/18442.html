Section 7 (Starting System & Charging System (HEV)): Battery: Installation

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
CAUTION:
  • If the battery terminal is worn and the terminal section is loose, replace it with a new battery.
  • Since the initial diagnosis of the electronic throttle control is conducted after the battery terminal is connected, wait for 10 seconds or more after turning the ignition switch to ON, and then start the engine.
  1. Install the battery.
  2. Install the battery holder.

    Tightening torque: 

    3.5 N.m (0.4 kgf-m, 2.6 ft-lb)

  3. Install the battery cable holder.
  4. Connect the battery positive terminal.

    Tightening torque: 

    6 N.m (0.6 kgf-m, 4.4 ft-lb)

  5. Connect the battery ground terminal.

    Refer. to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
