Turbine Speed Sensor: Removal

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

CAUTION: Be sure to prevent water or oil from contacting the connector terminal of turbine speed sensor. If adhesion occurs, replace with a new part.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Lift up the vehicle.
  3. Remove the harness connector from turbine speed sensor.
  4. Remove the turbine speed sensor.
