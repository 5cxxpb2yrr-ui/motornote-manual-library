Explanation For Circuits

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Resistance of the thermistor incorporated in ATF temperature sensor fluctuates according to the change in transaxle oil temperature. The lower the transaxle oil temperature is, the larger the thermistor resistance becomes. On the contrary, the higher the temperature is, the smaller the resistance becomes.
