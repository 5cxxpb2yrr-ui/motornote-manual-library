Inverter Front Water Pipe

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery.

    Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .

  2. Drain engine coolant. (Inverter assembly side)

    Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>ENGINE COOLANT>REPLACEMENT>DRAINING OF ENGINE COOLANT>INVERTER ASSEMBLY SIDE .

  3. Remove the screw (A) and clips, and turn over the mud guard - front RH.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the clamp.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Disconnect the quick connector from the inverter water pipe.
    NOTE:
    • Disconnect the quick connector as shown in the figure.
    • Be careful not to pull the slider too much.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Disconnect the inverter radiator inlet hose from the radiator.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Remove the crossmember support - rear RH.

    Refer to FRONT SUSPENSION>CROSSMEMBER SUPPORT>REMOVAL .

  8. Remove the inverter center water pipe outlet hose (B) and inverter center water pipe inlet hose (C) from the clamp (A).
  9. Disconnect the inverter center water pipe outlet hose (B) and inverter center water pipe inlet hose (C) from the inverter center water pipe.
    NOTE: Make markings, etc. to prevent mis-connection at the time of connecting the inverter center water pipe inlet hose and inverter center water pipe outlet hose.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  10. Remove the clip from the air intake boot, and loosen the clamp.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  11. Remove the air intake boot, and place it aside so that it does not interfere with the work.
    CAUTION: Be careful not to pull out the PCV hose.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  12. Remove the air cleaner case (rear).

    Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>AIR CLEANER CASE>REMOVAL .

  13. Remove the nut securing the inverter front water pipe to the bracket.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  14. Disconnect the connector (A) and connector (B), and remove the clip (C).
  15. Remove the bolt securing the inverter front water pipe, and remove the inverter front water pipe together with the inverter radiator inlet hose, inverter water pump outlet hose, inverter center water pipe inlet hose and inverter center water pipe outlet hose.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  16. Disconnect the inverter radiator inlet hose (A), inverter water pump outlet hose (B), inverter center water pipe outlet hose (C) and inverter center water pipe inlet hose (D) from the inverter front water pipe.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  17. When the bracket is replaced
    1. Remove the air cleaner element and the air cleaner case (front).

      Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>AIR CLEANER CASE>REMOVAL .

    2. Remove the clip (A) and remove the bracket.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  18. Disconnect the inverter water pump outlet hose from the quick connector.
    NOTE: Remove if necessary.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  19. Remove the clamp (A) and remove the bracket.
    NOTE: Remove if necessary.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
