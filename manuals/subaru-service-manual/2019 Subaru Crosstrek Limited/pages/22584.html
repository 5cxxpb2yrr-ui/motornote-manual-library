Rear Differential Gear Oil

CAUTION:
  • Each oil manufacturer has its base oil and additives. Thus, do not mix two or more brands.
  • Note that you may experience poor functionality and performance if gear oil equivalent to the VA1 type is used.

Recommended gear oil: 

VA1-type: 

SUBARU GEAR OIL EXTRA MT or equivalent

T-type: 

GL-5 (75W-90) or equivalent

API classification GL-5
SAE viscosity No. and applicable temperature
