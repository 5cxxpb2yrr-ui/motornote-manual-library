Select Lever: Removal

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Shift the select lever to "N" range.
  2. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  3. Lower the boot assembly vertically toward the lever. (Model with boot shifter)
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Lower the cover grip AT vertically toward the lever. (Model with gate shifter)
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the clamp grip pin.
    • Model with boot shifter
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • Model with gate shifter
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Remove the grip assembly.
    • Model with boot shifter
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • Model with gate shifter
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Remove the front console panel. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>CONSOLE BOX>REMOVAL .
  8. Remove the panel center LWR LH and RH. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>CONSOLE BOX>REMOVAL .
  9. Remove the harness clip from the select lever assembly.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  10. Separate the floor carpet to three layers, and cut them while staggering their cut position as shown in the figure.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  11. Disconnect the harness connector.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  12. Remove four bolts.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  13. Lift up the vehicle.
  14. Remove the center exhaust cover.
    • Except for Crosstrek hybrid model
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • Crosstrek hybrid model
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • Remove the inhibitor cover. (Crosstrek hybrid model)
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • Disconnect the select cable from the transmission main body.
      CAUTION: Do not apply excessive load while holding the part (a).
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • Except for Crosstrek hybrid model
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • Crosstrek hybrid model
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  15. Lower the vehicle, and remove the select lever assembly from the room side.
NOTE: Pass the select cable from the underside of the vehicle to the room side.
