IMPREZA Model

  1. Apply seal material to the bolt threads of oil pressure switch, and install the oil pressure switch.
    NOTE: Before applying the seal material, degrease the oil pressure switch and the thread portions of the chain cover.

    Seal material: 

    THREE BOND 1324 (Part No. 004403042) or equivalent

    Tightening torque: 

    18 N.m (1.8 kgf-m, 13.3 ft-lb)

  2. Connect the connector to the oil pressure switch.
  3. Check the engine oil level and replenish the engine oil if necessary. Refer to LUBRICATION (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE OIL>INSPECTION .
  4. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
