DTC P1AFD-00: Flying Capacitor Circuit Voltage Out Of Range: Notes

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

1. - 

Outline of diagnosis: 

Battery ECU assembly internal malfunction

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

  • Reduced output power
  • Engine not stopped
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK DTC. 

    Read the DTC of [P-HEV High Voltage Battery] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is a DTC other than DTC P1AFD00 displayed?

    Yes:  Perform the diagnosis for the simultaneously displayed DTCs. Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    No:  Replace Battery ECU assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>BATTERY CONTROL MODULE .
