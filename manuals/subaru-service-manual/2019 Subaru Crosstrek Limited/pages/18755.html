Manual Transmission Assembly: Removal

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery, refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the clip from the air intake boot, and loosen the clamp.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the air intake boot, and place it aside so that it does not interfere with the work.
    CAUTION: Be careful not to pull out the PCV hose.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the coupling harness.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the clips and remove the air breather hose.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Disconnect the transmission radio ground cord terminal.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Remove the engine hanger rear.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Remove the operating cylinder and suspend on a wire.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Remove the pitching stopper.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  10. Disconnect the engine ground terminal.
  11. Remove the engine harness stay and MT docking connector together as a unit.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  12. Set the ST.

    Special tool: 

    ST1: 41099AA012 ENGINE SUPPORT BRACKET

    ST2: 41099AA020 ENGINE SUPPORT

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  13. Remove the bolts which hold upper side of transmission to engine.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  14. Remove the starter, refer to STARTING/CHARGING SYSTEMS (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>STARTER>REMOVAL .
  15. Remove the front tires. refer to WHEEL AND TIRE SYSTEM>TIRE AND WHEEL>REMOVAL .
  16. Lift up the vehicle.
  17. Remove the crossmember support - rear.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  18. Remove the front exhaust pipe. refer to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FRONT EXHAUST PIPE>REMOVAL .
  19. Remove the rear exhaust pipe. refer to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>REAR EXHAUST PIPE>REMOVAL .
  20. Drain transmission gear oil completely. refer to MANUAL TRANSMISSION AND DIFFERENTIAL (6MT)>TRANSMISSION GEAR OIL>REPLACEMENT .
  21. Remove the center exhaust cover.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  22. Remove the hanger bracket.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  23. Remove the propeller shaft. refer to DRIVE SHAFT SYSTEM>PROPELLER SHAFT>REMOVAL .
  24. Remove the MT gear shift cable and MT gear select cable from MT gear shift lever. refer to CONTROL SYSTEMS>MT GEAR SHIFT LEVER>REMOVAL .
    CAUTION:
    • Do not bend MT gear shift cable and MT gear select cable at a sharp angle.
    • If the MT gear select cable is removed from the selector lever COMPL on the transmission side, replace with new MT gear select cable.
    • Do not apply excessive load while holding the part (A).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  25. Disconnect the stabilizer link.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  26. Remove the bolt securing the ball joint of the front arm to the housing, then separate the front arms and the housing.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  27. Using a crowbar, separate the front drive shaft.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  28. Remove the bolts and nuts which hold lower side of transmission to engine.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  29. Set the transmission jack.
    CAUTION: Always support the transmission case with a transmission jack.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  30. Remove the front crossmember and the rear crossmember.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  31. While lowering the transmission jack, tighten the turnbuckle of the ENGINE SUPPORT (41099AA020), and incline the engine unit rearward.
  32. Remove the transmission assembly.
    CAUTION: Move the transmission jack towards the rear until the main shaft is withdrawn from the clutch cover.
  33. Remove the transmission cushion rubber.
  34. Remove the release bearing & lever. refer to CLUTCH SYSTEM>RELEASE BEARING AND LEVER>REMOVAL .
