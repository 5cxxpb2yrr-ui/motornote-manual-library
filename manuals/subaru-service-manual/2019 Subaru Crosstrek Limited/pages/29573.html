DTC B281B: Steering Angle Sensor Fail

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

DTC detecting condition: 

Defective steering angle sensor

Trouble symptom: 

All functions of EyeSight system do not operate.

NOTE: When this DTC is detected, check VDC system. Refer to BRAKE CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE .
