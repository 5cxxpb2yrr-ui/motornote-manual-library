Explanation For Circuits

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Motor generator control ECU built in Inverter with converter assembly detects the motor inverter temperature with the temperature sensor which is built in Inverter with converter assembly. Inverter with converter assembly is cooled by the independent cooling system, which is isolated from the engine cooling system. Motor generator control ECU checks inverter cooling system effect based on the signal sent from the motor inverter temperature sensor. Motor generator control ECU limits inverter output to prevent overheat as necessary. Moreover, detects a connection error of the motor inverter temperature sensor and a sensor malfunction.
