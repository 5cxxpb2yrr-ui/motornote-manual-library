DTC B1695: Door Sensor LH Failure

Diagnosis start condition: 

When the ignition voltage is 10 V - 16 V.

DTC detecting condition: 

Front door impact sensor LH is faulty.

If DTC B1695 is displayed, the circuit within the front door impact sensor LH is faulty.

Replace the front door impact sensor LH. Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>FRONT DOOR IMPACT SENSOR>REMOVAL .
