DTC P1C10-14: Current Sensor After Boosting By PFC Boosting Circuit Circuit Short To Ground Or Open: Notes

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Outline of diagnosis: 

IVH sensor output voltage was smaller than the threshold value continually for the prescribed length of time.

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

Plug-in charging suspended

NOTE: This DTC is output when the Electric vehicle charger assembly detects a IVH sensor error.
Therefore if this DTC was detected, replace the Electric vehicle charger assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>ELECTRIC VEHICLE CHARGER .
