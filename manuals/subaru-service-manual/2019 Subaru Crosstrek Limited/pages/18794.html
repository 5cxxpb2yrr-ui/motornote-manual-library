Center Differential: Inspection

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Ball bearing

    Replace the bearings in the following cases.

    • In case of broken or rusty bearings
    • In case of worn or damaged bearings
    • When the bearings fail to turn smoothly or emit noise in rotation after transmission gear oil has been applied.
    • When bearing has other defects.
  2. Center differential

    If there is wear or damage, replace the center differential assembly.
