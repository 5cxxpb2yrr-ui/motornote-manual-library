Engine Control Module (ECM): Installation

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
CAUTION: When the ECM of model with immobilizer has been replaced, be sure to perform the registration of immobilizer system. (Refer to "Type F" described in "REGISTRATION INSTRUCTIONS FOR IMMOBILIZER".)
NOTE: When replacing the ECM, be careful not to use the ECM of wrong specification to avoid any damage on the fuel injection system.
  1. Install the ECM bracket.

    Tightening torque: 

    7.5 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.5 ft-lb)

  2. Temporarily install the ECM to the vehicle, tighten the fasteners in numerical order as shown in the figure.

    Tightening torque: 

    7.5 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.5 ft-lb)

    Fig 1: ECM Fasteners Bolts Tightening Sequence
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Install the air conditioner wire and power cable assembly to the ECM bracket.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Connect the connector in numerical order as shown in the figure.
    CAUTION: To prevent damage to the connector, when connecting the connector, insert the connector straight in until it stops, and while maintaining the position, lock the lever.
    NOTE: Check that the lever is located at the position shown in the figure (position beyond the lock portion (a)).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
