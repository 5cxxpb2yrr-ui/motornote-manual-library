Cylinder Head RH

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Remove the engine from the vehicle.

    Except for hybrid model: 

    Refer to MECHANICAL (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL>EXCEPT FOR HYBRID MODEL .

    Hybrid model: 

    Refer to MECHANICAL (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL>HYBRID MODEL .

  2. Remove the engine wiring harness.

    Except for hybrid model: 

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE WIRING HARNESS>REMOVAL .

    Hybrid model: 

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>ENGINE WIRING HARNESS>REMOVAL .

  3. Remove the cam carrier RH. Refer to MECHANICAL (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>CAM CARRIER>REMOVAL>CAM CARRIER RH .
  4. Remove the high-pressure fuel delivery pipe assembly.

    Except for hybrid model: 

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>HIGH PRESSURE FUEL DELIVERY PIPE>REMOVAL>HIGH-PRESSURE FUEL DELIVERY PIPE ASSEMBLY .

    Hybrid model: 

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>HIGH PRESSURE FUEL DELIVERY PIPE>REMOVAL>HIGH-PRESSURE FUEL DELIVERY PIPE ASSEMBLY .

  5. Remove the EGR cooler. (Except for hybrid model) Refer to EMISSION CONTROL (AUX. EMISSION CONTROL DEVICES) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>EGR COOLER>REMOVAL .
  6. When disassembling the cylinder head RH
    NOTE: When disassembling the cylinder head RH, perform the following steps also.
    1. Remove the spark plug #1 and spark plug #3. Refer to IGNITION (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>SPARK PLUG>REMOVAL>RH SIDE .
    2. Remove the fuel injector RH.

      Except for hybrid model: 

      Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>Fuel Injector>REMOVAL>FUEL INJECTOR RH .

      Hybrid model: 

      Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL INJECTOR>REMOVAL>FUEL INJECTOR RH .

  7. Set the part so that the cylinder head RH is on the upper side.
  8. Loosen the bolts securing the cylinder head RH equally, a little at a time in numerical sequence as shown in the figure and remove the cylinder head bolt.
    Fig 1: Cylinder Head RH Bolts Loosening Sequence
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. While tapping the cylinder head RH with a plastic hammer, separate to remove it from the cylinder block RH.
  10. Remove the cylinder head gasket RH.
    CAUTION: Be careful not to scratch the mating surface of cylinder head and cylinder block.
  11. Remove the water jacket spacer from the cylinder block RH.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  12. Remove the liquid gasket from the cylinder head RH and cylinder block RH.
