Front Hood: Disassembly

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

CAUTION: The hood COMPL - front is heavy. When removing or installing the hood COMPL - front, stay assembly - front hood, and hinge COMPL - front hood, be sure to work in a team of two or more persons.
  1. Remove the clips, and remove the insulator - front hood.
    CAUTION:
    • Do not reuse any clips that have been removed. Those clips cannot fix the insulator - front hood securely. Therefore, always replace with a new part. (Impreza model)
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • Do not reuse any clips damaged during removal. Those clips cannot fix the insulator - front hood securely. Therefore, always replace with a new part. (Except for Impreza model)
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Make alignment marks (A), and remove the striker - front hood from the hood COMPL - front.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Release the clips and claws, and then remove the cowl panel - on the left and right sides.
    CAUTION: Pulling with excessive force may damage the cowl panel - side. If it is difficult to remove, use a plastic remover or equivalent tool.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the clip, and remove the cover - front fender.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the hinge COMPL - front hood from the body.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
