Clutch Disc And Cover: Installation

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Install the release bearing & lever. Refer to CLUTCH SYSTEM>RELEASE BEARING AND LEVER>INSTALLATION .
  2. Insert the CLUTCH DISC GUIDE (499747100) into the clutch disc.
  3. Install it by inserting the end of the ST to the pilot bearing.
    CAUTION: Pay attention to the orientation of the clutch disc.

    Special tool: 

    ST: 499747100 CLUTCH DISC GUIDE

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Install the clutch cover and tighten the bolts to specified torque.
    NOTE:
    • When installing a clutch cover, position the clutch cover so that the spacing between the unbalance marks (paint mark) on the flywheel and clutch cover is 120° or more apart. (The unbalance mark indicates the direction of residual unbalance.)
    • Temporarily tighten each bolt by hand, and tighten them to the specified torque in a crisscross order.

    Tightening torque: 

    16 N.m (1.6 kgf-m, 11.8 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the CLUTCH DISC GUIDE (499747100).
  6. Install the manual transmission assembly.
    • 5MT model:

      Refer to MANUAL TRANSMISSION AND DIFFERENTIAL (5MT)>MANUAL TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY>INSTALLATION .

    • 6MT model:

      Refer to MANUAL TRANSMISSION AND DIFFERENTIAL (6MT)>MANUAL TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY>INSTALLATION .
