Front Door Partition Glass

  1. Remove the front door glass. Refer to GLASS/WINDOWS/MIRRORS>FRONT DOOR GLASS>REMOVAL>FRONT DOOR GLASS .
  2. Remove the glass - front door partition.
    CAUTION: Do not damage the sash or sash tape OUT while removing.
    1. Partially turn over the running channel - front door.
    2. Turn over the weather strip - door, and remove the screws.
    3. Remove the bolts, and remove the sash assembly - partition while lowering it.
