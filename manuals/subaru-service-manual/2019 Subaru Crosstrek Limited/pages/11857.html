Recommended Materials: Refrigerant

CAUTION: If any compressor oil other than specified is used, an electric leak may occur because the hybrid model uses high voltage insulating oil. Therefore, do not use any compressor oil other than specified.

Standard air conditioners installed on this vehicle uses HFC134a refrigerant. Do not mix it with other refrigerants. Also, do not use any compressor oil other than those listed in the following table.

Item Recommended materials Item number Alternative
Refrigerant HFC134a - -
Compressor oil (Except for hybrid model) ND-OIL8 - -
Compressor oil (Hybrid model) ND-OIL11 - -
