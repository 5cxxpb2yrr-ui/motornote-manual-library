General Tool

TOOL NAME REMARKS
Alignment tester Used for measuring wheel alignment.
Toe-in gauge Used for toe-in measurement.
Jack Used for removing and installing suspension.
Bearing puller Used for removing bushings.
Tie-rod ball joint puller Used for disconnecting the rear lateral link (front).
Coil spring compressor Used for disassembling and assembling strut assembly and shock absorber assembly.
