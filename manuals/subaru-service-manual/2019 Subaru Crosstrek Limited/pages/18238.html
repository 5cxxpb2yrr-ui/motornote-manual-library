Torsion Bar

CAUTION: Trunk lid COMPL is heavy. When removing and installing it, always work in a team of two or more persons.
  1. Remove the trunk room trim. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>TRUNK ROOM TRIM>REMOVAL .
  2. Remove the torsion bar - trunk lid.
    CAUTION: During removal and installation, handle the torsion bar - trunk lid carefully, because it will generate reactive force.
    NOTE: Make markings on the torsion bar - trunk lid LH and RH.

    Preparation tool: 

    ST: REMOVER (99099FJ000) and REMOVER (927780000)

    1. Remove the torsion bar - trunk lid (a) on the LH side from the hinge link.
    2. Remove the torsion bar - trunk lid (b) on the RH side from the hinge link.
