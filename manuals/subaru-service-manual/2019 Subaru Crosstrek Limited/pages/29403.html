Engine Coolant (Inverter Assembly Side)

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
CAUTION:
  • The engine coolant is pressurized when it is hot. Wait until engine coolant cools down before proceeding the work.
  • If the engine coolant splashes, completely remove and clean it.
NOTE: The SUBARU Super Coolant contains anti-freeze and anti-rust agents, and is especially made for Subaru engines with an aluminum cylinder block. Be sure to use SUBARU Super Coolant, since other coolant may cause corrosion.

Recommended engine coolant: 

Refer to "SPECIFICATION" for recommended engine coolant.

Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>SPECIFICATION .

Engine coolant concentration: 

Refer to "ADJUSTMENT" for the recommended engine coolant concentration.

Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>ENGINE COOLANT>ADJUSTMENT .

  1. Park the vehicle on a level surface.
  2. With the engine in a cold condition, check that the engine coolant level of the inverter tank lies between "FULL" and "LOW", and add the engine coolant if the coolant level is low.
    NOTE: If it is necessary to add the coolant, make sure that there are no engine coolant leaks.
