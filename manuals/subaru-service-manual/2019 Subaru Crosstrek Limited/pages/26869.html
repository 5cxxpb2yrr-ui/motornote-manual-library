Monitor Description

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

The Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly monitors its internal operation. If the internal operation is malfunctioning, the Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly illuminates the MIL and stores a DTC.
