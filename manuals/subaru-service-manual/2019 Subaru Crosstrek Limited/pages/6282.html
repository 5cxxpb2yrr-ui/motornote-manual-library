Bushing - Rear Axle Housing

  1. Remove the rear axle housing. Ref. to DRIVE SHAFT SYSTEM>REAR AXLE>REMOVAL .
  2. Pull out the bushing using ST and the hydraulic press.

Preparation tool: 

ST1: BUSHING REMOVER (20099FG000)

ST2: INSTALLER & REMOVER (BASE) (20099 Pa010)
