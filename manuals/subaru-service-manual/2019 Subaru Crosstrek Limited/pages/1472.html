Diagnostic Procedure with ECU Cancel Code: 15

Detected when CANCEL switch is pressed or malfunction related to CRUISE switch occurs.

Trouble symptom: 

  • Cruise control cannot be set. (Cancelled immediately.)
  • Cruise control cannot be released.

Refer to 11 for diagnostic procedure. Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE WITH ECU CANCEL CODE>11 .
