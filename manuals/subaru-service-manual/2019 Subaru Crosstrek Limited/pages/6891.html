DTC B2226: MFD Internal Circuit

DTC detecting condition: 

  • If the system is not restored within 30 to 60 seconds after restarting the control microcomputer due to timeout of startup sequence.
  • When CAN controller register freeze check detects malfunction immediately after startup.

Trouble symptom: 

MFD does not start.

NOTE: For the diagnostic procedure, refer to DTC B2225. Refer to MULTI-FUNCTION DISPLAY (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE WITH DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>DTC B2225 MFD INTERNAL COMMUNICATION .
