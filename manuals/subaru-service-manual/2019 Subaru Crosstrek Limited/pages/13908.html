DTC P1F05: System Voltage: Notes

1. - 

DTC detecting condition: 

Immediately at fault recognition

Trouble symptom: 

Driveability getting worse.

Wiring diagram: 

AWD control system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>AWD CONTROL SYSTEM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  1. CHECK DATA MONITOR  .

    Read the data of [Back-up Power Supply Voltage] using Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to AWD CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DATA MONITOR .

    Is the [Back-up Power Supply Voltage] less than 5 V?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Current condition is normal. Reproduce the failure, and then perform the diagnosis again.

  2. CHECK AWD control module INPUT SIGNAL  .
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the AWD control module connector.
    3. Measure the voltage between AWD control module connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (B648) No. 10 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    Is the voltage less than 5 V?

    Yes:  Repair the open circuit of harness.

    No:  Check for poor contact of AWD control module connector. Replace the AWD control module if no fault is found. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TH2A)>AWD CONTROL MODULE .
