IMPREZA Model

  1. Replace the ring seal of the master cylinder assembly with a new part.
  2. Install the master cylinder assembly.

    Tightening torque: 

    25 N.m (2.5 kgf-m, 18.4 ft-lb)

  3. Turn and tighten the flare nut (b) by hand while pressing the brake pipe (a) toward the master cylinder assembly side.
    CAUTION: Be careful not to make scratches or other damage to the inside surface of the brake pipe flare.

    Tightening torque: 

    19 N.m (1.9 kgf-m, 14 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Bleed air from the master cylinder and clutch system (MT model). Refer to BRAKE>AIR BLEEDING>PROCEDURE>MASTER CYLINDER (EXCEPT FOR HYBRID MODEL) .
  5. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY>GROUND TERMINAL REMOVAL OR INSTALLATION .
  6. Install the front and rear wheels.

    Tightening torque: 

    120 N.m (12.2 kgf-m, 88.5 ft-lb)
