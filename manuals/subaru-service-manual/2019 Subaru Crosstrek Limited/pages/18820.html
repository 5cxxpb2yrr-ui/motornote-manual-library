Hypoid Driven Gear Backlash

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Set the ST1, ST2 and ST3. Insert the dial gauge probe through drain plug hole so that the probe comes in contact with the tooth surface on the right corner, and check the backlash.

    Special Tool: 

    ST1: 498247001 MAGNET BASE

    ST2: 498247100 DIAL GAUGE

    ST3: 498255400 PLATE

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Install SUBARU genuine axle shafts to both sides, rotate in the inversion direction so that the gauge contacts the tooth surface, and read the dial gauge.
    NOTE: If the backlash is outside the specified range, adjust it by turning the differential side retainer on the left and right side.

    Specification: 

    0.13 - 0.18 mm (0.0051 - 0.0071 in)

    Part No. 38415AA100 

    AXLE SHAFT
