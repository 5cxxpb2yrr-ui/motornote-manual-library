Rear Differential (VA-type): Disassembly

To detect the real cause of trouble, inspect the following items before disassembling.

  • Tooth contact and backlash between hypoid driven gear and drive pinion
    1. Set the ST on a vise, and install the differential assembly to the ST.

      Special tool: 

      ST: 398217700 ATTACHMENT SET

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Remove the drain plug and filler plug.
    3. Remove the bolts, and remove the rear cover while lightly tapping with a plastic hammer.
      NOTE: For Crosstrek hybrid model, remove the ground bracket tightened together.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    4. Remove the stud bolts from rear cover if necessary.
    5. Remove the air breather cap.
      CAUTION:
      • Do not attempt to remove the air breather cap unless necessary.
      • Whenever the air breather cap is removed, replace it with a new part.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    6. Remove the lock plate.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    7. Remove the side retainer using the ST.
      NOTE: When keeping the side retainers aside, use labels etc. to avoid confusing the left and right.

      Special tool: 

      ST: 18630AA010 WRENCH COMPL RETAINER

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    8. Remove the differential case assembly.
      CAUTION: Be careful not to hit the teeth of hypoid driven gear against the differential carrier.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    9. Remove the side bearing race using the ST1 and ST2.

      Special tool: 

      ST1: 499705401 PULLER ASSY

      ST2: 499705404 SEAT

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    10. Remove the side oil seal from the side retainer.
      NOTE: Perform this operation only when changing oil seal.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    11. Remove the O-rings.
    12. Remove the side bearing cone using the ST1 and ST2.
      NOTE:
      • Do not attempt to disassemble the parts unless necessary.
      • Set the puller so that its claws catch the edge of the bearing cone.
      • Store so that the right and left side bearing races and cones are not mixed together.

      Special tool: 

      ST1: 899524100 PULLER SET

      ST2: 399520105 SEAT

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    13. Using the ST, loosen the hypoid driven gear bolt and remove the hypoid driven gear.

      Special tool: 

      ST: 18270KA020 SOCKET (E20)

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    14. Remove the spring pin from hypoid driven gear side using the ST.

      Special tool: 

      ST: 899904100 STRAIGHT PIN REMOVER

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    15. Draw out the pinion mate shaft and remove pinion mate gears, side gears and side gear thrust washers.
      NOTE: The gears and washers should be marked with front or rear, RH or LH, or kept separately.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    16. Remove the self-locking nut while securing the companion flange with the ST.

      Special tool: 

      ST: 498427200 FLANGE WRENCH

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    17. Extract the companion flange with a puller.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    18. Press the end of drive pinion shaft using the ST and remove the rear bearing cone, pinion height adjusting washer, preload adjusting spacer and washer.
      CAUTION: Hold the drive pinion so as not to drop it.

      Special tool: 

      ST: 398467700 DRIFT

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    19. Support the rear bearing cone with the ST, and remove it from the drive pinion.
      NOTE: Place the remover so that its center-recessed side faces the bearing cone.

      Special tool: 

      ST: 498515500 REMOVER

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    20. Remove the front oil seal using the PULLER ASSY (398527700).
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    21. Remove the pilot bearing together with the front bearing cone and spacer using the ST.

      Special tool: 

      ST: 398467700 DRIFT

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    22. When replacing the bearings, tap out and remove the front bearing race and rear bearing race in this order using a brass bar.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
