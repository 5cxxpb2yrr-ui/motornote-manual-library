Blower Motor: Removal

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the pocket assembly and the back panel - pocket. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>GLOVE BOX>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the duct - foot passenger. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>HEATER DUCT>REMOVAL .
  4. Remove the blower motor assembly.
    1. Disconnect the connector.
    2. Remove the screws and detach the blower motor assembly.
