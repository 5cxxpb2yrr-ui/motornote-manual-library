NOTE: Before installation, remove mud and foreign matter from the disc brake assembly.
  1. Check the rear disc rotor. Refer to BRAKE>REAR DISC ROTOR>INSPECTION .
  2. Install the rear disc rotor.
    NOTE: When installing the rear disc rotor, match the alignment mark (a) of the rear disc rotor and the alignment mark (b) of the bolt - hub.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Adjust the parking brake. Refer to PARKING BRAKE>PARKING BRAKE ASSEMBLY (REAR DISC BRAKE)>ADJUSTMENT .
  4. Install the disc brake assembly.

    Tightening torque: 

    Mounting bolt: Refer to "COMPONENT" of "General Description" for the tightening torque. Refer to BRAKE>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>SPECIFICATION>REAR DISC BRAKE .

    Brake hose bracket: 33 N.m (3.4 kgf-m, 24.3 ft-lb)

  5. Install the rear wheels.

    Tightening torque: 

    120 N.m (12.2 kgf-m, 88.5 ft-lb)
