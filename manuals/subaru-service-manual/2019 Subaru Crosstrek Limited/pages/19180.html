Air Mix Door Actuator: Installation

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Rotate the gear - mix slowly in the arrow direction shown in the figure until it stops.
    CAUTION:
    • Do not rotate excessively.
    • Be sure to prevent dust or other foreign matters from contacting the greased gear portion.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Install the motor - actuator mix.
    NOTE: Install it so that the gear position is not displaced.
  3. Install the heat pump control module. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>CONTROL UNIT>INSTALLATION .
  4. Install the pocket assembly and the back panel - pocket. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>GLOVE BOX>INSTALLATION .
  5. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
