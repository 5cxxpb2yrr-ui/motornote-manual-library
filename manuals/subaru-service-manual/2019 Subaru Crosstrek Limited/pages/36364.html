Rear Spoiler: Removal

  1. Remove the trim panel - trunk lid. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>TRUNK ROOM TRIM>REMOVAL .
  2. Detach the rear spoiler assembly.
    CAUTION:
    • Be careful not to damage the vehicle body during removal and installation.
    • When removing the nut, be careful not to drop the nut inside the trunk lid panel.
    • The reactive force of trunk lid increases when the rear spoiler is removed.
    1. Attach protective tape (a) to the rear spoiler assembly, the cap - rear spoiler, and a screwdriver.
    2. Remove the cap - rear spoiler, and remove the bolts.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Remove the nut from the inside of the trunk lid.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    4. Disengage the clips and remove the rear spoiler assembly.
