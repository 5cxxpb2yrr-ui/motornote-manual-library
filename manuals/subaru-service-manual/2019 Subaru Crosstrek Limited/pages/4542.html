Power Window Control Switch: Installation: Notes

CAUTION:
  • After installing the switch - power window main, always perform the initial setting.
  • Failure to do so may cause the improper activation of auto-reverse operation for pinching hazard prevention. Refer to POWER WINDOW (DIAGNOSTICS)>WORK SUPPORT>OPERATION .
  • Use protective paper to avoid damage to the trim panel.
