CAN Communication Circuit Check: Procedure: C-Can

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Trouble causes: 

  • C-CAN communication circuit is shorted to ground.
  • C-CAN communication circuit is shorted to power supply.
  • C-CAN communication circuit is shorted between lines.

Wiring diagram: 

CAN communication system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>CAN COMMUNICATION SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
NOTE:
  • When measuring the resistance of CAN communication circuit, measure it in sleep status. To enter sleep status
    • With ignition switch OFF and key or switch operation stopped, keep the doors, trunk, and rear gate all closed for one minute or more.
    • Keep the access key 2 m or more away from the vehicle.
  • When the bus line is measured, combined resistance of the end resistance (120 Ω) in the central gateway control module and the end resistance (120 Ω) in VDC control module & H/U can be measured. The combined resistance is more than 56 Ω and less than 64 Ω. If the measured resistance value becomes 56 Ω or less, main wiring harness or related lines may be shorted. Or, the combined resistance may have changed because of a resistance other than the end resistance created on the circuit. If the measured value is 64 Ω or more, there may be a malfunction such as open circuit in one of the end resistances, in the stabilizing circuit, or in the main wiring harness.

    Also, even when the resistance value falls more than 56 Ω and less than 64 Ω, related lines may be open if an error of communication for initializing or a CAN system U-code has occurred (the resistance cannot be more than 56 Ω and less than 64 Ω if the main wiring harness is open).

  1. CHECK BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE. 

    Check that the basic diagnosis has been performed up to STEP 3.

    NOTE: Possible defective parts can be narrowed easily by inspection using Subaru Select Monitor before performing "CHECK CAN COMMUNICATION CIRCUIT" using a tester.

    Was the basic diagnostic procedure performed?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Perform the basic diagnostic procedure. Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .

  2. CHECK FOR SHORT TO GROUND IN C-CAN COMMUNICATION CIRCUIT. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the central gateway control module connector.
    3. Using a tester, measure the resistance between the central gateway control module connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (i366) No. 23 - Chassis ground:

      (i366) No. 8 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance less than 10 Ω?

    Yes:  Check for ground short. Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>CAN COMMUNICATION CIRCUIT CHECK>INSPECTION>GROUND SHORT INSPECTION (C-CAN) .

    No:  Go to  3.

  3. CHECK FOR SHORT TO POWER SUPPLY IN C-CAN COMMUNICATION CIRCUIT. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    2. Using a tester, measure the voltage between the central gateway control module connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (i366) No. 23 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

      (i366) No. 8 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    Is the voltage 5 V or less?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Check for power supply short. Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>CAN COMMUNICATION CIRCUIT CHECK>INSPECTION>POWER SHORT INSPECTION (C-CAN) .

  4. CHECK FOR SHORT IN C-CAN COMMUNICATION CIRCUIT. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between the central gateway control module connector terminals.

      Connector & terminal 

      (i366) No. 23 - (i366) No. 8:

    Is the resistance 56 Ω or less?

    Yes:  Perform the inspection for resistance of 56 Ω or less. Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>CAN COMMUNICATION CIRCUIT CHECK>INSPECTION>56 Ω OR LESS (C-CAN) .

    No:  Go to  5.

  5. CHECK FOR OPEN CIRCUIT IN C-CAN COMMUNICATION CIRCUIT. 

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between the central gateway control module connector terminals.

    Connector & terminal 

    (i366) No. 23 - (i366) No. 8:

    Is the resistance 64 Ω or more?

    Yes:  Perform the inspection for resistance of 64 Ω or more. Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>CAN COMMUNICATION CIRCUIT CHECK>INSPECTION>64 Ω OR MORE (C-CAN) .

    No:  If the display of CAN system U-code disappears from the current malfunction, the CAN network is currently normal. If the U-code has detected as current malfunction, related lines may be open. Perform the inspection for the related line corresponding to the detected DTC. Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>CAN COMMUNICATION CIRCUIT CHECK>LIST .
