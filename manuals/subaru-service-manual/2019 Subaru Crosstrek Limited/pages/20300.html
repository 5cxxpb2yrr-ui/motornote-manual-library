Explanation For Circuits

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

BATTERY CURRENT SENSOR, located at the positive cable side of HV battery, detects the current flowing through the HV battery. The Battery ECU assembly receives at the IB0 terminal an inputs of voltage that varies from 0 to 5 V according to the amount of current from the BATTERY CURRENT SENSOR and receives at the IB1 terminal an input of voltage that varies from 0 to 5 V contrary to the amount of current. When the IB0 terminal voltage is less than 2.8 V and the IB1 terminal voltage exceeds 2.2 V, it means that the HV battery is discharging. Meanwhile, when the IB0 terminal voltage exceeds 2.8 V and the IB1 terminal voltage is less than 2.2 V, it means that the HV battery is charged. The Battery ECU assembly determines the charge/discharge amount of the HV battery from the input signals of the IB0 terminal and the IB1 terminal, and calculates the SOC of the HV battery by integrating the current amount.
