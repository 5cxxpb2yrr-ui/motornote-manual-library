Rear Wiper Arm: Adjustment

  1. Operate the rear wiper once.
  2. If the wiper blade is not aligned with the point mark, perform the removal of the rear wiper to align with the marking. Refer to WIPER AND WASHER SYSTEMS>REAR WIPER ARM>REMOVAL .
