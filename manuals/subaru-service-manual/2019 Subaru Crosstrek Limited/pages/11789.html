Installation Of Service Disconnect Plug

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

CAUTION:
  • Before attaching the service disconnect plug, make sure that no parts or tools are left inside, and double check the tightening conditions of high voltage terminals and connector connections.
  • Always replace faulty service disconnect plug with a new part.
  • Always use the original service disconnect plug when installing it. Never use a service disconnect plug removed from another vehicle.

Install in the reverse order of removal.

NOTE: Slide the lever completely towards the right side of the vehicle.
