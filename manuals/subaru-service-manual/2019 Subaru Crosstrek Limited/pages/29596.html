DTC B28AF: Stereo Camera Adjustment Incomplete

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Detected when adjustment or inspection of stereo camera has not been completed normally.

DTC detecting condition: 

  • Operation is aborted during adjustment or inspection of the stereo camera.
  • After the replacement of the stereo camera, adjustment or inspection of camera has not yet been performed.

Trouble symptom: 

All functions of EyeSight system do not operate.

For diagnostic procedure, perform adjustment/inspection of camera. Refer to EYESIGHT>CAMERA ADJUSTMENT, INSPECTION . When the adjustment or inspection of camera is performed, and if it is not completed successfully, replace the camera. Refer to EYESIGHT>STEREO CAMERA .
