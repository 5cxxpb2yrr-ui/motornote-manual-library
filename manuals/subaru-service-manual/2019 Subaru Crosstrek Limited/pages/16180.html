Fuel Delivery Pipe

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Release the fuel pressure. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL>PROCEDURE>RELEASING OF FUEL PRESSURE .
  2. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  3. Remove the fuel pipe protector No. 1.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Disconnect the fuel delivery pipe A. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>REMOVAL>QUICK CONNECTOR DISCONNECTION (TYPE C) .
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the fuel delivery pipe B.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
