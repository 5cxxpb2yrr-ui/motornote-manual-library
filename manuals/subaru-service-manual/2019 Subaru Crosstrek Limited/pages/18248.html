Weather Strip: Replacement: Flange

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Remove each weather strip by pulling the rubber part. Be careful not to tear the rubber.
    • Weather strip - body side flange
    • Weather strip - trunk lid (4 door model)
    • Weather strip - rear gate (5 door model)
      NOTE:
      • While removing the weather strip - trunk lid, be careful not to allow adhesive to contact the trim panel - trunk side.
      • While removing the weather strip - rear gate, be careful not to allow adhesive to contact the trim panel - roof assembly.
  2. Install each weather strip.
    • Weather strip - body side flange
    • Weather strip - trunk lid (4 door model)
    • Weather strip - rear gate (5 door model)
      NOTE: When installing the weather strip - body side flange, install so that the alignment mark (a) and the cutout portion on the body side (b) are aligned.
