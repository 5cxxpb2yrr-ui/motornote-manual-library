Master Cylinder: Installation

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Install the master cylinder.

    Tightening torque: 

    18 N.m (1.8 kgf-m, 13.3 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Tighten the clutch pipe.

    Tightening torque: 

    15 N.m (1.5 kgf-m, 11.1 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Install the push rod of the master cylinder, and install the clevis pin and clip.
    CAUTION: Always use a new clevis pin.
    NOTE: Apply grease to the clevis pin.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Install the clutch pedal assembly. Refer to CLUTCH SYSTEM>CLUTCH PEDAL>INSTALLATION .
  5. Install the steering column. Refer to POWER ASSISTED SYSTEM (POWER STEERING)>STEERING COLUMN>INSTALLATION .
  6. Add the recommended brake fluid. Refer to CLUTCH SYSTEM>CLUTCH FLUID .
  7. After bleeding air from the clutch system, ensure that no fluid leaks and the clutch operates properly. Refer to CLUTCH SYSTEM>CLUTCH FLUID AIR BLEEDING .
  8. Install the instrument panel lower cover. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>INSTRUMENT PANEL LOWER COVER>INSTALLATION .
  9. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
