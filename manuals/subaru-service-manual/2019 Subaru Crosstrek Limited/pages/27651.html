Malfunction Description

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Motor generator control ECU detects a malfunction when over voltage abnormality occurs in the motor inverter and generator inverter. The following cases are assumed to be causes of this symptom.

Part Main trouble contents
Transmission assembly
  • Short or open circuit of motor or generator coil
  • Malfunction inside motor or generator (damages caused by iron powder and foreign matter)
Resolver Open or short circuit of Motor resolver or GENERATOR RESOLVER CIRCUIT
HV battery high voltage system
  • Open circuit of system main relay
  • Open circuit of Electric vehicle fuse
  • Open circuit of frame wire
  • Malfunction of HV battery
Inverter
  • Inverter inner circuit malfunction
  • Malfunction of unit that controls inverter
  • Inverter controlling sensor malfunction (current sensor, voltage sensor (VH and VL), etc.)
