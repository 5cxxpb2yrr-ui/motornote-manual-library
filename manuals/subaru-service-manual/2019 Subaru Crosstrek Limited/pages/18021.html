Transmission Case: Adjustment

NOTE: When replacing the transmission case with a new part, perform the following check and adjustment for the selection.
  • Select the transfer driven gear shim. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>TRANSFER CLUTCH>ADJUSTMENT .
  • Select the transfer drive gear shim. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>REDUCTION DRIVEN GEAR>ADJUSTMENT .
  • Select the reduction drive gear shim. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>REDUCTION DRIVE GEAR>ADJUSTMENT .
