Exhaust System: Muffler: Removal

CAUTION: Vehicle components are extremely hot after driving. Be wary of receiving burns from heated parts.
  1. Disconnect the rear exhaust pipe and muffler.
    • Impreza 4 door model
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • Impreza 5 door model and Crosstrek model
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Apply a coat of spray type lubricant to the mating area of cushion rubber.
  3. Remove the muffler.
    CAUTION: Be careful not to let the muffler contact the rear bumper.
    • Impreza 4 door model
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • Impreza 5 door model and Crosstrek model
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the rear exhaust cover.
    NOTE: Remove if necessary.
    • Impreza 4 door model
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • Impreza 5 door model and Crosstrek model (except for hybrid)
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • Crosstrek hybrid model
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
