RH Side

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Install the ignition coil.
    CAUTION: Degrease the mounting bolts, rocker cover installation portion and ignition coil installation hole.

    Tightening torque: 

    8 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.9 ft-lb)

  2. Connect the connector to the ignition coil.
  3. Set the inverter front water pipe, and secure the pipe - evaporator cooling to the bracket with the clip (A). (Hybrid model)
  4. Install the bolt to secure the inverter front water pipe to the vehicle. (Hybrid model)

    Tightening torque: 

    7.5 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.5 ft-lb)

  5. Install the inverter center water pipe inlet hose and the inverter center water pipe outlet hose to the clamp (B), and secure the harness to the inverter front water pipe with the clip (C). (Hybrid model)
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Install the air intake boot. (Hybrid model) Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>Air Intake Boot>INSTALLATION .
  7. Install the air cleaner case. Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>AIR CLEANER CASE>INSTALLATION .
  8. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
