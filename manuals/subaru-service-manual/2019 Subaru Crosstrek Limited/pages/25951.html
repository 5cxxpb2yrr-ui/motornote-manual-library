Explanation For Circuits

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Battery ECU assembly monitors the HV battery heater temperature via HV battery heater temperature sensors (thermistors) and, at the same time, controls the HV battery temperature to raise to the target temperature. Battery ECU assembly detects the malfunction of HV battery heater temperature sensor (thermistor) and outputs the DTC.
