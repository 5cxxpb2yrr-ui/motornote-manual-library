Refrigerant Leak Check: Inspection

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Check the refrigerant gas pressure. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>REFRIGERANT PRESSURE WITH MANIFOLD GAUGE SET>PROCEDURE>CHECK REFRIGERANT GAS PRESSURE .

    Preparation tool: 

    Manifold gauge set

    1. Attach the manifold gauge set.
    2. Turn READY ON, operate the A/C system for approx. 10 minutes, and check that the high-pressure side indicates 0.69 MPa (7 kgf/cm2, 100 psi) or more.
  2. Turn READY OFF and start the leak test.

Preparation tool: 

Electronic leak detector (leak tester)

Items Condition Corrective action
Pipe Check the connection between the pipe and air conditioner unit. Check the O-ring at connection and tightening torque. If necessary, replace each part.
Check the connection between pipe and pressure sensor. Replace the pressure sensor.
Check the connection between pipe and hose. Check the O-ring at connection and tightening torque. If necessary, replace each part.
Check the connection between pipe and valve. Check the O-ring at connection and tightening torque. If necessary, replace each part.
Check the valve and cap in the service port. Check the rubber seal of the valve and cap. If necessary, replace valve or cap.
Condenser Check the welded part of condenser and core. Replace the condenser.
Check the connection between valve and condenser. Check the O-ring at connection and tightening torque. If necessary, replace each part.
Check the connection between pipe and condenser. Check the O-ring at connection and tightening torque. If necessary, replace each part.
Hose Check the connection between hose and compressor. Check the O-ring at connection and tightening torque. If necessary, replace each part.
Check the connection between hose and pipe. Check the O-ring at connection and tightening torque. If necessary, replace each part.
Check the connection between hose and valve. Check the O-ring at connection and tightening torque. If necessary, replace each part.
Check the connection between hose and air conditioner unit. Check the O-ring at connection and tightening torque. If necessary, replace each part.
Check the connection between hose and accumulator. Check the O-ring at connection and tightening torque. If necessary, replace each part.
Check the rubber part of the flexible hose and pipe seam.
CAUTION: Carefully check the external surface of flexible hoses and pipes at approx. 25 mm (0.98 in) per second.
 Replace the hose.
Check the valve and cap in the service port. Check the rubber seal of the valve and cap. If necessary, replace valve or cap.
Expansion valve Check the connection between pipe and expansion valve. Check the O-ring at connection and tightening torque. If necessary, replace each part.
Check the connection between hose and expansion valve. Check the O-ring at connection and tightening torque. If necessary, replace each part.
Check the connection between condenser and expansion valve. Check the O-ring at connection and tightening torque. If necessary, replace each part.
Check the rubber part of the flexible hose and pipe seam.
CAUTION: Carefully check the external surface of flexible hoses and pipes at approx. 25 mm (0.98 in) per second.
 Check the O-ring at connection and tightening torque. If necessary, replace each part.
Evaporator Remove the drain hose from the heater case, and check the end part for 10 seconds or more. Replace the evaporator.
Check the air vent grille.
NOTE: Turn the ignition switch to ON, and run the blower at high speed for approx. 1 minute. Stop the blower to check the air vent grille on the instrument panel. While moving the tester closer to the grille, run the blower for 1 or 2 seconds, then stop it. Check the grille at that position for at least 10 seconds.
 Replace the evaporator.
