Mass Air Flow And Intake Air Temperature Sensor: Installation

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Install the mass air flow and intake air temperature sensor, and connect the connector.

    Tightening torque: 

    1 N.m (0.1 kgf-m, 0.7 ft-lb)

  2. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
