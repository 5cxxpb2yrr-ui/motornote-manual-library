Telematics Button

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the light assembly - map. Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>SPOT MAP LIGHT>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the telematics button.
    1. Release the claws and remove the front side of the frame.
      CAUTION: Be careful not to pull the harness.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
      NOTE: The frame cannot be removed.
    2. Remove the screws from the frame clearance, and remove the telematics button.
