Work Support: List

Work Support item Contents
Brake Air Bleeding Bleed air caught in the brake line. Ref. to BRAKE>AIR BLEEDING .
  • Brake Line Air Bleeding (when brake booster assembly (with master cylinder), VDC control module & hydraulic control unit (VDCCM & H/U), front brake pipe assembly (pipe between VDC control module & hydraulic control unit (VDCCM & H/U) and master cylinder), center brake pipe assembly, front brake hose, rear brake hose, front disc brake assembly, and rear disc brake assembly are replaced)
  • Brake System Air Bleeding (when brake booster assembly (with master cylinder), brake accumulator assembly, front brake pipe assembly (pipe between brake accumulator assembly and master cylinder), and reservoir joint pipe are replaced)
  • Actuator Forced Refresh (when abnormal noise occurs from the engine compartment caused by air)
Prohibiting Motor Control Lower accumulator pressure.
Driving Accumulator and Lowering Pressure Set the accumulator pressure to 0.
Learning Offset of Liner Solenoid Valve Calibrate the linear valves.
Erase Backup Memory Reset the learning values.
Brake lamp Lighting Operation Make sure that the brake light relay operates and the brake lights illuminate normally.
