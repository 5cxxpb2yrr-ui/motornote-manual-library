Rear Differential (VA-type): Assembly

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

CAUTION:
  • Keep the shims and washers in order, so that they are not improperly installed.
  • Be careful not to mix up the side bearing race RH and LH.
  • Be careful not to mix up the side oil seal RH and LH.
NOTE:
  • Check and adjust each part during assembly.
  • Thoroughly clean the surfaces on which the shims, washers and bearings are to be installed.
  • Apply differential gear oil when installing the bearings and thrust washers.
  • Apply differential gear oil to the lips when installing the oil seal.
Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  1. Adjusting preload for front and rear bearings
    NOTE: Adjust the bearing preload between front and rear bearings with preload adjusting spacer and washer. Pinion height adjusting washer is not affected by this adjustment. The adjustment must be carried out with oil seal removed.
    1. Install the rear bearing race using the ST1 and ST2.

      Special tool: 

      ST1: 398477701 HANDLE

      ST2: 398477702 DRIFT

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Using the HANDLE (398477701) and DRIFT (498447110), install a new front bearing race to the differential carrier.
    3. Insert a new front bearing cone.
    4. Measure and record the thickness of the pinion height adjusting washer.
      NOTE:
      • At this time, install a provisionally selected or previously used pinion height adjusting washer.
      • If tooth contact (drive pinion and hypoid driven gear) is normal in the inspection before disassembling, verify that the washer is not deformed, and then reuse the used washer.
    5. Insert the ST into the differential carrier with the pinion height adjusting washer and rear bearing cone fitted onto it.
    6. Install the preload adjusting spacer, washer, spacer, new pilot bearing, companion flange, and self-locking nut.

      Special tool: 

      ST: 498447150 DUMMY SHAFT

      Part No. 32285AA000 Spacer 

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    7. Turn the ST1 by hand to smooth the bearing. Tighten a new self-locking nut while measuring the initial load or initial torque with a spring scale or torque wrench. Select the preload adjusting washer and spacer so that the specified preload is obtained when nut is tightened to the specified torque.
      CAUTION: Be careful not to give excessive preload.
      NOTE:
      • Measure the preload in direction of tangent to the flange.
      • When tightening the self-locking nut, lock the ST1 with the ST2 as shown in the figure.

      Special tool: 

      ST1: 498447150 DUMMY SHAFT

      ST2: 398507704 BLOCK

      Tightening torque: 

      191 N.m (19.5 kgf-m, 140.9 ft-lb)

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.

      Initial load: 

      12.7 - 32.2 N (1.3 - 3.3 kgf, 2.9 - 7.2 lbf)

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.

      Initial torque:

  2. Adjusting drive pinion height:

    Adjust the drive pinion height with pinion height adjusting washer installed between the rear bearing cone and the back of pinion gear.

    1. Install the ST2.

      Special tool: 

      ST1: 498447150 DUMMY SHAFT

      ST2: 498505501 DIFFERENTIAL CARRIER GAUGE

      Part No. 32285AA000 Spacer 

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Measure the clearance "N" between the end of ST2 and the end surface of ST1 by using a thickness gauge.
      NOTE: Make sure there is no clearance between the differential carrier and ST2.

      Special tool: 

      ST1: 498447150 DUMMY SHAFT

      ST2: 498505501 DIFFERENTIAL CARRIER GAUGE

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Obtain the thickness of pinion height adjusting washer to be inserted from the following formula, and replace the temporarily installed washer with this one.
      NOTE: Adjust it using the 1 - 3 washers.

      T = To + N - 0.05 mm (0.0020 in)

      T Thickness of pinion height adjusting washer mm (in)  
      To Thickness of washer temporarily inserted mm (in)  
      N Clearance of thickness gauge mm (in)  
      Memo:

      (Example of calculation)

      To = 0.15 mm (0.0059 in)

      N = 0.1 mm (0.0039 in)

      T = 0.15+ 0.1- 0.05 = 0.2 mm (0.0079 in)

      Result: Thickness = 0.2 mm (0.0079 in)

      Therefore use part number 32295AA220.

      Pinion height adjusting washer
      Part No. Thickness mm (in)
      32295AA200 0.150 (0.0059)
      32295AA210 0.175 (0.0069)
      32295AA220 0.200 (0.0079)
      32295AA230 0.225 (0.0089)
      32295AA240 0.250 (0.0098)
      32295AA250 0.275 (0.0108)
  3. Install the selected pinion height adjusting washer on drive pinion, and press-in the rear bearing cone with the ST.

    Special tool: 

    ST: 498175500 INSTALLER

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Insert the drive pinion, and install the preselected preload adjusting spacer and washer.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Press-fit a new front bearing cone with the ST1, ST2 and spacer.

    Special tool: 

    ST1: 399780104 WEIGHT

    ST2: 899580100 INSTALLER

    Part No. 32285AA000 Spacer 

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Insert the spacer, then press-fit a new pilot bearing with the ST1 and ST2.

    Special tool: 

    ST1: 399780104 WEIGHT

    ST2: 899580100 INSTALLER

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Using the ST, install a new front oil seal.
    NOTE:
    • Press-fit until the oil seal end comes 1 mm (0.04 in) inward from end of carrier.
    • Apply differential gear oil to the oil seal lips.

    Special tool: 

    ST: 498447120 INSTALLER

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Press-fit the companion flange with the ST1 and ST2.
    CAUTION: Be careful not to damage the pilot bearing.

    Special tool: 

    ST1: 899874100 INSTALLER

    ST2: 399780104 WEIGHT

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Apply seal material on the drive pinion shaft thread and new self-locking nut seat.

    Seal material: 

    THREE BOND 1324 (Part No. 004403042) or equivalent

  10. Tighten a new self-locking nut while securing the companion flange with the ST.

    Tightening torque: 

    191 N.m (19.5 kgf-m, 140.9 ft-lb)

    Special tool: 

    ST: 498427200 FLANGE WRENCH

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  11. Check the initial torque or initial load.

    Specification: 

    Initial load: 

    12.7 - 32.2 N (1.3 - 3.3 kgf, 2.9 - 7.2 lbf)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.

    Initial torque: 

    0.48 - 1.22 N.m (0.05 - 0.12 kgf-m, 0.35 - 0.90 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  12. Assembling differential case
    1. Check that oil grooves of side gear RH and LH face in the same direction.
      CAUTION: When replacing the side gear, replace both RH and LH as a set.
      NOTE: There are 2 types of side gear oil groove directions.
      • Type A
        Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
      • Type B
        Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Install the side gears and pinion mate gears together with the side gear thrust washers and pinion mate shaft.
      NOTE:
      • Apply gear oil on both sides of the washer and on the side gear shaft before installing.
      • Insert the pinion mate shaft into the differential case by aligning the pin holes.
      • The side gear thrust washer inner surface has a chamfered side. Install the washer with the chamfered side facing toward the side gear.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Measure the side gear backlash.

      Specification: 

      0.05 - 0.15 mm (0.002 - 0.006 in)

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    4. Adjust the side gear backlash within the specified value by selecting the side gear thrust washer.
      Side gear thrust washer
      Part No. Thickness mm (in)
      803135011 0.925 - 0.950 (0.0364 - 0.0374)
      803135012 0.950 - 0.975 (0.0374 - 0.0384)
      803135013 0.975 - 1.000 (0.0384 - 0.0394)
      803135014 1.000 - 1.025 (0.0394 - 0.0404)
      803135015 1.025 - 1.050 (0.0404 - 0.0413)
    5. Check the condition of rotation after applying oil to the gear tooth surfaces and washer surfaces.
    6. Using the STRAIGHT PIN REMOVER (899904100), drive a new spring pin into the differential case.
  13. Install the hypoid driven gear to differential case.
    NOTE:
    • Make sure there is no clearance between the differential case and driven gear.
    • Tighten opposing bolts in order.
    1. Tighten the hypoid driven gear mounting bolts to the specified torque using the ST.

      Special tool: 

      ST: 18270KA020 SOCKET (E20)

      Tightening torque: 

      20 N.m (2.0 kgf-m, 14.8 ft-lb)

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. While checking the tightening angle with the angle gauge, further tighten the hypoid driven gear mounting bolts.

      Tightening angle: 

      46°±1°

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  14. Press-fit the side bearing cone using the ST.

    Special tool: 

    ST: 498485400 DRIFT

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  15. Assemble the side retainer.
    1. Install a new side oil seal to the side retainer.
      CAUTION: Pay attention to the left and right of the oil seal.
      NOTE: Apply differential gear oil to the oil seal lip.

      Special tool: 

      ST: 498447100 INSTALLER

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Install the side bearing race into the side retainer.
      CAUTION: Make sure that bearing races and cones are properly assembled.

      Special tool: 

      ST: 398477702 DRIFT

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Install the differential case assembly.
      CAUTION: Be careful not to hit the teeth of hypoid driven gear against the differential carrier.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    4. Lightly tighten the side retainer in the differential carrier.
  16. Perform the backlash adjustment between the hypoid driven gear and drive pinion, and side bearing preload adjustment.
    1. Turn the drive pinion with the ST to seat the side bearing securely.

      Special tool: 

      ST: 498427200 FLANGE WRENCH

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Using the ST, tighten the side retainer RH, and then tighten the side retainer LH until there is no backlash.

      Special tool: 

      ST: 18630AA010 WRENCH COMPL RETAINER

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Loosen the side retainer LH by approx. 1.5 teeth, and tighten the side retainer RH by approx. 2 teeth [amount that the side retainer LH is turned back (approx. 1.5 teeth) + approx. 0.5 teeth]. Difference between [amount that the side retainer LH is turned back (approx. 1.5 teeth)] and [amount that the side retainer RH is tightened (approx. 2 teeth)] gives preload.
    4. Temporarily tighten the lock plate.
      NOTE: Turn over the lock plate to shift the holder by 0.5 teeth.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    5. Measure the hypoid driven gear-to-drive pinion backlash. Set the magnet base on differential carrier. Align the contact point of dial gauge with tooth face of hypoid driven gear, and move hypoid driven gear while holding drive pinion still. Read the value indicated on dial gauge.
      NOTE: If it is outside the standard value, repeat the procedures for the backlash adjustment between hypoid driven gear and drive pinion, and the side bearing preload adjustment.

      Specification: 

      0.10 - 0.15 mm (0.004 - 0.006 in)

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  17. Put alignment marks on both the differential carrier and side retainer. Remove the side retainers one side at a time. After installing a new O-ring and applying oil to the threaded portion, restore them to the original position.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  18. Install the lock plate.

    Tightening torque: 

    25 N.m (2.5 kgf-m, 18.4 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  19. Recheck the backlash between hypoid driven gear and drive pinion.

    Specification: 

    0.10 - 0.15 mm (0.004 - 0.006 in)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  20. Checking and adjusting the tooth contact of hypoid driven gear
    1. Apply lead-free red dye evenly on the both sides of three to four teeth of the hypoid driven gear. Check the contact pattern after rotating the hypoid driven gear several revolutions back and forth until a definite contact pattern appears on the hypoid driven gear.
    2. When the contact pattern is not correct, readjust.
      NOTE: Be sure to wipe off the lead-free red dye completely after the adjustment is completed.
      • Correct tooth contact

        Check item: Tooth contact pattern is slightly shifted toward toe side under no-load rotation. (When driving, it moves towards the heel side.) 

        Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
      • Face contact

        Check item: Backlash is too large  .

        Contact pattern

        Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.

        Corrective action: Increase thickness of pinion height adjusting washer according to the procedure for bringing drive pinion close to hypoid driven gear side.

        Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
      • Flank contact

        Check item: Backlash is too small  .

        Contact pattern

        Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.

        Corrective action: Reduce the thickness of pinion height adjusting washer according to the procedure for bringing drive pinion away from hypoid driven gear.

        Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
      • Toe contact (inside contact)

        Check item: Teeth contact area is too small  .

        Contact pattern

        Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.

        Corrective action: Reduce the thickness of pinion height adjusting washer according to the procedure for bringing drive pinion away from hypoid driven gear side.

        Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
      • Heel contact (outside end contact)

        Check item: Teeth contact area is too small  .

        Contact pattern

        Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.

        Corrective action: Increase thickness of pinion height adjusting washer according to the procedure for bringing drive pinion close to hypoid driven gear side.

        Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  21. If proper tooth contact is not obtained, readjust the drive pinion height, side bearing preload (previously described) and the hypoid driven gear backlash.
  22. Replace the gasket with a new part and install the rear cover.
    NOTE: For Crosstrek hybrid model, install the ground bracket (B) by tightening together.

    Tightening torque: 

    25 N.m (2.5 kgf-m, 18.4 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  23. When the stud bolts are removed from the rear cover, install the stud bolts so that the exposed length of stud bolt is within the range of "L".
    CAUTION:
    • Do not tighten forcibly if the exposed length of stud bolt does not fall within the specified range. Remove the stud bolt, and check for contamination of foreign matter and any defects in the screw hole.
    • Do not tighten with a tightening torque of 55 N.m (5.6 kg-m, 40.6 ft-lb) (reference value) or more.

    Exposed length L of stud bolt: 

    59.0 - 61.0 mm (2.32 - 2.40 in)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  24. Install the air breather cap.
    CAUTION: When installing the air breather cap, do not tap section (A). Be sure to tap section (B) to install.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  25. Replace the gasket with a new part, and install the drain plug.

    Tightening torque: 

    50 N.m (5.1 kg-m, 36.9 ft-lb)

  26. Install the filler plug.
    NOTE: After installing the rear differential assembly to the vehicle, add gear oil, then use a new gasket and tighten to the specified torque.
    Refer to DIFFERENTIALS>DIFFERENTIAL GEAR OIL>REPLACEMENT .
