DTC P0E51-1F: DC/DC Converter Current Sensor Circuit Intermittent: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Detecting condition: 

When DTC P0C7917, P0D3319, P1C5D19 or P1C5F19 error judgment occurs and short to ground, short to +B or open circuit of reactor current sensor is detected

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom:

NOTE: When DTC is output, replace Inverter with converter assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>INVERTER AND CONVERTER ASSEMBLY .
