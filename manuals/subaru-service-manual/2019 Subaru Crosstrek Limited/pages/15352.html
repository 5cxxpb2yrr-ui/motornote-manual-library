Check Turn Signal Light System

  1. CHECK TURN SIGNAL LIGHT OPERATION  .
    1. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    2. Turn the turn signal light switch LH and RH to ON.

    Does the turn signal light blink?

    Yes:  The turn signal light system is normal.

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK CURRENT DATA  .

    Display the data of [Left turn signal input] and [Right turn signal input] using Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to BODY CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DATA MONITOR .

    Does the display change as [OFF] ←→ [ON], when the turn signal switch is turned from OFF to ON?

    Yes:  Check the body integrated unit and replace it if defective.

    • Inspection: Refer to BODY CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE .
    • Replacement: Refer to SECURITY AND LOCKS>BODY INTEGRATED UNIT .

    No:  Go to  3.

  3. CHECK TURN SIGNAL SWITCH  .

    Check the turn signal switch. Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>COMBINATION SWITCH (LIGHT)>INSPECTION .

    Is the turn signal switch OK?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Replace the combination switch (light).

  4. CHECK HARNESS  .
    1. Disconnect the connectors of body integrated unit and combination switch (light).
    2. Check the harness between body integrated unit and combination switch (light).

    Is harness normal?

    Yes:  Check the body integrated unit and replace it if defective.

    • Inspection: Refer to BODY CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE .
    • Replacement: Refer to SECURITY AND LOCKS>BODY INTEGRATED UNIT .

    No:  Repair or replace the harness.
