Explanation For Circuits

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

DC/DC Converter controls output voltage variably depending on the vehicle condition using a voltage switching signal (VLO signal line) from the Motor generator control ECU. If the driver continues to drive while the DC/DC Converter is malfunctioning, Battery (12V) voltage drop occurs and the driver cannot drive any longer. Therefore, Motor generator control ECU monitors the DC/DC Converter operation and warns the driver of malfunction detection.
