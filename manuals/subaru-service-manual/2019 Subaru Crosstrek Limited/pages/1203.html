Pre-Collision Brake Off & Lane Departure Warning Off Switch

NOTE: The pre-collision brake OFF switch and the lane departure warning OFF switch are integrated.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Ref. to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the stereo camera cover assembly. Ref. to EYESIGHT>STEREO CAMERA>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the pre-collision brake OFF & lane departure warning OFF switch.
    1. Remove the connector clip, and disconnect the connector.
    2. Remove the screws, and remove the pre-collision brake OFF & lane departure warning OFF switch.
