Transmitter (Tire Inflation Pressure Sensor)

  1. Remove the wheels from the vehicle.

    Refer to WHEEL AND TIRE SYSTEM>TIRE AND WHEEL>REMOVAL .

  2. Remove the tires from wheels.
    CAUTION: Use a tire changer when removing the tire from the wheel.
  3. Loosen the screw to remove the transmitter from the valve stem.
    CAUTION: Do not reuse the valve and screw.
    Replace the valve and screw with a new part even when reusing transmitter.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the valve from the wheel.
