DTC P0D56-11: Charging Connector Proximity Detection Circuit Short To Ground: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. - 

    Outline of diagnosis: 

    PISW terminal voltage was 0.35 V or less continuously for the prescribed length of time.

    (1 trip detection logic)

    Trouble symptom: 

    Plug-in charging suspended

    Wiring diagram: 

    Charging system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>CHARGING SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to PLUG-IN BATTERY CHARGER (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK Electric vehicle charger cable assembly UNIT (OPEN CIRCUIT). 

    Check the Electric vehicle charger cable assembly unit. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>CHARGE CABLE>INSPECTION .

    Is the Electric vehicle charger cable assembly OK?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Replace Electric vehicle charger cable assembly.

  2. CHECK THE Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Electric vehicle charger assembly - Charging inlet). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Check that the Service plug is disconnected. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .
    2. Disconnect Electric vehicle charger assembly connector (HV31).
    3. Using a tester, measure the resistance between AC charger inlet cable (Charging inlet) connector and chassis ground, and between AC charger inlet cable (Charging inlet) terminals.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV29) No. 4 - (HV29) No. 5:

      (HV29) No. 4 - Chassis ground:

      Is the resistance 2.3 - 3.0 kΩ?

      Yes:  Replace Electric vehicle charger assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>ELECTRIC VEHICLE CHARGER .

      No:  Go to  3.

  3. CHECK AC charger inlet cable. 
    1. Disconnect AC charger inlet cable connector (HV27).
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between AC charger inlet cable (Charging inlet) connector and chassis ground, and between AC charger inlet cable (Charging inlet) terminals.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV29) No. 4 - (HV29) No. 5:

      (HV29) No. 4 - Chassis ground:

      Is the resistance 2.3 - 3.0 kΩ?

      Yes:  Repair or replace the Wire harness between the AC charger inlet cable and chassis ground.

      No:  Repair or replace the AC charger inlet cable. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>CHARGE INLET .
