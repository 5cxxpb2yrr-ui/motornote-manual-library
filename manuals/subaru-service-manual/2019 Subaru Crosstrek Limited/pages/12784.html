Steering Heater Switch

EXCEPT FOR HYBRID MODEL 

  1. Measure the resistance between connector terminals.

    Preparation tool: 

    Circuit tester

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Terminal No. Inspection conditions Standard
    5 - 6 Operate the switch and hold it. Less than 1 Ω
    Do not operate the switch. 1 MΩ or more
  2. Apply battery voltage between the connector terminals to check lighting condition of illumination and indicator inside the switch.
    Terminal No. Inspection conditions Specification
    4 (+) - 1 (-) Apply battery voltage. Illumination (mark) turns on.
    5 (+) - 7 (-) Indicator turns on.
    4 (+) - 1 (-)
    5 (+) - 7 (-)    		 Apply battery voltage simultaneously. Illumination (mark) and indicator turn on in dimmed condition at one level.
  3. Replace the steering heater switch if it is found defective.
