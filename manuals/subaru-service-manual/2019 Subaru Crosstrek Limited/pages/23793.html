EGR Control Valve: Removal

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the clip from the air intake boot, and loosen the clamp.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the air intake boot, and place it aside so that it does not interfere with the work.
    CAUTION: Be careful not to pull out the PCV hose.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Loosen the bolts holding the EGR cooler to the cylinder head RH.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the EGR pipe. Refer to EMISSION CONTROL (AUX. EMISSION CONTROL DEVICES) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>EGR PIPE>REMOVAL .
  6. Remove the PCV hose No. 1. Refer to EMISSION CONTROL (AUX. EMISSION CONTROL DEVICES) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>PCV Hose>REMOVAL>PCV HOSE NO. 1 .
  7. Disconnect the connector.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Remove the nuts securing the EGR cooler, and remove the stud bolts using TORX® E8.
  9. Remove the EGR control valve from the EGR pipe adapter.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  10. Remove the EGR pipe adapter from the intake manifold assembly.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
