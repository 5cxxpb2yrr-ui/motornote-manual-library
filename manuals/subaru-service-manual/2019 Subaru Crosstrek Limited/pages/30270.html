Section 3 (HVAC System (Heater, Ventilator And A/C) (HEV)): Condenser: Inspection

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Check to see that the condenser fins are not clogged with debris or insects. Blow with compressed air or flush fins with water as needed.
  2. If any oil leak is found, replace the internal condenser or condenser assembly.
