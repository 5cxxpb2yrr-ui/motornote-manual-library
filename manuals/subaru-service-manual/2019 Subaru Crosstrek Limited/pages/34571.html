Cam Clearance: Adjustment

  1. Remove the engine from the vehicle.

    EXCEPT FOR HYBRID MODEL: 

    Refer to MECHANICAL (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL>EXCEPT FOR HYBRID MODEL .

    Hybrid model: 

    Refer to MECHANICAL (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL>HYBRID MODEL .

  2. Remove the chain cover. Refer to MECHANICAL (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>CHAIN COVER>REMOVAL .
  3. When adjusting #1 and #3 cylinders
    1. Remove the cam carrier RH. Refer to MECHANICAL (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>CAM CARRIER>REMOVAL>CAM CARRIER RH .
    2. Measure the thickness of valve shim using micrometer.
      NOTE: Measurement should be performed at a temperature of 20°C (68°F).
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Select a valve shim of suitable thickness using the measured cam clearance and valve shim thickness.
      NOTE: Use a new valve shim.

      Intake side: S = T+1.69 x (V-0.13 mm (0.0051 in))

      Exhaust side: S = T+1.87 x (V-0.22 mm (0.0087 in))

      S: Valve shim thickness required

      V: Measured cam clearance

      T: Current valve shim thickness

    4. Install the cam carrier RH. Refer to MECHANICAL (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>CAM CARRIER>INSTALLATION>CAM CARRIER RH .
    5. Check all the cam clearance of RH side at this time. If the cam clearance is not within the standard value, repeat the procedure over again from step  1.
      NOTE: When the removing/installing of cam carrier RH has been performed, cam clearance may be outside the standard value. Checking of all cam clearance of RH side is necessary. Refer to INSPECTION of "Cam Clearance" for the cam clearance inspection. Refer to MECHANICAL (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>CAM CLEARANCE>INSPECTION>WHEN TIMING CHAIN ASSEMBLY IS REMOVED .

      Cam clearance: 

      Intake 

      Standard 

      0.13+0.02-0.03 mm (0.0051+0.0008-0.0012 in)

      Exhaust 

      Standard 

      0.22±0.02 mm (0.0087±0.0008 in)

  4. When adjusting #2 and #4 cylinders
    1. Remove the cam carrier LH. Refer to MECHANICAL (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>CAM CARRIER>REMOVAL>CAM CARRIER LH .
    2. Measure the thickness of valve shim using micrometer.
      NOTE: Measurement should be performed at a temperature of 20°C (68°F).
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Select a valve shim of suitable thickness using the measured cam clearance and valve shim thickness.
      NOTE: Use a new valve shim.

      Intake side: S = T+1.69 x (V-0.13 mm (0.0051 in))

      Exhaust side: S = T+1.87 x (V-0.22 mm (0.0087 in))

      S: Valve shim thickness required

      V: Measured cam clearance

      T: Current valve shim thickness

    4. Install the cam carrier LH. Refer to MECHANICAL (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>CAM CARRIER>INSTALLATION>CAM CARRIER LH .
    5. Check all the cam clearance of LH side at this time. If the cam clearance is not within the standard value, repeat the procedure over again from step  1.
      NOTE: When the removing/installing of cam carrier LH has been performed, cam clearance may be outside the standard value. Checking of all cam clearance of LH side is necessary. Refer to INSPECTION of "Cam Clearance" for the cam clearance inspection. Refer to MECHANICAL (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>CAM CLEARANCE>INSPECTION>WHEN TIMING CHAIN ASSEMBLY IS REMOVED .

      Cam clearance: 

      Intake 

      Standard 

      0.13+0.02-0.03 mm (0.0051+0.0008-0.0012 in)

      Exhaust 

      Standard 

      0.22±0.02 mm (0.0087±0.0008 in)

  5. After adjustment, install the related parts in the reverse order of removal.
