Rear Wiper Motor: Inspection

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

CAUTION: Fix the motor to prevent the motor from being shorted by moving during operation.
  1. Connect the battery to the motor assembly - rear wiper connector, and check that the motor assembly - rear wiper operates.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Connect the battery to terminals of the connector, and remove the terminal connection with motor rotating, and stop the motor in mid-operation.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Connect the battery and check that the motor stops at the automatic stop position after the motor operates at low speed again.
Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
