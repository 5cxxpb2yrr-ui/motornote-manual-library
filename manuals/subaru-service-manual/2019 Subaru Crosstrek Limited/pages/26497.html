Malfunction Description

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly issues a power command to the charger in order for the charging power to the battery not to exceed the permissible charging power. The condition that the charging power cannot be adjusted and the permissible charging power is exceeded is detected.

The following cases are assumed to be malfunction causes:

Internal fault of Electric vehicle charger assembly

  • Malfunction of Electric vehicle charger assembly
