Airbag Control Module

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. How to disconnect:
    1. Push the lock lever plate (a).
    2. While holding down the lock lever plate (a), pull the lever toward you.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. How to separate:
    1. Remove the lock plate (a) behind the integrated connector.
    2. Release the lock levers of the connectors, and pull out the connectors.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. How to connect:
CAUTION: Be sure to insert the connector in until it is locked. Then pull it gently to make sure that it is locked.

Insert the connector and push the lock lever in securely.
