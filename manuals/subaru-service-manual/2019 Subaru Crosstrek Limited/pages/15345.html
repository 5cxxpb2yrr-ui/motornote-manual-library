Clearance Light and Illumination Light System: Note

For operation procedures of each component of the clearance light & illumination light system, refer to the respective service informations.

  • Headlight assembly: Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>HEADLIGHT ASSEMBLY .
  • Combination switch (light): Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>COMBINATION SWITCH (LIGHT) .
  • Parking light bulb: Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>HEADLIGHT ASSEMBLY>DISASSEMBLY .
  • Rear combination light assembly: Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>REAR COMBINATION LIGHT ASSEMBLY .
  • Tail light/stop light bulb: Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>REAR COMBINATION LIGHT ASSEMBLY>DISASSEMBLY .
  • License plate light: Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>LICENSE PLATE LIGHT .
  • LED illumination light: Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>LED ILLUMINATION LIGHT .
  • Ignition switch illumination: Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>IGNITION SWITCH ILLUMINATION .
