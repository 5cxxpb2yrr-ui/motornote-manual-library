Compressor Oil

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  • Electric compressor oil is not interchangeable with belt-driven compressor oil.
  • Use only the manufacturer-authorized compressor oil ND-OIL11 for the electric compressor system.
  • Do not mix multiple compressor oils.

    If belt-driven compressor oil is used with a electric compressor system, there is the risk of short circuit and electrical leakage due to oil conductivity.

    On the other hand, if electric compressor oil is used in belt-driven compressor system, the durability of the A/C system will be dramatically lowered.

  • Compressor oil is very hygroscopic. When replacing or installing/removing A/C parts, immediately isolate the oil from atmosphere using a plug or tape. In order to avoid moisture, store the oil in a container with its cap tightly closed.
