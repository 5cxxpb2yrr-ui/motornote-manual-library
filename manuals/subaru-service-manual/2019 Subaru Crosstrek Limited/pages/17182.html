Intake Side

  1. Install the backup ring to the oil control solenoid.
  2. Using a new O-ring, install the intake oil control solenoid to the chain cover.
    NOTE: Apply engine oil to O-ring.

    Tightening torque: 

    6.4 N.m (0.7 kgf-m, 4.7 ft-lb)

  3. Connect the connector to the intake oil control solenoid.
  4. Install the air intake duct. (RH side only) Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>AIR INTAKE DUCT>INSTALLATION .
  5. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
