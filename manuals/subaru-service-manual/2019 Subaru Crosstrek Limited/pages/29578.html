DTC B2823: Eyesight Prohibits Accepting A Request

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Detected when error occurs in the electric brake booster control module (EBBCM) or when stereo camera abnormality is detected.

DTC detecting condition: 

  • Defective CAN system
  • Defective electric brake booster control module (EBBCM)
  • Defective stereo camera

Trouble symptom: 

  • All functions of EyeSight system do not operate.
  • EyeSight warning light illuminates.
    NOTE: Check the LAN system, electric brake booster control module (EBBCM), and EyeSight when this DTC is displayed. Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE . Refer to ELECTRIC BRAKE BOOSTER (DIAGNOSTICS)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE . Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .
