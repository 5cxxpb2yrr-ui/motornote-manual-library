Warning Light Does Not Illuminate

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

Detecting condition: 

  • Airbag warning light is faulty.
  • Airbag control module to combination meter circuit is shorted or open.
  • Grounding circuit is faulty.
  • Airbag control module is faulty.
  • Connector connection is improper.
  • Connections of (AB6, AB17, AB18) to airbag control module are improper.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to AIRBAG (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK LAN SYSTEM. 

    Perform the diagnosis for LAN system. Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE . Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Perform the inspection according to the diagnosis for LAN system.

  2. CHECK FOR POOR CONTACT. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF, disconnect the battery ground terminal, and wait 60 seconds or more. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    2. Check the connection status of airbag control module connector.

    Is there poor contact of connector?

    Yes:  Reconnect the connector. Or repair the connector.

    No:  Go to  3.

  3. CHECK COMBINATION METER. 

    Check the combination meter. Refer to COMBINATION METER (DIAGNOSTICS)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Replace the airbag control module. Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>AIRBAG CONTROL MODULE>REMOVAL .

    No:  Repair it according to COMBINATION METER (DIAGNOSTICS). Refer to COMBINATION METER (DIAGNOSTICS)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE .
