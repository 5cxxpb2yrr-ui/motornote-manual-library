DTC B1446: Air Mix Door Actuator Circuit Stuck (Driver)

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

DTC detecting condition: 

  • Air mix door actuator is locked.
  • The potentiometer value of the actuator does not change.

Trouble symptom: 

  • Temperature cannot be adjusted for model without left/right independent air conditioner.
  • Temperature of the driver's side cannot be adjusted for model with left/right independent air conditioner.

Wiring diagram: 

Air conditioning system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (IMPREZA MODEL)>AIR CONDITIONING SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>AIR CONDITIONING SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK CONNECTOR. 
    1. Check connecting condition of the A/C control panel connector and air mix door actuator LH connector (with left/right independent air conditioner) or air mix door actuator connector (without left/right independent air conditioner).
    2. Read the DTC of [Air Conditioner] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is DTC B1446 displayed? (Current malfunction)

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Even if DTC is displayed, the circuit has returned to a normal condition at this time.

    Reproduce the failure, and then perform the diagnosis again.

    NOTE: In this case, temporary poor contact of connector, temporary open or short circuit of harness may be the cause.
  2. CHECK ACTUATOR. 

    Using the Subaru Select Monitor, check the following items in the [Active Test] of [Air Conditioner]. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>ACTIVE TEST .

    • Air mix door actuator target open (driver's side)

    Does the air mix door actuator LH (with left/right independent air conditioning function) or air mix door actuator (without left/right independent air conditioning function) move to the set target opening angle?

    Yes:  The circuit of the air mix door actuator LH (with left/right independent air conditioning function) or air mix door actuator (without left/right independent air conditioning function) is normal.

    No:  Go to  3.

  3. CHECK HARNESS. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    2. Using a tester, measure the voltage between terminals in the air mix door actuator LH connector (with left/right independent air conditioning function) or air mix door actuator connector (without left/right independent air conditioning function).

      Connector & terminal 

      With left/right independent air conditioning function 

      (i174) No. 3 (+) - (i174) No. 1 (-):

      Without left/right independent air conditioning function 

      (i175) No. 1 (+) - (i175) No. 3 (-):

    Is the voltage 4.5 - 5.0 V?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Repair or replace the open, short circuit of the harness.

  4. CHECK HARNESS (OPEN CIRCUIT). 
    1. Disconnect the connector from the A/C control panel.
    2. Using a tester, check the continuity between A/C control panel connector and air mix door actuator LH (with left/right independent air conditioning function) or air mix door actuator connector (without left/right independent air conditioning function) terminals.

      Connector & terminal 

      With left/right independent air conditioning function 

      (i174) No. 3 - (i88) No. 16:

      (i174) No. 1 - (i88) No. 30:

      (i174) No. 2 - (i88) No. 35:

      (i174) No. 5 - (i88) No. 4:

      (i174) No. 4 - (i88) No. 3:

      Without left/right independent air conditioning function 

      (i175) No. 1 - (i88) No. 16:

      (i175) No. 3 - (i88) No. 30:

      (i175) No. 2 - (i88) No. 35:

      (i175) No. 4 - (i88) No. 4:

      (i175) No. 5 - (i88) No. 3:

    Is there continuity?

    Yes:  Go to  5.

    No:  Repair or replace the open circuit of harness.

  5. CHECK AIR MIX DOOR ACTUATOR LH. 

    Check the parts of the air mix door actuator LH (with left/right independent air conditioning function) or air mix door actuator (without left/right independent air conditioning function). Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>AIR MIX DOOR ACTUATOR .

    Is the air mix door actuator LH (with left/right independent air conditioning function) or air mix door actuator (without left/right independent air conditioning function) normal?

    Yes:  Replace the A/C control panel. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>CONTROL PANEL .

    No:  Replace the air mix door actuator LH (with left/right independent air conditioning function) or air mix door actuator (without left/right independent air conditioning function). Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>AIR MIX DOOR ACTUATOR .
