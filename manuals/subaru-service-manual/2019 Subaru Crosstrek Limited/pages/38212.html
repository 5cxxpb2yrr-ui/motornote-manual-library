SERVICE CAMPAIGN BULLETIN

Reference Number(s): 09-51-08R, Date of Issue:  March 24, 2025
SUBARU: All Models

APPLICABILITY

All Vehicle Lines/All Model Years

INTRODUCTION

The purpose of this bulletin is to advise of SOA's revised Catalytic Converter Recycling Program shipping procedure.

Since 2012, 100% of all converters shipped from the RDCs are packaged in boxes. Saving and re-using those boxes for the return shipment is practical from many perspectives including reducing costs and generation of waste. While less than 10% of all pipes returned to SOA are shipped "loose" (no packaging), UPS charges SOA a surcharge on all return shipments for these few exceptions. As a result, "Loose" shipping of catalytic converter pipes is no longer an acceptable practice.

Upon claim approval, retailers will receive a Part Return Notice for most converter pipes. Do NOT return converter pipes that have not been requested for return. Certain oversized pipes will again be on return using modified return procedures. The proper procedure for returning these converter pipes is described below. Please review closely as improper shipping procedures may result in additional shipping charges that will be passed on to the retailer. Oversized pipes returned that are not requested will result in a full or partial debit of the claim. 

PARTS/MATERIAL NEEDED

Subaru Warranty Parts Tag part number MSA5W1901A.

  • Miscellaneous common shipping material. (i.e.: Tape/Staples/Packing Paper)
  • A copy of the Repair Order
  • A copy of the related return notice or voucher
  • Miscellaneous common shipping material. (i.e.: Tape/Staples/Packing Paper)
  • Box from received converter shipment or equivalent

SOA will issue a Part Return Request for all catalytic converters requested. It is important the dealer send the catalytic converters to the address listed in the "ship to" box of the part return request by means of the new shipping method. To return Catalytic Converters, use the link for Catalytic Converter Returns found on the Routing Guide in Subarunet. See below for additional details.

A properly completed Subaru Warranty Parts Tag and a legible copy of the repair order including detailed technician comments are mandatory for each part returned. Any part(s) received without all the required information will be subject to a claim debit for the full amount of the claim.

The retailer is required to package the catalytic converter in a shipping box. Any catalytic converters returned "loose" are subject to debit, regardless of the reason. Oversized shipments are subject to full or partial debit of the claim. See below to determine package "Girth" per UPS guidelines. It is for this reason we highly recommend returning the converter in the same packaging in which it was received.

PREPARATION FOR SHIPPING

NOTE: One converter/pipe part number per package. Multiple converters/pipes should never be packaged together unless specifically directed to do so on an individual basis by an FSE or SOA employee. Parts must be unbolted and separated before being packaged, boxed and shipped. Certain part number Subaru Global Platform (SGP) type one-piece pipes will require the retailer to cut them in the designated locations prior to packaging into the supplied return box. See SGP converter packaging section of this document for packaging procedures. Debits may occur if multiple converters are shipped in a single box without proper authorization, or if SGP type pipes are not properly packaged
NOTE: If a shipment is made outside of the UPS girth limits for ground shipments, you may incur a debit to cover the shipping charges that SOA incurs from UPS.

Girth can be determined using the following UPS guidelines:

  • 2xWidth + 2xHeight + Length = Must equal less than 155"
  • Length must be less than 108"
  • Attach a properly completed Subaru Warranty Parts Tag to each converter/pipe.
  • Place the Parts Return Notice and a legible copy of the Repair Order inside the shipping box or in a clear plastic shipping pouch on the outside of the shipping box.
  • Add VIN/Repair Order number to the 2nd reference field on the UPS label.

There is an additional returns category on the Routing Guide Portal - Catalytic Converter Returns.

  • With the release of the Subaru Global Platform (SGP) came the implementation of a large one-piece catalytic converter. This part is too big to ship by UPS, and not cost efficient to ship by freight means. The following procedure will outline the steps that are needed in order to ship these converters back for recycling in the most cost-effective manner.

  • NOTE: This only applies to Naturally Aspirated ICE models.
  • These converters are too large to be returned in the same manner that as the replacement parts are shipped to a retailer. UPS charges in the range of $1,000-$1,500 per occurrence.
  • Upon removal of the old converter from the vehicle. It will be required to shorten the pipe prior to being returned to the Parts Collection Center in Lafayette, IN.
    • NOTE: There are circumstances where SOA or your DSQM will request the converter to be returned in it's original condition. Please accommodate those particular requests for pipes from a specific VIN.
  • There has been an increase in labor time allotted to account for this additional procedure. The technician is being paid for the additional time required for this process. Refer to the Subaru Labor Time Guide for the applicable models/PNs.
  • If this procedure is not followed and the uncut converter/pipe is returned, those charges will be passed on to the retailer in the form of a debit to the claim.
  1. Once the converter is removed, cut the pipe into three equal sections as designated below using ONLY a mechanical cutting device (cut off wheel, Sawzall, hacksaw etc). A plasma cutter or oxyacetylene torch may damage the catalyst material in the event of part investigation.
    1. Please take note of any foreign material that may be stuck or lodged in any part of the pipe. Bag and send this possible foreign material with the shipment.
    2. Once cut, you should be left with these three pieces.
  2. Inside of the box that the new converter came in, there is an additional smaller box for the return shipment of the cut pipe.
    1. If a situation arises where the box is missing, damaged, or otherwise unusable. Please return the converter in box PN# M1017-03 box (dimensions of 35"x24"x10"). This is a normally stocked box number for all RDCs. If you run into an issue and need any of these boxes, please reach out to your facing RDC Operations Coordinator.
  3. Remove the smaller box and assemble/tape as necessary.
  4. Place the cut pipe into the box assuring there is adequate packaging to avoid damage.
    1. If there is any foreign material stuck inside of the pipe or converter, please bag that material and return it with the pipe inside of the box.
  5. Secure the box closed and suitable for shipping.
  6. Return this part with a copy of the repair order and part return notice just like a regular catalytic converter return.

SHIPPING PROCEDURE FOR ALL CONVERTERS

When you have converters to return, click on the link listed under Catalytic Converter Returns. You will be directed to the UPS returns page. Your "Ship From" information will pre-populate into the form along with your Dealer Code. You will be required to select a weight from one of the available options (15, 20, 25, etc.) and also enter the VIN or RO Number. As indicated by the red arrows in the screen shot below, 4 new check boxes have been added for retailers to select the type of tracking notification E-mail(s) they would like to receive. If you are returning multiple units, click the "Add" button and enter the information for the additional units. When the information for all units has been entered, click "Process Shipment".

Shipment label(s) will be displayed for printing. The information entered in the VIN/RO# field will print on the shipping label as Reference No. 2. This will assist in applying the correct labels to the correct cartons.

When all labels have been printed, you will receive the message "Shipment Successful" and have the option of closing your browser or you may click the back button to return to the UPS returns page or the Routing Guide Portal.

Please note, the label is designed to print on 8-1/2" x 11" paper. You have three options for applying labels to the cartons:

  • Tape the label to the carton with clear shipping tape. Please do not apply tape over the bar codes as this might affect the scanning process at UPS.
  • Print the label on self adhesive label stock available from UPS supplies (Item Number 01774501)
  • Place the label in a clear plastic pouch available from UPS supplies (Item Number 171604).

Place the prepared part in the outgoing shipping location ready for the next available UPS driver.

NOTE: Please destroy any of the A.R.S. labels you have on-hand as these will no longer be acceptable for the return of converters.

If you have any questions about this new procedure, please contact: The CLAIMS HELPLINE at 1-866-782-2782.

It is highly recommended that every dealer maintain a record of these shipments including relevant information on the VIN, RO#, Part Number, Return Request Date, Return Shipment Date, and Shipper Tracking Number for each shipment. The easiest way to do this is to make a copy of the shipping label just printed (which included the VIN/RO#) and attach it to a copy of the request form and the original RO in the vehicle's service file. This way all information is consolidated should any future appeal be necessary due to lost shipment or other concern.

SPECIAL INFORMATION REGARDING DEBITED CLAIMS

  • Debits must be appealed in writing through Service Claims Section using proper documentation. (See Section 13.7 of the Subaru Claims Policies and Procedures Manual) Separate UPS tracking numbers for EACH converter pipe must be included. Please include a contact name at the dealership on the appeal form.
  • In cases where the converter pipe is still at the dealership at the time the appeal is filed, do not return the pipe unless you are contacted by SOA with return instruction. If notified by SOA that an adjustment or debit will be reversed based on the return of a converter pipe, the dealer will have 10 calendar days to ship the pipe to the PCC. Failure to return the converter pipe within 10 calendar days may result in denial of the appeal.

CHARGES FOR IMPROPER SHIPPING

Improper use of correct shipping methods will result in the claim being partially debited for those cost as outlined below.

Unpackaged/"Loose" Converter Shipment Debit of total claim amount
Damaged Converter due to insufficient packaging Debit of total claim amount
Part not returned within prescribed time limits Debit of total claim amount
Part(s) received without all the required information/documentation Debit of total claim amount
Return of incorrect PN converters not listed or requested on the claim Debit of total claim amount
Use of old ARS label on any shipment Partial Debit for $40.00 per occurrence
Use of any SOA 3rd party billing accounts (UPS) to return catalytic converters (this includes any SOA UPS account other than the Subaru Converter Return account) Partial Debit for $40.00 per occurrence
Return of oversized converter PNs not requested by SOA Partial Debit of total shipping costs
Use of shipping company other than UPS to ship converters and billed to SOA Partial Debit of total shipping costs
Multiple converters PNs shipped in one box Partial Debit of total shipping costs
