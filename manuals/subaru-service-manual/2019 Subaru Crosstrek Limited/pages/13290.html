Inhibitor Switch: Inspection

When the driving condition or starter motor operation is improper, first check the shift linkage for improper operation. If the shift linkage is functioning properly, check the inhibitor switch.

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>SAFETY .
  2. Remove the clip from the air intake boot, and loosen the clamp.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the air intake boot, and place it aside so that it does not interfere with the work.
    NOTE: Be careful not to pull out the PCV hose.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the transmission radio ground terminal.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Disconnect the transmission harness connector.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Remove the transmission case cover (small).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Move the lock lever in the arrow direction while pressing the lock button, and disconnect the connector.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Set the ST between the TCM and transmission harness. Refer to TRANSMISSION (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .

    Preparation tool: 

    ST: CHECK BOARD (18460AA040)

  9. Check for continuity in inhibitor switch circuit by shifting the select lever in "P", "R", "N" and "D" respectively.
    NOTE:
    • Check that there is no continuity in the starter circuit when the select lever is in the "R" and "D" ranges.
    • When inhibitor switch is normal, check there is no poor contact in vehicle side connector and no open circuit in harness.
      Range Terminal No. Standard
    Signal sent to TCM P 38 - 42 Less than 1 Ω
    R 37 - 42
    N 36 - 42
    D 35 - 42
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
      Range Terminal No. Standard
    Starter signal P/N 1 - 5 Less than 1 Ω
    Back-up light circuit R 22 - 26
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  10. Check that there is continuity at equal points when the select lever is moved 1.5° in both directions from the "N" range.

    If there is continuity in only one direction or in other points, adjust the inhibitor switch. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>INHIBITOR SWITCH>ADJUSTMENT .

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  11. Repeat the above inspection in other gear ranges. If there is fault, adjust the inhibitor switch and select cable. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>INHIBITOR SWITCH>ADJUSTMENT . Refer to CONTROL SYSTEMS>SELECT CABLE>ADJUSTMENT .
