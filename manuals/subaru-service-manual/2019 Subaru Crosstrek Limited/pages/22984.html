Oil Pan Upper

  1. Remove the engine assembly.

    Except for hybrid model: 

    Refer to MECHANICAL (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL>EXCEPT FOR HYBRID MODEL .

    Hybrid model: 

    Refer to MECHANICAL (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL>HYBRID MODEL .

  2. Remove the water pump.

    Except for hybrid model: 

    Refer to COOLING (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>WATER PUMP>REMOVAL>WATER PUMP .

    Hybrid model: 

    Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>WATER PUMP>REMOVAL>WATER PUMP .

  3. Remove the thermostat cover.

    Except for hybrid model: 

    Refer to COOLING (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>THERMOSTAT>REMOVAL>ENGINE SIDE .

    Hybrid model: 

    Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>THERMOSTAT>REMOVAL .

  4. Remove the water pipe RH. (Except for hybrid model) Refer to COOLING (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>WATER PIPE>REMOVAL>WATER PIPE RH .
  5. Remove the water hose. (Hybrid model)
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Remove the water pipe LH.

    Except for hybrid model: 

    Refer to COOLING (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>WATER PIPE>REMOVAL>WATER PIPE LH .

    Hybrid model: 

    Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>WATER PIPE>REMOVAL>WATER PIPE LH .

  7. Remove the chain cover. Refer to MECHANICAL (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>CHAIN COVER>REMOVAL .
  8. Remove the oil level switch. Refer to LUBRICATION (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>OIL LEVEL SWITCH>REMOVAL .
  9. Remove the oil pan. Refer to LUBRICATION (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>OIL PAN>REMOVAL>OIL PAN .
  10. Remove the oil strainer.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  11. Remove the oil pan upper.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  12. Remove the O-ring from the cylinder block.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  13. Remove the liquid gasket from oil pan upper and cylinder block.
