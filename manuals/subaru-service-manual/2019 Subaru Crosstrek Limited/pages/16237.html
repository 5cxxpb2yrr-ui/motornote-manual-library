Rear Oxygen Sensor: Installation

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
CAUTION: If lubricant is split over the exhaust pipe, wipe it off with cloth to avoid emission of smoke or causing a fire.
  1. Before installing rear oxygen sensor, apply the anti-seize compound only to the threaded portion of rear oxygen sensor to make the next removal easier.
    CAUTION: Never apply anti-seize compound to the protector of rear oxygen sensor.

    Anti-seize compound: 

    NEVER-SEEZ NSN, JET LUBE SS-30 or equivalent

  2. Install the rear oxygen sensor and connect the connector.
    CAUTION: Do not cross the rear oxygen sensor harness with the ground cord during installation.

    Tightening torque: 

    21 N.m (2.1 kgf-m, 15.5 ft-lb)

  3. Install the front under cover. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>FRONT UNDER COVER>INSTALLATION .
  4. Lower the vehicle.
  5. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
