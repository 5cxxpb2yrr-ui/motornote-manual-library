Related Lines, More Than 52 Ω And Less Than 62 Ω (MAIN-CAN_Stereo Camera)

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Wiring diagram: 

CAN communication system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>CAN COMMUNICATION SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

  1. CHECK BETWEEN RELATED LINES AND MAIN WIRING HARNESS. 
    1. Disconnect the stereo camera connector.
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between terminals in stereo camera connector.

      Connector & terminal 

      (R137) No. 1 - (R137) No. 2:

    Is the resistance 400 Ω or more?

    Yes:  A related line between stereo camera and main wiring harness is open, or main wiring harness is open at two places or more.

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK RELATED LINES. 
    1. Disconnect the steering sensor connector.
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between stereo camera connector and steering sensor connector.

      Connector & terminal 

      (R137) No. 1 - (i74) No. 3:

      (R137) No. 2 - (i74) No. 2:

    Is the resistance 10 Ω or more?

    Yes:  Repair or replace the open circuit of stereo camera related lines.

    No:  Check DTC of stereo camera. Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .
