DTC B2255: USB2 (DCM) Faults

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

DTC detecting condition: 

Detected when there is an open circuit in USB2 (DCM).

Trouble symptom: 

Communication between audio or navigation unit and DCM is impossible.

Wiring diagram: 

  • Navigation system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (IMPREZA MODEL)>NAVIGATION SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM . (Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>NAVIGATION SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>NAVIGATION SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .
  • Audio system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (IMPREZA MODEL)>AUDIO SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>AUDIO SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>AUDIO SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to INFOTAINMENT (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK DTC. 
    1. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, perform the clear memory of [Infotainment]. Refer to COMMON (DIAG)>CLEAR MEMORY .
    2. Turn the ignition switch OFF → ON.
    3. Read the DTC of [Infotainment] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to INFOTAINMENT (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is DTC B2255 displayed? (Current malfunction)

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Even if DTC is displayed, the circuit has returned to a normal condition at this time. Reproduce the failure, and then perform the diagnosis again.

    NOTE: In this case, temporary poor contact of connector, temporary open or short circuit of harness may be the cause.
  2. CHECK HARNESS (OPEN CIRCUIT). 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the audio connector or navigation unit connector.
    3. Disconnect the DCM connector.
    4. Using a tester, measure the resistance between audio connector or navigation unit connector and DCM connector.

      Connector & terminal 

      (AD63) No. 1 - (AD59) No. 2:

      (AD63) No. 2 - (AD59) No. 3:

      (AD63) No. 3 - (AD59) No. 1:

      (AD63) No. 4 - (AD59) No. 4:

      (AD63) Shield connector - (AD59) Shield connector:

    Is the resistance 1 Ω or less?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Repair or replace the open circuit of harness.

  3. CHECK HARNESS (GROUND SHORT CIRCUIT). 

    Using the tester, measure the resistance between audio connector or navigation unit connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (AD63) No. 1 - Chassis ground:

    (AD63) No. 2 - Chassis ground:

    (AD63) No. 3 - Chassis ground:

    (AD63) No. 4 - Chassis ground:

    (AD63) Shield connector - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance 1 MΩ or more?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Repair or replace the short circuit of the harness.

  4. CHECK HARNESS (SHORT CIRCUIT TO POWER SUPPLY). 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    2. Using the tester, measure the voltage between audio connector or navigation unit connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (AD63) No. 1 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

      (AD63) No. 2 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

      (AD63) No. 3 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

      (AD63) No. 4 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

      (AD63) Shield connector (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    Is the voltage less than 1 V?

    Yes:  Go to  5.

    No:  Repair or replace the short circuit of the harness.

  5. CHECK DTC. 
    1. Connect the DCM. Refer to ENTERTAINMENT & MONITORING>DATA COMMUNICATION MODULE .
    2. Read the DTC of [Infotainment] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to INFOTAINMENT (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is DTC B2255 displayed? (Current malfunction)

    Yes:  Replace the audio or navigation unit. Refer to ENTERTAINMENT & MONITORING>AUDIO . Refer to ENTERTAINMENT & MONITORING>NAVIGATION BODY .

    No:  Malfunction occurred in DCM.
