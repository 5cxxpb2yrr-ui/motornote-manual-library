Seat Belt Switch Inspection

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

Preparation tool:

Circuit tester

  • Driver's seat Measure the resistance between the terminals when the seat belt inner - front is in each of the following operation conditions.
Terminal No. Inspection conditions Standard Circuit
2 - 1 Tongue plate inserted 1 MΩ or more
Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
2 - 1 Tongue plate detached 1 Ω or less

Replace the seat belt inner - front if the inspection result is not within the standard.

  • Passenger's seat Measure the current value between the terminals when the seat belt inner - front is in each of the following operation conditions.
Terminal No. Inspection conditions Standard Circuit
1 - 2 Tongue plate inserted 12 - 18 mA
Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
1 - 2 Tongue plate detached 4 - 7 mA

Replace the seat belt inner - front if the inspection result is not within the standard.
