DTC C1C20: Supply Voltage Low: Notes

1. - 

DTC detecting condition: 

  • When the brake system power supply circuit voltage stays either high or low.
  • When voltage at the solenoid valve power supply circuit and IG1/IG2 circuit stays low.

Trouble symptom: 

  • Brake cannot be controlled (except detail codes 552-554).
  • Regenerative brake cannot be controlled.
  • Brake assist does not operate.
  • EyeSight functions (ACC/PCS) do not operate.

  1. CHECK DTC OF KEYLESS ACCESS CM. 

    Read the DTC of [Keyless Access with Push Button Start] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Ref. to KEYLESS ACCESS WITH PUSH BUTTON START (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Are any DTCs displayed?

    Yes:  Perform the diagnosis according to DTC. Ref. to KEYLESS ACCESS WITH PUSH BUTTON START (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>LIST .

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK DTC OF HYBRID VEHICLE CONTROL ECM. 

    Read the DTC of [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Ref. to PHEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Are any DTCs displayed?

    Yes:  Perform the diagnosis according to DTC. Ref. to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>LIST .

    No:  Go to  3.

  3. CHECK DTC OF EBB Control Module. 

    Read the DTC of [Electric Brake Booster] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Ref. to ELECTRIC BRAKE BOOSTER (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is only DTC C1C20, C1C2E, C1C30 or C1C31 displayed?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Perform the diagnosis according to DTC. Ref. to ELECTRIC BRAKE BOOSTER (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>LIST .

  4. CHECK BATTERY. 

    Check the battery voltage.

    Is the voltage 11 - 14 V when the IG switch is ON?

    When the vehicle's state is READY ON, is the voltage in 11 -15.5 V range?

    Yes:  Go to  5.

    No:  Charge or replace the battery.

  5. CHECK CONNECTOR. 

    Check the connecting condition of the intermediate connectors (AD81, B652).

    Is there looseness, deformation, or corrosion at the connectors?

    Yes:  Repair or replace the connector.

    No:  Go to  6.

  6. CHECK HARNESS. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the coupling connector. (AD81, B652)
    3. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    4. Measure the voltage between intermediate connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (B652) No. 11 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    (B652) No. 23 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    (B652) No. 27 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    (B652) No. 28 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    (B652) No. 30 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    Is the voltage 11 - 14 V?

    Yes:  Go to  7.

    No:  Repair or replace the harness.

  7. CHECK HARNESS. 

    Measure the resistance between intermediate connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (B652) No. 26 - Chassis ground:

    (B652) No. 29 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  8.

    No:  Repair or replace the harness.

  8. RECHECK DTC OF EBB Control Module. 
    1. Connect the coupling connector.
    2. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, perform the clear memory. Ref. to COMMON (DIAG)>CLEAR MEMORY .
    3. Read the DTC of [Electric Brake Booster] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Ref. to ELECTRIC BRAKE BOOSTER (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Are DTCs C1C20, C1C2E, C1C30 and C1C31 displayed?

    Yes:  Replace the brake booster assembly (with the master cylinder). Ref. to BRAKE>BRAKE BOOSTER .

    No:  Currently, it is normal. There may have been a temporary poor contact in the harness and connector or a temporary noise interference.
