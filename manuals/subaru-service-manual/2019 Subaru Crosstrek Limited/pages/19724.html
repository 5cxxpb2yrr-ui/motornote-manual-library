Overview Of Error

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly calculates the accelerator pedal opening angle, using the voltage signals from the main sensor (VPA) and sub sensor (VPA2). Detect the malfunction of output voltage properties of the main sensor (VPA) and sub sensor (VPA2).

The following are possible causes of the error.

  • Malfunction of Accelerator pedal sensor
  • Malfunction of Wire harness or connector
  • Malfunction of Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly
