Clutch Disc And Cover: Removal

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Remove the manual transmission assembly.
    • 5MT model:

      Refer to MANUAL TRANSMISSION AND DIFFERENTIAL (5MT)>MANUAL TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL .

    • 6MT model:

      Refer to MANUAL TRANSMISSION AND DIFFERENTIAL (6MT)>MANUAL TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL .

  2. Install the ST to the flywheel.

    Special tool: 

    ST: 499747100 CLUTCH DISC GUIDE

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the clutch cover and clutch disc.
    CAUTION:
    • Take care not to allow oil to adhere to the clutch disc face.
    • Do not disassemble the clutch cover or clutch disc.
  4. Remove the release bearing & lever. Refer to CLUTCH SYSTEM>RELEASE BEARING AND LEVER>REMOVAL .
