Monitor Description

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

The Battery ECU assembly estimates the thermal load of the high voltage fuse. If the accumulated thermal load exceeds the threshold, the Battery ECU assembly illuminates the MIL and set a DTC.
