Inspecting The Spray Position

  1. Turn the wiper switch to OFF position.
  2. While the vehicle is stopped, check that the washer spray position is as shown in the figure.
  3. After inspection, if the spray position is significantly out of the specified range, replace the nozzle - rear washer with a new part.
