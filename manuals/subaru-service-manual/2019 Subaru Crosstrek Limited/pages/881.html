Check Operation

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

Check if the lever assembly and lock assembly operate normally.

  • If deformation or unsmooth operation of the lever is observed, replace the lever assembly - safety.
  • If deformation, abnormal wear, or unsmooth lock operation of the latch is observed, replace the lock assembly - front hood.
