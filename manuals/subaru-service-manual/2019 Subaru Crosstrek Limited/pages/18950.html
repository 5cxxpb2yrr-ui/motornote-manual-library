DTC P309F-49: High Voltage Charging Circuit Consumption Circuit Short: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

1. - 

Outline of diagnosis: 

Fault of high voltage circuit between HV battery and charger.

Load short circuit occurred during precharging (between CHRP ON to CHRG ON).

Battery current excessive during precharging (Current equivalent to short circuit flew)

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

Plug-in charging suspended

Wiring diagram: 

Hybrid system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

    No:  -

  2. CHECK HV battery charger cable  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Check that the Service plug is disconnected. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .
    2. Disconnect Electric vehicle charger assembly connector (HV33).
    3. Using a tester, measure the resistance between Electric vehicle charger assembly connector terminals.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV33) No. 1 - (HV33) No. 2:

    Is the resistance 10 kΩ or more?

    Yes:  Replace Electric vehicle charger assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>ELECTRIC VEHICLE CHARGER .

    No:  Go to  3.

  3. CHECK HV battery charger cable  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Disconnect HV battery junction block connectors (HV45) and (HV48).
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between Electric vehicle charger assembly connector terminals.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV33) No. 1 - (HV33) No. 2:

    Is the resistance 10 kΩ or more?

    Yes:  Replace HV battery junction block. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>RELAY AND FUSE .

    No:  Repair or replace Wire harness between Electric vehicle charger assembly connector and HV battery junction block connector.
