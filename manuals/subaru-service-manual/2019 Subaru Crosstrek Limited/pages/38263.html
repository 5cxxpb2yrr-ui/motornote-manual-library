INTRODUCTION

This bulletin announces a design change made to the outer hinge cover on the driver's 10-way adjustable power seats. The change has been made to address several reports about the cover coming loose over time as a result of getting into and out of the driver's seat. The retaining claw portion securing the cover to the seat frame has been changed to improve its strength and durability.
