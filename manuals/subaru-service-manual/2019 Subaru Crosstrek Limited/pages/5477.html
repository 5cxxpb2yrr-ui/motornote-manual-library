Brake Pipe: Installation: Notes

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

CAUTION:
  • Before handling the airbag system components, always refer to "CAUTION" of "General Description" in "AIRBAG SYSTEM". Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  • When installing the brake pipe, do not bend.
  • After installing the brake pipe and brake hose, perform air bleed.
  • After installing the brake hoses, make sure that they do not contact the tires or suspension assembly, etc.
