Explanation Of Circuits

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

The Charge control ECU installed inside the Electric vehicle charger assembly monitors internal operation, and outputs a DTC when an error is detected. If this DTC was detected, replace the Electric vehicle charger assembly.
