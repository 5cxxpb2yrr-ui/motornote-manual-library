Door Trim: Installation

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Before installation, check the following items.
    • Cable is free from deformation such as fray.
    • Grease is applied sufficiently to cable joints.

      If grease is insufficient, add it as necessary before assembling the cable.

    • Make sure that the sealing cover is securely installed. Refer to EXTERIOR BODY PANELS>SEALING COVER>INSTALLATION .
  2. Install the removed parts to the trim panel - door.
  3. Install the trim panel - door.
    1. Attach the harness connector.
    2. Install the cable to the remote assembly - door.
      NOTE: Attach the door lock cable (a) to the upper side, and attach the door open cable (b) to the lower side.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Insert the upper portion of the trim panel - door into the window shoulder portion of the panel assembly - door.
      NOTE: For an easier procedure, attach the front side first.
    4. Check if the screw position is correct, and slowly push in the trim (A) portion in order to securely install the clip (B) to the panel.
      NOTE: When insertion of the clip is not possible, check the installation status of the clip and clip hole of the trim panel - door.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    5. Attach the remaining clips and tighten the screws.
  4. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
