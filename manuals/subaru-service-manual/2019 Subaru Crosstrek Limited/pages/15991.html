Extension Case Oil Seal: Replacement

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Release the parking brake.
  2. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery.

    Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .

  3. Release the shift lock using a screwdriver, etc. and shift the select lever to the "N" range.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the propeller shaft.

    Refer to DRIVE SHAFT SYSTEM>PROPELLER SHAFT>REMOVAL .

  5. Remove the front exhaust pipe.

    Refer to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FRONT EXHAUST PIPE>REMOVAL .

  6. Using the ST, remove the extension case oil seal.

    Preparation tool: 

    ST: PULLER ASSY (398527700)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Using the ST, install a new extension case oil seal.
    NOTE: Apply ATF to the oil seal lip and peripheral surface.

    Preparation tool: 

    ST: OIL SEAL INSTALLER (498057300)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Install the front exhaust pipe.

    Refer to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FRONT EXHAUST PIPE>INSTALLATION .

  9. Install the propeller shaft.

    Refer to DRIVE SHAFT SYSTEM>PROPELLER SHAFT>INSTALLATION .

  10. Shift the select lever to "P range".
  11. Connect the battery ground terminal.

    Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .

  12. Apply the parking brake.
  13. Check the ATF level.

    Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TH2A)>AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION FLUID>INSPECTION .
