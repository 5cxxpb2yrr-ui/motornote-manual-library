Rear Gate: Adjustment

Adjust the clearance around the panel - rear gate as follows.

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
Part Standard
A Panel - rear gate to Roof panel 6±1 mm (0.24±0.04in)
B Surface level gap: Panel - rear gate to Roof panel 1±1 mm (0.04±0.04 in)
C Panel - rear gate to Panel - side outer 4.6±1 mm (0.18±0.04in)
D Surface level gap: Panel - rear gate to Panel - side outer 2.2±1 mm (0.09±0.04in)
E Panel - rear gate to Light assembly - rear combination 5±2 mm (0.2 ± 0.08 in)
F Surface level gap: Panel - rear gate to Light assembly - rear combination 3.5±1.6 mm (0.14±0.06 in)
G Panel - rear gate to Bumper face - rear 8±1.8 mm (0.31±0.07in)
  1. Adjust the vertical and horizontal positions of the panel - rear gate.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    1. Loosen the mounting bolts of the hinge - rear gate.
    2. Adjust the vertical and horizontal clearance of the panel - rear gate.

      Tightening torque: 

      25 N.m (2.55 kgf-m, 18.4 ft-lb)

  2. Adjust the surface level gap between the panel - rear gate and the body.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    1. Remove the trim panel - rear skirt.
    2. Loosen the bolt.
    3. Tap the striker - rear gate using a plastic hammer to adjust the surface level gap between the panel - rear gate and the body.

      Tightening torque: 

      25 N.m (2.5 kgf-m, 18.4 ft-lb)
