4 Door Model

  • BUMPER FACE 
    1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    2. Remove the trunk room mat.
    3. Remove the trim panel - trunk rear.
      CAUTION: Pull up the trim panel - trunk rear vertically. If it is pulled up obliquely, the claws may be damaged.
      1. Remove the clip.
      2. Release the claws, and remove the trim panel - trunk rear.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    4. Remove the clips, and turn over the trim panel - trunk side.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    5. Remove the rear combination light assembly. Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>REAR COMBINATION LIGHT ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL>4 DOOR MODEL .
    6. Remove the bumper face - rear.
      CAUTION:
      • Pulling with excessive force may damage the bracket or bumper face.
      • For models with Reverse Automatic Braking, the connector of the cord assembly - sonar is secured on the bumper beam COMPL. Pulling the bumper face with excessive force may break the harness.
      1. Remove the clips, and remove the bumper face - rear.
        Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
      2. Detach (b) and (c) while pulling up (a) of the bumper face - rear.
      3. Detach (e) while pulling up (d) of the bumper face - rear.
      4. Perform the same procedures when detaching (f) to (h) on the opposite side.
      5. While pulling up the center part of the bumper face - rear, carefully pull it toward you to detach (i) to (k) in this order.
      6. For models with Reverse Automatic Braking, disconnect the connector (gray arrow section) located on the bumper beam COMPL.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  • BUMPER BEAM 
    1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    2. Remove the bumper face - rear.
    3. Remove the bolts and nuts, and remove the bumper beam COMPL - rear.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  • BRACKET 
    1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    2. Remove the bumper face - rear.
    3. Remove bumper brackets.
      1. Remove the bracket - rear bumper corner.
        1. Remove the screws.
        2. Release the claws, and then remove the bracket - rear bumper corner.
      2. Remove the bracket - rear bumper side.
        1. Remove the screws.
        2. Turn over the trim panel - trunk side, and remove by detaching the claws from the inside of the trunk.
        Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
      3. Remove the clips, and remove the cover - side LWR.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  • SONAR SENSOR & HARNESS 
    1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    2. Remove the bumper face - rear.
    3. If removing the following parts, refer to "Sensor & Harness" of the "Reverse Automatic Braking" service information. Refer to REVERSE AUTOMATIC BRAKING>SENSOR AND HARNESS>REMOVAL .
      • Cord ASSY - sonar
      • Sonar - sensor
