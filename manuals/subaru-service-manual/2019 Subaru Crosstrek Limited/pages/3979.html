Trunk Lid Latch and Actuator Assembly: Removal

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the trim panel - trunk lid. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>TRUNK ROOM TRIM>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the lock assembly - trunk lid.
    1. Release the claws, and open the cover of the lock assembly - trunk lid.
    2. Remove the cable assembly - trunk lid from the lock assembly - trunk lid.

    3. Disconnect the connector.
    4. Remove the bolts, then remove the lock assembly - trunk lid.
