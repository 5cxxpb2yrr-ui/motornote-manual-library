Tire and Wheel: Removal

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Remove the hubcap. (Model with full hubcap)

    Refer to WHEEL AND TIRE SYSTEM>FULL WHEEL CAP>REMOVAL .

  2. Lift up the vehicle.
  3. Remove the wheel nut.
  4. Remove the wheels.
    CAUTION: When removing the wheels, be careful not to damage the bolt - hub.
