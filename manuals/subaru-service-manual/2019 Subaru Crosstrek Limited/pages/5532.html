Drum In Disc Type Model

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Set the wheel stoppers to tires.
  2. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY>GROUND TERMINAL REMOVAL OR INSTALLATION .
    NOTE:
    • For CVT models, release the shift lock and shift the select lever to the "N" position. Refer to CONTROL SYSTEMS>SELECT LEVER>REMOVAL .
    • On power seat models, move the front seat all the way forward before disconnecting the battery ground terminal.
  3. Remove the driver's seat. Refer to SEATS>FRONT SEAT>REMOVAL .
  4. Remove the console box assembly. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>CONSOLE BOX>REMOVAL .
  5. Remove the switch assembly - hand brake.
