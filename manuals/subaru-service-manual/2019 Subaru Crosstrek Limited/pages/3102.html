Registration (Comm Check)

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

CAUTION: Do not press and hold the i-button for more than two seconds during communication check, or settings will return to factory mode in poor radio wave environment. If the settings has returned to factory mode, perform the communication check again. To check the mode status, see "Subscription Status" in the current data display on Subaru Select Monitor.
  1. Replace the data communication module with a new part.
  2. Check that the LED of overhead console or stereo camera cover assembly illuminates in green.
  3. Register the immobilizer. For detailed operation procedure, refer to "Type B" or "Type D" described in "REGISTRATION MANUAL FOR IMMOBILIZER".
  4. Press and hold the i-button for 2 seconds or more.
    NOTE: When the subscription is completed, the telematics system will automatically activate.
