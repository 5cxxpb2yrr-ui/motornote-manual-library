INTRODUCTION

This bulletin announces a design change made to the Slide Rail components of the left and right front power seat frame/track assemblies used on the models listed above. This bulletin does NOT apply to models equipped with manually operated seats. The change was made in response to customer concerns of the front seat(s) feeling "loose" or not fully tightened.

NOTE: This concern is not a safety risk. The seating system continues to comply with all federal safety standards. Additionally, in the unlikely event the retainer portion were to come loose, the design of the seat track does not allow the seat tracks and slide rails to separate. This change is being made solely to improve the customer experience.
CAUTION: VEHICLE SERVICING PERFORMED BY UNTRAINED PERSONS COULD RESULT IN SERIOUS INJURY TO THOSE PERSONS OR TO OTHERS.
