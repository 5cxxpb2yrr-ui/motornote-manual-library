Automatic Transmission Fluid: Inspection

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
CAUTION:
  • When checking the ATF level, check at the specified ATF temperature.
  • Do not add ATF when cold since the oil may spray out.
NOTE: Unless the conditions are severe, it is not necessary to change the ATF.
  1. Prepare the ATF for refilling.
  2. Connect the Subaru Select Monitor.
    NOTE: For detailed operation procedures, refer to "Help" of application.
  3. Navigate to Inspection Mode for measuring Exhaust Gas.

    Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>Work Support>OPERATION>SERVICE MODE .

    NOTE: If it is necessary to run the engine for 10 minutes or more, fasten the driver seat seat belt.
  4. Display [ATF Temp.] and raise it to 35 - 45°C (95 - 113°F).
    CAUTION: Make sure to perform the ATF level inspection within the [ATF Temp.] range of 35 - 45°C (95 - 113°F).
  5. Circulate the ATF while running the engine at idle.
  6. With the engine idling, lift up the vehicle and remove the overflow plug (A).
    NOTE:
    • Prepare the container for draining of engine oil.
    • Oil may spray out from the overflow plug.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. If the ATF level is below the bottom of the overflow plug hole, check for oil leakage, and add the specified ATF.
    CAUTION: Always use specified ATF. ATF other than specified ATF will cause malfunction.

    ATF: 

    Refer to RECOMMENDED MATERIALS>FLUID .

  8. Using a new gasket, install the overflow plug.

    Tightening torque: 

    50 N.m (5.1 kgf-m, 36.9 ft-lb)
