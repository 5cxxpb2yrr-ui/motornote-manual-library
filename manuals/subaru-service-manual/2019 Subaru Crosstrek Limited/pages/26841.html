Explanation For Circuits

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Power supply to the generator or motor is shut down by the shutdown signal from Motor generator control ECU to the inverter.

At READY OFF, GSDN (generator shutdown signal) and MSDN (motor shutdown signal) are sent to the inverter from Motor generator control ECU, and the shutdown operation function is checked. DTC is detected for malfunctions of the shutdown operation function.
