Diagnostic Method

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Detect a malfunction by measuring the rear oxygen sensor response time during fuel cut.

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.

Judge as NG when the following conditions are established.

JUDGMENT VALUE SPECIFICATION

Malfunction Criteria Threshold Value
Time needed for rear oxygen sensor voltage to change from 0.5V to 0.2V More than 1000 ms

Time needed for diagnosis:  Less than 1 s

Malfunction indicator light illumination:  Illuminates when malfunction occurs in 2 continuous driving cycles.
