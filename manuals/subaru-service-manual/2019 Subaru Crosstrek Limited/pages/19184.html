Heater Duct: Removal: Front

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Disconnect the battery ground terminal and wait for at least 60 seconds before starting work. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    NOTE:
    • Shift the select lever into "N" before disconnecting the battery ground terminal.
    • For power seat models, remove the console box before removing the battery ground terminal.
  2. Remove the cover assembly - front and the console box assembly. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>CONSOLE BOX>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the cover assembly - instrument panel LWR driver INN. (When removing LH side) Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>INSTRUMENT PANEL LOWER COVER>REMOVAL .
  4. Remove the pocket assembly and the back panel - pocket. (When removing RH side) Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>GLOVE BOX>REMOVAL .
  5. Remove the duct - rear heater front.
    1. Remove the slide lock.
    2. Release the claws, and remove the duct - rear heater front.
