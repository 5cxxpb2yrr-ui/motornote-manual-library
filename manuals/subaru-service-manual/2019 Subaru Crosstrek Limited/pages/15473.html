Door Switch: Inspection

  1. Measure the resistance between connector terminals.

    Preparation tool: 

    Circuit tester

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Terminal No. Inspection conditions Standard
    1 - 2 When the switch is not pressed Less than 1 Ω
    When the switch is pressed 1 MΩ or more
  2. Replace the switch assembly - door if the inspection result is not within the standard value.
