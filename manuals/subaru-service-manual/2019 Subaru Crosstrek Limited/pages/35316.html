VDC OFF Switch: Removal

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery and wait for at least 60 seconds before starting work. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY>GROUND TERMINAL REMOVAL OR INSTALLATION .
  2. Remove the cover assembly - instrument panel LWR driver OUT. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>INSTRUMENT PANEL LOWER COVER>REMOVAL .
  3. Release the claws and remove the VDC OFF switch (e).
