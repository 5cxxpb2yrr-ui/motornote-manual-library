Except For Hybrid Model

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Lift up the vehicle, and then remove the rear wheels.
  2. Remove the pad - disc brake rear.
    1. Remove the brake hose bracket.
    2. Remove the caliper bolt on the lower side.
    3. Raise the caliper body assembly and remove the pad - disc brake rear.
      NOTE: Do not disconnect the brake hose from the caliper body assembly.
