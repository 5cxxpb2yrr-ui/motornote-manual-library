Inspection Without Using Can Diagnostic

Trouble causes: 

  • Electrical system malfunction
  • Faulty harness and harness connector
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK BATTERY. 

    Check the battery. Refer to STARTING/CHARGING SYSTEMS (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>BATTERY>INSPECTION .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Charge or replace the battery.

  2. CHECK FUSE. 

    Check the fuse which might affect the problem.

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Replace the fuse. If the replaced fuse blows out easily, some type of fault is present. Therefore, repair the faulty portion.

  3. CHECK HARNESS. 

    Check the harness and harness connector connection which might affect the problem.

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to step  3 in the Basic Diagnostic Procedure. Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .

    No:  Repair or replace the faulty harness or harness connector.
