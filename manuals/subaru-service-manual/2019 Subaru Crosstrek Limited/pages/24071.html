Diagnostic Procedure for Subaru Select Monitor Communication: Inspection

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

Trouble symptom: 

Cannot communicate with pedestrian alert control module.

Detecting condition: 

  • Defective harness connector
  • Power supply circuit malfunction
  • Defective pedestrian alert control module
  • Defective CAN communication circuit
  • Defective Subaru Select Monitor

Wiring diagram: 

Pedestrian alert system Ref. to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>PEDESTRIAN ALERT SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Ref. to PEDESTRIAN ALERT (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK FUSE. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Check the fuse.

      Is the check result OK?

      Yes:  Go to  2.

      No:  Replace the fuse. If the replaced fuse blows out easily, repair the short circuit to ground in harness between the fuse - pedestrian alert control module connector.

  2. CHECK HARNESS (OPEN CIRCUIT). 
    1. Disconnect the pedestrian alert control module connector.
    2. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    3. Using the tester, measure the voltage between pedestrian alert control module connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (B641) No. 1 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

      Is the voltage 10 V or more?

      Yes:  Go to  3.

      No:  Repair the open circuit in the harness between fuse and pedestrian alert control module connector.

  3. CHECK HARNESS (OPEN CIRCUIT). 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Using the tester, measure the resistance between pedestrian alert control module connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (B641) No. 6 - Chassis ground:

      Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

      Yes:  Go to  4.

      No:  Repair the open circuit in harness between pedestrian alert control module connector and chassis ground.

  4. CHECK LAN SYSTEM. 

    Inspect LAN system. Ref. to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  5.

    No:  Perform the inspection according to the diagnosis for LAN system.

  5. CHECK SUBARU SELECT MONITOR. 

    Connect the Subaru Select Monitor to another vehicle.

    Is the communication possible with the vehicle?

    Yes:  Go to  6.

    No:  Replace or repair the Subaru Select Monitor.

  6. CHECK PEDESTRIAN ALERT control module. 

    Communicate with [Pedestrian Alert] using the select monitor.

    Is communication possible?

    Yes:  Even if DTC is displayed, the circuit has returned to a normal condition at this time.

    Reproduce the failure, and then perform the diagnosis again.

    NOTE: In this case, temporary poor contact of connector, temporary open or short circuit of harness may be the cause.

    No:  Replace the pedestrian alert control module. Ref. to PEDESTRIAN ALERT SYSTEM>PEDESTRIAN ALERT CONTROL UNIT .
