Related Lines, More Than 52 Ω And Less Than 62 Ω (BODY-CAN_Infotainment System)

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Wiring diagram: 

CAN communication system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>CAN COMMUNICATION SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  1. CHECK BETWEEN RELATED LINES AND MAIN WIRING HARNESS. 
    1. Disconnect the audio or navigation unit connector.
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between audio or navigation unit connector terminals.

      Connector & terminal 

      (i87) No. 9 - (i87) No. 10:

    Is the resistance 400 Ω or more?

    Yes:  Related lines between audio or navigation unit connector and main wiring harness is open, or main wiring harness is open at two places or more.

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK RELATED LINES. 
    1. Disconnect the central gateway control module.
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between audio or navigation unit connector and central gateway control module.

      Connector & terminal 

      (i87) No. 9 - (i366) No. 15:

      (i87) No. 10 - (i366) No. 16:

    Is the resistance 10 Ω or more?

    Yes:  Repair or replace the open circuit of infotainment system related lines.

    No:  Check DTC of infotainment system. Refer to INFOTAINMENT (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .
