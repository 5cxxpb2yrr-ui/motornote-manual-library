Collector Cover: Removal

NOTE: The collector cover is equipped only on hybrid models.
  1. Remove the collector cover in the order shown in the figure.
    CAUTION: The operation must be carefully performed; otherwise the collector cover may be damaged.
    NOTE: Check the cushion (a) and cushion (b) for peel-off or disengagement.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    1. Pull up the collector cover at portion (A) and pull out the protrusion at the collector cover backside from the cushion (B).
    2. Slide the collector cover towards the front of the vehicle to remove the collector cover from the collector cover bracket (C).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Remove the collector cover bracket.
    NOTE: Perform this procedure only when required.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
