Transmission Gear Oil

Check the transmission gear oil amount. If the amount of gear oil is insufficient, check for leaks. Then, add the necessary amount of the specified gear oil.

  • 5MT model

    Refer to "5MT" instructions for transmission gear oil inspection. Refer to MANUAL TRANSMISSION AND DIFFERENTIAL (5MT)>TRANSMISSION GEAR OIL>INSPECTION .

  • 6MT model

Refer to "6MT" instructions for transmission gear oil inspection. Refer to MANUAL TRANSMISSION AND DIFFERENTIAL (6MT)>TRANSMISSION GEAR OIL>INSPECTION .
