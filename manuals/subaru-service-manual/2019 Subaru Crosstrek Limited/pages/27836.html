Section 21 (Emission Control Systems (Aux. Emission Control Devices) (HEV)): Canister: Installation

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Connect the purge tube No. 1 (B) to the fuel pipe assembly, and install to the tube clamp.

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>INSTALLATION>QUICK CONNECTOR CONNECTION (TYPE E) .

  2. Connect the vent tube No. 2 (A) to the electric vent solenoid valve assembly, and install to the tube clamp.

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>INSTALLATION>QUICK CONNECTOR CONNECTION (TYPE F) .

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Connect the purge tube No. 2 (C).

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>INSTALLATION>QUICK CONNECTOR CONNECTION (TYPE D) .

  4. Connect the vent tube No. 1 (B).

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>INSTALLATION>QUICK CONNECTOR CONNECTION (TYPE H) .

  5. Install the canister protector (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    8 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.9 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Install the drain filter.

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>INSTALLATION>QUICK CONNECTOR CONNECTION (TYPE G) .

  7. Install the nut (A) securing the drain filter to the canister protector.

    Tightening torque: 

    8 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.9 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Secure the harness to the canister protector, and connect the connector (A).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Install the canister and drain filter as an assembly, and connect the connector (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    8 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.9 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  10. Connect the purge tube No. 2 (B) and install to the tube clamp,

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>INSTALLATION>QUICK CONNECTOR CONNECTION (TYPE E) .

  11. Connect the vent tube No. 1 (A) and install to the tube clamp,

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>INSTALLATION>QUICK CONNECTOR CONNECTION (TYPE H) .

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  12. Install the mud guard - rear LH.

    Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>MUD GUARD>INSTALLATION .

  13. Lower the vehicle.
  14. Install the rear wheel LH.

    Refer to WHEEL AND TIRE SYSTEM>TIRE AND WHEEL>INSTALLATION .

  15. Connect the battery ground terminal.

    Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
