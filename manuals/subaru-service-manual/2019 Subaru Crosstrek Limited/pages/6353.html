Rear Differential Mount Bushing: Replacement

CAUTION:
  • If there was so much rust in the rear differential mount bushing, remove the rust before starting work.
  • Apply the molybdenum grease on the square thread of the ST (shaft and nut) before use.
  1. Remove the rear differential.
    • T-type:

      Refer to DIFFERENTIALS>REAR DIFFERENTIAL (T-TYPE)>REMOVAL .

    • VAl-type:

      Refer to DIFFERENTIALS>REAR DIFFERENTIAL (VA-TYPE)>REMOVAL .

  2. Fit the ST to the periphery of the sub frame cylinder, and make sure that the ST does not contact with welded spots or spatters.
    CAUTION: If the ST rises on the welded spots or spatters, the ST may be damaged during the operation. To avoid this, remove the excessive welds or spatters with a sander or equivalent so that the ST contacts the cylinder peripheral part.

    Special tool: 

    ST: 41399FG010 SPECIAL TOOL A

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Set the ST1, ST2, ST3, ST4 and ST5 as shown in the figure.

    Special tool: 

    ST1: 41399FG091 SPECIAL TOOL SHAFT

    ST2: 41399FG070 SPECIAL TOOL NUT

    ST3: 41399FG050 SPECIAL TOOL SLEEVE

    ST4: 41399FG080 SPECIAL TOOL BEARING

    ST5: 41399FG010 SPECIAL TOOL A

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Fit and hold the ST1 and ST2 to the rear differential mount bushing from the rear side of vehicle.

    Special tool: 

    ST1: 41399FG031 SPECIAL TOOL C

    ST2: 41399FG061 SPECIAL TOOL RING

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Insert the ST set in the step 3. through the rear differential mount bushing hole from the front side of vehicle, and screw-in the ST1 by hand until the front end of ST1 comes out slightly from the rear end of ST2.

    Special tool: 

    ST1: 41399FG091 SPECIAL TOOL SHAFT

    ST2: 41399FG061 SPECIAL TOOL RING

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Hold the ST1 to prevent it from rotating, and screw-in the ST3 by hand until there is no loose fit on the ST2.
    CAUTION: When setting the ST to the vehicle, always make sure that the ST2 fits the periphery of the sub frame cylinder and is not tilted.

    Special tool: 

    ST1: 41399FG091 SPECIAL TOOL SHAFT

    ST2: 41399FG010 SPECIAL TOOL A

    ST3: 41399FG070 SPECIAL TOOL NUT

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Hold the ST1 with a tool to prevent it from rotating, and screw-in the ST2 to remove the rear differential mount bushing.
    CAUTION:
    • Rotation of ST1 will damage the screw at the rear end of rear differential mount bushing. Never rotate the ST1.
    • If the ST starts to tilt while removing the rear differential mount bushing, stop the work and set the ST again.

    Special tool: 

    ST1: 41399FG091 SPECIAL TOOL SHAFT

    ST2: 41399FG070 SPECIAL TOOL NUT

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Set the ST1, ST2, ST3, ST4, ST5 and rear differential mount bushing as shown in the figure.
    NOTE:
    • Set the ST2 nut near to the end of ST1 screw.
    • Hold the rear differential mount bushing with the arrow marked side facing toward the rear of the vehicle, and set the ST so that the arrow mark faces upward.
    • Mark the bottom end of rear differential mount bushing to identify the installing direction.

    Special tool: 

    ST1: 41399FG091 SPECIAL TOOL SHAFT

    ST2: 41399FG070 SPECIAL TOOL NUT

    ST3: 41399FG050 SPECIAL TOOL SLEEVE

    ST4: 41399FG080 SPECIAL TOOL BEARING

    ST5: 41399FG020 SPECIAL TOOL B

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Attach the ST1 to the ST2, and fit and hold the STs as a unit to the sub frame from the rear side of vehicle.

    Special tool: 

    ST1: 41399FG061 SPECIAL TOOL RING

    ST2: 41399FG041 SPECIAL TOOL D

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  10. Insert the ST and rear differential mount bushing, that have been set in step 8., into the sub frame from the front side of vehicle. Then, screw-in the ST1 by hand until the front end of ST1 comes out slightly from the rear end of ST2.
    CAUTION: Set the rear differential mount bushing with its mark facing the bottom end direction.

    Special tool: 

    ST1: 41399FG091 SPECIAL TOOL SHAFT

    ST2: 41399FG061 SPECIAL TOOL RING

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  11. Hold the ST1 to prevent it from rotating, and screw-in the ST2 by hand until there is no loose fit on the ST and the rear differential mount bushing.
    CAUTION: Make sure that the ST and rear differential mount bushing are not tilted.

    Special tool: 

    ST1: 41399FG091 SPECIAL TOOL SHAFT

    ST2: 41399FG070 SPECIAL TOOL NUT

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  12. Screw-in the ST2 while holding the ST1 with a tool to prevent it from rotating, and press-fit the rear differential mount bushing to the front end of sub frame cylinder.

    Special tool: 

    ST1: 41399FG091 SPECIAL TOOL SHAFT

    ST2: 41399FG070 SPECIAL TOOL NUT

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  13. Make sure that the rear differential mount bushing is inserted to the front end of sub frame cylinder.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  14. Install the rear differential.
    • T-type:

      Refer to DIFFERENTIALS>REAR DIFFERENTIAL (T-TYPE)>INSTALLATION .

    • VA1-type:

      Refer to DIFFERENTIALS>REAR DIFFERENTIAL (VA-TYPE)>INSTALLATION .
