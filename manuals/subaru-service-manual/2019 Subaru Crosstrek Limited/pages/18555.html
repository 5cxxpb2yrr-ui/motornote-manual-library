Electronic Parking Brake System

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Start the engine, and operate and release the electronic parking brake a few times with the vehicle at a standstill.
  2. Check if the warning light illuminates or blinks, or DTC is displayed.
    NOTE: If the DTC is displayed, perform the diagnosis using "Diagnostic Procedure with Diagnostic Trouble Code (DTC)". Refer to BRAKE CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE WITH DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .
  3. Drive the vehicle at least 10 minutes, and operate and release the electronic parking brake a few times.
    CAUTION: Do not drive the vehicle with the warning light illuminated or blinked.
