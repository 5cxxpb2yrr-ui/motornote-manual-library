Front Hood: Installation

  1. Install the hood COMPL - front.

    Tightening torque: 

    25 N.m (2.5 kgf-m, 18.4 ft-lb)

  2. Adjust the clearance around the hood COMPL - front. Refer to EXTERIOR BODY PANELS>FRONT HOOD>ADJUSTMENT .
