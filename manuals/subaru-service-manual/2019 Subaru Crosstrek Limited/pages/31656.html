WUR-03R BRAKE HOSE ORDERING INFORMATION

TECHNICAL SERVICE BULLETIN

Reference Number(s): 06-75-20, Date of Issue:  May 15, 2020
SUBARU: 2017-18 Impreza; 2017-19 Crosstrek

APPLICABILITY

2017-18MY Impreza

2017-19MY Crosstrek

INTRODUCTION

This Service Information Bulletin is intended to clarify the proper ordering procedure to use in the rare case when an individual brake hose component of the WUR-03R Service Campaign kit is needed.

PART INFORMATION

The WUR-03R Brake Hose Kit part numbers are:

  • 26592FL000  (Impreza)
  • 26592FL010  (Crosstrek)
IMPORTANT: When performing the WUR-03R campaign and the need for an individual brake hose included in one of the above listed kits arises (e.g. missing, damaged etc.), a complete replacement WUR-03R brake hose kit must be ordered.  DO NOT order the individual brake hose listed in the Electronic Parts Catalog to complete the repair kit.

SERVICE PROCEDURE/INFORMATION

REMINDER  : Customer satisfaction and retention starts with performing quality repairs.

The WUR-03R Service Campaign service procedure remains unchanged. Always refer to the applicable Service Manual and review the full requirements of the repair being performed. The Service Manual procedures contain information critical to performing an effective repair the first time, every time. This includes but is not limited to: important SAFETY precautions, inspection criteria, necessary special tools, required processes and related one-time-use parts needed for a complete and lasting repair.

IMPORTANT REMINDERS: 

  • SOA strongly discourages the printing and/or local storage of service information as previously released information and electronic publications may be updated at any time.
  • Always check for any open recalls or campaigns anytime a vehicle is in for servicing.
  • Always refer to STIS for the latest service information before performing any repairs.
