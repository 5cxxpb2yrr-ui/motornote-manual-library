Roof Antenna/Telematics Antenna

  1. Install the antenna assembly - radio or the telematics.

    Tightening torque: 

    4.5 N.m (0.46 kgf-m, 3.3 ft-lb)

  2. Install the trim panel - roof assembly. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>ROOF TRIM>INSTALLATION .
  3. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
