Sunroof Assembly: Disassembly

  1. Remove the following parts referring the respective procedures.
    • Lid assembly - sunroof: Refer to SUNROOF/T-TOP/CONVERTIBLE TOP (SUNROOF)>GLASS LID>REMOVAL>GLASS LID .
    • Deflector assembly: Refer to SUNROOF/T-TOP/CONVERTIBLE TOP (SUNROOF)>GLASS LID>REMOVAL>DEFLECTOR ASSEMBLY .
    • Sunshade assembly: Refer to SUNROOF/T-TOP/CONVERTIBLE TOP (SUNROOF)>SUNSHADE>REMOVAL .
    • Sunroof motor assembly: Refer to SUNROOF/T-TOP/CONVERTIBLE TOP (SUNROOF)>SUNROOF ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL .
  2. Remove the drain plate - sunroof.
    1. Release the claws on the front side.
    2. Slide the drain plate - sunroof and remove from the rail section.
