DTC P0747: Pressure Control Solenoid "A" Stuck ON: Notes

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

1. - 

DTC detecting condition: 

Detected when two consecutive driving cycles with fault occur.

Trouble symptom: 

  • Shift control malfunction
  • CVT chain slippage

Wiring diagram: 

CVT control system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (IMPREZA MODEL)>CVT CONTROL SYSTEM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>CVT CONTROL SYSTEM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION:
  • Before performing diagnosis, record the freeze frame data.
  • Use the check board when measuring the TCM terminal voltage and resistance.
  • After servicing or replacing faulty parts, perform the last step of the basic diagnostic procedure. Refer to TRANSMISSION (DIAGNOSTICS)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE .
  1. CHECK DTC  .

    Read the DTC using Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to TRANSMISSION (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Besides DTC P0747 and P0867, is DTC P0842, P0843, P0962, or P0963 displayed?

    Yes:  Perform the diagnosis according to DTCs other than P0747 and P0867. Refer to TRANSMISSION (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>LIST .

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK SECONDARY SOLENOID (ACTIVE TEST)  .
    1. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    2. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, perform forced operation of the secondary solenoid. Refer to TRANSMISSION (DIAGNOSTICS)>ACTIVE TEST .

    Does the indication of 0 → 500 mA appear repeatedly during forced operation, and does operating sound emit during indicating 500 mA?

    Yes:  Go to  5.

    No:  Go to  3.

  3. CHECK SECONDARY SOLENOID  .
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the TCM connector.
    3. Measure the resistance between TCM connector and transmission body.

    Connector & terminal 

    (T10) No. 32 - Transmission body:

    Is the resistance approximately 5 - 7 Ω? (When cold)

    Yes:  Go to  7.

    No:  Go to  4.

  4. CHECK HARNESS  .

    Check the transmission harness. (Open circuit, short circuit to power supply, short circuit to ground, and poor contact)

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  7.

    No:  Repair the harness.

  5. CHECK TRANSMISSION FLUID  .

    Check the condition of ATF. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>CVTF>CONDITION CHECK .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Return ATF. Go to  6.

    No:  Replace the ATF. Go to  6.

  6. CHECK TRANSMISSION FLUID  .
    1. Connect the TCM connector.
    2. Check the amount of ATF. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>CVTF>ADJUSTMENT .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  7.

    No:  Adjust the amount of ATF. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>CVTF>ADJUSTMENT . Go to  7.

  7. CHECK INPUT SIGNAL FOR TCM  .
    1. Lift up the vehicle.
    2. Start the engine.
    3. Warm up until the ATF temperature reaches to 40 - 70°C (104 - 158°F).
    4. Depress the brake pedal and move the select lever to each range at an interval of five seconds.
      NOTE: Move the select lever in the following order: "P" → "R" → "N" → "D" → "N" → "R" → "P".
    5. Stabilize the engine speed at idle.
    6. Read the data of [Actual Secondary Pressure] using Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to TRANSMISSION (DIAGNOSTICS)>DATA MONITOR .

    Is [Actual Secondary Pressure] 0.5 - 1.5 MPa?

    Yes:  Go to  8.

    No:  Replace the control valve body. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>CONTROL VALVE BODY .

  8. CHECK INPUT SIGNAL FOR TCM  .
    1. Shift the select lever to "P" range.
    2. Keep the engine speed at 3, 000 rpm.
    3. Read the data of [Actual Secondary Pressure] using Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to TRANSMISSION (DIAGNOSTICS)>DATA MONITOR .

    Is [Actual Secondary Pressure] 1.5 - 2.5 MPa? And is the difference of the actual oil pressure between the value measured in Step 7 0.2 MPa or more?

    Yes:  Go to  9.

    No:  Replace the control valve body. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>CONTROL VALVE BODY .

  9. CHECK FREEZE FRAME DATA  .
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Check the recorded freeze frame data for DTC P0867. Refer to TRANSMISSION (DIAGNOSTICS)>FREEZE FRAME DATA .
    3. Apply the values of [Actual Secondary Pressure] and [Secondary Actual Current] recorded in the freeze frame data onto the graph shown in the following figure, and judge if either low-voltage malfunction or high-voltage malfunction occurs.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    NOTE: Symptom that the DTC P0747 illuminates can be classified into 2 patterns.
    • Low-voltage malfunction: detected due to insufficient oil pressure
    • High-voltage malfunction: detected due to excessive oil pressure

    Was the detected symptom low-voltage malfunction?

    Yes:  Replace the transmission assembly. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY .

    No:  Go to  10.

  10. CHECK TCM INPUT SIGNAL (STALL TEST)  .
    1. Apply the parking brake.
    2. Set the select lever to "D" range.
    3. Depress the brake pedal firmly.
    4. Slowly open the accelerator fully, and stabilize the engine speed.
    5. Read the data of [Actual Secondary Pressure] using Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to TRANSMISSION (DIAGNOSTICS)>DATA MONITOR .
    CAUTION: Do not perform a stall test for over 5 seconds at a time.

    Is the value of [Actual Secondary Pressure] 6.0 MPa or more?

    Yes:  Replace the control valve body. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>CONTROL VALVE BODY .

    No:  Replace the transmission assembly. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY .
