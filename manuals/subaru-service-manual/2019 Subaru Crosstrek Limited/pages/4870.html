Diagnostic Method

Case 1 

At a vehicle speed of 6.22 MPH or more, noise occurs once per rotor rotation.

Case 2 

At a vehicle speed of 12.44 MPH or more, noise occurs in the sensor signals of RF wheel.

Case 3 

An open in the sensor signal of RF wheel occurs.

JUDGMENT VALUE SPECIFICATION

Malfunction Criteria Threshold Value
Case 1 
Periodic Noise for RF Wheel Speed Sensor		 Existed
Case 2 
ABS (Current Wheel Speed - Last Wheel Speed) of RF Wheel		 6.73 MPH or more
Case 3 
ABS (Current Wheel Speed - Last Wheel Speed) of RF Wheel		 2.1 MPH or more

Time needed for diagnosis: 

  • Case 1:  15 s
  • Case 2:  450 ms
  • Case 3:  255 times

Malfunction indicator light illumination:  Illuminates as soon as a malfunction occurs.
