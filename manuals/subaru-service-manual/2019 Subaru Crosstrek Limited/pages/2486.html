Basic Inspection

Before performing diagnosis, check the following items which might affect the problems related to auto headlight beam leveler system or steering responsive headlight.

  1. Check the battery. Refer to STARTING/CHARGING SYSTEMS (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>BATTERY>INSPECTION . Refer to STARTING/CHARGING SYSTEMS (H4DO (HEV))>BATTERY>INSPECTION .
  2. Check the relay and fuse condition. Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>RELAY AND FUSE .
  3. Check the connecting condition of harness and harness connector.
