Rear Height Sensor: Removal

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

CAUTION: Always remove the sensor assembly - headlight beam leveler before removing any parts related to the suspension.
Do not apply impact to the sensor assembly - headlight beam leveler or forcibly move the arm. Doing so may cause sensor damage and malfunction.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Lift up the vehicle.
  3. Remove the sensor assembly - headlight beam leveler.
    CAUTION: Do not disassemble the sensor and lever.
    1. Disconnect the connector.
    2. Remove the nuts, and remove the sensor assembly - headlight beam leveler.
