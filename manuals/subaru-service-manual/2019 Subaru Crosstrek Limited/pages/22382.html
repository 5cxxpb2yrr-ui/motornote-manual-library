Outline Of Diagnosis

When the vehicle is being driven, if the voltage at the solenoid power supply is a specific value or more due to a malfunction in the auxiliary battery and hybrid control system (charging circuit), the electric brake booster control module judges that there is an increase in the power supply voltage of the solenoid power supply and illuminates the MIL and stores a DTC.
