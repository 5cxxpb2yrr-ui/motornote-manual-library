X MODE Switch: Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery.

    Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .

  2. Release the shift lock using a screwdriver, etc. and shift the select lever to the "N" range.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the cover assembly - front.

    Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>CONSOLE BOX>REMOVAL>CROSSTREK MODEL>HYBRID MODEL .

  4. Remove the X mode switch.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
