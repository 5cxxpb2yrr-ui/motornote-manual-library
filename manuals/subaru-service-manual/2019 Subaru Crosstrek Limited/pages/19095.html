Check Relay

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Measure the resistance between relay terminals.
    • Blower motor relay
      Terminal No. Inspection conditions Standard Circuit
      1 - 4 Always 1 MΩ or more
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
      Apply battery voltage between terminals 2 and 3. Less than 1 Ω
    • PTC heater relay
      Terminal No. Inspection conditions Standard Circuit
      2 - 3 Always 1 MΩ or more
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
      Apply battery voltage between terminals 1 and 4. Less than 1 Ω
    • IGB relay
    Terminal No. Inspection conditions Standard Circuit
    1 - 2 Always 1 MΩ or more
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Apply battery voltage between terminals 3 and 4. Less than 1 Ω
  2. Replace the relay if the inspection result is not within the standard value.
