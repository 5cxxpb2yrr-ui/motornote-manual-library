Explanation For Circuits

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

When the following condition is established, MIL illuminates and DTC is detected.

The Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly monitors the insulation monitoring circuit located in the Battery ECU assembly and detects malfunctions.
