Disc Rotor RUNOUT Check

  1. Check the hub unit COMPL - front axle for free play and runout before the inspection of disc rotor runout limit. Refer to DRIVE SHAFT SYSTEM>FRONT HUB UNIT BEARING>INSPECTION .
  2. Check the disc rotor runout.
    1. Remove the caliper body assembly. Refer to BRAKE>FRONT DISC ROTOR>REMOVAL .
    2. Secure the disc rotor by tightening the five wheel nuts.
    3. Set a dial gauge 10 mm (0.39 in) inward from the disc rotor outer circumference, and check the outer disc rotor runout while rotating the disc rotor.

      Preparation tool: 

      ST: MAGNET BASE (498247001)

      Disc rotor runout limit 

      0.05 mm (0.002 in)

      NOTE: <Example of magnet stand and dial gauge installation>
      The location where the magnet stand is installed varies to stabilize the magnet stand.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    4. Set a dial gauge 10 mm (0.39 in) inward from the disc rotor outer circumference, and check the inner disc rotor runout while rotating the disc rotor.

    Preparation tool: 

    ST: MAGNET BASE (498247001)

    Disc rotor runout limit 

    0.05 mm (0.002 in)

    NOTE: <Example of magnet stand and dial gauge installation>
    The location where the magnet stand is installed varies to stabilize the magnet stand.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. If the disc rotor runout exceeds service limit, grind the disc rotor.
  4. Check the disc rotor thickness after grinding. Refer to BRAKE>FRONT DISC ROTOR>INSPECTION>DISC ROTOR THICKNESS CHECK .
