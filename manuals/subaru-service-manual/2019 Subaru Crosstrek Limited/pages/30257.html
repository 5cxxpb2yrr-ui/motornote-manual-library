Heater Core: Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
CAUTION: Before handling the airbag system components, refer to "CAUTION" of "General Description" in "AIRBAG SYSTEM". Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. Using the refrigerant recovery system, discharge refrigerant. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>REFRIGERANT RECOVERY PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .
  2. Disconnect the battery ground terminal and wait for at least 60 seconds before starting work. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  3. Drain engine coolant. (Engine side) Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>ENGINE COOLANT>REPLACEMENT>DRAINING OF ENGINE COOLANT .
  4. Remove the heater and cooling unit assembly. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>HEATER AND COOLING UNIT>REMOVAL .
  5. Remove the internal condenser. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>CONDENSER>REMOVAL>INTERIOR CONDENSER .
  6. Remove the clamp - heater pipe.
    1. Release the claws, and remove the clamp - heater pipe.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    NOTE: Do not remove the packing.
