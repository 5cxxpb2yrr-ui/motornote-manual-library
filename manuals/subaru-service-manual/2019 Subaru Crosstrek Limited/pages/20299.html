DTC P0ABF-11: Hybrid/EV Battery Current Sensor "A" Circuit Short To Ground: Notes

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

1. - 

Outline of diagnosis: 

When the BATTERY CURRENT SENSOR output value is unusually low (IB0)

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

  • Reduced output power
  • Engine not stopped

Wiring diagram: 

Hybrid system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK DTC. 

    Read the following DTCs using Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) . Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    • P-HEV High Voltage Battery
    • P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control
    NOTE:
    System Related DTC
    P-HEV High Voltage Battery P060A47 HYBRID/EV BATTERY ENERGY CONTROL MODULE MONITORING PROCESSOR WATCHDOG/SAFETY MCU FAILURE
    P060B49 HYBRID/EV BATTERY ENERGY CONTROL MODULE A/D PROCESSING INTERNAL ELECTRONIC FAILURE
    P060687 HYBRID/EV BATTERY ENERGY CONTROL MODULE PROCESSOR TO MONITORING PROCESSOR MISSING MESSAGE
    P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control P0A1F94 HYBRID/EV BATTERY ENERGY CONTROL MODULE UNEXPECTED OPERATION

    When "Any of DTC P0ABF11, P0ABF15, P0B0E11, P0B0E15, P1CBB12 or P1CBB14 is displayed" or "DTC other than above is displayed", go to  A.

    When DTCs related to [P-HEV High Voltage Battery], mentioned above, are simultaneously displayed, go to  B.

    When DTCs related to [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control], mentioned above, are simultaneously displayed, go to  C.

    Yes: 

    1. Go to  2.
    2. Perform the diagnosis for the simultaneously displayed DTCs.
      NOTE: If more than one DTC is simultaneously displayed, perform the diagnosis in the order described in the above.
    3. Perform the diagnosis for the simultaneously displayed DTCs.
    NOTE: If more than one DTC is simultaneously displayed, perform the diagnosis in the order described in the above.

    No:  -

  2. CHECK Battery ECU assembly (IGCT VOLTAGE). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Check that the Service plug is disconnected. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .
    2. Disconnect Battery ECU assembly connector (HV42).
    3. Connect the ground terminal to Battery (12V). Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    4. Turn to IG ON.
    5. Using a tester, measure the voltage between Battery ECU assembly connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV42) No. 24 (+) - (HV42) No. 9 (-):

    Is the voltage 12 - 14 V?

    Yes:  Even when the measured voltage is within the standard, after checking the connecting condition of fuse and connector for IGCT circuit, Go to  3.

    No:  Replace the Wire harness between Battery ECU assembly connector and chassis ground.

  3. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Battery ECU assembly - HV battery junction block) (OPEN). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Turn the ignition to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the ground terminal from Battery (12V). Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    3. Using a tester, measure the resistance between Battery ECU assembly connector and HV battery junction block (BATTERY CURRENT SENSOR) connector.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV42) No. 30 - (HV54) No. 2:

      (HV42) No. 31 - (HV54) No. 3:

      (HV42) No. 7 - (HV54) No. 4:

      (HV42) No. 8 - (HV54) No. 1:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Replace the Wire harness between Battery ECU assembly connector and HV battery junction block (BATTERY CURRENT SENSOR) connector.

  4. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Battery ECU assembly - HV battery junction block) (SHORT TO GROUND AND LINE-TO-LINE SHORT). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between terminals in Battery ECU assembly connector, and between Battery ECU assembly connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (HV42) No. 30 - (HV42) No. 1 - 69:

    (HV42) No. 30 - Chassis ground:

    (HV42) No. 31 - (HV42) No. 1 - 69:

    (HV42) No. 31 - Chassis ground:

    (HV42) No. 7 - (HV42) No. 1 - 69:

    (HV42) No. 7 - Chassis ground:

    (HV42) No. 8 - (HV42) No. 1 - 69:

    (HV42) No. 8 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance 10 kΩ or more?

    Yes:  Go to  5.

    No:  Replace the Wire harness between Battery ECU assembly connector and HV battery junction block (BATTERY CURRENT SENSOR) connector.

  5. CHECK Battery ECU assembly (VIB VOLTAGE). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Connect the ground terminal to Battery (12V). Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    2. Turn to IG ON.
    3. Using a tester, measure the voltage between terminals in HV battery junction block (BATTERY CURRENT SENSOR) connector.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV54) No. 1 (+) - (HV54) No. 3 (-):

    Is the voltage 4.6 - 5.4 V?

    Yes:  Go to  6.

    No:  Go to  14.

  6. CHECK Battery ECU assembly (GIB TERMINAL - GND TERMINAL). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Turn the ignition to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the ground terminal from Battery (12V). Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    3. Remove Battery ECU assembly.
    4. Using a tester, measure the resistance in Battery ECU assembly terminals.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV42) No. 9 - (HV42) No. 31:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  7.

    No:  Replace Battery ECU assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>BATTERY CONTROL MODULE .

  7. CHECK Battery ECU assembly (BATTERY CURRENT SENSOR IB1 OUTPUT VOLTAGE). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Install Battery ECU assembly.
    2. Connect the ground terminal to Battery (12V). Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    3. Turn to IG ON.
    4. Using a tester, measure the voltage between terminals in HV battery junction block (BATTERY CURRENT SENSOR) connector. (With the connector connected)

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV54) No. 2 (+) - (HV54) No. 3 (-):

    Is the voltage 2.1 - 2.3 V?

    Yes:  Go to  8.

    No:  Go to  12.

  8. CHECK Battery ECU assembly (BATTERY CURRENT SENSOR IB0 OUTPUT VOLTAGE). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Keep to IG ON.
    2. Using a tester, measure the voltage between terminals in HV battery junction block (BATTERY CURRENT SENSOR) connector. (With the connector connected)

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV54) No. 4 (+) - (HV54) No. 3 (-):

    Is the voltage 2.7 - 2.9 V?

    Yes:  Replace Battery ECU assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>BATTERY CONTROL MODULE .

    No:  Go to  9.

  9. CHECK Battery ECU assembly (BATTERY CURRENT SENSOR IB0 OUTPUT VOLTAGE).

      (HV54) No. 4 (+) - (HV54) No. 3 (-):

    When the voltage is 0.4 - 4.6 V, go to  A.

    When the voltage is less than 0.4 V, go to  B.

    When the voltage is 4.6 V or more, go to  C.

    Yes: 

    1. Replace HV battery junction block. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>RELAY AND FUSE .

    3. Go to  11.
