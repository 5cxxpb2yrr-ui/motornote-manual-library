Back-Up Light And Neutral Position Switch

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Replace the gasket with a new part, and install the back-up light switch & neutral position switch together with the harness.

    Tightening torque: 

    32.3 N.m (3.3 kgf-m, 23.8 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Secure the harness on each clip.
  3. Install the front exhaust pipe. refer to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FRONT EXHAUST PIPE>INSTALLATION .
  4. Install the crossmember support - rear LH.

    Tightening torque: 

    70 N.m (7.1 kgf-m, 51.6 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Lower the vehicle.
  6. Secure the harness connector to the bracket.
  7. Install the coupling harness.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Install the air intake boot. refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>AIR INTAKE BOOT>INSTALLATION .
  9. Connect the battery ground terminal. refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
