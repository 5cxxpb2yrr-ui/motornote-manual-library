Generator High-Voltage Circuit

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .

Wiring diagram: 

Hybrid system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

  1. CHECK Inverter with converter assembly (CHECK Power cable CONNECTING CONDITION)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Check that the Service plug is disconnected. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .
    2. Check if the Power cable mounting bolt for Inverter with converter assembly is tightened to the specified torque, and is free from looseness and poor contact.

      Standard: 

      7.5 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.5 ft-lb)

    Is it tightened to the specified torque, and free from looseness and poor contact?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Connect Power cable securely. Or repair the poor contact of Power cable.

  2. CHECK Inverter with converter assembly (CHECK Power cable CONNECTING CONDITION)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Disconnect Power cable (HV99) from Inverter with converter assembly.
    2. Check the Power cable mounting bolt for trace of arc.

    Is there any trace of arc?

    Yes:  Replace the faulty parts.

    No:  Go to  3.

  3. CHECK Transmission assembly (GENERATOR)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.

    Using a milliohm tester, measure the resistance between Power cable terminals.

    NOTE:
    • When the motor temperature is too high, the resistance value changes largely. Therefore, measure the resistance after stopping the vehicle and leaving it for eight hours or more.
    • To correct a change of the resistance value due to temperature, calculate the resistance value at 20°C (68°F) by applying the following formula:

    R20 = Rt ÷ (1 + 0.00393 x (T - 20))
    R20: Resistance value (mΩ) at 20°C (68°F)
    Rt: Measured resistance value (mΩ)
    T: Temperature (°C (°F)) at measuring the resistance

    Connector & terminal 

    (HV99) W1 - (HV99) U1:

    (HV99) U1 - (HV99) V1:

    (HV99) V1 - (HV99) W1:

    Is the resistance as follows?

    (HV99) W1 - (HV99) U1: 49.30 - 78.96 mΩ

    (HV99) U1 - (HV99) V1: 49.30 - 78.96 mΩ

    (HV99) V1 - (HV99) W1: 49.30 - 78.96 mΩ

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Go to  5.

  4. CHECK Transmission assembly (GENERATOR)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.

    Using a megohmmeter (500 V range), measure the resistance between Power cable (on Inverter with converter assembly side) terminal and chassis ground and shield line ground.

    CAUTION: When performing this check, be sure to set megohmmeter to 500 V. Using a range higher than 500 V may damage the part being checked.

    Connector & terminal 

    (HV99) W1 - Chassis ground and shield line ground:

    (HV99) U1 - Chassis ground and shield line ground:

    (HV99) V1 - Chassis ground and shield line ground:

    Is the resistance 10 MΩ or more?

    Yes:  GENERATOR HIGH-VOLTAGE CIRCUIT is normal.

    No:  Go to  5.

  5. CHECK Transmission assembly (CHECK Power cable CONNECTING CONDITION)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Remove the Inverter with converter assembly.
    2. Check if the Power cable mounting bolt for Transmission assembly is tightened to the specified torque, and is free from looseness and poor contact.

      Standard: 

      7.5 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.5 ft-lb)

    Is it tightened to the specified torque, and free from looseness and poor contact?

    Yes:  Go to  6.

    No:  Connect Power cable securely. Or repair the poor contact of Power cable.

  6. CHECK Transmission assembly (CHECK Power cable CONNECTING CONDITION)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Disconnect Power cable from Transmission assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>POWER CABLE .
    2. Check the Power cable mounting bolt for trace of arc.

    Is there any trace of arc?

    Yes:  Replace the faulty parts.

    No:  Go to  7.

  7. CHECK Power cable (SHORT TO GROUND)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Remove the Power cable.
    2. Using a megohmmeter (500 V range), measure the resistance between Power cable (on Inverter with converter assembly side) terminal and chassis ground and shield line ground.
      CAUTION:
      • When performing this check, be sure to set megohmmeter to 500 V. Using a range higher than 500 V may damage the part being checked.
      • To prevent the terminals of three-phase AC cable from contacting the chassis ground, be sure to wrap with protection tape.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV99) W1 - Chassis ground and shield line ground:

      (HV99) U1 - Chassis ground and shield line ground:

      (HV99) V1 - Chassis ground and shield line ground:

    Is the resistance 10 MΩ or more?

    Yes:  Go to  8.

    No:  Replace Power cable. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>POWER CABLE .

  8. CHECK THE Power cable UNIT (OPEN CIRCUIT)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.

    Using the tester, measure the resistance between Power cable terminals.

    Connector & terminal 

    (HV99) W1 - (HV98) W1:

    (HV99) U1 - (HV98) U1:

    (HV99) V1 - (HV98) V1:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  9.

    No:  Replace Power cable. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>POWER CABLE .

  9. CHECK Power cable UNIT (SHORTED BETWEEN LINES)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.

    Using the tester, measure the resistance between Power cable terminals.

    Connector & terminal 

    (HV99) W1 - (HV98) U1:

    (HV99) U1 - (HV98) V1:

    (HV99) V1 - (HV98) W1:

    Is the resistance 100 MΩ or more?

    Yes:  Replace Transmission assembly. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TH2A)>TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY .

    No:  Replace Power cable. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>POWER CABLE .

GENERAL DESCRIPTION 

Explanation of circuits 

The malfunction cause may be GENERATOR HIGH-VOLTAGE CIRCUIT.

Check the generator internal resistance and connecting condition of the high voltage wires from the inverter to generator in order to check for open and short circuit.

Check related parts 

Part Inspection
Check Power cable connection Check connecting condition of the cable from the inverter to the generator
Generator, Power cable Measure internal resistance of the generator (including cable) and insulation resistance between the generator and chassis ground to check for open or short circuit
