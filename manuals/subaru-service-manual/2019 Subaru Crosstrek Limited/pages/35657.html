Diagnostic Method

The solenoid power supply voltage is 3.5 V or more amount of time, when operation of the main relay is not requested.

JUDGMENT VALUE SPECIFICATION

Malfunction Criteria Threshold Value
Case 1 
Solenoid power supply voltage		 3.5 V or more
Case 2 
Solenoid power supply voltage		 3.5 V or more

Time needed for diagnosis: 

  • Case 1:  5 s
  • Case 2:  2 s

Malfunction indicator light illumination:  Illuminates as soon as a malfunction occurs.
