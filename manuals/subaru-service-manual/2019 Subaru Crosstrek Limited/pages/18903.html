Hybrid mode switch SYSTEM

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .

  1. INTERVIEW  .

    Ask the customer if "Mode Change/Not Available" was displayed on the combination meter when he/she operated Hybrid mode switch.

    Was it displayed on the combination meter?

    Yes:  Finish the diagnosis.

    NOTE: If it was displayed, the mode switching condition was not satisfied.

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK LAN SYSTEM  .

    Inspect LAN system. Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE .

    Is LAN system OK?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Perform the diagnosis for LAN system. Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE .

  3. CHECK DTC  .

    Using the Subaru Select Monitor, read all DTCs. Refer to COMMON (DIAG)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Are any DTCs displayed?

    Yes:  Perform the diagnosis for the displayed DTCs.

    No:  Go to  4.

  4. CHECK DATA MONITOR (Hybrid mode switch)  .

    Using the Subaru Select Monitor, display the following items in the [Data monitor] of [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control]. Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DATA MONITOR .

    • HV/EV Mode Switch

    Does the display change according to the Hybrid mode switch operation?

    Yes:  Perform the diagnosis for combination meter and MFD. Refer to COMBINATION METER (DIAGNOSTICS)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE . Refer to MULTI-FUNCTION DISPLAY (DIAGNOSTICS)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE .

    No:  Go to  5.

  5. CHECK THE Hybrid mode switch UNIT  .
    1. Remove the Hybrid mode switch. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SWITCHES AND HARNESS .
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between Hybrid mode switch terminals.

      Connector & terminal 

      (AD95) No. 1 - (AD95) No. 4:

    Is the resistance as follows?

    When Hybrid mode switch is pressed: Less than 50 Ω

    When Hybrid mode switch is not operated: 10 kΩ or more

    Yes:  Go to  6.

    No:  Replace Hybrid mode switch. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SWITCHES AND HARNESS .

  6. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Hybrid mode switch - CHASSIS GROUND) (OPEN CIRCUIT)  .

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between Hybrid mode switch connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (AD95) No. 4 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  7.

    No:  Repair or replace the Wire harness between Hybrid mode switch connector and chassis ground.

  7. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly - Hybrid mode switch) (OPEN CIRCUIT)  .
    1. Disconnect Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector (i370).
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between Hybrid mode switch connector and Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector.

      Connector & terminal 

      (i370) No. 15 - (AD95) No. 1:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  8.

    No:  Repair or replace the Wire harness between Hybrid mode switch connector and Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector.

  8. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly - Hybrid mode switch) (SHORT TO GROUND OR LINE-TO-LINE SHORT)  .

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (i370) No. 15 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance 10 kΩ or more?

    Yes:  Replace Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL MODULE .

    No:  Repair or replace the Wire harness between Hybrid mode switch connector and Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector.

GENERAL DESCRIPTION 

Explanation of circuits 

  • Normal Mode

    In Normal Mode, switch-over of EV traveling and hybrid traveling is performed automatically according to the change of charge amount for the high voltage battery.

    • When the charge amount for the high voltage battery is large, EV traveling is actively performed. However, at high load or high vehicle speed, the engine operates automatically.
    • When the charge amount for the high voltage battery low, the hybrid traveling that uses the engine and motor concurrently is performed. However, if the charge amount recovers by regeneration, etc., the EV traveling is performed again.
  • Hybrid battery save mode

    In the hybrid battery save mode, the vehicle uses the hybrid traveling actively so as not to reduce the power charged in the high voltage battery. This allows the vehicle to maintain the charged power in such a case of performing the EV traveling at destinations.

    • Even when the charge amount of the high voltage battery is high, the charged power can be maintained by restraining the EV traveling and performing the hybrid traveling.
  • Hybrid battery charge mode

    In the hybrid battery charge mode, the high voltage battery is actively charged while the vehicle is being driven. This allows to charge the battery by using the engine driving force to generate power also without depending on external charging in such a case as performing EV traveling at destinations.

    • By increasing the engine load during driving, the high voltage battery is charged.
    • When the high voltage battery is charged sufficiently, the mode is automatically switched to the hybrid battery save mode.
