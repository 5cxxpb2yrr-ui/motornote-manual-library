Front Suspension System And Wheel Alignment: Stabilizer: Installation: Notes

CAUTION:
  • Always tighten the stabilizer bushing in the state where the vehicle is at curb weight and the wheels are in full contact with the ground.
  • Install the clamp - stabilizer bushing with the peak of triangle facing the front of the vehicle.
