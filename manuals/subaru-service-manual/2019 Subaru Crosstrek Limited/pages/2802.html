Basic Inspection

Before performing diagnosis, check the following items which might affect MFD problems.

  1. Check the battery. Refer to STARTING/CHARGING SYSTEMS (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>BATTERY>INSPECTION . Refer to STARTING/CHARGING SYSTEMS (H4DO (HEV))>BATTERY>INSPECTION .
  2. Check the main and other fuses, harnesses and connectors. Also check grounding.
  3. Verify the destination of the vehicle.
  4. Verify type of wheel-drive.
  5. Verify availability and grade of the head unit installed to the vehicle.
  6. Verify availability of EyeSight.
  7. Verify availability of RAB.
  8. Verify availability of BSD/RCTA.
