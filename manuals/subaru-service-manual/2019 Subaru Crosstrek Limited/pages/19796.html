Monitor Description

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

The Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly monitors speed of the inverter water pump assembly. If there is an abnormality in speed, the Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly will illuminate the MIL and store a DTC.
