DTC P1AFD-1C: Flying Capacitor/Internal Control Module Hybrid/EV Battery Monitor Voltage Out Of Range: Notes

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

1. - 

Outline of diagnosis: 

Faulty voltage detection circuit inside Battery ECU assembly

When the difference between the sum of cell voltages and stack voltage is large.

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

  • Reduced output power
  • Engine not stopped
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK CONNECTOR CONNECTION CONDITION (Battery ECU assembly). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Check that the Service plug is disconnected. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .
    2. Check the connecting condition of connectors (HV37), (HV38), (HV39), (HV40) and (HV41) in Battery ECU assembly and check the relevant terminals for contact pressure.

    Is there looseness at the connector connection, or any poor contact at the relevant terminals?

    Yes:  Connect the connector securely. If the terminal is damaged, replace the HV battery. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY .

    No:  Replace Battery ECU assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>BATTERY CONTROL MODULE .
