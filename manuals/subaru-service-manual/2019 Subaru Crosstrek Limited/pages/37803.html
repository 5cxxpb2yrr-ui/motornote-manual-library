Lock Assembly

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

IMPREZA MODEL 

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery and wait for at least 60 seconds before starting work. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the air intake duct. Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>AIR INTAKE DUCT>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the cover UPR - front.
    1. Remove the clips, and remove the cover UPR - front.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the lock assembly - front hood.
    1. Disconnect the connector and the clip.
    2. Remove the cable - front hood (a) and the cable - safety (b).
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Remove the bolts and clips, and remove the lock assembly - front hood.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
