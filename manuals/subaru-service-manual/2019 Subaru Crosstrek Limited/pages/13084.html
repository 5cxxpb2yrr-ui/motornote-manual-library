Rear Shock Absorber: Disassembly

  1. Using a coil spring compressor, compress the coil spring.
  2. Using a hexagon wrench to prevent the shock absorber piston rod from turning, remove the self-locking nut with ST.
    CAUTION: When installing the coil spring compressor to the coil spring, follow the operation instructions accompanied with the coil spring compressor during operation.

    Preparation tool: 

    ST: STRUT MOUNT SOCKET (20399FG000)

    NOTE: <Example of coil spring compressor installation>
    The installing position of coil spring compressor varies depending on the coil spring shape and winding number.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the shock mount - rear from the shock absorber COMPL - rear.
  4. Gradually decrease the compression pressure of compressor, and remove the coil spring - rear.
  5. Remove the helper - rear and the dust cover - rear shock.
