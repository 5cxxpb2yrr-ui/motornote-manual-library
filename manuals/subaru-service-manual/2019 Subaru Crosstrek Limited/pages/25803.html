Diagnostic Procedure for Subaru Select Monitor Communication: Inspection

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Trouble symptom: 

Cannot communicate with [P-HEV High Voltage Battery].

Detecting condition: 

  • Defective Wire harness connector
  • Power supply circuit malfunction
  • Defective Battery ECU assembly
  • Defective CAN communication circuit
  • Defective Subaru Select Monitor

Wiring diagram: 

Hybrid system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK FUSE. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Check the fuse. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>RELAY AND FUSE .

    Is there any fault?

    Yes:  Replace the fuse. If the replaced fuse has blown out immediately, repair the short circuit to ground in Wire harness between fuse - Battery ECU assembly.

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK POWER SUPPLY CIRCUIT (OPEN). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Check that the Service plug is disconnected. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .
    2. Disconnect Battery ECU assembly connector (HV42).
    3. Connect the ground terminal to Battery (12V). Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    4. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    5. Using a tester, measure the voltage between Battery ECU assembly connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV42) No. 1 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

      (HV42) No. 24 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

      (HV42) No. 47 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    Is the voltage 10 V or more?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Repair or replace the Wire harness between fuse and Battery ECU assembly connector.

  3. CHECK GROUND CIRCUIT (OPEN). 
    1. Turn the ignition to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the ground terminal from Battery (12V). Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    3. Using a tester, measure the resistance between Battery ECU assembly connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV42) No. 9 - Chassis ground:

      (HV42) No. 29 - Chassis ground:

      (HV42) No. 48 - Chassis ground:

      (HV42) No. 53 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Repair or replace the Wire harness between Battery ECU assembly connector and chassis ground.

  4. CHECK LAN SYSTEM. 
    1. Restore the vehicle to its original state.
    2. Inspect LAN system. Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .

    Is there any fault?

    Yes:  Perform the inspection according to the diagnosis for LAN system.

    No:  Go to  5.

  5. CHECK Battery ECU assembly. 

    Communicate with [P-HEV High Voltage Battery].

    Is communication possible?

    Yes:  The circuit has returned to a normal condition at this time. Reproduce the failure, and then perform the diagnosis again.

    NOTE: In this case, temporary poor contact of connector, temporary open or short circuit of Wire harness may be the cause.

    No:  Replace Battery ECU assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>BATTERY CONTROL MODULE .
