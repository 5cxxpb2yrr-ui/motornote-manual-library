Warning Light Illumination Pattern: Inspection

  • EyeSight warning indicator (yellow)
    • Illuminates or blinks when error occurs in EyeSight system.
  • EyeSight temporary stop indicator light (white)
    • Illuminates when EyeSight system is stopped temporarily.
    • When the ignition switch is on, it illuminates if the cruise control main switch or the lane keep switch is depressed within approximately 7 seconds after the engine starts, and goes off when approximately 7 seconds have elapsed since the engine started.
  • Lane departure warning OFF indicator light
    • Illuminates when the lane departure warning and the sway warning are OFF.
    • Illuminates when the ignition switch is turned ON, and in approximately 7 seconds after engine starts, and it goes off or illuminates depending on the current status (ON or OFF).
  • Pre-collision brake OFF indicator light
    • Illuminates when pre-collision brake, AT rapid start prevention control are OFF.
    • Illuminates when the ignition switch is turned ON, and goes off in approximately 7 seconds since the engine started.
NOTE:
  • When warning indicators and/or indicators are faulty, refer to Diagnostics with Phenomenon. Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON .
