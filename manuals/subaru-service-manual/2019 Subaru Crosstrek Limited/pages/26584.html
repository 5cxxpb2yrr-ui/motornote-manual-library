Overview Of Error

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly monitors the high voltage wiring between HV battery and Inverter with converter assembly, and detects malfunctions of power supply to Inverter with converter assembly.

The following are possible causes of the error.

  • Fault of Inside of inverter voltage sensor (VH) system
    • Voltage sensor (VH) malfunction
    • Malfunction of Motor generator control ECU
    • Malfunction of communication (Wire harness)
  • High voltage system fault
    • Malfunction of HV battery
    • Malfunction of HV battery junction block
    • Malfunction of Inverter with converter assembly
    • High voltage Wire harness malfunction
    • High voltage connector, connection malfunction
  • Low voltage system (12 V) fault
    • Malfunction of Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly
    • Malfunction of HV battery junction block
    • Low voltage Wire harness malfunction
    • Low voltage connector malfunction
