Except For Hybrid Model

NOTE: The Reverse Automatic Braking Control Module is installed under the front seat LH. Remove the Reverse Automatic Braking Control Module after sliding the front seat LH as necessary.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Ref. to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the Reverse Automatic Braking Control Module.
    1. Remove the nut to remove the harness clip.
    2. Disconnect the connector and remove the Reverse Automatic Braking Control Module.
