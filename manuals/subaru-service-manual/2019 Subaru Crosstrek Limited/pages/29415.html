Water Pipe LH

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Temporarily install the water pipe LH with a new O-ring.
  2. Tighten the bolts in the numerical order as shown in the figure.

    Tightening torque: 

    6.4 N.m (0.7 kgf-m, 4.7 ft-lb)

    Fig 1: Water Pipe LH Bolts Tightening Sequence
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Lower the engine and remove the lifting device and wire ropes.
  4. Install the nuts which hold the engine mounting to the front crossmember.
    CAUTION: Use new nuts.
    NOTE: Make sure that locators (a) of the engine mounting are securely inserted.

    Tightening torque: 

    90 N.m (9.2 kgf-m, 66.4 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Install the front exhaust pipe.

    Refer to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FRONT EXHAUST PIPE>INSTALLATION .

  6. Install the A/C accumulator assembly.

    Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>ACCUMULATOR>INSTALLATION .

  7. Install the transmission bracket.

    Tightening torque: 

    8 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.9 ft-lb)

  8. Install the pitching stopper.

    Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TH2A)>TRANSMISSION MOUNTING SYSTEM>INSTALLATION>PITCHING STOPPER .

  9. Connect the preheater hose (A) and heater outlet hose (B) to the water pipe LH.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  10. Install the air intake boot.

    Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>Air Intake Boot>INSTALLATION .

  11. Install the air intake duct.

    Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>AIR INTAKE DUCT>INSTALLATION .

  12. Install the collector cover.

    Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>COLLECTOR COVER>INSTALLATION .

  13. Connect the battery ground terminal.

    Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .

  14. Charge refrigerant.

    Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>REFRIGERANT CHARGING PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .

  15. Fill engine coolant. (Engine side)

    Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>ENGINE COOLANT>REPLACEMENT>FILLING OF ENGINE COOLANT>ENGINE SIDE .

  16. Set the front hood to the normal position.

    Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>FRONT HOOD STAY .

  17. Install the striker - front hood.

    Refer to EXTERIOR BODY PANELS>FRONT HOOD>ASSEMBLY .
