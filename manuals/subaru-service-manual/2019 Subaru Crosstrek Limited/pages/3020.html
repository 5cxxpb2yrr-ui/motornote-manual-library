Ignition Switch Illumination: Inspection

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. CHECK IGNITION SWITCH ILLUMINATION  .
    1. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    2. Check the lighting condition of the ignition switch illumination.

    Does the ignition switch illumination blink?

    Yes:  Check the function setting of body integrated unit. Refer to BODY CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>REGISTRATION BODY INTEGRATED UNIT .

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK IGNITION SWITCH ILLUMINATION OPERATION  .
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Open and close the driver's side door.

    When door is opened/closed, does the ignition switch illumination switch between ON ←→ OFF?

    Yes:  Ignition switch illumination is normal.

    No:  Go to  3.

  3. CHECK CURRENT DATA  .

    Display the [Driver's door SW input] data using Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to BODY CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DATA MONITOR .

    Does the display change to [ON] ←→ [OFF], when the door is opened/closed?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Check the door switch circuit. Refer to SECURITY AND LOCKS>DOOR LOCK CONTROL SYSTEM>INSPECTION>CHECK DOOR SWITCH .

  4. CHECK CURRENT DATA  .

    Display the [key illumination lamp o/p] data using Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to BODY CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DATA MONITOR .

    Does the display change to [ON] ←→ [OFF], when the door is opened/closed?

    Yes:  Check the ignition switch illumination circuit, and repair or replace the harness.

    No:  Check the body integrated unit and replace it if defective. Refer to BODY CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE .
