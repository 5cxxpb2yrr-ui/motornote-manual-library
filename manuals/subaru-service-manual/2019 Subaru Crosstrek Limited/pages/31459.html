INTRODUCTION

This Service Information Bulletin announces a design change made to the side curtain and knee airbag modules on the models listed above to optimize commonization with the Japanese production parts. The change involves the mixture ratio of Argon to Helium used in the inflator gas:

  • OLD  gas ratio: 94% Argon, 6% Helium
  • NEW  gas ratio: 96% Argon, 4% Helium.

All other specifications of these modules are unchanged.

PRODUCTION CHANGE INFORMATION

The new airbag modules listed below were incorporated into production during November 2018.
