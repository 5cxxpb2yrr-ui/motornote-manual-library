HVAC System (Heater, Ventilator And A/C) (Except For HEV): Evaporator: Inspection

  1. Check the evaporator fin for dust. Blow with compressed air or flush fins with water as needed.
  2. If any oil leak is found from the evaporator, replace the evaporator assembly - cooling.
