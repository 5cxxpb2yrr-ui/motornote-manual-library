Related Lines, More Than 52 Ω And Less Than 62 Ω (MAIN-CAN_Steering Angle Sensor)

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Wiring diagram: 

CAN communication system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>CAN COMMUNICATION SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

  1. CHECK BETWEEN RELATED LINES AND MAIN WIRING HARNESS. 
    1. Disconnect the steering angle sensor connector.
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between terminals of steering angle sensor connector.

      Connector & terminal 

      (i74) No. 2 - (i74) No. 3:

    Is the resistance 400 Ω or more?

    Yes:  A related line between steering angle sensor and main wiring harness is open, or main wiring harness is open at two places or more.

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK RELATED LINES. 
    1. Disconnect the central gateway control module connector.
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between the steering angle sensor connector and central gateway control module.

      Connector & terminal 

      (i74) No. 2 - (i366) No. 7:

      (i74) No. 3 - (i366) No. 22:

    Is the resistance 10 Ω or more?

    Yes:  Repair or replace the open circuit of steering angle sensor related lines.

    No:  Check DTC of VDC. Refer to BRAKE CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .
