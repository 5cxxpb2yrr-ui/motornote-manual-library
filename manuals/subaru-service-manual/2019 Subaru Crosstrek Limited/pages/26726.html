Malfunction Description

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Faulty condition of Inverter water pump is detected. The following cases are assumed to be causes of this symptom.

The following cases are assumed to be malfunction causes:

  • Malfunction of Inverter water pump
    • Malfunction of Inverter water pump inner circuit
    • Open or short circuit of Wire harness
    • Improperly mated connector
  • Hybrid cooling system failure
    • Foreign matter caught in Inverter water pump
