Fuel Filler Hose Connection

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

• FILLER PIPE SIDE 

Securely insert the hose to the spool, and then install the clamp as shown in the figure.

Tightening torque: 

2.5 N.m (0.3 kgf-m, 1.8 ft-lb)

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.

• FUEL TANK SIDE 

  1. Using a marker pen, make alignment marks (A) on the fuel filler hose and the clamp.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Make an alignment mark (B) on the position which is shifted by 45 degrees to the alignment mark (A) on the fuel filler hose. Then, align the alignment mark (B) with the alignment mark (A) on the clamp, and install the clamp.

    Tightening torque: 

    2 N.m (0.2 kgf-m, 1.5 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
