Fender Panel

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY>GROUND TERMINAL REMOVAL OR INSTALLATION .
  2. Remove the bumper face - front. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>FRONT BUMPER>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the light assembly - head. Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>HEADLIGHT ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL .
  4. Release the clips and claws, and then remove the cowl panel - on the left and right sides.
    CAUTION: Pulling with excessive force may damage the cowl panel - side. If it is difficult to remove, use a plastic remover or equivalent tool.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the cover - fender UPR.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Remove the insulator - fender rear. (Equipped model only)
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Remove the mud guard - front. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>MUD GUARD>REMOVAL .
  8. Remove the spoiler assembly - side. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>SIDE GARNISH & SIDE SPOILER>REMOVAL .
  9. Remove the cover - A pillar assembly.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  10. Remove the fender - front.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
