DTC P0A81-96: Hybrid/EV Battery Cooling Fan 1 Component Internal Failure: Notes

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

1. - 

Outline of diagnosis: 

When the actual speed of Battery cooling blower assembly RH is out of specification with respect to the target speed of Battery ECU assembly

(At malfunction of battery cooling fan frequency Lo (or Hi), this DTC is not detected)

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

Normal driving

Wiring diagram: 

Hybrid system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK DTC. 

    Read the following DTCs using Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) . Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    • P-HEV High Voltage Battery
    • P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control
    NOTE:
    System Related DTC
    P-HEV High Voltage Battery P060A47 HYBRID/EV BATTERY ENERGY CONTROL MODULE MONITORING PROCESSOR WATCHDOG/SAFETY MCU FAILURE
    P060B49 HYBRID/EV BATTERY ENERGY CONTROL MODULE A/D PROCESSING INTERNAL ELECTRONIC FAILURE
    P060687 HYBRID/EV BATTERY ENERGY CONTROL MODULE PROCESSOR TO MONITORING PROCESSOR MISSING MESSAGE
    P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control P0A1F94 HYBRID/EV BATTERY ENERGY CONTROL MODULE UNEXPECTED OPERATION

    When "Either DTC P0A8196 or P0A9696 is displayed" or "DTC other than above is displayed", go to  A.

    When DTCs related to [P-HEV High Voltage Battery], mentioned above, are simultaneously displayed, go to  B.

    When DTCs related to [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control], mentioned above, are simultaneously displayed, go to  C.

    Yes: 

    1. Go to  2.
    2. Perform the diagnosis for the simultaneously displayed DTCs.
      NOTE: If more than one DTC is simultaneously displayed, perform the diagnosis in the order described in the above.
    3. Perform the diagnosis for the simultaneously displayed DTCs.
    NOTE: If more than one DTC is simultaneously displayed, perform the diagnosis in the order described in the above.

    No:  -

  2. CHECK DTC. 
    1. Turn the ignition to OFF.
    2. Check DTC when the vehicle is brought in.

    When "DTC P0A8196" is displayed, go to  A.

    When "DTC P0A9696" is displayed, go to  B.

    Yes: 

    1. Go to  3.
    2. Go to  11.

    No:  -

  3. CHECK FILTER, DUCT AND BLOWER. 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Turn the ignition to OFF.
    2. Check that the Service plug is disconnected. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .
    3. Check the filter and ducts for dislocation, clogging with foreign matters or breakage. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY COOLING SYSTEM .

    Is there any dislocation, clogging with foreign matters or breakage in the filter, ducts and blower?

    Yes:  Remove the clogging foreign matters or replace the relevant part.

    No:  Go to  4.

  4. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Battery ECU assembly - Battery cooling blower assembly RH) (OPEN). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Disconnect Battery ECU assembly connector (HV42).
    2. Disconnect Battery cooling blower assembly RH connector (R538).
    3. Using a tester, measure the resistance between Battery ECU assembly connector and Battery cooling blower assembly RH connector.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV42) No. 26 - (R538) No. 1:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  5.

    No:  Repair or replace the Wire harness between Battery ECU assembly connector and Battery cooling blower assembly RH connector.

  5. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Battery ECU assembly - Battery cooling blower assembly RH) (SHORT TO GROUND AND LINE-TO-LINE SHORT). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between terminals in Battery cooling blower assembly connector, and between Battery cooling blower assembly connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (R538) No. 1 - (R538) No. 1 - 4:

    (R538) No. 1 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance 10 kΩ or more?

    Yes:  Go to  6.

    No:  Repair or replace the Wire harness between Battery ECU assembly connector and Battery cooling blower assembly RH connector.

  6. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Battery ECU assembly - Battery cooling blower assembly RH) (SHORT TO POWER SUPPLY). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Connect the ground terminal to Battery (12V). Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    2. Turn to IG ON.
    3. Using a tester, measure the voltage between Battery ECU assembly connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV42) No. 26 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    Is the voltage less than 1 V?

    Yes:  Go to  7.

    No:  Repair or replace the Wire harness between Battery ECU assembly connector and Battery cooling blower assembly RH connector.

  7. CHECK Battery ECU assembly AS A SINGLE UNIT (GND SHORT CHECK). 
    1. Turn the ignition to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the ground terminal from Battery (12V). Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    3. Remove Battery ECU assembly.
    4. Using a tester, measure the resistance between Battery ECU assembly connector terminals.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV42) No. 25 - (HV42) No. 9:

      (HV42) No. 25 - (HV42) No. 29:

    Is the resistance 10 kΩ or more?

    Yes:  Go to  8.

    No:  Replace Battery ECU assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>BATTERY CONTROL MODULE .

  8. CHECK DATA MONITOR (Hybrid Battery Cooling Fan 1 Frequency). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Turn the ignition to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the ground terminal from Battery (12V). Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    3. Connect the connector (R538) of Battery cooling blower assembly RH.
    4. Connect Battery ECU assembly connector (HV42).
    5. Connect the ground terminal to Battery (12V). Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    6. Connect the Subaru Select Monitor.
    7. Turn to IG ON.
    8. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, perform the clear memory of [P-HEV High Voltage Battery]. Refer to COMMON (DIAG)>CLEAR MEMORY .
    9. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, select the following items in the [Active Test] of [P-HEV High Voltage Battery]. Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>ACTIVE TEST .
      • Active test item: Control the Hybrid Battery Cooling Fan
      • Signal selection: Hybrid Battery Cooling Fan 1 Frequency
      NOTE: Perform with HV battery intake air temperature at -10°C (14°F) or more.
    10. Select fan mode (1 - 6) to operate the Battery cooling blower assembly RH.
      NOTE: If the active test cannot be performed, a command for Battery cooling blower assembly RH to drive may be executed due to failsafe of Battery cooling blower assembly RH. In this case, proceed to the following operation check without performing active test.
    11. During the operation of Battery cooling blower assembly RH, use the data monitor value [Hybrid Battery Cooling Fan 1 Frequency] and an oscilloscope to check the actual value in Rear harness LH connector (R534).

      Connector & terminal 

      (R534) No. 17 - Chassis ground:

    Are both data monitor value [Hybrid Battery Cooling Fan 1 Frequency] and the actual value 0 Hz?

    NOTE: Compare individual fan modes (1 - 6). If the active test cannot be performed, comparison must be made with the current mode only.

    Yes:  Go to  9.

    No:  Go to  10.

  9. CHECK Battery ECU assembly. 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Disconnect Battery cooling blower assembly RH connector (R538).
    2. Connect the ground terminal to Battery (12V). Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    3. Turn to IG ON.
    4. Using a tester, measure the voltage between terminals in Battery cooling blower assembly RH connector (R538).

      Connector & terminal 

      (R538) No. 1 (+) - (R538) No. 3 (-):

    Is the voltage 4.5 - 5.5 V?

    Yes:  Replace Battery cooling blower assembly RH. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY COOLING SYSTEM .

    No:  Replace Battery ECU assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>BATTERY CONTROL MODULE .

  10. CHECK Battery ECU assembly (Hybrid Battery Cooling Fan 1 Frequency). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Connect the ground terminal to Battery (12V). Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    2. Connect the Subaru Select Monitor.
    3. Turn to IG ON.
    4. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, perform the clear memory of [P-HEV High Voltage Battery]. Refer to COMMON (DIAG)>CLEAR MEMORY .
    5. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, select the following items in the [Active Test] of [P-HEV High Voltage Battery]. Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>ACTIVE TEST .
      • Active test item: Control the Hybrid Battery Cooling Fan
      • Signal selection: Hybrid Battery Cooling Fan 1 Frequency
      NOTE: Perform with HV battery intake air temperature at -10°C (14°F) or more.
    6. Select fan mode (1 - 6) to operate the Battery cooling blower assembly RH.
      NOTE: If the active test cannot be performed, a command for Battery cooling blower assembly RH to drive may be executed due to failsafe of Battery cooling blower assembly RH. In this case, proceed to the following operation check without performing active test.
    7. During the operation of Battery cooling blower assembly RH, use the data monitor value [Hybrid Battery Cooling Fan 1 Frequency] and an oscilloscope to check the actual value in Rear harness LH connector (R534).

      Connector & terminal 

      (R534) No. 17 - Chassis ground:

    Is the difference between the data monitor value [Hybrid Battery Cooling Fan 1 Frequency] and the actual value 10% or less?

    NOTE: Compare individual fan modes (1 - 6). If the active test cannot be performed, comparison must be made with the current mode only.

    Yes:  Replace Battery cooling blower assembly RH. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY COOLING SYSTEM .

    No:  Replace Battery ECU assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>BATTERY CONTROL MODULE .
