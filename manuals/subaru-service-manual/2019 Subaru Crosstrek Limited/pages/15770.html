Chain Cover: Disassembly

  1. Remove the oil filler cap.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Remove the engine oil filter and the oil pump union. Refer to LUBRICATION (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>OIL FILTER>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the oil pressure switch. Refer to LUBRICATION (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>OIL PRESSURE SWITCH>REMOVAL .
  4. Remove the engine oil temperature sensor.

    Except for hybrid model: 

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE OIL TEMPERATURE SENSOR>REMOVAL .

    Hybrid model: 

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>ENGINE OIL TEMPERATURE SENSOR>REMOVAL .

  5. Remove the oil control solenoid.

    Except for hybrid model: 

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>OIL CONTROL SOLENOID>REMOVAL .

    Hybrid model: 

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>OIL CONTROL SOLENOID>REMOVAL .

  6. Remove the camshaft position sensor.

    Except for hybrid model: 

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>CAMSHAFT POSITION SENSOR>REMOVAL .

    Hybrid model: 

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>CAMSHAFT POSITION SENSOR>REMOVAL .
