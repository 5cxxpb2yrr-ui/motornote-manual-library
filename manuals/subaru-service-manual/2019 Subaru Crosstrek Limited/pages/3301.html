Floor Under Protector: Removal

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Lift up the vehicle.
    CAUTION: Do not use a plate type lift.
  2. Remove the two bolts tightened together with the fuel tank protector and inverter assembly (with converter) cover No. 2 (hybrid model only).
  3. Remove the bolts and clips, and remove the floor under protector.
