DTC P0138: O2 Sensor Circuit High Voltage Bank 1 Sensor 2: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

1. - 

DTC detecting condition: 

Detected when two consecutive driving cycles with fault occur.

Wiring diagram: 

Engine Electrical System Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>ENGINE ELECTRICAL SYSTEM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: Use the check board when measuring the ECM terminal voltage and resistance. Refer to ENGINE (DIAGNOSTICS) (H4DO (HEV))>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>PREPARATION TOOL>HOW TO USE CHECK BOARD .
NOTE: After the faulty parts are repaired or replaced, perform the final check in Basic Diagnostic Procedure. Refer to ENGINE (DIAGNOSTICS) (H4DO (HEV))>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .
  1. CHECK REAR OXYGEN SENSOR DATA  .
    1. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    2. Navigate to Inspection Mode for measuring Exhaust Gas. Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>Work Support>OPERATION>SERVICE MODE .
      NOTE: If it is necessary to run the engine for 10 minutes or more, fasten the driver's seat belt.
    3. Warm up the engine until the engine coolant temperature exceeds 75°C (167°F), and decrease the engine speed rapidly from 2000 rpm.
    4. Using the Subaru Select Monitor or a general scan tool, read the value of [Oxygen sensor #12].
      NOTE: For detailed operation procedures, refer to "Data Monitor". Refer to COMMON (DIAG)>DATA MONITOR .

    Is the value of [Oxygen sensor #12] 0.25 V or less?

    Yes:  Go to  5.

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK REAR OXYGEN SENSOR CONNECTOR  .

    Check the rear oxygen sensor connector.

    Has water entered the connector?

    Yes:  Completely remove any water inside.

    Yes:  Go to  3.

  3. CHECK HARNESS BETWEEN ECM AND REAR OXYGEN SENSOR (OPEN)  .
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the connector from ECM.
    3. Disconnect the connector from the rear oxygen sensor.
    4. Measure the resistance of harness between ECM connector and rear oxygen sensor connector.

      Connector & terminal 

      (B134) No. 33 - (B19) No. 3:

      (B134) No. 44 - (B19) No. 4:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Repair the open circuit of harness between ECM connector and rear oxygen sensor connector.

  4. CHECK HARNESS BETWEEN ECM AND REAR OXYGEN SENSOR  .
    1. Connect the connector to ECM.
    2. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    3. Measure the voltage between rear oxygen sensor connector and engine ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (B19) No. 3 (+) - Engine ground (-):

    Is the voltage 2.2 V or less?

    Yes:  Replace the rear oxygen sensor. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>REAR OXYGEN SENSOR .

    No:  Replace the ECM. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>ENGINE CONTROL MODULE (ECM) .

  5. CHECK EXHAUST SYSTEM  .

    Check exhaust system parts.

    NOTE: Check the following items.
    • Looseness and improper fitting of exhaust system parts
    • Damage (crack, hole etc.) of parts
    • Loose part and improper installation between front oxygen (A/F) sensor and rear oxygen sensor

    Is there any fault in exhaust system?

    Yes:  Repair or replace faulty parts.

    Yes:  Replace the rear oxygen sensor. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>REAR OXYGEN SENSOR .
