Rear Window Defogger System: Repair

  1. Clean the broken portion with alcohol or appropriate cleaning solvent.
  2. Mask both side of wire with masking tape.
  3. Apply the conductive silver composition to the broken portion.

    Preparation items: 

    Conductive silver composition: DUPONT No. 4817 or equivalent

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. After repair, check the wire.
