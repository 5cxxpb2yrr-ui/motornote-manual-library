INTRODUCTION

This bulletin announces the availability of new oxygen sensors developed to address the following Diagnostic Trouble Codes (DTCs) detected by the Engine Control Module:

  • P0137:  O2 Sensor Circuit Low Voltage Bank 1 Sensor 2
  • P0138:  O2 Sensor Circuit High Voltage Bank 1 Sensor 2
  • P0140:  O2 Sensor Circuit No Activity Detected Bank 1 Sensor 2
  • P0141:  O2 Sensor Heater Circuit Bank 1 Sensor 2

The new sensors have an optimized coating application to help prevent cracking within the internal surfaces reducing any possibility of short/open circuits. If any of the DTCs listed above are stored in the ECM, perform the sensor identification procedures outlined below.
