Rear Disc Brake Assembly: Inspection

  1. Check the piston sliding part of caliper body and the piston - disc brake for uneven wear, damage or rust.
  2. Check the rubber parts for damage or deterioration.
  3. If faulty is found in the inspection, replace the relevant part.
