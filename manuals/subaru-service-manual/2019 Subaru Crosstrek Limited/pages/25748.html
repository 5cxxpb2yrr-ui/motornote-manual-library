Warning Light Does Not Illuminate

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Detecting condition: 

  • Hybrid fail lamp faulty
  • Short or open circuit between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly and combination meter
  • Grounding circuit is faulty.
  • Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly is faulty
  • Connector connection is improper.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK LAN SYSTEM  .

    Perform the diagnosis for LAN system. Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Perform the inspection according to the diagnosis for LAN system.

  2. CHECK FOR POOR CONTACT  .
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Check the connecting condition of the connector for Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL MODULE .

    Is there poor contact of connector?

    Yes:  Connect the connector securely. Or repair the poor contact of connector.

    No:  Go to  3.

  3. CHECK COMBINATION METER  .

    Check the combination meter. Refer to COMBINATION METER (DIAGNOSTICS)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Replace Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL MODULE .

    No:  Perform the inspection according to the diagnosis for combination meter. Refer to COMBINATION METER (DIAGNOSTICS)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE .
