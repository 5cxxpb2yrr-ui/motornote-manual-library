Control Valve Body: Installation

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Clean the mating surface of valve cover and transmission side.
    CAUTION: When cleaning the mating surface of the transmission side, be careful not to allow any dust, foreign matter and used liquid gasket to enter the transmission.
  2. Check the control valve body for dust and other foreign matter.
  3. Install the O-rings.
    NOTE:
    • Use new O-rings.
    • Apply CVTF to the O-rings.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Install the control valve body.
    1. Install the control valve body to the transmission.
      CAUTION:
      • Do not damage the O-ring.
      • Perform installation so that the O-ring is not displaced.
    2. Temporarily tighten the bolt (A: silver).
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Attach the bolt (B).

      Tightening torque: 

      9 N.m (0.9 kgf-m, 6.6 ft-lb)

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    4. Tighten the bolt (A: silver).

      Tightening torque: 

      9 N.m (0.9 kgf-m, 6.6 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Install the O-ring to the transmission harness.
    NOTE:
    • Use new O-rings.
    • Apply CVTF to the O-rings.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Install the transmission harness to the valve cover.

    Tightening torque: 

    7 N.m (0.7 kgf-m, 5.2 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Install the gasket to the transmission.
    NOTE: Use a new gasket.
  8. Apply liquid gasket to the oval hole of gasket.

    Liquid gasket: 

    THREE BOND 1215B or equivalent

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Install the valve cover.
    CAUTION: Be careful not to catch the sheet of the ST.

    Tightening torque: 

    8 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.9 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  10. Remove the ST (SHEET SPECIAL TOOL).
  11. Install the throttle body. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>THROTTLE BODY>INSTALLATION .
  12. Install the transmission harness clip and transmission ground terminal.

    Tightening torque: 

    14 N.m (1.4 kgf-m, 10.3 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  13. Install the TCM. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>TRANSMISSION CONTROL MODULE (TCM)>INSTALLATION .
  14. Insert the transmission case cover (small) between transmission case cover (large) and transmission to install.

    Tightening torque: 

    Cover (small): 7 N.m (0.7 kgf-m, 5.2 ft-lb)

    Cover (large): 8 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.9 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  15. Install the pitching stopper bracket.

    Tightening torque: 

    41 N.m (4.2 kgf-m, 30.2 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  16. Install the pitching stopper.

    Tightening torque: 

    T1: 50 N.m (5.1 kgf-m, 36.9 ft-lb)

    T2: 58 N.m (5.9 kgf-m, 42.8 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  17. Install the transmission radio ground cord.

    Tightening torque: 

    7 N.m (0.7 kgf-m, 5.2 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  18. Install the air breather hose and air breather hose bracket.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  19. Connect the transmission harness connector.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  20. Connect the transmission radio ground terminal.

    Tightening torque: 

    32.3 N.m (3.3 kgf-m, 23.8 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  21. Install the air intake boot. Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>Air Intake Boot>INSTALLATION .
  22. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  23. Adjust the CVTF level. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>CVTF>ADJUSTMENT .
  24. Perform the operation of AT learning mode. Refer to TRANSMISSION (DIAGNOSTICS)>LEARNING CONTROL .
