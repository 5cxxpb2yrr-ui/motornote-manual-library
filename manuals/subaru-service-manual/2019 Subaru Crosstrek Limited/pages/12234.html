Cold Air Does Not Come Out Even When The A/C Switch Is Pressed. The Glass Cannot Be Defogged. (Compressor Does Not Operate. (Variable))

Trouble symptom: 

Compressor does not operate even when the A/C switch is turned on and the fan switch is set to LO - HI.

Trouble causes: 

Compressor failure

Wiring diagram: 

Air conditioning system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (IMPREZA MODEL)>AIR CONDITIONING SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>AIR CONDITIONING SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK A/C CONTROL PANEL. 
    1. Leave the vehicle under the condition at ambient temperature of 15°C (59°F) or more.
    2. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    3. Set the fan switch to maximum position.
    4. Press the A/C switch.

    Is A/C indicator displayed on the A/C control panel? (Current malfunction)

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Replace the A/C control panel. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>CONTROL PANEL .

  2. CHECK VARIABLE FLOW CHANGE SOLENOID DUTY VALUE. 

    Using the Subaru Select Monitor, check the following items in the [Data monitor] of [Air Conditioner]. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>DATA MONITOR .

    • Variable Flow Change Solenoid Duty

    Does the display indicate the value greater than zero?

    Yes:  Go to  7.

    No:  Go to  3.

  3. CHECK EVAPORATOR SENSOR. 

    Using the Subaru Select Monitor, check the following items in the [Data monitor] of [Air Conditioner]. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>DATA MONITOR .

    • Evaporator Temperature

    Does the display indicate -0.5 degree or more?

    Yes:  Go to  5.

    No:  Go to  4.

  4. CHECK EVAPORATOR SENSOR. 

    Check the evaporator sensor. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>EVAPORATOR SENSOR .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  5.

    No:  Replace the evaporator sensor. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>EVAPORATOR SENSOR .

  5. CHECK REFRIGERANT PRESSURE SENSOR. 

    Using the Subaru Select Monitor, check the following items in the [Data monitor] of [Air Conditioner]. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>DATA MONITOR .

    • Refrigerant Pressure

    Does the display indicate 0.21 Mpa - 3.03 Mpa?

    Yes:  Go to  7.

    No:  Go to  6.

  6. CHECK AMOUNT OF REFRIGERANT PRESSURE. 

    Check the refrigerant pressure. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>REFRIGERANT PRESSURE WITH MANIFOLD GAUGE SET .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  7.

    No:  Perform repair according to refrigerant pressure inspection.

  7. CHECK FLOW SENSOR CIRCUIT. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    2. Wait at least 1 second.
    3. Read the DTC of [Air Conditioner] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>DATA MONITOR .

    Is DTC B14A5 or B14A6 displayed? (Current malfunction)?

    Yes:  Perform the diagnosis for the displayed DTC B14A5 or B14A6. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE WITH DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>DTC B14A5 REFRIGERANT FLOW SENSOR CIRCUIT OPEN . Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE WITH DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>DTC B14A6 REFRIGERANT FLOW SENSOR CIRCUIT SHORT-CIRCUIT .

    No:  Go to  8.

  8. CHECK VARIABLE SOLENOID CIRCUIT. 
    1. Start the engine.
    2. Turn the A/C switch to ON.
    3. Set the fan switch to maximum position.
    4. Set the temperature control switch to LO, and wait for 30 seconds or more.
    5. Read the DTC of [Air Conditioner] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>DATA MONITOR .

    Is DTC B14A7 displayed? (Current malfunction)

    Yes:  Perform the diagnosis for the displayed DTC B14A7.

    No:  Replace the compressor. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>COMPRESSOR .
