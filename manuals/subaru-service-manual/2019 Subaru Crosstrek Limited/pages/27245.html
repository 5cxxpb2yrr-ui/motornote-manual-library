DTC P0A3F-1F: Drive Motor "A" Position Sensor Circuit Intermittent: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Detecting condition: 

When DTC P0C7917, P0D3319, P1C5D19 or P1C5F19 error judgment occurs and Motor resolver phase-to-phase short circuit and short circuit between the resolver phase signal lines are electrically detected (1 trip detection logic)
