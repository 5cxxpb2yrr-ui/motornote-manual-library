Center Brake Pipe

  1. Perform the accumulator down drive. (Hybrid model) Refer to BRAKE>BRAKE SYSTEM>OPERATION>ACCUMULATOR DOWN DRIVE .
  2. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY>GROUND TERMINAL REMOVAL OR INSTALLATION .
  3. Lift up the vehicle, and then remove the rear wheels.
  4. Remove the crossmember support - rear RH. Refer to FRONT SUSPENSION>CROSSMEMBER SUPPORT>REMOVAL .
  5. Remove the fuel tank protector.
    • Except for hybrid model: Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FUEL TANK PROTECTOR>REMOVAL .
    • Hybrid model: Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL TANK PROTECTOR>REMOVAL .
  6. Remove the floor under protector RH. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>FLOOR UNDER PROTECTOR>REMOVAL .
  7. Remove the rear exhaust pipe. Refer to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>REAR EXHAUST PIPE>REMOVAL .
  8. Remove the propeller shaft assembly. Refer to DRIVE SHAFT SYSTEM>PROPELLER SHAFT>REMOVAL .
  9. Remove the rear differential.
    • T-type: Refer to DIFFERENTIALS>REAR DIFFERENTIAL (T-TYPE)>REMOVAL .
    • VA-type: Refer to DIFFERENTIALS>REAR DIFFERENTIAL (VA-TYPE)>REMOVAL .
  10. Remove the fuel tank.
    • Except for hybrid model: Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FUEL TANK>REMOVAL .
    • Hybrid model: Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL TANK>REMOVAL .
  11. Remove the support - sub frame.

  12. Remove the brake pipe.
    1. Separate each brake pipe using a flare nut wrench.
