Fuel Filter: Installation

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Install O-ring (A), new O-ring (B), and backup ring (C) to the pressure regulator.
    NOTE: Apply gasoline to the O-ring and backup ring.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Install the pressure regulator to the fuel filter assembly.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Install the pressure regulator cap.
    NOTE: After installation, make sure 3 claws are securely engaged in each position.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Install new O-ring (A), spacer (B), and new connector cable (C) to the fuel pump.
    NOTE: Apply gasoline to the O-rings and the spacer.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Install the fuel pump to the fuel filter assembly.
    NOTE: After installation, make sure 3 claws are securely engaged in each position.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Install the fuel pump holder.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Install the fuel filter assembly to the fuel chamber assembly.
    NOTE:
    • Set the tube (a) and spring (b) for the fuel filter assembly as shown in the figure.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • Check that the claws fastening the fuel camber assembly and the fuel filter assembly are securely engaged.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Connect the tube assembly to the fuel chamber assembly.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Push the fuel filter assembly in the direction of the arrow to compress, and attach the new clip.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  10. Connect the connector cable.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  11. Install the fuel level sensor. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL LEVEL SENSOR>INSTALLATION .
  12. Inspect the fuel level sensor. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL LEVEL SENSOR>INSPECTION .
  13. Install the fuel pump assembly. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL PUMP>INSTALLATION .
