HVAC System (Heater, Ventilator And A/C) (Except For HEV): Condenser: Removal

  1. Using the refrigerant recovery system, discharge refrigerant. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>REFRIGERANT RECOVERY PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .
  2. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  3. Remove the air intake duct. Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>AIR INTAKE DUCT>REMOVAL .
  4. Remove the lock assembly - front hood. Refer to SECURITY AND LOCKS>FRONT HOOD LOCK ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL .
  5. Remove the bumper face - front. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>FRONT BUMPER>REMOVAL .
  6. Remove the bracket - bumper UPR and radiator upper frame. Refer to COOLING (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>RADIATOR>REMOVAL .
  7. Detach the hose - pressure discharge and the pipe - evaporator cooling.
    1. Remove the bolts and remove the hose - pressure discharge.
    2. Remove the bolt, and detach the pipe - evaporator cooling.
      CAUTION: Seal the disconnected hose, pipe and engaging part of condenser assembly with a plug or vinyl tape to prevent foreign matter from entering.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Remove the condenser assembly - air conditioner.
    1. Release the hook and remove the guide.
    2. Remove the condenser assembly - air conditioner as shown in the figure.
      CAUTION: Be careful not to damage the condenser and radiator fin. If a damaged fin is found, repair it using a thin screwdriver.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
