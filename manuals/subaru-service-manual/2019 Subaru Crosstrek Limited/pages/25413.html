Diagnostic Chart

Symptoms Repair order
Seat heater does not operate. 1. Check the fuse. Refer to SEATS>SEAT HEATER SYSTEM>INSPECTION>CHECK SEAT HEATER FUSE .
2. Check the seat heater system power supply and ground circuit. Refer to SEATS>SEAT HEATER SYSTEM>INSPECTION>CHECK POWER SUPPLY AND GROUND CIRCUIT .
3. Check the seat heater module. Refer to SEATS>SEAT HEATER SYSTEM>INSPECTION>SEAT HEATER UNIT .
4. Check the seat heater switch circuit. Refer to SEATS>SEAT HEATER SYSTEM>INSPECTION>SEAT HEATER SWITCH .
