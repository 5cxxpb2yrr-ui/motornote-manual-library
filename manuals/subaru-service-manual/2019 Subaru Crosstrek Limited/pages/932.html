Rear Antenna

• 4 DOOR MODEL 

CAUTION: Do not detach the antenna except when replacing it. Detaching unnecessarily will lead to insecure attachment of the antenna.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the rear antenna assembly - interior.
    1. Disconnect the connector.
    2. Release the clips, and remove the rear antenna assembly - interior.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.

• 5 DOOR MODEL (EXCEPT FOR HYBRID) 

CAUTION: Do not detach the antenna except when replacing it. Detaching unnecessarily will lead to insecure attachment of the antenna.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the mat - rear floor, spacer - rear floor side LH, and spacer - rear floor side RH.
  3. Remove the spacer - rear floor CTR.
    1. Release the clips, and fold back the bottom of the cover COMPL - rear backrest LH and RH of the rear seat back.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Release the clips, then detach the spacer - rear floor CTR.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the rear antenna assembly - interior.
    1. Disconnect the connector.
    2. Release the clips, and remove the rear antenna assembly - interior.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.

• 5 DOOR MODEL (HYBRID) 

CAUTION: Do not detach the antenna except when replacing it. Detaching unnecessarily will lead to insecure attachment of the antenna.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Take out the mat rear floor.
    1. Release the clips and hooks, and remove the mat - rear floor.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the clips, and remove the box - sub-trunk.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the rear antenna assembly - interior.
    1. Disconnect the connector.
    2. Release the clips, and remove the rear antenna assembly - interior.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
