52 Ω Or Less (BODY-CAN)

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Wiring diagram: 

CAN communication system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>CAN COMMUNICATION SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
NOTE: When the bus line is measured, combined resistance of the end resistance (120 Ω) in the central gateway control module and the end resistance (120 Ω) in body integrated unit can be measured. The combined resistance is more than 52 Ω and less than 62 Ω with the stabilizing circuit included. When the measured resistance value is 52 Ω or less, main wiring harness or related lines may be shorted, or combined resistance may have changed because resistance other than end resistance is created on the circuit.
  1. CHECK BETWEEN RELATED LINES AND MAIN WIRING HARNESS. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between audio or navigation unit connector terminals.

      Connector & terminal 

      (i87) No. 9 - (i87) No. 10:

    Is the resistance less than 10 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Go to  3.

  2. CHECK MAIN WIRING HARNESS AND RELATED LINES. 

    With a tester connected, disconnect control module connectors in order.

    NOTE: Disconnect the central gateway control module at the end.

    Connector & terminal 

    (i87) No. 9 - (i87) No. 10:

    Is there any control module whose condition has changed from short state?

    Yes:  Replace the control module whose resistance has changed. When the value changed at disconnecting the central gateway control module, replace the central gateway control module. Refer to SECURITY AND LOCKS>GATEWAY control module .

    Go to  3.

    No:  Repair or replace the short circuit of the harness between the central gateway control module and body integrated unit.

  3. CHECK CONTROL MODULE. 
    1. Disconnect the connectors of the central gateway control module and body integrated unit.
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between control module terminals.

      Connector & terminal 

      (i366) No. 15 - (i366) No. 16:

      (B281) No. 17 - (B281) No. 19:

    Is the resistance 114 - 126 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Replace the control module whose end resistance value is out of the specified range.

  4. CHECK HARNESS. 

    Measure the resistance between body integrated unit connector terminals using tester.

    Connector & terminal 

    (B281) No. 17 - (B281) No. 19:

    Is the resistance 1 MΩ or more?

    Yes:  CAN communication circuit is normal.

    No:  Go to  5.

  5. CHECK CONTROL MODULE. 

    With a tester connected, disconnect control modules in order.

    Connector & terminal 

    (B281) No. 17 - (B281) No. 19:

    Are there any control modules whose resistance changed to 1 MΩ or more?

    Yes:  Replace the control module that has changed.

    No:  Repair or replace the harness part which has resistance component.
