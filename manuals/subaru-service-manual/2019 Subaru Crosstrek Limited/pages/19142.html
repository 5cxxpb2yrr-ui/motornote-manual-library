Cooling Pipe

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Using the refrigerant recovery system, discharge refrigerant. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>REFRIGERANT RECOVERY PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .
  2. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  3. Remove the bumper face - front. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>FRONT BUMPER>REMOVAL .
  4. Remove the radiator assembly. Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>RADIATOR>REMOVAL .
  5. Remove the inverter front water pipe.
    1. Remove the bolts and nuts, and remove the inverter front water pipe.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Remove the pipe - evaporator cooling.
    1. Remove the bolt, clip, and clamp, and remove the pipe - evaporator cooling.
    CAUTION:
    • Do not apply excessive force to the pipe.
    • Seal the engaging parts of the disconnected pipe, expansion valve - and cooler assembly, and LOW solenoid valve with plugs or vinyl tape to prevent foreign matter from entering.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
