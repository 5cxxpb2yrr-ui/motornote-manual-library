DTC P0A4B-22: Generator Position Sensor Signal Amplitude>Maximum: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Detecting condition: 

Generator resolver out of range

Detects that the Generator resolver signal deviates from the proper value (large amplitude).

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

  • Reduced output power
  • Engine used while driving and engine speed becomes high
  • When Engine always stopped, A driving range is limited.
NOTE: For the diagnostic procedure, refer to DTC P0A4B21. Refer to P-HEV DRIVE MOTOR (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE WITH DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>DTC P0A4B21 GENERATOR POSITION SENSOR SIGNAL AMPLITUDE < MINIMUM .
