Except For Hybrid Model

NOTE: Before installation, remove the pad clip - rear brake and remove mud, foreign matter and rust from the caliper body assembly and the support - rear disc brake.
  1. Check each part. Refer to BRAKE>REAR DISC BRAKE ASSEMBLY>INSPECTION .
  2. Apply a thin coat of grease to the support - rear disc brake.

    Preparation items: 

    Grease: An item contained in the pad kit, Molykote M7439 or equivalent

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Install the support - rear disc brake to the housing assembly - rear axle.

    Tightening torque: 

    Impreza model: 66 N.m (6.7 kgf-m, 48.7 ft-lb)

    Crosstrek: 110 N.m (11.2 kgf-m, 81.1 ft-lb)

  4. Apply a thin coat of grease to the pad clip, both sides of the shim - disc brake rear inner and the shim mounting surface of the pad - disc brake rear.

    Preparation items: 

    Grease (pad clip (A)): An item contained in the pad kit, Molykote M7439 or equivalent

    Grease (shim and pad (B)): An item contained in the pad kit or WAKO grease V160

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Install the pad clip - rear brake.
  6. Press back the piston - disc brake with a tool as needed.
    CAUTION: When pressing back the piston - disc brake, the clearance between pad - disc brake and disc rotor becomes large, which makes the brake pedal effort softer. After the installation of rear wheel, be sure to perform the adjustment of the clearance and the pedal effort by depressing the brake pedal several times.

    Preparation tool: 

    Disc brake piston tool

  7. Install the pad - disc brake rear.
    NOTE: Install the pad wear indicator in proper direction.
  8. Install the caliper body assembly.

    Tightening torque: 

    27 N.m (2.8 kgf-m, 19.9 ft-lb)

  9. Install the brake hose bracket.

    Tightening torque: 

    33 N.m (3.4 kgf-m, 24.3 ft-lb)

  10. Connect the brake hose using a new brake hose gasket.

    Tightening torque: 

    26 N.m (2.7 kgf-m, 19.2 ft-lb)

  11. Bleed air from the brake line. Refer to BRAKE>AIR BLEEDING>PROCEDURE>BRAKE LINE (EXCEPT FOR HYBRID MODEL) .
  12. Install the rear wheels.

    Tightening torque: 

    120 N.m (12.2 kgf-m, 88.5 ft-lb)
