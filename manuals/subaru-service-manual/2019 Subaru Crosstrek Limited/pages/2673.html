Rear Height Sensor: Installation

CAUTION: Do not apply impact to the sensor assembly - headlight beam leveler or forcibly move the arm. Doing so may cause sensor damage and malfunction.
  1. Install the sensor assembly - headlight beam leveler.

    Tightening torque: 

    7.5 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.5 ft-lb)

  2. Lower the vehicle.
  3. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  4. Perform reinitialization of the auto headlight beam leveler system. Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>AUTO HEADLIGHT BEAM LEVELER SYSTEM>PROCEDURE>REINITIALIZATION .
    NOTE: When replacing the rear vehicle height sensor with a new part, perform the initialization. Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>AUTO HEADLIGHT BEAM LEVELER SYSTEM>PROCEDURE>INITIALIZATION .
