Day Time Running Light System: Note

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

For operation procedures of each component of the daytime running light system, refer to the respective service information.

  • Headlight assembly: Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>HEADLIGHT ASSEMBLY .
  • Headlight bulb: Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>HEADLIGHT ASSEMBLY>DISASSEMBLY .
  • Daytime running light assembly: Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>DAY TIME RUNNING LIGHT ASSEMBLY .
  • Combination switch (light): Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>COMBINATION SWITCH (LIGHT) .
