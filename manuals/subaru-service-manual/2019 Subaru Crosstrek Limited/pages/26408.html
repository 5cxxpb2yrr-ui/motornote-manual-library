Battery Status Information Update

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
NOTE: If HV battery is replaced, be sure to perform [Battery Status Info Update].
After HV battery replacement, it is necessary to update the battery aging status information written in Battery ECU assembly to correct value and turn off the light indicated on Combination meter assembly.
  1. Connect the Subaru Select Monitor.
  2. Turn to IG OFF, and wait for two minutes or more.
  3. Turn to IG ON.
  4. On [Start] screen, select [Diagnosis].
  5. On [Vehicle selection] screen, enter vehicle information and select [OK].
  6. On [Main Menu] screen, select [Each System].
  7. On [Select System] screen, select [P-HEV High Voltage Battery].
  8. On [Select Function] screen, select [Work Support].
  9. On [Work Support] screen, select [Battery Status Info Update].
  10. Turn the ignition to OFF.
