Overview Of Error

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly detects T/M (transmission) lock or malfunction.

The following are possible causes of the error.

  • Engine failure
    • Seizure due to lack of engine oil
    • Seizure due to lack of coolant
    • Engine malfunction
  • Transmission failure
    • Seizure due to lack of ATF
    • Seizure due to lack of differential oil
    • Transmission gear damage
    • Damper (with torque limiter) damage
