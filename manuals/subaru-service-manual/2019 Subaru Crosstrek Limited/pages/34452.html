Fuel Filler Pipe: Disassembly

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Remove the drain filter. Refer to EMISSION CONTROL (AUX. EMISSION CONTROL DEVICES) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>DRAIN FILTER>REMOVAL .
  2. Disassemble the fuel filler pipe assembly. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>COMPONENT>FUEL FILLER PIPE .
NOTE: Using a marker pen, make alignment marks (a) to use for positioning brackets during assembly.
