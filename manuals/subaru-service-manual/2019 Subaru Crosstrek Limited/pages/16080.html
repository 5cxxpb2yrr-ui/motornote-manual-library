Draining Of Engine Coolant

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
NOTE: The radiator has a double-layer design for cooling the engine and the inverter assembly respectively. Therefore draining of engine coolant must be performed to the appropriate part of the radiator.
  • ENGINE SIDE 
    CAUTION:
    • The engine coolant is pressurized when it is hot. Wait until engine coolant cools down before proceeding the work.
    • If the engine coolant splashes, completely remove and clean it.
    1. Remove the radiator cap.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Remove the over flow hose.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Remove the under cover - front.

      Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>FRONT UNDER COVER>REMOVAL .

    4. Connect the removed over flow hose (A) to the drain pipe of the radiator.
      NOTE: When the over flow hose is not used, use a hose with an inner diameter of 8.0 (0.315 in).
    5. Loosen the radiator drain plug to drain engine coolant.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    6. After draining the engine coolant, remove the radiator drain plug.
    7. Install the radiator drain plug with a new O-ring.
    8. Remove the over flow hose from the radiator drain pipe.
    9. Install the under cover - front.

      Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>FRONT UNDER COVER>INSTALLATION .

    10. Install the over flow hose to the original position.
    11. Drain the coolant from reservoir tank.
  • INVERTER ASSEMBLY SIDE 
    CAUTION:
    • The engine coolant is pressurized when it is hot. Wait until engine coolant cools down before proceeding the work.
    • If the engine coolant splashes, completely remove and clean it.
    1. Remove the inverter tank cap.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Remove the under cover - front.

      Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>FRONT UNDER COVER>REMOVAL .

    3. Remove the inverter tank outlet hose from the clamp.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    4. Remove the water pipe (A) from the clamp.
    5. Disconnect the inverter tank outlet hose from the water pipe (A) and drain the engine coolant.
      NOTE: There is no drain plug for draining the engine coolant on the inverter assembly side. Therefore, disconnect the inverter tank outlet hose to drain the engine coolant.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    6. Connect the inverter tank outlet hose to the water pipe (A).
    7. Install the water pipe (A) to the clamp.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    8. Install the inverter tank outlet hose to the clamp.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
