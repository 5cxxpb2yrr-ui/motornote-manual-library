4 Door Model

CAUTION:
  • Before handling the airbag system components, refer to "CAUTION" of "General Description" in "AIRBAG SYSTEM". Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  • Airbag system satellite safing sensor is located under the front center of the rear seat cushion. Be careful not to apply strong impact to the sensor when working with the rear seat cushion removed.
  1. Adjust the passenger's seat position.
    1. Remove the headrest.
    2. Move the passenger's seat to the front-most position, and move the backrest to the front-most position.
  2. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery and wait for at least 60 seconds before starting work. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    NOTE:
    • For CVT models, release the shift lock and shift the select lever to the "N" position. Refer to CONTROL SYSTEMS>SELECT LEVER>REMOVAL .
    • For models with power seat, remove the front seat before removing the battery ground terminal.
  3. Remove the driver's seat. Refer to SEATS>FRONT SEAT>REMOVAL .
  4. Remove the console box assembly and the cover - shift lever. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>CONSOLE BOX>REMOVAL .
  5. Cover the following areas to protect from damage.
    • Steering wheel surface
    • Instrument panel surface
    • Glove box surface
  6. Remove the rear seat cushion assembly. Refer to SEATS>REAR SEAT>REMOVAL .
  7. Remove the lower inner trim on the left and right sides. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>LOWER INNER TRIM>REMOVAL .
  8. Remove the upper inner trim on the left and right sides. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>UPPER INNER TRIM>REMOVAL .
  9. Remove the trim panel - rear pillar LWR.
    1. Remove the clips, and turn over the trim panel - trunk side.
    2. Remove the clips, and remove the trim panel - rear pillar LWR.
    CAUTION: Forcibly removing the clips near the seat belt lower anchor bolts may deform or damage the clip base. Use a clip remover for operation.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  10. Remove the clips, and remove the trim panel - rear pillar UPR.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  11. Remove the following parts from the driver's side rear door.
    • Rear door trim assembly: Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>DOOR TRIM>REMOVAL>REAR DOOR .
    • Rear speaker assembly: Refer to ENTERTAINMENT & MONITORING>REAR SPEAKER>REMOVAL .
  12. Remove the light assembly - map. (Models without EyeSight) Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>SPOT MAP LIGHT>REMOVAL .
  13. Remove the stereo camera cover assembly. (Models with EyeSight) Refer to EYESIGHT>STEREO CAMERA>REMOVAL .
  14. Remove the left and right sun visor assembly. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>SUN VISOR>REMOVAL .
  15. Remove the light assembly - room. Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>ROOM LIGHT>REMOVAL .
  16. Remove the light assembly - high-mounted. Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>HIGH-MOUNTED STOP LIGHT>REMOVAL>4 DOOR MODEL .
  17. Remove all assist grips. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>ASSIST GRIP>REMOVAL .
  18. Remove the garnish - roof. (Model with sunroof)
  19. Remove the trim panel - roof assembly.
    CAUTION: When taking out the trim panel - roof assembly from the vehicle, be sure to fold it to the roof side. Do not fold it to the cabin side. Otherwise it may be wrinkled.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    1. Remove the harness clamp of the cord assembly - antenna feeder and the cord - roof, and disconnect the connectors.
    2. Remove the clip.
    3. Remove the weather strip - body side flange from the front/rear and left/right doors, and remove the trim panel - roof assembly.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    4. While folding the rear end of the trim panel - roof assembly to the roof side along with the mark-off line (a), take the part out of the vehicle.
    5. Align the recess (b) on the center pillar of the trim panel - roof assembly with the flange of the door opening, and bend the entire trim to take it out of the vehicle.
Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
