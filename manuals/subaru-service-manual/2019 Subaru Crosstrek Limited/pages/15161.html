Manual A/C Model

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the grille assembly - CTR ventilation. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>AIR VENT GRILLE>REMOVAL>CENTER GRILLE ASSEMBLY .
  3. Remove the audio assembly or navigation assembly. Refer to ENTERTAINMENT & MONITORING>AUDIO>REMOVAL .
  4. Remove the pocket assembly and the back panel - pocket. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>GLOVE BOX>REMOVAL .
  5. Remove the heater control assembly.
    1. Release the claw, and disconnect the cable - mode and the cable - temperature.
      CAUTION: Check the dial position before disconnecting the cable.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Disconnect the connector.
    3. Press the heater control assembly from behind to release the clips and claws, and then remove the heater control assembly.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    4. Remove the screws and remove the panel assembly - heater.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Remove the cable - mode and the cable - temperature.
    1. Release the claw, and disconnect the cable - mode and the cable - temperature.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
