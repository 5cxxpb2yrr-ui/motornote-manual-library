DTC B1477: A/C Inverter Local Communication

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

DTC detecting condition: 

When the A/C inverter local communication is abnormal.

Trouble symptom: 

Compressor does not operate.

Wiring diagram: 

Air conditioning system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>AIR CONDITIONING SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK DTC. 

    Read the DTC of [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is DTC P058A01 or P162B87 displayed? (Current malfunction)

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Go to  6.

  2. CHECK DTC. 
    1. Disconnect the 12V engine restart battery sensor connector (B9).
    2. Read the DTC of [Air Conditioner] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is DTC B1477 displayed? (Current malfunction)

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Replace the 12V engine restart battery sensor. Refer to STARTING/CHARGING SYSTEMS (H4DO (HEV))>BATTERY SENSOR .

  3. CHECK DTC. 
    1. Connect the 12V engine restart battery sensor connector (B9).
    2. Disconnect Electric compressor ASSEMBLY connector (E175).
    3. Read the DTC of [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is DTC P058A01 or P162B87 displayed? (Current malfunction)

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Replace Electric compressor ASSEMBLY. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>COMPRESSOR .

  4. CHECK HARNESS (OPEN CIRCUIT). 
    1. Disconnect Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector (B605).
    2. Disconnect the 12V engine restart battery sensor connector (B9).
    3. Using a tester, measure the resistance between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector and Electric compressor ASSEMBLY connector, and Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector and 12V engine restart battery sensor connector.

      Connector & terminal 

      (B605) No. 12 - (E175) No. 3:

      (B605) No. 12 - (B9) No. 2:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  5.

    No:  Repair or replace the open circuit of harness.

  5. CHECK HARNESS (GROUND SHORT CIRCUIT). 

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (B605) No. 12 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance 10 kΩ or more?

    Yes:  Replace Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL MODULE .

    No:  Repair or replace the short circuit of the harness.

  6. CHECK IGB RELAY UNIT. 

    Check the IGB relay unit. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>RELAY AND FUSE .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  7.

    No:  Replace the IGB relay. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>RELAY AND FUSE .

  7. CHECK HARNESS (OPEN CIRCUIT). 
    1. Disconnect the keyless access control module connector (i75).
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between the IGB relay connector and keyless access control module connector.

      Connector & terminal 

      (B649) No. 9 - (i75) No. 15:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  8.

    No:  Repair or replace the open circuit of harness.

  8. CHECK HARNESS (GROUND SHORT CIRCUIT). 

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between the IGB relay connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (B649) No. 9 - (i75) No. 15:

    Is the resistance 10 kΩ or more?

    Yes:  Go to  9.

    No:  Repair or replace the short circuit of the harness.

  9. CHECK KEYLESS ACCESS control module. 
    1. Connect the keyless access control module connector (i75).
    2. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    3. Using a tester, measure the voltage between the keyless access control module connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (i75) No. 15 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    Is the voltage 8.5 V or more?

    Yes:  Go to  10.

    No:  Replace the keyless access control module. Refer to SECURITY AND LOCKS>KEYLESS ACCESS control module .

  10. CHECK HARNESS (OPEN CIRCUIT). 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Using a tester, measure the voltage between the IGB relay connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (B649) No. 11 - Chassis ground:

    Is the voltage 11 - 14 V?

    Yes:  Go to  11.

    No:  Repair or replace the open circuit of harness.

  11. CHECK HARNESS (OPEN CIRCUIT). 

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between the IGB relay connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (B649) No. 7 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  12.

    No:  Repair or replace the open circuit of harness.

  12. CHECK HARNESS (OPEN CIRCUIT). 
    1. Install the IGB relay.
    2. Disconnect Electric compressor ASSEMBLY connector (E175).
    3. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    4. Using a tester, measure the voltage between Electric compressor ASSEMBLY connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (E175) No. 1 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    Is the voltage 11 - 14 V?

    Yes:  Go to  13.

    No:  Repair or replace the open circuit of harness.

  13. CHECK HARNESS (OPEN CIRCUIT). 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between Electric compressor ASSEMBLY connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (E175) No. 2 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  14.

    No:  Repair or replace the open circuit of harness.

  14. CHECK HARNESS (OPEN CIRCUIT). 
    1. Disconnect Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector (B605).
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector and Electric compressor ASSEMBLY connector.

      Connector & terminal 

      (B605) No. 12 - (E175) No. 3:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  15.

    No:  Repair or replace the open circuit of harness.

  15. CHECK HARNESS (OPEN CIRCUIT). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Disconnect the connector (HV104) of Electric compressor ASSEMBLY.
    2. Disconnect the connector (HV36) of Inverter with converter assembly.
    3. Using a tester, measure the resistance between the Electric compressor ASSEMBLY connector and the connector of Inverter with converter assembly.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV104) No. 2 - (HV36) No. 1:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  16.

    No:  Go to  17.

  16. CHECK HARNESS (OPEN CIRCUIT). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between the Electric compressor ASSEMBLY connector and the connector of Inverter with converter assembly.

    Connector & terminal 

    (HV104) No. 1 - (HV36) No. 2:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Replace Electric compressor ASSEMBLY. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>COMPRESSOR .

    No:  Replace the HV air conditioning wire. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>POWER CABLE .

  17. CHECK HARNESS (GROUND SHORT CIRCUIT). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between Electric compressor ASSEMBLY connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (HV104) No. 2 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance 10 kΩ or more?

    Yes:  Replace the Electric compressor ASSEMBLY and HV air conditioning wire. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>COMPRESSOR . Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>POWER CABLE .

    No:  Replace the HV air conditioning wire. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>POWER CABLE .
