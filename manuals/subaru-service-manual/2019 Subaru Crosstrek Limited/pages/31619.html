SIDE COWL PANEL TRIM-DESIGN CHANGE

SIDE COWL PANEL TRIM-DESIGN CHANGE

TECHNICAL SERVICE BULLETIN

Reference Number(s): 12-311-21, Date of Issue:  February 25, 2021
SUBARU: 2018-21 Crosstrek

APPLICABILITY

2018-21MY Crosstrek

INTRODUCTION

This bulletin announces a design change made to the windshield side cowl panels to address isolated concerns of a buzzing/rattle-type sound heard while driving. The size of the foam cushion portion and the production process to secure it to the part have been optimized to prevent the condition.

PRODUCTION CHANGE INFORMATION

The new COWL PANEL-SIDE trims were incorporated into Crosstrek production starting with VIN MH277078.

The Service Manual procedures contain information critical to performing an effective repair the first time, every time. This includes but is not limited to important SAFETY precautions, proper inspection criteria, necessary special tools, required processes and related one-time-use parts needed for a complete and lasting repair.
