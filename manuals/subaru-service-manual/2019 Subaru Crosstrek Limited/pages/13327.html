CVTF Cooler (With Warmer Function): Installation

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Install the CVTF cooler (with warmer feature) to the transmission.

    Tightening torque: 

    23 N.m (2.3 kgf-m, 17.0 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Install the water hose (inlet).
    NOTE: With the paint on the water hose (inlet) facing the vehicle outside, install it to the EGR cooler.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Install the CVTF cooler inlet hose and CVTF cooler outlet hose to transmission.
    NOTE: Use new CVTF cooler inlet hose and CVTF cooler outlet hose.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Connect the water hose (outlet).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Check installation condition of each hose.
    • Make sure the hoses do not interfere with each other or with other components.
    • Check each hose for bent, excess curve, and twisting conditions.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Install the purge hose, purge control solenoid valve harness connector and harness clip.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Install the air intake boot. Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>Air Intake Boot>INSTALLATION .
  8. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  9. Fill engine coolant. Refer to COOLING (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE COOLANT>REPLACEMENT .
  10. Adjust the CVTF level. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>CVTF>ADJUSTMENT .
