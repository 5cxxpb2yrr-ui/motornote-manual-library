System Block Diagram

  • The auto headlight beam leveler is a system that automatically adjusts the headlight beam level (vertical directions) according to the longitudinal inclination angle of the vehicle due to heavy load or passengers' getting in/off a car.
  • The steering responsive headlight is a system that calculates the corner R from the steering angle and vehicle speed to illuminate beyond the corner by moving the headlight beam to left and right.
