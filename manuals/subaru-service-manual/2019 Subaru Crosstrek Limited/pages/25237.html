Neutral Position Switch Is Turned To On, And Then The Cruise Control Is Released

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Trouble symptom: 

Cruise control cannot be set.

CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .

Wiring diagram: 

EyeSight system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>EYESIGHT SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  1. CHECK INHIBITOR SWITCH CIRCUIT. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the inhibitor switch connector.
    3. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    4. Measure the voltage between connector terminal and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (T7) No. 7 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    Is the voltage 7.5 - 14 V?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No: 

    • Check the fuse.
    • Check for open or short circuit in the harness between inhibitor switch and hybrid vehicle control computer.
  2. CHECK INHIBITOR SWITCH CIRCUIT. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the hybrid vehicle control computer connector.
    3. Measure the resistance between the inhibitor switch connector terminal and the hybrid vehicle control computer.

      Connector & terminal 

      (T7) No. 2 - (B605) No. 24:

      (T7) No. 4 - (B605) No. 7:

    Is the resistance less than 10 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Repair the harness.

  3. CHECK INHIBITOR SWITCH CIRCUIT. 

    Check the inhibitor switch circuit. Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE WITH DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>DTC P070562 TRANSMISSION RANGE SENSOR "A" CIRCUIT (PRNDL INPUT) SIGNAL COMPARE FAILURE .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Replace the stereo camera. Refer to EYESIGHT>STEREO CAMERA>REMOVAL .

    No:  Repair or replace the faulty portion according to the inspection result.
