DTC B2826: Callback Diagnosis NG

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Detected when stereo camera abnormality is detected by the hybrid vehicle control computer.

DTC detecting condition: 

  • Defective CAN system
  • Defective hybrid vehicle control computer
  • Defective stereo camera

Trouble symptom: 

  • All functions of EyeSight system do not operate.
  • EyeSight warning light illuminates.
    NOTE: Check the LAN system, hybrid vehicle control computer and EyeSight when this DTC is displayed. Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE . Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE . Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .
