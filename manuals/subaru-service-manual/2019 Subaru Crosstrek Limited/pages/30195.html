Charging Inlet

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
WARNING: The hybrid system includes a high voltage circuit. Mishandling could cause accidents such as electric shock or leak. Always check "WARNING (HYBRID SYSTEM)" and perform the proper operation. Refer to PRECAUTION/WARNING>WARNING (HYBRID SYSTEM) .
CAUTION: Airbag system satellite safing sensor is located under the front center of the rear seat cushion. Be careful not to apply strong impact to the sensor when working with the rear seat cushion removed.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery and wait for at least 60 seconds before starting work. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the service disconnect plug. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the rear seat cushion assembly. Refer to SEATS>REAR SEAT>REMOVAL .
  4. Remove the trim panel - rear apron LH. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>REAR QUARTER TRIM>REMOVAL .
  5. Remove the duct - battery cooling blower LH. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY COOLING SYSTEM>REMOVAL>FRONT DUCT .
  6. Remove the battery cooling blower assembly LH. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY COOLING SYSTEM>REMOVAL>COOLING BLOWER .
  7. Remove the duct assembly - cooling out LH. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY COOLING SYSTEM>REMOVAL>REAR DUCT .
  8. Remove the charge lid lock assembly with motor and the saucer SUB-assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>CHARGE INLET>REMOVAL>CHARGING INLET DOOR .
  9. Remove the bumper face - rear. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>REAR BUMPER>REMOVAL .
  10. Remove the EV charger cable lock assembly.
WARNING: Be sure to wear insulated gloves.
  1. Disconnect the HV battery charger wire No. 1 connector.
    CAUTION: Be sure to insulate the disconnected connector by wrapping it with insulating tape.
    1. Use protective tape to wrap a flat tip screwdriver of an appropriate size for the insertion opening, and disengage lock (a).
    2. Press the tab (b) to disconnect the connector.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Remove the cable (yellow), connector clips, and cable clips.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Release the claws, and then remove the grille - air ventilation LH.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the hose clip and pull out the drain hose.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the bolts and remove the EV charger cable lock assembly.
Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
