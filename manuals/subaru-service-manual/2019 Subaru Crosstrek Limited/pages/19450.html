Explanation For Circuits

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly monitors the internal operation of the module, and outputs DTC if it detects a malfunction. Therefore, when these DTCs are output, replace Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly.
