Oil Control Solenoid: Removal: Exhaust

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

• RH SIDE 

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the air intake duct. Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>AIR INTAKE DUCT>REMOVAL .
  3. Disconnect the connector (A) and remove the oil control solenoid.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the backup ring from the oil control solenoid.

• LH SIDE 

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Disconnect the connector (A) and remove the oil control solenoid.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the backup ring from the oil control solenoid.
