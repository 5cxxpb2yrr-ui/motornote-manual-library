Select Cable: Removal

  1. Shift the select lever to "N" range.
  2. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  3. Lift up the vehicle.
  4. Remove the center exhaust cover.
    • Except for Crosstrek hybrid model
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • Crosstrek hybrid model
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the inhibitor cover. (Crosstrek hybrid model)
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Disconnect the select cable from the transmission main body.
    CAUTION: Do not apply excessive load while holding the part (a).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • Except for Crosstrek hybrid model
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • Crosstrek hybrid model
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Disconnect the cable from arm COMPL.
    CAUTION: Do not apply excessive load while holding the part (a).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Raise the claw of clamp to remove the cable from bracket.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Remove the select cable from plate assembly.
