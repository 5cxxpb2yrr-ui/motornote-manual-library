EGR Control Valve: Installation

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Set a new gasket and O-rings.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Install the EGR control valve.

    Tightening torque: 

    19 N.m (1.9 kgf-m, 14.0 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Attach the bracket.

    Tightening torque: 

    19 N.m (1.9 kgf-m, 14.0 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Connect the preheater hose (B) and preheater hose (C).
  5. Connect the connector (A).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Install the air intake duct.

    Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>AIR INTAKE DUCT>INSTALLATION .

  7. Connect the battery ground terminal.

    Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .

  8. Fill the engine coolant (engine side),

    Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>ENGINE COOLANT>REPLACEMENT>FILLING OF ENGINE COOLANT>ENGINE SIDE .
