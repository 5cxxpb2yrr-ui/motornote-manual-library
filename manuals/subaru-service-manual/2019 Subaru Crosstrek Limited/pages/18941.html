DTC P308F-49: High Voltage Charging Circuit Short During Pre-Charge: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

1. - 

Outline of diagnosis: 

Voltage in the charger does not increase during precharging (between CHRP ON to CHRG ON)

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

Plug-in charging suspended

Wiring diagram: 

Hybrid system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

  2. CHECK CONNECTION CONDITION OF CONNECTOR (Battery ECU assembly CONNECTOR)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Check that the Service plug is disconnected. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .
    2. Check the connecting condition of connector (HV42) in Battery ECU assembly and check the relevant terminals for contact pressure, deformation and intrusion of water or foreign matter.

    Is there looseness at the connector mating, or any poor contact, deformation or intrusion of water or foreign matter at the relevant terminals?

    Yes:  Connect the connector securely. Or repair the poor contact of connector.

    No:  Go to  3.

  3. CHECK CONNECTION CONDITION OF CONNECTOR (HV battery junction block CONNECTOR)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.

    Check the connecting condition of connectors (HV49), (HV51) and (HV52) in HV battery junction block, and check the relevant terminals for contact pressure, deformation and intrusion of water and foreign matter.

    Is there looseness at the connector mating, or any poor contact, deformation or intrusion of water or foreign matter at the relevant terminals?

    Yes:  Connect the connector securely. Or repair the poor contact of connector.

    No:  Go to  4.

  4. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Battery ECU assembly - HV battery junction block) (OPEN)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Disconnect Battery ECU assembly connector (HV42).
    2. Disconnect HV battery junction block connector (HV49).
    3. Using a tester, measure the resistance between HV battery junction block connector and Battery ECU assembly connector.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV42) No. 52 - (HV49) No. 3:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  5.

    No:  Repair or replace Wire harness between HV battery junction block connector and Battery ECU assembly connector.

  5. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Battery ECU assembly - HV battery junction block) (OPEN)  .

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV42) No. 50 - (HV51) No. 1:

    No:  Repair or replace Wire harness between HV battery junction block connector and Battery ECU assembly connector.

  6. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (HV battery junction block - CHASSIS GROUND) (OPEN)  .

      Connector & terminal

    No:  Replace HV battery junction block. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>RELAY AND FUSE .

  12. CHECK HV battery junction block (CHECK HV battery high voltage connector CONNECTING CONDITION)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Check the connecting condition of HV battery junction block connectors (HV43) and (HV46).
    2. Disconnect connectors (HV43) and (HV46) from HV battery junction block.
      CAUTION: Be sure to insulate the disconnected high voltage connector by wrapping it with insulating tape. To do this, wrap the insulating tape from Wire harness along to the connector.
    3. Check connector or HV battery high voltage connector and HV battery junction block for trace of arc.

    Is there any trace of arc?

    Yes:  Replace the faulty parts.

    No:  Go to  13.

  13. CHECK Hybrid battery terminal block (CHECK HV battery high voltage connector CONNECTING CONDITION)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Disconnect connectors (HV92) and (HV94) from Hybrid battery terminal block.
      CAUTION: Be sure to insulate the disconnected high voltage connector by wrapping it with insulating tape. To do this, wrap the insulating tape from Wire harness along to the connector.
    2. Check connector or HV battery high voltage connector and Hybrid battery terminal block for trace of arc.

    Is there any trace of arc?

    Yes:  If there is trace of arc on the connector (HV92), replace HV battery. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY .

    If there is trace of arc on the connector (HV94) or Hybrid battery terminal block, replace the faulty parts.

    No:  Go to  14.

  14. CHECK Hybrid battery terminal block  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Disconnect connectors (HV92) and (HV94) from Hybrid battery terminal block.
      CAUTION: Be sure to insulate the disconnected high voltage connector by wrapping it with insulating tape. To do this, wrap the insulating tape from Wire harness along to the connector.
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between Hybrid battery terminal block connector terminals.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV92) No. 1 - (HV94) No. 1:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  15.

    No:  Replace Hybrid battery terminal block.

  20. CHECK HV battery junction block (CHECK HV battery charger cable CONNECTING CONDITION)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Disconnect connectors (HV45) and (HV52) from HV battery junction block.
    2. Check HV battery charger cable and HV battery junction block for trace of arc.

  21. CHECK HV battery junction block (CHECK HV battery charger cable CONNECTING CONDITION)  .

    1. Disconnect the connector (HV33) from Electric vehicle charger assembly.
    2. Check HV battery charger cable and Electric vehicle charger assembly for trace of arc.

    Is there any trace of arc?

    Yes:  Replace the faulty parts.

    No:  Go to  22.

  22. CHECK HV battery charger cable  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.

    Connector & terminal 

    (HV33) No. 1 - (HV45) No. 1:

    (HV33) No. 2 - (HV48) No. 1:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  23.

    No:  Repair or replace Wire harness between Electric vehicle charger assembly connector and HV battery junction block connector.

  23. CHECK DTC  .
    1. Restore the vehicle to its original state.
    2. Turn to IG OFF, and wait for two minutes or more.
    3. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, display the following items in the [Data monitor] of [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control], and record them. Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DATA MONITOR .
      • Hybrid Battery SOC
    4. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, check [Data monitor] of [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control] to see if [Hybrid Battery SOC] is less than 70%.
    5. Turn to IG OFF, and wait for two minutes or more.
    6. Connect Electric vehicle charger cable assembly, and charge it for 30 seconds or more.
    7. Disconnect Electric vehicle charger cable assembly, and wait for 30 seconds or more.
    8. Read the DTC of [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is DTC P308F49 displayed?

    Yes:  Replace Inverter with converter assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>INVERTER AND CONVERTER ASSEMBLY .

    No:  Go to  24.

  24. REPLACEMENT OF HV battery junction block  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Replace HV battery junction block. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>RELAY AND FUSE .
    2. Restore the vehicle to its original state.
    3. Turn to IG ON.
    4. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, perform the clear memory of [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control]. Refer to COMMON (DIAG)>CLEAR MEMORY .
    5. Turn to IG OFF, and wait for two minutes or more.
    6. Connect Electric vehicle charger cable assembly, and charge it for 30 seconds or more.
    7. Disconnect Electric vehicle charger cable assembly, and wait for 10 seconds or more.
    8. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, display the following items in the [Data monitor] of [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control]. Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DATA MONITOR .
      • VL-Voltage before Boosting
      • Hybrid Battery Voltage

    Is the deviation between [Hybrid Battery Voltage] and [VL-Voltage before Boosting] less than 50 V constantly?

    Yes:  There was something wrong with HV battery junction block.

    Finish the diagnosis.

    No:  Replace Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL MODULE .
