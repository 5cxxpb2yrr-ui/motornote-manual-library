Pedestrian Alert Speaker: Removal

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Lift up the vehicle.
  3. Turn over the mud guard - front.
    1. Remove the screw and clips, and turn over the mud guard - front.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the pedestrian alert speaker assembly.
    1. Disconnect the connector.
    2. Remove the bolt, and remove the pedestrian alert speaker and bracket as a unit.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
