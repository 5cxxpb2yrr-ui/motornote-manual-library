Air Cleaner Case: Removal

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer. to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the air intake duct. Refer. to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>AIR INTAKE DUCT>REMOVAL .
  3. Disconnect the connector (A) from the mass air flow and intake air temperature sensor, and remove the clip (B).
  4. Disconnect the air intake boot (C), remove the clip (D), and remove the air cleaner case (rear) and air cleaner element.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the nut securing the inverter front water pipe to the bracket. (Hybrid model)
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Remove the air cleaner case (front).
    NOTE: For hybrid models, perform the procedure while sliding the inverter front water pipe.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
