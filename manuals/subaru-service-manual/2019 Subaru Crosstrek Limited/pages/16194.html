Engine Coolant Temperature Sensor: Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Drain engine coolant. (Engine side) Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>ENGINE COOLANT>REPLACEMENT>DRAINING OF ENGINE COOLANT>ENGINE SIDE .
  3. Remove the collector cover. Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>COLLECTOR COVER>REMOVAL .
  4. Remove the air intake duct. Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>AIR INTAKE DUCT>REMOVAL .
  5. Remove the cover UPR - front.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Remove the bracket - hood stay.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Lift up the vehicle.
  8. Remove the front under cover. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>FRONT UNDER COVER>REMOVAL .
  9. Remove the inverter radiator outlet hose and inverter tank outlet hose from the clamp.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  10. Lower the vehicle.
  11. Remove the bolt securing the inverter tank, and place it aside so that it does not interfere with the work.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  12. Remove the compressor bracket - front.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  13. Move the electric compressor assembly in the direction of arrow.
  14. Disconnect the connector (A) and remove the engine coolant temperature sensor using the ST.
    NOTE: STRUT MOUNT SOCKET (20099 Pa000) can also be used.

    Preparation tool: 

    ST: STRUT MOUNT SOCKET (20399AG000)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
