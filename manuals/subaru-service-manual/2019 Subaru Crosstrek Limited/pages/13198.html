DTC C2521: Speed Abnormal

DTC detecting condition  :

Detected when the vehicle speed became a failure value during IG ON.

Trouble symptom: 

Tire pressure warning light blinks 25 times and then illuminates.

Wiring diagram: 

Tire pressure monitoring system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (IMPREZA MODEL)>TIRE PRESSURE MONITORING SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>TIRE PRESSURE MONITORING SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>TIRE PRESSURE MONITORING SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to TIRE PRESSURE MONITOR (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK LAN COMMUNICATION. 

    Inspect LAN system. Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE . Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Perform the inspection according to the diagnosis for LAN system.

  2. CHECK VDC. 

    Check the VDC. Refer to BRAKE CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Perform the inspection according to the VDC diagnosis.

  3. CHECK CONNECTOR. 

    Check each connector.

    Is there poor contact or any other faults?

    Yes:  Repair the connector.

    No:  Replace the TPMS & keyless entry control module or TPMS control module. Refer to WHEEL AND TIRE SYSTEM>TIRE PRESSURE MONITORING SYSTEM .
