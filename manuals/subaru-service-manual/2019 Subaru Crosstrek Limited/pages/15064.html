Engine Coolant (Inverter Assembly Side): Inspection

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

CAUTION:
  • Do not use water instead of engine coolant.
  • Refer to "RECOMMENDED MATERIALS" instructions for recommended engine coolant. Refer to RECOMMENDED MATERIALS>ENGINE COOLANT .
  1. Park the vehicle on a level surface.
  2. Make sure the fluid level in the inverter tank is between "FULL" and "LOW" when the inverter assembly (with converter) is cold.
NOTE: If the engine coolant level drops, make sure that there are no engine coolant leakage, and add engine coolant to the "FULL" line.
