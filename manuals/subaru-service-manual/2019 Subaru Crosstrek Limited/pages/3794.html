DTC B2B50: Power Window Main Switch Failure

DTC detecting condition: 

When D-seat AUTO SW, P-seat MANUAL UP SW, P-seat MANUAL DOWN SW, or P-seat AUTO SW is ON for 20±1 second or longer.

Trouble symptom: 

Switch cannot be turned OFF.

CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to POWER WINDOW (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
NOTE: Replace the power window main switch when this DTC is detected. Refer to GLASS/WINDOWS/MIRRORS>POWER WINDOW CONTROL SWITCH .
