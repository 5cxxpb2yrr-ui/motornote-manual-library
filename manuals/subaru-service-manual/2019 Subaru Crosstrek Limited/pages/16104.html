Radiator Fan and Fan Motor Assembly: Removal

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery.

    Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .

  2. Remove the collector cover.

    Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>COLLECTOR COVER>REMOVAL .

  3. Collect the refrigerant.

    Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>REFRIGERANT RECOVERY PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .

  4. Remove the inverter tank.

    Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>INVERTER TANK>REMOVAL .

  5. Remove the lower side bolts (A) securing the radiator upper frame.
  6. Disconnect main fan motor connector (B) and sub fan motor connector (C).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Disconnect the hood switch connector (A).
  8. Disconnect the hose - pressure suction valve from the hose - pressure suction COMPL.

    Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>HOSE AND PIPE>REMOVAL>LOW PRESSURE VALVE HOSE .

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Disconnect the over flow hose (A) from the radiator and disconnect the over flow hose (B) from the filler neck.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  10. Remove the reservoir tank.

    Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>RESERVOIR TANK>REMOVAL .

  11. Remove the front bumper.

    Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>FRONT BUMPER>REMOVAL .

  12. Remove the bracket - bumper UPR.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  13. Remove the front hood lock assembly.

    Refer to SECURITY AND LOCKS>FRONT HOOD LOCK ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL .

  14. Disconnect the connector from the horn, and remove the clip.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  15. Disconnect the hose - pressure suction (A) from the A/C accumulator assembly.

    Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>HOSE AND PIPE>REMOVAL>LOW-PRESSURE HOSE .

  16. Disconnect the hose - pressure suction COMPL (B) from the pipe - return assembly.

    Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>HOSE AND PIPE>REMOVAL>RETURN HOSE .

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  17. Remove the upper side bolts securing the radiator upper frame, and remove the radiator upper frame together with the A/C accumulator assembly.
    CAUTION: Do not tilt the removed A/C accumulator assembly.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  18. Remove the radiator upper cushion.
  19. Disconnect the connectors from the engine oil temperature sensor.
    NOTE: This procedure is performed to ensure the clearance at the time of removing/installing the radiator fan & fan motor assembly.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  20. Remove the bolt securing the radiator fan & fan motor assembly, and remove the radiator fan & fan motor assembly.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
