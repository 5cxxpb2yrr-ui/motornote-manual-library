Battery Sensor: Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Disconnect the connector and ground terminal.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Remove the 12V engine restart battery sensor.
    CAUTION: The end of the bolt (a) of the 12V engine restart battery sensor is crimped.
    Therefore, do not loosen the nut (b) to the crimped portion.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
