DTC C1C21: Supply Voltage High: Notes

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

1. - 

DTC detecting condition: 

ECM internal voltage is 16.44 V or more and this condition continues for 0.8 seconds or more.

Trouble symptom: 

  • Regenerative brake cannot be controlled.
  • Brake assist does not operate.
  • EyeSight functions (ACC/PCS) do not operate.

Wiring diagram: 

Electronic controlled brake system Ref. to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>ELECTRIC CONTROL BRAKE SYSTEM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  1. CHECK BATTERY. 

    Check the battery voltage.

    Is the voltage 11 - 14 V when the IG switch is ON?

    When the vehicle's state is READY ON, is the voltage in 11 -15.5 V range?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Charge or replace the battery.

  2. CHECK CONNECTOR. 

    Check the connecting condition of the intermediate connectors (AD81, B652).

    Is there looseness, deformation, or corrosion at the connectors?

    Yes:  Repair or replace the connector.

    No:  Go to  3.

  3. CHECK HARNESS. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the coupling connector. (AD81, B652)
    3. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    4. Measure the voltage between intermediate connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (B652) No. 11 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    (B652) No. 23 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    (B652) No. 27 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    (B652) No. 28 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    (B652) No. 30 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    Is the voltage 11 - 14 V?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Repair or replace the harness.

  4. CHECK CONNECTOR. 

    Check the connecting condition of the EBB Control Module connector.

    Is there looseness, deformation, or corrosion at the connectors?

    Yes:  Repair or replace the connector.

    No:  Go to  5.

  5. CHECK HARNESS. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Connect the coupling connector. (AD81, B652)
    3. Disconnect the EBB Control Module connector.
    4. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    5. Measure the voltage between EBB Control Module connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (AD82) No. 10 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    (AD82) No. 13 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    (AD82) No. 17 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    (AD82) No. 22 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    (AD82) No. 30 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    Is the voltage 11 - 14 V?

    Yes:  Go to  6.

    No:  Repair or replace the harness.

  6. CHECK DTC. 
    1. Connect the EBB Control Module connector.
    2. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, perform the clear memory. Ref. to COMMON (DIAG)>CLEAR MEMORY .
    3. Read the DTC using Subaru Select Monitor. Ref. to ELECTRIC BRAKE BOOSTER (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Are DTCs C1C21 and C1C2F displayed?

    Yes:  Replace the brake booster assembly (with the master cylinder). Ref. to BRAKE>BRAKE BOOSTER .

    No:  Currently, it is normal. There may have been a temporary poor contact in the harness and connector or a temporary noise interference.
