Manifold Absolute Pressure Sensor: Installation

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Using a new O-ring, install the manifold absolute pressure sensor to the intake manifold assembly.

    Tightening torque: 

    3.4 N.m (0.3 kgf-m, 2.5 ft-lb)

  2. Connect the connector to the manifold absolute pressure sensor.
  3. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
