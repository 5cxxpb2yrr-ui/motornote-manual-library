Sonar Sensor

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Ref. to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the bumper face - rear. Ref. to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>REAR BUMPER>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the sonar sensor.
    CAUTION: When reusing the sensor, make sure to place a mark on the sensor and the bumper face - rear or others, and surely return each sensor to the original position before removed.
    1. Disconnect the connector.
    2. Release the claws and then remove the sonar sensor.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
      NOTE:
      • The sonar sensors are equipped at four portions on the bumper face - rear.
      • The figure indicates the sonar sensor (right corner). Remove the rest of the sensors in the same manner.
    3. Using a flat tip screwdriver wrapped with a protection tape, release the lock from the front side of the sonar sensor to remove the cord assembly - sonar.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
      NOTE: Use a flat tip screwdriver with an appropriate size for the insertion opening.
    4. Release the claws, and then remove the bezel - sonar.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
