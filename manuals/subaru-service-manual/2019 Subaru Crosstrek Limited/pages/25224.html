Cruise Control Was Cancelled Without Operating The Cruise Switch

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. PERFORM REAL TIME DIAGNOSIS. 

    Check the input signal of cruise control system. Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>REAL-TIME DIAGNOSIS .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK EyeSight STEERING SWITCH. 

    Check the EyeSight steering switch. Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>CHECK EYESIGHT STEERING SWITCH .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Replace the EyeSight steering switch. Refer to EYESIGHT>SWITCHES AND HARNESS .

  3. CHECK COMBINATION METER. 

    Check the indicator readings using the active test of combination meter [All lamps lighted]. Refer to COMBINATION METER (DIAGNOSTICS)>ACTIVE TEST .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Replace the meter case assembly. Refer to INSTRUMENTATION/DRIVER INFO>COMBINATION METER .

  4. INSPECTION OF CRUISE INDICATOR. 

    Check the CRUISE indicator. Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>CHECK ADAPTIVE CRUISE CONTROL/CONVENTIONAL CRUISE CONTROL INDICATOR LIGHT (WHITE OR GREEN) .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  The circuit has returned to a normal condition at this time.

    NOTE: In this case, temporary poor contact of connector, temporary open or short circuit of harness may be the cause.

    No:  Repair or replace the faulty portion according to the inspection result.
