Malfunction Description

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

This DTC indicates that the output signal of Generator temperature sensor became abnormal.

The following cases are assumed to be malfunction causes:

  • Internal malfunction of Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly
    • Malfunction of Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly
  • Malfunction of Generator temperature sensor
    • Internal malfunction of Generator temperature sensor
    • Open or short circuit of Generator temperature sensor
  • Malfunction of Wire harness system between Generator temperature sensor - Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly
    • Improperly mated connector
    • Foreign matter caught, water intrusion
    • Open or short circuit
NOTE: When special operation that places a high load on the generator is performed while this DTC occurred, the generator may break down and the vehicle may drive in the evacuation state because of the protection control not working due to the generator overheat.
