FRESH/RECIRC Door Actuator: Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Disconnect the battery ground terminal and wait for at least 60 seconds before starting work. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the pocket assembly and the back panel - pocket. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>GLOVE BOX>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the heat pump control module. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>CONTROL UNIT>REMOVAL .
  4. Remove the motor - actuator intake.
    1. Disconnect the connector.
    2. Remove the screws, and remove the motor - actuator intake.
