Malfunction Description

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly detects that the electrical charge stored in the high voltage condenser of Electric vehicle charger assembly cannot be discharged when charging is completed.

The following cases are assumed to be malfunction causes:

  • Fault of voltage sensor (VCHG) in Electric vehicle charger assembly
    • Voltage sensor (VCHG) malfunction
    • Malfunction of Electric vehicle charger assembly
    • Malfunction of communication (Wire harness)
  • Discharge resistance malfunction
    • Discharge resistance malfunction in Electric vehicle charger assembly
