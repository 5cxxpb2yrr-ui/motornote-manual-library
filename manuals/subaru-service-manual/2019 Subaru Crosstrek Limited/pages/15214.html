Driver's Side

CAUTION:
  • Before handling the airbag system components, refer to "CAUTION" of "General Description" in "AIRBAG SYSTEM". Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  • If reusing the motor - actuator mix, perform the following procedures to prevent incorrect installation.
    1. While the part is installed to the vehicle, turn the temperature control dial to the MAX COOL position.
    2. Wait for 30 seconds in the condition 1, and remove the motor - actuator mix.
  1. Disconnect the battery ground terminal and wait for at least 60 seconds before starting work. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the cover assembly - instrument panel LWR driver. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>INSTRUMENT PANEL LOWER COVER>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the knee airbag module. Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>KNEE AIRBAG MODULE>REMOVAL .
  4. Remove the motor - actuator mix.
    1. Disconnect the connector.
    2. Remove the screws and detach the motor - actuator mix.
