Check The Intermittent Operation (Inspection Of The Wiper Switch Alone)

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  1. Set the voltmeter between terminal No. B2 (+) - No. A2 (-).
  2. Connect the battery to connector. (Terminal No. B2 (+), Terminal No. A2 (-))
  3. Turn the switch assembly - combination wiper select to INT.
  4. Connect the battery (+) to the terminal No. B2 for 5 seconds.
  5. Connect the battery (-) to the terminal No. A2, and check the voltage between terminals No. B2 - and No. A2 during intermittent operation.
  6. Perform step  1 to  5 above when intermittent control switch is in MIN or MAX, and replace the switch if the operation is not as specified.

    Intermittent stationary time 

    MIN: Approx. 2 seconds

    MAX: Approx. 16 seconds
