Diagnostic Method

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.

If the duration of time while the following conditions are met is longer than the time indicated, judge as NG.

JUDGMENT VALUE SPECIFICATION

Malfunction Criteria Threshold Value
<Judgment 1: Short to battery> 
Secondary oxygen sensor offset signal (A)
<Judgment 2: Short to battery> 
Secondary oxygen sensor signal (B)
<Judgment 3: Out of range> 
Secondary oxygen sensor output voltage {(B) - (A)}		 More than 2.2V
More than 3.25V
More than 1.2V

Time needed for diagnosis:  1000 ms

Malfunction indicator light illumination:  Illuminates when malfunction occurs in 2 continuous driving cycles.
