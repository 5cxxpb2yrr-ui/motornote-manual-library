Transfer Drive Gear: Installation

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Install the transfer drive gear.

    Tightening torque: 

    26 N.m (2.7 kgf-m, 19.2 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Install the transfer driven gear. refer to MANUAL TRANSMISSION AND DIFFERENTIAL (6MT)>TRANSFER DRIVEN GEAR>INSTALLATION .
  3. Select the adjusting washer. refer to MANUAL TRANSMISSION AND DIFFERENTIAL (6MT)>TRANSFER CASE AND EXTENSION CASE ASSEMBLY>INSTALLATION .
  4. Install the transfer case and the extension case assembly. refer to MANUAL TRANSMISSION AND DIFFERENTIAL (6MT)>TRANSFER CASE AND EXTENSION CASE ASSEMBLY>INSTALLATION .
  5. Install the back-up light switch and the neutral position switch, refer to MANUAL TRANSMISSION AND DIFFERENTIAL (6MT)>SWITCHES AND HARNESS>INSTALLATION .
  6. Install the manual transmission assembly to the vehicle, refer to MANUAL TRANSMISSION AND DIFFERENTIAL (6MT)>MANUAL TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY>INSTALLATION .
