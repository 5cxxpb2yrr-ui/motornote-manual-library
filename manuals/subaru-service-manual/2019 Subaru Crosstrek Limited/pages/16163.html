Intake Manifold Assembly: Inspection

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Check that the intake manifold assembly and the fuel pipe have no deformation, cracks and other damages.
  2. Check that the hose has no cracks, damage or loose part.
  3. Check tumble generator valve for contamination or clogging.
