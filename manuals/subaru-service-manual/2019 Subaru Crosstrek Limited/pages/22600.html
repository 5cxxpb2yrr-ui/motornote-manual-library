Rear Differential (T-type): Assembly

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

CAUTION:
  • Keep the shims and washers in order, so that they are not improperly installed.
  • Be careful not to mix up the side bearing race RH and LH.
  • Be careful not to mix up the side oil seal RH and LH.
NOTE:
  • Check and adjust each part during assembly.
  • Thoroughly clean the surfaces on which the shims, washers and bearings are to be installed.
  • Apply differential gear oil when installing the bearings and thrust washers.
  • Apply differential gear oil to the lips when installing the oil seal.
Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  1. Adjusting preload for front and rear bearings
    NOTE: Adjust the bearing preload between front and rear bearings with preload adjusting spacer and washer. Pinion height adjusting washer is not affected by this adjustment. The adjustment must not be carried out with oil seal inserted.
    1. Install the rear bearing race using the ST1 and ST2.

      Special tool: 

      ST1: 398477701 HANDLE

      ST2: 398477703 DRIFT 2

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Using the HANDLE (398477701) and DRIFT (398477702), install a new front bearing race to the differential carrier.
    3. Insert the ST1 into the differential carrier with the pinion height adjusting washer and rear bearing cone fitted onto it.
      NOTE:
      • At this time, install a provisionally selected or previously used pinion height adjusting washer. Measure and record the thickness.
      • If tooth contact (drive pinion, hypoid driven gear) is normal in the inspection before disassembling, verify that the washer is not deformed, and then re-use the used washer.
    4. Install the preload adjusting spacer and washer, new front bearing cone, ST2, companion flange and self-locking nut.

      Special tool: 

      ST1: 398507702 DUMMY SHAFT

      ST2: 398507703 DUMMY COLLAR

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    5. Turn the ST1 by hand to smooth the bearing. Tighten a new self-locking nut while measuring the initial load or initial torque with a spring scale or torque wrench. Select the preload adjusting washer and spacer so that the specified preload is obtained when nut is tightened to the specified torque.
      CAUTION: Be careful not to give excessive preload.
      NOTE:
      • Measure the preload in direction of tangent to the flange.
      • When tightening the self-locking nut, lock the ST1 with the ST2 as shown in the figure.

      Special tool: 

      ST1: 398507702 DUMMY SHAFT

      ST2: 398507704 BLOCK

      Tightening torque: 

      181.5 N.m (18.5 kgf-m, 133.9 ft-lb)

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.

  2. Adjusting drive pinion height:

    Adjust the drive pinion height with pinion height adjusting washer installed between the rear bearing cone and the back of pinion gear.

    1. Install the ST2.

      Special tool: 

      ST1: 398507702 DUMMY SHAFT

      ST2: 398507701 DIFFERENTIAL CARRIER GAUGE

      ST3: 398507703 DUMMY COLLAR

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Measure the clearance "N" between the end of ST2 and the end surface of ST1 by using a thickness gauge.
      NOTE: Make sure there is no clearance between the differential carrier and ST2.

      Special tool: 

      ST1: 398507702 DUMMY SHAFT

      ST2: 398507701 DIFFERENTIAL CARRIER GAUGE

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Obtain the thickness of pinion height adjusting washer to be inserted from the following formula, and replace the temporarily installed washer with this one.

      T = To + N - (H x 0.01) - 0.20 mm (0.0079 in)

      T Thickness of pinion height adjusting washer mm (in)  
      To Thickness of washer temporarily inserted mm (in)  
      N Clearance of thickness gauge mm (in)  
      H Figure marked on drive pinion head  
      Memo:

      (Example of calculation)

      To = 3.39 mm (0.1335 in)

      N = 0.24 mm (0.0094 in)

      H = + 1

      T = 3.39 mm (0.1335 in) +0.24 mm (0.0094 in) -0.01 mm (0.0004 in) -0.20 mm (0.0079 in) = 3.42 mm (0.1346 in)

      Result: Thickness = 3.42 mm (0.1346 in)

      Therefore use washer 383605200.

  3. Install the selected pinion height adjusting washer on drive pinion, and press-in the rear bearing cone with the ST.

    Special tool: 

    ST: 398177700 INSTALLER

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Insert the drive pinion, and install the preselected preload adjusting spacer and washer.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Press-fit a new front bearing cone with the ST1, ST2 and ST3.

    Special tool: 

    ST1: 398507703 DUMMY COLLAR

    ST2: 399780104 WEIGHT

    ST3: 899580100 INSTALLER

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Insert the spacer, then press-fit a new pilot bearing with the ST1 and ST2.

    Special tool: 

    ST1: 399780104 WEIGHT

    ST2: 899580100 INSTALLER

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Using the ST, install a new front oil seal.
    NOTE:
    • Press-fit until the oil seal end comes 1 mm (0.04 in) inward from end of carrier.
    • Apply differential gear oil to the oil seal lips.

    Special tool: 

    ST: 498447120 INSTALLER

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Press-fit the companion flange with the ST1 and ST2.
    CAUTION: Be careful not to damage the pilot bearing.

    Special tool: 

    ST1: 899874100 INSTALLER

    ST2: 399780104 WEIGHT

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Apply seal material on the drive pinion shaft thread and new self-locking nut seat.

    Seal material: 

    THREE BOND 1324 (Part No. 004403042) or equivalent

  10. Tighten a new self-locking nut while securing the companion flange with the ST.

    Tightening torque: 

    181.5 N.m (18.5 kgf-m, 133.9 ft-lb)

    Special tool: 

    ST: 498427200 FLANGE WRENCH

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  11. Check the initial torque or initial load.

    Specification: 

    Initial load: 

    18.1 - 38.8 N (1.8 - 4.0 kgf, 4.1 - 8.7 lbf)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.

    Initial torque: 

    0.69 - 1.47 N.m (0.07 - 0.15 kgf-m, 0.51 - 1.08 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  12. Assembling differential case
    1. Install the side gears and pinion mate gears together with the side gear thrust washers and pinion mate shaft.
      NOTE:
      • Apply gear oil on both sides of the washer and on the pinion mate shaft before installing.
      • Insert the pinion mate shaft into the differential case by aligning the pin holes.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Measure the side gear backlash.

      Specification: 

      0.10 - 0.20 mm (0.004 - 0.008 in)

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Adjust the side gear backlash within the specified value by selecting the side gear thrust washer.
      Side gear thrust washer
      Part No. Thickness mm (in)
      383445201 0.75 - 0.80 (0.0295 - 0.0315)
      383445202 0.80 - 0.85 (0.0315 - 0.0335)
      383445203 0.85 - 0.90 (0.0335 - 0.0354)
    4. Check the condition of rotation after applying oil to the gear tooth surfaces and washer surfaces.
    5. Using the STRAIGHT PIN REMOVER (899904100), drive a new pinion shaft lock pin into the differential case.
  13. Install the hypoid driven gear to differential case.
    NOTE:
    • Before installing bolts, apply seal material to bolt threads.

      Seal material:

      THREE BOND 1324 (Part No. 004403042) or equivalent

    • Make sure there is no clearance between the differential case and driven gear.
    • Tighten opposing bolts in order.

    Tightening torque: 

    103 N.m (10.5 kgf-m, 76.0 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  14. Press-fit the side bearing cone using the ST.

    Special tool: 

    ST: 398487700 DRIFT

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  15. Using the ST, press-fit the side bearing race to the side retainer.
    CAUTION: Make sure that bearing races and cones are properly assembled.

    Special tool: 

    ST: 398417700 DRIFT

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  16. Side retainer shim adjustment
    NOTE: The hypoid driven gear backlash and side bearing preload can be adjusted by the side retainer shim thickness.
    1. Install the differential case assembly.
      CAUTION: Be careful not to hit the teeth of hypoid driven gear against the differential carrier.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Install the side retainer shim.
      CAUTION:
      • Be careful not to mix up the side retainer shim RH and LH.
      • If the side retainer shim is broken or corroded, replace it with a new part.
      Side retainer shim
      Part No. Thickness mm (in)
      383475201 0.20 (0.0079)
      383475202 0.25 (0.0098)
      383475203 0.30 (0.0118)
      383475204 0.40 (0.0157)
      383475205 0.50 (0.0197)
    3. Align the arrow mark on the differential carrier with the arrow mark on the side retainer when installing the side retainer.
      CAUTION: Be careful not to damage the bearing roller by the side bearing race.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    4. Tighten the side retainer bolts.

      Tightening torque: 

      10.5 N.m (1.1 kgf-m, 7.7 ft-lb)

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    5. Measure the hypoid driven gear to drive pinion backlash. Set the magnet base on differential carrier. Align the contact point of dial gauge with tooth face of hypoid driven gear, and move hypoid driven gear while holding drive pinion still. Read the value indicated on dial gauge. If it is outside the standard value, adjust the side retainer shim by the following method.
      • When backlash is less than 0.1 mm (0.004 in): 

        Reduce the thickness of shim on the back side of the hypoid driven gear and increase the thickness of shims on the teeth side of the hypoid driven gear.

      • When backlash exceeds 0.2 mm (0.008 in): 

        Increase the thickness of shim on the back side of the hypoid driven gear and reduce the thickness of shims on the teeth side of the hypoid driven gear.

        Specification: 

        0.10 - 0.20 mm (0.004 - 0.008 in)

        Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    6. Measure the total preload of the drive pinion. If it is outside the standard value, adjust the thickness of side retainer shims, increasing/reducing both shims by an even amount at a time.

      Specification: 

      20.7 - 54.4 N (2.1 - 5.5 kgf, 4.7 - 12.2 lbf)

  17. Recheck the hypoid driven gear to drive pinion backlash.

    Specification: 

    0.10 - 0.20 mm (0.004 - 0.008 in)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  18. Check pinion and hypoid driven gears rotate smoothly and make sure of the hypoid driven gear runout on its back surface. If it exceeds the limit value, check for any foreign objects between the hypoid driven gear and differential case, and for any deformation of the differential case or hypoid driven gear.

    Service limit: 

    0.05 mm (0.002 in)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  19. Check and adjustment of the tooth contact of hypoid driven gear and drive pinion
    1. Apply lead-free red dye evenly on the both sides of three to four teeth of the hypoid driven gear. Check the contact pattern after rotating the hypoid driven gear several revolutions back and forth until a definite contact pattern appears on the hypoid driven gear.
    2. When the contact pattern is not correct, readjust.
      NOTE: Be sure to wipe off the lead-free red dye completely after the adjustment is completed.
      • Correct tooth contact

        Check item: Tooth contact pattern is slightly shifted toward toe side under no-load rotation. (When driving, it moves towards the heel side.) 

        Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
      • Face contact

        Check item: Backlash is too large  .

        Contact pattern

        Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.

        Corrective action: Increase thickness of pinion height adjusting washer according to the procedure for bringing drive pinion close to hypoid driven gear side.

        Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
      • Flank contact

        Check item: Backlash is too small  .

        Contact pattern

        Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.

        Corrective action: Reduce the thickness of pinion height adjusting washer according to the procedure for bringing drive pinion away from hypoid driven gear.

        Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
      • Toe contact (inside contact)

        Check item: Teeth contact area is too small  .

        Contact pattern

        Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.

        Corrective action: Reduce the thickness of pinion height adjusting washer according to the procedure for bringing drive pinion away from hypoid driven gear.

        Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
      • Heel contact (outside end contact)

        Check item: Teeth contact area is too small  .

        Contact pattern

        Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.

        Corrective action: Increase thickness of pinion height adjusting washer according to the procedure for bringing drive pinion close to hypoid driven gear side.

        Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.

  21. Remove the side retainer.
  22. Install a new O-ring to the side retainer.
  23. Install a new side oil seal to the side retainer.

    Refer to DIFFERENTIALS>REAR DIFFERENTIAL SIDE OIL SEAL>REPLACEMENT .

  24. Align the arrow mark on the differential carrier with the arrow mark on the side retainer when installing the side retainer.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  25. Apply liquid gasket to the bolts indicated by arrows, and tighten the bolts.

    Liquid gasket: 

    THREE BOND 1110F, THREE BOND 1110B or equivalent

    Tightening torque: 

    10.5 N.m (1.1 kgf-m, 7.7 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  26. Replace the gasket with a new part and install the rear cover.

    Tightening torque: 

    29.5 N.m (3.0 kgf-m, 21.8 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  27. When the stud bolts are removed from the rear cover, install the stud bolts so that the exposed length of stud bolt is within the range of "L".
    CAUTION:
    • Do not tighten forcibly if the exposed length of stud bolt does not fall within the specified range. Remove the stud bolt, and check for contamination of foreign matter and any defects in the screw hole.
    • Do not tighten with a tightening torque of 49 N.m (5.0 kgf-m, 36.1 ft-lb) (reference value) or more.

    Exposed length L of stud bolt: 

    59.0 - 61.0 mm (2.32 - 2.40 in)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  28. Install the air breather cap.
    CAUTION: When installing the air breather cap, do not tap section (A). Be sure to tap section (B) to install.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  29. Install the drain plug.
    NOTE: Apply liquid gasket to the drain plug.

    Liquid gasket: 

    THREE BOND 1105 (Part No. 004403010) or equivalent

    Tightening torque: 

    49 N.m (5.0 kgf-m, 36.1 ft-lb)

  30. Install the filler plug.
    CAUTION: After installing the rear differential assembly to the vehicle, add gear oil, apply liquid gasket, and then tighten to the specified torque.
    Refer to DIFFERENTIALS>DIFFERENTIAL GEAR OIL>REPLACEMENT .
