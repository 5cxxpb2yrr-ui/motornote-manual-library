Drive Start Control System

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
NOTE: The system output may become large at uphill and small at downhill even when the accelerator pedal is depressed at constant amount. However, this is due to operation of the driving torque control by the drive start control, and not a malfunction.
  1. CHECK SYMPTOMS  .

    Interview the customer and check the vehicle condition.

    If hunting, go to  A.

    If breathing or poor acceleration, go to  B.

    Yes: 

    1. Go to  2.
    2. Go to  8.

    No:  -

  2. CHECK OPERATION HISTORY  .

    Check if an operation to activate the drive start control is performed.

    NOTE: During control at reversing and control at shift change, the driving torque is corrected based on slope inclination and steering angle.
    • Condition 1 (Control at reversing)

      Control operating condition

      • Shift position R
      • Accelerator pedal ON
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.

      Control items

      • Restraining of driving torque

      Recovery conditions

      • Shift position other than R
      • Accelerator pedal OFF
    • Condition 2 (Control a at shift change)

      Control operating condition

      • From shift position P to D, or P to R
      • Accelerator pedal ON
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.

      Control items

      • Restraining of driving torque

      Recovery conditions

      • Shift position P or N
      • Accelerator pedal OFF
    • Condition 3 (Control b at shift change)

      Control operating condition

      • From shift position R to D, D (including position B) to R, or N to R
      • Accelerator pedal ON
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.

      Control items

      • Shift position P or N

      Recovery conditions

      • Shift position P or N
      • Accelerator pedal OFF

    Was the operation in the note above performed?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  System is normal.

  3. CHECK DTC  .

    Read the DTC of [Brake Control] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to BRAKE CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Are any DTCs displayed?

    Yes:  Perform the diagnosis for the displayed DTCs.

    No:  Go to  4.

  4. CHECK DATA MONITOR (FR Wheel Speed, FL Wheel Speed, RR Wheel Speed, RL Wheel Speed)  .

    Using the Subaru Select Monitor, display the following items in the [Data monitor] of [Brake Control]. Refer to BRAKE CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DATA MONITOR .

    • FR Wheel Speed
    • FL Wheel Speed
    • RR Wheel Speed
    • RL Wheel Speed

    Is the data as follows?

    At stall: 0 km/h (0 MPH)

    During driving at a constant speed (16 - 64 km/h (10 - 40 MPH)): Displayed speed does not fluctuate widely

    Yes:  Go to  5.

    No:  Perform the diagnosis for the brake control. Refer to BRAKE CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE .

  5. CHECK DATA MONITOR (Vehicle Speed Signal)  .

    Using the Subaru Select Monitor, display the following items in the [Data monitor] of [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control]. Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DATA MONITOR .

    • Vehicle Speed Signal

    Is the data as follows?

    At stall: 0 km/h (0 MPH)

    During driving at a constant speed (16 - 64 km/h (10 - 40 MPH)): Displayed speed does not fluctuate widely

    Yes:  Go to  6.

    No:  Perform the diagnosis for the brake control. Refer to BRAKE CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE .

  6. CHECK DATA MONITOR (Yaw Rate Sensor)  .

    Using the Subaru Select Monitor, display the following items in the [Data monitor] of [Brake Control]. Refer to BRAKE CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DATA MONITOR .

    • Yaw Rate Sensor

    Is the data as follows?

    When decelerating: Change continuously

    When accelerating: Change continuously

    Yes:  Go to  7.

    No:  Perform the diagnosis for the brake control. Refer to BRAKE CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE .

  7. CHECK DATA MONITOR (Steering Angle Sensor)  .

    Using the Subaru Select Monitor, display the following items in the [Data monitor] of [Brake Control]. Refer to BRAKE CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DATA MONITOR .

    • Steering Angle Sensor

    Is the data as follows?

    When operating the steering to the left: Change to positive numbers continuously

    When operating the steering to the right: Change to negative numbers continuously

    Yes:  Go to  8.

  10. CHECK DATA MONITOR (Accelerator Position Sensor No. 1 Voltage %, Accelerator Position Sensor No. 2 Voltage %)  .

    Using the Subaru Select Monitor, display the following items in the [Data monitor] of [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control]. Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DATA MONITOR .

    Does the display change according to accelerator pedal operation?

    Yes:  System is normal.

    No:  Replace Accelerator pedal sensor. Refer to SPEED CONTROL SYSTEMS (H4DO (HEV))>GENERAL DESCRIPTION. Refer to ACCELERATOR PEDAL .

GENERAL DESCRIPTION 

Explanation of circuits 

The drive start control is controlled by Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly.

When it is judged that the accelerator pedal and shift are operated differently from usual, the driving torque is restrained and operation notice is indicated on the combination meter as necessary.

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
