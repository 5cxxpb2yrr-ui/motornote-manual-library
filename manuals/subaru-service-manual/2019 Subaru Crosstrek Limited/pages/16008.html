Resolver Sensor Harness

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
WARNING: The hybrid system includes a high voltage circuit. Mishandling could cause accidents such as electric shock or leak. Always check "WARNING (HYBRID SYSTEM)" and perform the proper operation.
Refer to PRECAUTION/WARNING>WARNING (HYBRID SYSTEM) .
  1. Install the bolt and clip (A) shown in the figure, and install the resolver sensor harness.

    Tightening torque: 

    8 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.9 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Connect the resolver sensor harness to the inhibitor harness connector.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Install the cord cover clips.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Install the inverter assembly (with converter).

    Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>INVERTER AND CONVERTER ASSEMBLY>INSTALLATION .

  5. Install the floor under protector RH.

    Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>FLOOR UNDER PROTECTOR>INSTALLATION .

  6. Install the propeller shaft.

    Refer to DRIVE SHAFT SYSTEM>PROPELLER SHAFT>INSTALLATION .

  7. Install the service disconnect plug.

    Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG>INSTALLATION .

  8. Connect the battery ground terminal.

    Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
