Engine Mounting: Installation

  1. Install the engine mounting onto the engine.

    Tightening torque: 

    35 N.m (3.6 kgf-m, 25.8 ft-lb)

  2. Install the front crossmember. Refer to FRONT SUSPENSION>CROSSMEMBER>INSTALLATION .
