Check Non-Permanent Occurrence Malfunctions

  1. Clear the memory. Refer to COMMON (DIAG)>CLEAR MEMORY .
  2. Perform the reproduction test.
  3. Perform visual inspection of the connectors and the respective terminals and check the connection.
  4. Check that excessive force is not applied to the wiring harness and connector.
