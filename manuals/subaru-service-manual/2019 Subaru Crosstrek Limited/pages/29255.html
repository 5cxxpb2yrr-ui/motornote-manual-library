Monitor Description

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

The Charge control ECU built into the Electric vehicle charger assembly monitors its internal operation. If it detects a power output malfunction in the Electric vehicle charger assembly, it illuminates the MIL and stores a DTC.
