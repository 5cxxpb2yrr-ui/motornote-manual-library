Hose and Pipe: Inspection

  1. Check the hoses for cracks, damage and expansion. If there is a malfunction, replace the hose - pressure suction or the hose - pressure discharge.
  2. Check the pipes for crack or damage. If there is a malfunction, replace the pipe - evaporator cooling.
