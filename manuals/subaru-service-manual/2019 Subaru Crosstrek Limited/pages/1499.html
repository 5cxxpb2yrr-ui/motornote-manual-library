Cruise Control Cannot Be Set

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

NOTE:
  • Do not turn the ignition switch to OFF after the cruise control is deactivated.
  • Do not operate the EyeSight steering switch after the cruise control is deactivated.

If the above is performed, the cancel code the ECM will be cleared.

  1. PERFORM REAL TIME DIAGNOSIS. 

    Check the input signal of cruise control system. Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>REAL-TIME DIAGNOSIS .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  5.

    No: 

    • When EyeSight steering switch signal is faulty, Go to  2.
    • When the stop light switch or brake switch signal is faulty, Go to  3.
    • When the sift lever signal is faulty, Go to  4.
  2. CHECK EyeSight STEERING SWITCH. 

    Check the EyeSight steering switch. Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>CHECK EYESIGHT STEERING SWITCH .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Replace the stereo camera. Refer to EYESIGHT>STEREO CAMERA>REMOVAL .

    No:  Replace the EyeSight steering switch. Refer to EYESIGHT>SWITCHES AND HARNESS .

  3. INSPECTION OF STOP LIGHT SWITCH AND BRAKE SWITCH. 

    Check stop light switch and brake switch. Refer to BRAKE>BRAKE PEDAL . Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE WITH ECU CANCEL CODE>12 .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Replace the ECM. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE CONTROL MODULE (ECM)>REMOVAL .

    No:  Repair or replace the faulty portion of the stop light switch or brake switch.

  4. CHECK NEUTRAL POSITION SWITCH. 

    Check the neutral position switch. Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE WITH ECU CANCEL CODE>12 . Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE WITH ECU CANCEL CODE>62 .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Replace the ECM. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE CONTROL MODULE (ECM)>REMOVAL .

    No:  Repair or replace the faulty portion of the neutral position switch.

  5. INSPECTION OF VEHICLE SPEED SENSOR. 

    Check the vehicle speed sensor. Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE WITH ECU CANCEL CODE>22 .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Replace the VDC Control Module. Refer to VEHICLE DYNAMICS CONTROL (VDC)>VDC CONTROL MODULE AND HYDRAULIC CONTROL UNIT (VDCCM & H/U) .

    No:  Repair or replace the faulty portion of the vehicle speed sensor.
