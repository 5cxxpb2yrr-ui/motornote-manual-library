Keyless Transmitter Battery

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Measure the keyless transmitter battery voltage.

    Preparation tool: 

    Circuit tester

    NOTE: Complete the measurement within 5 seconds because the battery discharges during measurement.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Battery terminal Inspection conditions Standard
    (+) - (-) Connect resistor (A) 47 Ω 2.5 - 3 V
  2. Replace the battery if the inspection result is not within the standard value. (Use CR1620 or equivalent.)
