Inhibitor Harness

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Remove the transmission body.

    Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TH2A)>TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL .

  2. Remove the inhibitor harness from the transmission body.
    1. Remove the clips and disconnect the connector from the sensor. (LH side)

    2. Remove the clips and disconnect the connectors from the inhibitor switch and sensor. (RH side)
