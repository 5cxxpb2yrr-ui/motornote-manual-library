Circuit Description

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

A DTC is output if an insulation failure occurs in the high voltage system of the heater & air conditioner system. A possible cause is insulation failure in Electric compressor ASSEMBLY or compressor assembly with a motor, or insulation failure due to oil other than ND-OIL 11 entered in the refrigerant system.

CAUTION:
  • If oil other than ND-OIL 11 is used (enters) in the refrigerant cycle even if only slightly, electric insulation may extremely be degraded, causing DTC.
  • If refrigerant cycle-related parts (evaporator, condenser, Electric compressor ASSEMBLY, expansion valve, piping) are not replaced when a large amount of oil other than ND-OIL 11 enters, electric insulation may not be recovered, causing DTC again.
NOTE: Electric compressor ASSEMBLY has a built-in motor driven by high voltage which is directly cooled by the refrigerant. Therefore, high-insulation compressor oil (ND-OIL 11) is used as the normal compressor oil (ND-OIL 8) causes leakage.
