  1. CHECK BETWEEN RELATED LINES AND MAIN WIRING HARNESS. 
    1. Disconnect the keyless entry control module, TPMS control module or keyless entry & TPMS control module connector.
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between keyless entry control module, TPMS control module or keyless entry & TPMS control module connector terminals.

      Connector & terminal 

      Keyless entry control module 

      (R80) No. 2 - (R80) No. 1:

      TPMS control module 

      (R221) No. 2 - (R221) No. 1:

      Keyless entry & TPMS control module 

      (R211) No. 2 - (R211) No. 1:

      Is the resistance 400 Ω or more?

    Yes:  A related line between VDC control module and main wiring harness is open, or main wiring harness is open at two places or more.

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK RELATED LINES. 
    1. Disconnect the steering sensor connector.
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between keyless entry control module, TPMS control module or keyless entry & TPMS control module connector and steering sensor connector.

      Connector & terminal 

      Keyless entry control module 

      (R80) No. 2 - (i74) No. 3:

      (R80) No. 1 - (i74) No. 2:

      TPMS control module 

      (R211) No. 2 - (i74) No. 3:

      (R211) No. 1 - (i74) No. 2:

      Keyless entry & TPMS control module 

      (R221) No. 2 - (i74) No. 3:

      (R221) No. 1 - (i74) No. 2:

      Is the resistance 10 Ω or more?

      Yes:  Repair or replace the open circuit of keyless entry/TPMS related lines.

      No:  The communication harness is normal. Check DTC of keyless entry/TPMS. Refer to KEYLESS ENTRY (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) . Refer to TIRE PRESSURE MONITOR (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .
