  1. CHECK DTC  .

    Using the Subaru Select Monitor or a general scan tool, read the DTC of [Engine Control System]. Refer to ENGINE (DIAGNOSTICS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Refer to ENGINE (DIAGNOSTICS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is DTC other than P0617 displayed? (Current malfunction)

    Yes: 

    Check the appropriate DTC using the "Diagnostic Trouble Code (DTC)". Refer to ENGINE (DIAGNOSTICS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    No: 

    Go to  2.

  2. CHECK HARNESS BETWEEN ECM AND M/B (STARTER RELAY) (SHORT TO POWER SUPPLY)  .
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the connector from ECM.
    3. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    4. Measure the voltage between ECM connector and engine ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (B134) No. 27 (+) - Engine ground (-):

      Is the voltage 10 V or more?

      Yes: 

      Repair the short circuit to power supply in harness between ECM connector and M/B (starter relay).

      No: 

      Repair the poor contact of ECM connector.
