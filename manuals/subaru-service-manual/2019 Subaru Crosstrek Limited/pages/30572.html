Shift Link Assembly: Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Remove the manual transmission assembly from the vehicle. refer to MANUAL TRANSMISSION AND DIFFERENTIAL (6MT)>MANUAL TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL .
  2. Remove the back-up light switch and the neutral position switch. refer to MANUAL TRANSMISSION AND DIFFERENTIAL (6MT)>SWITCHES AND HARNESS>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the transfer case together with the extension case assembly, refer to MANUAL TRANSMISSION AND DIFFERENTIAL (6MT)>TRANSFER CASE AND EXTENSION CASE ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL
  4. Remove the extension case assembly.
  5. Remove the center differential and transfer driven gear as a unit. refer to MANUAL TRANSMISSION AND DIFFERENTIAL (6MT)>TRANSFER DRIVEN GEAR>REMOVAL
  6. Pull out the straight pin.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Use the ST to remove the straight pin, and remove the selector lever COMPL.

    Special tool: 

    ST: 398791700 REMOVER

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Pull out the selector arm No. 2.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Remove the bolts and remove the shifter arm collar and neutral set spring.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  10. Remove the shifter arm No. 2, adjusting washers and thrust bearing.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  11. Use the ST to remove the straight pin, and remove the shifter arm and selector arm.

    Special tool: 

    ST: 398791700 REMOVER

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  12. Remove the oil seal using a screwdriver wrapped with tape, etc.
    NOTE: The illustration shows the oil seal of selector lever COMPL. Perform the same procedures for removing the shifter arm No. 2.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  13. Remove the needle bearing by using a tube with the diameter of 20 mm (0.79 in) or 21 mm (0.83 in) and the ST.

    Special tool: 

    ST: 18763AA000 COMPRESSOR SHAFT

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  14. Set the STI, ST2 and transfer case to the press.
    NOTE:
    • Set the ST2 under the STI.
    • Set the transfer case so that the hole for shifter arm is positioned vertically.

    Special tool: 

    STI: 498267300 CYLINDER HEAD TABLE

    ST2: 498267200 CYLINDER HEAD TABLE

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  15. Remove the roller bearing by using a round bar with the diameter of 22 mm (0.87 in) or 23 mm (0.91 in).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  16. Flip over the transfer case, and set an iron plate between transfer case and ST.
    NOTE:
    • Insert the steel plate, which is thicker than the exposed length of the transfer case knock pin, between the ST and transfer case.
    • Set the iron plate so that the transfer case knock pin does not ride on the iron plate.
  17. Remove the roller bearing by using a tube with the diameter of 22 mm (0.87 in) or 23 mm (0.91 in).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  18. Remove the shift accent plunger and the check ball spring.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
