DTC P0E04-14: Generator Phase V Current Sensor Circuit Short To Ground Or Open: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Detecting condition: 

Open circuit or short to ground of generator inverter V-phase current sensor (main)

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

  • Reduced output power
  • A driving range is limited
  • Engine always stopped
NOTE: For the diagnostic procedure, refer to DTC P0E0012. Refer to P-HEV DRIVE MOTOR (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE WITH DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>DTC P0E0012 GENERATOR PHASE U CURRENT SENSOR CIRCUIT SHORT TO BATTERY .
