Low-Pressure Hose

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Using the refrigerant recovery system, discharge refrigerant. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>REFRIGERANT RECOVERY PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .
  2. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  3. With the PCV hose connected, remove the air intake boot from the air cleaner case (rear) and throttle body, and move the air intake boot. Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>AIR INTAKE BOOT>REMOVAL .
  4. Remove the hose - pressure suction.
    1. Remove the nut.
    2. Remove the bolts and remove the hose - pressure suction.
      CAUTION:
      • Do not apply excessive force to the hose.
      • Seal the engaging parts of the disconnected hose, compressor assembly and expansion valve - cooling with a plug or vinyl tape to prevent foreign matter from entering.
