Rear Drive Shaft: Disassembly

  1. Take out the outer race (DOJ) from the shaft.
    CAUTION: Be careful not to damage the boot.
    1. Using a flat tip screwdriver or pliers, remove the boot band on the large end of boot (DOJ).
      CAUTION: Be careful not to damage the boot.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Remove the boot band on the small end of boot (DOJ) in the same manner.
    3. Remove the large end of boot (DOJ) from the outer race (DOJ).
    4. Remove the round snap ring at the neck of outer race (DOJ) with a flat tip screwdriver.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    5. Take out the outer race (DOJ) from the shaft assembly.
    6. Wipe off the grease and take out the ball bearings.
    CAUTION: The grease is a special grease (grease for constant velocity joints). Do not mix with other greases.
    NOTE: Disassemble the parts taking care not to lose balls.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Remove the cage from the inner race.
    1. Turn the cage by a half pitch to the track groove of inner race and shift the cage.
    2. Using pliers, remove the snap ring fixing the inner race to the shaft.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Take out the inner race.
    4. Take off the cage from shaft and remove the boot (DOJ).
    CAUTION: Be sure to wrap the spline sections with vinyl tape to prevent boot damages.
  3. Remove the boot (BJ or EBJ) in the same procedure as the boot (DOJ).
NOTE: BJ and EBJ cannot be disassembled.
