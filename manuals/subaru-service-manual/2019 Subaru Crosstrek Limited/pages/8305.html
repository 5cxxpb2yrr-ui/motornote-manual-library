Main Relay: Inspection

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Check that the main relay has no deformation, cracks or other damages.
  2. Measure the resistance between main relay terminals.
Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
Terminal No. Inspection conditions Standard
1 - 3 Always 1 MΩ or more
1 - 3 Apply battery voltage between terminals 2 and 4. Less than 1 Ω
