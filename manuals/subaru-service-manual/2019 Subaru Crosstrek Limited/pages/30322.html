Exterior Condenser Exit Temperature Sensor

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Install the exterior condenser exit temperature sensor together with the LOW solenoid valve. When installing the exterior condenser exit temperature sensor, refer to "Low pressure solenoid valve". Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>SOLENOID VALVE>INSTALLATION>LOW PRESSURE SOLENOID VALVE .
