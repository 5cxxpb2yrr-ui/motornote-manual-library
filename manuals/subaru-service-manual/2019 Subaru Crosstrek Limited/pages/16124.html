Inverter Center Water Pipe

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Set the inverter center water pipe.
  2. Secure the resolver sensor harness to the inverter center water pipe with the claw (B) and claw (C).
  3. Install the clip (A) securing the resolver sensor harness to the inverter center water pipe.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Install the bolt and clip (A) securing the inverter center water pipe and the resolver sensor harness.

    Tightening torque: 

    T1: 7.5 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.5 ft-lb)

    T2: 18 N.m (1.3 kgf-m, 9.6 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Connect the inverter rear water pipe outlet hose (A) and inverter rear water pipe inlet hose (B) to the inverter center water pipe.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Connect the inverter center water pipe outlet hose (B) and inverter center water pipe inlet hose (C) to the inverter center water pipe.
  7. Install the inverter center water pipe outlet hose (B) and inverter center water pipe inlet hose (C) to the clamp (A).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Install the crossmember support - rear RH.

    Refer to FRONT SUSPENSION>CROSSMEMBER SUPPORT>INSTALLATION .

  9. Install the propeller shaft.

    Refer to DRIVE SHAFT SYSTEM>PROPELLER SHAFT>INSTALLATION .

  10. Install the tunnel stay.

    Tightening torque: 

    18 N.m (1.8 kgf-m, 13.3 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  11. Install the rear exhaust pipe and muffler.

    Rear exhaust pipe: 

    Refer to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>REAR EXHAUST PIPE>INSTALLATION .

    Muffler: 

    Refer to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>MUFFLER>INSTALLATION .

  12. Install the floor under protector.

    Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>FLOOR UNDER PROTECTOR>INSTALLATION .

  13. Install the fuel tank protector and inverter assembly (with converter) cover.

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL TANK PROTECTOR>INSTALLATION .

  14. Fill engine coolant. (Inverter assembly side)

    Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>ENGINE COOLANT>REPLACEMENT>FILLING OF ENGINE COOLANT>INVERTER ASSEMBLY SIDE .
