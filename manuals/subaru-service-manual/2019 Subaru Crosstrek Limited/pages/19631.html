DTC P1C81-49: High Voltage Power Resource Circuit Consumption Circuit Short: Notes

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

1. - 

Outline of diagnosis: 

High-voltage circuit malfunctions between the HV battery and power control unit.

Excessive overcurrent equal to a short occurs during precharge

(time from when SMRP turns on until SMRG turns on).

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

Hybrid system stopped

Wiring diagram: 

Hybrid system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

    No:  -

  2. CHECK Electric compressor ASSEMBLY  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Check that the Service plug is disconnected. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .
    2. Disconnect the connector (HV36) from Inverter with converter assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>INVERTER AND CONVERTER ASSEMBLY .
    3. Using a tester, measure the resistance between Inverter with converter assembly connector terminals.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV36) No. 1 (+) - (HV36) No. 2 (-):

    Is the resistance 100 kΩ or more?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Go to  4.

  3. CHECK Main battery cable ASSY  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Disconnect the connector (HV35) from Inverter with converter assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>INVERTER AND CONVERTER ASSEMBLY .
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between Inverter with converter assembly connector terminals.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV35) No. 1 - (HV35) No. 2:

    Is the resistance 10 kΩ or more?

    Yes:  Replace Inverter with converter assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>INVERTER AND CONVERTER ASSEMBLY .

    No:  Replace Main battery cable ASSY. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>POWER CABLE .

  4. CHECK Air conditioning wire  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Disconnect the connector from Electric compressor ASSEMBLY. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>COMPRESSOR .
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between Inverter with converter assembly connector (HV104) terminals.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV104) No. 1 - (HV104) No. 2:

    Is the resistance 10 kΩ or more?

    Yes:  Replace Electric compressor ASSEMBLY. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>COMPRESSOR .

    No:  Replace Air conditioning wire. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>POWER CABLE .
