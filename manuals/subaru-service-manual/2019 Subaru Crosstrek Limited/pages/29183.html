DTC P1C00-12: Voltage Sensor After Boosting By PFC Boosting Circuit Short To Auxiliary Battery: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Outline of diagnosis: 

VH sensor output voltage exceeded the threshold value.

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

Plug-in charging suspended

NOTE: This DTC is output when the Electric vehicle charger assembly detects a VH sensor error. Therefore if this DTC was detected, replace the Electric vehicle charger assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>ELECTRIC VEHICLE CHARGER .
