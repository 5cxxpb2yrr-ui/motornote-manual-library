Hybrid Model

  1. Install the Reverse Automatic Braking Control Module.

    Tightening torque:

    7.5 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.5 ft-lb)

  2. Install the box - sub-trunk and the mat - rear floor. Ref. to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>REAR QUARTER TRIM>INSTALLATION>5 DOOR MODEL (HYBRID) .
  3. Connect the battery ground terminal. Ref. to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
