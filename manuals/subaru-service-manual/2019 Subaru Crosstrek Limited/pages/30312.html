Evaporator Sensor: Installation

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
CAUTION:
  • Replace the O-rings with new parts, and then apply compressor oil to install completely.
  • Before handling the airbag system components, refer to "CAUTION" of "General Description" in "AIRBAG SYSTEM". Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. Install the evaporator sensor at the position shown in the figure below.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Install the evaporator assembly. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>EVAPORATOR>INSTALLATION .
  3. Install the heater and cooling unit assembly. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>HEATER AND COOLING UNIT>INSTALLATION .
  4. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  5. Charge refrigerant. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>REFRIGERANT CHARGING PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .
  6. Fill engine coolant. (Engine side) Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>ENGINE COOLANT>REPLACEMENT>FILLING OF ENGINE COOLANT .
