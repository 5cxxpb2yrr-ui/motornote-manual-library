COWL PANEL - DESIGN CHANGE

Reference Number(s): 12-321-21R, Date of Issue:  March 26, 2024
SUBARU: 2018-21MY Crosstrek; 2017-23MY Impreza; 2020-23MY Legacy & Outback

APPLICABILITY

2018-21MY Crosstrek

2017-23MY Impreza

2020-23MY Legacy & Outback

INTRODUCTION

This bulletin announces a design change made to the cowl panel. These new parts utilize ten additional fixing points between the cowl panel and its rubber protector. This change was made to address a small number of cases where the rubber sealing portion had become separated as shown below. If this issue is confirmed, the cowl panel must be replaced.
