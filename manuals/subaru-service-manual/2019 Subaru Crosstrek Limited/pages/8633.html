Outline Of Diagnosis

Detect the malfunction of engine coolant temperature sensor output property.

Judge as NG when the actual engine coolant temperature does not reach the predetermined value and Σ (Estimated engine coolant temperature - actual engine coolant temperature) exceeded the predetermined value.
