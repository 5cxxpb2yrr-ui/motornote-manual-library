Remote Climate Control (RCC) General Description

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  • RCC operates the A/C system for a maximum of 10 minutes while parking if operating conditions are met.
  • Operation can be performed by operating the access key A/C button or smartphone (mobile terminal) when operating conditions are met.
  • Power of HV battery or household power supply is used as a system startup power to operate A/C system.
    NOTE:
    • Operate RCC by using power of HV battery or household power supply when connected to the charging outlet.
    • Operate RCC by using only HV battery power when not connected to the charging outlet.
