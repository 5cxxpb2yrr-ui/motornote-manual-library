Rear Alignment (Inspection Value)

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  • Impreza model
    Suspension height (Tolerance: +12 mm-24 mm (+0.47 in-0.94 in )) mm (in) -15 (-0.59)
    Camber (tolerance: ±0°45' Differences between RH and LH: 45' or less) -1°20'
    Toe-in mm (in) IN3±3 (IN 0.12±0.12)
    Toe angle (sum of both wheels): IN0°15'±15'
    Thrust angle (tolerance: 0°00'±30') 0°00'
  • Crosstrek model
    Suspension height (Tolerance: + 12 mm-24 mm (+0.47 in-0.94 in )) Depends on the shape of the flange bolt A. (1)(2)
    Camber (tolerance: ±0°45' Differences between RH and LH: 45' or less) -0°45'
    Toe-in mm (in) IN3±3 (IN 0.12±0.12)
    Toe angle (sum of both wheels): IN0°15'±15'
    Thrust angle (tolerance: 0°00'±30') 0°00'
    (1) -80 mm (-3.15 in) Dimension L 12.5 mm (0.49 in)

    (2) -78 mm (-3.07 in) Dimension L 14.5 mm (0.57 in)
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.

    The flange bolt A in dimension L 14.5 mm (0.57 in) has grooves on its seating surface.

    NOTE:
    • Adjust with the value less than the inspection value, taking aging variation into consideration.
    • Front toe-in, rear toe-in and front camber can be adjusted. Adjust if the value of toe-in or camber exceeds the tolerance range of the specification chart.
    • Other items except for front toe-in, rear toe-in and front camber that are described in the specification chart cannot be adjusted.
    • If other items exceed the tolerance range of the specification chart, check the suspension parts and connections for deformation. If defective, replace them with new parts.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.

A - B = Positive: Toe-in, Negative: Toe-out
