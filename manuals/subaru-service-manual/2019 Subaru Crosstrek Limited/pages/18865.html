Clutch Pipe And Hose: Removal

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
CAUTION: Before handling the airbag system components, refer to "CAUTION" of "General Description" in "AIRBAG SYSTEM". Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
  2. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery and wait for at least 60 seconds before starting work. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  3. Remove the clip from the air intake boot, and loosen the clamp.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the air intake boot, and place it aside so that it does not interfere with the work.
    CAUTION: Be careful not to pull out the PCV hose.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Drain the brake fluid. Refer to CLUTCH SYSTEM>CLUTCH FLUID .
  6. Disconnect the vacuum pipe & hose assembly.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Remove the clutch pipe.
    CAUTION: Be careful not to spill the brake fluid. Brake fluid spilled on the vehicle body will harm the paint surface; wash it off with water and wipe clean quickly if spilled.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Remove the clutch hose bracket.
  9. Remove the clamp, and then remove the clutch hose.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  10. Remove the clutch hose from operating cylinder.
  11. Disconnect the clutch pipe.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  12. Remove the two-way connector and the clutch connector bracket.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  13. Remove the tank hose.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  14. Remove the instrument panel lower cover. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>INSTRUMENT PANEL LOWER COVER>REMOVAL .
  15. Remove the steering column. Refer to POWER ASSISTED SYSTEM (POWER STEERING)>STEERING COLUMN>REMOVAL .
  16. Remove the clutch pedal assembly. Refer to CLUTCH SYSTEM>CLUTCH PEDAL>REMOVAL .
  17. Remove the clutch pipe from the mass damper, clutch damper, master cylinder and clutch pipe grommet.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
