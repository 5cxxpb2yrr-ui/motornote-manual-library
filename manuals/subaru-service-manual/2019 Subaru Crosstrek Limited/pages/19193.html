PTC Heater: Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
CAUTION: Before handling the airbag system components, always refer to "CAUTION" of "General Description" in "AIRBAG SYSTEM". Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. Disconnect the battery ground terminal and wait for at least 60 seconds before starting work. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the instrument panel assembly and the beam COMPL - steering. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>INSTRUMENT PANEL ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the duct - foot passenger. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>HEATER DUCT>REMOVAL>FOOT .
  4. Remove the filter cover. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>A/C FILTER>REPLACEMENT .
  5. Remove the PTC heater.
    1. Remove the screws and remove the PTC heater.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    NOTE: Pull out the PTC heater so that it does not contact the heater case.
