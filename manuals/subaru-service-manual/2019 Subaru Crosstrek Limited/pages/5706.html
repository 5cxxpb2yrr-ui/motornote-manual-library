DTC C0501: Left Front Wheel Speed Sensor Range/Performance: Notes

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

1. - 

DTC detecting condition: 

Under at 10 km/h or more speed, the output of a single wheel speed sensor is lower than output from other wheel sensors, and this condition continues for 30 seconds or more.

Trouble symptom: 

  • Regenerative brake cannot be controlled.
  • Brake assist does not operate.
  • EyeSight functions (ACC/PCS) do not operate.

Wiring diagram: 

Electronic controlled brake system Ref. to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>ELECTRIC CONTROL BRAKE SYSTEM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  1. CHECK INSTALLATION CONDITION OF WHEEL SPEED SENSOR. 

    Check the installation condition of the wheel speed sensor.

    Is the wheel speed sensor installed properly?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Install the wheel speed sensor properly.

  2. CHECK WHEEL SPEED SENSOR AND MAGNETIC ENCODER. 

    Inspect wheel speed sensor and magnetic encoder for contamination or damage.

    Is there adherence of any foreign materials or oil, or damage to the components?

    Yes:  Clean or replace the wheel speed sensor and the magnetic encoder.

    No:  Go to  3.

  3. CHECK DATA MONITOR. 

    Display the following items in [Data monitor] using Subaru Select Monitor. Ref. to ELECTRIC BRAKE BOOSTER (DIAGNOSTICS)>DATA MONITOR .

    • [Speed sensor FR]
    • [Speed sensor FL]
    • [Speed sensor RR]
    • [Speed sensor RL]
    NOTE: Check that the speed is 0 km/h when the vehicle stopped.

    Is there a significant change in the sensor compared with sensors of other wheels while driving?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Currently, it is normal. There may have been a temporary poor contact in the harness and connector or a temporary noise interference.

  4. CHECK CONNECTOR. 

    Check the connecting condition of the intermediate connectors (AD81, B652).

    Is there looseness, deformation, or corrosion at the connectors?

    Yes:  Repair or replace the connector.

    No:  Go to  5.

  5. CHECK HARNESS. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the coupling connector. (AD81, B652)
    3. Disconnect the VDC CM connector.
    4. Measure the resistance between the intermediate connector and the VDC CM connector.

    Connector & terminal 

    FR wheel 

    (B652) No. 4 - (B310) No. 34:

    FL wheel 

    (B652) No. 5 - (B310) No. 45:

    RR wheel 

    (B652) No. 9 - (B310) No. 40:

    RL wheel 

    (B652) No. 10 - (B310) No. 31:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  6.

    No:  Repair or replace the harness.

  6. CHECK HARNESS. 

    Measure the resistance between intermediate connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    FR wheel 

    (B652) No. 4 - Chassis ground:

    FL wheel 

    (B652) No. 5 - Chassis ground:

    RR wheel 

    (B652) No. 9 - Chassis ground:

    RL wheel 

    (B652) No. 10 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance 10 kΩ or more?

    Yes:  Go to  7.

    No:  Repair or replace the harness.

  7. CHECK HARNESS BETWEEN EBB Control Module AND INTERMEDIATE CONNECTOR. 
    1. Remove brake booster assembly (with the master cylinder) from vehicle.
    2. Disconnect the EBB Control Module connector.
    3. Measure the resistance between EBB Control Module connector and intermediate connector.

    Connector & terminal 

    FR wheel 

    (AD82) No. 12 - (AD81) No. 4:

    FL wheel 

    (AD82) No. 36 - (AD81) No. 5:

    RR wheel 

    (AD82) No. 16 - (AD81) No. 9:

    RL wheel 

    (AD82) No. 39 - (AD81) No. 10:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Replace the brake booster assembly (with the master cylinder). Ref. to BRAKE>BRAKE BOOSTER .

    No:  Repair or replace the harness.
