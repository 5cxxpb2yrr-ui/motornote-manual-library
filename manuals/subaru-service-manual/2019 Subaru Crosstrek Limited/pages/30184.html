Hybrid Powertrain Control Module: Installation

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
CAUTION:
  • To avoid the damage to bracket and ECM, do not reuse the bracket.
  • After installing the bracket to ECM, do not separate the bracket.
  • If the bracket has been installed to ECM in the wrong way, replace both parts to new parts.
  1. Install the AWD control module onto the hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly.

    Tightening torque: 

    5 N.m (0.5 kgf-m, 3.7 ft-lb)

  2. Install the hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly together with the AWD control module.

    Tightening torque: 

    18 N.m (1.8 kgf-m, 13.3 ft-lb)

  3. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
