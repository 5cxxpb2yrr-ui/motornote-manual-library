Inspection When Charge Warning Light Illuminates

NOTE: If the alternator overheats, the charge warning light illuminates temporarily.
  1. CHECK DTC  .

    Read the DTC using the Subaru Select Monitor.

    NOTE: For detailed operation procedures, refer to "Help" of application.

    Is DTC detected?

    Yes: 

    1. Repair the corresponding system.
    2. Go to  2.

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK V-BELT  .

    Check the V-belt for abnormalities.

    Is the V-belt normal?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Replace the V-belt or V-belt tensioner assembly.

  3. CHECK GENERATOR B TERMINAL  .

    Check the voltage of the generator terminal B.

    Is the battery voltage applied?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Repair the harness between generator and MAIN SBF.

  4. CHECK CONTINUITY BETWEEN GENERATOR MAIN BODY AND CHASSIS GROUND  .

    Check continuity between generator main body and chassis ground.

    Is there continuity?

    Yes:  Go to  5.

    No:  Check and repair the installation condition of the generator, chassis ground and engine ground.

  5. CHECK COMBINATION METER  .

    Perform the self-diagnosis of combination meter.

    Is the charge warning light OK?

    Yes:  Repair or replace the generator.

    No:  Replace the combination meter.
