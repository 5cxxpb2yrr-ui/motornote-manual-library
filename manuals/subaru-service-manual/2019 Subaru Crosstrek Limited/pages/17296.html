Front Hub Unit Bearing: Inspection

  1. Moving the front tire up and down by hand, check there is no looseness in bearing, and check the wheel rotates smoothly.
    CAUTION: If there is unsmooth sliding operation or abnormal noise in the bearing, replace the hub unit bearing.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Inspect the hub unit bearing for axial looseness.
    1. Remove the front tire, front disc brake and front disc rotor.
    2. Using a dial gauge, check the axial looseness. Replace the hub unit bearing if looseness exceeds the limit.

    Service limit: 

    0.05 mm (0.0020 in)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
