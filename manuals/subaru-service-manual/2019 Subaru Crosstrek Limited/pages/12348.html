Side Grille

  1. Remove the grille assembly - ventilation.
    1. Attach the protective tape (a).
    2. Release the claws and clips, then detach the grille assembly - ventilation.
      CAUTION: Wrap a remover, etc. with protective tape or cloth. Insert a remover, etc. from inside the grille ventilation side. Be careful not to damage the grille ventilation side.
