Intake Manifold Assembly: Disassembly

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. When the engine coolant (engine side) is drained before removing the intake manifold assembly
    1. Remove the throttle body. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>THROTTLE BODY>REMOVAL .
  2. Remove the purge control solenoid valve. Refer to EMISSION CONTROL (AUX. EMISSION CONTROL DEVICES) (H4DO (HEV))>PURGE CONTROL SOLENOID VALVE>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the EGR pipe. Refer to EMISSION CONTROL (AUX. EMISSION CONTROL DEVICES) (H4DO (HEV))>EGR PIPE>REMOVAL .
  4. Remove the manifold absolute pressure sensor. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>MANIFOLD ABSOLUTE PRESSURE SENSOR>REMOVAL .
  5. Remove the engine wiring harness stay (A) and collector cover bracket (B).
