Trunk Lid Panel

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY>GROUND TERMINAL REMOVAL OR INSTALLATION .
  2. Remove the trunk room trim. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>TRUNK ROOM TRIM>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the lock assembly - trunk lid. Refer to SECURITY AND LOCKS>TRUNK LID LATCH AND ACTUATOR ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL .
  4. Disconnect the connector to remove the trunk lid harness.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the rear finisher light assembly. Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>REAR FINISHER LIGHT ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL .
  6. Remove the rearview camera assembly. Refer to ENTERTAINMENT & MONITORING>CAMERA>REMOVAL>REAR .
  7. Detach the rear spoiler. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>REAR SPOILER>REMOVAL .
  8. Remove the trunk lid COMPL.
    CAUTION: Trunk lid COMPL is heavy. When removing and installing it, always work in a team of two or more persons.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Remove the rear seat cushion assembly. Refer to SEATS>REAR SEAT>REMOVAL .
  10. Release the clips and claws, and remove the side sill covers - rear INN on the left and right sides.
    CAUTION: Do not pull with excessive force. Doing so may damage the claws of the side sill cover - rear INN.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  11. Remove the trim panel - rear pillar UPR and trim panel - rear pillar LWR on the left and right sides. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>REAR QUARTER TRIM>REMOVAL .
  12. Remove the bolts, and remove the lower anchor of the seat belt - rear shoulder CTR.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  13. Remove the trim panel assembly - rear shelf. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>REAR SHELF TRIM>REMOVAL .
  14. Remove the trunk lid arm assembly.
    1. Remove the torsion bar - trunk lid from the trunk lid arm. Refer to EXTERIOR BODY PANELS>TRUNK LID>REMOVAL>TORSION BAR .
    2. Remove the left and right bolts, then remove the trunk lid arm assembly.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
