High-Pressure Fuel Delivery Pipe

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
CAUTION:
  • Be careful not to spill fuel.
  • Catch the fuel from the pipes using a container or cloth.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the intake manifold assembly. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>INTAKE MANIFOLD ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the fuel pipe insulator No. 3. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL INSULATOR>REMOVAL .
  4. Remove the high-pressure fuel delivery pipes.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the fuel pipe protector No. 2.
    NOTE: Perform this procedure when required.
