5 Door Model

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the light assembly - rear combination.
    1. Release the claws, and then remove the cap - rear combination light.
      CAUTION: When using a flat tip screwdriver, use an appropriate sized tool for the insertion opening, apply protective tape or cloth around the tool, and be careful not to cause damage.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Release the bolts and clips, then pull out the light assembly - rear combination to the rear of the vehicle as shown in the figure.
      CAUTION: Be careful not to damage the clips.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Disconnect the connector and remove the light assembly - rear combination.
