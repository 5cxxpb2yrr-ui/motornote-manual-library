DTC U1433: Invalid Data Received From Eyesight

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

DTC detecting condition: 

CAN data from stereo camera is abnormal.

Trouble symptom: 

  • EyeSight screen is not displayed.
  • Forward vehicle is not displayed in the EyeSight screen.
  • When EyeSight detects white lines, the white lines are not displayed in the EyeSight screen.
  • In lane keep status, lanes are not displayed in the EyeSight screen.
  • Braking status of driver's own vehicle is not displayed correctly in the EyeSight screen.
  • Lane departure warning OFF indicator is not displayed correctly in the electrical parts operation status screen.
  • Pre-collision OFF indicator is not displayed correctly in the electrical parts operation status screen.
  • RAB status is not displayed correctly in the electrical parts operation status screen.
NOTE: For the diagnostic procedure, refer to DTC U0074. Refer to MULTI-FUNCTION DISPLAY (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE WITH DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>DTC U0074 CONTROL MODULE COMMUNICATION BUS "BODY-CAN" OFF .
