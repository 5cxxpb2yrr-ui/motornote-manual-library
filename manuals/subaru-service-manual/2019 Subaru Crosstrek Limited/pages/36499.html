Light Control Sensor: Inspection

  1. Connect the connector.
  2. Turn the ignition switch to ON, and set the lighting switch to the AUTO position.
  3. Measure the voltage between connector terminals.

    Preparation tool: 

    Circuit tester

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Terminal No. Inspection conditions Standard
    2 (+) - 1 (-) Measure the voltage when the area around the light control sensor, which was dark, becomes bright. Dark condition: Approx. 0.6 V or less
    Bright condition: Approx. 3 V or more
  4. Replace the light control sensor if the inspection result is not within the standard value.
