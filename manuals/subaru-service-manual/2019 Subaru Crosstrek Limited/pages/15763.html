Hybrid Model

• V-BELT 

  1. Set the V-belt (A) as shown in the figure.
    CAUTION:
    • Be sure to use a new V-belt.
    • Be careful not to let oil, grease or coolant contact the V-belt.
    • Be careful not to rub the V-belt end surface with bare hands; exposed core may cause injury.
    • Wipe off any dust, oil and water on the groove of each pulley with cloth.
  2. Insert the wrench into the crank pulley bolt and hold it with your fingers to ensure the V-belt (A) rib will not be misaligned from the rib groove of the water pump pulley.
  3. With the state described in step 2, slowly turn the crank pulley clockwise until the V-belt (A) rib is seated properly on the rib groove of the water pump pulley. Then install the V-belt (A).
    CAUTION:
    • Before rotating, make sure that the V-belt rib is properly seated on the rib groove of the crank pulley and idler pulley.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • While rotating, be careful not to get your finger pinched between the V-belt and pulley to avoid injury.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. If the V-belt rib is dislocated from the rib groove of the crank pulley while installing the V-belt
    NOTE: If the V-belt rib is dislocated from the rib groove of the crank pulley while installing the V-belt, perform the following steps for correction.
    1. Insert the wrench into the crank pulley bolt, and press the point shown in the figure with your fingers to ensure the V-belt at the rib side is tilted to the engine side.
    2. With the state described in step (1), slowly turn the crank pulley clockwise for correction until the V-belt rib is seated properly on the rib groove of the crank pulley.
      CAUTION: While rotating, be careful not to get your finger pinched between the V-belt and pulley to avoid injury.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Turn the crank pulley clockwise slowly by approx. two turns until the V-belt is fitted.
    CAUTION: Before rotating, check again that the V-belt rib is properly seated on the rib groove of each pulley.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Fig 1: Engine Drive Belt Routing Diagram (Hybrid)
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. When working on the vehicle
    NOTE: When working on the vehicle, perform the following steps also.
    1. Install the front under cover. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>FRONT UNDER COVER>INSTALLATION .

• IDLER PULLEY 

  1. Install the idler pulley.
    NOTE: When installing the idler pulley, be careful of the idler pulley cover direction.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.

    Tightening torque: 

    36 N.m (3.7 kgf-m, 26.6 ft-lb)

  2. Install the V-belts. Refer to MECHANICAL (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>V-BELT>INSTALLATION>HYBRID MODEL>V-BELT .
