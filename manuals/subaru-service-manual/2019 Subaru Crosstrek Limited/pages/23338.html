Diagnostic Method

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

Judge as NG when the following conditions are established.

JUDGMENT VALUE SPECIFICATION

Malfunction Criteria Threshold Value
Evaporative Leak Check Module pressure (gauge) More than
-400 Pa (-3 mmHg,
-0.1 inHg)
Sum value*
(Purge gas - generated evaporating gas)		 3g (0.11oz) or more

*: It is approximately 2sec per the integration of purge to reach 1g at vehicle speed of 40 MPH.

Time needed for diagnosis:  Less than 1 s

Malfunction indicator light illumination:  Illuminates when malfunction occurs in 2 continuous driving cycles.
