Monitor Description

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

The Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly monitors signals of HV battery voltage (VB) and boost converter voltage (VL) sensors. When a large difference occurs between the voltages from the VB and VL sensors, the Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly interprets this as a failure of either of the sensors.

The Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly will illuminate the MIL and store a DTC.
