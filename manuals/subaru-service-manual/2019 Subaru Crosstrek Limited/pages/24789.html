<Mode A (vacuum pump operation confirmation and characteristics stability)> 

Detect the vacuum pump operation trouble.

Judge as NG when the following conditions are established.

Judge as OK if the following conditions are not established, and warm up for five minutes to stabilize the vacuum pump characteristics.

Malfunction Criteria Threshold Value DTC
Pressure sensor output value More than -224 Pa (-1.68 mmHg, -0.1 inHg) P2404

<Mode C> 

Judgment 1 (Switching valve operation confirmation)

Measure the pressure increase when switching valve is changed from open to close, and detect the stuck to open/close malfunctions of the switching valve.

Judge as NG when the following conditions are established.

Malfunction Criteria Threshold Value DTC

<Mode F (tank side leak advanced normal judgment)> 

Judge as normal if the fuel tank pressure is within the specified value range, and go to the diagnosis flow 2.

JUDGMENT VALUE SPECIFICATION

Malfunction Criteria Threshold Value DTC
Fuel tank pressure 3.5 kPa (26.4 mmHg, 1inHg) or more
Or,
-2.6 kPa (-19.8 mmHg, -0.8 inHg) or less		 P04EC or P04EE

<Mode G (sealing valve close stuck)> 

Judge as sealing valve close stuck if the amount of change of fuel tank pressure is equal to or smaller than the amount of change of EL Contol Module pressure.

Detected as NG when the following conditions are established.

JUDGMENT VALUE SPECIFICATION

Malfunction Criteria Threshold Value DTC
Fuel tank pressure change amount - Fuel tank pressure More than 0.8 kPa (6.18 mmHg, 0.2 inHg) P2451

Time needed for diagnosis: 

  • Diagnosis flow 1: 39 min

    Confirm the malfunction at next engine start.

  • Diagnosis flow 2: 23 min

    Confirm the malfunction at next engine start.

Malfunction indicator light illumination:  Illuminates when malfunction occurs in 2 continuous driving cycles.
