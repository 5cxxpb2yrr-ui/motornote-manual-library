DTC B1805: Short In Passenger's Airbag

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

Diagnosis start condition: 

When the ignition voltage is 10 - 16 V.

DTC detecting condition: 

  • Airbag main harness circuit is shorted.
  • Airbag module harness circuit (passenger's side) is shorted.
  • Passenger's airbag module is faulty.
  • Airbag control module is faulty.

Wiring diagram:

CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to AIRBAG (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK POOR CONTACT OF CONNECTORS. 

    Check for poor contact of the connectors between the airbag control module and the passenger's airbag module.

    Is there poor contact?

    Yes:  Replace the airbag harness.

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK AIRBAG MAIN HARNESS (PASSENGER'S AIRBAG HARNESS). 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF, disconnect the battery ground terminal, and wait 60 seconds or more. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    2. Disconnect the connectors (AB7) and (AB2).
    3. Disconnect the connector (AB66) from the driver's knee airbag module.
    4. Disconnect the connectors (AB6, AB17, AB18) from airbag control module.
    5. Connect the connectors (AB6, AB17, AB18) to the connector (1AG) in the test harness AG, and disengage the short mechanism of the connectors (AB6, AB17, AB18).
    6. Disconnect the passenger's airbag module connector (AB9).
    7. Using a probe, measure the resistance between the terminals of connector (AB9). Refer to AIRBAG (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>PREPARATION TOOL>HOW TO USE PROBE .
      CAUTION: When measuring the resistance, make sure that the probe is inserted from the back side (harness side) of the connector. Also, do not insert the probe forcibly.

      Connector & terminal 

      (AB9) No. 1 - (AB9) No. 2:

    Is the resistance 1 MΩ or more?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Replace the airbag main harness along with body harness.

  3. CHECK AIRBAG CONTROL MODULE. 
    1. Disconnect the test harness AG from the connectors (AB6, AB17, AB18).
    2. Connect the connectors (AB6, AB17, AB18) and the airbag control module.
    3. Connect the connectors (AB7) and (AB2).
    4. Connect the connector (AB66) to the driver's knee airbag module.
    5. Connect the battery ground terminal and turn the ignition switch to ON. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    6. Execute the inspection mode. Refer to AIRBAG (DIAGNOSTICS)>INSPECTION MODE .
    7. Read the DTC of [Airbag] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to AIRBAG (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is DTC B1805 displayed? (Current malfunction)

    Yes:  Replace the airbag control module. Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>AIRBAG CONTROL MODULE>REMOVAL .

    No:  Go to  4.

  4. CHECK PASSENGER'S AIRBAG MODULE. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF, disconnect the battery ground terminal, and wait 60 seconds or more. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    2. Connect the passenger's airbag module connector (AB9).
    3. Connect the battery ground terminal and turn the ignition switch to ON. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    4. Execute the inspection mode. Refer to AIRBAG (DIAGNOSTICS)>INSPECTION MODE .
    5. Read the DTC of [Airbag] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to AIRBAG (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is DTC B1805 displayed? (Current malfunction)

    Yes:  Replace the passenger's airbag module. Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>PASSENGER'S AIRBAG MODULE>REMOVAL .

    No:  Go to  5.

  5. CHECK FOR ANY OTHER DTC ON DISPLAY. 

    Read the DTC of [Airbag] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to AIRBAG (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is any other DTC displayed? (Current malfunction)

    Yes:  Perform the diagnosis for other displayed DTCs. Refer to AIRBAG (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>LIST .

    No:  Finish the diagnosis.
