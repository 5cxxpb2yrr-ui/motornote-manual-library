Center Antenna

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

CAUTION: When replacing the antenna, do not reuse the tapping screw. Always replace with new tapping screws.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery and wait for at least 60 seconds before starting work. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the rear seat cushion assembly. Refer to SEATS>REAR SEAT>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the center antenna assembly - interior.
    1. Disconnect the connector.
    2. Remove the screw, and remove the center antenna assembly - interior.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    NOTE: Do not remove the antenna assembly from the cover. If removed, the antenna assembly and cover mounting becomes loose, causing abnormal noises.
