Charging Inlet Door

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  • CHARGE LID LOCK ASSEMBLY WITH MOTOR 
    1. Check operation
    1. Check the operation when battery voltage is applied to the connector terminal.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
      Terminal No. Inspection conditions Specification
      1 (+) - 2 (-) Apply battery voltage. The shaft operates.
    2. Check the shaft stroke (b) when lever (a) is operated.
      Measurement range Inspection conditions Standard
      Shaft stroke When lever is operated 11 mm
    3. Replace the charge lid lock assembly with motor if it is found to be defective.
  • PUSH-LIFTER SWITCH 
  1. INSPECT THE RESISTANCE
  1. Measure the resistance between connector terminals.

    Preparation tool: 

    Circuit tester

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Terminal No. Inspection conditions Standard
    1 - 5 When the lifter is not pressed in (OFF) 10 kΩ or more
    1 - 5 When the lifter is pressed in (ON) Less than 1 Ω
    2 - 4 When the lifter is not pressed in (OFF) Less than 1 Ω
    2 - 4 When the lifter is pressed in (ON) 10 kΩ or more
  2. Replace the EV charger lid switch assembly if the inspection result is not within the standard value.
  • LID SWITCH 
  1. Check the charging connector lock/unlock switch.
    1. Measure the resistance between connector terminals.

      Preparation tool: 

      Circuit tester

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
      Terminal No. Inspection conditions Standard
      2 - 4 When the lock/unlock switch is pressed Less than 50 Ω
      When the lock/unlock switch is not pressed 10 kΩ or more
    2. Replace the EV charger lid switch assembly if the inspection result is not within the standard value.
  2. Check illumination of the charging inlet light.
  1. Apply battery voltage between the connector terminals to check the lighting condition of the light.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Terminal No. Inspection conditions Specification
    1 (+) - 4 (-) Apply battery voltage. Light ON
  2. Replace the EV charger lid switch assembly if the inspection result is not within the standard value.
