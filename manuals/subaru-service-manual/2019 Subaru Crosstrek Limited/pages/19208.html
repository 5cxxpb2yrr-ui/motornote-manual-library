DTC B1443: Mode Door Actuator Circuit Stuck

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

DTC detecting condition: 

Mode door actuator is locked.

Trouble symptom: 

Vent does not change.

Wiring diagram: 

Air conditioning system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>AIR CONDITIONING SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK CONNECTOR. 
    1. Check connecting condition of the A/C control panel connector and mode door actuator connector.
    2. Read the DTC of [Air Conditioner] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is DTC B1443 displayed? (Current malfunction)

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Even if DTC is displayed, the circuit has returned to a normal condition at this time.

    Reproduce the failure, and then perform the diagnosis again.

    NOTE: In this case, temporary poor contact of connector, temporary open or short circuit of harness may be the cause.
  2. CHECK ACTUATOR. 

    Using the Subaru Select Monitor, check the following items in the [Active Test] of [Air Conditioner]. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>ACTIVE TEST .

    • Mode Door Actuator Opening Angle Target

    Did the mode door actuator move to the specified target opening angle?

    Yes:  Mode door actuator circuit is normal.

    No:  Go to  3.

  3. CHECK HARNESS. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    2. Measure the voltage between mode door actuator connector terminals using a tester.

      Connector & terminal 

      (i176) No. 3 (+) - (i176) No. 1 (-):

    Is the voltage 4.5 - 5.5 V?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Repair or replace the open, short circuit of the harness.

  4. CHECK HARNESS (OPEN CIRCUIT). 
    1. Disconnect the connector from the A/C control panel.
    2. Using a tester, check the continuity between A/C control panel connector and mode door actuator connector terminals.

      Connector & terminal 

      (i176) No. 3 - (i181) No. 7:

      (i176) No. 1 - (i181) No. 19:

      (i176) No. 2 - (i80) No. 11:

      (i176) No. 4 - (i181) No. 28:

      (i176) No. 5 - (i181) No. 4:

    Is there continuity?

    Yes:  Go to  5.

    No:  Repair or replace the open circuit of harness.

  5. CHECK MODE DOOR ACTUATOR. 

    Check the mode door actuator parts. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>MODE DOOR ACTUATOR .

    Is the mode door actuator OK?

    Yes:  Replace the A/C control panel. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>CONTROL PANEL .

    No:  Replace the mode door actuator. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>MODE DOOR ACTUATOR .
