CROSSTREK Model

CAUTION: Be careful not to damage the mirror surface and the glass - front window.
  1. Release the claws, and then remove the cover.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Release the lock lever, and slide the mirror assembly - inner rearview to remove.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Replace the glass - front window if the base - inner mirror is damaged.
