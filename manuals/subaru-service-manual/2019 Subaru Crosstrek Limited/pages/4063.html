Push Button Ignition Switch: Removal

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery and wait for at least 60 seconds before starting work. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the cover assembly - instrument panel LWR driver. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>INSTRUMENT PANEL LOWER COVER>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the grille assembly - CTR ventilation LH. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>AIR VENT GRILLE>REMOVAL>CENTER GRILLE ASSEMBLY .
  4. Remove the visor assembly - combination meter.
    1. Release the clips of the cover assembly - visor LWR.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Release the screws, clips and claws.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Disconnect the connector, and remove the visor assembly - combination meter.
  5. Remove the panel - starter SW.
    1. Release the claws.
    2. Detach the connectors and remove the panel - starter SW.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Remove the switch - push button start.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
