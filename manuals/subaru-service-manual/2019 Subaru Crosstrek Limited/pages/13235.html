Hydraulic Control And Lubrication

Type Electronic hydraulic control (gear ratio is changed by signals of vehicle speed and accelerator opening angle.)
Fluid Specified fluid: SUBARU CVT FLUID LINEARTRONIC II
CAUTION: Always use specified CVTF. Using other fluid will cause malfunction.
Fluid capacity L (US qt, Imp qt) 10.48 - 10.98 (11.08 - 11.61, 9.22 - 9.66)
Lubrication system Forced feed lubrication with oil pump
