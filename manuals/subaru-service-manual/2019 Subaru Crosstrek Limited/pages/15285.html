DTC B14A7: Variable Solenoid Valve Circuit

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

DTC detecting condition: 

Variable flow change solenoid valve circuit is open or shorted.

Trouble symptom: 

Refrigerant pressure cannot be changed.

Wiring diagram: 

Air conditioning system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (IMPREZA MODEL)>AIR CONDITIONING SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>AIR CONDITIONING SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK CONNECTOR. 
    1. Check connecting condition of the A/C control panel connector and variable flow change solenoid connector.
    2. Read the DTC of [Air Conditioner] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is DTC B14A7 displayed? (Current malfunction)

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Even if DTC is displayed, the circuit has returned to a normal condition at this time.

    Reproduce the failure, and then perform the diagnosis again.

    NOTE: In this case, temporary poor contact of connector, temporary open or short circuit of harness may be the cause.
  2. ACTIVE TEST. 
    1. Attach the manifold gauge.
    2. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, check the following items in the [Active Test] of [Air Conditioner]. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>ACTIVE TEST .
      • Variable Flow Change Sol Duty Setting

    Did the variable flow change solenoid operate to the specified target, and the pressure change?

    Yes:  Variable flow change solenoid circuit is normal.

    No:  Go to  3.

  3. CHECK VARIABLE FLOW CHANGE SOLENOID. 
    1. Disconnect the variable flow change solenoid connector.
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between terminals of the solenoid.

      Terminals 

      (Compressor solenoid side) No. 1 - No. 2:

    Is the resistance 10 - 12 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Replace the compressor. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>COMPRESSOR .

  4. CHECK HARNESS (OPEN CIRCUIT). 
    1. Disconnect the A/C control panel connector.
    2. Using a tester, check for continuity between the harness terminals.

      Connector & terminal 

      (F160) No. 2 - (i88) No. 11:

      (F160) No. 1 - Chassis ground:

    Is there continuity?

    Yes:  Go to  5.

    No:  Repair or replace the open circuit of harness.

  5. CHECK HARNESS (SHORTED BETWEEN LINES AND SHORTED TO GROUND). 

    Using a tester, check for continuity between the harness terminals.

    Connector & terminal 

    (F160) No. 2 - Chassis ground:

    (F160) No. 1 - (F160) No. 2:

    Is there continuity?

    Yes:  Repair or replace the short circuit of the harness.

    No:  Replace the A/C control panel. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>CONTROL PANEL .
