Transmission Control Device: Removal

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Remove the transmission assembly from the vehicle. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL .
  2. Remove the inhibitor switch. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>INHIBITOR SWITCH>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the oil pan and oil strainer. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>OIL PAN AND STRAINER>REMOVAL .
  4. Shift the manual plate to "N" range, and remove the spring pin.
    NOTE: Prevent the spring pin from dropping in the transmission using paper towel etc.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the detent spring.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Remove the bolt, and remove the shifter arm shaft.
    NOTE: Do not damage the lip of oil seal press-fitted in the case.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Remove the manual plate from the shifter arm shaft, and remove the shift connecting rod of the manual valve.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Remove the manual plate and parking rod.
  9. Remove the manual valve.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  10. Remove the extension case. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>EXTENSION CASE>REMOVAL .
  11. Remove the parking support.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
