Front Exhaust Pipe: Disassembly

  1. Remove the front oxygen (A/F) sensor and rear oxygen sensor.

    Except for hybrid model: 

    Front oxygen (A/F) sensor 

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FRONT OXYGEN (A/F) SENSOR>REMOVAL .

    Rear oxygen sensor 

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>REAR OXYGEN SENSOR>REMOVAL .

    Hybrid model: 

    Front oxygen (A/F) sensor 

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FRONT OXYGEN (A/F) SENSOR>REMOVAL .

    Rear oxygen sensor 

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>REAR OXYGEN SENSOR>REMOVAL .

  2. Remove each exhaust cover.
