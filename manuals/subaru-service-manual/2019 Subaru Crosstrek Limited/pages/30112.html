Clutch Switch: Adjustment

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Loosen the lock nut of the clutch start switch.
  2. Fully depress the clutch pedal and hold it.
  3. Adjust the gap (A) between the clutch pedal plate and the clutch start switch to be 7.3 mm (0.29 in).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Tighten the lock nut.

    Tightening torque: 

    8 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.9 ft-lb)
