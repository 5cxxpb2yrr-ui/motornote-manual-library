Unit Inspection

LOCK ASSY - FUEL 

  1. Check lock assembly - fuel

    Check the fuel lock operation when battery voltage is applied between the connector terminals.

    Preparation tool: 

    Circuit tester

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Terminal No. Inspection conditions Specification
    1 (+) - 2 (-) Connect battery to the terminals OPEN
  2. Replace the lock assembly - fuel if faulty is found in the inspection.

FUEL FLAP STATUS SWITCH 

  1. Check fuel flap status switch

    Measure the resistance between connector terminals.

    Preparation tool: 

    Circuit tester

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Terminal No. Inspection conditions Standard
    1 - 2 When the switch is not pressed Less than 1 Ω
    When the switch is pressed 1 MΩ or more
  2. Replace the saucer - COMPL if faulty is found in the inspection.

REFUELING REQUEST SWITCH 

  1. Measure the resistance between connector terminals.

    Preparation tool: 

    Circuit tester

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Terminal No. Inspection conditions Standard
    1 - 4 OFF 1 MΩ or more
    ON Less than 1 Ω
  2. Apply battery voltage between the connector terminals to check lighting condition of illumination inside the switch.
    CAUTION: When applying battery voltage, do not mix up the positive (+) side and the negative (-) side.
    Incorrect polarity connection may cause LED damage inside the switch.
    Terminal No. Inspection conditions Specification
    2 (+) - 3 (-) Apply battery voltage. Light ON
  3. Replace the refueling request switch if faulty is found in the inspection.
