Main Shaft Assembly: Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Remove the manual transmission assembly from the vehicle. refer to MANUAL TRANSMISSION AND DIFFERENTIAL (6MT)>MANUAL TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL .
  2. Remove the transfer case together with the extension case assembly, refer to MANUAL TRANSMISSION AND DIFFERENTIAL (6MT)>TRANSFER CASE AND EXTENSION CASE ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL
  3. Remove the transmission case. refer to MANUAL TRANSMISSION AND DIFFERENTIAL (6MT)>TRANSMISSION CASE>REMOVAL 
  4. Remove the drive pinion shaft assembly. refer to MANUAL TRANSMISSION AND DIFFERENTIAL (6MT)>DRIVE PINION SHAFT ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL .
  5. Remove the main shaft assembly.
