RH Side

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the air cleaner case. Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>AIR CLEANER CASE>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the clip from the air intake boot, and loosen the clamp. (Hybrid model)
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the air intake boot, and place it aside so that it does not interfere with the work. (Hybrid model)
    CAUTION: Be careful not to pull out the PCV hose.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the clip (A) securing the harness to the inverter front water pipe, and remove the inverter center water pipe inlet hose and the inverter center water pipe outlet hose from the clamp (B). (Hybrid model)
  6. Remove the bolt securing the inverter front water pipe to the vehicle. (Hybrid model)
  7. Remove the clip (C) securing the pipe - evaporator cooling to the bracket, and place it aside so that it does not interfere with work. (Hybrid model)
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Disconnect the connector from ignition coil.
    NOTE: While pressing the spring (a) shown in the figure, slide it in the direction of the arrow with a clip remover.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Remove the ignition coil.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
