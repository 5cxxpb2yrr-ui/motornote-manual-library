Brake Accumulator ASSY: Installation

  1. Install the brake accumulator assembly to the bracket - accumulator.

    Tightening torque: 

    7.5 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.5 ft-lb)

  2. Install the brake accumulator assembly and the bracket - accumulator.

    Tightening torque: 

    33 N.m (3.4 kgf-m, 24.3 ft-lb)

  3. Install bracket - pipe A, bracket - pipe B, and the front brake pipe assembly (pipe between brake accumulator assembly - master cylinder).

    Refer to "COMPONENT" of "General Description" for the tightening torque. Refer to BRAKE>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>COMPONENT>BRAKE BOOSTER .

  4. Install reservoir joint pipe.
    1. Remove the nuts securing the reservoir joint pipe.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Raise the reservoir joint hose B so that the hose end becomes higher than the reservoir tank.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Remove the cap - reservoir tank, and add the brake fluid.
    4. Confirm that the brake fluid flows out, and install the reservoir joint hose B to the brake accumulator assembly.
    5. Install the nuts securing the reservoir joint pipe.

      Tightening torque: 

      7.5 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.5 ft-lb)

  5. Bleed air from the reservoir joint pipe. Refer to BRAKE>AIR BLEEDING>PROCEDURE>RESERVOIR JOINT PIPE (HYBRID MODEL) .
  6. Install expansion valve - cooler assembly. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>EXPANSION VALVE>INSTALLATION>COOLER VALVE .
  7. Install the air intake boot. Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>Air Intake Boot>INSTALLATION .
  8. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  9. Bleed air from the brake system. Refer to BRAKE>AIR BLEEDING>PROCEDURE>BRAKE SYSTEM (HYBRID MODEL) .
  10. Install the front and rear wheels.

    Tightening torque: 

    120 N.m (12.2 kgf-m, 88.5 ft-lb)
