Multi-function Display (MFD) System: Wiring Diagram

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

Refer to "Multi-function Display (MFD) System" in WI service information.

  • Impreza model: Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (IMPREZA MODEL)>MULTI-FUNCTION DISPLAY (MFD) SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .
  • Crosstrek model (except for hybrid): Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>MULTI-FUNCTION DISPLAY (MFD) SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .
  • Crosstrek hybrid model: Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>MULTI-FUNCTION DISPLAY (MFD) SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .
