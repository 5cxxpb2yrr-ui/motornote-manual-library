Link Protector (Crosstrek Model)

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

CAUTION:
  • When removing/installing the link protector of the rear lateral link (front), do not remove the rear lateral link (front).
  • When cutting off the band, be careful not to damage the adjacent parts.
  1. Lift up the vehicle, and then remove the rear wheels.
  2. Disconnect the cable assembly - parking brake.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Cut off the band.

    Preparation tool: 

    Nippers

  4. Remove the link protector.
