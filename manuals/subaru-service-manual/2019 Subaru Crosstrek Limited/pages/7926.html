Starting System & Charging System (Except For HEV): Starter: Installation

  1. Install the starter and cable stay.
    NOTE: For the MT model, a bolt is used in the lower side of the starter.

    Tightening torque: 

    50 N.m (5.1 kgf-m, 36.9 ft-lb)

  2. Connect the connector, terminal B and ground terminal.

    Tightening torque: 

    Terminal B 

    11 N.m (1.1 kgf-m, 8.1 ft-lb)

    Ground terminal 

    14 N.m (1.4 kgf-m, 10.3 ft-lb)

  3. Install the front wheel LH. Refer to WHEEL AND TIRE SYSTEM>TIRE AND WHEEL>INSTALLATION .
  4. Install the air intake boot. Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>Air Intake Boot>INSTALLATION .
  5. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
