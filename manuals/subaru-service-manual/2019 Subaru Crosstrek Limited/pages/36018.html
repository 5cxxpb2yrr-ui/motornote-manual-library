Check Motor Operation

  1. Apply battery voltage between the outer mirror connector terminals and check the mirror operation.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • Impreza model
      Terminal No. Inspection conditions Switch position
      - - OFF
      4 (+) - 3 (-) Apply battery voltage between terminals. UP
      3 (+) - 4 (-) Apply battery voltage between terminals. DOWN
      5 (+) - 4 (-) Apply battery voltage between terminals. LEFT
      4 (+) - 5 (-) Apply battery voltage between terminals. RIGHT
    • Crosstrek model
    Terminal No. Inspection conditions Switch position
    - - OFF
    10 (-) - 9 (+) Apply battery voltage between terminals. UP
    9 (+) - 10 (-) Apply battery voltage between terminals. DOWN
    8 (+) - 10 (-) Apply battery voltage between terminals. LEFT
    10 (-) - 8 (+) Apply battery voltage between terminals. RIGHT
  2. If it does not operate normally, replace the outer mirror assembly.
