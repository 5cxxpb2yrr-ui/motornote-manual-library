Oil Filter

  1. Remove the oil filter.
    NOTE: If there is water or dust on the oil filter installation portion, clean it before removing the oil filter.
    • When removing oil filter with an outer diameter of 68 mm (2.68 in) (black)

      Special tool: 

      OIL FILTER WRENCH (18332AA000)

    • When removing oil filter with an outer diameter of 67.4 mm (2.65 in) (blue)

    Preparation tool: 

    Oil filter wrench (65/67 mm 14 Flutes)
