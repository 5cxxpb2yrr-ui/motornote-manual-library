DTC B1788: Occupant Detection Sensor Power Supply

Diagnosis start condition: 

When the ignition voltage is 10 V - 16 V.

DTC detecting condition: 

Occupant detection control module malfunction

Trouble symptom: 

Occupant judgment of occupant detection control module stops.

CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to OCCUPANT DETECTION (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK DTC. 

    Read the DTC of [Occupant Detection] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to OCCUPANT DETECTION (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is any of DTC 177F, B1780, B1781 or B1782 displayed? (Current malfunction)

    Yes:  Replace the cushion frame assembly. Refer to SEATS>FRONT SEAT>DISASSEMBLY>PASSENGER'S SEAT .

    No:  Replace the occupant detection control module. Refer to SEATS>FRONT SEAT>DISASSEMBLY>PASSENGER'S SEAT .
