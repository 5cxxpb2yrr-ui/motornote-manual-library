Intake Manifold Assembly: Disassembly

  1. Remove the throttle body. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>THROTTLE BODY>REMOVAL .
  2. Remove the purge control solenoid valve. Refer to EMISSION CONTROL (AUX. EMISSION CONTROL DEVICES) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>PURGE CONTROL SOLENOID VALVE>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the EGR pipe. Refer to EMISSION CONTROL (AUX. EMISSION CONTROL DEVICES) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>EGR PIPE>REMOVAL .
  4. Remove the EGR control valve. Refer to EMISSION CONTROL (AUX. EMISSION CONTROL DEVICES) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>EGR CONTROL VALVE>REMOVAL .
  5. Remove the manifold absolute pressure sensor. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>MANIFOLD ABSOLUTE PRESSURE SENSOR>REMOVAL .
