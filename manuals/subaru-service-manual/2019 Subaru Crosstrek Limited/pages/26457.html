Range in system diagram Contents of inspection Reason Inspection steps
*a Check the connecting condition of connector between Battery ECU assembly - HV battery junction block and continuity of Wire harness Check the connector for mating failure, and CHRP and CHRB exciting lines for open circuit From 2 to 8
*b Check charge relay operation Check CHRB and CHRP for OFF stuck From 9 to 11
*c Check high voltage cable between HV battery - HV battery junction block for connecting condition Check for poor connection (trace of arc) and open circuit due to looseness 12
*d Check HV battery. Check if HV battery voltage decreased significantly from the common range From 13 to 21
*e Check high voltage cable between HV battery junction block - Electric vehicle charger assembly for connecting condition and continuity Check for poor connection (trace of arc) and open circuit due to looseness From 22 to 26
