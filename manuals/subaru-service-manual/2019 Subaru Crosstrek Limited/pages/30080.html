Section 5 (M/T Clutch System): Flywheel: Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Remove the manual transmission assembly.
    • 5MT model:

      Refer to MANUAL TRANSMISSION AND DIFFERENTIAL (5MT)>MANUAL TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL .

    • 6MT model:

      Refer to MANUAL TRANSMISSION AND DIFFERENTIAL (6MT)>MANUAL TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL .

  2. Remove the clutch cover and clutch disc. Refer to CLUTCH SYSTEM>CLUTCH DISC AND COVER>REMOVAL .
  3. Install the ST.

    Special tool: 

    ST: 498497100 CRANKSHAFT STOPPER

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the flywheel and crankshaft position sensor plate.
  5. Drive out the pilot bearing from the flywheel.
