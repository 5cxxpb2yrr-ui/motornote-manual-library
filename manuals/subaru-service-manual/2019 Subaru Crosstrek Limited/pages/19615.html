DTC P0AA7-49: Hybrid/EV Battery Voltage Isolation Sensor Circuit Internal Electronic Failure: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

1. - 

Outline of diagnosis: 

Malfunction in the insulation monitoring circuit (output is above threshold during self diagnosis)

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

Normal driving

CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK DTC  .
    1. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, perform the clear memory of [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control]. Refer to COMMON (DIAG)>CLEAR MEMORY .
    2. Turn to IG OFF, and wait for two minutes or more.
    3. Turn to IG ON, and wait for 70 seconds or more.
      NOTE: Hereafter, do not turn to IG OFF until completing step 2. Check DTC.
    4. Read the DTC of [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is DTC P0AA749 displayed?

    Yes:  Replace Battery ECU assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>BATTERY CONTROL MODULE .

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK DTC  .
    1. Shift to P position and turn to READY ON in the vehicle stop state and wait for 70 seconds or more. (Wait until the engine stops if it started)
    2. Shift to D position while depressing the brake pedal without depressing the accelerator pedal, and wait for one minute.
    3. Move the vehicle forward 0.5 m (1.6 ft) and perform step  2.
    4. Move the vehicle further forward 0.5 m (1.6 ft) and perform step  2. Perform this (equivalent to 2 m (6.6 ft)) five times in total.
    5. Read the DTC of [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is DTC P0AA649 displayed?

    Yes:  Perform the diagnosis of DTC P0AA649. Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE WITH DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>DTC P0AA649 HYBRID/EV BATTERY VOLTAGE SYSTEM ISOLATION INTERNAL ELECTRONIC FAILURE .

    No:  Replace Battery ECU assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>BATTERY CONTROL MODULE .
