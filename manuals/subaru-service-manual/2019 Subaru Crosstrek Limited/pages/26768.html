DTC P0D4C-1C: Hybrid/EV Battery Charger Hybrid/EV Battery Input Voltage Sensor Voltage Out Of Range: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

1. - 

Outline of diagnosis: 

Difference between the battery voltage (VB) and charger output voltage sensor (VCHG) is large during plug-in charging

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

Plug-in charging suspended

Wiring diagram: 

Hybrid system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

  3. CHECK CONNECTION CONDITION OF CONNECTOR (Battery ECU assembly CONNECTOR)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Check that the Service plug is disconnected. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .
    2. Check the connecting condition of connector (HV42) in Battery ECU assembly and check the relevant terminals for contact pressure, deformation and intrusion of water or foreign matter.

    Is there looseness at the connector connection, or any poor contact, deformation or intrusion of water or foreign matters at the relevant terminals?

    Yes:  Connect the connector securely. Or repair the poor contact of connector.

    No:  Go to  4.

  4. CHECK CONNECTION CONDITION OF CONNECTOR (HV battery junction block CONNECTOR)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.

    Check the connecting condition of connectors (HV46), (HV48) and (HV52) in HV battery junction block, and check the relevant terminals for contact pressure, deformation and intrusion of water and foreign matter.

    Is there looseness at the connector connection, or any poor contact, deformation or intrusion of water or foreign matters at the relevant terminals?

    Yes:  Connect the connector securely. Or repair the poor contact of connector.

    No:  Go to  5.

  5. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Battery ECU assembly - HV battery junction block) (OPEN CIRCUIT)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Disconnect Battery ECU assembly connector (HV42).
    2. Disconnect HV battery junction block connector (HV52).
    3. Using a tester, measure the resistance between HV battery junction block connector and Battery ECU assembly connector.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV42) No. 51 - (HV52) No. 2:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  6.

    No:  Repair or replace Wire harness between HV battery junction block connector and Battery ECU assembly connector.

  6. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (HV battery junction block - CHASSIS GROUND) (OPEN CIRCUIT)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between HV battery junction block connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (HV52) No. 1 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  7.

    No:  Repair or replace Wire harness between HV battery junction block connector and chassis ground.

  7. CHECK HV battery junction block UNIT (CHRG)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Remove the HV battery junction block.
    2. Apply Battery (12V) voltage between terminals of (HV52) No. 2 (+) - (HV52) No. 1 (-).
    3. Using a tester, measure the resistance between HV battery junction block connector terminals.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV46) No. 1 - (HV48) No. 1:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  8.

    No:  Replace HV battery junction block. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>RELAY AND FUSE .

  8. CHECK HV battery junction block UNIT (CHRG)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Release the Battery (12V) voltage between terminals of (HV52) No. 2 (+) - (HV52) No. 1 (-).
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between HV battery junction block connector terminals.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV52) No. 2 - (HV52) No. 1:

    Is the resistance 20.6 - 40.8 Ω? (When -40 - 80°C (-40 - 176°F))

    Yes:  Go to  9.

    No:  Replace HV battery junction block. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>RELAY AND FUSE .

  9. CHECK DTC  .
    1. Turn to IG ON.
    2. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, read and record the value of the freeze frame data of [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control] (DTC P0D4C1C). Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>FREEZE FRAME DATA .
    3. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, perform the clear memory of [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control]. Refer to COMMON (DIAG)>CLEAR MEMORY .
    4. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, display the following items in the [Data monitor] of [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control], and record them. Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DATA MONITOR .
      • Hybrid Battery SOC
    5. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, check [Data monitor] of [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control] to see if [Hybrid Battery SOC] is less than 70%.
    6. Turn to IG OFF, and wait for two minutes or more.
    7. Operate the remote air conditioner system.
    8. Connect Electric vehicle charger cable assembly, and charge it for 30 seconds or more.
    9. Disconnect Electric vehicle charger cable assembly, stop the remote air conditioner system, and wait for 10 seconds or more.
    10. Turn to IG ON.
    11. Read the DTC of [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is DTC P0D4C1C displayed?

    Yes:  Go to  10.

    No:  Replace Electric vehicle charger assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>ELECTRIC VEHICLE CHARGER .

  10. CHECK FREEZE FRAME DATA (HV)  .

    Using Subaru Select Monitor, read the value of the freeze frame data of [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control] (DTC P0D4C1C). Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>FREEZE FRAME DATA .

    Are all of the following conditions met?

    1. Deviation between [Charging Voltage for Hybrid Battery] and [Hybrid Battery Voltage] is 33 V or more
    2. Deviation between [VL-Voltage before Boosting] and [Hybrid Battery Voltage] is 50 V or more

    Yes:  Replace Battery ECU assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>BATTERY CONTROL MODULE .

    No:  Replace Electric vehicle charger assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>ELECTRIC VEHICLE CHARGER .
