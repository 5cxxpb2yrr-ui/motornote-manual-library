EyeSight Main Display Is Not Displayed In The Electrical Parts Operation Status Screen

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

Detecting condition: 

Communication circuit, stereo camera or MFD is abnormal.

Wiring diagram: 

  • Multi-function display (MFD) system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (IMPREZA MODEL)>MULTI-FUNCTION DISPLAY (MFD) SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>MULTI-FUNCTION DISPLAY (MFD) SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>MULTI-FUNCTION DISPLAY (MFD) SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .
  • CAN communication system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (IMPREZA MODEL)>CAN COMMUNICATION SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>CAN COMMUNICATION SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>CAN COMMUNICATION SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to MULTI-FUNCTION DISPLAY (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK IGNITION POWER SUPPLY CIRCUIT. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the MFD connector.
    3. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    4. Using a tester, measure the voltage between the MFD connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (i122) No. 23 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    Is the voltage 10 V or more?

    Yes:  Repair or replace the open circuit of harness. (Check battery as well as fuse.)

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK LAN SYSTEM. 

    Perform the diagnosis for LAN system. Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE . Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Perform the inspection according to the diagnosis for LAN system.

  3. CHECK EyeSight. 

    Read the DTC of [EyeSight] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) . Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Are any DTCs displayed? (Current malfunction)

    Yes:  Perform the diagnosis according to displayed DTC.

    No:  Replace the MFP. Refer to INSTRUMENTATION/PRIVER INFO>MULTI-FUNCTION DISPLAY (MFD) .
