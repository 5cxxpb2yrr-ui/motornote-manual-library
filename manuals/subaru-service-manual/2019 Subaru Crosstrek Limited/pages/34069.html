Malfunction Indicator Light Does Not Come On

Trouble symptom: 

When the ignition switch is turned to ON (engine OFF), malfunction indicator light does not illuminate.

NOTE: For the diagnostic procedure, refer to Combination Meter instructions. Refer to COMBINATION METER (DIAGNOSTICS)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE .
