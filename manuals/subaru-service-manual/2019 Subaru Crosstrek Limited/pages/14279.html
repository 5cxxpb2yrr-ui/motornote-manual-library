Hybrid Powertrain Control Module: Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly.
    1. Disconnect the connector.
    2. Remove the bolts and nuts, and remove the hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly together with the AWD control module.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Remove the nuts and remove the AWD control module from the hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
