PCV Connector: Removal

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Drain engine coolant. Refer to COOLING (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>Engine Coolant>REPLACEMENT>DRAINING OF ENGINE COOLANT .
  3. Remove the A/C compressor. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>COMPRESSOR>REMOVAL .
    NOTE: This operation procedure would be possible by simply moving aside the A/C compressor to the position that it does not interfere with the work. In that case, it is not necessary to disconnect the hose - pressure suction and the hose - pressure discharge from the A/C compressor.
  4. Disconnect the water hose (A) and PCV hose No. 2 (B) from the PCV connector, and remove the PCV connector.
