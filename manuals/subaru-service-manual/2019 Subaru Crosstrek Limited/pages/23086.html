DTC P1451: EVAP System Clog Detected (Pipe): Notes

  1. - 

    DTC detecting condition: 

    Detected when two consecutive driving cycles with fault occur.

    NOTE: After the faulty parts are repaired or replaced, perform the final check in Basic Diagnostic Procedure. Refer to ENGINE (DIAGNOSTICS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .
  1. CHECK DRAIN TUBE BETWEEN CANISTER AND LEAK CHECK VALVE ASSEMBLY  .

    Check the drain tube between the canister and leak check valve assembly.

    Is the drain tube between canister and leak check valve assembly clogged?

    Yes: 

    Replace the drain tube between the canister and leak check valve assembly. Refer to EMISSION CONTROL (AUX. EMISSION CONTROL DEVICES) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>CANISTER .

    No: 

    Go to  2.

  2. CHECK HOSE AND TUBE BETWEEN CANISTER AND FUEL TANK  .

    Check the hose and tube between the canister and fuel tank.

    Are the hose and tube between the canister and fuel tank clogged?

    Yes: 

    Replace the hose or tube between the canister and fuel tank. Refer to EMISSION CONTROL (AUX. EMISSION CONTROL DEVICES) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>CANISTER . Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FUEL TANK .

    No: 

    Replace the canister. Refer to EMISSION CONTROL (AUX. EMISSION CONTROL DEVICES) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>CANISTER .

    After the operation is complete, go to the next step. Go to  3.

  3. CHECK EVAPORATIVE EMISSION CONTROL SYSTEM  .

    Perform the inspection mode I. Refer to ENGINE (DIAGNOSTICS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>INSPECTION MODE>PROCEDURE>INSPECTION MODE I .

    Is DTC P1451 displayed? (Current malfunction)

    Yes: 

    Replace the leak check valve assembly. Refer to EMISSION CONTROL (AUX. EMISSION CONTROL DEVICES) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>LEAK CHECK VALVE ASSEMBLY .

    No: 

    End.
