Explanation For Circuits

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Power supply to the motor is shut down by the shutdown signal from Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly to Motor generator control ECU.

With READY OFF, HSDN (MG shutdown signal) is sent from Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly to Motor generator control ECU, and the function of HV gate shutdown is checked. DTC is detected for malfunctions of the HV gate shutdown function.
