Transmission Harness: Removal

  1. Remove the transmission assembly. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL .
  2. Remove the transmission case cover.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the air breather hose. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>AIR BREATHER HOSE>REMOVAL .
  4. Disconnect the harness connector from the turbine speed sensor, primary speed sensor, secondary speed sensor and secondary pressure sensor.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Disconnect the harness connector from the inhibitor switch.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Disconnect the ground terminal and remove the harness clip.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Clean the transmission exterior.
  8. Remove the valve cover and gasket.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Disconnect the harness connector from the control valve body.
  10. Remove the transmission harness.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  11. Remove the O-rings.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  12. Remove the harness clip from the transmission, and remove the transmission harness.
