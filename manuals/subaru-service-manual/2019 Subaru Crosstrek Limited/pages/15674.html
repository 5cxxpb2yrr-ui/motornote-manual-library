Brake Accumulator ASSY: Disposal

CAUTION: The gas is colorless, odorless, and harmless nitrogen gas. Be careful of the following items during operation:
  • Be sure to wear dust-resistant goggles.
  • Gas pressure may scatter cutting powder or oil. Therefore, be sure to cover the metal cutting saw with a cloth while making a cut.
  • Perform the cutting operation slowly. If you make a cut at once, the gas will come out vigorously.
  1. Fix the brake accumulator assembly in a vise.
  2. Remove the brake accumulator.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Use a metal cutting saw and make a cut in the (a) area on the side of the brake accumulator to release the charged gas.
