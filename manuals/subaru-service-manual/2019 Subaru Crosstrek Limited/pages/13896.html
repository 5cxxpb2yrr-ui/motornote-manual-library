DTC P1F02: AWD Solenoid Circuit Low: Notes

1. - 

DTC detecting condition: 

Immediately at fault recognition

Trouble symptom: 

Driveability getting worse.

Wiring diagram: 

AWD control system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>AWD CONTROL SYSTEM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  1. CHECK HARNESS  .
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the AWD control module connector.
    3. Disconnect the AWD solenoid connector.
    4. Measure the resistance between AWD control module connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (B648) No. 13 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance 1 MΩ or more?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Repair the short circuit of harness.

  2. CHECK AWD SOLENOID  .
    1. Connected the AWD solenoid connector.
    2. Measure the resistance between AWD control module connector and transmission body.

      Connector & terminal 

      (B648) No. 13 - Transmission body:

    Is the resistance 1.6 - 3.8 Ω? (When cold)

    Yes:  Check for poor contact of AWD control module connector. Replace the AWD control module if no fault is found. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TH2A)>AWD CONTROL MODULE .

    No:  Replace the transmission assembly. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TH2A)>TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY .
