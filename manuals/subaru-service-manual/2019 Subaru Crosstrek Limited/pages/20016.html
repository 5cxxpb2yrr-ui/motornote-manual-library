DTC B16F8: Satellite Safing Sensor Recognition Error

Diagnosis start condition: 

When the ignition voltage is 10 - 16 V.

DTC detecting condition: 

  • Satellite safing sensor is misinstalled.
  • Airbag control module is faulty.
  1. REPLACE SATELLITE SAFING SENSOR. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF, disconnect the battery ground terminal, and wait 60 seconds or more. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    2. Replace the satellite safing sensor with a genuine sensor. Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>SATELLITE SAFING SENSOR>REMOVAL .

    Does the warning light go off?

    Yes:  Finish the diagnosis.

    No:  Replace the airbag control module. Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>AIRBAG CONTROL MODULE>REMOVAL .
