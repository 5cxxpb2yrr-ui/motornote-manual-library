Select Cable: Installation

  1. Position the select cable as shown in the figure, and install it to the plate assembly.
    CAUTION:
    • If the cable is installed in the wrong direction, loosen the bracket bolts, and then reinstall the cable in the correct direction.
    • Do not adjust the cable end direction by turning it forcibly to prevent the boot from being twisted.

    Tightening torque:

    T: 18 N.m (1.8 kgf-m, 13.3 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Install new clamp paying attention to the installing direction.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Insert the tip of select cable into connector hole of select lever, and fix to the clamp.
    CAUTION: Do not apply excessive load while holding the part (a).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Temporarily Install the adjusting nut to the select cable.
  5. Connect the select cable to the transmission main body.
    CAUTION: Do not apply excessive load while holding the part (a).

    Tightening torque:

    25 N.m (2.5 kgf-m, 18.4 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • Except for Crosstrek hybrid model
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • Crosstrek hybrid model
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Shift the select lever to the "N" range, and then adjust the select cable position. Refer to CONTROL SYSTEMS>SELECT CABLE>ADJUSTMENT .
  7. Install the inhibitor cover. (Crosstrek hybrid model)

    Tightening torque:

    7.5 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.5 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Install the center exhaust cover.

    Tightening torque:

    18 N.m (1.8 kgf-m, 13.3 ft-lb)

  9. Lower the vehicle.
  10. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
