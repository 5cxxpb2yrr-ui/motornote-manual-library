Engine Wiring Harness: Installation

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Set the engine wiring harness, and connect each connector. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>COMPONENT>ENGINE WIRING HARNESS .
    NOTE:
    • If the clip is damaged, replace it with a new part.
    • Before replacing the clip, put an alignment mark (a) on both engine wiring harness and clip to remove the clip, and then align to the alignment mark (a) to attach a new clip to the engine wiring harness.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Install the fuel insulator. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL INSULATOR>INSTALLATION .
  3. Install the intake manifold assembly. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>INTAKE MANIFOLD ASSEMBLY>INSTALLATION .
  4. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
