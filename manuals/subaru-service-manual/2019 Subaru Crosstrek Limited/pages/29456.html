Engine Cooling System Trouble in General: Inspection

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
Trouble Possible cause Corrective action
Over-heating a. Engine coolant leaks, insufficient engine coolant Check for coolant leaks, and repair or replace faulty parts if necessary.
If no faulty parts are found, fill engine coolant.
b. Clogged engine coolant passage Clean or replace faulty parts.
c. Defective radiator cap Replace the radiator cap.
d. Malfunction of thermostat Replace the thermostat.
e. Malfunction of water pump Replace water pump.
f. Clogged radiator fin Clean the radiator fin.
If it is not possible to clean, repair or replace the radiator.
g. Radiator fan system malfunction Check the radiator fan system, and repair or replace faulty parts.
h. Excessive back pressure in exhaust system Clean or replace faulty parts.
i. Engine oil leaking into engine coolant Repair or replace the faulty parts, clean the engine coolant passage, and then fill engine coolant.
Over cooling a. Ambient temperature extremely low Warm-up the engine sufficiently.
b. Defective thermostat Replace the thermostat.
Engine coolant leaks a. Leakage from water pump Replace water pump.
b. Leakage from inverter water pump Replace the inverter water pump.
c. Leakage from water hose Repair or replace faulty parts.
d. Leakage from water pipe Repair or replace faulty parts.
e. Leakage from inverter water pipe Replace faulty parts.
f. Leakage from cylinder head gasket Replace the cylinder head gasket.
g. Cracked or damaged cylinder head or cylinder block Replace faulty parts.
h. Cracked or damaged thermostat cover Replace the thermostat cover.
i. Leakage from radiator Repair or replace the radiator.
j. Leakage from heater core Replace the heater core.
Noise a. Air mixed in the engine coolant passage Bleed air from the engine coolant.
b. Malfunction of water pump Replace water pump.
c. Malfunction of inverter water pump Replace the inverter water pump.
d. Malfunction of radiator fan or fan motor Replace the radiator fan or fan motor.
