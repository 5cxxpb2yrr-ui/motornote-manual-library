DTC P1CCB-1E: Hybrid/EV Battery Stack 4 Voltage Difference Out Of Range: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Outline of diagnosis: 

If the difference of voltage between each cell of NO. 4 HV supply stack sub-assembly is equal to or greater than the standard

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

  • Reduced output power
  • Engine not stopped
