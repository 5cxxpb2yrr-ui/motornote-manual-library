Inhibitor Switch: Removal

  1. Shift the select lever to the "N" range.
  2. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  3. Lift up the vehicle.
  4. Remove the front exhaust pipe. Refer to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FRONT EXHAUST PIPE>REMOVAL .
  5. Remove the snap pin and washer from the shifter arm.
    CAUTION: Do not apply excessive load while holding the part (a).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Remove the spring pin and shifter arm.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Disconnect the inhibitor harness connector.
  8. Remove the inhibitor switch.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
