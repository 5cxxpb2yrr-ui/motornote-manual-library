Engine Oil: Replacement

CAUTION: If the engine oil is spilt over the engine, exhaust pipe or the under cover, completely wipe it off to avoid emission of smoke or causing a fire.

Engine oil: 

Refer to "RECOMMENDED MATERIALS" for engine oil. Refer to RECOMMENDED MATERIALS>LUBRICANT .

Engine oil capacity: 

Refer to "SPECIFICATION" for engine oil capacity. Refer to LUBRICATION (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>SPECIFICATION .

  1. Remove the oil filler cap.
  2. Drain engine oil by loosening the drain plug.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Install the drain plug using a new gasket.

    Tightening torque: 

    41.7 N.m (4.3 kgf-m, 30.8 ft-lb)

  4. Add engine oil up to the "F" line on oil level gauge.
  5. Install the oil filler cap.
  6. Check the engine oil level. Refer to LUBRICATION (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE OIL>INSPECTION .
