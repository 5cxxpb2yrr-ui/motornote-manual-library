Inspection Mode J

DTC Item Condition Number of driving cycles on the detecting condition
P2610 ECM/PCM ENGINE OFF TIMER PERFORMANCE - 2

Diagnostic procedure: 

  1. Idle the engine for 15 minutes or more.
  2. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
  3. After at least 10 hours passed from the start of step 2), read the temporary code using the Subaru Select Monitor or general scan tool. Refer to COMMON (DIAG)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .
