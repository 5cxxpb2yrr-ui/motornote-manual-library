Engine Coolant Temperature Sensor: Installation

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Set a new gasket, install the engine coolant temperature sensor using the ST.
    NOTE: STRUT MOUNT SOCKET (20099 Pa000) can also be used.

    Preparation tool: 

    ST: STRUT MOUNT SOCKET (20399AG000)

    Tightening torque: 

    16 N.m (1.6 kgf-m, 11.8 ft-lb)

  2. Connect the connector.
  3. Set the electric compressor assembly and temporarily install the compressor bracket - front.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Tighten the compressor bracket - front in the numerical order shown in the figure.

    Tightening torque: 

    T1: 26.5 N.m (2.7 kgf-m, 19.5 ft-lb)

    T2: 36 N.m (3.7 kgf-m, 26.6 ft-lb)

    Fig 1: Front Compressor Bracket Bolts Tightening Sequence
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Install the inverter tank.

    Tightening torque: 

    7.5 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.5 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Lift up the vehicle.
  7. Install the inverter radiator outlet hose and inverter tank outlet hose to the clamp.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Install the front under cover. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>FRONT UNDER COVER>INSTALLATION .
  9. Lower the vehicle.
  10. Install the bracket - hood stay.

    Tightening torque: 

    18 N.m (1.8 kgf-m, 13.3 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  11. Install the cover UPR - front.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  12. Install the air intake duct. Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>AIR INTAKE DUCT>INSTALLATION .
  13. Install the collector cover. Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>COLLECTOR COVER>INSTALLATION .
  14. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  15. Fill engine coolant. (Engine side) Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>ENGINE COOLANT>REPLACEMENT>FILLING OF ENGINE COOLANT>ENGINE SIDE .
