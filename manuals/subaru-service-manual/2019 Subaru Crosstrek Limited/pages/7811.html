Hybrid Model

WARNING: The hybrid system includes a high voltage circuit.
Mishandling could cause accidents such as electric shock or leak. Always check "WARNING (HYBRID SYSTEM)" and perform the proper operation. Refer to PRECAUTION/WARNING>WARNING (HYBRID SYSTEM) .
  1. Install the engine mounting onto the engine.

    Tightening torque: 

    35 N.m (3.6 kgf-m, 25.8 ft-lb)

  2. Apply a small amount of grease to the splines of the reduction gear.

    Grease: 

    NICHIMOLY N-130 or equivalent

  3. Position the engine in engine compartment and align it with transmission.
    NOTE: Be careful not to damage adjacent parts or body panels with crank pulley, oil level gauge, etc.
  4. With a lifting device and a garage jack, slowly lower the engine.
  5. Install the bolts which hold upper side of transmission to engine.

    Tightening torque: 

    50 N.m (5.1 kgf-m, 36.9 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Remove the garage jack.
  7. Remove the lifting device and wire ropes.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Lift up the vehicle.
  9. Install the bolts and nuts which hold lower side of the transmission to engine.

    Tightening torque: 

    50 N.m (5.1 kgf-m, 36.9 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  10. Install the crossmember support - rear RH. Refer to FRONT SUSPENSION>CROSSMEMBER SUPPORT>INSTALLATION .
  11. Install the nuts which hold the engine mounting to the front crossmember.
    CAUTION: Use new nuts.
    NOTE: Make sure that locators (a) of the engine mounting are securely inserted.

    Tightening torque: 

    90 N.m (9.2 kgf-m, 66.4 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  12. Connect the ground cord.

    Tightening torque: 

    7.5 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.5 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  13. Lower the vehicle.
  14. Connect the fuel delivery tube.
    1. Set the fuel delivery tube (B) together with the purge hose, and connect the quick connector on the fuel delivery tube (B) to the fuel pipe.
      CAUTION:
      • Check that there is no damage or dust on the quick connector. If necessary, clean the seal surface of the pipe.
      • When connecting the quick connector, make sure to insert it all the way in before locking the slider.
      • When it is difficult to lock the slider, check that the connector is fully inserted.
      • After locking the slider, pull the quick connector body in the removal direction and then push it in the connecting direction, in order to check that the quick connector is securely connected. Always make sure to perform this confirmation ending up with a pushing in.
      NOTE: Connect the quick connector as shown in the figure.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Install the clip (A).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  15. Connect the inhibitor harness connector (A) to the engine wiring harness.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  16. Install the pitching stopper. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TH2A)>TRANSMISSION MOUNTING SYSTEM>INSTALLATION>PITCHING STOPPER .
  17. Connect the heater inlet hose (A) and the heater outlet hose (B).
    NOTE: Be careful not to mix up the heater inlet hose and the heater outlet hose when connecting them.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  18. Set the engine wiring harness and secure the engine wiring harness to the bracket with the claw.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  19. Connect the battery positive terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  20. Install the engine wiring harness to the clip (C), and connect the connector (B) to the main fuse box.
  21. Connect connector (A) to ECM.
    CAUTION: To prevent damage to the connector (A), when connecting the connector (A), insert the connector (A) straight in until it stops, and while maintaining the position, lock the lever.
    NOTE: When connecting the connector (A), check that the lever is located at the position shown in the figure (position beyond the lock portion (a)).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  22. Install the battery cable part of the engine wiring harness. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>COMPONENT>ENGINE WIRING HARNESS>STRUCTURAL DIAGRAM 4 (BATTERY CABLE PART) .
  23. Connect the hose - pressure discharge to the electric compressor assembly. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>HOSE AND PIPE>INSTALLATION .
  24. Secure the air conditioner wire with the clip and connect the connector to the electric compressor assembly.
    WARNING: Be sure to wear insulated gloves.
    CAUTION:
    • Check the connection of connector for foreign matter adhesion.
    • If adhesion of water or oil occurs at the connection of connector, completely wipe it off.
    1. Secure the air conditioner wire with the clip (A).
    2. Connect the connector (B) and push the slide lock (C) in the direction of arrow to lock.
      CAUTION: Insert the connector until a click is heard and lock it securely.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  25. Install the inverter front water pipe. Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>INVERTER WATER PIPE>INSTALLATION>INVERTER FRONT WATER PIPE .
  26. Install the radiator. Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>RADIATOR>INSTALLATION .
  27. Install the air intake case (rear). Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>AIR CLEANER CASE>INSTALLATION .
  28. Install the air intake boot. Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>Air Intake Boot>INSTALLATION .
  29. Install the service disconnect plug. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG>INSTALLATION .
  30. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  31. Install the fuel filler cap, and close the fuel filler lid.
  32. Charge refrigerant. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>REFRIGERANT CHARGING PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .
  33. Fill engine coolant. Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>ENGINE COOLANT>REPLACEMENT>FILLING OF ENGINE COOLANT .
  34. Install the collector cover. Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>COLLECTOR COVER>INSTALLATION .
  35. Set the panel COMPL front hood to the normal position. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>FRONT HOOD STAY .
  36. Install the striker - front hood. Refer to EXTERIOR BODY PANELS>FRONT HOOD>ASSEMBLY .
  37. Close the front hood.
