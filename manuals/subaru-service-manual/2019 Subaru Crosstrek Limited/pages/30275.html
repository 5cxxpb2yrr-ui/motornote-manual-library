Section 3 (HVAC System (Heater, Ventilator And A/C) (HEV)): Evaporator: Inspection

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Check the evaporator fin for dust. Blow with compressed air or flush fins with water as needed.
  2. If any oil leak is found from the evaporator, replace the evaporator assembly.
