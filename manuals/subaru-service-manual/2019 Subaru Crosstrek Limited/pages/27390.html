DTC P0BCC-1C: Generator Inverter Temperature Sensor Circuit Voltage Out Of Range: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. - 

    Detecting condition: 

    The difference between the calculated value of generator inverter temperature and the actual temperature value exceeds a specific value, and this continues for 10 seconds. Otherwise, while temperature difference does not occur after soaked for a certain period of time, the value of the generator inverter temperature sensor is different from those of the other sensors.

    (1 trip detection logic)

    Trouble symptom: 

    Normal driving

    CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to P-HEV DRIVE MOTOR (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK DTC. 

    Read the DTC of [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    NOTE:
    Malfunction contents Related DTC
    Insulation malfunction P1C7C49 HYBRID/EV BATTERY VOLTAGE SYSTEM ISOLATION (A/C AREA) INTERNAL ELECTRONIC FAILURE
    P1C7D49 HYBRID/EV BATTERY VOLTAGE SYSTEM ISOLATION (HYBRID/EV BATTERY AREA) INTERNAL ELECTRONIC FAILURE
    P1C7E49 HYBRID/EV BATTERY VOLTAGE SYSTEM ISOLATION (TRANSAXLE AREA) INTERNAL ELECTRONIC FAILURE
    P1C7F49 HYBRID/EV BATTERY VOLTAGE SYSTEM ISOLATION (DIRECT CURRENT AREA) INTERNAL ELECTRONIC FAILURE
    Sensor and actuator circuit malfunction P0C7396 MOTOR ELECTRONICS COOLANT PUMP "A" COMPONENT INTERNAL FAILURE
    P314A31 MOTOR ELECTRONICS COOLANT PUMP "A" NO SIGNAL
    System malfunction P0A9300 INVERTER "A" COOLING SYSTEM PERFORMANCE

    When "Only DTC P0BCC1C is displayed" or "DTC other than above is displayed at the same time", go to  A.

    When DTCs related to [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control], mentioned above, are simultaneously displayed, go to  B.

    Yes: 

    1. Go to  2.
    2. Perform the diagnosis for the simultaneously displayed DTCs.
      NOTE: If more than one DTC is simultaneously displayed, perform the diagnosis in the order described in the above.

    No:  -

  2. CHECK THE CONNECTING CONDITION OF CONNECTOR (Inverter with converter assembly LOW PRESSURE CONNECTOR). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Check that the Service plug is disconnected. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .
    2. Check the connecting condition of low pressure connectors (HV23) and (HV24) in Inverter with converter assembly and check the relevant terminals for contact pressure, deformation and intrusion of water or foreign matter. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>INVERTER AND CONVERTER ASSEMBLY .
      NOTE: When connecting the connector, insert to the end with the lock lever raised, then fold the lock lever completely until a click is heard. After connected, make sure that the lock claw does not loosen and pull the connector to see that it does not come off.

      Is there looseness at the connector connection, or any poor contact, deformation or intrusion of water or foreign matter at the relevant terminals?

      Yes:  Connect the connector securely. Or repair the poor contact of connector.

      No:  Go to  3.

  3. CHECK Cooling System. 

    Check Cooling System. Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>COOLING SYSTEM .

    Is the Cooling System OK?

    Yes:  Replace Inverter with converter assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>INVERTER AND CONVERTER ASSEMBLY .

    No:  Repair according to Cooling System inspection.
