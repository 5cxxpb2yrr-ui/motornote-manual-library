DTC B1451: High-Pressure Solenoid Valve Circuit/Open

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

DTC detecting condition: 

When High pressure solenoid valve circuit is faulty.

Trouble symptom: 

Compressor does not operate.

Wiring diagram: 

Air conditioning system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>AIR CONDITIONING SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK CONNECTOR. 
    1. Check connecting condition of the A/C control panel connector, heat pump control module connector and High pressure solenoid valve connector.
    2. Read the DTC of [Air Conditioner] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is DTC B1451 displayed? (Current malfunction)

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Even if DTC is displayed, the circuit has returned to a normal condition at this time.

    Reproduce the failure, and then perform the diagnosis again.

    NOTE: In this case, temporary poor contact of connector, temporary open or short circuit of harness may be the cause.
  2. CHECK HEAT PUMP control module. 
    1. Disconnect the heat pump control module connector.
    2. Using a tester, measure the voltage between the heat pump control module connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (i365) No. 2 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    Is the voltage 11 - 14 V?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Go to  5.

  3. CHECK High pressure solenoid valve. 
    1. Disconnect the High pressure solenoid valve connector. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>SOLENOID VALVE .
    2. Using the tester, measure the resistance between High pressure solenoid valve terminals.

      Connector & terminal 

      (B654) No. 1 - (B654) No. 2:

    Is the resistance 13.15 - 14.65 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Replace High pressure solenoid valve. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>SOLENOID VALVE .

  4. CHECK HARNESS (SHORTED BETWEEN LINES). 

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between High pressure solenoid valve connector terminals.

    Connector & terminal 

    (B654) No. 1 - (B654) No. 2:

    Is the resistance 10 kΩ or more?

    Yes:  Replace the heat pump control module. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>CONTROL UNIT .

    No:  Repair or replace the short circuit of the harness.

  5. CHECK High pressure solenoid valve POWER SUPPLY CIRCUIT. 
    1. Disconnect the High pressure solenoid valve connector. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>SOLENOID VALVE .
    2. Using a tester, measure the voltage between High pressure solenoid valve connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (B654) No. 2 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    Is the voltage 11 - 14 V?

    Yes:  Go to  6.

    No:  Repair or replace the open circuit of harness.

  6. CHECK HARNESS (OPEN CIRCUIT). 

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between the High pressure solenoid valve connector and heat pump control module connector.

    Connector & terminal 

    (B654) No. 1 - (i365) No. 2:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  7.

    No:  Repair or replace the open circuit of harness.

  7. CHECK HARNESS (GROUND SHORT CIRCUIT). 

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between High pressure solenoid valve connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (B654) No. 1 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance 10 kΩ or more?

    Yes:  Replace High pressure solenoid valve. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>SOLENOID VALVE .

    No:  Repair or replace the open circuit of harness.
