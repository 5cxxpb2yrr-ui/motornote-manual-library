Related Lines, More Than 56 Ω And Less Than 64 Ω (P-CAN_Central Gateway)

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Wiring diagram: 

CAN communication system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>CAN COMMUNICATION SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
NOTE: When the measured resistance value is 57 - 63 Ω, main wiring harness or related lines may be shorted to ground, or shorted to power supply line, or related lines may be open.
  1. CHECK BETWEEN RELATED LINES AND MAIN WIRING HARNESS. 
    1. Disconnect the central gateway control module connector.
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between the central gateway control module connector terminals.

      Connector & terminal 

      (i366) No. 17 - (i366) No. 18:

    Is the resistance 400 Ω or more?

    Yes:  A related line between the central gateway control module and main wiring harness is open, or main wiring harness is open at two places or more.

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK RELATED LINES. 
    1. Disconnect the ECM connector.
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between the central gateway control module connector and ECM connector.

      Connector & terminal 

      (i366) No. 18 - (B134) No. 4:

      (i366) No. 17 - (B134) No. 15:

    Is the resistance 10 Ω or more?

    Yes:  Repair or replace the open circuit of the central gateway control module related lines.

    No:  Check DTC of central gateway control module. Refer to CENTRAL GATEWAY (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .
