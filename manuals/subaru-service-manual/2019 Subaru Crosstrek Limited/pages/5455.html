Hybrid Model

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

CAUTION: The DTC may be recorded when the inspection is performed. When the DTC is recorded, perform the clear memory after the inspection and confirm that the DTC is not displayed.

CHECK PRESSURE SENSOR 

  1. Perform the accumulator down drive. Refer to BRAKE>BRAKE SYSTEM>OPERATION>ACCUMULATOR DOWN DRIVE .
  2. Check that the voltage of the 12 volt auxiliary battery is correct.
  3. Connect a measuring device as shown in the figure.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. After bleeding air from the pressure gauge, perform each check.
  5. Check the reaction pressure sensor and servo pressure sensor.
    1. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, read the values of [Reaction pressure sensor] and [Servo pressure sensor] in [Data Monitor].
    2. Check the fluid pressure and each pressure sensor output value when the brake pedal is depressed.

      Specification: 

    Brake pedal force: N (kgf, lbf) 50 (5.1, 11.2) 100 (10.2, 22.5) 150 (15.3, 33.7)
    Fluid pressure: kPa (kgf/cm2 , psi) 2050 - 6050 (20.9 - 61.7, 297.2 - 877.2) 5570 - 9570 (56.8 - 97.6, 807.6 - 1387.6) 6800 - 10800 (69.3 - 110.1, 986 - 1566)
    Reaction pressure: kPa (kgf/cm2 , psi) 150 - 950 (1.5 - 9.7, 21.7 - 137.7) 880 - 1680 (9 - 17.1, 127.6 - 243.6) 1600 - 2400 (16.3 - 24.5, 232 - 348)
    Servo pressure: kPa (kgf/cm2 , psi) 2050 - 6050 (20.9 - 61.7, 297.2 - 877.2) 5570 - 9570 (56.8 - 97.6, 807.6 - 1387.6) 6800 - 10800 (69.3 - 110.1, 986 - 1566)
  6. Remove the pedal force gauge and pressure gauge, and bleed air from the brake line. Refer to BRAKE>AIR BLEEDING>PROCEDURE>BRAKE LINE (HYBRID MODEL) .
  7. Check the accumulator pressure sensor.
    1. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, read [Accumulator Pressure] and [Pump Motor Output] on the [Data Monitor] display.
    2. Check that [Pump Motor Output] turns ON once or more, while repeating the full stroke operation of the brake pedal 4 - 5 times.
      NOTE: [Pump Motor Output] is normally OFF.
    3. After confirming the pump motor stops, check the output value of [Accumulator pressure].

      Specification: 

      15, 000 - 21, 000 kPa (153 - 214.1 kgf/cm2 , 2, 175 - 3, 045 psi)

CHECK BRAKE BOOSTER ASSEMBLY (WITH MASTER CYLINDER) 

  1. Perform the accumulator down drive. Refer to BRAKE>BRAKE SYSTEM>OPERATION>ACCUMULATOR DOWN DRIVE .
  2. Check that the voltage of the 12 volt auxiliary battery is correct.
  3. Connect a measuring device as shown in the figure.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, perform the clear memory. Refer to COMMON (DIAG)>CLEAR MEMORY .
  5. After bleeding air from the pressure gauge, perform the check.
  6. Perform the check for lack of boost action.
    1. Check and adjust the brake pedal. Refer to BRAKE>BRAKE PEDAL>INSPECTION>BRAKE PEDAL .
    2. Check that the amount of brake fluid is between the lines of "MIN" and "MAX".
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, select [Prohibit motor control] on the [Work Support] screen. Refer to ELECTRIC BRAKE BOOSTER (DIAGNOSTICS)>WORK SUPPORT .
    4. Follow the instructions on the Subaru Select Monitor screen and prohibit motor control.
    5. Depress the brake pedal 40 times or more and return all brake fluid in the brake accumulator assembly to the reservoir tank.
      NOTE: There is a case that the buzzer may sound due to the lowering of accumulator pressure, but it is not abnormal.
    6. Check that the brake pedal does not stroke.
    7. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, read the values of [Stroke sensor 1 correction voltage] and [Stroke sensor 2 correction voltage] in [Data Monitor].
    8. Check the fluid pressure and each stroke sensor output value when the brake pedal is depressed.

      Specification: 

    Brake pedal force: N (kgf, lbf) 200 (20.4, 45) 500 (51, 112.4)
    Fluid pressure: kPa (kgf/cm2 , psi) 210 - 2210 (2.1 - 22.5, 30.4 - 320.4) 2460 - 4460 (25.1 - 45.5, 356.7 - 646.7)
    Stroke sensor 1 correction voltage: V 1.01 - 1.71 1.05 - 1.75
    Stroke sensor 2 correction voltage: V 3.29 - 3.99 3.25 - 3.95
    NOTE: Check the brake booster assembly (with master cylinder) by referring to the brake pedal operation force, fluid pressure and each stroke sensor output value.
    If there is a malfunction, check whether the brake booster assembly (with master cylinder) is normal or not by referring to each stroke sensor check result.
  7. Remove the pedal force gauge and pressure gauge, and bleed air from the brake line. Refer to BRAKE>AIR BLEEDING>PROCEDURE>BRAKE LINE (HYBRID MODEL) .
  8. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, read the value of [Accumulator pressure] in data monitor.

    Specification: 

    15, 000 - 21, 000 kPa (153 - 214.1 kgf/cm2 , 2, 175 - 3, 045 psi)

  9. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, perform the clear memory. Refer to COMMON (DIAG)>CLEAR MEMORY .

CHECK STROKE SIMULATOR 

  1. Check that the voltage of the 12 volt auxiliary battery is correct.
  2. Connect a measuring device as shown in the figure.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, perform the clear memory. Refer to COMMON (DIAG)>CLEAR MEMORY .
  4. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, read the values of [Stroke sensor 1 correction voltage] and [Stroke sensor 2 correction voltage] in [Data Monitor].
  5. Depress the brake pedal 4 - 5 times.
  6. Check each stroke sensor output value when the brake pedal is depressed.

Specification: 

Brake pedal force: N (kgf, lbf) 50 (5.1, 11.2) 100 (10.2, 22.5) 150 (15.3, 33.7)
Stroke sensor 1 correction voltage: V 1.09 - 1.79 1.23 - 1.93 1.32 - 2.02
Stroke sensor 2 correction voltage: V 3.21 - 3.91 3.07 - 3.77 2.98 - 3.68
