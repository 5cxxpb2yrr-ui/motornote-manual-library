Rear Lock Button: Removal

NOTE: The rear lock button is integrated with the switch - opener rear gate.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the button - opener rear gate. Refer to SECURITY AND LOCKS>REAR GATE OPENER BUTTON>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the switch - request (lock).
    CAUTION: When using a flat tip screwdriver, apply protective tape or cloth and be careful not to cause damage.
    1. Remove the screws, and then remove the garnish switch bracket.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Remove the switch - request (lock) by pressing the lock portions on both sides.
