TPMS (U.S. Model)

  • IMPREZA MODEL 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to ON, and check TPMS warning light for normal operation.
      • Normal operation (IG ON (light check))
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. If the TPMS warning light display does not operate normally, check and repair the system. Refer to TIRE PRESSURE MONITOR (DIAGNOSTICS)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE .
  • CROSSTREK MODEL 
  1. Turn the TPMS transmitter power supply to ON.
  1. Turn the ignition switch to ON, and check that the TPMS warning light is blinking as follows.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Make the transmitter registration tool come in contact with the side wall area near the air valve on each tire, and press the switch to irradiate a magnetic field of 125 kHz for 4 seconds or more.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Make sure that the TPMS warning light goes off when the power for 4 wheels is turned to ON.
    NOTE:
    • When the power for only 1 through 3 wheels is turned to ON, the light blinks as follows.
    • For Subaru Select Monitor, make sure that the transmitter power supply is set to «ON MODE».
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Turn the ignition switch to ON, and check TPMS warning light for normal operation.
  • Normal operation (IG ON (light check))
Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
