Inverter low-voltage circuit

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .

Wiring diagram: 

Hybrid system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  1. CHECK FUSE (PCM FR)  .
    1. Remove the F/B NO. 24 FUSE from the Relay & fuse box (F/B). Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>RELAY AND FUSE .
    2. Check F/B NO. 24 FUSE.

    Is the F/B NO. 24 FUSE OK?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Go to  8.

  2. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Inverter with converter assembly POWER SUPPLY CIRCUIT) (OPEN CIRCUIT)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Check that the Service plug is disconnected. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .
    2. Disconnect Inverter with converter assembly connector (HV23).
    3. Using a tester, measure the resistance between Inverter with converter assembly connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV23) No. 4 - Chassis ground:

      (HV23) No. 8 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Repair or replace the Wire harness between Inverter with converter assembly connector and chassis ground.

  3. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Inverter with converter assembly POWER SUPPLY CIRCUIT) (OPEN CIRCUIT)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Connect the ground terminal of Battery (12V). Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    2. Turn to IG ON.
    3. Using a tester, measure the voltage between Inverter with converter assembly connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV23) No. 1 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

      (HV23) No. 5 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    Is the voltage 11 - 14 V?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Repair or replace the power supply circuit of Inverter with converter assembly.

  4. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly - Inverter with converter assembly) (OPEN CIRCUIT)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Disconnect Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector (B606).
    2. Turn to IG ON.
    3. Using a tester, measure the resistance between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector and Inverter with converter assembly connector.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV23) No. 20 - (B606) No. 28:

      (HV23) No. 24 - (B606) No. 20:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  5.

    No:  Repair or replace the Wire harness between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector and Inverter with converter assembly connector.

  5. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly - Inverter with converter assembly) (SHORT TO GROUND OR LINE-TO-LINE SHORT)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between terminals in Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector, and between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (B606) No. 28 - (B606) No. 1 - 35:

    (B606) No. 28 - Chassis ground:

    (B606) No. 20 - (B606) No. 1 - 35:

    (B606) No. 20 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance 10 kΩ or more?

    Yes:  Go to  6.

    No:  Repair or replace the Wire harness between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector and Inverter with converter assembly connector.

  6. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly - Inverter with converter assembly) (SHORT TO POWER SUPPLY)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Connect the ground terminal of Battery (12V). Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    2. Turn to IG ON.
    3. Using a tester, measure the voltage between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (B606) No. 28 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

      (B606) No. 20 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    Is the voltage less than 1 V?

    Yes:  Go to  7.

    No:  Repair or replace the Wire harness between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector and Inverter with converter assembly connector.

  7. CHECK Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly (INTERNAL RESISTANCE)  .
    1. Turn the ignition to OFF.
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly terminals.

      Connector & terminal 

      (B606) No. 28 - (B606) No. 20:

    Is the resistance 80 - 170 Ω?

    Yes:  Inverter low-voltage circuit is normal.

    No:  Replace Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL MODULE .

  8. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Inverter with converter assembly - F/B NO. 24 FUSE)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between Inverter with converter assembly connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (HV23) No. 1 - Chassis ground:

    (HV23) No. 5 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance 10 kΩ or more?

    Yes:  Replace Inverter with converter assembly and then replace F/B NO. 24 FUSE. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>RELAY AND FUSE .

    No:  Repair or replace the power supply circuit of Inverter with converter assembly, and replace F/B NO. 24 FUSE. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>RELAY AND FUSE .

GENERAL DESCRIPTION 

The malfunction cause may be the low voltage system.

Check the inverter for open circuit in the +B low voltage power supply system, and check if there is any problems on the communication between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly and the inverter.
