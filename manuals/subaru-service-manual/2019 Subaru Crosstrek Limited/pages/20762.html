DTC B2B24: Passenger's Door Power Window Pulse 1 No Signal

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

DTC detecting condition: 

When the power window is rolling up (except in fully closed zone), change in pulse 1 phase (edge) is not detected while pulse 2 phase is producing 10 edges.

Trouble symptom: 

  • When the power window sub-switch is operated, passenger's side window does not work.
  • When the power window sub-switch is operated, passenger's side window rolls down only in manual down operation, or inches up. (Inch up: Operating the switch rolls up the window about 10 mm only)
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to POWER WINDOW (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. SYSTEM INITIALIZATION. 
    1. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, perform [System Initialization] of [Power Window (Passenger)]. Refer to POWER WINDOW (DIAGNOSTICS)>WORK SUPPORT>OPERATION>SYSTEM INITIALIZATION .
      NOTE: Record the DTCs and FFD because they will be erased when the initialization is performed.
    2. Operate the power window sub-switch and roll passenger's side window up and down.

      Does the window roll up and down?

      Yes:  Even if DTC is displayed, the circuit has returned to a normal condition at this time.

      Reproduce the failure, and then perform the diagnosis again.

      NOTE: In this case, temporary poor contact of connector, temporary open or short circuit of harness may be the cause.

      No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK DTC. 

    Read the DTC of [Power Window (Passenger)] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to POWER WINDOW (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is DTC B2B24 displayed? (Current malfunction)

    Yes:  Replace the front regulator & motor assembly (passenger's side). Refer to GLASS/WINDOWS/MIRRORS>FRONT REGULATOR AND MOTOR ASSEMBLY .

    No:  Even if DTC is displayed, the circuit has returned to a normal condition at this time.

    Reproduce the failure, and then perform the diagnosis again.

    NOTE: In this case, temporary poor contact of connector, temporary open or short circuit of harness may be the cause.
