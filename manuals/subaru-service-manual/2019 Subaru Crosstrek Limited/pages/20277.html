DTC P0AA7-16: Hybrid/EV Battery Voltage Isolation Sensor Circuit Voltage Below Threshold: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

1. - 

Outline of diagnosis: 

When insulation monitor circuit is defective

Insulation monitor circuit output is equal to or less than the standard

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

Normal driving

CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK DTC. 
    1. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, perform the following clear memory. Refer to COMMON (DIAG)>CLEAR MEMORY .
      • P-HEV High Voltage Battery
      • P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control
    2. Turn to IG OFF, and wait for 120 seconds or more.
    3. Turn to IG ON, and wait for 70 seconds or more.
    4. Read the following DTCs using Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) . Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .
      • P-HEV High Voltage Battery
      • P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control

    Is any of DTC P0AA716, P0AA717 or P0AA749 displayed?

    Yes:  Replace Battery ECU assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>BATTERY CONTROL MODULE .

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK DTC. 
    1. Shift to P position and turn to READY ON in the vehicle stop state and wait for 70 seconds or more. (Wait until the engine stops if it started)
    2. Shift to D position while the brake pedal is depressed without depressing the accelerator pedal, and wait for one minute.
    3. Move the vehicle forward 0.5 m (1.6 ft) and perform step  2.
    4. Move the vehicle further forward 0.5 m (1.6 ft) and perform step  2. Perform this (equivalent to 2 m (6.6 ft)) five times in total.
    5. Read the DTC of [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is DTC P0AA649 displayed?

    Yes:  Perform the diagnosis of DTC P0AA649. Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE WITH DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>DTC P0AA649 HYBRID/EV BATTERY VOLTAGE SYSTEM ISOLATION INTERNAL ELECTRONIC FAILURE .

    No:  Replace Battery ECU assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>BATTERY CONTROL MODULE .
