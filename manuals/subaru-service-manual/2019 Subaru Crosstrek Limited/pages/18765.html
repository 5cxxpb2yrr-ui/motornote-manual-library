Differential Side Retainer Oil Seal: Replacement

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the front tires. refer to WHEEL AND TIRE SYSTEM>TIRE AND WHEEL>REMOVAL .
  3. Lift up the vehicle.
  4. Using the TORX® bit T70, remove the drain plug, and drain the transmission gear oil completely.
    CAUTION:
    • Immediately after the vehicle has been running or after idling for a long time, the gear oil will be hot. Be careful not to receive burns.
    • Be careful not to spill the gear oil on the exhaust pipe, to prevent emission of smoke or causing a fire. If gear oil is spilled, wipe it off completely.
  5. Replace the gasket with a new part and install the drain plug using the TORX® bit T70.

    Tightening torque: 

    44 N.m (4.5 kgf-m, 32.5 ft-lb) (aluminum gasket, silver) 70 N.m (7.1 kgf-m, 51.6 ft-lb) (copper gasket, brown) 70 N.m (7.1 kgf-m, 51.6 ft-lb) (metal gasket, black)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Remove the crossmember support - rear.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Remove the front exhaust pipe. refer to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FRONT EXHAUST PIPE>REMOVAL .
  8. Disconnect the stabilizer link.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Remove the bolt securing the ball joint of the front arm to the housing, then separate the front arms and the housing.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  10. Using a crowbar, separate the front drive shaft.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  11. Using the PULLER ASSY (398527700), remove the differential side retainer oil seal.
    CAUTION: If removing the oil seal with a flat tip screwdriver, be careful not to scratch the differential side retainer.
  12. Using the ST, install a new differential side retainer oil seal by lightly tapping with a plastic hammer.
    CAUTION:
    • Apply transmission gear oil to the oil seal lips, and install the oil seal while being careful not to deform the lip.
    • Check the identification marks (L, R) during installation not to mix up the oil seals RH and LH.

    Special tool: 

    ST: 18675AA000 DIFFERENTIAL SIDE OIL SEAL INSTALLER

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  13. Set the OIL SEAL PROTECTOR (28399SA010) to differential side retainer.
  14. Replace the circlip of the front drive shaft with a new part.
  15. Install the front drive shaft and remove the ST.

    Special tool: 

    ST: 28399SA010 OIL SEAL PROTECTOR

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  16. Insert the front arm ball joint and tighten the mounting bolt.
    CAUTION:
    • Do not apply grease to the tapered portion of ball stud.
    • Before tightening, make sure the lower side of front axle housing and stepped section of ball joint are in contact.
    • Be careful not to damage the boot of the joint.

    Tightening torque: 

    50 N.m (5.1 kgf-m, 36.9 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  17. Replace the flange nut with a new part, and install the stabilizer link.

    Tightening torque: 

    60 N.m (6.1 kgf-m, 44.3 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  18. Install the front exhaust pipe. refer to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FRONT EXHAUST PIPE>INSTALLATION .
  19. Install the crossmember support - rear.

    Tightening torque: 

    70 N.m (7.1 kgf-m, 51.6 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  20. Lower the vehicle.
  21. Pour in transmission gear oil and check the oil level. refer to MANUAL TRANSMISSION AND DIFFERENTIAL (6MT)>TRANSMISSION GEAR OIL .
  22. Install the front tires. refer to WHEEL AND TIRE SYSTEM>TIRE AND WHEEL>INSTALLATION .
  23. Connect the battery ground terminal. refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
