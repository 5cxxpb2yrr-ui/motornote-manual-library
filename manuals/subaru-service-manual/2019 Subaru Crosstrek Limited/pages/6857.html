DTC U119B: Lost Communication With Battery Charger Control Module PU-CAN

DTC detecting condition: 

Detected when CAN data from Electric vehicle charger assembly does not arrive.

NOTE: Perform the diagnosis for LAN system. Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .
