DTC B2B10: Passenger's Door Power Window Driver's Door Auto Switch Stuck

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

DTC detecting condition: 

When AUTO SW signal ON is received for 20±1 seconds or longer.

Trouble symptom: 

  • When the power window main switch is operated, passenger's side window does not work.
  • When the power window main switch is operated, passenger's side window rolls down only in manual down operation, or inches up. (Inch up: Operating the switch rolls up the window about 10 mm only)

Wiring diagram: 

Power window system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (IMPREZA MODEL)>POWER WINDOW SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>POWER WINDOW SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>POWER WINDOW SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to POWER WINDOW (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK DTC. 

    Read the DTC of [Power Window (Switch)] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to POWER WINDOW (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    • [Power Window (Switch)]

    Is DTC B2B50 displayed? (Current malfunction)

    Yes:  Perform the diagnosis of DTC B2B50. Refer to POWER WINDOW (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE WITH DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>DTC B2B50 POWER WINDOW MAIN SWITCH FAILURE .

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK [Remote switch] (DATA MONITOR). 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    2. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, check the following items in the [Data monitor] of [Power Window (Passenger)]. Refer to POWER WINDOW (DIAGNOSTICS)>DATA MONITOR .
      • Remote switch

      Does the display change corresponding to the switch operation?

      Yes:  Go to  6.

      No:  Go to  3.

  3. CHECK HARNESS (OPEN CIRCUIT). 
    1. Disconnect the power window main switch connector.
    2. Disconnect the front regulator & motor assembly LH connector.
    3. Disconnect the front regulator & motor assembly RH connector.
    4. Disconnect the body integrated unit connector.
    5. Using a tester, measure the resistance between power window main switch connector and front regulator & motor assembly connector (passenger's side).

      Connector & terminal

      (D7) No. 13 - (D13) No. 7:

      Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

      Yes:  Go to  4.

      No:  Repair the open circuit of the harness between power window main switch connector and front regulator & assembly connector (passenger's side).

  4. CHECK HARNESS (GROUND SHORT CIRCUIT). 

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between the power window main switch connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal

    (D7) No. 13 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance 1 MΩ or more?

    Yes:  Go to  5.

    No:  Repair the open harness causing short-to ground condition between power window main switch connector and front regulator & motor assembly connector (passenger's side).

  5. CHECK HARNESS (SHORT CIRCUIT TO POWER SUPPLY). 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    2. Using a tester, measure the voltage between the power window main switch connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal

      (D7) No. 13 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

      Is the voltage less than 1 V?

      Yes:  Replace the power window main switch. Refer to GLASS/WINDOWS/MIRRORS>POWER WINDOW CONTROL SWITCH . After the repair, Go to  6.

      No:  Repair the open harness causing short-to power supply condition between power window main switch connector and front regulator & motor assembly connector.

  6. CHECK FRONT REGULATOR & MOTOR ASSEMBLY. 

    Using the Subaru Select Monitor, check the following items in the [Active Test] of [Power Window (Passenger)]. Refer to POWER WINDOW (DIAGNOSTICS)>ACTIVE TEST .

    • Power Window Operation Test

    Does the window roll up and down?

    Yes:  Go to  7.

    No:  Replace the front regulator & motor assembly (passenger's side). Refer to GLASS/WINDOWS/MIRRORS>FRONT REGULATOR AND MOTOR ASSEMBLY .

  7. CHECK DTC. 

    Read the DTC of [Power Window (Passenger)] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to POWER WINDOW (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is DTC B2B10 displayed? (Current malfunction)

    Yes:  Replace the front regulator & motor assembly (passenger's side). Refer to GLASS/WINDOWS/MIRRORS>FRONT REGULATOR AND MOTOR ASSEMBLY .

    No:  Even if DTC is displayed. The circuit has returned to a normal condition at this time.

    Reproduce the failure, and then perform the diagnosis again.

    NOTE: In this case, temporary poor contact of connector, temporary open or short circuit of harness may be the cause.
