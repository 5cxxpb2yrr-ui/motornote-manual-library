EGR Control Valve: Inspection

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Check that the EGR control valve has no deformation, cracks or other damages.
  2. Measure the resistance between EGR control valve terminals.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Terminal No. Standard
    2 and 1 15±2 Ω
    2 and 3 15±2 Ω
    5 and 4 15±2 Ω
    5 and 6 15±2 Ω
