Recovery Inspection

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
WARNING:
  • The hybrid system includes a high voltage circuit. Mishandling could cause accidents such as electric shock or leak. Always check "WARNING (HYBRID SYSTEM)" and perform the proper operation. Refer to PRECAUTION/WARNING>WARNING (HYBRID SYSTEM) .
  • Be sure to judge the HV battery before it is collected.
  • If the HV battery is hot, leave it to cool and begin work after the temperature has dropped.
  • For safety reasons, be sure to return the replaced HV battery to a qualified collection service.
  • If you use a collection service designated by manufacturer, the battery will be handled properly and safely.
  • If the HV battery is not properly processed and disposed of, or is left unprocessed, it may cause electrical shock accidents. Be sure to have the HV battery collected through the dealership.
  • Do not leave the removed HV battery in a place where water may contact it. It could cause fire accidents.
NOTE: So that the HV battery can be collected safely, as necessary discharge the HV battery power. By judging the HV battery prior to collection, it is possible to determine whether or not it is necessary to discharge the HV battery power and decide the discharge method.
  1. CHECK FOR ELECTROLYTE LEAKAGE  .
    WARNING: Be sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles.
    1. Prepare used tires or similar items.
    2. Tilt the HV battery as shown in the figure and leave it to rest for 5 minutes.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.

    Is it free from electrolyte leakage?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No: 

    1. Wear rubber gloves and protective goggles, and wipe up any electrolyte with a cloth. Do not throw away the cloth with electrolyte on it. Dispose of it correctly in accordance with laws and other regulations.
    2. Discharge the battery (by immersing it in salt water). Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY>DISCHARGING>DISCHARGING THE BATTERY BY IMMERSING IT IN SALT WATER .
  2. CHECK DTC  .

    Check the DTC that is the reason for replacing the HV battery.

    Perform the following check procedure according to the DTC.

    • If the DTC is unknown, go to  A.
    • When replacing the HV battery was for a reason other than the DTC listed below, go to  B.
    • When replacing the HV battery due to the DTC listed below, go to  C.

    P1A6317
    P1A6617
    P1A6917
    P31AA17
    P0C3000
    P31B300
    P1C7D49
    1. Go to  3.
    2. Go to  5.
    3. Go to  6.
  3. CHECK THE HV BATTERY VOLTAGE  .
    WARNING: Be sure to wear insulated gloves.
    1. Remove the battery cover.
    2. Disconnect the connector and measure the voltage as shown in the table below.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    WARNING: Do not cross the electrode parts of the circuit tester measurement terminals.
    Terminal Inspection conditions Standard
    A - C Always Less than 140.6 V
    B - D Always Less than 210.9 V

    Is the voltage the standard value?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Go to  6.

  4. CHECK THE HV BATTERY INSULATION  .
    WARNING: Be sure to wear insulated gloves.
    1. Using a circuit tester, measure the resistance as shown in the table below.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    CAUTION: When checking, be sure to set the circuit tester to 500 V. If a range higher than 500 V is used, the part may be damaged during the check.
    Terminal Inspection conditions Standard
    A - External case Always 1 MΩ or more
    B - External case Always 1 MΩ or more

    Is the resistance the standard value?

    Yes:  Go to  5.

    No:  Go to  6.

  5. CHECK THE HV BATTERY APPEARANCE  .
    WARNING: Be sure to wear insulated gloves.

    Check that there is no deformation of the HV battery.

    Is it free from deformation?

    Yes:  Package the battery in the supplied container and notify the collection service.

    No:  Go to  6.

  6. CHECK THE HV BATTERY  .
    WARNING: Be sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles.

    Check that there is no damage to the HV battery connector.

    NOTE: If there is damage to the HV battery connector (orange (connector that connects to the battery ECU assembly)), it will not be possible to connect the Li-ion battery discharge tool. Therefore discharge by immersing the battery in salt water.

    Is it free from damage?

    Yes:  Discharge the battery (using the Li-ion battery discharge tool). Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY>DISCHARGING>DISCHARGING THE BATTERY USING THE LI-ION BATTERY DISCHARGE TOOL .

    No:  Discharge the battery (by immersing it in salt water). Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY>DISCHARGING>DISCHARGING THE BATTERY BY IMMERSING IT IN SALT WATER .
