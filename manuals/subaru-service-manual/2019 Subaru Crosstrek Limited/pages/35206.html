Crosstrek Model

  1. Lift up the vehicle, and then remove the front wheels.
  2. Remove the axle nut.
    CAUTION: Do not loosen the axle nut while the front axle is loaded. Doing so may damage the hub unit bearing.
    1. Lift the crimped section of axle nut.
    2. Remove the axle nut using a socket wrench while depressing the brake pedal.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the crossmember support - rear.
    NOTE: Remove the crossmember support - rear only on the removal side if only one side of the front drive shaft assembly is removed.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the front ABS wheel speed sensor and clips.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the brake hose bracket.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Disconnect the stabilizer link.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Disconnect the front arm ball joint, and lower the front arm.
    CAUTION: Be careful not to damage the boot of the joint.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Remove the front drive shaft assembly from the front axle housing.
    NOTE: If it is hard to remove, use the ST.

    Preparation tool: 

    ST1: AXLE SHAFT PULLER (926470000)

    ST2: AXLE SHAFT PULLER PLATE (28099 Pa110)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Using a bar, remove the front drive shaft assembly from transmission.
CAUTION: Be careful not to allow the bar to damage holder area.
