DTC B2C22: Eyesight System

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

Outline of diagnosis: 

EyeSight is faulty.

Time needed for diagnosis: 

800 ms

Recovery conditions: 

Ignition switch OFF → ON

NOTE: Perform the diagnosis for EyeSight. Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE . Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .
