Overview Of Inspection

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
Range in system diagram Contents of inspection Reason Inspection steps
*a Check the connecting condition of connector between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly - HV battery junction block and continuity of Wire harness Check the connector for mating failure, and SMRP and SMRB exciting lines for open circuit From 3 to 8
*b Check the operation of system main relay Check SMRB and SMRP for OFF stuck From 9 to 12
*c Check high voltage cable between HV battery junction block - Inverter with converter assembly for connecting condition and continuity Check for poor connection (trace of arc) and open circuit due to looseness From 13 to 16
*d Check high voltage cable between HV battery - HV battery junction block for connecting condition Check for poor connection (trace of arc) and open circuit due to looseness From 17 to 22
*e Check HV battery. Check if HV battery voltage decreased significantly from the common range From 23 to 29
