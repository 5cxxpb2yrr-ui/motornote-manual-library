Service Disconnect Plug: Notes

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  • When performing maintenance of wires and parts for the high voltage system, make sure to remove the service disconnect plug in order to cut off the high voltage circuits.
  • Make sure to wear insulated gloves during the work.
  • Carry the removed service disconnect plug in your pocket in order to prevent other servicemen from accidentally installing during work.
  • All the wires and connectors for high voltage power cable assemblies are colored in orange.
