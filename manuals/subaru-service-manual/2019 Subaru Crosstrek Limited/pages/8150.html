Crosstrek Model

  1. Support the fuel tank with a transmission jack, set the fuel tank in place, and temporarily tighten the bolts of the fuel tank band.
    CAUTION: A helper is required to perform this work.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Tighten the bolts of the fuel tank band in the order shown in the figure.

    Tightening torque: 

    33 N.m (3.4 kgf-m, 24.3 ft-lb)

    Fig 1: Fuel Tank Band Bolts Tightening Sequence
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Connect the fuel delivery tube to the fuel delivery pipe. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>INSTALLATION>QUICK CONNECTOR CONNECTION (TYPE A) .
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Connect the vent tube (A) to the canister. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>INSTALLATION>QUICK CONNECTOR CONNECTION (TYPE F) .
  5. Connect the circulate tube (B) to the fuel tank. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>INSTALLATION>QUICK CONNECTOR CONNECTION (TYPE D) .
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Connect the fuel filler hose to the fuel filler pipe assembly. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>INSTALLATION>FUEL FILLER HOSE CONNECTION .
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Install the rear differential.

    T-type: 

    Refer to DIFFERENTIALS>REAR DIFFERENTIAL (T-TYPE)>INSTALLATION .

    VA-type: 

    Refer to DIFFERENTIALS>REAR DIFFERENTIAL (VA-TYPE)>INSTALLATION .

  8. Install the rear exhaust pipe and muffler.

    Rear exhaust pipe: 

    Refer to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>REAR EXHAUST PIPE>INSTALLATION .

    Muffler: 

    Refer to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>MUFFLER>INSTALLATION .

  9. Install the fuel tank protector. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FUEL TANK PROTECTOR>INSTALLATION .
  10. Lower the vehicle.
  11. Connect the connector to the fuel sub level sensor.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  12. Install the service hole cover of fuel sub level sensor.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  13. Connect the connector to the fuel pump.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  14. Install the service hole cover of fuel pump.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  15. Install the rear seat cushion. Refer to SEATS>REAR SEAT>INSTALLATION .
  16. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
