Handmade Tool

  • WASHER NOZZLE ADJUSTMENT JIG 
NOTE: Referring to the data described below, prepare the materials to make a nozzle - windshield washer adjustment tool.
Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.

Preparation items: 

Vertebra of wiper blade which is to be discarded

Vinyl tape

  1. Grind the vertebra edge until the width becomes 1 mm (0.04 in).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Bend the vertebra at the position 25 mm (0.98 in) away from its end by 45°.
  3. Bend the vertebra at the position 29 mm (1.14 in) away from the other end by 45°.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Double up the vertebra so that the longer end (29 mm (1.14 in)) comes upward.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Secure the vertebra with a vise (a) so that the tip clearance between upper and lower becomes 5 mm (0.2 in).
  6. Bend the vertebra at the position approximately 90 mm (3.54 in) away from the bending point for the longer end.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Check that the tip clearance between upper and lower is 5 mm (0.2 in), and secure the vertebra with vinyl tape (b).
Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
