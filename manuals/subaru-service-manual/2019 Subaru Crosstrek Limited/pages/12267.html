High-Pressure Side Is High

  1. CHECK CONDENSER  .

    Check the condenser. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>CONDENSER>INSPECTION .

    Is condenser OK?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Clean or replace the condenser.

  2. CHECK RADIATOR FAN  .

    Check the radiator fan system. Refer to COOLING (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>RADIATOR FAN SYSTEM .

    Is radiator fan system normal?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Repair the radiator fan system or replace the faulty parts.

  3. FILL PROPER AMOUNT OF REFRIGERANT  .

    Drain all refrigerant, and refill proper amount of refrigerant.

    • Recovery: Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>REFRIGERANT RECOVERY PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .
    • Fill: Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>REFRIGERANT CHARGING PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .

    Is refrigerant pressure within the standard value?

    Yes:  Refrigerant pressure is normal.

    No:  Check the high-pressure hose and condenser for kinks or clogging, and replace if defective.
