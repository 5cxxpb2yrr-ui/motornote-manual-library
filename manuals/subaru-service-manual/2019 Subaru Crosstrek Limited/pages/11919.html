Power Window

  • Operate the power window switches one by one to check that each of the power windows goes up and down without noises.

Check AUTO UP/AUTO DOWN operate properly by operating the power window switch on driver's and passenger's seat sides.

CAUTION: If battery has been removed, AUTO UP does not operate properly because initialized. In this case, perform the initial setting (reset A) and check AUTO UP operates properly.
NOTE: Check the power window if the door glass of the driver's seat does not open/close automatically. Refer to POWER WINDOW (DIAGNOSTICS)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE .
