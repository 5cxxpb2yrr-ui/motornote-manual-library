Upper Inner Trim: Removal

CAUTION:
  • Before handling the airbag system components, refer to "CAUTION" of "General Description" in "AIRBAG SYSTEM". Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  • Airbag system satellite safing sensor is located under the front center of the rear seat cushion. Be careful not to apply strong impact to the sensor when working with the rear seat cushion removed.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery and wait for at least 60 seconds before starting work. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    NOTE: On power seat models, move the front seat all the way forward before disconnecting the battery ground terminal.
  2. Remove the trim panel - front pillar UPR.
    1. Pull the trim panel - front pillar UPR towards the center of the vehicle to release the clips.
    2. Rotate the trim panel - front pillar UPR in the arrow direction.
    3. Release the hook at the end of the tether clip, and remove the trim panel - front pillar UPR.
      CAUTION:
      • Do not use any tool when releasing the hook at the end of the trim panel - front pillar UPR.
      • Be careful not to damage the surface of the trim panel - front pillar UPR and the curtain airbag module.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the left and right grille speaker sides. Refer to ENTERTAINMENT & MONITORING>FRONT SPEAKER>REMOVAL>TWEETER .
  4. Remove the rear seat cushion assembly. Refer to SEATS>REAR SEAT>REMOVAL .
  5. Remove the cover side sill - front INN, cover side sill - rear INN, and trim panel - center pillar LWR. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>LOWER INNER TRIM>REMOVAL .
  6. Remove the trim panel - center pillar UPR.
    1. Remove the cover through (a), and remove the seat belt anchor bolt.
    2. Remove the bolt on the lower side of the trim panel - center pillar UPR.
    3. Remove the clip by pulling the upper part of the trim toward you, and while lowering the entire trim, remove the trim panel - center pillar UPR.
    CAUTION: Do not reuse the upper clips of the trim panel - center pillar UPR. Once the clip is removed, its holding force is reduced. Be sure to replace it with a new clip.
