Extension Case: Removal

  1. Remove the transmission assembly from the vehicle. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL .
  2. Remove the extension case and transmission hanger.
    NOTE: The total number of extension case mounting bolts is 13.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the O-rings.
