Diagnostic Method

Ignition Switch Voltage or Brake System Voltage is high.

JUDGMENT VALUE SPECIFICATION

Malfunction Criteria Threshold Value
Case 1 
IG1 Voltage		 17.4 V or more
Case 2 
Main power supply		 16.44 V or more

Time needed for diagnosis:  770 ms

Malfunction indicator light illumination:  Illuminates as soon as a malfunction occurs.
