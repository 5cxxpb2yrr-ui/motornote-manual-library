The Auto Headlight Beam Leveler Does Not Operate

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

Detecting condition: 

Malfunction of power supply circuit, output circuit, and auto headlight beam leveler control module Wiring diagram: 

Headlight beam leveler system. Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (IMPREZA MODEL)>HEADLIGHT BEAM LEVELER SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>HEADLIGHT BEAM LEVELER SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>HEADLIGHT BEAM LEVELER SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION:
  • Before performing diagnosis, check the fuse in this circuit. Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>RELAY AND FUSE .
  • Initialization is required after replacing the auto headlight beam leveler control module. Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>AUTO HEADLIGHT BEAM LEVELER SYSTEM>PROCEDURE .
  1. CHECK DATA MONITOR. 

    Using the Subaru Select Monitor, check the following items in the [Data monitor] of [Headlight/Foglight]. Refer to HEADLIGHT/FOGLIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS)>DATA MONITOR .

    • Control module voltage

    Does the data monitor indicate the standard value?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK HARNESS BETWEEN POWER SUPPLY - AUTO HEADLIGHT BEAM LEVELER CM. 
    1. Disconnect the auto headlight beam leveler control module connector.
    2. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    3. Measure the voltage between the auto headlight beam leveler control module connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (B616) No. 1 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

      Is the voltage 8 - 16 V?

      Yes:  Replace the auto headlight beam leveler control module. Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>AUTO HEADLIGHT BEAM LEVELER CONTROL MODULE .

      No:  Repair or replace the harness.

  3. CHECK INDICATOR OUTPUT. 

    Turn the ignition switch to ON.

    Does the warning indicator turn on for three seconds?

    Yes:  Go to 4  .

    No:  Check the combination meter.

  4. CHECK INDICATOR OUTPUT. 

    Leave the ignition switch to ON for 10 seconds.

    Does the warning indicator light go off?

    Yes:  Go to  5.

    No:  Go to  9.

  5. CHECK HEADLIGHT ASSEMBLY (ACTUATOR) DRIVE. 
    1. Connect the auto headlight beam leveler control module connector.
    2. Turn the ignition switch to ON, and within 10 seconds, repeat the OFF → Low beam of headlight switch 5 times.
    3. Check that the headlight beam drops once, then returns to normal.
    4. Then, after waiting for 30 seconds or more with the ignition ON, turn the ignition switch to OFF.

      Does the headlight beam drop down once, and then return?

      Yes:  Replace the auto headlight beam leveler control module. Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>AUTO HEADLIGHT BEAM LEVELER CONTROL MODULE .

      No:  Go to  6.

  6. CHECK HEADLIGHT ASSEMBLY (ACTUATOR) OUTPUT. 
    1. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, check the following items in the [Data monitor] of [Headlight/Foglight]. Refer to HEADLIGHT/FOGLIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS)>DATA MONITOR .
      • Actuator Signal
    2. Turn the ignition switch to ON, and within 10 seconds, repeat the OFF → Low beam of headlight switch 5 times.
    3. Check that the headlight beam drops once, then returns to normal.
    4. Then, after waiting for 30 seconds or more with the ignition ON, turn the ignition switch to OFF.

      Does the data monitor indicate the standard value? (Does the value change at 10 - 90 %?)

      Yes:  Go to  7.

      No:  Replace the auto headlight beam leveler control module. Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>AUTO HEADLIGHT BEAM LEVELER CONTROL MODULE .

  7. CHECK OUTPUT VOLTAGE BETWEEN HEADLIGHT ASSEMBLY (ACTUATOR). 
    1. Disconnect the auto headlight beam leveler control module connector.
    2. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    3. Measure the voltage between the auto headlight beam leveler control module connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (B616) No. 10 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

      Is the voltage 8 - 16 V?

      Yes:  Replace the headlight assembly. Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>HEADLIGHT ASSEMBLY .

      No:  Go to  8.

  8. CHECK HARNESS BETWEEN AUTO HEADLIGHT BEAM LEVELER CM AND HEADLIGHT ASSEMBLY (ACTUATOR). 
    1. Disconnect the connector of headlight assembly.
    2. Measure the continuity between the auto headlight beam leveler control module connector and headlight assembly (actuator) connector.

      Connector & terminal 

      • Headlight beam leveler RH 

        (B616) No. 10 - (B487) No. 10:

        (B616) No. 17 - (B487) No. 11:

        (B616) No. 11 - (B487) No. 12:

      • Headlight beam leveler LH 

        (B616) No. 10 - (B477) No. 10:

        (B616) No. 17 - (B477) No. 11:

        (B616) No. 11 - (B477) No. 12:

      Is there continuity?

      Yes:  Replace the auto headlight beam leveler control module. Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>AUTO HEADLIGHT BEAM LEVELER CONTROL MODULE .

      No:  Repair the open circuit or poor contact of the connector in the harness between headlight assembly and auto headlight beam leveler control module.

  9. CHECK DTC. 

    Read the DTC of [Headlight/Foglight] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to HEADLIGHT/FOGLIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is DTC displayed? (Current malfunction)

    Yes:  Perform the diagnosis for the displayed DTCs. Refer to HEADLIGHT/FOGLIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>LIST .

    No:  Replace the auto headlight beam leveler control module. Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>AUTO HEADLIGHT BEAM LEVELER CONTROL MODULE .
