Outline Of Diagnosis

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Detect the malfunction of the engine oil temperature sensor output properties.

After the engine starts after the specified period of soaking time has elapsed, diagnose by correlation between engine oil temperature sensor value, engine coolant temperature sensor value and ambient temperature sensor value.

Judge as NG when the differences are both above the specified value by comparing between engine oil temperature and engine coolant temperature, engine oil temperature and ambient temperature.
