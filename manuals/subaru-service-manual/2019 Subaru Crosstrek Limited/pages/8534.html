Diagnostic Method

If the duration of time while the following conditions are met is longer than the time indicated, judge as NG.

JUDGMENT VALUE SPECIFICATION

Malfunction Criteria Threshold Value
|Ambient air temperature 21 sec. after engine start - Intake air temperature 21 sec. after engine start|
|Ambient air temperature at engine start - Engine coolant temperature at engine start|		 More than value of Map
More than 25°C (45°F)
TEMPERATURE SPECIFICATION - MAP

Intake air temperature (°C (°F)) -30 (-22) 45 (113) 60 (140) 80 (176)
|Ambient air temperature 21 sec. after engine start - Intake air temperature 21 sec. after engine start | (°C (°F)) 20 (36) 20 (36) 32 (57.6) 32 (57.6)

Time needed for diagnosis:  Less than 1 s

Malfunction indicator light illumination:  Illuminates when malfunction occurs in 2 continuous driving cycles.
