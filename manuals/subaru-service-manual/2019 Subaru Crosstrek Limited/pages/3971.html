Lock Actuator

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

• FRONT 

  1. Check door lock operation

    Check the door lock operation when battery voltage is applied between the connector terminals.

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Terminal No. Inspection conditions Specification
    4 (+) - 1 (-) Apply battery voltage. LOCK
    1 (+) - 4 (-) UNLOCK
  2. Check of position switch (model with keyless access with push button start only)

    Measure the resistance between connector terminals.

    Preparation tool: 

    Circuit tester

    Terminal No. Inspection conditions Standard
    8 - 7 LOCK 1 MΩ or more
    UNLOCK Less than 1 Ω
  3. Replace the latch & actuator assembly - front if it is found defective.

• REAR 

  1. Check door lock operation

    Check the door lock operation when battery voltage is applied between the connector terminals.

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Terminal No. Inspection conditions Specification
    4 (+) - 1 (-) Apply battery voltage. LOCK
    1 (+) - 4 (-) UNLOCK
  2. If it does not operate properly as a result of inspection, replace the latch & actuator assembly - rear.
