DTC U0293-87: Lost Communication With Hybrid Powertrain Control Module Missing Message: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

1. - 

Outline of diagnosis: 

Malfunction of CAN communication with Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly (CAN system malfunction)

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

Normal driving

CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK DTC. 

    Read the DTC of [P-HEV High Voltage Battery] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Are any DTCs displayed?

    Yes:  Perform the diagnosis for LAN system. Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE .
