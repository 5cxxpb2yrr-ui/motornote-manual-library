Rear Gate Glass: Removal

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY>GROUND TERMINAL REMOVAL OR INSTALLATION .
  2. Remove the trim panel - rear gate and the motor assembly - rear wiper. Refer to WIPER AND WASHER SYSTEMS>REAR WIPER MOTOR>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the roof spoiler. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>ROOF SPOILER>REMOVAL .
  4. Disconnect the rear defogger connector.
  5. Remove the glass - rear gate in the same procedure as for the glass - front window. Refer to GLASS/WINDOWS/MIRRORS>WINDSHIELD GLASS>REMOVAL .
