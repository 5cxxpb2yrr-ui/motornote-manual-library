Rear Differential Side Oil Seal: Replacement

  1. Remove the rear differential.
    • T-type:

      Refer to DIFFERENTIALS>REAR DIFFERENTIAL (T-TYPE)>REMOVAL .

    • VAl-type:

      Refer to DIFFERENTIALS>REAR DIFFERENTIAL (VA-TYPE)>REMOVAL .

  2. Remove the side oil seal using the ST or a screwdriver wrapped with vinyl tape to prevent side retainer damage.

    Special Tool: 

    ST: 398527700 PULLER ASSY

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Using the ST, install a new side oil seal.
    CAUTION: Pay attention to the left and right of the oil seal.
    NOTE: Apply differential gear oil to the oil seal lips.
    • T-type

      Special Tool: 

      ST: 398437700 OIL SEAL INSTALLER

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • VA1-type

      Special Tool: 

      ST: 498447100 INSTALLER

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Install the rear differential.
    • T-type:

      Refer to DIFFERENTIALS>REAR DIFFERENTIAL (T-TYPE)>INSTALLATION .

    • VAl-type:

      Refer to DIFFERENTIALS>REAR DIFFERENTIAL (VA-TYPE)>INSTALLATION .
