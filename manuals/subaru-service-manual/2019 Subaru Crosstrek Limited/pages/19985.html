DTC B1625: Side Airbag Sensor LH Failure

Diagnosis start condition: 

When the ignition voltage is 10 V - 16 V.

DTC detecting condition: 

Side airbag sensor LH is faulty.

When DTC B1625 is displayed, the circuit within the side airbag sensor LH is faulty.

Replace the side airbag sensor LH. Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>SIDE AIRBAG SENSOR .
