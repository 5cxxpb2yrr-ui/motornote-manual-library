Interior Condenser

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
CAUTION: Before handling the airbag system components, refer to "CAUTION" of "General Description" in "AIRBAG SYSTEM". Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. Using the refrigerant recovery system, discharge refrigerant. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>REFRIGERANT RECOVERY PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .
  2. Disconnect the battery ground terminal and wait for at least 60 seconds before starting work. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  3. Drain engine coolant. (Engine side) Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>ENGINE COOLANT>REPLACEMENT>DRAINING OF ENGINE COOLANT .
  4. Remove the heater and cooling unit assembly. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>HEATER AND COOLING UNIT>REMOVAL .
  5. Remove the cover - heater pipe.
    1. Remove the connector clip, and then remove the harness from the harness clamp.
    2. Remove the screws and detach the cover - heater pipe.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Remove the cover - evaporator.
    1. Remove the gasket.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Remove the screws and lock to remove the cover - evaporator.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Remove the pipe - condenser.
    1. Remove the bolt and remove the pipe - condenser.
    CAUTION: Seal the disconnected pipe and engaging part of condenser with a plug or vinyl tape to prevent foreign matter from entering.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Remove the internal condenser as shown in the figure.
Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
