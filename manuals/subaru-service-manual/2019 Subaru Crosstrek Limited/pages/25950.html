DTC P1B4A-62: Hybrid/EV Battery Heater Temperature Sensor Signal Compare Failure: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

1. - 

Outline of diagnosis: 

When the difference between the two temperature sensor (thermistor) values becomes equal to or larger than the predetermined value

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

Normal driving

Wiring diagram: 

Hybrid system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK DTC. 

    Read the following DTCs using Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) . Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    • P-HEV High Voltage Battery
    • P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control
    NOTE:
    System Related DTC
    P-HEV High Voltage Battery P060A47 HYBRID/EV BATTERY ENERGY CONTROL MODULE MONITORING PROCESSOR WATCHDOG/SAFETY MCU FAILURE
    P060B49 HYBRID/EV BATTERY ENERGY CONTROL MODULE A/D PROCESSING INTERNAL ELECTRONIC FAILURE
    P060687 HYBRID/EV BATTERY ENERGY CONTROL MODULE PROCESSOR TO MONITORING PROCESSOR MISSING MESSAGE
    P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control P0A1F94 HYBRID/EV BATTERY ENERGY CONTROL MODULE UNEXPECTED OPERATION

    When "Only DTC P1B4A62 is displayed" or "DTC other than above is displayed", go to  A.

    When DTCs related to [P-HEV High Voltage Battery], mentioned above, are simultaneously displayed, go to  B.

    When DTCs related to [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control], mentioned above, are simultaneously displayed, go to  C.

    Yes: 

    1. Go to  2.
    2. Perform the diagnosis for the simultaneously displayed DTCs.
    3. Perform the diagnosis for the simultaneously displayed DTCs.

    No:  -

  2. CHECK CONNECTOR CONNECTION CONDITION (Battery ECU assembly). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Check that the Service plug is disconnected. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .
    2. Check the connecting condition of connector (HV42) in Battery ECU assembly and check the relevant terminals for contact pressure.

    Is there looseness at the connector connection, or any poor contact, deformation or intrusion of water or foreign materials at the relevant terminals?

    Yes:  Connect the connector securely. Or repair the poor contact of connector.

    No:  Go to  3.

  3. CHECK HV battery heater (HV battery heater temperature sensor No. 1). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Disconnect Battery ECU assembly connector (HV42).
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between Battery ECU assembly connector terminals.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV42) No. 18 - (HV42) No. 63:

      NOTE:
      Thermistor temperature Inspection conditions Standard
      0 - 10°C (32 - 50°F) IG OFF 17.7 - 27.8 kΩ
      10 - 20°C (50 - 68°F) IG OFF 12.0 - 18.2 kΩ
      20 - 30°C (68 - 86°F) IG OFF 8.22 - 12.2 kΩ
      30 - 40°C (86 - 104°F) IG OFF 5.74 - 8.41 kΩ
      40 - 50°C (104 - 122°F) IG OFF 4.09 - 5.91 kΩ

    Is the resistance as shown above?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Go to  12.

  4. CHECK HV battery heater (HV battery heater temperature sensor No. 1). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between terminals in Battery ECU assembly connector, and between Battery ECU assembly connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (HV42) No. 18 - (HV42) No. 1 - 69 (except for No. 63):

    (HV42) No. 18 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance 10 kΩ or more?

    Yes:  Go to  5.

    No:  Go to  12.

  5. CHECK HV battery heater (HV battery heater temperature sensor No. 1). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Connect the ground terminal to Battery (12V). Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    2. Turn to IG ON.
    3. Using a tester, measure the voltage between Battery ECU assembly connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV42) No. 18 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    Is the voltage less than 1 V?

    Yes:  Go to  6.

    No:  Go to  12.
