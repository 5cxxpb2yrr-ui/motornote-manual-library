  1. CHECK Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly  .
    1. Turn to IG ON.
    2. Using a tester, measure the voltage between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector and chassis ground. (With the connector connected)

      Connector & terminal 

      (B606) No. 25 (+) - (i369) No. 6 (-):

    Is the voltage less than 4.5 V?

    Yes:  Shut Down Signal Circuit is normal.

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly - Inverter with converter assembly) (SHORT TO POWER SUPPLY)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Check that the Service plug is disconnected. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .
    2. Disconnect Inverter with converter assembly connector (B606).
    3. Disconnect Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector (HV23).
    4. Connect the ground terminal of Battery (12V). Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    5. Turn to IG ON.
    6. Using a tester, measure the voltage between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (B606) No. 25 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    Is the voltage less than 1 V?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Repair or replace the Wire harness between Inverter with converter assembly connector and Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector.

  3. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly - Inverter with converter assembly) (LINE-TO-LINE SHORT)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Turn the ignition to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the ground terminal of Battery (12V). Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    3. Disconnect Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connectors (i369) and (i370).
    4. Using a tester, measure the resistance between terminals in Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector, and between terminals in Inverter with converter assembly connector.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV23) No. 12 - (HV23) No. 1:

      (HV23) No. 12 - (HV23) No. 5:

      (B606) No. 25 - (i369) No. 4:

      (B606) No. 25 - (B606) No. 1:

      (B606) No. 25 - (i369) No. 3:

    Is the resistance 10 kΩ or more?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Repair or replace the Wire harness between Inverter with converter assembly connector and Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector.

  4. CHECK THE Inverter with converter assembly UNIT  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between Inverter with converter assembly terminals.

    Connector & terminal 

    (HV23) No. 12 - (HV23) No. 1:

    (HV23) No. 12 - (HV23) No. 5:

    Is the resistance 10 kΩ or more?

    Yes:  Replace Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL MODULE .

    No:  Replace Inverter with converter assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>INVERTER AND CONVERTER ASSEMBLY .

GENERAL DESCRIPTION 

The malfunction cause may be the shutdown signal.

Check for shorted +B of the shutdown signal.

Check related parts 

Part Inspection
HSDN terminal Check for open or short circuit between HSDN terminals with READY OFF (IG ON)
Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly, inverter, Wire harness Check Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly, inverter and Wire harness for open and short circuit

Explanation of circuits 

Shutdown power supply to the motor by the shutdown signal from Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly to Motor generator control ECU.
