DTC P058A-01: Auxiliary Battery Monitor Module General Electrical Failure: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

1. - 

Outline of diagnosis: 

Any of the following conditions is met with the power switch on (IG). (1 trip detection logic):

  • Current is 999.939 A or more, or -1000 A or less for 3.2 seconds or more.
  • The difference between the maximum and minimum current is 0.0305 A or less for 100 seconds or more.
  • A current detection circuit malfunction signal is received.
  • The Battery (12V) temperature is 105°C (221°F) or more or -40°C (-40°F) or less for 3.2 seconds or more.
  • A temperature detection circuit malfunction signal is received.
  • Voltage is 15.98 V or more, or 6.0 V or less for 3.2 seconds or more.
  • A voltage detection circuit malfunction signal is received.
  • A writing error occurs in the Battery (12V) state sensor internal memory (battery identification data).

Trouble symptom: 

DC/DC Converter command fixed

NOTE:
  • Before performing diagnosis, check the fuse in this circuit. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>RELAY AND FUSE .
  • When DTC P162B87 is detected simultaneously, perform the diagnosis for P162B87 first. Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE WITH DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>DTC P162B87 LOST COMMUNICATION WITH AUXILIARY BATTERY MONITOR MODULE MISSING MESSAGE .
  1. CHECK INSTALLATION CONDITION OF Battery (12V) sensor  .

    Check the installation condition of Battery (12V) sensor. Refer to STARTING/CHARGING SYSTEMS (H4DO (HEV))>BATTERY SENSOR .

    Is Battery (12V) sensor securely installed?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Securely install Battery (12V) sensor.

  2. CHECK CONNECTION CONDITION OF CONNECTOR (Battery (12V) sensor CONNECTOR)  .

    Check the connecting condition of connector in Battery (12V) sensor and check the relevant terminals for contact pressure, deformation and intrusion of water or foreign matter. Refer to STARTING/CHARGING SYSTEMS (H4DO (HEV))>BATTERY SENSOR .

    Is there looseness at the connector mating, or any poor contact, deformation or intrusion of water or foreign matter at the relevant terminals?

    Yes:  Connect the connector securely. Or repair the poor contact of connector.

    No:  Go to  3.

  3. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Battery (12V) sensor - Battery (12V))  .
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the Battery (12V) sensor connector.
    3. Using a tester, measure the voltage between Battery (12V) sensor connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (B9) No. 1 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    Is the voltage 11 - 14 V?

    Yes:  Replace Battery (12V) sensor. Refer to STARTING/CHARGING SYSTEMS (H4DO (HEV))>BATTERY SENSOR .

    No:  Repair or replace Wire harness between Battery (12V) sensor connector and chassis ground.
