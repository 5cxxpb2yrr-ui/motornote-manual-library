Actuator Link

  1. Visually check the operating range of the link, and remove the foreign matter if any.
  2. Operate the air flow control dial or switch to check that the link operates normally.
  3. If it does not operate normally as the result of inspection, perform a unit inspection of motor - actuator mode.
