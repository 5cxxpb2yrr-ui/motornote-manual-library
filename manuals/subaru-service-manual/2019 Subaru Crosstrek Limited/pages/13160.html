TPMS & Keyless Entry Control Module Or TPMS Control Module

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  • If the transmitter is replaced, ID registration for the transmitter is required. Refer to TIRE PRESSURE MONITOR (DIAGNOSTICS)>REGISTER TRANSMITTER (ID)>OPERATION .
  • When adjusting tire pressure indoors in winter, there is a big temperature difference between the indoor facilities and outside. Once the car is outside where the temperature is lower, the air pressure in the tires will drop, causing the tire pressure warning light to illuminate, even if the pressure in the tires was adjusted to standard values indoors. To avoid this, it is necessary to adjust the tire pressure to the high side in consideration of the difference in temperature between inside and outside according to the following table.
  • P205/55R16
    Temperature °C (°F) Indoor temperature 15.5 (60)
    Ambient air temperature -1 (30) -12 (10) -23 (-10)
    Air pressure kPa (psi) Front 250 (36) 265 (38) 280 (41)
    Rear 240 (35) 255 (37) 270 (39)
  • 205/50R17

    Rear 240 (35) 255 (37) 270 (39)
