Diagnostic Method

If the duration of time while the following conditions are met is longer than the time indicated, judge as NG.

JUDGMENT VALUE SPECIFICATION

Malfunction Criteria Threshold Value
|Engine oil temperature at engine start - Engine coolant temperature at engine start| More than 10°C (18°F)
|Engine oil temperature at engine start - Intake air temperature 21 sec. after engine start| More than value of Map

Map 

Ambient temperature (°C (°F)) -30 (-22) 30 (86) 45 (113) 60 (140)
|Engine oil temperature at engine start - Intake air temperature 21 sec. after engine start | (°C (°F)) 10 (18) 10 (18) 22 (39.6) 22 (39.6)

Time needed for diagnosis:  Less than 1 s

Malfunction indicator light illumination:  Illuminates when malfunction occurs in 2 continuous driving cycles.
