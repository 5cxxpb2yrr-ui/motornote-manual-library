Rear Door Panel

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY>GROUND TERMINAL REMOVAL OR INSTALLATION .
  2. Remove the trim panel - rear door. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>DOOR TRIM>REMOVAL>REAR DOOR .
  3. Remove the rear sealing cover. Refer to EXTERIOR BODY PANELS>SEALING COVER>REMOVAL>REAR SEALING COVER .
  4. Remove the cover B pillar - rear door INN and the cover C pillar - rear door INN.
    CAUTION:
    • Always replace with a new part, because engagement of the claw weakens.
    • Do not damage the sash or sash tape OUT while removing.
    • 4 door model
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • 5 door model
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the speaker assembly - rear. Refer to ENTERTAINMENT & MONITORING>REAR SPEAKER>REMOVAL .
  6. Remove the sash assembly - rear partition and the glass assembly - rear door. Refer to GLASS/WINDOWS/MIRRORS>REAR DOOR GLASS>REMOVAL .
  7. Remove the door sash tape, the cover B pillar - rear and the cover C pillar (5 door model). Refer to EXTERIOR BODY PANELS>DOOR SASH TAPE>REMOVAL .
  8. Remove the regulator and motor assembly - rear. Refer to GLASS/WINDOWS/MIRRORS>REAR REGULATOR AND MOTOR ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL .
  9. Remove the latch and actuator assembly - rear. Refer to SECURITY AND LOCKS>DOOR LATCH & DOOR LOCK ACTUATOR ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL>REAR .
  10. Remove the cover - handle rear outer. Refer to SECURITY AND LOCKS>DOOR OUTER HANDLE>REMOVAL>REAR .
  11. Remove the weather strip - rear door. Refer to EXTERIOR BODY PANELS>WEATHER STRIP>REPLACEMENT .
  12. Remove the bolts and nuts, and then detach the checker assembly - rear door.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  13. Disconnect the rear door harness connectors.
    1. Remove the grommet and pull out the rear door harness connector from inside the center pillar.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Disconnect the rear door harness connectors.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  14. Before removing the panel assembly - rear door, make the following preparations.
    CAUTION: Do not excessively lift the panel assembly - rear door with the floor jack. The door hinges may be damaged.
    1. Put a wooden block on a floor jack and place the jack under the panel assembly - rear door.
    2. Support the panel assembly - rear door with the jack to protect it from damage.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  15. Remove the door-side bolts for the upper hinge - rear door and the lower hinge - rear door to remove the panel assembly - rear door.
    CAUTION: The panel assembly - rear door is heavy. When removing and installing it, always work in a team of two or more persons.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  16. Remove the door hinge from vehicle. Refer to EXTERIOR BODY PANELS>REAR DOOR>REMOVAL>REAR DOOR HINGE .
