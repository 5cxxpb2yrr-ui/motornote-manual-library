Knee Airbag Module And Passenger's Lap Seat Belt Pretensioner

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. How to disconnect:
    1. Using a flat tip screwdriver, pry the push lock upward to unlock.
    2. Pull the connector to disconnect from the airbag module assembly.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. How to connect:
CAUTION:
  • Be sure to insert the connector in until it is locked.
  • Press in the push lock securely. Then pull it gently to make sure that it is locked.
Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.

Connect the connector in the reverse order of disconnecting. At this time, be sure to insert until a clicking sound is heard.
