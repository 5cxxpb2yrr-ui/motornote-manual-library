IMPREZA Model

CAUTION: When tightening the mounting TORX® screw, be sure to observe the tightening torque. Otherwise, the mount may be damaged in over-torque conditions.
  1. Slide the mirror assembly - inner rearview to the mirror mount to install.
NOTE: If the mirror mounting is damaged, replace the windshield glass.

Tightening torque: 

2 N.m (0.2 kgf-m, 1.5 ft-lb)
