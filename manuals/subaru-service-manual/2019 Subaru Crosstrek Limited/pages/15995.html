Inhibitor Switch: Removal

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery.

    Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .

  2. Release the shift lock using a screwdriver, etc. and shift the select lever to the "N" range.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the inhibitor cover.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Disconnect the select cable from the transmission main body.
    CAUTION: Do not apply excessive load while holding the part (a).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the spring pin (A) and remove the shifter arm (B).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Using a flat tip screwdriver or similar tool wrapped with plastic tape or similar item, raise the nut stopper (B) crimping and remove the lock nut (A) and the nut stopper (B).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Disconnect the connector and remove the inhibitor switch.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
