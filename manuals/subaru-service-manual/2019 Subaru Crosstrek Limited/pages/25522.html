Lever Assembly

IMPREZA MODEL 

  1. Remove the air intake duct. Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>AIR INTAKE DUCT>REMOVAL .
  2. Remove the cover UPR - front.
    1. Remove the clips, and remove the cover UPR - front.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the lever assembly - safety.
    1. Disconnect the cable - safety from the lock assembly - front hood.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Remove the clip of the cable - safety.
    3. Remove the bolts, and remove the lever assembly - safety.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.

CROSSTREK MODEL 

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery and wait for at least 60 seconds before starting work. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the air intake duct. Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>AIR INTAKE DUCT>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the cover UPR - front.
    1. Remove the clips, and remove the cover UPR - front.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the lever assembly - safety.
    1. Disconnect the connector and the clip.
    2. Disconnect the cable - safety from the lock assembly - front hood.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Remove the clip of the cable - safety.
    4. Remove the bolts, and remove the lever assembly - safety.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
