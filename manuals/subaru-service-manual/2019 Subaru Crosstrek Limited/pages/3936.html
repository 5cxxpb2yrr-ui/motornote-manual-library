Check Door Switch

  1. CHECK DATA MONITOR  .

    Display the following items using Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to BODY CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DATA MONITOR .

    • [Driver's door SW input]
    • [P-door SW input]
    • [Rear right door SW input]
    • [Rear left door SW input]
    • [R Gate SW input]

    Does the display switch between [OFF] ←→ [ON] when each door, trunk lid, or rear gate is opened/closed?

    Yes:  The door switches, trunk lid latch switch or rear gate latch switch are normal.

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK HARNESS  .
    1. Disconnect the connector of body integrated unit.
    2. Disconnect the connector of the door switch, trunk lid latch switch or rear gate latch switch that the display does not change.
    3. Check the harness between body integrated unit and defective switch.

      Connector & terminal 

      Front door LH 

      (i171) No. 39 - (R9) No. 2:

      Front door RH 

      (i171) No. 13 - (R12) No. 2:

      Rear door LH 

      (i171) No. 14 - (R22) No. 2:

      Rear door RH 

      (i171) No. 27 - (R16) No. 2:

      Trunk lid 

      (i171) No. 15 - (R186) No. 3:

      Rear gate 

      (i171) No. 15 - (D46) No. 3:

    Is harness normal?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Repair or replace the harness.

  3. CHECK HARNESS  .

    Measure the resistance between the faulty switch connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    Front door LH 

    (R9) No. 1 - Chassis ground:

    Front door RH 

    (R12) No. 1 - Chassis ground:

    Rear door LH 

    (R22) No. 1 - Chassis ground:

    Rear door RH 

    (R16) No. 1 - Chassis ground:

    Trunk lid 

    (R186) No. 2 - Chassis ground:

    Rear gate 

    (D46) No. 4 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance less than 10 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Repair or replace the harness.

  4. CHECK DOOR SWITCH  .

    Measure the resistance between faulty switch terminals.

    Terminal 

    Front LH door switch 

    No. 2 - No. 1:

    Front RH door switch 

    No. 2 - No. 1:

    Rear LH door switch 

    No. 2 - No. 1:

    Rear RH door switch 

    No. 2 - No. 1:

    Trunk lid latch switch 

    No. 3 - No. 2:

    Rear gate latch switch 

    No. 3 - No. 4:

    Is the resistance 1 MΩ or more when the door switch is pushed, or the trunk lid or rear gate is closed?

    Yes:  Replace the body integrated unit. Refer to SECURITY AND LOCKS>BODY INTEGRATED UNIT .

    No:  Replace the faulty parts.

    • Door switches
    • Trunk lid latch and actuator assembly
    • Rear gate latch and actuator assembly
