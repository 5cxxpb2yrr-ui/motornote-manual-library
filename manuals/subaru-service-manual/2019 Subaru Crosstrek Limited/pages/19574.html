DTC P0A36-15: Generator Temperature Sensor Circuit Short To Auxiliary Battery Or Open: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Outline of diagnosis: 

Open or +B power supply sneak path of Generator temperature sensor

(1 trip detection logic)

Normal driving (When shift lever is operated, switching to N position)
