Total Preload

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

Using a spring scale, check the total preload.

Specification: 

20.7 - 54.4 N (2.1 - 5.5 kgf, 4.7 - 12.2 lb)

If it is outside the standard value, adjust the side retainer shim.
