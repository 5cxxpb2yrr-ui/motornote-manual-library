Inhibitor Switch Signal Circuit

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery.

    Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .

  2. Remove the air intake boot.

    Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>AIR INTAKE BOOT>REMOVAL .

  3. Disconnect the inhibitor harness connector.
  4. Check the inhibitor switch signal circuit.
    1. Check the circuit of each range.
      NOTE: If faulty, check the inhibitor harness for open circuit or short circuit, or poor contact.
