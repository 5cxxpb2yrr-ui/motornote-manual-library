Inspection Mode: Procedure

  1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF for 5 seconds or more.
    • Start the engine. (Except for HEV model)
    • Start the hybrid system. (HEV model)
  3. Idle the engine for five seconds or more.
  4. Operate the steering wheel from side to side.
  5. Reproduce the malfunction occurrence condition as much as possible.
