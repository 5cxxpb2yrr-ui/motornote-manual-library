Wheel Alignment: Inspection: Camber

  1. Place the wheel on the turning radius gauge.
    NOTE: Make sure the ground contact surfaces of the front and rear wheels are at the same height.
  2. Set the adapter into the center of wheel, and then set the wheel alignment gauge.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Measure the camber angle in accordance with the operation manual for wheel alignment gauge.
    Model Front camber (difference between RH and LH 45' or less) Rear camber (difference between RH and LH 45' or less)
    Impreza model -0°30'±0°45' -1°20'±0°45'
    Crosstrek model 0°00'±0°45' -45'±0°45'
