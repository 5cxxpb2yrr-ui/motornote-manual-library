DTC P0C5A-16: Drive Motor "A" Position Sensor Circuit "B" Circuit Voltage Below Threshold: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Detecting condition: 

Detects that the Motor resolver cos-phase signal deviates from the proper value (large amplitude).

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

  • Greatly reduced output power
  • Driving in reverse not possible
  • A driving range is limited
  • Engine used while driving and engine speed becomes high
NOTE: For the diagnostic procedure, refer to DTC P0C5016. Refer to P-HEV DRIVE MOTOR (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE WITH DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>DTC P0C5016 DRIVE MOTOR "A" POSITION SENSOR CIRCUIT "A" CIRCUIT VOLTAGE BELOW THRESHOLD .
