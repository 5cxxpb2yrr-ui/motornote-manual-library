Antenna Feeder Cord

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Install the cord assembly - antenna feeder and the trim panel - roof assembly. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>ROOF TRIM>INSTALLATION .
  2. Install the floor mat. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>FLOOR MAT>INSTALLATION .
