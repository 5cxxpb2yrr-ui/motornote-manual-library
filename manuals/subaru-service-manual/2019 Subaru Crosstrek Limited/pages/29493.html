Inhibitor Switch: Installation

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Temporarily connect the inhibitor switch, and connect the connector.
  2. Align the projection on the nut stopper with the marking line (A) on the switch body, and install the lock nut.
    NOTE: Because there is some play (B) in the nut stopper projection, align the center of the play range with the marking line (A).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Tighten the nut stopper and lock nut.

    Tightening torque: 

    7 N.m (0.7 kgf-m, 5.2 ft-lb)

  4. Tighten the bolts holding the inhibitor switch.

    Tightening torque: 

    13 N.m (1.3 kgf-m, 9.6 ft-lb)

  5. Crimp the nut stopper using a flat tip screwdriver or similar tool wrapped with plastic tape or similar item.
  6. Install the shifter arm and fix with a new spring pin.
  7. Connect the select cable to the transmission main body.
    CAUTION: Do not apply excessive load while holding the part (a).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.

    Tightening torque: 

    25 N.m (2.5 kgf-m, 18.4 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Check the inhibitor switch position and adjust the N range position.
    • Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TH2A)>INHIBITOR SWITCH>INSPECTION>CHECK THE INHIBITOR SWITCH POSITION .
    • Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TH2A)>INHIBITOR SWITCH>ADJUSTMENT>N RANGE POSITION ADJUSTMENT .
  9. Shift the select lever to "P range".
  10. Connect the battery ground terminal.

    Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
