Overview Of Error

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

The charging voltage sensor located in Electric vehicle charger assembly detects the output voltage from the direct current high voltage circuit of Electric vehicle charger assembly, which is sent from Electric vehicle charger assembly to Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly. Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly compares the charging voltage (VCHG) and battery voltage (VB), and detects a malfunction when there is a difference between them.

The following are possible causes of the error.

  • Fault of voltage sensor (VCHG) in Electric vehicle charger assembly
    • Voltage sensor (VCHG) malfunction
  • Fault of voltage sensor (VB) in Battery ECU assembly
    • Voltage sensor (VB) malfunction
  • High voltage system fault
    • Malfunction of HV battery junction block (CHRG)
  • Low voltage system (12 V) fault
    • Malfunction of HV battery junction block (CHRG)
