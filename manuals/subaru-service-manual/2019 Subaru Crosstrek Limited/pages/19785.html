DTC P0B23-1C: Hybrid/EV Battery "A" Voltage Sensor Voltage Out Of Range: Notes

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

1. - 

Outline of diagnosis: 

Battery ECU assembly failure

With boosting not in progress, difference between battery voltage (VB) and voltage before boosting (VL) is large, and, at the same time, difference between battery voltage (VB) and voltage after boosting (VH) is large

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

  • Reduced output power
  • Engine not stopped
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
NOTE:
  • When a check operation is performed in N position for a long period of time, DTC P300000 for [P-HEV High Voltage Battery] may be output.
  • When Battery ECU assembly is replaced, each voltage must be checked after replacement. With HV battery voltage at 208 V or more, READY ON, P position and also Engine always stopped, [HV Battery Total Voltage], [VL-Voltage before Boosting] and [VH-Voltage after Boosting] of the data monitor must be checked. Three voltages of [HV Battery Total Voltage], [VL-Voltage before Boosting] and [VH-Voltage after Boosting] at normal condition appear approximately the same. When deviation of each voltage is as follows, it is judged as faulty.
Inspection voltage Malfunction voltage
Deviation between [HV Battery Total Voltage] and [VL-Voltage before Boosting] 33 V or more
Deviation between [HV Battery Total Voltage] and [VH-Voltage after Boosting] 54 V or more
Deviation between [VL-Voltage before Boosting] and [VH-Voltage after Boosting] 76 V or more
