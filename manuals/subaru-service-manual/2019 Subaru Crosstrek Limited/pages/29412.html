Water Pipe LH

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Remove the striker - front hood.

    Refer to EXTERIOR BODY PANELS>FRONT HOOD>DISASSEMBLY .

  2. Fully open the front hood.

    Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>FRONT HOOD STAY .

  3. Collect the refrigerant.

    Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>REFRIGERANT RECOVERY PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .

  4. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery.

    Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .

  5. Drain engine coolant. (Engine side)

    Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>ENGINE COOLANT>REPLACEMENT>DRAINING OF ENGINE COOLANT>ENGINE SIDE .

  6. Remove the collector cover.

    Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>COLLECTOR COVER>REMOVAL .

  7. Remove the air intake duct.

    Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>AIR INTAKE DUCT>REMOVAL .

  8. Remove the clip from the air intake boot, and loosen the clamp.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Remove the air intake boot, and place it aside so that it does not interfere with the work.
    CAUTION: Be careful not to pull out the PCV hose.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  10. Disconnect the preheater hose (A) and the heater outlet hose (B) from the water pipe LH.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  11. Remove the pitching stopper.

    Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TH2A)>TRANSMISSION MOUNTING SYSTEM>REMOVAL>PITCHING STOPPER .

  12. Remove the bolt securing the transmission bracket.
    NOTE: This procedure is required to prevent the transmission bracket and earth cord from being damaged during engine removal/installation.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  13. Remove the A/C accumulator assembly.

    Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>ACCUMULATOR>REMOVAL .

  14. Remove the front exhaust pipe.

    Refer to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FRONT EXHAUST PIPE>REMOVAL .

  15. Remove the nuts which secure the engine mounting to the front crossmember.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  16. Lift up the engine with a lifting device and wire ropes.
    CAUTION: When lifting up the engine, pay attention to the clearance of each part and be careful not to lift the engine too much, in order to prevent damaging the vehicle.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  17. Remove the water pipe LH.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
