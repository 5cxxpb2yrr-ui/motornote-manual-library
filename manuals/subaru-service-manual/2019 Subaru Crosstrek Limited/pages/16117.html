Inverter Water Pump: Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery.

    Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .

  2. Drain engine coolant. (Inverter assembly side)

    Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>ENGINE COOLANT>REPLACEMENT>DRAINING OF ENGINE COOLANT>INVERTER ASSEMBLY SIDE .

  3. Remove the screw (A) and clips, and turn over the mud guard - front RH.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the clamp (A), and disconnect the inverter water pump inlet hose from the inverter water pump.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Disconnect the quick connector from the inverter water pipe.
    NOTE:
    • Disconnect the quick connector as shown in the figure.
    • Be careful not to pull the slider too much.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Disconnect the connector (A) and remove the inverter water pump.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. When the water pipe and inverter water pump inlet hose are replaced
    1. Remove the water pipe (A) from the clamp.
    2. Disconnect the water pipe (A) and inverter water pump inlet hose (B) as a single unit from the inverter tank outlet hose.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Disconnect the inverter water pump inlet hose from the water pipe.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Remove the bracket.
    NOTE: Remove if necessary.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
