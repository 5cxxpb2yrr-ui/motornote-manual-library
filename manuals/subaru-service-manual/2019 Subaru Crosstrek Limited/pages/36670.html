Only Weather Screen Is Not Displayed

Detecting condition: 

Communication circuit, audio or navigation, or MFD is abnormal.

Wiring diagram: 

  • Multi-function display (MFD) system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (IMPREZA MODEL)>MULTI-FUNCTION DISPLAY (MFD) SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>MULTI-FUNCTION DISPLAY (MFD) SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>MULTI-FUNCTION DISPLAY (MFD) SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .
  • CAN communication system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (IMPREZA MODEL)>CAN COMMUNICATION SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>CAN COMMUNICATION SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>CAN COMMUNICATION SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to MULTI-FUNCTION DISPLAY (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK SUBSCRIPTION STATUS AND RECEIVING AREA. 

    Check user's XM subscription status, and also areas where displays were not available.

    Is there problem in XM subscription status and the areas?

    Yes:  System is normal. Displays are available only in subscription status and receiving areas.

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK HARNESS BETWEEN MFD AND HEAD UNIT. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the MFD connector.
    3. Disconnect the navigation unit or audio connector.
    4. Using a tester, measure the resistance of harness between MFD connector and navigation unit or audio connector.

      Connector & terminal 

      (i122) No. 19 - (i87) No. 9:

      (i122) No. 20 - (i87) No. 10:

    Is the resistance less than 10 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Repair or replace the open circuit of harness.

  3. CHECK HEAD UNIT. 

    Read the DTC of [Infotainment] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to INFOTAINMENT (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Are any DTCs displayed? (Current malfunction)

    Yes:  Perform the diagnosis according to displayed DTC. Refer to INFOTAINMENT (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    No:  Replace the MFD. Refer to INSTRUMENTATION/DRIVER INFO>MULTI-FUNCTION DISPLAY (MFD) .
