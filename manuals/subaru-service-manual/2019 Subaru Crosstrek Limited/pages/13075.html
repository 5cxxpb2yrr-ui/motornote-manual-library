Stabilizer Link

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Before installation, inspect the following items and replace any faulty part with a new one. Check the stabilizer link for damage.
  2. Install the stabilizer link.

    Tightening torque: 

    38 N.m (3.9 kgf-m, 28 ft-lb)

  3. Install the rear wheels and lower the vehicle.

    Tightening torque: 

    120 N.m (12.2 kgf-m, 88.5 ft-lb)
