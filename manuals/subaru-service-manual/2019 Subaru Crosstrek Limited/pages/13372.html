Transmission Control Device: Installation

  1. Install the parking support.

    Tightening torque: 

    25 N.m (2.5 kgf-m, 18.4 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Install the extension case. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>EXTENSION CASE>INSTALLATION .
  3. Install the manual valve.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Install the parking rod to the manual plate.
  5. Insert the parking rod into the transmission case, and install the shift connecting rod of the manual valve to the manual plate.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Insert the shifter arm shaft to the transmission assembly, install the manual plate to the shifter arm shaft, and secure the shifter arm shaft with a bolt.
    NOTE: Do not damage the lip of oil seal press-fitted in the case.

    Tightening torque: 

    7 N.m (0.7 kgf-m, 5.2 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Install the detent spring.

    Tightening torque: 

    7 N.m (0.7 kgf-m, 5.2 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Install the spring pin.
    NOTE: Use new spring pin.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Install the inhibitor switch. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>INHIBITOR SWITCH>INSTALLATION .
  10. Adjust the inhibitor switch. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>INHIBITOR SWITCH>ADJUSTMENT .
  11. Install the oil strainer and oil pan. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>OIL PAN AND STRAINER>INSTALLATION .
  12. Install the transmission assembly to the vehicle. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY>INSTALLATION .
