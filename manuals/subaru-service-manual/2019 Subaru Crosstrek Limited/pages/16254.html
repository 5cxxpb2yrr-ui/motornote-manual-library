Inverter Assembly (With Converter) Cover

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Install the inverter assembly (with converter) cover No. 1 (C).

    Tightening torque: 

    18 N.m (1.8 kgf-m, 13.3 ft-lb)

  2. Insert the inverter assembly (with converter) cover No. 2 (D) into the inlet port (A) of the floor under protector LH, and then install the bolt and clip (B).

    Tightening torque: 

    18 N.m (1.8 kgf-m, 13.3 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Lower the vehicle.
