Outline Of Diagnosis

When the voltage at brake light SW input is outside the range of that when stop light switch is on or the range of that when stop light switch is off for a certain amount of time, electric brake booster control module judges that there is an open in stop light switch circuit and illuminates the MIL and stores a DTC.
