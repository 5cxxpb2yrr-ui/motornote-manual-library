Inspection Of Switch Unit

  1. Operate the switches to check the continuity between terminals.

    Preparation tool: 

    Circuit tester

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • Front
      Switch position Terminal No. Standard
      OFF B3 - B1 Less than 1 Ω
      INT B3 - B1 Less than 1 Ω
      LO B2 - B3 Less than 1 Ω
      HI B2 - B4 Less than 1 Ω
      Washer ON A3 - A2 Less than 1 Ω
    • Rear
    Switch position Terminal No. Standard
    Washer ON (wiper interlock operation) A7 - A2
    B5 - A2    		 Less than 1 Ω
    OFF A7 - A2
    A6 - A2
    B5 - A2    		 1 MΩ or more
    LO A6 - A2 Less than 1 Ω
    HI A7 - A2 Less than 1 Ω
    Washer ON B5 - A2 Less than 1 Ω
  2. Replace the switch if the inspection result is not within the standard value.
  3. Install the switch assembly - combination wiper select. Refer to WIPER AND WASHER SYSTEMS>COMBINATION SWITCH (WIPER)>INSTALLATION .
