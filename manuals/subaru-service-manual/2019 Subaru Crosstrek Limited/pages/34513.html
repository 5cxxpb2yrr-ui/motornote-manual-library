Water Tank Pipe Assembly

CAUTION: If the engine coolant is spilt over the engine or the exhaust pipe, completely wipe it off to avoid emission of smoke or causing a fire.
  1. Release the fuel pressure. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FUEL>PROCEDURE>RELEASING OF FUEL PRESSURE .
  2. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  3. Drain engine coolant. Refer to COOLING (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE COOLANT>REPLACEMENT>DRAINING OF ENGINE COOLANT .
  4. Remove the generator. Refer to STARTING/CHARGING SYSTEMS (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>GENERATOR>REMOVAL .
  5. Remove the intake manifold assembly. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>INTAKE MANIFOLD ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL .
  6. Remove the high-pressure fuel delivery pipe assembly. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>HIGH PRESSURE FUEL DELIVERY PIPE>REMOVAL>HIGH-PRESSURE FUEL DELIVERY PIPE ASSEMBLY .
  7. Disconnect the connector from the engine coolant temperature sensor, and remove the engine wiring harness from the water tank pipe.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Disconnect each hose shown in the figure.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Remove the bolts securing the water tank pipe assembly, and disconnect the water hose (front side) to remove the water tank pipe assembly.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  10. Remove the filler neck from the water tank pipe assembly. Refer to COOLING (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>WATER PIPE>REMOVAL>FILLER NECK .
