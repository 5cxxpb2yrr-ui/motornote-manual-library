Intake Manifold Assembly: Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. If the fuel delivery tube is disconnected after removing the intake manifold assembly, perform the following work.
    1. Release the fuel pressure. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL>PROCEDURE>RELEASING OF FUEL PRESSURE .
    2. Open the fuel filler lid and remove the fuel filler cap.
      NOTE: This operation is required to release the inner pressure of the fuel tank.
  2. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  3. Remove the clip from the air intake boot, and loosen the clamp.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the air intake boot, and place it aside so that it does not interfere with the work.
    CAUTION: Be careful not to pull out the PCV hose.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the collector cover. Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>COLLECTOR COVER>REMOVAL .
  6. Remove the PCV hose No. 1. Refer to EMISSION CONTROL (AUX. EMISSION CONTROL DEVICES) (H4DO (HEV))>PCV HOSE>REMOVAL>PCV HOSE NO. 1 .
  7. When the engine coolant (engine side) is not drained before removing the intake manifold assembly
    1. Disconnect the connector (A).
    2. Remove the bolts securing the throttle body, and move the throttle body aside so that it does not interfere with the work.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Remove the EGR control valve. Refer to EMISSION CONTROL (AUX. EMISSION CONTROL DEVICES) (H4DO (HEV))>EGR CONTROL VALVE>REMOVAL .
      NOTE: The work can be performed without disconnecting the preheater hose.
    4. Remove the preheater hose.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. When the engine coolant (engine side) is drained before removing the intake manifold assembly
    1. Disconnect the connector (A), and disconnect the preheater hose (B).
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Remove the EGR control valve. Refer to EMISSION CONTROL (AUX. EMISSION CONTROL DEVICES) (H4DO (HEV))>EGR CONTROL VALVE>REMOVAL .
  9. Disconnect the connector (A).
  10. Disconnect the connector (B) and remove the clip (C).
  11. Disconnect the purge hose (D).
  12. Remove the bolt securing the fuel pipe.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  13. Disconnect the connector (A).
  14. Remove the fuel delivery tube clamp (B).
  15. Remove the clip.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  16. Disconnect connector (A) from ECM. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>ENGINE CONTROL MODULE (ECM)>REMOVAL .
  17. Disconnect the connector (B) from the main fuse box, and remove the engine wiring harness from the clip (C).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  18. Disconnect the positive terminal of battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  19. Release the claw securing the engine wiring harness to the bracket, and place it aside so that it does not interfere with work.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  20. Remove the clip (A) securing the air conditioner wire.
  21. Remove the fuel pipe protector No. 1.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  22. Disconnect the connector (A) and remove the clips.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  23. Remove the fuel delivery tube.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  24. Remove the intake manifold assembly.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  25. Remove the cylinder head plate.
    NOTE: Perform this procedure when required.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  26. Disconnect the fuel delivery pipe. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>REMOVAL>QUICK CONNECTOR DISCONNECTION (TYPE C) .
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  27. Disconnect the fuel delivery pipe (A). Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>REMOVAL>QUICK CONNECTOR DISCONNECTION (TYPE B) .
  28. Disconnect the fuel delivery tube (B). Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>REMOVAL>QUICK CONNECTOR DISCONNECTION (TYPE A) .
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
