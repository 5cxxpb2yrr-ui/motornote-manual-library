Combination Switch (Wiper): Removal

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the cover assembly - column. Refer to POWER ASSISTED SYSTEM (POWER STEERING)>STEERING COLUMN>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the switch assembly - combination wiper select.
    1. Disconnect the connector.
    2. Press the claws (a), and pull out the switch assembly - combination wiper select.
    CAUTION: Do not press the claws with excessive force. They may be damaged.
