DTC B14A3: Refrigerant Pressure Sensor Circuit Open

DTC detecting condition: 

Refrigerant pressure sensor circuit is open.

Trouble symptom: 

Compressor does not operate. (Variable compressor)

Wiring diagram: 

Air conditioning system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (IMPREZA MODEL)>AIR CONDITIONING SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>AIR CONDITIONING SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK CONNECTOR. 
    1. Check connecting condition of the A/C control panel connector, refrigerant pressure sensor connector and coupling connector.
    2. Read the DTC of [Air Conditioner] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is DTC B14A3 displayed? (Current malfunction)

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Even if DTC is displayed, the circuit has returned to a normal condition at this time.

    Reproduce the failure, and then perform the diagnosis again.

    NOTE: In this case, temporary poor contact of connector, temporary open or short circuit of harness may be the cause.
  2. CHECK PRESSURE SENSOR. 
    1. Disconnect the refrigerant pressure sensor.
    2. Short No. 1 and No. 2 of connector B505.
    3. Read the DTC of [Air Conditioner] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is DTC B14A4 displayed? (Current malfunction)

    Yes:  Replace the refrigerant pressure sensor. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>PRESSURE SENSOR .

    No:  Go to  3.

  3. CHECK HARNESS. 
    1. Restore the temporary shorted lines made in step 2.
    2. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    3. Using a tester, measure the voltage between terminals in refrigerant pressure sensor connector.

      Connector & terminal 

      (B505) No. 3 (+) - (B505) No. 1 (-):

    Is the voltage 4.5 - 5.0 V?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Repair or replace the open circuit of harness.

  4. CHECK HARNESS (OPEN CIRCUIT). 
    1. Disconnect the connector from the A/C control panel.
    2. Using a tester, check the continuity between A/C control panel connector and refrigerant pressure sensor connector terminals.

      Connector & terminal 

      (B505) No. 1 - (i88) No. 30:

      (B505) No. 2 - (i88) No. 31:

      (B505) No. 3 - (i88) No. 16:

    Is there continuity?

    Yes:  Replace the A/C control panel. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>CONTROL PANEL .

    No:  Repair or replace the open circuit of harness.
