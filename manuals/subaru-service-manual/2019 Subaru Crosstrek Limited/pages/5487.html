Brake Stroke Sensor

  1. Loosen the bolt.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Slowly rotate the brake stroke sensor so that the output value is within the standard.

    Specification: 

    1±0.2 V

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Tighten the bolt.
    CAUTION: After turning ignition ON, do not depress the brake pedal until after tightening the lock nut.

    Tightening torque: 

    8 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.9 ft-lb)
