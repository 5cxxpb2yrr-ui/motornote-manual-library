INTRODUCTION

This bulletin announces a design change made to the front seat backrest frame assemblies in response to a limited number of customer reports of a squeaking sound heard when leaning back in the front seats. Improvements to the nylon bushing design for the backrest springs prevents them from coming out of the frame and eliminates the metal-to-metal contact between the springs and frame which can cause the squeaking sound. Should the bushing become dislodged, there is no impact on the spring retention or function of the backrest.

VERY IMPORTANT:  Always duplicate the sound as described by the customer and confirm it is coming from the backrest before proceeding to ensure a quality repair.
