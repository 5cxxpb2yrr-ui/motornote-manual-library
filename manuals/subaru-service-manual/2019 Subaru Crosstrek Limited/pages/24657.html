Diagnostic Method

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Judge as NG when the status continues where the number of camshaft position sensor signals in two crankshaft revolutions is other than 3time(s).

JUDGMENT VALUE SPECIFICATION

Malfunction Criteria Threshold Value
Amount of camshaft sensor signal during 2 revs of crankshaft Not equal to 3time(s)

Time needed for diagnosis:  8 engine revs.

Malfunction indicator light illumination:  Illuminates as soon as a malfunction occurs.
