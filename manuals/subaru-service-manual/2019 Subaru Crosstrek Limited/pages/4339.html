Rear Door

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the trim panel - rear door.
    1. Attach the protective tape (a).
    2. Remove the cover of the inner handle, and remove the screw.
    3. Open the hand cup cover and remove the screw.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    4. Remove the clips, and remove the trim panel - rear door from the panel assembly - rear door.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    5. Remove the cable from the remote assembly - door.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    6. Disconnect the harness connector and remove the trim panel - rear door.
  3. Remove each part as required.
    • Remote assembly - door: Refer to SECURITY AND LOCKS>DOOR INNER REMOTE>REMOVAL>REAR .
    • Switch - power window sub: Refer to GLASS/WINDOWS/MIRRORS>POWER WINDOW CONTROL SWITCH>REMOVAL>SUB-SWITCH .
    • Weather strip - door INN: Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>DOOR TRIM>DISASSEMBLY .
