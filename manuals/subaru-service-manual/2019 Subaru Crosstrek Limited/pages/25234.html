Cruise Control Is Not Released After Brake Pedal Has Been Depressed

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. PERFORM REAL TIME DIAGNOSIS. 

    Check the input signal of cruise control system. Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>REAL-TIME DIAGNOSIS .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  The circuit has returned to a normal condition at this time.

    NOTE: In this case, temporary poor contact of connector, temporary open or short circuit of harness may be the cause.

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. INSPECTION OF STOP LIGHT SWITCH AND BRAKE SWITCH. 

    Check stop light switch and brake switch. Refer to BRAKE>BRAKE PEDAL . Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON INSPECTION>STOP LIGHT SWITCH OR BRAKE SWITCH IS TURNED TO ON, AND THEN THE CRUISE CONTROL IS RELEASED .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Replace the ECM. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>ENGINE CONTROL MODULE (ECM)>REMOVAL .

    No:  Repair or replace the faulty portion according to the inspection result.
