Overview Of Error

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

The Charge control ECU installed inside the Electric vehicle charger assembly uses an IAC sensor to monitor the input AC current, and outputs a DTC when an overcurrent error is detected. If this DTC was output, perform a charging check suing an AC power supply that was confirmed to operate normally. If the trouble occurred again, replace the Electric vehicle charger assembly.

The following are possible causes of the error.

  • Malfunction on the vehicle side
    • Malfunction of the Electric vehicle charger assembly
  • Malfunction not on the vehicle
    • Malfunction of the AC power supply
