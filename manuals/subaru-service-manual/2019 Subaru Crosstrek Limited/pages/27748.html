Malfunction Description

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Motor generator control ECU detects a malfunction when over voltage abnormality occurs in the boosting converter. The following cases are assumed to be causes of this symptom.

  • Malfunction of Inside of inverter
    • Inverter with converter assembly inner circuit malfunction
    • Malfunction of unit that controls inverter
