Fuel Injector RH

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

CAUTION:
  • Be careful not to spill fuel.
  • Catch the fuel from the pipes using a container or cloth.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the clip from the air intake boot, and loosen the clamp.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the air intake boot, and place it aside so that it does not interfere with the work.
    CAUTION: Be careful not to pull out the PCV hose.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the high-pressure fuel delivery pipe assembly. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>HIGH PRESSURE FUEL DELIVERY PIPE>REMOVAL>HIGH-PRESSURE FUEL DELIVERY PIPE ASSEMBLY .
  5. Disconnect the connector (A) from the fuel pressure sensor, and disconnect the connector (B) from the fuel injector.
  6. Remove the bolts securing the fuel injector pipe RH, and remove the fuel injector and the fuel injector pipe RH as an assembly.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Remove the fuel injectors from the fuel injector pipe RH.
Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
