Charging Inlet

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Remove the charging inlet lock assembly.
    1. Disconnect the connector.
    2. Remove the pin and remove the charging inlet lock assembly.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Remove the AC charger inlet cable.
    1. Remove the cable clips.
    2. Disengage claw (a) and flange (b), and remove the AC charger inlet cable.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
