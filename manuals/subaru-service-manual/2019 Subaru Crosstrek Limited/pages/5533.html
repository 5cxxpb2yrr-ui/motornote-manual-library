Electronic Parking Brake Model

  1. Set the wheel stoppers to tires.
  2. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY>GROUND TERMINAL REMOVAL OR INSTALLATION .
    NOTE: Release the shift lock and shift the select lever to the "N" position. Refer to CONTROL SYSTEMS>SELECT LEVER>REMOVAL .
  3. Release the clips, and detach the cover assembly - front.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the screws to remove the switch assembly - electronic parking brake.
