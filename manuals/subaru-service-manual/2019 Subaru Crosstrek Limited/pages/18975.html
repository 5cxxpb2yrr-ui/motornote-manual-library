DTC P31B3-00: Hybrid/EV Battery Voltage High: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

1. - 

Outline of diagnosis: 

Inverter voltage and charging voltage are specified values or more

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

  • Reduced output power
  • Engine not stopped

Wiring diagram: 

Hybrid system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

    • P-HEV Drive Motor

    When DTCs related to [P-HEV Drive Motor], mentioned above, are simultaneously displayed, go to  C.

    Yes: 

    1. Go to  2.
    2. Perform the diagnosis for the simultaneously displayed DTCs.
      NOTE: If more than one DTC is simultaneously displayed, perform the diagnosis in the order described in the above.
    3. Perform the diagnosis for the simultaneously displayed DTCs.
    NOTE: If more than one DTC is simultaneously displayed, perform the diagnosis in the order described in the above.

    No:  -

  2. CHECK HV battery  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Turn to IG ON.
    2. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, display the following items in the [Data monitor] of [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control], and record them. Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DATA MONITOR .
      • Hybrid Battery Voltage
    3. Turn the ignition to OFF.
    4. Remove the Service plug.
    5. Disconnect connectors (HV43) and (HV46) from HV battery junction block.
      CAUTION: Be sure to insulate the disconnected high voltage connector by wrapping it with insulating tape. To do this, wrap the insulating tape from Wire harness along to the connector.
    6. Using a tester, measure the voltage between HV battery junction block connector and HV battery junction block connector.
      CAUTION: Do not cross the electrode part of measurement terminals of a tester.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV43) No. 1 (+) - (HV46) No. 1 (-):

    Is the difference between [Hybrid Battery Voltage] of [Data monitor] and the measured value 5 V or more?

    Yes:  Replace HV battery. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY .

    No:  Go to  3.

  3. CHECK FREEZE FRAME DATA (HV)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Restore the vehicle to its original state.
    2. Turn to IG ON.
    3. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, display the following items in the freeze frame data of [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control]. Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>FREEZE FRAME DATA .
      • Charging Voltage for Hybrid Battery
      • Hybrid Battery Voltage

    If [Charging Voltage for Hybrid Battery] is 437 V or more, and [Hybrid Battery Voltage] is less than 388 V, go to  A.

    If [Charging Voltage for Hybrid Battery] is less than 437 V, and [Hybrid Battery Voltage] is less than 388 V, go to  B.

    For the cases other than above, go to  C.

    Yes: 

    1. Replace Electric vehicle charger assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>ELECTRIC VEHICLE CHARGER .
    2. Replace Inverter with converter assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>INVERTER AND CONVERTER ASSEMBLY .
    3. Replace HV battery. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY .
