Explanation For Circuits

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Motor generator control ECU located in Inverter with converter assembly detects the generator inverter temperature with the generator inverter temperature sensor, which is built in Inverter with converter assembly, and also controls to prevent overheat of the generator inverter. Motor generator control ECU detects sensor property malfunction based on the temperature sensor value sent from the generator inverter temperature sensor.
