Overview Of Error

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

If overvoltage occurs at the VCHG capacitor due to Electric vehicle charger assembly internal or external malfunction, the charger stops for withstand voltage protection and a DTC is output. The following are possible causes of the error.

Low voltage system (12V) malfunction

  • Charge relay drive command circuit malfunction (between Battery ECU assembly - HV battery junction block assembly (CHRB, CHRG))

High voltage system malfunction

  • Interruption of charging path
    • Malfunction in high voltage circuit inside Electric vehicle charger assembly (open circuit or instantaneous power failure)
    • High voltage circuit between Electric vehicle charger assembly - HV battery junction block assembly (open circuit or instantaneous power failure)
  • Malfunction of the HV battery junction block (CHRB, CHRG)
    • HV battery junction block assembly (CHRB, CHRG) (open circuit or instantaneous power failure)
    • High voltage circuit between HV battery - HV battery junction block assembly (CHRB, CHRG) (open circuit or instantaneous power failure)
