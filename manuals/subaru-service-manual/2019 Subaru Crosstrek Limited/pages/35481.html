DTC C0597: Brake System Control Module "A" Performance: Notes

1. - 

DTC detecting condition: 

When internal error is detected through the results from ECM self-diagnostic of EBB Control Module.

  • DTC C0597:

    Diagnosis result at initial check

  • DTC C1C40:

Diagnostic result during driving

Trouble symptom: 

  • Brake cannot be controlled.
  • Regenerative brake cannot be controlled.
  • Brake assist does not operate.
  • EyeSight functions (ACC/PCS) do not operate.

Wiring diagram: 

Electronic controlled brake system Ref. to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>ELECTRIC CONTROL BRAKE SYSTEM .

  1. CHECK DTC. 
    1. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, perform the clear memory. Ref. to COMMON (DIAG)>CLEAR MEMORY .
    2. Read the DTC using Subaru Select Monitor. Ref. to ELECTRIC BRAKE BOOSTER (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is DTC of brake accumulator assembly system error displayed?

    Yes:  Perform the diagnosis according to DTC. Ref. to ELECTRIC BRAKE BOOSTER (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>LIST .

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK FREEZE FRAME DATA. 

    Using the Subaru Select Monitor, check the detail codes of the freeze frame data when DTCs C0597 and C1C40 are detected. Ref. to ELECTRIC BRAKE BOOSTER (DIAGNOSTICS)>FREEZE FRAME DATA .

    If detail codes: 1028 - 1044 or 1047 - 1049 are displayed, go to  A.

    If detail code: 1045 is displayed, go to  B.

    Yes: 

    1. Replace the brake booster assembly (with the master cylinder). Ref. to BRAKE>BRAKE BOOSTER .
    2. Go to  3.

    No:  -

  3. CHECK CONNECTOR. 

    Check the connecting condition of the brake accumulator assembly connector.

    Is there looseness, deformation, or corrosion at the connectors?

    Yes:  Repair or replace the connector.

    No:  Go to  4.

  4. CHECK BRAKE ACCUMULATOR ASSEMBLY CONNECTOR. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the brake accumulator assembly connector.
    3. Measure the resistance between terminals of brake accumulator assembly connector.

    Terminal 

    No. 1 -No. 3: (Resistance: 10 Ω or less)

    No. 2 -No. 4: (Resistance: 10 Ω or less)

    No. 1 -No. 2: (Resistance: Less than 1 Ω)

    No. 3 -No. 4: (Resistance: Less than 1 Ω)

    Does the resistance indicate the standard value?

    Yes:  Go to  5.

    No:  Replace the brake accumulator assembly connector. Ref. to BRAKE>BRAKE ACCUMULATOR ASSY .

  5. CHECK HARNESS. 

    Measure the resistance between brake accumulator assembly connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (B653) No. 3 - Chassis ground:

    (B653) No. 4 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  6.

    No:  Repair or replace the harness.

  6. CHECK EBB Control Module. 
    1. Disconnect the EBB Control Module connector.
    2. Measure the resistance between EBB Control Module connector and brake accumulator assembly connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (AD82) No. 1 - (B653) No. 2: (Resistance: Less than 1 Ω)

    (AD82) No. 3 - (B653) No. 1: (Resistance: Less than 1 Ω)

    (AD82) No. 1 - Chassis ground: (Resistance: 10 kΩ or more)

    (AD82) No. 3 - Chassis ground: (Resistance: 10 kΩ or more)

    Does the resistance indicate the standard value?

    Yes:  Replace the brake booster assembly (with the master cylinder). Ref. to BRAKE>BRAKE BOOSTER .

    No:  Repair or replace the harness.
