PCV Connector: Installation

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. By using new O-rings, install the PCV connector, and connect water hose and PCV hose No. 2 to the PCV connector.

    Tightening torque: 

    6.4 N.m (0.7 kgf-m, 4.7 ft-lb)

  2. Install the A/C compressor. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>COMPRESSOR>INSTALLATION .
  3. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  4. Fill engine coolant. Refer to COOLING (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE COOLANT>REPLACEMENT>FILLING OF ENGINE COOLANT .
