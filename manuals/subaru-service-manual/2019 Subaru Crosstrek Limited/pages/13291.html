Inhibitor Switch: Adjustment

  1. Shift the select lever to "N" range.
  2. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>SAFETY .
  3. Loosen the two bolts holding the inhibitor switch.
  4. Insert the ST vertically into the holes of the shifter arm and switch body.

    Preparation tool: 

    ST: STOPPER PIN (499267300)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Tighten the two bolts holding the inhibitor switch.

    Tightening torque: 

    5 N.m (0.5 kgf-m, 3.7 ft-lb)

  6. Repeat the inspection of the inhibitor switch. If the inhibitor switch is determined to be "faulty", replace it.
