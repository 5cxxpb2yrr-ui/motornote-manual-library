Diagnostic Procedure For Subaru Select Monitor Communication: Inspection: Immobilizer

Detecting condition: 

  • Defective harness
  • Power supply circuit malfunction
  • Defective immobilizer control module
  • Body integrated unit malfunctioning
  • Defective CAN communication circuit
  • Defective Subaru Select Monitor

Trouble symptom: 

Communication with [Immobilizer] is faulty.

Wiring diagram: 

Immobilizer system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (IMPREZA MODEL)>IMMOBILIZER SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>IMMOBILIZER SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  1. CHECK FUSE. 

    Check the fuse.

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Replace the fuse. If the replaced fuse blows out easily, repair the ground short circuit in the harness between the battery - immobilizer control module.

  2. CHECK LAN SYSTEM. 

    Inspect LAN system. Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Perform the inspection according to the diagnosis for LAN system.

  3. CHECK DATA MONITOR. 

    Using the Subaru Select Monitor, check the following items in the [Data monitor] of [Immobilizer]. Refer to IMMOBILIZER (DIAGNOSTICS)>DATA MONITOR .

    • BATT voltage

      Is the display 10 V or more?

    Yes:  Replace the immobilizer control module. Refer to SECURITY AND LOCKS>IMMOBILIZER CONTROL MODULE .

    No:  Go to  4.

  4. CHECK HARNESS. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the immobilizer control module connector.
    3. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    4. Using a tester, measure the voltage between the immobilizer control module connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (i34) No. 2 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

      (i34) No. 1 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

      Is the voltage 10 - 13 V?

      Yes:  Go to  5.

      No:  Repair the open circuit of harness between the immobilizer control module connector and fuse.

  5. CHECK HARNESS. 

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between the immobilizer control module connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (i34) No. 5 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  6.

    No:  Repair the open circuit of harness between the immobilizer control module connector and chassis ground.

  6. CHECK IMMOBILIZER control module. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Connect the immobilizer control module connector.
    3. Perform communication with the immobilizer control module.

      Is communication possible?

      Yes:  The circuit has returned to a normal condition at this time. Reproduce the failure, and then perform the diagnosis again.

      NOTE: In this case, temporary poor contact of connector, temporary open or short circuit of harness may be the cause.

      No:  Replace the immobilizer control module. Refer to SECURITY AND LOCKS>IMMOBILIZER CONTROL MODULE .
