Control Valve Body: Removal

CAUTION:
  • Directly after the vehicle has been running or the engine has been idling for a long time, the CVTF is hot. Be careful not to burn yourself.
  • Be careful not to spill CVTF on the exhaust pipe to prevent it from emitting smoke or causing a fire. If the CVTF adheres, wipe it off completely.
  • Always clean the engine compartment before removal.
NOTE: The control valve body is replaced as an assembly only, because it is a non-disassembly part.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the clip from the air intake boot, and loosen the clamp.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the air intake boot, and place it aside so that it does not interfere with the work.
    NOTE: Be careful not to pull out the PCV hose.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the transmission radio ground terminal.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Disconnect the transmission harness connector.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Remove the air breather hose together with the bracket.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Remove the transmission radio ground cord.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Remove the pitching stopper.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Remove the pitching stopper bracket.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  10. Remove the transmission case cover (small).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  11. Remove the TCM. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>TRANSMISSION CONTROL MODULE (TCM)>REMOVAL .
  12. Remove the transmission harness clip and ground terminal.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  13. Remove the throttle body, and move it aside so that it will not interfere with the removal of the control valve. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>THROTTLE BODY>REMOVAL .
    NOTE: Do not remove the preheater hose.
  14. Clean the transmission exterior.
  15. Fix the ST with tape, and set the ST to the vehicle.
    NOTE: When replacing the control valve body, the sheet is included in the control valve body for repairs.

    Preparation tool: 

    ST: SHEET SPECIAL TOOL (18761AA010)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  16. Remove the valve cover and gasket.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  17. Remove the transmission harness from the valve cover.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  18. Remove the O-ring from the transmission harness.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  19. Disconnect the harness connector from the control valve body.
  20. Remove the control valve body.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  21. Remove the O-ring from the transmission.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
