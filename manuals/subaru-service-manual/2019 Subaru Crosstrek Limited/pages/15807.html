Cam Carrier RH

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Remove the oil spacer from cam carrier RH.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Loosen the bolts (front camshaft cap RH, intake center camshaft cap RH, intake rear camshaft cap RH, exhaust center camshaft cap RH, and exhaust rear camshaft cap RH) equally, a little at a time in numerical sequence as shown in the figure, and remove each camshaft cap.
    NOTE: Arrange camshaft caps in order so that they can be installed in their original positions.
    Fig 1: Camshaft Caps Bolts Loosening Sequence (RH)
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the intake camshaft RH and the exhaust camshaft RH from cam carrier RH.
  4. Remove the filter from cam carrier RH.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the plug from the cam carrier RH. (Hybrid model)
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Remove the liquid gasket from cam carrier RH, front camshaft cap RH, intake rear camshaft cap RH and exhaust rear camshaft cap RH.
