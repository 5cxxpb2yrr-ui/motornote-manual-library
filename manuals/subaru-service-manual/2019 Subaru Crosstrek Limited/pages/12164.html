Basic Inspection

Before performing diagnosis, check the following items which might affect the A/C problems.

  1. Check the battery. Refer to STARTING/CHARGING SYSTEMS (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>BATTERY .
  2. Check the main and other fuses, harnesses and connectors. Also check grounding.
  3. Check the refrigerant pressure. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>REFRIGERANT PRESSURE WITH MANIFOLD GAUGE SET .
