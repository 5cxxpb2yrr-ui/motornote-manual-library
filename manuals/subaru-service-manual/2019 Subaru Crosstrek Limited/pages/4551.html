Front Door Partition Glass

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Temporarily assemble the running channel - front door to the sash assembly - partition.
    NOTE: Assemble so that the end face of the running channel - front door is aligned with the mounting surface of the sash assembly - partition on vehicle side.
  2. Install the glass - front door partition and the sash assembly - partition temporarily assembled.
    CAUTION: Check that the running channel - front door is securely fixed to the panel assembly - front door and to the sash assembly - partition.
    NOTE:
    • Engage the claws on top of the sash assembly - partition with the panel assembly - front door.
    • Install the glass - front door partition so that its molding part does not come off.

    Tightening torque: 

    7.5 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.5 ft-lb)

  3. Install the glass assembly - front door. Refer to GLASS/WINDOWS/MIRRORS>FRONT DOOR GLASS>INSTALLATION>FRONT DOOR GLASS .
