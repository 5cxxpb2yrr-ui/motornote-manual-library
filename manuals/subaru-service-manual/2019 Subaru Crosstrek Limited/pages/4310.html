Front Bumper: Disassembly

  1. Remove the front grille assembly.
    1. Remove the screws on the back side.
    2. Release the claws, and remove the front grille assembly.
      • Impreza model
        Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
      • Crosstrek model
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Remove the fog light assembly - front or the light assembly - daytime running. Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>FRONT FOG LIGHT ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL .
  3. Release the claws, and then remove the cover - fog light.
    • Impreza model
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • Crosstrek model
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the clips, and remove the sealing bumper - front and the plate UPR - front.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the clips, and remove the energy absorber LWR - front.
    • Impreza model
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • Crosstrek model (except for hybrid)
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • Crosstrek hybrid model
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Release the claws, and then remove the cover intake - front.
    • Impreza model
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • Crosstrek model
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Remove the clips, and remove the bumper guard front. (Hybrid model)
Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
