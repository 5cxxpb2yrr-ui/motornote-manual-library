Intake Manifold Assembly: Removal

  1. If the fuel delivery tube is disconnected after removing the intake manifold assembly, perform the following work.
    1. Release the fuel pressure. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FUEL>PROCEDURE>RELEASING OF FUEL PRESSURE .
    2. Open the fuel filler lid and remove the fuel filler cap.
    NOTE: This operation is required to release the inner pressure of the fuel tank.
  2. Remove the battery. Refer to STARTING/CHARGING SYSTEMS (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>BATTERY>REMOVAL .
  3. Drain engine coolant. Refer to COOLING (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>Engine Coolant>REPLACEMENT>DRAINING OF ENGINE COOLANT .
  4. Remove the clip from the air intake boot, and loosen the clamp.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the air intake boot, and place it aside so that it does not interfere with the work.
    CAUTION: Be careful not to pull out the PCV hose.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Disconnect the preheater hose (A) from the throttle body, and disconnect the connector (B).
  7. Disconnect the connector (C) from manifold absolute pressure sensor.
  8. Disconnect the connector (D) from the purge control solenoid valve, and remove clip (E).
  9. Disconnect the purge hose (F) from the purge control solenoid valve.
  10. Disconnect the PCV hose No. 1 (G).
  11. Remove the bolt (H) securing the fuel pipe.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  12. Disconnect the connector (A) from the tumble generator valve RH.
  13. Remove the fuel delivery tube clamp (B).
  14. Remove clip (C) securing the engine wiring harness.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  15. Remove nut (A) securing the EGR cooler to the EGR control valve.
  16. Remove stud bolt (B) using TORX® E8.
  17. Loosen bolts (C) securing the EGR cooler to the cylinder head RH.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  18. Remove the V-belt covers.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  19. Disconnect the connector (A), connector (B) and terminal (C). (Fixed capacity A/C model)
  20. Remove the clip (D) and clip (E) that secure the battery cable. (Fixed capacity A/C model)
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  21. Disconnect the connector (A), connector (B), connector (C) and terminal (D). (Variable capacity A/C model)
  22. Remove the clip (E) and clip (F) that secure the battery cable. (Variable capacity A/C model)
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  23. Disconnect the connector (A) from the EGR control valve.
  24. Remove the fuel delivery tube.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  25. Disconnect the brake booster vacuum hose (A).
  26. Remove clip (B) securing battery cable.
  27. Disengage 2 claws (C) and remove the harness protector.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  28. Disconnect the connector (A) from the tumble generator valve LH.
  29. Use a pair of nippers to cut off the clip securing the engine wiring harness, and remove the clip from the intake manifold assembly.
    NOTE: Put alignment marks (a) on the clip and the engine wiring harness.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  30. Remove the fuel pipe protector. (Impreza model)
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  31. Remove the fuel pipe protector No. 1. (Crosstrek model)
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  32. Remove the intake manifold assembly.
    • RH side
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • LH side
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  33. Remove the cylinder head plate from cylinder head.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  34. Disconnect the fuel delivery pipe from the high-pressure fuel pump. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>REMOVAL>QUICK CONNECTOR DISCONNECTION (TYPE C) .
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  35. Disconnect the fuel delivery pipe (A) from the fuel pipe. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>REMOVAL>QUICK CONNECTOR DISCONNECTION (TYPE B) .
  36. Disconnect the fuel delivery tube (B) from the fuel pipe. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>Fuel Delivery and Evaporation Lines>REMOVAL>QUICK CONNECTOR DISCONNECTION (TYPE A) .
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  37. When removing fuel pipe protector No. 2 (Crosstrek model)
    1. Remove the camshaft position sensor (intake LH side). Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>CAMSHAFT POSITION SENSOR>REMOVAL>INTAKE SIDE .
    2. Remove the fuel pipe protector No. 2.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
