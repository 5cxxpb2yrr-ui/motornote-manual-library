Fuel Tank: Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
WARNING:
  • Place "NO OPEN FLAMES" signs near the working area.
  • The hybrid system includes a high voltage circuit. Mishandling could cause accidents such as electric shock or leak. Always check "WARNING (HYBRID SYSTEM)" and perform the proper operation. Refer to PRECAUTION/WARNING>WARNING (HYBRID SYSTEM) .
CAUTION:
  • Be careful not to spill fuel.
  • Catch the fuel from the tubes using a container or cloth.
  1. Release the fuel pressure. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL>PROCEDURE>RELEASING OF FUEL PRESSURE .
  2. Drain fuel. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL>PROCEDURE>DRAINING FUEL (WITH SUBARU SELECT MONITOR) .
  3. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  4. Remove the service disconnect plug. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG>REMOVAL .
    WARNING: After removal of the service disconnect plug, the condenser inside the inverter still has been charged to a high voltage. Wait for at least 10 minutes before handling the high voltage components, wiring, terminals or connectors.
  5. Remove the rear seat cushion. Refer to SEATS>REAR SEAT>REMOVAL .
  6. Remove the service hole cover of fuel pump.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Disconnect the connector from fuel pump.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Lift up the vehicle.
  9. Remove the fuel tank protector and inverter assembly (with converter) cover. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL TANK PROTECTOR>REMOVAL .
  10. Remove the floor under protector. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>FLOOR UNDER PROTECTOR>REMOVAL .
  11. Remove the rear exhaust pipe and muffler as a single unit.

    Rear exhaust pipe: 

    Refer to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>REAR EXHAUST PIPE>REMOVAL .

    Muffler: 

    Refer to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>MUFFLER>REMOVAL .

  12. Remove the tunnel stay.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  13. Remove the rear differential. Refer to DIFFERENTIALS>REAR DIFFERENTIAL (VA-TYPE)>REMOVAL .
  14. Remove the fuel filler hose (A).
  15. Disconnect the circulate tube (B) from the fuel tank. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>REMOVAL>QUICK CONNECTOR DISCONNECTION (TYPE F) .
  16. Disconnect the vent tube (C) from the electric vent solenoid valve assembly. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>REMOVAL>QUICK CONNECTOR DISCONNECTION (TYPE H) .
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  17. Remove the bolt and clip (A) securing the inverter center water pipe and the resolver sensor harness.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  18. Remove the bolt and nut securing the air conditioner wire (A) and power cable assembly (B).
    WARNING: Be sure to wear insulated gloves.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  19. Disconnect the connector (A) and remove the support - sub frame (B).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  20. Disconnect the connector (A) and remove the bracket securing the harness.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  21. Disconnect the hose - air breather. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>REMOVAL>QUICK CONNECTOR DISCONNECTION (TYPE E) .
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  22. Disconnect the ground cable.
    WARNING: Be sure to wear insulated gloves.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  23. Support the inverter assembly (with converter) with the transmission jack and remove the bolt from the inverter assembly (with converter).
    WARNING: Be sure to wear insulated gloves.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  24. Lower the inverter assembly (with converter) to the position that the work is possible.
    WARNING: Be sure to wear insulated gloves.
    CAUTION:
    • A helper is required to perform this work.
    • Be careful not to apply a load to the harness and pipe, etc. connected to the inverter assembly (with converter).
  25. Disconnect the fuel delivery tube from the fuel pipe assembly. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>REMOVAL>QUICK CONNECTOR DISCONNECTION (TYPE A) .
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  26. Support the fuel tank with a transmission jack, remove the bolts from the fuel tank band, and remove the fuel tank from the vehicle.
    CAUTION:
    • A helper is required to perform this work.
    • Fuel may remain in the fuel tank. This will cause the left and right sides to be unbalanced. Be careful not to drop it.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  27. Remove the protector and insulator from the fuel tank.
    NOTE: Remove if necessary.
    1. Remove the clip (A) and remove the protector (B).
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Remove the upper insulator (B) from the insulator holder (A).
    3. Remove the lower insulator (C) from the fuel tank.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    4. Remove the insulator holder (A) from the lower insulator (C).
