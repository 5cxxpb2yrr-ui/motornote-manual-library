Drum In Disc Type Model

SHOE CLEARANCE 

  1. Return the lever assembly - hand brake completely.
  2. Loosen the adjusting nut, and make the cable free.
  3. Remove the adjusting hole cover (A).
  4. Remove the adjusting hole cover (A), and turn the adjuster assembly - rear brake in the arrow (d) direction by inserting a flat tip screwdriver.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Loosen the adjuster assembly - rear brake by 10 notches in the direction of the arrow (e).
    CAUTION:
    • Check there is no brake drag.
    • Make sure that the adjuster assembly - rear brake is loosened by 10 notches. If it is not loosened sufficiently, dragging may occur.
  6. Install the adjusting hole cover to the disc rotor.
  7. Adjust the parking lever stroke. Refer to PARKING BRAKE>PARKING BRAKE ASSEMBLY (REAR DISC BRAKE)>ADJUSTMENT>DRUM IN DISC TYPE MODEL>LEVER STROKE .

LEVER STROKE 

  1. Adjust the shoe clearance before adjusting lever stroke. Refer to PARKING BRAKE>PARKING BRAKE ASSEMBLY (REAR DISC BRAKE)>ADJUSTMENT>DRUM IN DISC TYPE MODEL .
  2. Remove the boot - hand brake.
    1. Pull up the cup holder, and release the engagement of the clip at the end of the cover assembly - front.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Release the engagement of claws and clips, and then remove the boot - hand brake.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Pull the lever assembly - hand brake hard 3 to 5 times.
    4. Turn the adjusting nut (a) until the lever stroke is at the specified value.

    Lever stroke: 

    7 to 8 notches when pulled with a force of 200 N (20.4 kgf, 45 lbf)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Check there is no parking brake drag.
  4. Check that the brake warning light illuminates when the lever assembly - hand brake is operated.
    NOTE: The light must illuminate when the first notch is reached after pulling the lever.
  5. Install the boot - parking brake.
