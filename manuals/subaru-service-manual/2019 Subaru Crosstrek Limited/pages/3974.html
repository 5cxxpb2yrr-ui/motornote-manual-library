Rear Gate Opener Button: Inspection

  1. Measure the resistance between connector terminals.

    Preparation tool: 

    Circuit tester

    • Keyless entry model
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • Model with keyless access with push button start
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Terminal No. Inspection conditions Standard
    1 - 2 When the switch is not pressed (CLOSE) 1 MΩ or more
    When the switch is pressed (OPEN) Less than 5 Ω
  2. If the inspection result is not within the standard, replace the button - opener rear gate.
