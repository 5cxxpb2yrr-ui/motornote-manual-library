Fuel Delivery Pipe

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Using a new gasket, install the fuel delivery pipe B to the high-pressure fuel pump.

    Tightening torque: 

    48 N.m (4.9 kgf-m, 35.4 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Connect the fuel delivery pipe A. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>INSTALLATION>QUICK CONNECTOR CONNECTION (TYPE C) .
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Install the fuel pipe protector No. 1.

    Tightening torque: 

    19 N.m (1.9 kgf-m, 14.0 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
