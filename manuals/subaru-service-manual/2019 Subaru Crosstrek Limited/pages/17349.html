Trailing Link: Removal

  1. Lift up the vehicle, and then remove the rear wheels.
  2. Disconnect the cable assembly - parking brake.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the bolts and nuts, and then remove the trailing link.

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • Crosstrek model
