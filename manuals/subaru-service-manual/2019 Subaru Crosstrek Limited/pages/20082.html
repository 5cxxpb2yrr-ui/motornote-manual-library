Front Door Impact Sensor

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. How to disconnect:
    CAUTION: When pulling the slide lock or disconnecting connector, be sure to hold the connector, not the harness.

    Holding outer part (a), pull it in the direction of arrow.

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. How to connect:
CAUTION:
  • The outer part (a) returns to the original position. Therefore do not touch the outer part.
  • Be sure to insert the connector in until it is locked. Then pull it gently to make sure that it is locked.

Holding the connector, push it in securely until a clicking sound is heard.
