Overview Of Error

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

The Charge control ECU that is installed inside the Electric vehicle charger assembly detects when the sub DCDC converter is stopped due to a sub DCDC converter error.

The following are possible causes of the error.

  • Sub DCDC converter malfunction
    • Malfunction of the Electric vehicle charger assembly
  • Sub DCDC converter AMD circuit malfunction
    • Malfunction of AMD circuit fuse connector
    • Malfunction of Electric vehicle charger assembly connector
    • Harness malfunction
