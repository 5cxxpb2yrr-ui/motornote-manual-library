Rear Gate Panel

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY>GROUND TERMINAL REMOVAL OR INSTALLATION .
  2. Remove the trim panel - rear gate and the garnish assembly - rear gate. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>REAR GATE GARNISH>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the light assembly - rear finisher. Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>REAR FINISHER LIGHT ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL .
  4. Remove the license plate light. Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>LICENSE PLATE LIGHT>REMOVAL .
  5. Remove the rearview camera assembly. Refer to ENTERTAINMENT & MONITORING>CAMERA>REMOVAL>REAR .
  6. Remove the arm assembly - rear wiper and the motor assembly - rear wiper. Refer to WIPER AND WASHER SYSTEMS>REAR WIPER MOTOR>REMOVAL .
  7. Remove the rear gate latch and actuator - rear gate. Refer to SECURITY AND LOCKS>REAR GATE LATCH AND ACTUATOR ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL .
  8. Remove the buffer - rear gate.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Remove the clips, and remove the drip - rear gate.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  10. Remove the roof spoiler. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>ROOF SPOILER>REMOVAL .
  11. Disconnect the hose assembly - rear washer.
    1. Disconnect the joint portion of the hose assembly - rear washer.
    2. Remove the clip.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  12. Remove the nozzle and hose assembly - rear washer. Refer to WIPER AND WASHER SYSTEMS>REAR WASHER>REMOVAL .
  13. Remove the rubber duct connection.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  14. Disconnect the connector, and then pull out the harness from the panel - rear gate shown in the figure (a).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  15. Remove the harness clip from the panel - rear gate.
  16. Remove the rear gate glass. Refer to GLASS/WINDOWS/MIRRORS>REAR GATE GLASS>REMOVAL .
  17. Before removing the stay assembly - rear gate, prevent the panel - rear gate from closing by using a prop or the like.
    CAUTION: If the prop comes off, operators may get injured and vehicle may get damaged. Make sure to support the panel - rear gate with secure material to prevent injury or damage.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  18. Remove the stay assembly - rear gate.
    1. Remove the stopper - rear gate and remove the attachment section on the side of the stay assembly - rear gate.
    2. Remove the mounting bolts on the body side.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  19. Remove the bolts of the hinge - rear gate, and remove the panel - rear gate.
    CAUTION: The panel - rear gate is heavy. When removing and installing it, always work in a team of two or more persons.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  20. Remove the hinge - rear gate from the vehicle.
