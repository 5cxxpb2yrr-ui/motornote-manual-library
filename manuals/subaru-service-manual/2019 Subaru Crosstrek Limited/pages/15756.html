Preparation for Overhaul: Procedure

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. After engine has been removed from the vehicle, mount the engine to the engine stand while being careful of the following.
    1. When mounting the engine stand, follow the instructions of engine stand used.
    2. When disassembling the cylinder block, remove the following parts first, and then mount the engine stand.
      1. Remove the crankshaft position sensor plate together with the drive plate. (Except for hybrid model (CVT model)) Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>DRIVE PLATE>REMOVAL .
      2. Remove the crankshaft position sensor plate together with the flywheel. (MT model and hybrid model)

        MT model: 

        Refer to CLUTCH SYSTEM>FLYWHEEL>REMOVAL .

        Hybrid model: 

        Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TH2A)>FLYWHEEL>REMOVAL .

    3. Mount the engine stand using the points shown by the arrows in the figure.
      CAUTION: Be sure to mount the engine stand at four points. Otherwise, the engine damage may occur.
      NOTE:
      • For points (A), either of them can be used for mounting the engine stand.
      • The engine stand shown in the figure is just for reference.

  2. In this service information the procedures described under each index are all related to each other and described in order. The procedure for overhauling of the engine will be completed when you go through all steps in the process.

    Therefore, in this service information, to conduct the particular procedure within the flow of a service information, you need to go back and conduct the procedure described previously in order to do that particular procedure.
