Rear Differential (T-type): Removal

  1. Shift the select lever or gear shift lever to neutral.
  2. Release the parking brake.
  3. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery.

    Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .

  4. Lift up the vehicle.
  5. Remove the rear tires.

    Refer to WHEEL AND TIRE SYSTEM>TIRE AND WHEEL>REMOVAL .

  6. Drain differential gear oil.

    Refer to DIFFERENTIALS>DIFFERENTIAL GEAR OIL>REPLACEMENT .

  7. Remove the rear exhaust pipe.

    Refer to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>REAR EXHAUST PIPE>REMOVAL .

  8. Remove the center exhaust cover.
    • Except for Crosstrek hybrid model
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • Crosstrek hybrid model
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Remove the propeller shaft.

    Refer to DRIVE SHAFT SYSTEM>PROPELLER SHAFT>REMOVAL .

  10. Disconnect the ground cable. (Crosstrek hybrid model)
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  11. Loosen the self-lock nuts which hold the rear differential to the sub frame.
    • Except for Crosstrek hybrid model
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • Crosstrek hybrid model
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  12. Remove the DOJ of the rear drive shaft using the ST or a tire lever.
    CAUTION: When removing the DOJ, fit the tire lever or ST to the bolts as shown in the figure so as not to damage the side retainer.
    NOTE:
    • For the T-type, use the ST.
    • For the VA-type, use the tire lever.

    T-type 

    Special tool: 

    ST: 28099 Pa100 DRIVE SHAFT REMOVER

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.

    VA1-type 

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  13. Set the transmission jack.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  14. Remove the rear differential front member.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  15. Remove the self-lock nuts which hold the rear differential to the sub frame.
    • Except for Crosstrek hybrid model
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • Crosstrek hybrid model
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  16. Remove the mass damper.
  17. Pull out the rear differential stud bolt from rear differential mount bushing.
    NOTE: When pulling out the stud bolt from the rear differential mount bushing, carefully adjust the angle and location of transmission jack and jack stand, if necessary.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  18. Lower the transmission jack.
  19. Pull out the rear drive shaft.
    NOTE: If it is difficult to pull out the rear drive shaft, use the DRIVE SHAFT REMOVER (28099 Pa100) for T-type and the tire lever for VA1-type.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  20. Lower the transmission jack.
  21. Secure the rear drive shaft using a wire.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  22. Remove the rear differential member plate.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
