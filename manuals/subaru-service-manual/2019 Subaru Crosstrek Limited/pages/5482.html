Brake Stroke Sensor

CAUTION:
  • Before handling the airbag system components, always refer to "CAUTION" of "General Description" in "AIRBAG SYSTEM". Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  • Perform linear valve offset learning after the brake stroke sensor was removed and reinstalled or replaced. If learning was not performed, brake control will be prohibited (brake pedal will be heavy), a DTC is stored, the brake warning light illuminates, or other problems will occur.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery and wait for at least 60 seconds before starting work. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY>GROUND TERMINAL REMOVAL OR INSTALLATION .
  2. Remove the cover assembly - instrument panel LWR driver. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>INSTRUMENT PANEL LOWER COVER>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the knee airbag module. Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>KNEE AIRBAG MODULE>REMOVAL .
  4. Remove the duct - foot driver. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>HEATER DUCT>REMOVAL .
  5. Remove the hybrid powertrain control module. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL MODULE>REMOVAL .
  6. Remove the brake stroke sensor.
    1. Disconnect the brake stroke sensor connector.
    2. Remove the bolt and remove the brake stroke sensor.
