Charging Inlet Door

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Remove the charging inlet light.
    1. Disconnect the connector.
    2. Release the claws and remove the charging inlet light.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Remove the EV charger lid switch assembly.
    1. Remove the bolts, and remove the EV charger lid switch assembly.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the push-lifter.
  1. Release the claws and remove the push-lifter.
