Removal & Installation

  1. Remove the pocket assembly. Ref. to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>Glove Box>Removal.
    1. Remove the damper COMPL - pocket.
    2. Release the stoppers and remove the pocket assembly by pulling it toward you.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Remove the filter.
    1. Release the lock and guide, and remove the filter cover.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Remove the filter as shown in the figure.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    NOTE: Pull out while tilting the front side of the filter downward to prevent dirt or dust from falling inside the case.
  3. Install new filter and filter cover.
  4. Install the pocket assembly.
