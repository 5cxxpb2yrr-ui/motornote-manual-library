Diagnostic Method

Fluctuation of the sensor signal exceeds the threshold (7.5 MPa or more from the previous value).

JUDGMENT VALUE SPECIFICATION

Malfunction Criteria Threshold Value
ABS (current accumulator pressure - last accumulator pressure) Exceeds 7.5 MPa

Time needed for diagnosis:  100 ms

Malfunction indicator light illumination:  Illuminates as soon as a malfunction occurs.
