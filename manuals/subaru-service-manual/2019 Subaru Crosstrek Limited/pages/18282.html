Washer Level Sensor (Crosstrek Model)

CAUTION: Do not hit or apply any impact to the sensor - washer tank, because it has a poor resistance to impact. Sensor internal parts may be damaged when an impact is applied.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Lift up the vehicle.
  3. Remove the under cover - front. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>FRONT UNDER COVER>REMOVAL .
  4. Remove the clips and screws, and turn over the mud guard - front LH.
  5. Remove the sensor - washer tank.
    1. Disconnect the connector.
    2. Prepare a container before disconnecting the hose - washer, and drain all washer fluid in the tank - washer.
    3. Push the section (a) in the direction of the arrow with a thick towel or the like to remove the sensor - washer tank.
