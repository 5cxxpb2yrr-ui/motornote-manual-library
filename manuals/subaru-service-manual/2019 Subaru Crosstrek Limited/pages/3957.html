Door Inner Remote: Removal: Rear

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the trim panel - rear door. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>DOOR TRIM>REMOVAL>REAR DOOR .
  3. Remove the remote assembly - rear door.
    1. Remove the screws.
    2. Release the claws and guides, and remove the remote assembly - rear door.
