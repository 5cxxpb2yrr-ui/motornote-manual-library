Symptoms Maintenance parts Reference
"Reduced High voltage battery cooling Have vehicle inspected at retailer" is displayed on the multi-information display(1). 1. The air inlet of HV battery must not be blocked. -
2. The duct must be free from gaps. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY COOLING SYSTEM .
3. Clean HV battery intake filter and check that there is no foreign matter. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY COOLING SYSTEM .
4. Perform memory clear of DTC regardless of stored DTCs existing (to reset the learning values). Refer to COMMON (DIAG)>CLEAR MEMORY .
Noise comes around the battery pack air inlet in the rear seat. 1. The air inlet of HV battery must not be blocked. -
2. The duct must be free from gaps. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY COOLING SYSTEM .
3. Clean HV battery intake filter and check that there is no foreign matter. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY COOLING SYSTEM .
"Have high voltage battery inspected at retailer" is displayed. "Have high voltage battery inspected at retailer" is displayed. Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>"HAVE HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY INSPECTED AT RETAILER" IS DISPLAYED .
READY ON does not occur. Keyless access with push button start system (start function) Refer to KEYLESS ACCESS WITH PUSH BUTTON START (DIAGNOSTICS)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE .
Battery ECU assembly power supply system Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>BATTERY ECU ASSEMBLY .
Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly power supply system Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>HYBRID VEHICLE CONTROL ECU ASSEMBLY .
Check vehicle control history Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>WORK SUPPORT .
READY ON is slow to respond. Check vehicle control history Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>WORK SUPPORT .
(1) If there is Battery cooling blower assembly failure, any of DTC P0A8111, P0A8115, P0A8196, P0A9611, P0A9615 or P0A9696 is output. If only "Reduced High voltage battery cooling Have vehicle inspected at retailer" is displayed on the multi-information display, perform the air intake related check on HV battery Cooling System only.
