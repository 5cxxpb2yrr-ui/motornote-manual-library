Oil Pump: Inspection

Check the following items.

  • Check the oil pump for damage and wear.
  • Rotate the oil pump by hand, and check that it rotates smoothly.
  1. Measure the secondary pressure. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>SECONDARY PRESSURE (LINE PRESSURE) TEST>INSPECTION .
  2. Remove the oil pan and oil strainer. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>OIL PAN AND STRAINER>REMOVAL .
  3. Check oil strainer for clogging.

    When oil strainer has no clogging, replace the oil pump.
