Ignition Switch Illumination: Note

The ignition switch illumination is integrated into the immobilizer antenna assembly. For operation procedures, refer to the "SECURITY AND LOCKS" service information. Refer to SECURITY AND LOCKS>IMMOBILIZER ANTENNA .
