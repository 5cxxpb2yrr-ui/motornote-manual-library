Spot Map Light: Inspection

  1. Measure the resistance between connector terminals.

    Preparation tool: 

    Circuit tester

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • Light switch
      Terminal No. Inspection conditions Standard
      1 - 13 Switch OFF 1 MΩ or more
      Switch ON Approx. 18 Ω
    • Door switch
    Terminal No. Inspection conditions Specification
    1 - 4 Switch door Continuity exists
  2. Replace the light assembly - map if it is found defective.
