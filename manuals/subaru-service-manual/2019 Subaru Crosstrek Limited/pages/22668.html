Diagnostic Procedure For Subaru Select Monitor Communication: Inspection

Trouble symptom: 

Cannot communicate with [Engine Control System].

Detecting condition: 

  • Defective connector
  • Power supply circuit malfunction
  • Defective CAN communication circuit
  • Defective Subaru Select Monitor
NOTE: For diagnostic procedure, refer to the diagnosis for the engine. Refer to ENGINE (DIAGNOSTICS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE .
