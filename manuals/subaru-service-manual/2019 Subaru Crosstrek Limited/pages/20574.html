Diagnostics With Phenomenon: List

Symptoms Reference
1 No display is shown. Refer to MULTI-FUNCTION DISPLAY (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>NO DISPLAY IS SHOWN .
2 Light AUTO display is not displayed in the electrical parts operation status screen. Refer to MULTI-FUNCTION DISPLAY (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>LIGHT AUTO DISPLAY IS NOT DISPLAYED IN THE ELECTRICAL PARTS OPERATION STATUS SCREEN .
3 EyeSight main display is not displayed in the electrical parts operation status screen. Refer to MULTI-FUNCTION DISPLAY (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>EYESIGHT MAIN DISPLAY IS NOT DISPLAYED IN THE ELECTRICAL PARTS OPERATION STATUS SCREEN .
4 In the electrical parts operation status screen, RAB ON/OFF display cannot be switched. Refer to MULTI-FUNCTION DISPLAY (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>IN THE ELECTRICAL PARTS OPERATION STATUS SCREEN, RAB ON/OFF DISPLAY CANNOT BE SWITCHED .
5 In the electrical parts operation status screen, Blind Spot Detection/Rear Cross Traffic Alert ON/OFF display cannot be switched. Refer to MULTI-FUNCTION DISPLAY (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>IN THE ELECTRICAL PARTS OPERATION STATUS SCREEN, BLIND SPOT DETECTION/REAR CROSS TRAFFIC ALERT ON/OFF DISPLAY CANNOT BE SWITCHED .
6 Only weather screen is not displayed. Refer to MULTI-FUNCTION DISPLAY (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>ONLY WEATHER SCREEN IS NOT DISPLAYED .
7 Only orientation & Turn by turn screens are not displayed. Refer to MULTI-FUNCTION DISPLAY (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>ONLY ORIENTATION & AND TURN BY TURN SCREENS ARE NOT DISPLAYED .
8 Pressing the INFO switch does not change the screen. Refer to MULTI-FUNCTION DISPLAY (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>PRESSING THE INFO SWITCH DOES NOT CHANGE THE SCREEN .
9 Average fuel economy is not displayed. Refer to MULTI-FUNCTION DISPLAY (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>AVERAGE FUEL ECONOMY IS NOT DISPLAYED .
