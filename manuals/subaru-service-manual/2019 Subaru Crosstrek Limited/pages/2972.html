5 Door Model

  1. Install the light assembly - rear combination.
    CAUTION: Be careful not to catch the harness in between the parts.

    Tightening torque: 

    7.5 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.5 ft-lb)

  2. Install the cap - rear combination light.
  3. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
