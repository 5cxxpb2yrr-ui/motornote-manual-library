Control Valve Body: Inspection

  • Check each part for damage or dust.
  • Measure the resistance of each solenoid, sensor and ground wire.
    NOTE: Measurement should be performed at a temperature of 20°C (68°F).
Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  • Solenoid 
    Solenoid Terminal No. Standard
    Primary UP solenoid No. 2 - Control valve body Approx. 10 -14.2 Ω
    Secondary solenoid No. 3 - Control valve body Approx. 4.8 - 8.4 Ω
    F & R clutch solenoid No. 4 - Control valve body Approx. 3.5 - 7.1 Ω
    Primary DOWN solenoid No. 7 - Control valve body Approx. 10 -14.2 Ω
    Lock-up duty solenoid No. 9 - Control valve body Approx. 10 -14.2 Ω
    AWD solenoid No. 10 - Control valve body Approx. 1.5 - 4.9 Ω
  • Oil temperature sensor 
    Sensor Terminal No. Standard At 20°C (68°F)
    Oil temperature sensor No. 1 - No. 6 Approx. 2.5 kΩ
  • Transmission ground 
    Terminal No. Standard
    No. 8 - Control valve body Less than 1 Ω
