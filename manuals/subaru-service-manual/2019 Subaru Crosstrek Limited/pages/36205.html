Check Illumination Of Passenger's Seat Belt Warning Light

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

Check that the passenger's seat belt warning light illuminates when the passenger's seat is occupied.

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.

If it does not illuminate, check the seat belt warning system and the occupant detection system.

  • Seat belt warning system: Refer to SEAT BELT SYSTEM>SEAT BELT WARNING SYSTEM .
  • Occupant detection system: Refer to OCCUPANT DETECTION (DIAGNOSTICS)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE .
