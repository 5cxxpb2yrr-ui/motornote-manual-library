Release Bearing

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
CAUTION: Since this bearing is grease-sealed and is a non-lubrication type, do not wash with gasoline or any other solvent when servicing the clutch.
  1. Check the bearing play by applying force to the bearing in the radial direction.

    Specification: 

    1.4 mm (0.055 in) or more

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. While applying force to the bearing in the rotational direction, check the bearing for smooth rotation.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Check for wear and damage at the bearing case surface in contact with the lever.
