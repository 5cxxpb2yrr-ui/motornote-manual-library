Related Lines, More Than 52 Ω And Less Than 62 Ω (MAIN-CAN_AWD)

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Wiring diagram: 

CAN communication system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>CAN COMMUNICATION SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

NOTE: Perform inspection when no data is received, or faulty is detected. This is different from power supply shorted or ground shorted.
  1. CHECK BETWEEN RELATED LINES AND MAIN WIRING HARNESS. 
    1. Disconnect the AWD control module connector.
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between terminals of AWD control module connector.

      Connector & terminal 

      (B648) No. 1 - (B648) No. 14:

    Is the resistance 400 Ω or more?

    Yes:  A related line between AWD control module and main wiring harness is open, or main wiring harness is open at two places or more.

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK RELATED LINES. 
    1. Disconnect the steering sensor connector.
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between AWD control module connector and steering sensor connector.

      Connector & terminal 

      (B648) No. 1 - (i74) No. 3:

      (B648) No. 14 - (i74) No. 2:

    Is the resistance 10 Ω or more?

    Yes:  Repair or replace the open circuit of AWD control module related lines.

    No:  Check DTC of AWD control module. Refer to AWD CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .
