Headlight Assembly: Removal

CAUTION: Do not perform work with wet hands, because there is a possibility of electrical shock.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the air intake duct. Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>AIR INTAKE DUCT>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the cover UPR - front.
    1. Remove the clips, and remove the cover UPR - front.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the bumper face - front. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>FRONT BUMPER>REMOVAL .
  5. Remove the bracket - bumper UPR.
    1. Remove the bolts and clips, and remove the bracket - bumper UPR.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Remove the cover - fender UPR.
    1. Remove the clip, and remove the cover - fender UPR.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Remove the keyless buzzer or access buzzer. (When the light assembly - head on the left side is removed) Refer to SECURITY AND LOCKS>KEYLESS BUZZER>REMOVAL .
  8. Remove the bracket - front bumper corner.
    1. Remove the clip.
    2. Remove the bolts, and then remove the bracket - front bumper corner.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Remove the light assembly - head.
    CAUTION: Be careful not to damage the light assembly - head and the fender - front.
    1. Disconnect the connector.
    2. Remove the bolts and clips, and remove the light assembly - head.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
