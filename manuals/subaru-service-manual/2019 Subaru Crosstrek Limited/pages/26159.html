DTC P060A-47: Hybrid/EV Battery Energy Control Module Monitoring Processor Watchdog/Safety MCU Failure: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Outline of diagnosis: 

Battery ECU assembly internal malfunction

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

  • Reduced output power
  • Engine not stopped
NOTE: This DTC is output if Battery ECU assembly detects a malfunction of internal operation status. Therefore, if this DTC is output, replace Battery ECU assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>BATTERY CONTROL MODULE .
