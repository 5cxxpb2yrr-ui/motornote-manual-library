Nozzle - Wiper Arm Washer (Model With EyeSight)

CAUTION: Check and adjust the spray position of the nozzle - wiper arm at the position where the wiper arm stops.
  1. Turn the wiper switch to OFF position.
  2. While the vehicle is stopped, check that the washer spray position is as shown in the figure.
  3. After inspection, if the spray position is really out of the specified range, replace with a new arm assembly - windshield wiper assistor.
