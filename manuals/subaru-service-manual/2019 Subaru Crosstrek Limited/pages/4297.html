Mud Guard: Removal

  1. Lift up the vehicle.
  2. Remove the wheels.
  3. Remove the spoiler assembly - side sill. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>SIDE GARNISH & SIDE SPOILER>REMOVAL>IMPREZA MODEL .
  4. Remove the under cover - front. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>FRONT UNDER COVER>REMOVAL .
  5. Remove the screws and clips to remove the mud guard.
    • Impreza model
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
      NOTE: For model with the spoiler assembly - side sill, the number of clips is different.
    • Crosstrek model (except for hybrid)
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • Crosstrek hybrid model
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Remove the air flap as required.
