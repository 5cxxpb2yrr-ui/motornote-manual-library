DTC P0401: EGR Abnormal (EGR Amount Too Small): Notes

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

1. - 

DTC detecting condition: 

Detected when two consecutive driving cycles with fault occur.

Trouble symptom: 

  • Infrequent knocking (P0401)
  • Improper idling (P0402)
  • Movement performance problem (P0402)
NOTE: After the faulty parts are repaired or replaced, perform the final check in Basic Diagnostic Procedure. Refer to ENGINE (DIAGNOSTICS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .
  1. CHECK DTC  .
    1. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    2. Using the Subaru Select Monitor or a general scan tool, read the DTC of [Engine]. Refer to COMMON (DIAG)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

      Are DTCs other than P0401 and P0402 displayed? (Current malfunction)?

      Yes: 

      Check the appropriate DTC using the "Diagnostic Trouble Code (DTC)". Refer to ENGINE (DIAGNOSTICS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

      No: 

      • DTC P0401 displayed: Go to  2.
      • DTC P0402 displayed: Go to  3.
  2. CHECK DTC P0401  .
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Check for clogging in the pipes consisting of the EGR system.
      NOTE: Check the following items.
      • Throttle valve
      • EGR cooler
      • EGR control valve
      • EGR pipe
      • EGR pipe adapter

      Is the check result OK?

      Yes: 

      Go to  4.

      No: 

      Clean and replace the faulty parts.

  3. CHECK DTC P0402  .
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Check for leakage in the pipes consisting of the EGR system.
      NOTE: Check the following items.
      • Looseness of each flange connection
      • Improper installation of throttle body
      • Improper installation of manifold absolute pressure sensor
      • Foreign matter attached to EGR valve

      Is the check result OK?

      Yes: 

      Go to  4.

      No: 

      Clean and replace the faulty parts.

  4. CHECK FOR REPRODUCTION OF FAILURE  .

    Perform the corresponding inspection mode. Refer to ENGINE (DIAGNOSTICS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>INSPECTION MODE .

    Does the malfunction indicator light illuminate?

    Replace the EGR control valve and manifold absolute pressure sensor. Refer to EMISSION CONTROL (AUX. EMISSION CONTROL DEVICES) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>EGR CONTROL VALVE . Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>MANIFOLD ABSOLUTE PRESSURE SENSOR .

    End.
