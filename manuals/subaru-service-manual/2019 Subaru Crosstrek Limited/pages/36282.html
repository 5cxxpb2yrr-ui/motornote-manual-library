Rear Regulator and Motor Assembly: Removal

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY>GROUND TERMINAL REMOVAL OR INSTALLATION .
  2. Remove the trim panel - rear door. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>DOOR TRIM>REMOVAL>REAR DOOR .
  3. Remove the rear sealing cover. Refer to EXTERIOR BODY PANELS>SEALING COVER>REMOVAL>REAR SEALING COVER .
  4. Remove the glass assembly - rear door. Refer to GLASS/WINDOWS/MIRRORS>REAR DOOR GLASS>REMOVAL .
  5. Remove the regulator and motor assembly - rear.
    1. Disconnect the connector of the motor - rear.
