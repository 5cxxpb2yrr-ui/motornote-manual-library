Inverter Water Pump: Installation

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Attach the bracket.

    Tightening torque: 

    18 N.m (1.8 kgf-m, 13.3 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. When the water pipe and inverter water pump inlet hose were replaced
    1. Connect the inverter water pump inlet hose to the water pipe.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Connect the water pipe (A) and inverter water pump inlet hose (B) as a single unit to the inverter tank outlet hose.
    3. Install the water pipe (A) to the clamp.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Install the inverter water pump, and connect the connector (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    18 N.m (1.8 kgf-m, 13.3 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Connect the inverter water pump inlet hose to the inverter water pump.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Connect the battery ground terminal.

    Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .

  6. Fill engine coolant. (Inverter assembly side)

    Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>ENGINE COOLANT>REPLACEMENT>FILLING OF ENGINE COOLANT>INVERTER ASSEMBLY SIDE .
