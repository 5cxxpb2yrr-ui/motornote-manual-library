Cam Carrier LH

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Set the part so that the cylinder head LH is on the upper side.
  2. Apply engine oil to the valve shim and the roller rocker arm pivot, and install the valve shim and the roller rocker arm pivot to the cylinder head LH.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Apply engine oil to new O-ring and the roller rocker arm, and install the O-ring and the roller rocker arm to the cylinder head LH.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Apply liquid gasket to the mating surface of cam carrier LH as shown in the figure.
    NOTE:
    • Before applying liquid gasket, degrease the old liquid gasket seal surface of the cylinder head LH and cam carrier LH.
    • Install within 5 min. after applying liquid gasket.

    Liquid gasket: 

    THREE BOND 1217G (Part No. K0877Y0100), THREE BOND 1217H or equivalent

    Liquid gasket applying diameter: 

    4±0.5 mm (0.1575±0.0197 in)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Install the cam carrier LH to the cylinder head LH.
    1. Set the cam carrier LH to the cylinder head LH.
      NOTE: Position the intake camshaft LH and the exhaust camshaft LH to the zero-lift position as shown in the figure.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Tighten all bolts with a torque of 18 N.m (1.8 kgf-m, 13.3 ft-lb) in numerical order as shown in the figure.
    3. Loosen the bolts (3 places) by 180° in numerical order (3) → (2) → (1) as shown in the figure.
    4. Tighten the bolts (3 places) with a torque of 18 N.m (1.8 kgf-m, 13.3 ft-lb) in numerical order (1) → (2) → (3) as shown in the figure.
    5. Loosen the bolts (3 places) by 180° in numerical order (8) → (6) → (4) as shown in the figure.
    6. Tighten the bolts (3 places) with a torque of 18 N.m (1.8 kgf-m, 13.3 ft-lb) in numerical order (4) → (6) → (8) as shown in the figure.
    7. Loosen the bolts (3 places) by 180° in numerical order (7) → (9) → (5) as shown in the figure.
    8. Tighten the bolts (3 places) with a torque of 18 N.m (1.8 kgf-m, 13.3 ft-lb) in numerical order (5) → (9) → (7) as shown in the figure.
      NOTE: After tightening, if the liquid gasket is squeezed out onto the seal surface of the chain cover, completely remove any squeezed-out liquid gasket.
    Fig 1: Cam Carrier LH To Cylinder Head LH Bolts Loosening Sequence
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Set the part so that the installation surface of the intake manifold is on the upper side.
  7. When the cam carrier LH has been disassembled
    NOTE: When the cam carrier LH has been disassembled, perform the following steps also.
    1. Install the cam sprocket LH. Refer to MECHANICAL (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>CAM SPROCKET>INSTALLATION>CAM SPROCKET LH .
    2. Install the high-pressure fuel pump.

      Except for hybrid model: 

      Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>HIGH PRESSURE FUEL PUMP>INSTALLATION .

      Hybrid model: 

      Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>HIGH PRESSURE FUEL PUMP>INSTALLATION .

      NOTE: Install the high-pressure fuel pump so that the cam lobes on the intake camshaft LH are positioned as shown in the figure. Perform adjustment within the range of zero-lift (in range where it can be turned lightly by hand).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Check the cam clearance. Refer to MECHANICAL (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>CAM CLEARANCE>INSPECTION>WHEN TIMING CHAIN ASSEMBLY IS REMOVED .
  9. Secure the engine wiring harness to the cam carrier LH with a clip.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  10. Connect the connector to the high-pressure fuel pump.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  11. Install the high-pressure fuel delivery pipe.

    Except for hybrid model: 

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>HIGH PRESSURE FUEL DELIVERY PIPE>INSTALLATION>HIGH-PRESSURE FUEL DELIVERY PIPE .

    Hybrid model: 

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>HIGH PRESSURE FUEL DELIVERY PIPE>INSTALLATION>HIGH-PRESSURE FUEL DELIVERY PIPE .

  12. Install the water pipe LH.

    Except for hybrid model: 

    Refer to COOLING (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>WATER PIPE>INSTALLATION>WATER PIPE LH .

    Hybrid model: 

    Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>WATER PIPE>INSTALLATION>WATER PIPE LH .

  13. Install the rocker cover LH. Refer to MECHANICAL (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ROCKER COVER>INSTALLATION>ROCKER COVER LH .
  14. Install timing chain LH. Refer to MECHANICAL (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>TIMING CHAIN ASSEMBLY>INSTALLATION>TIMING CHAIN LH .
  15. Install the engine to the vehicle.

    Except for hybrid model: 

    Refer to MECHANICAL (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE ASSEMBLY>INSTALLATION>EXCEPT FOR HYBRID MODEL .

    Hybrid model: 

    Refer to MECHANICAL (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE ASSEMBLY>INSTALLATION>HYBRID MODEL .
