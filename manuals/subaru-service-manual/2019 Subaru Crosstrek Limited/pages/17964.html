When Switched To X MODE, "S", "I", "S#" OR "X" MODE Display In Combination Meter Flashes In Approx. 5 Seconds

  1. CHECK DTC  .

    Are any DTCs displayed?

    Yes:  Check the appropriate DTC using the "List of Diagnostic Trouble Code (DTC)" concerning the respective units.

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK COMBINATION METER AND CLOCK DISPLAY  .

    Check for nay abnormal displays other than the flashing of "S", "I", "S#" or "X".

    Examples:

    • Malfunction indicator light illuminates.
    • Fuel economy display area is not ON.
    NOTE: The system enters into fail mode with ignition ON and engine OFF.

    Is there an abnormal display other than the flashing of "S", "I", "S#" or "X"?

    Yes:  For the diagnostic procedure, refer to LAN instructions. Refer to COMMON (DIAG)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>OPERATION .

    No:  Go to  3.

  3. CHECK ECM AND COMBINATION METER  .

    Is the part number of ECM and combination meter correct?

    Yes:  Replace the meter case assembly. Refer to INSTRUMENTATION/DRIVER INFO>COMBINATION METER .

    No:  Replace ECM or meter case assembly with the one with the correct part number. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE CONTROL MODULE (ECM) . Refer to INSTRUMENTATION/DRIVER INFO>COMBINATION METER .
