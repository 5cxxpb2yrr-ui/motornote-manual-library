Rear Quarter Glass: Removal

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY>GROUND TERMINAL REMOVAL OR INSTALLATION .
  2. Remove the trim panel - rear pillar UPR. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>REAR QUARTER TRIM>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the glass - rear quarter in the same procedure as for the glass - front window. Refer to GLASS/WINDOWS/MIRRORS>WINDSHIELD GLASS>REMOVAL .
    CAUTION:
    • When removing with a windshield glass knife, for the front portion, insert the windshield glass knife from the outside of the compartment, and for the other portions, insert from the inside of the compartment.
    • Hold the glass - rear quarter to prevent it from falling down.
    • If the glass is reused, be careful not to scrape the adhesive surface of the locating pin.
    • If the glass is reused, do not remove the molding - rear quarter lower. If removed, replace with a new glass.
  4. Remove the locating pin. (When reusing the glass)
    1. Attach tape to the locating pin outer surface to identify the installation position.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Using a scraper, etc., remove locating pins (3 places).
    CAUTION:
    • Do not remove the tape because it is used as reference during installation.
    • Be careful not to damage the glass - rear quarter.
