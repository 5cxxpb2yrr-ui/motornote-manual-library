  1. CHECK BETWEEN RELATED LINES AND MAIN WIRING HARNESS. 
    1. Disconnect the RAB control module connector.
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between terminals in RAB control module connector.

    Connector & terminal 

    (R361) No. 4 - (R361) No. 16:

    Is the resistance 400 Ω or more?

    Yes:  A related line between RAB control module and main wiring harness is open, or main wiring harness is open at two places or more.

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK RELATED LINES. 
    1. Disconnect the steering sensor connector.
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between RAB control module connector and steering sensor connector.

    Connector & terminal 

    (R361) No. 16 - (i74) No. 3:

    (R361) No. 4 - (i74) No. 2:

    Is the resistance 10 Ω or more?

    Yes:  Repair or replace the open circuit of RAB related lines.

    No:  The communication harness is normal. Check DTC of RAB. Refer to REVERSE AUTOMATIC BRAKING (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .
