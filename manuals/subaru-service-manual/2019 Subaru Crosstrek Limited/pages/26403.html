Battery Diagnosis

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  • When HV battery is replaced, always perform [Battery Diagnosis] and [Battery Status Info Update] after completing the essential work.
  • Always perform 1. [Battery Diagnosis], then 2. [Battery Status Info Update] in this order.
  • When "Have high voltage battery inspected at retailer" is displayed, perform [Battery Diagnosis] by referring to interview results and then perform the diagnosis of degradation of Hybrid battery cell.
  • "Have high voltage battery inspected at retailer" remains until [Battery Diagnosis] is performed and judged as [Normal].
  • If the vehicle is used without performing [Battery Diagnosis] even after "Have high voltage battery inspected at retailer" is displayed, the use of HV battery will be restricted (I/O restriction) in a few weeks (depending on usage condition). Further extended use will cause prohibition of READY ON (vehicle start).
  • If READY ON (vehicle start) is prohibited, perform [Temporary Vehicle Start Up] and then [Battery Diagnosis].
    NOTE: Instruction when "Have high voltage battery inspected at retailer" is displayed. Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>"HAVE HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY INSPECTED AT RETAILER" IS DISPLAYED .
  1. Connect the Subaru Select Monitor.
  2. Turn to IG OFF, and wait for two minutes or more.
  3. Turn to IG ON.
  4. On [Start] screen, select [Diagnosis].
  5. On [Vehicle selection] screen, enter vehicle information and select [OK].
  6. On [Main Menu] screen, select [Each System].
  7. On [Select System] screen, select [P-HEV High Voltage Battery].
  8. On [Select Function] screen, select [Active Test].
  9. Select [Battery Diagnosis] on [Active Test] screen.
  10. Select the following items on [Data Monitor].
    • Battery Diagnosis Progress
    • Battery Diagnosis Result Module 1
    • Battery Diagnosis Result Module 2
    • Battery Diagnosis Result Module 3
    • Battery Diagnosis Result Module 4
    • Battery Diagnosis Result Module 5
  11. Confirm the items indicated on the screen, then press [Next].
  12. When [Battery Diagnosis Progress] becomes 100%, check the following diagnostic results.
    Data Monitor items Data Monitor values Diagnostic result After diagnosis
    Battery Diagnosis Result Module 1 - 5 All [0] Normal Perform [Battery Status Info Update]. Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>Work Support>OPERATION>BATTERY STATUS INFORMATION UPDATE .
    Other than above Replacement Replace HV battery. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY .
    NOTE:
    • When "Have high voltage battery inspected at retailer" is displayed on the combination meter, the message will not disappear even after the judgment says battery replacement not required (usable). Perform the subsequent [Battery Status Info Update] and it will disappear.
    • In case of replacement battery cell stack display (not usable), perform [Battery Status Info Update] after replacing the HV battery.
  13. Turn the ignition to OFF.
    NOTE: Turn to IG OFF as soon as completing [Battery Diagnosis] in case Battery (12V) may be depleted.
  14. If it is out of the execution condition for battery diagnosis, display the interruption content on the Subaru Select Monitor. In this case, confirm the interruption reason, check according to the following instruction and perform [Battery Diagnosis] again.

    Battery diagnosis - Measure during interruption

    Subaru Select Monitor display Interruption reason (condition range) Action to take
    Execution of battery diagnosis failed 1. The power switch was turned off or a SUBARU SELECT MONITOR communication error occurred.
    1. Turn the power switch on (IG), and then perform battery diagnosis again.
    2. Check that the connector of the SUBARU SELECT MONITOR is not disconnected from the Data Link Connector, perform battery diagnosis again.
    2. SOC (state of charge) learning has not completed.
    1. Lower the SOC (state of charge) of the HV battery by driving the vehicle in EV mode until the mode changes to HV mode.
    2. Make a note of the Data List items from "Hybrid Battery Temperature 1" to "Hybrid Battery Temperature 15".
    3. If the lowest measured battery temperature is 10°C (50°F) or lower, drive the vehicle until the temperature exceeds 15°C (59°F). If "Have high voltage battery inspected at retailer" is displayed on the combination meter and the hybrid vehicle system cannot be started, perform "Temporary Vehicle Start Up" and drive the vehicle.
    4. Perform plug-in charge after 90 minutes or more with power off. (Do not do IG ON or Ready ON before starting plug-in charging. If IG ON or Ready ON has been done, perform the procedure from step 4 again.)
      NOTE: Do not use the charging timer function.
    5. After plug-in charging, wait for 35 minutes or more, then turn the power switch on (IG) or on (READY). (Do not turn the power switch on (IG) or on (READY) for at least 35 minutes after plug-in charging. If the vehicle is not fully charged, perform the procedure from step  1 again.)
    6. Leave the vehicle with the power switch on (IG) or on (READY) for 10 minutes or more, turn the power switch off, and then perform battery diagnosis.
