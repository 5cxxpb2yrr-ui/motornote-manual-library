Rear Drive Shaft: Installation

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Replace the rear differential side oil seal. Ref. to DIFFERENTIALS>REAR DIFFERENTIAL SIDE OIL SEAL>REPLACEMENT .
    CAUTION: After pulling out the drive shaft, be sure to replace with a new oil seal.
  2. Insert the drive shaft assembly to the specified position in the spline of the rear hub unit bearing.
    CAUTION:
    • Do not hammer the drive shaft when installing it.
    • Be careful not to damage the spline portion of the drive shaft.
    • Be careful not to damage the magnetic encoder.
  3. Tighten the axle nut temporarily.
    CAUTION: Be sure to use a new axle nut.
  4. Check that circlip (1) is placed on the groove of side gear. (T-type)
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Using the ST, install the rear drive shaft assembly to the rear differential.

    Preparation tool: 

    ST: OIL SEAL PROTECTOR (28099 Pa090)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Install the rear differential.
    CAUTION: Be sure to use a new self-locking nut.
    1. Raise the transmission jack and insert the rear differential stud bolt into the rear differential mount bushing.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Install the mass damper to the rear differential stud bolt, and temporarily tighten the self-locking nut.
      • Except for Crosstrek hybrid model
        Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
      • Crosstrek hybrid model
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Install the rear differential front member, and temporarily tighten the self-locking nut.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    4. Remove the transmission jack.
    5. Tighten the self-locking nuts.

    Tightening torque: 

    Differential assembly - bushing - differential: 70 N.m (7.1 kgf-m, 51.6 ft-lb)

    Differential assembly - rear sub frame assembly: 110 N.m (11.2 kgf-m, 81.1 ft-lb)

  7. Install the propeller shaft. Ref. to DRIVE SHAFT SYSTEM>PROPELLER SHAFT>INSTALLATION .
  8. Install the center exhaust cover.

    Tightening torque 

    18 N.m (1.8 kgf-m, 13.3 ft-lb)

  9. Install the rear exhaust pipe. Ref. to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>REAR EXHAUST PIPE>INSTALLATION .
  10. Install the sensor assembly - headlight beam leveler. (Model with auto headlight beam leveler)

    Tightening torque: 

    7.5 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.5 ft-lb)

  11. While depressing the brake pedal, tighten the axle nut to the specified torque.
    CAUTION: Do not load the rear axle before tightening the axle nut. Doing so may damage the hub unit bearing.

    Tightening torque: 

    Impreza model: 240 N.m (24.5 kgf-m, 177 ft-lb)

    Crosstrek model: 190 N.m (19.4 kgf-m, 140.1 ft-lb)

  12. Lock the axle nut securely.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  13. Fill differential gear oil. Ref. to DIFFERENTIALS>DIFFERENTIAL GEAR OIL>REPLACEMENT .
  14. Install the rear wheels.

    Tightening torque: 

    120 N.m (12.2 kgf-m, 88.5 ft-lb)

  15. Connect the battery ground terminal. Ref. to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  16. Perform reinitialization of the auto headlight beam leveler system. (Model with auto headlight beam leveler) Ref. to LIGHTING SYSTEM>AUTO HEADLIGHT BEAM LEVELER SYSTEM>PROCEDURE .
