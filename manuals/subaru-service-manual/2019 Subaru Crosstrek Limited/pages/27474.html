DTC P0C3D-11: DC/DC Converter Temperature Sensor "B" Circuit Short To Ground: Notes

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Detecting condition: 

Open circuit or short to ground of converter temperature (lower arm) sensor signal line

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

Normal driving
