Tire Pressure Warning Light Is 25 Times Blinking And Turn On

Detecting condition: 

  • Defective TPMS & keyless entry control module or TPMS control module
  • Defective harness
  • Transmitter is faulty.

Trouble symptom: 

Every time the engine starts, tire pressure warning light blinks 25 times and then illuminates.

CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to TIRE PRESSURE MONITOR (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK DTC. 

    Read the DTC of [Tire Pressure Monitor] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to TIRE PRESSURE MONITOR (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Are any DTCs displayed? (Current malfunction)

    Yes:  Perform the diagnosis for the displayed DTCs. Refer to TIRE PRESSURE MONITOR (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>LIST .

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK LAN COMMUNICATION. 

    Inspect LAN system. Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE . Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Replace the TPMS & keyless entry control module or TPMS control module. Refer to WHEEL AND TIRE SYSTEM>TIRE PRESSURE MONITORING SYSTEM .

    No:  Perform the inspection according to the diagnosis for LAN system.
