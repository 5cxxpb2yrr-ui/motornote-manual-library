APPLICABILITY

2014-19MY Crosstrek

2014-18MY Forester 2014-19MY Impreza 2014MY WRX

INTRODUCTION

This bulletin announces availability of new front wheel hub assemblies incorporating bearing surfaces having increased resistance to imperfections resulting from shock loads. Excessive shock loads can occur when hitting a significant imperfection in the road surface (e.g. large pothole or expansion joint). If the bearing surface is affected, a growling-type sound may become audible when vehicle speed exceeds 25 MPH.
