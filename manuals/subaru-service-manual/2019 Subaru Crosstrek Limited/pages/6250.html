VDC OFF Switch: Installation

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Install the VDC OFF switch.
  2. Install the cover assembly - instrument panel LWR driver OUT.
  3. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY>GROUND TERMINAL REMOVAL OR INSTALLATION .
