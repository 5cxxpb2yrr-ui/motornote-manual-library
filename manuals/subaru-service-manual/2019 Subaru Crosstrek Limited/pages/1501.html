Vehicle Speed Is Not Held Within Set Speed ±3 Km/H (±2 Mph) On A Level Road

  1. INSPECTION OF VEHICLE SPEED SENSOR. 

    Check the vehicle speed sensor. Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE WITH ECU CANCEL CODE>22 .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Replace the ECM. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE CONTROL MODULE (ECM)>REMOVAL .

    No:  Repair or replace the faulty portion according to the inspection result.
