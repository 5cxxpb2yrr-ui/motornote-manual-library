Propeller Shaft: Inspection

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

Check the propeller shaft with the propeller shaft installed to the vehicle.

  1. Remove the rear exhaust pipe and muffler. Ref. to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>REAR EXHAUST PIPE>REMOVAL . Ref. to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>MUFFLER>REMOVAL .
  2. Remove the bolts, and then remove the center exhaust cover.
    • Except for Crosstrek hybrid model
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • Crosstrek hybrid model
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Check the propeller shaft mounting bolt for looseness.
    1. Flange yoke mounting bolts and nuts which connect to the rear differential
    2. Center bearing bracket mounting bolts
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Check the spline and bearing for the propeller shaft.
    1. Turn the propeller shaft by hand to check if abnormal looseness exists at splines.
    2. Also move yokes to check if abnormal looseness exists at spiders and bearings.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Measure the deflection of the propeller shaft.
    1. Set the dial gauge (B) with its indicator stem at the center of the propeller shaft (A).
    2. Turn the propeller shaft (A) slowly by hands to check for runout of the propeller shaft.

    Service limit: 

    0.6 mm (0.024 in)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Check the center bearing for rattling.

    Move the propeller shaft near the center bearing up, down, left, right by hand, to check for any abnormal free play of the bearings.

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Replace the propeller shaft assembly if faulty is found in the inspection.
