Power Amplifier: Removal

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

NOTE: Remove the power amplifier assembly after sliding the passenger's seat back and forth as necessary.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the power amplifier assembly.
    1. Remove the harness clip, and disconnect each connector.
    2. Remove the nut and remove the power amplifier assembly.
