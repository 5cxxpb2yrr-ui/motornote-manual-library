Radar Sensor LH (Master)

  • 4 DOOR MODEL
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Ref. to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the bumper face - rear. Ref. to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>REAR BUMPER>REMOVAL>4 DOOR MODEL .
  3. Remove the radar sensor LH (master).
    1. Disconnect the connector.
    2. Remove the nut and remove the radar sensor LH (master).
  4. Remove the radar bracket.
    1. Remove the harness clamp.
    2. Remove the nut and remove the radar bracket.
