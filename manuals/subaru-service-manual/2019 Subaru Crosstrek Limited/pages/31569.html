A-PILLAR TRIM COVER-DESIGN CHANGE

TECHNICAL SERVICE BULLETIN

Reference Number(s): 12-277-19, Date of Issue:  December 13, 2019
SUBARU: 2018-19 Crosstrek

APPLICABILITY

2018-19MY Crosstrek

INTRODUCTION

This bulletin describes a design change made to the exterior "A" pillar cover. The change involves use of materials in critical areas having added rigidity along with an extra strengthening rib. These changes will prevent the upper corner portion from being deformed should the leading edge of the front door contact it during opening and closing. The affected area and example photos of what the damaged component may look like are shown below.

PRODUCTION CHANGE INFORMATION

The new A-Pillar covers were incorporated into production starting with VIN KH387837  .

ART INFORMATION

NOTE: When replacing these covers, they BOTH should be replaced as a set (LH and RH). New retaining clips are included with the replacement covers.

REMINDER  : Customer satisfaction and retention starts with performing quality repairs.

The service procedures for replacing the A-Pillar trim covers remain unchanged. Always refer to the applicable Service Manual and review the full requirements of the repair being performed.
