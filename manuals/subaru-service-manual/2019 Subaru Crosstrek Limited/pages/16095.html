Section 12 (Engine Cooling System (HEV)): Thermostat: Removal

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery.

    Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .

  2. Drain engine coolant. (Engine side)

    Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>ENGINE COOLANT>REPLACEMENT>DRAINING OF ENGINE COOLANT>ENGINE SIDE .

  3. Remove the front exhaust pipe.

    Refer to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FRONT EXHAUST PIPE>REMOVAL .

  4. Disconnect radiator outlet hose.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the thermostat cover, and then remove the thermostat.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Remove the gasket from thermostat.
