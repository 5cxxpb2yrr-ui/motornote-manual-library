Crankshaft Position Sensor Plate: Removal

The crankshaft position sensor plate is tightened together with the drive plate or flywheel; therefore, refer to "Drive Plate" or "Flywheel" for removal procedure.

Drive plate: 

Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>DRIVE PLATE>REMOVAL .

Flywheel: 

Refer to CLUTCH SYSTEM>FLYWHEEL>REMOVAL .
