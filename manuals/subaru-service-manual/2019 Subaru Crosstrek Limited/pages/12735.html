Pretensioner Connector: Procedure

For connectors of seat belt pretensioner, lap seat belt pretensioner and buckle switch RH, refer to "Airbag Connector" of "AIRBAG SYSTEM" instructions or "OCCUPANT DETECTION SYSTEM" instructions.

  • Airbag system: Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>AIRBAG CONNECTOR>PROCEDURE .
  • Occupant detection system: Refer to OCCUPANT DETECTION (DIAGNOSTICS)>AIRBAG CONNECTOR>PROCEDURE .
