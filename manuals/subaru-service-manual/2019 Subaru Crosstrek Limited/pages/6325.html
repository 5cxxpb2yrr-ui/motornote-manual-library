HYPOID Driven Gear RUNOUT On Its Back Surface

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

Using a dial gauge, check the hypoid driven gear back surface runout.

Service limit: 

0.05 mm (0.002 in)

If it exceeds the limit value, replace the hypoid driven gear.
