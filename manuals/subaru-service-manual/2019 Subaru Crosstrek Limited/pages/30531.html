Manual Transmission Assembly: Installation

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Install the release bearing & lever. refer to CLUTCH SYSTEM>RELEASE BEARING AND LEVER>INSTALLATION .
  2. Replace the differential side retainer oil seal with a new part. refer to MANUAL TRANSMISSION AND DIFFERENTIAL (6MT)>DIFFERENTIAL SIDE RETAINER OIL SEAL>REPLACEMENT .
    CAUTION: Be sure to replace the oil seal after removing the front drive shaft.
    NOTE: When a new oil seal has been installed, replacement is not required.
  3. Strike and bend the transmission hanger of transmission rear with a rubber hammer etc. so that it gets in contact with the transmission case.
    CAUTION: Do not apply excessive load or impact to the transmission case.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Install the transmission cushion rubber to the transmission, and tighten the bolt (A).
  5. Install the transmission cushion rubber to the center crossmember, and temporarily tighten the nut (B).

    Tightening torque: 

    Bolt (A) 

    35 N.m (3.6 kgf-m, 25.8 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Install the transmission assembly onto the engine.
    1. Lift up the transmission gradually using a transmission jack.
    2. Engage at the spline section.
      CAUTION: Be careful not to hit the main shaft against the clutch cover.
  7. While raising the transmission jack, loosen the turnbuckle of the ENGINE SUPPORT (41099AA020), and set the engine unit to the original position.
  8. Install the front crossmember and rear crossmember.
  9. Tighten the transmission cushion rubber mounting nut.

    Tightening torque: 

    T1: 70 N.m (7.1 kgf-m, 51.6 ft-lb)

    T2: 140 N.m (14.3 kgf-m, 103.3 ft-lb)

    T3: 35 N.m (3.6 kgf-m, 25.8 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  10. Take out the transmission jack.
  11. Tighten the bolts and nuts which hold the lower side of transmission to the engine.

    Tightening torque: 

    50 N.m (5.1 kgf-m, 36.9 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  12. Lower the vehicle.
  13. Install the starter. refer to STARTING/CHARGING SYSTEMS (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>STARTER>INSTALLATION .
  14. Tighten the bolts which hold the upper side of the transmission to the engine.

    Tightening torque: 

    50 N.m (5.1 kgf-m, 36.9 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  15. Remove the ENGINE SUPPORT BRACKET (41099AA012) and ENGINE SUPPORT (41099AA020).
  16. Install the engine ground terminals.

    Tightening torque: 

    19 N.m (1.9 kgf-m, 14.0 ft-lb)

  17. Install the engine harness stay and MT docking connector together as a unit.

    Tightening torque: 

    19 N.m (1.9 kgf-m, 14.0 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  18. Install the pitching stopper.

    Tightening torque: 

    T1: 50 N.m (5.1 kgf-m, 36.9 ft-lb)

    T2: 58 N.m (5.9 kgf-m, 42.8 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  19. Lift up the vehicle.
  20. Set the OIL SEAL PROTECTOR (28399SA010) to differential side retainer.
  21. Replace the circlip of the front drive shaft with a new part.
  22. Remove the front drive shaft and remove the ST.

    Special tool: 

    ST: 28399SA010 OIL SEAL PROTECTOR

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  23. Insert the front arm ball joint and tighten the mounting bolt.
    CAUTION:
    • Do not apply grease to the tapered portion of ball stud.
    • Before tightening, make sure the lower side of front axle housing and stepped section of ball joint are in contact.
    • Be careful not to damage the boot of the joint.

    Tightening torque: 

    50 N.m (5.1 kgf-m, 36.9 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  24. Replace the flange nut with a new part, and install the stabilizer link.

    Tightening torque: 

    60 N.m (6.1 kgf-m, 44.3 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  25. Install the MT gear shift cable and MT gear select cable to MT gear shift lever. refer to CONTROL SYSTEMS>MT GEAR SHIFT LEVER>INSTALLATION .
    CAUTION:
    • Do not bend MT gear shift cable and MT gear select cable at a sharp angle.
    • If the MT gear select cable is removed from the selector lever COMPL on the transmission side, replace with new MT gear select cable.
    • Do not apply excessive load while holding the part (A).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  26. Install the propeller shaft. refer to DRIVE SHAFT SYSTEM>PROPELLER SHAFT>INSTALLATION .
  27. Install the hanger bracket.

    Tightening torque: 

    23 N.m (2.3 kgf-m, 17.0 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  28. Install the center exhaust cover.

    Tightening torque: 

    18 N.m (1.8 kgf-m, 13.3 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  29. Install the rear exhaust pipe. refer to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>REAR EXHAUST PIPE>INSTALLATION .
  30. Install the front exhaust pipe. refer to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FRONT EXHAUST PIPE>INSTALLATION .
  31. Install the crossmember support - rear.

    Tightening torque: 

    70 N.m (7.1 kgf-m, 51.6 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  32. Lower the vehicle.
  33. Install the operating cylinder.

    Tightening torque: 

    37 N.m (3.8 kgf-m, 27.3 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  34. Install the engine hanger rear.

    Tightening torque: 

    21 N.m (2.1 kgf-m, 15.5 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  35. Install the transmission radio ground cord terminal.

    Tightening torque: 

    32.3 N.m (3.3 kgf-m, 23.8 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  36. Connect the air breather hose.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  37. Install the coupling harness.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  38. Pour in transmission gear oil and check the oil level. refer to MANUAL TRANSMISSION AND DIFFERENTIAL (6MT)>TRANSMISSION GEAR OIL .
  39. Install the air intake boot. refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>AIR INTAKE BOOT>INSTALLATION .
  40. Install the front tires. refer to WHEEL AND TIRE SYSTEM>TIRE AND WHEEL>INSTALLATION .
  41. Connect the battery ground terminal. refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
