Both High And Low Pressure Sides Are Low

  1. CHECK REFRIGERANT LEAKS  .

    Check the refrigerant for leaks. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>REFRIGERANT LEAK CHECK>INSPECTION .

    NOTE: When high-pressure side is less than 0.69 MPa: Go to step  2.

    Are there refrigerant leaks?

    Yes:  Repair the refrigerant leaking points.

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. FILL PROPER AMOUNT OF REFRIGERANT  .

    Drain all refrigerant, and refill proper amount of refrigerant.

    • Recovery: Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>REFRIGERANT RECOVERY PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .
    • Fill: Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>REFRIGERANT CHARGING PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .

    Is refrigerant pressure within the standard value?

    Yes:  Refrigerant pressure is normal.

    No:  Perform corresponding "INSPECTION WITH PRESSURE SYMPTOM".
