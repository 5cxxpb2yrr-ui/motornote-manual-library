CVTF Cooler (With Warmer Function): Removal

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

CAUTION: If the CVTF and engine coolant is spilt over exhaust pipe, wipe it off with cloth to avoid emitting smoke or causing a fire.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Drain engine coolant. Refer to COOLING (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE COOLANT>REPLACEMENT .
  3. Remove the clip from the air intake boot, and loosen the clamp.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the air intake boot, and place it aside so that it does not interfere with the work.
    CAUTION: Be careful not to pull out the PCV hose.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the purge hose, purge control solenoid valve harness connector and harness clip.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Remove the water hose (outlet).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Remove the CVTF cooler inlet hose and CVTF cooler outlet hose.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Remove the water hose (inlet).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Remove the CVTF cooler (with warmer feature) from the transmission.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
