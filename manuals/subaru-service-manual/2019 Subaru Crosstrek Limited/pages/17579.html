Inspection Mode J

DTC Item Condition
P0746 PRESSURE CONTROL SOLENOID "A" PERFORMANCE/STUCK OFF Perform inspection mode J twice
P0747 PRESSURE CONTROL SOLENOID "A" STUCK ON Perform inspection mode J twice

Diagnostic procedure: 

  1. Start the engine.
  2. Warm up until the ATF temperature displayed on the Subaru Select Monitor is 20°C (68°F) or more.
  3. Check the readiness code using Subaru Select Monitor and confirm that the diagnosis is not completed. Refer to TRANSMISSION (DIAGNOSTICS)>ALL READINESS DIAGNOSTIC CODE(S) .
  4. Drive in any driving pattern for 20 minutes. (Include driving at a constant legal speed (for 20 seconds) at least once.)
  5. Turn the ignition switch to OFF and wait for 30 seconds or more.
  6. Start the engine.
  7. Drive in any driving pattern for 20 minutes. (Include driving at a constant legal speed (for 20 seconds) at least once.)
  8. Turn the ignition switch to OFF and wait for 30 seconds or more.
  9. Start the engine.
  10. Check the readiness code using Subaru Select Monitor and confirm that the diagnosis is completed.
