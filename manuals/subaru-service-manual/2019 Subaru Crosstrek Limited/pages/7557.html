Rear Exhaust Pipe: Removal

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

CAUTION: Vehicle components are extremely hot after driving. Be wary of receiving burns from heated parts.
  1. Separate the front exhaust pipe and rear exhaust pipe.
    CAUTION: Be careful not to let the muffler contact the rear bumper.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Disconnect the rear exhaust pipe and muffler.
    CAUTION: Be careful not to drop the rear exhaust pipe.
    • Impreza 4 door model
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • Impreza 5 door model and Crosstrek model
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Apply a coat of spray type lubricant to the mating area of cushion rubber.
  4. Remove the rear exhaust pipe.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the center exhaust cover.
    NOTE: Remove if necessary.
    • Except for hybrid model
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • Hybrid model
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
