Diagnostic Procedure For Subaru Select Monitor Communication: Inspection

Trouble symptom: 

Cannot communicate with [Airbag].

DTC detecting condition: 

  • Defective harness connector
  • Power supply circuit malfunction
  • Defective CAN communication circuit
  • Defective Subaru Select Monitor

Wiring diagram: 

  • Impreza model
    • Airbag system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (IMPREZA MODEL)>AIRBAG SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .
    • CAN communication system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (IMPREZA MODEL)>CAN COMMUNICATION SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  • Crosstrek model
    • Airbag system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>AIRBAG SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>AIRBAG SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .
    • CAN communication system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>CAN COMMUNICATION SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>CAN COMMUNICATION SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .
Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to AIRBAG (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK BODY INTEGRATED UNIT (EXCEPT FOR HYBRID MODEL) OR CENTRAL Gateway control module (HYBRID MODEL) AND SUBARU SELECT MONITOR COMMUNICATION. 

    Connect the Subaru Select Monitor, and check that communication is possible with [Body Control] (except for hybrid model) or [Central Gateway] (hybrid model).

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  5.

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK SUBARU SELECT MONITOR. 

    Connect the Subaru Select Monitor to another vehicle, and check for the communication.

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Repair or replace the Subaru Select Monitor.

  3. CHECK DATA LINK CONNECTOR. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    2. Using the tester, measure the voltage between data link connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (i288) No. 16 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

      (i288) No. 8 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    Is the voltage 10 V or more?

    Yes:  Inspect the body integrated unit (except for hybrid model) or central gateway control module (hybrid model). Refer to BODY CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE . Refer to CENTRAL GATEWAY (DIAGNOSTICS)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .

    No:  Go to  4.

  4. CHECK FUSE. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Remove a fuse in the data link connector and visually check the fuse.

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Repair the harness between the battery and the data link connector.

    No:  Replace the fuse. If the fuse is blown out again, check the power supply circuit.

  5. CHECK AIRBAG CONTROL MODULE CONNECTOR. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF, disconnect the battery ground terminal, and wait 60 seconds or more. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    2. Confirm that the connectors of airbag control module (AB6, AB17, AB18) are securely connected.

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  6.

    No:  Connect the connector of the airbag control module.

  6. CHECK FUSE. 

    Remove fuse No. 20 from the fuse & relay box, and perform visual inspection.

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  7.

    No:  Replace the fuse. If the fuse is blown out again, check the power supply circuit.

  7. CHECK AIRBAG control module POWER SUPPLY CIRCUIT. 
    1. Disconnect the connectors (AB6, AB17, AB18) from airbag control module.
    2. Connect the connector (1AG) in the test harness AG to the connectors (AB6, AB17, AB18).
    3. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    4. Measure the voltage between connector (2AG) in the test harness AG and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (2AG) No. 4 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    Is the voltage 10 V or more?

    Yes:  Go to  8.

    No:  Repair the harness between the airbag control module and the battery. Or replace the airbag main harness along with the bulkhead harness.

  8. CHECK BETWEEN AIRBAG CONTROL MODULE AND CHASSIS GROUND (OPEN CIRCUIT). 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Measure the resistance between connector (2AG) in the test harness AG and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (2AG) No. 4 - Chassis ground:

      (2AG) No. 3 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance less than 10 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  9.

    No:  Repair the harness between the airbag control module and the chassis ground. Or replace the airbag main harness along with the bulkhead harness.

  9. CHECK POOR CONTACT OF CONNECTOR. 

    Is there poor contact of control module power supply, ground line and data link connector?

    Yes:  Repair the connector. (Replace the harness without repairing the airbag system connector.)

    No:  Replace the airbag control module. Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>AIRBAG CONTROL MODULE>REMOVAL .
