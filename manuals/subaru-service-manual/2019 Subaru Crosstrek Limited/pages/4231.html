Front Hood: Removal

CAUTION: The hood COMPL - front is heavy. When removing and installing it, always work in a team of two or more persons.
  1. Open the hood COMPL - front.
  2. Remove the nut on the hood COMPL - front side, and remove the hood COMPL - front.
