Cylinder Block

  1. Remove the oil separator cover from cylinder block RH.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Remove the liquid gasket from cylinder block RH.
  3. Remove the cylinder block plug (A) and the main gallery plug (B) from cylinder block RH.
    NOTE:
    • Remove if necessary.
    • After removing the parts, remove the liquid gasket from the thread holes of the cylinder block RH, and from the threaded portions of cylinder block plug and main gallery plug.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the cylinder block plate from cylinder block LH.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the main gallery plug from cylinder block LH.
    NOTE:
    • Remove if necessary.
    • After removing the parts, remove the liquid gasket from the thread holes of the cylinder block LH and main gallery plug.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
