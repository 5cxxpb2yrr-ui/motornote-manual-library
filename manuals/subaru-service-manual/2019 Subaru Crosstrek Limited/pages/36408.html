Sun Visor: Removal

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. (Only model with vanity mirror light) Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the sun visor assembly.
    1. Remove the cap - sun visor, and then remove the screws.
    2. Disconnect the connector. (Only model with vanity mirror light)
    3. Disconnect the connector and remove the sun visor assembly.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the hook - sun visor.
    1. Press both sides using a flat tip screwdriver.
    2. Remove the hook - sun visor by pulling it toward you.
Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
