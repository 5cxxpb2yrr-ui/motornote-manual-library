4 Door Model

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the license plate light.
    1. Release the claws as shown in the figure, and pull out the license plate light.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Disconnect the connector, and remove the license plate light.
  3. Remove the license plate light bulb.
    CAUTION: Use a dry clean cloth so that no grease or water adheres to the glass portion of the bulb.
    1. Rotate the bulb socket in the direction of arrow shown in the figure to remove the license plate light bulb.
