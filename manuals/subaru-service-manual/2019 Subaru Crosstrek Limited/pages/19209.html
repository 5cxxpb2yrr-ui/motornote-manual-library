DTC B1446: Air Mix Door Actuator Circuit Stuck (Driver)

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

DTC detecting condition: 

  • Air mix door actuator is locked.
  • The potentiometer value of the actuator does not change.

Trouble symptom: 

Temperature cannot be adjusted.

Wiring diagram: 

Air conditioning system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>AIR CONDITIONING SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK CONNECTOR. 
    1. Check connecting condition of the A/C control panel connector and air mix door actuator connector.
    2. Read the DTC of [Air Conditioner] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is DTC B1446 displayed? (Current malfunction)

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Even if DTC is displayed, the circuit has returned to a normal condition at this time.

    Reproduce the failure, and then perform the diagnosis again.

    NOTE: In this case, temporary poor contact of connector, temporary open or short circuit of harness may be the cause.
  2. CHECK ACTUATOR. 

    Using the Subaru Select Monitor, check the following items in the [Active Test] of [Air Conditioner]. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>ACTIVE TEST .

    • Air mix door actuator target open (driver's side)

    Did the air mix door actuator move to the specified target opening angle?

    Yes:  Air mix door actuator circuit is normal.

    No:  Go to  3.

  3. CHECK HARNESS. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    2. Using a tester, measure the voltage between air mix door actuator connector terminals.

      Connector & terminal 

      (i174) No. 1 (+) - (i174) No. 3 (-):

    Is the voltage 4.5 - 5.5 V?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Repair or replace the open, short circuit of the harness.

  4. CHECK HARNESS (OPEN CIRCUIT). 
    1. Disconnect the connector from the A/C control panel.
    2. Using a tester, check the continuity between A/C control panel connector and air mix door actuator connector terminals.

      Connector & terminal 

      (i174) No. 1 - (i181 No. 7

      (i174) No. 3 - (i181 No. 19

      (i174) No. 2 - (i80 No. 24

      (i174) No. 4 - (i181 No. 18

      (i174) No. 5 - (i181 No. 5

    Is there continuity?

    Yes:  Go to  5.

    No:  Repair or replace the open circuit of harness.

  5. CHECK AIR MIX DOOR ACTUATOR. 

    Check the air mix door actuator parts. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>AIR MIX DOOR ACTUATOR .

    Is the air mix door actuator normal?

    Yes:  Replace the A/C control panel. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>CONTROL PANEL .

    No:  Replace the air mix door actuator. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>AIR MIX DOOR ACTUATOR .
