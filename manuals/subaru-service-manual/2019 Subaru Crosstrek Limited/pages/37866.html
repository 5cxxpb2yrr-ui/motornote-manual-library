Charge Antenna

CAUTION: Do not detach the antenna except when replacing it. Detaching unnecessarily will lead to insecure attachment of the antenna.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the bumper face - rear. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>REAR BUMPER>REMOVAL .
  3. Take out the mat - rear floor.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the clips, and remove the box - sub-trunk.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the antenna assembly - charging inlet door.
    1. Disconnect the connector.
    2. Release the clips from the inside of the compartment, and remove the antenna assembly - charging inlet door.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
