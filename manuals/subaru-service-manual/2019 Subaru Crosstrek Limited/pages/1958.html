Pedestrian Alert Speaker: Inspection

Using the Subaru Select Monitor, perform the compulsory sound generation of the speaker. Refer to PEDESTRIAN ALERT (DIAGNOSTICS)>ACTIVE TEST>OPERATION .

When a malfunction is detected for the speaker, refer to "Diagnostics with Phenomenon" of "PEDESTRIAN ALERT SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS)" service information. Refer to PEDESTRIAN ALERT (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>LIST .
