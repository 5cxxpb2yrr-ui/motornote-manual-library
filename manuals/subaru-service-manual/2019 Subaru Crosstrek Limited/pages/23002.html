Oil Filter

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

CAUTION: Do not tighten excessively, or engine oil may leak.
  1. Clean the oil filter installation surface.
  2. Obtain a new oil filter and apply a thin coat of engine oil to the seal rubber.
  3. Install the oil filter by turning it by hand, being careful not to damage the seal rubber of the oil filter.
    • When installing oil filter with an outer diameter of 68 mm (2.68 in) (black)

      Further tighten the oil filter (approx. one rotation) after the seal rubber of the oil filter comes in contact with the installation surface.

      When using a torque wrench, tighten to 14 N.m (1.4 kgf-m, 10.3 ft-lb).

      Special tool: 

      OIL FILTER WRENCH (18332AA000)

    • When installing oil filter with an outer diameter of 67.4 mm (2.65 in) (blue)

      Further tighten the oil filter (approx. 7/8 rotation) after the seal rubber of the oil filter comes in contact with the installation surface.

      When using a torque wrench, tighten to 12 N.m (1.2 kgf-m, 8.9 ft-lb).

      Preparation tool: 

      Oil filter wrench (65/67 mm 14 Flutes)

  4. Check the engine oil level and replenish the engine oil if necessary. Refer to LUBRICATION (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE OIL>INSPECTION .
