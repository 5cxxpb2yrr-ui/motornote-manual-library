DTC P1A05-1C: Hybrid Battery Stack 3 Cell Voltage Detection Voltage Out Of Range: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Outline of diagnosis: 

Detection line of cell voltage in NO. 3 HV supply stack sub-assembly is open.

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

  • Reduced output power
  • Engine not stopped
NOTE: For the diagnostic procedure, refer to DTC P1A001C. Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE WITH DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>DTC P1A001C HYBRID BATTERY STACK 2 CELL VOLTAGE DETECTION VOLTAGE OUT OF RANGE .
