Operating Cylinder: Installation

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Install the operating cylinder.
    NOTE: Before installing, apply grease to the contact point of the release lever and operating cylinder.

    Grease 

    NICHIMOLY N-130 or equivalent

    Tightening torque: 

    37 N.m (3.8 kgf-m, 27.3 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Replace the gasket with a new part, and install the clutch hose.
    CAUTION:
    • Be sure to install with the mark side facing upward.
    • Do not twist the parts during installation.

    Tightening torque: 

    18 N.m (1.8 kgf-m, 13.3 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. After bleeding air from the operating cylinder, ensure that the clutch operates properly, Refer to CLUTCH SYSTEM>CLUTCH FLUID AIR BLEEDING>PROCEDURE .
  4. Install the air intake boot. Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>Air Intake Boot>INSTALLATION .
  5. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
