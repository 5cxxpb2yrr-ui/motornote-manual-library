Front Speaker: Removal: Tweeter

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the trim panel - front pillar UPR. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>UPPER INNER TRIM>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the grille speaker side.
    1. Release the clips, hooks and claws, then detach the grille speaker side.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    NOTE: For the grille speaker side RH, disconnect the light control sensor connector.
  4. Remove the speaker assembly - tweeter.
    1. Remove the screws.

      Preparation tool: 

      Stubby screwdriver

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Disconnect the connector and remove the speaker assembly - tweeter.
