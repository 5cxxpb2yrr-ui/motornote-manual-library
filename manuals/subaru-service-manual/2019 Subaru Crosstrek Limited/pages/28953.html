DTC B145D: Refrigerant Pressure

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

DTC detecting condition: 

When the refrigerant pressure is abnormal.

Trouble symptom: 

Compressor does not operate.

Wiring diagram: 

Air conditioning system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>AIR CONDITIONING SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK DTC. 

    Read the DTC of [Air Conditioner] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is DTC B14A3 or B14A4 displayed? (Current malfunction)

    Yes:  Perform the diagnosis for the displayed DTC B14A3 or B14A4. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK REFRIGERANT PRESSURE. 
    1. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, check the following items in the [Data monitor] of [Air Conditioner]. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DATA MONITOR .
      • Refrigerant Pressure
    2. Attach the manifold gauge set. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>REFRIGERANT PRESSURE WITH MANIFOLD GAUGE SET .
    3. Open all doors.
    4. Set the A/C control panel dials as follows.
      • A/C switch: ON
      • FRESH/RECIRC switch: RECIRC
      • Temperature control dial: MAX COOL
      • Fan dial: HI (MAX)
      • Inlet opening temperature: 25 - 35°C (77 - 95°F)

    Do the [Refrigerant Pressure] value displayed on the data monitor and the manifold gauge value match?

    Yes:  Check the refrigerant pressure function. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>REFRIGERANT PRESSURE WITH MANIFOLD GAUGE SET .

    No:  Go to  3.

  3. CHECK A/C CONTROL PANEL (PRESSURE SENSOR POWER SUPPLY CIRCUIT). 
    1. Disconnect the connector (B505) of the pressure sensor.
    2. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    3. Using a tester, measure the voltage between the pressure sensor connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (B505) No. 3 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    Is the voltage 4.5 - 5.5 V?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Go to  11.

  4. CHECK HEAT PUMP control module. 
    1. Disconnect the heat pump control module connector (i365).
    2. Using a tester, measure the voltage between the heat pump control module connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (i365) No. 23 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    Is the voltage 11 - 14 V?

    Yes:  Go to  5.

    No:  Replace the heat pump control module. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>CONTROL UNIT .

  5. CHECK PRESSURE SENSOR (PRESSURE SENSOR GROUND CIRCUIT). 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between the pressure sensor connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (B505) No. 1 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  6.

    No:  Go to  9.

  6. CHECK HARNESS (OPEN CIRCUIT). 
    1. Disconnect the A/C control panel connector (i181).
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between the pressure sensor connector and A/C control panel connector.

      Connector & terminal 

      (B505) No. 2 - (i181) No. 8:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  7.

    No:  Repair or replace the open circuit of harness.

  7. CHECK HARNESS (GROUND SHORT CIRCUIT). 

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between the pressure sensor connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (B505) No. 2 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance 10 kΩ or more?

    Yes:  Go to  8.

    No:  Repair or replace the short circuit of the harness.

  8. CHECK PRESSURE SENSOR (PRESSURE SENSOR OUTPUT CIRCUIT). 
    1. Connect the A/C control panel connector (i181).
    2. Open all doors.
    3. Set the A/C control panel dials as follows.
      • A/C switch: ON
      • FRESH/RECIRC switch: RECIRC
      • Temperature control dial: MAX COOL
      • Fan dial: HI (MAX)
      • Inlet opening temperature: 25 - 35°C (77 - 95°F)
    4. Using a tester, measure the voltage between the pressure sensor connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (B505) No. 2 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    Is the voltage 0.5 - 4.9 V?

    Yes:  Replace the A/C control panel. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>CONTROL PANEL .

    No:  Replace the pressure sensor. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>PRESSURE SENSOR .

  9. CHECK HARNESS (OPEN CIRCUIT). 
    1. Disconnect the A/C control panel connector (i181).
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between the pressure sensor connector and A/C control panel connector.

      Connector & terminal 

      (B505) No. 1 - (i181) No. 19:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  10.

    No:  Repair or replace the open circuit of harness.

  10. CHECK HARNESS (GROUND SHORT CIRCUIT). 

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between the pressure sensor connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (B505) No. 1 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance 10 kΩ or more?

    Yes:  Replace the A/C control panel. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>CONTROL PANEL .

    No:  Repair or replace the short circuit of the harness.

  11. CHECK HARNESS (OPEN CIRCUIT). 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the A/C control panel connector (i181).
    3. Using a tester, measure the resistance between the pressure sensor connector and A/C control panel connector.

      Connector & terminal 

      (B505) No. 3 - (i181) No. 7:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  12.

    No:  Repair or replace the open circuit of harness.

  12. CHECK HARNESS (GROUND SHORT CIRCUIT). 
    1. Disconnect the A/C control panel connector (i181).
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between the pressure sensor connector and A/C control panel connector, and the pressure sensor connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (B505) No. 3 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance 10 kΩ or more?

    Yes:  Replace the A/C control panel. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>CONTROL PANEL .

    No:  Repair or replace the short circuit of the harness.
