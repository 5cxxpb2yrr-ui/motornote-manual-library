EGR Temperature Sensor: Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery.

    Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .

  2. Remove the air intake duct.

    Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>AIR INTAKE DUCT>REMOVAL .

  3. Disconnect the connector (A) and remove the EGR temperature sensor.
