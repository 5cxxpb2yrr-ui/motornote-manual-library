DTC B28A1: Eyesight Communication (ECM)

Detected when the engine control module (ECM) detects the malfunction of stereo camera, or when the stereo camera or ECM is assembled incorrectly.

DTC detecting condition: 

  • Defective CAN system
  • Defective engine control module (ECM)
  • Defective stereo camera
  • Incorrect assembly of stereo camera (when the stereo camera designed for EyeSight (Ver. 2) type vehicle is installed to the EyeSight (Ver. 3) type vehicle, etc.)
  • Incorrect assembly of ECM (when the ECM designed for another model is installed, etc.)

Trouble symptom: 

  • All functions of EyeSight system do not operate.
  • EyeSight warning light illuminates.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK ECM. 

    Check the part number of the ECM.

    Is the ECM genuine?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Replace the ECM. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE CONTROL MODULE (ECM)>REMOVAL .

  2. CHECK STEREO CAMERA. 
    1. Remove the camera cover.
    2. Check the part number of the stereo camera.

    Is the stereo camera genuine?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Replace the stereo camera. Refer to EYESIGHT>STEREO CAMERA>REMOVAL .

  3. CHECK LAN SYSTEM. 

    Perform the diagnosis for LAN system. Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Perform the diagnosis for LAN system.

  4. CHECK ECM. 

    Perform the diagnosis for the engine. Refer to ENGINE (DIAGNOSTICS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  5.

    No:  Perform the diagnosis for the engine.

  5. CHECK CONNECTOR. 

    Check the ECM connector and the stereo camera connector.

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  6.

    No:  Repair or replace the connector.

  6. CHECK STEREO CAMERA. 

    Read the DTC of [EyeSight] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is DTC other than DTC B28A1 displayed?

    Yes:  Perform the diagnosis for DTC other than DTC B28A1.

    No:  Go to  7.

  7. CHECK STEREO CAMERA. 
    1. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, perform the clear memory of [EyeSight]. Refer to COMMON (DIAG)>CLEAR MEMORY .
    2. Read the DTC of [EyeSight] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is DTC B28A1 displayed?

    Yes:  Replace the stereo camera. Refer to EYESIGHT>STEREO CAMERA>REMOVAL .

    No:  Even if DTC is displayed, the circuit has returned to a normal condition at this time. Reproduce the failure, and then perform the diagnosis again.

    NOTE: In this case, temporary poor contact of connector, temporary open or short circuit of harness may be the cause.
