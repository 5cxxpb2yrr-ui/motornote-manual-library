DTC B145B: Evaporator Exit Temperature Sensor Circuit Short

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

DTC detecting condition: 

When Evaporator outlet temperature sensor circuit is shorted.

Trouble symptom: 

Compressor does not operate.

Wiring diagram: 

Air conditioning system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>AIR CONDITIONING SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK CONNECTOR. 
    1. Check connecting condition of the A/C control panel connector, heat pump control module connector and Evaporator outlet temperature sensor connector.
    2. Read the DTC of [Air Conditioner] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is DTC B145B displayed? (Current malfunction)

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Even if DTC is displayed, the circuit has returned to a normal condition at this time.

    Reproduce the failure, and then perform the diagnosis again.

    NOTE: In this case, temporary poor contact of connector, temporary open or short circuit of harness may be the cause.
  2. CHECK Interior condenser outlet temperature sensor. 
    1. Disconnect Interior condenser outlet temperature sensor. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>PIPE TEMPERATURE SENSOR .
    2. Read the DTC of [Air Conditioner] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is DTC B145A displayed? (Current malfunction)

    Yes:  Replace Evaporator outlet temperature sensor. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>PIPE TEMPERATURE SENSOR .

    NOTE: If it is not possible to remove Evaporator outlet temperature sensor unit, replace it together with the return pipe. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>HOSE AND PIPE .

    No:  Go to  3.

  3. CHECK HARNESS. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    2. Using a tester, measure the voltage between Evaporator outlet temperature sensor connector terminals.

      Connector & terminal 

      (i658) No. 1 (+) - (i658) No. 2 (-):

    Is the voltage 4.5 - 5.5 V?

    Yes:  Even if DTC is displayed, the circuit has returned to a normal condition at this time.

    Reproduce the failure, and then perform the diagnosis again.

    NOTE: In this case, temporary poor contact of connector, temporary open or short circuit of harness may be the cause.

    No:  Go to  4.

  4. CHECK HARNESS (SHORTED BETWEEN LINES). 
    1. Disconnect the heat pump control module connector.
    2. Using a tester, check the continuity between Evaporator outlet temperature sensor connector terminals.

      Connector & terminal 

      (i658) No. 1 - (i658) No. 2:

    Is there continuity?

    Yes:  Repair or replace the short circuit of the harness.

    No:  Replace the heat pump control module. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>CONTROL UNIT .
