Rear Spoiler: Installation

  1. Clean the trunk lid and spoiler attachment surface before installation.
    CAUTION: Make sure that there is a gasket attached on the clip and bolt of the spoiler assembly or garnish assembly. If a gasket is not attached, water leakage may occur.
  2. Install the rear spoiler assembly.

    Tightening torque: 

    7.5 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.5 ft-lb)
