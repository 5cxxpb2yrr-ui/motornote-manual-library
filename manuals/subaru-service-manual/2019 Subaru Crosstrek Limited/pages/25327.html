Vehicle Speed Does Not Increase Or Does Not Return To Set Speed After Res/+ Switch Has Been Pressed

  1. PERFORM REAL TIME DIAGNOSIS. 

    Check the input signal of cruise control system. Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>REAL-TIME DIAGNOSIS .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  The circuit has returned to a normal condition at this time.

    NOTE: In this case, temporary poor contact of connector, temporary open or short circuit of harness may be the cause.

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. INSPECTION OF RES/+ SWITCH. 

    Check the RES/+ switch. Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>CHECK EYESIGHT STEERING SWITCH .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Replace the ECM. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE CONTROL MODULE (ECM)>REMOVAL .

    No:  Replace the EyeSight steering switch. Refer to EYESIGHT>SWITCHES AND HARNESS .
