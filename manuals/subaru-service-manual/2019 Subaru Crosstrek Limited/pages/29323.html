DTC P1CCE-14: Sub DC/DC Converter Temperature Sensor (For Charging) Circuit Short To Ground Or Open: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Outline of diagnosis: 

Output voltage of sub DCDC converter temperature sensor was smaller than the threshold value.

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

-

NOTE: This DTC is output when the Electric vehicle charger assembly detects an error in the sub DCDC converter temperature sensor.
Therefore if this DTC was detected, replace the Electric vehicle charger assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>ELECTRIC VEHICLE CHARGER .
