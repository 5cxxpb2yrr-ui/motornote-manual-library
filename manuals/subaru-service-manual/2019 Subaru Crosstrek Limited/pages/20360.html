DTC P0BC2-1C: Hybrid/EV Battery Temperature Sensor "E" Voltage Out Of Range: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Outline of diagnosis: 

When characteristic of HV battery temperature sensor 4 is faulty

Deviation with any other HV battery temperature sensor output is large

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

Normal driving

NOTE: For the diagnostic procedure, refer to DTC P0A9B1C. Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE WITH DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>DTC P0A9B1C HYBRID/EV BATTERY TEMPERATURE SENSOR "A" VOLTAGE OUT OF RANGE .
