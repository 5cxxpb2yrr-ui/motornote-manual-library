Intake Manifold Assembly: Installation

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Connect the fuel delivery pipe (B). Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>INSTALLATION>QUICK CONNECTOR CONNECTION (TYPE A) .
  2. Connect the fuel delivery tube (A). Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>INSTALLATION>QUICK CONNECTOR CONNECTION (TYPE B) .
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Connect the fuel delivery pipe. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>INSTALLATION>QUICK CONNECTOR CONNECTION (TYPE C) .
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Install the cylinder head plate.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Set a new gasket and install the intake manifold assembly.
    NOTE: Be careful not to let the engine wiring harness be caught by the parts.

    Tightening torque: 

    25 N.m (2.5 kgf-m, 18.4 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Install the fuel delivery tube.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Connect the connector (A) and install the clip.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Install the fuel pipe protector No. 1.

    Tightening torque: 

    19 N.m (1.9 kgf-m, 14.0 ft-lb)

  9. Install the air conditioner wire with the clip (A).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  10. Set the engine wiring harness and secure the engine wiring harness to the bracket with the claw.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  11. Connect the battery positive terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  12. Install the engine wiring harness to the clip (C), and connect the connector (B) to the main fuse box.
  13. Connect connector (A) to ECM. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>ENGINE CONTROL MODULE (ECM)>INSTALLATION .
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  14. Install the clips.
  15. Install the fuel delivery tube clamp (B).
  16. Connect the connector (A).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  17. Secure the fuel pipe with the bolt.

    Tightening torque: 

    6.4 N.m (0.7 kgf-m, 4.7 ft-lb)

  18. Connect the purge hose (D).
  19. Connect the connector (B) and install the clip (C).
  20. Connect the connector (A).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  21. When the engine coolant (engine side) is not drained before removing the intake manifold assembly
    1. Install the throttle body. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>THROTTLE BODY>INSTALLATION .
    2. Install the EGR control valve. Refer to EMISSION CONTROL (AUX. EMISSION CONTROL DEVICES) (H4DO (HEV))>EGR CONTROL VALVE>INSTALLATION .
    3. Install the preheater hose.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  22. When the engine coolant (engine side) is drained before removing the intake manifold assembly
    1. Connect the preheater hose (B) and connect the connector (A).
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Install the EGR control valve. Refer to EMISSION CONTROL (AUX. EMISSION CONTROL DEVICES) (H4DO (HEV))>EGR CONTROL VALVE>INSTALLATION .
  23. Attach the PCV hose No. 1. Refer to EMISSION CONTROL (AUX. EMISSION CONTROL DEVICES) (H4DO (HEV))>PCV HOSE>INSTALLATION>PCV HOSE NO. 1 .
  24. Install the collector cover. Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>COLLECTOR COVER>INSTALLATION .
  25. Install the air intake boot. Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>Air Intake Boot>INSTALLATION .
  26. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
