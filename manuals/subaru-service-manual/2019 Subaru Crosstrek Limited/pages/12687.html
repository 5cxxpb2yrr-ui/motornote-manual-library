Seat Belt Outer - Rear RH And LH (4 Door Model)

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Before installation, perform a unit inspection of retractor. Refer to SEAT BELT SYSTEM>REAR SEAT BELT>INSPECTION .
  2. Install the seat belt outer - rear.
    CAUTION:
    • During installation, make sure that the seat belts are not twisted.
    • After installation, make sure that the seat belts are smoothly extended and wound.

    Tightening torque:

    Refer to "COMPONENT" of "General Description" for the tightening torque. Refer to SEAT BELT SYSTEM>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>COMPONENT>REAR SEAT BELT (4 DOOR MODEL) .

  3. Install the trim panel - rear pillar LWR, trim panel - rear pillar UPR, and cover side sill - rear INN.
  4. Install the rear seat cushion assembly.
  5. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  6. After installation, check the following points.
    • The seat belt is not twisted.
    • The seat belt can be pulled out and retracted smoothly.
