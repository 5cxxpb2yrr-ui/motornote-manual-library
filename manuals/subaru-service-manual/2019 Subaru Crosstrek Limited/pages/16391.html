Combination Meter: Removal

CAUTION: When the combination meter assembly is replaced, perform the variant work. Refer to INSTRUMENTATION/DRIVER INFO>COMBINATION METER SYSTEM>OPERATION>VARIANT WORK .
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery and wait for at least 60 seconds before starting work. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Release the lock, tilt the steering column to the lowest end and fully extend the column by the telescopic system.
  3. Remove the cover assembly - instrument panel LWR driver. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>INSTRUMENT PANEL LOWER COVER>REMOVAL .
  4. Remove the grille assembly - CTR ventilation LH. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>AIR VENT GRILLE>REMOVAL>CENTER GRILLE ASSEMBLY .
  5. Remove the visor assembly - combination meter.
    1. Release the clips and claws of the cover assembly - visor LWR.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Release the screws, clips and claws.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Disconnect the connector, and remove the visor assembly - combination meter.
  6. Remove the combination meter assembly.
    CAUTION: Be careful not to damage the meter glass or instrument panel.
    1. Remove the screws.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Pull the combination meter assembly toward you and disconnect the connector.
