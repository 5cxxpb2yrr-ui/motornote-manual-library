Reduction Driven Gear: Adjustment

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Measure the height "A" from the ST upper face to the ball bearing catch surface.

    Preparation tool: 

    ST: GAUGE (499575600)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Measure the height "B" from the ST to the mating surface of the transmission case.

    Preparation tool: 

    ST: GAUGE (499575600)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Obtain the thickness of transfer drive gear shim using the following formula to select one to three transfer drive gear shims.

    T mm = A - B - (0.05 - 0.25)

    [T in = A - B - (0.002 - 0.01)]

    T: Shim thickness

    1. Height from the ST upper face to the ball bearing catch surface
    2. Height from ST to transmission case mating surface

    0.05 - 0.25 mm (0.002 - 0.01 in): Clearance

    Transfer drive gear shim
    Part No. Thickness mm (in)
    33279AA090 0.3 (0.012)
    33279AA100 0.4 (0.016)
    33279AA110 0.5 (0.020)
