Sun Visor: Installation

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Install the hook - sun visor.
  2. Install the sun visor assembly.
    CAUTION: Do not rotate the sun visor assembly when it is temporarily installed. The claw for temporary installation may be damaged.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.

    Tightening torque: 

    2 N.m (0.2 kgf-m, 1.5 ft-lb)

  3. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
