Outline Of Diagnosis

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

When communication by control modules on CAN Bus takes longer than a certain time when in the bus off state (number of sending errors exceeds a certain value) or the load on the bus is high, the electric brake booster control module judges that there is a CAN communication error and illuminates the MIL and stores a DTC.
