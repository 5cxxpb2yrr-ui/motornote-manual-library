Power Connect Cover

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
WARNING: The hybrid system includes a high voltage circuit. Mishandling could cause accidents such as electric shock or leak. Always check "WARNING (HYBRID SYSTEM)" and perform the proper operation.
Refer to PRECAUTION/WARNING>WARNING (HYBRID SYSTEM) .
  1. Apply liquid gasket to the mating surface of the power connect cover as shown in the figure.
    NOTE:
    • Before applying liquid gasket, degrease the old liquid gasket seal surfaces of the power connect cover and transmission body.
    • Install within 5 min. after applying liquid gasket.

    Liquid gasket: 

    THREE BOND 1217B (Part No. K0877YA020) or equivalent

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Temporarily attach the power connect cover.
    WARNING: Be sure to wear insulated gloves.
  3. Tighten the bolts equally.
    WARNING: Be sure to wear insulated gloves.

    Tightening torque: 

    5 N.m (0.5 kgf-m, 3.7 ft-lb)

  4. Install the service disconnect plug.

    Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG>INSTALLATION .

  5. Connect the battery ground terminal.

    Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
