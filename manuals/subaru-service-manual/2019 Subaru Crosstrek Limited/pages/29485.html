Pitching Stopper

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Install the air breather hose and install the pitching stopper bracket.

    Tightening torque: 

    41 N.m (4.2 kgf-m, 30.2 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Install the pitching stopper.

    Tightening torque: 

    T1: 50 N.m (5.1 kgf-m, 36.9 ft-lb)

    T2: 58 N.m (5.9 kgf-m, 42.8 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Install the air intake boot.

    Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>Air Intake Boot>INSTALLATION .
