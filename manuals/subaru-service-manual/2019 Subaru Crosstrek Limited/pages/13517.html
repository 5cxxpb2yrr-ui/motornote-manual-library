DTC P0713: Transmission Fluid Temperature Sensor "A" Circuit High: Notes

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

1. - 

DTC detecting condition: 

Immediately at fault recognition

Trouble symptom: 

  • Excessive shift shock
  • Shift characteristics malfunction

Wiring diagram: 

CVT control system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (IMPREZA MODEL)>CVT CONTROL SYSTEM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>CVT CONTROL SYSTEM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: Use CHECK BOARD when measuring the TCM terminal voltage and resistance. After servicing or replacing faulty parts, perform the last step of the basic diagnostic procedure. Refer to TRANSMISSION (DIAGNOSTICS)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE .
  1. CHECK HARNESS  .
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the TCM connector.
    3. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    4. Measure the voltage between TCM connectors.

    Connector & terminal 

    (T10) No. 6 (+) - (T10) No. 1 (-):

    Is the voltage 5 V or more?

    Yes:  Repair the short circuit of harness.

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK ATF TEMPERATURE SENSOR  .
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Measure the resistance between TCM connector terminals.

    Connector & terminal 

    (T10) No. 1 - No. 6:

    Is the resistance 1 MΩ or more?

    Yes:  Repair the open circuit of transmission harness.

    No:  Go to  3.

  3. CHECK ATF TEMPERATURE SENSOR  .
    1. Connect the connector to TCM.
    2. Start the engine.
    3. Warm up until the ATF temperature exceeds 50°C (122°F).
    4. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    5. Disconnect the TCM connector.
    6. Measure the resistance between TCM connector terminals.

    Connector & terminal 

    (T10) No. 1 - No. 6:

    Is the resistance 650 - 990 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Go to  6.

  4. CHECK ATF TEMPERATURE SENSOR  .

    Measure the resistance between TCM connector terminals.

    Connector & terminal 

    (T10) No. 1 - No. 6:

    Does the resistance value increase gradually while the ATF temperature decreases?

    Yes:  Go to  5.

    No:  Replace the control valve body. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>CONTROL VALVE BODY .

  5. CHECK INPUT SIGNAL FOR TCM  .
    1. Connect the connector to TCM.
    2. Turn the ignition switch to ON. (Do not start engine.)
    3. Read the data of [ATF Temp.] using Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to TRANSMISSION (DIAGNOSTICS)>DATA MONITOR .

    Does the ATF temperature gradually decrease?

    Yes:  Check for poor contact of the ATF temperature sensor and TCM connector harness, and repair the defective part.

    No:  Go to  6.

  6. CHECK FOR POOR CONTACT  .

    Is there poor contact of ATF temperature sensor circuit?

    Yes:  Repair the poor contact.

    No:  Replace the TCM. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>TRANSMISSION CONTROL MODULE (TCM) .
