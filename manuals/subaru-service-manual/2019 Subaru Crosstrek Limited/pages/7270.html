Engine Oil Pressure: Inspection

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the oil pressure switch. Refer to LUBRICATION (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>OIL PRESSURE SWITCH>REMOVAL .
  3. Install the oil pressure gauge to the installation area for the oil pressure switch of the chain cover.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  5. Navigate to the service mode (for exhaust gas measurement). (Hybrid model) Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>Work Support>OPERATION>SERVICE MODE .
    NOTE: If it is necessary to run the engine for 10 minutes or more, fasten the driver's seat belt.
  6. Start the engine, and check the oil pressure.
    NOTE:
    • Standard value is based on an engine oil temperature of 80°C (176°F).
    • If the oil pressure is out of the specification, check oil pump, oil filter and lubrication line. Refer to LUBRICATION (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE LUBRICATION SYSTEM TROUBLE IN GENERAL>INSPECTION .
    • If the oil pressure warning light is ON and oil pressure is within standard, check the oil pressure switch. Refer to LUBRICATION (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE LUBRICATION SYSTEM TROUBLE IN GENERAL>INSPECTION .

    Engine oil pressure: 

    Except for hybrid model 

    At 600 r/min 

    Standard 

    50 kPa (0.5 kg/cm2 , 7 psi) or more

    At 3, 000r/min 

    Standard 

    230 kPa (2.3 kg/cm2 , 33 psi) or more

    Hybrid model 

    At 2, 000r/min 

    Standard 

    200 kPa (2.0 kg/cm2 , 29 psi) or more

  7. After inspection, install the related parts in the reverse order of removal.
