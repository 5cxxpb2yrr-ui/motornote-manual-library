Inspection Mode 29

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
Code Note Number of driving cycles on the detecting condition
P33E600 - 1
P33E700 - 1
P33E800 - 1
P33E900 - 1
P33EA00 - 1

Diagnostic procedure:

  1. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, perform the clear memory of [P-HEV High Voltage Battery]. Refer to COMMON (DIAG)>CLEAR MEMORY .
  2. Drive until the display changes from READY ON EV to HV.
  3. Turn to IG OFF, and wait for 1.5 hours or more.
  4. Charge until the plug-in charging is fully completed.
  5. After charging is completed, wait for 35 minutes or more.
  6. Turn to IG ON, and wait for 10 seconds or more.
  7. Turn to IG OFF, and wait for two minutes or more.
  8. Drive until the display changes from READY ON EV to HV.
  9. Turn to IG OFF, and wait for 1.5 hours or more.
  10. Charge until the plug-in charging is fully completed.
  11. After charging is completed, wait for 35 minutes or more.
  12. Turn to IG ON, and wait for 10 seconds or more.
  13. Read the [Readiness code] of [P-HEV High Voltage Battery] using Subaru Select Monitor and check that the concerned self-diagnosis is completed. Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>ALL READINESS DIAGNOSTIC CODE(S) .
    NOTE: If the concerned DTC is displayed, the self-diagnosis of the DTC is not complete. Repeat from step  2.
  14. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, read the DTC of [P-HEV High Voltage Battery] to check that there is no DTC displayed. Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .
    NOTE: If the DTC is displayed on the screen, the trouble is still present. Perform the diagnosis for the displayed DTCs.
