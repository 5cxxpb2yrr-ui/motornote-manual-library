Preparation For The Inspection Mode

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Check that the battery voltage is 12 V or more and fuel remains approx. half [20 - 40 L (5.3 - 10.6 US gal, 4.4 - 8.8 Imp gal)].
  2. After performing the diagnostics and clearing memory, check that no DTC remains.
NOTE: Perform the inspection mode after warming up the engine except when the ATF temperature at engine start is specified.
