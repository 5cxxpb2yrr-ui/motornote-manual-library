Diagnostic Method

Power supply voltage ratio of brake stroke sensor input 1 and brake stroke sensor power supply 1 is higher than 0.94.

JUDGMENT VALUE SPECIFICATION

Malfunction Criteria Threshold Value
Brake stroke sensor input 1/Brake stroke sensor power supply 1 More than 0.94

Time needed for diagnosis:  200 ms

Malfunction indicator light illumination:  Illuminates as soon as a malfunction occurs.
