DTC B2B21: Passenger's Door Power Window Manual Up Switch Stuck

DTC detecting condition: 

When UP terminal input ON is received for 20±1 seconds or longer.

When the front power window sub-switch is operated, passenger's side window does not work.

Trouble symptom: 

  • When the power window sub-switch is operated, passenger's side window does not work.
  • When the power window sub-switch is operated, passenger's side window rolls down only in manual down operation, or inches up. (Inch up: Operating the switch rolls up the window about 10 mm only)

CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to POWER WINDOW (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK FRONT POWER WINDOW SUB-SWITCH UNIT. 

    Perform the inspection of front power window sub-switch unit. Refer to GLASS/WINDOWS/MIRRORS>POWER WINDOW CONTROL SWITCH .

    Is the front power window sub-switch OK?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Replace the front power window sub-switch. Refer to GLASS/WINDOWS/MIRRORS>POWER WINDOW CONTROL SWITCH .

  2. CHECK HARNESS (OPEN CIRCUIT). 
    1. Disconnect the front power window sub-switch connector.
    2. Disconnect the front regulator & motor assembly (passenger's side) connector.
    3. Using a tester, measure the resistance between front power window sub-switch connector and front regulator & motor assembly connector (passenger's side).

      Connector & terminal 

      (D17) No. 6 - (D13) No. 8:

      Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

      Yes:  Go to  3.

      No:  Repair the open circuit of the harness between front power window sub-switch connector and front regulator & motor assembly connector (passenger's side).

  3. CHECK HARNESS (GROUND SHORT CIRCUIT). 

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between the front power window sub-switch connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (D17) No. 6 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance 1 MΩ or more?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Repair the open harness causing short-to ground condition between front power window sub-switch connector and front regulator & motor assembly connector (passenger's side).

  4. CHECK HARNESS (SHORT CIRCUIT TO POWER SUPPLY). 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    2. Using a tester, measure the voltage between the front power window sub-switch connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (D17) No. 6 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

      Is the voltage less than 1 V?

      Yes:  Go to  5.

      No:  Repair the open harness causing short-to power supply condition between front power window sub-switch connector and front regulator & motor assembly connector (passenger's side).

  5. ACTIVE TEST. 

    Using the Subaru Select Monitor, check the following items in the [Active Test] of [Power Window (Passenger)]. Refer to POWER WINDOW (DIAGNOSTICS)>ACTIVE TEST .

    • Power Window Operation Test

    Does the window roll up and down?

    Yes:  Go to  6.

    No:  Replace the front regulator & motor assembly (passenger's side). Refer to GLASS/WINDOWS/MIRRORS>FRONT REGULATOR AND MOTOR ASSEMBLY .

  6. CHECK DTC. 

    Read the DTC of [Power Window (Passenger)] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to POWER WINDOW (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is DTC B2B21 displayed? (Current malfunction)

    Yes:  Replace the front regulator & motor assembly (passenger's side). Refer to GLASS/WINDOWS/MIRRORS>FRONT REGULATOR AND MOTOR ASSEMBLY .

    No:  Even if DTC is displayed, the circuit has returned to a normal condition at this time.

    Reproduce the failure, and then perform the diagnosis again.

    NOTE: In this case, temporary poor contact of connector, temporary open or short circuit of harness may be the cause.
