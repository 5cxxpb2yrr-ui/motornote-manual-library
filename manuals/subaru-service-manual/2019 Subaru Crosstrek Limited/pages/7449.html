Basic Inspection Of Radiator Fan System

  1. CHECK DTC  .

    Read the DTC using the Subaru Select Monitor.

    NOTE: For detailed operation procedures, refer to "Help" of application.

    Is DTC detected?

    Yes: 

    1. Repair the corresponding system.
    2. Go to  2.

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. RADIATOR FAN SYSTEM OPERATION CHECK  .
    NOTE: If the radiator fans are always running, go to "INSPECTION WHEN RADIATOR FANS DO NOT STOP". Refer to COOLING (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>RADIATOR FAN SYSTEM>INSPECTION>INSPECTION WHEN RADIATOR FANS DO NOT STOP .
    1. Install the delivery mode fuse. Refer to PRE-DELIVERY INSPECTION>PRE-DELIVERY INSPECTION (PDI) PROCEDURE>DELIVERY MODE .
    2. Turn to IG ON.
    3. Check the radiator fan operation.

    Do the radiator main fan and sub fan rotate in the order of "OFF → Low speed revolution → High speed revolution"?

    Yes:  System is normal.

    Restore the vehicle to its original state.

    No:  Inspect corresponding malfunction using "Table for Diagnostics Procedure with Phenomenon".
