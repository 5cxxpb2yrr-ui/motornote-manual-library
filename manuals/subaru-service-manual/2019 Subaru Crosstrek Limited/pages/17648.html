DTC P0724: Brake Switch "B" Circuit High: Notes

1. - 

DTC detecting condition: 

Immediately at fault recognition

Trouble symptom: 

Gear is not shifted down when climbing a hill or driving down a hill.

Wiring diagram: 

  • Model without EyeSight and model without X mode Stop light system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (IMPREZA MODEL)>STOP LIGHT SYSTEM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>STOP LIGHT SYSTEM .
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  • Model with EyeSight or model with X mode

Stop light system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (IMPREZA MODEL)>STOP LIGHT SYSTEM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>STOP LIGHT SYSTEM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: Use CHECK BOARD when measuring the TCM terminal voltage and resistance. After servicing or replacing faulty parts, perform the last step of the basic diagnostic procedure. Refer to TRANSMISSION (DIAGNOSTICS)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE .
  1. CHECK STOP LIGHT SWITCH  .
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the stop light switch connector.
    3. Measure the resistance between stop light switch.

    Connector & terminal 

    (B65) No. 1 - No. 2:

    Is the resistance 1 MΩ or more?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Replace the stop light switch. Refer to BRAKE>STOP LIGHT SWITCH .

  2. CHECK HARNESS  .
    1. Disconnect the TCM connector.
    2. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    3. Measure the voltage between TCM connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (T10) No. 29 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    Is the voltage 10 V or more?

    Yes:  Repair the short circuit of harness.

    No:  Go to  3.

  3. CHECK INPUT SIGNAL FOR TCM  .
    1. Connect all connectors.
    2. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    3. Read the data of [Stop Light Switch] using Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to TRANSMISSION (DIAGNOSTICS)>DATA MONITOR .

    Is "OFF" displayed?

    Yes:  Current condition is normal. Check for poor contact in connectors or harnesses, and repair the defective part.

    No:  Go to  4.

  4. CHECK FOR POOR CONTACT  .

    Is there poor contact in the stop light switch input signal circuit?

    Yes:  Repair the poor contact.

    No:  Replace the TCM. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>TRANSMISSION CONTROL MODULE (TCM) .
