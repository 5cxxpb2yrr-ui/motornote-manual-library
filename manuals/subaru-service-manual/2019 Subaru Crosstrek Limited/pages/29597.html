DTC B28B1: Stereo Camera Automatic Adjustment

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Detected when the optical axis of stereo camera is deviated in lateral direction or when the fluctuation range of automatic adjustment value has expanded.

NOTE: Perform the adjustment of the stereo camera. Refer to EYESIGHT>CAMERA ADJUSTMENT, INSPECTION>PROCEDURE .
If the same DTC is still detected after adjustment, replace the stereo camera. Refer to EYESIGHT>STEREO CAMERA>REMOVAL .
