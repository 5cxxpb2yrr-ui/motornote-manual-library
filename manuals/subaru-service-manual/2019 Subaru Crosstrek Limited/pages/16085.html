Water Pump

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Set a new gasket, and install the water pump.

    Tightening torque: 

    6.4 N.m (0.7 kgf-m, 4.7 ft-lb)

  2. Install the front exhaust pipe.

    Refer to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FRONT EXHAUST PIPE>INSTALLATION .

  3. Install the water pump pulley.

    Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>WATER PUMP>INSTALLATION>WATER PUMP PULLEY .

  4. Connect the battery ground terminal.

    Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .

  5. Fill engine coolant. (Engine side)

    Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>ENGINE COOLANT>REPLACEMENT>FILLING OF ENGINE COOLANT>ENGINE SIDE .
