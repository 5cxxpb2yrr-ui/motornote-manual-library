METHOD OF DIAGNOSIS (MT model)

Judge NG when the malfunction criteria below are completed determined times or more after the neutral SW change.

JUDGMENT VALUE SPECIFICATION

Malfunction Criteria Threshold Value
Neutral switch output voltage Battery voltage x 0.19 V or less

Time needed for diagnosis:  1time(s)

Malfunction indicator light illumination:  Illuminates when malfunction occurs in 2 continuous driving cycles.
