Following Distance Does Not Change, Even After The Following Distance Switch Is Pressed

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. PERFORM REAL TIME DIAGNOSIS. 

    Check the input signal of cruise control system. Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>REAL-TIME DIAGNOSIS .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  The circuit has returned to a normal condition at this time.

    NOTE: In this case, temporary poor contact of connector, temporary open or short circuit of harness may be the cause.

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. INSPECTION OF FOLLOWING DISTANCE SWITCH. 

    Check the following distance switch. Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>CHECK EYESIGHT STEERING SWITCH .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Replace the stereo camera. Refer to EYESIGHT>STEREO CAMERA>REMOVAL .

    No:  Replace the EyeSight steering switch. Refer to EYESIGHT>SWITCHES AND HARNESS .
