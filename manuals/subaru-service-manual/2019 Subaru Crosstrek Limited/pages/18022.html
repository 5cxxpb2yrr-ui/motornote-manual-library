CVTF Filter: Removal

NOTE:
  • Although CVTF filter is a maintenance-free part, replace it if a large quantity of wear debris and metal particles are found in CVTF and CVTF filter.
  • For removal of CVTF filter, refer to "Transmission Case". Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>TRANSMISSION CASE>REMOVAL . Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>TRANSMISSION CASE>DISASSEMBLY .
