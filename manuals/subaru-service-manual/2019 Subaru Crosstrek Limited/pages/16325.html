DTC B1503: Meter Control Module CAN Register Stuck

DTC detecting condition: 

Combination meter CAN controller malfunction

Trouble symptom: 

Normal operation becomes impossible because the communication with other parts are not established.

  1. CHECK DTC. 

    Read the DTC of [Combination Meter] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to COMBINATION METER (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is DTC B1502 displayed? (Current malfunction)

    Yes:  Replace the combination meter. (Defective hardware or software) Refer to INSTRUMENTATION/DRIVER INFO>COMBINATION METER .

    No:  Replace the combination meter. (Temporarily defective hardware or software) Refer to INSTRUMENTATION/DRIVER INFO>COMBINATION METER .
