Drum In Disc Type Model

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Measure the inner diameter of the rear disc rotor. If scoring or worn is found on the disc, replace the rear disc rotor.

    Disc rotor inner diameter: 

    Specification: 170 mm (6.69 in)

    Service limit: 171 mm (6.73 in)

  2. Measure the lining thickness. If it exceeds the limit, replace the brake shoe.

Lining thickness: 

Standard: 3.5 mm (0.14 in)

Service limit: 1.5 mm (0.059 in)

NOTE: Replace the right and left parking brake shoe as a set.
