Engine Coolant Temperature Sensor: Removal

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Drain engine coolant. Refer to COOLING (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>Engine Coolant>REPLACEMENT>DRAINING OF ENGINE COOLANT .
  3. Remove the clip from the air intake boot, and loosen the clamp.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the air intake boot, and place it aside so that it does not interfere with the work.
    CAUTION: Be careful not to pull out the PCV hose.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Disconnect the connector (A) from the engine coolant temperature sensor, and remove the engine coolant temperature sensor using the ST.
NOTE: STRUT MOUNT SOCKET (20099 Pa000) can also be used.

Preparation tool: 

ST: STRUT MOUNT SOCKET (20399AG000)
