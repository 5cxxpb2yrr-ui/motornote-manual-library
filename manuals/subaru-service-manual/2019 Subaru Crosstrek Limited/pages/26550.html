DTC U019B-87: Lost Communication With Hybrid/EV Battery Charger Control Module Missing Message: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

1. - 

Outline of diagnosis: 

Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly could not receive the signal from Electric vehicle charger assembly

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

Normal driving

Wiring diagram: 

Hybrid system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK DTC  .

    Read the DTC of [Plug-in Battery Charger] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to PLUG-IN BATTERY CHARGER (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Are any DTCs displayed?

    Yes:  Perform the diagnosis for the displayed DTCs. Refer to PLUG-IN BATTERY CHARGER (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK Electric vehicle charger assembly (AMD2 VOLTAGE)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Check that the Service plug is disconnected. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .
    2. Turn the ignition to OFF.
    3. Connect the ground terminal to Battery (12V). Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    4. Using a tester, measure the voltage between Electric vehicle charger assembly connector and chassis ground. (With the connector connected)

      Connector & terminal 

      (R516) No. 1 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    Is the voltage 11 - 14 V?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Check the power supply circuit of Electric vehicle charger assembly.

  3. CHECK Electric vehicle charger assembly (IGCT VOLTAGE) (OPEN CIRCUIT)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Turn to IG ON.
    2. Using a tester, measure the voltage between Electric vehicle charger assembly connector and chassis ground. (With the connector connected)

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV31) No. 6 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    Is the voltage 11 - 14 V?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Check the ignition power supply circuit of Electric vehicle charger assembly.

  4. CHECK Electric vehicle charger assembly (GROUND)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Turn the ignition to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the ground terminal from Battery (12V). Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    3. Using a tester, measure the resistance between the unit case of Electric vehicle charger assembly and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      Electric vehicle charger assembly unit case - chassis ground:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  5.

    No:  Electric vehicle charger assembly has a case ground structure. Therefore, check the ground bolt tightening condition when the inspection result is outside specification.

  5. CHECK THE Electric vehicle charger assembly UNIT  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Disconnect Electric vehicle charger assembly connector (HV31).
    2. Using the tester, measure the resistance between Electric vehicle charger assembly terminals.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV31) No. 16 - (HV31) No. 17:

    Is the resistance 54 - 69 Ω?

    Yes:  Replace Electric vehicle charger assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>ELECTRIC VEHICLE CHARGER .

    No:  Go to  6.

  6. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly - Electric vehicle charger assembly)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Disconnect Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector (i369).
    2. Disconnect Battery ECU assembly connector (HV42).
    3. Using a tester, measure the resistance between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector and Electric vehicle charger assembly connector.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV31) No. 16 - (i369) No. 35:

      (HV31) No. 17 - (i369) No. 34:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  7.

    No:  Repair or replace the Wire harness between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector and Electric vehicle charger assembly connector.

  7. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly - Electric vehicle charger assembly) (SHORT TO GROUND OR LINE-TO-LINE SHORT)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between terminals in Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector, and between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (i369) No. 16 - (i369) No. 1 - 35:

    (i369) No. 16 - Chassis ground:

    (i369) No. 17 - (i369) No. 1 - 35:

    (i369) No. 17 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance 10 kΩ or more?

    Yes:  Replace Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL MODULE .

    No:  Repair or replace the Wire harness between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector and Electric vehicle charger assembly connector.
