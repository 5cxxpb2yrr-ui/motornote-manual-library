Discharging The Battery Using The Li-ION Battery Discharge Tool

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
WARNING: Be sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles.
CAUTION: When installing the Li-ion battery discharge tool, pay attention to the following items.
  • Install in a stable location.
  • Do not install in a location where there is much dust or where there is contact with water.
  • Do not install facing in a direction that will make it difficult to check the LED.
  • Do not block the air intake hole or cooling fan.
  1. Select the Li-ion battery connection harness.
    1. Check the Li-ion battery connection harness model at the position shown in the figure below. (Model: TM4216-54, TM4216-55)
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Install the Li-ion battery connection harness.
    1. Install the Li-ion battery discharge tool facing in the direction shown in the figure.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Connect connector A of the Li-ion battery connection harness to connector A on the Li-ion battery discharge tool.
      CAUTION: Check that they are securely locked.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Connect connector B of the Li-ion battery connection harness to connector B on the Li-ion battery discharge tool.
      CAUTION: Check that they are securely locked.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    4. Install the Li-ion battery discharge tool facing in the direction shown in the figure.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    NOTE: Lift up the Li-ion battery discharge tool so that the cooling fan intake hole and exhaust hole are not blocked.
  3. Install the Li-ion battery connection harness.
    1. Remove the EV charger assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>ELECTRIC VEHICLE CHARGER>REMOVAL .
    2. As shown in the figure, raise the lock lever and disconnect the 5 HV battery high voltage connectors (orange) from the battery ECU assembly.
      CAUTION:
      • Because the lock lever cannot be operated unless the lever lock is released, be sure to release the lever lock.
        Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
      • If the connectors cannot be disconnected, check that the lock lever is in the unlock position.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Discharge the HV supply stack sub-assemblies.
      1. When discharging the No. 1 and No. 2 HV supply stack sub-assemblies:
        1. Connect the 2 HV battery high voltage connectors (orange) to the connectors of the Li-ion battery connection harness.
          Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
        2. Check that all LED shown in the figure are illuminated.
        WARNING: If not all of the LED shown in the figure are illuminated, it will not be possible to discharge all of the cells. Therefore after finishing discharge work with the Li-ion battery discharge tool, also perform discharge by immersing the battery in salt water.
        Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.

    4. Check that the cooling fan is running.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    NOTE:
    • In order to reliably discharge the HV battery voltage, continue discharge for at least 1 hour after all LED have turned OFF.
    • If discharge was started when the HV battery is fully charged, discharge will be completed in a maximum of approximately 25 hours. (This number includes the 1 hour of continuing discharge after the LEDs turn OFF.)
  4. Check that discharge was completed.
    1. Check that all LED on the Li-ion battery discharge tool are OFF.
    2. After all LED on the Li-ion battery discharge tool turn OFF, continue discharge for at least 1 hour.
  5. Disconnect the Li-ion battery connection harness.
    1. When discharging the No. 1 and No. 2 HV supply stack sub-assemblies:
      1. Disconnect the 2 HV battery high voltage connectors (orange) from the Li-ion battery connection harness.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. When discharging the No. 3, No. 4, and No. 5 HV supply stack sub-assemblies:
      1. Disconnect the 3 HV battery high voltage connectors (orange) from the Li-ion battery connection harness.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Disconnect the Li-ion battery connection harness.
    1. Install the Li-ion battery discharge tool facing in the direction shown in the figure.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Disconnect connector B of the Li-ion battery connection harness from connector B on the Li-ion battery discharge tool.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Disconnect connector A of the Li-ion battery connection harness from connector A on the Li-ion battery discharge tool.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
