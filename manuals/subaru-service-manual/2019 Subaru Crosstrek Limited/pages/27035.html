Motor Resolver Circuit

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
NOTE:
  • When the faulty phenomenon is not reproduced, perform a driving test on a road where vehicle vibration tends to occur so that the phenomenon may occur.
  • When the resolver has a malfunction, the vehicle may not be able to drive smoothly.
  • When it is difficult to check the connector for connecting condition, terminal deformation. poor crimping, reconnect the connector after disconnecting once, and read DTCs again to check reproduction of the condition.
  • It may be poor connection of the connector if the diagnostic condition is not reproduced. However, the diagnostic detection value may temporarily be outside the threshold due to "vibrations", "temperature change due to time passage", etc. because of the connector inspection procedure. Therefore, be sure to perform the following inspection to check that there is no abnormality in other areas.

      T: Temperature (°C (°F)) at measuring the resistance

GENERAL DESCRIPTION 

The malfunction cause may be the resolver of the motor.

Check the internal resistance of Motor resolver and connecting condition from the inverter to the resolver.

Check related parts 

Part Inspection
Wire harness and connector between the inverter and Motor resolver Check for short circuit between Wire harness and +B side
Resolver, Wire harness, connector Check the resistance including the resolver and Wire harness, resistance between the parts and chassis ground, and connecting condition of the connector
Wire harness Check Wire harness unit for open and short circuit

Explanation of circuits 

The resolver is a sensor that detects the magnetic pole position of the motor rotor. Grasping the magnetic pole position is essential in order to control the motor correctly. The resolver has a built-in stator with a magnetizing coil and two detection coils (S and C) attached. Because the rotor is oval, the gap between the stator and rotor changes as the rotor rotates. Alternating current of a constant frequency flows in the magnetizing coil, and the alternating current is output from the detection coils (S and C) according to the sensor rotor position.

Motor generator control ECU incorporated in Inverter with converter assembly detects the absolute position of the rotor based on the waveform phase and waveform height of the detection coils (S and C). Also, it is used as a speed sensor by calculating the amount of position change by CPU within a predetermined period of time.

Motor generator control ECU incorporated in Inverter with converter assembly monitors the output signals of Motor resolver and detects abnormal.
