Pressure Sensor: Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Using the refrigerant recovery system, discharge refrigerant. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>REFRIGERANT RECOVERY PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .
  2. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  3. Remove the pressure sensor.
    1. Disconnect the connector.
    2. Remove the pressure sensor as shown in the figure.
    CAUTION:
    • Do not deform the pipe.
    • Seal the engaging instructionss of the disconnected pipe and the sensor with a plug or vinyl tape to prevent foreign matter from entering.
    • Do not reuse the sensor.
