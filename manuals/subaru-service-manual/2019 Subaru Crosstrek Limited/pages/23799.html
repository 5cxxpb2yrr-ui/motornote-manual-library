EGR Cooler: Installation

  1. Set new gasket to the stud bolt.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Set the EGR cooler, and temporarily tighten the nuts and bolts securing the EGR cooler to the EGR control valve and cylinder head RH.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Tighten the nuts which secure the EGR cooler to the cylinder head RH in the order of (1) → (2) → (3).

    Tightening torque: 

    T1: 9 N.m (0.9 kgf-m, 6.6 ft-lb)

  4. Tighten the nuts which secure the EGR cooler to the EGR control valve in the order of (4) → (5).

    Tightening torque: 

    T2: 19 N.m (1.9 kgf-m, 14.0 ft-lb)

  5. Tighten the bolts (6) which secure the EGR cooler to the cylinder head RH.

    Tightening torque: 

    T3: 6.4N.m (0.7 kgf-m, 4.7 ft-lb)

    Fig 1: EGR Cooler To Cylinder Head RH Nuts Tightening Sequence
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Connect the water hose (A), water hose (B) and the water hose (C).
    NOTE: Connecting the water hose (C) is only for the CVT model.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Install the air intake boot. Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>Air Intake Boot>INSTALLATION .
  8. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  9. Fill engine coolant. Refer to COOLING (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE COOLANT>REPLACEMENT>FILLING OF ENGINE COOLANT .
