Explanation For Circuits

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Battery ECU assembly performs warning to the driver and failsafe control based on the faulty signals. DTC is output due to decreased SOC because of the shift position remaining N, lack of fuel or faulty HV system.
