Repair Contents: Note: Fuse

Backup fuse (30 A) remains removed to prevent the battery consumption when vehicles are delivered. Install the backup fuse by following the illustration. Besides, removing the backup fuse from the fuse holder deactivates the control during shipping (delivery mode).

NOTE: For models with LED headlight, park the vehicle on a horizontal ground when performing this procedure.
