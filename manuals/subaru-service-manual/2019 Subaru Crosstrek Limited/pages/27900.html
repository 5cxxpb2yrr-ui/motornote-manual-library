Diagnostic Method

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

If the duration of time while the following conditions are met is longer than the time indicated, judge as NG.

JUDGMENT VALUE SPECIFICATION

Malfunction Criteria Threshold Value
Minimum stop position Or, |Throttle opening angle when the ignition switch is ON - Throttle minimum stop position| Less than 10.036° or More than 19.964° Less than 1.07°

Time needed for diagnosis:  8ms - 80ms

Malfunction indicator light illumination:  Illuminates as soon as a malfunction occurs.
