DTC P1CE2-92: PCU Interlock Performance Or Incorrect Operation: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Outline of diagnosis: 

In the case of any one of the following conditions

  • The Service plug, Inverter cover, etc. were removed, while the vehicle was stopped
  • The interlock signal line was disconnected, while the vehicle was stopped

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

Hybrid system stopped

NOTE: For the diagnostic procedure, refer to DTC P1CE213. Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE WITH DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>DTC P1CE213 PCU INTERLOCK CIRCUIT OPEN .
