Section 5 (M/T Clutch System): Flywheel: Installation

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Press-in a new pilot bearing to the position where it is flush with the flywheel surface or up to 0.4 mm (0.016 in) deeper than that.
    CAUTION: Do not press the bearing inner ring.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Temporarily install the crankshaft position sensor plate and flywheel.
    NOTE: Align the knock pin hole of the crankshaft position sensor plate to the knock pin of the crankshaft to secure the knock pin.
  3. Install the ST.

    Special tool: 

    ST: 498497100 CRANKSHAFT STOPPER

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Tighten the flywheel mounting bolts in two stages.
    1. Tighten the flywheel attachment bolts.

      Tightening torque: 

      30 N.m (3.1 kgf-m, 22.1 ft-lb)

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. While checking the tightening angle with the angle gauge, further tighten the flywheel mounting bolts.

      Tightening angle: 

      30° - 35°

  5. Install the clutch disc and clutch cover. Refer to CLUTCH SYSTEM>CLUTCH DISC AND COVER>INSTALLATION .
  6. Install the manual transmission assembly.
    • 5MT model:

      Refer to MANUAL TRANSMISSION AND DIFFERENTIAL (5MT)>MANUAL TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY>INSTALLATION .

    • 6MT model:

      Refer to MANUAL TRANSMISSION AND DIFFERENTIAL (6MT)>MANUAL TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY>INSTALLATION .
