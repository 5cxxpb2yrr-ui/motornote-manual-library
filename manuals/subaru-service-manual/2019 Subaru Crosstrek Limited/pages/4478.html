Power Seat Switch

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Remove the seat from vehicle. Refer to SEATS>FRONT SEAT>REMOVAL .
  2. Remove the cover - hinge front seat OUT.
    1. Release the claw in the upper section of the cover - hinge front seat OUT.
    2. Release the clips, and release the claws (a) in the front section of the cover - hinge front seat OUT.
    3. Disconnect the power seat switch connector, and remove the cover - hinge front seat OUT.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the power seat switch assembly.
    1. Remove the knob - power seat.
    2. Remove the screws to remove the power seat switch assembly.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
