Inspection Mode B

DTC Item
P1500 VDC WHEEL SPEED SENSOR SYSTEM
P1F02 AWD SOLENOID CIRCUIT LOW
P1F03 AWD SOLENOID CIRCUIT HIGH
P1F04 AWD SOLENOID CIRCUIT RANGE/PERFORMANCE
P1F0C TRANSFER INPUT TORQUE

Diagnostic procedure: 

  1. Turn to READY ON.
  2. Drive for 20 s or more at a vehicle of 40 km/h (24.9 MPH) or more.
