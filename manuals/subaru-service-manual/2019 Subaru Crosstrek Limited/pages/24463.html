DTC P1449: EVAP System Clog Detected (Air Filter): Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

1. - 

DTC detecting condition: 

Detected when two consecutive driving cycles with fault occur.

NOTE: After the faulty parts are repaired or replaced, perform the final check in Basic Diagnostic Procedure. Refer to ENGINE (DIAGNOSTICS) (H4DO (HEV))>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .
  1. CHECK DTC  .

    Using the Subaru Select Monitor or a general scan tool, read the DTC of [Engine]. Refer to COMMON (DIAG)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is DTC other than P1449 displayed? (Current malfunction)

    Yes:  Check the appropriate DTC using the "Diagnostic Trouble Code (DTC)". Refer to ENGINE (DIAGNOSTICS) (H4DO (HEV))>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>LIST .

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK CANISTER TUBE ASSEMBLY AND DRAIN TUBE ASSEMBLY BETWEEN CANISTER AND DRAIN FILTER  .

    Check the canister tube assembly and drain tube assembly between canister and drain filter.

    Is the canister tube assembly and/or the drain tube assembly clogged between canister and drain filter?

    Yes:  Replace the canister tube assembly and/or the drain tube assembly between canister and drain filter. Refer to EMISSION CONTROL (AUX. EMISSION CONTROL DEVICES) (H4DO (HEV))>CANISTER . Refer to EMISSION CONTROL (AUX. EMISSION CONTROL DEVICES) (H4DO (HEV))>DRAIN FILTER .

    No:  Go to  3.

  3. CHECK DRAIN FILTER  .

    Check the drain filter.

    Is the drain filter clogged?

    Yes:  Replace the drain filter. Refer to EMISSION CONTROL (AUX. EMISSION CONTROL DEVICES) (H4DO (HEV))>DRAIN FILTER .

    No:  Go to  4.

  4. CHECK LEAK CHECK VALVE ASSEMBLY (PRESSURE SENSOR)  .
    1. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    2. Start monitoring of [Evap System Absolute Pressure] using [OBD System] of Subaru Select Monitor.
      NOTE: For detailed operation procedures, refer to "Help" of application.
    3. Check the value of [Evap System Absolute Pressure] by shaking the harness and connector between leak check valve assembly and ECM.

    Does the value of [Evap System Absolute Pressure] change?

    Yes:  Repair the following item.

    • Open circuit in harness between ECM connector and the leak check valve assembly connector
    • Poor contact of coupling connector
    • Poor contact of J/C

    No:  Replace the leak check valve assembly. Refer to EMISSION CONTROL (AUX. EMISSION CONTROL DEVICES) (H4DO (HEV))>LEAK CHECK VALVE ASSEMBLY .
