FRONT DIFFERENTIAL (MT model)

  • 5MT

    Front differential gear oil of MT model lubricates the transmission and differential together. Refer to "5MT" instructions for inspection procedures. Refer to MANUAL TRANSMISSION AND DIFFERENTIAL (5MT)>TRANSMISSION GEAR OIL>INSPECTION .

  • 6MT

    Front differential gear oil of MT model lubricates the transmission and differential together. Refer to "6MT" instructions for inspection procedures. Refer to MANUAL TRANSMISSION AND DIFFERENTIAL (6MT)>TRANSMISSION GEAR OIL>INSPECTION .
