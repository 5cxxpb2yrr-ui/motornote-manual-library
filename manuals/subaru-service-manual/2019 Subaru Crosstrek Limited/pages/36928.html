Vanity Mirror Light: Installation

  1. Install the vanity mirror light bulb and lens.
    CAUTION: Use a dry clean cloth so that no grease or water adheres to the glass portion of the bulb.
  2. Install the sun visor assembly.
  3. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
