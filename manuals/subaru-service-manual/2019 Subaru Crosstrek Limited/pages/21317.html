DTC P0A08-14: DC/DC Converter Status Circuit Short To Ground Or Open: Notes

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Detecting condition: 

Open circuit or short to ground of DC/DC Converter (VLO) signal line

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

Normal driving
