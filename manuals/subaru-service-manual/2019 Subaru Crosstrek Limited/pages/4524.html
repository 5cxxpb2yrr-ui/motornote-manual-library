Sunroof Assembly: Assembly

  1. Remove the drain plate - sunroof.
  2. Remove the following parts referring the respective procedures.
    • Lid assembly - sunroof: Refer to SUNROOF/T-TOP/CONVERTIBLE TOP (SUNROOF)>GLASS LID>INSTALLATION>GLASS LID .
    • Deflector assembly: Refer to SUNROOF/T-TOP/CONVERTIBLE TOP (SUNROOF)>GLASS LID>INSTALLATION>DEFLECTOR ASSEMBLY .
    • Sunshade assembly: Refer to SUNROOF/T-TOP/CONVERTIBLE TOP (SUNROOF)>SUNSHADE>INSTALLATION .
    • Sunroof motor assembly: Refer to SUNROOF/T-TOP/CONVERTIBLE TOP (SUNROOF)>SUNROOF ASSEMBLY>INSTALLATION .
