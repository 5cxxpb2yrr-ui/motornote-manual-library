Except For IMPREZA Model

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Apply seal material to the bolt threads of oil pressure switch, and install the oil pressure switch.
    NOTE: Before applying the seal material, degrease the oil pressure switch and the thread portions of the chain cover.

    Seal material: 

    THREE BOND 1324 (Part No. 004403042) or equivalent

    Tightening torque: 

    18 N.m (1.8 kgf-m, 13.3 ft-lb)

  2. Connect the terminal, and attach the rubber cap.

    Tightening torque: 

    1.5 N.m (0.2 kgf-m, 1.1 ft-lb)

    NOTE: Fix the terminal within the range of 90° as shown in the figure.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Install the reservoir tank. (Hybrid model) Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>RESERVOIR TANK>INSTALLATION .
  4. Check the engine oil level and replenish the engine oil if necessary. Refer to LUBRICATION (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE OIL>INSPECTION .
  5. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
