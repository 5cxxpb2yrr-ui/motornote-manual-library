Explanation For Circuits

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Battery ECU assembly performs warning to the driver and failsafe control based on the faulty signals. DTC is output when HV battery depletion or hybrid battery system malfunction occurs.

NOTE: If HV battery voltage decreases due to malfunction of parts (Inverter with converter assembly, Transmission assembly), restoration is done by recharging. If the HV battery is degraded by more than the predetermined value due to extended time of leave, etc., replace the HV battery.
