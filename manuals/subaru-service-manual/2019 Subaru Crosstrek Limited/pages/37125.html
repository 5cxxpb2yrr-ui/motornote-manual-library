Antenna Feeder Cord

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery and wait for at least 60 seconds before starting work. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the grille assembly - CTR ventilation. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>AIR VENT GRILLE>REMOVAL>CENTER GRILLE ASSEMBLY .
  3. Remove the audio assembly or navigation assembly. Refer to ENTERTAINMENT & MONITORING>AUDIO>REMOVAL .
  4. Remove the cover assembly - instrument panel LWR driver. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>INSTRUMENT PANEL LOWER COVER>REMOVAL .
  5. Remove the panel center LWR LH. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>CENTER CONSOLE .
  6. Remove the cover side sill - front LH and the cover side sill - front INN LH. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>LOWER INNER TRIM>REMOVAL .
  7. Turn over the floor mat. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>FLOOR MAT>REMOVAL .
  8. Lower the trim panel - roof assembly in the same manner as the removal procedure of the antenna assembly - radio or the telematics. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>ROOF TRIM>REMOVAL .
  9. Remove the cord assembly - antenna feeder.
