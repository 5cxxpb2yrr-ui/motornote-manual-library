Inspect The Specified Position

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Measure the clearance between the end of the stop light switch and the stopper.
    CAUTION: Be careful not to rotate the stop light switch.

    Stop light switch clearance A: 

    1.5 mm (0.06 in)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Adjust the position of the stop light switch if the inspection result is not within the standard value. Refer to BRAKE>STOP LIGHT SWITCH>ADJUSTMENT .
  3. If the stop light switch does not operate properly or it is not secured at the specified position after adjustment, replace the stop light switch.
