Differential Side Retainer Oil Seal: Replacement

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery.

    Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .

    NOTE: Perform this procedure only when removing the LH side.
  2. Drain differential gear oil.

    Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TH2A)>DIFFERENTIAL GEAR OIL>REPLACEMENT .

  3. Remove the front drive shaft.

    Refer to DRIVE SHAFT SYSTEM>FRONT DRIVE SHAFT>REMOVAL .

  4. Remove the differential side retainer oil seal using a flat tip screwdriver wrapped with vinyl tape, etc.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Install the new differential side retainer oil seal using the ST.
    CAUTION: Since the differential side retainer oil seal has an identification mark (R or L), be careful not to mix up the RH and LH when installing.
    NOTE:
    • Apply differential gear oil to the peripheral surface of differential side retainer oil seal and the inner circumference of differential side retainer.
    • Apply differential gear oil to the differential side retainer oil seal lip.

    Preparation tool: 

    ST: DIFFERENTIAL SIDE OIL SEAL INSTALLER (18675AA000)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Install the front drive shaft.

    Refer to DRIVE SHAFT SYSTEM>FRONT DRIVE SHAFT>INSTALLATION .

  7. Fill differential gear oil.

    Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TH2A)>DIFFERENTIAL GEAR OIL>REPLACEMENT .

  8. Connect the battery ground terminal.

    Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
