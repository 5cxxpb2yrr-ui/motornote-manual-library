Malfunction Description

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly performs a function check for the charging shutdown signal from Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly to Electric vehicle charger assembly when starting normal charging.

Charging shutdown ON stuck is a fault that power is output following the charging power command despite CHEN is turned off and normal operation of CHEN cannot be guaranteed.

The following cases are assumed to be malfunction causes:

  • Internal malfunction of Electric vehicle charger assembly
    • Malfunction of Electric vehicle charger assembly
