High-Pressure Fuel Pump

CAUTION:
  • Be careful not to spill fuel.
  • Catch the fuel from the pipes using a container or cloth.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the clip from the air intake boot, and loosen the clamp.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the air intake boot, and place it aside so that it does not interfere with the work.
    CAUTION: Be careful not to pull out the PCV hose.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the high-pressure fuel delivery pipes. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>High Pressure Fuel Delivery Pipe>REMOVAL>HIGH-PRESSURE FUEL DELIVERY PIPE .
  5. Disconnect the connector from high-pressure fuel pump.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Using TORX PLUS® 40IP, remove the high-pressure fuel pump.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Remove the fuel pump lifter from the fuel pump case.
Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
