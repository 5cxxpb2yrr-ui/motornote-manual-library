Crossmember And Cushion Rubber

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Install the transmission cushion rubber to the transmission, and tighten the bolt (A).
  2. Install the transmission cushion rubber to the center crossmember, and temporarily tighten the nut (B).

    Tightening torque: 

    Bolt (A) 

    35 N.m (3.6 kgf-m, 25.8 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Install the front crossmember and rear crossmember.
  4. Tighten the transmission cushion rubber mounting nut.

    Tightening torque: 

    T1: 70 N.m (7.1 kgf-m, 51.6 ft-lb)

    T2: 140 N.m (14.3 kgf-m, 103.3 ft-lb)

    T3: 35 N.m (3.6 kgf-m, 25.8 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Take out the transmission jack.
  6. Install the center exhaust cover.

    Tightening torque: 

    18 N.m (1.8 kgf-m, 13.3 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Install the front exhaust pipe, refer to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FRONT EXHAUST PIPE>INSTALLATION .
  8. Install the crossmember support - rear.

    Tightening torque: 

    70 N.m (7.1 kgf-m, 51.6 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Lower the vehicle.
  10. Connect the battery ground terminal. refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
