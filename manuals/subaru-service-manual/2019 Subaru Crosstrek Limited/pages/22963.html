Rear Gate Latch and Actuator Assembly: Removal

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the trim panel - rear gate. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>REAR GATE TRIM>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the latch and actuator - rear gate.
    1. Disconnect the connector.
    2. Remove the bolts, and remove the latch and actuator - rear gate.
