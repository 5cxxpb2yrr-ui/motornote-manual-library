PCV Valve: Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
CAUTION: Do not remove unless the PCV valve is broken.
  1. Remove the clip from the air intake boot, and loosen the clamp.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Remove the air intake boot, and place it aside so that it does not interfere with the work.
    CAUTION: Be careful not to pull out the PCV hose.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the PCV hose No. 1.

    Refer to EMISSION CONTROL (AUX. EMISSION CONTROL DEVICES) (H4DO (HEV))>PCV HOSE>REMOVAL>PCV HOSE NO. 1 .

  4. Remove the PCV valve from the cylinder block RH.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove liquid gasket from the PCV valve and the mating surface of the cylinder block RH.
