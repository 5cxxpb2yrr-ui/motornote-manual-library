Malfunction Description

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

This DTC is stored if the value of the reactor current sensor does not change. The cause of this malfunction may be one of the following:

Part Main trouble contents
Inverter
  • Inverter inner circuit malfunction
  • Malfunction of ECM that controls the inverter
  • Inverter controlling sensor malfunction (current sensor, voltage sensor (VH and VL), etc.)
HV battery
  • Open circuit of frame wire
