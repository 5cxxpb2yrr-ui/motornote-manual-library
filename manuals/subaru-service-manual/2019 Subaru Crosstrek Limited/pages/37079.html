Power Will Not Turn On

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

Probable cause: 

Power supply circuit malfunction, unit body malfunction

Wiring diagram: 

  • Audio system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (IMPREZA MODEL)>AUDIO SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>AUDIO SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>AUDIO SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .
  • Navigation system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (IMPREZA MODEL)>NAVIGATION SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>NAVIGATION SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>NAVIGATION SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to INFOTAINMENT (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK CONNECTOR CONNECTION. 

    Check that the connector is connected to the unit.

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Connect the connector.

  2. CHECK CONNECTOR. 

    Check for poor contact of connectors or terminals.

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Repair or replace the harness.

  3. CHECK POWER SUPPLY VOLTAGE. 
    1. Disconnect the connector.
    2. Turn the ignition switch to ACC.
    3. Using a tester, measure the voltage between connector and chassis ground.
      NOTE: Turn the electrical parts to OFF.

      Connector & terminal 

      (i85) No. 4 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

      (i85) No. 3 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    Is the voltage 12 V or more?

    Yes:  Go to  8.

    No:  Go to  4.

  4. CHECK FUSE. 
    1. Turn the ignition to OFF.
    2. Check the fuse No. 29 inside M/B and fuse No. 4 inside F/B.

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  5.

    No:  Replace the fuse. If the fuse is blown again, check or replace the short circuit of the power supply harness.

  5. CHECK HARNESS (POWER SUPPLY). 

    Measure the resistance between main fuse box and connector.

    Connector & terminal 

    M/B fuse No. 29 - (i85) No. 4:

    F/B fuse No. 4 - (i85) No. 3:

    Is the resistance 1 Ω or less?

    Yes:  Go to  6.

    No:  Repair or replace the open circuit of harness.

  6. CHECK HARNESS (GROUND). 

    Measure the resistance between connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (i85) No. 7 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance 1 Ω or less?

    Yes:  Go to  7.

    No:  Repair or replace the open circuit of harness.

  7. CHECK BATTERY. 

    Check the battery. Refer to STARTING/CHARGING SYSTEMS (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>BATTERY . Refer to STARTING/CHARGING SYSTEMS (H4DO (HEV))>BATTERY .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  8.

    No:  Charge or replace the battery.

  8. CHECK OPERATION. 
    1. Connect all connectors.
    2. Turn the ignition switch to ACC and the power supply switch of the unit to ON.

    Does the power turn ON?

    Yes:  Currently no failure is found.

    No:  Replace the audio or navigation unit. Refer to ENTERTAINMENT & MONITORING>AUDIO . Refer to ENTERTAINMENT & MONITORING>NAVIGATION BODY .
