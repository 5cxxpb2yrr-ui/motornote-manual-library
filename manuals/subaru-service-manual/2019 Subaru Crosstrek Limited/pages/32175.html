Seat Belt - Rear Shoulder CTR (4 Door Model)

  1. Install the retractor of seat belt - rear shoulder CTR.

    Tightening torque:

    Refer to "COMPONENT" of "General Description" for the tightening torque. Refer to SEAT BELT SYSTEM>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>COMPONENT>REAR SEAT BELT (4 DOOR MODEL) .

  2. Install the trim panel assembly - rear shelf.
  3. Install the lower anchor of seat belt - rear shoulder CTR.
    CAUTION:
    • During installation, make sure that the seat belts are not twisted.
    • After installation, make sure that the seat belts are smoothly extended and wound.

    Tightening torque:

    30 N.m (3.1 kgf-m, 22.1 ft-lb)

  4. Install the trim panel - rear pillar LWR, trim panel - rear pillar UPR, and cover side sill - rear INN.
  5. Install the rear seat cushion assembly.
  6. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  7. After installation, check the following points.
    • The seat belt is not twisted.
    • The seat belt can be pulled out and retracted smoothly.
