Outline Of Diagnosis

During normal operation, the voltage at IG1 and IG2 change mutually; however, if the voltages do not change mutually such as when IG1 is off and IG2 is on, the electric brake booster control module judges that there is an open in the IG1 circuit and illuminates the MIL and stores a DTC.
