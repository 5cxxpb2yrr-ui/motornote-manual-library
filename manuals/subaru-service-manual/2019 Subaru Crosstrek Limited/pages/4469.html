Seat Cover

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

Check that there is no tear or fray on the cover COMPL - front backrest and the cover - front cushion.

NOTE: If the door side of the cover COMPL - front backrest is torn or frayed, the side airbag may not be deployed properly. In this case, replace the cover COMPL - front backrest with a new part.
