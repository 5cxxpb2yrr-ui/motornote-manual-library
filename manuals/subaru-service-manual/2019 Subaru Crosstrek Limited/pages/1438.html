DTC B28B9: Eyesight Steering Switch 2

Detected when steering switch for EyeSight is not installed, is open-circuited or is stuck to ON. Refer to DTC B28B8 for diagnostic procedure. Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE WITH DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>DTC B28B8 EYESIGHT STEERING SWITCH 1 .
