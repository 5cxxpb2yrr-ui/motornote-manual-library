Diagnostic Method

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

When the brake pedal is not depressed, the servo pressure sensor output value is -0.63 MPa or less or 1.53 MPa or more.

JUDGMENT VALUE SPECIFICATION

Malfunction Criteria Threshold Value
Servo pressure Less than -0.63 MPa or
Exceeds 1.53 MPa

Time needed for diagnosis:  3 s

Malfunction indicator light illumination:  Illuminates as soon as a malfunction occurs.
