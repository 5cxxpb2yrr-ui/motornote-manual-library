Low Pressure Solenoid Valve

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Visually check the LOW solenoid valve for cracks or other damage, and replace as necessary.
  2. Check the operation of the LOW solenoid valve when battery voltage is applied between the connector terminals.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Terminal No. Inspection conditions Specification
    2 (+) - 1 (-) Apply battery voltage and discharge compressed air in the direction of the arrow. Must not discharge.
  3. If the check results show that it does not operate normally, replace the LOW solenoid valve.
