Explanation For Circuits

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Battery ECU assembly controls the drive relay of HV battery heater. Battery ECU assembly detects a malfunction and outputs the DTC when the temperature of battery rises contrary to the intention of temperature rise due to ON stuck of drive relay in HV battery heater.
