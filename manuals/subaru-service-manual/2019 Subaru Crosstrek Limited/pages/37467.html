Pedestrian Alert System: General Description: Caution

  • When performing service operation, refer to "Repair Contents" in "General Description". Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS .
  • Before disconnecting connectors of sensors or units, be sure to disconnect the ground terminal from battery. When replacing the electrical parts provided with memory functions that store contents specified by a customer, record the memory contents before disconnecting the battery ground terminal.
  • The warning sound may not be heard well in loud ambient sound, or to people behind the vehicle.
  • When the front of speaker is covered with foreign matter such as mud and snow, it may change the tone of warning sound.
