Check Speaker Output With Line Diag Mode And Antenna Connection

  1. Turn the ignition switch to ACC.
  2. Press the button (b) twice with the button (a) pressed.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    NOTE: Pressing the button (a) for 3 seconds or more, or turning the ignition switch from the OFF to ACC position can exit the diagnostic mode.
  3. {Line Diag} screen is displayed.
  4. Perform the speaker output check and antenna connection check with the {Audio Check} in the {Line Diag} screen.
Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
Item Contents Inspection
MODE Radio band switching Check that the radio band (AM/FM) can be switched.
Seek UP/DOWN Seek UP/DOWN operation Check that the Seek UP/DOWN operation allows radio reception.
Speaker Check Speaker check operation
  • 4 speakers: Front door right → front door left → rear door left → rear door right speakers will sound in this order for 0.5 seconds each.
  • 6 speakers: Front door right → front tweeter right → front tweeter left → front door left → rear door left → rear door right speakers will sound in this order for 0.5 seconds each.
NOTE: The sound volume during the speaker check is different depending on the speakers.
  • Standard speaker: Sounds at the maximum volume.
  • Brand speaker: Sounds at 30 steps.
