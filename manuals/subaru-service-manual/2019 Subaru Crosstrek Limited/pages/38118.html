INTRODUCTION

This bulletin announces a design change to the leather transmission selector lever grip. The change will address a small number of concerns regarding stickiness and/or roughness felt on a portion (Panel A shown in the illustration below) of the grip surface. The new part also enhances parts commonality among models.

PRODUCTION CHANGE INFORMATION

This bulletin will be revised with the starting VINs for this production change when they become available.
