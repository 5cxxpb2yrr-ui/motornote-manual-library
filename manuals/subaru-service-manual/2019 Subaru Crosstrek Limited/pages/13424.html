Oil Pump Chain: Removal

  1. Remove the transmission assembly from the vehicle. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL .
  2. Remove the torque converter assembly. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>TORQUE CONVERTER ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the oil pump chain, oil pump sprocket, driven sprocket and oil pump chain cover.
    NOTE: Oil pump chain cover may be hard to remove because the driven sprocket is installed to the shaft side of the oil pump. Do not remove it forcibly. Remove it while holding the driven sprocket by hand.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the seal rings.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the driven sprocket from oil pump chain cover to remove the oil pump chain.
    NOTE:
    • The driven sprocket is replaced as an assembly only, because it is a non-disassembly part.
    • If the ball bearing is removed from the driven sprocket, replace it with a new part.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Remove the snap ring.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Remove the drive sprocket.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Remove the oil seal from the oil pump chain cover.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
