High-Pressure Hose

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
CAUTION: Replace the O-rings with new parts, and then apply compressor oil to install completely.
  1. Install the hose - pressure suction discharge.

    Tightening torque: 

    Compressor: Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>COMPONENT>COMPRESSOR .

  2. Install the bracket - reservoir joint pipe.

    Tightening torque: 

    7.5 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.5 ft-lb)

  3. Install the air intake boot. Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>Air Intake Boot>INSTALLATION .
  4. Install the collector cover. Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>COLLECTOR COVER>INSTALLATION .
  5. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  6. Charge refrigerant. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>REFRIGERANT CHARGING PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .
