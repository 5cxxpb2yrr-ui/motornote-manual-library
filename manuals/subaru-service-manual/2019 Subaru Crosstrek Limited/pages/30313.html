Evaporator Sensor: Inspection

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Prepare the vehicle.
    NOTE: Check that the ambient temperature is 25 - 40°C (77 - 104°F) and that the humidity is 30 - 80%.
    • Place the vehicle in the workshop or in the shade and windless condition.
    • Open all windows.
  2. Set the vehicle to the following conditions.
    Item Condition
    READY ON
    Air vent grille Shutter is fully open.
    A/C switch OFF
    Temperature adjustment dial LO (MAX COOL)
    FRESH/RECIRC switch RECIRC
    Air flow control dial VENT
    Fan dial 5/7 level
  3. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, check the evaporator sensor.
    NOTE: For detailed operation procedures, refer to "Help" of application.
    1. Connect the Subaru Select Monitor.
    2. On [Start] screen, select [Diagnosis].
    3. On [Select Vehicle] screen, enter vehicle information and select [Confirmed].
    4. On [Select System] screen, select [Air Conditioner], and then select [Confirmed].
    5. On [Select Function] screen, select [Data Monitor].
    6. From the data monitor item list, select [Evaporator Temperature Target].
    7. Turn READY ON for 15 minutes, and then compare the outlet temperature with [Evaporator

      Temperature Target].

      Preparation tool: 

      Thermometer and hygrometer

      NOTE: For outlet opening temperature, measure the average temperature of center grille assembly and side grille assembly.
    8. Is the difference between outlet opening temperature and [Evaporator Temperature Target] by 3°C (5.4°F) or more?
    • Yes  → Go to step 4.
    • No  → Evaporator sensor is normal.
  4. Check the evaporator sensor.
    1. Disconnect the connector.
    2. Measure the resistance between connector terminals.

      Preparation tool: 

      Circuit tester

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
      Terminal No. Inspection conditions Standard
      1 - 2 -20°C (-4°F) 13.584 - 14.541 kΩ
      -15°C (5°F) 10.34 - 10.946 kΩ
      -10°C (14°F) 7.938 - 8.313 kΩ
      -5°C (23°F) 6.143 - 6.366 kΩ
      0°C (32°F) 4.79 - 4.914 kΩ
      5°C (41°F) 3.718 - 3.829 kΩ
      10°C (50°F) 2.889 - 3.031 kΩ
      15°C (59°F) 2.247 - 2.435 kΩ
      20°C (68°F) 1.785 - 1.952 kΩ
      25°C (77°F) 1.427 - 1.574 kΩ
      30°C (86°F) 1.149 - 1.278 kΩ
      35°C (95°F) 0.931 - 1.044 kΩ
      40°C (104°F) 0.759 - 0.858 kΩ
    3. Is the resistance within the standard?
    • Yes  → Evaporator sensor is normal.
    • No  → Replace the evaporator sensor.
