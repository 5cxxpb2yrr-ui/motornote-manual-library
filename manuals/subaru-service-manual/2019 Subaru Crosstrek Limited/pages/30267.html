Exterior Condenser

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Using the refrigerant recovery system, discharge refrigerant. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>REFRIGERANT RECOVERY PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .
  2. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  3. Remove the bumper face - front. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>FRONT BUMPER>REMOVAL .
  4. Remove the radiator assembly. Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>RADIATOR>REMOVAL .
  5. Remove the hose - pressure suction valve. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>HOSE AND PIPE>REMOVAL>LOW PRESSURE VALVE HOSE .
  6. Remove the LOW solenoid valve. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>SOLENOID VALVE>REMOVAL>LOW PRESSURE SOLENOID VALVE .
  7. Remove the condenser assembly.
    CAUTION: Be careful not to damage the condenser assembly and radiator fins. If a damaged fin is found, repair it using a thin screwdriver.
