Instrument Panel Lower Cover: Installation

CAUTION: The clips "C62" and "C63" shown in the figure may have different engaging forces. Always use the specified parts when installing.
  1. Install the cover assembly - instrument panel LWR driver INN and OUT.
  2. Install the cover - instrument panel side LH.
  3. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
