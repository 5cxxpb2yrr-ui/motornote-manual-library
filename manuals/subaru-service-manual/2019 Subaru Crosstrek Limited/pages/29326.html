Circuit Explanation

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

The Charge control ECU installed inside the Electric vehicle charger assembly monitors the unit internal operation, and outputs a DTC when an error is detected. If this DTC was detected, replace the Electric vehicle charger assembly.

For an explanation of the circuit, refer to DTC P1CCD96. Refer to PLUG-IN BATTERY CHARGER (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE WITH DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>DTC P1CCD96 SUB DC/DC CONVERTER (FOR CHARGING) COMPONENT INTERNAL FAILURE .
