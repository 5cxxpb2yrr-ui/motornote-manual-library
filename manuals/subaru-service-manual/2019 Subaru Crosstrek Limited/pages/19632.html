Malfunction Description

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly monitors the high voltage wiring between HV battery and Inverter with converter assembly, and detects short circuit malfunctions or operation malfunctions of the high voltage parts.

The following cases are assumed to be malfunction causes:

  • Battery current sensor (IB) system fault
    • Battery current sensor (IB) malfunction
    • Malfunction of Motor generator control ECU
    • Malfunction of communication (Wire harness)
  • High voltage system fault
    • HV battery junction block
    • Malfunction of HV battery
    • Malfunction of Inverter with converter assembly
    • Malfunction of air conditioner system
    • High voltage Wire harness malfunction
    • High voltage connector, connection malfunction
  • Low voltage system (12 V) fault
    • Malfunction of Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly
    • Low voltage Wire harness malfunction
    • Low voltage connector malfunction
