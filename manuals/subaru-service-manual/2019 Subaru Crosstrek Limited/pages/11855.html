Recommended Materials: Fluid

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

Use the fluids specified in the table below. Do not mix two different kinds or makes of fluid.

CAUTION:
  • Be sure to use the recommended CVTF and ATF. Using material except recommended one would cause trouble.
  • If an alternative transmission gear oil is used, you may not have expected functionality and performance.
Item Recommended materials Item number Alternative
Transmission fluid Except for hybrid model SUBARU CVT FLUID LINEARTRONIC II K0425Y0711 -
Hybrid model ATF WS NWS9638 - -
TRANSMISSION GEAR OIL SUBARU GEAR OIL EXTRA MT - GL-5 (75W-90)
Front differential gear oil SUBARU GEAR OIL EXTRA MT - GL-5 (75W-90)
Rear differential gear oil (VA type) SUBARU GEAR OIL EXTRA MT - GL-5 (75W-90)
Brake fluid and clutch fluid FMVSS No. 116 DOT3 or DOT4 - -
