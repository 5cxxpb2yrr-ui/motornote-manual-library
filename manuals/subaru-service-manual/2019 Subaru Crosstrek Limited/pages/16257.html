Fuel Tank: Installation

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
WARNING:
  • Place "NO OPEN FLAMES" signs near the working area.
  • The hybrid system includes a high voltage circuit. Mishandling could cause accidents such as electric shock or leak. Always check "WARNING (HYBRID SYSTEM)" and perform the proper operation. Refer to PRECAUTION/WARNING>WARNING (HYBRID SYSTEM) .
  1. Install the insulator and protector to the fuel tank.
    1. Install the insulator holder (A) to the lower insulator (C) and set it on the fuel tank.
    2. Install the upper insulator (B) to the insulator holder (A).
      CAUTION: Be sure to fix the insulator holder and the insulator without looseness as shown in the figure.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Set the protector (B) on the fuel tank and install the clip (A).
      CAUTION: Be sure to insert the clip all the way to the end.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Support the fuel tank with a transmission jack, set the fuel tank in place, and temporarily tighten the bolts of the fuel tank band.
    CAUTION: A helper is required to perform this work.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Tighten the bolts of the fuel tank band in the order shown in the figure.

    Tightening torque: 

    33 N.m (3.4 kgf-m, 24.3 ft-lb)

    Fig 1: Fuel Tank Band Bolts Tightening Sequence
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Connect the fuel delivery tube to the fuel pipe assembly. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>INSTALLATION>QUICK CONNECTOR CONNECTION (TYPE A) .
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Support the inverter assembly (with converter) with the transmission jack and temporarily tighten the bolt.
    WARNING: Be sure to wear insulated gloves.
    CAUTION: A helper is required to perform this work.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Tighten the bolts in the order shown in the figure.
    WARNING: Be sure to wear insulated gloves.

    Tightening torque: 

    T1: 18 N.m (1.8 kgf-m, 13.3 ft-lb)

    T2: 23 N.m (2.3 kgf-m, 17.0 ft-lb)

    Fig 2: Inverter Assembly (With Converter) Bolts Tightening Sequence
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Connect the ground cable.
    WARNING: Be sure to wear insulated gloves.

    Tightening torque: 

    13 N.m (1.3 kgf-m, 9.6 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Connect the hose - air breather. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>INSTALLATION>QUICK CONNECTOR CONNECTION (TYPE E) .
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Connect the connector (A) and install the bracket.

    Tightening torque: 

    18 N.m (1.8 kgf-m, 13.3 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  10. Install the support - sub frame (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    T1: 70 N.m (7.1 kgf-m, 51.6 ft-lb)

    T2: 145 N.m (14.8 kgf-m, 106.9 ft-lb)

  11. Connect the connector (B) and install the bracket.

    Tightening torque: 

    T3: 18 N.m (1.8 kgf-m, 13.3 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  12. Install the bolt and nut securing the air conditioner wire (A) and power cable assembly (B).
    WARNING: Be sure to wear insulated gloves.

    Tightening torque: 

    7.5 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.5 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  13. Install the bolt and clip (A) securing the inverter center water pipe and the resolver sensor harness.

    Tightening torque: 

    T1: 7.5 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.5 ft-lb)

    T2: 18 N.m (1.3 kgf-m, 9.6 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  14. Connect the fuel filler hose (A). Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>INSTALLATION>FUEL FILLER HOSE CONNECTION .
  15. Connect the circulate tube (B) to the fuel tank. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>INSTALLATION>QUICK CONNECTOR CONNECTION (TYPE F) .
  16. Connect the vent tube (C) to the electric vent solenoid valve assembly. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>INSTALLATION>QUICK CONNECTOR CONNECTION (TYPE H) .
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  17. Install the rear differential. Refer to DIFFERENTIALS>REAR DIFFERENTIAL (VA-TYPE)>INSTALLATION .
  18. Install the tunnel stay.

    Tightening torque: 

    18 N.m (1.8 kgf-m, 13.3 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  19. Install the rear exhaust pipe and muffler.

    Rear exhaust pipe: 

    Refer to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>REAR EXHAUST PIPE>INSTALLATION .

    Muffler: 

    Refer to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>MUFFLER>INSTALLATION .

  20. Install the floor under protector. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>FLOOR UNDER PROTECTOR>INSTALLATION .
  21. Install the fuel tank protector and inverter assembly (with converter) cover. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL TANK PROTECTOR>INSTALLATION .
  22. Lower the vehicle.
  23. Connect the connector to the fuel pump.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  24. Install the service hole cover of fuel pump.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  25. Install the rear seat cushion. Refer to SEATS>REAR SEAT>INSTALLATION .
  26. Install the service disconnect plug. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG>INSTALLATION .
  27. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
