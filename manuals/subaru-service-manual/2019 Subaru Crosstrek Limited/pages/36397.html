5 Door Model (Except For Hybrid)

CAUTION:
  • Before handling the airbag system components, refer to "CAUTION" of "General Description" in "AIRBAG SYSTEM". Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  • Airbag system satellite safing sensor is located under the front center of the rear seat cushion. Be careful not to apply strong impact to the sensor when working with the rear seat cushion removed.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery and wait for at least 60 seconds before starting work. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the rear seat cushion assembly. Refer to SEATS>REAR SEAT>REMOVAL .
  3. Release the clips and claws, and then remove the cover side sill - rear INN.
    CAUTION: Do not pull with excessive force. Doing so may damage the claws of the cover side sill - rear INN.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the mat - rear floor and the spacer - rear floor side.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the clips, and remove the trim panel - rear skirt.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Remove the spacer - rear floor CTR.
    1. Tilt the backrest forward and remove the clips, and then turn over the backrest backside mat.
    2. Release the clips, then detach the spacer - rear floor CTR.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Remove the trim panel - rear apron.
    1. Remove the screws and bolts.
    2. Disengage the claws and clips, and remove the trim panel - rear apron.
      CAUTION: Forcibly removing the clips near the seat belt lower anchor bolts may deform or damage the clip base. Use a clip remover for operation.
      • Impreza model
        Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
      • Crosstrek model
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    NOTE: For LH side, disconnect the connector of the luggage light.
  8. Remove the trim panel - rear pillar UPR.
    1. Remove the lower anchor bolts of seat belts.
    2. Remove the belt cover.
      NOTE: If the belt cover cannot be removed, remove the trim panel - rear pillar UPR first.
    3. Disengage the claws and clips, and remove the trim panel - rear apron.
Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
