DTC P3004-49: High Voltage Power Resource Circuit Short During Pre-Charge: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

1. - 

Outline of diagnosis: 

Although SMR connection was commanded at the power switch operation, the voltage after boosting did not increase and the battery current did not flow

Malfunction of high voltage circuit between HV battery and inverter

Blow-out of EV battery fuse, unattached Service plug, Limiting resistor burn-out, SMRP open, SMRB open, or open-circuit of the high pressure cable occurred

(2 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

Hybrid system stopped

Wiring diagram: 

Hybrid system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

    Yes:  Connect the connector securely. Or repair the poor contact of connector.

  7. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly - HV battery junction block) (OPEN CIRCUIT)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Disconnect HV battery junction block connector (HV49).
    2. Disconnect Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector (i369).
    3. Using a tester, measure the resistance between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector and HV battery junction block connector.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV49) No. 1 - (i369) No. 5:

      (HV51) No. 2 - (i369) No. 15:

    If there is trace of arc on the connector (HV94) or Hybrid battery terminal block, replace the faulty parts.
