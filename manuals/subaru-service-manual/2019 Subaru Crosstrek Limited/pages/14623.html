The Charging Connector Does Not Lock

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
CAUTION:
  • Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to PLUG-IN BATTERY CHARGER (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  • The Charging connector lock system, model uses keyless access & push button start (keyless access function). A malfunction in the keyless access function may prevent the charging lid lock and Charging connector lock functions from operating.

    When there is a malfunction of the keyless access function, first check the keyless access & push button start (keyless access function).

  • When checking, set the keyless access system function of the keyless access & push button start (keyless access function) to "Active" in the customization function.
  • When operation of the charging lid lock/unlock and Charging connector lock/unlock are linked, lock/unlock operation will not occur until the motor temperature has dropped in order to protect the motor. If this occurs, wait for 3 minutes or more to allow the lock motor to cool, then check its operation.
  • After linked operation of charging lid lock/unlock and Charging connector lock/unlock occurred, commands are not accepted for a certain time in order to protect the motor. If this occurs, wait for 3 seconds or more after the previous operation before checking operation.
  • It is possible that Charging connector lock/unlock was not possible because the Charging connector lock was dirty or frozen, or a foreign object was caught in it. Therefore if there is dirt or freezing, remove it before checking.
  • If an Electric vehicle charger cable assembly from another company was used, it is possible that the Charging connector lock/unlock function will not operate correctly. Use the Electric vehicle charger cable assembly that was provided with the vehicle to check Charging connector lock/unlock operation before inspecting.
  • After connecting the Charging connector, using the MFD charging settings to change the Charging connector lock setting (changing from the current setting to automatic locking or automatic locking & automatic unlocking) does not cause Charging connector lock/unlock to operate.
  • Charging connector lock/unlock may not be possible after emergency lid unlock. After emergency lid unlock, open the charging lid (charging lid push-lifter (charging lid courtesy switch) extended) → then connect the Charging connector.
  • Charging connector lock/unlock may not be possible after emergency connector unlock. After emergency connector unlock, pull out the Charging connector → then close the charging lid (charging lid courtesy switch (Charge inlet switch) push-lifter retracted).
  • Use the MFD charging settings to set the Charging connector lock setting to "Manual lock" before checking.

  3. CHECK THE CHARGING LID LIGHTING FUNCTION. 
    1. Close all doors of the vehicle.
    2. Lock the vehicle doors.
    3. Hold the Access key at the same height as the charging lid and bring it within approximately 70 cm of the charging lid. Check that the Access key LED blinks.
      NOTE:
      • The charging lid is located on the rear left side of the vehicle.
      • With the vehicle doors locked, check the status of the keyless access function and charging lid lighting function. (When the vehicle doors are unlocked, the status of the keyless access function and charging lid lighting function do not operate.)
    4. Hold the Access key and bring it within approximately 30 cm of the Keyless access outdoor antenna (charging inlet door). Check that the Access key LED blinks.
      NOTE:
      • The Keyless access outdoor antenna (charging inlet door) is located on the rear left side of the vehicle below the charging lid.
      • In some cases, communication is not possible within 20 cm of the Keyless access outdoor antenna (charging inlet door).
      • Because bringing the Access key closer to the Keyless access outdoor antenna (charging inlet door) reduces the effects of outside radio interference, it is possible to check whether there are effects from outside radio interference.
      • If the check results show that operation fails only at certain locations and times, then there is a strong possibility that the cause is outside radio interference. Because it is also possible that the function is affected by outside radio interference from parts or accessories that were installed by the dealer, remove any parts or accessories that were installed by the dealer and then check operation.
      • If there is a broadcast antenna, large-screen monitor, wireless open/close garage, wireless security camera, home security system, or similar equipment close to the vehicle, then there may be outside radio interference. In this case, move the vehicle to another location and check the operation.
      • If a radio wave checker is available, it is possible to follow the radio environment check procedure and determine whether or not there are effects from outside radio interference.

    If the Access key LED does not blink, go to  A.

    (The charging lid lighting function is not operating.)

  6. CHECK Cable EV charger lock assembly. 

    Check the Cable EV charger lock assembly unit. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>CHARGE INLET>INSPECTION .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Analyze the cause as shown below in order: environment → user.

    Environment

    • Charging connector lock motor temperature is high.
      • Explain that the Charging connector lock motor temperature was high, and connector lock/unlock was prevented in order to protect the motor.
      • Wait for 3 minutes or more after the previous lock/unlock operation before checking operation
    • Charging connector cannot be locked due to foreign object, ice, or similar reason.
      • Explain that Charging connector lock/unlock operation is not possible due to intruding foreign object, ice, or similar reason.

    User

    • Keyless access cancel is set.
      • Explain that when keyless access is canceled, the keyless access function stops, and consequently the Charging connector lock does not operate.
        NOTE: Charging connector lock operation when keyless access is canceled
        • Manual setting: With the doors unlocked, connect the Charging connector and press the connector lock switch.
        • Automatic locking setting or automatic locking & automatic unlocking setting: Connect the Charging connector.
    • Charging connector lock/unlock operation was performed repeatedly in succession.
      • Explain that when the Charging connector lock/unlock operation was performed repeatedly in succession, then in order to protect the motor, the operation may not be possible for a time.
    • The charging lid was unlocked by the emergency lid unlock operation.
      • Explain that the Charging connector lock operation may not be possible after emergency lid unlock.
      • After emergency lid unlock, open the charging lid (charging lid courtesy switch (Charge inlet switch) push-lifter extended), → then connect the Charging connector.
    • The Charging connector was unlocked by the emergency connector unlock operation.
      • Explain that Charging connector lock operation may not be possible after emergency connector unlock.
      • After emergency connector unlock, pull out the Charging connector → then connect the Charging connector.
    • Charging connector lock/unlock was performed after the Battery (12V) was disconnected and reconnected.
      • Explain that Charging connector lock/unlock operation may not be possible after the Battery (12V) was disconnected and reconnected.
      • Perform Charging connector lock/unlock operation again and it will operate correctly.

    No:  Replace Cable EV charger lock assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>CHARGE INLET .

      • Explain that the Charging connector lock motor temperature was high, and connector lock/unlock was prevented in order to protect the motor.
      • Wait for 3 minutes or more after the previous lock/unlock operation before checking operation
    • Charging connector cannot be locked due to foreign object, ice, or similar reason.
      • Explain that Charging connector lock/unlock operation is not possible due to intruding foreign object, ice, or similar reason.

    User

    • Keyless access cancel is set.
      • Explain that when keyless access is canceled, the keyless access function stops, and consequently the Charging connector lock does not operate.
        NOTE: Charging connector lock operation when keyless access is canceled
        • Manual setting: With the doors unlocked, connect the Charging connector and press the connector lock switch.
        • Automatic locking setting or automatic locking & automatic unlocking setting: Connect the Charging connector.
    • Charging connector lock/unlock operation was performed repeatedly in succession.
      • Explain that when the Charging connector lock/unlock operation was performed repeatedly in succession, then in order to protect the motor, the operation may not be possible for a time.
    • The charging lid was unlocked by the emergency lid unlock operation.
      • Explain that the Charging connector lock operation may not be possible after emergency lid unlock.
      • After emergency lid unlock, open the charging lid (charging lid courtesy switch (Charge inlet switch) push-lifter extended), → then connect the Charging connector.
    • The Charging connector was unlocked by the emergency connector unlock operation.
      • Explain that Charging connector lock operation may not be possible after emergency connector unlock.
      • After emergency connector unlock, pull out the Charging connector → then connect the Charging connector.
    • Charging connector lock/unlock was performed after the Battery (12V) was disconnected and reconnected.
      • Explain that Charging connector lock/unlock operation may not be possible after the Battery (12V) was disconnected and reconnected.
      • Perform Charging connector lock/unlock operation again and it will operate correctly.

    No:  Repair or replace the Wire harness between Electric vehicle charger assembly connector and Charging indicator connector.
