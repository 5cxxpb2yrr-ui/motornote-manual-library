CVTF Cooler Pipe No. 2

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
CAUTION: Always use specified ATF. ATF other than specified ATF will cause malfunction.
  1. Install the CVTF cooler pipe No. 2 to the vehicle.

    Tightening torque: 

    7.5 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.5 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Install a new CVTF cooler (air cool) inlet hose and a new CVTF cooler (air cool) outlet hose.
  3. Position the radiator upper frame together with the A/C accumulator assembly, and install the upper side bolts securing the radiator upper frame.
    NOTE: Securely install the radiator upper cushion to the radiator upper frame.

    Tightening torque: 

    18 N.m (1.8 kgf-m, 13.3 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Connect the hose - pressure suction COM PL to the pipe - return assembly.

    Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>HOSE AND PIPE>INSTALLATION>RETURN HOSE .

  5. Connect the hose - pressure suction to the A/C accumulator assembly.

    Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>HOSE AND PIPE>INSTALLATION>LOW-PRESSURE HOSE .

  6. Connect the connector to the horn and install the clip.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Install the front hood lock assembly.

    Refer to SECURITY AND LOCKS>FRONT HOOD LOCK ASSEMBLY>INSTALLATION .

  8. Install the bracket - bumper UPR

    Tightening torque: 

    7.5 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.5 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Install the bumper face - front.

    Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>FRONT BUMPER>INSTALLATION .

  10. Install the engine wiring harness to the bracket.
  11. Install the engine wiring harness to the clip (C), and connect the connector (B) to the main fuse box.
  12. Connect connector (A) to ECM.
    CAUTION: To prevent damage to the connector (A), when connecting the connector (A), insert the connector (A) straight in until it stops, and while maintaining the position, lock the lever.
    NOTE: When connecting the connector (A), check that the lever is located at the position shown in the figure (position beyond the lock portion (a)).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  13. Connect the hose - pressure suction valve (A) to the hose - pressure suction COMPL.

    Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>HOSE AND PIPE>INSTALLATION>LOW PRESSURE VALVE HOSE .

  14. Connected the lock assembly - front hood connector (B).
  15. Install the lower side bolts (C) securing the radiator upper frame.

    Tightening torque: 

    18 N.m (1.8 kgf-m, 13.3 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  16. Install a new CVTF CVT inlet hose and a new CVTF CVT outlet hose.
  17. Install the front exhaust pipe.

    Refer to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FRONT EXHAUST PIPE>INSTALLATION .

  18. Install the inverter tank.

    Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>INVERTER TANK>INSTALLATION .

  19. Install the battery.

    Refer to STARTING/CHARGING SYSTEMS (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>BATTERY>INSTALLATION .

  20. Charge refrigerant.

    Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>REFRIGERANT CHARGING PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .

  21. Fill engine coolant. (Inverter side)

    Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>ENGINE COOLANT>REPLACEMENT>FILLING OF ENGINE COOLANT>INVERTER ASSEMBLY SIDE .

  22. Install the collector cover.

    Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>COLLECTOR COVER>INSTALLATION .

  23. Adjust the ATF level.

    Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TH2A)>AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION FLUID>REPLACEMENT .
