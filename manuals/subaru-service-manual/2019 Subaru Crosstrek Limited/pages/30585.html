Section 1 (Manual Transaxle & Differential (6MT)): Installation: Disassembly

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
NOTE:
  • Attach a cloth to the end of driven shaft (on the frictional side of the thrust needle bearing) to prevent damage during disassembly or reassembly.
  • When replacing the coupling sleeve and synchronizer hub, replace them as a set. Because these must engage at the specified point, avoid disassembly as much as possible. If it is necessary to disassemble, mark the engaging points on the splines beforehand.
  1. Remove the lock nut using the ST1, ST2 and ST3.
    NOTE: Flatten the lock nut tab before removing the lock nut.

    Special tool: 

    ST1: 18680AA020 HOLDER

    ST2: 18667AA020 HOLDER

    ST3: 18662AA010 SOCKET (27)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Remove the lock washer, washer, differential bevel gear sleeve, adjusting washer No. 1, thrust bearing and adjusting washer No. 2.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Draw out the drive pinion shaft from driven shaft.
  4. Remove the needle bearing and drive pinion collar.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the needle bearing and thrust bearing.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Remove the roller bearing and washer using ST and a press.

    Special tool: 

    ST: 498077000 REMOVER

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Remove the lock nut using the ST1 and ST2.
    NOTE: Flatten the lock nut tab before removing the lock nut.

    Special tool: 

    ST1: 499987300 SOCKET WRENCH (50)

    ST2: 18680AA020 HOLDER

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Remove the ball bearing, 6th driven gear and drive pinion spacer using the ST1, ST2 and a press.

    Special tool: 

    ST1: 899754112 PRESS

    ST2: 899714110 REMOVER

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Remove the 5th driven gear, double ball bearing and 3rd-4th driven gear using ST1, ST2 and a press.

    Special tool: 

    ST1: 899754112 PRESS

    ST2: 899714110 REMOVER

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  10. Remove the key.
  11. Remove the 2nd driven gear, 2nd inner baulk ring, 2nd synchro cone, 2nd outer baulk ring, lst-2nd shifting insert and lst-2nd coupling sleeve.
    CAUTION: When removing 1st-2nd coupling sleeve, be careful not to lose the 1st-2nd shifting insert.
  12. Remove the 1st driven gear, 1st inner baulk ring, 1st synchro cone, 1st outer baulk ring, 2nd driven gear bushing and 1st-2nd synchronizer hub using ST and a press.

    Special tool: 

    ST: 18762AA001 COMPRESSOR SPECIAL TOOL

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
