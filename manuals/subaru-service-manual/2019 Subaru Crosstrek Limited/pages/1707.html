Related Lines, More Than 52 Ω And Less Than 62 Ω (Immobilizer)

Wiring diagram: 

CAN communication system. Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (IMPREZA MODEL)>CAN COMMUNICATION SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>CAN COMMUNICATION SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  1. CHECK BETWEEN RELATED LINES AND MAIN WIRING HARNESS. 
    1. Disconnect the immobilizer control module connector.
    2. Using the tester, measure the resistance between terminals.

    Connector & terminal 

    (i34) No. 10 - (i34) No. 11:

    Is the resistance 400 Ω or more?

    Yes:  A related line between immobilizer control module and main wiring harness is open, or main wiring harness is open at two places or more.

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK RELATED LINES. 
    1. Disconnect the audio or navigation unit connector.
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between audio or navigation unit connector and immobilizer control module connector.

    Connector & terminal 

    (i34) No. 11 - (i87) No. 10:

    (i34) No. 10 - (i87) No. 9:

    Is the resistance 10 Ω or more?

    Yes:  Repair or replace the open circuit of immobilizer related lines.

    No:  The communication harness is normal. Check DTC of immobilizer system. Refer to IMMOBILIZER (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .
