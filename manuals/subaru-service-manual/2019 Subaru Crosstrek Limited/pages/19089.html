Low-Pressure Side Is Low

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. CHECK REFRIGERANT LEAKS  .

    Check the refrigerant for leaks. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>REFRIGERANT LEAK CHECK>INSPECTION .

    NOTE: When high-pressure side is less than 0.69 MPa: Go to step  2.

    Are there refrigerant leaks?

    Yes:  Repair the refrigerant leaking points.

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. FILL PROPER AMOUNT OF REFRIGERANT  .

    Drain all refrigerant, and refill proper amount of refrigerant.

    • Recovery: Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>REFRIGERANT RECOVERY PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .
    • Fill: Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>REFRIGERANT CHARGING PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .

    Is refrigerant pressure within the standard value?

    Yes:  Refrigerant pressure is normal.

    No:  Go to  3.

  3. REPLACE THE EXPANSION VALVE - COOLER ASSEMBLY  .

    Replace the expansion valve - cooler assembly. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>EXPANSION VALVE>REMOVAL>COOLER VALVE .

    Is refrigerant pressure within the standard value?

    Yes:  Refrigerant pressure is normal.

    No:  Check the electric compressor. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>COLD AIR DOES NOT COME OUT EVEN WHEN THE A/C SWITCH IS PRESSED. THE GLASS CANNOT BE DEFOGGED. (COMPRESSOR DOES NOT OPERATE (ELECTRIC)) .
