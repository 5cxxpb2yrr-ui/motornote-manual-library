Audio / Video System: Camera: Installation: Rear

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  • 4 DOOR MODEL 
    1. Install the rearview camera assembly.
      NOTE: Press-in the rearview camera assembly until a locking sound is heard.
    2. Install the trim panel - trunk lid.
    3. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    4. Confirm the marker position. Refer to ENTERTAINMENT & MONITORING>CAMERA>INSPECTION .
  • 5 DOOR MODEL 
    1. Install the rearview camera assembly.
      NOTE: Align the hook of the rearview camera assembly with the lower part of the panel, and then press-in the rearview camera assembly until a locking sound is heard.
    2. Install the garnish assembly - rear gate. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>REAR GATE GARNISH>INSTALLATION .
    3. Install the trim panel - rear gate.
    4. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    5. Confirm the marker position. Refer to ENTERTAINMENT & MONITORING>CAMERA>INSPECTION .
