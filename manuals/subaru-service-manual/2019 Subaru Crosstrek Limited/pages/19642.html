Malfunction Description

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly monitors the high voltage wiring between HV battery and Inverter with converter assembly, and detects open circuit malfunctions.

NOTE:
  • Differentiation from DTC P300449 is made based on the difference in detection timing. (P1C8449 is detected after READY ON.)

    When DTC P300449 is displayed simultaneously, check P300449 first.

  • In the case of SMRG OFF malfunction, only DTC P1C8449 is output.

The following cases are assumed to be malfunction causes:

  • Fault of Inside of inverter voltage sensor (VH) system
    • Voltage sensor (VH) malfunction
    • Malfunction of Motor generator control ECU
    • Malfunction of communication (Wire harness)
  • High voltage system fault
    • Malfunction of HV battery
    • Malfunction of HV battery junction block
    • Malfunction of Inverter with converter assembly
    • High voltage Wire harness malfunction
    • High voltage connector, connection malfunction
  • Low voltage system (12 V) fault
    • Malfunction of Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly
    • Malfunction of HV battery junction block
    • Low voltage Wire harness malfunction
    • Low voltage connector malfunction
