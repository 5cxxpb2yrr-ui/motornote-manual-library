Intake Cam Sprocket LH

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Install the intake cam sprocket LH by aligning the knock hole (A) of intake cam sprocket LH and the knock pin (B) of intake camshaft LH.
    NOTE: Before installation, check that there is no foreign matter on the intake cam sprocket LH and intake camshaft LH.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Tighten the bolts securing the intake cam sprocket LH by hand.
    NOTE:
    • Apply a coat of engine oil to the bolt thread.
    • Tighten the bolts until the intake cam sprocket LH contacts the intake camshaft LH by hand.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Install timing chain LH. Refer to MECHANICAL (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>TIMING CHAIN ASSEMBLY>INSTALLATION>TIMING CHAIN LH .
