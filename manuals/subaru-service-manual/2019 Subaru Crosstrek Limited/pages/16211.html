Camshaft Position Sensor

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Prepare an oscilloscope.
  3. Disconnect the connector from ECM. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>ENGINE CONTROL MODULE (ECM)>REMOVAL .
  4. Attach the ST between the ECM, engine wiring harness and the bulkhead wiring harness. Refer to ENGINE (DIAGNOSTICS) (H4DO (HEV))>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>PREPARATION TOOL>HOW TO USE CHECK BOARD .

    Special tool: 

    CHECK BOARD (18460AA180)

  5. Connect the probe to ST.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Camshaft position sensor Terminal No. Probe
    Intake RH (E158) No. 78 +
    LH (E158) No. 77 +
    Exhaust RH (E158) No. 65 +
    LH (E158) No. 64 +
    RH and LH (B134) No. 69 -
  6. Start the engine and let it idle.
  7. Check the waveforms and voltage.
    NOTE: For waveform and voltage, refer to "Control Module (ECM) I/O Signal". Refer to ENGINE (DIAGNOSTICS) (H4DO (HEV))>CONTROL MODULE I/O SIGNAL .
  8. After inspection, install the related parts in the reverse order of removal.
    NOTE: For installation procedures of ECM connectors, refer to Engine Control Module (ECM). Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>ENGINE CONTROL MODULE (ECM)>INSTALLATION .
