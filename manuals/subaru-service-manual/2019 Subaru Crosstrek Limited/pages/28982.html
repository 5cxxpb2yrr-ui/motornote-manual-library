DTC P1C7C-49: HYBRID/EV Battery Voltage System Isolation (A/C AREA) Internal Electronic Failure: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

1. - 

DTC detecting condition: 

When insulation resistance at the motor portion or the air conditioner inverter portion in Electric compressor ASSEMBLY is decreased

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

Normal driving

Wiring diagram: 

Hybrid system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK COMPRESSOR OIL. 

    Check if compressor oil other than ND-OIL 11 such as ND-OIL 8 was used in the past.

    NOTE:
    • If oil other than ND-OIL 11 is used (enters) in the refrigerant cycle even if only slightly, electric insulation may extremely be degraded, causing DTC.
    • If refrigerant system-related parts (evaporator, condenser, Electric compressor ASSEMBLY, expansion valve, piping) are not replaced when a large amount of oil other than ND-OIL 11 enters, electric insulation may not be recovered, causing DTC again.

    Is it free from any incorrect use?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Replace all refrigerant system-related parts (evaporator, condenser, Electric compressor ASSEMBLY, expansion valve, piping). Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>EVAPORATOR . Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>CONDENSER . Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>COMPRESSOR . Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>EXPANSION VALVE . Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>HOSE AND PIPE .

  2. CHECK THE Electric compressor ASSEMBLY UNIT. 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, perform the clear memory of [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control]. Refer to COMMON (DIAG)>CLEAR MEMORY .
    2. Turn to READY ON.
    3. Set the fan dial to HI (MAX), set temperature to MAX COOL, A/C switch to ON and maintain this state for three minutes.
    4. Turn the ignition to OFF.
    5. Remove the service disconnect plug grip. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .
    6. Disconnect the connector (HV104) from Electric compressor ASSEMBLY.
    7. Using a megaohm tester (500 V range), measure the resistance between Electric compressor ASSEMBLY and chassis ground.
      CAUTION: When performing this check, set the megaohm tester to 500 V range. Using a range higher than 500 V may damage parts during check.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV104) No. 1 - Chassis ground:

      (HV104) No. 2 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance 2 MΩ or more?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Oil other than ND-OIL 11 might have entered in the refrigerant system. As oil replacement and cleaning does not recover electric insulation, replace all refrigerant system-related parts (evaporator, condenser, Electric compressor ASSEMBLY, expansion valve, piping). Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>EVAPORATOR . Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>CONDENSER . Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>COMPRESSOR . Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>EXPANSION VALVE . Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>HOSE AND PIPE .

  3. CHECK INSULATION IN A/C SYSTEM. 
    1. Connect the Electric compressor ASSEMBLY connector.
    2. Attach the Service plug. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .
    3. Turn to READY ON.
    4. Set the set temperature to 25°C (77°F), fan dial to LO (MIN) and operate Electric compressor ASSEMBLY for 10 minutes to cause circulation in the refrigerant cycle in order to collect compressor oil remaining in each functional parts into Electric compressor ASSEMBLY.
    5. Using a spot cooler, etc. directly cool Electric compressor ASSEMBLY for 30 minutes. Or, leave the vehicle overnight unattended.
    6. Turn the ignition to OFF.
    7. Remove the service disconnect plug grip. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .
    8. Disconnect the connector (HV104) from Electric compressor ASSEMBLY.
    9. Using a megaohm tester (500 V range), measure the resistance between Electric compressor ASSEMBLY and chassis ground.
      CAUTION: When performing this check, set the megaohm tester to 500 V range. Using a range higher than 500 V may damage parts during check.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV104) No. 1 - Chassis ground:

      (HV104) No. 2 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance 3 MΩ or more?

    Yes:  Replace Electric compressor ASSEMBLY. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>COMPRESSOR .

    No:  Oil other than ND-OIL 11 might have entered in the refrigerant system. As oil replacement and cleaning does not recover electric insulation, replace all refrigerant system-related parts (evaporator, condenser, Electric compressor ASSEMBLY, expansion valve, piping). Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>EVAPORATOR . Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>CONDENSER . Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>COMPRESSOR . Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>EXPANSION VALVE . Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>HOSE AND PIPE .
