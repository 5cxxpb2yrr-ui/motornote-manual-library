EGR Temperature Sensor: Installation

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
CAUTION: Always use a new EGR temperature sensor.
  1. Temporarily tighten the EGR temperature sensor by hand until the sensor is seated.
  2. Tighten the EGR temperature sensor then connect the connector.

    Tightening torque: 

    45 N.m (4.6 kgf-m, 33.2 ft-lb)

  3. Install the air intake duct.

    Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>AIR INTAKE DUCT>INSTALLATION .

  4. Connect the battery ground terminal,

    Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
