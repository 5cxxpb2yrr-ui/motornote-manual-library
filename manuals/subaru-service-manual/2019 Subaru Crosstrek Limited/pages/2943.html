Headlight Assembly: Disassembly: Notes

CAUTION:
  • Use a dry clean cloth so that no grease or water adheres to the glass portion of the bulb.
  • Do not leave the light assembly - head without the bulb for a long time. Dust, moisture, etc. entering the light may affect its performance.
  • When removing with the light assembly - head installed, turn over the mud guard - front and remove each bulb.
    • Halogen headlight: Remove the parking light bulb/turn signal light bulb and side marker light bulb.
    • LED headlight: Remove the side marker light bulb.
