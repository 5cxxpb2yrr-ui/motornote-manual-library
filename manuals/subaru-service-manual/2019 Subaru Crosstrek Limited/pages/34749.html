Rotor Assembly

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Inspect the slip rings for contamination or any roughness on the sliding surface.
  2. Measure the slip ring outer diameter.

    Slip ring outer diameter: 

    Standard 

    14.2 - 14.8 mm (0.559 - 0.583 in)

    Limit 

    14.0 mm (0.551 in)

  3. Check the resistance between slip rings.

    Specification: 

    Approx. 2.0 - 2.4 Ω

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Check insulation between slip ring and rotor core or shaft. Replace the rotor assembly if continuity exists.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Check the ball bearings (rotor side). Check the following items and replace the rotor assembly if necessary.
    • Check that there is no deformation, rust or other damages.
    • Check that there is no resistance or looseness when rotating the ball bearings by hand.
    • Check for grease leakage.
