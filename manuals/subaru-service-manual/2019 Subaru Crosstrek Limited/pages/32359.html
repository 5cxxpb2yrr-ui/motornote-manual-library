ID Registration Confirmation

NOTE:
  • It takes several minutes in order to update the tire pressure data. If the data is not displayed, perform troubleshooting according to the procedures from DTC C2121 to C2124.
  • If the normal tire pressure is displayed, ID registration has been finished properly. If the correct tire pressure is not displayed after several minutes, IDs are wrong or system is malfunctioning. If the IDs are not registered, and 3 minutes or more passes, DTC C2171 is recorded in the ECM/receiver.
  • After registration of all IDs, DTC C2126 is registered in the ECM/receiver, and the TPMS light illuminates, after blinking for 1 minute. If the data is sent to the ECM/receiver from all registered transmitters, DTC C2126 is cleared and the tire pressure warning light goes off.
  1. Enter the following menus: EACH SYSTEM CHECK > TIRE PRESSURE MONITOR> CURRENT DATA DISPLAY & SAVE.
  2. Read the value of the "Tire pressure".
  3. Confirm that data of all transmitters are displayed in SSM3 correctly.
