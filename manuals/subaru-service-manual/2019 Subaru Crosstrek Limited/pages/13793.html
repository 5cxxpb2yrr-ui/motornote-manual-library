6MT Model

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Lift up the vehicle.
  3. Remove the front exhaust pipe. Refer to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FRONT EXHAUST PIPE>REMOVAL .
  4. Remove the center exhaust cover.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the cable cover plate.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Disconnect the MT gear shift cable and MT gear select cable.
    CAUTION:
    • Be careful not to deform the cable inner.
    • Do not twist the cable inner excessively.
    • Do not apply excessive load while holding the part (a).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    1. Pull out down the MT gear shift cable to spread the spring.
    2. Push the MT gear select cable in the direction of arrow, and remove it.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Remove the cable cover assembly.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Lower the vehicle.
  9. Remove the gear shift knob and remove the front console panel. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>CONSOLE BOX>REMOVAL .
  10. Remove the panel center LWR LH and RH. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>CONSOLE BOX>REMOVAL .
  11. Remove the harness clamp.
  12. Remove the gear shift lever assembly.
NOTE: The MT gear shift lever cannot be disassembled.
Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
