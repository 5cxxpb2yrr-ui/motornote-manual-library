DTC P0A7A-73: Generator Inverter Actuator Stuck Closed: Notes

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

1. - 

Detecting condition: 

When any phase current flows at a certain value or more after generator inverter shutdown after a DTC that indicates generator inverter malfunction (overheat, overcurrent or circuit error) is output

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

  1. Normal driving
  2. When malfunction continues
    • Reduced output power
    • A driving range is limited
    • Engine always stopped
  3. In addition, When malfunction continues Hybrid system stopped

Wiring diagram: 

Hybrid system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION:
  • Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to P-HEV DRIVE MOTOR (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  • Since this DTC is detected after either of DTC P0A7A9E or P1C5F19 is confirmed, the DTC that was output simultaneously must be checked after the inspection was complete.
  • Depending on the vehicle situation that short circuit has occurred in the Inverter with converter assembly, the Transmission assembly may be adversely affected. Therefore, if this DTC is detected, perform Transmission assembly performance test and replace abnormal parts.
  • After all repair processes including previously output DTC repair procedures are completed, test drive the vehicle at approx. 40 km/h (25 MPH) for 1 minute to ensure that DTC P0A9200 is not output. If DTC P0A9200 is detected, replace the Transmission assembly before the vehicle is delivered.
  1. CHECK DTC. 

    Read the DTC of [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control] using the Subaru Select Monitor, Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    NOTE:
    Malfunction contents Related DTC
    Insulation malfunction P1C7C49 HYBRID/EV BATTERY VOLTAGE SYSTEM ISOLATION (A/C AREA) INTERNAL ELECTRONIC FAILURE
    P1C7D49 HYBRID/EV BATTERY VOLTAGE SYSTEM ISOLATION (HYBRID/EV BATTERY AREA) INTERNAL ELECTRONIC FAILURE
    P1C7E49 HYBRID/EV BATTERY VOLTAGE SYSTEM ISOLATION (TRANSAXLE AREA) INTERNAL ELECTRONIC FAILURE
    P1C7F49 HYBRID/EV BATTERY VOLTAGE SYSTEM ISOLATION (DIRECT CURRENT AREA) INTERNAL ELECTRONIC FAILURE

    When "Only DTC P0A7A73 is displayed" or "DTC other than above is displayed at the same time", go to  A.

    When DTCs related to [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control], mentioned above, are simultaneously displayed, go to  B.

    Yes: 

    1. Go to  2.
    2. Perform the diagnosis for the simultaneously displayed DTCs.
      NOTE: If more than one DTC is simultaneously displayed, perform the diagnosis in the order described in the above.

    No:  -

  2. CHECK Transmission assembly (GENERATOR). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Check that the Service plug is disconnected. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .
    2. Disconnect Power cable (HV99) from Inverter with converter assembly.
    3. Using a milliohm tester, measure the resistance between Power cable terminals.
      NOTE:
      • When the generator temperature is too high, the resistance value changes largely. Therefore, measure the resistance after stopping the vehicle and leaving it for eight hours or more.
      • To correct a change of the resistance value due to temperature, calculate the resistance value at 20°C (68°F) by applying the following formula:

        R20 = Rt/(1 + 0.00393 x (T - 20))

        R20: Resistance value (mΩ) at 20°C (68°F)

        Rt: Measured resistance value (mΩ)

        T: Temperature (°C (°F)) at measuring the resistance

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV99) W1 - (HV99) U1:

      (HV99) U1 - (HV99) V1:

      (HV99) V1 - (HV99) W1:

      Is the resistance as follows?

      (HV99) W1 - (HV99) U1: 49.30 - 78.96 mΩ

      (HV99) U1 - (HV99) V1: 49.30 - 78.96 mΩ

      (HV99) V1 - (HV99) W1: 49.30 - 78.96 mΩ

      Yes:  Go to  3.

      No:  Go to  4.

  3. CHECK Transmission assembly (GENERATOR). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.

    Using a megohmmeter (500 V range), measure the resistance between Power cable (on Inverter with converter assembly side) terminals and chassis ground and shield line ground.

    CAUTION: When performing this check, set megohmmeter to 500 V. Using a range higher than 500 V may cause damages to part during check.

    Connector & terminal 

    (HV99) W1 - Chassis ground and shield line ground:

    (HV99) U1 - Chassis ground and shield line ground:

    (HV99) V1 - Chassis ground and shield line ground:

    Is the resistance 10 MΩ or more?

    Yes:  Replace Inverter with converter assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>INVERTER AND CONVERTER ASSEMBLY .

    After replacement. Go to  9.

    NOTE: Since this DTC is detected after either of DTC P0A7A9E or P1C5F19 is confirmed, the DTC that was output simultaneously must be checked after the inspection was complete.

    No:  Go to  4.

  4. CHECK Power cable (SHORT TO GROUND). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Remove the Power cable.
    2. Using a megohmmeter (500 V range), measure the resistance between Power cable (on Inverter with converter assembly side) terminals and chassis ground and shield line ground.
      CAUTION:
      • When performing this check, set megohmmeter to 500 V. Using a range higher than 500 V may cause damages to part during check.
      • To prevent the terminals of three-phase AC cable from contacting the chassis ground, be sure to wrap with protection tape.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV99) W1 - Chassis ground and shield line ground:

      (HV99) U1 - Chassis ground and shield line ground:

      (HV99) V1 - Chassis ground and shield line ground:

      Is the resistance 10 MΩ or more?

      Yes:  Go to  5.

      No:  Replace Power cable. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>POWER CABLE .

    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.

        R20 = Rt/(1 + 0.00393 x (T - 20))

        Rt: Measured resistance value (mΩ)

        T: Temperature (°C (°F)) at measuring the resistance

      (HV98) U1 - (HV98) V1:

      (HV98) V1 - (HV98) W1:

      (HV98) U1 - (HV98) V1: 49.30 - 54.30 mΩ

      No:  Replace the Power cable, Transmission assembly and Inverter with converter assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>POWER CABLE . Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TH2A)>TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY . Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>INVERTER AND CONVERTER ASSEMBLY .

      NOTE: Since this DTC is detected after either of DTC P0A7A9E or P1C5F19 is confirmed, the DTC that was output simultaneously must be checked after the inspection was complete.
