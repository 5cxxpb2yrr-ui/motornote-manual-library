Power Cable Assembly

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
WARNING: The hybrid system includes a high voltage circuit. Mishandling could cause accidents such as electric shock or leak. Always check "WARNING (HYBRID SYSTEM)" and perform the proper operation. Refer to PRECAUTION/WARNING>WARNING (HYBRID SYSTEM) .
  1. Install the power cable assembly.
    WARNING: Be sure to wear insulated gloves and use insulated tools.
    CAUTION:
    • Do not apply excessive force to the power cable assembly.
    • Because there is the risk of damaging the threads, be sure to work by hand when connecting the terminals.
    • First partially tighten all of the terminal bolts before fully tightening them.
    • When installing the power cable assembly, be careful not to scratch the terminals, connector housing, or inverter assembly (with converter).
    • Replace the power cable assembly clip and cover - power connector with new parts.
    • Be careful so that foreign matter, water drops, or other substance does not enter the opening or connector seal after the terminal is disconnected.

    Tightening torque: 

    Refer to "COMPONENT" of "General Description". Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>COMPONENT>POWER CABLE .

  2. Install the transmission assembly.
    CAUTION:
    • A helper is required to perform this work.
    • Raise the transmission jack a little at a time and be careful not to contact and damage any surrounding parts.

    Tightening torque: 

    Refer to "COMPONENT" of "General Description".

    CAUTION:
    • Radio ground: Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TH2A)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>COMPONENT>AIR BREATHER HOSE & TRANSMISSION COVER .
    • Pitching stopper and transmission rear crossmember: Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TH2A)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>COMPONENT>TRANSMISSION MOUNTING .
  3. Install the protector - inverter.

    Tightening torque: 

    18 N.m (1.8 kgf-m, 13.3 ft-lb)

  4. Install the crossmember support - rear LH. Refer to FRONT SUSPENSION>CROSSMEMBER SUPPORT>INSTALLATION .
  5. Install the propeller shaft. Refer to DRIVE SHAFT SYSTEM>PROPELLER SHAFT>INSTALLATION .
  6. Install the rear exhaust pipe. Refer to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>REAR EXHAUST PIPE>INSTALLATION .
  7. Install the under cover - front. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>FRONT UNDER COVER>INSTALLATION .
  8. Install the floor under protector. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>FLOOR UNDER PROTECTOR>INSTALLATION .
  9. Install the fuel tank protector and inverter assembly (with converter) cover. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL TANK PROTECTOR>INSTALLATION .
  10. Lower the vehicle.
  11. Install the pitching stopper. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>TRANSMISSION MOUNTING SYSTEM>INSTALLATION>PITCHING STOPPER .
  12. Install the transmission radio ground terminal. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY>INSTALLATION .
  13. Install the air intake boot. Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>Air Intake Boot>INSTALLATION .
  14. Install the service disconnect plug. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG>INSTALLATION .
  15. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
