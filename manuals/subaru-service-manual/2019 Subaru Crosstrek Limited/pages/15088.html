Rear Suspension Ball Joint

  1. Lift up the vehicle until rear wheels are off ground.
  2. Grasp the bottom of tire and move it in and out in axial direction.
  3. If movement is observed between the brake disc cover (A) and end of rear lateral link (front) (B), ball joint may be excessively worn.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Grasp the end of rear lateral link (front) (B) and move it up and down. If movement is observed between the housing (C) and rear lateral link (front) (B) boss, ball joint may be excessively worn.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. If the movement related to the previous two steps is observed, replace the rear lateral link (front). Refer to REAR SUSPENSION>REAR LATERAL LINK (FRONT) .
  6. Damage of dust boots

    Visually inspect the ball joint dust boots. Replace if rear lateral link (front) is damaged.

  7. Check the upper arm ball joint (D) and the pillow ball bushing (E) of housing in the same manner.
Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
