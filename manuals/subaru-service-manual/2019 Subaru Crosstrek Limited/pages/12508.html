DTC B11F3: Front Impact Deployment

Diagnosis start condition: 

When the ignition voltage is 10 V - 16 V.

DTC detecting condition: 

This DTC is indicated when the front airbag module and the pretensioner are deployed.

Once this DTC is displayed, the memory cannot be cleared. Therefore replace the following parts.

  • Airbag control module Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>AIRBAG CONTROL MODULE .
  • Driver's airbag module Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>DRIVER'S AIRBAG MODULE .
  • Passenger's airbag module Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>PASSENGER'S AIRBAG MODULE .
  • Driver's knee airbag module Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>KNEE AIRBAG MODULE .
  • Front sub sensor of both sides Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>FRONT SUB SENSOR .
  • Front outer seat belt with pretensioner of both sides Refer to SEAT BELT SYSTEM>FRONT SEAT BELT .
  • Steering roll connector Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>ROLL CONNECTOR .
  • Occupant detection system (passenger's seat cushion & frame assembly) Refer to SEATS>FRONT SEAT .
  • Curtain airbag module on both sides (when activated) Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>CURTAIN AIRBAG MODULE .
