Turn Signal Light and Hazard Light System: Note

For operation procedures of each component of the turn signal and hazard light system, refer to the respective service informations.

  • Headlight assembly: Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>HEADLIGHT ASSEMBLY .
  • Combination switch (light): Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>COMBINATION SWITCH (LIGHT) .
  • Front turn signal light bulb: Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>HEADLIGHT ASSEMBLY>DISASSEMBLY .
  • Side turn signal light assembly: Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>SIDE TURN SIGNAL LIGHT ASSEMBLY .
  • Rear combination light assembly: Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>REAR COMBINATION LIGHT ASSEMBLY .
  • Rear turn signal light bulb: Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>REAR COMBINATION LIGHT ASSEMBLY>DISASSEMBLY .
  • Hazard switch: Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>HAZARD SWITCH .
