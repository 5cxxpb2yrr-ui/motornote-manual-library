Crossmember And Cushion Rubber

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Lift up the vehicle.
  3. Remove the crossmember support - rear.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the front exhaust pipe, refer to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FRONT EXHAUST PIPE>REMOVAL .
  5. Remove the center exhaust cover.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Set the transmission jack.
    CAUTION: Always support the transmission case with a transmission jack.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Remove the front crossmember and the rear crossmember.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Remove the transmission cushion rubber.
