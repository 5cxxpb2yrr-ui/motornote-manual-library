Roof Spoiler: Installation

CAUTION:
  • Make sure that there is a gasket attached on the clip and bolt of the spoiler assembly or garnish assembly. If a gasket is not attached, water leakage may occur.
  • Make sure the bolt is tightened securely because improper installation may cause the water leakage.
  1. Install the roof spoiler.

    Tightening torque: 

    7.5 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.5 ft-lb)

  2. Install the drip - rear gate.
  3. Install the trim panel - rear gate UPR and the trim panel - rear gate pillar side. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>REAR GATE TRIM>INSTALLATION .
  4. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
