DTC C1412: Selected Parameter

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

DTC detecting condition: 

  • A fault is detected if abnormal set value is found on the vehicle model that were given some parameters.
  • A fault is detected if 0 point setting compensation of the sensor is not yet performed.

Trouble symptom: 

  • ABS does not operate.
  • VDC does not operate.
  • Hill hold does not function.
  • EyeSight does not operate.
NOTE: When the VDCCM & H/U is replaced, this DTC may be stored.
  1. CHECK VDCCM & H/U IDENTIFICATION SYMBOL. 

    Check the identification symbol attached on the H/U. Refer to VEHICLE DYNAMICS CONTROL (VDC)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>SPECIFICATION .

    Is the identification symbol correct?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Replace the VDCCM & H/U. Refer to VEHICLE DYNAMICS CONTROL (VDC)>VDC CONTROL MODULE AND HYDRAULIC CONTROL UNIT (VDCCM & H/U) .

  2. CHECK PARAMETER SELECTED IN VDCCM. 

    Check the parameters. Refer to VEHICLE DYNAMICS CONTROL (VDC)>VDC CONTROL MODULE AND HYDRAULIC CONTROL UNIT (VDCCM & H/U)>MAINTENANCE MODE>VDCCM PARAMETER CHECK .

    Does the parameter registered in the VDCCM match the relevant vehicle?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Select and register the correct parameter. Refer to VEHICLE DYNAMICS CONTROL (VDC)>VDC CONTROL MODULE AND HYDRAULIC CONTROL UNIT (VDCCM & H/U)>MAINTENANCE MODE>VDCCM PARAMETER SELECTION .

  3. CHECK ECM SPECIFICATION. 

    Check the ECM specification.

    Is the specification of ECM same as vehicle specification?

    Yes:  Replace the VDCCM & H/U. Refer to VEHICLE DYNAMICS CONTROL (VDC)>VDC CONTROL MODULE AND HYDRAULIC CONTROL UNIT (VDCCM & H/U) .

    No:  Replace the ECM. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE CONTROL MODULE (ECM) . Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>ENGINE CONTROL MODULE (ECM) .
