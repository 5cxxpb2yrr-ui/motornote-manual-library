Trunk Lid: Adjustment

Adjust the clearance around the trunk lid COMPL as follows.

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
Part Standard
A Trunk lid COMPL and Panel - side outer 3.5±1 mm (0.14±0.04 in)
B Surface level gap between Trunk lid COMPL and Panel - side outer 0.5±1 mm (0.02±0.04)
C Trunk lid COMPL and Panel - side outer 3.5±1 mm (0.14±0.04 in)
D Surface level gap between Trunk lid COMPL and Panel - side outer 0.5±1 mm (0.02±0.04)
E Trunk lid COMPL to Bumper face - rear 7.5±1.5 mm (0.3±0.06 in)
  1. Adjust the horizontal position of the trunk lid COMPL.
    1. Loosen the mounting bolt.
    2. Adjust the horizontal clearance of the trunk lid COMPL.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Adjust the surface level gap between the trunk lid COMPL and the body.
    1. Remove the trim panel - trunk rear.
    2. Loosen the bolt.
    3. Tap the striker - trunk using a plastic hammer to adjust the surface level gap between the trunk lid COMPL and the body.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
