DTC B1630: Curtain Airbag Sensor RH Failure

Diagnosis start condition: 

When the ignition voltage is 10 V - 16 V.

DTC detecting condition: 

Curtain airbag sensor RH is faulty.

If DTC B1630 is displayed, the circuit within the curtain airbag sensor RH is faulty.

Replace the curtain airbag sensor RH. Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>CURTAIN AIRBAG SENSOR .
