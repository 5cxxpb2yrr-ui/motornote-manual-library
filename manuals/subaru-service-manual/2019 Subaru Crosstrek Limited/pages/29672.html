Lane Keep Switch Is Not Turned To On. (Lane Keep Indicator Light Does Not Illuminate In White.)

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. PERFORM REAL TIME DIAGNOSIS. 

    Check the input signal of cruise control system. Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>REAL-TIME DIAGNOSIS .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK LANE KEEP SWITCH. 

    Check the lane keep switch. Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>CHECK EYESIGHT STEERING SWITCH .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Replace the EyeSight steering switch. Refer to EYESIGHT>SWITCHES AND HARNESS .

  3. CHECK COMBINATION METER. 

    Check the combination meter. Refer to COMBINATION METER (DIAGNOSTICS)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Replace the stereo camera. Refer to EYESIGHT>STEREO CAMERA>REMOVAL .

    No:  Repair or replace the faulty portion of the combination meter.
