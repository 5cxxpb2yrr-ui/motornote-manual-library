Seat Belt Outer - Front

CAUTION:
  • Before handling the airbag system components, refer to "CAUTION" of "General Description" in "AIRBAG SYSTEM". Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  • Airbag system satellite safing sensor is located under the front center of the rear seat cushion. Be careful not to apply strong impact to the sensor when working with the rear seat cushion removed.
  • Do not drop or subject the pretensioner to any impact.
  • Since the pretensioner and bracket are integrated as a unit, do not disassemble them.
  1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
  2. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery and wait for at least 60 seconds before starting work. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    NOTE: On power seat models, move the front seat all the way forward before disconnecting the battery ground terminal.
  3. Tilt the front seat backrest assembly forward, and move the front seat all the way forward.
    CAUTION: For the power seat, move the seat, disconnect the battery ground terminal, and then wait for 60 seconds or more before starting work.
  4. Remove the rear seat cushion assembly. Refer to SEATS>REAR SEAT>REMOVAL .
  5. Remove the cover side sill - front INN, cover side sill - rear INN, and trim panel - center pillar LWR. (On the side where seat belt assembly is removed) Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>LOWER INNER TRIM>REMOVAL .
  6. Remove the seat belt outer - front.
    1. Turn over the floor mat to disconnect the belt tension sensor connector. Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>AIRBAG CONNECTOR>PROCEDURE>PASSENGER'S AIRBAG MODULE AND SEAT BELT PRETENSIONER .
      NOTE: Also disconnect the connector of the passenger's lap seat belt pretensioner. Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>AIRBAG CONNECTOR>PROCEDURE>KNEE AIRBAG MODULE AND PASSENGER'S LAP SEAT BELT PRETENSIONER .
    2. Remove the cover through, and remove the seat belt anchor bolt.
    3. Remove the bolt to remove the seat belt retractor.
    4. Remove the lower anchor bolt, and remove the seat belt outer - front.

  7. Remove the adjuster assembly - seat belt.
    1. Remove the bolt on the lower side of the trim panel - center pillar UPR.
    2. Remove the clip by pulling the upper part of the trim toward you, and while lowering the entire trim, remove the trim panel - center pillar UPR.
      CAUTION: Do not reuse the upper clips of the trim panel - center pillar UPR. Once the clip is removed, its holding force is reduced. Be sure to replace it with a new clip.

    3. Remove the bolts, and then remove the adjuster assembly - seat belt.
