Diagnostic Procedure For Subaru Select Monitor Communication: Inspection

Communication error with auto headlight beam leveler control module

Detecting condition: 

  • Defective harness connector
  • Power supply circuit malfunction
  • Defective auto headlight beam leveler control module
  • Defective CAN communication circuit
  • Defective Subaru Select Monitor

Trouble symptom: 

Communication is impossible between auto headlight beam leveler control module and Subaru Select Monitor.

Wiring diagram: 

Headlight beam leveler system. Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (IMPREZA MODEL)>HEADLIGHT BEAM LEVELER SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>HEADLIGHT BEAM LEVELER SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>HEADLIGHT BEAM LEVELER SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: Initialization is required after replacing the auto headlight beam leveler control module. Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>AUTO HEADLIGHT BEAM LEVELER SYSTEM>PROCEDURE .
  1. CHECK FUSE. 

    Check the fuse. Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>RELAY AND FUSE .

    Yes:  Is the fuse OK?

    No:  Go to  2.

    Replace the fuse. If the replaced fuse has blown out immediately, repair the short circuit to ground in harness between battery and auto headlight beam leveler control module.

  2. CHECK HARNESS. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the connector of combination meter.
    3. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    4. Using a tester, measure the voltage between auto headlight beam leveler control module connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (B616) No. 1 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

      Is the voltage 10 - 13 V?

      Yes:  Go to  3.

      No:  Repair the open circuit in the harness between auto headlight beam leveler control module connector and fuse.

  3. CHECK HARNESS. 

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between the auto headlight beam leveler control module connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (B616) No. 2 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Repair the open circuit of harness between auto headlight beam leveler control module connector and chassis ground.

  4. CHECK LAN SYSTEM. 

    Inspect LAN system. Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE . Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  5.

    No:  Perform the inspection according to the diagnosis for LAN system.

  5. CHECK SUBARU SELECT MONITOR. 
    1. Connect the select monitor to another vehicle.
    2. Check whether the communication for initializing can be executed normally.

      Is communication normal?

      Yes:  Go to  6.

      No:  Repair or replace the select monitor.

  6. CHECK AUTO HEADLIGHT BEAM LEVELER control module. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Connect the auto headlight beam leveler control module connector.
    3. Communicate with [Headlight/Foglight] using the Subaru Select Monitor.

      Is communication possible?

      Yes:  The circuit has returned to a normal condition at this time. Reproduce the failure, and then perform the diagnosis again.

      NOTE: In this case, temporary poor contact of connector, temporary open or short circuit of harness may be the cause.

      No:  Replace the auto headlight beam leveler control module. Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>AUTO HEADLIGHT BEAM LEVELER CONTROL MODULE .
