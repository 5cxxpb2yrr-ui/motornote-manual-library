Transmission Harness: Inspection

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Visually check the harness and connector for damage or crack.
  2. Check the harness terminal for rust, disconnection or poor contact.
  3. Check the continuity between harness terminals.
    NOTE: For details of transmission harness circuit, refer to wiring diagram. Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>CVT CONTROL SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

    Harness continuity standard 

    Less than 1 Ω
