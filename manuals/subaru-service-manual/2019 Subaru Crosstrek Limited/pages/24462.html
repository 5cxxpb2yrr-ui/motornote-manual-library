Diagnostic Method

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

If the duration of time while the following conditions are met is longer than the time indicated, judge as NG.

  1. Judgment 1: Diagnosis for open circuit 
    JUDGMENT VALUE SPECIFICATION

    Malfunction Criteria Threshold Value
    Operating current 0.2 A or less
  2. Judgment 2: Diagnosis for overcurrent 
    JUDGMENT VALUE SPECIFICATION

    Malfunction Criteria Threshold Value
    Operating current 32.4 A or more
  3. Judgment 3: Diagnosis for ground short 
    JUDGMENT VALUE SPECIFICATION

    Malfunction Criteria Threshold Value
    Terminal voltage 1 V or less
  4. Judgment 4: Diagnosis for power supply short 
    JUDGMENT VALUE SPECIFICATION

    Malfunction Criteria Threshold Value
    Terminal voltage 5 V or more
  5. Judgment 5: Diagnosis for short circuit to ground (between ECM and injector driver) 
    JUDGMENT VALUE SPECIFICATION

    Malfunction Criteria Threshold Value
    Injector control voltage Less than 3.92 V
  6. Judgment 6: Diagnosis for short circuit to power supply (between ECM and injector driver) 
JUDGMENT VALUE SPECIFICATION

Malfunction Criteria Threshold Value
Injector control voltage 3.92 V or more

Time needed for diagnosis: 

Judgment 1, 2, 5: Map1

Judgment 3, 4, 6: Map2

Map1 

Engine speed (rpm) 500 2000 4000
Time when calculation was performed 10 times (sec) 2.4 0.6 0.3

Map2 

Engine speed (rpm) 0 500 2000 4000
Time when calculation was performed 10 times (sec) 0.04 2.4 0.6 0.3

Malfunction indicator light illumination:  Illuminates as soon as a malfunction occurs.
