Lane Keep (Lane Departure Prevention) Is Cancelled Without Releasing Operation

  1. CHECK CONDITION OF OCCURRENCE. 

    Interview the customer to check if it was canceled by the cancel conditions described in OM.

    Is it cancelled by the cancel conditions (releasing of lane departure prevention function) described in the OM?

    Yes:  This is normal.

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK FOR PRESENCE OF LANE DEPARTURE PREVENTION DEACTIVATION CODES. 

    Check for the presence of ECM lane departure prevention deactivation codes. Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>LANE DEPARTURE PREVENTION ASSIST DEACTIVATE CODE(S) DISPLAY .

    Is a lane departure prevention deactivation code displayed?

    Yes:  Perform the diagnosis for the displayed lane departure prevention deactivation code. Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>LANE DEPARTURE PREVENTION ASSIST DEACTIVATE CODE(S) DISPLAY .

    No:  Replace the stereo camera. Refer to EYESIGHT>STEREO CAMERA>REMOVAL .
