EGR Temperature Sensor: Inspection

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Check the EGR temperature sensor for deformation, cracks or other damages.
  2. Check the resistance between the EGR temperature sensor terminals.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Terminal No. Standard
    1 and 2 Approx. 10 kΩ (at 25°C (77°F))
