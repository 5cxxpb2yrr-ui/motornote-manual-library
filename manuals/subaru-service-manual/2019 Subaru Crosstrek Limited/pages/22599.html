Rear Differential (T-type): Disassembly

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

To detect the real cause of trouble, inspect the following items before disassembling.

  • Tooth contact and backlash between hypoid driven gear and drive pinion
  • Hypoid driven gear runout on its back surface
  • Total preload of drive pinion
    1. Set the ST on a vise, and install the differential assembly to the ST.

      Special tool: 

      ST: 398217700 ATTACHMENT SET

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Remove the drain plug and filler plug.
    3. Remove the air breather cap.
      CAUTION:
      • Do not attempt to remove the air breather cap unless necessary.
      • Whenever the air breather cap is removed, replace it with a new part.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    4. Remove the bolts, and then remove the rear cover.
    5. Remove the stud bolts from rear cover if necessary.
    6. Remove the side retainer attachment bolts, set the ST to the differential case, and remove the side retainer with a puller.
      NOTE:
      • Side retainer shim of each side should be kept together with its mating retainer.
      • Keep the side retainers separate by attaching tags or in similar ways to make it possible to identify RH and LH sides during reassembly.

      Special tool: 

      ST: 398457700 ATTACHMENT

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    7. Remove the side oil seal and O-ring from the side retainer.
    8. Remove the differential case assembly.
      CAUTION: Be careful not to hit the teeth of hypoid driven gear against the differential carrier.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    9. When replacing the side bearing, remove the side bearing race from the side retainer using the ST.

      Special tool: 

      ST: 398527700 PULLER ASSY

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    10. Remove the side bearing cone using the ST.
      NOTE:
      • Do not attempt to disassemble the parts unless necessary.
      • Set the ST so that its claws catch the edge of the side bearing cone.
      • Never mix up the RH and LH side bearing races and cones.

      Special tool: 

      ST: 18759AA000 PULLER ASSY

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    11. Remove the hypoid driven gear by loosening hypoid driven gear bolts.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    12. Remove the pinion shaft lock pin from hypoid driven gear side using the ST.

      Special tool: 

      ST: 899904100 STRAIGHT PIN REMOVER

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    13. Draw out the pinion mate shaft and remove pinion mate gears, side gears and side gear thrust washers.
      NOTE: The gears and washers should be marked with front or rear, RH or LH, or kept separately.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    14. Remove the self-locking nut while securing the companion flange with the ST.

      Special tool: 

      ST: 498427200 FLANGE WRENCH

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    15. Extract the companion flange with a puller.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    16. Press the end of drive pinion shaft using the ST and remove the rear bearing cone, pinion height adjusting washer, preload adjusting spacer and washer.
      CAUTION: Hold the drive pinion so as not to drop it.

      Special tool: 

      ST: 398467700 DRIFT

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    17. Support the rear bearing cone with the ST, and remove it from the drive pinion.
      NOTE: Place the replacer so that its center-recessed side faces the bearing cone.

      Special tool: 

      ST: 398517700 REPLACER

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    18. Remove the front oil seal using the PULLER ASSY (398527700).
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    19. Remove the pilot bearing together with the front bearing cone and spacer using the ST.

      Special tool: 

      ST: 398467700 DRIFT

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    20. When replacing the bearings, tap out and remove the front bearing race and rear bearing race in this order using a brass bar.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
