DTC B1413: Immobilizer Control Module Battery Power Supply

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

Diagnosis start condition 

  • Battery voltage is 10 V or more.
  • The remote engine starter was not used to start the engine.

DTC detecting condition: 

Battery voltage for the immobilizer control module was outside the specification for a certain period of time.

Trouble symptom: 

The security indicator does not blink after the ignition key is turned to OFF.

Wiring diagram: 

Immobilizer system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (IMPREZA MODEL)>IMMOBILIZER SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>IMMOBILIZER SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

CAUTION: When the immobilizer control module is replaced, registration of the immobilizer system is required. For details, refer to the "REGISTRATION INSTRUCTIONS FOR IMMOBILIZER" provided as a separate volume.
  1. CHECK FUSE. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Check the fuse.

      Is the check result OK?

      Yes:  Go to  2.

      No:  Replace the fuse. If the fuse blows out easily, repair the short circuit to power supply in harness between the battery and immobilizer control module.

  2. CHECK DATA MONITOR. 

    Using the Subaru Select Monitor, check the following items in the [Data monitor] of [Immobilizer]. Refer to IMMOBILIZER (DIAGNOSTICS)>DATA MONITOR .

    • BATT voltage

    Is the display 10 V or more?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Go to  5.

  3. CHECK DATA MONITOR. 
    1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery.
    2. Wait for at least 10 seconds.
    3. Connect the ground cable to battery.
    4. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, check the following items in the [Data monitor] of [Immobilizer]. Refer to IMMOBILIZER (DIAGNOSTICS)>DATA MONITOR .
      • Battery Voltage

      Is the display 10 V or more?

      Yes:  Go to  4.

      No:  Go to  5.

  4. CHECK DTC. 

    Read the DTC of [Immobilizer] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to IMMOBILIZER (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is DTC B1413 displayed? (Current malfunction)

    Yes:  Replace the immobilizer control module. Refer to SECURITY AND LOCKS>IMMOBILIZER CONTROL MODULE .

    No:  Even if DTC is displayed, the circuit has returned to a normal condition at this time. Reproduce the failure, and then perform the diagnosis again.

    NOTE: In this case, temporary poor contact of connector, temporary open or short circuit of harness may be the cause.
  5. CHECK IMMOBILIZER control module POWER SUPPLY CIRCUIT. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the connector from immobilizer control module.
    3. Measure the voltage between immobilizer control module connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (i34) No. 1 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

      Is the voltage 10 V or more?

      Yes:  Go to  6.

      No:  Repair the open circuit of harness between the immobilizer control module and fuse.

  6. CHECK IMMOBILIZER control module GROUND CIRCUIT. 

    Measure the resistance between immobilizer control module connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (i34) No. 5 (+) - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance less than 10 Ω?

    Yes:  Replace the immobilizer control module. Refer to SECURITY AND LOCKS>IMMOBILIZER CONTROL MODULE .

    No:  Repair the open circuit of harness between the immobilizer control module connector and chassis ground.
