DTC U0424: Invalid Data Received From HVAC Control Module

DTC detecting condition: 

CAN data from A/C control panel is abnormal.

Trouble symptom: 

  • Air conditioner status is not displayed in the regularly display bar.
  • Air conditioner status is not displayed correctly in the regularly display bar.
  • Setting temperature reading in the regularly displayed bar is not displayed.
  • Operating the air conditioner does not display air conditioner interruption.
  • Air conditioner status is not displayed correctly in the air conditioner interruption display.
NOTE: For the diagnostic procedure, refer to DTC U0074. Refer to MULTI-FUNCTION DISPLAY (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE WITH DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>DTC U0074 CONTROL MODULE COMMUNICATION BUS "BODY-CAN" OFF .
