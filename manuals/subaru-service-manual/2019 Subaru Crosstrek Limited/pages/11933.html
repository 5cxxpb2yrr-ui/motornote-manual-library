Subaru EyeSight

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  • Operate the EyeSight system. Check that the system is activated and deactivated correctly.
  • Check the stereo camera cover opening for foreign matter.
  • Check the glass in front of stereo camera for dirt and stickers.
  • Check the lens and filter of the stereo camera for dirt such as fingerprints.
  • Check the filter at lens portion of stereo camera for damage.
  • According to the "CAUTION" instructions, check the windshield glass and dashboard in front of stereo camera to make sure that no aftermarket part is adhered or attached in the prohibited area. Refer to PRECAUTION/WARNING>CAUTION>CHECK WINDSHIELD GLASS INCLUDING PROHIBITED AREA FOR MODEL WITH HBA AND EYESIGHT .
  • When delivering the vehicle, explain to the customer to make sure not to touch the filter at lens portion of the stereo camera.
