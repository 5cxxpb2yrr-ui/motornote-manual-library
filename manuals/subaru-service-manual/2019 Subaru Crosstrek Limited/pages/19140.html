Return Hose

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Using the refrigerant recovery system, discharge refrigerant. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>REFRIGERANT RECOVERY PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .
  2. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  3. Remove the collector cover. Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>COLLECTOR COVER>REMOVAL .
  4. Remove the under cover - front. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>FRONT UNDER COVER>REMOVAL .
  5. Drain engine coolant. (Inverter assembly side) Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>ENGINE COOLANT>REPLACEMENT>DRAINING OF ENGINE COOLANT .
  6. Remove the air intake duct. Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>AIR INTAKE DUCT>REMOVAL .
  7. Remove the cover UPR - front.
    1. Remove the clips, and remove the cover UPR - front.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Remove the bracket - hood stay.
    1. Remove the bolts and remove the bracket - hood stay.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Remove the inverter tank. Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>INVERTER TANK>REMOVAL .
  10. Remove the A/C accumulator assembly and bracket - A/C accumulator. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>ACCUMULATOR>REMOVAL .
  11. Remove the hose - pressure suction COMPL.
  1. Remove the bolt and remove the hose - pressure suction COMPL.
CAUTION:
  • Do not apply excessive force to the hose.
  • Seal the engaging parts of the disconnected hose and A/C accumulator assembly with plugs or vinyl tape to prevent foreign matter from entering.
