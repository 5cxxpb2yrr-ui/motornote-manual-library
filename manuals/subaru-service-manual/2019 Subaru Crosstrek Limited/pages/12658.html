DTC B1789: B+ Power Supply

Diagnosis start condition: 

When the ignition voltage is 10 - 16 V.

DTC detecting condition: 

  • Poor contact in harness between occupant detection control module and battery
  • Battery malfunction
  • Occupant detection control module malfunction

Trouble symptom: 

Occupant judgment of occupant detection control module stops.

Wiring diagram: 

Occupant detection system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (IMPREZA MODEL)>OCCUPANT DETECTION SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>OCCUPANT DETECTION SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>OCCUPANT DETECTION SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to OCCUPANT DETECTION (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
NOTE: Before starting work, prepare two sets of TEST HARNESS BC (98299FL030).
  1. CHECK BATTERY. 

    Check the battery. Refer to STARTING/CHARGING SYSTEMS (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>BATTERY>INSPECTION . Refer to STARTING/CHARGING SYSTEMS (H4DO (HEV))>BATTERY>INSPECTION .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Charge or replace the battery.

  2. CHECK FUSE. 

    Check the fuse of occupant detection control module.

    Is the fuse OK?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Replace the fuse. If the replaced fuse blows out easily, repair or replace the short circuit in the harness between battery and occupant detection control module.

  3. CHECK OCCUPANT DETECTION CONTROL MODULE CONNECTOR. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF, disconnect the battery ground terminal, and wait 60 seconds or more. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    2. Confirm that the connector of the occupant detection control module (AB61) is securely connected.

    Is the connector of the occupant detection control module securely connected?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Reconnect the connector of the occupant detection control module.

  4. CHECK HARNESS (OPEN CIRCUIT BETWEEN BATTERY AND OCCUPANT DETECTION CONTROL MODULE). 
    1. Disconnect the connector (AB61) of occupant detection control module.
    2. Connect the battery ground terminal and turn the ignition switch to ON. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    3. Using a tester, measure the voltage between occupant detection control module connector and chassis ground.
    CAUTION: Connect the terminal in test harness BC to connector (AB61) terminal, and apply the tester to test harness BC.

    Connector & terminal 

    (AB61) No. 1 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    Is the voltage 10 V or more?

    Yes:  Go to  8.

    No:  Go to  5.

  5. CHECK HARNESS (OPEN CIRCUIT BETWEEN BATTERY AND REAR HARNESS). 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF, disconnect the battery ground terminal, and wait 60 seconds or more. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    2. Disconnect the connector (AB59) of seat harness.
    3. Connect the connector (1AB) in the test harness AB to the connector (AB53).
    4. Connect the battery ground terminal and turn the ignition switch to ON. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    5. Using a tester, measure the voltage between connector (2AB) in test harness AB and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (2AB) No. 6 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    Is the voltage 10 V or more?

    Yes:  Go to  6.

    No:  Check rear harness, instrument panel wiring harness, bulkhead wiring harness in this order, and replace the faulty harness.

  6. CHECK SEAT HARNESS (OPEN CIRCUIT). 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF, disconnect the battery ground terminal, and wait 60 seconds or more. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    2. Connect the connector (1BB) in the test harness BB to the connector (AB59).
    3. Using a tester, measure the resistance between occupant detection control module connector and connector (2BB) in test harness BB.
    CAUTION: Connect the terminal in test harness BC to connector (AB61) terminal, and apply the tester to test harness BC.

    Connector & terminal 

    (AB61) No. 1 - (2BB) No. 6:

    Is the resistance less than 10 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  7.

    No:  Replace the seat harness.

  7. CHECK SEAT HARNESS (SHORT). 

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between occupant detection control module connector and chassis ground, and between occupant detection control module connector terminals.

    CAUTION: Connect the terminal in test harness BC to connector (AB61) terminal, and apply the tester to test harness BC. Two sets of test harness BC are required.

    Connector & terminal 

    (AB61) No. 1 - Chassis ground:

    (AB61) No. 1 - All terminals other than (AB61) No. 1:

    Is the resistance 1 MΩ or more?

    Yes:  Go to  8.

    No:  Replace the seat harness.

  8. CHECK HARNESS (OPEN CIRCUIT BETWEEN OCCUPANT DETECTION CONTROL MODULE AND GROUND). 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF, disconnect the battery ground terminal, and wait 60 seconds or more. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    2. Connect all the connectors other than (AB61) of occupant detection control module.
    3. Using a tester, measure the resistance between occupant detection control module connector (AB61) and chassis ground.
    CAUTION: Connect the terminal in test harness BC to connector (AB61) terminal, and apply the tester to test harness BC.

    Connector & terminal 

    (AB61) No. 3 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance less than 10 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  11.

    No:  Go to  9.

  9. CHECK HARNESS (OPEN CIRCUIT BETWEEN REAR HARNESS AND GROUND). 
    1. Disconnect the connector (AB59) of seat harness.
    2. Connect the connector (1AB) in the test harness AB to the connector (AB53).
    3. Using a tester, measure the resistance between connector (2AB) in test harness AB and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (2AB) No. 2 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance less than 10 Ω?

    Yes:  Replace the seat harness.

    No:  Go to  10.

  10. CHECK HARNESS (OPEN CIRCUIT BETWEEN AIRBAG control module AND GROUND). 
    1. Disconnect the connectors (AB6, AB17, AB18) from airbag control module.
    2. Connect the connector (1AG) in the test harness AG to the connectors (AB6, AB17, AB18).
    3. Using a tester, measure the resistance between connector (2AG) in test harness AG and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (2AG) No. 4 - Chassis ground:

    (2AG) No. 3 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance less than 10 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  11.

    No:  Check for poor contact in grounding point. If faulty, repair it.

  11. CHECK POOR CONTACT OF CONNECTORS. 

    Check for poor contact in the connector of the occupant detection control module.

    Is there poor contact?

    Yes:  Repair or replace the connector. (Replace the harness without repairing the airbag system connector.)

    No:  Replace the occupant detection control module. Refer to SEATS>FRONT SEAT>DISASSEMBLY>PASSENGER'S SEAT .
