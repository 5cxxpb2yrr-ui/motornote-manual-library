Pedestrian Alert Control Unit: Removal

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the hybrid powertrain control module. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL MODULE>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the pedestrian alert control unit.
    1. Disconnect the connector.
    2. Remove the nuts and remove the pedestrian alert control unit.
