Rear Left Power Window Does Not Work By Rear Left Seat Switch

Trouble causes: 

  • Rear seat switch malfunction
  • Rear regulator & motor assembly malfunction
  • Defective harness between the rear seat switch and the rear regulator & motor assembly
  • Power supply circuit malfunction

Wiring diagram: 

Power window system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (IMPREZA MODEL)>POWER WINDOW SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>POWER WINDOW SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>POWER WINDOW SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to POWER WINDOW (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK REAR POWER WINDOW SWITCH. 

    Perform unit inspection of rear power window switch. Refer to GLASS/WINDOWS/MIRRORS>POWER WINDOW CONTROL SWITCH .

    Is the rear power window switch OK?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Replace the rear power window switch. Refer to GLASS/WINDOWS/MIRRORS>POWER WINDOW CONTROL SWITCH .

  2. CHECK POWER SUPPLY CIRCUIT (OPEN). 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the rear power window switch connector.
    3. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    4. Using a tester, measure the voltage between the rear power window switch connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (D25) No. 3 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    Is the voltage 10 - 15 V?

    Yes:  Repair the power supply circuit.

    No:  Go to  3.

  3. CHECK HARNESS (OPEN CIRCUIT). 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the rear regulator & motor assembly connector.
    3. Using a tester, measure the resistance between the rear power window switch connector and the rear regulator & motor assembly connector.

      Connector & terminal 

      (D25) No. 2 - (D24) No. 2:

      (D25) No. 4 - (D24) No. 1:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Perform unit inspection of the rear regulator & motor assembly. Refer to GLASS/WINDOWS/MIRRORS>REAR REGULATOR AND MOTOR ASSEMBLY .

    No:  Repair the open circuit of harness between the rear power window switch connector and the rear regulator & motor assembly connector.
