DTC P0ABF-28: Hybrid/EV Battery Current Sensor "A" Signal Bias Level Out Of Range/Zero Adjustment Failure: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

1. - 

Outline of diagnosis: 

When the BATTERY CURRENT SENSOR offset value is unusually large

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

  • Reduced output power
  • Engine not stopped

Wiring diagram: 

Hybrid system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
NOTE:
  • After BATTERY CURRENT SENSOR is replaced, perform current sensor offset learning. Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE WITH DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>DTC P0C3000 HYBRID/EV BATTERY STATE OF CHARGE HIGH .
  • If the current sensor offset learning is not completed, this DTC may be output temporarily.
  1. CHECK DTC. 

    Read the following DTCs using Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) . Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    • P-HEV High Voltage Battery
    • P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control
    NOTE:
    System Related DTC
    P-HEV High Voltage Battery P060A47 HYBRID/EV BATTERY ENERGY CONTROL MODULE MONITORING PROCESSOR WATCHDOG/SAFETY MCU FAILURE
    P060B49 HYBRID/EV BATTERY ENERGY CONTROL MODULE A/D PROCESSING INTERNAL ELECTRONIC FAILURE
    P060687 HYBRID/EV BATTERY ENERGY CONTROL MODULE PROCESSOR TO MONITORING PROCESSOR MISSING MESSAGE
    P062F46 HYBRID/EV BATTERY ENERGY CONTROL MODULE EEPROM CALIBRATION/PARAMETER MEMORY FAILURE
    P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control P0A1F94 HYBRID/EV BATTERY ENERGY CONTROL MODULE UNEXPECTED OPERATION

    When "Only DTC P0ABF28 is displayed" or "DTC other than above is displayed", go to  A.

    When DTCs related to [P-HEV High Voltage Battery], mentioned above, are simultaneously displayed, go to  B.

    When DTCs related to [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control], mentioned above, are simultaneously displayed, go to  C.

    Yes: 

    1. Go to  2.
    2. Perform the diagnosis for the simultaneously displayed DTCs.
      NOTE: If more than one DTC is simultaneously displayed, perform the diagnosis in the order described in the above.
    3. Perform the diagnosis for the simultaneously displayed DTCs.
    NOTE: If more than one DTC is simultaneously displayed, perform the diagnosis in the order described in the above.

    No:  -

  2. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Battery ECU assembly - HV battery junction block) (OPEN). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Check that the Service plug is disconnected. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .
    2. Turn the ignition to OFF.
    3. Disconnect the ground terminal from Battery (12V). Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    4. Disconnect HV battery junction block connector (HV54).
    5. Disconnect Battery ECU assembly connector (HV42).
    6. Using a tester, measure the resistance between Battery ECU assembly connector and HV battery junction block (BATTERY CURRENT SENSOR) connector.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV42) No. 30 - (HV54) No. 2:

      (HV42) No. 31 - (HV54) No. 3:

      (HV42) No. 7 - (HV54) No. 4:

      (HV42) No. 8 - (HV54) No. 1:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Replace the Wire harness between Battery ECU assembly connector and HV battery junction block (BATTERY CURRENT SENSOR) connector.

  3. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Battery ECU assembly - HV battery junction block) (SHORT TO GROUND AND LINE-TO-LINE SHORT). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between terminals in Battery ECU assembly connector, and between Battery ECU assembly connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (HV42) No. 30 - (HV42) No. 1 - 69:

    (HV42) No. 30 - Chassis ground:

    (HV42) No. 31 - (HV42) No. 1 - 69:

    (HV42) No. 31 - Chassis ground:

    (HV42) No. 7 - (HV42) No. 1 - 69:

    (HV42) No. 7 - Chassis ground:

    (HV42) No. 8 - (HV42) No. 1 - 69:

    (HV42) No. 8 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance 10 kΩ or more?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Replace the Wire harness between Battery ECU assembly connector and HV battery junction block (BATTERY CURRENT SENSOR) connector.

  4. CHECK HV battery junction block (BATTERY CURRENT SENSOR (IB0)). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Disconnect the ground terminal from Battery (12V). Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    2. Turn to IG ON.
    3. Using a tester, measure the voltage between terminals in HV battery junction block (BATTERY CURRENT SENSOR) connector.

      Connector & terminal 

      VIB voltage: (HV54) No. 1 (+) - (HV54) No. 3 (-):

      IB0 voltage: (HV54) No. 4 (+) - (HV54) No. 3 (-):

    Are all of the following conditions established?

    • IB0 voltage - 0.56 x VIB voltage = less than 0.084 V
    • IB0 voltage - 0.56 x VIB voltage = -0.084 V or more

    Yes:  Go to  5.

    No:  Replace HV battery junction block. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>RELAY AND FUSE .

  5. CHECK HV battery junction block (BATTERY CURRENT SENSOR (IB1)). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.

    Using a tester, measure the voltage between terminals in HV battery junction block (BATTERY CURRENT SENSOR) connector.

    Connector & terminal 

    VIB voltage: (HV54) No. 1 (+) - (HV54) No. 3 (-):

    IB1 voltage: (HV54) No. 2 (+) - (HV54) No. 3 (-):

    Are all of the following conditions established?

    • IB1 voltage - 0.44 x VIB voltage = less than 0.078 V
    • IB1 voltage - 0.44 x VIB voltage = -0.078 V or more

    Yes:  Go to  6.

    No:  Replace HV battery junction block. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>RELAY AND FUSE .

  6. CHECK DTC. 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Replace Battery ECU assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>BATTERY CONTROL MODULE .
    2. Restore the vehicle to its original state.
    3. Turn to IG ON.
    4. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, perform the clear memory of [P-HEV High Voltage Battery]. Refer to COMMON (DIAG)>CLEAR MEMORY .
    5. Perform city driving for about 10 minutes.
    6. Turn to IG OFF, and wait for two minutes or more.
    7. Turn to IG ON, and wait for 10 seconds or more.
    8. Read the DTC of [P-HEV High Voltage Battery] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is DTC P0ABF28 displayed?

    Yes:  Replace HV battery junction block. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>RELAY AND FUSE .

    No:  Finish the diagnosis.
