Malfunction Description

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly detects safety device (interlock) operation, and open of its detection circuit. (Even when there is an open circuit during the vehicle still, it is judged as operation.)

The following cases are assumed to be malfunction causes:

  • Detection switch system fault
    • Malfunction of Service plug
  • Low voltage system fault
    • Malfunction of Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly
    • Malfunction of Service plug
    • Malfunction of Wire harness
    • Connector malfunction
