Transmission Case: Disassembly

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Remove the oil stopper plate.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Remove all plugs from the transmission case.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the oil seal using a screwdriver wrapped with cloth etc.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the ball bearing of the secondary pulley using the ST1 and ST2.
    1. Remove the claw of ST1, and attach the claw of ST2.

      Preparation tool: 

      ST1: PULLER ASSY (398527700)

      ST2: CLAW (18760AA000)

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Remove the ball bearing of the secondary pulley using the ST1 and ST2.

      Preparation tool: 

      ST1: PULLER ASSY (398527700)

      ST2: CLAW (18760AA000)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the oil guide.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Remove the roller bearing of the reduction driven gear.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Remove the thrust bearing.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Remove the CVTF filter cover and gasket.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Remove the CVTF filter.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
