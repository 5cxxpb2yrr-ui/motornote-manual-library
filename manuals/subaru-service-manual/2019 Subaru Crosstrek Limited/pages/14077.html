DTC P1CC8-1E: Hybrid/EV Battery Stack 1 Voltage Difference Out Of Range: Notes

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

1. - 

Outline of diagnosis: 

If the difference of voltage between each cell of NO. 1 HV supply stack sub-assembly is equal to or greater than the standard

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

  • Reduced output power
  • Engine not stopped

Wiring diagram: 

Hybrid system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

  3. CHECK CONNECTOR CONNECTION CONDITION (Battery ECU assembly CONNECTOR). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Check that the Service plug is disconnected. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .
    2. Check the connecting condition of connector (HV42) in Battery ECU assembly and check the relevant terminals for contact pressure.

    Is there looseness at the connector connection, or any poor contact at the relevant terminals?

    Yes:  Connect the connector securely. Or if the terminal is defective, replace the HV battery. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY .

    No:  Go to  4.

  4. CHECK Battery ECU assembly (VA1 -VA19). 
    CAUTION:
    • Wrong terminal polarity will cause inaccurate measurement.
    • Be sure to read the resistance after it becomes stable.
    • When checking Battery ECU assembly, select a thin probe (approx. 0.5 mm) from the test lead set before measurement; otherwise the terminals would be deformed.
    1. Turn to IG ON.
    2. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, read the value of [Hybrid Battery Cell 1 Voltage] - [Hybrid Battery Cell 19 Voltage] in [Freeze frame data] of DTC P1CC81E. Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>FREEZE FRAME DATA .
    3. Turn the ignition to OFF.
    4. Remove Battery ECU assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>BATTERY CONTROL MODULE .
    5. Using a tester, measure the resistance in Battery ECU assembly terminals.
      NOTE: Measure the point only related to Hybrid battery cell where the voltage is minimum from the result of freeze frame data.
