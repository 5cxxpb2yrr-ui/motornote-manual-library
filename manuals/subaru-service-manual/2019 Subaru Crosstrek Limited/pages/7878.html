Cylinder Head LH

  1. Set the part so that the cylinder block LH is on the upper side.
  2. Install the water jacket spacer to the cylinder block LH.
    NOTE: When installing a water jacket spacer, make sure that the notch (a) comes to the intake side.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Clean the bolt holes in the cylinder block LH.
    CAUTION: To avoid erroneous tightening of the bolts, clean out the bolt holes sufficiently by blowing with compressed air to eliminate engine coolant etc.
  4. Apply liquid gasket to both sides of new cylinder head gasket LH as shown in the figure.
    NOTE:
    • Before applying liquid gasket, degrease the mating surface of cylinder blocks LH and cylinder head LH.
    • Install within 5 min. after applying liquid gasket.

    Liquid gasket: 

    THREE BOND 1217G (Part No. K0877Y0100), THREE BOND 1217H or equivalent

    Liquid gasket applying diameter: 

    3±1 mm (0.1181±0.0394 in)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Attach the cylinder head gasket LH.
    NOTE: Check that liquid gasket is squeezed out from the cylinder head gasket LH.
  6. Install the cylinder head LH to the cylinder block LH.
    CAUTION: Be careful not to scratch the mating surface of cylinder head LH and cylinder block LH.
    1. Clean the cylinder head bolt threads and apply sufficient engine oil to the washer and cylinder head bolts threads.
    2. Mount the cylinder head LH onto the cylinder block LH, and tighten all cylinder head bolts with a torque of 29 N.m (3.0 kgf-m, 21.4 ft-lb) in numerical order as shown in the figure.
    3. Tighten all cylinder head bolts further with a torque of 100 N.m (10.2 kgf-m, 73.8 ft-lb) in numerical order as shown in the figure.
      CAUTION: If the bolt makes stick-slip sound during tightening, repeat the procedure from step  3. In that case, the cylinder head gasket LH can be reused. But it is necessary to remove liquid gasket completely from cylinder block LH, cylinder head LH and cylinder head gasket LH and re-apply to them.
    4. Loosen all cylinder head bolts by 180° in the reverse order of tightening in step (3).
    5. Once again, loosen all cylinder head bolts by 180° in the reverse order of tightening in step (3).
    6. Tighten all cylinder head bolts with a torque of 42 N.m (4.3 kgf-m, 31.0 ft-lb) in numerical order as shown in the figure.
    7. Using an angle gauge, tighten all cylinder head bolts by 78°- 82° in numerical order as shown in the figure.
    8. Using an angle gauge, tighten the cylinder head bolts (2 places) by 73°- 77° in the order of (1) → (2) as shown in the figure.
    9. Using an angle gauge, tighten the cylinder head bolts (4 places) by 28°- 32° in the order of (3) → (4) → (5) → (6) as shown in the figure.
    Fig 1: Cylinder Head LH Bolts (Tightening Sequence)
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Set the part so that the installation surface of the intake manifold is on the upper side.
  8. When the cylinder head LH has been disassembled
    NOTE: When the cylinder head LH has been disassembled, perform the following steps also.
    1. Install the fuel injector LH.

      Except for hybrid model: 

      Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FUEL INJECTOR>INSTALLATION>FUEL INJECTOR LH .

      Hybrid model: 

      Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL INJECTOR>INSTALLATION>FUEL INJECTOR LH .

    2. Install the spark plug #2 and spark plug #4. Refer to IGNITION (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>SPARK PLUG>INSTALLATION>LH SIDE .
  9. Install the high-pressure fuel delivery pipe assembly.

    Except for hybrid model: 

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>HIGH PRESSURE FUEL DELIVERY PIPE>INSTALLATION>HIGH-PRESSURE FUEL DELIVERY PIPE ASSEMBLY .

    Hybrid model: 

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>HIGH PRESSURE FUEL DELIVERY PIPE>INSTALLATION>HIGH-PRESSURE FUEL DELIVERY PIPE ASSEMBLY .

  10. Install the cam carrier LH. Refer to MECHANICAL (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>CAM CARRIER>INSTALLATION>CAM CARRIER LH .
  11. Install the engine wiring harness.

    Except for hybrid model: 

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE WIRING HARNESS>INSTALLATION .

    Hybrid model: 

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>ENGINE WIRING HARNESS>INSTALLATION .

  12. Install the engine to the vehicle.

    Except for hybrid model: 

    Refer to MECHANICAL (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE ASSEMBLY>INSTALLATION>EXCEPT FOR HYBRID MODEL .

    Hybrid model: 

    Refer to MECHANICAL (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE ASSEMBLY>INSTALLATION>HYBRID MODEL .
