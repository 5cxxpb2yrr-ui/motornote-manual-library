Diagnostics With Phenomenon: List

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
Symptoms Reference
DTC other than U-code is displayed. Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>DTC OTHER THAN U-CODE IS DISPLAYED .
Faster/slower vehicle speed than the actual speed is displayed. Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>FASTER/SLOWER VEHICLE SPEED THAN THE ACTUAL SPEED IS DISPLAYED .
More/less fuel than the actual amount is displayed. Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>MORE/LESS FUEL THAN THE ACTUAL AMOUNT IS DISPLAYED .
Fuel level warning light illuminates despite of sufficient fuel. Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>FUEL LEVEL WARNING LIGHT ILLUMINATES DESPITE OF SUFFICIENT FUEL .
Engine coolant temperature light blinks in red and blue alternately. Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>ENGINE COOLANT TEMPERATURE LIGHT BLINKS IN RED AND BLUE ALTERNATELY .
[S], [I] or [S#] indicator is blinking. Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>[S], [I] OR [S#] INDICATOR IS BLINKING .
The VDC warning light illuminates. Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>THE VDC WARNING LIGHT ILLUMINATES .
VDC-OFF indicator illuminates. Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>VDC-OFF INDICATOR ILLUMINATES .
Brake warning light illuminates. Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>BRAKE WARNING LIGHT ILLUMINATES .
Shift position is not displayed. Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>SHIFT POSITION IS NOT DISPLAYED .
Door indicator is not displayed. Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>DOOR INDICATOR IS NOT DISPLAYED .
Clearance indicator does not illuminate. Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>CLEARANCE INDICATOR DOES NOT ILLUMINATE .
HI beam indicator does not illuminate. Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>HI BEAM INDICATOR DOES NOT ILLUMINATE .
Front fog light indicator does not illuminate. Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>FRONT FOG LIGHT INDICATOR DOES NOT ILLUMINATE .
Illumination is fixed at the maximum brightness. Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>ILLUMINATION IS FIXED AT THE MAXIMUM BRIGHTNESS .
Cruise control indicator does not illuminate. Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>CRUISE CONTROL INDICATOR DOES NOT ILLUMINATE .
AWD indicator illuminates. Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>AWD INDICATOR ILLUMINATES .
Hill hold indicator does not illuminate. Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>HILL HOLD INDICATOR DOES NOT ILLUMINATE .
