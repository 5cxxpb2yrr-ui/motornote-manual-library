    Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .

    Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>COMPRESSOR>REMOVAL .

    Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>AIR INTAKE DUCT>REMOVAL .
