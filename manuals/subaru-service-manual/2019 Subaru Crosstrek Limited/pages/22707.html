Related Lines, More Than 52 Ω And Less Than 62 Ω (Infotainment System)

Wiring diagram: 

CAN communication system. Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (IMPREZA MODEL)>CAN COMMUNICATION SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>CAN COMMUNICATION SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  1. CHECK BETWEEN RELATED LINES AND MAIN WIRING HARNESS. 
    1. Disconnect the audio or navigation unit connector.
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between audio or navigation unit connector terminals.

    Connector & terminal 

  2. No. 9 - (i87) No. 10:

    Is the resistance 400 Ω or more?

    Yes:  A related line between the navigation unit and the main wiring harness is open, or main wiring harness is open at two places or more.

    No:  Go to  3.

  3. CHECK RELATED LINES. 
    1. Disconnect the body integrated unit connector.
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between audio or navigation unit connector and body integrated unit.

    Connector & terminal 

  4. No. 9 - (B281) No. 17:
  5. No. 10 - (B281) No. 19:

    Is the resistance 10 Ω or more?

    Yes:  Repair or replace the open circuit of infotainment system related lines.

    No:  Check DTC of infotainment system. Refer to INFOTAINMENT (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .
