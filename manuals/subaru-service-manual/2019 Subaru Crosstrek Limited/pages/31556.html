INTRODUCTION

This Service Information Bulletin announces discontinuation of both the "Stitcher" app and the related News Radio feature from the STARLINK app lineup effective 12:00 midnight on Thursday, September 24, 2020. This change effects Denso TEN (a.k.a. Fujitsu TEN) Generation 2.0 and 2.1 along with Harman Generation 3.0 and 3.1 infotainment/audio systems. Users will likely receive notification on their display as shown below.

With the departure of Stitcher, there will no longer be support for the Application Programming Interface (API) software which allowed the Stitcher and News Radio applications to communicate with each other. As a result, the News Radio  feature will be replaced by "Music  " as shown in the screen shots below. The News Radio icon will also disappear.
