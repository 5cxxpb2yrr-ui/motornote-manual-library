Radiator Fan and Fan Motor Assembly: Disassembly

  1. Remove the over flow hose and the over flow pipe.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Remove the main fan motor harness and the sub fan motor harness.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the radiator main fan and radiator sub fan.
    NOTE: Remove locking agent from the thread of the main fan motor and the sub fan motor.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the main fan motor and the sub fan motor.
Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
