Diagnostic Method

If the duration of time while the following conditions are met is longer than the time indicated, judge as NG.

JUDGMENT VALUE SPECIFICATION 1

Malfunction Criteria Threshold Value
<Judgment 1: Short to ground> 
Secondary oxygen sensor offset signal (A)		 Less than 1.8V
JUDGMENT VALUE SPECIFICATION 2

Malfunction Criteria Threshold Value
<Judgment 2: Out of range> 
Secondary oxygen sensor output voltage {(B) - (A)}
Secondary oxygen sensor signal (B)		 Less than -0.22V
0.825V or more
or
Secondary oxygen sensor signal (B) 0.825V or more and Less than 1.7V

Time needed for diagnosis:  1000 ms

Malfunction indicator light illumination:  Illuminates when malfunction occurs in 2 continuous driving cycles.
