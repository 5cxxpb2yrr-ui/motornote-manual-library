Emissions Maintenance Reminder Reset Procedure

EGR warning light will come on every 60, 000 miles as a reminder to service EGR system. To reset light after servicing, remove left cover under instrument panel. Pull down connectors behind fuse panel. Unplug connector from Blue connector, and plug it into Green connector.
