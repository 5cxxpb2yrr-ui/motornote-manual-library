DTC B2B28: Passenger's Door Power Window Continuous Reversal

DTC detecting condition: 

When the number of times for detected continuous reverse operation counts 10 times or more. (Condition for addition: completion of reserve operation due to anti-pinch feature)

Trouble symptom: 

  • When the power window sub-switch is operated, passenger's side window does not work.
  • When the power window sub-switch is operated, passenger's side window rolls down only in manual down operation, or inches up. (Inch up: Operating the switch rolls up the window about 10 mm only)
    CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to POWER WINDOW (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK GLASS SLIDING SURFACE. 

    Check sliding surface of glass and running channel.

    Is it free from any foreign matter?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Remove the foreign matter, and Go to  2.

  2. PERFORM SYSTEM RESTORATION PROCEDURES. 
    1. Operate the power window sub-switch and roll down the window to the full open position.
    2. Operate the power window sub-switch and roll up the window to the full close position.
    3. Read the DTC of [Power Window (Passenger)] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to POWER WINDOW (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is DTC B2B28 displayed? (Current malfunction)

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Even if DTC is displayed, the circuit has returned to a normal condition at this time.

    Reproduce the failure, and then perform the diagnosis again.

    NOTE: In this case, temporary poor contact of connector, temporary open or short circuit of harness may be the cause.
  3. ACTIVE TEST. 

    Using the Subaru Select Monitor, check the following items in the [Active Test] of [Power Window (Passenger)]. Refer to POWER WINDOW (DIAGNOSTICS)>ACTIVE TEST .

    • Power Window Operation Test

    Does the window roll up and down?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Replace the front regulator & motor assembly (passenger's side). Refer to GLASS/WINDOWS/MIRRORS>FRONT REGULATOR AND MOTOR ASSEMBLY .

  4. CHECK DTC. 

    Read the DTC of [Power Window (Passenger)] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to POWER WINDOW (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is DTC B2B28 displayed? (Current malfunction)

    Yes:  Replace the front regulator & motor assembly (passenger's side). Refer to GLASS/WINDOWS/MIRRORS>FRONT REGULATOR AND MOTOR ASSEMBLY .

    No:  Even if DTC is displayed, the circuit has returned to a normal condition at this time.

    Reproduce the failure, and then perform the diagnosis again.

    NOTE: In this case, temporary poor contact of connector, temporary open or short circuit of harness may be the cause.
