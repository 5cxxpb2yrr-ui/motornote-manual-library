Battery ECU assembly POWER SUPPLY SYSTEM

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .

Wiring diagram: 

Hybrid system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  1. CHECK Battery ECU assembly (IGCT VOLTAGE). 
    1. Check that the Service plug is disconnected. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .
    2. Disconnect Battery ECU assembly connector (HV42).
    3. Connect the ground terminal to Battery (12V). Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    4. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    5. Using a tester, measure the voltage between Battery ECU assembly connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV42) No. 24 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    Is the voltage 10 - 14 V?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Go to  3.

  2. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Battery ECU assembly-CHASSIS GROUND) (OPEN). 
    1. Turn the ignition to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the ground terminal from Battery (12V). Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    3. Using a tester, measure the resistance between Battery ECU assembly connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV42) No. 9 - Chassis ground:

      (HV42) No. 29 - Chassis ground:

      (HV42) No. 48 - Chassis ground:

      (HV42) No. 53 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Check the list of Diagnostics with Phenomenon. Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>LIST .

    No:  Repair or replace the Wire harness between Battery ECU assembly connector and chassis ground.

  3. CHECK FUSE (IGCT SCENE FUSE). 
    1. Remove the F/B fuse No. 21 from Relay & fuse box (F/B). Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>RELAY AND FUSE .
    2. Check the M/B fuse No. 5. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>RELAY AND FUSE .

    Is the fuse OK?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Go to  6.

  4. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Battery ECU assembly-Relay & fuse box (F/B)) (OPEN). 

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between Battery ECU assembly connector and Relay & fuse box (F/B).

    Connector & terminal 

    (HV42) No. 24 - (i265) No. 3:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  5.

    No:  Repair or replace the Wire harness between Battery ECU assembly connector and Relay & fuse box (F/B).

  5. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Relay & fuse box (F/B)-Main fuse box (M/B)) (OPEN). 
    1. Remove the IGCT scene relay from the Main fuse box (M/B). Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>RELAY AND FUSE .
    2. Using the tester, measure the resistance between Relay & fuse box (F/B) and Main fuse box (M/B).

      Connector & terminal 

      (B56) No. 1 -IGCT scene relay No. 3:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Check the Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly power supply circuit. Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>HYBRID VEHICLE CONTROL ECU ASSEMBLY .

    No:  Repair or replace the Wire harness between Relay & fuse box (F/B) and Main fuse box (M/B).

  6. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Battery ECU assembly-Relay & fuse box (F/B)) (LINE-TO-LINE SHORT OR SHORT TO GROUND). 

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between Battery ECU assembly connector and chassis ground, and between Battery ECU assembly connector terminals.

    Connector & terminal 

    (HV42) No. 24 - (HV42) No. 1 - 69:

    (HV42) No. 24 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Replace F/B fuse No. 21. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>RELAY AND FUSE .

    No:  Repair or replace the Wire harness between Battery ECU assembly connector and Relay & fuse box (F/B) connector.

    Then, replace the F/B fuse No. 21. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>RELAY AND FUSE .

GENERAL DESCRIPTION 

Explanation for circuits 

At IG ON, Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly provides the current to MREL terminal to turn the IGCT relay ON. Thus, IGCT terminal of Battery ECU assembly is powered.
