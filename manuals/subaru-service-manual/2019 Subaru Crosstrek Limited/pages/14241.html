Service Plug: Inspection

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Measure the resistance between connector terminals.

    Preparation tool: 

    Circuit tester

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Terminal No. Inspection conditions Standard
    1 - 2 Always Less than 1 Ω
  2. Replace the service disconnect plug if the inspection result is not within the standard value.
