DTC P0E2D-00: Hybrid/EV Battery Energy Control Module Hybrid/EV Battery Monitor Performance: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

1. - 

Outline of diagnosis: 

Malfunction of cell voltage detection internal circuit inside Battery ECU assembly

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

  • Reduced output power
  • Engine not stopped

Wiring diagram: 

Hybrid system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

  2. CHECK CONNECTOR CONNECTION CONDITION (Battery ECU assembly CONNECTOR). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Check that the Service plug is disconnected. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .
    2. Check the connecting condition of connectors (HV37), (HV38), (HV39), (HV40) and (HV41) in Battery ECU assembly and check the relevant terminals for contact pressure.

    When there is no problem, go to  A.

    When there is a faulty connection but no contamination or damage in the terminal, go to  B.

    When there is a faulty connection in connectors (HV37), (HV38), (HV39), (HV40) or (HV41) and contamination or damage in the terminal, go to  C.

    Yes: 

    1. Go to  3.
    2. Connect the connector securely.
    3. Replace HV battery. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY .

    No:  -

  3. CHECK FREEZE FRAME DATA. 

    Using the Subaru Select Monitor, read the value of [Hybrid Battery Cell 1 Voltage] - [Hybrid Battery Cell 95 Voltage] in [Freeze frame data] of DTC P0E2D00. Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>FREEZE FRAME DATA .

    NOTE: Record the Hybrid battery cell whose Hybrid battery cell voltage is 1.6 V or less.

    When there is a Hybrid battery cell with 1.6 V or less in [Hybrid Battery Cell 1 Voltage] - [Hybrid Battery Cell 19 Voltage], go to  A.

    When there is a Hybrid battery cell with 1.6 V or less in [Hybrid Battery Cell 20 Voltage] - [Hybrid Battery Cell 38 Voltage], go to  B.

    When there is a Hybrid battery cell with 1.6 V or less in [Hybrid Battery Cell 39 Voltage] - [Hybrid Battery Cell 57 Voltage], go to  C.

    When there is a Hybrid battery cell with 1.6 V or less in [Hybrid Battery Cell 58 Voltage] - [Hybrid Battery Cell 76 Voltage], go to  D.

    When there is a Hybrid battery cell with 1.6 V or less in [Hybrid Battery Cell 77 Voltage] - [Hybrid Battery Cell 95 Voltage], go to  E.

    Other than above, go to  F.

    Yes: 

    1. Go to  4.
    2. Go to  5.
    3. Go to  6.
    4. Go to  7.
    5. Go to  8.
    6. Replace Battery ECU assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>BATTERY CONTROL MODULE .

    No:  -

  4. CHECK NO. 1 HV supply stack sub-assembly (Hybrid battery cell voltage). 
    CAUTION:
    • Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    • Disconnect the connector to inspect the Hybrid battery cell voltage. Leave the other connectors connected.
    • When checking Hybrid battery cell voltage, select a probe of proper diameter (approx. 0.5 mm) from the test lead set before measurement.
    • Do not cross the electrode part of measurement terminals of electrical tester.
    • Keep in mind that the terminals have + pole or - pole when connecting the tester before measurement.
    1. Disconnect Battery ECU assembly connector (HV37).
      CAUTION:
      • Release the lever lock completely so that the lock lever can be operated.
      • If the connector cannot not be disengaged, check that the lock lever is in the unlock position.
    2. Using a tester, measure the voltage between terminals in Battery ECU assembly connector.
      NOTE: Measure the point related to Hybrid battery cell where the voltage is 1.6 V or less from the result of freeze frame data.
