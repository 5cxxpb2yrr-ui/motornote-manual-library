DTC C2024: RL Tire Air Pressure Decrease Normal Mode

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

DTC detecting condition  :

Inflation pressure of tires dropped below the specified value.

NOTE: For the specifications, refer to [Data monitor]. Refer to TIRE PRESSURE MONITOR (DIAGNOSTICS)>DATA MONITOR .

Trouble symptom: 

Tire pressure warning light illuminates.

CAUTION:
  • When driving vehicle to perform driving test, there should be always 2 persons (driver and checker) to check.
  • Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to TIRE PRESSURE MONITOR (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK TIRES. 

    Lift up the vehicle and check for damage in the tires.

    Are there cracks or damage?

    Yes:  Replace the tire.

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK TIRES. 

    Check the tire air pressure.

    Is the tire pressure in the specifications?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Adjust the air pressure.

  3. CHECK TRANSMITTER DATA. 
    1. Drive at 40 km/h (25 MPH) or more.
    2. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, check the following items in the [Data monitor] of [Tire Pressure Monitor]. Refer to TIRE PRESSURE MONITOR (DIAGNOSTICS)>DATA MONITOR .
    • FL Tire air pressure
    • FR Tire air pressure
    • RR Tire air pressure
    • RL Tire air pressure

    Is there a transmitter with different display?

    Yes:  Replace the transmitter (tire pressure sensor) with different display. Refer to WHEEL AND TIRE SYSTEM>TIRE PRESSURE MONITORING SYSTEM .

    No:  Go to  4.

  4. PERFORM DRIVING TEST. 
    1. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, perform the clear memory of [Tire Pressure Monitor]. Refer to COMMON (DIAG)>CLEAR MEMORY .
    2. Perform a driving test. Refer to TIRE PRESSURE MONITOR (DIAGNOSTICS)>INSPECTION MODE>PROCEDURE .
    3. Read the DTC of [Tire Pressure Monitor] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to TIRE PRESSURE MONITOR (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is C2021, C2022, C2023 or C2024 displayed? (Current malfunction)

    Yes:  Replace the TPMS & keyless entry control module or TPMS control module. Refer to WHEEL AND TIRE SYSTEM>TIRE PRESSURE MONITORING SYSTEM .

    No:  Even if the DTC is displayed, the system has returned to a normal condition at this time. Reproduce the failure, and then perform the diagnosis again.
