Outline Of Diagnosis

The electric brake booster control module monitors the output value of the brake pedal stroke sensor assembly, and the electric brake booster control module judges that the brake pedal stroke sensor assembly has a malfunction and illuminates the MIL and stores a DTC if the power supply voltage ratio of the brake pedal stroke sensor 2 is outside the normal range.
