Diagnostic Procedure With Cancel Code: 13

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

Detected when clutch pedal is depressed or malfunction related to clutch switch occurs.

Trouble symptom: 

  • Cruise control cannot be set.
  • Cruise control cannot be released.

Wiring diagram: 

Cruise control system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (IMPREZA MODEL)>CRUISE CONTROL SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>CRUISE CONTROL SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to CRUISE CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. PERFORM UNIT INSPECTION OF CLUTCH SWITCH. 

    Check the clutch switch. Refer to CRUISE CONTROL SYSTEM>CLUTCH SWITCH .

    Is the check result of the clutch switch unit and installation position OK?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Replace the clutch switch. Or adjust the installation position.

  2. CHECK CLUTCH SWITCH CIRCUIT. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the clutch switch connector.
    3. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    4. Measure the voltage between the clutch switch connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (B107) No. 1 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    Is the voltage 10 V or more?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No: 

    • Check fuse No. 19 (fuse & relay box).
    • Check open or shorted circuit of harness between clutch switch and fuse & relay box.
  3. CHECK CLUTCH SWITCH CIRCUIT. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the ECM connector.
    3. Measure the resistance between clutch switch connector and ECM connector.

      Connector & terminal 

      (B107) No. 2 - (B134) No. 28:

    Is the resistance less than 10 Ω?

    Yes:  Replace the ECM.

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE CONTROL MODULE (ECM) .

    No:  Repair the harness.
