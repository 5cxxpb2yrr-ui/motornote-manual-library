Malfunction Description

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

This DTC is stored if the value of the reactor current sensor fluctuates excessively. The cause of this malfunction may be one of the following:

Part Main trouble contents
Transmission assembly
  • Short or open circuit of motor and generator coils
  • Malfunction inside motor and generator (damages caused by iron powder and foreign matter)
Resolver Open or short circuit of Motor resolver or GENERATOR RESOLVER CIRCUIT
Inverter
  • Inverter inner circuit malfunction
  • Malfunction of unit that controls inverter
  • Inverter controlling sensor malfunction (current sensor, voltage sensor (VH and VL), etc.)
HV battery system Open circuit of frame wire
