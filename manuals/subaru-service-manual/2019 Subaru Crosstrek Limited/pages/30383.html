Fuel Insulator: Installation

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Install the fuel pipe insulator.
    CAUTION:
    • If substantial amount of fuel or parts cleaner come into contact with the fuel pipe insulator, replace it with a new fuel pipe insulator.
    • If only small amount of fuel comes into contact with the fuel pipe insulator, wait for the fuel to evaporate before installing it.
  2. Install the intake manifold assembly. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>INTAKE MANIFOLD ASSEMBLY>INSTALLATION .
  3. Install the air intake boot. Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>Air Intake Boot>INSTALLATION .
  4. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
