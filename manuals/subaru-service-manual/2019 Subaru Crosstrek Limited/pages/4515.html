Sunroof Motor: Removal

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Completely close the lid assembly - sunroof.
  2. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  3. Remove the light assembly - map. (Models without EyeSight) Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>SPOT MAP LIGHT>REMOVAL .
  4. Remove the stereo camera cover assembly. (Models with EyeSight) Refer to EYESIGHT>STEREO CAMERA>REMOVAL .
  5. Remove the motor assembly - sunroof.
    1. Disconnect the harness connector.
    2. Remove the TORX® screws and remove the motor assembly - sunroof.
    CAUTION: When removing the motor assembly - sunroof, secure the cable assembly so that it does not move.

    Preparation tool: 

    TORX® T25
