DTC P1C6A-9F: Motor Shutdown Stuck Off: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Outline of diagnosis: 

At the inspection of the HV gate shutdown function (with READY ON → OFF), the shutdown inspection result (decrease of the inverter current value and the inverter voltage value (VH)) was conflicted, when Motor generator control ECU output the shutdown signal to the motor inverter.

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

Hybrid system stopped

NOTE: This DTC is output when a malfunction in Inverter with converter assembly is detected. Therefore, when this DTC is output, replace Inverter with converter assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>INVERTER AND CONVERTER ASSEMBLY .
