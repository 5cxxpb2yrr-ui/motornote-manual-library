Adjustment Of Difference Between Right And Left Steering Angles

  1. Operate the steering system from lock to lock and stop operating it at the center position from lock to lock, and then install the steering wheel in the straight-ahead position.
    NOTE: Using of the steering wheel angle sensor output values shown on Subaru Select Monitor will facilitate your work.
  2. Before adjusting toe-in, be sure to adjust the steering wheel in the straight-ahead position (steering angle sensor output: 0 deg).
