Automatic Transmission Assembly: Removal

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the clip from the air intake boot, and loosen the clamp.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the air intake boot, and place it aside so that it does not interfere with the work.
    CAUTION: Be careful not to pull out the PCV hose.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the transmission radio ground terminal.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Disconnect the transmission harness connector.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Remove the air breather hose together with the bracket.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Remove the starter. Refer to STARTING/CHARGING SYSTEMS (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>STARTER>REMOVAL .
  8. Remove the pitching stopper.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Remove the throttle position sensor harness connector (1), purge control solenoid valve harness connector (2), harness clip (3), vacuum hose (4) and PCV hose (5).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  10. Remove the engine hanger rear.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  11. Remove the pitching stopper bracket.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  12. Remove the service hole plug.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  13. Remove the six bolts combining the torque converter and drive plate while rotating the crank pulley a little at a time in the same direction as engine revolution.
    CAUTION:
    • Be careful not to drop bolts into the converter housing.
    • Be careful not to damage the mounting bolts.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  14. Make sure the torque converter moves freely by rotating with finger through the starter installation hole.
  15. Attach the ST to the converter case.

    Preparation tool: 

    ST: STOPPER SET (498277200)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  16. Remove the engine harness connector bracket.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  17. Remove the engine ground terminal.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  18. Remove the CVTF cooler (with warmer feature) from the transmission, and using a piece of wire, affix to a location of the body where it will not interfere with the removal/installation of the transmission.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  19. Disconnect the CVTF cooler inlet hose and CVTF cooler outlet hose from the pipe.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  20. Remove the transmission case cover (small).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  21. Remove the TCM. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>TRANSMISSION CONTROL MODULE (TCM)>REMOVAL .
  22. Set the ST1 and ST2.

    Preparation tool: 

    ST1: ENGINE SUPPORT BRACKET (41099AA012)

    ST2: ENGINE SUPPORT (41099AA020)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  23. Remove the three transmission connecting bolts.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  24. Loosen the nut of the front suspension strut mount by two turns. (Impreza model)
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  25. Lift up the vehicle.
  26. Remove the front tires. Refer to WHEEL AND TIRE SYSTEM>TIRE AND WHEEL>REMOVAL .
  27. Remove the under cover.
  28. Remove the crossmember support - rear.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  29. Remove the front exhaust pipe. Refer to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FRONT EXHAUST PIPE>REMOVAL .
  30. Remove the center exhaust cover.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  31. Remove the exhaust hanger bracket.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  32. Remove the CVTF drain plug to drain CVTF. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>CVTF>REPLACEMENT .
  33. Drain differential gear oil. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>DIFFERENTIAL GEAR OIL>REPLACEMENT .
  34. Remove the snap pin and washer from shifter arm and remove the select cable from shifter arm.
    CAUTION: Do not apply excessive load while holding the part (a).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  35. Remove the plate assembly from the transmission case.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  36. Remove the propeller shaft. Refer to DRIVE SHAFT SYSTEM>PROPELLER SHAFT>REMOVAL .
  37. Remove the brake hose bracket.
    • Impreza model
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • Crosstrek model
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  38. Remove the front ABS wheel speed sensor. Refer to VEHICLE DYNAMICS CONTROL (VDC)>FRONT ABS WHEEL SPEED SENSOR>REMOVAL .
  39. Remove the stabilizer link.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  40. Separate the front arm assembly from the ball joint. (Impreza model)
    NOTE:
    • Mark the engaging position of the ball joint and front arm assembly before loosening the flange nuts. Doing so will facilitate aligning the position when assembling and lessen adjusting allowance for wheel alignment.
    • To prevent damage of the front arm assembly ball joint boot, place a wooden block, etc. between front arm assembly and ball joint.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  41. Remove the bolts which secure to the front housing, and separate the front arm and housing. (Crosstrek model)
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  42. Using a tire lever or a crow bar, etc., pull out until the front drive shaft transmission side joint slides move smoothly.
    NOTE: Place cloth between the tire lever or bar and the transmission in order to avoid damaging the transmission side retainer.
  43. Hold the transmission side joint of the front drive shaft by hand and extract the housing from the transmission while pressing the housing outward, so as not to stretch the boot.
    NOTE:
    • Before pulling the RH front drive shaft from transmission, turn the steering wheel to the right hand at full lock.
    • Before pulling the LH front drive shaft from transmission, turn the steering wheel to the left hand at full lock.
  44. Remove the two transmission connecting bolts and two nuts (lower side).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  45. Set the transmission jack under the transmission.
    NOTE: Make sure that the support plates of transmission jack do not touch the oil pan.
  46. Remove the hanger bracket.
  47. Remove the transmission rear crossmember from the vehicle.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  48. While lowering the transmission jack gradually, fully retract the engine support, and then tilt the engine rearward.
  49. Remove the transmission assembly.
    NOTE: Remove it while moving the transmission jack up and down so that the engine and transmission remain directly aligned.
  50. Remove the rear cushion rubber.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
