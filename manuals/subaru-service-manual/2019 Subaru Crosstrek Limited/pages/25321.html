Cruise Control Was Cancelled Without Operating The Cruise Switch

  1. CHECK FOR PRESENCE OF CANCEL CODE. 

    Check for the presence of ECM cancel codes. Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>ECM CANCEL CODE(S) DISPLAY .

    Is a cancel code displayed?

    Yes:  Perform the diagnosis for the displayed cancel code. Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>ECM CANCEL CODE(S) DISPLAY .

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. PERFORM REAL TIME DIAGNOSIS. 

    Check the input signal of cruise control system. Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>REAL-TIME DIAGNOSIS .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Go to  3.

  3. CHECK EyeSight STEERING SWITCH. 

    Check the EyeSight steering switch. Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>CHECK EYESIGHT STEERING SWITCH .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Replace the EyeSight steering switch. Refer to EYESIGHT>SWITCHES AND HARNESS .

  4. CHECK COMBINATION METER. 

    Check the indicator readings using the active test of combination meter [All lamps lighted].

    Refer to COMBINATION METER (DIAGNOSTICS)>ACTIVE TEST .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  5.

    No:  Replace the meter case assembly. Refer to INSTRUMENTATION/DRIVER INFO>COMBINATION METER .

  5. INSPECTION OF CRUISE INDICATOR. 

    Check the CRUISE indicator. Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>CHECK ADAPTIVE CRUISE CONTROL/CONVENTIONAL CRUISE CONTROL INDICATOR LIGHT (WHITE OR GREEN) .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  The circuit has returned to a normal condition at this time.

    NOTE: In this case, temporary poor contact of connector, temporary open or short circuit of harness may be the cause.

    No:  Repair or replace the faulty portion according to the inspection result.
