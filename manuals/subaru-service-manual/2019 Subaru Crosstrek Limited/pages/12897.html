Check Steering Heater System

Symptoms Reference
The steering heater does not operate. Refer to POWER ASSISTED SYSTEM (POWER STEERING)>STEERING HEATER SYSTEM>INSPECTION>THE STEERING HEATER DOES NOT OPERATE .
The temperature of steering heater increases excessively. Refer to POWER ASSISTED SYSTEM (POWER STEERING)>STEERING WHEEL>INSPECTION>STEERING WHEEL HEATER .
The steering heater does not stop. Refer to POWER ASSISTED SYSTEM (POWER STEERING)>STEERING WHEEL>INSPECTION>STEERING HEATER SWITCH .
