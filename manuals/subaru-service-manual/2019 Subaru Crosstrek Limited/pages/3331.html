Rear Gate Garnish: Removal

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the rear gate trim. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>REAR GATE TRIM>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the garnish assembly - rear gate.
    1. Remove the nuts.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Remove the clips and remove the garnish assembly - rear gate.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. As necessary, remove each part from the garnish assembly - rear gate.
    • Switch - opener rear gate: Refer to SECURITY AND LOCKS>REAR GATE OPENER BUTTON>REMOVAL .
