Clutch Pedal: Disassembly

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Disconnect the clutch pipe.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Remove the clutch damper mounting nut, and disconnect the clutch pipe from the clamp.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the clutch switches.
  4. Remove the clip and pull out the clevis pin.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the master cylinder.
    NOTE: Secure the push rod with a tape etc. to prevent it from falling off.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Remove the assist pin, bushing A, assist rod A, clip, assist bushing A, assist spring, assist rod B and assist bushing B.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Remove the clutch pedal.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Remove the pedal bushing, spacer, bushing A, clutch pedal stopper and clutch pedal pad.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Remove the brake pedal.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  10. Remove the pedal bushing, spacer, brake pedal stopper and brake pedal pad.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
