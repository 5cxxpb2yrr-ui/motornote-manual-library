For C5

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

Diagnosis start condition: 

  • Battery voltage is 10 V or more.
  • The remote engine starter was not used to start the engine.

DTC detecting condition: 

Immobilizer antenna is faulty.

Trouble symptom: 

  • Engine will not start.
  • The security indicator illuminates.

Wiring diagram: 

Immobilizer system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (IMPREZA MODEL)>IMMOBILIZER SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>IMMOBILIZER SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

CAUTION: When the immobilizer control module is replaced, registration of the immobilizer system is required. For details, refer to the "REGISTRATION INSTRUCTIONS FOR IMMOBILIZER" provided as a separate volume.
  1. CHECK FUSE. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Check the fuse.

      Is the check result OK?

      Yes:  Go to  2.

      No:  Replace the fuse. If the fuse blows out easily, repair the short circuit to power supply in harness between the battery and immobilizer control module.

  2. CHECK DATA MONITOR. 

    Using the Subaru Select Monitor, check the following items in the [Data monitor] of [Immobilizer]. Refer to IMMOBILIZER (DIAGNOSTICS)>DATA MONITOR .

    • BATT voltage

    Is the display 10 V or more?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Check the power supply circuit of immobilizer control module.

  3. CHECK IMMOBILIZER ANTENNA UNIT. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the connector from immobilizer antenna.
    3. Using a tester, measure the resistance between the immobilizer antenna connector terminals.

      Connector & terminal 

      No. 1 - No. 5:

      Is the resistance less than 8 Ω?

      Yes:  Replace the immobilizer antenna. Refer to SECURITY AND LOCKS>IMMOBILIZER ANTENNA .

      No:  Go to  4.

  4. CHECK IMMOBILIZER ANTENNA COMMUNICATION CIRCUIT (OPEN CIRCUIT). 
    1. Disconnect the immobilizer control module connector.
    2. Measure the resistance between immobilizer control module connector and immobilizer antenna connector.

      Connector & terminal 

      (i34) No. 4 - (i60) No. 1:

      (i34) No. 15 - (i60) No. 5:

      Is the resistance less than 10 Ω?

      Yes:  Replace the immobilizer control module. Refer to SECURITY AND LOCKS>IMMOBILIZER CONTROL MODULE .

      No:  Repair the open circuit of harness between the immobilizer control module connector and immobilizer antenna connector.
