DTC U128F: Lost Communication With Hybrid Powertrain Control Module C-CAN

DTC detecting condition: 

When reception ID frame is not normally and continuously received for 10 seconds during CAN bus Wake-up.

(Including when frame is abandoned by DLC (data length code) check error)

NOTE: Perform the diagnosis for LAN system. Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .
