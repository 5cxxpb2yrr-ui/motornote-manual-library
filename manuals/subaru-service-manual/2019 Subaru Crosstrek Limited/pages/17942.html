Primary Speed Sensor: Removal

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

CAUTION: Be sure to prevent water or oil from contacting the connector terminal of primary speed sensor. If adhesion occurs, replace with a new part.
  1. Remove the transmission assembly. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL .
  2. Remove the primary speed sensor.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the harness connector from primary speed sensor.
