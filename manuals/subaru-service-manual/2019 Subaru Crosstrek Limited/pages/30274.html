Section 3 (HVAC System (Heater, Ventilator And A/C) (HEV)): Evaporator: Installation

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
CAUTION:
  • If the evaporator has been replaced, add an appropriate amount of compressor oil to the compressor. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>COMPRESSOR OIL>ADJUSTMENT .
  • Replace the O-rings with new parts, and then apply compressor oil to install completely.
  • Before handling the airbag system components, refer to "CAUTION" of "General Description" in "AIRBAG SYSTEM". Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. Install the evaporator sensor to the specified location. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>EVAPORATOR SENSOR>INSTALLATION .
  2. Install the evaporator assembly.
  3. Install the case assembly - heater unit.
  4. Install the motor - actuator mix. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>AIR MIX DOOR ACTUATOR>INSTALLATION .
  5. Attach the heater core. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>HEATER CORE>INSTALLATION .
  6. Install the internal condenser. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>CONDENSER>INSTALLATION>INTERIOR CONDENSER .
  7. Install the heater and cooling unit assembly. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>HEATER AND COOLING UNIT>INSTALLATION .
  8. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  9. Charge refrigerant. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>REFRIGERANT CHARGING PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .
  10. Fill engine coolant. (Engine side) Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>ENGINE COOLANT>REPLACEMENT>FILLING OF ENGINE COOLANT .
