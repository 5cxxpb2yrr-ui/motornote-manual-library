Roof Spoiler: Removal

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the trim panel - rear gate UPR. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>REAR GATE TRIM>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the clips, and remove the drip - rear gate.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the roof spoiler assembly.
    1. Disconnect the connector of high-mounted stop light.
    2. Remove the bolt.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Remove the clips and remove the spoiler assembly - roof CTR.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    4. Remove the clips and remove the spoiler assembly - roof LH/RH.
Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
