Deflector Assembly

  1. Open the lid assembly - sunroof and the sunshade assembly.
  2. Remove the deflector assembly - sunroof.
    1. Remove the spring from the rail assembly - sunroof.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. While lifting up the deflector assembly - sunroof, remove the deflector assembly - sunroof from the hook on the rail assembly - sunroof.
