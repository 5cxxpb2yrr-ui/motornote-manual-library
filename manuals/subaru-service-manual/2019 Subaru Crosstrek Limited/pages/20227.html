DTC P0A81-15: Hybrid/EV Battery Cooling Fan 1 Circuit Short To Auxiliary Battery Or Open: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

1. - 

Outline of diagnosis: 

If the speed calculated from the output frequency (FP0) of Battery cooling blower assembly RH is out of specification with respect to the control target speed

At a rotation command less than a certain value, the rotation speed of Battery cooling blower assembly RH is high.

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

Normal driving

Wiring diagram: 

Hybrid system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.

  2. CHECK DTC. 
    1. Turn the ignition to OFF.
    2. Check DTC when the vehicle is brought in.

    When "DTC P0A8115" is displayed, go to  A.

    When "DTC P0A9615" is displayed, go to  B.

    Yes: 

    1. Go to  3.
    2. Go to  4.

    No:  -

  3. CHECK Battery ECU assembly (Hybrid Battery Cooling Fan 1 Frequency). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Check that the Service plug is disconnected. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .
    2. Connect the ground terminal to Battery (12V). Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    3. Connect the Subaru Select Monitor.
    4. Turn to IG ON.
    5. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, perform the clear memory of [P-HEV High Voltage Battery]. Refer to COMMON (DIAG)>CLEAR MEMORY .
    6. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, select the following items in the [Active Test] of [P-HEV High Voltage Battery]. Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>ACTIVE TEST .
      • Active test item: Control the Hybrid Battery Cooling Fan
      • Signal selection: Hybrid Battery Cooling Fan 1 Frequency
      NOTE: Perform with HV battery intake air temperature at -10°C (14°F) or more.
    7. Select fan mode (1 - 6) to operate the Battery cooling blower assembly RH.
      NOTE: If the active test cannot be performed, a command for Battery cooling blower assembly RH to drive may be executed due to failsafe of Battery cooling blower assembly RH. In this case, proceed to the following operation check without performing active test.
    8. During the operation of Battery cooling blower assembly RH, use the data monitor value [Hybrid Battery Cooling Fan 1 Frequency] and an oscilloscope to check the actual value in Rear harness LH connector (R534).

      Connector & terminal 

      (R534) No. 17 - Chassis ground:

    Is the difference between the data monitor value [Hybrid Battery Cooling Fan 1 Frequency] and the actual value 10% or less?

    NOTE: Compare individual fan modes (1 - 6). If the active test cannot be performed, comparison must be made with the current mode only.

    Yes:  Replace Battery cooling blower assembly RH. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY COOLING SYSTEM .

    No:  Replace Battery ECU assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>BATTERY CONTROL MODULE .
