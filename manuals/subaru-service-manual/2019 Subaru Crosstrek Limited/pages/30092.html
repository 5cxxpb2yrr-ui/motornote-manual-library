Master Cylinder: Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
CAUTION:
  • Be careful not to spill the brake fluid. Brake fluid spilled on the vehicle body will harm the paint surface; wash it off with water and wipe clean quickly if spilled.
  • Before handling the airbag system components, refer to "CAUTION" of "General Description" in "AIRBAG SYSTEM". Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
  2. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery and wait for at least 60 seconds before starting work. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  3. Drain the brake fluid. Refer to CLUTCH SYSTEM>CLUTCH FLUID .
  4. Remove the instrument panel lower cover. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>INSTRUMENT PANEL LOWER COVER>REMOVAL .
  5. Remove the steering column. Refer to POWER ASSISTED SYSTEM (POWER STEERING)>STEERING COLUMN>REMOVAL .
  6. Remove the clutch pedal assembly. Refer to CLUTCH SYSTEM>CLUTCH PEDAL>REMOVAL .
  7. Remove the clip and clevis pin, and then remove the push rod of the master cylinder.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Disconnect the clutch pipe.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Remove the master cylinder.
