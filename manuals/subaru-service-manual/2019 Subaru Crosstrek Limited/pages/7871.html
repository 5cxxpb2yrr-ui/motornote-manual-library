Cam Carrier LH

  1. Remove the oil spacer from cam carrier LH.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Loosen the bolts (front camshaft cap LH, intake center camshaft cap LH, intake rear camshaft cap LH, exhaust center camshaft cap LH and exhaust rear camshaft cap LH) equally, a little at a time in numerical sequence as shown in the figure, and remove each camshaft cap.
    NOTE: Arrange camshaft caps in order so that they can be installed in their original positions.
    Fig 1: Camshaft Cap LH Bolts Loosening Sequence
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the intake camshaft LH and the exhaust camshaft LH from cam carrier LH.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the filter from cam carrier LH.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the liquid gasket from cam carrier LH, front camshaft cap LH, intake rear camshaft cap LH and exhaust rear camshaft cap LH.
