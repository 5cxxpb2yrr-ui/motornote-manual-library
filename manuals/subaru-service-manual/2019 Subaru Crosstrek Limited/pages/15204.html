Evaporator Sensor: Installation

CAUTION:
  • Since the auto A/C model uses the variable displacement compressor, be careful of the following items.
    • Do not start the engine before charging refrigerant.
    • If the engine is started with no refrigerant charge, replace the compressor assembly.
  • Replace the O-rings with new parts, and then apply compressor oil to install completely.
  • Before handling the airbag system components, refer to "CAUTION" of "General Description" in "AIRBAG SYSTEM". Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. Install the evaporator sensor at the position shown in the figure below.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Install the evaporator assembly - cooling. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>EVAPORATOR>INSTALLATION .
  3. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  4. Charge refrigerant. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>REFRIGERANT CHARGING PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .
  5. Fill engine coolant. Refer to COOLING (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE COOLANT>REPLACEMENT>FILLING OF ENGINE COOLANT .
