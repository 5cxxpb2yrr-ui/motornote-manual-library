Brake Accumulator ASSY: Inspection

  1. Check the resistance between connector terminals.

    Preparation tool: 

    Circuit tester

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Terminal No. Inspection conditions Standard
    2 - 4 Always 10 Ω or less
    1 - 3 Always 10 Ω or less
    2 - 1 Always Less than 1 Ω
    4 - 3 Always Less than 1 Ω
  2. If the inspection result is not the standard, replace the brake accumulator assembly.
