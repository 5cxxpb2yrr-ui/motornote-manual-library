Headlight System: Note

For operation procedures of each component of the headlight system, refer to the respective service information.

  • Headlight assembly: Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>HEADLIGHT ASSEMBLY .
  • Headlight bulb: Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>HEADLIGHT ASSEMBLY>DISASSEMBLY .
  • Combination switch (light): Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>COMBINATION SWITCH (LIGHT) .
  • Combination switch (wiper): Refer to WIPER AND WASHER SYSTEMS>COMBINATION SWITCH (WIPER) .
  • Light control sensor: Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>LIGHT CONTROL SENSOR .
