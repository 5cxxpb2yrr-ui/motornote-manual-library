Explanation For Circuits

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Motor generator control ECU built in Inverter with converter assembly detects the preboosting high voltage (VL) with the voltage sensor which is built in the boosting converter and is used for boosting control. Motor generator control ECU detects malfunction by monitoring the boosting converter voltage sensor signal line.
