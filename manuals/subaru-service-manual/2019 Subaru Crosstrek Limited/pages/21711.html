DTC P0E31-16: DC/DC Converter Voltage Sensor "A" (Vl) Circuit Voltage Below Threshold: Notes

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. - 

    Detecting condition: 

    Boosting converter voltage (VL) signal stuck to Low

    (1 trip detection logic)

    Trouble symptom: 

    Reduced output power

    Wiring diagram: 

    Hybrid system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to P-HEV DRIVE MOTOR (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK DTC. 

    Read the following DTCs using Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) . Refer to P-HEV DRIVE MOTOR (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    • P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control
    • P-HEV Drive Motor
    NOTE:
    Malfunction contents Related DTC
    P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control P0AD915 HYBRID/EV BATTERY POSITIVE CONTACTOR CIRCUIT SHORT TO AUXILIARY BATTERY OR OPEN
    P0ADD15 HYBRID/EV BATTERY NEGATIVE CONTACTOR CIRCUIT SHORT TO AUXILIARY BATTERY OR OPEN
    P1C8449 HIGH VOLTAGE POWER RESOURCE CIRCUIT SHORT DURING READY ON
    P312387 LOST COMMUNICATION WITH DRIVE MOTOR CONTROL MODULE "A" FROM HYBRID/EV CONTROL MODULE MISSING MESSAGE
    P-HEV Drive Motor P312487 LOST COMMUNICATION BETWEEN DRIVE MOTOR "A" AND HV ECU MISSING MESSAGE

    When "Only DTC P0E3116 is displayed", go to  A.

    When "Only DTC P0E3116 or P0E311F is displayed" or "DTC other than above is displayed at the same time", go to  B.

    When DTCs related to [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control], mentioned above, are simultaneously displayed, go to  C.

    When DTCs related to [P-HEV Drive Motor], mentioned above, are simultaneously displayed, go to  D.

    Yes: 

    1. Go to  2.
    2. Replace Inverter with converter assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>INVERTER AND CONVERTER ASSEMBLY .
    3. Perform the diagnosis for the simultaneously displayed DTCs.
      NOTE: If more than one DTC is simultaneously displayed, perform the diagnosis in the order described in the above.
    4. Perform the diagnosis for the simultaneously displayed DTCs.
      NOTE: If more than one DTC is simultaneously displayed, perform the diagnosis in the order described in the above.

    No: - 

  2. CHECK FREEZE FRAME DATA. 

    Using the Subaru Select Monitor, display the [VH Voltage] in the freeze frame data of [P-HEV Drive Motor]. Refer to P-HEV DRIVE MOTOR (DIAGNOSTICS)>FREEZE FRAME DATA .

    Is [VH Voltage] 55 V or more?

    Yes:  Replace Inverter with converter assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>INVERTER AND CONVERTER ASSEMBLY .

    No:  Go to  3.

  3. CHECK DTC. 
    1. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, record the following DTCs and freeze frame data. Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) . Refer to P-HEV DRIVE MOTOR (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .
      • P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control
      • P-HEV Drive Motor
    2. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, perform the following clear memory. Refer to COMMON (DIAG)>CLEAR MEMORY .
      • P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control
      • P-HEV Drive Motor
    3. Set the parking brake with the vehicle parked and turn to READY ON.
      NOTE:
      • If READY ON cannot be set, turn to READY ON again after turning to IG OFF, then check the DTC.
      • DTC P300449 uses 2 trips detection logic. Therefore, the DTC detection condition is necessary to be met 2 times.
    4. Ensure that the areas in front of and behind the vehicle are safe.
    5. Place the shift lever in D, and depress the acceleration pedal and the brake pedal simultaneously.
      NOTE: When depressing the accelerator pedal and the brake pedal together, HV battery current flows. This indicates that there is no malfunction in the high voltage lines.
    6. Read the DTC of [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

      When "DTC P1C8449 is displayed or DTC is not displayed", go to  A.

      When "READY ON cannot be set and DTC P300449 is displayed", go to  B.

      Yes: 

      1. Go to  4.
      2. Perform the diagnosis of DTC P300449. Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE WITH DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>DTC P300449 HIGH VOLTAGE POWER RESOURCE CIRCUIT SHORT DURING PRE-CHARGE .

      No:  -

  4. CHECK CONNECTOR CONNECTION CONDITION (Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly CONNECTOR). 

    Check the connecting condition of connector (i369) in Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly and check the relevant terminals for contact pressure, deformation and intrusion of water or foreign matter. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL MODULE .

    Is there looseness at the connector connection, or any poor contact, deformation or intrusion of water or foreign matter at the relevant terminals?

    Yes:  Connect the connector securely. Or repair the poor contact of connector.

    No:  Go to  5.

  5. CHECK CONNECTOR CONNECTION CONDITION (Rear harness LH CONNECTOR). 

    Check the connecting condition of connector (R467) in Rear harness LH and check the relevant terminals for contact pressure, deformation and intrusion of water or foreign matter.

    Is there looseness at the connector connection, or any poor contact, deformation or intrusion of water or foreign matter at the relevant terminals?

    Yes:  Connect the connector securely. Or repair the poor contact of connector. And then, Go to  6.

    No:  Go to  6.

  6. CHECK CONNECTOR CONNECTION CONDITION (HV battery junction block CONNECTOR). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Check that the Service plug is disconnected. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .
    2. Check the connecting condition of connector (HV49) in HV battery junction block and check the relevant terminals for contact pressure, deformation and intrusion of water or foreign matter.

      Is there looseness at the connector connection, or any poor contact, deformation or intrusion of water or foreign matter at the relevant terminals?

      Yes:  Connect the connector securely. Or repair the poor contact of connector. And then, Go to  7.

      No:  Go to  7.

  7. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly - HV battery junction block) (OPEN). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Disconnect HV battery junction block connector (HV49).
    2. Disconnect Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector (i369).
    3. Using a tester, measure the resistance between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector and HV battery junction block connector.

      Connector & terminal 

      (i369) No 5 - (HV49) No. 1:

      Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

      Yes:  Go to  8.

      No:  Repair or replace the Wire harness between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector and HV battery junction block connector. And then, Go to  8.

  8. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly - HV battery junction block) (LINE-TO-LINE SHORT). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector terminals.

    Connector & terminal 

    (i369) No. 5 - (B606) No. 1 - 35:

    Is the resistance 10 kΩ or more?

    Yes:  Go to  9.

    No:  Repair or replace the Wire harness between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector and HV battery junction block connector. And then, Go to  9.

  9. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (HV battery junction block - CHASSIS GROUND) (OPEN). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between HV battery junction block connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (HV49) No. 2 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  10.

    No:  Repair or replace the Wire harness between HV battery junction block connector and chassis ground. And then, Go to  10.

  10. CHECK HV battery junction block UNIT (SMRB). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.

    Using the tester, measure the resistance between HV battery junction block terminals.

    Connector & terminal 

    (HV49) No. 1 - (HV49) No. 2:

    Is the resistance 25.0 - 59.0 Ω? (When -40 - 80°C (-40 - 176°F))

    Yes:  Go to  11.

    No:  Go to  12.

  11. CHECK HV battery junction block UNIT (SMRB). 
    CAUTION:
    • Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    • If SMR is welded, it may be removed from the welded position with a small vibration. Therefore, check while it is installed in the vehicle.
    1. Disconnect connectors (HV43) and (HV44) from HV battery junction block.
      CAUTION: Be sure to insulate the disconnected high voltage connector by wrapping it with insulating tape. To do this, wrap the insulating tape from Wire harness along to the connector.
    2. Using the tester, measure the resistance between HV battery junction block terminals.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV43) No. 1 - (HV44) No. 1:

      Is the resistance 10 kΩ or more?

      NOTE: If it is 10 kΩ or more, a past malfunction occurs. If it is less than 10 kΩ, a current malfunction occurs.

      Yes:  Go to  12.

      No:  Go to  12.

  12. REPLACEMENT OF HV battery junction block. 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Replace HV battery junction block. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>RELAY AND FUSE .
    2. Restore the vehicle to its original state.
    3. Attach the Service plug. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .
    4. Turn to IG ON.
    5. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, perform the clear memory of [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control]. Refer to COMMON (DIAG)>CLEAR MEMORY .
    6. Turn to IG OFF, and wait for two minutes or more.
    7. Turn to READY ON.
    8. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, display the following items in the [Data monitor] of [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control], and record them. Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DATA MONITOR .
      • VL-Voltage before Boosting
      • Hybrid Battery Voltage

      Is the difference of the data between [Hybrid Battery Voltage] and [VL-Voltage before Boosting] less than 100 V constantly?

      Yes:  There was something wrong with HV battery junction block.

      Finish the diagnosis.

      No:  Replace Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly and HV battery junction block. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL MODULE .
