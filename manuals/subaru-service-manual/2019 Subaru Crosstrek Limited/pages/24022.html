Related Lines, More Than 52 Ω And Less Than 62 Ω (MAIN-CAN_VDC)

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Wiring diagram: 

CAN communication system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>CAN COMMUNICATION SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

  1. CHECK BETWEEN RELATED LINES AND MAIN WIRING HARNESS. 
    1. Disconnect the VDC control module & H/U connector.
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between terminals in VDC control module & H/U connector.

      Connector & terminal 

      (B310) No. 42 - (B310) No. 43:

    Is the resistance 400 Ω or more?

    Yes:  A related line between VDC control module and main wiring harness is open, or main wiring harness is open at two places or more.

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK RELATED LINES. 
    1. Disconnect the steering sensor connector.
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between VDC control module & H/U connector and steering sensor connector.

      Connector & terminal 

      (B310) No. 42 - (i74) No. 3:

      (B310) No. 43 - (i74) No. 2:

    Is the resistance 10 Ω or more?

    Yes:  Repair or replace the open circuit of VDC related lines.

    No:  The communication harness is normal. Check DTC of VDC. Refer to BRAKE CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .
