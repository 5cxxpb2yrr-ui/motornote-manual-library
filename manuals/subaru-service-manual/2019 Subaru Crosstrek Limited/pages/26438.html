Malfunction Description

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

This DTC indicates that the output signal of ATF temperature sensor became abnormal.

The following cases are assumed to be malfunction causes:

Internal malfunction of Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly

  • Malfunction of Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly

Malfunction of ATF temperature sensor

  • Internal malfunction of ATF temperature sensor
  • Open or short circuit of ATF temperature sensor

Malfunction of Wire harness system between ATF temperature sensor - Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly

  • Improperly mated connector
  • Foreign matter caught, water intrusion
  • Open or short circuit
