Diagnostic Method

If the duration of time while the following conditions are met is longer than the time indicated, judge as NG.

JUDGMENT VALUE SPECIFICATION

Malfunction Criteria Threshold Value
Diagnosis 1 
Absolute change of measured Transmission fluid temperature sensor input voltage
Transmission fluid temperature		 Less than 0.049 V
Less than 20 degC
Diagnosis 2 
Transmission fluid temperature at engine start - Engine coolant temperature at engine start		 Exceeds 18 degC

Time needed for diagnosis: 

  • Diagnosis 1:  600 s
  • Diagnosis 2:  Immediately

Malfunction indicator light illumination:  Illuminates when malfunction occurs in 2 continuous driving cycles.
