Purge Control Solenoid Valve: Installation

  1. Connect the vacuum hose to the purge control solenoid valve.
  2. Install the purge control solenoid valve bracket.

    Tightening torque: 

    6.4 N.m (0.7 kgf-m, 4.7 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Install the purge control solenoid valve.

    Tightening torque: 

    6.4 N.m (0.7 kgf-m, 4.7 ft-lb)

  4. Connect the vacuum hose (D) to the intake manifold assembly, and install the vacuum hose (D) to the clip (E).
  5. Connect the purge hose (C).
  6. Connect the clip (B) which secures the engine wiring harness to the purge control solenoid valve bracket, and connect the connector (A).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Install the air intake boot. Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>Air Intake Boot>INSTALLATION .
  8. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
