Front Strut: Removal

  1. Lift up the vehicle, and then remove the front wheels.
  2. Remove the left and right stabilizer links (upper part).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the brake hose bracket.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the ABS wheel speed sensor.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the strut assembly.
    1. Place an alignment mark (a) on the adjusting bolt and the strut.
    2. Remove the adjusting bolts and flange bolts for the strut assembly.
      CAUTION: While holding the adjusting bolt side, tighten the nut side.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Remove the three nuts securing strut mount to body.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
