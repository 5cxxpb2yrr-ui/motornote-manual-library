Malfunction Description

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

This indicates that the boosting converter lower arm temperature sensor value became abnormal. The following cases are assumed to be causes of this symptom.

  • Malfunction of Inside of inverter
    • Circuit malfunction of Inside of inverter
  • Hybrid cooling system failure
    • Abnormal coolant circulation (freezing, leakage, etc.)
  • Inverter low-voltage circuit failure
    • Improperly connected connector
