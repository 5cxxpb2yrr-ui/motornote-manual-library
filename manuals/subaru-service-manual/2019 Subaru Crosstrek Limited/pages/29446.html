Inverter Center Water Pipe

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Drain engine coolant. (Inverter assembly side)

    Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>ENGINE COOLANT>REPLACEMENT>DRAINING OF ENGINE COOLANT>INVERTER ASSEMBLY SIDE .

  2. Remove the fuel tank protector and inverter assembly (with converter) cover.

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL TANK PROTECTOR>REMOVAL .

  3. Remove the floor under protector.

    Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>FLOOR UNDER PROTECTOR>REMOVAL .

  4. Remove the rear exhaust pipe and muffler as a single unit.

    Rear exhaust pipe: 

    Refer to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>REAR EXHAUST PIPE>REMOVAL .

    Muffler: 

    Refer to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>MUFFLER>REMOVAL .

  5. Remove the tunnel stay.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Remove the propeller shaft.

    Refer to DRIVE SHAFT SYSTEM>PROPELLER SHAFT>REMOVAL .

  7. Remove the crossmember support - rear RH.

    Refer to FRONT SUSPENSION>CROSSMEMBER SUPPORT>REMOVAL .

  8. Remove the inverter center water pipe outlet hose (B) and inverter center water pipe inlet hose (C) from the clamp (A).
  9. Disconnect the inverter center water pipe outlet hose (B) and inverter center water pipe inlet hose (C) from the inverter center water pipe.
    NOTE: Make markings, etc. to prevent mis-connection at the time of connecting the inverter center water pipe inlet hose and inverter center water pipe outlet hose.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  10. Disconnect the inverter rear water pipe outlet hose (A) and inverter rear water pipe inlet hose (B) from the inverter center water pipe.
    NOTE: Make markings, etc. to prevent mis-connection at the time of connecting the inverter rear water pipe inlet hose and inverter rear water pipe outlet hose.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  11. Remove the bolt and clip (A) securing the inverter center water pipe and the resolver sensor harness.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  12. Remove the clip (A) securing the resolver sensor harness to the inverter center water pipe.
  13. Remove the claw (B) and claw (C) securing the resolver sensor harness to the inverter center water pipe, and remove the inverter center water pipe.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
