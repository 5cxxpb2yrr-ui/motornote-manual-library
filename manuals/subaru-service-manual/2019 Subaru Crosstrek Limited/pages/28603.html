Diagnostic Method

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

When the differences of target timing advance amount and actual timing advance amount is calculated during AVCS control, and the difference per predetermined time is the specified value or larger. Judge as NG when the following conditions are established within the predetermined time.
