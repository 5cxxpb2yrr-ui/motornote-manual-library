Check Key Switch Circuit

Trouble symptom: 

Engine does not start.

Trouble causes: 

  • Key warning switch circuit malfunction
  • Key warning switch malfunction

Wiring diagram: 

Immobilizer system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (IMPREZA MODEL)>IMMOBILIZER SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>IMMOBILIZER SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: When the body integrated unit (except for C5 model) or the immobilizer control module (C5 model) is replaced, registration of the immobilizer system is required. For details, refer to the "REGISTRATION INSTRUCTIONS FOR IMMOBILIZER" provided as a separate volume.
  1. CHECK POWER SUPPLY CIRCUIT. 
    1. Disconnect the connector from key warning switch.
    2. Measure the voltage between key warning switch connector terminal and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (i63) No. 1 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

      Is the voltage 10 V or more?

      Yes:  Go to  2.

      No:  Check the harness for an open or short between the key warning switch and fuse.

  2. CHECK KEY WARNING SWITCH. 
    1. Insert the ignition key in the ignition switch. (OFF or ACC)
    2. Measure the resistance between key warning switch connector terminals.

      Terminals 

      No. 1 - No. 2:

      Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

      Yes:  Go to  3.

      No:  Replace the key warning switch. Refer to SECURITY AND LOCKS>KEY LOCK CYLINDERS .

  3. CHECK KEY WARNING SWITCH. 
    1. Remove the ignition key from ignition switch.
    2. Measure the resistance between key warning switch connector terminals.

      Terminals 

      No. 1 - No. 2:

      Is the resistance 1 MΩ or more?

      Yes:  Go to  4.

      No:  Replace the key warning switch. Refer to SECURITY AND LOCKS>KEY LOCK CYLINDERS .

  4. CHECK DATA MONITOR. 

    Using the Subaru Select Monitor, check the following items under the [Data monitor] of [Body Control] or [Immobilizer]. Refer to IMMOBILIZER (DIAGNOSTICS)>DATA MONITOR .

    • Body Control
      • key-lock warning SW
    • Immobilizer
      • key-lock warning SW Input

    Is the display 10 V or more?

    Yes:  Replace the body integrated unit (except for C5 model) Refer to SECURITY AND LOCKS>BODY INTEGRATED UNIT . or the immobilizer control module (C5 model) Refer to SECURITY AND LOCKS>IMMOBILIZER CONTROL MODULE .

    No:  Go to  5.

  5. CHECK HARNESS BETWEEN KEY WARNING SWITCH AND BODY INTEGRATED UNIT (EXCEPT FOR C5 MODEL) OR IMMOBILIZER control module (C5 MODEL). 
    1. Disconnect the connector from the body integrated unit (except for C5 model) or the immobilizer control module (C5 model).
    2. Measure the resistance between key warning switch connector terminal and body integrated unit (except for C5 model) or immobilizer control module (C5 model) connector terminal.

      Connector & terminal 

      Body integrated unit (except for C5 model) 

      (i63) No. 2 - (i84) No. 1:

      Immobilizer control module (C5 model) 

      (i63) No. 2 - (i34) No. 3:

      Is the resistance less than 10 Ω?

    Yes:  Replace the body integrated unit (except for C5 model) Refer to SECURITY AND LOCKS>BODY INTEGRATED UNIT . or the immobilizer control module (C5 model) Refer to SECURITY AND LOCKS>IMMOBILIZER CONTROL MODULE .

    No:  Repair harness between key warning switch and body integrated unit (except for C5 model) or immobilizer control module (C5 model).
