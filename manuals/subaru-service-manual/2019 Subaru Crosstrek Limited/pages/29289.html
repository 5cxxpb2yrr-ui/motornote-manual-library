Overview Of Error

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

The Charge control ECU installed inside the Electric vehicle charger assembly monitors internal operation, and outputs a DTC when an overvoltage error is detected in the DCDC converter of the Electric vehicle charger assembly. If this DTC was output, perform a charging check suing an AC power supply that was confirmed to operate normally. If the trouble occurred again, replace the Electric vehicle charger assembly.

The following are possible causes of the error.

  • Malfunction on the vehicle side
    • Malfunction of the Electric vehicle charger assembly
  • Malfunction not on the vehicle
    • Malfunction of the AC power supply
