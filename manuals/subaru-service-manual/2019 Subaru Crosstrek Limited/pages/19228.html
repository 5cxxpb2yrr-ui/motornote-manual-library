DTC B1475: A/C Inverter Load

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

DTC detecting condition: 

When the A/C inverter load system is faulty.

Trouble symptom: 

Compressor does not operate.

CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. ACTIVE TEST. 

    Using the Subaru Select Monitor, check the following items in the [Active Test] of [Engine]. Refer to ENGINE (DIAGNOSTICS) (H4DO (HEV))>ACTIVE TEST .

    • Radiator Fan Relay

    Does the radiator fan operate with [Radiator Fan Relay] set to ON?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>RADIATOR FAN SYSTEM .

  2. CHECK AMOUNT OF REFRIGERANT. 

    Check the refrigerant pressure and filling amount. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>REFRIGERANT PRESSURE WITH MANIFOLD GAUGE SET .

    Is the filling amount of refrigerant OK?

    Yes:  Replace Electric compressor ASSEMBLY. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>COMPRESSOR .

    No:  Fill or collect refrigerant so that specified filling amount of refrigerant is obtained.
