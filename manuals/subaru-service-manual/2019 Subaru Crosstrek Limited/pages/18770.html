Air Breather Hose: Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the clip from the air intake boot, and loosen the clamp.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the air intake boot, and place it aside so that it does not interfere with the work.
    CAUTION: Be careful not to pull out the PCV hose.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the clips and remove the air breather hose.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the air breather cap.
