DTC P04C2: Fuel Fill Door Unlock Control Circuit High: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

1. - 

DTC detecting condition: 

Immediately at fault recognition

Trouble symptom: 

Fuel lid does not open.

Wiring diagram: 

Engine Electrical System Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>ENGINE ELECTRICAL SYSTEM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: Use the check board when measuring the ECM terminal voltage and resistance. Refer to ENGINE (DIAGNOSTICS) (H4DO (HEV))>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>PREPARATION TOOL>HOW TO USE CHECK BOARD .
NOTE: After the faulty parts are repaired or replaced, perform the final check in Basic Diagnostic Procedure. Refer to ENGINE (DIAGNOSTICS) (H4DO (HEV))>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .
  1. CHECK HARNESS BETWEEN ECM AND M/B (FUEL FLAP RELAY) (SHORT TO POWER SUPPLY)  .
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Remove the fuel flap relay.
    3. Disconnect the ECM connector.
    4. Measure the voltage between ECM connector and engine ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (B134) No. 34 - Engine ground:

    Is the voltage 1 V or more?

    Yes:  Repair the short circuit to power supply in the harness between ECM connector and M/B (fuel flap relay) terminal.

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK FUEL FLAP RELAY  .

    Check the fuel flap relay. Refer to SECURITY AND LOCKS>RELAY AND FUSE>INSPECTION>CHECK RELAY .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Repair the poor contact of ECM connector.

    No:  Replace the fuel flap relay. Refer to SECURITY AND LOCKS>RELAY AND FUSE>LOCATION .
