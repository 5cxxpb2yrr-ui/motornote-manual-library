Crosstrek Model

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Install a new gasket to the fuel tank.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Connect the fuel sub delivery tube to the fuel pump assembly. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>INSTALLATION>QUICK CONNECTOR CONNECTION (TYPE D) .
    NOTE: Connect so that the rib (a) of fuel pump assembly aligns between the retainers (b) of quick connector.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Set the fuel pump to the position shown in the figure.
    CAUTION:
    • Be careful not to spill any fuel over the gasket. If any fuel is spilled onto the gasket, wipe it off completely. Otherwise the fuel pump may rotate together with the ring nut.
    • Set the arm and float of the fuel level sensor while paying attention to prevent them from contacting the fuel tank. If the arm of the fuel level sensor is bent, the fuel gauge may not read correctly.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Set a new ring nut plate.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Set a new ring nut, and align the fuel tank installation start position mark with the ring nut position mark.
    NOTE:
    • Make sure that the ring nut, ring nut plate and gasket are set as shown in the figure.
    • Set the ring nut by firmly pressing against the fuel tank.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Using the ST, tighten the ring nut by one turn, and then tighten until the ring nut position mark is located within the installation end position range mark on the fuel tank.
    CAUTION:
    • Be careful not to damage the tube with the ST.
    • Before setting the ST, move the tube above the ST.
    • Make sure that the ring nut position mark is located within the installation position end range mark on the fuel tank as shown in the figure.
    • Make sure that the position mark on the fuel pump is located within the installation position range mark on the fuel tank as shown in the figure.

    Preparation tool: 

    ST: ST FUEL PUMP (42099FL000)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Using a caliper with depth gauge, measure the dimension (3 positions) of the ring nut and the top of fuel tank to check the tightening condition of the ring nut.
    CAUTION:
    • If the measured value exceeds the standard, retighten the ring nut to correct erroneous tightening of the ring nut.
    • If the ring nut and ring nut plate are deformed, cracked or damaged, replace them with new parts.

    Dimension of ring nut and top of fuel tank: 

    Standard 

    23.8 ± 2.5 mm (0.937 ± 0.098 in)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Connect the fuel delivery tube. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>INSTALLATION>QUICK CONNECTOR CONNECTION (TYPE D) .
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Connect the connector to the fuel pump.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  10. Install the service hole cover of fuel pump.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  11. Install the rear seat cushion. Refer to SEATS>REAR SEAT>INSTALLATION .
  12. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
