Rear Seat: Removal

CAUTION:
  • Before handling the airbag system components, refer to "CAUTION" of "General Description" in "AIRBAG SYSTEM". Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  • Airbag system satellite safing sensor is located under the front center of the rear seat cushion. Be careful not to apply strong impact to the sensor when working with the rear seat cushion removed.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery and wait for at least 60 seconds before starting work. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the rear seat cushion assembly.
    1. While pushing the corners of the rear seat cushion from above, pull the knob of the hook - seat cushion rear towards yourself.
    2. While pulling the knob towards yourself, disconnect the hooks by lifting up the rear seat cushion.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Remove the hook (a) by pushing it back while lifting the front side of the rear seat cushion, and remove the rear seat cushion.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the backrest assembly LH.
    1. Tilt the backrest assembly forward.
    2. Remove the clips located at the bottom of the cover COMPL - rear backrest LH and RH of the rear seat back.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Turn over the cover COMPL - rear backrest LH to remove the bolts.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    4. While raising the backrest assembly LH, remove the backrest assembly LH from the hinge assembly.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    NOTE: The backrest assembly cannot be removed unless pin positions on the hinge assembly side and backrest assembly side are aligned.
  4. Remove the backrest assembly RH. (Except for hybrid model)
    1. Remove the bolts and nuts.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. While raising the backrest assembly RH, remove the backrest assembly RH from the hinge assembly.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    NOTE: The backrest assembly cannot be removed unless pin positions on the hinge assembly side and backrest assembly side are aligned.
  5. Remove the backrest assembly RH. (Hybrid model)
    1. Turn over the cover COMPL - rear backrest RH to remove the bolts.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Remove the backrest assembly RH from the hinge assembly.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    NOTE: The backrest assembly cannot be removed unless pin positions on the hinge assembly side and backrest assembly side are aligned.
  6. Remove the bolts and nuts, and remove the hinge assembly. (Hybrid model)
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Remove the cover side sill rear - INN, and remove the trim panel - rear pillar LWR or the trim panel - rear apron.
    • 4 door model: Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>REAR QUARTER TRIM>REMOVAL>4 DOOR MODEL .
    • 5 door model
    • Except for hybrid model: Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>REAR QUARTER TRIM>REMOVAL>5 DOOR MODEL (EXCEPT FOR HYBRID) .
    • Hybrid model: Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>REAR QUARTER TRIM>REMOVAL>5 DOOR MODEL (HYBRID) .
  8. Remove the bolts, and then remove the striker - backrest rear.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Remove the bolts, then remove the plate - anchor LWR.
    • Except for hybrid model
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • Hybrid model
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  10. Remove the nuts and bolts, and remove the stay - rear floor. (Except for hybrid model)
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  11. Remove the stay - rear floor. (Hybrid model)
    1. Remove the high voltage battery assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY>REMOVAL .
      WARNING: The hybrid system includes a high voltage circuit. Mishandling could cause accidents such as electric shock or leak. Always check "WARNING (HYBRID SYSTEM)" and perform the proper operation. Refer to PRECAUTION/WARNING>WARNING (HYBRID SYSTEM) .
    2. Remove the bolts, and remove the stay - rear floor.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
