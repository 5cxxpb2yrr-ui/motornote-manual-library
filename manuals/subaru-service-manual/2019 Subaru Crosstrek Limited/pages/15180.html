Expansion Valve: Removal

  1. Using the refrigerant recovery system, discharge refrigerant. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>REFRIGERANT RECOVERY PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .
  2. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  3. Move the air intake boot. Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>AIR INTAKE BOOT>REMOVAL .
    NOTE: With the PCV hose connected, remove the air intake boot from the air cleaner case (rear) and throttle body.
  4. Disconnect the hose - pressure suction and the pipe - evaporator cooling.
    1. Remove the bolts and nuts, and detach the hose - pressure suction.
    2. Remove the clips, and detach the pipe - evaporator cooling.
      CAUTION: Seal the engaging parts of the disconnected hose, pipe and expansion valve - cooling with a plug or vinyl tape to prevent foreign matter from entering.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the expansion valve - cooling.
    1. Remove the bolt, and remove the expansion valve - cooling.
      CAUTION: Seal the engaging parts of the pipe and expansion valve - cooling with a plug or vinyl tape to prevent foreign matter from entering.

      Preparation tool: 

      Hexagon wrench: 4 mm (0.16 in)
