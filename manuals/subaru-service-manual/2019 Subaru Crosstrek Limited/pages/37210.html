DTC B281B: Steering Angle Sensor Fail

DTC detecting condition: 

Defective steering angle sensor

Trouble symptom: 

All functions of EyeSight system do not operate.

NOTE: When this DTC is detected, check VDC system. Refer to BRAKE CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE .
