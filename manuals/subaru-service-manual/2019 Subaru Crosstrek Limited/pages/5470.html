Rear Brake Hose

  1. Perform the accumulator down drive. (Hybrid model) Refer to BRAKE>BRAKE SYSTEM>OPERATION>ACCUMULATOR DOWN DRIVE .
  2. Lift up the vehicle, and then remove the rear wheels.
  3. Remove the rear brake hose.
    1. Disconnect the brake hose (a) and the brake pipe (b).

      Preparation tool: 

      Flare nut wrench

    2. Remove the clamp, brake hose bracket bolt and union bolt, and remove the rear brake hose.
