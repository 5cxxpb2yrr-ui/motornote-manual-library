DTC B11F7: Side Impact Deployment

Diagnosis start condition: 

When the ignition voltage is 10 V - 16 V.

DTC detecting condition: 

This DTC is displayed when the side airbag module and curtain airbag module are deployed.

Once this DTC is displayed, the memory cannot be cleared. Replace the following parts.

  • Airbag control module Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>AIRBAG CONTROL MODULE .
  • Side airbag module (operating side) Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>SIDE AIRBAG MODULE .
  • Side airbag sensor (operating side) Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>SIDE AIRBAG SENSOR .
  • Curtain airbag module (operating side) Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>CURTAIN AIRBAG MODULE .
  • Curtain airbag sensor (operating side) Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>CURTAIN AIRBAG SENSOR .
  • Satellite safing sensor Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>SATELLITE SAFING SENSOR .
  • Front door impact sensor (operating side) Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>FRONT DOOR IMPACT SENSOR .
  • Front outer seat belt with pretensioner on the collision side (when activated) Refer to SEAT BELT SYSTEM>FRONT SEAT BELT .
