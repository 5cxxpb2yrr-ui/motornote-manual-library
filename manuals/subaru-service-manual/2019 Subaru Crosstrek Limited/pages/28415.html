Diagnostic Method

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

If the duration of time while the following conditions are met is longer than the time indicated, judge as NG.

JUDGMENT VALUE SPECIFICATION

Malfunction Criteria Threshold Value
Tank pressure sensor output value after pressing the refueling request switch 3.52 kPa (1inHg, 26.4 mmHg, 0.5 psi) or more
Or,
-2.65 kPa (-0.8 inHg, -19.9 mmHg, -0.4 psi) or less

Time needed for diagnosis:  Less than value of Map

FUEL TANK PRESSURE SPECIFICATION - MAP

Fuel tank pressure (kPa) when monitoring starts 5 10 20
Elapsed time (sec) 15 30 50

Engine warning light illumination:  Illuminates when refueling is performed twice under the faulty condition.
