DTC P3107-15: Lost Communication With Airbag System Control Module Circuit Short To Auxiliary Battery Or Open: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

1. - 

Outline of diagnosis: 

Open circuit or +B power supply sneak path of communication line

Pulse was lost, and High continued for a predetermined period of time

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

Normal driving

Wiring diagram: 

Hybrid system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK DTC  .

    Read the DTC of [Airbag] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to AIRBAG (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Are any DTCs displayed?

    Yes:  Perform the diagnosis for the displayed DTCs. Refer to AIRBAG (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK AIRBAG CONTROL MODULE (WAVEFORM MEASUREMENT)  .
    CAUTION: To measure the voltage and resistance of airbag system component, be sure to use the specified test Wire harness. Refer to AIRBAG (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>PREPARATION TOOL .
    1. Turn the ignition to OFF.
    2. Connect an oscilloscope to each terminal of AIRBAG CONTROL MODULE.
    3. Turn to READY ON.
    4. Using an oscilloscope, measure the waveform between the connector terminals of each terminal in AIRBAG CONTROL MODULE. (With the connector connected)

      Connector & terminal 

      (AB6) No. 14 - (AB6) No. 25:

      Gauge set: 5 V/DIV, 500 ms/DIV

      Measurement condition: READY ON

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.

    Can the waveform above be measured?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Go to  4.

  3. CHECK DTC  .
    1. Turn to IG ON.
    2. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, perform the clear memory of [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control]. Refer to COMMON (DIAG)>CLEAR MEMORY .
    3. Read the DTC of [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is DTC P310715 displayed?

    Yes:  Replace Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL MODULE .

    No:  Even if DTC is displayed, the circuit has returned to a normal condition at this time.

    Reproduce the failure, and then perform the diagnosis again.

    NOTE: In this case, temporary poor contact of connector, temporary open or short circuit of Wire harness may be the cause.
  4. CHECK CONNECTION CONDITION OF CONNECTOR (Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly CONNECTOR)  .
    1. Turn the ignition to OFF.
    2. Check the connecting condition of connector (i369) in Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly and check the relevant terminals for contact pressure, deformation and intrusion of water or foreign matter. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL MODULE .

    Is there looseness at the connector mating, or any poor contact, deformation or intrusion of water or foreign matter at the relevant terminals?

    Yes:  Connect the connector securely. Or repair the poor contact of connector.

    No:  Go to  5.

  5. CHECK CONNECTION CONDITION OF CONNECTOR (AIRBAG CONTROL MODULE CONNECTOR)  .

    Check the connecting condition of connector (AB6) in AIRBAG CONTROL MODULE and check the relevant terminals for contact pressure, deformation and intrusion of water or foreign matter. Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>AIRBAG CONTROL MODULE .

    Is there looseness at the connector mating, or any poor contact, deformation or intrusion of water or foreign matter at the relevant terminals?

    Yes:  Connect the connector securely. Or repair the poor contact of connector.

    No:  Go to  6.

  6. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly - AIRBAG CONTROL MODULE) (SHORT TO POWER SUPPLY)  .
    1. Disconnect Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector (i369).
    2. Disconnect AIRBAG CONTROL MODULE connector (AB6).
    3. Connect the Battery (12V) ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    4. Turn to IG ON.
    5. Using a tester, measure the voltage between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (i369) No. 7 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    Is the voltage less than 1 V?

    Yes:  Go to  7.

    No:  Repair or replace the Wire harness between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector and AIRBAG CONTROL MODULE connector.

  7. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly - AIRBAG CONTROL MODULE) (OPEN)  .
    CAUTION: To measure the voltage and resistance of airbag system component, be sure to use the specified test Wire harness.
    1. Turn the ignition to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the ground terminal from Battery (12V). Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    3. Using a tester, measure the resistance between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector and AIRBAG CONTROL MODULE connector.

      Connector & terminal 

      (AB6) No. 14 - (i369) No. 7:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  8.

    No:  Repair or replace the Wire harness between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector and AIRBAG CONTROL MODULE connector.

  8. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly - AIRBAG CONTROL MODULE) (SHORT TO GROUND OR LINE-TO-LINE SHORT)  .

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between terminals in Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector, and between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector and chassis ground.

    CAUTION: To measure the voltage and resistance of airbag system component, be sure to use the specified test Wire harness.

    Connector & terminal 

    (i369) No. 7 - (i369) No. 1 - 35:

    (i369) No. 7 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance 10 kΩ or more?

    Yes:  Go to  9.

    No:  Repair or replace the Wire harness between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector and AIRBAG CONTROL MODULE connector.

  9. CHECK Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly  .
    CAUTION: To measure the voltage and resistance of airbag system component, be sure to use the specified test Wire harness. Refer to AIRBAG (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>PREPARATION TOOL .
    1. Connect the connector (i369) of Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly.
    2. Connect the Battery (12V) ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    3. Turn to IG ON.
    4. Using a tester, measure the voltage between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector and chassis ground. (With the connector connected)

      Connector & terminal 

      (AB6) No. 14 - (i369) No. 7:

    Is the voltage 11 - 14 V?

    Yes:  Replace AIRBAG CONTROL MODULE. Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>AIRBAG CONTROL MODULE .

    No:  Replace Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL MODULE .
