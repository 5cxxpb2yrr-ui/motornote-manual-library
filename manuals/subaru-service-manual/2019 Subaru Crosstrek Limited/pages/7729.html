Inspection When Oil Pressure Warning Light Is Illuminated

  1. CHECK DTC  .

    Read the DTC using the Subaru Select Monitor.

    NOTE: For detailed operation procedures, refer to "Help" of application.

    Is DTC detected?

    Yes: 

    1. Repair the corresponding system.
    2. Go to  2.

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK ENGINE OIL LEVEL  .

    Check the engine oil level. Refer to LUBRICATION (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE OIL>INSPECTION .

    Is engine oil level normal?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No: 

    1. Adjust the engine oil level to a proper amount.
    2. Go to  3.
  3. CHECK OIL PRESSURE WARNING LIGHT  .
    1. Navigate to the service mode (for exhaust gas measurement). (Hybrid model) Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>Work Support>OPERATION>SERVICE MODE .
      NOTE: If it is necessary to run the engine for 10 minutes or more, fasten the driver's seat belt.
    2. Start the engine, and check if the oil pressure warning light goes off.

    Does the oil pressure warning light go off?

    Yes:  System is normal.

    Restore the vehicle to its original state.

    No:  Go to  4.

  4. CHECK OIL PRESSURE SWITCH  .
    1. Turn the ignition to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    3. Disconnect the terminal from oil pressure switch, and wrap the terminal with vinyl tape for insulation.
    4. Connect the battery ground terminal.
    5. Start the engine.
    6. Check if the oil pressure warning light goes off.

    Does the oil pressure warning light go off?

    Yes:  Go to  6.

    No:  Go to  5.

  5. CHECK INSULATION OF HARNESS BETWEEN COMBINATION METER AND OIL PRESSURE SWITCH  .
    1. Turn the ignition to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    3. Disconnect the connector from the combination meter.
    4. Check insulation of harness and chassis ground between combination meter and oil pressure switch.

    Are they insulated?

    Yes: 

    1. Replace the combination meter. Refer to INSTRUMENTATION/DRIVER INFO>COMBINATION METER>REMOVAL .
    2. Restore the vehicle to its original state.

    No: 

    1. Repair the harness between combination meter and oil pressure switch.
    2. Restore the vehicle to its original state.
  6. CHECK OIL PRESSURE  .

    Check the oil pressure by using the oil pressure gauge.

    Is the oil pressure is normal?

    Yes: 

    1. Replace the oil pressure switch. Refer to LUBRICATION (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>OIL PRESSURE SWITCH>REMOVAL .
    2. Restore the vehicle to its original state.

    No:  Repair the engine assembly.
