Monitor Description

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

A malfunction is detected and the MIL is illuminated when following condition is met:

Monitoring battery charging power exceeds charge power limit while Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly limits battery charger commanded power.
