Section 13 (Plug-In Battery Charger (Diagnostics)): General Description: Note

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
Term Definition
Monitor Description Description of what the Electric vehicle charger assembly monitors and how to detects malfunctions (monitoring purpose and its details).
Related DTCs A group of diagnostic trouble codes that are output by the Electric vehicle charger assembly based on the same malfunction detection logic.
Typical Enabling Conditions Preconditions that allow the Electric vehicle charger assembly to detect malfunctions.
With all preconditions satisfied, the Electric vehicle charger assembly stores DTCs when the monitored value(s) exceeds malfunction threshold(s).
Sequence of operation Order of monitor priority, applied if multiple sensors and components are involved in a single malfunction detection process.
Each sensor and component are monitored in turn and subsequent items are not monitored until the previous detection operation completes.
Required sensors/components Sensors and components used by the Electric vehicle charger assembly to detect each malfunction.
Frequency of operation Number of times the Electric vehicle charger assembly checks for each malfunction during each driving cycle. "Once per driving cycle" means that the Electric vehicle charger assembly only checks for malfunctions once during a single driving cycle. "Continuous" means that the Electric vehicle charger assembly checks for malfunctions whenever enabling conditions are met.
Duration Minimum time for which the Electric vehicle charger assembly must detect continuous deviation in monitored value(s) in order to store a DTC. Timing begins when typical enabling conditions are met.
Malfunction Thresholds Value beyond which the Electric vehicle charger assembly determines malfunctions exist and stores DTCs.
MIL operation Timing of MIL illumination after a malfunction is detected.
"Immediate" means that the Electric vehicle charger assembly illuminates the MIL as soon as a malfunction is detected.
"2 driving cycles" means that the Electric vehicle charger assembly illuminates the MIL if the same malfunction is detected again during the next driving cycle.
Intellectual property This information is not necessary for troubleshooting.
