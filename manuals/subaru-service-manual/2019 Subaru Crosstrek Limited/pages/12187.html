DTC B1431: In-Vehicle (Post Evaporator) Temperature Sensor Circuit Short-Circuit

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

DTC detecting condition: 

In-vehicle sensor circuit is shorted.

Trouble symptom: 

In-vehicle air temperature is falsely recognized as 25°C (77°F), and the compartment temperature is adjusted.

Wiring diagram: 

Air conditioning system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (IMPREZA MODEL)>AIR CONDITIONING SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>AIR CONDITIONING SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK CONNECTOR. 
    1. Check connecting condition of the A/C control panel and in-vehicle sensor connector.
    2. Read the DTC of [Air Conditioner] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is DTC B1431 displayed? (Current malfunction)

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Even if DTC is displayed, the circuit has returned to a normal condition at this time.

    Reproduce the failure, and then perform the diagnosis again.

    NOTE: In this case, temporary poor contact of connector, temporary open or short circuit of harness may be the cause.
  2. CHECK IN-VEHICLE SENSOR. 
    1. Disconnect the in-vehicle sensor. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>IN-VEHICLE SENSOR (AUTO A/C MODEL) .
    2. Read the DTC of [Air Conditioner] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is DTC B1430 displayed? (Current malfunction)

    Yes:  Replace the in-vehicle sensor. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>IN-VEHICLE SENSOR (AUTO A/C MODEL) .

    No:  Go to  3.

  3. CHECK HARNESS. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    2. Using a tester, measure the voltage between terminals in in-vehicle sensor connector.

      Connector & terminal 

      (i173) No. 1 (+) - (i173) No. 2 (-):

    Is the voltage 4.5 - 5.0 V?

    Yes:  Even if DTC is displayed, the circuit has returned to a normal condition at this time.

    Reproduce the failure, and then perform the diagnosis again.

    NOTE: In this case, temporary poor contact of connector, temporary open or short circuit of harness may be the cause.

    No:  Go to  4.

  4. CHECK HARNESS (SHORTED BETWEEN LINES). 
    1. Disconnect the connector from the A/C control panel.
    2. Using a tester, check the continuity between terminals in in-vehicle sensor connector.

      Connector & terminal 

      (i173) No. 1 - (i173) No. 2:

    Is there continuity?

    Yes:  Repair or replace the short circuit of the harness.

    No:  Replace the A/C control panel. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>CONTROL PANEL .
