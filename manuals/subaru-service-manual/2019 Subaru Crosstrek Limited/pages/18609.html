DTC C1732: Lateral G Sensor

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

DTC detecting condition: 

  • A fault is detected if the sensor signal value is not correct.
  • A fault is detected if the steering angle sensor signal/yaw rate sensor signal change while the lateral G sensor signal indicates a constant value.
  • A fault is detected if the communication status of lateral G sensor is abnormal.

Trouble symptom: 

  • VDC does not operate.
  • Hill hold does not function.
  • EyeSight does not operate.
  1. CHECK INSTALLATION OF VDCCM & H/U. 

    Is VDCCM & H/U installed properly without being tilted?

    Is the bracket deformation-free?

    Are the VDCCM & H/U installation bolts installed without missing or getting loose?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Repair the defective part.

    • Install VDCCM & H/U properly.
    • Replace the bracket if faulty.
    • Tighten the VDCCM & H/U installation bolt. Refer to VEHICLE DYNAMICS CONTROL (VDC)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>COMPONENT>VDC CONTROL MODULE AND HYDRAULIC CONTROL UNIT (VDCCM & H/U) .

    Go to  2.

  2. CHECK OUTPUT OF STEERING ANGLE SENSOR USING SUBARU SELECT MONITOR. 
    1. Park the vehicle straight on a level surface.
    2. Using Subaru Select Monitor, display [Steering Angle Sensor] of [Data Monitor]. Refer to BRAKE CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DATA MONITOR .

    Is the value of [Steering Angle Sensor] -10 - 10°?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Check the installation of steering angle sensor.

  3. CHECK OUTPUT OF LATERAL G SENSOR AND LONGITUDINAL G SENSOR WITH SUBARU SELECT MONITOR. 
    1. Park the vehicle straight on a level surface.
    2. Using Subaru Select Monitor, display [Lateral G Sensor] and [Longitudinal G Sensor] of [Data Monitor]. Refer to BRAKE CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DATA MONITOR .

    Are the values of [Lateral G Sensor] and [Longitudinal G Sensor] -2 - 2 m/s2 ?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Recheck from step 1, and if the problem is not solved, Go to  7.

  4. SET 0 POINT OF LATERAL G SENSOR AND LONGITUDINAL G SENSOR WITH SUBARU SELECT MONITOR. 
    1. Select the [Work Support] using Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to BRAKE CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>WORK SUPPORT .
    2. Perform the VDC sensor midpoint setting mode. Refer to VEHICLE DYNAMICS CONTROL (VDC)>VDC CONTROL MODULE AND HYDRAULIC CONTROL UNIT (VDCCM & H/U)>ADJUSTMENT>VDC SENSOR MIDPOINT SETTING MODE .

    Is 0 point setting successful?

    Yes:  Go to  5.

    No:  Recheck from step 1, and when the 0 point setting is not possible, replace the VDCCM & H/U. Refer to VEHICLE DYNAMICS CONTROL (VDC)>VDC CONTROL MODULE AND HYDRAULIC CONTROL UNIT (VDCCM & H/U) .

  5. PERFORM DRIVING TEST. 

    Execute the inspection mode. Refer to BRAKE CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>INSPECTION MODE .

    Does the ABS warning light or VDC warning light illuminate during driving?

    Does ABS or VDC operate abnormally?

    Yes:  Recheck from step 1, and when the warning lights illuminate or improperly operate, replace the VDCCM & H/U. Refer to VEHICLE DYNAMICS CONTROL (VDC)>VDC CONTROL MODULE AND HYDRAULIC CONTROL UNIT (VDCCM & H/U) .

    No:  Go to  6.

  6. CHECK OUTPUT OF LATERAL G SENSOR AND LONGITUDINAL G SENSOR WITH SUBARU SELECT MONITOR. 
    1. Park the vehicle on a level surface.
    2. Using Subaru Select Monitor, display [Lateral G Sensor] and [Longitudinal G Sensor] of [Data Monitor]. Refer to BRAKE CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DATA MONITOR .

    Are the values of [Lateral G Sensor] and [Longitudinal G Sensor] -1.5 - 1.5 m/s2 ?

    Yes:  Currently, it is normal. There may have been a temporary poor contact in the harness and connector or a temporary noise interference.

    No:  Recheck from step 1, and if the problem is not solved, replace the VDCCM & H/U. Refer to VEHICLE DYNAMICS CONTROL (VDC)>VDC CONTROL MODULE AND HYDRAULIC CONTROL UNIT (VDCCM & H/U) .

  7. CHECK OUTPUT OF LATERAL G SENSOR AND LONGITUDINAL G SENSOR WITH SUBARU SELECT MONITOR. 
    1. Remove the VDCCM & H/U installation bolt and bracket.
    2. Keep VDCCM & H/U in a horizontal position.
    3. Using Subaru Select Monitor, display [Lateral G Sensor] and [Longitudinal G Sensor] of [Data Monitor]. Refer to BRAKE CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DATA MONITOR .

    When the VDCCM & H/U is in a horizontal position, are the values of [Lateral G Sensor] and

    [Longitudinal G Sensor] -1.5 - 1.5 m/s2 ?

    Yes:  Check the bracket and brake pipe, and install VDCCM & H/U in a horizontal position to the vehicle.

    No:  Replace the VDCCM & H/U. Refer to VEHICLE DYNAMICS CONTROL (VDC)>VDC CONTROL MODULE AND HYDRAULIC CONTROL UNIT (VDCCM & H/U) .
