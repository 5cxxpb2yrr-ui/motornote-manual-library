Explanation For Circuits

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

In the inverter there is a three-phase bridge circuit consisting of 6 power transistors per motor and generator. The inverter converts high voltage DC, supplied from HV battery, into three-phase AC for motors and generators, and converts three-phase AC, supplied from motors and generators, into DC for HV battery. Power transistor (IGBT) drive is controlled by Motor generator control ECU. The inverter sends information, such as current and voltage, necessary to control to Motor generator control ECU.

Motor generator control ECU detects the post boosting high voltage (VH) with the inverter voltage sensor which is built in the inverter and is used for boosting control. The inverter voltage sensor outputs voltage that varies between 0 - 5 V depending on high voltage (VH) variations. Motor generator control ECU detects malfunction by monitoring the inverter voltage sensor.
