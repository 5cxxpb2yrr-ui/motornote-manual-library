Disable Conditions

On Subaru vehicles equipped with the auto stop/start feature, there may or may not be an auto stop/start off button. For vehicles without the button, any or some of the following conditions may prevent the engine from automatically stopping while parked:

  • Transmission is in P position
  • Engine compartment hood is open
  • Driver's seat belt is unlatched
  • Driver's door is open
  • Windshield defroster is on
  • A/C is set to Manual and is running at Max.
