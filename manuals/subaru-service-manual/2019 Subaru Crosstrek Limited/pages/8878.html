DTC P0455: EVAP System (CPC) Leak Detected (Large Leak): Notes

1. - 

DTC detecting condition: 

Detected when two consecutive driving cycles with fault occur.

Trouble symptom: 

  • Fuel odor
  • There is a hole of more than 1.0 mm (0.04 in) dia. in evaporation system or fuel tank.
  • Fuel filler cap loose or lost
NOTE: After the faulty parts are repaired or replaced, perform the final check in Basic Diagnostic Procedure. Refer to ENGINE (DIAGNOSTICS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .
  1. CHECK FUEL FILLER CAP  .
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Check the installation condition of fuel filler cap.
      NOTE: The DTC is stored in memory if fuel filler cap is or was loose or if the cap chain has caught while tightening.

      Is the fuel filler cap tightened securely?

      Yes: 

      Go to  2.

      No: 

      Tighten fuel filler cap securely.

  2. CHECK FUEL FILLER CAP  .

    Check the fuel filler cap.

    Is the fuel filler cap genuine?

    Yes: 

    Go to  3.

    No: 

    Replace with a genuine fuel filler cap.

  3. CHECK FUEL FILLER CAP  .

    Check the gasket of fuel filler cap.

    Does the gasket of fuel filler cap has any damage?

    Yes: 

    Replace the fuel filler cap. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FUEL FILLER PIPE

    No: 

    Go to  4.

  4. CHECK PURGE CONTROL SOLENOID VALVE  .

    Check air-tightness of the purge control solenoid valve. Refer to EMISSION CONTROL (AUX. EMISSION CONTROL DEVICES) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>PURGE CONTROL SOLENOID VALVE>INSPECTION .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes: 

    Go to  5.

    No: 

    Replace the purge control solenoid valve. Refer to EMISSION CONTROL (AUX. EMISSION CONTROL DEVICES) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>PURGE CONTROL SOLENOID VALVE .

  5. CHECK EVAPORATIVE EMISSION CONTROL SYSTEM LINE  .

    Check the evaporative emission control system line.

    Are there holes on the evaporation line?

    Yes: 

    Repair or replace the evaporation line. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES .

    No: 

    Go to  6.

  6. CHECK CANISTER  .

    Check the canister.

    Are there holes on the canister?

    Yes: 

    Replace the canister. Refer to EMISSION CONTROL (AUX. EMISSION CONTROL DEVICES) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>CANISTER .

    No: 

    Go to  7.

  7. CHECK LEAK CHECK VALVE ASSEMBLY  .

    Check the leak check valve assembly.

    Are there damage or holes on the leak check valve assembly?

    Yes: 

    Replace the leak check valve assembly. Refer to EMISSION CONTROL (AUX. EMISSION CONTROL DEVICES) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>LEAK CHECK VALVE ASSEMBLY .

    No: 

    Go to  8.

  8. CHECK FUEL TANK  .
    WARNING: Place "NO OPEN FLAMES" signs near the working area.

    Remove the fuel tank. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FUEL TANK .

    Are there damage or holes on the fuel tank?

    Yes: 

    Repair or replace the fuel tank. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FUEL TANK .

    No: 

    Go to  9.

  9. CHECK ANY OTHER MECHANICAL TROUBLE IN EVAPORATIVE EMISSION CONTROL SYSTEM  .

    Check the evaporative emission control system.

    Are there holes, cracks, clogging, or disconnection, misconnection of hoses or pipes in evaporative emission control system?

    Yes: 

    Repair or replace the hoses or pipes.

    No: 

    Repair the poor contact of ECM connector.
