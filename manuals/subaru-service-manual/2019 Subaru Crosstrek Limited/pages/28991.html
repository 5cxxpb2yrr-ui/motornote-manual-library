Warm Air Not Emitted

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Trouble symptom: 

Warm air not emitted.

Trouble causes: 

  • Airflow capacity failure
  • Coolant level failure
  • CAN communication failure
  • A/C control panel failure
  • Air mix actuator failure
  • Intake door actuator failure
  • Evaporator sensor failure
  • In-vehicle sensor failure
  • Ambient sensor failure
  • Sunload sensor failure
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK MAX HOT. 
    1. Leave the vehicle under the condition at ambient temperature of -10°C (14°F) or more.
    2. Turn to READY ON.
    3. Set the A/C control panel dials as follows.

      Temperature control dial: MAX HOT

      FRESH/RECIRC switch: FRESH

      Mode switch or mode dial: HEAT

      A/C switch: OFF

    Does it fail to blow the hot air?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Go to  4.

  2. CHECK AIRFLOW CAPACITY. 

    Check the airflow capacity. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>AIR DOES NOT COME OUT, OR AIRFLOW CAPACITY IS INSUFFICIENT .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Perform repair according to inspection procedure.

  3. CHECK AMOUNT OF REFRIGERANT PRESSURE. 

    Check the refrigerant pressure. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>REFRIGERANT PRESSURE WITH MANIFOLD GAUGE SET .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Perform repair according to refrigerant pressure inspection.

  4. CHECK A/C CONTROL PANEL. 
    1. Set the temperature control dial to MAX COOL.
    2. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, check the following items in the [Data monitor] of [Air Conditioner]. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DATA MONITOR .
      • Heater Control Panel Setting Value (Driver's)

    Does [Heater Control Panel Setting Value (Driver's)] indicate "60"*1?

    (*1: For the 72°F center specification. For the 25°C center specification, the value is 18)

    Yes:  Go to  5.

    No:  Replace the A/C control panel. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>CONTROL PANEL .

  5. CHECK A/C CONTROL PANEL. 
    1. Set the temperature control dial to MAX HOT.
    2. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, check the following items in the [Data monitor] of [Air Conditioner]. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DATA MONITOR .
      • Heater Control Panel Setting Value (Driver's)

    Does [Heater Control Panel Setting Value (Driver's)] indicate "85"*2?

    (*2: For the 72°F center specification. For the 25°C center specification, the value is 32)

    Yes:  Go to  6.

    No:  Replace the A/C control panel. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>CONTROL PANEL .

  6. CHECK DTC. 
    1. Turn to READY ON.
    2. Turn the temperature control dial at MAX COOL and leave for 16 seconds or more.
    3. Read the following DTCs using Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) . Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .
      • Air Conditioner
      • P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control

    Are any DTCs displayed? (Current malfunction)

    Yes:  Perform the diagnosis for the displayed DTCs. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) . Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    No:  Go to  7.

  7. CHECK DTC. 
    1. Turn the temperature control dial at MAX HOT and leave for 16 seconds or more.
    2. Read the following DTCs using Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) . Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .
      • Air Conditioner
      • P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control

    Are any DTCs displayed? (Current malfunction)

    Yes:  Perform the diagnosis for the displayed DTCs. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) . Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    No:  Go to  8.

  8. CHECK INTAKE DOOR ACTUATOR. 

    Visually check the intake door actuator operation.

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  9.

    No:  Check the intake door actuator. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>UNABLE TO SWITCH SUCTION VENTS .

  9. CHECK EVAPORATOR SENSOR. 

    Using the Subaru Select Monitor, check the following items in the [Data monitor] of [Air Conditioner]. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DATA MONITOR .

    • Evaporator Temperature

    Does the display indicate -0.5°C (31°F) or more?

    Yes:  Go to  11.

    No:  Go to  10.

  10. CHECK EVAPORATOR SENSOR UNIT. 

    Perform the inspection of evaporator sensor unit. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>EVAPORATOR SENSOR .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  11.

    No:  Replace the evaporator sensor. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>EVAPORATOR SENSOR .

  11. CHECK IN-VEHICLE SENSOR UNIT. 

    Check in-vehicle sensor. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>IN-VEHICLE SENSOR (AUTO A/C MODEL) .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  12.

    No:  Replace the in-vehicle sensor. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>IN-VEHICLE SENSOR (AUTO A/C MODEL) .

  12. CHECK AMBIENT SENSOR UNIT. 

    Check the ambient sensor. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>AMBIENT SENSOR .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  13.

    No:  Replace the ambient sensor. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>AMBIENT SENSOR .

  13. CHECK SUNLOAD SENSOR UNIT. 

    Check the sunload sensor unit. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>SUNLOAD SENSOR (AUTO A/C MODEL) .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  14.

    No:  Replace the sunload sensor. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>SUNLOAD SENSOR (AUTO A/C MODEL) .

  14. CHECK HEAT PUMP control module. 
    1. Disconnect the heat pump control module connector.
    2. Using a tester, measure the voltage between the heat pump control module connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (i365) No. 23 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    Is the voltage 11 - 14 V or more?

    Yes:  Go to  15.

    No:  Replace the heat pump control module. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>CONTROL UNIT .

  15. PERFORM UNIT INSPECTION OF HEAT PUMP SYSTEM PARTS. 

    Perform unit inspection of the following parts.

    • Low pressure solenoid valve Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>SOLENOID VALVE>INSPECTION .
    • High pressure solenoid valve Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>SOLENOID VALVE>INSPECTION .
    • Heating expansion valve Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>EXPANSION VALVE>INSPECTION .
    • Cooling expansion valve Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>EXPANSION VALVE>INSPECTION .
    • Interior condenser outlet temperature sensor Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>PIPE TEMPERATURE SENSOR>INSPECTION .
    • Exterior condenser outlet temperature sensor Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>PIPE TEMPERATURE SENSOR>INSPECTION .
    • Discharge temperature sensor Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>PIPE TEMPERATURE SENSOR>INSPECTION .
    • Evaporator outlet temperature sensor Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>PIPE TEMPERATURE SENSOR>INSPECTION .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  The circuit has returned to a normal condition at this time. Reproduce the failure, and then perform the diagnosis again.

    NOTE: In this case, temporary poor contact of connector, temporary open or short circuit of harness may be the cause.

    No:  Repair it according to unit inspection of parts.
