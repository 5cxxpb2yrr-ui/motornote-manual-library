DTC P1C7C-49: Hybrid/EV Battery Voltage System Isolation (A/C Area) Internal Electronic Failure: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Outline of diagnosis: 

When insulation resistance at the motor portion or the air conditioner inverter portion in Electric compressor ASSEMBLY is decreased

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

Normal driving

NOTE:
  • For the diagnostic procedure, refer to DTC P0AA649. Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE WITH DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>DTC P0AA649 HYBRID/EV BATTERY VOLTAGE SYSTEM ISOLATION INTERNAL ELECTRONIC FAILURE .
  • After only DTC P0AA649 is recorded, if the following operation is performed continuously within the same trip, one of the related DTCs (P1C7C49, P1C7D49, P1C7E49, P1C7F49 or P1CF749) is recorded.
    1. Apply the parking brake.
    2. While firmly depressing the brake pedal, wait for one minute or more with READY ON, D position, the vehicle stopped, and the air conditioner ON (temperature Lo/COOL MAX, air flow maximum).
    3. Turn to IG OFF, and wait for two minutes or more.
    4. Connect Electric vehicle charger cable assembly, and charge it for three minutes or more.
