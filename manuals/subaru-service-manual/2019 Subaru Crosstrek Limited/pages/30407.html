Engine Wiring Harness: Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the intake manifold assembly. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>INTAKE MANIFOLD ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the fuel insulator. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL INSULATOR>REMOVAL .
  4. Disconnect each connector to remove the engine wiring harness. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>COMPONENT>ENGINE WIRING HARNESS .
