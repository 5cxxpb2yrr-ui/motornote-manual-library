Seat Belt Warning System: Inspection: Notes

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

CAUTION:
  • Before handling the airbag system components, refer to "CAUTION" of "General Description" in "AIRBAG SYSTEM". Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  • When inspecting the airbag main harness, disconnect the connectors of the driver's airbag module, passenger's airbag module and knee airbag module for safety reasons.
  • When inspecting the airbag rear harness, disconnect the side airbag module connector, curtain airbag module connector and seat belt pretensioner connector for safety reasons.
