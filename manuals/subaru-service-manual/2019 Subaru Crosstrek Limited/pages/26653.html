DTC P1CE2-13: PCU Interlock Circuit Open: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

1. - 

Outline of diagnosis: 

Signal line of interlock was open-circuited during vehicle driving (at vehicle speed of 5 km/h (3.1 MPH) or more)

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

Normal driving

CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .

  2. CHECK DTC  .
    1. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, perform the clear memory of [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control]. Refer to COMMON (DIAG)>CLEAR MEMORY .

    NOTE: Check feasibility of reproduction.

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Go to  3.

  3. CHECK Inverter cover (INSTALLATION CONDITION)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Check that the Service plug is disconnected. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .
    2. Check that Inverter cover of Inverter with converter assembly is installed properly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>INVERTER AND CONVERTER ASSEMBLY .

    Is Inverter cover installed securely?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Securely install Inverter cover.

  4. CHECK Inverter cover  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.

    Check the connecting condition of interlock of Inverter cover.

    Are there any trace of entering or adhering of dirt or foreign objects that can cause poor mating?

    Yes:  Replace Inverter cover.

    No:  Replace Inverter with converter assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>INVERTER AND CONVERTER ASSEMBLY .
