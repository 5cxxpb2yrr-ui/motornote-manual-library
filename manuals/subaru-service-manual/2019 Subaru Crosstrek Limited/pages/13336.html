Drive Plate: Removal

  1. Remove the transmission assembly from the vehicle. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL .
  2. Set the ST.

    Preparation tool: 

    ST: CRANKSHAFT STOPPER (498497300)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the drive plate and reinforcement drive plate.
