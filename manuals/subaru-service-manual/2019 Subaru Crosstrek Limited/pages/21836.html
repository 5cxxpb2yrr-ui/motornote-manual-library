DTC P1CAC-49: Generator Position Sensor Internal Electronic Failure: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Detecting condition: 

Generator resolver angle malfunction

When the difference between control resolver angle and estimated resolver angle is equal to or larger than the allowed value.

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

  • Reduced output power
  • Engine used while driving and engine speed becomes high
  • When Engine always stopped, A driving range is limited.
NOTE: For the diagnostic procedure, refer to DTC P1C641F. Refer to P-HEV DRIVE MOTOR (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE WITH DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>DTC P1C641F GENERATOR CONTROL MODULE CIRCUIT INTERMITTENT .
