Indicator Assembly

  1. Disconnect the harness connector from the console front cover assembly, and remove the indicator assembly.
    • Model with gate shifter
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • Model with boot shifter
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Remove the housing from the indicator assembly. (Model with gate shifter)
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the boot assembly from the indicator assembly. (Model with boot shifter)
    CAUTION: When removing the boot assembly, be careful not to damage the boot assembly claws and adjacent parts.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the blind B. (Model with gate shifter)
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the blind A from the housing. (Model with gate shifter)
Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
