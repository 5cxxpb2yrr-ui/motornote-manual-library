DTC P059F: Active Grille Air Shutter "A" Performance/Stuck Off: Notes

1. - 

DTC detecting condition: 

Detected when two consecutive driving cycles with fault occur.

NOTE: After the faulty parts are repaired or replaced, perform the final check in Basic Diagnostic Procedure. Refer to ENGINE (DIAGNOSTICS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .
  1. CHECK DTC  .

    Using the Subaru Select Monitor or a general scan tool, read the DTC of [Engine]. Refer to ENGINE (DIAGNOSTICS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is DTC P059F displayed? (Current malfunction)

    Yes: 

    Go to  2.

    No: 

    Even if DTC is detected, it has returned to a normal condition at this time. Reproduce the failure, and then perform the diagnosis again.

    NOTE: In this case, it is assumed that the active grille shutter may have temporarily had a foreign matter adhered/pinched or a frost.
  2. CHECK ACTIVE GRILLE SHUTTER  .

    Check the area around the active grille shutter louver, louver link and actuator.

    Is there any abnormality such as a foreign matter adhered or pinched and a frost?

    Yes: 

    Remove the foreign matter from the active grille shutter and go to the next step. Go to  3.

    No: 

    Go to  3.

  3. CHECK ACTIVE GRILLE SHUTTER  .
    1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery sensor. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    2. Remove the front bumper. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>FRONT BUMPER>REMOVAL
    3. Check the area around the active grille shutter louver, louver link and actuator.

      Is there any damage in each part of the active grille shutter?

      Yes: 

      Replace the active grille shutter. Refer to COOLING (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ACTIVE GRILLE SHUTTER .

      No: 

      Go to  4.

  4. CHECK ACTIVE GRILLE SHUTTER  .
    1. Turn the ignition switch to ON (engine OFF).
    2. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, perform the operation check of the active grille shutter to measure time needed for open or close operation.
      NOTE: For detailed operation procedures, refer to the "Active Test". Refer to ENGINE (DIAGNOSTICS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ACTIVE TEST .

      Is the time less than 15 seconds?

      Yes: 

      Even if DTC is detected, it has returned to a normal condition at this time. Reproduce the failure, and then perform the diagnosis again.

      No: 

      Go to  5.

  5. CHECK DATA MONITOR  .
    1. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    2. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, read the value in [Active Grille Shutter Voltage Error Status].
      NOTE: For detailed operation procedures, refer to "Data Monitor". Refer to ENGINE (DIAGNOSTICS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>DATA MONITOR .

      Is [Normal] displayed?

      Yes: 

      Go to  6.

      No: 

      Check the power supply circuit. If the power supply circuit is normal. replace the active grille shutter. Refer to COOLING (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ACTIVE GRILLE SHUTTER .

  6. CHECK DATA MONITOR  .
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF, and cool down the engine enough.
      NOTE: Cool down the engine unit until it can be easily touched by hand.
    2. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    3. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, read the value in [Active Grille Shutter Temperature Error Status].
      NOTE: For detailed operation procedures, refer to "Data Monitor". Refer to ENGINE (DIAGNOSTICS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>DATA MONITOR .

      Is [Normal] displayed?

      Even if DTC is detected, it has returned to a normal condition at this time. Reproduce the failure, and then perform the diagnosis again.

      Replace the active grille shutter. Refer to COOLING (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ACTIVE GRILLE SHUTTER .
