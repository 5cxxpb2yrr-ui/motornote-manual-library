Sunroof Motor: Inspection

Check the auto-reverse mechanism.

CAUTION: Do not place objects in the way to check the auto-reverse mechanism.
  1. Open the lid assembly - sunroof.
  2. Push the lid assembly - sunroof in the opening direction with a force of 100 N (10.2 kgf, 22.5 lbf) during auto-closing, and check if the window auto-reverse mechanism operates properly.
NOTE: If the auto function is cancelled, perform the initialize operation. Refer to SUNROOF/T-TOP/CONVERTIBLE TOP (SUNROOF)>SUNROOF CONTROL SYSTEM>OPERATION>INITIALIZE OPERATION .
