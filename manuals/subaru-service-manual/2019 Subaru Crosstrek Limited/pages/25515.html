Door Latch & Door Lock Actuator Assembly: Installation: Rear

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Before installation, check the following items.
    • Cable and lever are free from deformation.
    • Grease is applied sufficiently to cable and lever joints.

      If grease is insufficient, add it as necessary before assembling the cable and lever.

  2. Install the latch and actuator assembly - rear.
    CAUTION:
    • Do not re-use the screws. Always replace with a new part.
    • Hook the lever of the frame assembly - rear door outer on the shaft of the latch & actuator assembly - rear.

    Preparation tool: 

    TORX® T30

    Tightening torque: 

    6.5 N.m (0.7 kgf-m, 5.2 ft-lb)

  3. Install the sealing cover - rear door.
  4. Install the trim panel - rear door.
  5. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
