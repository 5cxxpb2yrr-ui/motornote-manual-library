Front Door: Adjustment

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

Adjust the clearance around the panel assembly - front door as follows.

  • 4 door model
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  • 5 door model
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
Part Standard
A Fender - front to Panel assembly - front door 3.9±0.9 mm (0.16±0.04 in)
B Panel assembly - front door to Panel - side outer 6±1 mm (0.24±0.04 in)
C Panel - side outer to Front door sash 5.1+1.5, -1 mm (0.2+0.06, -0.04 in)
D Surface level gap between Panel - side outer to Front door sash 1.9±1.5 mm (0.07±0.06 in)
E Cover B pillar - front to Cover B pillar - rear 5.4+1.5, -1 mm (0.21+0.06, -0.04 in)
F Panel assembly - front door to Panel assembly - rear door 4±1 mm (0.16±0.04 in)
  1. Adjust the vertical and horizontal positions of the panel assembly - front door.
    1. Using ST, loosen the bolts on vehicle side of the upper hinge - front door.

      Preparation tool: 

      ST: WRENCH (925610000)

    2. Turn over the mud guard - front, and loosen the bolts on vehicle side of the lower hinge - front door.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Adjust the vertical and horizontal clearance of the panel assembly - front door.
  2. Adjust the surface level gap between the panel assembly - front door and the panel assembly - rear door.
    CAUTION: Do not use an impact wrench. Welding area on the striker nut plate is easily broken.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    1. Loosen the screws (A).
    2. Tap the striker - front door (B) using a plastic hammer to adjust the surface level gap between the rear end of the panel assembly - front door and the front end of the panel assembly - rear door.
