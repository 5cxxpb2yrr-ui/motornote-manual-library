Explanation For Circuits

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

When 3-phase AC current is applied to the 3-phase winding wire of the stat or coil, a rotating magnetic field is generated in the motor. The system controls this rotating magnetic field according to the rotor rotating position and speed. As a result, the permanent magnet located in the rotor is pulled toward the rotating magnetic field, then torque force is generated. Generated torque force is proportional to the amount of current. The system controls the motor speed by adjusting AC frequency. Also, it controls the angle between rotating magnet field and rotor magnet properly so that it can generate high torque efficiently up to high rotation.
