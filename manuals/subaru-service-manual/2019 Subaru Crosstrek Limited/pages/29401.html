Inspection When Radiator Fans Do Not Stop

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  • INSPECTION WHEN MAIN FAN AND SUB FAN DO NOT STOP 
    1. CHECK SUB FAN RELAY  .
      1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery.

        Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .

      2. Remove the sub fan relay.

        Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>RADIATOR FAN RELAY>LOCATION .

      3. Perform unit check for the sub fan relay.

        Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>RADIATOR FAN RELAY>INSPECTION .

      Is the sub fan relay OK?

      Yes: 

      Go to  2.

      No: 

      1. Replace the sub fan relay.
      2. Restore the vehicle to its original state.
    2. INSULATION CHECK OF HARNESS BETWEEN SUB FAN RELAY AND SUB FAN MOTOR  .
      1. Connect the battery ground terminal.

        Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .

        NOTE: Sub fan relay is removed.
      2. Turn to IG ON.
      3. Check the radiator fan operation.

      Do the sub fan motor and the main fan motor rotate?

      Yes: 

      1. Repair the harness between sub fan relay and sub fan motor.
      2. Restore the vehicle to its original state.

      No: 

      Go to  3.

    3. CHECK INSULATION OF HARNESS BETWEEN SUB FAN RELAY - ECM AND A/C CONTROL MODULE  .
      1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery.

        Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .

      2. Disconnect the ECM connector.
      3. Check the insulation of harness and chassis ground between sub fan relay - ECM and A/C CONTROL MODULE.

      Are they insulated?

      Yes: 

      1. Replace the ECM.

        Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>ENGINE CONTROL MODULE (ECM) .

      2. Restore the vehicle to its original state.

      No: 

      Go to  4.

    4. CHECK INSULATION OF HARNESS BETWEEN SUB FAN RELAY - ECM AND A/C CONTROL MODULE  .
      1. Disconnect the A/C CONTROL MODULEconnector.
      2. Check the insulation of harness and chassis ground between sub fan relay - ECM and A/C control module.

      Are they insulated?

      Yes: 

      1. Replace the A/C Control Module.

        Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>CONTROL UNIT .

      2. Restore the vehicle to its original state.

      No: 

      1. Repair the harness between sub fan relay - ECM and A/C control module.
      2. Restore the vehicle to its original state.
  • INSPECTION WHEN ONLY MAIN FAN DOES NOT STOP 
    1. CHECK MAIN FAN RELAY 1  .
      1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery.

        Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .

      2. Remove the main fan relay 1.

        Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>RADIATOR FAN RELAY>LOCATION .

      3. Perform unit check for the main fan relay 1.

        Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>RADIATOR FAN RELAY>INSPECTION .

      Is the main fan relay 1 OK?

      Yes: 

      Go to  2.

      No: 

      1. Replace the main fan relay 1.
      2. Restore the vehicle to its original state.
    2. INSULATION CHECK OF HARNESS BETWEEN MAIN FAN MOTOR AND SUB FAN MOTOR  .
      1. Connect the battery ground terminal.

        Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .

        NOTE: Main fan relay 1 is removed.
      2. Turn to IG ON.
      3. Check the radiator fan operation.

      Does the main fan motor rotate?

      Yes: 

      1. Repair the harness between the main fan motor and the sub fan motor.
      2. Restore the vehicle to its original state.

      No: 

      1. Replace the ECM.

        Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>ENGINE CONTROL MODULE (ECM) .

      2. Restore the vehicle to its original state.
