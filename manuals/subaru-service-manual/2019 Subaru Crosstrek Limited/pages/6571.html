Starting System & Charging System (Except For HEV): Generator: Installation

  1. Temporarily install the generator.
  2. Tighten the bolts in the numerical order as shown in the figure.

    Tightening torque: 

    25 N.m (2.5 kgf-m, 18.4 ft-lb)

    Fig 1: Generator Bolts Tightening Sequence
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Connect the connector and terminal B.

    Tightening torque: 

    15.5 N.m (1.6 kgf-m, 11.4 ft-lb)

  4. Install the V-belts. Refer to MECHANICAL (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>V-BELT>INSTALLATION>EXCEPT FOR HYBRID MODEL .
  5. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
