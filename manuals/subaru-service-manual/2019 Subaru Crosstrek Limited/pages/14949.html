AWD Circuit Measures

  1. Full-time AWD MT model

    Since viscous coupling (limited slip differential) is used in the center differential, cut-off of AWD circuit cannot be carried out.

  2. Full-time AWD CVT model
    • Since electronically controlled MP-T hydraulic multi-plate clutch is adapted for center differential, switch to FWD by using Subaru Select Monitor. (TR580 model)
    • Since electronically controlled torque coupling, electric current type clutch is adapted for center differential, switch to FWD by using Subaru Select Monitor. (TH2A model)
