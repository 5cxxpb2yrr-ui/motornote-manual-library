Rear Differential Front Member: Installation

  1. Install the rear differential front member, and temporarily attach and tighten a new self-locking nut.
    • T-type
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • VA1 type (Impreza model)
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • VA1 type (except for Impreza model)
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Remove the transmission jack.
  3. Tighten the self-locking nut.

    Tightening torque: 

    T1: 50 N.m (5.1 kgf-m, 36.9 ft-lb)

    T2: 110 N.m (11.2 kgf-m, 81.1 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Lower the vehicle.
  5. Connect the battery ground terminal.
