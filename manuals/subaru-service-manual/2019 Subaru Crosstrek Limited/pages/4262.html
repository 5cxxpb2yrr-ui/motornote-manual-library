Trunk Lid: Installation

  1. Install the trunk lid arm assembly.

    Tightening torque: 

    18 N.m (1.8 kgf-m, 13.3 ft-lb)

  2. Install the trim panel assembly - rear shelf.
  3. Install the lower anchor of seat belt - rear shoulder CTR.

    Tightening torque: 

    30 N.m (3.1 kgf-m, 22.1 ft-lb)

  4. Install the trim panel - rear pillar UPR and the trim panel - rear pillar LWR.
  5. Install the side sill cover - rear INN.
  6. Install the rear seat cushion assembly.
  7. Install the torsion bar - trunk lid.
    CAUTION:
    • When performing installation, pay close attention to the original marks and shapes of the torsion bar - trunk lid on the RH and LH sides.
    • When installing the remover to the torsion bar - trunk lid, leave a slight clearance (a).

    Preparation tool: 

    ST: REMOVER (99099FJ000) and REMOVER (927780000)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    1. Insert the left end of the torsion bar - trunk lid LH (c) into the hole of the hinge bracket LH (b).
    2. Using the ST, hook the right end of the torsion bar - trunk lid LH (c) on the hinge bracket RH (f).
    3. Install the hinge link RH (g) to the center of the U-shape portion of the torsion bar - trunk lid LH (c).
    4. Tuck it in the cutout of bracket - torsion bar (d).
    5. Insert the right end of the torsion bar - trunk lid RH (e) into the hole of the hinge bracket RH (f).
    6. Using the ST, hook the left end of the torsion bar - trunk lid RH (e) on the hinge bracket LH (b).
    7. Install the hinge link LH (h) to the center of the U-shape portion of the torsion bar - trunk lid RH (e).
    8. Tuck it in the cutout of bracket - torsion bar (d).
  8. Install the trunk lid COMPL.

    Tightening torque: 

    14 N.m (1.4 kgf-m, 10.3 ft-lb)

  9. Install the rear spoiler.

    Tightening torque: 

    7.5 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.5 ft-lb)

  10. Install the rearview camera assembly. Refer to ENTERTAINMENT & MONITORING>CAMERA>INSTALLATION>REAR .
  11. Install the rear finisher light assembly. Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>REAR FINISHER LIGHT ASSEMBLY>INSTALLATION .
  12. Connect the connector and install the trunk lid harness.
  13. Install the lock assembly - trunk lid.

    Tightening torque: 

    7.5 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.5 ft-lb)

  14. Apply grease to the joint portion of torsion bar and the rotating area of hinges.
  15. Install while paying attention to make a uniform clearance around the trunk lid COMPL. Refer to EXTERIOR BODY PANELS>TRUNK LID>ADJUSTMENT .

    Tightening torque: 

    Refer to "COMPONENT" of "General Description". Refer to EXTERIOR BODY PANELS>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>COMPONENT>TRUNK LID PANEL .

  16. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY>GROUND TERMINAL REMOVAL OR INSTALLATION .
