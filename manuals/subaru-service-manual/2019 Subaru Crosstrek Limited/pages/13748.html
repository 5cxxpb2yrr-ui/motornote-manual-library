MODE $01 (Current Powertrain Diagnostic Data)

Refer to data denoting the current operating condition of analog input/output, digital input/output or the powertrain system.

A list of the support data and PID (Parameter Identification) codes are shown in the following table.

PID Data Unit of measure
$01 Number of emission-related powertrain DTC, and malfunction indicator light status and diagnosis support information -
$05 Engine coolant temperature Celsius
$0C Engine speed rpm
$0D Vehicle speed km/h or MPH
$1F Time elapsed after engine start sec
$21 Travel distance after the malfunction indicator light illuminates km/miles
$30 Number of warm ups after DTC clear time
$31 Travel distance after DTC clear km/miles
$41 Diagnostic monitor of each driving cycle -
NOTE: Refer to general scan tool manufacturer's operation instructions to access the present powertrain diagnostic data (MODE $01).
