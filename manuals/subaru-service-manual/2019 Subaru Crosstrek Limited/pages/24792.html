DTC P0500: Vehicle Speed Sensor "A" Circuit: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

1. - 

DTC detecting condition: 

Immediately at fault recognition

NOTE: After the faulty parts are repaired or replaced, perform the final check in Basic Diagnostic Procedure. Refer to ENGINE (DIAGNOSTICS) (H4DO (HEV))>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .
  1. CHECK DTC OF VDC  .
    1. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    2. Read the DTC of VDC using the Subaru Select Monitor or a general scan tool. Refer to COMMON (DIAG)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is DTC for VDC displayed? (Current malfunction)

    Yes:  Perform the diagnosis of corresponding DTC using "Diagnostic Trouble Code (DTC)". Refer to BRAKE CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>LIST .

    No:  Repair the poor contact of ECM connector.
