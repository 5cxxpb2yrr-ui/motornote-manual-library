Sunroof Assembly: Sunshade: Installation

CAUTION: For installation of the stereo camera cover assembly, refer to "INSTALLATION", "Stereo Camera" in the "EyeSight" service information and observe the precautions for installation. Refer to EYESIGHT>STEREO CAMERA>INSTALLATION .
  1. Attach protective tape on the handle and the edge surface of the sunshade assembly, and install the sunshade assembly.
  2. Install the left and right stopper rubbers.
  3. Install the trim panel - roof assembly. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>ROOF TRIM>INSTALLATION .
  4. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
