Check Power Seat Switch

Measure resistance between terminals while operating each switch.

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
Switch position Terminal No. Standard
Slide forward 1 and 8
4 and 7		 Less than 10 Ω
Slide rearward 1 and 7
4 and 8		 Less than 10 Ω
Lifter up 1 and 6
4 and 9		 Less than 10 Ω
Lifter down 1 and 9
4 and 6		 Less than 10 Ω
Reclining forward 1 and 10
4 and 5		 Less than 10 Ω
Reclining rearward 1 and 5
4 and 10		 Less than 10 Ω

Replace the power seat switch if the inspection result is not within the standard value.
