DTC P33DC-1E: Hybrid/EV Battery Stack 3 Circuit Resistance Out Of Range: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Outline of diagnosis: 

If the difference of resistance between each cell of NO. 3 HV supply stack sub-assembly is equal to or greater than the standard

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

  • Reduced output power
  • Engine not stopped
NOTE: For the diagnostic procedure, refer to DTC P33DA1E. Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE WITH DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>DTC P33DA1E HYBRID/EV BATTERY STACK 1 CIRCUIT RESISTANCE OUT OF RANGE .
