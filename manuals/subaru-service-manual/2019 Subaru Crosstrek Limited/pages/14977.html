Operation Check Of Trunk Lid Release Handle

CAUTION: Do not check the trunk lid release handle when in the trunk. If the trunk lid release handle is defective, you may be trapped in.
  1. Use a flat tip screwdriver. Slide the screwdriver blade from the slit aperture of the lock assembly fully to the end until you hear a click. This places the latch in the locked position.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Make sure the latch is released by pulling the yellow trunk lid release handle in the direction of arrow.
Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
