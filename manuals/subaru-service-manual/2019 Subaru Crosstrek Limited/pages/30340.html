Heater Duct: Removal: Rear

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
CAUTION: Before handling the airbag system components, refer to "CAUTION" of "General Description" in "AIRBAG SYSTEM". Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. Disconnect the battery ground terminal and wait for at least 60 seconds before starting work. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    NOTE:
    • Shift the select lever into "N" before disconnecting the battery ground terminal.
    • For models with power seat, remove the seat mounting bolts before removing the battery ground terminal.
  2. Remove the duct - rear heater front. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>HEATER DUCT>REMOVAL>FRONT .
  3. Remove the front seats. Refer to SEATS>FRONT SEAT>REMOVAL .
  4. Turn over the floor mat. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>FLOOR MAT>REMOVAL .
  5. Remove the duct - rear heater rear.
    1. Release the clips.
    2. Release the claws, and then remove the duct - rear heater rear.
