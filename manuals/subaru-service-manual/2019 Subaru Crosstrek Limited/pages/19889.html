Explanation For Circuits

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly and Battery (12V) sensor detects reception fault individually. The reception fault of Battery (12V) sensor detected by Battery (12V) sensor is sent to Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly via LIN communication. When fault occurs at either of them, Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly judges as LIN communication fault and outputs DTC.
