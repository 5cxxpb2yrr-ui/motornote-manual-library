Malfunction Description

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly detects that the electrical charge stored in the high voltage condenser of Inverter with converter assembly cannot be discharged at READY OFF.

The following cases are assumed to be malfunction causes:

  • Fault of Inside of inverter voltage sensor (VH) system
    • Voltage sensor (VH) malfunction
    • Malfunction of Motor generator control ECU
    • Malfunction of communication (Wire harness)
  • High voltage system fault
    • Malfunction of Inverter with converter assembly
  • Shut Down Signal Circuit failure
    • Malfunction of Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly
    • Malfunction of Wire harness
    • Malfunction of Inverter with converter assembly
