DTC P0560-14: System Voltage (Batt) Circuit Short To Ground Or Open: Notes

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

1. - 

Outline of diagnosis: 

Battery ECU assembly backup power supply system malfunction

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

Normal driving

Wiring diagram: 

Hybrid system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK FUSE (F/B FUSE NO. 25). 
    1. Remove the F/B FUSE NO. 25 from the Relay & fuse box (F/B). Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>RELAY AND FUSE .
    2. Check F/B FUSE NO. 25.

    Is the F/B FUSE NO. 25 OK?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Replace F/B FUSE NO. 25.

  2. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (F/B FUSE NO. 25 - BATTERY POSITIVE TERMINAL) (OPEN). 
    1. Disconnect the ground terminal from Battery (12V). Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    2. Disconnect the positive terminal from Battery (12V). Refer to STARTING/CHARGING SYSTEMS (H4DO (HEV))>BATTERY .
    3. Using the tester, measure the resistance between F/B FUSE NO. 25 and Battery (12V) positive terminal.

      Connector & terminal 

      F/B FUSE NO. 25 (battery side) - Battery (12V) positive cable:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Repair or replace the Wire harness between F/B FUSE NO. 25 and Battery (12V) positive terminal.

  3. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (F/B FUSE NO. 25 - BATTERY POSITIVE TERMINAL) (SHORT TO GROUND). 

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between F/B FUSE NO. 25 (battery side) and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    F/B FUSE NO. 25 (battery side) - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance 10 kΩ or more?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Repair or replace the Wire harness between F/B FUSE NO. 25 and Battery (12V) positive terminal.

  4. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (F/B FUSE NO. 25 - Battery ECU assembly) (OPEN). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Disconnect connector (R534) of Rear harness LH.
    2. Using the tester, measure the resistance between F/B FUSE NO. 25 (module side) and connector (R534) of Rear harness LH.

      Connector & terminal 

      F/B FUSE NO. 25 (module side) - (R534) No. 15:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  5.

    No:  Repair or replace the Wire harness between F/B FUSE NO. 25 and connector (R534) of Rear harness LH.

  5. CHECK CONNECTOR CONNECTION CONDITION (Battery ECU assembly). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Check that the Service plug is disconnected. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .
    2. Check the connecting condition of connector (HV42) in Battery ECU assembly and check the relevant terminals for contact pressure, deformation and intrusion of water or foreign matters. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>BATTERY CONTROL MODULE .

    Is there looseness at the connector connection, or any poor contact, deformation or intrusion of water or foreign matters at the relevant terminals?

    Yes:  Connect the connector securely. Or repair the poor contact of connector.

    No:  Go to  6.

  6. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (AM VOLTAGE). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Disconnect Battery ECU assembly connector (HV42).
    2. Install F/B FUSE NO. 25 to Relay & fuse box (F/B). Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>RELAY AND FUSE .
    3. Connect the ground terminal to Battery (12V). Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    4. Using a tester, measure the voltage between terminals in Battery ECU assembly connector.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV42) No. 1 (+) - (HV42) No. 9 (-):

    Is the voltage 11 - 14 V?

    Yes:  Replace Battery ECU assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>BATTERY CONTROL MODULE .

    No:  Repair or replace the Wire harness between connector (HV75) and connector (HV42).
