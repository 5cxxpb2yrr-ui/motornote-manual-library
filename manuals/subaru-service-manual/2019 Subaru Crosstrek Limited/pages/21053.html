DTC U1150-87: Lost Communication With Hybrid Powertrain Control Module (Hybrid/EV Battery Local Bus) Missing Message: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

1. - 

Outline of diagnosis: 

When Battery ECU assembly cannot receive the data from Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly continuously for approx. one second or more

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

  • Reduced output power
  • Engine not stopped

Wiring diagram: 

Hybrid system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK DTC. 

    Read the DTC of [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Are any DTCs displayed?

    Yes:  Perform the diagnosis for the displayed DTCs. Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly (VOLTAGE CHECK). 
    1. Turn to IG ON.
    2. Using a tester, measure the voltage between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (B606) No. 1 (+) - Chassis ground:

      (i369) No. 4 (+) - Chassis ground:

      (i370) No. 3 (+) - Chassis ground:

    Is the voltage 11 - 14 V?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Repair or replace the Wire harness between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly and chassis ground.

  3. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly - Battery ECU assembly). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Check that the Service plug is disconnected. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .
    2. Turn the ignition to OFF.
    3. Disconnect Battery ECU assembly connector (HV42).
    4. Using a tester, measure the resistance between Battery ECU assembly connector terminals.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV42) No. 5 - (HV42) No. 6:

    Is the resistance 54 - 69 Ω?

    Yes:  Replace Battery ECU assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>BATTERY CONTROL MODULE .

    No:  Go to  4.

  4. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly - Battery ECU assembly) (OPEN). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Disconnect Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector (i369).
      NOTE: Check connecting condition and looseness of each connector between Battery ECU assembly and Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly.
    2. Disconnect Electric vehicle charger assembly connector (HV31).
    3. Using a tester, measure the resistance between Battery ECU assembly connector and Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV42) No. 5 - (i369) No. 34:

      (HV42) No. 6 - (i369) No. 35:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  5.

    No:  Repair or replace the Wire harness between Battery ECU assembly connector and Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector.

  5. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly - Battery ECU assembly) (SHORT TO GROUND AND LINE-TO-LINE SHORT). 

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between terminals in Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector, and between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (i369) No. 34 - (i369) No. 1 - 35:

    (i369) No. 34 - Chassis ground:

    (i369) No. 35 - (i369) No. 1 - 35:

    (i369) No. 35 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance 10 kΩ or more?

    Yes:  Replace Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL MODULE .

    No:  Repair or replace the Wire harness between Battery ECU assembly connector and Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector.
