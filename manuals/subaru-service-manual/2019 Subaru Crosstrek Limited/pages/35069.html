Starting System & Charging System (Except For HEV): Battery: Charge: Notes

WARNING: Do not bring an open flame close to the battery.
CAUTION:
  • If the terminals are corroded, clean with a brush and common caustic soda solution.
  • As batteries produce flammable gases while charging, be careful not to bring an open flame close to the batteries.
  • Ventilate sufficiently when charging batteries.
  • Observe instructions when handling the battery charger.
  • Before charging the battery on the vehicle, disconnect the ground terminal from the 12V engine restart battery sensor to prevent damage of generator diodes or other electrical units.
