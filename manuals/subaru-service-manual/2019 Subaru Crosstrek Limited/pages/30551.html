Transfer Case And Extension Case Assembly: Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Remove the manual transmission assembly from the vehicle, refer to MANUAL TRANSMISSION AND DIFFERENTIAL (6MT)>MANUAL TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL .
  2. Remove the back-up light switch and the neutral position switch. refer to MANUAL TRANSMISSION AND DIFFERENTIAL (6MT)>SWITCHES AND HARNESS>REMOVAL
  3. Remove the transfer case together with the extension case assembly.
    1. Remove the transmission cover.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Set and hold the selector lever COMPL to the 1st-2nd side, and remove the transfer case and extension case assembly as a unit.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the transfer cover.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Use the ST to push out the spring pin, and remove the shift lever COMPL.

    Special Tool: 

    ST: 398791700 REMOVER

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Remove the extension case assembly.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Remove the transfer driven gear and center differential as a unit.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Remove the taper roller bearing (extension case side) outer race and the adjusting washer.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
