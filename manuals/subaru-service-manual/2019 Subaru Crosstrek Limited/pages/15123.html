HVAC System (Heater, Ventilator And A/C) (Except For HEV): General Description: Caution: Notes

Before disconnecting connectors of sensors or units, be sure to disconnect the ground terminal from battery. When replacing the electrical parts provided with memory functions that store contents specified by a customer, record the memory contents before disconnecting the battery ground terminal.
