DTC U128F: Lost Communication With Hybrid Powertrain Control Module C-Can

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Detected when CAN data from hybrid vehicle control computer is not transmitted to stereo camera.

NOTE: Perform the diagnosis for LAN system. Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE .
