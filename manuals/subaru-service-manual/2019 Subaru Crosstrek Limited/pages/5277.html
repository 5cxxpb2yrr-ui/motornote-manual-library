DTC C1C6F: Accumulator Pressure Leak

DTC detecting condition: 

  • Before braking and when the pump motor is being driven, accumulator pressure sensor input does not increase and this condition occurs the predetermined times or more.
  • Before braking and when the pump motor is not driven, a dropping rate of accumulator pressure sensor input is larger than the specified value and this condition occurs the predetermined times or more.

Trouble symptom: 

Regenerative brake cannot be controlled.

  1. CHECK BRAKE FLUID LEAKAGE. 

    Check the brake fluid lines between the brake booster assembly (with master cylinder) and the brake accumulator assembly for brake fluid leakage.

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Repair or replace the appropriate parts.

  2. CHECK PUMP MOTOR DRIVE TIME. 
    1. Turn to READY ON.
    2. Wait for a while in the READY ON state, check ON/OFF operation of the pump motor.
    NOTE: The pump motor drive one or more time (OFF → ON) within 20 seconds, or duration of operation at a single time is for 10 seconds or more.

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Replace the brake booster assembly (with the master cylinder) and the brake accumulator assembly. Ref. to BRAKE>BRAKE BOOSTER . Ref. to BRAKE>BRAKE ACCUMULATOR ASSY . Go to  3.

  3. CHECK DTC. 
    1. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, perform the clear memory. Ref. to COMMON (DIAG)>CLEAR MEMORY .
    2. Read the DTC using Subaru Select Monitor. Ref. to ELECTRIC BRAKE BOOSTER (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is DTC C1C6F displayed?

    Yes:  Replace the brake booster assembly (with the master cylinder) and the brake accumulator assembly. Ref. to BRAKE>BRAKE BOOSTER . Ref. to BRAKE>BRAKE ACCUMULATOR ASSY .

    No:  Currently, it is normal. There may have been a temporary poor contact in the harness and connector or a temporary noise interference.
