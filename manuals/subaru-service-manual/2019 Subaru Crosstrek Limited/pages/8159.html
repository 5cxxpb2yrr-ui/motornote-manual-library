Impreza Model

  1. Install a new gasket to the fuel tank.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Set the fuel pump to the position shown in the figure.
    CAUTION:
    • Be careful not to spill any fuel over the gasket. If any fuel is spilled onto the gasket, wipe it off completely.
    • Set the arm and float of the fuel level sensor while paying attention to prevent them from contacting the fuel tank. If the arm of the fuel level sensor is bent, the fuel gauge may not read correctly.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Use a new ring nut, temporarily tighten the ring nut.
  4. Using the ST, tighten the ring nut.
    CAUTION:
    • Be careful not to damage the tube with the ST.
    • Before setting the ST, move the tube above the ST.
    • Be sure to tighten firmly to the specified torque in order to avoid looseness.
    • Make sure that the position mark on the fuel pump is located within the installation position range mark on the fuel tank as shown in the figure.

    Preparation tool: 

    ST: ST FUEL PUMP (42099FL000)

    Tightening torque: 

    130 N.m (13.3 kgf-m, 95.9 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Check tightening condition of ring nut.
    1. Using a caliper with depth gauge, measure the dimension (3 positions) of the ring nut and the top of fuel tank.
    2. Check that the difference between the median value of the measured three positions and the other two positions is within ±1.5 mm (0.059 in).
    CAUTION:
    • If the measured value exceeds the standard, retighten the ring nut to correct erroneous tightening of the ring nut.
    • If the ring nut and fuel pump gasket are deformed, cracked or damaged, replace them with new parts.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Connect the fuel delivery tube. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>INSTALLATION>QUICK CONNECTOR CONNECTION (TYPE D) .
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Connect the connector to the fuel pump.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Install the service hole cover of fuel pump.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Install the rear seat cushion. Refer to SEATS>REAR SEAT>INSTALLATION .
  10. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
