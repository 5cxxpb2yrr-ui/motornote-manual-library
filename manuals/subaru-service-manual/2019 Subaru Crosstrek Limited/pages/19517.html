DTC P0A0A-92: High Voltage System Interlock Performance Or Incorrect Operation: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Outline of diagnosis: 

In the case of any one of the following conditions

  • The Service plug was removed, while the vehicle was stopped
  • The interlock signal line was disconnected, while the vehicle was stopped

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

Hybrid system stopped

NOTE: For the diagnostic procedure, refer to DTC P0A0A13. Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE WITH DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>DTC P0A0A13 HIGH VOLTAGE SYSTEM INTERLOCK CIRCUIT OPEN .
