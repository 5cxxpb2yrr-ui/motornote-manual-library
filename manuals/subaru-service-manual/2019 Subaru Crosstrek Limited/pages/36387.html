CROSSTREK Model

EXCEPT FOR HYBRID MODEL 

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    NOTE:
    • For CVT models, release the shift lock and shift the select lever to the "N" position. Refer to CONTROL SYSTEMS>SELECT LEVER>REMOVAL .
    • On power seat models, move the front seat all the way forward before disconnecting the battery ground terminal.
  2. Affix protective tape (a) to the ornament panel - console.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the boot - hand brake.
    1. Pull up the cup holder, and release the engagement of the clip at the end of the cover assembly - front.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Release the engagement of claws and clips, and then remove the boot - hand brake.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the cover assembly - front.
    CAUTION:
    • Remove the mat - center tray and the tray - center LWR together with the cover assembly - front. Removing the mat - center tray first may damage the parts.
    • For models with smart keyless access with push button start, the interior antenna is installed behind the tray - center LWR. Be careful not to damage the harness when removing the cover assembly - front.
    1. Remove the shift knob. (MT model)
    2. Remove the select lever knob. (CVT model) Refer to CONTROL SYSTEMS>SELECT LEVER>REMOVAL .
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Remove the screws and release the clips, then detach the cover assembly - front.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    4. Disconnect the harness connector.
    5. Remove the mat - center tray and the tray - center LWR.
  5. Remove the console box assembly.
    1. Remove the caps and remove the bolts.
    2. Remove the screws and release the clip.
    3. Disconnect the harness connector, and remove the console box assembly.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Disassemble the cover assembly - front and the console box assembly as required.
