High-Pressure Fuel Delivery Pipe

  1. Temporarily tighten the flare nut of the high-pressure fuel delivery pipe by hand until it seats, and tighten the flare nuts on both ends.
    CAUTION:
    • Always use a new high-pressure fuel delivery pipe.
    • Check that the seal surface of the fuel injector pipe LH and the high-pressure fuel pump have no deformation, cracks or other damages.
    • Check that the seal surface of the fuel injector pipe LH and the high-pressure fuel pump have no foreign materials.

    Tightening torque: 

    25 N.m (2.5 kgf-m, 18.4 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Install the fuel pipe insulator No. 3 and fuel pipe insulator No. 4. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FUEL INSULATOR>INSTALLATION .
  3. Install the intake manifold assembly. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>INTAKE MANIFOLD ASSEMBLY>INSTALLATION .
  4. Install the air intake boot. Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>Air Intake Boot>INSTALLATION .
  5. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
