Explanation For Circuits

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Motor generator control ECU located in Inverter with converter assembly detects the boosting converter temperature with the boosting converter temperature sensor, which is built in Inverter with converter assembly, and also controls to prevent overheat of the boosting converter. Motor generator control ECU detects sensor property malfunction based on the temperature sensor value sent from the boosting converter temperature sensor. Also, Inverter with converter assembly detects a connection error of the boosting converter temperature sensor.
