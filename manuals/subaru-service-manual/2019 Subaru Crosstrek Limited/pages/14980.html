Charging Inlet Door And Charging Connector (Hybrid Model)

  1. Check the charging inlet door unlocks normally when you move close to the charging inlet door while carrying the access key.
  2. With unlock condition, check the charging inlet door opens normally when pressing the charging inlet door with all doors locked.
  3. While carrying the access key, check the charging indicator illuminates when inserting the charging connector connected to the device outlet into the charge inlet. Then, check the charging connector locks and unlocks normally when pressing the charge cable connector lock switch (A) with all doors locked.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
