VDC OFF Switch: Inspection

  1. Measure the resistance between the VDC OFF switch terminals.

    Preparation tool: 

    Circuit tester

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Terminal No. Inspection conditions Standard
    6 - 8 Switch OFF 1 MΩ or more
    Switch ON Less than 1 Ω
  2. Replace the VDC OFF switch if the inspection result is not within the standard value.
