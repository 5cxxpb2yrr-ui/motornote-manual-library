Center Grille Assembly

  1. Remove the grille assembly - CTR ventilation.
    1. Attach the protective tape (a).
    2. Release the clips, and then remove the grille assembly - CTR ventilation LH and RH.
      CAUTION: Wrap a remover, etc. with protective tape or cloth. Use a remover, etc. by pressing it in the instrument panel side so that the grille assembly - CTR ventilation should not be damaged.
