A/C Pipe

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Using the refrigerant recovery system, discharge refrigerant. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>REFRIGERANT RECOVERY PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .
  2. Remove the battery and battery tray. Refer to STARTING/CHARGING SYSTEMS (H4DO (HEV))>BATTERY>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the bumper face - front. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>FRONT BUMPER>REMOVAL .
  4. Remove the radiator assembly. Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>RADIATOR>REMOVAL .
  5. Remove the hose - pressure discharge. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>HOSE AND PIPE>REMOVAL>HIGH-PRESSURE HOSE .
  6. Remove the pipe - return assembly. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>HOSE AND PIPE>REMOVAL>RETURN PIPE .
  7. Remove the pipe - sensor.
CAUTION:
  • Do not apply excessive force to the pipe.
  • Seal the engaging parts of the disconnected pipe, expansion valve - heater assembly, and air conditioner unit with plugs or vinyl tape to prevent foreign matter from entering.
  • Do not pull the harness and cable forcibly.
  1. Disconnect the connector of the pressure sensor.
  2. Remove the bolt and release the claw, then remove the bracket - air conditioner wire.
  3. Release the claw and remove the engine harness bracket.
  4. Disconnect the main fuse box connector, and detach the harness from the clip.
  5. Disconnect the upper connector of the engine control module (ECM). Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>ENGINE CONTROL MODULE (ECM)>REMOVAL .
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Disconnect the battery cable assembly. Refer to STARTING/CHARGING SYSTEMS (H4DO (HEV))>BATTERY CABLE ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL .
    NOTE: Disconnect the battery cable assemblies at the locations necessary for removing the pipe - sensor.
  7. Remove the bolt, nut, clamp, and clip, and remove the pipe - sensor.
