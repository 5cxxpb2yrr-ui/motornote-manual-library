Differential Side Retainer Oil Seal: Replacement

CAUTION:
  • Immediately after the vehicle has been running or after idling for a long time, the differential gear oil will be hot. Be careful not to burn yourself.
  • Be careful not to spill the differential gear oil on exhaust pipe to prevent it from emitting smoke or fire. If differential gear oil is spilled on the exhaust pipe, wipe it off completely.
  1. Loosen the self-locking nut of the front suspension strut mount by two turns. (Impreza model)
    NOTE: Loosen the self-locking nut only on the removal side if only one side of the differential side retainer oil seal is replaced.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Lift up the vehicle.
  3. Remove the front tires. Refer to WHEEL AND TIRE SYSTEM>TIRE AND WHEEL>REMOVAL .
  4. Remove the crossmember support - rear.
    NOTE: Remove the crossmember support - rear only on the removal side if only one side of the differential side retainer oil seal is replaced.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the front exhaust pipe. Refer to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FRONT EXHAUST PIPE>REMOVAL .
  6. Remove the differential gear oil drain plug using TORX® bit T70, and then drain differential gear oil.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Tighten the differential gear oil drain plug.
    NOTE: Use a new gasket.

    Tightening torque: 

    70 N.m (7.1 kgf-m, 51.6 ft-lb)

  8. Remove the brake hose bracket.
    • Impreza model
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • Crosstrek model
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Remove the front ABS wheel speed sensor. Refer to VEHICLE DYNAMICS CONTROL (VDC)>FRONT ABS WHEEL SPEED SENSOR>REMOVAL .
  10. Remove the stabilizer link.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  11. Separate the front arm assembly from the ball joint. (Impreza model)
    NOTE:
    • Mark the engaging position of the ball joint and front arm assembly before loosening the flange nuts. Doing so will facilitate aligning the position when assembling and lessen adjusting allowance for wheel alignment.
    • To prevent damage of the front arm assembly ball joint boot, place a wooden block, etc. between front arm assembly and ball joint.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  12. Remove the bolts which secure to the front housing, and separate the front arm and housing. (Crosstrek model)
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  13. Using a crowbar, etc., pull out until the front drive shaft transmission side joint slides move smoothly.
    NOTE: Place cloth between the crowbar and the transmission in order to avoid damaging the transmission side retainer.
  14. Hold the transmission side joint of the front drive shaft by hand and extract the housing from the transmission while pressing the housing outward, so as not to stretch the boot.
    NOTE:
    • Before pulling the RH front drive shaft from transmission, turn the steering wheel to the right hand at full lock.
    • Before pulling the LH front drive shaft from transmission, turn the steering wheel to the left hand at full lock.
  15. Remove the differential side retainer oil seal using driver wrapped with vinyl tape etc.
  16. Using the ST, install the differential side retainer oil seal by lightly tapping with a hammer.
    NOTE:
    • Apply differential gear oil to the lip surface, so that the oil seal lip is not deformed.
    • Apply differential gear oil to the press-fitting surface of oil seal and the differential side retainer.
    • Oil seal has an identification mark (R, L). When installing oil seals, do not confuse the left and right.

    Preparation tool: 

    ST: DIFFERENTIAL SIDE OIL SEAL INSTALLER (18675AA000)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  17. Set the ST to the side retainer.

    Preparation tool: 

    ST: OIL SEAL PROTECTOR (28399SA010)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  18. Replace the circlip of the drive shaft with a new part.
  19. Insert the front drive shaft spline section into transmission and remove the ST (DIFFERENTIAL SIDE OIL SEAL INSTALLER).
    NOTE:
    • Before inserting the RH front drive shaft into transmission, turn the steering wheel to the right hand at full lock.
    • Before inserting the LH front drive shaft into transmission, turn the steering wheel to the left hand at full lock.
  20. Insert the drive shaft into the transmission securely by pressing the housing from outside of the vehicle.
    CAUTION:
    • To prevent the front lower arm assembly from damaging the ball joint dust cover, place the ball joint on the upper side of the front lower arm assembly before starting the operation.
    • Be careful not to damage the front lower arm assembly and ball joint by covering the upper side of the front lower arm assembly with a cloth.
  21. Replace the flange nut with a new part, and install the ball joint to the front arm. (Impreza model)

    Tightening torque: 

    100 N.m (10.2kgf-m, 73.8 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  22. Install the ball joint assembly. (Crosstrek model)
    CAUTION:
    • Before tightening, make sure the bottom surface of the housing assembly - front axle and the stepped section of ball joint are in contact.
    • Be careful not to damage the boot of the joint.

    Tightening torque: 

    50 N.m (5.1 kgf-m, 36.9 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  23. Replace the flange nut with a new part, and install the stabilizer link.

    Tightening torque: 

    60 N.m (6.1 kgf-m, 44.3 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  24. Install the front ABS wheel speed sensor. Refer to VEHICLE DYNAMICS CONTROL (VDC)>FRONT ABS WHEEL SPEED SENSOR>INSTALLATION .
  25. Install the brake hose bracket.

    Tightening torque: 

    33 N.m (3.4kgf-m, 24.3 ft-lb)

    • Impreza model
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • Crosstrek model
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  26. Install the front exhaust pipe. Refer to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FRONT EXHAUST PIPE>INSTALLATION .
  27. Install the crossmember support - rear.

    Tightening torque: 

    70 N.m (7.1 kgf-m, 51.6 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  28. Refill differential gear oil to adjust the differential gear oil amount. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>DIFFERENTIAL GEAR OIL>ADJUSTMENT .
  29. Install the front tires. Refer to WHEEL AND TIRE SYSTEM>TIRE AND WHEEL>INSTALLATION .
  30. Using the ST, tighten the self-locking nut of the front suspension strut mount. (Impreza model)
    CAUTION:
    • Use a new self-lock nut.
    • Tighten in the state where the wheels are in full contact with the ground.

    Preparation tool: 

    ST: STRUT MOUNT SOCKET (20399AG000)

    Tightening torque: 

    55 N.m (5.6 kgf-m, 40.6 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  31. Inspect the wheel alignment and adjust if necessary. (Impreza model) Refer to FRONT SUSPENSION>WHEEL ALIGNMENT>ADJUSTMENT .
