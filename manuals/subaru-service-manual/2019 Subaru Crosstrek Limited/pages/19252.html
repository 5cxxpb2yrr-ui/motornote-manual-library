Cold Air Does Not Come Out Even When The A/C Switch Is Pressed. The Glass Cannot Be Defogged. (Compressor Does Not Operate (Electric))

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Trouble symptom: 

Compressor does not operate after turning the A/C switch to ON and fan dial between LO - HI.

Trouble causes: 

  • Compressor failure
  • Outside temperature -12°C (10°F) or less.
  • Power supply permission OFF from Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly.
  • Heat pump system failure.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK A/C CONTROL PANEL. 
    1. Leave the vehicle under the condition at ambient temperature of 15°C (59°F) or more.
    2. Turn to READY ON.
    3. Turn the fan dial to the maximum position.
    4. Turn the A/C switch to ON.

    Is A/C indicator displayed on the A/C control panel?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Replace the A/C control panel. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>CONTROL PANEL .

  2. CHECK DTC. 
    1. Turn the temperature control dial at MAX COOL and leave for 16 seconds or more.
    2. Read the following DTCs using Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) . Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .
      • Air Conditioner
      • P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control

    Are any DTCs displayed? (Current malfunction)

    Yes:  Perform the diagnosis for the displayed DTCs. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) . Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    No:  Go to  3.

  3. CHECK ELECTRIC COMPRESSOR TARGET REVOLUTION SPEED. 

    Using the Subaru Select Monitor, check the following items in the [Data monitor] of [Air Conditioner]. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DATA MONITOR .

    • Compressor target revolution speed

    Does the display indicate the value greater than zero?

    Yes:  Go to  7.

    No:  Go to  4.

  4. CHECK EVAPORATOR SENSOR. 

    Using the Subaru Select Monitor, check the following items in the [Data monitor] of [Air Conditioner]. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DATA MONITOR .

    • Evaporator Temperature

    Does the display indicate -0.5°C (31°F) or more?

    Yes:  Go to  6.

    No:  Go to  5.

  5. CHECK EVAPORATOR SENSOR. 

    Check the evaporator sensor. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>EVAPORATOR SENSOR .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  6.

    No:  Replace the evaporator sensor. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>EVAPORATOR SENSOR .

  6. CHECK AMOUNT OF REFRIGERANT PRESSURE. 

    Check the refrigerant pressure. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>REFRIGERANT PRESSURE WITH MANIFOLD GAUGE SET .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  7.

    No:  Perform repair according to refrigerant pressure inspection.

  7. CHECK HEAT PUMP control module. 
    1. Disconnect the heat pump control module connector.
    2. Using a tester, measure the voltage between the heat pump control module connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (i365) No. 23 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    Is the voltage 11 - 14 V or more?

    Yes:  Go to  8.

    No:  Replace the heat pump control module. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>CONTROL UNIT .

  8. PERFORM UNIT INSPECTION OF HEAT PUMP SYSTEM PARTS. 

    Perform unit inspection of the following parts.

    • Low pressure solenoid valve Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>SOLENOID VALVE>INSPECTION .
    • High pressure solenoid valve Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>SOLENOID VALVE>INSPECTION .
    • Heating expansion valve Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>EXPANSION VALVE>INSPECTION .
    • Cooling expansion valve Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>EXPANSION VALVE>INSPECTION .
    • Interior condenser outlet temperature sensor Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>PIPE TEMPERATURE SENSOR>INSPECTION .
    • Exterior condenser outlet temperature sensor Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>PIPE TEMPERATURE SENSOR>INSPECTION .
    • Discharge temperature sensor Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>PIPE TEMPERATURE SENSOR>INSPECTION .
    • Evaporator outlet temperature sensor Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>PIPE TEMPERATURE SENSOR>INSPECTION .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  The circuit has returned to a normal condition at this time. Reproduce the failure, and then perform the diagnosis again.

    NOTE: In this case, temporary poor contact of connector, temporary open or short circuit of harness may be the cause.

    No:  Repair it according to unit inspection of parts.
