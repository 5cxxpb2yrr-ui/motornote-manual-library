Section 4 (Hybrid Electric Vehicle System): General Description: Caution

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  • When performing service operation, refer to "Repair Contents" in "General Description". Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS .
  • Before disconnecting connectors of sensors or units, be sure to disconnect the ground terminal from battery. When replacing the audio and MFD parts provided with memory functions, record the memory contents before disconnecting the battery ground terminal.
  • Be careful of the following items. Failing to do so may cause the airbag system malfunction.
    • Yellow connectors and harnesses with yellow tapes around them are the connectors and harnesses for the airbag system. When using a tester on these circuits, follow the cautions of "AIRBAG SYSTEM". Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
    • Be careful not to damage the airbag system wiring harness when servicing the electrical parts around the steering column.
