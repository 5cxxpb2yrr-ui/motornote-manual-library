Active Grille Shutter: Removal

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

NOTE: Active grille shutter is installed on only some models.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the front bumper. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>FRONT BUMPER>REMOVAL .
  3. Disconnect the connector from ambient sensor.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Disconnect the connector from the active grille shutter actuator.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the active grille shutter.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Remove the clip (A) to remove the ambient sensor.
Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
