Explanation For Circuits

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Motor generator control ECU detects the HV coolant temperature with the coolant temperature sensor and the reactor temperature with the reactor temperature sensor, which are built in Inverter with converter assembly, and also checks cooling efficiency of the inverter cooling system and controls to prevent overheat of the inverter and reactor. Motor generator control ECU detects sensor malfunction based on the deviation between HV coolant temperature sensor value and reactor temperature sensor value.
