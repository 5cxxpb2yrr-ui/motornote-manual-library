Telematics Sub Antenna

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery and wait for at least 60 seconds before starting work. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the grille assembly - CTR ventilation. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>AIR VENT GRILLE>REMOVAL>CENTER GRILLE ASSEMBLY .
  3. Remove the audio assembly or navigation assembly. Refer to ENTERTAINMENT & MONITORING>AUDIO>REMOVAL .
  4. Remove the cover assembly - instrument panel LWR driver. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>INSTRUMENT PANEL LOWER COVER>REMOVAL .
  5. Remove the combination meter assembly. Refer to INSTRUMENTATION/DRIVER INFO>COMBINATION METER>REMOVAL .
  6. Remove the GPS antenna assembly. Refer to ENTERTAINMENT & MONITORING>ANTENNA>REMOVAL>GPS ANTENNA .
    NOTE: Remove the antenna only. Do not pull the cord.
  7. Remove the telematics sub antenna.
    1. Attach a string to the telematics sub antenna connector and the GPS antenna assembly connector on the main body side.
      NOTE:
      • A string makes operation easier during installation.
      • The telematics sub antenna is integrated into the GPS antenna assembly cord.
    2. Remove the bolts, cord clips and clamps, and pull out the telematics sub antenna from the combination meter side.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. After the telematics sub antenna has been pulled out, remove the string attached to the connector in step (1).
    NOTE: Leave the attached string inside the instrument panel, and use it during installation.
