Discharge Temperature Sensor

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Install the discharge temperature sensor together with the pipe - condenser. When installing the discharge temperature sensor, refer to "Interior condenser". Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>CONDENSER>INSTALLATION>INTERIOR CONDENSER .
