Stabilizer Bushing

  1. Lift up the vehicle.
  2. Remove the under cover - front.

    Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>FRONT UNDER COVER>REMOVAL .

  3. Remove the crossmember support - rear.

    Refer to FRONT SUSPENSION>CROSSMEMBER SUPPORT>REMOVAL .

  4. Remove the front exhaust pipe.

    Refer to EXHAUST (H4DO) EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FRONT EXHAUST PIPE>REMOVAL .

  5. Remove the clamp - stabilizer bushing on the left and right sides.
