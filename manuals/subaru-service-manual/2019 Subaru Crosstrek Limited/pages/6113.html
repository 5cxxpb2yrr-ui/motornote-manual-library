Diagnostic Method

When communication with the hybrid vehicle control ECM is normal or the vehicle speed is 6.22 MPH or more, and the IG1 terminal voltage is 9.5 V or more, the IG2 switch is OFF.

JUDGMENT VALUE SPECIFICATION

Malfunction Criteria Threshold Value
IG2 OFF

Time needed for diagnosis:  4 s

Malfunction indicator light illumination:  Illuminates as soon as a malfunction occurs.
