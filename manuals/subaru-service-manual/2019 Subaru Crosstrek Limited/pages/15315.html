Illumination Does Not Come On Or Cannot Dim

Trouble symptom: 

Even when the lighting switch is operated, the illumination does not come on. Even when the illumination control is operated, the illumination does not dim.

Trouble symptom: 

Open circuit in illumination circuit

Wiring diagram: 

Clearance light and illumination light system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (IMPREZA MODEL)>CLEARANCE LIGHT AND ILLUMINATION LIGHT SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>CLEARANCE LIGHT AND ILLUMINATION LIGHT SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK ILLUMINATION. 

    Make sure that all illuminations except A/C come on.

    Do other illuminations come on?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Check the illumination circuit. Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>CLEARANCE LIGHT AND ILLUMINATION LIGHT SYSTEM>INSPECTION .

  2. CHECK CONNECTOR. 

    Check for poor contact of connector.

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Repair the connector.

  3. CHECK HARNESS (OPEN CIRCUIT). 
    1. Turn the lighting switch to ON.
    2. Using a tester, measure the voltage between the A/C control panel connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (i88) No. 39 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    Is the voltage 10 V or more?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Repair or replace the open circuit of harness.

  4. CHECK HARNESS (OPEN CIRCUIT). 
    1. Turn the lighting switch to OFF.
    2. Measure the resistance between A/C control panel connector and combination meter connector.

      Connector & terminal 

      (i88) No. 29 - (i10) No. 34:

    Is the resistance less than 10 Ω?

    Yes:  Replace the A/C control panel. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>CONTROL PANEL .

    No:  Repair or replace the open circuit of harness.
