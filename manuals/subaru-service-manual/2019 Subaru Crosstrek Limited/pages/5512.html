Parking Brake Cable: Removal

CAUTION: Airbag system satellite safing sensor is located in the lower of the rear seat cushion center. Before removing the rear seat, refer to "CAUTION" of "General Description" in "AIRBAG SYSTEM" information. Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY>GROUND TERMINAL REMOVAL OR INSTALLATION .
    NOTE:
    • For CVT models, release the shift lock and shift the select lever to the "N" position. Refer to CONTROL SYSTEMS>SELECT LEVER>REMOVAL .
    • On power seat models, move the front seat all the way forward before disconnecting the battery ground terminal.
  2. Remove the console box assembly. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>CONSOLE BOX>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the lever assembly - hand brake. Refer to PARKING BRAKE>PARKING BRAKE LEVER>REMOVAL .
  4. Remove the nut and remove the clamp A - hand brake cable (a).
  5. Remove the inner cable end (c) from the equalizer (b).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Remove the rear seat cushion assembly. Refer to SEATS>REAR SEAT>REMOVAL .
  7. Release the clips and claws, and remove the side sill covers - rear INN on the left and right sides.
    CAUTION: Do not pull with excessive force. Doing so may damage the claws of the side sill cover - rear INN.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Remove the clips and knobs of the hook - seat cushion rear.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Turn over the floor carpet and remove the grommet of the cable assembly - parking brake from the rear floor.
  10. Lift up the vehicle, and then remove the rear wheels.
  11. Remove the fuel tank protector. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FUEL TANK PROTECTOR>REMOVAL .
  12. Remove the rear exhaust pipe and muffler.
    • Rear exhaust pipe: Refer to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>REAR EXHAUST PIPE>REMOVAL .
    • Muffler: Refer to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>MUFFLER>REMOVAL .
  13. Separate the propeller shaft assembly from the rear differential.
    1. Place alignment marks (a) on the flange yoke and rear differential.
    2. Remove the four nuts holding the propeller shaft to the rear differential, and remove the four bolts.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Support the propeller shaft assembly using a transmission jack.
  14. Remove the cable assembly - parking brake from the lever section of parking brake shoe. Refer to PARKING BRAKE>PARKING BRAKE ASSEMBLY (REAR DISC BRAKE)>REMOVAL .
  15. Remove the cable assembly - parking brake.
Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
