Universal Joint Assembly - Steering

If steering wheel play is excessive, check the universal joint assembly - steering of steering shaft for looseness. If the universal joint assembly - steering is loose, tighten the mounting bolts to the specified torque.

Tightening torque: 

24 N.m (2.4 kgf-m, 17.4 ft-lb)

If the play is still excessive even after tightening the mounting bolts to the specified torque, detach the universal joint assembly - steering and then check the play where the joints cross and yawing torque. Also, check the seal for damage or serrations for wear. Refer to POWER ASSISTED SYSTEM (POWER STEERING)>UNIVERSAL JOINT .
