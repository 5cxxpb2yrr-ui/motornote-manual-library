Front Door

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery and wait for at least 60 seconds before starting work. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the trim panel - front door.
    1. Attach the protective tape (a).
    2. Remove the cover of the inner handle, and remove the screw.
    3. Open the hand cup cover and remove the screw.
    4. Remove the clips, and remove the cover sash - inner front.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    5. Remove the clips, and remove the trim panel - front door from the panel assembly - front door.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
      NOTE: It is easier to remove the door trim if the removal is performed from the gray arrow section.
    6. Remove the cable from the remote assembly - door.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    7. Disconnect the harness connector and remove the trim panel - front door.
  3. Remove each part as required.
    • Remote assembly - door: Refer to SECURITY AND LOCKS>DOOR INNER REMOTE>REMOVAL>FRONT .
    • Switch - power window main: Refer to GLASS/WINDOWS/MIRRORS>POWER WINDOW CONTROL SWITCH>REMOVAL>MAIN SWITCH .
    • Weather strip - door INN: Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>DOOR TRIM>DISASSEMBLY .
