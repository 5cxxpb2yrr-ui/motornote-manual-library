Malfunction Description

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

At starting normal charging, Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly checks the charger gate permission signal (CHEN) from Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly to Electric vehicle charger assembly, and the answer-back signal from Electric vehicle charger assembly.

The charger permission signal ON stuck indicates a condition that, even though the charger gate permission signal was not output from Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly, the answer-back signal from Electric vehicle charger assembly was detected. In this state, operation of the charger gate permission signal cannot be guaranteed.

The following cases are assumed to be malfunction causes:

Internal malfunction of Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly

  • Malfunction of Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly

Malfunction of Wire harness system between Electric vehicle charger assembly - Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly

  • Improperly mated connector
  • Foreign matter caught, water intrusion
  • Open or short circuit

Internal malfunction of Electric vehicle charger assembly

  • Malfunction of Electric vehicle charger assembly

Communication failure

  • Communication failure between Electric vehicle charger assembly - Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly
