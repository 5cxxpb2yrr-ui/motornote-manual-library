CAN Communication Circuit Check: List

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  • MAIN-CAN
    Resistance value between CAN Hi and Lo Contents of inspection REMARKS
    Ground short inspection Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>CAN COMMUNICATION CIRCUIT CHECK>INSPECTION>GROUND SHORT INSPECTION (MAIN-CAN) . Shorted to ground in the communication circuit or control module.
    Check power supply short Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>CAN COMMUNICATION CIRCUIT CHECK>INSPECTION>POWER SHORT INSPECTION (MAIN-CAN) . Shorted to battery power supply in the communication circuit or control module.
    More than 52 Ω and less than 62 Ω Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>CAN COMMUNICATION CIRCUIT CHECK>INSPECTION>MORE THAN 52 Ω AND LESS THAN 62 Ω (MAIN-CAN) . Combined resistance of end resistance does not have malfunction; short to ground or +B short of the CAN communication circuit is possible, however.
    52 Ω or less Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>CAN COMMUNICATION CIRCUIT CHECK>INSPECTION>52 Ω OR LESS (MAIN-CAN) . Resistance is lower than combined resistance of end resistance. Short to CAN Hi and CAN Lo on the CAN communication circuit is possible.
    62 Ω or more Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>CAN COMMUNICATION CIRCUIT CHECK>INSPECTION>62 Ω OR MORE (MAIN-CAN) . Resistance is higher than combined resistance of end resistance. Open circuit of CAN communication circuit is possible.
    Related lines, more than 52 Ω and less than 62 Ω (AWD) Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>CAN COMMUNICATION CIRCUIT CHECK>INSPECTION>RELATED LINES, MORE THAN 52 Ω AND LESS THAN 62 Ω (MAIN-CAN_AWD) . No AWD control module data is received. Perform inspection when faulty is detected.
