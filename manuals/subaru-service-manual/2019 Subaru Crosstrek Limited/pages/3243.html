DTC U1146: Lost Communication With Gateway "A" BODY-CAN

Detected when CAN data from central gateway control module does not arrive.

NOTE: Perform the diagnosis for LAN system. Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .
