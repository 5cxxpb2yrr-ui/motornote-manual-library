Diagnostic Method

If the duration of time while the following conditions are met is longer than the time indicated, judge as NG.

JUDGMENT VALUE SPECIFICATION

Malfunction Criteria Threshold Value
|Engine coolant temperature at engine start - Intake air temperature 21 sec. after engine start|
|Engine coolant temperature at engine start - Ambient air temperature at engine start|		 More than value of Map
More than 25°C (45°F)
TEMPERATURE SPECIFICATION - MAP

Ambient temperature (°C (°F)) -30 (-22) 30 (86) 45 (113) 60 (140)
|Engine coolant temperature at engine start - Intake air temperature 21 sec. after engine start | (°C (°F)) 12 (21.6) 12 (21.6) 22 (39.6) 22 (39.6)

Time needed for diagnosis:  Less than 1 s

Malfunction indicator light illumination:  Illuminates when malfunction occurs in 2 continuous driving cycles.
