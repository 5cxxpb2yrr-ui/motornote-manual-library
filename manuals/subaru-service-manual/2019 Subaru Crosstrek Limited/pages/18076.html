Converter Case: Disassembly

  1. Remove the pitching stopper bracket.
  2. Remove the filler plug, oil drain plug and overflow drain plug. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>DIFFERENTIAL GEAR OIL>REPLACEMENT .
  3. Remove all plugs.
