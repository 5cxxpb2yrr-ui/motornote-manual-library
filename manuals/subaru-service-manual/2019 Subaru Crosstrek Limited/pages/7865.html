Cam Carrier RH

  1. Remove the engine from the vehicle.

    Except for hybrid model: 

    Refer to MECHANICAL (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL>EXCEPT FOR HYBRID MODEL .

    Hybrid model: 

    Refer to MECHANICAL (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL>HYBRID MODEL .

  2. Remove the timing chain RH. Refer to MECHANICAL (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>TIMING CHAIN ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL>TIMING CHAIN RH .
  3. Remove the rocker cover RH. Refer to MECHANICAL (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ROCKER COVER>REMOVAL>ROCKER COVER RH .
  4. Remove the water pipe RH. (Except for hybrid model) Refer to COOLING (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>WATER PIPE>REMOVAL>WATER PIPE RH .
  5. Remove the clip holding the engine wiring harness from cam carrier RH.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. When disassembling the cam carrier RH
    NOTE: When disassembling the cam carrier RH, perform the following steps also.
    1. Remove the oil pipe. (Except for hybrid model) Refer to LUBRICATION (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>OIL PIPE>REMOVAL .
    2. Remove the vacuum pump. (Except for hybrid model) Refer to MECHANICAL (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>VACUUM PUMP>REMOVAL .
    3. Remove the plate. (Hybrid model)
      1. Remove the plate from the cam carrier RH.
        Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
      2. Remove the liquid gasket from the cam carrier RH plate fitting surface and the plate.
    4. Remove the cam sprocket RH. Refer to MECHANICAL (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>CAM SPROCKET>REMOVAL>CAM SPROCKET RH .
  7. Set the part so that the cam carrier RH is on the upper side.
  8. Loosen the bolts holding the cam carrier RH equally, a little at a time in numerical sequence as shown in the figure and remove the cam carrier RH.
    • Except for hybrid model
      Fig 1: Cam Carrier RH Bolts Loosening Sequence (Except For Hybrid Model)
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • Hybrid model
    Fig 2: Cam Carrier RH Bolts Loosening Sequence (Hybrid Model)
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Remove the O-ring and the roller rocker arm from cylinder head RH.
    NOTE: Be careful not to confuse the roller rocker arms.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  10. Remove the valve shim and the roller rocker arm pivot from cylinder head RH.
    NOTE: Be careful not to confuse the valve shim and the roller rocker arm pivot.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  11. Remove the liquid gasket from cam carrier RH and cylinder head RH.
