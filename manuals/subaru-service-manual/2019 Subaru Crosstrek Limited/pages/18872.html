Clutch Pedal: Installation

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Install the clutch pedal assembly.

    Tightening torque: 

    18 N.m (1.8 kgf-m, 13.3 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Install the snap pin and clevis pin connecting the operating rod and brake pedal.
    CAUTION: Always use a new clevis pin.
    NOTE: Apply grease to the clevis pin.
  3. Connect the connector to the stop light switch.
  4. Connect the connector to the clutch switch and clutch start switch.
  5. Install the duct - foot driver. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>HEATER DUCT>INSTALLATION>FOOT>LH SIDE .
  6. Install the steering column. Refer to POWER ASSISTED SYSTEM (POWER STEERING)>STEERING COLUMN>INSTALLATION .
  7. Connect the tank hose.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Temporarily install the two-way connector and the clutch connector bracket, and engage the clutch pipe.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Install the two-way connector and the clutch connector bracket.

    Tightening torque: 

    7.5 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.5 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  10. Tighten the clutch pipe.

    Tightening torque: 

    15 N.m (1.5 kgf-m, 11.1 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  11. Install the clutch pipe.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  12. Install the vacuum pipe & hose assembly.

    Tightening torque: 

    7.5 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.5 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  13. Add the recommended brake fluid. Refer to CLUTCH SYSTEM>CLUTCH FLUID .
  14. After bleeding air from the clutch system, ensure that no fluid leaks and the clutch operates properly. Refer to CLUTCH SYSTEM>CLUTCH FLUID AIR BLEEDING>PROCEDURE .
  15. Adjust the clutch pedal. Refer to CLUTCH SYSTEM>CLUTCH PEDAL>ADJUSTMENT .
  16. Install the instrument panel lower cover. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>INSTRUMENT PANEL LOWER COVER>INSTALLATION .
  17. Install the air intake boot. Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>Air Intake Boot>INSTALLATION .
  18. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  19. Check the clutch start system.
