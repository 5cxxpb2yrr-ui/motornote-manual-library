Sunroof Assembly: Sunshade: Removal

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the trim panel - roof assembly.
    • 4 door model: Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>ROOF TRIM>REMOVAL>4 DOOR MODEL .
    • 5 door model: Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>ROOF TRIM>REMOVAL>5 DOOR MODEL .
  3. Remove the sunshade assembly.
CAUTION: Be careful not to damage the sunshade assembly.
  1. Remove the left and right stopper rubbers (a).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Attach protective tape (b) on the handle of sunshade assembly.
  3. Remove the sunshade assembly while pulling the center of the brace downward.
Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
