Differential Case Assembly

  1. Remove the taper roller bearing using ST1, ST2 and ST3.

    Preparation tool: 

    ST1: REMOVER (498077000)

    ST2: REMOVER (899864100)

    ST3: SEAT (398497701)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Using the ST, remove the spring pin.

    Preparation tool: 

    ST: STRAIGHT PIN REMOVER (899904100)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the pinion shaft, and remove the differential bevel pinion, differential bevel gear and washer.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
