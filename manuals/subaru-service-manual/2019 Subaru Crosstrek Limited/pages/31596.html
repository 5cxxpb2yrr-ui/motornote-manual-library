INTRODUCTION

This Service Information bulletin describes a change made to the labels applied to the push to start switches used on the models listed above. There has been NO change made to the operation or functionality of the switches. The label change was made for compliance reasons in the Indonesia, Argentina and China markets.

PART INFORMATION

The part number switches listed below include the new labels. Any changes identified between the OLD and NEW part labels can be disregarded.
