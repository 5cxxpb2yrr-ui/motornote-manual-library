Steering Responsive Headlight (The Initialize Function)

  1. Stop the vehicle at approximately 1 m (3.3 ft) away and perpendicular to the wall.
  2. Make the vehicle vicinity dark.
  3. Set the vehicle headlight switch to Low beam.
  4. Make sure that the headlight cut line moves to left and right immediately after the engine started.
