Diagnostic Method

Pressure-increasing linear solenoid valve malfunction (stuck, etc.)

JUDGMENT VALUE SPECIFICATION

Malfunction Criteria Threshold Value
Increasing Amount of Servo Pressure 0.1 MPa or less

Time needed for diagnosis:  1.1 s

Malfunction indicator light illumination:  Illuminates as soon as a malfunction occurs.
