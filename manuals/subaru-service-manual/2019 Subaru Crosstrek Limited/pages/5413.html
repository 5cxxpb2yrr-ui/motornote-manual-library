Front Disc Brake Assembly: Installation

NOTE: Before installation, remove the pad clip - front brake and remove mud, foreign matter and rust from the caliper body assembly and the support - front disc brake.
  1. Check each part. Refer to BRAKE>FRONT DISC BRAKE ASSEMBLY>INSPECTION .
  2. Apply a thin coat of grease to the support - front disc brake.

    Preparation items: 

    Grease: An item contained in the pad kit, or Molykote M7439 equivalent

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Install the pad clip - front brake
  4. Install the support - front disc brake.
    CAUTION:
    • Use a new mounting bolt. (Impreza model)
    • Use a wobble extension to tighten the upper side mounting bolt with the specified torque. (Except for Impreza model)
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.

    Tightening torque: 

    Mounting bolt: Refer to "COMPONENT" of "General Description" for the tightening torque. Refer to BRAKE>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>COMPONENT>FRONT DISC BRAKE .

  5. Apply a thin coat of grease to the pad - disc brake front.

    Preparation items: 

    Grease: An item contained in the pad kit or equivalent

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Press back the piston - disc brake with a tool as needed.
    CAUTION: When pressing back the piston - disc brake, the clearance between pad - disc brake and brake disc becomes large, which makes the brake pedal effort softer. After the installation of front wheel, be sure to perform the adjustment of the clearance and the pedal effort by depressing the brake pedal several times.

    Preparation tool: 

    Disc brake piston tool

  7. Install the pad - disc brake front.
    CAUTION: When installing, make sure that the pad wear indicator comes on the lower side.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Install the caliper body assembly.

    Tightening torque: 

    27 N.m (2.8 kgf-m, 19.9 ft-lb)

  9. Connect the brake hose using a new brake hose gasket.

    Tightening torque: 

    26 N.m (2.7 kgf-m, 19.2 ft-lb)

  10. Connect the battery ground terminal. (Hybrid model) Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY>GROUND TERMINAL REMOVAL OR INSTALLATION .
  11. Bleed air from the brake line.
    • Except for hybrid model: Refer to BRAKE>AIR BLEEDING>PROCEDURE>BRAKE LINE (EXCEPT FOR HYBRID MODEL) .
    • Hybrid model: Refer to BRAKE>AIR BLEEDING>PROCEDURE>BRAKE LINE (HYBRID MODEL) .
  12. Install the front wheels.

    Tightening torque: 

    120 N.m (12.2 kgf-m, 88.5 ft-lb)
