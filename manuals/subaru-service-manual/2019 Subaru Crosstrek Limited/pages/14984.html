Pre-Delivery Inspection (PDI) Procedure: Battery

  • IMPREZA model

    Check the battery terminals to make sure that there are no rust or corrosions due to electrolyte leaks.

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  • CROSSTREK model

Check the battery terminals to make sure that there are no rust or corrosion due to fluid leaks. Check that the battery caps are securely tightened. (Including battery (12 V))

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
