Turbine Speed Sensor: Installation

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

CAUTION: Be sure to prevent water or oil from contacting the connector terminal of turbine speed sensor. If adhesion occurs, replace with a new part.
  1. Install the turbine speed sensor.
    NOTE:
    • Use new O-rings.
    • Apply CVTF to the O-rings.

    Tightening torque: 

    7 N.m (0.7 kgf-m, 5.2 ft-lb)

  2. Connect the harness connector.
  3. Lower the vehicle.
  4. Connect the ground terminal to battery sensor. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
