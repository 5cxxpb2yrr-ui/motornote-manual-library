Outline Of Diagnosis

When the volume of brake fluid within the accumulator is below a certain amount, the electric brake booster control module operates the pump motor. When the pump motor is being operated and the output value of the accumulator pressure sensor does not change substantially for a certain period of time, the electric brake booster control module judges that the accumulator pressure sensor has a stuck malfunction and illuminates the MIL and stores this DTC.
