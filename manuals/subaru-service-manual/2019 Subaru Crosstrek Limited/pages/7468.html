Filler Neck

  1. Install the filler neck with new O-rings.

    Tightening torque: 

    7 N.m (0.7 kgf-m, 5.2 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Install the water tank pipe cap, and connect the over flow hose (A).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Install the air intake duct. Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>AIR INTAKE DUCT>INSTALLATION .
  4. Fill engine coolant. Refer to COOLING (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE COOLANT>REPLACEMENT>FILLING OF ENGINE COOLANT .
