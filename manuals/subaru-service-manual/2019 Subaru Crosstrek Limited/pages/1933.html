Pre-Collision Brake Off & Lane Departure Warning Off Switch

  1. Install the pre-collision brake OFF & lane departure warning OFF switch.
    NOTE: Align the positioning pin of connector clip to the positioning hole of stereo camera cover assembly.
  2. Install the stereo camera cover assembly. Ref. to EYESIGHT>STEREO CAMERA>INSTALLATION .
  3. Connect the battery ground terminal. Ref. to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
