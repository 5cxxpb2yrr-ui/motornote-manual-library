Seat Heater Switch

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery and wait for at least 60 seconds before starting work. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    NOTE:
    • For CVT models, release the shift lock and shift the select lever to the "N" position. Refer to CONTROL SYSTEMS>SELECT LEVER>REMOVAL .
    • For models with power seat, remove the front seat before removing the battery ground terminal.
  2. Remove the cover assembly - front. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>CONSOLE BOX>REMOVAL .
  3. Release the claws, and remove the seat heater switch from the back of the cover assembly - front.
