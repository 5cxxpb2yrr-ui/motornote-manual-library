Transmission Rear Crossmember And Rear Cushion Rubber

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the front exhaust pipe.

    Refer to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FRONT EXHAUST PIPE>REMOVAL .

  3. Remove the center exhaust cover.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Set the transmission jack under the transmission.
    CAUTION: Make sure that the support plates of transmission jack do not touch the oil pan.
    NOTE: Place wooden block or similar items at the transmission rear to adjust the height.
  5. Remove the clip which holds the inhibitor harness to the transmission rear crossmember.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Remove the transmission rear crossmember.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Remove the rear cushion rubber.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
