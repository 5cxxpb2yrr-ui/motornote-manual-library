INTRODUCTION

This Service Information Bulletin outlines design changes made to the coolant hoses for the CVT fluid cooler and the spring-type clamps used to secure them.

PRODUCTION CHANGE INFORMATION

The new coolant hoses and clamps were incorporated into production per the starting VIN table below:

NOTE:
  • The new coolant hoses appear the same as the originals except for the outer diameter which is 1mm smaller (the inner diameter remains unchanged).
  • The new clamps are now black and silver (instead of all silver) as shown in the illustration. They also have a 2.1 mm smaller inside diameter to work with the new hoses.

VERY IMPORTANT:  There is NO interchangeability between the old and new-style parts. NEVER use an old-style clamp on a new-style hose and/or vice-versa.
