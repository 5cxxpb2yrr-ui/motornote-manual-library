Parking Brake

  • Except for hybrid model

    Check that the parking brake operates normally. When pulling the parking brake lever with a force of 200 N (20 kgf, 45 lb), check that the lever stroke of parking brake lever is 7 to 8 notches.

  • Hybrid model

Check the parking brake operates properly by depressing brake pedal and operating the electronic parking brake switch.
