Discharging The Battery By Immersing It In Salt Water

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
WARNING: Be sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles.
  1. Prepare the HV battery.
    CAUTION:
    • This work must be performed by 2 or more persons.
    • Be sure to install in a stable location so that the salt water immersion vessel does not move during work.
    • Perform the discharge work in a well-ventilated location.
    • Perform the discharge work in a location where rain does not contact.
    1. Prepare the guide wire, cable, mini crane, and salt water immersion vessel. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>PREPARATION TOOL>GENERAL TOOL .
    2. Pass the cables through the frame battery - UPR and frame battery - MDL.
      1. Insert the guide wires into the frame, and press them in until they emerge from the frame on the opposite side.
      2. Pass the cables through the emerged guide wires, and again press the guide wires in until they emerge from the frame on the opposite side.
      3. Pull the 2 guide wires and pass the cables through the frame.
        Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
      4. Secure the cables to the crane hook.
        Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
      5. Use the mini crane and place the HV battery into the salt water immersion vessel.
        Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
      6. Remove the guide wires and cables.
  2. Prepare for discharge (add water).
    1. Use a hose or other means and add water until the HV battery is fully immersed.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    NOTE: Amount of water too add: Approx. 200 L
  3. Prepare the salt.
    1. Fill a bucket approximately half full with water.
    2. Add approximately 2.1 kg of salt to the water and mix well.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Add the salt water.
    1. Add the salt water into the salt water immersion vessel.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Discharge
    1. Leave for 24 hours or more in order to fully discharge the battery.
    WARNING:
    • Never place a lid on the vessel.
    • Be sure to leave immersed for 24 hours or more.
    • During the discharge work, place a "Discharge Work in Progress" sign in the area and take other steps to warn persons in the surrounding area.
  6. Check that discharge was completed.
    1. Check that no bubbles are being produced in the salt water immersion vessel.
      CAUTION: If bubbles are being produced, discharge may be continuing. Therefore absolutely do not place a lid on the vessel.
    2. Place a lid on the salt water immersion vessel.
    CAUTION: Do not drain out the water which was added to the salt water immersion vessel.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
