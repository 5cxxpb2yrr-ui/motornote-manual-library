Clutch Pedal: Assembly

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Attach the brake pedal pad, brake pedal stopper, spacer and pedal bushing.
    CAUTION: Before installing the pedal bushing and spacer, clean the sliding surface and apply grease.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Install the brake pedal.

    Tightening torque: 

    30 N.m (3.1 kgf-m, 22.1 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Install the clutch pedal pad, clutch pedal stopper, bushing A, spacer and pedal bushing.
    CAUTION: Before installing the pedal bushing and spacer, clean the sliding surface and apply grease.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Install the clutch pedal.

    Tightening torque: 

    30 N.m (3.1 kgf-m, 22.1 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Install the assist bushing B, assist rod B, assist spring, assist bushing A, clip, assist rod A, bushing A and assist pin.
    NOTE: Apply grease to the assist bushing B, assist rod B, assist spring seating surface, assist bushing A, assist rod A, and bushing A.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Install the master cylinder.
    NOTE: Secure the push rod with a tape etc. to prevent it from falling off.

    Tightening torque: 

    18 N.m (1.8 kgf-m, 13.3 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Secure the clevis pin with mounting clip.
    CAUTION: Always use a new clevis pin.
    NOTE: Before installing the clevis pin, apply grease.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Install the clutch switch.
  9. Install the clutch pipe to the clamp.
  10. Install the clutch damper, and install the clutch pipe to the master cylinder.

    Tightening torque: 

    T1: 15 N.m (1.5 kgf-m, 11.1 ft-lb)

    T2: 18 N.m (1.8 kgf-m, 13.3 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
