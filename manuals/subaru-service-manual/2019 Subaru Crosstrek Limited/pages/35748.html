Diagnostic Method

When the pump motor is operating, the accumulator pressure sensor signal does not change.

JUDGMENT VALUE SPECIFICATION

Malfunction Criteria Threshold Value
Change of Accumulator Pressure Less than 0.12 MPa
Brake Booster Motor Intrusively Operated

Time needed for diagnosis:  7 or 14s (Time depends on battery voltage)

Battery Voltage Time Required (sec)
13 V or more 7
Less than 13 V 14

Malfunction indicator light illumination:  Illuminates as soon as a malfunction occurs.
