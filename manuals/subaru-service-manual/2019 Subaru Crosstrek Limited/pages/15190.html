Pressure Switch (Triple Pressure Switch): Installation

CAUTION:
  • Do not start the engine before charging refrigerant.
  • If the engine is started with no refrigerant charge, replace the compressor assembly.
  • Apply compressor oil to O-rings of the switch and install it completely.
  1. Install the pressure switch.

    Tightening torque: 

    10.8 N.m (1.1 kgf-m, 8 ft-lb)

  2. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  3. Charge refrigerant. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>REFRIGERANT CHARGING PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .
