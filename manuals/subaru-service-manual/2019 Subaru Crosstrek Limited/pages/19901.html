Explanation For Circuits

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

For the circuit explanation, refer to DTC P0CA300. Refer to P-HEV DRIVE MOTOR (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE WITH DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>DTC P0CA300 DC/DC CONVERTER STEP UP VOLTAGE PERFORMANCE .

Motor generator control ECU detects the high voltage before boosting (VL) with the voltage sensor built in the boosting converter. Also, the battery voltage (VB) is detected by Battery ECU assembly.
