Explanation For Circuits

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

The rotation speed of Battery cooling blower assembly is controlled by Battery ECU assembly. The power of Battery cooling blower assembly is supplied by turning IGCT scene relay ON via MREL terminal of Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly. Battery ECU assembly adjusts the command signals (SI0 and SI1) to Battery cooling blower assembly so that the fan speed is matched with HV battery temperature. In addition, the frequency (FP0 and FP1) of Battery cooling blower assembly is sent as a monitoring signal to Battery ECU assembly.
