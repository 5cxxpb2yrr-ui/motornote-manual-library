Rear Differential Front Member: Removal

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery.

    Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .

  2. Lift up the vehicle.
  3. Set the transmission jack.
  4. Remove the rear differential front member.
