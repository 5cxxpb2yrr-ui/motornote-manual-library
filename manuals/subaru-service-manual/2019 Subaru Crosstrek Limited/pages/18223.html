Sealing Cover: Installation: Notes

CAUTION:
  • If the sealing cover gets broken, replace it with a new part.
  • Attach the sealing cover, keeping it from becoming wrinkled.
  • If the sealing cover position is not aligned, it may cause improper door trim installation or water leakage.
  • Apply a butyl tape evenly.
  • Breaks in the bead will allow water leakage and contamination.
