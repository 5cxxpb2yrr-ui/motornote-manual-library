Battery Diagnosis Discharge the HV battery and perform diagnosis of how much HV battery cells are degraded with reference to HV battery capacity.
NOTE: Refer to [Battery Diagnosis], outside the table, for more details. Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>ACTIVE TEST>LIST>BATTERY DIAGNOSIS .
  • Do not perform battery diagnosis while the Hybrid Fail Lamp is illuminated.
  • Perform battery diagnosis with the HV battery installed correctly.
  • Do not perform battery diagnosis while changing from inspection mode to another mode.
  • Make sure to turn the power switch off after battery diagnosis to prevent the Battery (12V) from being discharged.
  • If the SOC (state of charge) learning has not been performed(1), perform battery diagnosis after plug-in charging the vehicle as specified.

    (1) Refer to "Battery diagnosis - Measure during interruption".
