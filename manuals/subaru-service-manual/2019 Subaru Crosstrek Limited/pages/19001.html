Malfunction description

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly detects a malfunction that the emergency shutdown line (HSDN) for the inverter cannot shut down. While detection is performed at starting with IG ON and at READY OFF, this DTC is confirmed when the following portion is faulty. (When a faulty portion cannot be determined, DTC P321E9F is output.)

  • Malfunction of Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly
