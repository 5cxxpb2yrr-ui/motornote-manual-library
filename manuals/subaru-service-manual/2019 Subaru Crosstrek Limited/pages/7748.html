Oil Pipe: Installation

NOTE: The oil pipe is installed only on non-hybrid models.
  1. Apply engine oil to the threaded portion for mounting the union bolt on the vacuum pump and cam carrier.
  2. Use a new gasket to temporarily attach the oil pipe.
  3. Tighten the union bolts and bolt in the numerical order as shown in the figure.

    Tightening torque: 

    T1: 6.4 N.m (0.7 kgf-m, 4.7 ft-lb)

    T2: 20 N.m (2.0 kgf-m, 14.8 ft-lb)

    Fig 1: Oil Pipe Union Bolts Tightening Sequence
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Install the air intake boot. Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>Air Intake Boot>INSTALLATION .
  5. Check the engine oil level and replenish the engine oil if necessary. Refer to LUBRICATION (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE OIL>INSPECTION .
