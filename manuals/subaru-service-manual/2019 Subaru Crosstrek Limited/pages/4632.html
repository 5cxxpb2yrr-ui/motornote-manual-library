How To Reset The Full Open Stop Position

When the full open stop position becomes misaligned, it can be reset by performing the following procedure.

  1. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
  2. Operate and hold the switch - power window in "AUTO DOWN" direction (fully push down the switch) for three seconds or more.
    NOTE: For the driver's side window, this can be done with the switch - power window main, and for the passenger's side window the switch - power window main or the passenger's side switch - power window can be used.
  3. Check that the glass assembly - front door starts rolling down and fully opens.
  4. Operate the switch - power window in "AUTO UP" direction to check whether the window fully closes properly.
