DTC P0345: Camshaft Position Sensor "A" Circuit Bank 2: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

1. - 

DTC detecting condition: 

Immediately at fault recognition

Trouble symptom: 

  • Engine stall
  • Failure of engine to start

Wiring diagram: 

Engine Electrical System Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>ENGINE ELECTRICAL SYSTEM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: Use the check board when measuring the ECM terminal voltage and resistance. Refer to ENGINE (DIAGNOSTICS) (H4DO (HEV))>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>PREPARATION TOOL>HOW TO USE CHECK BOARD .
NOTE: After the faulty parts are repaired or replaced, perform the final check in Basic Diagnostic Procedure. Refer to ENGINE (DIAGNOSTICS) (H4DO (HEV))>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .
  1. CHECK POWER SUPPLY OF CAMSHAFT POSITION SENSOR LH  .
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the connector from camshaft position sensor LH.
    3. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    4. Measure the voltage between camshaft position sensor LH connector and engine ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (E35) No. 1 (+) - Engine ground (-):

    Is the voltage 10 V or more?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Repair the harness and connector.

    NOTE: In this case, repair the following item:
    • Open circuit or short circuit to ground in harness between main relay connector and camshaft position sensor LH connector
    • Poor contact of coupling connector
  2. CHECK HARNESS BETWEEN ECM AND CAMSHAFT POSITION SENSOR LH CONNECTOR (OPEN)  .
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the connector from ECM.
    3. Measure the resistance between ECM connector and camshaft position sensor LH connector.

      Connector & terminal 

      (E158) No. 77 - (E35) No. 2:

      (E158) No. 51 - (E35) No. 3:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Repair the open circuit in the harness between the ECM connector and camshaft position sensor LH connector.

  3. CHECK HARNESS BETWEEN ECM AND CAMSHAFT POSITION SENSOR LH CONNECTOR (SHORT TO GROUND)  .

    Measure the resistance between camshaft position sensor LH connector and engine ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (E35) No. 2 - Engine ground:

    Is the resistance 1 MΩ or more?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Repair short circuit to ground in harness between ECM connector and camshaft position sensor LH connector.

  4. CHECK HARNESS BETWEEN ECM AND CAMSHAFT POSITION SENSOR LH CONNECTOR (SHORT TO POWER SUPPLY)  .

    Measure the voltage between camshaft position sensor LH connector and engine ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (E35) No. 2 (+) - Engine ground (-):

    Is the voltage 5 V or more?

    Yes:  Repair the short circuit to power in the harness between ECM connector and camshaft position sensor LH connector.

    No:  Go to  5.

  5. CHECK CONDITION OF CAMSHAFT POSITION SENSOR LH  .
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Check the installation condition of camshaft position sensor LH.

    Is the camshaft position sensor LH installation bolt tightened securely?

    Yes:  Go to  6.

    No:  Tighten the camshaft position sensor LH installation bolt securely. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>CAMSHAFT POSITION SENSOR>INSTALLATION .

  6. CHECK CAMSHAFT POSITION SENSOR LH  .

    Check the waveform of the camshaft position sensor LH. Refer to ENGINE (DIAGNOSTICS) (H4DO (HEV))>CONTROL MODULE I/O SIGNAL .

    Is there any abnormality in waveform?

    Yes:  Replace the camshaft position sensor LH. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>CAMSHAFT POSITION SENSOR .

    No:  Repair the following item.

    • Poor contact of ECM connector
    • Poor contact of camshaft position sensor LH connector
    • Poor contact of coupling connector
