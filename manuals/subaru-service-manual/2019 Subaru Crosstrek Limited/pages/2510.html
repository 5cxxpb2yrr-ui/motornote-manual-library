DTC B2905: LED Head Light Circuit LH

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

DTC detecting condition: 

LED headlight (LH) malfunction signal line error is detected for 2 seconds or more.

Trouble symptom: 

LED headlight malfunction, LED headlight indicator illuminates.

Wiring diagram: 

  • Headlight beam leveler system. Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (IMPREZA MODEL)>HEADLIGHT BEAM LEVELER SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>HEADLIGHT BEAM LEVELER SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>HEADLIGHT BEAM LEVELER SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .
  • Headlight system. Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (IMPREZA MODEL)>HEADLIGHT SYSTEM AND DAYTIME RUNNING LIGHT SYSTEM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>HEADLIGHT SYSTEM AND DAYTIME RUNNING LIGHT SYSTEM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>HEADLIGHT SYSTEM AND DAYTIME RUNNING LIGHT SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

CAUTION:
  • Initialization is required after replacing the auto headlight beam leveler control module. Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>AUTO HEADLIGHT BEAM LEVELER SYSTEM>PROCEDURE .
  • After replacing the headlight assembly, headlight beam adjustment is required. Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>HEADLIGHT ASSEMBLY>ADJUSTMENT .
  1. CHECK DTC. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, perform the clear memory of [Headlight/Foglight]. Refer to COMMON (DIAG)>CLEAR MEMORY .
    3. Turn the ignition switch to OFF → ON.
    4. Turn the lighting switch to 2 (HEAD).
    5. Read the DTC of [Headlight/Foglight] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to HEADLIGHT/FOGLIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

      Is DTC B2905 displayed? (Current malfunction)

      Yes:  Go to  2.

      No:  Even if DTC is displayed, the circuit has returned to a normal condition at this time.

      Reproduce the failure, and then perform the diagnosis again.

      NOTE: In this case, temporary poor contact of connector, temporary open or short circuit of harness may be the cause.
  2. CHECK DATA MONITOR. 

    Using the Subaru Select Monitor, check the following items in the [Data monitor] of [Headlight/Foglight]. Refer to HEADLIGHT/FOGLIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS)>DATA MONITOR .

    • LED HEADLAMP (LH) error Signal

    Does the data display [Extinction]?

    Yes:  Replace the auto headlight beam leveler control module. Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>AUTO HEADLIGHT BEAM LEVELER CONTROL MODULE .

    No:  Go to  3.

  3. CHECK THE HEADLIGHT SYSTEM POWER SUPPLY CIRCUIT. 
    1. Disconnect the headlight assembly LH and RH connectors.
    2. Turn the ignition switch to OFF → ON.
    3. Turn the lighting switch to 2 (HEAD).
    4. Using a tester, measure the voltage between headlight assembly LH connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (B477) No. 3 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

      Is the voltage 10 - 13 V?

      Yes:  Go to  4.

      No:  Repair the headlight system.

  4. CHECK HARNESS (OPEN CIRCUIT). 

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between headlight assembly LH connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (B477) No. 4 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance less than 10 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  5.

    No:  Repair or replace the open circuit of harness.

  5. CHECK HEADLIGHT SYSTEM. 
    1. Connect the headlight assembly LH connector.
    2. Turn the ignition switch to OFF → ON.
    3. Turn the lighting switch to 2 (HEAD).

      Does the headlight come on at normal brightness?

      Yes:  Go to  6.

      No:  Replace the headlight. Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>HEADLIGHT ASSEMBLY .

  6. CHECK HARNESS (OPEN CIRCUIT). 
    1. Disconnect the auto headlight beam leveler control module connector.
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between auto headlight beam leveler control module connector and headlight assembly LH connector.

      Connector & terminal 

      (B616) No. 16 - (B477) No. 5:

      Is the resistance less than 10 Ω?

      Yes:  Go to  7.

      No:  Repair or replace the open circuit of harness.

  7. CHECK HARNESS (OPEN CIRCUIT). 

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between the auto headlight beam leveler control module connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (B616) No. 2 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance less than 10 Ω?

    Yes:  Replace the auto headlight beam leveler control module. Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>AUTO HEADLIGHT BEAM LEVELER CONTROL MODULE .

    No:  Repair or replace the open circuit of harness.
