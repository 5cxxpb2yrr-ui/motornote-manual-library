MT Gear Shift Cable: Installation

CAUTION:
  • Do not bend MT gear shift cable at a sharp angle.
  • When replacing the new MT gear shift cable, secure it to shift bracket with the clamps, be careful not to mistake the installing direction.
Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  1. Install the shift bracket together with the MT gear shift cable.
    CAUTION:
    • Do not twist the cable inner excessively.
    • Do not apply excessive load while holding the part (a).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.

    Tightening torque:

    18 N.m (1.8 kgf-m, 13.3 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Install the snap pin and washer.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Install the dust cover.

    Tightening torque:

    15 N.m (1.5 kgf-m, 11.1 ft-lb)

  4. Insert the MT gear shift cable into cable cover assembly, and install the clamp.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Connect the MT gear shift cable.
    CAUTION: Do not apply excessive load while holding the part (a).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Attach the cable cover plate.

    Tightening torque:

    7.5 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.5 ft-lb)

  7. Insert the MT gear shift cable into cable clamp, and install to the transmission.

    Tightening torque:

    18 N.m (1.8 kgf-m, 13.3 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Install the center exhaust cover.

    Tightening torque:

    18 N.m (1.8 kgf-m, 13.3 ft-lb)

  9. Install the front exhaust pipe. Refer to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FRONT EXHAUST PIPE>INSTALLATION .
  10. Lower the vehicle.
  11. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  12. Make sure the gears can be shifted accurately into each gear.
