Transfer Driven Gear: Assembly

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Using the ST, install the taper roller bearing (transfer case side).
    CAUTION:
    • Do not apply a load in excess of 10 kN (1 ton, 1.1 US ton, 1.0 Imp ton).
    • Be careful when handling, because the outer race and inner race of the taper roller bearing are used as a set.

    Special Tool: 

    ST: 927640000 INSTALLER

    CAUTION:
    • Do not apply a load in excess of 10 kN (1 ton, 1.1 US ton, 1.0 Imp ton).
    • Be careful when handling, because the outer race and inner race of the taper roller bearing are used as a set.
