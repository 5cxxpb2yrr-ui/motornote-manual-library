Front Antenna

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

CAUTION: Do not detach the antenna except when replacing it. Detaching unnecessarily will lead to insecure attachment of the antenna.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery and wait for at least 60 seconds before starting work. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    NOTE: For CVT models, release the shift lock and shift the select lever to the "N" position. Refer to CONTROL SYSTEMS>SELECT LEVER>REMOVAL .
  2. Remove the cover assembly - front. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>CONSOLE BOX>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the front antenna assembly - interior.
    1. Release the clips, and remove the front antenna assembly - interior.
