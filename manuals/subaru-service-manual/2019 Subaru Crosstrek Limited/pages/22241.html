DTC C1A7F: Brake Pedal Position Sensor Calibration Not Initialized: Notes

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

1. - 

DTC detecting condition: 

Brake pedal position sensor 0 point setting compensation not yet performed

Trouble symptom: 

  • Brake cannot be controlled.
  • Regenerative brake cannot be controlled.
  • Brake assist does not operate.
  • EyeSight functions (ACC/PCS) do not operate.
NOTE: If this DTC is displayed, replace the brake stroke sensor. Ref. to BRAKE>BRAKE PEDAL .
