Related Lines, More Than 52 Ω And Less Than 62 Ω (Remote Engine Starter)

Wiring diagram: 

CAN communication system. Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (IMPREZA MODEL)>CAN COMMUNICATION SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>CAN COMMUNICATION SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  1. CHECK BETWEEN RELATED LINES AND MAIN WIRING HARNESS. 
    1. Disconnect the remote engine start control module connector.
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between remote engine start control module connector terminals.

    Connector & terminal 

    Without push button start 

    (B180) No. 2 - (B180) No. 1:

    With push button start 

    (i291) No. 3 - (i291) No. 2:

    Is the resistance 400 Ω or more?

    Yes:  A related line between remote engine start control module and main wiring harness is open, or main wiring harness is open at two places or more.

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK RELATED LINES. 
    1. Disconnect the audio or navigation unit connector.
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between audio or navigation unit connector and remote engine start control module connector.

    Connector & terminal 

    Without push button start 

    (B180) No. 1 - (i87) No. 9:

    (B180) No. 2 - (i87) No. 10:

    With push button start 

    (i291) No. 2 - (i87) No. 9:

    (i291) No. 3 - (i87) No. 10:

    Is the resistance 10 Ω or more?

    Yes:  Repair or replace the open circuit of remote engine start control module related lines.

    No:  The communication harness is normal. Check the remote engine starter control module or the mobile key.
