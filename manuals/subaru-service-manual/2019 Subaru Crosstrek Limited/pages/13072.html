Stabilizer Bushing

  1. Lift up the vehicle, and then remove the rear wheels.
  2. Remove the bolts holding the bracket COMPL - stabilizer on the left and right sides.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the clamp - stabilizer bushing on the left and right sides.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the bushing - stabilizer.
