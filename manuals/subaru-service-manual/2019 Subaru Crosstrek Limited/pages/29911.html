DTC Other Than U-Code Is Displayed

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Trouble causes: 

Many and unspecified

CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK DTC (CURRENT MALFUNCTION). 
    1. Start the engine.
    2. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, read all DTCs. Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>OPERATION .

    Is DTC other than U-code displayed? (Current malfunction)

    Yes:  Perform the diagnosis for DTC other than the U-code.

    No:  Perform troubleshooting according to phenomenon of the problem. (If no phenomenon of the problem is present, it is normal currently.)
