CVTF Cooler Pipe No. 2

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
CAUTION:
  • Vehicle components are extremely hot immediately after driving. Be wary of receiving burns from heated parts.
  • If ATF spills over the engine, completely wipe it off to avoid emission of smoke and causing a fire.
  1. Remove the battery.

    Refer to STARTING/CHARGING SYSTEMS (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>BATTERY>REMOVAL .

  2. Remove the collector cover.

    Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>COLLECTOR COVER>REMOVAL .

  3. Collect the refrigerant.

    Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>REFRIGERANT RECOVERY PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .

  4. Drain engine coolant. (Inverter side)

    Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>ENGINE COOLANT>REPLACEMENT>DRAINING OF ENGINE COOLANT>INVERTER ASSEMBLY SIDE .

  5. Disconnect connector (A) from ECM.
    CAUTION: In order to prevent damaging the connector (A), check that the lock is released completely when moving the lever.
    NOTE: To disconnect the connector (A), press the portion (a) shown in the figure to release the lock and move the lever in the direction of arrow.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Disconnect the connector (B) from the main fuse box, and remove the engine wiring harness from the clip (C).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Release the claw securing the engine wiring harness, and place it aside so that it does not interfere with work.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Remove the inverter tank.

    Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>INVERTER TANK>REMOVAL .

  9. Drain the ATF.

    Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TH2A)>AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION FLUID>REPLACEMENT .

  10. Remove the front exhaust pipe.

    Refer to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FRONT EXHAUST PIPE>REMOVAL .

  11. Remove the CVTF CVT inlet hose and CVTF CVT outlet hose.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  12. Disconnect the hose - pressure suction valve (A) from the hose - pressure suction COMPL, and disconnect the lock assembly - front hood connector (B).
  13. Remove the lower side bolts (C) securing the radiator upper frame.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  14. Remove the bumper face - front.

    Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>FRONT BUMPER>REMOVAL>BUMPER FACE .

  15. Remove the bracket - bumper UPR.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  16. Remove the front hood lock assembly.

    Refer to SECURITY AND LOCKS>FRONT HOOD LOCK ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL .

  17. Disconnect the connector from the horn, and remove the clip.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  18. Disconnect the hose - pressure suction (A) from the A/C accumulator assembly, and disconnect the hose - pressure suction COMPL (B) from the pipe - return assembly.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  19. Remove the upper side bolts securing the radiator upper frame, and remove the radiator upper frame together with the A/C accumulator assembly.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  20. Remove the CVTF cooler (air cool) inlet hose (A) and the CVTF cooler (air cool) outlet hose (B).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  21. Remove the CVTF cooler pipe No. 2.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
