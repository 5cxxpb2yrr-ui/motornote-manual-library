Clutch Start Switch

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Perform inspections for the clutch start system, and if it does not operate normally, adjust the clutch start switch and check it again. Refer to CLUTCH SYSTEM>CLUTCH SWITCH>ADJUSTMENT .
  2. When the clutch start switch does not operate normally even if it is adjusted, check the clutch start switch for continuity.
    1. Remove the clutch start switch. Refer to CLUTCH SYSTEM>CLUTCH SWITCH>REMOVAL .
    2. Measure the resistance between terminal 1 and 2 of the switch. If the resistance is not within the standard value, replace the switch.
      Condition Terminal No. Standard
      ON No. 1 - No. 2 Less than 1 Ω
      OFF No. 1 - No. 2 1 MΩ or more
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Check that the switch is turned on and off in dimension L.

      Dimension L: 

      9 - 10 mm (0.35 - 0.39 in)

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    4. Install the clutch start switch. Refer to CLUTCH SYSTEM>CLUTCH SWITCH>INSTALLATION>CLUTCH START SWITCH .
