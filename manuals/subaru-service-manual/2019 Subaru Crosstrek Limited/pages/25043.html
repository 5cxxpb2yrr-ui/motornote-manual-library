Outline Of Diagnosis

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Detect the malfunction of the intake air temperature sensor output properties.

After the hybrid system starts after the specified period of soaking time has elapsed, judge by correlation between intake air temperature sensor value, EGR temperature sensor value and ambient temperature sensor value.

Judge as NG when the differences are both above the specified value by comparing between intake air temperature and EGR gas temperature, intake air temperature and ambient temperature.
