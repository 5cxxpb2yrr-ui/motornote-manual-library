MODELS WITHOUT EyeSight

NOTE: Do not remove the light assembly - map if removing the bulb only.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the light assembly - map.
    CAUTION: When using a flat tip screwdriver, use an appropriate sized tool for the insertion opening, apply protective tape or cloth around the tool, and be careful not to cause damage.
    1. Release the claws and then remove the cap.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Remove the screws and release the clip.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Disconnect the connector and remove the light assembly - map.
  3. Remove the map light bulb.
    CAUTION:
    • Use a dry clean cloth so that no grease or water adheres to the glass portion of the bulb.
    • When using a flat tip screwdriver, use an appropriate sized tool for the insertion opening, apply protective tape or cloth around the tool, and be careful not to cause damage.
    1. Release the claws and remove the lens.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Remove the map light bulb.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
