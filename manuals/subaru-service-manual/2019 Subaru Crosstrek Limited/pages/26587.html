DTC P1C84-49: High Voltage Power Resource Circuit Short During Ready On: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

1. - 

Outline of diagnosis: 

Malfunction of high voltage circuit between battery and inverter

During READY ON, the voltage before boosting is stuck outside the battery voltage range, or the cumulative value of variation amount in the voltage before boosting for a predetermined period of time is large

Blow-out of EV battery fuse, unattached Service plug, SMRB and SMRG open, or open-circuit of the high voltage cable occurred

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

Hybrid system stopped

Wiring diagram: 

Hybrid system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

  3. CHECK DTC  .
    1. With the vehicle stopped, set the wheel stoppers to the tires, and apply the parking brake.
    2. Turn to READY ON.
      • When READY ON was not possible, turn to IG OFF and then to READY ON again, and check DTC.
      • Because DTC P300449 is a 2-trip detection type, the DTC detection conditions should be met twice.
    3. Check the safety ahead and behind the vehicle.
    4. While depressing the brake pedal fully in D position, depress the accelerator pedal.
      NOTE: Depressing accelerator brake pedals simultaneously checks that HV battery current is flowing and the high voltage wiring system is normal.
    5. Read the DTC of [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    When DTC P1C8449 is displayed or DTC is not displayed, go to  A.

    When READY ON is not turned on and DTC P300449 is displayed, go to  B.

    Yes: 

    1. Go to  4.
    2. Perform the diagnosis of DTC P300449. Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE WITH DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>DTC P300449 HIGH VOLTAGE POWER RESOURCE CIRCUIT SHORT DURING PRE-CHARGE .
    NOTE: If more than one DTC is simultaneously displayed, perform the diagnosis in the order described in the above.

    No:  -

  4. CHECK CONNECTION CONDITION OF CONNECTOR (Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly CONNECTOR)  .

    Check the connecting condition of connector (i369) in Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly and check the relevant terminals for contact pressure, deformation and intrusion of water or foreign matter. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL MODULE .

    Is there looseness at the connector mating, or any poor contact, deformation or intrusion of water or foreign matter at the relevant terminals?

    Yes:  Connect the connector securely. Or repair the poor contact of connector.

    No:  Go to  5.

  5. CHECK CONNECTION CONDITION OF CONNECTOR (Rear harness LH CONNECTOR)  .

    Check the connecting condition of connector (R467) in Rear harness LH and check the relevant terminals for contact pressure, deformation and intrusion of water or foreign matter.

    Is there looseness at the connector mating, or any poor contact, deformation or intrusion of water or foreign matter at the relevant terminals?

    Yes:  Connect the connector securely. Or repair the poor contact of connector.

    No:  Go to  6.

  6. CHECK CONNECTION CONDITION OF CONNECTOR (Rear harness LH CONNECTOR)  .

    Check the connecting condition of connector (R534) in Rear harness LH and check the relevant terminals for contact pressure, deformation and intrusion of water or foreign matter.

    Is there looseness at the connector mating, or any poor contact, deformation or intrusion of water or foreign matter at the relevant terminals?

    Yes:  Connect the connector securely. Or repair the poor contact of connector.

    No:  Go to  7.

  7. CHECK CONNECTION CONDITION OF CONNECTOR (HV battery junction block CONNECTOR)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Check that the Service plug is disconnected. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .
    2. Check the connecting condition of connectors (HV49), (HV51) and (HV52) in HV battery junction block and check the relevant terminals for contact pressure, deformation and intrusion of water or foreign matters.

    Is there looseness at the connector mating, or any poor contact, deformation or intrusion of water or foreign matter at the relevant terminals?

    Yes:  Connect the connector securely. Or repair the poor contact of connector.

    No:  Go to  8.

  8. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly - HV battery junction block) (OPEN)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Disconnect Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector (i369).
    2. Disconnect HV battery junction block connectors (HV49) and (HV51).
    3. Using a tester, measure the resistance between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector and HV battery junction block connector.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV49) No. 1 - (i369) No. 5:

      (HV51) No. 3 - (i369) No. 1:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  9.

    No:  Repair or replace the Wire harness between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector and HV battery junction block connector.

  9. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (HV battery junction block - CHASSIS GROUND) (OPEN)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between HV battery junction block connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (HV49) No. 2 - Chassis ground:

    (HV52) No. 1 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  10.

    No:  Repair or replace Wire harness between HV battery junction block connector and chassis ground.

  10. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (HV battery junction block - CHASSIS GROUND) (OPEN)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Disconnect HV battery junction block connector (HV52).
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between HV battery junction block connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV49) No. 2 - Chassis ground:

      (HV52) No. 1 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  11.

    No:  Repair or replace Wire harness between HV battery junction block connector and chassis ground.

  11. CHECK HV battery  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Disconnect connectors (HV43) and (HV46) from HV battery junction block.
    2. Using a tester, measure the voltage between HV battery junction block connector and Service plug connector.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV89) No. 1 (+) - (HV46) No. 1 (-):

      (HV43) No. 1 (+) - (HV89) No. 2 (-):

    Is the voltage 30 V or more?

    Yes:  Go to  12.

    No:  Go to  13.

  12. REPLACEMENT OF HV battery junction block  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Replace HV battery junction block. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>RELAY AND FUSE .
    2. Restore the vehicle to its original state.
    3. Turn to IG ON.
    4. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, perform the clear memory of [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control]. Refer to COMMON (DIAG)>CLEAR MEMORY .
    5. Turn to IG OFF, and wait for two minutes or more.
    6. Turn to READY ON.
    7. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, display the following items in the [Data monitor] of [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control]. Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DATA MONITOR .
      • VL-Voltage before Boosting
      • Hybrid Battery Voltage

    Is the deviation between [Hybrid Battery Voltage] and [VL-Voltage before Boosting] less than 100 V constantly?

    Yes:  There was something wrong with HV battery junction block.

    Finish the diagnosis.

    No:  Replace Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL MODULE .

  13. REPLACEMENT OF Battery ECU assembly  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Replace Battery ECU assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>BATTERY CONTROL MODULE .
    2. Restore the vehicle to its original state.
    3. Turn to IG ON.
    4. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, perform the clear memory of [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control]. Refer to COMMON (DIAG)>CLEAR MEMORY .
    5. Turn to IG OFF, and wait for two minutes or more.
    6. Turn to IG ON, and wait for two minutes or more.
    7. Turn the ignition to OFF.
    8. Read the DTC of [P-HEV High Voltage Battery] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Are any DTCs displayed?

    Yes:  Perform the diagnosis for the displayed DTCs. Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    No:  There was something wrong with Battery ECU assembly.

    Finish the diagnosis.
