Emission Control Systems (Aux. Emission Control Devices) (Except For HEV): Canister: Installation

  1. Install the canister bracket.

    Tightening torque: 

    8 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.9 ft-lb)

  2. Connect the purge tube to the canister. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>Fuel Delivery and Evaporation Lines>INSTALLATION>QUICK CONNECTOR CONNECTION (TYPE D) .
  3. Install the canister.

    Tightening torque: 

    8 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.9 ft-lb)

    • Impreza model
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • Crosstrek model
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Connect the drain tube (A) and purge tube (B).

    Drain tube: 

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>Fuel Delivery and Evaporation Lines>INSTALLATION>QUICK CONNECTOR CONNECTION (TYPE E) .

    Purge tube: 

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>Fuel Delivery and Evaporation Lines>INSTALLATION>QUICK CONNECTOR CONNECTION (TYPE G) .

  5. Connect the vent tube (C). Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>Fuel Delivery and Evaporation Lines>INSTALLATION>QUICK CONNECTOR CONNECTION (TYPE F) .
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Install the rear differential.

    T-type: 

    Refer to DIFFERENTIALS>REAR DIFFERENTIAL (T-TYPE)>INSTALLATION .

    VA-type: 

    Refer to DIFFERENTIALS>REAR DIFFERENTIAL (VA-TYPE)>INSTALLATION .
