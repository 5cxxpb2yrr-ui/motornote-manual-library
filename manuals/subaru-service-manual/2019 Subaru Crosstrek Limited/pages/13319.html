Check Switch Unit

Measure the resistance between harness connector terminals of the X mode switch.

  Terminal No. Standard
OFF (when measured without operating the switch) No. 1 - No. 4 1 MΩ or more
ON (when measured with the switch held down) Less than 1 Ω
