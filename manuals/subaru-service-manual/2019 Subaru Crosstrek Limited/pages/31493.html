EYESIGHT/LANE DEPARTURE DEACTIVATE DTCS

APPLICABILITY

INTRODUCTION

This Service Information Bulletin describes how to proceed when during EyeSight (Version 3) system diagnosis, specific DTCs relating to the Lane Departure Prevention function are not found in the applicable Service Manual.

SERVICE PROCEDURE/INFORMATION

REMINDER:  Customer satisfaction and retention starts with performing quality repairs.

Specific Lane Departure Prevention Deactivate Codes (a.k.a. "Stop Codes") not shown in the Service Manual may be stored and shown on the Subaru Select Monitor (SSM) display during EyeSight diagnosis. Stop codes are set when the Lane Departure Prevention function of the EyeSight system is cancelled during normal system operation and do NOT indicate malfunctions in the EyeSight system. Therefore, if any of the codes shown below are displayed, none of them require further diagnosis.

DTC Description of DTC Displayed by SSM:
2C  Yaw Rate Sensor Abnormal (Control)
2D  Lateral Acceleration Is Large
2E  Lane Recognition Prohibition (Environmental Factor)
2F  Real Yaw Rate Excessive
5A  Camera Adjustment Abnormal 1
5B  Yaw Rate Sensor Abnormal (Recognition)
30  Out of Control Return Possibility Range

VERY IMPORTANT:  If any DTCs OTHER THAN  those specifically listed in the table above are found, the vehicle must be diagnosed (and repaired if necessary) according to the procedures in applicable Service Manual. The Service Manual procedures contain information critical to performing effective repairs the first time, every time.
