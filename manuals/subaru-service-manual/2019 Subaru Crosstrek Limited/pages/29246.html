DTC P1C1B-00: PFC Boosting Circuit Malfunction: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Outline of diagnosis: 

IAC current flowed when PFC-INV was operating.

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

Plug-in charging suspended

NOTE: This DTC is output when the Electric vehicle charger assembly detects an abnormality in the internal operation conditions.
Therefore if this DTC was detected, replace the Electric vehicle charger assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>ELECTRIC VEHICLE CHARGER .
