Diagnostic Method

If the duration of time while the following conditions are met is longer than the time indicated, judge as NG.

JUDGMENT VALUE SPECIFICATION

Malfunction Criteria Threshold Value
Diagnosis 1 
Difference between AWD solenoid set current and AWD solenoid actual current		 More than 950 mA
Diagnosis 2 
AWD solenoid actual current		 More than 3121 mA

Time needed for diagnosis:  1.2 s
