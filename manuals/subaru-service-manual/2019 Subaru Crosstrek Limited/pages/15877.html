Inspection Mode J

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
DTC Item Condition Number of driving cycles on the detecting condition
P2610 ECM/PCM ENGINE OFF TIMER PERFORMANCE - 2
  1. Turn to READY ON and idle the engine for at least 15 minutes.
  2. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
  3. After 10 hours or more passed from the start of step 2), turn to READY ON.
  4. Read the temporary code using the Subaru Select Monitor or general scan tool. Refer to COMMON (DIAG)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .
