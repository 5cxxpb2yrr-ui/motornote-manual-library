4 Door Model

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

CAUTION: Before installing the bumper face, match the claws on the bracket - rear bumper with the engaging position of flange section on the bumper face side. If the engaging position is not correct, the flange section may be broken or the clearance between fender panel and bumper face may not be uniform.
  1. If installing the following parts, refer to "Sensor & Harness" of the "Reverse Automatic Braking" service information. Refer to REVERSE AUTOMATIC BRAKING>SENSOR AND HARNESS>INSTALLATION .
    • Sonar - sensor
    • Cord ASSY - sonar
    • Holder - sonar
  2. Install each bumper bracket.
  3. Install the bumper beam COMPL - rear.

    Tightening torque: 

    53 N.m (5.4 kgf-m, 39.1 ft-lb)

  4. Install the bumper face - rear.
    CAUTION: For models with Reverse Automatic Braking, securely fasten the connector of the cord assembly - sonar.
    NOTE: If the bumper face - rear comes off too easily, adjust the hook of the center bracket side.
  5. Install the light assembly - rear combination.
    CAUTION: Replace the gasket - rear combination with a new part. If a gasket is reused, water leakage may occur.

    Tightening torque: 

    4.5 N.m (0.5 kgf-m, 3.3 ft-lb)

  6. Install the trim panel - trunk rear and trunk room mat.
  7. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
