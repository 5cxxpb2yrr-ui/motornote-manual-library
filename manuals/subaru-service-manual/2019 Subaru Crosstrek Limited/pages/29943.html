When "12V Battery Inspection Required" Is Displayed On The Meter

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. CHARGE BATTERY  .

    Charge the battery,

    Refer. to STARTING/CHARGING SYSTEMS (H4DO (HEV))>BATTERY>CHARGE .

    Is the battery charged?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No: 

    1. Replace the battery with a new part.

      Refer. to STARTING/CHARGING SYSTEMS (H4DO (HEV))>BATTERY .

    2. Ba

      Go to  3.

  2. CHECK BATTERY  .

    Check the battery.

    Refer. to STARTING/CHARGING SYSTEMS (H4DO (HEV))>BATTERY>INSPECTION .

    Is the battery OK?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No: 

    1. Replace the battery with a new part.

      Refer. to STARTING/CHARGING SYSTEMS (H4DO (HEV))>BATTERY .

    2. Go to  3.

  3. CHECK WIRING AND TERMINAL (BETWEEN BATTERY (12V) - HYBRID VEHICLE CONTROL COMPUTER)  .

    Check the wiring and terminals.

    Are the wiring and the contact of the terminal normal?

    Yes:  System is normal.

    Restore the vehicle and repeat IG OFF (for three seconds) ↔ IG ON (for three seconds) three times or more with all the electric load turned OFF. Then confirm that "12V battery inspection required" is not displayed on the meter.

    No: 

    1. Repair the defective part.
    2. Repeat IG OFF (for three seconds) ↔ IG ON (for three seconds) three times or more with all the electric load turned OFF. Then confirm that "12V battery inspection required" is not displayed on the meter.
