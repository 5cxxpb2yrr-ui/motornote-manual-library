Monitor Description

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

A malfunction is detected and the MIL is illuminated when one of the following conditions is met:

  • Battery charger hybrid/EV battery output voltage sensor is appeared when CHRG (Negative electrode) is disconnected and CHRB (Positive electrode) is connected.
  • Battery charger hybrid/EV battery output voltage sensor is appeared when CHRB (Positive electrode), CHRG (Negative electrode) and CHRP (Precharge) is disconnected.
