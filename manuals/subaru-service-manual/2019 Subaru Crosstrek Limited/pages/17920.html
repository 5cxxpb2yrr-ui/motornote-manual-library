Transfer Clutch Pressure Test: Inspection

  1. Lift up the vehicle.
  2. Disconnect the rear exhaust pipe from the front exhaust pipe.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the center exhaust cover.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the crossmember support - rear LH.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Set the transmission jack under the transmission. (Impreza model)
  6. Remove the mounting bolt of rear crossmember. (Impreza model)
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Lower the rear side of the transmission, and set the ST at four positions in the rear crossmember. (Impreza model)

    Preparation tool: 

    ST: ADAPTER (18362AA020)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Install the rear crossmember with M10 x 30 mm bolts. (Impreza model)
    CAUTION: Be careful not to use longer bolts than the specified length. Those bolts may interfere with the floor panel.

    Part No. (reference) 10510307

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Using the ST, remove the transfer clutch pressure test plug.

    Preparation tool: 

    ST: SOCKET (18270AA040)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  10. Set the ST1 and ST2 to the transmission.
    NOTE: Use ST1 with genuine union screw (Part No. 801914010) and gasket (Part No. 803914060) attached.

    Preparation tool: 

    ST1: ADAPTER PRESSURE GAUGE (18681AA050)

    ST2: OIL PRESSURE GAUGE ASSY (498575400)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  11. Temporarily tighten the front exhaust pipe and rear exhaust pipe.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  12. Lower the vehicle.
  13. Connect the Subaru Select Monitor to the data link connector and read the current data.
  14. Check the transfer clutch pressure as in secondary pressure (line pressure) test. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>SECONDARY PRESSURE (LINE PRESSURE) TEST .
    NOTE:
    • For models with X mode switch, turn OFF the switch for inspection.
    • Use Subaru Select Monitor for switching to FWD mode. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>AWD ON/OFF SWITCHING MODE .
    • If no oil pressure is produced, if it does not change in AWD mode or if oil pressure is produced in FWD mode, there may be a problem in the control valve body.

  15. Lift up the vehicle.
  16. Remove the ST.
  17. Install the test plug using ST1 and ST2.
    NOTE:
    • Use new O-rings.
    • Apply CVTF to the O-rings.
    • Tighten the test plug while directly aligning ST2 and torque wrench.

    Preparation tool: 

    ST1: SOCKET (18270AA040)

    ST2: SPECIAL TOOL CONDENSER (73099SG000)

    Tightening torque: 

    Calculation formula
    T = L/(100 mm (3.94in) +L) x 22 N.m (2.2kgf-m, 16.2ft-lb)
    T: Tightening torque
    L: Effective length of torque wrench
    NOTE: If the effective length of the torque wrench used is unknown, consult the manufacturer of the torque wrench.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  18. Set the transmission jack under the transmission. (Impreza model)
  19. Remove the ST (ADAPTER) and M10 x 30 mm bolts from the rear crossmember. (Impreza model)
  20. Raise the transmission assembly, and install the rear crossmember. (Impreza model)

    Tightening torque: 

    75 N.m (7.6 kgf-m, 55.3 ft-lb)

  21. Install the center exhaust cover.

    Tightening torque: 

    18 N.m (1.8 kgf-m, 13.3 ft-lb)

  22. Install the rear exhaust pipe to the front exhaust pipe.
    NOTE: Use a new gasket.

    Tightening torque: 

    18 N.m (1.8 kgf-m, 13.3 ft-lb)

  23. Install the crossmember support - rear LH.

    Tightening torque: 

    70 N.m (7.1 kgf-m, 51.6 ft-lb)

  24. Lower the vehicle.
