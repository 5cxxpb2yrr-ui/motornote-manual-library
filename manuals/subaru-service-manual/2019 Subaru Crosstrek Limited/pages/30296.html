Low Pressure Valve Hose

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
CAUTION: Replace the O-rings with new parts, and then apply compressor oil to install completely.
  1. Install the hose - pressure suction valve.

    Tightening torque: 

    7.5 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.5 ft-lb)

  2. Install the radiator assembly. Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>RADIATOR>INSTALLATION .
  3. Install the bumper face - front. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>FRONT BUMPER>INSTALLATION .
  4. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  5. Charge refrigerant. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>REFRIGERANT CHARGING PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .
