DTC P0A00-11: Motor Electronics Coolant Temperature Sensor Circuit Short To Ground: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Detecting condition: 

Short to ground of HV coolant temperature sensor

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

Normal driving

NOTE:
  • When DTC is output, replace Inverter with converter assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>INVERTER AND CONVERTER ASSEMBLY .
  • After repair, perform DTC memory clear and judge normal if [Inverter Coolant Water Temperature] of the data monitor is not an abnormal value (110°C or -40°C (230°F or -40°F)) at IG ON.

    Indicates 110°C (230°F) when DTC P0A0011 is detected, and -40°C (-40°F) when P0A0015 is detected.
