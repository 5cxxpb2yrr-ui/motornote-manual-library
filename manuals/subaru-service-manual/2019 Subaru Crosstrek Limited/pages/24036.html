Related Lines, More Than 52 Ω And Less Than 62 Ω (BODY-CAN_Headlight Beam Leveler)

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Wiring diagram: 

CAN communication system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>CAN COMMUNICATION SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  1. CHECK BETWEEN RELATED LINES AND MAIN WIRING HARNESS. 
    1. Disconnect the headlight beam leveler control module connector.
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between the headlight beam leveler control module connector terminals.

      Connector & terminal 

      (B616) No. 23 - (B616) No. 24:

    Is the resistance 400 Ω or more?

    Yes:  A related line between headlight beam leveler control module and main wiring harness is open, or main wiring harness is open at two places or more.

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK RELATED LINES. 

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between the headlight beam leveler control module connector and audio or navigation unit connector.

    Connector & terminal 

    (B616) No. 23 - (i87) No. 9:

    (B616) No. 24 - (i87) No. 10:

    Is the resistance 10 Ω or more?

    Yes:  Repair or replace the open circuit of headlight leveler related lines.

    No:  Check DTC of headlight beam leveler system. Refer to HEADLIGHT/FOGLIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .
