SRS Airbag System

Airbag system wiring harness is routed near the ABS wheel speed sensor and VDC Contol Module & H/U.

CAUTION:
  • Do not use electrical test equipment on wiring harness and connector circuits of the airbag system.
  • Be careful not to damage the airbag system wiring harness when servicing the ABS wheel speed sensor and VDC Contol Module & H/U.
