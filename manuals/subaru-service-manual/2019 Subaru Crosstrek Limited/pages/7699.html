Fuel Delivery And Evaporation Lines: Removal: Notes

  • Impreza model
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  • Crosstrek model
Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
WARNING: Place "NO OPEN FLAMES" signs near the working area.
CAUTION: Be careful not to spill fuel.
  1. Release the fuel pressure. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FUEL>PROCEDURE>RELEASING OF FUEL PRESSURE .
  2. Drain fuel. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FUEL>PROCEDURE>DRAINING FUEL (WITH SUBARU SELECT MONITOR) .
  3. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  4. Open the fuel filler lid and remove the fuel filler cap.
    NOTE: This operation is required to release the inner pressure of the fuel tank.
  5. Remove the fuel delivery tube clamp from the fuel delivery tube.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Disconnect the quick connector on the fuel delivery tube from the fuel pipe assembly. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>Fuel Delivery and Evaporation Lines>REMOVAL>QUICK CONNECTOR DISCONNECTION (TYPE A) .
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Disconnect the evaporation hose from the fuel pipe assembly.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Remove the connector from the fuel pipe bracket A.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Lift up the vehicle.
  10. Remove the bolts securing the fuel bracket A, and remove the pipe clamp from the fuel pipe assembly.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  11. Remove the fuel tank. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FUEL TANK>REMOVAL .
  12. Remove individual tubes and hoses from the fuel tank. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>COMPONENT>FUEL TANK .
    • For information about disconnecting fuel delivery tube, refer to "Fuel Delivery and Evaporation Lines". Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>REMOVAL>QUICK CONNECTOR DISCONNECTION (TYPE D) .
    • For information about disconnecting the circulate tube, refer to "Fuel Delivery and Evaporation Lines". Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>REMOVAL>QUICK CONNECTOR DISCONNECTION (TYPE G) .
    • For information about disconnecting the vent tube, refer to "Fuel Delivery and Evaporation Lines". Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>INSTALLATION>QUICK CONNECTOR CONNECTION (TYPE F) . Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>INSTALLATION>QUICK CONNECTOR CONNECTION (TYPE H) .
  13. Disconnect the purge tube from the fuel pipe assembly. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>REMOVAL>QUICK CONNECTOR DISCONNECTION (TYPE G) .
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  14. Remove the floor under protector RH. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>FLOOR UNDER PROTECTOR>REMOVAL .
  15. Disconnect the brake pipe. Refer to BRAKE>BRAKE HOSE>REMOVAL>FRONT BRAKE HOSE . Refer to BRAKE>BRAKE PIPE>REMOVAL>CENTER BRAKE PIPE .
  16. Remove the bolts securing the fuel pipe bracket B and the fuel pipe bracket C, and remove the pipe clamp from the fuel pipe assembly.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  17. Remove the fuel pipe assembly from the pipe clamp.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  18. Remove the fuel sub delivery tube. (Crosstrek model)
    1. Remove the fuel pump. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FUEL PUMP>REMOVAL .
    2. Remove the fuel sub level sensor. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FUEL SUB LEVEL SENSOR>REMOVAL .
    3. Remove the fuel sub delivery tube from the fuel tank.
