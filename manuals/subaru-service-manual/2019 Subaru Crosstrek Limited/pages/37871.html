Trunk Opener Button: Removal

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the trim panel - trunk lid. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>TRUNK ROOM TRIM>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the switch - request.
    CAUTION: When using a flat tip screwdriver, apply protective tape or cloth and be careful not to cause damage.
    1. Disconnect the connector.
    2. Remove it by pressing the lock portion of the switch - request as shown in the figure.
