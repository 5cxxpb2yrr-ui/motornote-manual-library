Malfunction Description

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

When VCHG outputs the value exceeding the specified value for a predetermined period of time, the charging system overvoltage is detected. Then, Electric vehicle charger assembly is stopped and this DTC is stored.

The following cases are assumed to be malfunction causes:

Low voltage system (12 V) fault

  • Malfunction of Electric vehicle charger assembly (CPU fault, VCHG sensor fault)
  • Malfunction of Battery ECU assembly (open circuit/instantaneous power failure)
  • CHR operation command path shutdown (between Battery ECU assembly and HV battery junction block)

High voltage system fault

  • Charging path shutdown
    • Malfunction of high voltage circuit in Electric vehicle charger assembly (open circuit/instantaneous power failure)
    • High voltage circuit between Electric vehicle charger assembly - HV battery junction block (open circuit/instantaneous power failure)
  • Malfunction of HV battery junction block (CHRB, CHRG)
    • HV battery junction block (CHRB, CHRG) (open circuit/instantaneous power failure)
    • High voltage circuit between HV battery - HV battery junction block (CHRB, CHRG) (open circuit/instantaneous power failure)
