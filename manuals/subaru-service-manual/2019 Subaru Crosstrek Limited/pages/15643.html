Disc Rotor Thickness Check

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Remove the caliper body assembly. Refer to BRAKE>REAR DISC BRAKE ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL .
  2. Set a micrometer 10 mm (0.39 in) inward from the disc rotor outer perimeter, and then measure the disc rotor thickness (A).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Size Standard Wear limit Disc rotor outer diameter
    Disc rotor thickness (A) 15-inch 10 mm (0.39 in) 8.5 mm (0.335 in) 274 mm (10.79 in)
    16-inch 17 mm (0.67 in) 15 mm (0.59 in) 285 mm (11.22 in)
  3. If the wear limit is exceeded in the inspection, replace the disc rotor.
