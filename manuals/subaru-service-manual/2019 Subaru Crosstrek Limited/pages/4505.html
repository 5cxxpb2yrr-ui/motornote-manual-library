Initialize Operation

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

CAUTION: During initialization, perform the position learning and the sliding learning. Sliding learning mode can not be initiated unless the position learning is performed.
NOTE: When initialize operation is performed, it is necessary to press the switch repeatedly as it operates for only at the moment when the switch is pressed, even when the switch is held down.

POSITION IS NOT MEMORIZED (AUTOMATIC OPEN/CLOSE CANNOT BE PERFORMED) 

«Position learning»

  1. Position the lid assembly - sunroof at the fully tilt up position, and press and hold the tilt up switch for ten seconds.
  2. The motion of the glass lid shifts to inching operation until it reaches the mechanical lock position (further tilt up position than the fully tilt up position). After the mechanical lock, it returns to the fully tilt up position and then stops.
  3. Release the tilt up switch to finish the position learning.

    «Sliding learning»

  4. Within six seconds after the position learning is finished, press and hold the tilt up switch again.
  5. The lid assembly - sunroof moves to the full close position, full open position, and the full close position successively, and stops at the full close position.
  6. Release the tilt up switch and turn the ignition switch to OFF to finish the initializing.

POSITION IS MEMORIZED (AUTOMATIC OPEN/CLOSE CAN BE PERFORMED) 

«Position learning»

  1. Position the lid assembly - sunroof at the fully tilt up position, and press and hold the tilt up switch for ten seconds.
  2. The motion of the glass lid shifts to inching operation until it reaches the mechanical lock position (further tilt up position than the fully tilt up position). After the mechanical lock, it returns to the fully tilt up position and then stops.
  3. Release the tilt up switch to finish the position learning.
    NOTE: If the glass lid does not shift to inching operation until it reaches to the mechanical lock position in step 2, perform steps  4 -  6.
  4. Position the lid assembly - sunroof at the fully open position, and press and hold the tilt up switch for ten seconds.
  5. The motion of the glass lid shifts to inching operation until it reaches the mechanical lock position. After that, it returns to the fully tilt up position and then stops.
  6. Release the tilt up switch to finish the position learning.

    «Sliding learning» 

  7. Within six seconds after the position learning is finished, press and hold the tilt up switch again.
  8. The lid assembly - sunroof moves to the full close position, full open position, and the full close position successively, and stops at the full close position.
  9. Release the tilt up switch and turn the ignition switch to OFF to finish the initializing.
