DTC B2A03: GPS Antenna Circuit

Diagnosis start condition: 

When ignition switch is ON.

DTC detecting condition: 

Any of the followings continues for 5 seconds or more.

  • GPS antenna impedance is more than 1 MΩ. (Detached antenna, etc.)
  • GPS antenna impedance is less than 10 Ω. (Short-circuited antenna, etc.)
  • GPS antenna load current is less than 10 mA. (Antenna open circuit)
  • GPS antenna load current is more than 40 mA. (Short-circuited antenna)

Trouble symptom: 

  • Telematics function cannot be used.
  • RED LED illuminates.
CAUTION:
  • After the DCM is replaced, registrations of CommCheck and immobilizer system are required. Refer to ENTERTAINMENT & MONITORING>TELEMATICS SYSTEM>OPERATION>REGISTRATION (COMM CHECK) .
  • Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to TELEMATICS SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK DTC. 

    Read the DTC of [Telematics] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to TELEMATICS SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is DTC B2A03 displayed? (Current malfunction)

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Even if DTC is displayed, the circuit has returned to a normal condition at this time.

    Reproduce the failure, and then perform the diagnosis again.

    NOTE: In this case, temporary poor contact of connector, temporary open or short circuit of harness may be the cause.
  2. CHECK GPS ANTENNA. 

    Visually check the GPS antenna. Refer to ENTERTAINMENT & MONITORING>ANTENNA .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Replace the DCM. Refer to ENTERTAINMENT & MONITORING>DATA COMMUNICATION MODULE .

    No:  Replace the GPS antenna. Refer to ENTERTAINMENT & MONITORING>ANTENNA .
