DTC P0A90-00: Drive Motor "A" Performance: Notes

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. - 

    Detecting condition: 

    Motor demagnetization

    When motor magnetic force dropped

    (1 trip detection logic)

    Trouble symptom: 

    • Greatly reduced output power
    • Driving in reverse not possible
    • A driving range is limited
    • Engine used while driving and engine speed becomes high
      CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to P-HEV DRIVE MOTOR (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK DTC.

    When "Only DTC P0A9000 is displayed" or "DTC other than above is displayed at the same time", go to  A.

    When DTCs related to [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control], mentioned above, are simultaneously displayed, go to  B.

    When DTCs related to [P-HEV Drive Motor], mentioned above, are simultaneously displayed, go to  C.

    Yes: 

    1. Go to  2.
    2. Perform the diagnosis for the simultaneously displayed DTCs.
      NOTE: If more than one DTC is simultaneously displayed, perform the diagnosis in the order described in the above.
    3. Perform the diagnosis for the simultaneously displayed DTCs.
      NOTE: If more than one DTC is simultaneously displayed, perform the diagnosis in the order described in the above.

    No:  -

  2. CHECK FREEZE FRAME DATA AND DIAGNOSTIC RELATED INFORMATION. 
    1. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, read [Diagnosis Related Information] of [P-HEV Drive Motor]. Refer to P-HEV DRIVE MOTOR (DIAGNOSTICS)>WORK SUPPORT .
    2. Read the DTC of [P-HEV Drive Motor] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to P-HEV DRIVE MOTOR (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

      When diagnostic related information DTC P312487 is displayed, go to  A.

      When diagnostic related information DTC P312487 is not displayed and freeze frame data [Emergency Shutdown Signal] is ON, go to  B.

      For cases other than above, go to  C.

      Yes: 

      1. Perform the diagnosis of DTC P31241F. Refer to P-HEV DRIVE MOTOR (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE WITH DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>DTC P31241F LOST COMMUNICATION BETWEEN DRIVE MOTOR "A" AND HV ECU CIRCUIT INTERMITTENT .
      2. Perform the diagnosis of DTC P321E9F. Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE WITH DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>DTC P321E9F MOTOR/GENERATOR SHUTDOWN SIGNAL STUCK OFF .
      3. Go to  3.

      No:  -

  3. CHECK DTC. 
    1. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, perform the clear memory of [P-HEV Drive Motor]. Refer to COMMON (DIAG)>CLEAR MEMORY .
    2. Turn to READY ON.
    3. Move to P position.
    4. Turn the air conditioner ON and wait for 1 minute in the status of Engine always stopped.
      NOTE: When the engine starts, run the engine until it stalls. Wait for 1 minute after Engine always stopped.
    5. Read the DTC of [P-HEV Drive Motor] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to P-HEV DRIVE MOTOR (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

      Is DTC other than P0A9000 or P0BFF1D displayed?

      Yes:  Perform the diagnosis for the displayed DTCs. Refer to P-HEV DRIVE MOTOR (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>LIST .

      No:  Go to  4.

  4. CHECK DTC. 
    1. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, perform the clear memory of [P-HEV Drive Motor]. Refer to COMMON (DIAG)>CLEAR MEMORY .
    2. Turn to READY ON.
    3. Move to D position.
    4. Accelerate to 20 km/h (12 MPH) with the accelerator pedal opened 50% or more.
      WARNING: During the driving test, the traffic laws and regulations such as speed limit must always be observed.
    5. Read the DTC of [P-HEV Drive Motor] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to P-HEV DRIVE MOTOR (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

      Is DTC other than P0A9000 or P0BFF1D displayed?

      Yes:  Perform the diagnosis for the displayed DTCs. Refer to P-HEV DRIVE MOTOR (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>LIST .

      No:  Go to  5.

  5. CHECK THE CONNECTING CONDITION OF CONNECTOR (Inverter with converter assembly LOW PRESSURE CONNECTOR). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Check that the Service plug is disconnected. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .
    2. Check the connecting condition of low pressure connectors (HV23) and (HV24) in Inverter with converter assembly and check the relevant terminals for contact pressure, deformation and intrusion of water or foreign matter. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>INVERTER AND CONVERTER ASSEMBLY .
      NOTE: When connecting the connector, insert to the end with the lock lever raised, then fold the lock lever completely until a click is heard. After connected, make sure that the lock claw does not loosen and pull the connector to see that it does not come off.

      Is there looseness at the connector connection, or any poor contact, deformation or intrusion of water or foreign matter at the relevant terminals?

      Yes:  Connect the connector securely. Or repair the poor contact of connector.

      No:  Go to  6.

  6. CHECK Shut Down Signal Circuit. 

    Check Shut Down Signal Circuit. Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>SHUT DOWN SIGNAL CIRCUIT .

    Is the Shut Down Signal Circuit OK?

    Yes:  Go to  7.

    No:  Repair according to Shut Down Signal Circuit inspection. And then, Go to  7.

  7. CHECK MOTOR HIGH-VOLTAGE CIRCUIT. 

    Check MOTOR HIGH-VOLTAGE CIRCUIT. Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>MOTOR HIGH-VOLTAGE CIRCUIT .

    Is the MOTOR HIGH-VOLTAGE CIRCUIT OK?

    Yes:  Replace Transmission assembly. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TH2A)>TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY .

    No:  Repair according to MOTOR HIGH-VOLTAGE CIRCUIT inspection.
