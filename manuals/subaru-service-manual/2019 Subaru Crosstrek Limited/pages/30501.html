Fuel Delivery And Evaporation Lines: Installation: Notes

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Install the fuel pipe assembly.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Install the pipe clamp to the fuel pipe assembly, and then install the fuel pipe bracket B and fuel pipe bracket C.

    Tightening torque: 

    18 N.m (1.8 kgf-m, 13.3 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Connect the brake pipes. Refer to BRAKE>BRAKE HOSE>INSTALLATION>REAR BRAKE HOSE . Refer to BRAKE>BRAKE PIPE>INSTALLATION>CENTER BRAKE PIPE .
  4. Install the floor under protector RH. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>FLOOR UNDER PROTECTOR>INSTALLATION .
  5. Connect the purge tube to the fuel pipe assembly. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>INSTALLATION>QUICK CONNECTOR CONNECTION (TYPE E) .
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Connect individual hoses to the fuel tank. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>COMPONENT>FUEL TANK 1 .

    For information about connecting fuel filler hose, refer to "Fuel Delivery and Evaporation Lines". Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>INSTALLATION>FUEL FILLER HOSE CONNECTION .

  7. Install the fuel tank. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL TANK>INSTALLATION .
  8. Install the pipe clamp to the fuel pipe assembly, and then install the fuel pipe bracket A.

    Tightening torque: 

    18 N.m (1.8 kgf-m, 13.3 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Lower the vehicle.
  10. Install the connector to the fuel pipe bracket A.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  11. Connect the evaporation hose to fuel pipe assembly.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  12. Connect the quick connector on the fuel delivery tube to the fuel pipe assembly. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>INSTALLATION>QUICK CONNECTOR CONNECTION (TYPE A) .
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  13. Install the fuel delivery tube clamp to the fuel delivery tube.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
