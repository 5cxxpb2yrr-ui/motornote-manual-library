Fuel Pump Relay: Inspection

  1. Check that the fuel pump relay has no deformation, cracks or other damages.
  2. Measure the resistance between fuel pump relay terminals.
Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
Terminal No. Inspection conditions Standard
3 - 4 Always 1 MΩ or more
3 - 4 Apply battery voltage between terminals 1 and 2. Less than 1 Ω
