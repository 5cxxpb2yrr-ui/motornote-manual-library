Impreza Model

  1. Lift up the vehicle, and then remove the front wheels.
  2. Remove the axle nut.
    CAUTION: Do not loosen the axle nut while the front axle is loaded. Doing so may damage the hub unit bearing.
    1. Lift the crimped section of axle nut.
    2. Remove the axle nut using a socket wrench while depressing the brake pedal.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the front disc brake from the front axle housing.
    1. Remove the mounting bolts and the brake hose bracket, and then remove the front disc brake.
    2. Prepare wiring harnesses etc. to be discarded, and suspend the front disc brake from the front strut.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the front disc rotor.
    NOTE: When the disc rotor is difficult to be removed from the front hub unit bearing, screw in 8 mm (0.31 in) bolt to the threaded part of the disc rotor (A), and remove the disc rotor.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the bolts, and remove the front ABS wheel speed sensor and clips.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Disconnect the tie-rod end.
    1. Pull out the cotter pin (a).
    2. Remove the castle nut (b) and washer (c).
    3. Using the tie-rod ball joint puller (d), detach the tie-rod end (e) from the housing (f).
    CAUTION:
    • Be careful not to damage the boot of the joint.
    • Securely place the tie-rod ball joint puller (d) on the steel collar (g).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Remove the stabilizer link.
    CAUTION: Be careful not to damage the boot of the joint.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Remove the three nuts of the ball joint, and disconnect the front lower arm from the front axle housing.
    NOTE:
    • To prevent damage of the front arm ball joint boot, place a wooden block, etc. between front arm and ball joint.
    • Mark the engaging position of the ball joint and front arm before loosening the flange nuts. Doing so will facilitate aligning the position when assembling and lessen adjusting allowance for wheel alignment.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Remove the front drive shaft assembly from the front hub unit bearing.
    NOTE: If it is hard to remove, use the ST.

    Preparation tool: 

    ST1: AXLE SHAFT PULLER (926470000)

    ST2: AXLE SHAFT PULLER PLATE (28099 Pa110)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  10. Remove the front axle housing.
    1. Place an alignment mark (a) on the adjusting bolt and the strut.
    2. Remove the adjusting bolts and flange bolts for the front strut, and then remove the front axle housing.
    CAUTION:
    • While holding the head of the adjusting bolt, loosen the flange nut.
    • Since the front axle housing is heavy, be careful not to drop it.
    • Be careful not to damage the spline portion of the drive shaft.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  11. Remove the front ball joint from the front axle housing. Ref. to FRONT SUSPENSION>BALL JOINT>REMOVAL .
  12. Refer to "Front Hub Unit Bearing" for removal of the front hub unit bearing. Ref. to DRIVE SHAFT SYSTEM>FRONT HUB UNIT BEARING>REMOVAL .
