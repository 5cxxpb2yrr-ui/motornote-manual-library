Vacuum Pump: Removal

NOTE:
  • Vacuum pump is equipped only on the models other than hybrid model.
  • When replacing a single part, perform the work with the engine assembly installed to body.
  1. When working on the vehicle
    NOTE: When working on the vehicle, perform the following steps also.
    1. Remove the clip from the air intake boot, and loosen the clamp.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Remove the air intake boot, and place it aside so that it does not interfere with the work.
      CAUTION: Be careful not to pull out the PCV hose.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Disconnect the brake booster vacuum hose from the vacuum pump.
      NOTE: Before disconnecting the brake booster vacuum hose, use a marker to put an alignment mark (a) at the position shown in the figure when the clip is slided.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    4. Remove the crossmember support - rear RH. Refer to FRONT SUSPENSION>CROSSMEMBER SUPPORT>REMOVAL .
  2. Remove the union bolts (A) securing the oil pipe.
  3. Remove the vacuum pump.
    NOTE: The vacuum pump cannot be disassembled.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove liquid gasket from the vacuum pump mounting surface of the engine, and the surface of the vacuum pump.
