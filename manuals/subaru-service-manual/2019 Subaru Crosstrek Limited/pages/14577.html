Overview Of Error

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

The Charge control ECU installed inside the Electric vehicle charger assembly monitors the speed of Charger cooling blower and outputs a DTC when it detects a Charger cooling blower speed error. The following are possible causes of the error.

  • Circuit malfunction of Charger cooling blower
    • Malfunction of Electric vehicle charger assembly (charge control ECU)
    • Malfunction of Charger cooling blower (open circuit or instantaneous power failure, intrusion of foreign substance, Charger cooling blower speed sensor error)
    • Malfunction of the Charger cooling blower operation command path (between Electric vehicle charger assembly - Charger cooling blower)
