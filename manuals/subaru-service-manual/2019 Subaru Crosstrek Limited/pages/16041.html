Inverter Tank: Installation

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Connect the over flow hose (A) and inverter tank outlet hose (B) to the inverter tank.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Connect the inverter tank to the inverter radiator outlet hose (A) and install the bolt securing the inverter tank.

    Tightening torque: 

    7.5 N.m (0.8 gf-m, 5.5 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Install the bracket - hood stay.

    Tightening torque: 

    18 N.m (1.8 kgf-m, 13.3 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Install the cover UPR - front.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Install the air intake duct.

    Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>AIR INTAKE DUCT>INSTALLATION .

  6. Install the inverter radiator outlet hose to the clamp.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Connect the inverter tank outlet hose to the water pipe (A).
  8. Install the water pipe (A) to the clamp.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Install the inverter tank outlet hose to the clamp.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  10. Fill engine coolant. (Inverter assembly side)

    Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>ENGINE COOLANT>REPLACEMENT>FILLING OF ENGINE COOLANT>INVERTER ASSEMBLY SIDE .
