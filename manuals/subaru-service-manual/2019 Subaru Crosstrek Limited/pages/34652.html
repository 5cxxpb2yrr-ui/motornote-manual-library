Cylinder Block: Removal

  1. Remove the engine from the vehicle.

    Except for hybrid model: 

    Refer to MECHANICAL (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL>EXCEPT FOR HYBRID MODEL .

    Hybrid model: 

    Refer to MECHANICAL (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL>HYBRID MODEL .

  2. Remove the crankshaft position sensor plate with drive plate. (Except for hybrid model (CVT model)) Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>DRIVE PLATE>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the crankshaft position sensor plate with flywheel. (MT model and hybrid model)

    MT model: 

    Refer to CLUTCH SYSTEM>FLYWHEEL>REMOVAL .

    Hybrid model: 

    Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TH2A)>FLYWHEEL>REMOVAL .

  4. Remove the cylinder head. Refer to MECHANICAL (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>CYLINDER HEAD>REMOVAL .
  5. Remove the oil pan upper. Refer to LUBRICATION (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>OIL PAN>REMOVAL>OIL PAN UPPER .
  6. Remove the crank sprocket. Refer to MECHANICAL (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>CRANK SPROCKET>REMOVAL .
  7. Remove the water tank pipe assembly.

    Except for hybrid model: 

    Refer to COOLING (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>WATER PIPE>REMOVAL>WATER TANK PIPE ASSEMBLY .

    Hybrid model: 

    Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>WATER PIPE>REMOVAL>WATER TANK PIPE ASSEMBLY .

  8. Remove the PVC connector.

    Except for hybrid model: 

    Refer to EMISSION CONTROL (AUX. EMISSION CONTROL DEVICES) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>PCV CONNECTOR>REMOVAL .

    Hybrid model: 

    Refer to EMISSION CONTROL (AUX. EMISSION CONTROL DEVICES) (H4DO (HEV))>PCV CONNECTOR>REMOVAL .

  9. Remove the knock sensor.

    Except for hybrid model: 

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>KNOCK SENSOR>REMOVAL .

    Hybrid model: 

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>KNOCK SENSOR>REMOVAL .

  10. Remove the PCV valve.

    Except for hybrid model: 

    Refer to EMISSION CONTROL (AUX. EMISSION CONTROL DEVICES) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>PCV VALVE>REMOVAL .

    Hybrid model: 

    Refer to EMISSION CONTROL (AUX. EMISSION CONTROL DEVICES) (H4DO (HEV))>PCV VALVE>REMOVAL .

  11. Remove the engine rear hanger from cylinder block RH.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  12. Remove the bolts securing the crankshaft position sensor holder from the cylinder block LH, and remove the crankshaft position sensor with crankshaft position sensor holder.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  13. Set the part so that the cylinder block RH is on the upper side.
  14. Remove the #1 and #3 connecting rod caps and pistons from the cylinder block.
    NOTE: Mark each connecting rod cap and piston with a cylinder number.
    1. Using the ST, turn the crankshaft so that the #1 connecting rod cap (A) is located at the position shown in the figure.

      Preparation tool: 

      ST: CRANKSHAFT SOCKET (18252AA000)

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Using the ST, loosen the #1 connecting rod cap bolt, and remove the #1 connecting rod cap bolt and #1 connecting rod cap.

      Preparation tool: 

      ST: SOCKET (18270AA020)

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Using the ST, turn the crankshaft counterclockwise and separate the positions of the #1 pin (A) of crankshaft and the large end (B) of #1 connecting rod.

      Preparation tool: 

      ST: CRANKSHAFT SOCKET (18252AA000)

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    4. Push the #1 connecting rod in the direction of the arrow, and remove the #1 piston with #1 connecting rod from the cylinder block.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    5. Using the ST, turn the crankshaft so that the #3 connecting rod cap (A) is located at the position shown in the figure.

      Preparation tool: 

      ST: CRANKSHAFT SOCKET (18252AA000)

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    6. Using the ST, loosen the #3 connecting rod cap bolt, and remove the #3 connecting rod cap bolt and #3 connecting rod cap.

      Preparation tool: 

      ST: SOCKET (18270AA020)

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    7. Using the ST, turn the crankshaft counterclockwise and separate the positions of the #3 pin (A) of crankshaft and the large end (B) of #3 connecting rod.

      Preparation tool: 

      ST: CRANKSHAFT SOCKET (18252AA000)

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    8. Push the #3 connecting rod in the direction of the arrow, and remove the #3 piston with #3 connecting rod from the cylinder block.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  15. Set the part so that the cylinder block LH is on the upper side.
  16. Remove #2 and #4 connecting rod caps and pistons in the same removal procedure as #1 and #3 connecting rod caps and pistons.
  17. Set the part so that the cylinder block RH is on the upper side, and separate the cylinder block.
    1. Remove the engine stand from the cylinder block RH.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Remove the bolt shown in the figure.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Loosen the cylinder block mounting bolts in numerical order as shown in the figure, and then remove the bolts.
      Fig 1: Cylinder Block Mounting Bolts Loosening Sequence
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    4. While tapping the cylinder block RH with a plastic hammer, separate the cylinder block RH from the cylinder block LH.
      NOTE: Lift the cylinder block RH gradually, and confirm that the crankshaft is remaining in the cylinder block LH. Lifting the cylinder block RH carelessly may cause the crankshaft to fall off.
  18. Remove the crankshaft from cylinder block LH, and remove the rear oil seal.
  19. Remove the O-ring from the cylinder block LH.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  20. Remove the crankshaft bearings from the cylinder block.
    NOTE:
    • Be careful not to confuse the crankshaft bearing combination.
    • Press the bearing at the end opposite to locking lip (A).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  21. Remove the liquid gasket from cylinder block.
  22. Remove the cylinder block LH from the engine stand.
