Malfunction Description

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly detects a malfunction that the shutdown path for the generator inverter cannot shut down. Detection is performed during the end sequence at READY OFF. Motor generator control ECU detects a malfunction, when judging that shutdown is not performed after it outputs a shutdown signal to the generator inverter.

The following cases are assumed to be malfunction causes:

  • Malfunction of the shutdown path (generator) in Inverter with converter assembly
    • Malfunction of Inverter with converter assembly
