Diagnostic Method

Judge as NG when the following conditions are established.

JUDGMENT VALUE SPECIFICATION

Malfunction Criteria Threshold Value
(1) Monitoring A/D voltage More than 0.02 V
(2) Monitoring A/D voltage Less than 4.98 V
(3) Monitoring A/D voltage Less than 1.1 V
or
More than 1.4 V
(4) Inner power supply voltage/2 Less than 1.819 V
or
More than 3 V

Time needed for diagnosis: 

  1. 200 ms
  2. 200 ms
  3. 200 ms
  4. 200 ms

Malfunction indicator light illumination:  Illuminates as soon as a malfunction occurs.
