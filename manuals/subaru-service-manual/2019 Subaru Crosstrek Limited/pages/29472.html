Differential Gear Oil: Replacement

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
CAUTION: Vehicle components are extremely hot immediately after driving. Be wary of receiving burns from heated parts.
  1. Remove the filler plug (A).
  2. Drain the differential gear oil by loosening the drain plug (B) using TORX® bit T70.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the iron powder, etc. from the drain plug.
  4. Attach the drain plug (B) using a new gasket, using TORX® bit T70.

    Tightening torque: 

    70 N.m (7.1 kgf-m, 51.6 ft-lb)

  5. Remove the overflow plug (C).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Add differential gear oil through the filler plug hole, and let the oil flow out of the overflow plug hole.

    Differential gear oil: 

    Refer to RECOMMENDED MATERIALS>FLUID .

    Differential gear oil capacity: 

    Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TH2A)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>SPECIFICATION .

  7. Check the differential gear oil level.

    Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TH2A)>DIFFERENTIAL GEAR OIL>INSPECTION .
