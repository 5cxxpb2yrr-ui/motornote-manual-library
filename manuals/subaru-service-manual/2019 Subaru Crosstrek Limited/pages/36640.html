DTC U0416: Invalid Data Received From Vehicle Dynamics Control Module

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

DTC detecting condition: 

CAN data from VDCCM is abnormal.

Trouble symptom: 

  • Tire rotation indication is not displayed correctly in the energy flow screen. (Hybrid model)
  • The on-screen tire angle in the left side of the preventive safety screen (powertrain display) does not change.
  • The on-screen tire display in the left side of the preventive safety screen (powertrain display) is not shown properly.
  • The on-screen powertrain in the left side of the preventive safety screen (powertrain display) is not shown properly.
  • The X-MODE icon in the left side of the preventive safety screen (powertrain display) is not shown properly.
  • The VDC operation icon in the left side of the preventive safety screen (powertrain display) is not shown properly.
  • The HDC operation icon in the left side of the preventive safety screen (powertrain display) is not shown properly.
NOTE: For the diagnostic procedure, refer to DTC U0074. Refer to MULTI-FUNCTION DISPLAY (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE WITH DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>DTC U0074 CONTROL MODULE COMMUNICATION BUS "BODY-CAN" OFF .
