Cylinder Head LH

  1. Remove the engine from the vehicle.

    Except for hybrid model: 

    Refer to MECHANICAL (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL>EXCEPT FOR HYBRID MODEL .

    Hybrid model: 

    Refer to MECHANICAL (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL>HYBRID MODEL .

  2. Remove the engine wiring harness.

    Except for hybrid model: 

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE WIRING HARNESS>REMOVAL .

    Hybrid model: 

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>ENGINE WIRING HARNESS>REMOVAL .

  3. Remove the cam carrier LH. Refer to MECHANICAL (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>CAM CARRIER>REMOVAL>CAM CARRIER LH .
  4. Remove the high-pressure fuel delivery pipe assembly.

    Except for hybrid model: 

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>HIGH PRESSURE FUEL DELIVERY PIPE>REMOVAL>HIGH-PRESSURE FUEL DELIVERY PIPE ASSEMBLY .

    Hybrid model: 

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>HIGH PRESSURE FUEL DELIVERY PIPE>REMOVAL>HIGH-PRESSURE FUEL DELIVERY PIPE ASSEMBLY .

  5. When disassembling the cylinder head LH
    NOTE: When disassembling the cylinder head LH, perform the following steps also.
    1. Remove the spark plug #2 and spark plug #4. Refer to IGNITION (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>SPARK PLUG>REMOVAL>LH SIDE .
    2. Remove the fuel injector LH. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>Fuel Injector>REMOVAL>FUEL INJECTOR LH .
  6. Set the part so that the cylinder head LH is on the upper side.
  7. Loosen the bolts securing the cylinder head LH equally, a little at a time in numerical sequence as shown in the figure and remove the cylinder head bolt.
    Fig 1: Cylinder Head LH Bolts Loosening Sequence
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. While tapping the cylinder head LH with a plastic hammer, separate to remove it from the cylinder block LH.
  9. Remove the cylinder head gasket LH.
    CAUTION: Be careful not to scratch the mating surface of cylinder head and cylinder block.
  10. Remove the water jacket spacer from the cylinder block LH.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  11. Remove the liquid gasket from cylinder head LH and cylinder block LH.
