DTC P0A0A-13: High Voltage System Interlock Circuit Open: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

1. - 

Outline of diagnosis: 

Signal line of interlock was open-circuited during vehicle driving (at vehicle speed of 5 km/h (3.1 MPH) or more)

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

Normal driving

Wiring diagram: 

Hybrid system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK DTC  .

    Read the DTC of [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to PHEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    NOTE:

    P060687 HYBRID/EV POWERTRAIN CONTROL MODULE PROCESSOR TO MONITORING PROCESSOR MISSING MESSAGE
    P060A47 HYBRID/EV POWERTRAIN CONTROL MODULE MONITORING PROCESSOR WATCHDOG/SAFETY MCU FAILURE
    P060A87 HYBRID/EV POWERTRAIN CONTROL MODULE PROCESSOR FROM MONITORING PROCESSOR MISSING MESSAGE

    P1CE349 HYBRID/EV POWERTRAIN CONTROL MODULE MONITORING PROCESSOR A/D PROCESSING INTERNAL ELECTRONIC FAILURE
    P1CE371 HYBRID/EV POWERTRAIN CONTROL MODULE MONITORING PROCESSOR A/D PROCESSING ACTUATOR STUCK

    Power supply system malfunction P06881F ECM/PCM POWER RELAY SENSE CIRCUIT INTERMITTENT
    System malfunction P1C9E9F HYBRID/EV SYSTEM RESET STUCK OFF

    Are any DTCs listed in the reference above displayed with P0A0A13 or P0A0A92?

    Yes:  Perform the diagnosis for the simultaneously displayed DTCs.

    NOTE: If more than one DTC is simultaneously displayed, perform the diagnosis in the order described in the above.

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK DTC  .
    1. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, perform the clear memory of [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control]. Refer to COMMON (DIAG)>CLEAR MEMORY .
    2. Turn to IG OFF, and wait for 30 seconds or more.
    3. Turn to IG ON, and wait for five seconds or more.
    4. Read the DTC of [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is DTC P0A0A13 or P0A0A92 displayed?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Go to  9.

  3. CHECK Service plug (IN THE INSTALLED CONDITION)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.

    Check that Service plug is installed properly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .

    CAUTION: Insert Service plug securely until a clicking sound is heard.
    NOTE: DTC P0A0A92 is displayed when Service plug is removed and IG ON is turned on also. Therefore, check the condition at the fault occurrence.

    Is Service plug securely installed?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Securely install Service plug.

    NOTE: Insert Service plug securely until a clicking sound is heard.
  4. CHECK interlock CONNECTOR (INSTALLATION CONDITION)  .
    CAUTION:
    • Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    • Never turn to READY ON with Service plug removed to prevent faults.
    1. Check that the Service plug is disconnected. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .
    2. Check the installation condition of the interlock connector (HV80) at Service plug.

    Is the interlock connector securely installed?

    Yes:  Go to  5.

    No:  Install the interlock connector securely.

  5. CHECK CONNECTION CONDITION OF CONNECTOR (Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly CONNECTOR)  .

    Check the connecting condition of connector (i370) in Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly and check the relevant terminals for contact pressure, deformation and intrusion of water or foreign matter. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL MODULE .

    Is there looseness at the connector mating, or any poor contact, deformation or intrusion of water or foreign matter at the relevant terminals?

    Yes:  Connect the connector securely. Or repair the poor contact of connector.

    No:  Go to  6.

  6. CHECK Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly  .
    CAUTION:
    • Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    • Never turn to READY ON with Service plug removed to prevent faults.
    1. Disconnect interlock connector (HV80) at Service plug.
    2. Connect the ground terminal to Battery (12V). Refer to STARTING/CHARGING SYSTEMS (H4DO (HEV))>BATTERY .
    3. Turn to IG ON.
    4. Using a tester, measure the voltage between interlock connector at Service plug and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (HV80) No. 1 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    Is the voltage 11 - 14 V?

    Yes:  Go to  7.

    No:  Go to  10.

  7. CHECK THE Service plug UNIT  .
    1. Turn the ignition to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the ground terminal from Battery (12V). Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    3. Check the interlock connecting condition of Service plug.

    Is it free from any trace of entering or adhering of dirt or foreign matter that can cause mating failure?

    Yes:  Go to  8.

    No:  Replace Service plug. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .

  8. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Service plug - CHASSIS GROUND)  .
    CAUTION:
    • Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    • Never turn to READY ON with Service plug removed to prevent faults.

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between the interlock connector at Service plug and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (HV80) No. 2 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  9.

    No:  Repair or replace Wire harness between interlock connector at Service plug and chassis ground.

  9. CHECK CONNECTING CONDITION OF CONNECTOR  .

    Check the connecting condition of each connector and check the relevant terminals for contact pressure, deformation and intrusion of water or foreign matter.

    Is it free from any trace of entering or adhering of dirt or foreign matter that can cause mating failure? Is there looseness at the connector mating, or any poor contact, deformation or intrusion of water or foreign matter at the relevant terminals?

    Yes:  Replace Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL MODULE .

    No:  Connect the connector securely. Or repair the poor contact of connector.

  10. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Hybrid vehicle control ECU 

    assembly - interlock CONNECTOR AT Service plug) (OPEN).

    CAUTION:
    • Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    • Never turn to READY ON with Service plug removed to prevent faults.
    1. Check that the Service plug is disconnected. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .
    2. Turn the ignition to OFF.
    3. Disconnect the ground terminal from Battery (12V). Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    4. Disconnect Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector (i370).
    5. Using a tester, measure the resistance between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector and interlock connector in Service plug portion.

    Connector & terminal 

    (i370) No. 20 - (HV80) No. 1:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Replace Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL MODULE .

    No:  Repair or replace Wire harness between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector and interlock connector at Service plug.
