Malfunction Description

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly detects a malfunction that the emergency shutdown line (HSDN) for the inverter cannot shut down. Perform detection at starting with IG ON and with READY OFF.

The following cases are assumed to be malfunction causes:

Malfunction on the shutdown path from Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly to Inverter with converter assembly

  • Malfunction of Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly
  • Malfunction of connector and Wire harness (between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly - Inverter with converter assembly)
  • Malfunction of Inverter with converter assembly
