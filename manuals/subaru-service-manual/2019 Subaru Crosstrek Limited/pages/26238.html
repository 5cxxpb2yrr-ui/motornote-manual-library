DTC P0A96-15: Hybrid/EV Battery Cooling Fan 2 Circuit Short To Auxiliary Battery Or Open: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Outline of diagnosis: 

If the speed calculated from the output frequency (FP0) of Battery cooling blower assembly LH is out of specification with respect to the control target speed

At a rotation command less than a certain value, the rotation speed of Battery cooling blower assembly LH is high.

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

Normal driving

NOTE: For the diagnostic procedure, refer to DTC P0A8115. Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE WITH DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>DTC P0A8115 HYBRID/EV BATTERY COOLING FAN 1 CIRCUIT SHORT TO AUXILIARY BATTERY OR OPEN .
