Front Door

  1. Raise the glass assembly - front door to the top position.
  2. Remove the cover - handle front outer.
    1. Remove the plug at the rear end of the panel assembly - front door.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Loosen the TORX® bolt and remove the cover - handle front outer.

      Preparation tool: 

      TORX® T30

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Replace the key cylinder.
    1. Release the claws.
    2. Remove the key cylinder (b) from the cover - handle front outer (a), and replace the key cylinder.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Install the cover - handle front outer.
    CAUTION:
    • When installing the cover - handle front outer on driver's side, make sure that the key cylinder shaft is correctly inserted into the latch & actuator assembly - front.
    • After installation, perform the operation check using the key.

    Preparation tool: 

    TORX® T30

    Tightening torque: 

    7.5 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.5 ft-lb)
