DTC P0A81-11: Hybrid/EV Battery Cooling Fan 1 Circuit Short To Ground: Notes

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

1. - 

Outline of diagnosis: 

If the speed calculated from the output frequency (FP0) of Battery cooling blower assembly RH is out of specification with respect to the control target speed

At a rotation command at a certain value or more, the rotation speed of Battery cooling blower assembly RH is unusually low (assuming GND short/open in related circuits, +B short, motor lock).

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

Normal driving

Wiring diagram: 

Hybrid system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .

  3. PERFORM ACTIVE TEST (Control the Hybrid Battery Cooling Fan). 
    1. Turn to IG ON.
    2. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, perform the clear memory of [P-HEV High Voltage Battery]. Refer to COMMON (DIAG)>CLEAR MEMORY .
    3. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, select the following items in the [Active Test] of [P-HEV High Voltage Battery]. Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>ACTIVE TEST .
      • Active test item: Control the Hybrid Battery Cooling Fan
      NOTE: Perform with HV battery intake air temperature at -10°C (14°F) or more.
    4. Select fan mode 6 to operate the Battery cooling blower assembly RH.
      NOTE: If the active test cannot be performed, a command for Battery cooling blower assembly RH to drive may be executed due to failsafe of Battery cooling blower assembly RH. In this case, proceed to the following operation check without performing active test.
    5. Check that the Battery cooling blower assembly RH operates, the air is drawn at the intake air port of HV battery, and operating sound is heard.
      NOTE: Even when the [Control the Hybrid Battery Cooling Fan] is stopped, Battery cooling blower assembly RH may continue to operate under HV system control. However this is not a malfunction.
