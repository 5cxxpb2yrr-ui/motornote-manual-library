Explanation For Circuits

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Inverter water pump transmits the revolution speed information to Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly. Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly monitors this revolution speed, and detects malfunctions.
