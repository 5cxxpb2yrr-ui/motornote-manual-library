Front Hood Opener (CROSSTREK Model)

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery and wait for at least 60 seconds before starting work. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the cover assembly - instrument panel LWR driver OUT. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>INSTRUMENT PANEL LOWER COVER>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the mud guard - front LH. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>MUD GUARD>REMOVAL .
  4. Remove the cable - front hood.
    1. Remove the cable - front hood from the lock assembly - front hood, and then attach a string to the end of the cable - front hood.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
      NOTE: A string makes operation easier during installation.
    2. Remove the nut.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Remove the cable clip, and remove the cable - front hood.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    4. Remove the attached string from the end of the cable - front hood.
      NOTE: Leave the string attached to the body, and use it during installation.
  5. Remove the bolt, and remove the bracket - opener handle.
