Backup Battery

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the data communication module. Refer to ENTERTAINMENT & MONITORING>DATA COMMUNICATION MODULE>REMOVAL>DATA COMMUNICATION MODULE .
  3. Remove the backup battery.
    1. Remove the screws, and then remove the battery cover.

      Preparation tool: 

      TORX® T10

    2. Disconnect the connector and remove the backup battery.
