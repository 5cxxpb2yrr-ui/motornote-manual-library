Clutch Pedal: Inspection

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Move the clutch pedal pads in the lateral direction with a force of approximately 10 N (1 kgf, 2 lbf) to check that the clutch pedal deflection is within the limit.

CAUTION: If the deflection exceeds the limit, replace the clutch pedal assembly with a new part.

Service limit: 

4.0 mm (0.157 in) or less
