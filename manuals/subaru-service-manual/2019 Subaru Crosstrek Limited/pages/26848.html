DTC P0560-14: System Voltage (Batt) Circuit Short To Ground Or Open: Notes

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

1. - 

Outline of diagnosis: 

Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly backup power supply system malfunction (1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

-

Wiring diagram: 

Hybrid system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK CONNECTION CONDITION OF CONNECTOR (Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly CONNECTOR)  .

    Check the connecting condition of connector in Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly and check the relevant terminals for contact pressure, deformation and intrusion of water or foreign matter. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL MODULE .

    Is there looseness at the connector mating, or any poor contact, deformation or intrusion of water or foreign matter at the relevant terminals?

    Yes:  Connect the connector securely. Or repair the poor contact of connector.

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly - F/B NO. 25 FUSE)  .

    Using a tester, measure the voltage between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector terminals. (With the connector connected)

    Connector & terminal 

    (i370) No. 1 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    Is the voltage 11 - 14 V?

    Yes:  Replace Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL MODULE .

    No:  Go to  3.

  3. CHECK FUSE (F/B NO. 25 FUSE)  .
    1. Remove the F/B NO. 25 FUSE from the Relay & fuse box (F/B). Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>RELAY AND FUSE .
    2. Check F/B NO. 25 FUSE.

    Is the fuse OK?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Replace the fuse.

  4. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (F/B NO. 25 FUSE - BATTERY POSITIVE TERMINAL) (OPEN)  .
    1. Disconnect the ground terminal from Battery (12V). Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    2. Disconnect the positive terminal of Battery (12V). Refer to STARTING/CHARGING SYSTEMS (H4DO (HEV))>BATTERY .
    3. Using a tester, measure the resistance between Relay & fuse box (F/B) and Battery (12V) positive cable.

    Connector & terminal 

    F/B NO. 25 FUSE (battery side) - Battery (12V) positive cable:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  5.

    No:  Repair or replace Wire harness between Relay & fuse box (F/B) and Battery (12V) positive cable.

  5. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (F/B NO. 25 FUSE - BATTERY POSITIVE TERMINAL) (SHORT TO GROUND)  .

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between Relay & fuse box (F/B) and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    F/B NO. 25 FUSE (battery side) - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance 10 kΩ or more?

    Yes:  Go to  6.

    No:  Repair or replace Wire harness between Relay & fuse box (F/B) and Battery (12V) positive cable.

  6. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (F/B NO. 25 FUSE - Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly) (OPEN)  .
    1. Connect the ground terminal to Battery (12V). Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    2. Disconnect Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector (i370).
    3. Using a tester, measure the resistance between Relay & fuse box (F/B) and Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector.

    Connector & terminal 

    F/B NO. 25 FUSE (module side) - (i370) No. 1:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Replace Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL MODULE .

    No:  Repair or replace Wire harness between Relay & fuse box (F/B) and Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly connector.
