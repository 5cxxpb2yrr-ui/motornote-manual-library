Back-Up Light Switch

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
  2. Disconnect the back light switch connector.
  3. Measure the resistance between the back-up light switch terminals.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Gear shift position Terminal No. Specified resistance
    Reverse position 1 and 2 Less than 1 Ω
    Other positions 1 MΩ or more
  4. Replace faulty parts.
