DTC B2824: Stop Lamp Relay Abnormal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

DTC detecting condition: 

  • Defective stop light relay

Trouble symptom: 

  • All functions of EyeSight system do not operate.
  • EyeSight warning light illuminates.
    NOTE: Check the electric brake booster control module (EBBCM) when this DTC is displayed. Refer to ELECTRIC BRAKE BOOSTER (DIAGNOSTICS)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .
