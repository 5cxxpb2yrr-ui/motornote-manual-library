Electric Power Steering Gearbox: Removal

CAUTION:
  • The power steering control module continues to operate after the engine stops and calculates the temperature in the control module. Therefore, before starting service of the power steering system which requires disconnection of the connector, stop the engine and allow approx. 30 minutes until the control module becomes cold.
  • Be careful not to let any foreign matter (dust, water, oil, etc.) enter into the power steering control module connector when removing or installing. If a foreign matter enters, completely remove it.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery, Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY>GROUND TERMINAL REMOVAL OR INSTALLATION .
  2. Lift up the vehicle, and then remove the front wheels.
  3. Remove the under cover - front, Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>FRONT UNDER COVER>REMOVAL .
  4. Remove the crossmember support - rear and the crossmember support - front, Refer to FRONT SUSPENSION>CROSSMEMBER SUPPORT>REMOVAL .
  5. Remove the front exhaust pipe. Refer to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FRONT EXHAUST PIPE>REMOVAL .
  6. Remove the stabilizers. Refer to FRONT SUSPENSION>STABILIZER>REMOVAL>STABILIZER .
  7. Disconnect the tie-rod end. (Impreza model)
    NOTE: Securely hook the tie-rod ball joint puller (e) to the collar (f).
    1. Pull out the cotter pin (a).
    2. Remove the castle nut (b) and washer (c).
    3. Using a tie-rod ball joint puller (e), detach the tie-rod end (d).

      Preparation tool: 

      Tie-rod ball joint puller

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Disconnect the tie-rod end. (Crosstrek model)
    NOTE: Securely hook the tie-rod ball joint puller (d) to the collar.
    1. Pull out the cotter pin (a).
    2. Remove the castle nut (b).
    3. Using a tie-rod ball joint puller (d), remove the tie-rod end (c).

      Preparation tool: 

      Tie-rod ball joint puller

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Remove the front arm assembly. Refer to FRONT SUSPENSION>FRONT ARM>REMOVAL .
  10. Remove the universal joint assembly - steering. Refer to POWER ASSISTED SYSTEM (POWER STEERING)>UNIVERSAL JOINT>REMOVAL .
  11. Disconnect the power steering harness connector and harness clamp.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  12. Remove the steering gearbox assembly from the crossmember COMPL.
    CAUTION: When lowering the transmission jack, make sure that the tie-rod end and the drive shaft boot do not interfere. The drive shaft boot may be damaged.
    1. Support the steering gearbox assembly from the bottom side using a transmission jack.
    2. Remove the bolts securing the steering gearbox assembly and stiffener to the crossmember COMPL.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Lower the transmission jack slowly, and lower the steering gearbox assembly.
