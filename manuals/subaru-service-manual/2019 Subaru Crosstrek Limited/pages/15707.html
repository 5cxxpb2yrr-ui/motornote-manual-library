Clearance Adjustment

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Loosen the lock nut, and adjust the stop light switch position until the clearance (A) between the threaded end of the stop light switch and stopper becomes 1.5 mm (0.06 in mm). Then, tighten the lock nut so that the stop light switch position is not displaced.

    Tightening torque: 

    8 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.9 ft-lb)

  2. Tighten the lock nut.
