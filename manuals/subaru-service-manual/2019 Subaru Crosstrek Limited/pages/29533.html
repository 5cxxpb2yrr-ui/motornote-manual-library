Symptoms And Causes: Inspection

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
Symptoms Problem parts etc.
Vehicle does not move when the select lever is in the "D range", "B range", or "R range".
  • Select cable
  • Inhibitor switch
  • Transmission body
Vehicle stalls and does not move when the select lever is in the "D range", "B range", or "R range".
  • Select cable
  • Transmission body
Vehicle does not keep at standstill with select lever in "P range", or parking cannot be released when shifting from "P range" to another range.
  • Select cable
  • Transmission body
Select lever does not shift smoothly.
  • Select cable
  • Inhibitor switch
  • Transmission body
