Bushing Front - Front Arm

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Before assembly, inspect the following items and replace any faulty part with a new one.
    • Check the front arm assembly for damage or cracks, and replace if defective.
    • Visually check the bushing for abnormal cracks, fatigue or damage.
    • Visually check the boot on the ball joint for damage.
  2. Align the alignment mark on the arm COMPL front to the split portion of the bushing intermediate plate of the bushing front - front arm.
  3. Using the ST and a press, install the bushing front - front arm.

    Preparation tool:
