Section 12 (Engine Cooling System (HEV)): Radiator: Installation

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Install the radiator lower cushion.
  2. Install the radiator side gasket (A) and radiator lower gasket (B) to the radiator.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Connect the radiator outlet hose (A), inverter radiator outlet hose (B) and radiator inlet hose (C) to the radiator.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Install the radiator fan & fan motor assembly to the radiator.

    Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>RADIATOR FAN AND FAN MOTOR ASSEMBLY>INSTALLATION .

  5. Install the condenser assembly to the radiator.

    Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>CONDENSER>INSTALLATION>EXTERIOR CONDENSER .

  6. Remove the clamp from the radiator fan shroud.
    NOTE: This procedure is performed to ensure the clearance at the time of installing the radiator.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Install the radiator, radiator fan & fan motor assembly, and the condenser assembly as an assembly.
  8. Install the clamp to the radiator fan shroud.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Remove the ropes or other items used to secure the CVTF cooler (air cool) to the vehicle.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  10. Install the CVTF cooler (air cool).

    Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TH2A)>CVTF COOLER (AIR COOLING)>INSTALLATION .

  11. Connect the pipe - evaporator cooling to the LOW solenoid valve.

    Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>HOSE AND PIPE>INSTALLATION>COOLING PIPE .

  12. Connect the connectors to the LOW solenoid valve.
  13. Connect the expansion valve - heater assembly to the condenser assembly.

    Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>EXPANSION VALVE>INSTALLATION>HEATER VALVE .

  14. Install the radiator upper cushion to the pin at the upper part of the radiator.
  15. Set the radiator upper frame together with the A/C accumulator assembly, and install the upper side bolts securing the radiator upper frame.
    NOTE: Securely install the radiator upper cushion to the radiator upper frame.

    Tightening torque: 

    18 N.m (1.8 kgf-m, 13.3 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  16. Connect the hose - pressure suction COMPL to the pipe - return assembly.

    Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>HOSE AND PIPE>INSTALLATION>RETURN HOSE .

  17. Connect the hose - pressure suction to the A/C accumulator assembly.

    Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>HOSE AND PIPE>INSTALLATION>LOW-PRESSURE HOSE .

  18. Connect the radiator inlet hose (A) and over flow hose (B).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  19. Connect the connector to the horn and install the clip.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  20. Install the front hood lock assembly.

    Refer to SECURITY AND LOCKS>FRONT HOOD LOCK ASSEMBLY>INSTALLATION .

  21. Install the bracket - bumper UPR.

    Tightening torque: 

    7.5 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.5 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  22. Install the front bumper.

    Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>FRONT BUMPER>INSTALLATION .

  23. Install the reservoir tank.

    Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>RESERVOIR TANK>INSTALLATION .

  24. Connect the hose - pressure suction valve to the hose - pressure suction COMPL.

    Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>HOSE AND PIPE>INSTALLATION>LOW PRESSURE VALVE HOSE .

  25. Connect the radiator outlet hose to the thermostat cover and connect the inverter radiator inlet hose to the radiator.
  26. Connect the main fan motor connector, sub fan motor connector and hood switch connector.
  27. Install the lower side bolts securing the radiator upper frame.

    Tightening torque: 

    18 N.m (1.8 kgf-m, 13.3 ft-lb)

  28. Install the front exhaust pipe.

    Refer to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FRONT EXHAUST PIPE>INSTALLATION .

  29. Install the inverter tank.

    Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>INVERTER TANK>INSTALLATION .

  30. Install the collector cover.

    Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>COLLECTOR COVER>INSTALLATION .

  31. Charge refrigerant.

    Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>REFRIGERANT CHARGING PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .

  32. Connect the battery ground terminal.

    Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .

  33. Fill engine coolant. (Inverter assembly side)

    Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>ENGINE COOLANT>REPLACEMENT>FILLING OF ENGINE COOLANT>INVERTER ASSEMBLY SIDE .

  34. Fill engine coolant. (Engine side)

    Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>ENGINE COOLANT>REPLACEMENT>FILLING OF ENGINE COOLANT>ENGINE SIDE .
