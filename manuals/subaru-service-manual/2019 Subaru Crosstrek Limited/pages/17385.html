Poor Ride Comfort

  1. Large rebound shock
  2. Rocking of the vehicle continues too long after running over bump and hump.
  3. Excessive shock in bumping
    Possible cause Corrective action
    (1) Damaged coil spring Replace.
    (2) Overinflation of tires Adjust to the specified air pressure.
    (3) Improper suspension height Replace the coil springs with new parts.
    (4) Defective operation of shock absorber Replace.
    (5) Damage or deformation of shock absorber mount Replace.
    (6) Unsuitable length (maximum or minimum) of shock absorber Replace with appropriate parts.
    (7) Deformation or loss of bushing Replace.
    (8) Deformation or damage of helper in shock absorber Replace.
    (9) Oil leakage from the shock absorber Replace.
