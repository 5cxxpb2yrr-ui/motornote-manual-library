Glove Box Lid

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Remove the pocket assembly. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>GLOVE BOX>REMOVAL .
  2. Replace the key cylinder.
    1. Pull up the lock knob.
    2. Push out the key cylinder from the pocket assembly while pressing in the metal plate.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Install the pocket assembly. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>GLOVE BOX>INSTALLATION .
