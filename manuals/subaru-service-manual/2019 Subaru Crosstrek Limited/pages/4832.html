Outline Of Diagnosis

When the vehicle is being driven, if the value of the speed sensor which is outputting the lowest vehicle speed is significantly lower than the values of the other speed sensors, or the value of the speed sensor which is outputting the lowest vehicle speed is 0 MPH, the electric brake booster control module judges that the speed sensor is malfunctioning and illuminates the MIL and stores a DTC.
