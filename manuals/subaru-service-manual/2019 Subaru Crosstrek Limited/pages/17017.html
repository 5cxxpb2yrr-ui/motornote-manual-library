Outline Of Diagnosis

Detect malfunctions of the thermostat function.

Judge as NG when any one of the following conditions is established.

  • When the actual engine coolant temperature does not reach the maximum temperature necessary to perform other OBDII diagnosis and Z (Estimated engine coolant temperature - actual engine coolant temperature) exceeded the predetermined value. (Judgment 1)
  • When the actual engine coolant temperature does not reach the predetermined value and Σ(Estimated engine coolant temperature - actual engine coolant temperature) exceeded the predetermined value. (Judgment 2)
  • When the actual engine coolant temperature does not reach the predetermined value. (Judgment 3)
