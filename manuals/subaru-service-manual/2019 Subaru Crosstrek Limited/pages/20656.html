Power Window Control Switch: Inspection: Sub-Switch

SWITCH - POWER WINDOW SUB FRONT 

  1. Measure the resistance between terminals of the switch - power window sub front.

    Preparation tool: 

    Circuit tester

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • Passenger's seat sub
    Terminal No. Inspection conditions Standard
    6 - 5
    6 - 7    		 OFF Less than 1 Ω
    2 - 5
    2 - 6    		 1 MΩ or more
    2 - 6
    5 - 7    		 Manual UP Less than 1 Ω
    2 - 5
    6 - 7    		 1 MΩ or more
    2 - 5
    6 - 7    		 Manual DOWN Less than 1 Ω
    2 - 6
    5 - 7    		 1 MΩ or more
  2. If the inspection result is not within the standard, replace the switch - power window sub front.
