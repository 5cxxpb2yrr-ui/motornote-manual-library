DTC B2328: Rear Radar Internal Failure (Radar Misalignment)

Detecting condition: 

  • Excessive impact was given to the installation area of the radar sensor, which resulted in deviation of the radar sensor axis.
  • Mounting bracket for the radar sensor is deformed.
  • Defective radar sensor

TROUBLE SYMPTOMS: 

  • All functions of BSD/RCTA are temporarily suspended.
  • The icon indicating function suspended will be displayed in the combination meter LCD display.
  • Function-suspended confirmation will be displayed for approx. two seconds on LCD display in the combination meter.
    NOTE: If DTC B2328 is detected repeatedly, check or replace the part corresponding to the cancel code.
  1. CHECK REAR BUMPER. 

    Check the rear bumper. (Stickered, trace of repainting, etc.)

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Replace or repair the bumper. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>REAR BUMPER . Go to  2.

  2. CHECK RADAR SENSOR BRACKET. 
    1. Remove the rear bumper. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>REAR BUMPER .
    2. Check the mounting bracket of the radar sensor as well as its installation position. (Visible damage, deformation, etc.) Refer to BLIND SPOT DETECTION/REAR CROSS TRAFFIC ALERT>RADAR SENSOR .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Replace the mounting bracket of the radar sensor, or repair the installation position. Refer to BLIND SPOT DETECTION/REAR CROSS TRAFFIC ALERT>RADAR SENSOR .

    Go to  3.

  3. ADJUSTMENT OF RADAR SENSOR AXIS. 
    1. Install the rear bumper.
    2. Perform the adjustment of radar axis. Refer to BLIND SPOT DETECTION/REAR CROSS TRAFFIC ALERT>RADAR SENSOR>ADJUSTMENT .
    3. Read the [Cancel Code] of [Blind Spot Detection/Rear Cross Traffic Alert (RH)] or [Blind Spot Detection/Rear Cross Traffic Alert (LH)] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to BLIND SPOT DETECTION/REAR CROSS TRAFFIC ALERT (DIAGNOSTICS)>CANCEL CODE .

    Is B2328 displayed?

    Yes:  Replace the radar sensor. Refer to BLIND SPOT DETECTION/REAR CROSS TRAFFIC ALERT>RADAR SENSOR .

    No:  The circuit has returned to a normal condition at this time. Reproduce the failure, and then perform the diagnosis again.

    NOTE: In this case, temporary poor contact of connector, temporary open or short circuit of harness may be the cause.
