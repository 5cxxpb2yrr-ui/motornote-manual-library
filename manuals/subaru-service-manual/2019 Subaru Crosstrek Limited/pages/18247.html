Rear Gate Damper Stay

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

CAUTION: Gas is colorless, odorless, and harmless. However, gas pressure may spray cutting powder or oil. Be sure to wear dust-resistant goggles.
  1. Cover with a vinyl sack as shown in the figure.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    NOTE: Prevent the vinyl sack from being caught by the cutting edge of a sander or saw.
  2. Lift the body side slightly with piston rods fully extended, and secure the body side with vise.

    Make a hole of 2 - 3 mm (0.08 - 0.12 in) diameter at a point 20 - 200 mm (0.79 - 7.87 in) from the body side by using a sander or saw, and bleed the gas in the rear gate damper stay completely.

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
