Day Time Running Light Assembly: Inspection

  1. Apply battery voltage between the connector terminals to check the lighting condition of the light.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Terminal No. Inspection conditions Specification
    1 (+) - 2 (-) Apply battery voltage. Light ON
  2. Replace the daytime running light assembly if it is found defective.
    NOTE: Since the daytime running light assembly uses LED (Light Emitting Diode), replace the daytime running light assembly if the light does not illuminate.
