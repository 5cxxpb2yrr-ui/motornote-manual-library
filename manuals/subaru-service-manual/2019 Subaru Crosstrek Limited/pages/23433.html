DTC P05C0: Active Grille Air Shutter Module "A" Over Temperature: Notes

1. - 

DTC detecting condition: 

Immediately at fault recognition

NOTE: After the faulty parts are repaired or replaced, perform the final check in Basic Diagnostic Procedure. Refer to ENGINE (DIAGNOSTICS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .
  1. CHECK DTC  .
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF, and cool down the engine enough.
      NOTE: Cool down the engine unit until it can be easily touched by hand.
    2. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    3. Using the Subaru Select Monitor or a general scan tool, read the DTC of [Engine]. Refer to ENGINE (DIAGNOSTICS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

      Is DTC P05C0 displayed? (Current malfunction)

      Replace the active grille shutter. Refer to COOLING (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ACTIVE GRILLE SHUTTER .

      Even if DTC is detected, the circuit has returned to a normal condition at this time.

      NOTE: In this case, there may have been a temporary rise of the temperature in the engine compartment.
