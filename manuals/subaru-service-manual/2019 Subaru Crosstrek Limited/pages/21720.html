DTC P0E31-1F: DC/DC Converter Voltage Sensor "A" (Vl) Circuit Intermittent: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Detecting condition: 

When DTC P0C7917, P0D3319, P1C5D19 or P1C5F19 error judgment occurs and generator inverter current sensor property malfunction, boosting converter voltage (VL) signal stuck to Low or stuck to High is detected

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

NOTE: For the diagnostic procedure, refer to DTC P0E3116. Refer to P-HEV DRIVE MOTOR (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE WITH DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>DTC P0E3116 DC/DC CONVERTER VOLTAGE SENSOR "A" (VL) CIRCUIT VOLTAGE BELOW THRESHOLD .
