DTC P0A2A-1F: Drive Motor "A" Temperature Sensor Circuit Intermittent: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Outline of diagnosis: 

Sudden change and hunting of Motor temperature sensor

When the value of Motor temperature sensor changed suddenly in a degree that cannot occur ordinarily and its offset continued for a predetermined amount of time or more, or when a temperature change that cannot occur ordinarily occurred repeatedly

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

Normal driving

NOTE: For the diagnostic procedure, refer to DTC P0A2A11. Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE WITH DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>DTC P0A2A11 DRIVE MOTOR "A" TEMPERATURE SENSOR CIRCUIT SHORT TO GROUND .
