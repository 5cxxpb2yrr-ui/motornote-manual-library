Engine Cooling System (Except For HEV): Radiator: Removal

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the V-belt covers.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Collect the refrigerant from A/C system. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>REFRIGERANT RECOVERY PROCEDURE .
    NOTE: This step is necessary only if the radiator and the condenser are removed as an assembly.
  4. Drain engine coolant. Refer to COOLING (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE COOLANT>REPLACEMENT>DRAINING OF ENGINE COOLANT .
  5. Remove the front exhaust pipe. Refer to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FRONT EXHAUST PIPE>REMOVAL .
  6. Remove the lower side bolts (A) securing the radiator upper frame.
  7. Disconnect main fan motor connector (B) and sub fan motor connector (C).
  8. Disconnect radiator outlet hose (D).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Remove the reservoir tank. Refer to COOLING (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>RESERVOIR TANK>REMOVAL .
  10. Remove the air intake duct. Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>AIR INTAKE DUCT>REMOVAL .
  11. Remove the front bumper. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>FRONT BUMPER>REMOVAL .
  12. Remove the cover UPR - front.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  13. Remove the bracket - bumper UPR.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  14. Remove the front hood lock assembly. Refer to SECURITY AND LOCKS>FRONT HOOD LOCK ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL .
  15. Disconnect the connector from the horn, and remove the clip.
    • Impreza model
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • Crosstrek model
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  16. Disconnect the radiator inlet hoses and over flow hose.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  17. Remove the upper side bolts securing the radiator upper frame, and remove the radiator upper frame.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  18. Remove the radiator upper cushion.
  19. When removing the condenser together
    1. Detach the hose - pressure discharge and the pipe - evaporator cooling. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>CONDENSER>REMOVAL .
    2. Remove the radiator, radiator fan & fan motor assembly, and the condenser as an assembly.
    3. Remove the condenser. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>CONDENSER>REMOVAL .
    4. Remove the radiator fan and fan motor assembly. Refer to COOLING (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>RADIATOR FAN AND FAN MOTOR ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL .
    5. Remove the radiator lower cushion.
    NOTE: Remove if necessary.
  20. When condenser is not removed
  1. Remove the pipe - evaporator cooling from the clip.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Disengage the claws, and separate the radiator and the condenser a part.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the radiator and the radiator fan & fan motor assembly as an assembly.
  4. Remove the radiator fan and fan motor assembly. Refer to COOLING (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>RADIATOR FAN AND FAN MOTOR ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL .
  5. Remove the radiator lower cushion.
NOTE: Remove if necessary.
