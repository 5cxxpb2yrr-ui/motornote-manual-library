Hybrid Model

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery.

    Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY>GROUND TERMINAL REMOVAL OR INSTALLATION .

  2. Take out the mat - rear floor.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the cap - rear suspension. (When removing the RH side)
    1. Release the claws (2 places) in the rear side of the vehicle.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
      NOTE: Remove from the slit part in the rear side of the vehicle using a plastic remover.
    2. Release the remaining claws (2 places) using the (a) line as an axis, then remove the cap - rear suspension by pulling it upward.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the trim panel - rear apron LH. (When removing the LH side)

    Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>REAR QUARTER TRIM>REMOVAL>5 DOOR MODEL (HYBRID) .

  5. Lift up the vehicle, and then remove the rear wheels.
  6. Remove the sensor assembly - headlight beam leveler.

    Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>REAR HEIGHT SENSOR>REMOVAL .

  7. Remove the bolts and nuts, and lower the rear lateral link (rear).
    1. Remove the bolts from the lower side of rear shock absorber assembly.
    2. Disconnect the rear axle housing from the rear lateral link (rear).
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Remove the nuts on the upper side of rear shock absorber assembly.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    NOTE: Use a tool such as a magnet tool to prevent the nuts from falling.
  9. Lower the rear lateral link (rear), and remove the rear shock absorber assembly.
