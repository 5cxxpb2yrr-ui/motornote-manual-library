Washer Fluid Level Sensor

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

CAUTION: Install the mud guard - front so that the front end of the mud guard (b), except for the clip fixing point, comes inside the bumper face (a).
Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  1. Align the protrusion (a) of the sensor - washer tank with a center position of double line marks (b) on the tank - washer side, and push the flat surface (c) to install.
    CAUTION:
    • Do not hit or apply any impact to the sensor - washer tank when it is reused, because it has a poor resistance to impact.
    • When the installation is difficult, apply washer fluid to the packing. Do not use silicone grease.
    • Impreza model
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • Crosstrek model
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. After installing the sensor - washer tank, perform the following confirmations.
    • There is no gap between the tank - washer and the packing.
    • There is no gap between the packing and the sensor - washer tank.
    • There is no turn over to the packing and its peripheral area.
  3. Connect the hose washer.
  4. Connect each connector.
  5. Add new washer fluid.
  6. Install the mud guard - front LH.
  7. Install the under cover - front.

    Tightening torque: 

    18 N.m (1.8 kgf-m, 13.3 ft-lb)

  8. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
