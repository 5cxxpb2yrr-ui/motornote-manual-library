Forced Drive

If the lid assembly - sunroof does not operate or the motor assembly is not supplied with power, move the lid assembly - sunroof using the hexagon wrench.

Preparation tool: 

Hexagon wrench: width across flat 4 mm (0.16 in)

CAUTION: After a forced operation, make sure to perform an initialize operation.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the light assembly - map. (Models without EyeSight) Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>SPOT MAP LIGHT>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the stereo camera cover assembly. (Models with EyeSight) Refer to EYESIGHT>STEREO CAMERA>REMOVAL .
  4. Insert the hexagon wrench securely until it touches the motor shaft end.
  5. Turn the hexagon wrench, and move the lid assembly - sunroof.
    • Turning right, the lid assembly - sunroof opens.
    • Turning left, the lid assembly - sunroof closes.
    CAUTION: Be careful not to damage the adjacent parts with the rod end when moving the lid assembly - sunroof with the hexagon wrench.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Install the light assembly - map. (Models without EyeSight) Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>SPOT MAP LIGHT>INSTALLATION .
  7. Install the stereo camera cover assembly. (Models with EyeSight) Refer to EYESIGHT>STEREO CAMERA>INSTALLATION .
  8. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  9. Perform the initialize operation. Refer to SUNROOF/T-TOP/CONVERTIBLE TOP (SUNROOF)>SUNROOF CONTROL SYSTEM>OPERATION>INITIALIZE OPERATION .
