INTRODUCTION

This bulletin provides a procedure and precautions to use when addressing a customer concern of an improper fit or gap condition of the center vent grille assembly as shown in the comparison photos below.

PRODUCTION CHANGE INFORMATION

The new procedure for vent grille installation was incorporated into production starting with VIN:

  • Impreza 4-Door: J3600807 
  • Impreza 5-Door: J3701944 
  • Crosstrek: TBD

REMOVAL

INSTALLATION

The vent grille assembly has a guide rib on the inside (audio head unit side) of the housing as shown in the photo below. The guide rib must align with the guide rib notch which is on the corresponding side of the audio head unit. Once the two are properly aligned, CAREFULLY  slide the vent grille assembly into position until seated in place.

NOTE: DO NOT force it into position.

Once the vent grille assembly is in position the use both hands and press firmly down from bottom to top  making sure it "clicks" into place. Insure there are no gaps as shown below. If the vent grille assembly still has a gap or does not feel like it is seating properly or binds as it is being installed, confirm the guide rib is properly aligned in the notch. Once alignment is confirmed, the vent grille assembly should slide into place normally.

After installation is completed, always confirm airflow direction and volume from the vent grille can be adjusted smoothly and any adjustments made are maintained regardless of fan speed selected.
