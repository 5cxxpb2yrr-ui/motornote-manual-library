Malfunction Description

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

This indicates that large current flew to the generator converter. The following cases are assumed to be causes of this symptom.

Part Main trouble contents
Transmission assembly
  • Short or open circuit of generator coil
  • Malfunction inside generator (damages caused by iron powder and foreign matter)
Resolver Open or short circuit of Generator resolver
Inverter
  • Inverter inner circuit malfunction
  • Malfunction of control module that controls inverter
  • Inverter controlling sensor malfunction (current sensor, voltage sensor (VH and VL), etc.)
Cooling System
  • Malfunction of Inverter water pump
  • Leakage of coolant
  • Freezing of coolant
  • Clogging in coolant passage
