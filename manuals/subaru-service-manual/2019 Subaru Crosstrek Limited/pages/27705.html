DTC P1C64-1F: Generator Control Module Circuit Intermittent: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. - 

    Detecting condition: 

    When DTC P0C7917, P0D3319, P1C5D19 or P1C5F19 error judgment occurs and excitation signal (REF signal) cycle malfunction for resolver angle detection are detected (1 trip detection logic)

    Trouble symptom: 

    -

    CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to P-HEV DRIVE MOTOR (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
    NOTE:
    • When the faulty phenomenon is not reproduced, perform a driving test on a road where vehicle vibration tends to occur so that the phenomenon may occur.
    • When the resolver has a malfunction, the vehicle may not be able to drive smoothly.
    • When it is difficult to check the connector for connecting condition, terminal deformation
    • poor crimping, reconnect the connector after disconnecting once, and read DTCs again to check reproduction of the condition.
    • It may be poor connector connection if the DTC condition is not reproduced. However, the value may temporarily be outside the DTC detection threshold due to "vibrations", "temperature change due to elapsed time", etc. because of the connector inspection procedure. Therefore, be sure to perform the following inspection to check that there is no abnormality in other areas.
  1. CHECK DTC. 

    Read the DTC of [P-HEV Drive Motor] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to P-HEV DRIVE MOTOR (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    NOTE:
    Related DTC
    P0A3F16 DRIVE MOTOR "A" POSITION SENSOR CIRCUIT VOLTAGE BELOW THRESHOLD
    P0A4B16 GENERATOR POSITION SENSOR CIRCUIT VOLTAGE BELOW THRESHOLD
    P0C6413 GENERATOR POSITION SENSOR CIRCUIT "A" CIRCUIT OPEN
    P0C6416 GENERATOR POSITION SENSOR CIRCUIT "A" CIRCUIT VOLTAGE BELOW THRESHOLD
    P0C6417 GENERATOR POSITION SENSOR CIRCUIT "A" CIRCUIT VOLTAGE ABOVE THRESHOLD
    P0C6913 GENERATOR POSITION SENSOR CIRCUIT "B" CIRCUIT OPEN
    P0C6916 GENERATOR POSITION SENSOR CIRCUIT "B" CIRCUIT VOLTAGE BELOW THRESHOLD
    P0C6917 GENERATOR POSITION SENSOR CIRCUIT "B" CIRCUIT VOLTAGE ABOVE THRESHOLD

    When "Only DTC P1CAC49, P1CAF38 or P1C641F is displayed" or "DTC other than above is displayed at the same time", go to  A.

    When DTCs related to [P-HEV Drive Motor], mentioned above, are simultaneously displayed, go to  B.

    Yes: 

    1. Go to  2.
    2. Perform the diagnosis for the simultaneously displayed DTCs.
      NOTE: If more than one DTC is simultaneously displayed, perform the diagnosis in the order described in the above.

    No:  -

  2. CHECK THE CONNECTING CONDITION OF CONNECTOR (Inverter with converter assembly LOW PRESSURE CONNECTOR). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Check that the Service plug is disconnected. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .
    2. Check the connecting condition of low pressure connectors (HV23) and (HV24) in Inverter with converter assembly and check the relevant terminals for contact pressure, deformation and intrusion of water or foreign matter. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>INVERTER AND CONVERTER ASSEMBLY .
      NOTE: When connecting the connector, insert to the end with the lock lever raised, then fold the lock lever completely until a click is heard. After connected, make sure that the lock claw does not loosen and pull the connector to see that it does not come off.

      Is there looseness at the connector connection, or any poor contact, deformation or intrusion of water or foreign matter at the relevant terminals?

      Yes:  Connect the connector securely. Or repair the poor contact of connector.

      No:  Go to  3.

  3. CHECK GENERATOR RESOLVER CIRCUIT. 

    Check GENERATOR RESOLVER CIRCUIT. Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>GENERATOR RESOLVER CIRCUIT .

    Is the GENERATOR RESOLVER CIRCUIT OK?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Repair according to GENERATOR RESOLVER CIRCUIT inspection. And then, Go to  4.

  4. CHECK MOTOR RESOLVER CIRCUIT. 

    Check MOTOR RESOLVER CIRCUIT. Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>MOTOR RESOLVER CIRCUIT .

    Is the MOTOR RESOLVER CIRCUIT OK?

    Yes:  Replace Inverter with converter assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>INVERTER AND CONVERTER ASSEMBLY .

    No:  Repair according to MOTOR RESOLVER CIRCUIT inspection.
