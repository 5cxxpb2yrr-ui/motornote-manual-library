Section 3 (HVAC System (Heater, Ventilator And A/C) (HEV)): General Description: Caution: Refrigerant

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  • A refrigerant other than HFC-134a cannot be used in a HFC-134a A/C system. HFC-134a refrigerant also cannot be used in any system other than a HFC-134a A/C system.
  • If an incorrect or no refrigerant is used, it will result in poor lubrication and the compressor itself may be damaged.
