BODY PLUGS FOR TIE-DOWN ACCESS

BODY PLUGS FOR TIE-DOWN ACCESS

TECHNICAL SERVICE BULLETIN

Reference Number(s): 01-178-18, Date of Issue:  December 28, 2018
SUBARU: 2019 Legacy and Outback, Impreza 2.0, Ascent

APPLICABILITY

2019MY Legacy and Outback

2019MY Impreza 2.0

2019MY Ascent

INTRODUCTION

This Service Information bulletin announces a procedure change regarding installation of the body plugs used to close the tie-down chain access holes in the vehicle underbody. Previously, these body plugs were placed in the glove box during vehicle assembly and installed by Retailers as part of the Pre-Delivery Inspection (PDI) procedure. Recently, the use of "soft" tie-down straps for vehicle securement during transport has replaced the tie-down chains (no longer a SOA-accepted securement method) used previously. For this reason, the access hole body plugs are now installed during vehicle assembly.

IMPORTANT NOTES: 

  • SOA strongly  recommends the chain-type tie down method for vehicle securement not be used other than for emergency transport.
  • If any of these body plugs are found missing, they must  be installed or replaced.
