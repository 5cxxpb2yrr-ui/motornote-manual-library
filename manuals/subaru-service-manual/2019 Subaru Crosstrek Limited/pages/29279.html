DTC P1C26-17: Hybrid/EV Battery Charger Hybrid/EV Battery Input Voltage Circuit Voltage Above Threshold: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. - 

    Outline of diagnosis: 

    VCHG voltage was at overvoltage level continuously for 3 seconds or more.

    (1 trip detection logic)

    Trouble symptom: 

    Plug-in charging suspended

    Wiring diagram: 

    Hybrid system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

    No:  -

  2. CHECK THE CONNECTION CONDITIONS OF THE CONNECTOR (Battery ECU assembly). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Turn the ignition to OFF.
    2. Check that the Service plug is disconnected. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .
    3. Check the connection conditions of Battery ECU assembly connector (HV42), and check the terminals for contact pressure, deformation, and intrusion of water or foreign substance.

      Is the connector connection loose or is there poor contact at the terminals, or intruding water or foreign substance?

      Yes:  Connect the connector securely. Or repair the poor contact of connector.

      No:  Go to  3.

  3. CHECK THE CONNECTION CONDITIONS OF THE CONNECTOR (HV battery junction block CONNECTOR). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.

    Check the connection conditions of the HV battery junction block connectors (HV49), (HV51) and (HV52), and check the terminals for contact pressure, deformation, and intrusion of water or foreign substance.

    Is the connector connection loose or is there poor contact at the terminals, or intruding water or foreign substance?

    Yes:  Connect the connector securely. Or repair the poor contact of connector.

    No:  Go to  4.

  4. CHECK THE Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Battery ECU assembly - HV battery junction block) (OPEN CIRCUIT). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Disconnect Battery ECU assembly connector (HV42).
    2. Disconnect HV battery junction block connectors (HV49) and (HV52).
    3. Using a tester, measure the resistance between Battery ECU assembly connector and HV battery junction block connector.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV42) No. 52 - (HV49) No. 3:

      (HV42) No. 51 - (HV52) No. 2:

      Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

      Yes:  Go to  5.

      No:  Repair or replace the Wire harness between Battery ECU assembly connector and HV battery junction block connector.

  5. CHECK THE Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Battery ECU assembly - HV battery junction block) (SHORTED TO GROUND AND SHORTED BETWEEN LINES). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between terminals in Battery ECU assembly connector, and between Battery ECU assembly connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (HV42) No. 52 - (HV42) No. 1 - 69:

    (HV42) No. 51 - (HV42) No. 1 - 69:

    (HV42) No. 52 - Chassis ground:

    (HV42) No. 51 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance 10 kΩ or more?

    Yes:  Go to  6.

    No:  Repair or replace the Wire harness between Battery ECU assembly connector and HV battery junction block connector.

  6. CHECK THE Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (HV battery junction block - CHASSIS GROUND) (OPEN CIRCUIT). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between HV battery junction block connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (HV49) No. 2 - Chassis ground:

    (HV52) No. 1 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  7.

    No:  Repair or replace the Wire harness between the HV battery junction block connector and chassis ground.

  7. CHECK THE CONNECTION CONDITIONS OF THE CONNECTOR (HV battery junction block CONNECTOR). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.

    Check the connection conditions of the HV battery junction block connectors (HV43) and (HV46), and check the terminals for contact pressure, deformation, and intrusion of water or foreign substance.

    Is the connector connection loose or is there poor contact at the terminals, or intruding water or foreign substance?

    Yes:  Connect the connector securely. Or repair the poor contact of connector.

    No:  Go to  8.

  8. CHECK THE CONNECTION CONDITIONS OF THE CONNECTOR (Electric vehicle charger assembly CONNECTOR). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.

    Check the connection conditions of Electric vehicle charger assembly connector (HV33), and check the terminals for contact pressure, deformation, and intrusion of water or foreign substance.

    Is the connector connection loose or is there poor contact at the terminals, or intruding water or foreign substance?

    Yes:  Connect the connector securely. Or repair the poor contact of connector.

    No:  Go to  9.

  9. CHECK THE Electric vehicle charger assembly UNIT. 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Disconnect Electric vehicle charger assembly connector (HV33).
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between Electric vehicle charger assembly terminals.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV33) No. 1 - (HV33) No. 2:

      Is the resistance less than 700 kΩ?

      Yes:  Go to  10.

      No:  Replace Electric vehicle charger assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>ELECTRIC VEHICLE CHARGER .

  10. CHECK THE CONNECTION CONDITIONS OF THE CONNECTOR (HV battery junction block CONNECTOR). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.

    Check the connection conditions of the HV battery junction block connectors (HV45) and (HV48), and check the terminals for contact pressure, deformation, and intrusion of water or foreign substance.

    Is the connector connection loose or is there poor contact at the terminals, or intruding water or foreign substance?

    Yes:  Connect the connector securely. Or repair the poor contact of connector.

    No:  Go to  11.

  11. CHECK HV battery charger cable. 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Disconnect HV battery junction block connectors (HV45) and (HV48).
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between Electric vehicle charger assembly connector and HV battery junction block connector.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV33) No. 1 - (HV45) No. 1:

      (HV33) No. 2 - (HV48) No. 1:

      Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

      Yes:  Go to  12.

      No:  Replace HV battery charger cable.

  12. CHECK THE HV battery junction block UNIT (CHRB). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Remove the HV battery junction block.
    2. Apply Battery (12V) voltage between terminals (HV49) No. 3 - (HV49) No. 2.
    3. Using a tester, measure the resistance between HV battery junction block terminals.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV45) No. 1 - (HV43) No. 1:

      Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

      Yes:  Replace HV battery junction block. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>RELAY AND FUSE .

      No:  Replace HV battery junction block. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>RELAY AND FUSE .
