Rear Brake Pad: Inspection

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Check the thickness of the pad - disc brake rear.
    NOTE:
    • Always replace the pads of both wheels and both sides as a set.
    • Replace pad clips if they are twisted or worn.
    • Replace the pad if there is oil or grease on it.
    • Wear indicators are installed on the pad - disc brake rear inner, and a squeaking sound is heard if the pad is worn to the limit.
    • Except for hybrid model

    • Hybrid model
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Standard (A) Wear limit (B)
    Pad thickness 9 mm (0.354 in) 1.5 mm (0.059 in)
  2. If the wear limit is exceeded in the inspection, replace the brake pad.
