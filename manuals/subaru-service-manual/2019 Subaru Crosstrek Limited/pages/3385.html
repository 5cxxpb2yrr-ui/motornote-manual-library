5 Door Model

CAUTION:
  • Before handling the airbag system components, refer to "CAUTION" of "General Description" in "AIRBAG SYSTEM". Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  • Airbag system satellite safing sensor is located under the front center of the rear seat cushion. Be careful not to apply strong impact to the sensor when working with the rear seat cushion removed.
  1. Adjust the front seat position.
    1. Remove the headrest.
    2. Move the seat to the front-most position and tilt the backrest rearward.
  2. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery and wait for at least 60 seconds before starting work. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  3. Remove the rear seat cushion assembly. Refer to SEATS>REAR SEAT>REMOVAL .
  4. Remove the lower inner trim on the left and right sides. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>LOWER INNER TRIM>REMOVAL .
  5. Remove the upper inner trim on the left and right sides. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>UPPER INNER TRIM>REMOVAL .
  6. Remove the right and left trim panels - rear apron and rear quarter trims UPR.
    • Except for hybrid model: Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>REAR QUARTER TRIM>REMOVAL>5 DOOR MODEL (EXCEPT FOR HYBRID) .
    • Hybrid model: Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>REAR QUARTER TRIM>REMOVAL>5 DOOR MODEL (HYBRID) .
  7. Remove the light assembly - map. (Models without EyeSight) Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>SPOT MAP LIGHT>REMOVAL .
  8. Remove the stereo camera cover assembly. (Models with EyeSight) Refer to EYESIGHT>STEREO CAMERA>REMOVAL .
  9. Remove the left and right sun visor assembly. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>SUN VISOR>REMOVAL .
  10. Remove the light assembly - room. Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>ROOM LIGHT>REMOVAL .
  11. Remove all assist grips. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>ASSIST GRIP>REMOVAL .
  12. Remove the garnish - roof. (Model with sunroof)
  13. Remove the trim panel - roof assembly.
    CAUTION: When taking out the trim panel - roof assembly from the vehicle, be sure to fold it to the roof side. Do not fold it to the cabin side. Otherwise it may be wrinkled.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    1. Remove the harness clamp of the cord assembly - antenna feeder and the cord - roof, and disconnect the connectors.
    2. Remove the clip.
    3. Remove the weather strip - body side flange from the front/rear and left/right doors, and remove the trim panel - roof assembly.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
