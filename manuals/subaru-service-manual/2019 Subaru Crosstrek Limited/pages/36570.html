Trunk Room Light: Removal

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

NOTE: Do not remove the light assembly - trunk room if removing the bulb only.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the light assembly - trunk room.
    1. Release the claws and pull out the light assembly - trunk room as shown in the figure.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Disconnect the connector and remove the light assembly - trunk room.
  3. Remove the trunk room light bulb.
    CAUTION:
    • Use a dry clean cloth so that no grease or water adheres to the glass portion of the bulb.
    • When using a flat tip screwdriver, use an appropriate sized tool for the insertion opening, apply protective tape or cloth around the tool, and be careful not to cause damage.
    1. Release the claws, and open the bulb cover.
    2. Remove the trunk room light bulb.
