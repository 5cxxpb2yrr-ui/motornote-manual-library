Passenger's Airbag Module

  1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
  2. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery and wait for at least 60 seconds before starting work. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  3. Remove the glove box assembly. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>GLOVE BOX>REMOVAL .
  4. Disconnect the passenger's airbag module connector. Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>AIRBAG CONNECTOR>PROCEDURE>PASSENGER'S AIRBAG MODULE AND SEAT BELT PRETENSIONER .
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Short the terminal to the alligator clip furnished as AIRBAG DEPLOYMENT TOOL.
    CAUTION: The AIRBAG DEPLOYMENT TOOL should be kept shorted on the terminals until just before activation of airbag module.

    Preparation tool:

    ST: AIRBAG DEPLOYMENT TOOL (98299 Pa030)

  6. Connect the AIRBAG DEPLOYMENT TOOL (A) and TEST HARNESS ADAPTER AM (B).

    Preparation tool:

    ST: TEST HARNESS ADAPTER AM (98299FJ000)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Connect the TEST HARNESS ADAPTER AM to the airbag connector.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Make sure there is no one inside the vehicle.
  9. Extend the wiring of AIRBAG DEPLOYMENT TOOL to the limit, and close all the windows and sunroof completely.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  10. Move the battery at least 5 m (16 ft) from the vehicle, and confirm safety in the area.
  11. Connect the AIRBAG DEPLOYMENT TOOL alligator clip to the battery negative (-) terminal.
  12. Connect the other cable of AIRBAG DEPLOYMENT TOOL to the battery positive (+) terminal, and activate the airbag module.
    CAUTION:
    • After activation, the airbag module will be extremely hot. Let it cool down for at least 40 minutes in an area with no one around.
    • Do not expose the activated airbag module to water.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  13. Remove the passenger's airbag module. Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>PASSENGER'S AIRBAG MODULE>REMOVAL .
  14. Place the activated airbag module in a sealed vinyl bag, then discard.
Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
