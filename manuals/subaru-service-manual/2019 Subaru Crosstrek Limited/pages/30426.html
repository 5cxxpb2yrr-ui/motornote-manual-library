Camshaft Position Sensor: Installation: Exhaust

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

• RH SIDE 

  1. Install the camshaft position sensor with new O-ring.
    NOTE: Apply engine oil to O-ring.

    Tightening torque: 

    6.4 N.m (0.7 kgf-m, 4.7 ft-lb)

  2. Connect the connector.
  3. Install the front under cover. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>FRONT UNDER COVER>INSTALLATION .
  4. Lower the vehicle.
  5. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .

  1. Install the camshaft position sensor with new O-ring.
    NOTE: Apply engine oil to O-ring.

    Tightening torque:

  2. Connect the connector.
  3. Install the front under cover. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>FRONT UNDER COVER>INSTALLATION .
