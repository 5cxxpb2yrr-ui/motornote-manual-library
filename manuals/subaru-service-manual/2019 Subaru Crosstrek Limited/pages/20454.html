DTC P0D0A-15: Hybrid/EV Battery Charging System Positive Contactor Control Circuit Short To Auxiliary Battery Or Open: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

1. - 

Outline of diagnosis: 

Open circuit in charge relay CHRB line or detour of +B power supply

Defective circuit on primary side of CHR (+)

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

Plug-in charging suspended

Wiring diagram: 

Hybrid system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK DATA MONITOR. 

    Using the Subaru Select Monitor, display the following items in the [Data monitor] of [P-HEV High Voltage Battery]. Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>DATA MONITOR .

    • AC Charging Positive Relay Status

    Is [AC Charging Positive Relay Status] OFF?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Go to  4.

  2. CHECK CONNECTOR CONNECTION CONDITION (Battery ECU assembly). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Check that the Service plug is disconnected. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .
    2. Check the connecting condition of connector (HV42) in Battery ECU assembly and check the relevant terminals for contact pressure.

    Is there looseness at the connector connection, or any poor contact at the relevant terminals?

    Yes:  Connect the connector securely. Or repair the poor contact of connector.

    No:  Go to  3.

  3. CHECK CONNECTOR CONNECTION CONDITION (HV battery junction block). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.

    Check the connecting condition of connector (HV49) in HV battery junction block and check the relevant terminals for contact pressure.

    Is there looseness at the connector connection, or any poor contact at the relevant terminals?

    Yes:  Connect the connector securely. Or repair the poor contact of connector.

    No:  Even if DTC is detected, the circuit has returned to a normal condition at this time. Reproduce the failure, and then perform the diagnosis again.

    NOTE: In this case, temporary poor contact of connector, temporary open or short circuit of Wire harness may be the cause.
  4. CHECK CONNECTOR CONNECTION CONDITION (Battery ECU assembly). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Check that the Service plug is disconnected. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .
    2. Check the connecting condition of connector (HV42) in Battery ECU assembly and check the relevant terminals for contact pressure.

    Is there looseness at the connector connection, or any poor contact at the relevant terminals?

    Yes:  Connect the connector securely. Or repair the poor contact of connector.

    No:  Go to  5.

  5. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (CHRB VOLTAGE). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Disconnect Battery ECU assembly connector (HV42).
    2. Connect the ground terminal to Battery (12V). Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    3. Turn to IG ON.
    4. Using a tester, measure the voltage between Battery ECU assembly connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV42) No. 52 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    Is the voltage less than 1 V?

    Yes:  Replace Battery ECU assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>BATTERY CONTROL MODULE .

    No:  Go to  6.

  6. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Battery ECU assembly - CHASSIS GROUND). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Turn the ignition to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the ground terminal from Battery (12V). Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    3. Using a tester, measure the resistance between Battery ECU assembly connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV42) No. 52 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance 20.6 - 40.8 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  7.

    No:  Go to  11.

  7. CHECK CONNECTOR CONNECTION CONDITION (HV battery junction block). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.

    Check the connecting condition of connector (HV49) in HV battery junction block and check the relevant terminals for contact pressure.

    Is there looseness at the connector connection, or any poor contact at the relevant terminals?

    Yes:  Connect the connector securely. Or repair the poor contact of connector.

    No:  Go to  8.

  8. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (OPEN). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Disconnect HV battery junction block connector (HV49).
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between Battery ECU assembly connector and HV battery junction block connector.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV42) No. 52 - (HV49) No. 3:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  9.

    No:  Replace the Wire harness between Battery ECU assembly connector and HV battery junction block.

  9. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (SHORT TO GROUND AND LINE-TO-LINE SHORT). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between terminals in Battery ECU assembly connector, between Battery ECU assembly connector and chassis ground, between terminals in HV battery junction block connector, and between HV battery junction block connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (HV42) No. 52 - (HV42) No. 1 - 69:

    (HV42) No. 52 - Chassis ground:

    (HV49) No. 3 - (HV49) No. 1 - 3:

    (HV49) No. 3 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance 10 kΩ or more?

    Yes:  Go to  10.

    No:  Replace the Wire harness between Battery ECU assembly connector and HV battery junction block.

  10. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (SHORT TO POWER SUPPLY). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Connect the ground terminal to Battery (12V). Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    2. Turn to IG ON.
    3. Using a tester, measure the voltage between terminals in Battery ECU assembly connector, between Battery ECU assembly connector and chassis ground, between terminals in HV battery junction block connector, and between HV battery junction block connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV42) No. 52 (+) - (HV42) No. 1 - 69 (-):

      (HV42) No. 52 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

      (HV49) No. 3 (+) - (HV49) No. 1 - 3 (-):

      (HV49) No. 3 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    Is the voltage less than 1 V?

    Yes:  Replace Battery ECU assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>BATTERY CONTROL MODULE .

    No:  Replace the Wire harness between Battery ECU assembly connector and HV battery junction block connector.

  11. CHECK CONNECTOR CONNECTION CONDITION (HV battery junction block). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.

    Check the connecting condition of connector (HV49) in HV battery junction block and check the relevant terminals for contact pressure.

    Is there looseness at the connector connection, or any poor contact at the relevant terminals?

    Yes:  Connect the connector securely. Or repair the poor contact of connector.

    No:  Go to  12.

  12. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (OPEN). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Disconnect HV battery junction block connector (HV49).
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between Battery ECU assembly connector and HV battery junction block connector.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV42) No. 52 - (HV49) No. 3:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  13.

    No:  Replace the Wire harness between Battery ECU assembly connector and HV battery junction block.

  13. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (SHORT TO GROUND AND LINE-TO-LINE SHORT). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between terminals in Battery ECU assembly connector, between Battery ECU assembly connector and chassis ground, between terminals in HV battery junction block connector, and between HV battery junction block connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (HV42) No. 52 - (HV42) No. 1 - 69:

    (HV42) No. 52 - Chassis ground:

    (HV49) No. 3 - (HV49) No. 1 - 3:

    (HV49) No. 3 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance 10 kΩ or more?

    Yes:  Go to  14.

    No:  Replace the Wire harness between Battery ECU assembly connector and HV battery junction block.

  14. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (OPEN). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between HV battery junction block connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (HV49) No. 2 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  15.

    No:  Replace the Wire harness between HV battery junction block and chassis ground.

  15. CHECK HV battery junction block AS A SINGLE UNIT (CHRB). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between HV battery junction block connector terminals.

    Connector & terminal 

    (HV49) No. 2 - (HV49) No. 3:

    NOTE:
    Inspection conditions Standard
    -40 - 80°C (-40 - 176°F) 20.6 - 40.8 Ω

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Even if DTC is detected, the circuit has returned to a normal condition at this time. Reproduce the failure, and then perform the diagnosis again.

    NOTE: In this case, temporary poor contact of connector, temporary open or short circuit of Wire harness may be the cause.

    No:  Replace HV battery junction block. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>RELAY AND FUSE .
