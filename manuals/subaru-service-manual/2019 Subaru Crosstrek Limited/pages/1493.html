Check Adaptive Cruise Control/Conventional Cruise Control Indicator Light (White Or Green)

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

Trouble symptom: 

Adaptive cruise control or conventional cruise control can be set, but the indicator light does not illuminate.

  1. CHECK ADAPTIVE CRUISE CONTROL INDICATOR LIGHT/CONVENTIONAL CRUISE CONTROL INDICATOR LIGHT (WHITE OR GREEN). 

    Check the combination meter. Refer to COMBINATION METER (DIAGNOSTICS)>ACTIVE TEST .

    Does the adaptive cruise control indicator light/conventional cruise control indicator light (white or green) illuminate?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Replace the meter case assembly. Refer to INSTRUMENTATION/DRIVER INFO>COMBINATION METER .

  2. CHECK LAN SYSTEM. 

    Perform the diagnosis for LAN system. Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Perform the diagnosis for LAN system.

  3. PERFORM REAL TIME DIAGNOSIS. 

    Check the input signal of cruise control system. Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>REAL-TIME DIAGNOSIS .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  The circuit has returned to a normal condition at this time.

    NOTE: In this case, temporary poor contact of connector, temporary open or short circuit of harness may be the cause.

    No:  Go to  4.

  4. CHECK EyeSight STEERING SWITCH. 

    Check EyeSight steering switch. Refer to EYESIGHT>SWITCHES AND HARNESS .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Replace the stereo camera. Refer to EYESIGHT>STEREO CAMERA>REMOVAL .

    No:  Replace the EyeSight steering switch. Refer to EYESIGHT>SWITCHES AND HARNESS .
