Outline Of Diagnosis

Detect the short circuit in the front oxygen (A/F) sensor heater.

The front oxygen (A/F) sensor heater performs duty control, and the output terminal voltage at ON is 0 V, and the output terminal voltage at OFF is the battery voltage.

Judge as NG when the terminal voltage remains High.
