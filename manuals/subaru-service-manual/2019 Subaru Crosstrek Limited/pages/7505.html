Inspection When Radiator Fans Do Not Stop

  • INSPECTION WHEN MAIN FAN AND SUB FAN DO NOT STOP 
  1. CHECK SUB FAN RELAY  .
    1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    2. Remove the sub fan relay. Refer to COOLING (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>RADIATOR FAN RELAY>LOCATION .
    3. Perform unit check for the sub fan relay. Refer to COOLING (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>RADIATOR FAN RELAY>INSPECTION .

    Is the sub fan relay OK?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No: 

    1. Replace the sub fan relay.
    2. Restore the vehicle to its original state.
  2. INSULATION CHECK OF HARNESS BETWEEN SUB FAN RELAY AND SUB FAN MOTOR  .
    1. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
      NOTE: Sub fan relay is removed.
    2. Turn to IG ON.
    3. Check the radiator fan operation.

    Do the sub fan motor and the main fan motor rotate?

    Yes: 

    1. Repair the harness between sub fan relay and sub fan motor.
    2. Restore the vehicle to its original state.

    No:  Go to  3.

  3. INSULATION CHECK OF HARNESS BETWEEN SUB FAN RELAY AND ECM  .
    1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    2. Disconnect the ECM connector.
    3. Check insulation of harness and chassis ground between sub fan relay and ECM.

    Are they insulated?

    Yes: 

    1. Replace the ECM. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE CONTROL MODULE (ECM) .
    2. Restore the vehicle to its original state.

    No: 

    1. Repair the harness between sub fan relay and ECM.
    2. Restore the vehicle to its original state.
  • INSPECTION WHEN ONLY MAIN FAN DOES NOT STOP 
  1. CHECK MAIN FAN RELAY 1  .
    1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    2. Remove the main fan relay 1. Refer to COOLING (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>RADIATOR FAN RELAY>LOCATION .
    3. Perform unit check for the main fan relay 1. Refer to COOLING (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>RADIATOR FAN RELAY>INSPECTION .

    Is the main fan relay 1 OK?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No: 

    1. Replace the main fan relay 1.
    2. Restore the vehicle to its original state.
  2. INSULATION CHECK OF HARNESS BETWEEN MAIN FAN MOTOR AND SUB FAN MOTOR  .
    1. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
      NOTE: Main fan relay 1 is removed.
    2. Turn to IG ON.
    3. Check the radiator fan operation.

    Does the main fan motor rotate?

    Yes: 

    1. Repair the harness between the main fan motor and the sub fan motor.
    2. Restore the vehicle to its original state.

    No: 

    1. Replace the ECM. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE CONTROL MODULE (ECM) .
    2. Restore the vehicle to its original state.
