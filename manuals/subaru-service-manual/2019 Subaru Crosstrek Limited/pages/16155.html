Throttle Body: Installation

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Connect the preheater hose.
  2. Install the preheater hose to the intake manifold assembly.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Install the collector cover. Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>COLLECTOR COVER>INSTALLATION .
  4. Place a new gasket and install the throttle body.

    Tightening torque: 

    8 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.9 ft-lb)

  5. Connect the connector and preheater hose.
  6. Install the air intake boot. Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>Air Intake Boot>INSTALLATION .
  7. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  8. Fill engine coolant. (Engine side) Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>ENGINE COOLANT>REPLACEMENT>FILLING OF ENGINE COOLANT>ENGINE SIDE .
