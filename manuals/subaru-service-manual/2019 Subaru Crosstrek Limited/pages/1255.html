DTC U0122 Lost Communication With Vehicle Dynamics Control Module

Detected when CAN data from VDC control module and hydraulic control unit (VDCCM & H/U) does not arrive.

NOTE: Perform the diagnosis for LAN system. Ref. to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .
