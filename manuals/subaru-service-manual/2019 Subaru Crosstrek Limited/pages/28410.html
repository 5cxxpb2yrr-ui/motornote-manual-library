Diagnostic Method

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Judge as NG when the following conditions are established.

JUDGMENT VALUE SPECIFICATION

Malfunction Criteria Threshold Value
Evaporative Leak Check Module pressure
Purge gas amount integrated value		 More than -400 Pa (-3 mmHg, -0.1 inHg)
3g (0.11oz) or more(1)
(1) It is approximately 2 sec per the integration of purge to reach 1 g (0.04 oz) at vehicle speed of 64.4 km/h (40 MPH).

Time needed for diagnosis:  Less than 1 s

Malfunction indicator light illumination:  Illuminates when malfunction occurs in 2 continuous driving cycles.
