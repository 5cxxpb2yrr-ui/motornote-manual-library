DTC P0E6D-11: Hybrid/EV Battery Charging System PRECHARGE Contactor Control Circuit Short To Ground: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

1. - 

Outline of diagnosis: 

Short circuit to ground in charge relay CHRP line

Defective circuit on primary side of CHR (pre-charge)

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

Plug-in charging suspended

Wiring diagram: 

Hybrid system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK CONNECTOR CONNECTION CONDITION (Battery ECU assembly). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Check that the Service plug is disconnected. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .
    2. Check the connecting condition of connector (HV42) in Battery ECU assembly and check the relevant terminals for contact pressure.

    Is there looseness at the connector connection, or any poor contact at the relevant terminals?

    Yes:  Connect the connector securely. Or repair the poor contact of connector.

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Battery ECU assembly - CHASSIS GROUND). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Disconnect Battery ECU assembly connector (HV42).
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between Battery ECU assembly connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV42) No. 50 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance 140 - 290 Ω?

    Yes:  Replace Battery ECU assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>BATTERY CONTROL MODULE .

    No:  Go to  3.

  3. CHECK CONNECTOR CONNECTION CONDITION (HV battery junction block CONNECTOR). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.

    Check the connecting condition of connector (HV51) in HV battery junction block and check the relevant terminals for contact pressure.

    Is there looseness at the connector connection, or any poor contact at the relevant terminals?

    Yes:  Connect the connector securely. Or repair the poor contact of connector.

    No:  Go to  4.

  4. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Battery ECU assembly - HV battery junction block). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Disconnect HV battery junction block connector (HV51).
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between terminals in Battery ECU assembly connector, and between Battery ECU assembly connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV42) No. 50 - (HV42) No. 1 - 69:

      (HV42) No. 50 - Chassis ground:

      (HV51) No. 1 - (HV51) No. 1 - 3:

      (HV51) No. 1 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance 10 kΩ or more?

    Yes:  Replace HV battery junction block. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>RELAY AND FUSE .

    No:  Replace the Wire harness between Battery ECU assembly connector and HV battery junction block connector.
