Transmitter (Tire Inflation Pressure Sensor)

CAUTION: Use the new transmitter assembly or replace the new valve and screw, when installing.
  1. Replace the valve and screw with a new part when reusing transmitter.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.

    Tightening torque: 

    1.4 N.m (0.1 kgf-m, 1 ft-lb)

  2. Install the transmitter to the wheel by aligning it with valve hole.
    NOTE: When using the jig that pulls the valve cap by hooking its neck part, use another short-type cap.
  3. Install the tires to wheels.
    CAUTION:
    • Use a tire changer when installing tire to wheel.
    • To prevent damaging the transmitter, set the tire changer boom in the position as shown in the figure.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Install the wheels to vehicle.

    Refer to WHEEL AND TIRE SYSTEM>TIRE AND WHEEL>INSTALLATION .

  5. Register the transmitter ID when the transmitter has been replaced.

    Refer to TIRE PRESSURE MONITOR (DIAGNOSTICS)>REGISTER TRANSMITTER (ID) .
