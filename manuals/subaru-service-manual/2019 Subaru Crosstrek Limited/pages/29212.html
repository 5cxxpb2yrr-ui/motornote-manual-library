DTC P1C0A-14: DC/DC Converter Temperature Sensor (For Charging) Circuit Short To Ground Or Open: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Outline of diagnosis: 

VH temperature sensor output voltage was smaller than the threshold value.

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

-

NOTE: This DTC is output when the Electric vehicle charger assembly detects a VHT sensor error.
Therefore if this DTC was detected, replace the Electric vehicle charger assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>ELECTRIC VEHICLE CHARGER .
