PROPELLER SHAFT/DESIGN CHANGE

PROPELLER SHAFT/DESIGN CHANGE

TECHNICAL SERVICE BULLETIN

Reference Number(s): 03-88-22, Date of Issue:  August 02, 2022
SUBARU: 2013-22 Crosstrek; 2018-22 Forester

APPLICABILITY

2013-22MY Crosstrek

2018-22MY Forester

INTRODUCTION

The bulletin announces the design change made the propeller shaft to address the small number of customer concerns regarding a growling-type sound being heard from under the vehicle when driving. The center support bearing may become contaminated by heavy exposure to water, snow, mud, and dirt. The new propeller shafts have water-blocking flanges incorporated to prevent any contamination.
