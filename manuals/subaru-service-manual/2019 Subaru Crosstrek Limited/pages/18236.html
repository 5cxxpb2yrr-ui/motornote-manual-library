Rear Door (4 Door Model)

  1. Apply the rear door sash tape - medium from the front of the vehicle towards the rear.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Apply the rear door sash tape - rear from the top of the vehicle towards the bottom.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Fold the corners at the door upper end area in the order of (1) - (3).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Install a new cover B pillar - rear.
  5. Install the weather strip outer - rear door.
  6. Install the running channel - rear door.
  7. Install the glass assembly - rear door.

    Tightening torque: 

    7.5 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.5 ft-lb)

  8. Install the rear sealing cover.
  9. Install the trim panel - rear door.
  10. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY>GROUND TERMINAL REMOVAL OR INSTALLATION .
