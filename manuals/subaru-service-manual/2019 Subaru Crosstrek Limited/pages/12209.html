DTC B14E9: Intake Door Actuator Circuit Open

DTC detecting condition: 

Intake door actuator potentiometer circuit is open.

Trouble symptom: 

FRESH/RECIRC does not operate.

Wiring diagram: 

Air conditioning system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (IMPREZA MODEL)>AIR CONDITIONING SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>AIR CONDITIONING SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK CONNECTOR. 
    1. Check connecting condition of the A/C control panel and intake door actuator connector.
    2. Read the DTC of [Air Conditioner] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is DTC B14E9 displayed?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Even if DTC is displayed, the circuit has returned to a normal condition at this time.

    Reproduce the failure, and then perform the diagnosis again.

    NOTE: In this case, temporary poor contact of connector, temporary open or short circuit of harness may be the cause.
  2. CHECK HARNESS. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    2. Measure the voltage between intake door actuator connector terminals using a tester.

      Connector & terminal 

      (i186) No. 3 (+) - (i186) No. 1 (-):

    Is the voltage 4.5 - 5.0 V?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Repair or replace the open circuit of harness.

  3. CHECK HARNESS (OPEN CIRCUIT). 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Check the continuity between terminals in intake door actuator connector using a tester.

      Connector & terminal 

      (i186) No. 3 - (i88) No. 16:

      (i186) No. 1 - (i88) No. 30:

      (i186) No. 5 - (i88) No. 5:

      (i186) No. 4 - (i88) No. 6:

      (i186) No. 2 - (i88) No. 32:

    Is there continuity?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Repair or replace the open circuit of harness.

  4. CHECK ACTUATOR. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    2. Measure the voltage between intake door actuator connector terminals using a tester.

      Connector & terminal 

      (i186) No. 2 (+) - (i186) No. 1 (-):

    Is the voltage 0.5 - 4.5 V?

    Yes:  Replace the A/C control panel. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>CONTROL PANEL .

    No:  Go to  5.

  5. CHECK HARNESS (SHORTED BETWEEN LINES). 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Measure the resistance between intake door actuator connector terminals using a tester.

      Connector & terminal 

      (i186) No. 2 - (i186) No. 3:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Repair or replace the short circuit of the harness.

    No:  Replace the intake door actuator. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>FRESH/RECIRC DOOR ACTUATOR .
