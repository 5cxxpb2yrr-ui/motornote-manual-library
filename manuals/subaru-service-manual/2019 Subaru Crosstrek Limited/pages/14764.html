Trunk Room Trim: Removal

  1. Remove the clips, and remove the trim panel - trunk lid.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Tilt the backrest assembly forward.
  3. Remove the trunk room mat.
  4. Remove the trim panel - trunk rear.
    1. Remove the clip.
    2. Release the claws, and remove the trim panel - trunk rear.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the clips, and remove the trim panel - trunk side.
Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
