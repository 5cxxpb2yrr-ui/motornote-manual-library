Inverter Tank: Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Drain engine coolant. (Inverter assembly side)

    Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>ENGINE COOLANT>REPLACEMENT>DRAINING OF ENGINE COOLANT>INVERTER ASSEMBLY SIDE .

  2. Remove the inverter radiator outlet hose from the clamp.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the air intake duct.

    Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>AIR INTAKE DUCT>REMOVAL .

  4. Remove the cover UPR - front.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the bracket - hood stay.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Remove the bolt securing the inverter tank, disconnect the inverter radiator outlet hose (A) and remove the inverter tank.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Disconnect the over flow hose (A) and inverter tank outlet hose (B) from the inverter tank.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
