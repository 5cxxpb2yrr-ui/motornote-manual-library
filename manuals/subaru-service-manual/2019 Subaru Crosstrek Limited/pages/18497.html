DTC B28B3: Stereo Camera Low Temperature

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Detected when the temperature of the stereo camera excessively decreases.

NOTE: When this DTC is detected, performing adjustment or inspection of the camera will not return it to the normal state. In this case, always replace the stereo camera. Refer to EYESIGHT>STEREO CAMERA>REMOVAL .
