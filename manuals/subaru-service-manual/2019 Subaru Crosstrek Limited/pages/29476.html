P Range Operation

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Stop the vehicle on an uphill grade of 5% or more and shift to the "P range" and apply the parking brake.
  2. Check that the vehicle does not move when the parking brake is released.
    CAUTION: Release the parking brake while being prepared to depress the brake pedal anytime.
