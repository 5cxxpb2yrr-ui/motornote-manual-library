Diagnostic Method

Either of the following is detected:

  1. When the ignition switch is on (READY): The solenoid power supply voltage is less than 3.5 V, when the IG1 terminal voltage is 9.54 V or more and operation of the main relay is requested.
  2. When the ignition switch is off: The solenoid power supply voltage is less than 3.5 V, when operation of the main relay is requested.
