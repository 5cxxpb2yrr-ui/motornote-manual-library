Rear Differential (T-type): Installation

  1. Install a new side oil seal to the side retainer.

    Refer to DIFFERENTIALS>REAR DIFFERENTIAL SIDE OIL SEAL>REPLACEMENT .

  2. Install the rear differential member plate.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Set the rear differential to transmission jack.
  4. Set the ST to the rear differential.

    Special tool: 

    ST: 28099 Pa090 OIL SEAL PROTECTOR

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Insert the rear drive shaft until the spline portion of the rear drive shaft exceeds the side oil seal.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Remove the OIL SEAL PROTECTOR (28099 Pa090) from rear differential.
  7. Push the rear differential from side to side to insert the DOJ completely.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Adjust the transmission jack, if necessary, and insert the rear differential stud bolt into the rear differential mount bushing.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Raise the transmission jack, and adjust the height to the rear differential mounting position.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  10. Install the mass damper.
  11. Tighten a new self-locking nut temporarily to the rear differential stud bolt.
    • Except for Crosstrek hybrid model
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • Crosstrek hybrid model
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  12. Install the rear differential front member, and temporarily attach and tighten a new self-locking nut.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  13. Remove the transmission jack.
  14. Tighten the self-locking nut.

    Tightening torque: 

    T1: 50 N.m (5.1 kgf-m, 36.9 ft-lb)

    T2: 110 N.m (11.2 kgf-m, 81.1 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  15. Tighten the self-locking nut.

    Tightening torque: 

    70 N.m (7.1 kgf-m, 51.6 ft-lb)

    • Except for Crosstrek hybrid model
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • Crosstrek hybrid model
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  16. Connect the ground cable. (Crosstrek hybrid model)
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  17. Install the propeller shaft.

    Refer to DRIVE SHAFT SYSTEM>PROPELLER SHAFT>INSTALLATION .

  18. Install the center exhaust cover.

    Tightening torque: 

    18 N.m (1.8 kgf-m, 13.3 ft-lb)

  19. Install the rear exhaust pipe.

    Refer to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>REAR EXHAUST PIPE>INSTALLATION .

  20. Add differential gear oil.

    Refer to DIFFERENTIALS>DIFFERENTIAL GEAR OIL>REPLACEMENT .

  21. Lower the vehicle.
  22. Install the rear tires.

    Refer to WHEEL AND TIRE SYSTEM>TIRE AND WHEEL>INSTALLATION .

  23. Connect the battery ground terminal.

    Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .

  24. Perform "Rear differential inspection mode" using the Subaru Select Monitor. (CVT model)

    Refer to DIFFERENTIALS>REAR DIFFERENTIAL INSPECTION MODE .

    CAUTION: Be sure to perform rear differential inspection mode when the following work has been performed.
    • Replacement of rear differential
    • Replacement of rear differential hypoid gear set
    • Replacement of transmission assembly
    • Replacement of front differential hypoid gear set (except for Crosstrek hybrid model)
