DTC B1700 Approach Information Speaker

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

DTC detecting condition: 

Detected when harness between pedestrian alert control module and pedestrian alert speaker is open or shorted, or when internal voice coil of pedestrian alert speaker is open or shorted.

Trouble symptom: 

  • No sound comes out from the pedestrian alert speaker during EV mode driving.
  • OFF indicator blinks after IGN ON.

Wiring diagram: 

Pedestrian alert system Ref. to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>PEDESTRIAN ALERT SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Ref. to PEDESTRIAN ALERT (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK DTC. 

    Read the DTC of [Pedestrian Alert] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Ref. to PEDESTRIAN ALERT (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is B1700 detected?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Go to  4.

  2. CHECK CONNECTOR. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the pedestrian alert control module connector.
    3. Check the pedestrian alert control module connector.

      Is the check result OK?

      Yes:  Go to  3.

      No:  Repair or replace the connector.

  3. CHECK HARNESS (SHORT). 

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between terminals in the pedestrian alert control module connector.

    Connector & terminal 

    (B641) No. 7 - (B641) No. 8:

    Is the resistance less than 2 Ω?

    Yes:  Repair the pedestrian alert speaker or the short circuit in harness. Go to  5.

    No:  Go to  4.

  4. CHECK HARNESS (OPEN CIRCUIT). 

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between terminals in the pedestrian alert control module connector.

    Connector & terminal 

    (B641) No. 7 - (B641) No. 8:

    Is the resistance 20 Ω or more?

    Yes:  Repair the pedestrian alert speaker or the open circuit, short circuit to power supply or short circuit to ground in harness. Go to  5.

    No:  Replace the pedestrian alert control module. Ref. to PEDESTRIAN ALERT SYSTEM>PEDESTRIAN ALERT CONTROL UNIT .

  5. CHECK PEDESTRIAN ALERT SYSTEM. 
    1. Connect the disconnected connectors.
    2. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, check the following items in the [Active Test] of [Pedestrian Alert]. Ref. to PEDESTRIAN ALERT (DIAGNOSTICS)>ACTIVE TEST .
      • Speaker Check

      Does it sound?

      Yes:  Even if DTC is detected, the circuit has returned to a normal condition at this time.

      Reproduce the failure, and then perform the diagnosis again.

      NOTE: In this case, temporary poor contact of connector, temporary open or short circuit of harness may be the cause.

      No:  Replace the pedestrian alert control module. Ref. to PEDESTRIAN ALERT SYSTEM>PEDESTRIAN ALERT CONTROL UNIT .
