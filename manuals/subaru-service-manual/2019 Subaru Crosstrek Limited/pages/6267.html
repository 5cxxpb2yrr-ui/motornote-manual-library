Propeller Shaft: Removal

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

CAUTION:
  • Before removing propeller shaft, wrap metal parts with a cloth or rubber material.
  • Do not disassemble the center joint of the propeller shaft.
  • Before removing propeller shaft, wrap the metal parts attached to the rubber boot of the center joint with a cloth or rubber material, as shown in the figure. If the joint is bent during removal, the rubber boot may be damaged due to interference with metal parts.
Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  1. Shift the select lever or gear shift lever to neutral.
  2. Release the parking brake.
  3. Lift up the vehicle.
  4. Remove the rear exhaust pipe and muffler. Ref. to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>REAR EXHAUST PIPE>REMOVAL . Ref. to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>MUFFLER>REMOVAL .
  5. Remove the bolts, and then remove the center exhaust cover.
    • Except for Crosstrek hybrid model
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • Crosstrek hybrid model
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Remove the propeller shaft.
CAUTION: Do not disassemble the propeller shaft.
  1. Place alignment marks (A) on the flange yoke and rear differential.
  2. Remove the bolts and nuts holding the propeller shaft to the rear differential.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the center bearing.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the propeller shaft from transmission.
CAUTION: Be careful not to damage oil seals and contact surface of the sleeve yoke.
NOTE:
  • Use a container to catch CVTF flowing from propeller shaft.
  • To prevent CVTF from leaking, install extension cap etc. to the transmission.
