Water Tank Pipe Assembly

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
CAUTION: If the engine coolant is spilt over the engine or the exhaust pipe, completely wipe it off to avoid emission of smoke or causing a fire.
  1. Release the fuel pressure.

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL>PROCEDURE>RELEASING OF FUEL PRESSURE .

  2. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery.

    Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .

  3. Drain engine coolant. (Engine side)

    Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>ENGINE COOLANT>REPLACEMENT>DRAINING OF ENGINE COOLANT>ENGINE SIDE .

  4. Remove the high-pressure fuel delivery pipe assembly.

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>HIGH PRESSURE FUEL DELIVERY PIPE>REMOVAL>HIGH-PRESSURE FUEL DELIVERY PIPE ASSEMBLY .

  5. Disconnect the connector (A) and remove the clip (B) and clip (C).
  6. Disconnect the connector (D).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Disconnect the water hose (A), over flow hose (B), radiator inlet hose (C) and heater inlet hose (D).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Remove the bolts securing the water tank pipe assembly, and disconnect the water hose (A) to remove the water pipe assembly.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Disconnect the preheater hose from the water tank pipe assembly.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  10. Remove the engine coolant temperature sensor from the water tank pipe assembly.

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>ENGINE COOLANT TEMPERATURE SENSOR>REMOVAL .

  11. Remove the filler neck from the water tank pipe assembly.

    Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>WATER PIPE>REMOVAL>FILLER NECK .

  12. When water hose (A) and water hose (B) are replaced
    1. Disconnect the water hose (A).

      Refer to EMISSION CONTROL (AUX. EMISSION CONTROL DEVICES) (H4DO (HEV))>PCV CONNECTOR>REMOVAL .

    2. Disconnect the water hose (B).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
