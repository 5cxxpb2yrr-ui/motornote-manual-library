DTC P1A60-17: Hybrid/EV Battery Stack 2 Cell Circuit Voltage Above Threshold: Notes

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

1. - 

Outline of diagnosis: 

When the voltage of one cell of NO. 2 HV supply stack sub-assembly is equal to or more than the standard with charging in progress

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

  • Reduced output power
  • Engine not stopped
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK DTC. 

    Read the DTC of [P-HEV High Voltage Battery] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is DTC P0C3000 or P31B300 displayed?

    Yes:  Replace Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly and HV battery. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL MODULE . Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY .

    No:  Replace HV battery. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY .
