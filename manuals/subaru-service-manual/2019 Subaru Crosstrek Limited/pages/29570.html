DTC B2814: Power Supply Low Voltage

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Detected when the status of 7.0 V or less continues approximately for 5 seconds and is judged to be low-voltage malfunction, or when the +B harness of the stereo camera is broken.

DTC detecting condition: 

  • Input voltage to stereo camera is out of specifications.
  • Defective stereo camera control harness (open circuit in +B harness)
  • Defective stereo camera

Trouble symptom: 

  • All functions of EyeSight system do not operate.
  • EyeSight warning light blinks or illuminates.

Wiring diagram: 

EyeSight system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>EYESIGHT SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK GENERATOR. 
    1. Start the hybrid system and idle for a while.
    2. Measure the voltage between generator terminal B and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      Generator terminal B (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    Is the voltage 10 V or more?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Check the generator. Refer to STARTING/CHARGING SYSTEMS (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>GENERATOR .

  2. CHECK BATTERY TERMINAL. 

    Turn the ignition switch to OFF.

    Is the battery terminal connected securely?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Tighten the battery terminal securely.

  3. CHECK FUSE. 

    Check fuses No. 19 and No. 38 in the fuse & relay box. Refer to EYESIGHT>RELAY AND FUSE .

    Is the fuse OK?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Replace the defective fuse. When the replaced fuse blows out easily, check the short circuit in harness.

  4. CHECK HARNESS (POWER SUPPLY CIRCUIT). 
    1. Disconnect the stereo camera.
    2. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    3. Measure the voltage between connector of stereo camera and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (R137) No. 6 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

      (R137) No. 8 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    Is the voltage 10 V or more?

    Yes:  Go to  5.

    No:  Check the power supply system circuit, and if any fault is found, repair the defective parts or replace the harness.

  5. CHECK HARNESS (GROUND CIRCUIT). 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery.
    3. Measure the resistance between stereo camera and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (R137) No. 7 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance less than 10 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  6.

    No:  Check the ground system circuit, and if any fault is found, repair the defective parts or replace the harness.

  6. CHECK POOR CONTACT OF CONNECTORS. 

    Check stereo camera connector.

    Is there poor contact of connector?

    Yes:  Repair the connector.

    No:  Go to  7.

  7. CHECK STEREO CAMERA. 
    1. Connect all connectors and battery terminals securely.
    2. Start the hybrid system, drive the vehicle at 40 km/h (24.9 MPH) or more, stop the vehicle and then stop the power supply.
    3. After 3 seconds or more have elapsed, restart the hybrid system.
    4. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, perform the clear memory of [EyeSight]. Refer to COMMON (DIAG)>CLEAR MEMORY .
    5. Read the DTC of [EyeSight] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is DTC B2814 or B2815 displayed?

    Yes:  Go to  8.

    No:  Even if DTC is displayed, the circuit has returned to a normal condition at this time. Reproduce the failure, and then perform the diagnosis again.

    NOTE: In this case, temporary poor contact of connector, temporary open or short circuit of harness may be the cause.
  8. CHECK DATA MONITOR. 

    Using the Subaru Select Monitor, check the following items in the [Data Monitor] of [EyeSight]. Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DATA MONITOR .

    • ECU Power Supply Voltage (IG)

    Is the voltage within 7 - 16 V?

    Yes:  Even if DTC is displayed, the circuit has returned to a normal condition at this time. Reproduce the failure, and then perform the diagnosis again.

    NOTE: In this case, temporary poor contact of connector, temporary open or short circuit of harness may be the cause.

    No:  Go to  9.

  9. CHECK BATTERY VOLTAGE. 

    Measure the battery voltage.

    Is the voltage within 7 - 16 V?

    Yes:  Replace the stereo camera. Refer to EYESIGHT>STEREO CAMERA>REMOVAL .

    No:  When the battery voltage is out of the range above, check the battery unit. After the battery voltage becomes 7 - 16 V, perform the diagnosis again.
