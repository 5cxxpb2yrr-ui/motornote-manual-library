Related Lines, More Than 52 Ω And Less Than 62 Ω (Keyless Access System)

Wiring diagram: 

CAN communication system. Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (IMPREZA MODEL)>CAN COMMUNICATION SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>CAN COMMUNICATION SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  1. CHECK BETWEEN RELATED LINES AND MAIN WIRING HARNESS. 
    1. Disconnect the keyless access control module connector.
    2. Using the tester, measure the resistance between terminals.

    Connector & terminal 

    (i241) No. 29 - (i241) No. 30:

    Is the resistance 400 Ω or more?

    Yes:  A related line between keyless access control module and main wiring harness is open, or main wiring harness is open at two places or more.

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK RELATED LINES. 
    1. Disconnect the audio or navigation unit connector.
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between audio or navigation unit connector and keyless access control module connector.

    Connector & terminal 

    (i241) No. 30 - (i87) No. 9:

    (i241) No. 29 - (i87) No. 10:

    Is the resistance 10 Ω or more?

    Yes:  Repair or replace the open circuit of keyless access control module related lines.

    No:  Check DTC of keyless access control module. Refer to KEYLESS ACCESS WITH PUSH BUTTON START (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .
