DTC P062F-44: Internal Control Module EEPROM Data Memory Failure: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Detecting condition: 

ECU internal malfunction (EEPROM malfunction)

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

Normal driving

NOTE: This DTC is output when a malfunction of EEPROM incorporated with Inverter with converter assembly is detected. Therefore, when this DTC is output, replace Inverter with converter assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>INVERTER AND CONVERTER ASSEMBLY .
