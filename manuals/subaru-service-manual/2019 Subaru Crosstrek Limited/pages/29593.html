DTC B28AC: Stereo Camera ASIC

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Detected when communication error occurs due to malfunction of ASIC.

DTC detecting condition: 

Communication error occurs due to malfunction of ASIC.

Trouble symptom: 

All functions of EyeSight system do not operate.

Refer to DTC B28A6 for diagnostic procedure. Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE WITH DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>DTC B28A6 INTERNAL STEREO CAMERA COMMUNICATION 1 .
