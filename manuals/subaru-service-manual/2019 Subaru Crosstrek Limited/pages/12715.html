Seat Belt Outer - Rear RH And LH (5 Door Model)

CAUTION:
  • Before handling the airbag system components, refer to "CAUTION" of "General Description" in "AIRBAG SYSTEM". Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  • Airbag system satellite safing sensor is located under the front center of the rear seat cushion. Be careful not to apply strong impact to the sensor when working with the rear seat cushion removed.
  1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
  2. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery and wait for at least 60 seconds before starting work. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  3. Remove the rear seat cushion assembly. Refer to SEATS>REAR SEAT>REMOVAL .
  4. Remove the trim panel - rear apron. (On the side where seat belt assembly is removed) (Except for hybrid model) Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>REAR QUARTER TRIM>REMOVAL>5 DOOR MODEL (EXCEPT FOR HYBRID) .
  5. Remove the trim panel - rear apron. (On the side where seat belt assembly is removed) (Hybrid model) Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>REAR QUARTER TRIM>REMOVAL>5 DOOR MODEL (HYBRID) .
  6. Remove the duct - battery cooling blower. (On the side where seat belt assembly is removed) (Hybrid model) Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY COOLING SYSTEM>REMOVAL>FRONT DUCT .
  7. Remove the bolts, and remove the seat belt outer - rear.
