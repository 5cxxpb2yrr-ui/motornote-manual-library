Door Latch & Door Lock Actuator Assembly: Removal: Rear

  1. Raise the glass assembly - rear door to the top position.
  2. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  3. Remove the trim panel - rear door. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>DOOR TRIM>REMOVAL>REAR DOOR .
  4. Remove the sealing cover - rear door. Refer to EXTERIOR BODY PANELS>SEALING COVER>REMOVAL>REAR SEALING COVER .
  5. Remove the latch and actuator assembly - rear.
    1. Disconnect the connector.
    2. Remove the TORX® bolts, and remove the latch & actuator assembly - rear.

      Preparation tool: 

      TORX® T30
