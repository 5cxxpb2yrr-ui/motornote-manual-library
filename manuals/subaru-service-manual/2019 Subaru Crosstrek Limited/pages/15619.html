Front Brake Pad: Removal

  1. Lift up the vehicle, and then remove the front wheels.
  2. Remove the pad - disc brake front.
    1. Remove the caliper bolt on the lower side.
    2. Raise the caliper body assembly and remove the pad - disc brake front.
      NOTE: Do not disconnect the brake hose from the caliper body assembly.
