Steering Lock

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery and wait for at least 60 seconds before starting work. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the immobilizer antenna assembly. Refer to SECURITY AND LOCKS>IMMOBILIZER ANTENNA>REMOVAL .
  3. Replace the key lock assembly.
    1. Insert the key into the key lock assembly, and turn the key to the ACC position.
    2. Release the lock by inserting a flat tip screwdriver from the steering lock frame hole (A).
    3. Pull the key lock assembly until the key lock assembly claws touch the steering lock frame stopper (B).
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    4. Insert a flat tip screwdriver from the steering lock frame hole (A), and release the claws to pull out the key lock assembly.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    5. Install the key lock assembly in the reverse order of removal.
  4. Install the immobilizer antenna assembly. Refer to SECURITY AND LOCKS>IMMOBILIZER ANTENNA>INSTALLATION .
  5. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
