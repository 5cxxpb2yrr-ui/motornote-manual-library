Pipe Temperature Sensor: Inspection

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Visually check each pipe temperature sensor for dirt or damage, and clean or replace as necessary.
  2. Measure the resistance between connector terminals.
    CAUTION: During inspection, be careful not to touch the sensor end in order to avoid misjudgment due to body temperature.
