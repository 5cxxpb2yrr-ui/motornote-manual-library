Collector Cover: Installation

  1. Install the collector cover bracket.

    Tightening torque: 

    6.4 N.m (0.7 kgf-m, 4.7 ft-lb)

  2. Install the collector cover in the order shown in the figure.
    CAUTION: The operation must be carefully performed; otherwise the collector cover may be damaged.
    NOTE: Check the cushion (a) and cushion (b) for peel-off or disengagement.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    1. Insert the bracket insertion part at the collector cover backside into the collector cover bracket (A).
    2. Install the collector cover by inserting the protrusion at the collector cover backside into the cushion (B).
      NOTE: Check the cushion for peel-off or disengagement.
