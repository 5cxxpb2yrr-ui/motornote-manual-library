Control Panel: Inspection

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Check the illumination operation when battery voltage is applied between the connector terminals.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Terminal No. Inspection conditions Specification
    8 (+) - 2 (-) Apply battery voltage. Light ON
  2. If it does not operate normally, replace the heater control assembly.
