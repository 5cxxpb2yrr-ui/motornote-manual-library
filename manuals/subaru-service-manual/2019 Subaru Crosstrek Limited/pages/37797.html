Rear Gate Latch and Actuator Assembly: Inspection: Actuator

  1. Check door lock operation

    Check the door lock operation when battery voltage is applied between the connector terminals.

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Terminal No. Inspection conditions Specification
    1 (+) - 2 (-) Connect battery to the terminals OPEN
  2. Check switch

    Measure the resistance between connector terminals.

    Preparation tool: 

    Circuit tester

    Terminal No. Inspection conditions Standard
    3 - 4 LOCK 1 MΩ or more
    UNLOCK Less than 1.5 Ω
  3. Replace the latch and actuator - rear gate if it is found defective.
