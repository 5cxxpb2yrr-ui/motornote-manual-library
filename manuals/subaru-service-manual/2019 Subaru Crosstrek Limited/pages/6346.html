Rear Differential Front Oil Seal: Replacement

  1. Shift the select lever or gear shift lever to neutral.
  2. Release the parking brake.
  3. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery.

    Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .

  4. Lift up the vehicle.
  5. Drain differential gear oil.

    Refer to DIFFERENTIALS>DIFFERENTIAL GEAR OIL>REPLACEMENT .

  6. Remove the rear exhaust pipe.

    Refer to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>REAR EXHAUST PIPE>REMOVAL .

  7. Remove the center exhaust cover.
    • Except for Crosstrek hybrid model
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • Crosstrek hybrid model
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Remove the propeller shaft.

    Refer to DRIVE SHAFT SYSTEM>PROPELLER SHAFT>REMOVAL .

  9. Check the initial torque and write it down.
  10. Remove the self-locking nut while securing the companion flange with the ST.

    Special tool: 

    ST: 498427200 FLANGE WRENCH

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  11. Extract the companion flange using the ST.

    Special tool: 

    ST: 399703600 PULLER ASSY

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  12. Remove the front oil seal using the ST or a screwdriver.

    Special tool: 

    ST: 398527700 PULLER ASSY

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  13. Using the ST, install a new front oil seal.
    NOTE: Apply differential gear oil to the oil seal lips.

    Special tool: 

    ST: 498447120 INSTALLER

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  14. Use a plastic hammer to install companion flange.
  15. Apply seal material on the drive pinion shaft thread and new self-locking nut seat.

    Seal material: 

    THREE BOND 1324 (Part No. 004403042) or equivalent

  16. Tighten a new self-locking nut to the specified torque while securing the companion flange with the ST.
    CAUTION: Tighten it so that the initial torque of companion flange becomes the same as that of before oil seal replacement.

    Tightening torque: 

    T-type: 

    167 - 196 N.m (17.0 - 20.0 kgf-m, 123.2 - 144.6 ft-lb)

    VA1-type: 

    162 - 220 N.m (16.5 - 22.5 kgf-m, 119.5 - 162.3 ft-lb)

    Special tool: 

    ST: 498427200 FLANGE WRENCH

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  17. Install the propeller shaft.

    Refer to DRIVE SHAFT SYSTEM>PROPELLER SHAFT>INSTALLATION .

  18. Install the center exhaust cover.

    Tightening torque: 

    18 N.m (1.8 kgf-m, 13.3 ft-lb)

  19. Install the rear exhaust pipe.

    Refer to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>REAR EXHAUST PIPE>INSTALLATION .

  20. Add differential gear oil.

    Refer to DIFFERENTIALS>DIFFERENTIAL GEAR OIL>REPLACEMENT .

  21. Lower the vehicle.
  22. Connect the battery ground terminal.

    Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
