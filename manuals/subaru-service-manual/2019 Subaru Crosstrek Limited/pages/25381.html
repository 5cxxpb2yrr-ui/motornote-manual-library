Sunroof Assembly: Installation

CAUTION:
  • Before handling the airbag system components, refer to "CAUTION" of "General Description" in "AIRBAG SYSTEM". Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  • For installation of the stereo camera cover assembly, refer to "INSTALLATION", "Stereo Camera" in the "EyeSight" service information and observe the precautions for installation. Refer to EYESIGHT>STEREO CAMERA>INSTALLATION .
  • Be careful not to snag the harness.
  1. Install the drain tube - sunroof.
  2. Install the sunroof bracket and the rail assembly - sunroof.

    Tightening torque: 

    6 N.m (0.6 kgf-m, 4.4 ft-lb)

  3. Connect the harness connector of the drain tube - sunroof and the motor assembly.
    CAUTION:
    • Insert the drain tubes on the front side until they contact all the way to the end (a) of drain pipes.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • For the drain tubes on the rear side, securely insert the resin connector until a clicking sound is heard.
  4. Check for the water leakage for each part and if there is no problem install the following parts.
    • Front side
      1. Mud guard - front
      2. Glove box assembly
      3. Cover assembly - instrument panel LWR driver
    • Rear side
      1. Mat - trunk and trim panel - trunk side (4 door model)
      2. Bumper face - rear
  5. Install the trim panel - roof assembly. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>ROOF TRIM>INSTALLATION .
  6. Using a new TORX® bolt, install the lid assembly - sunroof.

    Preparation tool: 

    TORX® T25

    Tightening torque: 

    4.5 N.m (0.5 kgf-m, 3.3 ft-lb)

  7. Install the cover - lid.
  8. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
