Radiator Fan Relay: Inspection

  1. Check that there is no deformation, cracks or other damages.
  2. Check the resistance between terminals. Replace the relay if it is faulty.

    3 - 4 Apply battery voltage between terminals 1 and 2. Less than 1 Ω
