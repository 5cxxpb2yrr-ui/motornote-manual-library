DTC P0712: Transmission Fluid Temperature Sensor "A" Circuit Low: Notes

1. - 

DTC detecting condition: 

Immediately at fault recognition

Trouble symptom: 

  • Excessive shift shock
  • Shift characteristics malfunction

Wiring diagram: 

CVT control system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (IMPREZA MODEL)>CVT CONTROL SYSTEM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>CVT CONTROL SYSTEM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: Use CHECK BOARD when measuring the TCM terminal voltage and resistance. After servicing or replacing faulty parts, perform the last step of the basic diagnostic procedure. Refer to TRANSMISSION (DIAGNOSTICS)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE .
  1. CHECK HARNESS  .
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the TCM connector.
    3. Measure the resistance of harness between TCM connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (T10) No. 1 - Chassis ground:

    (T10) No. 6 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance 1 MΩ or more?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK HARNESS INSIDE TRANSMISSION  .
    CAUTION: Start work after ATF cools down.
    1. Remove the transmission valve cover.
    2. Check for the harness pinch, damage.

    Is there any fault in the harness?

    Yes:  Replace the transmission harness.

    No:  Go to  3.

  3. CHECK ATF TEMPERATURE SENSOR  .

    Measure the resistance between TCM connectors.

    Connector & terminal 

    (T10) No. 1 - No. 6:

    NOTE: Perform the measurements using multiple oil temperatures.

    Is resistance as follows?

    Fluid temperature 0°C → Approx. 6.0 kΩ

    Fluid temperature 20°C → Approx. 2.5 kΩ

    Fluid temperature 80°C → Approx. 330 Ω

    Yes:  Replace the TCM. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>TRANSMISSION CONTROL MODULE (TCM) .

    No:  Replace the control valve body. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>CONTROL VALVE BODY .
