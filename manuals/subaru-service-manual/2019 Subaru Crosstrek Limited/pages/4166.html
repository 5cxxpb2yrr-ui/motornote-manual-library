DTC U1422: Invalid Data Received From Body Control Module BODY-CAN

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

DTC detecting condition: 

When the following conditions are all established.

  • Data no-receive DTC of monitored node is not in current malfunction.
  • Fail detection counter of monitored node is not updated 32 times in a row.
NOTE: Perform the diagnosis for LAN system. Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE . Refer to LAN SYSTEM (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .
