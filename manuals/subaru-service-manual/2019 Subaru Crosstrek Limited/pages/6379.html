Rear Axle: Removal

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Ref. to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Lift up the vehicle, and then remove the rear wheels.
  3. Remove the axle nut.
    CAUTION: Do not loosen the axle nut while the rear axle is loaded. Doing so may damage the hub unit bearing.
    1. Lift the crimped section of axle nut.
    2. Remove the axle nut using a socket wrench while depressing the brake pedal.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Disconnect the sensor assembly - headlight beam leveler. (Model with auto headlight beam leveler) Ref. to LIGHTING SYSTEM>REAR HEIGHT SENSOR>REMOVAL .
  5. Remove the rear ABS wheel speed sensor from the rear axle housing. Ref. to VEHICLE DYNAMICS CONTROL (VDC)>REAR ABS WHEEL SPEED SENSOR>REMOVAL .
  6. Remove the rear disc brake and the rear disc rotor. Ref. to BRAKE>REAR DISC ROTOR>REMOVAL .
  7. Remove the electronic parking brake harness clamp.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Remove the bolts and nuts and lower the rear lateral link.
    1. Remove the nut and disconnect the rear stabilizer link. (Model with rear stabilizer)
    2. Remove the bolts from the lower side of rear shock absorber.
    3. Disconnect the rear axle housing from the rear lateral link.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Remove the rear drive shaft assembly from the rear hub unit bearing.
    NOTE: If it is hard to remove, use the ST.

    Preparation tool: 

    ST1: AXLE SHAFT PULLER (926470000)

    ST2: AXLE SHAFT PULLER PLATE (28099 Pa110)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  10. Remove the lateral link (front).
    1. Remove the snap pin (a) and nut (b).
    2. Remove the ball joint (c) from the rear axle housing (e) using the tie-rod ball joint puller (d).
    CAUTION:
    • Be careful not to damage the boot of the joint.
    • Securely place the tie-rod ball joint puller (d) on the steel collar (f).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  11. Remove the rear axle housing.
    1. Separate the rear axle housing by removing the bolts of the upper arm and trailing link.
      CAUTION: Be careful not to damage the boot of the joint.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Remove the rear axle housing.
    CAUTION:
    • Since the rear axle housing is heavy, be careful not to drop it.
    • Be careful not to damage the spline portion of the drive shaft.
  12. Refer to "Rear Hub Unit Bearing" for removal of the rear hub unit bearing. Ref. to DRIVE SHAFT SYSTEM>REAR HUB UNIT BEARING>REMOVAL .
