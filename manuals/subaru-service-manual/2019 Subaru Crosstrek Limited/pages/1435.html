DTC B28B6: Pre-Collision Off Switch

Detected when pre-collision brake OFF switch circuit is not installed, open-circuited or is stuck to ON.

DTC detecting condition: 

  • Wiring of pre-collision brake OFF switch is not connected.
  • Pre-collision brake OFF switch circuit is open.
  • Pre-collision brake OFF switch circuit stays ON for 45 seconds or more.

Trouble symptom: 

All functions of EyeSight system do not operate.

Wiring diagram: 

EyeSight system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (IMPREZA MODEL)>EYESIGHT SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>EYESIGHT SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK DATA MONITOR. 

    Using the Subaru Select Monitor, check the following items in the [Data Monitor] of [EyeSight]. Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>DATA MONITOR .

    • Pre-Collision Brake OFF SW

    Does the display change according to ON/OFF operation of the pre-collision brake OFF switch?

    Yes:  Even if DTC is displayed, the circuit has returned to a normal condition at this time. Reproduce the failure, and then perform the diagnosis again.

    NOTE: In this case, temporary poor contact of connector, temporary open or short circuit of harness may be the cause.

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK HARNESS. 
    1. Disconnect the stereo camera connector and the pre-collision brake OFF switch connector.
    2. Using the tester, measure the resistance between the stereo camera connector and pre-collision brake OFF switch connector as well as chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (R406) No. 3 - (R137) No. 12:

    (R406) No. 5 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Repair or replace an open circuit in harness between the stereo camera connector and pre-collision brake OFF switch connector.

  3. CHECK HARNESS. 

    Using the tester, measure the resistance between the pre-collision brake OFF switch connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (R406) No. 3 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance 1 MΩ or more?

    Yes:  Check the pre-collision brake OFF switch. Refer to EYESIGHT>SWITCHES AND HARNESS>INSPECTION .

    No:  Repair or replace the short circuit to ground in harness between the stereo camera connector and pre-collision brake OFF switch connector.
