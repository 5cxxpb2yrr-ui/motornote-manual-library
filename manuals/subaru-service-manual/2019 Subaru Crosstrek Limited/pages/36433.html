Check Horn Switch

CAUTION: Before handling the airbag system components, refer to "CAUTION" of "General Description" in "AIRBAG SYSTEM". Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK HORN SWITCH TERMINAL  .
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery and wait for at least 60 seconds before starting work. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    3. Remove the driver's airbag module. Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>DRIVER'S AIRBAG MODULE>REMOVAL .
    4. Check that the connection of the horn switch harness connector is correct.
    5. Measure the resistance between connectors.

    Preparation tool: 

    Circuit tester

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Terminal No. Standard
    Horn switch terminal - ground terminal Less than 1 Ω

    Is the measured value within the standard value?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  It is possible that there is an open circuit in horn harness (horn switch terminal - ground terminal). Replace the satellite switch assembly.

  2. CHECK DRIVER'S AIRBAG MODULE  .

    Measure the resistance between horn plate and snap pin hook.

    Preparation tool: 

    Circuit tester

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Terminal No. Standard
    Horn plate - Snap pin hook 1 MΩ or more

    Is the measured resistance 1 MΩ or more?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Replace the driver's airbag module.

  3. CHECK DRIVER'S AIRBAG MODULE  .

    Measure the resistance between horn plate and snap pin hook with the snap pin hook pressed-in firmly.

    Preparation tool: 

    Circuit tester

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Terminal No. Standard
    Horn plate - Snap pin hook Less than 1 Ω

    Is the measured resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Driver's airbag module is normal.

    No:  Replace the driver's airbag module.
