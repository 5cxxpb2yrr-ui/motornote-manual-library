Explanation For Circuits

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly detects that the operation (removal of Service plug or Inverter cover, etc.) of safety device (interlock) was performed, and performs startup prohibition and the system main relay shutdown. Safety device is installed at one location at Service plug, and at one location at Inverter cover for the Power cable terminal installation in Inverter with converter assembly. When Service plug or Inverter cover is removed, the interlock signal line becomes open circuit (open condition). However, during driving, it is judged as an open circuit malfunction and the system main relay is not turned off. If safety device is installed properly, the operation recovers to normal by turning to IG ON again. The system main relay is turned off until the operation is recovered to normal from the trip immediately after the open circuit was detected.
