Multi-function Display (MFD): Removal

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the grille assembly - CTR ventilation. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>AIR VENT GRILLE>REMOVAL>CENTER GRILLE ASSEMBLY .
  3. Remove the panel - center UPR, and remove the audio assembly or navigation assembly. Refer to ENTERTAINMENT & MONITORING>AUDIO>REMOVAL .
  4. Remove the cover - center UPR.
    1. Release the clip and hook, and then remove the cover - center UPR.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    NOTE: Lightly push up the back of the cover - center UPR, hook the finger to the end portion and pull it toward you to remove it.
  5. Remove the multi-function display.
CAUTION: Be careful not to damage the multi-function display or instrument panel.
  1. Remove the screws and release the clip.
  2. Pull the multi-function display assembly toward yourself, and disconnect the connector.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the screws and then remove the multi-function display.
