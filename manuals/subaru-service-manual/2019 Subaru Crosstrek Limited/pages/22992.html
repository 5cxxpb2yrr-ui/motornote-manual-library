Except For IMPREZA Model

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the reservoir tank. (Hybrid model) Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>RESERVOIR TANK>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the rubber cap and disconnect the terminal.

  4. Remove the oil pressure switch.
    CAUTION: Use cloth to catch the engine oil in order to prevent it from splashing.
