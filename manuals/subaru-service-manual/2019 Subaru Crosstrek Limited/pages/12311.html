A/C Pipe

  1. Using the refrigerant recovery system, discharge refrigerant. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>REFRIGERANT RECOVERY PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .
  2. Remove the battery and battery tray. Refer to STARTING/CHARGING SYSTEMS (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>BATTERY>REMOVAL .
  3. Move the air intake boot. Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>AIR INTAKE BOOT>REMOVAL .
    NOTE: With the PCV hose connected, remove the air intake boot from the air cleaner case (rear) and throttle body.
  4. Remove the hose - pressure discharge. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>HOSE AND PIPE>REMOVAL>HIGH-PRESSURE HOSE .
  5. Remove the hose - pressure suction. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>HOSE AND PIPE>REMOVAL>LOW-PRESSURE HOSE .
  6. Remove the pipe - evaporator cooling.
    CAUTION:
    • Do not apply excessive force to the pipe.
    • Seal the engaging parts of the disconnected pipe, expansion valve - cooling and condenser assembly air conditioner with a plug or vinyl tape to prevent foreign matter from entering.
    • Do not pull the harness and cable forcibly.
    1. Remove the clamp and remove the vacuum hose.
    2. Release the claws, and remove the battery cable bracket and engine harness bracket.
    3. Disconnect the main fuse box connector, and detach the harness from the clip.
    4. Disconnect the upper connector of the engine control module (ECM). Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE CONTROL MODULE (ECM)>REMOVAL .
    5. Disconnect the connector of the pressure switch or pressure sensor.
    6. Remove the bolt, and detach the pipe - evaporator cooling.

    7. Remove the clip - pipe and the clip, and remove the pipe - evaporator cooling.
