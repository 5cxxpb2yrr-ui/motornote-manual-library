Lever Assembly

  1. Operate the lever assembly - hand brake 3 to 4 times and fully return the lever assembly - hand brake.
  2. While slowly pulling up the lever assembly - hand brake, count the notches.

Lever stroke: 

7 to 8 notches when pulled with a force of 200 N (20.4 kgf, 45 lbf)

If it is not within the specified value, adjust the parking brake. Refer to PARKING BRAKE>PARKING BRAKE ASSEMBLY (REAR DISC BRAKE)>ADJUSTMENT .
