Check The Electric Vehicle Charger Cable Assembly Unit

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Visual inspection (*A)
    NOTE:
    • For the visual inspection, perform a comparison check with an Electric vehicle charger cable assembly that was confirmed to operate normally.
    • If the power plug is damaged, replace the cable EV charger plug assembly.
    • Due to the characteristics of the Electric vehicle charger cable assembly, the Electric vehicle charger cable assembly may harden or become kinked.
    • If the Electric vehicle charger cable assembly is repeatedly stored wound up or folded, these issues become more likely and may eventually result in an open circuit.
    1. Check that there is no foreign substance on the Electric vehicle charger cable assembly (charging connector).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    NOTE: There have been cases when a piece of wood was caught inside the connector, preventing charging.
  2. Check the unlock button.
    1. Check that operation is smooth when the unlock button (a) is operated.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Check the connection.
    1. Check that smooth connection is possible when the Electric vehicle charger cable assembly and AC charger inlet cable (charging inlet) are connected.

      Standard: 

      A smooth connection must be possible.

      NOTE: If the check result is different from the standard, perform visual inspection (*A), and also the following inspection.
    2. Check that there is no bending or deformation of the pins (a) in the Electric vehicle charger cable assembly (charging connector).

      Standard: 

      No bending or deformation of pins a a a

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
      NOTE: If the inspection result is not within the standard, replace the Electric vehicle charger cable assembly.
    3. Check that there is no bending or deformation of the pins in the AC charger inlet cable (charging inlet).

    Standard: 

    No bending or deformation of pins

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    NOTE: If the inspection result is not within the standard, replace the AC charger inlet cable (charging inlet).
  4. Check for short circuit.
    WARNING: Be sure to wear insulated gloves.
    1. Connect the Electric vehicle charger cable assembly to the outlet.
      NOTE: Do not connect the Electric vehicle charger cable assembly to the AC charger inlet cable (charging inlet).
    2. Measure the voltage between pins at the Electric vehicle charger cable assembly (charging connector).

    Preparation tool: 

    Circuit tester

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Terminal No. Inspection conditions Standard
    3 - 5 The unlock button is pressed. Less than 1 V
    The unlock button is not pressed. Less than 1 V
    4 - 5
    • Power indicator illuminated
    • Error indicator OFF
    		 11.4 - 12.6 V
  5. Check for open circuit.
    1. Measure the resistance between pins in the Electric vehicle charger cable assembly (charging connector).

      Preparation tool: 

      Circuit tester

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
      Terminal No. Inspection conditions Standard
      3 - 5 The unlock button is pressed. 430 - 530 Ω
      The unlock button is not pressed. 135 - 165 Ω
    2. Insert the plug cable EV charger key as shown in the figure and release the lock, then rotate the connector and disconnect the cable EV charger plug assembly from CCID (electrical leakage detector).
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Measure the resistance between terminals of the cable EV charger plug assembly.

      Preparation tool: 

      Circuit tester

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
      Terminal No. Inspection conditions Standard
      1 - 1' Always Less than 0.1 Ω
      2 - 2' Always Less than 0.1 Ω
      3 - 3' Always Less than 0.1 Ω
      4 - 5 Always 130 - 660 Ω
    4. If the inspection result is not within the standard, replace the cable EV charger plug assembly.
