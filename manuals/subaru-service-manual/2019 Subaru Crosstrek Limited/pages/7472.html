Water Tank Pipe Side

NOTE: Thermostat on the water tank pipe side is featured on CVT models only.
  1. Drain engine coolant. Refer to COOLING (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE COOLANT>REPLACEMENT>DRAINING OF ENGINE COOLANT .
  2. Remove the clip from the air intake boot, and loosen the clamp.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the air intake boot, and place it aside so that it does not interfere with the work.
    CAUTION: Be careful not to pull out the PCV hose.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Disconnect the water hoses.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the thermostat cover, and then remove the thermostat.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Remove the gasket from thermostat.
