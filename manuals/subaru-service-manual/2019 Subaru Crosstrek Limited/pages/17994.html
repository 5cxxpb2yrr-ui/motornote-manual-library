Transfer Clutch: Disassembly

  1. Remove the snap ring, and then remove the pressure plate, drive plate and driven plate.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Using the ST, compress the return spring with a press and remove the snap ring.

    Preparation tool: 

    ST: COMPRESSOR SPECIAL TOOL (18762AA001)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the transfer clutch piston seal.
  4. Remove the return spring.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the transfer clutch piston by blowing compressed air through transfer clutch assembly hole.
    NOTE: Plug the holes through which the compressed air is not blown.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Remove the ball bearing using the ST1 and ST2.

    Preparation tool: 

    ST1: BEARING REMOVER (18767AA010)

    ST2: REMOVER (18723AA000)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
