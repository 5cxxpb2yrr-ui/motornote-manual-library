The Lighting Does Not Switch From High Beam To Low Beam Automatically

Trouble symptom: 

High beam light does not go off automatically even when the high beam assist indicator is illuminating.

CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to BODY CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK HIGH BEAM ACTIVATION (CHECK BY MANUAL OPERATION). 

    Check for the activation of the high beam.

    • Light SW position: Second notch
    • Dimmer Hi SW: ON

    Does the high beam light come on?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Check the headlight system. Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>HEADLIGHT SYSTEM .

  2. CHECK LOW BEAM ACTIVATION (CHECK BY MANUAL OPERATION). 

    From the condition in step 1, turn the dimmer Hi SW to OFF.

    Does it switch to low beam?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Check the headlight system. Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>HEADLIGHT SYSTEM .

  3. RECOGNITION TEST (CHECK OF HBA HI PERMISSION FLAG). 
    1. Set the vehicle in a stopped state.
    2. Set the light SW position to Auto.
    3. Set the dimmer Hi SW to ON.
    4. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, check the following items in the [Data monitor] of [EyeSight]. Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>DATA MONITOR . Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DATA MONITOR .
      • HBA Control Permission

    Is [HBA Control Permission] set to [Allowance]?

    Yes:  Replace the stereo camera. Refer to EYESIGHT>STEREO CAMERA .

    No:  Check the headlight system. Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>HEADLIGHT SYSTEM .
