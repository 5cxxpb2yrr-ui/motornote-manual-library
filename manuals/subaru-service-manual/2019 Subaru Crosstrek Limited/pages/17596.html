DTC P0500: Vehicle Speed Sensor "A" Circuit: Notes

1. - 

DTC detecting condition: 

Immediately at fault recognition

Trouble symptom: 

VDC does not operate.

CAUTION: After servicing or replacing faulty parts, perform the last step of the basic diagnostic procedure. Refer to TRANSMISSION (DIAGNOSTICS)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE .
  1. CHECK DTC  .

    Read the DTC of [Brake Control] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to BRAKE CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Are any DTCs displayed?

    Yes:  Perform the diagnosis according to DTC. Refer to BRAKE CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>LIST .

    No:  Repair the poor contact of connector and harness between VDC control module & H/U and wheel speed sensor.
