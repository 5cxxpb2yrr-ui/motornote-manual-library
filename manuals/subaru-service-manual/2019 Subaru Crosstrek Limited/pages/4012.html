4 Door Model

  1. Install the keyless entry control module.

    Tightening torque: 

    7.5 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.5 ft-lb)

  2. Install the trim panel assembly - rear shelf. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>REAR SHELF TRIM>INSTALLATION .
  3. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  4. When the keyless entry control module is replaced, register the transmitter ID. (Model with TPMS) Refer to TIRE PRESSURE MONITOR (DIAGNOSTICS)>REGISTER TRANSMITTER (ID)>OPERATION .
