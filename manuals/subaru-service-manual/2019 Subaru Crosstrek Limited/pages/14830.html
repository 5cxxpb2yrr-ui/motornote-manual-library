Front Seat: Removal

CAUTION:
  • Before handling the airbag system components, refer to "CAUTION" of "General Description" in "AIRBAG SYSTEM". Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  • When any of the following operation has been done, be sure to perform [Rezeroing] of occupant detection system. Refer to OCCUPANT DETECTION (DIAGNOSTICS)>REZERO .
    • Replacing occupant detection system
    • Removing and disassembling passenger's seat
    • Loosening passenger's seat bolt
  1. Remove the headrest assembly - front.
  2. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery and wait for at least 60 seconds before starting work. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    NOTE: For models with power seat, remove the slide rail installing bolt before removing the battery ground terminal.
  3. Remove the slide rail assembly installing bolt.
    CAUTION: Install the front seat with low head bolts. Use a suitable tool to prevent bolt damage.
    1. Tilt the backrest assembly forward and move the front seat to the front most position.
    2. Remove the cover - bolt rear, and remove the bolts.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
      NOTE: The illustration shows the LH side.
    3. Move the front seat to the rearmost position, and remove the bolts.
      NOTE:
      • After the bolts are removed, slide the seat to the middle position in order to make it easier to take out of the vehicle.
      • For the power seat models, disconnect the battery ground terminal.
  4. Disconnect the connector under the front seat.
    • Seat belt warning light connector (driver's seat)
    • Occupant detection system harness connector (passenger's seat) Refer to OCCUPANT DETECTION (DIAGNOSTICS)>AIRBAG CONNECTOR>PROCEDURE>OCCUPANT DETECTION SYSTEM (BETWEEN AIRBAG REAR HARNESS AND SEAT HARNESS) .
    • Side airbag connector Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>AIRBAG CONNECTOR>PROCEDURE>SIDE AIRBAG MODULE .
    • Seat heater connector (manual seat) (model with seat heater)
    • Seat harness connector & power seat connector (power seat)
    • Buckle switch RH connector (passenger's seat) Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>AIRBAG CONNECTOR>PROCEDURE>BUCKLE SWITCH RH .
  5. Take out the front seat from the vehicle.
CAUTION: Be careful not to damage the body.
