Check Of Lane Change Signal

Trouble symptom: 

Lane change signal function does not operate.

  1. CHECK HAZARD LIGHT SYSTEM  .

    Check the hazard light system. Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>TURN SIGNAL LIGHT AND HAZARD LIGHT SYSTEM>INSPECTION>CHECK HAZARD LIGHT OPERATION .

    Is the hazard light system normal?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Repair the hazard light system.

  2. CHECK TURN SIGNAL LIGHT SYSTEM  .

    Check the turn signal light system. Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>TURN SIGNAL LIGHT AND HAZARD LIGHT SYSTEM>INSPECTION>CHECK TURN SIGNAL LIGHT SYSTEM .

    Is the turn signal light system normal?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Repair the turn signal light system.

  3. CHECK BODY INTEGRATED UNIT SETTING  .

    Display the [Lane change signal setting] data using Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to BODY CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>CUSTOMIZE .

    Is the setting in the [Support]?

    Yes:  Check the body integrated unit and replace it if defective.

    • Inspection: Refer to BODY CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE .
    • Replacement: Refer to SECURITY AND LOCKS>BODY INTEGRATED UNIT .

    No:  Turn the setting to [Support].
