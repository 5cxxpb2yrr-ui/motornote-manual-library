Rear Differential

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  • Impreza model

    When replacing a rear differential assembly, select the correct one according to the following table.

    NOTE:
    • Using a different rear differential assembly will cause the drive train and tires to drag or emit abnormal noise.
    • For option code, refer to "IDENTIFICATION" service information.

      Refer to IDENTIFICATION .

    Model CVT 5MT
    Rear differential type VA1-type T-type
    Identification 42 T2
    LSD type -
    Type of gear Hypoid gear
    Gear ratio (number of gear teeth) 3.700 (37/10) 4.111 (37/9)
    Oil capacity 0.8 L (0.8 US qt, 0.7 Imp qt)
    Rear differential gear oil SUBARU GEAR OIL EXTRA MT or equivalent GL-5 (75W-90) or equivalent
