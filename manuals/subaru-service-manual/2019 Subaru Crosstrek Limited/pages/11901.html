Delivery Mode

  1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF and install the delivery mode fuse.
    CAUTION: Do not use any fuses that are installed on the vehicle.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Turn the ignition switch to ON and check that the malfunction indicator light starts blinking.
  3. If the malfunction indicator light blinks, return the ignition switch to LOCK.
  4. Remove the delivery mode fuse and attach it to the spare fuse holder provided on the fuse box cover.
  5. Then, turn the ignition switch to ON again.
  6. Make sure that the malfunction indicator light is off. If the malfunction indicator light blinks, carry out an engine diagnosis.
