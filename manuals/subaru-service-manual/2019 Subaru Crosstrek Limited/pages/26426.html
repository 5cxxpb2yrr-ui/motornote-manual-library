Explanation For Circuits

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly detects the IG2 signal ON stuck by comparing with the IG control status signal sent from Keyless access Control Module.

NOTE: When DTC P253012 is detected, READY OFF is turned on.
