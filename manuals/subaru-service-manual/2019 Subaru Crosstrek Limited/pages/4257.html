Front Door

  1. Apply the front door sash tape - medium from the rear of the vehicle towards the front.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Apply the front door sash tape - front from the top of the vehicle towards the bottom.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Fold the corners at the door upper end area in the order of (1) - (3).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Install a new the cover B pillar - front.
  5. Install the weather strip outer - front door.
  6. Install the running channel - front door.
  7. Install the glass assembly - front door.

    Tightening torque: 

    7.5 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.5 ft-lb)

  8. Install the front sealing cover.
  9. Install the trim panel - front door.
  10. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY>GROUND TERMINAL REMOVAL OR INSTALLATION .
