Door Trim: Disassembly

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Remove the trim panel - door.
  2. Disengage the rivets and claws, and remove the weather strip - door INN from the trim panel - door.

    NOTE: Remove the rivets with a rivet cutter or nipper, etc.
