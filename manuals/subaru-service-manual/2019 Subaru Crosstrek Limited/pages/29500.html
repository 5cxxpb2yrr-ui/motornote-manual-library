Oil Pan: Installation

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Install a new O-ring to the oil strainer.
    NOTE: Apply ATF to the O-ring.
  2. Install the oil strainer.

    Tightening torque: 

    9 N.m (0.9 kgf-m, 6.6 ft-lb)

  3. Attach the magnet on the oil pan.
  4. Apply liquid gasket to the mating surface of the oil pan as shown in the figure, and temporarily install the oil pan.
    NOTE:
    • Before applying liquid gasket, degrease the old liquid gasket seal surfaces of the oil pan and transmission body.
    • Install within 5 min. after applying liquid gasket.

    Liquid gasket: 

    THREE BOND 1217B (Part No. K0877YA020) or equivalent

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Tighten the bolts equally.

    Tightening torque: 

    5 N.m (0.5 kgf-m, 3.7 ft-lb)

  6. Fill with ATF.

    Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TH2A)>AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION FLUID>REPLACEMENT .
