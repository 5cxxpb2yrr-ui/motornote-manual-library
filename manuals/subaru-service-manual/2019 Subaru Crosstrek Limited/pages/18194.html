Battery Sensor: Removal

  1. Disconnect the connector and ground terminal.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Remove the 12V engine restart battery sensor.
    CAUTION: The end of the bolt (a) of the battery sensor is crimped. Therefore, do not loosen the nut (b) to the crimped portion.
