Cruise Control Is Cancelled Without Releasing Operation

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
NOTE:
  • Do not turn the ignition switch to OFF after the cruise control is deactivated.
  • Do not operate the EyeSight steering switch after the cruise control is deactivated.
  1. PERFORM REAL TIME DIAGNOSIS. 

    Check the input signal of cruise control system. Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>REAL-TIME DIAGNOSIS .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  5.

    • When EyeSight steering switch signal is faulty. Go to  2.
    • When the stop light switch or brake switch signal is faulty. Go to  3.
    • When the sift lever signal is faulty. Go to  4.
  2. CHECK EyeSight STEERING SWITCH. 

    Check the EyeSight steering switch. Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS (HEV)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>STOP LIGHT SWITCH OR BRAKE SWITCH IS TURNED TO ON, AND THEN THE CRUISE CONTROL IS RELEASED .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Replace the stereo camera. Refer to EYESIGHT>STEREO CAMERA>REMOVAL .

    No:  Replace the EyeSight steering switch. Refer to EYESIGHT>SWITCHES AND HARNESS .

  3. INSPECTION OF STOP LIGHT SWITCH AND BRAKE SWITCH. 

    Check stop light switch and brake switch. Refer to BRAKE>BRAKE PEDAL . Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>STOP LIGHT SWITCH OR BRAKE SWITCH IS TURNED TO ON, AND THEN THE CRUISE CONTROL IS RELEASED .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Replace the ECM. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>ENGINE CONTROL MODULE (ECM) .

    No:  Repair or replace the faulty portion of the stop light switch or brake switch.

  4. CHECK SHIFT POSITION SENSOR. 

    Check the shift position sensor. Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE WITH DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>DTC P070562 TRANSMISSION RANGE SENSOR "A" CIRCUIT (PRNDL INPUT) SIGNAL COMPARE FAILURE .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  5.

    No:  Repair or replace the faulty portion according to the inspection result.

  5. CHECK STEREO CAMERA POWER SUPPLY VOLTAGE. 

    Check the stereo camera power supply voltage. Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE WITH DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>DTC B2814 POWER SUPPLY LOW VOLTAGE .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Replace the stereo camera. Refer to EYESIGHT>STEREO CAMERA>REMOVAL .

    No:  Repair or replace the faulty portion according to the inspection result.
