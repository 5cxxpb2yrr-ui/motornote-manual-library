Transmission Control Module (TCM): Installation

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Attach the TCM.

    Tightening torque: 

    7 N.m (0.7 kgf-m, 5.2 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Attach the harness connector to the TCM.
    NOTE: Move the lock lever in the arrow direction, and confirm that a clicking sound is heard.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Insert the transmission case cover (small) between transmission case cover (large) and transmission to install.
    CAUTION: When inserting, be careful that the insulator inside transmission case cover is not turned over.

    Tightening torque: 

    7 N.m (0.7 kgf-m, 5.2 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Install the pitching stopper.

    Tightening torque: 

    T1: 50 N.m (5.1 kgf-m, 36.9 ft-lb)

    T2: 58 N.m (5.9 kgf-m, 42.8 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Install the air breather hose and air breather hose bracket.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Connect the transmission radio ground terminal.

    Tightening torque: 

    32.3 N.m (3.3 kgf-m, 23.8 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Install the air intake boot. Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>Air Intake Boot>INSTALLATION .
  8. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  9. Perform the operation of AT learning mode. Refer to TRANSMISSION (DIAGNOSTICS)>LEARNING CONTROL .
