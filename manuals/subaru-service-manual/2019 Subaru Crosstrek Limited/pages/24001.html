Electric Vent Solenoid Valve Assembly: Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Open the fuel filler lid and remove the fuel filler cap.
  2. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery.

    Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .

  3. Disconnect the connector (A).
  4. Disconnect the vent tube No. 2 (B) and vent tube No. 3 (C).

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>REMOVAL>QUICK CONNECTOR DISCONNECTION (TYPE H) .

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the electric vent solenoid valve assembly.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Remove the bracket from the electric vent solenoid valve assembly.
    NOTE: The electric vent solenoid valve assembly cannot be disassembled.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
