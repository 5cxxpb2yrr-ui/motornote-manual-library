Circulate Tube Assy: Installation

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Attach the bracket.

    Tightening torque: 

    8 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.9 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Connect the circulate tube assembly to the fuel tank and fuel filler pipe, and install the circulate tube assembly. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>INSTALLATION>QUICK CONNECTOR CONNECTION (TYPE F) .

    Tightening torque: 

    8 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.9 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Connect the connector (A) and air hose (B).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
