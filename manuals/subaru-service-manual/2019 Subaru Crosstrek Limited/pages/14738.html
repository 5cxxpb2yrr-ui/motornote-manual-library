NOTE: Refer to "Security and Locks" service information for key cylinder replacement. Refer to SECURITY AND LOCKS>KEY LOCK CYLINDERS>REPLACEMENT>GLOVE BOX LID .
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the pocket assembly.
    1. Remove the damper COMPL - pocket.
    2. Release the stoppers and remove the pocket assembly by pulling it toward you.

  3. Remove the back panel - pocket.
    1. Release the clips, and remove the under cover assembly - passenger.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Remove the clips, and remove the cover - instrument panel side RH.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
      NOTE:
      • Use a plastic remover.
      • If removing is difficult, remove the weather strip, then remove the part from the gap using a plastic remover.
    3. Release the clips, and remove the ornament - panel assembly passenger.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    4. Remove the glove box light LED, and disconnect the connector.
    5. Release the claws and remove the screws, then remove the back panel - pocket.
