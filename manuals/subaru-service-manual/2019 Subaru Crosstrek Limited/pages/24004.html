Check Mechanical Relief Valve

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Check the mechanical relief valves for deformation, cracks or other damages.
  2. Connect the Mighty Vac to the electric vent solenoid valve on the fuel tank side.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Using the Mighty Vac, generate the positive pressure. Check that the Mighty Vac gauge needle rises at the pressure [20 - 27 kPa (0.20 - 0.28 kgf/cm2 , 2.90 - 3.91 psi)] then stops rising further.
  4. Using the Mighty Vac, generate the vacuum pressure. Check that the Mighty Vac gauge needle drops at the pressure [-8 - 15 kPa (-0.08 - -0.15 kgf/cm2 , -1.16 - 2.17 psi)], then stops dropping further.
