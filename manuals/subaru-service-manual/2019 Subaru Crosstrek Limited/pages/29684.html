Continuity Check

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Disconnect the battery terminal or connector so there is no voltage between the check points.

    Contact the leads of an ohmmeter between the check points, and check that there is continuity.

  2. When checking the diode continuity using an ohmmeter, allow the positive lead to contact the diode positive side and the negative lead to the negative side. At this time, there must be continuity. Also, when contacting the leads in reverse, there should be no continuity.
    NOTE: Some testers have reverse polarity, therefore, refer to the instructions of the tester.
