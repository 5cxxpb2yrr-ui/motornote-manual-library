Malfunction Description

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

This indicates that the resolver output signal value became abnormal. The following cases are assumed to be causes of this symptom.

  • Malfunction of Inside of inverter
    • Inverter with converter assembly inner circuit malfunction
  • Malfunction of Inverter low-voltage circuit
    • Improperly connected connector
    NOTE: If this DTC is output, Motor generator control ECU circuit board malfunction or defective fitting of low pressure connector is assumed. Resolver inspection is not necessary.
