DTC B1731: Central Gateway Control Module Can Register Stuck

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

Outline of diagnosis: 

When the microcomputer was reset (power turned ON), a failsafe judgment error was detected in one of the CH.

Trouble symptom: 

CAN sending and receiving (including Ack) stops at the CH where register freeze occurred.

NOTE: If this DTC was detected, replace the central gateway control module. Ref. to SECURITY AND LOCKS>GATEWAY CONTROL MODULE .
