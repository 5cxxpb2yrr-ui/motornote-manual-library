Rear Lateral Link (Rear): Assembly

  1. Before assembly, inspect the following items and replace any faulty part with a new one.
    • Visually check the rear lateral link (rear) for damage and deformation.
    • Visually check the bushing for abnormal cracks, fatigue or damage.
  2. Using the ST, press-fit the bushing - rear lateral link (rear).

    Preparation tool: 

    ST-A & ST-B: INSTALLER & REMOVER (20099AE010)
