High Voltage Battery: Removal

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
WARNING:
  • The hybrid system includes a high voltage circuit. Mishandling could cause accidents such as electric shock or leak. Always check "WARNING (HYBRID SYSTEM)" and perform the proper operation. Refer to PRECAUTION/WARNING>WARNING (HYBRID SYSTEM) .
  • For safety reasons, be sure to return the replaced HV battery to a qualified collection service. If you use a collection service designated by manufacturer, the battery will be handled properly and safely.
  • If the HV battery is not properly processed and disposed of, or is left unprocessed, it may cause electrical shock accidents. Be sure to have the HV battery collected through the dealership.
  • Do not leave the removed HV battery in a place where water may contact it. It could cause fire accidents.
CAUTION:
  • Airbag system satellite safing sensor is located under the front center of the rear seat cushion. Be careful not to apply strong impact to the sensor when working with the rear seat cushion removed.
  • When transporting, removing, and installing the HV battery, keep the angle of inclination to 80° or less.
  • Do not climb onto the HV battery. Do not insert hands into openings or allow foreign matter to enter.
  1. Check the HV battery temperature.
    1. Using Subaru Select Monitor, select [Hybrid Battery Temperature 1] - [Hybrid Battery Temperature 15] from the list of Data Monitor items. Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DATA MONITOR .
    2. Check that the battery temperature is below 50°C (122°F).
    NOTE: If the battery temperature is 50°C (122°F) or higher, leave the battery to cool and begin the work after the temperature has dropped to below 50°C (122°F).
  2. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery and wait for at least 60 seconds before starting work. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  3. Remove the service disconnect plug. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG>REMOVAL .
  4. Remove the rear seat cushion assembly, backrest assembly, hinge assembly - backrest CTR, and plate - anchor LWR LH. Refer to SEATS>REAR SEAT>REMOVAL .
  5. Remove the trim panel - rear apron. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>REAR QUARTER TRIM>REMOVAL .
  6. Remove the rear antenna assembly - interior. Refer to SECURITY AND LOCKS>KEYLESS ACCESS INDOOR ANTENNA>REMOVAL>REAR ANTENNA .
  7. Remove the Reverse Automatic Braking control module. Refer to REVERSE AUTOMATIC BRAKING>REVERSE AUTOMATIC BRAKING CONTROL MODULE >REMOVAL .
  8. Remove the duct - battery cooling blower. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY COOLING SYSTEM>REMOVAL>FRONT DUCT .
  9. Remove the battery cooling blower assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY COOLING SYSTEM>REMOVAL>COOLING BLOWER .
  10. Remove the duct assembly - cooling out. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY COOLING SYSTEM>REMOVAL>REAR DUCT .
  11. Remove the charger duct. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>ELECTRIC VEHICLE CHARGER>REMOVAL>CHARGER DUCT .
  12. Remove the duct assembly - gas out. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HOSE AND PIPE>REMOVAL .
  13. Disconnect the main battery cable assembly.
    WARNING: Be sure to wear insulated gloves.
    1. Release the clip and hook, and then remove the cover - interlock.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Disconnect the ground terminal and remove the cable clip.
    3. Disconnect the connector and disconnect the main battery cable assembly.
    CAUTION:
    • Be sure to insulate the disconnected connector by wrapping it with insulating tape.
    • Apply tape or similar item (that will not leave adhesive residue) to close the opening after the cable was disconnected so that foreign matter, water drops, or other substance does not enter.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  14. Perform zero-voltage test.
    WARNING: Be sure to wear insulated gloves.
    1. Measure the voltage of the high voltage line between HV battery connector terminals.

    Preparation tool: 

    Circuit tester

    Standard: 0 V 

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  15. Move the main battery - sensor cord.
    WARNING: Be sure to wear insulated gloves.
    1. Remove the cover - sensor.
      1. Using the service disconnect plug, release the interlock clip.
        1. Align the projecting portion on the service disconnect plug with the interlock clip groove.
          CAUTION: To avoid damaging the projecting portion and the clip, fit the service disconnect plug completely.
        2. While holding the Interlock clip, rotate and detach the service disconnect plug in the direction of arrow shown in the figure.
        NOTE: Store the removed Interlock clip together with the service disconnect plug.
      2. Remove the bolts, and then remove the cover - sensor.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Disconnect the connector, and remove the connector clip.
    3. Remove the bolt and clamp, and move the main battery - sensor cord.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  16. Remove the bar - floor front COMPL.
    1. Remove the bolts, and remove the bar - floor front COMPL.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  17. Remove the bracket - battery front.
    1. Remove the bolts, and then remove the bracket - battery front.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  18. Remove the bar - floor rear COMPL.
    WARNING: Be sure to wear insulated gloves.
    1. Disconnect the connector, and remove the connector clip.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Release the clips and remove the wire harness.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Disconnect the HV battery charger wire No. 1 connector.
      CAUTION: Be sure to insulate the disconnected connector by wrapping it with insulating tape.
      1. Use protective tape to wrap a flat tip screwdriver of an appropriate size for the insertion opening, and disengage lock (a).
      2. Press the tab (b) to disconnect the connector.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    4. Remove the connector clips and wire clips, and disconnect the HV battery charger wire No. 1.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    5. Remove the bolts, and remove the ground terminal bar - floor rear COMPL.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  19. Remove the HV battery.
WARNING: Be sure to wear insulated gloves.
CAUTION: Since the HV battery is heavy, removal work must be performed by 2 or more persons using a mini crane. Use caution to prevent damage to surrounding parts.
  1. Remove the center antenna assembly - interior harness clips.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Remove the bolts and nuts.
    CAUTION: Do not touch bolts and nuts if they have grease or other oil component on them.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Use the mini crane and remove the HV battery.
WARNING: Because the HV battery is heavy, pay careful attention to the work procedure and balance when hoisting the HV battery in order to prevent injury or accident.
CAUTION:
  • Be careful that the mini crane, cable, and HV battery do not contact the body, glass, or other objects.
  • If the HV battery was dropped or subjected to a strong impact, replace it with a new battery.
  1. Prepare the guide wire, cable, protective rubber, and mini crane. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>PREPARATION TOOL>GENERAL TOOL .
  2. Pass the cables through the frame battery - UPR and frame battery - MDL.
    1. Insert the guide wires into the frame, and press them in until they emerge from the frame on the opposite side.
    2. Pass the cables through the emerged guide wires, and again press the guide wires in until they emerge from the frame on the opposite side.
    3. Pull the 2 guide wires and pass the cables through the frame.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    NOTE: If it is difficult to pull the guide wire on the rear side, remove the bumper face - rear and grille - air ventilation, and pull the wire from the grille - air ventilation mounting hole.
  3. Insert the mini crane from the rear of the cargo space, and install the cables onto the crane hook.
    1. Install protective rubber at 4 locations where the HV battery and cable contact each other.
      NOTE: As necessary, apply protection by inserting cloth into the gaps.
    2. Adjust so that the distance (a) from crane hook to HV battery is approx. 180 mm (7.09 in).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Pull out the HV battery while adjusting the mini crane height.
    CAUTION:
    • In order to prevent the wire harnesses and cables from becoming caught, bundle together the wire harnesses, cables, and similar items with vinyl tape.
    • In order to prevent scratches, protect the vehicle with cardboard or other material while working.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the clips, and remove the cover - battery LWR.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Place the HV battery on a rigid rack and disconnect the mini crane and cables.
