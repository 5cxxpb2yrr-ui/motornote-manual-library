Light Control Sensor: Removal

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery and wait for at least 60 seconds before starting work. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the trim panel - front pillar UPR RH. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>UPPER INNER TRIM>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the grille speaker side RH.
    1. Release the clips, hooks and claws.
    2. Disconnect the connector and remove the grille speaker side RH.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the light control sensor.
    1. Release the claws, and then remove the light control sensor.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
