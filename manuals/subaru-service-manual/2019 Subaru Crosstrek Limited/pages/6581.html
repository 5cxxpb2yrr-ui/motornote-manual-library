Starting System & Charging System (Except For HEV): Battery: Removal

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from 12V engine restart battery sensor. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Disconnect the connector from 12V engine restart battery sensor.
  3. Remove the 12V engine restart battery sensor. (When replacing the battery) Refer to STARTING/CHARGING SYSTEMS (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>BATTERY SENSOR>REMOVAL .
  4. Disconnect the positive terminal of battery.
  5. Remove the battery cable holder.
  6. Remove the battery holder.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Remove the battery.
