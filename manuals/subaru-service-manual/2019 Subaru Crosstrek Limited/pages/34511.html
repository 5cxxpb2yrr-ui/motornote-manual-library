Water Pipe RH

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Drain engine coolant. Refer to COOLING (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>Engine Coolant>REPLACEMENT>DRAINING OF ENGINE COOLANT .
  3. Remove the front exhaust pipe. Refer to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>FRONT EXHAUST PIPE>REMOVAL .
  4. Disconnect the water hoses (rear side).

  5. Remove the bolts securing the water pipe RH.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Remove the water hose (front side) and the water pipe RH as an assembly.
