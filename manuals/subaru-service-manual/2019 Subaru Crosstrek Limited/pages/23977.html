Purge Control Solenoid Valve: Removal

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery.

    Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .

  2. Remove the clip from the air intake boot, and loosen the clamp.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the air intake boot, and place it aside so that it does not interfere with the work.
    CAUTION: Be careful not to pull out the PCV hose.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Disconnect the connector (A), and remove the clip (B) securing the engine wiring harness.
  5. Disconnect the purge hose (C).
  6. Disconnect the vacuum hose (D) from the intake manifold assembly, and remove the vacuum hose (D) from the clip (E).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Remove the purge control solenoid valve.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Remove the purge control solenoid valve bracket.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Remove the vacuum hose from the purge control solenoid valve.
