Propeller Shaft: Installation

  1. Before installation, check the following items, and replace the propeller shaft as necessary.
    • Dents or cracks on the tube surface
    • Splines for deformation or abnormal wear
    • Unsmooth joint operation or abnormal noise
    • Center bearing for looseness, unsmooth operation or noise.
    • Oil seals for abnormal wear or damage
    • Damaged center bearing
  2. Apply CVTF to the oil seal lip and propeller shaft. Ref. to RECOMMENDED MATERIALS>FLUID .
  3. Insert the sleeve yoke into the transmission and attach center bearing to body.

    Tightening torque: 

    52 N.m (5.3 kgf-m, 38.4 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Align the alignment marks (A), and connect the flange yoke and rear differential.
  5. Install the bolts and nuts.

    Tightening torque: 

    31 N.m (3.2 kgf-m, 22.9 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Check the propeller shaft with the propeller shaft installed to the vehicle. Ref. to DRIVE SHAFT SYSTEM>PROPELLER SHAFT>INSPECTION .
  7. Install the center exhaust cover.

    Tightening torque: 

    18 N.m (1.8 kgf-m, 13.3 ft-lb)

  8. Install the rear exhaust pipe and muffler. Ref. to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>REAR EXHAUST PIPE>INSTALLATION . Ref. to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>MUFFLER>INSTALLATION .
  9. Lower the vehicle.
