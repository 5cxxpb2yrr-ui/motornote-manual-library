Explanation For Circuits

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

The boosting converter boosts DC voltage rating 352 V of the HV battery up to the maximum of DC voltage approx. 650 V. The voltage boosted by the boosting converter is converted into AC through the inverter, and used to drive the motor/generator. Also, when the motor/generator operates as a generator, AC voltage supplied from the motor/generator is converted into DC through the inverter. Then, it is dropped down through the boosting converter into DC voltage rating 352 V, and used to charge the HV battery.

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.

Motor generator control ECU detects the high voltage before boosting (VL) with the voltage sensor built in the boosting converter. Moreover, it detects the post boosting high voltage (VH) with the voltage sensor which is built in the inverter. Motor generator control ECU boosts into the target voltage by controlling the boosting converter operation based on the voltage before/after boosting.
