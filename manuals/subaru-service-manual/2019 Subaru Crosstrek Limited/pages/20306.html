DTC P0ABF-15: Hybrid/EV Battery Current Sensor "A" Circuit Short To Auxiliary Battery Or Open: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Outline of diagnosis: 

When the BATTERY CURRENT SENSOR output value is unusually high (IB0)

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

  • Reduced output power
  • Engine not stopped
