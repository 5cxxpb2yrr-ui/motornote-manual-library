HV Battery High-Voltage Line Circuit

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .

  1. CHECK Inverter with converter assembly (CHECK Main battery cable ASSY CONNECTING CONDITION)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Check that the Service plug is disconnected. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .
    2. Check that the connecting bolt for the connector (HV35) for Inverter with converter assembly is tightened to the specified torque, and is free from looseness and poor contact. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>INVERTER AND CONVERTER ASSEMBLY .

      Standard: 

      7.5 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.5 ft-lb)

    Is it tightened to the specified torque, and free from looseness and poor contact?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Connect Main battery cable ASSY securely. Or repair the poor contact of Main battery cable ASSY.

  2. CHECK Inverter with converter assembly (CHECK Main battery cable ASSY CONNECTING CONDITION)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Disconnect Inverter with converter assembly connector (HV35).
    2. Check Inverter with converter assembly and Wire harness for trace of arc.

    Is there any trace of arc?

    Yes:  Replace the parts if defective.

    No:  Go to  3.

  3. CHECK Service plug  .

    Check the connecting condition of Service plug. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .

    Is it free from any trace of entering or adhering of dirt or foreign matter that can cause mating failure?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Replace Service plug. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .

  4. CHECK THE Service plug UNIT  .

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between the removed Service plug terminals.

    Terminals 

    (HV89) No. 1 - (HV89) No. 2:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  5.

    No:  Replace Service plug. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .

  5. CHECK THE Hybrid battery terminal block UNIT  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Disconnect HV battery high voltage connectors (HV92) and (HV94) from Hybrid battery terminal block.
      CAUTION: Be sure to insulate the disconnected high voltage connector by wrapping it with insulating tape. To do this, wrap the insulating tape from Wire harness along to the connector.
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between Hybrid battery terminal block terminals.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV92) No. 1 - (HV94) No. 1:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  6.

    No:  Replace Hybrid battery terminal block.

  6. CHECK HV battery junction block (CHECK Main battery cable ASSY CONNECTING CONDITION)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Disconnect HV battery junction block connectors (HV44) and (HV47).
    2. Check the connectors (HV44) and (HV47) of HV battery junction block for trace of arc.

    Is there any trace of arc?

    Yes:  Replace the parts if defective.

    No:  Go to  7.

  7. CHECK Power cable (OPEN CIRCUIT)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between Inverter with converter assembly connector and HV battery junction block connector.

    Connector & terminal 

    (HV44) No. 1 - (HV35) No. 2:

    (HV47) No. 1 - (HV35) No. 1:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  8.

    No:  Replace Main battery cable ASSY. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>POWER CABLE .

  8. CHECK Main battery cable ASSY (SHORT TO GROUND OR LINE-TO-LINE SHORT)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.

    Using a megohmmeter (500 V range), measure the resistance between Inverter with converter assembly connector terminals, between HV battery junction block connector terminals, between Inverter with converter assembly connector and chassis ground and shield line ground, and between HV battery junction block connector and chassis ground.

    CAUTION: When performing this check, be sure to set megohmmeter to 500 V. Using a range higher than 500 V may damage the part being checked.

    Connector & terminal 

    (HV35) No. 1 - (HV35) No. 2:

    (HV35) No. 1 - Chassis ground and shield line ground:

    (HV35) No. 2 - Chassis ground and shield line ground:

    (HV44) No. 1 - (HV47) No. 1:

    (HV44) No. 1 - Chassis ground:

    (HV47) No. 1 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance 10 MΩ or more?

    Yes:  Go to  9.

    No:  Replace Main battery cable ASSY. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>POWER CABLE .

  9. CHECK HV battery junction block UNIT (SMRB)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Remove the HV battery junction block.
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between HV battery junction block terminals.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV43) No. 1 - (HV44) No. 1:

    Is the resistance as follows?

    When Battery (12V) voltage is applied between terminals of (HV49) No. 1 (+) - (HV49) No. 2 (-): Less than 1 Ω

    When Battery (12V) voltage is not applied between terminals of (HV49) No. 1 (+) - (HV49) No. 2 (-): Less than 1 Ω

    Yes:  Go to  10.

    No:  Replace HV battery junction block. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>RELAY AND FUSE .

  10. CHECK HV battery junction block UNIT (SMRB)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between HV battery junction block connector terminals.

    Connector & terminal 

    (HV49) No. 1 - (HV49) No. 2:

    Is the resistance 20.6 - 40.8 Ω? (When -40 - 80°C (-40 - 176°F))

    Yes:  Go to  11.

    No:  Replace HV battery junction block. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>RELAY AND FUSE .

  11. CHECK HV battery junction block UNIT (SMRG)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Remove the HV battery junction block.
    2. Using a tester, measure the resistance between HV battery junction block connector terminals.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV46) No. 1 - (HV47) No. 1:

    Is the resistance as follows?

    When Battery (12V) voltage is applied between terminals of (HV51) No. 3 (+) - (HV52) No. 1 (-): Less than 1 Ω

    When Battery (12V) voltage is not applied between terminals of (HV51) No. 3 (+) - (HV52) No. 1 (-): 10 kΩ or more

    Yes:  Go to  12.

    No:  Replace HV battery junction block. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>RELAY AND FUSE .

  12. CHECK HV battery junction block UNIT (SMRG)  .
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between HV battery junction block connector terminals.

    Connector & terminal 

    (HV51) No. 3 - (HV52) No. 1:

    Is the resistance 20.6 - 40.8 Ω? (When -40 - 80°C (-40 - 176°F))

    Yes:  HV BATTERY HIGH-VOLTAGE LINE CIRCUIT is normal.

    No:  Replace HV battery junction block. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>RELAY AND FUSE .

GENERAL DESCRIPTION 

The malfunction cause may be HV BATTERY HIGH-VOLTAGE LINE CIRCUIT.

Check the continuity of the high voltage line from HV battery to the inverter.

Check Service plug, EV battery fuse, and the high voltage wires from HV battery to the inverter for connecting condition and open circuit, and check the function of the system main relay (SMR).

Check related parts 

Part Inspection Procedure
High voltage system from HV battery to the inverter Check the connecting condition and open circuit 1 - 2, 6 - 8
Service plug Check the connecting condition and open circuit 3 - 4
EV battery fuse Check the open circuit 5
System main relay Check the operation condition as relay 9 - 12

Explanation of circuits 

High voltage of HV battery is supplied to the inverter via the system main relay operation.
