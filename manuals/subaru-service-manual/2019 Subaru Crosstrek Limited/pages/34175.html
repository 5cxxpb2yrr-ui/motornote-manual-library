Exhaust Side

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Install the camshaft position sensor to the chain cover with new O-ring.
    NOTE: Apply engine oil to O-ring.

    Tightening torque: 

    6.4 N.m (0.7 kgf-m, 4.7 ft-lb)

  2. Connect the connectors to camshaft position sensor.
  3. Install the under cover. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>FRONT UNDER COVER>INSTALLATION .
  4. Lower the vehicle.
  5. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
