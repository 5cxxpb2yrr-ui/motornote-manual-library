Front Disc Brake Assembly: Assembly

  1. Check each part. Refer to BRAKE>FRONT DISC BRAKE ASSEMBLY>INSPECTION .
  2. Clean the inside of the caliper body using brake fluid.

    Preparation items: 

    Brake fluid: FMVSS No. 116, DOT3 or DOT4

  3. Install the piston seal and the piston - disc brake.
    1. Apply a coat of brake fluid to piston seal and install the piston seal to the groove inside caliper body.
    2. Apply a coat of brake fluid to the piston sliding part of caliper body and the entire outer surface of the piston - disc brake.

      Preparation items: 

      Brake fluid: FMVSS No. 116, DOT3 or DOT4

    3. Apply a coat of grease to the piston boot and fit to the groove on the ends of piston - disc brake.

      Preparation items: 

      Brake grease: An item contained in the piston seal kit or Nippon Grease NIGLUBE RX-2

    4. Install the piston - disc brake to the caliper body.
      CAUTION: Do not force the piston - disc brake into the sliding part of caliper body.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Apply grease to the following locations.
    • Lock pin - front brake
    • Periphery of the guide pin - front brake
    • Support - front disc brake pin sliding section, and grooves of pin boot

    Preparation items: 

    Brake grease: An item contained in the piston seal kit or Nippon Grease NIGLUBE RX-2

  5. Install the pin boot to the lock pin - front brake and the guide pin - front brake, and insert them into the support - front disc brake.
    CAUTION: Insert the lock pin - front brake and guide pin - front brake into specified position, and make sure that they slide and seat properly.
