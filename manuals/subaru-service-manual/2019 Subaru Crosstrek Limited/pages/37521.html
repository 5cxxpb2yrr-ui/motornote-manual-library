Basic Inspection

Before performing diagnosis, check the following items that might provoke the problems related to BSD/RCTA.

  1. Check the battery. Refer to STARTING/CHARGING SYSTEMS (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>BATTERY . Refer to STARTING/CHARGING SYSTEMS (H4DO (HEV))>BATTERY .
  2. Check the relay and fuse condition. Refer to BLIND SPOT DETECTION/REAR CROSS TRAFFIC ALERT>RELAY AND FUSE .
  3. Check the connecting condition of harness and connector. Also check grounding.
