IGNITION COIL PARTS INFORMATION

IGNITION COIL PARTS INFORMATION

TECHNICAL SERVICE BULLETIN

Reference Number(s): 07-166-20, Date of Issue:  January 29, 2020
SUBARU: 2017-19 Impreza; 2018-19 Crosstrek

APPLICABILITY

2017-19MY Impreza

2018-19MY Crosstrek

INTRODUCTION

This Service Information Bulletin announces part number change information for the ignition coils used on the models listed above.

PART INFORMATION

Description NEW Part Number OLD Part Number Interchangeability
COIL ASSEMBLY-IGNITION 22433AA741 22433AA740 New Parts to Old Vehicles: YES.
Old Parts to New Vehicles: NO.
NOTE: The new part number above is the same ignition coil specified in the WUQ-02R Service Campaign.

REMINDER:  Always order the most up-to-date replacement parts based on the specific VIN being repaired.

SERVICE PROCEDURE/INFORMATION

REMINDER:  Customer satisfaction and retention starts with performing quality repairs.

The service procedures for ignition coil replacement remain unchanged. Always refer to the applicable Service Manual and review the full requirements of the repair being performed. The Service Manual procedures contain information critical to performing an effective repair the first time and every time. This includes but is not limited to: important SAFETY precautions, proper inspection criteria, necessary special tools, processes and one-time-use parts needed for a complete and lasting repair.

IMPORTANT REMINDERS: 

  • SOA strongly discourages the printing and/or local storage of service information as previously released information and electronic publications may be updated at any time.
  • Always check for any open recalls or campaigns anytime a vehicle is in for servicing.
  • Always refer to STIS for the latest service information before performing any repairs.
