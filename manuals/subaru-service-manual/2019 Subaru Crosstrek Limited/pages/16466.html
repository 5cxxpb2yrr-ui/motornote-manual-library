Data Communication Module

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the grille assembly - CTR ventilation. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>AIR VENT GRILLE>REMOVAL>CENTER GRILLE ASSEMBLY .
  3. Remove the audio assembly or navigation assembly. Refer to ENTERTAINMENT & MONITORING>AUDIO>REMOVAL .
  4. Remove the data communication module.
    1. Disconnect the cable connected to the audio assembly.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Remove the screws and remove the data communication module.
    CAUTION: Do not drop or apply any impact to the data communication module.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    NOTE: Remove the screws on LH side in the same procedure as on the RH side.
