DTC P1A80-00: Hybrid/EV Battery Stack 1 Delta Soc High: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

1. - 

Outline of diagnosis: 

If the difference of voltage between each cell of NO. 1 HV supply stack sub-assembly is equal to or greater than the standard

(2 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

Regular driving

Wiring diagram: 

Hybrid system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
NOTE:
  • This DTC, after DTC memory clear, will not be output unless about 12 minutes' driving is performed.
  • Securing the cooling performance is important to maintain the HV battery performance. Therefore, perform the following items as necessary. Here, always perform the following items if "Reduced High voltage battery cooling Have vehicle inspected at retailer" is displayed on the multi-information display depending on the degraded cooling performance.
    • Check that the air intake port of HV battery is not blocked.
    • Check that the duct has no gap.
    • Clean HV battery intake filter. After cleaning the filter, clear the DTC memory regardless of presence/absence of stored DTCs (to reset the learning values).

  3. CHECK Battery ECU assembly (VA1 -VA19). 
    CAUTION:
    • Wrong terminal polarity will cause inaccurate measurement.
    • Be sure to read the resistance after it becomes stable.
    • When checking Battery ECU assembly, select a thin probe (approx. 0.5 mm) from the test lead set before measurement; otherwise the terminals would be deformed.
    1. Turn to IG ON.
    2. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, read the value of [Hybrid Battery Cell 1 Voltage] - [Hybrid Battery Cell 19 Voltage] in [Freeze frame data] of DTC P1A8000. Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>FREEZE FRAME DATA .
    3. Turn the ignition to OFF.
    4. Remove Battery ECU assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>BATTERY CONTROL MODULE .
    5. Using a tester, measure the resistance in Battery ECU assembly terminals.
      NOTE: Measure the point only related to Hybrid battery cell where the voltage is minimum from the result of freeze frame data.
