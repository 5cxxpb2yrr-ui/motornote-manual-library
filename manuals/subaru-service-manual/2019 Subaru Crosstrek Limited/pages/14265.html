Switches And Harness: Removal: Switch

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  • TIMER CHARGING SETTING SWITCH 
    1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery and wait for at least 60 seconds before starting work. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY>GROUND TERMINAL REMOVAL OR INSTALLATION .
    2. Remove the cover assembly - instrument panel LWR driver OUT. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>INSTRUMENT PANEL LOWER COVER>REMOVAL .
    3. Remove the timer charging setting switch.
    1. Release the claws and remove the timer charging setting switch (b).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  • HYBRID MODE SWITCH 
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY>GROUND TERMINAL REMOVAL OR INSTALLATION .
    NOTE: Release the shift lock and shift the select lever to the "N" position. Refer to CONTROL SYSTEMS>SELECT LEVER>REMOVAL .
  2. Remove the cover assembly - front. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>CONSOLE BOX>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the hybrid mode switch.
    1. Disconnect the connector.
    2. Release the claws and remove the hybrid mode switch.
Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
