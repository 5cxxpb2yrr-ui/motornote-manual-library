Charging Inlet

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  • CHECK THE CHARGING INLET LOCK ASSEMBLY ON THE VEHICLE  .
    1. Check operation of the charging connector.
    1. Press in the push-lifter and check that the lock pin of the charging connector lock operates.
    WARNING: Do not insert a finger or hand under the lock pin while working. There is the risk of injury resulting from being pinched by the lock pin.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    NOTE:
    • Lock all doors and carry the access key while working within the charging lid unlock detection area.
    • Check that the battery (12V) is normal.
  • CHECK THE CHARGING INLET LOCK ASSEMBLY UNIT  .
  1. Visual inspection
    1. Check that there is no foreign matter or other substance on the lock pin of the charging inlet lock assembly.
    2. Check that there is no bending, deformation, or damage of the lock pin of the charging inlet lock assembly.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    NOTE:
    • If there is foreign matter on the lock pin, remove the foreign matter. If the foreign matter cannot be removed, replace the charging inlet lock assembly.
    • If the lock pin does not operate correctly due to bending or deformation, or if the lock pin is damaged, replace the charging inlet lock assembly.
  2. Check lock pin operation.
    1. Check the operating conditions of the lock pin when battery voltage is applied to the connector terminal.
      CAUTION:
      • Complete the check within a battery connection lasting approximately 2 or 3 seconds. If voltage is applied for a longer time, there is the risk of damage to the charging inlet lock assembly.
      • Check the lock pin conditions before connecting battery voltage, and check the connection sequence.
        • If the lock pin is locked, check the unlock operation.
        • If the lock pin is unlock, check the lock operation.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
      Terminal No. Inspection conditions Specification
      10 (+) - 6 (-) Apply battery voltage. Unlocked to locked
      3 (+) - 6 (-) Locked to unlocked
    2. Replace the charging inlet lock assembly if faulty is found to be defective.
  3. Check the lock pin detection circuit.
    1. Measure the resistance between connector terminals.
      CAUTION: The lock pin locked to unlocked and unlocked to locked operation must be performed by operating the unlock lever.

      Preparation tool: 

      Circuit tester

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
      Terminal No. Inspection conditions Standard
      4 - 6 Locked Less than 1 Ω
      Unlocked 10 kΩ or more
    2. Replace the charging inlet lock assembly switch if the inspection result is not within the standard value.
  4. Check the lock power supply circuit.
    1. Measure the resistance between connector terminals.

      Preparation tool: 

      Circuit tester

      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
      Terminal No. Inspection conditions Standard
      7 - 6 When battery voltage is not applied between terminals 10 and 6 Less than 1 Ω
      1 - 6 When battery voltage is not applied between terminals 3 and 6
    2. Replace the charging inlet lock assembly switch if the inspection result is not within the standard value.
