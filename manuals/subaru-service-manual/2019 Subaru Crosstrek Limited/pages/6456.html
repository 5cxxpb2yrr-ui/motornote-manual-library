Precautions When Working With The Parts Mounted On The Vehicle

  1. Always refer to "CAUTION" and "NOTE" of "General Description" in the same model's service instructions before starting the service.
  2. When working under a vehicle which is jacked-up, always be sure to use rigid rack.
  3. The parking brake must always be applied during working. Also, in automatic transmission vehicles, keep the select lever set to the P (Parking) range.
  4. Be sure the workshop is properly ventilated when running the engine.
  5. Be careful not to touch the rotating parts such as a belt or a fan while the engine is operating.
  6. Be careful not to touch hot metal parts immediately after the engine has been turned on or off.
