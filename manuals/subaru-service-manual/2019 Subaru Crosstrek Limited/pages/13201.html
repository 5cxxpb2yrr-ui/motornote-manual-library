DTC C2921: ID Registration Error

DTC detecting condition: 

When the ID registration failed.

Trouble symptom: 

Tire pressure warning light blinks 25 times and then illuminates.

CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Ref. to Caution .
  1. CHECK DTC  .

    Read the DTC of [Tire Pressure Monitor] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Ref. to Diagnostic Trouble Code (DTC) .

    Is any of DTCs other than DTC C2921 displayed as "Current malfunction" or "Past malfunction"?

    Yes  : Perform the diagnosis for the displayed DTCs other than DTC C2921. Ref. to Diagnostic Trouble Code (DTC) .

    NOTE: When any of DTCs C2121 - C2124 is displayed as past malfunction, perform from step 2 of the diagnosis for DTCs C2121 - C2124.

    No  : Go to  2.

  2. REPRODUCTION OF FAILURE  .
    1. Perform the drive test.

      Drive the vehicle at 40 km/h (25 MPH) or faster for at least 10 minutes.

      NOTE: Drive until the tire status BIT 4 (PAL Condition) becomes "0".
    2. Read the DTC of [Tire Pressure Monitor] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Ref. to Diagnostic Trouble Code (DTC) .

    Is DTC displayed as "Current malfunction"?

    Yes  : Go to  3.

    No  : When DTC is displayed as "Past malfunction", the system has returned to a normal condition at this time.

    NOTE: In this case, there may be temporary poor communication due to radio wave environment inside/outside the vehicle.
  3. ID REGISTRATION  .

    Register the ID. Ref. to Register Transmitter (ID) .

    Can the ID be registered?

    Yes  : Go to  1.

    No  : Replace the TPMS & keyless entry Control Module or TPMS Control Module. Ref. to TIRE PRESSURE MONITORING SYSTEM .
