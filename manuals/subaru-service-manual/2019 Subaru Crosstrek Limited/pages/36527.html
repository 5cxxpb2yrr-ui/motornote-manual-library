Side Turn Signal Light Assembly: Inspection

  1. Apply battery voltage between the connector terminals to check the lighting condition of the light.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Terminal No. Inspection conditions Specification
    1 (+) - 2 (-) Apply battery voltage. Light ON
  2. Replace the light assembly - side turn mirror if faulty is found in the inspection.
    NOTE: Since LED (Light Emitting Diode) is used for the light, replace the light assembly - side turn mirror if the light does not illuminate.
