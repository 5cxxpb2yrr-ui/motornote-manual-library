CENTER CONSOLE ARMREST COVER-WRINKLED APPEARANCE

TECHNICAL SERVICE BULLETIN

Reference Number(s): 12-312-21, Date of Issue:  February 19, 2021
SUBARU: 2017-21 Impreza; 2018-21 Crosstrek; 2019-20 Forester

APPLICABILITY

2017-21MY Impreza

2018-21MY Crosstrek

2019-20MY Forester

INTRODUCTION

This bulletin announces availability of new center console armrest covers to address the wrinkling condition shown in the photos below. When the cover comes in contact with oily substances (e.g. some sunscreen products), the pictured condition may result over time. The new center console armrest covers are made with a material with higher durability and improved resistance to oily substances.

PRODUCTION CHANGE INFORMATION

The new covers were incorporated into production starting with the following VINs:

  • Impreza 4-Door: M3600903 
  • Impreza 5-Door: M3701523 
  • Crosstrek: MH206323 
  • Forester: LH572688
