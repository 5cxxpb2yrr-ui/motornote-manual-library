VDC Control Module and Hydraulic Control Unit (VDCCM & H/U): Inspection: Notes

  1. Check the identification (a) of the VDC control module & hydraulic control unit (VDCCM & H/U).
    NOTE: For the identification, refer to "SPECIFICATION" in "General Description". Refer to VEHICLE DYNAMICS CONTROL (VDC)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>SPECIFICATION .
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Check the condition of connection and settlement of connector, and correct or replace if defective.
