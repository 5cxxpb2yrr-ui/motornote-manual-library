Diagnostic Method

Malfunction in one of the two circuits (leakage, etc.).

JUDGMENT VALUE SPECIFICATION

Malfunction Criteria Threshold Value
Case 1 
Current reaction pressure		 4.5 MPa or more
Case 2 
Current reaction pressure		 1.5 MPa or more

Time needed for diagnosis: 

  • Case 1:  3 ms
  • Case 2:  500 ms

Malfunction indicator light illumination:  Illuminates as soon as a malfunction occurs.
