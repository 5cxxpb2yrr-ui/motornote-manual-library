Forward Clutch Assembly

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Remove the snap ring.
  2. Remove the retaining plate, drive plate, driven plate and dish plate.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Compress the return spring using the ST1 and ST2 to remove the snap ring.

    Preparation tool: 

    ST1: COMPRESSOR SPECIAL TOOL (18762AA010)

    ST2: COMPRESSOR (398673600)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the chamber COMPL and snap ring.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the return spring.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Remove the forward clutch piston by blowing compressed air intermittently from the forward clutch carrier hole.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
