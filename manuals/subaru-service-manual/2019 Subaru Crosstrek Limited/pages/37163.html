Accessory Power Supply Socket: Installation: Rear

  1. Install the socket assembly.
    NOTE: Align the groove of the console box assembly with the claw of the socket assembly.
  2. Install the cover assembly - front and the console box assembly. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>CONSOLE BOX>INSTALLATION .
  3. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
