DTC P0D56-15: Charging Connector Proximity Detection Circuit Short To Auxiliary Battery Or Open: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. - 

    Outline of diagnosis: 

    PISW terminal voltage was 4.73 V or more continuously for the prescribed length of time.

    (1 trip detection logic)

    Trouble symptom: 

    Plug-in charging suspended

    Wiring diagram: 

    Charging system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>CHARGING SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to PLUG-IN BATTERY CHARGER (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK Electric vehicle charger cable assembly (SHORT CIRCUIT CHECK). 

    Check the Electric vehicle charger cable assembly unit. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>CHARGE CABLE>INSPECTION .

    Is the Electric vehicle charger cable assembly OK?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Replace Electric vehicle charger cable assembly.

  2. CHECK THE CONNECTION CONDITIONS OF THE CONNECTOR (AC charger inlet cable CONNECTOR). 
    1. Disconnect the Electric vehicle charger cable assembly from the outlet.
    2. Check the connection conditions of AC charger inlet cable connector (HV27), and check the terminals for contact pressure, deformation, and intrusion of water or foreign substance. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>CHARGE INLET .

      Is the connector connection loose or is there poor contact at the terminals, or intruding water or foreign substance?

      Yes:  Connect the connector securely. Or repair the poor contact of connector.

      No:  Go to  3.

  3. CHECK GROUNDING POINT CONNECTION (AC charger inlet cable). 

    Check connection of AC charger inlet cable grounding point (GB11). Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>CHARGE INLET .

    Is the grounding point connected securely?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Connect the grounding point securely.

  4. CHECK THE Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Charging inlet - Electric vehicle charger assembly) (POWER SUPPLY SHORT CIRCUIT). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Check that the Service plug is disconnected. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .
    2. Disconnect Electric vehicle charger assembly connector (HV31).
    3. Connect the negative terminal of the Battery (12V). Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    4. Turn to IG ON.
    5. Using a tester, measure the voltage between AC charger inlet cable (Charging inlet) and chassis ground, and between AC charger inlet cable (Charging inlet) terminals.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV29) No. 4 (+) - (HV29) No. 5 (-):

      (HV29) No. 4 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

      Is the voltage less than 1 V?

      Yes:  Go to  5.

      No:  Go to  8.

  5. CHECK AC charger inlet cable. 
    1. Turn the ignition to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the negative terminal of the Battery (12V). Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    3. Disconnect AC charger inlet cable connector (HV27).
    4. Using the tester, measure the resistance between the AC charger inlet cable (Charging inlet) terminals.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV31) No. 3 - (HV29) No. 5:

      Is the resistance 2.3 - 3.0 kΩ?

      Yes:  Go to  6.

      No:  Repair or replace the AC charger inlet cable. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>CHARGE INLET .

  6. CHECK AC charger inlet cable. 

    Using a tester, measure the Voltage between AC charger inlet cable (Charging inlet) and chassis ground, and between AC charger inlet cable (Charging inlet) terminals.

    Connector & terminal 

    (HV29) No. 3 - (HV27) No. 11:

    (HV29) No. 5 - Chassis ground:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  7.

    No:  Repair or replace the AC charger inlet cable. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>CHARGE INLET .

  7. CHECK THE Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (AC charger inlet cable - Electric vehicle charger assembly) (OPEN CIRCUIT). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.

    Using a tester, measure the resistance between AC charger inlet cable connector and Electric vehicle charger assembly connector.

    Connector & terminal 

    (HV31) No. 3 - (R523) No. 11:

    Is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Replace Electric vehicle charger assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>ELECTRIC VEHICLE CHARGER .

    No:  Repair or replace the harness or connector.

  8. CHECK AC charger inlet cable (SHORT CIRCUIT CHECK). 
    1. Turn the ignition to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the negative terminal of the Battery (12V). Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    3. Connect the connector (HV31) of Electric vehicle charger assembly.
    4. Disconnect AC charger inlet cable connector (HV27).
    5. Connect the negative terminal of the Battery (12V). Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    6. Turn to IG ON.
    7. Using a tester, measure the Voltage between AC charger inlet cable (Charging inlet) and chassis ground, and between AC charger inlet cable (Charging inlet) terminals.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV29) No. 4 (+) - (HV29) No. 5 (-):

      (HV29) No. 4 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

      Is the voltage less than 1 V?

      Yes:  Repair or replace the Wire harness or connector.

      No:  Repair or replace the AC charger inlet cable. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>CHARGE INLET .
