Power Window Control Switch: Inspection: Notes

CAUTION: Since the power window switch is controlled by CPU, do not check continuity for switch alone with the circuit tester, except for the specified terminals. Performing continuity check with circuit tester may damage the power window switch circuit.

For power window system inspection, refer to "POWER WINDOW (DIAGNOSTICS)" service information. Refer to POWER WINDOW (DIAGNOSTICS)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE .
