DTC P0AA6-49: Hybrid/EV Battery Voltage System Isolation Internal Electronic Failure: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

1. - 

Outline of diagnosis: 

Insulation resistance of high voltage circuit and body is decreased

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

Normal driving

Wiring diagram: 

Hybrid system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
NOTE:
  • The insulation malfunction detection circuit in Battery ECU assembly is detecting insulation resistance of the high voltage circuit and body. When the insulation resistance decreased at any of the locations, Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly stores DTC P0AA649 first, and Hybrid Fail Lamp illuminates.
  • After only DTC P0AA649 is recorded, if the following operation is performed continuously within the same trip, one of the related DTCs (P1C7C49, P1C7D49, P1C7E49, P1C7F49 or P1CF749) is recorded.
  • The high voltage insulation malfunction may recover to normal state depending on the vehicle condition. When DTC P0AA649 is output, complete the following operation immediately.
    1. Apply the parking brake.
    2. While firmly depressing the brake pedal, wait for one minute or more with READY ON, D position, the vehicle stopped, and the air conditioner ON (temperature Lo/COOL MAX, air flow maximum).
    3. Turn to IG OFF, and wait for two minutes or more.
    4. Connect Electric vehicle charger cable assembly, and charge it for three minutes or more.
  • To measure insulation resistance using megohmmeter, perform inspection while shaking the high voltage cable.

    1. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, perform the clear memory of [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control]. Refer to COMMON (DIAG)>CLEAR MEMORY .

    6. Shift to D position while depressing the brake pedal without depressing the accelerator pedal, and wait for five minutes.
    7. Read the DTC of [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

      • When DTC is not displayed, go to step 8).
    8. Drive the vehicle approximately five minutes while referring to [Vehicle Speed Sensor], [Shift Position], [Accelerator Position], [Engine Speed], [Coolant Temperature], [Master Cylinder Control Torque], [Inverter Coolant Water Temperature], [Generator Inverter Calculated Temperature] and [Motor Inverter Temperature] of the freeze frame data. (When the freeze frame data [Vehicle Speed Sensor] is less than 10 km/h (6.2 MPH), drive the vehicle at 10 km/h (6.2 MPH) or more.)

    10. After waiting for one minute or more with READY ON, P position, the vehicle stopped, and the air conditioner ON (temperature Lo/COOL MAX, air flow maximum), turn to READY OFF and wait for two minutes or more.

    12. Read the DTC of [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

  4. CHECK Transmission assembly (Power cable (FOR MOTOR))  .
    CAUTION:
    • Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    • Never turn to READY ON with Service plug removed to prevent faults.
    1. Check that the Service plug is disconnected. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .
    2. Disconnect Power cable (HV99) from Inverter with converter assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>POWER CABLE .
      NOTE: Check that there is no foreign matter, coolant leakage and water intrusion into Inverter with converter assembly. Pay attention to the coolant level.
    3. Connect the ground terminal of Battery (12V). Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    4. Turn to IG ON.
      NOTE: Turning to IG ON with Service plug removed stores other DTC. Therefore, clear the memory for DTC after the inspection.
    5. Shift to N position and lift up the vehicle.
    6. Using a megohmmeter (500 V range), measure the resistance between Power cable (for motor) (on Inverter with converter assembly side) terminals and chassis ground and shield line ground, while turning the front tires two rotations simultaneously by hand to the same direction.
    CAUTION:
    • When the tire is rotated by hand, current may be generated from the motor. Therefore, pay special attention to the operation.
    • When performing this check, be sure to set megohmmeter to 500 V. Using a range higher than 500 V may damage the part being checked.

    Connector & terminal
