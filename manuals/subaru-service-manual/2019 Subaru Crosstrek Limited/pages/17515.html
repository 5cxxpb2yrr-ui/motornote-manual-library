DTC C1412: Selected Parameter

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

DTC detecting condition: 

  • A fault is detected if abnormal set value is found on the vehicle model that were given some parameters.
  • A fault is detected if 0 point setting compensation of the sensor is not yet performed.

Trouble symptom: 

  • ABS does not operate.
  • VDC does not operate.
  • Hill start assist does not operate.
  • EyeSight does not operate. (Models with EyeSight)
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to BRAKE CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
NOTE: When the VDC Contol Module & H/U is replaced, this DTC may be stored.
  1. CHECK VDC CONTOL MODULE & H/U IDENTIFICATION SYMBOL. 

    Check the identification symbol attached on the H/U. Refer to VEHICLE DYNAMICS CONTROL (VDC)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>SPECIFICATION .

    Is the identification symbol correct?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Replace the VDC Contol Module & H/U. Refer to VEHICLE DYNAMICS CONTROL (VDC)>VDC CONTROL MODULE AND HYDRAULIC CONTROL UNIT (VDCCM & H/U) .

  2. CHECK PARAMETER SELECTED IN VDC CONTOL MODULE. 

    Refer to VEHICLE DYNAMICS CONTROL (VDC)>VDC CONTROL MODULE AND HYDRAULIC CONTROL UNIT (VDCCM & H/U)>MAINTENANCE MODE>VDCCM PARAMETER CHECK .

    Does the parameter registered in the VDC Contol Module match the relevant vehicle?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Select and register the correct parameter. Refer to VEHICLE DYNAMICS CONTROL (VDC)>VDC CONTROL MODULE AND HYDRAULIC CONTROL UNIT (VDCCM & H/U)>MAINTENANCE MODE>VDCCM PARAMETER SELECTION.

  3. CHECK SPECIFICATIONS OF OTHER CONTROL MODULES. 

    Check the specifications of ECM and TCM.

    Are the specifications of ECM and TCM the same as vehicle specification?

    Yes:  Replace the VDC Contol Module & H/U. Refer to VEHICLE DYNAMICS CONTROL (VDC)>VDC CONTROL MODULE AND HYDRAULIC CONTROL UNIT (VDCCM & H/U) .

    No:  Replace the ECM and TCM. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE CONTROL MODULE (ECM) . Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>TRANSMISSION CONTROL MODULE (TCM) .
