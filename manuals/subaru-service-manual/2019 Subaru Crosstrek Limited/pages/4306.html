Bumper Face

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the bumper face - front.
    1. Remove the bolt and clip.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. While pushing up the lower side (a) of the bumper face - front, release the engagement in the order from (b) to (f).
      CAUTION: Do not pull forcibly. It may damage the flange section on the bumper face - front side when it comes off from the claws of the bracket - front bumper.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Disconnect the connector of the fog light assembly or the daytime light assembly.
      NOTE: Detach the opposite side in the same manner.
    4. Remove the bumper face while pulling up the center part of the bumper.
Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
