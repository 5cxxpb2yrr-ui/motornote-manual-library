DTC P1B4B-73: Hybrid/EV Battery Heater Relay Actuator Stuck Closed: Notes

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

1. - 

Outline of diagnosis: 

Temperature of HV battery rises even when the command to drive relay in HV battery heater indicates OFF.

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

Normal driving

Wiring diagram: 

Hybrid system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK Battery (12V) VOLTAGE. 
    1. Turn the headlight (high beam) to ON approximately 30 seconds at IG OFF to remove the portion of surface charge.
    2. Measure the voltage and resistance according to the table below.
      NOTE:
      Inspection terminal Inspection conditions Standard
      Battery (12V) positive terminal - Battery (12V) ground terminal
      • Liquid temperature 20°C (68°F)
      • IG OFF
      		 12.0 V or more

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Charge or replace Battery (12V), Go to  2.

  2. CHECK DTC. 

    Read the following DTCs using Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) . Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    • P-HEV High Voltage Battery
    • P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control
    NOTE:
    System Related DTC
    P-HEV High Voltage Battery P060A47 HYBRID/EV BATTERY ENERGY CONTROL MODULE MONITORING PROCESSOR WATCHDOG/SAFETY MCU FAILURE
    P060B49 HYBRID/EV BATTERY ENERGY CONTROL MODULE A/D PROCESSING INTERNAL ELECTRONIC FAILURE
    P060687 HYBRID/EV BATTERY ENERGY CONTROL MODULE PROCESSOR TO MONITORING PROCESSOR MISSING MESSAGE
    P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control P0A1F94 HYBRID/EV BATTERY ENERGY CONTROL MODULE UNEXPECTED OPERATION

    When "Only DTC P1B4B73 is displayed" or "DTC other than above is displayed", go to  A.

    When DTCs related to [P-HEV High Voltage Battery], mentioned above, are simultaneously displayed, go to  B.

    When DTCs related to [P-HEV Hybrid Powertrain Control], mentioned above, are simultaneously displayed, go to  C.

    Yes: 

    1. Go to  3.
    2. Perform the diagnosis for the simultaneously displayed DTCs.
    3. Perform the diagnosis for the simultaneously displayed DTCs.

    No:  -

  3. CHECK CONNECTOR CONNECTION CONDITION (Battery ECU assembly). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Check that the Service plug is disconnected. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .
    2. Check the connecting condition of connector (HV42) in Battery ECU assembly and check the relevant terminals for contact pressure.

    Is there looseness at the connector connection, or any poor contact, deformation or intrusion of water or foreign matters at the relevant terminals?

    Yes:  Connect the connector securely. Or repair the poor contact of connector.

    No:  Go to  4.

  4. CHECK CONNECTOR CONNECTION CONDITION (HV battery heater relay). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.

    Check the connecting condition of connector (HV81) in HV battery heater relay and check the relevant terminals for contact pressure.

    Is there looseness at the connector connection, or any poor contact, deformation or intrusion of water or foreign matters at the relevant terminals?

    Yes:  Connect the connector securely. Or repair the poor contact of connector.

    No:  Go to  5.

  5. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Battery ECU assembly - HV battery heater relay). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Disconnect HV battery heater relay connector (HV81).
    2. Connect the ground terminal to Battery (12V). Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    3. Turn to IG ON.
    4. Using a tester, measure the voltage between HV battery heater relay connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV81) No. 11 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    Is the voltage less than 1 V?

    Yes:  Replace HV battery heater relay. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>RELAY AND FUSE .

    No:  Go to  6.

  6. CHECK Wire harness AND CONNECTOR (Battery ECU assembly - HV battery heater relay). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Turn the ignition to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the ground terminal from Battery (12V). Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    3. Disconnect Battery ECU assembly connector (HV42).
    4. Connect the ground terminal to Battery (12V). Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    5. Turn to IG ON.
    6. Using a tester, measure the voltage between HV battery heater relay connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (HV81) No. 11 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

    Is the voltage less than 1 V?

    Yes:  Replace Battery ECU assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>BATTERY CONTROL MODULE .

    No:  Replace the Wire harness between Battery ECU assembly connector and chassis ground.
