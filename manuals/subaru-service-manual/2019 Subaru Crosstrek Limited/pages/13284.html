Transmission Rear Crossmember And Rear Cushion Rubber

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Attach the rear cushion rubber to the transmission.

    Tightening torque: 

    40 N.m (4.1 kgf-m, 29.5 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Install the transmission rear crossmember.
    • Impreza model

      Tightening torque: 

      T1: 35 N.m (3.6 kgf-m, 25.8 ft-lb)

      T2: 75 N.m (7.6kgf-m, 55.3 ft-lb)

    • Crosstrek model

      Tightening torque: 

      T1: 35 N.m (3.6 kgf-m, 25.8 ft-lb)

      T2: 70 N.m (7.1kgf-m, 51.6 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the transmission jack.
  4. Install the rear exhaust pipe. Refer to EXHAUST (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>REAR EXHAUST PIPE>INSTALLATION .
  5. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
