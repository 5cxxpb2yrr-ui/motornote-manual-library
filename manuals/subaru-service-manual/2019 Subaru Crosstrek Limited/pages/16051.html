Air Cleaner Element: Removal

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Disengage the clip, and remove the air cleaner element.
    CAUTION: Be sure to lift up the air cleaner case (rear) before releasing the claws on the bottom of the air cleaner case. Otherwise the air cleaner element may be damaged.
