Filling Of Engine Coolant

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
NOTE: The radiator has a double-layer design for cooling the engine and the inverter assembly respectively. Therefore filling of engine coolant must be performed to the appropriate part of the radiator.
  • ENGINE SIDE 
    CAUTION:
    • If filling of engine coolant on the inverter assembly side is also required, fill to the inverter assembly side first.
    • During work, set the select lever to "P" range and do not shift the lever.
    • If the engine coolant splashes, completely remove and clean it.
    NOTE: The SUBARU Super Coolant contains anti-freeze and anti-rust agents, and is especially made for Subaru engines with an aluminum cylinder block. Be sure to use SUBARU Super Coolant, since other coolant may cause corrosion.

    1. Remove the water tank pipe cap.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Loosen the air bleed plug on the heater outlet hose.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Pour in cooling system conditioner.
      NOTE: Pour it from the radiator side.
    4. Pour engine coolant into the radiator and top off to the opening of the water tank pipe cap.
      NOTE: Pour it from the radiator side.
    5. Install the water tank pipe cap.
      CAUTION: Always use the water tank pipe cap, since its function is different from the radiator cap.
    6. After pouring in the engine coolant, close the air bleeding plug when the engine coolant comes out of the air bleeding plug on the heater outlet hose.
    7. Pour engine coolant into the radiator up to the filler neck position.
    8. Fill engine coolant into the reservoir tank up to "FULL" level.
    9. Install the reservoir tank cap.
    10. Install the radiator cap.
    11. Navigate to Inspection Mode for measuring Exhaust Gas.

      Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>Work Support>OPERATION>SERVICE MODE .

      NOTE: If it is necessary to run the engine for 10 minutes or more, fasten the driver's seat belt.
    12. Start the engine, and race 5 to 6 times, then stop the engine.
      NOTE: Complete this operation within 40 seconds.
    13. After stopping the engine, wait for at least 1 minute and then remove the radiator cap. If the engine coolant level drops, add engine coolant into radiator up to the filler neck position.
      NOTE: Do not remove the water tank pipe cap, or the engine coolant may over flow.
    14. Perform the steps  10,  11,  12 and  13 again.
    15. Perform the steps  10 and  11 again.
    16. Start the engine and operate the heater at maximum hot position and the blower speed setting at "LO", and the A/C switch at "OFF".
    17. Run the engine with the accelerator pedal fully opened, until the radiator fan starts and then stops.
      CAUTION: Frequently check the engine coolant temperature in order to avoid overheat.
    18. Stop the engine and wait until the engine coolant temperature lowers to 30°C (86°F) or less.
    19. Remove the radiator cap. If the engine coolant level drops, add engine coolant into the radiator up to the filler neck position and the reservoir tank to "FULL" level.
    20. Attach the radiator cap and reservoir tank cap properly.
    21. Perform step  11 again with the heater at maximum hot position and the blower speed setting at "LO", and the A/C switch at "OFF".
    22. Start the engine and race it. If the flowing sound is heard from the heater core, repeat the procedures from step  17.

    1. Remove the screw (A) and clips, and turn over the mud guard - front RH.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Remove the clamp.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Disconnect the quick connector from the inverter water pipe.
      NOTE:
      • Disconnect the quick connector as shown in the figure.
      • Be careful not to pull the slider too much.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    4. Fill the inverter tank with engine coolant until the engine coolant comes out of the outlet pipe (A) of the inverter water pump, and connect the quick connector.
      CAUTION:
      • Add up enough engine coolant to prevent it from being depleted in the inverter tank.
      • Check that there is no damage or dust on the quick connector. If necessary, clean the seal surface of the pipe.
      • When connecting the quick connector, make sure to insert it all the way in before locking the slider.
      • When it is difficult to lock the slider, check that the connector is fully inserted.
      • After locking the slider, pull the quick connector body in the removal direction and then push it in the connecting direction, in order to check that the quick connector is securely connected. Always make sure to perform this confirmation ending up with a pushing in.
      NOTE: Connect the quick connector as shown in the figure.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    5. Install the clamp.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    6. Fill the inverter tank with cooling system conditioner.
    7. Fill the inverter tank with engine coolant until the engine coolant level comes up to "FULL".
    8. Navigate to Inspection Mode for measuring Exhaust Gas.

      Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>Work Support>OPERATION>SERVICE MODE .

      CAUTION: When navigated to Inspection Mode for measuring Exhaust Gas, the inverter water pump starts to operate, then the engine coolant in the inverter tank begins to decrease. Therefore be sure to prepare for filling the engine coolant beforehand.
      NOTE: If it is necessary to run the engine for 10 minutes or more, fasten the driver's seat belt.
    9. Add up enough engine coolant to prevent it from being depleted in the inverter tank.
      NOTE: Engine coolant will decrease until the air is completely bled from the inverter cooling system.
    10. When the engine coolant in the inverter tank stops decreasing, exit Inspection Mode for measuring Exhaust Gas.
    11. Fill the inverter tank with engine coolant until the engine coolant level comes up to "FULL".
    12. Install the inverter tank cap.
    13. Set the mud guard - front RH to the original position and install the screw (A) and clips.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    14. Install the under cover - front.

      Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>FRONT UNDER COVER>INSTALLATION .
