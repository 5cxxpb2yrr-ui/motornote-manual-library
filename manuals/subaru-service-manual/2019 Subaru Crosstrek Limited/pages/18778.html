Transfer Case

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Install the shift link assembly to the transfer case. refer to MANUAL TRANSMISSION AND DIFFERENTIAL (6MT)>SHIFT LINK ASSEMBLY>INSTALLATION .
  2. Replace the precoat bolt and gasket with new parts, and Install the interlock plate.
    NOTE: Clean the bolt threads before installing the interlock plate.

    Tightening torque: 

    20 N.m (2.0 kgf-m, 14.8 ft-lb)
