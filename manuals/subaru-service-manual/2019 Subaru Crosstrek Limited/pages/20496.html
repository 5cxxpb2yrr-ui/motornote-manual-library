DTC P1A00-1C: Hybrid Battery Stack 2 Cell Voltage Detection Voltage Out Of Range: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

1. - 

Outline of diagnosis: 

Detection line of cell voltage in NO. 2 HV supply stack sub-assembly is open.

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

  • Reduced output power
  • Engine not stopped

Wiring diagram: 

Hybrid system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

  3. CHECK CONNECTOR CONNECTION CONDITION (Battery ECU assembly CONNECTOR). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Check that the Service plug is disconnected. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .
    2. Check the connecting condition of connector (HV38) in Battery ECU assembly and check the relevant terminals for contact pressure.

    Is there looseness at the connector connection, or any poor contact at the relevant terminals?

    Yes:  Connect the connector securely. Or if the terminal is defective, replace the HV battery. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY .

    No:  Go to  4.

  4. CHECK NO. 2 HV supply stack sub-assembly (Hybrid battery cell voltage). 
    CAUTION:
    • Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    • Disconnect the connector to inspect the Hybrid battery cell voltage. Leave the other connectors connected.
    • When checking Hybrid battery cell voltage, select a probe of proper diameter (approx. 0.5 mm) from the test lead set before measurement.
    • Do not cross the electrode part of measurement terminals of electrical tester.
    • Keep in mind that the terminals have + pole or - pole when connecting the tester before measurement.
    1. Turn to IG ON.
    2. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, read the value of [Hybrid Battery Cell 20 Voltage] - [Hybrid Battery Cell 38 Voltage] in [Freeze frame data] of DTC P1A001C. Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>FREEZE FRAME DATA .
    3. Turn the ignition to OFF.
    4. Disconnect Battery ECU assembly connector (HV38).
      CAUTION:
      • Release the lever lock completely so that the lock lever can be operated.
      • If the connector cannot not be disengaged, check that the lock lever is in the unlock position.
    5. Using a tester, measure the voltage between terminals in Battery ECU assembly connector.
      NOTE: Measure the point related to Hybrid battery cell where the voltage is 1.6 V or less from the result of freeze frame data.
