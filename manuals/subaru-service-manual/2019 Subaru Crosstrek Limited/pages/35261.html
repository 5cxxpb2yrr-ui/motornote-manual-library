Rear Drive Shaft: Inspection

Check the removed parts for damage, wear, corrosion etc. Repair or replace if defective.

  • DOJ: 

    Check for seizure, corrosion, damage, wear and excessive looseness.

  • EBJ: 

    Check for seizure, corrosion, damage and excessive looseness.

  • BJ: 

    Check for seizure, corrosion, damage and excessive looseness.

  • Shaft: 

    Check for excessive bending, twisting, damage and wear.

  • Boot: 

    Check for wear, warping, breakage and scratches.

  • Grease: 

Check for discoloration and fluidity.
