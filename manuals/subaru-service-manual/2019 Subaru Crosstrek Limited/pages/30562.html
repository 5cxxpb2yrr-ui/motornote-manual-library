Transfer Driven Gear: Removal

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Remove the manual transmission assembly from the vehicle. refer to MANUAL TRANSMISSION AND DIFFERENTIAL (6MT)>MANUAL TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL .
  2. Remove the back-up light switch and the neutral position switch. refer to MANUAL TRANSMISSION AND DIFFERENTIAL (6MT)>SWITCHES AND HARNESS>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the transfer case together with the extension case assembly. refer to MANUAL TRANSMISSION AND DIFFERENTIAL (6MT)>TRANSFER CASE AND EXTENSION CASE ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL .
  4. Remove the extension case assembly.
  5. Remove the transfer driven gear and center differential as a unit.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Remove the taper roller bearing outer race.
