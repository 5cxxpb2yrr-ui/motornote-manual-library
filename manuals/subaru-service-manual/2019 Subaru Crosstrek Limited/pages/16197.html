Engine Oil Temperature Sensor: Removal

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
CAUTION: If the engine oil is split over exhaust pipe or the under cover, wipe it off with cloth to avoid emission of smoke or causing a fire.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Disconnect the connector (A), and remove the engine oil temperature sensor from the chain cover using the ST.
    NOTE: STRUT MOUNT SOCKET (20099 Pa000) can also be used.

    Preparation tool: 

    ST: STRUT MOUNT SOCKET (20399AG000)
