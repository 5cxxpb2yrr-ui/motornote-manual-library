Bushing Front - Front Arm (IMPREZA Model)

  1. Put an alignment mark on the front arm assembly based on the split portion of the bushing intermediate plate of the bushing front - front arm.
    CAUTION: Always put an alignment mark for aligning the position on bushing installation.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Using the ST and a press, remove the bushing front - front arm.

    Preparation tool: 

    ST: INSTALLER & REMOVER SET (927680000)
