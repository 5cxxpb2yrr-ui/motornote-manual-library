Adaptive Cruise Control Indicator Light/Conventional Cruise Control Indicator Light Does Not Turn To Green

  1. CHECK ADAPTIVE CRUISE CONTROL INDICATOR LIGHT/CONVENTIONAL CRUISE CONTROL INDICATOR LIGHT. 

    Check the adaptive cruise control indicator light/conventional cruise control indicator light.

    Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>CHECK ADAPTIVE CRUISE CONTROL/CONVENTIONAL CRUISE CONTROL INDICATOR LIGHT (WHITE OR GREEN) .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Replace the ECM. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE CONTROL MODULE (ECM)>REMOVAL .

    No:  Repair or replace the faulty portion according to the inspection result.
