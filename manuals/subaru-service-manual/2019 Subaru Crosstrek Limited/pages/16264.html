Circulate Tube Assy: Removal

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
WARNING: Place "NO OPEN FLAMES" signs near the working area.
CAUTION:
  • Be careful not to spill fuel.
  • Catch the fuel from the tubes using a container or cloth.
  • If the fuel gauge indicates that two thirds or more of the fuel is remaining, be sure to drain fuel before starting work to avoid the fuel to spill.
  1. Release the fuel pressure. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL>PROCEDURE>RELEASING OF FUEL PRESSURE .
  2. Drain fuel. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL>PROCEDURE>DRAINING FUEL (WITH SUBARU SELECT MONITOR) .
  3. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  4. Disconnect the connector (A) and air hose (B).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Disconnect the circulate tube assembly from the fuel tank and fuel filler pipe, and remove the circulate tube assembly. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>REMOVAL>QUICK CONNECTOR DISCONNECTION (TYPE F) .
    NOTE: Circulate tube assembly is a non-disassembled part.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Remove the bracket.
    NOTE: Remove if necessary.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
