Inspection Of Clearance

This table lists various clearances that must be correctly adjusted to ensure the normal vehicle driving without interfering noise, or any other faults.

Location Minimum allowance mm (in)
(1) Stub housing to DOJ (MT model) 15 (0.59)
(2) Torque sensor to Crossmember 5 (0.2)
(3) Worm wheel housing to Crossmember There must be no contact
(4) Stub housing t o Engine mount 10 (0.39)
(5) Universal joint column side yoke to Master cylinder (closest point of approach when the universal joint turns by 360°) 5 (0.2)
(6) Wheel housing t o Exhaust pipe 13 (0.51)
(7) Motor ECM to Crossmember There must be no contact
