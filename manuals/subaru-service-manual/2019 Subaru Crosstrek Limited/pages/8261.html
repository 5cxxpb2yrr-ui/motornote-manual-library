Engine Oil Temperature Sensor: Installation

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Set a new gasket, install the engine oil temperature sensor to the chain cover using the ST.
    NOTE: STRUT MOUNT SOCKET (20099 Pa000) can also be used.

    Preparation tool: 

    ST: STRUT MOUNT SOCKET (20399AG000)

    Tightening torque: 

    16 N.m (1.6 kgf-m, 11.8 ft-lb)

  2. Connect the connector to the engine oil temperature sensor.
  3. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
