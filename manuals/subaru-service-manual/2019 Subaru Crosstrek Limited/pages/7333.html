Cam Carrier LH

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Remove the engine from the vehicle.

    Except for hybrid model: 

    Refer to MECHANICAL (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL>EXCEPT FOR HYBRID MODEL .

    Hybrid model: 

    Refer to MECHANICAL (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL>HYBRID MODEL .

  2. Remove the timing chain LH. Refer to MECHANICAL (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>TIMING CHAIN ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL>TIMING CHAIN LH .
  3. Remove the rocker cover LH. Refer to MECHANICAL (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ROCKER COVER>REMOVAL>ROCKER COVER LH .
  4. Remove the water pipe LH.

    Except for hybrid model: 

    Refer to COOLING (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>WATER PIPE>REMOVAL>WATER PIPE LH .

    Hybrid model: 

    Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>WATER PIPE>REMOVAL>WATER PIPE LH .

  5. Remove the high-pressure fuel delivery pipes.

    Except for hybrid model: 

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>HIGH PRESSURE FUEL DELIVERY PIPE>REMOVAL>HIGH-PRESSURE FUEL DELIVERY PIPE .

    Hybrid model: 

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>HIGH PRESSURE FUEL DELIVERY PIPE>REMOVAL>HIGH-PRESSURE FUEL DELIVERY PIPE .

  6. Disconnect the connector from high-pressure fuel pump.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Remove the clip holding the engine wiring harness from cam carrier LH.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. When disassembling the cam carrier LH
    NOTE: When disassembling the cam carrier LH, perform the following steps also.
    1. Remove the high-pressure fuel pump.

      Except for hybrid model: 

      Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>HIGH PRESSURE FUEL PUMP>REMOVAL .

      Hybrid model: 

      Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>HIGH PRESSURE FUEL PUMP>REMOVAL .

    2. Remove the cam sprocket LH. Refer to MECHANICAL (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>CAM SPROCKET>REMOVAL>CAM SPROCKET LH .
  9. Set the part so that the cam carrier LH is on the upper side.
  10. Loosen the bolts holding the cam carrier LH equally, a little at a time in numerical sequence as shown in the figure and remove the cam carrier LH.
    Fig 1: Cam Carrier LH Bolts Loosening Sequence
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  11. Remove the O-ring and the roller rocker arm from cylinder head LH.
    NOTE: Be careful not to confuse the roller rocker arms.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  12. Remove the valve shim and the roller rocker arm pivot from cylinder head LH.
    NOTE: Be careful not to confuse the valve shim and the roller rocker arm pivot.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  13. Remove the liquid gasket from cam carrier LH and cylinder head LH.
