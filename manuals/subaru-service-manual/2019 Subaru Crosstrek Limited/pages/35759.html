DTC C1C00: Reservoir Level Switch Circuit Open: Notes

1. - 

DTC detecting condition: 

Open circuit condition of the fluid level warning switch inside the brake booster assembly (with master cylinder) continues for 2 seconds or more.

Trouble symptom: 

  • Regenerative brake cannot be controlled.
  • Brake assist does not operate.
  • EyeSight functions (ACC/PCS) do not operate.

Wiring diagram: 

Electronic controlled brake system Ref. to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>ELECTRIC CONTROL BRAKE SYSTEM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  1. CHECK BRAKE FLUID LEAKAGE. 

    Check the brake booster assembly (with master cylinder), brake accumulator assembly, and brake calipers for brake fluid leakage. (Connections, piping)

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Repair the brake fluid leakage.

  2. AIR BLEEDING FROM BRAKE SYSTEM. 
    1. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, perform the clear memory. Ref. to COMMON (DIAG)>CLEAR MEMORY .
    2. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, perform [Air Bleeding from Brake System] under [Brake System Bleeding] in [Work Support]. Ref. to ELECTRIC BRAKE BOOSTER (DIAGNOSTICS)>WORK SUPPORT .

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  -

  3. CHECK DATA MONITOR. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the brake stroke sensor connector.
    3. Using Subaru Select Monitor, display [Accumulator Pressure] in [Data monitor]. Ref. to ELECTRIC BRAKE BOOSTER (DIAGNOSTICS)>DATA MONITOR .
    4. Depress the brake pedal several times so that the pump motor operates, then wait until it stops.
    5. Wait for 30 seconds from the moment the pump motor stops, then check the amount of [Accumulator Pressure] drop and the condition of the pump motor.

    Is the amount of [Accumulator Pressure] drop after 30 seconds less than 2.50 MPa, and does the pump motor fail to start operating within 30 seconds?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Repair the brake fluid leakage.

  4. CHECK CONNECTOR. 

    Check the connecting condition of the brake fluid sensor connector.

    Is there looseness, deformation, or corrosion at the connectors?

    Yes:  Repair or replace the connector.

    No:  Go to  5.

  5. CHECK BRAKE FLUID SENSOR. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Connect the brake stroke sensor connector.
    3. Disconnect the brake fluid sensor connector.
    4. Measure the resistance between brake fluid sensor terminals.

    Terminal 

    No. 1 - No. 2:

    During Switch OFF (Float up) state, is the resistance within 1.84 - 2.16 kΩ range?

    During Switch ON (Float down) state, is the resistance less than 1 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  6.

    No:  Replace the brake booster assembly (with the master cylinder). Ref. to BRAKE>BRAKE BOOSTER .

  6. CHECK CONNECTOR. 

    Check the connecting condition of the EBB Control Module connector.

    Is there looseness, deformation, or corrosion at the connectors?

    Yes:  Repair or replace the connector.

    No:  Go to  7.

  7. CHECK EBB Control Module. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the EBB Control Module connector.
    3. Measure the resistance between EBB Control Module connector and brake fluid sensor connector and chassis ground.

    Connector & terminal 

    (AD82) No. 23 - (AD83) No. 2: (Resistance: Less than 1 Ω)

    (AD82) No. 23 - Chassis ground: (Resistance: 10 kΩ or more)

    (AD83) No. 1 - Chassis ground: (Resistance: Less than 1 Ω)

    Does the resistance indicate the standard value?

    Yes:  Go to  8.

    No:  Repair or replace the harness.

  8. CHECK DISC ROTOR. 
    1. Connect the EBB Control Module connector.
    2. Connect the brake fluid sensor connector.
    3. Disconnect the brake stroke sensor connector.
    4. Repeat driving and braking tests based on result from the interview or freeze frame data.

    Does brake pedal vibrate when depressed?

    Yes:  Resurface or replace the disc rotor.

    No:  Go to  9.

  9. CHECK DTC. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Connect the brake stroke sensor connector.
    3. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, perform the clear memory. Ref. to COMMON (DIAG)>CLEAR MEMORY .
    4. Read the DTC using Subaru Select Monitor. Ref. to ELECTRIC BRAKE BOOSTER (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is DTC C1C00 displayed?

    Yes:  Replace the brake booster assembly (with the master cylinder). Ref. to BRAKE>BRAKE BOOSTER .

    No:  Currently, it is normal. There may have been a temporary poor contact in the harness and connector or a temporary noise interference.
