DTC P1C2A-1C: Generator A/D Converter Circuit Circuit Voltage Out Of Range: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Detecting condition: 

Motor generator control ECU internal malfunction

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

  • Greatly reduced output power
  • A driving range is limited
  • Engine always stopped
NOTE: This DTC is output when Motor generator control ECU incorporated with Inverter with converter assembly detects the operation malfunction within Motor generator control ECU. Therefore, when this DTC is output, replace Inverter with converter assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>INVERTER AND CONVERTER ASSEMBLY .
