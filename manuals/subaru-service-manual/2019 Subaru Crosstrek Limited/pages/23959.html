Purge Control Solenoid Valve: Installation

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Connect the vacuum hose to the purge control solenoid valve.
  2. Install the purge control solenoid valve bracket.

    Tightening torque: 

    12 N.m (1.2 kgf-m, 8.9 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Install the purge control solenoid valve.

    Tightening torque: 

    6.4 N.m (0.7 kgf-m, 4.7 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Connect the vacuum hose (D) to the intake manifold assembly, and install the vacuum hose (D) to the clip (E).
  5. Connect the purge hose (C).
  6. Connect the connector (A), and secure the engine wiring harness with the clip (B).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Install the air intake boot.

    Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>Air Intake Boot>INSTALLATION .

  8. Connect the battery ground terminal,

    Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
