DTC P0E31-17: DC/DC Converter Voltage Sensor "A" (Vl) Circuit Voltage Above Threshold: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Detecting condition: 

Boosting converter voltage (VL) signal stuck to High

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

Reduced output power

NOTE: For the diagnostic procedure, refer to DTC P0E3116. Refer to P-HEV DRIVE MOTOR (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE WITH DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>DTC P0E3116 DC/DC CONVERTER VOLTAGE SENSOR "A" (VL) CIRCUIT VOLTAGE BELOW THRESHOLD .
