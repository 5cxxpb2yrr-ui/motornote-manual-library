Low-Pressure Hose

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

CAUTION:
  • Since the auto A/C model uses the variable displacement compressor, be careful of the following items.
    • Do not start the engine before charging refrigerant.
    • If the engine is started with no refrigerant charge, replace the compressor assembly.
  • If the hose and pipe has been replaced, add an appropriate amount of compressor oil to the compressor. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>COMPRESSOR OIL>ADJUSTMENT .
  • Replace the O-rings with new parts, and then apply compressor oil to install completely.
  • When connecting hoses, do not apply an excessive force to them. After installing, check that no torsion or excessive tension applied to the hoses.
  1. Install the hose - pressure suction.

    Tightening torque: 

    Air conditioning unit: Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>COMPONENT>AIR CONDITIONING UNIT .

  2. Install the air intake boot. Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>Air Intake Boot>INSTALLATION .
  3. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  4. Charge refrigerant. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>REFRIGERANT CHARGING PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .
