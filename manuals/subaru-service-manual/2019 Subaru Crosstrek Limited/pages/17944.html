Primary Speed Sensor: Inspection

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the clip from the air intake boot, and loosen the clamp.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the air intake boot, and place it aside so that it does not interfere with the work.
    NOTE: Be careful not to pull out the PCV hose.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the transmission radio ground terminal.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Disconnect the transmission harness connector.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Remove the transmission case cover (small).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Move the lock lever in the arrow direction while pressing the lock button, and disconnect the connector.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Set the ST between TCM and transmission harness. Refer to TRANSMISSION (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .

    Preparation tool: 

    ST: CHECK BOARD (18460AA040)

  9. Connect the transmission radio ground terminal and transmission harness connector.

    Tightening torque: 

    32.3 N.m (3.3 kgf-m, 23.8 ft-lb)

  10. Install the air intake boot. Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>Air Intake Boot>INSTALLATION .
  11. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  12. Set the probe of oscilloscope to the check board connector.

    Connector & terminal 

    (B54) No. 14 (+) - No. 42 (-):

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  13. Start and warm up the engine.
  14. Lift up the vehicle.
    CAUTION: Lift up the vehicle until the tire bottom is 0.3 m (0.98 ft) or more above the ground.
  15. Shift the select lever to the "D" range.
  16. Check the waveform and output voltage of the primary speed sensor with engine idling.
    NOTE: The waveform cycle changes as the speed changes.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
