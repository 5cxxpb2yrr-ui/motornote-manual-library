DTC P1CCD-96: Sub DC/DC Converter (For Charging) Component Internal Failure: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. - 

    Outline of diagnosis: 

    The following conditions were met 2 times during charging.*

    An error occurred at the sub DCDC converter, and the Battery (12V) voltage was 11 V or less.

    *: A DTC is not output when the above conditions are satisfied during the first charging. The DTC is output at the time of the second charging.

    (1 trip detection logic)

    Trouble symptom: 

    -

    Wiring diagram: 

    Hybrid system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (HEV))>HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to PLUG-IN BATTERY CHARGER (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK DTC. 

    Read the DTC of [Plug-in Battery Charger] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to PLUG-IN BATTERY CHARGER (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    NOTE:
    Malfunction contents System Related DTC
    Microcomputer malfunction Plug-in Battery Charger P060649 PLUG-IN CONTROL MODULE INTERNAL ELECTRONIC FAILURE
    P060B49 PLUG-IN CONTROL MODULE A/D PROCESSING INTERNAL ELECTRONIC FAILURE
    P0E5E49 HYBRID/EV BATTERY CHARGER CONTROL MODULE INTERNAL ELECTRONIC FAILURE
    P0E5E87 PLUG-IN CONTROL MODULE PROCESSOR FROM HYBRID/EV BATTERY CHARGER CONTROL MODULE PROCESSOR MISSING MESSAGE
    Electrical system malfunction P1CED1C PLUG-IN CONTROL ECU POWER SUPPLY (+12.5V) CIRCUIT VOLTAGE OUT OF RANGE
    P1CF01C PLUG-IN CONTROL ECU POWER SUPPLY (-7.5V) CIRCUIT VOLTAGE OUT OF RANGE
    P1CF31C PLUG-IN CONTROL ECU POWER SUPPLY (+1.15V) CIRCUIT VOLTAGE OUT OF RANGE
    P319414 INTERNAL CONTROL MODULE CIRCUIT SHORT TO GROUND OR OPEN
    Communication system malfunction U115087 LOST COMMUNICATION WITH HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL MODULE (HYBRID/EV BATTERY LOCAL BUS) MISSING MESSAGE

    If only DTC P1CCD96 is displayed, or a DTC other than those shown in the above reference is displayed, go to  A.

    If a DTC shown in the reference above is displayed at the same time, go to  B.

    Yes: 

    1. Go to  2.
    2. Perform the diagnosis for the DTC that is displayed at the same time.
      NOTE: If multiple DTC are displayed at the same time, perform diagnosis in the order that the DTC are listed in the reference above.

    No:  -

  2. CHECK FUSE (AMD2). 
    1. Remove the SBF-9 30A FUSE from the Main fuse box (M/B).
    2. Check SBF-9 30A FUSE. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>RELAY AND FUSE .

      Is the fuse OK?

      Yes:  Go to  3.

      No:  Replace the fuse.

  3. CHECK THE CONNECTION CONDITIONS OF THE CONNECTOR (Electric vehicle charger assembly CONNECTOR). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Check that the Service plug is disconnected. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>SERVICE PLUG .
    2. Check the connection conditions of Electric vehicle charger assembly connector (R516), and check the terminals for contact pressure, deformation, and intrusion of water or foreign substance.

      Is the connector connection loose or is there poor contact at the terminals, or intruding water or foreign substance?

      Yes:  Connect the connector securely. Or repair the poor contact of connector.

      No:  Go to  4.

  4. CHECK THE Electric vehicle charger assembly (AMD2 VOLTAGE). 
    CAUTION: Make sure to wear insulated gloves and protective goggles during the work.
    1. Connect the negative terminal of the Battery (12V). Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    2. Using a tester, measure the voltage between Electric vehicle charger assembly connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (R516) No. 1 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

      Is the voltage 11 - 14 V?

      Yes:  Replace Electric vehicle charger assembly. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>ELECTRIC VEHICLE CHARGER .

      No:  Repair or replace the Wire harness between the Electric vehicle charger assembly connector and chassis ground.
