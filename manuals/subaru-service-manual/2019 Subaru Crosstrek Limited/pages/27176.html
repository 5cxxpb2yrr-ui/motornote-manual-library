Inspection Of Each System

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Below are shown the inspection range and main inspection contents for each system inspection.

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
INSPECTION CONTENTS OF EACH SYSTEM INSPECTION

System inspection Possibility of malfunction Contents of inspection
Cooling System Temperature is extremely high.
  • Grille clogged
  • Coolant level
  • Check if there is a possibility that the coolant has frozen when a malfunction occurs.
  • Coolant hose clogged
  • HV radiator fan operating
Shut Down Signal Circuit Shutdown signal Open or short circuit of shutdown signal communication line between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly and Motor generator control ECU
Inverter low-voltage circuit Power supply from +B voltage or communication between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly and Motor generator control ECU
  • Open or short circuit of communication line between Hybrid vehicle control ECU assembly and Motor generator control ECU
  • Open or short circuit of +B and GND line
  • Fuse blown state
HV BATTERY HIGH-VOLTAGE LINE CIRCUIT High voltage power supply from HV battery Open or short circuit of "system main relay, connector and cable"
MOTOR RESOLVER CIRCUIT Motor resolver signal Open or short circuit of "Motor resolver body, connector and cable"
GENERATOR RESOLVER CIRCUIT Generator resolver signal Open or short circuit of "Generator resolver body, connector and cable"
MOTOR HIGH-VOLTAGE CIRCUIT Motor output Open or short circuit of "motor body, T/M bus bar, terminal block and cable"
GENERATOR HIGH-VOLTAGE CIRCUIT Generator output Open or short circuit of "generator body, T/M bus bar, terminal block and cable"
