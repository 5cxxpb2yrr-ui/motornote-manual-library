Fuel Insulator: Installation

  1. Install the fuel insulator.
    CAUTION:
    • If substantial amount of fuel or parts cleaner come into contact with the fuel insulator, replace it with a new one.
    • If only small amount of fuel comes into contact with the fuel insulator, wait for the fuel evaporate before installing it.
  2. Install the intake manifold assembly. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>INTAKE MANIFOLD ASSEMBLY>INSTALLATION .
  3. Install the air intake boot. Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>Air Intake Boot>INSTALLATION .
  4. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
