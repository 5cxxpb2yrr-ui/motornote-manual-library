Roof Rail: Removal

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery and wait for at least 60 seconds before starting work. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the trim panel - roof. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>ROOF TRIM>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the bolts (2 places) securing the curtain airbag module.
    CAUTION:
    • Do not remove the clips securing the curtain airbag module. If any of the clips are removed, the curtain airbag module may not deploy properly in collision. If the clip is removed, replace the curtain airbag module assembly.
    • Before handling the airbag system components, refer to "CAUTION" of "General Description" in "AIRBAG SYSTEM". Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
    • Be careful not to damage the curtain airbag module during removal.

  4. Remove the six attachment nuts and remove the roof rail.

  5. Remove the gasket. (Except for hybrid model)
  6. Remove the protector and seal washer. (Hybrid model)
