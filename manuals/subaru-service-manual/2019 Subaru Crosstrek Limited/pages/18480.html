DTC B2827: HEV Accelerator Pedal Position Sensor

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

DTC detecting condition: 

  • Defective accelerator sensor

Trouble symptom: 

  • All functions of EyeSight system do not operate.
  • EyeSight warning light illuminates.
    NOTE: Check the hybrid vehicle control computer when this DTC is displayed. Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .
