Fuel Filler Pipe: Removal

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
WARNING: Place "NO OPEN FLAMES" signs near the working area.
CAUTION: Be careful not to spill fuel.
NOTE: For the removal procedure of fuel filler lid, refer to "Fuel filler lid". Refer to SECURITY AND LOCKS>REMOTE OPENERS>REMOVAL>FUEL LID>HYBRID MODEL .
  1. Release the fuel pressure. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL>PROCEDURE>RELEASING OF FUEL PRESSURE .
  2. Drain fuel. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL>PROCEDURE>DRAINING FUEL (WITH SUBARU SELECT MONITOR) .
  3. Open the fuel filler lid and remove the fuel filler cap.
  4. Turn the fuel filler pipe protector in the direction of the arrow to release the lock (A) and remove it.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the rear sub frame assembly. Refer to REAR SUSPENSION>SUB FRAME>REMOVAL .
  6. Remove the mud guard - rear RH. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>MUD GUARD>REMOVAL .
  7. Disconnect the fuel filler hose (A) from the fuel filler pipe assembly.
  8. Disconnect the circulate tube assembly (B) from the fuel filler pipe assembly. Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>FUEL DELIVERY AND EVAPORATION LINES>REMOVAL>QUICK CONNECTOR DISCONNECTION (TYPE F) .
  9. Remove the bolts which secure the fuel filler pipe assembly.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  10. Remove the nuts which secure the fuel filler pipe assembly, and remove the fuel filler pipe assembly from underneath of the vehicle.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
