Select Lever: Installation

  1. Set the select lever assembly to the vehicle body.
  2. Tighten the four mounting bolts to attach the select lever assembly to the vehicle body.

    Tightening torque:

    18 N.m (1.8 kgf-m, 13.3 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Connect the harness connector.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Secure the cut carpet with double sided tape or touch fastener.
  5. Install the harness clip to the select lever assembly.
  6. Shift the select lever to "N" range.
  7. Install the panel center LWR LH and RH. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>CONSOLE BOX>INSTALLATION .
  8. Install the front console panel. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>CONSOLE BOX>INSTALLATION .
  9. Accessing from the button side of the grip, attach the clamp grip pin.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  10. Install the cover grip AT securely. (Model with gate shifter)
    NOTE: After installation, check that the cover grip AT cannot be detached.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  11. Insert the grip assembly to the select lever and press it down until a click is heard.
    NOTE: For the model with boot shifter, insert the grip assembly while holding with your fingers so that the clamp grip pin does not come out.
    • Model with boot shifter
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • Model with gate shifter
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  12. Securely install the boot assembly. (Model with boot shifter)
    NOTE: After installation, check that the boot assembly does not come off.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  13. After installation of grip assembly, check the following points.
    • The grip assembly will not come off.
    • The button on the grip operates normally.
  14. Install the console box. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>CONSOLE BOX>INSTALLATION .
  15. Shift the select lever to "N" range.
  16. Lift up the vehicle.
  17. Connect the select cable to the transmission main body.
    CAUTION: Do not apply excessive load while holding the part (a).

    Tightening torque:

    25 N.m (2.5 kgf-m, 18.4 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • Except for Crosstrek hybrid model
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • Crosstrek hybrid model
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  18. Adjust the select cable position. Refer to CONTROL SYSTEMS>SELECT CABLE>ADJUSTMENT .
  19. After adjustment, confirm that the select lever operates properly at all range positions using the shift lock release button.
  20. Install the inhibitor cover. (Crosstrek hybrid model)

    Tightening torque:

    7.5 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.5 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  21. Install the center exhaust cover.

    Tightening torque:

    18 N.m (1.8 kgf-m, 13.3 ft-lb)

  22. Lower the vehicle.
  23. Connect the battery ground terminal. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  24. Inspect the following items. If a malfunction is found in the inspection, adjust the select cable or inhibitor switch.
    1. The shift lock operates normally. Refer to CONTROL SYSTEMS>AT SHIFT LOCK CONTROL SYSTEM>INSPECTION>SHIFT LOCK OPERATION .
    2. Starter motor starts when the select lever is in "P" or "N" range, but not in other ranges.
    3. Back-up light illuminates when the select lever is in the "R" range, but not in other ranges.
    4. Check the parking lock operation when the select lever is in "P" range.
    5. Select lever and indicator positions are matched.
