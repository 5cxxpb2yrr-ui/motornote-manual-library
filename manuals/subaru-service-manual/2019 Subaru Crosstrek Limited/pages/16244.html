Main Relay: Inspection

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Check that there is no deformation, cracks or other damages.
  2. Check the resistance between terminals.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Terminal No. Inspection conditions Standard
    1 - 3 Always 1 MΩ or more
    1 - 3 Apply battery voltage between terminals 2 and 4. Less than 1 Ω
