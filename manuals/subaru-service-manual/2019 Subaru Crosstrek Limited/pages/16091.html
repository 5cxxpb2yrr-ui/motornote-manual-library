Water Tank Pipe Assembly

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
CAUTION: If the engine coolant is spilt over the engine or the exhaust pipe, completely wipe it off to avoid emission of smoke or causing a fire.
  1. When water hose (A) and water hose (B) were replaced
    1. Connect the water hose (A).

      Refer to EMISSION CONTROL (AUX. EMISSION CONTROL DEVICES) (H4DO (HEV))>PCV CONNECTOR>INSTALLATION .

    2. Connect the water hose (B).
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Install the filler neck to the water tank pipe assembly.

    Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>WATER PIPE>INSTALLATION>FILLER NECK .

  3. Install the engine coolant temperature sensor to the water tank pipe assembly.

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>ENGINE COOLANT TEMPERATURE SENSOR>INSTALLATION .

  4. Connect the preheater hose to the water tank pipe assembly.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Set new O-rings.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Connect the water tank pipe assembly to the water hose (A) and install the bolt that holds the water tank pipe assembly.

    Tightening torque: 

    6.4 N.m (0.7 kgf-m, 4.7 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Connect the water hose (A), over flow hose (B), radiator inlet hose (C) and heater inlet hose (D).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Connect the connector (D).
  9. Connect the connector (A) and install the clip (B) and clip (C).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  10. Install the high-pressure fuel delivery pipe assembly.

    Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (HEV))>HIGH PRESSURE FUEL DELIVERY PIPE>INSTALLATION>HIGH-PRESSURE FUEL DELIVERY PIPE ASSEMBLY .

  11. Connect the battery ground terminal.

    Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .

  12. Fill engine coolant. (Engine side)

    Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>ENGINE COOLANT>REPLACEMENT>FILLING OF ENGINE COOLANT>ENGINE SIDE .
