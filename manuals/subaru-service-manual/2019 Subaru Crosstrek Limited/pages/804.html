For C5

Diagnosis start condition: 

  • Battery voltage is 10 V or more.
  • The remote engine starter was not used to start the engine.

DTC detecting condition: 

Communication failure between immobilizer control module and ECM

Trouble symptom: 

  • Engine will not start.
  • The security indicator illuminates.

Wiring diagram: 

Immobilizer system Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (IMPREZA MODEL)>IMMOBILIZER SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM . Refer to WIRING SYSTEM (CROSSTREK MODEL (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>IMMOBILIZER SYSTEM>WIRING DIAGRAM .

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
CAUTION: When the immobilizer control module or ECM is replaced, registration of the immobilizer system is required. For details, refer to the "REGISTRATION INSTRUCTIONS FOR IMMOBILIZER" provided as a separate volume.
  1. CHECK DTC. 

    Read the DTC of [Immobilizer] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to IMMOBILIZER (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is DTC for [Immobilizer] displayed? (Current malfunction)

    Yes:  Perform the diagnosis for the displayed DTCs.

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK GROUNDING POINT. 

    Check the grounding point of the engine ground and chassis ground.

    Is there any looseness? Are there any foreign matters caught on the ground or contact surface? Are there any oil-soiled areas?

    Yes:  Remove the foreign matters, and tighten to the specified torque. Then start the engine, and check that the fault was removed. Read the DTC Refer to IMMOBILIZER (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) ., and when DTC B1572 is still displayed, Go to  3.

    No:  Go to  3.

  3. CHECK FUSE. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Check the fuse.

      Is the check result OK?

      Yes:  Go to  4.

      No:  Replace the fuse. If the fuse blows out easily, repair the short circuit to power supply in harness between the battery and immobilizer control module.

  4. CHECK DATA MONITOR. 

    Using the Subaru Select Monitor, check the following items in the [Data monitor] of [Immobilizer]. Refer to IMMOBILIZER (DIAGNOSTICS)>DATA MONITOR .

    • Battery Voltage

    Is the display 10 V or more?

    Yes:  Go to  7.

    No:  Go to  5.

  5. CHECK GROUND CIRCUIT FOR ECM (OPEN CIRCUIT). 
    1. Disconnect the ECM connector.
    2. Measure the resistance between the ECM connector and engine ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (B134) No. 69 - Engine ground:

      (B134) No. 70 - Engine ground:

      (B134) No. 72 - Engine ground:

      (B134) No. 71 - Engine ground:

      Is the resistance less than 10 Ω?

      Yes:  Go to  6.

      No:  Repair the open circuit in harness between ECM connector and chassis ground.

  6. CHECK IMMOBILIZER control module BATTERY POWER SUPPLY CIRCUIT. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Disconnect the immobilizer control module connector.
    3. Measure the voltage between immobilizer control module connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (i34) No. 1 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

      Is the voltage 10 V or more?

      Yes:  Go to  7.

      No:  Repair the open or short circuit of harness between the immobilizer control module connector and fuse.

  7. CHECK IMMOBILIZER control module IGN POWER SUPPLY CIRCUIT. 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    2. Measure the voltage between immobilizer control module connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (i34) No. 2 (+) - Chassis ground (-):

      Is the voltage 10 V or more?

      Yes:  Go to  8.

      No:  Repair the open or short circuit of harness between the immobilizer control module connector and ignition switch.

  8. CHECK IMMOBILIZER control module GROUND CIRCUIT (OPEN CIRCUIT). 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Measure the resistance between immobilizer control module connector and chassis ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (i34) No. 5 - Chassis ground:

      Is the resistance less than 10 Ω?

      Yes:  Go to  9.

      No:  Repair the open circuit of harness between the immobilizer control module connector and chassis ground.

  9. CHECK HARNESS BETWEEN IMMOBILIZER control module AND ECM (OPEN CIRCUIT). 

    Measure the resistance between immobilizer control module connector and ECM connector.

    Connector & terminal 

    (i34) No. 12 - (B134) No. 6:

    Is the resistance less than 10 Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  10.

    No:  Repair the open circuit of harness between the immobilizer control module connector and ECM.

  10. CHECK COMMUNICATION LINE HARNESS (SHORT CIRCUIT TO POWER SUPPLY). 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    2. Measure the voltage between ECM connector and engine ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (B134) No. 6 (+) - Engine ground (-):

      Is the voltage 6 V or more?

      Yes:  Repair the short circuit to power supply in harness between immobilizer control module connector and ECM connector.

      No:  Go to  11.

  11. CHECK COMMUNICATION CIRCUIT HARNESS (SHORT CIRCUIT TO GROUND). 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Measure the resistance between immobilizer control module connector and each ground.

      Connector & terminal 

      (i34) No. 12 - Chassis ground:

      (I34) No. 12 - Engine ground:

      Is the resistance less than 10 Ω?

      Yes:  Repair the short circuit to ground in harness between immobilizer control module connector and ECM connector.

      No:  Go to  12.

  12. CHECK ECM. 
    1. Replace the ECM. (Do not perform ECM registration.) Refer to FUEL INJECTION (FUEL SYSTEMS) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>ENGINE CONTROL MODULE (ECM) .
    2. Connect all connectors.
    3. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    4. Wait for 5 seconds.
    5. Read the DTC of [Engine Control System] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to IMMOBILIZER (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

      Is DTC B1572 displayed? (Current malfunction)

      Yes:  Replace the immobilizer control module. Refer to SECURITY AND LOCKS>IMMOBILIZER CONTROL MODULE .

      No:  Malfunction occurred in ECM. Register the ECM. Refer to the "REGISTRATION INSTRUCTIONS FOR IMMOBILIZER" provided as a separate volume.
