Sunroof Assembly: Removal

CAUTION:
  • Before handling the airbag system components, refer to "CAUTION" of "General Description" in "AIRBAG SYSTEM". Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  • When removing the clip, use great care not to damage the trim panel - roof assembly.
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery and wait for at least 60 seconds before starting work. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    NOTE: Completely close the lid assembly - sunroof, then disconnect the ground terminal.
  2. Remove the light assembly - map. (Models without EyeSight) Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>SPOT MAP LIGHT>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the stereo camera cover assembly. (Models with EyeSight) Refer to EYESIGHT>STEREO CAMERA>REMOVAL .
  4. Disconnect the connector of the motor assembly - sunroof.
  5. Remove the trim panel - roof assembly.
    • 4 door model: Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>ROOF TRIM>REMOVAL>4 DOOR MODEL .
    • 5 door model: Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>ROOF TRIM>REMOVAL>5 DOOR MODEL .
  6. Remove the drain tube - sunroof.
    NOTE: When removing the drain tube - sunroof completely, perform the step 8).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Remove the rail assembly - sunroof.
    1. Remove the bolt, and then remove the rail assembly - sunroof.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Remove the bolts (2 places) securing the curtain airbag module.
      CAUTION:
      • Do not remove the clips securing the curtain airbag module. If any of the clips are removed, the curtain airbag module may not deploy properly in collision. If the clip is removed, replace the curtain airbag module assembly.
      • Before handling the airbag system components, refer to "CAUTION" of "General Description" in "AIRBAG SYSTEM". Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
      • Be careful not to damage the curtain airbag module during removal.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Remove the bolts and then remove the sunroof bracket.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Remove the drain tube - sunroof.
NOTE: For easy replacement work, attach a string when pulling the drain tube - sunroof.
  • Front side
    1. Remove the cover assembly - instrument panel LWR driver. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>INSTRUMENT PANEL LOWER COVER>REMOVAL .
    2. Remove the glove box assembly. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>GLOVE BOX>REMOVAL .
    3. Turn over the toe board insulator while avoiding to touch the harness around the drain tube.
    4. Remove the clips, and turn over the rear side of the mud guard - front.
      NOTE: Remove the front wheels as necessary.
    5. Remove the front side of drain tube - sunroof.
    6. Remove the drain tube - sunroof.
  • Rear side
    1. Remove the mat - trunk and trim panel - trunk side. (4 door model) Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>TRUNK ROOM TRIM>REMOVAL .
    2. Remove the bumper face - rear.
    3. Remove the rear side of drain tube - sunroof.
    4. Remove the drain tube - sunroof.
