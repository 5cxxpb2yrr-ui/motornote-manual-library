Control Panel: Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Disconnect the battery ground terminal and wait for at least 60 seconds before starting work. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the grille assembly - CTR ventilation. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>AIR VENT GRILLE>REMOVAL>CENTER GRILLE ASSEMBLY .
  3. Remove the audio assembly or navigation assembly. Refer to ENTERTAINMENT & MONITORING>AUDIO>REMOVAL .
  4. Remove the heater control assembly.
    1. Disconnect the connector.
    2. Press the heater control assembly from behind to release the clips and claws, and then remove the heater control assembly.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Remove the screws and remove the panel assembly - heater.
