CROSSTREK Model

  1. Remove the clips and remove the garnish assembly - side sill.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Remove the garnish assembly at the fender and rear quarter portions.
    CAUTION: Do not reuse the garnish assembly - fender and the garnish assembly - rear quarter that have been deformed during removal.
    1. Attach the protective tape to the periphery of the garnish.
    2. Heat the garnish section for 3 - 5 minutes to 40°C - 60°C using a heat light or a drier.
      CAUTION: Be careful not to burn yourself.
    3. Separate the bonded part of double sided tape with a nylon cord or equivalent.
    4. Remove the clips, and turn over the bumper face - front and the bumper face - rear.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    5. Disengage the clips and remove the garnish assembly - fender and the garnish assembly - rear quarter.
