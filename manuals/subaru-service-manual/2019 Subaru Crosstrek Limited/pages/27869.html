PCV Connector: Installation

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. By using new O-rings, install the PCV connector, and connect the water hose and PCV hose No. 2.

    Tightening torque: 

    6.4 N.m (0.7 kgf-m, 4.7 ft-lb)

  2. Install the electric compressor assembly,

    Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>COMPRESSOR>INSTALLATION .

  3. Connect the battery ground terminal,

    Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .

  4. Fill the engine coolant (engine side),

    Refer to COOLING (H4DO (HEV))>ENGINE COOLANT>REPLACEMENT>FILLING OF ENGINE COOLANT>ENGINE SIDE .
