DTC B28B2: Stereo Camera High Temperature

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Detected when the temperature of the stereo camera excessively increases.

NOTE: When this DTC is detected, performing adjustment or inspection of the camera will not return it to the normal state. In this case, always replace the stereo camera. Refer to EYESIGHT>STEREO CAMERA>REMOVAL .
