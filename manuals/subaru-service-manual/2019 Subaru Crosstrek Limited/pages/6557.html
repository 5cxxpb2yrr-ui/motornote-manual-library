Starting System & Charging System (Except For HEV): Starter: Disassembly

CAUTION: The starter should be placed through a no-load test whenever it has been overhauled.
  1. Disconnect the cable from the M terminal.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Remove the magnet switch assembly.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the bolt (A) and through bolts (B), and remove the starter cover.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the brush holder assembly.
    NOTE: Hold the brush with your fingers so that the brush spring does not come flying out.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the armature assembly and yoke assembly together as a single unit.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Separate the armature assembly and yoke assembly.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Remove the pinion gear.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Remove the seal rubber (A) and plate (B).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  9. Lightly tap the starter housing with a plastic hammer as shown in the figure, and remove the overrunning clutch, internal gear, shaft and shift lever together as one unit.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  10. Use the following procedures to remove the overrunning clutch.
    1. Set an appropriate tool (suitable attachment, etc.), and remove the stopper from snap ring by lightly tapping the stopper with a plastic hammer.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Remove the snap ring (A), and remove the stopper (B).
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Remove the overrunning clutch.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  11. Remove the internal gear.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
