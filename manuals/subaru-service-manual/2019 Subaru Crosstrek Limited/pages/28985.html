Symptom Table

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
Symptoms Diagnosis procedure
1 Air inlet selection indicator blinks. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>AIR INLET SELECTION INDICATOR BLINKS .
2 Nothing is displayed on the screen or indicators do not illuminate. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>NOTHING IS DISPLAYED ON THE SCREEN OR INDICATORS DO NOT ILLUMINATE .
3 Air conditioner does not stop even when the OFF switch is pressed. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>AIR CONDITIONER DOES NOT STOP EVEN WHEN THE OFF SWITCH IS PRESSED .
4 The windshield glass is not cleared even when the DEF switch is pressed. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>THE WINDSHIELD GLASS IS NOT CLEARED EVEN WHEN THE DEF SWITCH IS PRESSED .
5 Cold air not emitted. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>COLD AIR NOT EMITTED .
6 Warm air not emitted. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>WARM AIR NOT EMITTED .
7 Compartment temperature is excessively lower than setting temperature. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>COMPARTMENT TEMPERATURE IS EXCESSIVELY LOWER THAN SETTING TEMPERATURE .
8 Compartment temperature is excessively higher than setting temperature. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>COMPARTMENT TEMPERATURE IS EXCESSIVELY HIGHER THAN SETTING TEMPERATURE .
9 Air does not come out, or airflow capacity is insufficient. (Blower motor does not rotate) Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>AIR DOES NOT COME OUT, OR AIRFLOW CAPACITY IS INSUFFICIENT .
10 Air goes out of control. (Blower motor rotates at high speed) Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>AIR GOES OUT OF CONTROL .
11 Cold air does not come out even when the A/C switch is pressed. The glass cannot be defogged. (Compressor does not operate.) Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>COLD AIR DOES NOT COME OUT EVEN WHEN THE A/C SWITCH IS PRESSED. THE GLASS CANNOT BE DEFOGGED. (COMPRESSOR DOES NOT OPERATE (ELECTRIC)) .
12 Unable to switch suction vents. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>UNABLE TO SWITCH SUCTION VENTS .
13 Unable to switch blow vents. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>UNABLE TO SWITCH VENTS .
14 Illumination does not come on, or cannot dim. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>ILLUMINATION DOES NOT COME ON OR CANNOT DIM .
15 Remote climate control (RCC) does not operate normally. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>DIAGNOSTICS WITH PHENOMENON>INSPECTION>REMOTE CLIMATE CONTROL .
