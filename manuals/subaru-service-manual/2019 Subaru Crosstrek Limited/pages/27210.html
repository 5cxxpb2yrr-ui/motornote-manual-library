Explanation For Circuits

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Motor generator control ECU detects the HV coolant temperature with the coolant temperature sensor, which are built in Inverter with converter assembly, and also checks cooling efficiency of the inverter cooling system and controls to prevent overheat of the inverter. Motor generator control ECU detects sensor property malfunction based on the HV coolant temperature sensor value. Also, it detects a connection error of the HV coolant temperature sensor.
