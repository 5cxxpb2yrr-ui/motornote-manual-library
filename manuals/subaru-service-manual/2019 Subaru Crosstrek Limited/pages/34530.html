Radiator Fan and Fan Motor Assembly: Removal

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  2. Remove the V-belt covers.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the under cover - front. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>FRONT UNDER COVER>REMOVAL .
  4. Remove the lower side bolts (A) securing the radiator upper frame.
  5. Disconnect main fan motor connector (B) and sub fan motor connector (C).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Disconnect the over flow hose.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  7. Remove the reservoir tank. Refer to COOLING (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>RESERVOIR TANK>REMOVAL .
  8. Remove the air intake duct. Refer to INTAKE (INDUCTION) (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>AIR INTAKE DUCT>REMOVAL .
  9. Remove the front bumper. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>FRONT BUMPER>REMOVAL .
  10. Remove the cover UPR - front.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  11. Remove the bracket - bumper UPR.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  12. Remove the front hood lock assembly. Refer to SECURITY AND LOCKS>FRONT HOOD LOCK ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL .
  13. Disconnect the connector from the horn, and remove the clip.
    • Impreza model
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    • Crosstrek model
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  14. Remove the upper side bolts securing the radiator upper frame, and remove the radiator upper frame.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  15. Remove the radiator upper cushion.
  16. Disengage the claws and remove the radiator fan and fan motor assembly.
Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
