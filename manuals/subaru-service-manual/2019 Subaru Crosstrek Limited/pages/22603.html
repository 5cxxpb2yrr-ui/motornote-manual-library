HYPOID Driven Gear Backlash

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

Using a dial gauge, check the backlash of hypoid driven gear.

Specification: 

0.10 - 0.20 mm (0.004 - 0.008 in)

If it is outside the standard value, adjust the side bearing preload and repair if necessary.
