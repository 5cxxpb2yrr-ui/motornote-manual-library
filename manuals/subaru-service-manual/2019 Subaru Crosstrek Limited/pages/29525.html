Section 11 (CVT Transaxle (Th2A)): Flywheel: Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Remove the transmission body.

    Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TH2A)>TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL .

  2. Remove the damper (with torque limiter).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the flywheel and crankshaft position sensor plate.
    1. Attach the ST.

      Preparation tool: 

      ST: CRANKSHAFT STOPPER (498497100)

    2. Remove the bolt securing the flywheel.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    3. Remove the flywheel and crankshaft position sensor plate.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
