Front Hood: Adjustment

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

Adjust the clearance around the hood COMPL - front as follows.

Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
Part Standard
a Hood COMPL - front to Bumper face - front 3.5±1.7 mm (0.14±0.07 in)
b Surface level gap between Hood COMPL - front and Bumper face - front 0.7±1.2 mm (0.03±0.05 in)
c Hood COMPL - front to Bumper face - front 3.5±1.7 mm (0.14±0.07 in)
d Surface level gap between Hood COMPL - front and Bumper face - front 1±1.2 mm (0.04±0.05 in)
e Hood COMPL - front to Light assembly - head 3±1.8 mm (0.12±0.07 in)
f Hood COMPL - front to Fender - front 3.5±1 mm (0.14±0.04 in)
g Surface level gap: Hood COMPL - front to Fender - front 0±1 mm (0±0.04 in)
h Hood COMPL - front to Fender - front 3±1 mm (0.12±0.04 in)
i Surface level gap: Hood COMPL - front to Fender - front 0±1 mm (0±0.04 in)
J Hood COMPL - front to Fender - front 4±1 mm (0.16±0.04 in)
k Surface level gap: Hood COMPL - front to Fender - front 0.5±1 mm (0.02±0.04 in)
  1. Adjust the horizontal position of the hood COMPL - front.
    1. Loosen the attaching nuts of the hinge COMPL - front hood on the left and right sides.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Adjust the horizontal clearance of the hood COMPL - front, and tighten the attaching nuts of the hinge COMPL - front hood.

      Tightening torque: 

      25 N.m (2.55 kgf-m, 18.4 ft-lb)

  2. Adjust the front end height of the hood COMPL - front.
    1. Loosen the bolts on the lock assembly - front hood.
    2. Adjust the lock assembly - front hood by moving it up and down.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.

      Tightening torque: 

      33 N.m (3.4 kgf-m, 24 ft-lb)

  3. Rotate the buffer - front hood of the hood COMPL - front to adjust the height.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
