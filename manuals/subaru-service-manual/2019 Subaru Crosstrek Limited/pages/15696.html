Brake Pedal

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

CVT MODEL 

CAUTION:
  • Before handling the airbag system components, always refer to "CAUTION" of "General Description" in "AIRBAG SYSTEM". Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  • Perform linear valve offset learning after the brake pedal was removed and reinstalled or replaced. If learning was not performed, brake control will be prohibited (brake pedal will be heavy), a DTC is stored, the brake warning light illuminates, or other problems will occur. (Hybrid model)
  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery and wait for at least 60 seconds before starting work. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY>GROUND TERMINAL REMOVAL OR INSTALLATION .
  2. Remove the cover assembly - instrument panel LWR driver. Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>INSTRUMENT PANEL LOWER COVER>REMOVAL .
  3. Remove the knee airbag module. Refer to AIRBAG SYSTEM>KNEE AIRBAG MODULE>REMOVAL .
  4. Remove the universal joint assembly - steering. Refer to POWER ASSISTED SYSTEM (POWER STEERING)>UNIVERSAL JOINT>REMOVAL .
    CAUTION: To prevent damage to the universal joint assembly - steering and improper steering effort, make sure to remove the universal joint assembly - steering.
  5. Remove the column assembly - steering. Refer to POWER ASSISTED SYSTEM (POWER STEERING)>STEERING COLUMN>REMOVAL .
  6. Remove the duct - foot driver.
    • Except for hybrid model: Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>HEATER DUCT>REMOVAL .
    • Hybrid model: Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (HEV)>HEATER DUCT>REMOVAL .
  7. Remove the hybrid powertrain control module. (Hybrid model) Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL MODULE>REMOVAL .
  8. Remove the brake pedal assembly.
    1. Disconnect the stop light switch connector.
    2. Disconnect the brake stroke sensor connector. (Hybrid model)
    3. Remove the snap pin (a) and clevis pin (b), and remove the operating rod from the brake pedal.
      CAUTION:
      • Be careful not to apply excessive force to the operating rod when handling the operating rod.

        The angle may change by ±3°, and it may result in damage to power piston cylinder.

      • Do not change the push rod length.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    4. Remove the bolt and nut, and then detach the brake pedal assembly.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.

    MT model 

    NOTE: Brake pedal is integrated with the clutch pedal.

    For removal procedures of the brake pedal, refer to Clutch service information. Refer to CLUTCH SYSTEM>CLUTCH PEDAL>REMOVAL .
