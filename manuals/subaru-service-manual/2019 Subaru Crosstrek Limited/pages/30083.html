Release Bearing And Lever: Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Remove the manual transmission assembly.
    • 5MT model:

      Refer to MANUAL TRANSMISSION AND DIFFERENTIAL (5MT)>MANUAL TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL .

    • 6MT model:

      Refer to MANUAL TRANSMISSION AND DIFFERENTIAL (6MT)>MANUAL TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY>REMOVAL .

  2. Remove the two clips from the release lever and remove the release bearing.
    CAUTION: Be careful not to deform the clips.
  3. Remove the dust cover.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Remove the attachment point of lever spring by inserting a screwdriver through the clutch housing release lever hole. Then remove the release lever.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
