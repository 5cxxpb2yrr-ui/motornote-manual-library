Passenger's Side Power Window Does Not Work

Trouble causes: 

  • Front regulator & motor assembly (passenger's side) power supply circuit failure
  • Front regulator & motor assembly (passenger's side) failure
  • Power window main switch failure
  • Power window sub-switch malfunction
  • LIN communication error
  • Body integrated unit malfunction

CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to POWER WINDOW (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  1. CHECK BATTERY. 

    Check the battery. Refer to STARTING/CHARGING SYSTEMS (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>BATTERY>INSPECTION .

    Is the battery OK?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Charge or replace the battery.

  2. CHECK DTC. 

    Read the DTC of [Power Window (Passenger)] using the Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to POWER WINDOW (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Are any DTCs displayed? (Current malfunction)

    Yes:  Perform the diagnosis for the displayed DTCs. Refer to POWER WINDOW (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>LIST .

    No:  Go to  3.

  3. CHECK [Window Lock Switch Signal] (DATA MONITOR). 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    2. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, check the following items in the [Data monitor] of [Power Window (Passenger)]. Refer to POWER WINDOW (DIAGNOSTICS)>DATA MONITOR .
      • [Window Lock Switch Signal]

    Is [Unlock] displayed?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Unlock the window lock to operate.

  4. CHECK [Power Supply Voltage] (DATA MONITOR). 
    1. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    2. Using the Subaru Select Monitor, check the following items in the [Data monitor] of [Power Window (Passenger)]. Refer to POWER WINDOW (DIAGNOSTICS)>DATA MONITOR .
      • Power Supply Voltage

    Is the displayed voltage approximately 10.0 V or more?

    Yes:  Go to  5.

    No:  Inspect the body integrated unit. Refer to BODY CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE .

    If no fault is found in the body integrated unit, replace the front regulator & motor assembly (passenger's side). Refer to GLASS/WINDOWS/MIRRORS>FRONT REGULATOR AND MOTOR ASSEMBLY .

  5. CHECK [Operation Enable Signal] (DATA MONITOR). 

    Using the Subaru Select Monitor, check the following items in the [Data monitor] of [Power Window (Passenger)]. Refer to POWER WINDOW (DIAGNOSTICS)>DATA MONITOR .

    • Operation Enable Signal

    Is [ON] displayed?

    Yes:  Go to  6.

    No:  Inspect the body integrated unit. Refer to BODY CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>BASIC DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE .

    If no fault is found in the body integrated unit, replace the front regulator & motor assembly (passenger's side). Refer to GLASS/WINDOWS/MIRRORS>FRONT REGULATOR AND MOTOR ASSEMBLY .

  6. CHECK POWER WINDOW SUB-SWITCH. 

    Operate the power window sub-switch and roll down the window to the full open position. Alternatively, operate the power window sub-switch and roll up the window to the full close position.

    Does the window roll up and down?

    Yes:  Go to  7.

    No:  Perform unit inspection of power window sub switch. Refer to GLASS/WINDOWS/MIRRORS>POWER WINDOW CONTROL SWITCH .

  7. CHECK HARNESS (OPEN CIRCUIT). 
    1. Disconnect the power window main switch connector.
    2. Disconnect the front regulator & motor assembly (passenger's side) connector.
    3. Using a tester, measure the resistance between the body integrated unit connector and the front regulator & motor assembly (passenger's side) connector.

      Connector & terminal 

      (i81) No. 2 - (D13) No. 7:

      (D17) No. 1 - (D13) No. 4:

      (D17) No. 5 - (D13) No. 6:

      (D17) No. 6 - (D13) No. 8:

    Is the resistance less than 10 Ω?

    Yes:  Replace the front regulator & motor assembly (passenger's side). Refer to GLASS/WINDOWS/MIRRORS>FRONT REGULATOR AND MOTOR ASSEMBLY .

    No:  Repair the open circuit of harness between the body integrated unit connector and the front regulator & motor assembly (passenger's side) connector.
