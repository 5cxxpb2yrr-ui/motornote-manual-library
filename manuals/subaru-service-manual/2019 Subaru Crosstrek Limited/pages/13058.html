Rear Lateral Link (Front)

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

  1. Lift up the vehicle, and then remove the rear wheels.
  2. Disconnect the cable assembly - parking brake.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Remove the trailing link.

    Refer to REAR SUSPENSION>TRAILING LINK>REMOVAL .

  4. Remove the rear lateral link (front).
    1. Remove the snap pin (a) and nut (b).
    2. Disconnect the rear axle housing and the ball joint (c).

      Preparation tool: 

      Tie-rod ball joint puller

    3. Put alignment marks (f) on the adjusting bolt for the rear lateral link (front) and on the sub frame assembly.
    4. Remove the adjusting bolt (e), and remove the rear lateral link (front).
      CAUTION: When removing the adjusting bolt (e), make sure to fix the bolt head in place when loosening the nut (d).
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
