Front Differential Assembly: Inspection

  • Check each component for scratches, damage or other faults.
  • Check the pinion gear backlash with ST1 and ST2.

    Preparation tool: 

    ST1: MAGNET BASE (498247001)

    ST2: DIAL GAUGE (498247100)

    Specification: 

    0.13 - 0.18 mm (0.0051 - 0.0071 in)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  • Measure the hypoid gear backlash, and then adjust it to be within specification. Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TR580)>FRONT DIFFERENTIAL ASSEMBLY>ADJUSTMENT .
