D Range Position Adjustment

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Release the shift lock using a screwdriver, etc. and shift the select lever to the "N" range.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Remove the inhibitor cover.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Loosen the bolts holding the inhibitor switch.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  4. Rotate the inhibitor switch body to the right.
    NOTE: Adjust so that amount of movement (A) and amount of movement (B) are the same.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Tighten the bolts fastening the inhibitor switch in the sequence shown by the numbers in the figure.

    Tightening torque: 

    13 N.m (1.3 kgf-m, 9.6 ft-lb)

    Fig 1: Inhibitor Switch Bolts Tightening Sequence
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Check the inhibitor switch position.

    Refer to CONTINUOUSLY VARIABLE TRANSMISSION (TH2A)>INHIBITOR SWITCH>INSPECTION>CHECK THE INHIBITOR SWITCH POSITION .

  7. Install the inhibitor cover.

    Tightening torque: 

    7.5 N.m (0.8 kgf-m, 5.5 ft-lb)

    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
