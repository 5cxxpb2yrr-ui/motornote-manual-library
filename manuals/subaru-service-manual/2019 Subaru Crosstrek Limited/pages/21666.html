DTC P0D33-19: DC/DC Converter Circuit Current Above Threshold: Notes

Do you like it dark? Easier on the eyes.

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Detecting condition: 

Boosting converter fail signal detection (circuit malfunction)

Any of the boosting converter system is a target for error detection (inverter, Transmission assembly, Motor generator control ECU, etc.).

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

  • Reduced output power
  • A driving range is limited
  • Engine not stopped
CAUTION: Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to P-HEV DRIVE MOTOR (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
NOTE: For the diagnostic procedure, refer to DTC P0A949E. Refer to P-HEV DRIVE MOTOR (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE WITH DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>DTC P0A949E DC/DC CONVERTER STUCK ON .
