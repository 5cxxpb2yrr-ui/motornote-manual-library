Auto A/C Model

  1. Using the refrigerant recovery system, discharge refrigerant. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>REFRIGERANT RECOVERY PROCEDURE>PROCEDURE .
  2. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
  3. Remove the V-belts. Refer to MECHANICAL (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>V-BELT>REMOVAL .
  4. Disconnect the hose - pressure discharge and the hose - pressure suction.
    1. Remove the bolts and remove the hose - pressure discharge.
    2. Remove the bolts and detach the hose - pressure suction.
      CAUTION: Seal the disconnected hose and engaging part of compressor with a plug or vinyl tape to prevent foreign matter from entering.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  5. Remove the compressor assembly.
    1. Remove the harness clip and connector.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    2. Remove the bolts and remove the compressor assembly.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  6. Remove the bracket - air conditioner.
    1. Remove the bolts and remove the bracket - air conditioner.
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
