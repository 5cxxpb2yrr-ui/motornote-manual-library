Exhaust Cam Sprocket LH

  1. Install the exhaust cam sprocket LH by aligning the knock hole (A) of exhaust cam sprocket LH and the knock pin (B) of exhaust camshaft LH.
    NOTE: Before installation, check that there is no foreign matter on the exhaust cam sprocket LH and exhaust camshaft LH.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  2. Tighten the bolts securing the exhaust cam sprocket LH by hand.
    NOTE:
    • Apply a coat of engine oil to the bolt thread.
    • Tighten the bolts until the exhaust cam sprocket LH contacts the exhaust camshaft LH by hand.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  3. Install timing chain LH. Refer to MECHANICAL (H4DO (EXCEPT FOR HEV))>TIMING CHAIN ASSEMBLY>INSTALLATION>TIMING CHAIN LH .
