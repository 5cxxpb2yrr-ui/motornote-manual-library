Lane Keep (Lane Departure Prevention) Cannot Be Ready (Lane Indicator Does Not Illuminate)/Lane Keep (Lane Departure Prevention) Does Not Operate (Lane Keep Indicator Does Not Illuminate In Green)/Though Lane Keep (Lane Departure Prevention) Is Operating, The Vehicle Departs Out Of The Lane Due To Poor Assistance

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. CHECK FOR PRESENCE OF LANE DEPARTURE PREVENTION DEACTIVATION CODES. 

    Check for the presence of ECM lane departure prevention deactivation codes. Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>LANE DEPARTURE PREVENTION ASSIST DEACTIVATE CODE(S) DISPLAY .

    Is a lane departure prevention deactivation code displayed?

    Yes:  Perform the diagnosis for the displayed lane departure prevention deactivation code. Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>LANE DEPARTURE PREVENTION ASSIST DEACTIVATE CODE(S) DISPLAY .

    No:  Go to  2.

  2. CHECK USING CHECK LIST. 

    Using CHECK LIST (LANE KEEP), check the following and respond to it.

    • Does it apply to the condition that use of lane keeping is not desired?
    • Does it apply to the condition or the object that is hard to be recognized by the stereo camera?
    • Does it apply to the road condition that the white line is difficult to be recognized?

    Is there a relevant item in the check list?

    Yes:  When there is a relevant item, it can be the cause of malfunction. Eliminate the cause, and check that the malfunction has been improved.

    No:  Go to  3.

  3. INSPECTION OF WINDSHIELD GLASS AND DASHBOARD. 

    Check the windshield glass and the dashboard. Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>General Description>INSPECTION>WINDSHIELD GLASS AND DASHBOARD .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Repair or replace the faulty portion according to the inspection result.

  4. INSPECTION OF FRONT WIPER. 

    Check the front wiper. Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>INSPECTION>FRONT WIPER .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  5.

    No:  Repair or replace the faulty portion according to the inspection result.

  5. CHECK OPTICAL AXIS OF HEADLIGHT. 

    Check the optical axis of the headlight. Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>HEADLIGHT ASSEMBLY>ADJUSTMENT .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  6.

    No:  Repair or replace the faulty portion according to the inspection result.

  6. CHECK STEREO CAMERA. 

    Check the stereo camera. Refer to EYESIGHT (DIAGNOSTICS) (HEV)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>INSPECTION>STEREO CAMERA .

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  7.

    No:  Repair or replace the faulty portion according to the inspection result.

  7. CHECK TIRE AND WHEEL ALIGNMENT. 

    Check the tires and wheel alignment.

    Is the check result OK?

    Yes:  Go to  8.

    No:  Repair or replace the faulty portion according to the inspection result.

  8. ADJUST OPTICAL AXIS OF THE STEREO CAMERA. 

    Perform optical axis adjustment of the stereo camera. Perform optical axis adjustment of the stereo camera. Refer to EYESIGHT>CAMERA ADJUSTMENT, INSPECTION>PROCEDURE .

    Was the fault fixed after adjusting the optical axis?

    Yes:  The current state is normal.

    No:  Replace the stereo camera. Refer to EYESIGHT>STEREO CAMERA>REMOVAL .
