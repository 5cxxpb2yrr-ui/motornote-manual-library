CROSSTREK Model

  1. Disconnect the ground terminal from battery. Refer to REPAIR CONTENTS>REPAIR CONTENTS>NOTE>BATTERY .
    NOTE:
    • For CVT models, release the shift lock and shift the select lever to the "N" position. Refer to CONTROL SYSTEMS>SELECT LEVER>REMOVAL .
    • On power seat models, move the front seat all the way forward before disconnecting the battery ground terminal.
  2. Remove the center grille assembly. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>AIR VENT GRILLE>REMOVAL>CENTER GRILLE ASSEMBLY .
  3. Remove the panel - center UPR, and remove the audio assembly or navigation assembly. Refer to ENTERTAINMENT & MONITORING>AUDIO>REMOVAL .
  4. Remove the heater control assembly. Refer to AIR CONDITIONER (EXCEPT FOR HEV)>CONTROL PANEL>REMOVAL .
  5. Remove the boot - hand brake, the cover assembly - front and the console box assembly. (Except for hybrid model) Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>CONSOLE BOX>REMOVAL>CROSSTREK MODEL>EXCEPT FOR HYBRID MODEL .
  6. Remove the cover assembly - front and the console box assembly. (Hybrid model) Refer to EXTERIOR/INTERIOR TRIM>CONSOLE BOX>REMOVAL>CROSSTREK MODEL>HYBRID MODEL .
  7. Remove the left and right panel center LWR.
    1. Remove the screws.
    2. Release the claws and clips, and then pull the panel center LWR to remove.
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
  8. Remove the tray - center UPR.
    1. Disconnect the connector and remove the screw.
    2. Release the claws, and then remove the tray - center UPR.
Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
