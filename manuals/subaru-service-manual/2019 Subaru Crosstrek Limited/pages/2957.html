Hazard Switch: Inspection

  1. Measure the resistance between connector terminals.

    Preparation tool: 

    Circuit tester

    • Manual A/C model
      Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
      Terminal No. Inspection conditions Standard
      12 - 18 Switch OFF 1 MΩ or more
      Switch ON Less than 1 Ω
    • Auto A/C model
    Courtesy of SUBARU OF AMERICA, INC.
    Terminal No. Inspection conditions Standard
    26 - 10 Switch OFF 1 MΩ or more
    Switch ON Less than 1 Ω
  2. Replace the heater control assembly if it is found defective.
