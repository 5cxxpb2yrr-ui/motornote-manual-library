Check Daytime Running Light System (Led Headlight Model)

  1. CHECK FUSE  .
    1. Turn the ignition switch to OFF.
    2. Check the fuse on the upstream of the daytime running light relay.

    Is the fuse OK?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Replace the fuse.

  2. CHECK RELAY  .

    Check the daytime running light relay. Refer to LIGHTING SYSTEM>RELAY AND FUSE>INSPECTION .

    Is the relay OK?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Replace the relay.

  3. CHECK HEADLIGHT ILLUMINATION  .
    1. Turn the ignition switch to ON.
    2. Turn the lighting switch to the switch II (HEAD) position.
    3. Turn the dimmer & passing switch to the switch UP position.

    Does the headlight (high beam) illuminate?

    Yes:  Go to  4.

    No:  Check the combination switch (light) and headlight bulb.

  4. CHECK BODY INTEGRATED UNIT  .

    Read the DTC of body integrated unit using Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to BODY CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC) .

    Is DTC displayed?

    Yes:  Perform the diagnosis according to DTC. Refer to BODY CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>OPERATION .

    No:  Go to  5.

  5. CHECK CURRENT DATA  .

    Display the following items using Subaru Select Monitor. Refer to BODY CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>DATA MONITOR .

    • [Parking Brake Switch Input]
    • [Shift Position]
    • [Lighting II Switch Input]

    Is the input signal normal?

    Yes:  Go to  6.

    No:  Check the defective part.

  6. CHECK DAYTIME RUNNING LIGHT ILLUMINATION  .
    1. Disconnect the headlight connector.
    2. Apply battery voltage to the DRL terminal input of the headlight unit.

      Terminal 

      NO. 7 (+) - NO. 9 (-):

    Does the daytime running light illuminate?

    Yes:  Go to  7.

    No:  Replace the headlight.

  7. CHECK HARNESS  .
    1. Disconnect the connectors of body integrated unit, daytime running light relay and headlight.
    2. Check each harness.

      Connector & terminal 

      Except for hybrid model 

      (B287) No. 5 - (B225) No. 56:

      (B487) No. 7 - (B225) No. 59:

      (B477) No. 7 - (B225) No. 59:

      Hybrid model 

      (B287) No. 5 - (B225) No. 47:

      (B487) No. 7 - (B225) No. 50:

      (B477) No. 7 - (B225) No. 50:

    Is harness normal?

    Yes:  Replace the body integrated unit. Refer to SECURITY AND LOCKS>BODY INTEGRATED UNIT .

    No:  Repair or replace the harness.
