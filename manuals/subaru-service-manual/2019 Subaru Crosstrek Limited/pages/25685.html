"Have High Voltage Battery Inspected At Retailer" Is Displayed

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
CAUTION:
  • Before performing diagnosis, refer to "CAUTION" in "General Description". Refer to P-HEV HYBRID POWERTRAIN CONTROL (DIAGNOSTICS)>GENERAL DESCRIPTION>CAUTION .
  • If "Have high voltage battery inspected at retailer" is displayed on the combination meter, the message remains until the following checks are completed.
  • If the vehicle is used without performing [Battery Diagnosis] even after "Have high voltage battery inspected at retailer" is displayed, the use of HV battery will be restricted (I/O restriction) in a few weeks (depending on usage condition). Further extended use will cause prohibition of READY ON (vehicle start).
  • With [Battery Diagnosis] result normal, perform [Battery Status Info Update].
  • Perform plug-in charging several times under the specified conditions.
  1. CHECK DATA MONITOR (INTERNAL RESISTANCE). 

    Using the Subaru Select Monitor, display the following items in the [Data monitor] of [P-HEV High Voltage Battery]. Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>DATA MONITOR .

    • Hybrid Battery Cell 1 Internal Resistance

      to

    • Hybrid Battery Cell 95 Internal Resistance

    Are the internal resistances all less than 0.006Ω?

    Yes:  Go to  2.

    No:  Replace HV battery. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY .

  2. INTERVIEW. 

    Ask the customer whether he/she wishes [Battery Diagnosis] to be performed.

    NOTE:
    • [Battery Diagnosis] takes about 30 minutes. If battery status information is not acquired, it takes time for plug-in charge and another about 2 hours.
    • Depending on the condition of use of the vehicle, HV battery can be used continuously. However, if the judgment result shows that it is not allowable to use, HV battery must be replaced.

    Will [Battery Diagnosis] be performed?

    Yes:  Go to  3.

    No:  Replace HV battery. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY .

  3. PERFORM WORK SUPPORT (BATTERY DIAGNOSIS). 

    Perform [Battery Diagnosis]. Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>ACTIVE TEST>LIST>BATTERY DIAGNOSIS .

    NOTE:
    • Do not perform battery diagnosis while the hybrid Fail Lamp is illuminated.
    • Perform battery diagnosis with the HV battery installed correctly.
    • Do not perform battery diagnosis while changing from inspection mode to another mode.
    • Make sure to turn the power switch off after battery diagnosis to prevent the Battery (12V) from being discharged.
    • If the learning of SOC (state of charge) has not been performed, perform battery diagnosis after plug-in charging the vehicle as specified.
    • The diagnosis should be performed with the engine coolant temperature at -10°C (14°F) or more.
    • The diagnosis should be performed with HV battery temperature between 0°C (32°F) and 47°C (117°F).
    • The IG OFF timer automatically turns to IG OFF 10 minutes after the end of [Battery Diagnosis]. Therefore check the diagnosis results immediately after [Battery Diagnosis] is ended.
    • In the case of the HV battery in which the remaining capacity of each battery cell varies, the Hybrid Fail Lamp may come on to notify the diagnosis result (the [Battery Diagnosis] ends). In this case, check the DTC of [P-HEV High Voltage Battery], and if the output DTC is P300016, leave it for 10 minutes or more in the READY ON state waiting for the voltage recovery and then clear the DTC.
    • After [Battery Diagnosis] ends, if you leave it in the IG ON state, Hybrid Fail Lamp may come on. In this case, check the DTC of [P-HEV High Voltage Battery], and if the output DTC is P300000, perform diagnosis for P300000. Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE WITH DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>DTC P300000 HYBRID/EV BATTERY DISCHARGE CONTROL MALFUNCTION .

    Is the [Battery Diagnosis] result OK?

    • When "Have high voltage battery inspected at retailer" is displayed on the combination meter, the message will not disappear even if the judgment says [Battery Diagnosis] result is OK. The message disappears after [Battery Status Info Update] is completed.
    • After HV battery is replaced, perform [Battery Status Info Update]. Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>WORK SUPPORT>OPERATION>BATTERY STATUS INFORMATION UPDATE .

    Yes:  Perform [Battery Status Info Update]. Refer to P-HEV HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY (DIAGNOSTICS)>WORK SUPPORT>OPERATION>BATTERY STATUS INFORMATION UPDATE .

    NOTE:
    • Do not perform battery status info update while the hybrid fail lamp is illuminated.
    • Perform battery status info update with the HV battery installed correctly.
    • If [Battery Status Info Update] is performed more than necessary, it may deteriorate the HV battery.
    • Make sure to perform [Battery Status Info Update] if the HV battery is replaced.
    • Make sure to turn the power switch off after battery status info update to prevent the Battery (12V) from being discharged.

    No:  Replace HV battery. Refer to HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLE>HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY .

GENERAL DESCRIPTION 

Explanation for circuits 

The Battery ECU assembly monitors the SOC (state of charge) of the HV battery. When it finds the HV battery has deteriorated excessively, it will display "Have high voltage battery inspected at retailer" on the combination meter. If "Have high voltage battery inspected at retailer" is displayed, perform "Battery Diagnosis" and replace the HV battery as necessary.
