DTC P0A3F-22: Drive Motor "A" Position Sensor Signal Amplitude>Maximum: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Detecting condition: 

Motor resolver out of range

Detects that the Motor resolver signal deviates from the proper value (large amplitude).

(1 trip detection logic)

Trouble symptom: 

  • Greatly reduced output power
  • Driving in reverse not possible
  • A driving range is limited
  • Engine used while driving and engine speed becomes high
NOTE: For the diagnostic procedure, refer to DTC P0A3F21. Refer to P-HEV DRIVE MOTOR (DIAGNOSTICS)>DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE WITH DIAGNOSTIC TROUBLE CODE (DTC)>DTC P0A3F21 DRIVE MOTOR "A" POSITION SENSOR SIGNAL AMPLITUDE < MINIMUM .
